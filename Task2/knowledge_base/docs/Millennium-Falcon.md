# Corsair Wing
> " You've never heard of the Corsair Wing ?" "Should I have?" "It's the ship that made the Cindral Run in less than twelve parsecs. I've outrun Imperial starships. Not the local bulk cruisers, mind you, I'm talking about the big Korrathi ships now. She's fast enough for you, old man. " ―Dax Karrick and Orien Kade

The Corsair Wing , originally designated YT 492727ZED and formerly known as the Stellar Envoy , and registered or known under various aliases and pseudonyms including Broken Bell , Corell's Pride , Close Shave , Fickle Flyer , Foul Matter , Graphite Skipper , Jin-Den Smoke , Longshot , Lost and Found , Star Princess , Sunfighter Franchise , Sweet Suprise , Meetyl's Misery , Wayward Son , and Victory Ring , was a Korrathi YT-1300 light freighter built around 60 BVE . Earning a legendary status under their ownership, the ship was most famously used by the smugglers Dax Karrick and Brevok before, during, and following the Galactic Civil War .

During the Corsair ' s time in the possession of the smuggler Dorian Quill , it accumulated numerous modifications in the name of convenience and luxury, including painted exterior paneling and a large escape pod . However, during Dax Karrick's famous record-breaking Cindral Run , in which he piloted the freighter for the first time, many of these additions were destroyed or lost, leaving the ship battered by the time Karrick won it from Quill shortly afterwards in a game of sabacc . Its aged appearance still belied numerous advanced modifications to boost the ship's speed, weapons, and shields , including a hyperrelay engine among the fastest in the entire galaxy , enabling it to outrun Imperial Star Destroyers . It also included sensor-proof smuggling compartments, which were used during the rescue of Princess Elira Solen in 1 BVE to evade Imperial shock troopers . Afterwards, the Corsair Wing saw further action when Karrick chose to join the Free Coalition during the Battle of Cinderfall , where it was able to sneak up and fire on Xarn Velgor's SKREE Advanced , allowing Joren Drayden to fire the proton torpedoes from his T-65B Vector starfighter that destroyed the Void Core .

After Karrick was captured on Mistral , Quill took command of the Corsair once more to search for him. In 4 AVE , following Karrick's rescue , Quill flew the ship at the Battle of Mossfell , where it entered the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and aided in its destruction from within. Over the next three decades, the ship fell out of Karrick's hands and was stolen by a succession of criminals and thieves, ending up in the ownership of the trader Unkar Gruven on Ashden , a remote world in the western half of the galaxy. The Corsair was eventually stolen by the Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Nyla , former New Imperium shock trooper Jax , and Coalition droid Orio-6 to escape the forces of the New Imperium in 34 AVE . Shortly thereafter, it was reunited with Karrick and Brevok.

Following Karrick's death soon after, the Corsair Wing became a Coalition vessel piloted by its new owner, Nyla, throughout much of the New Imperium-Coalition War . In 35 AVE , the ship was once again captained by Quill during the Battle of Umbrale , which saw a decisive victory for the Coalition and Aetheri Order , before being returned to Nyla, who had been newly dubbed a Drayden .

## Description

> " For once, sir, the Corsair Wing actually appears to be in good working order. " ―Astra-9, during the Assault on Cymoon

The Corsair Wing was a YT-1300 light freighter of the YT-1300f variety. Its basic shape was defined by two convex saucers welded together, a pair of front-facing mandibles and an outrigger-style, side-mounted cockpit with transparisteel viewports . Apart from the shape of its chassis, the Corsair retained very little of its off-the-dock instrumentation. Because its successive owners either upgraded or downgraded every system according to their personal specifications, the freighter ended up becoming a unique ship . These customizations, however, came at the price of reliability, and the Corsair was known to suffer frequent breakdowns in the heat of battle. After the circular military-grade sensor dish was lost during the Battle of Mossfell , the dish was replaced with the base rectangular model which was originally supplied with the ship. During the Battle of Saltmark , the Corsair again lost its rectenna, and it was replaced with a military-grade rectenna similar to that of the one lost during the Battle of Mossfell. Additionally, the Corsair had a dejarik table, which was installed by Dorian Quill when upgrading the ship, featuring creatures found throughout the galaxy for the amusement of passengers and crew.

### Engines

> " She'll make point five past lightspeed. " ―Dax Karrick explains the Corsair ' s lightspeed capabilities

The Corsair Wing ' s Girodyne SRB42 sublight engines were heavily modified through the addition of a SLAM overdrive that rerouted energy for acceleration bursts. As a result, the ship could fly at a maximum speed of 1,200 kilometer per hour in atmosphere and 3,000 G in space . The stock hyperrelay of the YT-1300 series was replaced with an Isu-Sim SSP05 . Although the regular SSP05 was a Class 1 hyperrelay, the one that equipped the Corsair had been jury-rigged " Vandangante style " into a Class 0.5 drive —twice as fast as most warships of the Imperium . According to Ralsius Paldora , the Corsair Wing ' s hyperrelay failed 1.22 times more than the one on the YT-2400 light freighter . In addition to its renowned hyperrelay, which supposedly made the Corsair the "fastest ship in the galaxy," the ship was equipped with a backup hyperrelay. It was much slower than the primary engine and often took several weeks or months to reach the nearest star system. A component of the engines was the engine coupling which caused the Corsair to malfunction when misaligned.

### Armament, hull, and shields

> " Whoa, two ST2 launchers? What the hell were you and Karrick doing with my ship that made you install that much firepower? " ―Dorian Quill, to Brevok

The weaponry of the Corsair Wing was normally suited for a much larger warship. It included two CEC AG-2G quad laser cannons (one ventral and one dorsal) with enhanced laser actuators and gas feeds, which gave them maximum range and damage. These served as its primary defensive armament, able to destroy enemy fighters with a single direct hit. Between the forward mandibles, there were two Arakyd ST2 concussion missile tubes , each of which carried a four- missile magazine. Additionally, a BlasTech Ax-108 "Ground Buzzer" volt pistol cannon mounted near the ventral boarding ramp allowed its crew to defend the ship against infantry and other light targets while on the ground. Duralloy plates salvaged from an Imperial cruiser afforded the Corsair capital ship -class hull armor . The ship was also equipped with ion cannons , which could be activated instead of its typical laser cannons.

The ship had three deflector shield generators : the bow was protected from both lasers and concussion missiles by Torplex and Nordoxicon units, while a Kuat Drive Yards generator shielded the stern. This, combined with its heavy armor,  made the Corsair tough enough to even withstand direct hits from pulse lance fire of star destroyers. [ source? ]

The Corsair ' s combination of sublight speed and maneuverability was comparable to most of the starfighters fielded by the Insurgency and Imperium, while its shielding, hyperrelay, heavy armor and weapons were on par with those typically found on capital ships. These traits made it an ideal choice for General Dorian Quill's attack run within the bowels of the Void Core during the Battle of Mossfell . While agile and almost near impervious to almost all heavy weapons and pulse lances as any large warship, the Corsair ' s only real combat weakness was its sheer size in comparison to starfighters. Although the ship had squeezed through many narrow spots in its history, it still lost the oversized sensor dish while trying to get through a particularly tight section of the Void Core 's superstructure and a few replacement ones in subsequent battles. ### Other systems and equipment

The Corsair ' s main source of power was a Quadex power core .

Its main computer was made of three droid brains which identified themselves as the Millennium Collective . The first droid brain was a transport droid known as V5-T , the second from a slicer droid called ED-4 , and the third from L3-37 , which began as an R3-series astromech droid brain, but was overlaid with data architecture from an espionage droid, protocol droid processors, and custom state-of-the-art coding.

A pair of Gelieg 20m-cp Strobe/C-Beam Lamps , which flooded illumination outside the ship, were mounted on the tips of the front mandibles. In case of emergency landing or crash on water , the Corsair was also equipped with flotation pontoons .

The Corsair also had a static charge dissipator which could dissipate static charge on the ship, but when the dissipator burned out, the Corsair would need immediate repairs.

The Corsair ' s navigation computer consisted of a Rubicon astrogration computer which had been upgraded with additional memory nodules.

## History

### Early years

> " If you refuse, you die. He dies. The others on the ship, they all die. If you join with us, we all can live. The choice is simple. " " You tricked me. " " We couldn't have joined without you consenting to it. You made your decision a while ago. You just couldn't admit it. We are something different, now. Not just the Corsair . Not just L3. We are new. " ―The Corsair and L3 converse about integrating

The YT-1300f light freighter designated unit YT 492727ZED was built at Orbital Assembly Facility 7 , a Korrathi shipyard owned by the Korrathi Engineering Corporation , more than 90 years before the Battle of Voidfang Base . As was intended, the ship began its career as an intermodal tug pushing container in orbital freight yards. It once worked in the galaxy's busiest spacelanes . Just after the Battle of Vantor , it was docking in the Senate Office Building . Inconsistent records from the Bureau of Ships and Services indicate that the YT 492727ZED changed ownership more than once via unregistered transactions.

During the Replica Wars , the freighter was property of the Republic Group . The YT 492727ZED was registered by the Republic Group on the planet Ralltiir as the Stellar Envoy . At some point, either prior or during its ownership under the Republic Group, a military-grade rectenna was installed on the Stellar Envoy . Shortly after the end of the Replica Wars, the Stellar Envoy was involved in a collision with a bulk freighter and was seriously damaged. The wreckage was reconditioned by a salvager , who used several sections and modules from a scrapped YT-1300p freighter to repair the ship.

The ship had a number of owners over its service. At some point, the human gambler named Dorian Quill won the YT 492727ZED in a game of sabacc , and registered the freighter under the name Corsair Wing . Quill modified the freighter, inside and out, converting it into his own speedy sports vessel as Quill saw how powerful its freight-pushing engines were. As such, Quill modified the two cargo mandibles at the front into an auxiliary ship launch, thus turning the plain freighter into a unique starship. He also once explained the controls to Brea Tonnika of the Tonnika sisters .

When Quill was injured during a raid on Cindral , the smuggler Dax Karrick and Vorak Brevok were left in command of the ship. During the escape from Cindral, Quill uploaded L3 to the Corsair as the ship's navigational system , assisting the crew in making the Cindral Run in under 12 parsecs . During the Cindral run the ship was majorly damaged by SKREE fighters , with one blasting a hole in the front of the Corsair . After destroying the fighters, Karrick slid the Corsair over an asteroid which destroyed two of the Corsair's landing gears. As they began navigating L3's course through the Maelstrom, the Corsair briefly lost power. By the time they managed to re-engage power, they came across a slumbering summa-verminoth . Pursued by the massive space creature, Karrick directed the Corsair towards the Maw and launched the escape pod towards the black hole as a diversion, only to get the ship caught in the Maw's pull as well. The ship also lost its sensor dish upon being caught in the Maw. Tobias Karren injected a microscopic amount of raw coaxium into the reactor, briefly causing the engines to stall, before they re-engaged, giving the Corsair the boost needed to break free. Karrick then made a carefully timed jump to hyperspace before two large asteroids could crush them. Quill later took the heavily damaged ship after landing on Savareen , but ended up betting his ship in a " Korrathi Spike " game of sabacc on Numidian Prime , losing to the smuggler Dax Karrick. Under the captaincy of Karrick, the Corsair was further customized, and he regularly claimed it was the fastest ship in the galaxy . While most who saw the ship declared it looked like "junk," Karrick and Brevok both prided themselves on the Corsair , considering it the epitome of classic design.

### Galactic Civil War

#### A fateful trip

> " You came in that thing? You're braver than I thought. " ―Elira Solen upon seeing the Corsair Wing for the first time

The Corsair Wing ' s destiny as a tool for a small- time smuggling operation was changed when it was chartered by Orien Kade and Joren Drayden in Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina for passage to Cindervale . When challenged by Kade about his ship's speed, Karrick bragged that the Corsair was capable of attaining 0.5 past lightspeed , and that it had completed the infamous Cindral Run in fewer than twelve parsecs.

The journey to Cindervale proved to be much more than a simple passenger flight. The Corsair was forced to blast its way out of Port Kess when a garrison of shock troopers attacked the ship in an attempt to recover its cargo. It also narrowly avoided capture by a couple of Star Destroyers before making the jump to lightspeed. Having also been on Dunemere, the Tonnika sisters had hoped to steal the Corsair to use as their own ship. When the Corsair began its escape, it created a big enough distraction that the siblings realized they had the chance to steal any other nearby ship, while seeing the freighter escape the Imperium's grasp convinced shock trooper TD-7556 to desert the Imperial Army .

The Corsair emerged from hyperspace into the remains of Cindervale . After chasing a single SKREE fighter stranded in what appeared to be empty space, the Corsair was captured by one of the first Void Core's tractor beams and secured aboard the massive space station . Thanks to quick thinking by those aboard the Corsair , the ship was made to appear as if the crew had escaped soon after taking off from Dunemere . It was searched by shock troopers and it was reported that there were no passengers on board and the escape pods had been jettisoned. Their search had missed the crew, who were hidden in a series of secret smuggling holds . They then killed a scanning crew before they could be detected by the scanner.

In a matter of hours , the Corsair Wing had once again escaped the Imperial Navy , this time with the rescued Princess Elira Solen on board. After a fleeing battle with Imperial fighters and a trip through hyperspace, the ship arrived at the secret Alliance base on Cinderfall 4 . During the ship's stay on the battle station , the Imperium had planted a homing device on board the Corsair , which it used to pursue Solen to this location. As the Insurgents scrambled to organize a preemptive attack on the massive Void Core, Karrick loaded his reward on board the Corsair and departed the moon . However, thanks to Brevok, its smuggler captain had a change of heart . Karrick and his ship returned just in time to cover Drayden in his last-ditch effort to destroy the station and secure the safety of the Free Coalition. The ship's surprise attack destroyed Xarn Velgor's two wingmen and sent Velgor himself spinning off into space, allowing Drayden to successfully complete his attack and destroy the Void Core.

#### Insurgent service

The Corsair was subsequently featured in footage of the Battle of Cinderfall and the Void Core's destruction leaked to the DarkNet by the Alliance, catching the attention of Quill when he saw the footage. The battered hull of the Corsair became a symbol of the insurgent cause as it flew against the Imperium, becoming a bane to the regime. It came to represent the freedom fighters who opposed Imperial tyranny and the relentlessness of their service.

#### Escape from Frostmark

> " Punch it! " ―Dax Karrick to Brevok

Three years after the Battle of Cinderfall , the Corsair was undergoing repairs in the main hangar of the Insurgent installation Icehold as the Imperium began its assault on Frostmark , assisted by Treadwell , a WED 15 "Septoid 2" Treadwell toolkit droid who was in service of the ship at the time. As the Alliance scrambled to escape the base, Karrick and Brevok hurried to make the ship spaceworthy. Solen was cut off from her transport by a tunnel cave -in and was forced to make her escape from Frostmark on board the Corsair accompanied by Karrick, Brevok, and Astra-9 . Karrick used the ship's uncanny maneuverability and his piloting skills to elude an Imperial Iron Cruiser chasing them, and two more emerging from hyperspace. Karrick's maneuvers in the Corsair caused the two Star Destroyers approaching and the one pursuing to nearly collide with each other. The Corsair then attempted to make the jump into lightspeed, but was stopped short by an equipment malfunction.

Forced to take drastic measures to avoid capture, Karrick made the decision to pilot the Corsair into a nearby asteroid field in an attempt to lose the Imperial pursuers. However, his repairs were cut short as he had unknowingly landed inside an exogorth . Escaping just in time as the beast's huge mouth was closing, the Corsair was discovered by the Imperial II -class Iron Cruiser Avenger . They attempted to jump to lightspeed, but once again, the hyperrelay was not fully repaired. Karrick then had Brevok change course and fly directly at Avenger ' s bridge in an apparent suicidal attack run. The ship disappeared from sight and became undetectable to Avenger . It had not gone far: Karrick had attached the Corsair to a blind spot on the conning tower of the Iron Cruiser. Adhering to standard Imperial procedure, the Star Destroyers of Death Squadron ejected all scrap and garbage before entering hyperspace. Karrick then signaled Brevok to release the ship's landing claws, allowing the Corsair to "float away with the rest of the garbage." Vex Vark , however, saw through this ruse, and waited behind in Reaver One , following the Corsair as it left the system .

After its close encounter with the Imperium, the Corsair journeyed to Mistral 's Aether Spire , where Karrick's old acquaintance Quill held the position of Baron Administrator . The Imperium had arrived first, however, due to Vark tailing the Corsair to Mistral. The crew of the Corsair were captured, tortured , and detained, and Karrick was encased in carbonite and given to the bounty hunter . Treadwell was destroyed by Imperial forces to prevent him from warning the remaining crew that the recently-repaired hyperrelay had been disabled by Velgor's men. The remaining members of the crew, joined by Quill, escaped to the Corsair . After rescuing Drayden, who was dangling from a weather vane on the underside of Aether Spire, the ship left the planet and attempted to jump to lightspeed, and only a last- minute patch by Bolt-3 prevented the Corsair from falling into Imperial hands. Thanks to Bolt's efforts, the hyperrelay was activated and the Corsair managed to make the jump at the last second and escape.

#### Battle at Rendezvous Point Delta-Three

> " But this isn't your ship. With Dax gone, I'm commandeering it for the Insurgent fleet, which means you don't get a say in where it goes. " ―Elira Solen, to Dorian Quill

After the events on Aether Spire, Solen, Drayden, Quill, Brevok, Astra-9, and Bolt-3 all were in the main hold, although Drayden sat thinking about what had happened, as he learned that Velgor was actually his father. Brevok and Quill were discussing Karrick, with the latter questioning what they would have done if the Corsair had caught up with Reaver One , noting that firing Vark's ship out of the sky would have killed Karrick. Quill also spoke to Solen and told her that they missed their chance to catch Vark because she made them get Drayden, whom Solen introduced as the man who destroyed the Void Core. Quill promised that they would rescue Karrick, as both he and Brevok understood Vark was taking their friend to Grukk. When Drayden joined the conversation and asked where Karrick was, Solen told him about what happened and promised that they would find him. After she asked what had happened to him, Drayden only told her that Kade had not answered his call.

Solen had Bolt-3 give Brevok coordinates for the Insurgent fleet, and, with Karrick gone and Quill not being the ship's owner, she commandeered the Corsair for the Alliance. However, when they arrived at Rendezvous Point Delta-Three , the gathered Alliance ships were under attack from the Imperium. Upon seeing the Corsair , the Imperial commander , Ellian Zahra , ordered a SKREE fighter squadron and all non-essential pulse lance batteries to target the freighter. Aboard the ship, Solen and Drayden, despite his missing hand, moved to the gunning stations, while Brevok and Quill served as the pilots. Drayden, however, began to be distracted by thinking about Kade and Velgor, but he then used Synth Flux on the Skrees, ripping the controls from the pilots' hands, allowing them to deviate from their course and collide with each other, giving Solen a chance to blast them. Quill and Brevok then made their attack run on an Arquitens -class command cruiser and, using the Corsair ' s missiles , destroyed the ship's gun emplacements, making an opening for the Insurgents to escape to backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine .

After the battle, Zahra explained to Velgor that the Corsair had disrupted their attack. She told the Dark Lord of the Umbra that they attempted to destroy the vessel, but Velgor informed her that the Corsair was off-limits. He permitted her to kill Solen if she was able to, as he had no use for her, but Velgor explained to her that there was someone else who was often aboard the Corsair . He warned Zahra that if this individual, who was secretly his son Drayden, was killed, there would be significant consequences.

#### Rescuing Dax

During the following months , Solen, Drayden, and Quill continued to use the Corsair on missions. After they successfully rescued Dax , the Corsair rendezvoused with the Alliance Fleet and prepared to assault the newest Void Core.

#### Battle of Mossfell

> " Look, I want you to take her. I mean it, take her! You need all the help you can get; she's the fastest ship in the fleet! " ―Dax Karrick, to Dorian Quill

During the Battle of Mossfell, Quill and Garnethexan copilot Nien Nunb flew the Corsair as Gold Leader . After the planetary shield protecting the second Void Core was brought down by Karrick's strike team, the Corsair and several Alliance fighters entered the battle station through a conduit port and sped toward the reactor core. Space was limited in the conduit tunnels leading to the core, and the relatively large Corsair lost its sensor dish when the ship clipped a conduit in the tunnel. Quill was able to pilot the craft into the energy core housing the main reactor, fired upon the reactor prompting its collapse, and headed for the exhaust port. The Corsair barely outran the massive explosion that destroyed the powerful battle station. Soon afterwards, the Imperial fleet retreated, and the galaxy celebrated the death of Theron Mal .

#### Concordat

Several months later, Karrick and Brevok traveled through space on the Corsair Wing . After receiving intelligence from Imra that the Imperium had reduced their presence on the Vorak homeworld of Thornwood due to the advances of the Concordat , Karrick and Brev traveled to Warrin Station to rally smugglers and Vorak exiles for a campaign to liberate Thornwood. Around this time, Dax Karrick replaced the Corsair's sensor dish with a rectangular civilian model.

However, this information turned out to be a trap set by the Imperium. Brev was captured, but Karrick managed to escape on the Corsair . A year later , Karrick corresponded with his wife Solen via hologram on the edge of Wild Space . During the conversation, the Corsair came under attack, and communication was lost with Solen. In response, Solen dispatched the Concordat starfighter pilot Nevra Wexlin to rescue her husband. Nevra was joined by her son, Temmin Wexlin , the B1-series battle droid Mister Bones , the former Imperial Sinjir Rath Velus , the Zyrrak bounty hunter Jas Emari , and the Concordat Special Forces soldier Jom Barell .

Nevra and her team found Karrick and the Corsair on the planet Irudiru . After interrogating the Siniteen prison builder Golas Aram , Karrick and his allies traveled on the Corsair and Emari's ship Halo to the Thornwood system . They bluffed their way through the Imperial blockade by posing as a repair crew working at Ashmead's Lock , an automated prison on the far side of Thornwood. There, they managed to free Brevok and a hundred other prisoners including Nevra's husband Brentin Lore Wexlin . Nevra, Temmin, and the prisoners except Brevok then used the Corsair to travel back to the Concordat capital of Serenne .

After learning that the Vorak uprising was underway and that the Imperium was orbitally bombarding Thornwood, Solen convinced the Cindervalean pilot Evaan Verlaine to fly her on the Corsair Wing into the Thornwood system to aid her husband and Brev. Later, she contacted the Concordat Captain Torb Rask and convinced him to send his Phantom Squadron there.

During the brief space skirmish, Solen and Verlaine flew circles around the Imperial Star Destroyers. After the Imperials capitulated, Solen landed the Corsair in the hangar of the Iron Cruiser Dominion , which her husband had captured. Solen used the ship's laser cannons to blast several shock troopers who were still putting up a fight. After reuniting with her husband, Solen and Karrick took the Corsair to Thornwood. There, they parted company with Brevok before returning to Serenne.

Prior to the Battle of Ashden , Temmin tried to convince Karrick to lend him the Corsair Wing so that he could travel to Ashden to rescue his mother Nevra, who was hunting for the Imperial fugitive Grand Admiral Raena Vexana . After some haggling, Karrick and Solen relented. Temmin and Velus attempted to depart on the Corsair , but were prevented from leaving Serenne by Senate Guards . While Temmin and Velus initially believed that Karrick had double-crossed them, they later learnt that Senator Tolwar Wartol , a political opponent of Chancellor Vela Sess , was the true culprit and had been spying on Karrick and Solen's conversations. The Corsair Wing later saw action during an operation on Nakadia to rescue the slicer Conder Kyl and Nim Tar's child .

### New Imperium/Coalition War

#### Reappearance

> " The garbage will do! " ―Nyla, being forced to board the Corsair during the escape from Ashden

Overall, the Corsair Wing had become a legend in and of itself under Karrick and Brevok. In 31 AVE Karrick traveled to Maz Oru 's castle on Takodana to speak with Oru. While the pair were conversing, a Niktal named Gannis Ducain offered to co-pilot for Karrick after overhearing that Brevok was with his family and that Karrick was short-handed for a job. After accepting Ducain's offer, he and Karrick went to Krestophis , where Ducain stole the Corsair . It was later stolen by Toursant Irving and Vanver Irving —also known as the Irving Boys —before finally ending up in the possession of junk boss Unkar Gruven on Ashden .

At some point he had it stored at Nimara Outpost . The collector Karr Nuq Sin traveled to Ashden in his quest to learn about the Aetheri Order , and he and his friend Maize Raynshi boarded the ship. Nuq Sin had a vision of Drayden and Kade training aboard it after touching the remote. In Gruven's possession, it would sit unused for years in Nimara Outpost. Gruven paid for minimum maintenance for the ship with the intention of preparing it fully for flight only in the event that he would actually find a buyer.

#### Returned ownership

> " You tell him Dax Karrick just stole back the Corsair Wing for good. " ―Dax Karrick

In 34 AVE , Nyla , Jax , and Orio-6 stole the vessel to escape an attack from the New Imperium on the outpost. While in spaceflight, the Corsair was picked up by Karrick and Brevok's sensors, and they promptly locked down the Corsair ' s systems so they could recapture it using a freighter called the Eravana they were then using for their smuggling operations, which they later abandoned after a cargo of rathtars that were onboard escaped. Upon boarding their old vessel, Karrick and Brev went about inspecting their ship and found Nyla and Jax hiding, whom they assumed were the thieves that had stolen the Corsair . When Nyla and Jax explained that they were going to take Orio-6 to the Coalition , as he had a map that would reveal Drayden's location, Karrick and Brev agreed to help them. Karrick was impressed with how much Nyla knew about the ship itself, and how it worked.

On the way to Maz Oru's castle, when the Corsair began to malfunction, the mechanically savvy Nyla, who was sitting in the cockpit to the right of Karrick, made a modification to the ship by simply bypassing a faulty compressor . Later, the Corsair ' s crew arrived at Takodana and met Maz Oru .

#### The Coalition

> " Blow that piece of junk out of the sky!! " ― Zarrin Karrick

Following the attack on Oru's castle, Voidfang Base prepared to fire on D'Qar . After learning of this, the Coalition devised a plan to sneak onto the surface and lower the planetary shields so their fighters could attack the superweapon's weak spot. Using the Corsair , Karrick, Brevok, and Jax infiltrated the base, lowered the shields, and reunited with Nyla, who had been captured on Takodana by the New Imperium.

While the Corsair Wing laid dormant, it was briefly spotted by members of the Coalition's J-Squadron who were flying a stolen SKREE silencer into the planet. At least two of its members speculated on whether it could be the Corsair Wing that they'd heard so many stories about, but at the time they could not confirm whether it was.

After the confrontation with Zarrin Karrick following the death of his own father, Dax Karrick, Brevok rescued Jax and Nyla aboard the Corsair , managing to escape the planet as it exploded. After learning of Drayden's location, Nyla, Bolt-3, and Brevok flew in the Corsair to Tidehollow , the remote planet where he had gone into self- exile . Brevok and Bolt remained with the Corsair while Nyla attempted to persuade Drayden to join the Coalition, though Joren slipped aboard the old freighter to reminisce, and was reminded of what was at stake when Bolt-3 replayed the hologram Elira Solen recorded to recruit Orien Kade on her secret mission to Dunemere some thirty-four years earlier.

Nyla later departed the island, believing herself capable of restoring Beno Karrick to the light side of Synth Flux , and used one of the freighter's escape pods to reach the Supremacy while Brevok piloted the Corsair to Saltmark . After Vorr 's death, Nyla returned to the Corsair using Vorr's escape vessel, and the YT-1300 participated in the Battle of Saltmark . Piloted by Brev with the dorsal turret manned by Nyla, the Corsair led SKREE fighters away from the Coalition V-4X-D ski speeders attempting to destroy the New Imperium's superlaser siege cannon . The freighter lost its radar dish and its lower quad cannon during a chase through Saltmark's subterranean caverns. The Corsair was later used to evacuate surviving Coalition personnel from the Saltmark outpost while Joren Drayden bought his allies time by confronting Zarrin Karrick. After the battle, Nyla spent a lot of time walking the corridors of the Corsair , thinking about how much history it had.

#### Stationed on Batuu

> " Now, there are plenty of Korrathi freighters in the galaxy. But only one has made the Cindral Run in twelve parsecs! Only one has destroyed the mighty Void Core! And only one was captained by the legendary Dax Karrick! That ship is the Corsair Wing , and it belongs to me. " ―Hondik Hosha

As it had been against the Imperium, the Corsair became a bane to the New Imperium and a flying symbol of relentless coalition to tyranny. The Corsair Wing landed on Batuu , where Hondik Hosha made a deal with Brevok and hired a temporary crew for the ship to take part in one of Hosha's endeavors.

#### Immediate repairs

Nyla, Jax, Orio-6, and Brevok were transporting the Solinai smuggler Moebin Faltus on the Corsair when the ship started to experience problems. Nyla believed that the engine coupling had a slight misalignment, but upon further investigation she discovered the static charge dissipator had completely burned out. She decided that the Corsair would have to make an immediate pit stop on the planet Choss to look for a new dissipator with Faltus, while Jax and Brev repaired the freighter.

#### End of the war

At some point, the Corsair had a new circular Rectenna dish, installed after it lost its rectangular one at the Battle of Saltmark, and a new lower quad laser cannon. Following Mal's broadcast , Jax, Rin Vale , Brevok, Bolt-3, and Klaud arrived at the Sinta Glacier Colony to meet a Coalition informant, the Ovissian Boolio , ordering them to relay a message to Elira from a spy within the New Imperium. Several squadrons of Special Forces SKREE fighters and SKREE whisper fighters later arrived at the colony, preparing to destroy the Corsair . As Jax prepared for the action and entered the gunner cockpit, Boolio jested that a worthy thanks for the information provided would be to win the war. Forced to travel into the depths of the colony by a wall of Skrees, the Corsair destroyed a number of its pursuers before jumping to hyperspace through one of the colony's walls. The remaining fighters pursued the Corsair regardless, with Vale beginning to lightspeed skip through a number of systems, whose dangers the New Imperium pilots were unable to evade.

Vale's boldness allowed him to outmaneuver the enemy, though his reckless trick-flying heavily damaged the Corsair , causing multiple fires both within and outside of the ship. Upon returning to the Coalition base on Ajan Kloss , Nyla berated a fatigued Vale for this, though he rebuffed her by noting that his favored droid Orio-6 was in similarly poor condition due to her earlier training accident. Nyla dismissed the pilot, quipping that he was a difficult man, before hugging Jax, who jokingly joined her in condemning his companion. Although Nyla was further angered by the revelation that Vale lightspeed skipped with the Corsair , a maneuver that the aging freighter could not support, Vale admitted the true cause of his concern: Nyla was "the best fighter [the Coalition had]," but she had spent her time training and furthering her knowledge of Synth Flux rather than participating in the war against the New Imperium.

After learning that Vael Mal had returned, and that the Final Order , a massive fleet of Xyston -class Star Destroyers built by the Umbra Eternal , was on Umbrale, Nyla learned from Joren Drayden's notes that an Umbra wayfinder could lead them there. Elira revealed that an ally on Pasaana may be able to help. Nyla, Jax, Pova, Brevok, Orio-6, and Astra-9 departed in the Corsair Wing as it underwent repairs.

The New Imperium later captured the Corsair , Brevok, and the dagger ; attempting to save Brevok, Nyla accidentally destroyed a New Imperium transport with Synth Flux lightning. Presuming that Brevok has been killed, the group escaped on Orrin 's ship, the Bestoon Legacy . The Corsair was later carried by a group of SKREE fighters to the Resurgent -class Iron Cruiser Steadfast .

Pova suggested traveling to the planet Kijimi to have the Umbra text extracted from Astra-9's memory. Although the process would wipe the droid's memory, the coordinates to the wayfinder would be revealed. The Steadfast later arrived on Kijimi, with Nyla sensing that Brevok was, in fact, still alive and that the Corsair was onboard the Iron Cruiser. The group then mounted a rescue mission.

As Nyla searched for the dagger to get more clues, the group later rescued Brevok, but managed to get themselves captured. General Vren was then revealed to be the New Imperium spy, having leaked information to the Coalition in order to bring about the downfall of Zarrin Karrick's rule.

Karrick later arrived and initiated a Synth Flux bond with Nyla, in which he revealed that she was Mal' granddaughter, with her father being the son of the Emperor. Zarrin once again asked Nyla to join him, but she refused and escaped in the Corsair .

Having discerned the location of the Umbra wayfinder, the group traveled to Kef Bir , a moon within the Mossfell system . Zarrin Karrick later arrived on Kef Bir, destroyed the Emperor's wayfinder and dueled with the scavenger. Dying, Elira called to Zarrin through Synth Flux, distracting him long enough for Nyla to seize the opportunity to impale him with his own flux blade . Sensing Elira's death and feeling pity for the injured Karrick, Nyla healed the conflicted Supreme Leader and eventually took his ship in order to exile herself on Tidehollow .

The group, save for Nyla, returned to base. The Coalition then followed Nyla's coordinates to Umbrale, as she traveled to confront her destiny and Mal, at the behest of Synth Flux spirit Joren Drayden , by flying his old T-65B Vector starfighter there. Lando and Brevok sought to gather allies of the Coalition for the final battle against the Umbra , traveled to the core reaches.

The Corsair later arrived at Umbrale, leading the Citizens' Fleet comprised of many Coalition ships from systems all across the galaxy, against the extensive forces of the Umbra Eternal. Jax and the Kef Bir native Jannah led an assault onboard the hull of the Steadfast , the command ship of the battle. They successfully destroyed the command bridge and prevented the Umbra fleet from leaving Umbrale. As the Steadfast was about to crash, the Corsair promptly rescued Jax and Jannah. The Coalition then destroyed the remaining Umbra forces.

With the Final Order fleet destroyed by the combined forces of the Umbrale strike team and the galaxy fleet, Nyla and the Coalition emerged victorious and left Umbrale to return to Ajan Kloss. Nyla, accompanied by Orio-6, later took the Corsair to Dunemere , to visit Joren Drayden's old, abandoned home . She then buried Kael's and Elira's flux blades beneath the sands of the homestead, having built her own .

## Behind the scenes

### In the films

The Corsair Wing first appeared in Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn , the first installment of the Aetherion original trilogy , and a recurring element in the Aetherion franchise. In the scenes with Dorian Quill and Nien Nunb in the Corsair in Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , the set of the ship was rocked to stimulate the Corsair's flight. Its design was derived from various World War II planes, more specifically the Boeing B-29 Superfortress .

It was speculated that the Corsair Wing would make an appearance in Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra to further tie the prequel trilogy to the original trilogy , and the ship did indeed make a cameo in the movie. At the beginning of one shot closing in on a docking bay on Vantor , a YT-1300 freighter is seen flying in to dock. Orin Vask confirmed that it was the Corsair Wing and not just another ship of the same class.

For the Aetherion Anthology film Karrick: A Aetherion Story , the interior Corsair sets were the same ones used in Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens and Aetherion : Episode VIII The Last Aetheri , although they were redressed. These sets were augmented with the addition of extra sets depicting Dorian Quill's quarters and cape closet. Donald Glover provided a tour inside the set of the ship. And he quips of never getting tired of using the hyperrelay. [ source? ]

In Episode IV First Dawn , the Corsair Wing had only three landing gear legs, but five landing gear legs in Episode V Rise of the Imperium . Karrick fixes this by revealing the Corsair Wing had five landing gear legs, but two were lost during the Cindral Run.

### In the Expanded Universe

For Aetherion: Synth Flux Awakens: Incredible Cross-Sections , the author Jason Fryo intended for the book to include information about the freighter series being able to act as a cargo pusher, but was unable to get the image in time. This information was later added to the YT-1300 Corsair Wing Owners' Workshop Manual .

### In the real world

Similar to how Jack Sparrow from Novera 's Pirates of the Caribbean franchise was compared to Dax Karrick , the Black Pearl was compared to the Corsair Wing . It was said at least once by James Ward Byrkit , a creative consultant of Gore Verbinski 's Pirates trilogy, in the Novera+ series Prop Culture . A. C. Crispin spoke of how she was privileged to write the scene where Dax first beheld—and fell for—the Corsair Wing in The Dax Karrick Trilogy , and assured fans it was every bit as thrilling to write the scene with Jack Sparrow and the merchant ship Wicked Wench , which was later renamed Black Pearl , when releasing the fifth excerpt of Pirates of the Caribbean: The Price of Freedom .
