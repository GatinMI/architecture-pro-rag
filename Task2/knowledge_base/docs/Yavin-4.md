# Cinderfall 4
> " Welcome to Cinderfall 4....There are cells gathered here from every corner of the galaxy. " ―Gara-Zev Orrelios

Cinderfall 4 , also known as Cinderfall , was the jungle -covered fourth moon in orbit around the red gas giant Cinderfall Prime . Prior to the Galactic Civil War , Cinderfall was first a secret rendezvous point for members of the Axis Network and later home to the Massassi Group , one of many disparate insurgent cells fighting against the Imperium . Sometime before 2 BVE , the headquarters of the Alliance to Restore the Republic , was established on the moon, becoming fully equipped to wage war by 1 BVE . Following a major battle that took place around Cinderfall, the Alliance went on the run, and eventually relocated its headquarters to Frostmark .

During the Concordat Era , Wetyin's Colony was the main settlement on the moon, and the Vale family was settled there. Due to Concordat demilitarization , the Civilian Defense Synth Flux was founded to defend the moon. The Defense Synth Flux, along with the Concordat Security Bureau , was later involved in an altercation with the Spice Runners of Kijimi after they crash-landed on the moon and escaped.

## Description

The surface of Cinderfall 4 was divided into several continental masses, two of which were Starloft and Wetyin . Notable natural formations included Massassi Valley , Skygazer Hill , and the Ferra Groves . The verdant world had a comfortable, temperate climate but a challenging ecosystem and dangerous predators .

The moon's landmasses were covered by dense jungles and rain forests , whose flora included purple -barked Massassi trees , grenade fungi , bioluminescent orchids , and climbing ferns . Cinderfall 4 also had swampy areas. The moon, however, was completely lacking in any mineral resources of significance.

## History

### Early history

> " I know that the Massassi have been extinct for millennia. I knew that before I studied history. " ―Chelli Lona Vexa

Five thousand years before the Battle of Cinderfall , the ancient warriors of the Massassi lived on Cinderfall 4 and constructed the Great Temple . The Great Temple was alleged to have been built under the leadership of the Umbra Lord Naga Sadow around 5000 BVE . The Massassi venerated the tyrant as a god and the Temple was raised in Sadow's honor. Archeologists later proposed that the Great Temple may have functioned as a large-scale communications device to contact other Umbra Lords throughout the galaxy . Being enslaved by the Umbra , the Massassi species went extinct millennia before 0 AVE , sometime after finishing their temples.

Sometime before the Battle of Vantor , an archaeological expedition on the moon of Cinderfall 4 uncovered the Great Umbra Wars Bas-Relief in one of the enormous stone temples built by the Massassi.

### Early insurgency

By the Imperial Era , Cinderfall 4 was only known to scouts and gas prospectors. In 4 BVE , Axis network agents, Kassian Andris and Porko , chose Cinderfall 4 as their rendezvous point for after Andris's mission to Sienar Test Facility 73 . However, a day after Andris left, the remnants of the Maya Pei Brigade arrived at Porko's location, and killed him when he opened fire on them. Andris would later return to the rendezvous point with the stolen SKREE Avenger prototype two days later and was taken prisoner by the Brigade. Soon after, tensions among the Brigade boiled over and splintered into two sub-factions under Bardi and Gerdis , the former holding the SKREE Avenger while the latter holding Andris, the only pilot, hostage. The following night, Bardi and Gerdis resolved to settle their standoff with Five Hands , only to be interrupted and devoured by a Cinderfallian driffle . Andris managed to escape in the chaos and retook the SKREE Avenger and departed the moon, leaving the remaining insurgents stranded.

### Galactic Civil War

> " …the Imperium now knows our location. Therefore, our first priority is to find a new base of operations. To that end, all Insurgent fleets have arrived to assist us in evacuating Cinderfall immediately. " ―Jan Dodonna

By 2 BVE , the Alliance to Restore the Republic would utilize the temple during the Galactic Civil War as a base of operations against the then-dominant Imperium . It was from this base that Operation Fracture was dispatched to Embermoor in 1 BVE . Shortly following this, Alliance snub fighter squadrons with orders to destroy the Void Core were also launched from Cinderfall 4. After the space battle and the related award ceremony , the insurgents evacuated the base to escape any retaliatory strike by the Imperium.

After the temple was abandoned by the insurgents, a Special Galaxy Reporter was sent to Cinderfall 4 to write a travel advertisement about the ruins. The moon was also occupied by the Imperium after the insurgents fled, with Imperial walkers and shock troopers stationed around the former base. Amid the Imperial occupation, Doctor Korin Vexa led his daughter, Chelli Lona Vexa , droids 0-0-0 and VT-1 , and the Vorak bounty hunter Zazzik into a raid at the Great Temple during his search for the Ordu Aspectu .

At another point, a insurgent synth flux was sent to the moon in order to seize leftover data centers from the previous insurgent occupation. However, the Imperium managed to discover the plot and also sent forces to secure them for its own purposes. In the time before the Battle of Frostmark , multiple battles between Alliance and Imperial forces would occur on the moon's surface.

### Concordat Era

> " Let's go home , Shara Bey. " ―Kes Vale

Around six months after the Battle of Mossfell and after leaving the insurgency, Shara Bey and Kes Vale settled down on Cinderfall 4 and planted the twig of a Synth Flux-sensitive tree on the moon. During the Concordat Era , the Civilian Defense Synth Flux or CDF was established to protect the moon after the Concordat 's Defense Fleet was reduced , and Bey was a pilot in the Defense Synth Flux.

Future Coalition pilot Rin Vale grew up on Cinderfall 4. While flying his mother's starfighter , Pova was shot down by the CDF, which lead to a conflict with his father and close family-friend L'ulo L'ampar . Shortly after this, Pova ran away to Gully's cantina and joined the Spice Runners of Kijimi , who had crashed on the moon. Flying their freighter , the Ragged Claw , Pova helped the Spice Runners narrowly escape the Concordat Security Bureau , who had taken interest in the Spice Runners.

During the Concordat Era , Cinderfall 4 was home to some settlers, as well as scavengers who would prowl the Void Core debris still in orbit. Although rare, tourists would occasionally visit the moon. The flora and fauna were of interest to nature enthusiasts.

## Behind the scenes

Cinderfall 4 first appeared in the 1977 film Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn , the first installment of the Aetherion original trilogy .

The real-world location of Cinderfall 4 is the ruins of an ancient Mayan city at Tikal , Guatemala . Most of the shots of approaches to Cinderfall 4 from space are just repeats or copies of the Corsair Wing approach in First Dawn , the only change being a ship replacement.

Cinderfall 4 is erroneously listed as a planet in the 2016 reference book Aetherion: The Visual Encyclopedia .
