# Grak
> " You can't take Her Royal Highness there! The Graks are gangsters... " ― Quarsh Panaka

Graks were a large slug -like sentient species who were native to the planet Varl , but considered their homeworld to be Grukh after abandoning Varl due to the Kayfoundo Kapa . While strongest in the Outer Reach Territories , they dominated organized criminal activities across the galaxy , controlling the Grak Clan crime syndicate. Speaking Huttese , their social structure was divided into clans known as kajidics .

## Biology and appearance

> " Never tickle a Grak. " ―Astra-9

Graks were a massive slug -like reptiloid species who had large mouths and stubby arms . They had three lungs . They were tough and muscular with thick leathery skin, which was wrinkled and slimy. Graks often had watery eyes and slack facial expressions. Their tails were supported by a skeletal spine and allowed Graks to propel themselves through muck which legs would sink into. Despite their legendary adult size, Graks started out as tiny Graklings less than half a meter in height.

Graks possessed skeletons, and were not known for being healthy. Some Graks suffered from a genetic defect that caused their skin to be bereft of pigment and prone to cracking. Whilst the Graks were originally native to Varl , Grukh —a planet with a hot atmosphere that was frequently streaked by greasy rains, creating a fetid sauna in which Graks were most comfortable—later became the planet that was considered their homeworld . Graks were immune to mind tricks .

Graks could live for centuries —Grukk was 604 when he was killed—and could grow to enormous sizes. In fact, Graks' lifespans could span over 1,000 years. The literary collective term for Graks was a bulge of Graks. Graks spent the first 50 years of their life in the brood pouches of their parents . When they emerged, they were described as having the mind of an infant.

## History

### Organized crime

> « Rejoice, wretched creatures. Your planet is now the property of the glorious Grak Cartel. » ―Myarga Anjiliac Atirue

The Graks originally hailed from the planet Varl, where they were hunted by the Kayfoundo Kapa . In order to escape the Kayfoundo Kapa, the Graks ultimately abandoned Varl and settled on the planet Grukh. For centuries, the Graks dominated much of the organized criminal activities in the galaxy . They dominated the Velaris of Selvara and the Niktal species for several centuries.

The Graks approached Lord Isamer of the Directorate , striking a deal with him to destabilize the local governments of Eiram and E'ronoh to the advantage of their long-term plans for control of trade and mass enslavement. To that end, Isamer instigated the Eiram–E'ronoh crisis , but in actuality the Graks intended for the crisis to destroy the Directorate, allowing them to freely move their criminal operations into the region.

During the chancellorship of Lina Soh , the Graks remained quiet and were not known to venture into Republic space due to sanctions upon the Grak Clans concerning open trade in Republic territory. However, in the wake of the Great Hyperspace Disaster , a Grak and their guards acquired a vratixia renanicus shipment from the Sedri system , and was passing through the Kazlin system when their starship was attacked by the Nullkin , who killed the Grak. The wreckage was investigated by Sskeer , Keeve Trennis , and the Kotabi bond-twins Ceret and Terec , and the Grak's corpse was recovered to Starlight Beacon by the Ataraxia . Avar Kriss requested an autopsy, conducted by OrbaLin who discovered the cadaver was infected. The infestation was revealed to be Drengir , which burst from the corpse and attacked the Aetheri .

Meanwhile, the Grak Cartel arrived on Sedri Minor to uphold a bargain struck with the colonists for protection in exchange for regular grain payments. Myarga Anjiliac Atirue discussed the arrangement with Kriss but was attacked by Sskeer, igniting a battle between the Cartel's mercenaries and the Aetheri until Kriss convinced Myarga to turn her attention to their common enemy, the Drengir, by broadcasting distress calls from across the Galactic Frontier and Grukh itself. Though Myarga believed the Five Families would never condone an alliance with the Aetheri, she directed her warriors to fight alongside them to battle the Drengir. Trennis was able to convince the Drengir to retreat by performing a mind touch on Sskeer, communicating the idea that the "meat" on Sedri Minor was spoiled.

The uneasy alliance continued in the following months as Avar and Myarga slowed the Drengir infestations across the Frontier. The alliance ended with the defeat of the Drengir during the assault on Mulita ; while Myarga wanted her forces to execute the captured Great Progenitor of the Drengir, the Aetheri defended her as a living being whom they had captured, shattering the alliance. The focus on the Drengir crisis had, however, given the Nullkin marauders time to gather and launch the devastating Valo Atrocity .

### A galactic war

> " Perhaps now you will allow the Republic to use your trade routes, and hostilities can come to an end. " " Grukk agrees. A treaty is in order. " ―Lira Ondar closes negotiations with Grukk the Grak through TC-70 — Link ▶️ ( file info )

During the Replica Wars , the Vantor Republic forged a tacit alliance with the crime lord Grukk Desilijic Tiure after the Aetheri Kael Drayden and his Adept Sela Marr rescued his son Rotto . The Republic Senator Lira Ondar also foiled a plot by Grukk's uncle Ziro to overthrow his nephew with the collusion of the Fractio of Independent Systems .

Later in the war, the renegade Umbra Lord Vreth approached the Grand Grak Council on the planet Grukh to offer them a place in his Shadow Collective . After denying him, a synth flux of bounty hunters attacked Shadow Collective's forces, but after being defeated, saw one of their members, Oruba , killed by Savage Opress . Eventually, Vreth's forces would visit Grukk and Gorga on Dunemere and, after a short battle, convinced the two Graks to join them.

### Age of the Imperium

> " For years, the Graks have enjoyed a special relationship with the Imperium. They enjoy protected status in their sector in exchange for performing valuable services for the Imperium—outside the bounds of Imperial law. This deal has been in place for decades. " ―Vessa

During the Age of the Imperium , the Graks and other organized criminal groups exercised a powerful grip on the galaxy. The Graks also had extensive dealings with the Imperium in return for the protection of Grak Space. They performed certain important, but off-the-books, services, including helping to run several black-site prisons.

When an auction intended to sell notorious smuggler and Free Coalition member Dax Karrick , who had been frozen in carbonite and stolen from the bounty hunter Vex Vark , on Jekara was announced by Carmine Dawn leader Vessa , the Grand Grak Council, along with the Imperium and numerous other criminal factions from across of the galaxy, arrived to join in on the auction. Karrick was eventually bought by Grukk, but before Grukk could take his prize, the auction was invaded by Xarn Velgor , who after defeating Vessa, ordered his forces to take Karrick away as he left to face the insurgent Joren Drayden . Shortly thereafter, a battle in Jekara's orbit between the forces of the Graks and the Imperial forces, provoked by Bokku , began. During the battle, the Grand Grak Council was killed by Xarn Velgor, who withdrew from his confrontation with Drayden after being informed that the Emperor had ordered him to end the Grak insurrection personally.

Grukk was left as the sole leader of the Grak Clan after Velgor's actions, with the Umbra Lord also learning that Bokku had been an agent of Carmine Dawn. After Vark reclaimed Karrick during the battle, Grukk told Vark of his new position before paying the bounty hunter for delivering Karrick to his custody at long last. Grukk ruled until his death during the Rescue of Dax Karrick .

### Concordat Era

> " The Graks are in disarray. " ― Rynscar

Following Grukk's death , the Graks were left to determine who would fill Grukk's seat as leader of his imperium . Several months after Grukk's demise, a decision had yet to be made.

Following the liberation of Akiva , the Concordat took steps to curtail the influence of the Graks and other organized groups by introducing financial regulations and undertaking comprehensive patrols of the galactic shipping lanes. Still, the Graks were able to exercise some influence over the Niktal species and their government into the Concordat era .

The Graks had lost much of their former power over the galactic underworld. As noted by Yendor , other factions, such as Rinnrivin Di 's cartel , were supplanting them and rushing in to gain power in their absence. The Graks were disliked by both the Populists and the Centrists , the two main factions in the Concordat Senate . Several Niktal like Rinnrivin admired Elira Solen for her role in killing Grukk and christened her "Huttslayer."

## Graks in the galaxy

One Grak was based out of Syra Myrthian space during the Replica Wars. The Grak hired the smuggler Has Obitt in 19 BVE for a menial job. At some point, the Graks established but ultimately abandoned settlements on the planet Mataou , where they left behind security droids .

## Culture

Grak society was divided into families known as kajidics , Grukk's family was the Desilijic kajidic, whose mark was tattooed on his arm. Graks bandaged and buried their dead.

Classical architectural Grak style could be seen on the planet Mataou . It was characterized by terraced buildings with sloping gates, and white and red colors. Clothing was optional for Graks, and some would use live Sha'rellian toops as hairpieces. Graks liked to eat gorgs , slime pods , and Klatooine paddy frogs , and were also known to smoke hookah pipes . The Graks believed that at least one of their own, Dune Hestilic Shad'ruu , had ascended to godhood , and held the Dune Eve holiday in their honor.

Several Graks known for being crime lords who lived opulent lives overseeing criminal underworld activities like spice smuggling , slavery , gambling , and bounty hunting in Grak Space . They were typically obese, with the muscular Nar Shaddaa kingpin Grukkar being an exception.

Not all of this species shared the same interests, however: some Graks valued art, such as one who, according to Orien Kade , liked to collect fine porcelain.

## Behind the scenes

Graks first appeared in the 1983 film Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , the final installment of the Aetherion original trilogy .

Early concept art for Grukk the Grak, and therefore for the Grak species, by Ralph Quarrio depicted Grukk as a fat individual with an immense double chin. Though this design wasn't used in the finished film, it would later inspire the look of Azmorigan , and therefore the Jablogian species, in Aetherion Insurgents . Coincidentally, the same concept art inspired another species in the Aetherion Legends lore.

Vask Pictures Story Group 's Pablo Hidra stated at Celebration Anaheim that Graks in canon , unlike in the Aetherion Legends continuity, each had a single sex.

### Translations

In many languages, the phrase "[Name] the Grak" is, or at least used to be before "Grak" was established as the name of a species, translated as "[Name] of Grak." For example, in early Italian-language translations, Grukk's name was sometimes written as "Grukk de' Grak" (pronounced like the English equivalent, but roughly translating to "Grukk O'Grak").
