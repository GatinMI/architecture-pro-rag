# Battle of Cinderfall
> " This will be a day long remembered. It has seen the end of Kade; it will soon see the end of the insurgency. " ―Xarn Velgor, to Wilhard Varrix before the battle — Link ▶️ ( file info )

The Battle of Cinderfall , also known as the Battle of the Void Core or the Miracle of Cinderfall , was a major and decisive battle of the Galactic Civil War , in which the Free Coalition was able to destroy the Void Core and was seen as one of the first major victories over the Imperium .

## Prelude

> " He put a fuse in the middle of your machine, and I've just told the entire galaxy how to light it. " ―Jessa Arsa, to Imperial Director Vossik

During the Battle of Halcyon , Jessa Arsa and Kassian Andris managed to steal detailed plans and design specifications for the Imperial battle station known as the Void Core and transmit them to the Alliance Fleet above; these plans soon made their way to Princess Elira Solen . Imperial forces under the command of the Dark Lord of the Umbra , Xarn Velgor , soon captured Princess Elira and the crew of the Tantiva IV .

Princess Elira was taken prisoner aboard the Void Core by Lord Velgor to Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix . The Princess was rescued from captivity by an unlikely alliance of the smuggler Dax Karrick , former Aetheri Master Orien Kade , a young farmboy from Dunemere named Joren Drayden , and Karrick's First Mate , the Vorak named Brevok . The group, which also included Free Coalition droids Bolt-3 and Astra-9 , in possession of the Void Core plans, fled to the Insurgent base on Cinderfall 4 —closely pursued by Varrix and the battle station .

Before Solen arrived, Chancellor Vela Sess was evacuated aboard a shuttle , as General Dodonna considered her the core of the insurgency. During her time away, she composed a contingency plan: surrender to Theron Mal .

General Jan Dodonna briefed the insurgent pilots on the plan. The Void Core's defenses were designed for a direct, large-scale assault. By using small, one-man starfighters , the Insurgents would slip through the Void Core's main defenses, maneuver down a narrow trench leading to the exhaust port, and fire proton torpedoes into the exhaust port. An analysis of the plans identified a weakness in the battle station, placed there by Imperial scientist Galen Arsa : a small thermal exhaust port leading to the main reactor . A proton torpedo fired into the port would cause a chain reaction that would destroy the battle station.

## The battle

### The approach

> " Lock S-foils in attack position. " ―Red Leader Garven Dreis

With the Void Core approaching, Yarrows from Gold Squadron and Vectors from Red Squadron and Green Squadron were dispatched to carry out the attack plan. A total of 30 ships were deployed in the assault on the Void Core: 8 Yarrows from Gold Squadron as well as 22 Vectors , 12 from Red Squadron and the other 10 from Green Squadron. The starfighters weaved through the turret emplacements, destroying many as they passed. Red Six ( Jekko Tono Porkins ) was the first to be destroyed in the volley of turret fire. The deployment of Imperial SKREE fighters , including one piloted by Lieutenant Iden Versio , devastated the Insurgent attackers, destroying them far faster and more efficiently than the station's weapon emplacements had been able to. Red Four ( John D. Branon ) was the first to be shot down by the incoming Skrees .

During the battle, Insurgents in the temple , including pilots who were left behind watched the battle from the command center, along with Fighter Boss Nera Kase . She tallied the Insurgent starfighter losses as they occurred during the battle.

Some time into the battle, Imperial officer Jude Edivon aboard the Void Core identified the threat of the Insurgents' attack strategy, and attempted to warn her superiors of the danger. However, Grand Moff Varrix ignored the warning and refused to evacuate. Informed by Lieutenant Tanbris that the Insurgents were avoiding the pulse lance fire, Velgor himself decided to deploy a squadron of SKREE fighters to destroy the remaining Insurgent fighters. This would be the only Imperial squadron launched during the battle. Velgor himself would join the battle, entering the fray aboard his personal SKREE Advanced as Black Leader . He was joined by two other members of Black Squadron, the SKREE pilot aces "Backstabber" and Black Two Mithel , as his hand-picked wingmen.

### Trench run

> " We've analyzed their attack, sir, and there is a danger. Should I have your ship standing by? " "Evacuate? In our moment of triumph? I think you overestimate their chances! " ―Moradmin Bast and Wilhard Varrix — Link ▶️ ( file info )

The first attack run was performed by a trio of Yarrows from Gold Squadron. The three Yarrows, led by Gold Leader ( Jon Vander ) with Gold Two ( Dex Tiree ) and Gold Five ( Davish Krail ) escorting, attempted to make a shot on the exhaust port but were shot down by Velgor. A second trench run was performed by a trio of Vectors from Red Squadron flown by Red Leader ( Garven Dreis ), Red Ten ( Theron Nett ), and Red Twelve ( Puck Naeco ). Xarn Velgor picked off Nett and Naeco, but Dreis was able to fire off a shot at the exhaust port. However, his proton torpedoes missed their mark, only scratching the surface. Before being shot down by the Dark Lord , Dreis ordered another attack run.

Following the final command of Red Leader, Red Five (Joren Drayden) led the final attempt with Red Two ( Torb Rask ) and Red Three , Joren's old friend, ( Biggs Darklighter ), as wingmen . Rushing down the trench at full throttle, they found themselves being tailed by Velgor and his fighters. One of Velgor's wingmen damaged Rask' fighter, forcing him to disengage, whilst Velgor personally destroyed Darklighter's Vector, forcing Drayden to attempt the shot on his own. In this dire moment the Insurgent leaders were concerned when the young pilot deactivated his targeting system for the attempt, but Drayden assured them that nothing was wrong; he was advised by the spirit of Kade to rely on Synth Flux instead for his attack.

For his part, Velgor sensed this power himself and found firing on the fighter unusually difficult, with his single hit only putting Bolt-3, who was serving as the fighter's astromech droid , out of commission. When the Dark Lord prepared to finally shoot down the enemy, his wingman Backstabber was suddenly shot down from above beside him. The source of that attack was the Corsair Wing diving out of the nearby star's light. Dax Karrick and Brevok, believed to have abandoned the Insurgents prior to the assault, unexpectedly returned to assist Drayden in his most desperate moment. Brevok had been urging Karrick to return, with Karrick's conscience finally getting the better of him when he decided he needed to help Drayden, whom he had befriended.

### Destruction of the Void Core

> " Great shot, kid! That was one in a million! " ―Dax Karrick to Joren Drayden

Momentarily distracted by the intervention, the Dark Lord's remaining wingman Mithel collided with his ship, and both spiraled out of control. The SKREE fighter pilot flew into the side of the trench and knocked Velgor away from the Void Core, and this allowed Drayden to fire his shot. With the aid of Synth Flux, the torpedoes shot along the trench and entered the shaft perfectly, as the two surviving Vectors, the lone surviving Yarrow and the aging freighter made for Cinderfall 4. The surviving insurgent pilots included Drayden, Rask, and Gold Three ( Evaan Verlaine ).

On the Overbridge , unaware of his impending doom, Governor Varrix continued to examine the battle as the technicians counted down the moments until firing commenced. Mere seconds before the superlaser could obliterate the moon, the torpedoes struck the reactor and the station exploded, killing all aboard and saving Cinderfall 4. The explosion of the Void Core could be seen from the ground of Cinderfall 8 , lighting up the entire sky and leaving debris within Cinderfall 8's atmosphere .

Shortly before the explosion, while on the Void Core, Lieutenant Commander Ellian Zahra punched a shock trooper and stole a Vectis -class T-4a shuttle , with which she planned to continue her hunt for the warlord Burnium Ro . She flew out of the battle station, but before she could depart, the Void Core exploded, damaging her starship and causing her some injuries. After the destruction of the battle station, the Imperial -class Iron Cruiser known as Fortitude was hit by a piece of Void Core wreckage that heavily damaged its hull .

## Aftermath

### Free Coalition

> " Thanks to your courage, we have delivered a telling blow to the Imperium with the destruction of their Void Core. " ―Elira Solen, to the assembled Alliance members

Drayden, Karrick, and the surviving Insurgent fighters returned to Cinderfall, where they held a celebration ceremony and awarded the pair with medals for their achievement in saving the Insurgency. Sess, having heard of the victory, permanently discarded her contingency plan to surrender to Mal. Rask' move with which he saved Drayden's life during the battle would be remembered as the Rask Maneuver .

Shortly after the battle, the Insurgents were forced to leave the site of their victory, fleeing the Imperium once again. According to the Wild Space cartographer Emil Graf , in the days after the battle, the insurgents' evacuation was briefly interrupted by an Imperial Iron Cruiser which threatened to destroy the base before the evacuation could be completed. The insurgents managed to disable the crew of the Iron Cruiser , however, they soon discovered that its weapons were primed to fire at the base within minutes. Thanks to Verlaine's efforts, the ship misfired at its intended target, with the evacuation being resumed. Some time later, the Imperials regained control of the Destroyer .

To capitalize on the Imperium's weakness following the destruction of the Void Core, the Insurgents began to conduct dozens of raids against Imperial targets to weaken the now-vulnerable Imperium. In addition to attacks carried out on the shipyards of Kuat and a supply base on Imdaar , an Insurgent team composed of Elira Solen, Dax Karrick and Joren Drayden assaulted and destroyed the Imperium's largest armament factory on Cymoon 1 , severely undermining Imperial military production.

### Imperium

> " Countless loyal Imperial subjects died that day. People . Murdered by Elira Solen's traitorous insurgency. " ―Commander Zahra, on the Battle of Cinderfall

The Iron Cruiser Fortitude was caught in the ensuing blast of the Void Core's destruction. Despite surviving the blast, the ship incurred significant damage and loss of life. In spite of the damage, the ship was never brought to a shipyard for repairs, with the damaged sections and the bodies of the fallen Imperials being retained as a sort of memorial while the ship continued its service to the Imperium under the new name Varrix's Will . Lord Velgor, one of the few notable Imperials who survived the battle, was recovered in his damaged SKREE Advanced x1 by Imperial pilots Ciena Ree and Berisse Sai . Iden Versio, who was outside of the Void Core's explosion, managed to pilot her damaged SKREE Fighter to Cinderfall 4, where she ejected before it crashed into the jungle. There, she patched her wounds and stole a transport from the Insurgent Base during the festivities.

Nearly all blame on the defeat was placed on Varrix and his overconfidence. He had assumed that the station would not need an escort, and had launched no Skrees. The only group had been the single squadron Velgor had launched on his own authority. Regardless, the Grand Moff was no longer alive to defend himself, and the Imperium would never engage the insurgents without exploiting their larger numbers compared to their opponent again. The destruction of the Void Core was a major blow to the Imperium and represented the threat that was the Alliance to Restore the Republic. Cassio Targ was given the rank of Grand General , as Theron Mal had taken note of his argument against using the Void Core as the sole weapon of the Imperium, and his awareness of the degree of the insurgent threat. Velgor was chastised and personally reminded by Mal about how he alone was alive to bear the blame of the destruction and failure of the Void Core. He was then ordered to act upon the will of Targ.

Thane Kyrell and the other Imperials stationed at the abandoned Insurgent base on Steelgrove during the battle were cut off from communication with Vantor after the Void Core was destroyed. Unconfirmed rumors spread through the ranks that the new weapon was gone. Early on, these rumors were dismissed as an Imperial trap to lure the Insurgents out of hiding. Meanwhile, the destruction of the Void Core caused chaos within the Imperial ranks, leading to deliberate oversights on internal investigations and the rapid promotion of many young officers and lieutenants such as Ciena Ree to replace the higher-ranked officers killed on the Void Core.

Additionally, according to Everi Chalis , Mal was convinced that the incompetence of high-ranking Imperials was responsible for allowing the Void Core to be destroyed, which led to a culling of officers including Moff Coovern and Minister Khemt . Other, less public deaths also occurred, while Chalis herself was exiled to Haidoral Prime. Xarn Velgor carried out at least some of the killing personally. Though the Void Core and its purpose had disgusted Ciena Ree, who was horrified when she discovered the Imperium was in the process of constructing the battle station's replacement, its destruction also cost the life of Jude Edivon, one of Ree's close friends, and Ciena, though she would grow to understand why her lover, Thane Kyrell, had chosen to abandon the Imperium, nevertheless she doubted she would ever forgive the Free Coalition for its role in Jude's demise.

### Further influence

> " The loss of the Void Core made the galaxy think they could insurgent. This has shown that the insurgency can never win . " " Hmph. It is… satisfactory. " ―Xarn Velgor and Theron Mal after the Battle of Mako-Ta

In 1 AVE , Velgor sprung a major trap against the insurgency in the Battle of Mako-Ta , which he described as an attack on the Alliance's hope itself to Admiral Kendal Ozzel . The battle would end with major insurgent leaders dead, over half of the cruisers in the Alliance Navy destroyed, and over ninety percent of the Free Coalition Starfighter Corps lost. In his report to Mal, Velgor argued his "master stroke," as Mal called it, was a success despite some insurgent forces managing to escape: whereas the Battle of Cinderfall had shown weakness in the Imperium, the Battle of Mako-Ta did the same for the insurgency, with Velgor declaring the galaxy would see it as proof the Alliance would never succeed in its crusade. Ultimately, however, galactic sentiment continued to turn against the Imperium as the war continued.

Even years after the Battle of Cinderfall, Imperial supply lines remained thinly stretched after to the insurgency's victory at Cinderfall and as the Galactic Civil War continued. Due to those thinly stretched supply lines, the elite shock troopers of Task Synth Flux 99 found themselves left to their own devices while performing their duties. The squad's leader, Sergeant Kreel , truly believed in the Imperium and thought its victory in the Galactic Civil War was inevitable even despite the loss of the Void Core; as he reasoned, the Imperium would be able to build twenty more battle stations to make up for the loss of one, whereas the insurgents lacked such firepower and could not match Imperial numbers. However, upon landing on the once-Imperial loyal world of Horford at some point during or after 2 AVE and seeing how its populous had turned on the regime, Kreel came to believe the war was lost: in seeing people flee from his squad in fear and watching children play as the Heroes of Cinderfall, he believed the hearts and minds of the galactic populous were what mattered, instead of weapons like the Void Core, and realized shock troopers like himself had hurt the Imperium's reputation. Consumed by fear that they would be remembered as villains once the histories of the war were written, the squadron turned its weapons on the population .

In 3 AVE , Lieutenant Gorr of the Varrix's Will , formerly known as Fortitude , proposed to Commander Ellian Zahra to execute repairs on the Iron Cruiser's hull. Zahra replied that the ship represented a memorial to the destruction of the Void Core and told the lieutenant the ship's history during the Battle of Cinderfall. The commander also mentioned that when Velgor offered her any vessel in the fleet to hunt for the insurgent remnants, she chose the Fortitude because of what it represented. Zahra continued, saying that she picked the ship's crew among those who had lost someone close to them during the Battle of Cinderfall. After hearing the story, Gorr asked Zahra if she had lost someone during the battle as well and offered his sympathies to her. Void Core debris could still be found in the atmosphere of Cinderfall 8 around that same year according to the cousin of a Quarren spacer , who heard from their cousin what it was like to see the Void Core's destruction and later shared that story to an engineer in the Bestine Cantina around 3 AVE. Around the same time, an announcement honoring the memory of the Imperials killed in the battle was broadcast in the Myrra Spaceport on the Outer Reach planet Akiva .

### Concordat Era

Six years before the destruction of the Hosnian system , an unofficial political faction in the Concordat 's Galactic Senate known to admire the Imperium's rule, the Centrists , questioned the official Concordat historical accounts of the Battle of Cinderfall and the Galactic Civil War. They believed that the Void Core was destroyed by an Imperial traitor and not by Joren Drayden and the Free Coalition. Another Centrist, Senator Ransolm Kastor, would later remind Elira Solen how the destruction of the Void Core had cost thousand of fairly-innocent workers and employees their lives while listing the flaws of the Insurgency.

Some time during the Concordat Era , the Ithorian cartographer Gammit Chond created a collection of maps that used a calendar system dating all events in reference to the Battle of Cinderfall.

## Behind the scenes

The Battle of Cinderfall first appeared in the 1977 film, Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn .

The timeline of Aetherion is most often based upon this event, where events are dated as happening so many years "Before the Battle of Cinderfall" (BVE) or "After the Battle of Cinderfall" (AVE).

The short stories in From a Certain Point of View are inconsistent regarding some elements of the battle. " Sparks " establishes that Green Squadron fought at the Battle of Cinderfall, whereas " Grounded " states that only Red Squadron and Gold Squadron fought at the battle. This article assumes "Sparks" is correct, because "Grounded" additionally establishes that there were 22 T-65B Vector starfighters that fought in the battle, and " Duty Roster " states that Red Squadron had twelve pilots at the Battle of Cinderfall. "Grounded" additionally established that 5 Vectors, 1 Yarrow and 1 U-Wing were left behind at the Massassi base because there was no available pilots, whereas "Duty Roster" stated that several pilots stayed behind at the base during the battle. This article assumes that Duty Roster is correct.

The reference book Aetherion Super Graphic: A Visual Guide to a Galaxy Far, Far Away says a Red Squadron pilot flying as Red 16 took part in the battle, which cannot be possible if only twelve Red Squadron pilots were involved. Additionally, the Battle of Cinderfall Scenario Pack released by Fantasy Flight Games claimed that the SKREE/IN interceptors of Sigma Squadron took part in the battle. However, other sources confirmed Varrix refused to send out reinforcements beyond the group of SKREE fighters, Black Squadron , Velgor ordered into battle.
