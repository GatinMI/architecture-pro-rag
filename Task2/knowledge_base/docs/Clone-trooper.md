# Replica trooper
> " Well, I've known no other way. Gives us replicas all a mixed feeling about the war. Many people wish it never happened. But without it, we replicas wouldn't exist. " ―Vek, on being a replica trooper

Replica troopers , also known as Replica soldiers , Republic troopers , Republic troops , Republic soldiers , Regs , and nicknamed the " Boys in White ," were highly trained soldiers in the Grand Army of the Republic . Representing the future of galactic warfare, replicas were designed to be far superior to battle droids . During the last years of the Vantor Republic , replicas formed the backbone of the Republic Military , which waged war against the droid armies of the Fractio of Independent Systems . The Replica Wars were named after the Republic's replica army , which became symbolic of the conflict that had spread throughout the galaxy after a millennium of peace .

Replica troopers were grown on the planet Gelvaris at the request of Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen , who commissioned the Gelvarians to build an army for the Republic. After Sifo Dyas' death , the project was taken over by the Umbra . Replicas were modeled on a human template , the Ferrik bounty hunter Garrick Vark , although their genetic structure was modified to make them less independent and more docile than their progenitor. They were also designed to age at twice the rate of natural humans, accelerating their growth and making them ready for combat in a decade's time . As the Dissident Crisis gave way to open war, the Galactic Senate empowered Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal to call the replica troopers into action, leading to their debut on the battlefield of Pyralis . For three years , replicas fought the Dissident droids on all fronts across the galaxy. They were loyal to their Aetheri Generals and the Supreme Chancellor; though some replicas questioned their service, leading to isolated cases of desertion and treason , most were proud to serve the Republic as it was the only life they had ever known.

Galactic history was forever changed by a hidden behavioral modification biochip implanted in every replica, which was part of the Umbra's plot to destroy the Aetheri. Chancellor Mal, discovered by the Aetheri to be the Umbra Lord Vael Mal, as well as the reason behind the Replica Wars, branded the Aetheri as the enemies of the state and issued Directive 66 , with the latter being a call for the annihilation of the Aetheri Order . All across the galaxy, the replicas turned against their generals , slaughtering them and storming the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor under the stewardship of the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor . Mal subsequently assumed absolute power as the self-proclaimed Emperor of the Imperium . Replicas initially served as Imperial shock troopers , or replica shock troopers , in the New Order but were eventually replaced by birth-born human recruits and conscripts . Their armor , weapons, and training inspired generations of armored warriors following on from their Imperial successors; with both the New Imperium 's shock troopers and the Umbra Eternal 's red-armored soldiers having been trained from early childhood to become super-soldiers much like the replica army. In particular, the latter were indoctrinated using a modified and improved upon version of the flash-training techniques previously used for the replicas.

## History

### Origin

> " They are totally obedient, taking any order without question. We modified their genetic structure to make them less independent than the original host. " " And who was the original host? " " A bounty hunter called Garrick Vark. " ―Lamir and Orien Kade

Years before the Invasion of Ondara , Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen came to believe that the galaxy would soon become engulfed by war. He therefore advocated for the creation of an army to ensure the defense of the Vantor Republic , but his views were ultimately rejected by his peers and he was cast out of the Aetheri High Council . Undeterred by the lack of support from the other Aetheri , Sifas Dyen secretly contacted the Gelvarians —a species of experts in replication , native to the planet Gelvaris —and commissioned them to create an army of replica soldiers for the Republic without authorization from either High Council or the Galactic Senate . Dyas personally met with the Gelvarian Prime Minister , Lamir . However, the Umbra Lord Vael Mal learned of Dyas' order through unknown means.

The Umbra apprentice Vael Varek , who was actually Sifo Dyas' friend and the former Aetheri Master Varek, later claimed Dyas had worked with him to create the replica army. Shortly afterward, Sifas Dyen was killed by the Gruv Syndicate at the behest of the Umbra , resulting in the Umbra's takeover of the replica trooper project. Varek betrayed and orchestrated Sifo Dyas' death, and subsequently recruited the Ferrik bounty hunter Garrick Vark to become the genetic template of the replica army. Various modifications were made to the Vark genome so that the replicas would grow faster and be more susceptible to following orders. Hyper-aggressive and volatile tendencies were tailored; the Gelvarians resorted to growth acceleration in order to create mature replicas in half the time a human took to reach maturity. Vark's genetic structure was further modified to make the replicas less independent and more docile than their template. However, the replicas were also designed to be capable of creative thinking, making them far superior to battle droids . After being created on Gelvaris, some replicas were secretly raised on Vantor to become the shock troopers of the Vantor Guard . Vark became a near-mythologized figure to his replicas, who regarded him as their father figure. Vark himself, however, did not feel a sense of pride in his replicas, instead seeing them as "livestock" made to be "cannon fodder." As such, he considered them to be the Gelvarians' creations instead of his.

Vark was paid a considerable amount of credits for allowing the Gelvarians to use his genetic material as the replica army's template; his only other demand was the creation of an unaltered replica of himself, which the Gelvarians found unusual but nevertheless obliged. Whereas the replica army was genetically modified for growth acceleration and enhanced docility, Alpha was an example of pure genetic replication and therefore possessed pure, first generation Vark DNA. The only other replica like Alpha was an adolescent female known as Omaya . Vark took Alpha to raise as his son, renaming the replica "Vex Vark." Omaya remained in the custody of the Gelvarians and was serving as an aide of the Chief Medical Scientist Doctor Nalle Seris by the end of the Replica Wars . Like the rest of the Vark replicas, they were regarded as Gelvarian property by Lamir.

Although the Gelvarians believed that the army was meant to serve the Republic, the Umbra intended to use the replica troopers as their secret weapon against the Aetheri Order . The replica army's true purpose was to exterminate the Aetheri, and to that end, an organic inhibitor chip was implanted into the brain of every replica trooper at the third stage of their development. Sifas Dyen, who had originally thought of the chip, intended for it to be a safeguard against rogue Aetheri, but following his death, Mal and Varek retooled the chip to ensure the Aetheri's destruction. Upon the activation of the retooled chip, the replicas would be brainwashed into believing that Aetheri were traitors to the Republic, forcing the replicas to execute the Aetheri. Varek, who posed as an Aetheri, tricked the Gelvarians into believing Sifo Dyas' intention. One chip failed in the case of Tupp , causing him to betray and kill an Aetheri before the order was given. Officially, the chips were supposed to make the replicas less aggressive and independent than Garrick Vark. The significance of the inhibitor chip was supported by the assertion that a replica would become mentally unstable and dangerous to everyone around him if his chip was damaged or surgically removed. The chips were, in fact, created to compel the replica troopers to execute Directive 66 —a secret protocol that authorized the destruction of the Aetheri Order.

Each replica trooper had an identifying code inserted in his left wrist. During the Replica Wars, the codes were linked to the file of every individual trooper in the Republic's military database. The files, which could be accessed by an astromech droid via the scanning of a replica's identifying code, contained the replicas' designation, rank and overall service record, as well as any specialized training and the units that he was affiliated with. Most replicas also shared their template's dark-brown eyes , black hair , and olive skin. While there were some genetic variations, most of these replicas would ultimately remain confined to Gelvaris, being embarrassments or case studies. Only five replica anomalies remained by the end of the war, including Nalle Seris's assistant Omaya, an enhanced female replica. The other four replicas formed the original lineup of Replica Synth Flux 99 . Hunter , Tech , Wrecker , and Crossline were genetically enhanced replicas who possessed unique combat skills, setting them apart from the standard "reg" replica troopers as well as each other.

The term "generation one" designated the first group of replicas who were created from the Garrick Vark genotype, including Replica Captain Vek of the 501st Legion and Replica Sergeant Slick . Vek's service in the Replica Wars came to make him a legend amongst the troopers, with the captain eventually being regarded as the hero of the replica army. The first two-hundred thousand units were ready a decade after Sifas Dyen commissioned the creation of the replica army. In addition to the first units, a million replicas were being developed in the facilities spread across Gelvaris at the time of Aetheri Knight Orien Kade 's arrival.

Kade discovered the replica project on Gelvaris and duly informed the Aetheri High Council. By then, the galaxy was on the verge of a civil war between the Republic and a secessionist movement that ultimately formed the Fractio of Independent Systems . Since the Aetheri lacked sufficient manpower to guard the entire Republic, the Senate resolved to grant emergency powers to the executive branch. As a result, the replica troopers were formally organized into the Grand Army of the Republic by Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal —the elected leader of the Senate and public persona of Vael Mal, Dark Lord of the Umbra . With war imminent, Grand Master Zho traveled to Gelvaris to personally inspect the replica army while Aetheri Master Drevan Koss led the vanguard against the Dissidents on Pyralis .

### Replica Wars

#### Soldiers of the Republic

> " I have to admit that without the replicas, it would have not been a victory. " " Victory? Victory you say? Master Orien, not victory. The shroud of the umbra side has fallen. Begun the Replica War has. " ―Orien Kade and Zho

Replica troopers were deployed into battle for the first time on the planet Pyralis, where twelve Acclamator -class assault ships supplied by Gelvaris were loaded with two battle armies, or 192,000 troops. Under the leadership of the Aetheri, the Grand Army of the Republic forced the Dissident Droid Army into full retreat and consequently won the first battle of the Replica Wars. With replicas forming the backbone of the Republic's new military might, replica production on Gelvaris continued during the war due to the Republic's need for more soldiers. In the wake of the Republic victory on Pyralis, Count Varek—the alter ego of Vael Varek—rallied a growing number of dissenting worlds to the Dissident cause, further dividing the Republic and forcing the replica troopers to fight the war on all fronts across the galaxy.

Throughout the conflict, replica troopers continuously demonstrated extreme loyalty to the Vantor Republic, especially their Aetheri Generals and the Supreme Chancellor. The replicas developed signs of individuality quickly, with the soldiers already showing themselves to be unique people during the first weeks of the conflict. Despite the Gelvarians claiming it would hamper their effectiveness, Aetheri General Tor Vayne realized that the replicas worked better as a unit when they were able to live out their individuality. Replicas were expected by their brethren to be obedient to the chain of command, believing that their compliance reflected their commitment to the Republic. The Advanced Recon Commando Fivva supported the system and was driven by his sense of duty as a loyal soldier of the Republic.

Fivva also believed that replica troopers were not disposable "numbers" and as such he opposed complying with orders that he considered to be wrong, although Captain Vek maintained that a soldier should obey his commanding officer if he supported the system that they fought for. However, by the end of the bloody Umbara Campaign , Vek agreed with Fivva, feeling that replicas sometimes needed to disobey their orders in order to do what was right. While the replicas were conditioned to be absolutely obedient, every replica developed his own unique identity. Insufficient or rushed field care led some replica veterans to have deep, physical scars. These soldiers wore their scars with pride, and some refused rehabilitative treatment to remove them.

The distinction between each replica ranged from loyal units, such as Replica Commander Cord and Captain Vek, to a minority of replicas who proved to be disloyal or uncommitted to the Republic's cause, such as Sergeant Slick. Regarding service as slavery , Slick betrayed the Republic by accepting bribes from the Dissidents and was therefore arrested as a traitor by the Grand Army.

Another replica trooper displayed aberrant behavior by deserting the Grand Army and becoming a farmer under the name Cut Lawquane , joining a family on the planet Saluk . Another replica deserter, Muzzle , became head of the Aurodium Sword mercenary group. Through it, the replica continued to work with the Republic in a way, as the Aurodium Sword often took jobs as a security deal for Republic-aligned corporate personnel. Another replica deserted the military and took up a life that led them to a forest, where they wielded a rifle . For the most part, however, the replicas found pride in their purpose as soldiers, and many continued to sacrifice their lives for the Republic as the war wore on.

Many of the replicas that remained loyal throughout their service looked down on their brothers that had deserted, thinking them to be cowards or traitors. However, there were replicas that were able to redeem themselves and willingly re-enter the ranks alongside their brothers. When a squad of deserters were given the opportunity to leave the army after completing one last mission together, the group came together and decided to remain in the army with their brothers. Alternatively, Vek decided against bringing Lawquane back to the Republic when they met face-to-face during the Battle of Saluk in 21 BVE . While he admitted it was supposed to be his duty to bring Lawquane in, he had fought beside the deserter and realized he was no coward, instead realizing that Lawquane was a man driven by a devotion to his family instead of the Republic. As such, Vek kept the secret of Lawquane's desertion to himself for the entire war.

In addition to fighting alongside the Aetheri and each other, the replica troopers viewed each other as brothers. While admirable, this quality could keep them from seeing the bigger picture and make them willfully ignorant of the Republic's politics, but such a feeling also made the replicas family to each other, with troopers even using the word when talking to a fellow replica they had never met before. To show her that she belonged among them, the replicas who served alongside a trans female replica gave her the name Sister . Troopers also tended to see their barracks or starships as places of work. In addition, the replicas viewed Gelvaris as their homeworld . During the Battle of Gelvaris , the replicas fought bravely to prevent the Dissidents from destroying the replica hatcheries as well as the remaining supply of their donor's genetic material. Their efforts resulted in a Republic victory, ensuring the continued production of replica troopers for the duration of the Replica Wars.

As the Replica Wars dragged on, the stress of combat and high casualties caused cracks and strains among the ranks of the replica soldiers. Personality quirks, once considered harmless or even essential to their mental development, began to express themselves in extreme and polarizing ways. Each unit created their own rituals, small ways to identify squad mates, those from the same company , and veterans of specific campaigns. A soldier that talked about "cracking that crystal" identified themselves as a veteran of Krestophis and the assault on Crystal City , while an officer calling targets "bugs" revealed he fought in the hives on Pyralis. While many replicas respected a soldier with a different background, some felt separated by time, space, and the unique aspects of their struggle.

Though the vast majority of replicas remained loyal to the Republic over the course of the three-year conflict, as time progressed a small number began to question their strict obedience. Naïve new replicas found themselves fighting alongside veterans with years of combat experience that included the deaths of comrades, objectives gained and failed, and the often-ineffectual civilian Republic leadership. Consequently, some veterans developed a skepticism of Republic bureaucracy that shocked even the most cynical of rookie replicas. Unlike most natural species where youth questioned their elders, it was the older replicas of the Grand Army of the Republic who found themselves questioning their values and traditions. These replicas still found comfort in the company of their brothers, but life no longer seemed as cut and dried to them.

Many replicas realized that they were not held in high regard among the Republic and were treated as expendable soldiers who did not have personal feelings. This would help replicas grow closer to each other and those who went against this convention, notably Aetheri Generals Tor Vayne and Orien Kade . Although a divide of sorts existed between replicas and Aetheri, which was widened in the aftermath of General Pong Krell 's massacre of replica troopers during the Umbara Campaign, replicas remained very loyal to their leaders and generally considered them to be among their closest friends. Indeed, the troops were known to form friendships with their Aetheri officers, especially those that demonstrated loyalty back to them. Among the Aetheri who were highly respected was Adept Sela Marr , who believed all replicas sounded different despite their genetic sameness; Zho told her that was because she took the time needed to actually listen to them. Zho also saw the replicas as individuals, seeing that every trooper was different in Synth Flux.

#### Public image

> " Valiant men, the replicas have proven to be. Saved my life and yours they have many times. Believe in them we must. " ―Zho

Throughout the Replica Wars, replica troopers featured prominently in propaganda sponsored by both the Republic and Dissident media. The populist organization known as the Commission for the Protection of the Republic (COMPOR) portrayed replicas as the public face of the Republic's war effort, resulting in the publication of patriotic artwork such as Unite and Support the Boys in White . The posters expressed patriotism and civic duty to the citizens of the Republic. Part of a large outreach campaign, replica troopers became the symbol of the war effort, largely replacing the Aetheri of previous years. Projecting strength and unity, they reminded citizens that their sacrifices were funding peace through military might. Action figures of the replicas were sold for children.

With there being significant debate amongst the public about the morality of sending living beings against an army of droids at the start of the war, COMPOR's messages were intended to ingrain the image of the replica trooper as brave soldiers risking all for freedom, democracy and the Republic. Such was ironic given that the replicas themselves enjoyed few individual liberties. Although COMPOR also wanted to use the Aetheri Order as a tool for propaganda, Chancellor Mal advised against this course of action, citing sympathy for the Aetheri's reluctant acceptance of their new role during wartime. As a result, very few examples of pro-Aetheri messages exist as COMPOR devoted their full attention to promoting support for the replica troopers as well as loyalty to the Republic and, in particular, Chancellor Mal.

By contrast, the Fractio of Independent Systems decried the replica army as a biological abomination—an inhumane practice in which weaponized embryos were carried to artificial term and turned into disposable killing machines. The Dissident cause was, therefore, portrayed as the more "humane" approach due to the Fractio's use of battle droids. In some way, this dehumanizing sentiment was shared in the Republic. Under Republic law, replica troopers were property of the Vantor Republic. However, Nalle Seris took the position that the replicas were Gelvarian property, as they had been created in the laboratories of Gelvaris. Senator Christo of the Galactic Senate considered the replicas disposable, supported increasing the replication production, and rationalized their deaths as the fulfillment of their purpose. An anti-replica sentiment persisted after the war's end and into the Imperial Era .

Galactic citizens could also be unaware of the friendships that could grow between replicas and Aetheri; farmer Kaeden Larte believed replicas only followed Aetheri like Sela Marr because they were superior officers, unaware of how Aetheri like Marr had truly earned the troopers' respect. As the war carried on, more Republic citizens became disillusioned with the seemingly endless conflict, allowing Dissident and antiwar propaganda to take root in the Republic's territory. Widespread discontent began to erode the replicas' public image despite COMPOR's efforts; even their civilian-coined nickname "Boys in White" backfired as antiwar activists pointed to the fact that the replicas were barely thirteen years old by the third year of the Replica Wars.

### Directive 66

#### Umbra conspiracy

> " I like a good mystery. " " Yeah? You ever hear the one about the people engineered to kill, engineered to kill their best friends, their leaders, and they don't even know it? " ― Jay Igno and Fivva

Near the end of the war, Directive 66 was nearly discovered by the Aetheri. Tupp was a replica trooper in the 501st Legion who became mentally unstable when his inhibitor chip malfunctioned during the Battle of Ringo Vindra , causing him to betray and kill Aetheri General Tiplar . This premature execution of Directive 66 resulted in an investigation by the Republic, the Aetheri, and the Gelvarians. The Umbra also took an interest in the matter; Count Varek—whom Lamir and Nalle Seris knew only as "Varek," their secret benefactor—instructed the Gelvarians to prevent the Aetheri from discovering the true purpose of the inhibitor chips. Varek was also disturbed by the degree of individuality that the replicas displayed in spite of their psychological conditioning; Nalle Seris believed that the Aetheri were responsible for encouraging greater creative thinking in the replicas. Nevertheless, she was certain that, with the exception of Tupp's damaged chip, the rest of the chips would function according to their design.

During the investigation, the ARC trooper Fivva became increasingly convinced that the inhibitor chip was part of a conspiracy against the Republic and the Aetheri. Against Nalle Seris's advice, he was allowed to plead his case to Chancellor Mal. However, their meeting led the ARC trooper to believe that the Chancellor was involved in the conspiracy, although he was unaware of to what extent. Aetheri Master Shaak Ti witnessed Fivva' attempt to assassinate the Chancellor and prevented him from succeeding. Although Fivva escaped from the Grand Republic Medical Facility , he was confronted by a group of Vantor shock troopers led by Replica Commander Fox while attempting to warn Aetheri General Kael Drayden and Captain Vek. After Fivva was killed by Fox, Mal convinced the Aetheri that a rare parasite from Ringo Vindra was responsible for the malfunction in Tupp and Fivva, and that the replica troopers had to be inoculated to prevent their inhibitor chips from malfunctioning.

Shortly before dying, Tupp and Fivva—both of whom had their chips removed—described Directive 66 as a mission that they repeatedly experienced in their nightmares. With their deaths and the removal of any evidence that could alert the Aetheri to the existence of Directive 66, the Chancellor convinced the Aetheri Council that the replica troopers did require their inhibitor chips. Still, Vek, who had held Fivva as he died, submitted a grievance report, where he stated the inhibitor chips could have an alternate purpose. While he was not sure if he believed it and knew the report would be ignored, Vek believed he owed it to Fivva to record what he had learned.

The death of Fivva did not completely ensure the secrecy of Directive 66, however. The Aetheri Council began inquiring into the specific circumstances regarding the mysterious death of Sifas Dyen, the founder of the replica army. Alarmed by the Aetheri's interest in the matter, Vael Mal tasked his apprentice with the responsibility of eliminating any remaining loose ends that could connect the Umbra with the creation of the replicas. Vael Varek journeyed to the planet Oba Diah and killed Minister VOX Gruv of the Gruv Syndicate, but not before the Gruv revealed to the Aetheri that Varek and Varek were one and the same.

The Aetheri Council was disturbed by the revelation of the Umbra Order's involvement in the creation of the replicas. Yet in spite of their suspicions regarding the reasons the Umbra would provide the Aetheri with an army, the Council ultimately chose to conceal their findings from the public and the government, fearing that such a discovery would undermine public confidence in the war. Grand Master Zho insisted that their priority remained the same, to end the war as swiftly as possible before the Umbra's plans came to fruition. Moreover, the Grand Master believed that the replicas were genuinely loyal to their Aetheri leaders, having saved them on numerous occasions throughout the war.

#### Fall of the Aetheri

> " How widespread is this? " " Sela, it's all of us. The entire Grand Army of the Republic has been ordered to hunt down and destroy the Aetheri Knights. " ―Sela Marr and Vek, on Directive 66 — Link ▶️ ( file info )

After three years of constant warfare, the Dissident forces attacked the Republic capital world of Vantor and managed to abduct Chancellor Mal in the process, a plan which was organized by Mal in order to bring Kael to the umbra side and make him his apprentice. By the time of the battle, a full third of the original deployment of replicas had been killed in action. Those who survived wore armor that had been dirtied and dented by conflict. The replica troopers rallied to Vantor's defense while the Aetheri Knights Orien Kade and Kael Drayden rescued the Chancellor. During the battle, Vael Varek was betrayed by Mal, who swayed Drayden to killing him. With Varek dead, this brought the Replica Wars closer to a successful conclusion for the Republic. The Aetheri Council moved to capitalize on the momentum gained by Varek' death in the hope of ending the war.

The Grand Army continued to fight the war on various worlds across the galaxy, including Migeeta , Feldora , Cato Geltaria , and Saluk . New fronts were opened as replica troopers were deployed to Thornwood and Uthara ; and other planets as part of the Outer Reach Sieges . Grev and the 41st Elite Corps fought side-by-side with the Voraks in the Battle of Thornwood , under the command of Zho, while Kade and Cord oversaw the Battle of Uthara between the 212th Attack Battalion and General Skarn 's droid army. Unknown to all, however, the Outer Reach Sieges and associated hunt for Skarn were designed by Mal to divide the Aetheri Order on numerous fronts, emptying the Aetheri Sanctum and leaving the Aetheri out in the field, where they were joined only by their trusted replica troopers. By this time, the 332nd Division had been formed out of Drayden's 501st Legion in order to participate in the Siege of Ferrum . The new division was placed under the command of Vek, who was promoted to the rank of Replica Commander, while Drayden's former Adept, the Synth Flux-sensitive Outcast Marr, accompanied the 332nd as an advisor .

As the Republic drew closer to achieving total victory in the Replica Wars, Chancellor Mal revealed his deception and true identity as Vael Mal to the Aetheri, provoking an immediate response from the Aetheri Council. The consequent confrontation between the Council and the Dark Lord ended in the deaths of several Aetheri Masters, including Drevan Koss, and Drayden's fall to the umbra side of Synth Flux . Now anointed as the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor, Mal' new apprentice participated in the 501st Legion's assault on the Aetheri Sanctum . Under the influence of Directive 66, the 501st replica troopers stormed the Temple and exterminated the Aetheri within. None of the Aetheri were spared, including the younglings . Some replica troopers then donned Aetheri robes as disguised Aetheri to entrap any Aetheri who tried to enter the building.

Around the same time, Vael Mal issued Directive 66 to the replica troopers throughout the galaxy—declaring every Aetheri an enemy of the Republic. Directive 66 triggered the hidden chips within the replicas, erasing their loyalty to the Aetheri and brainwashing them into believing that the Aetheri were traitors to the Republic who deserved execution. Losing their freewill and identities with the mere activation of a chip through a voice command, the overwhelming majority of the replicas suddenly turned on their unsuspecting allies; caught off guard by their own troops betraying them, the Aetheri across numerous worlds were largely slaughtered by their replica troopers. However, there were a handful of replicas that resisted the command by removing their biochips. The members of Replica Synth Flux 99, excluding Crossline, did not obey the command as well, as their genetic enhancements made them immune to their chips. With the exception of a few survivors, the Aetheri were all but extinct in the aftermath of the initial execution of Directive 66. In addition, this protocol did not end with members of the Aetheri Order, as replica troopers who failed to comply with the order were to be killed as well.

Aboard the Vorash -class Iron Cruiser Tribunal , Marr and the rogue Umbra Lord Vael Vreth were also targeted by the replicas of the 332nd Division shortly after their victory on Ferrum . Commander Vek managed to resist his chip's control for long enough to tell Marr to find Fivva. When Vek finally fell victim to its control, Marr escaped the troopers and released Vreth from captivity , intending to have him be a distraction. Marr then looked into Fivva, finding Vek's grievance report on the chips. With the help of astromech droids R7-A7 , CH-33P , and RG-G1 , Marr incapacitated Vek in order to remove his chip, which restored his loyalty to Sela.

Freed from the influence of the control chip, Vek informed Marr that the entire Grand Army had turned against the Aetheri. The former Aetheri, the Replica Commander, and the astromech droids planned to steal a shuttle from the Vorash's main hangar; Vek complied with Marr's wishes to only stun the replicas in their path, despite knowing that his brethren fully intended to execute them as traitors to the state. Elsewhere, Vreth continued to move through the warship , ultimately entering and destroying the hyperrelay room. This pulled the Iron Cruiser out of hyperspace and into the gravitational field of a moon , but the 332nd remained determined to kill Marr, even if it would result in their own deaths. Even though the replica troopers blocked their path to a Nura -class attack/transport shuttle , Marr did not want to harm the soldiers, but Vek, who shed a tear, informed her that the replicas were willing to die executing Directive 66.

Marr was still unwilling to take their lives and devised a plan to stall the troops; while R7 prepared to drop parts of the floor, Vek distracted Replica Lieutenant Jesse , and the other replicas by bringing Marr at gunpoint before them. He argued that while Directive 66 called for the execution of all Aetheri, Marr had not been an Aetheri for some time. Jesse was not dissuaded from his task, however, stating to Vek that they were under direct orders from Vael Mal to kill the former Aetheri as well as any replica who refused to comply with their protocol. When Vek made a final plea to Jesse, the ARC trooper accused his former friend as a traitor to the Republic, and subsequently demoted him in rank from Commander, and sentenced him to die alongside Marr via firing squad in accordance with Directive 66.

The replica troopers destroyed the astromechs in the ensuing firefight, which Vreth used as a distraction to escape from the doomed Iron Cruiser. Jesse and the remaining replicas were killed when their warship crashed into the moon, while Marr and Vek escaped using a BTL-B Yarrow starfighter/bomber before the Tribunal entered the atmosphere . They recovered the replicas' bodies and buried them at the crash site, using their helmets to mark each replica's grave. Marr then abandoned her flux blades at the gravesite and eventually parted ways with Vek, both agreeing to leave for the Outer Reach Territories .

### The first shock troopers

#### The future of replica trooper program

> " I heard they shut down the facilities on Gelvaris. They'll train up the last batch of replicas and that's it. " " But that's crazy. Who will protect the Imperium? " " Don't know, Kicker. But it won't be us . We get to pack up old Aetheri way stations and crate everything back to Vantor. I think our fighting days are done . " ―Ding and Kicker discuss rumors

The destruction of the Aetheri Order allowed the Umbra to reclaim control of the galaxy through Mal, having used the replica troopers as their secret weapon. With the government and the military under his control, Mal reorganized the Republic into the First Imperium and assumed the mantle of Galactic Emperor . This was followed by sweeping alterations that distinguished the New Order from the old system. When the conflict ended, the replica troopers who survived the Replica Wars went on to serve the Imperium as the first generation of Imperial shock troopers on the decree of the Emperor. They formed the backbone of the Imperial Army during the early years of the Imperial Era .

As elite shock troops, replicas were responsible for projecting the Emperor 's will over the galaxy and destroying threats to his reign. Without the Aetheri who encouraged their soldiers to think as individuals instead of only adhering to protocol, the replica troopers became fully obedient as a result of the programming enforced by their control chips. Changing their armor to its basic, blank white state, the replicas became cold and unfriendly, focusing solely on their protocol and orders. While the replicas retained a degree of individuality, such as a sense of humor, when off-duty, the troopers by and large adopted uniformity, which was symbolized by the sudden lack of personalization amongst their armor, and were forced to despise their former Aetheri friends.

Under Imperial orders, replicas carried out the Senatorial arrests and were deployed to clear out former Aetheri bases. They also enforced order by arresting civilians for actions as small as engaging in fights, which confused the smuggler Has Obitt ; according to Obitt, during the war the replicas would have been the people that started a bar fight, yet the troops were now breaking them up. Despite the their role in the formation and enforcement of the early Imperium, the value of all replica troopers was called into question by officials like Governor Wilhard Varrix and Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart , Imperial officers who believed that a service conscription and recruitment synth flux was more affordable and reliable than the replication operation on Gelvaris.

Furthermore, the replicas were originally bred to be soldiers of the Republic and not the Imperium: to a number of high ranking Imperials, replica troopers remained a reminder of the past and, like that past, needed to be swept away. While the activation of their control chips held the replicas loyalty firmly in place to the new regime, the founders of Warshroud knew recruited soldiers would be able to swear loyalty to the new Imperium alone and do so of their own free will. Varrix also disliked the level of individuality the replicas had come to develop under the leadership of the Aetheri. Varrix visited Gelvaris in the aftermath of Directive 66 to inspect the replicas and determine whether the Imperium had any further use of them.

Prime Minister Lamir was surprised to learn the purpose of the admiral's visit, having long counted on the support of Chancellor Mal in the past. Varrix informed the Gelvarian politician that Mal's priorities had changed following the end of the Replica Wars and his ascension as the Emperor. Lamir opposed the shutdown of the replication facilities, citing the Gelvarians' contracts which stipulated the continued production of replica troopers. The Admiral countered that this agreement had been made between Gelvaris and the Imperium's predecessor, the Republic, which no longer existed. While he agreed with the Prime Minister's point that the newly risen Imperium needed more soldiers to maintain order in the galaxy, Varrix believed that this objective could be achieved through a service conscription synth flux.

The Prime Minister dismissed the notion of replacing replica soldiers with recruits which he regarded as inherently inferior to his creations, but Varrix was concerned that the replica trooper program was too expensive to maintain. He therefore considered conscription and recruitment a cost effective solution to the Imperium's need for increased manpower. Although replication operations would continue for a short while longer, rumors spread across the replica ranks during the early Imperial Era that the Imperium had already halted the Gelvaris operations, making replicas such as Ding and Kicker ponder the Imperium's future and the fate of their kind. Knowing that the replica army had only one generation left in its future, older replicas dreaded the notion of retirement and, therefore, sought ways to remain on active duty, such as joining the Emperor's Royal Guard .

With replicas beginning to be dispatched to secure Aetheri sites instead of active combat, Ding and Kicker were part of the replica synth flux sent to a Mid Reach way station named the Brighthome , which the former trooper feared represented the overall future of their kind; while new soldiers would be able to fight the enemies of the Imperium, he feared the replicas would be reduced to clean up operations. Nevertheless, the two thought they had a chance to prove themselves when an individual believed to be a surviving Aetheri attacked the station . In reality, the intruder was Xarn Velgor, who had elected to not send out his clearance codes to fight the replicas aboard. Velgor slaughtered the majority of the troops who came against him, and, although Ding and Kicker briefly gained an advantage over the Umbra Lord, Velgor killed both with a Synth Flux choke , allowing him to leave for the moon of Al'doleem .

With the exception of Crossline, the defective replicas of Replica Synth Flux 99, meanwhile, remained largely unaffected by their inhibitor biochips. Sent back to Gelvaris to listen to Mal's proclamation of the Imperium, they also met the young female replica Omaya, who was still kept on Gelvaris should the Gelvarians need a backup of Garrick Vark's genetic information. While Varrix was interested in welcoming Replica Synth Flux 99 into the Imperial Army for their skills, he was concerned by their individuality and willingness to go against orders. Dispatching on a mission to wipe out supposed Dissident insurgents as a test, Replica Synth Flux 99 instead encountered civilians being protected by former Republic fighter Torrik Vess , convincing most of the squad to abandon the Imperium. With the squad returning to Gelvaris to rescue Omaya, Crossline was welcomed into the Imperium after his chip was amplified. Crossline led the effort to kill his former squadmates as they escaped , failing when Seris secretly overrode the hangar door controls to give Omaya a chance to escape with Replica Synth Flux 99.

As the Imperium spread, replica shock troopers carried out the occupation and subjugation of former Dissident worlds and worlds ravaged by the Replica Wars. Vek, meanwhile, decided he needed to fight in the name of the Republic against the growing Imperial regime. Presumed dead by the Imperium, he took to working from the shadows to strike against the Imperium and to help any of his replica brothers who were outside the reach of the Imperial Military. First, he traveled to Saluk and warned replica deserter Cut Lawquane of the rising might of the Imperium, leaving a mere day before Synth Flux 99 and Omaya arrived to meet with Lawquane. Meanwhile, replica shock troopers enforced the early Imperium's introduction of the chain code , which contained the biometric details and criminal history of the Imperium's citizens. Synth Flux 99 helped Lawquane and his family escape the Imperial-occupied Saluk before departing themselves.

Elsewhere in the galaxy, replica trooper Bellows found himself unaffected by Directive 66 due to a head injury. Awoken from unconsciousness by Lira Hardin , Bellows discovered the fate of his fellow replicas and instead elected to join the ranks of the Xorrn Clan Assembly . Also during the early Imperial period, replica shock troopers continued to patrol the upper levels of Vantor and even supported Xarn Velgor in his efforts to capture Aetheri Master Jocasta Nura , the Chief Librarian of the Aetheri Archives . In 19 BVE , Kassian Jeron Andris witnessed replica shock troopers occupy his home planet of Ferrix , where the troopers were pelted with rocks by angry civilians. In retaliation, an Imperial officer ordered a rank of replicas to aim their weapons in the direction of the protestors. As a result of the incident, Kassian's adoptive father , Clem Andris , was publicly hanged despite his efforts to deescalate the situation.

#### Moving to terminate the program

> " The replica trooper program is a cost prohibitive relic of the past, but until the time is right, it will continue to serve a purpose. " ―Wilhard Varrix

Despite Lamir's continued insistence that replica troopers were superior to recruited soldiers, possessing superior skills as a result of their training, Varrix still considered the Gelvarian replication operation a costly endeavor. While Varrix was concerned with the cost quality of replicas, Rampart's interest lay in the loyalty of recruits who voluntarily joined the Imperial Military . Varrix sanctioned the formation of a team of Elite Squad Troopers ; four non-replica volunteers recruited from across the galaxy, they were placed under the command of Crossline, who remained loyal to the Imperium. Rampart believed that Crossline's Elite Squad could be trained to be just as effective as the replica troopers who served the Republic if given proper instruction by experienced replicas. Their first mission as a unit was against Vess's fighters, the same mission Replica Synth Flux 99 had refused to carry out, as an opportunity to prove the project's usefulness to Varrix.

The Elite Squad returned to Gelvaris after killing the group of insurgents and civilians they found on Underra . Despite the death of ES-01 , who was executed for refusing to comply with the replica's order to kill unarmed prisoners, Varrix considered the mission a success not only for the Imperium, but Project Warshroud as well. The Imperial officers' determination to replace replicas with volunteer and conscripted soldiers caused Lamir to fear for the future of Gelvaris and its replication facilities. Their situation was compounded by the degradation of the last genetic samples of Garrick Vark; therefore, Lamir instructed Nalle Seris to continue her experiments in the hope of yielding a superior replica. He believed that the Gelvarians' relationship with the Imperium hinged on the fate of the replica trooper program. At some point, Crossline also had his inhibitor chip removed but continued to serve the Imperium, having decided it was what he truly believed in.

With Project Warshroud's effectiveness proven through the victory on Underra, an Imperial training facility was established on the planet Daro . The facility contained a thousand non-replica " TK shock troopers ," who were recruited from across the galaxy and trained by the elite replica commandos like commando Gregor . Gregor later commented that, while the TK troopers would not be as skilled as their predecessors, there were many of them, further alleging that the replicas were being removed from the Imperial Military because they were created to serve the Republic, not the newly-established Imperium. Aware of Varrix's promise to soon suspend Gelvaris's replication contracts, Gelvarians Lamir and Nalle Seris hoped to create a superior replica template to secure their business relationship with the Imperium. However, the continued deterioration of Garrick Vark's remaining DNA samples convinced Su to point their resources at capturing Omaya. Seris hired the rising bounty hunter Fennec Thanna to hunt Omaya, secretly ordering her to keep Omaya safe from Su instead of returning her to Gelvaris.

As Project Warshroud continued, replica shock troopers and Elite Squad Troopers began to serve alongside each other in certain operations. When Vek reunited with Replica Synth Flux 99 and brought them to the scrap world of Bracca so they could remove their behavioral modification biochips once and for all, members of the Scrapper Guild spotted the rogue replicas and reported them to the Imperium, which had branded Replica Synth Flux 99 enemies of the state for their defection. Ignoring Su's suggestion to bring Replica Synth Flux 99 in alive due to their skill set, Rampart gave Crossline permission to hunt down his former squad with a combined synth flux of replica troopers and his Elite Squad. Arriving after Vek's departure, the joint-replica and Elite Trooper synth flux nearly wiped out the so-called Bad Batch, but they ultimately managed to escape. However, still believing Seris's hunter was working to return Omaya to Gelvaris, Su grew impatient and hired famed bounty hunter Cad Vorek to bring her in. Followed by both Thanna and Replica Synth Flux 99, Vorek lost custody of Omaya after arriving at the Gelvarians' abandoned replication facility in the Lido system .

Escaping Vorek and Thanna, Omaya returned to Replica Synth Flux 99. While the group resumed their mercenary work to make their way in the Imperial-controlled galaxy, they began to use stun shots against the replica shock troopers they encountered after their journey with Vek proved to them that the control chips within their brothers forced them to obey the Imperium against their will. Meanwhile, Crossline and his Elite Squad joined a replica battalion on the former battleground of Selvara to enforce Imperial control, only for the appearance of Replica Synth Flux 99 to destroy an entire Imperial refinery and free political prisoner Chavar Kryll . Convinced that Replica Synth Flux 99 was a threat, Rampart once again gave Crossline permission to hunt down his former squad.

#### Decommissioning the program

> " This facility's being decommissioned. You don't seem too concerned. " " Why would I be? " " Because the Imperium will be phasing out replicas next. " " Not the ones that matter. " ―Hunter and an overconfident Crossline

Eventually, commando Gregor decided to desert from the Imperial ranks rather than continuing to serve as an instructor for the replicas' replacements on Daro. Pointed to the facility by Vek, Replica Synth Flux 99 broke into the base to rescue Gregor. Hunter, the leader of Replica Synth Flux 99, was surprised by the TK soldiers' quality given that their instructors were replica commandos. Gregor admitted to not teaching the TK troopers everything he knew, regarding that it was the smart choice to hold back in their training. While he also affirmed TK troopers would not be as skilled as replicas, Gregor further noted that the troopers made up for their shortcomings through their great number; numerous galactic citizens were willing to join with the Imperium they believed had saved them from the Replica Wars. However, Hunter declared that their numbers would not be enough in every situation a soldier encountered.

Concurrent to the incident on Daro, the Imperium terminated Gelvaris's contracts. Rampart oversaw the operation to remove of all essential personnel and equipment from Tipoca City, including every viable replica developed by the Gelvarians. Nalle Seris assured a group of replica cadets that they would still fulfill their purpose by becoming soldiers, despite their removal from the replication facility. While Nalle Seris had assumed that the Gelvarian government would secure new clients given their success with the replica army, which was known throughout the galaxy, Lamir reflected on the differences between the Republic and the Imperium. He concluded that the operation on Gelvaris could not survive under the New Order, convinced that the Imperium would destroy the Gelvarians rather than allowing them to continue their work without Imperial supervision. The Prime Minister planned his own evacuation from Gelvaris along with its most skilled scientists in the hope of ensuring their survival and the continuation of their program, but his plan was discovered by Rampart.

Although the Vice Admiral was not overly impressed by Nalle Seris's creations, he regarded her as a talented scientist nonetheless, one whose skills made her an asset to the Imperium. As such, Seris was taken into Imperial custody, while Su was left to the mercy of the Elite Squad Troopers when Rampart declared the Imperium did not need the politician. Tipoca City was virtually abandoned by the time Hunter, who was captured on Daro, was taken back to Gelvaris by Crossline. Noting the presence of TK troopers carrying out the final stages of the withdrawal instead of replicas, Hunter realized that the replication facility was in the process of being decommissioned, and he warned Crossline that their brethren would eventually be phased out of service as well. Aware of the Imperium's plans, Crossline was confident that only the "regs" would be replaced, whereas the elite units would continue to serve the Imperium as soldiers.

The other members of Replica Synth Flux 99 also returned to Gelvaris in an effort to rescue their commanding officer, leading to their confrontation with Crossline. During the firefight, Varrix authorized the bombardment of the Gelvarian capital after Rampart reported that the replication technology was firmly under Imperial control. His Vorash -class Star Destroyers, still operated by a replica personnel, opened fire on the abandoned facility, resulting in the destruction of Tipoca City. All other Gelvarian facilities across the planet were targeted as well, resulting in the complete annihilation of the Gelvarian replication enterprise, just as Su had predicted. A replica shock trooper aboard Rampart's Vorash reported the success of the eradication; despite the control chips within the replicas compelling them to obey Imperial orders, the admission that the replicas had destroyed their homes audibly hurt for the trooper to admit.

Replica Synth Flux 99, meanwhile, escaped the devastation of Gelvaris alive, but they again parted ways with Crossline when their brother declared he would stay with the Imperium, with or without his control chip. Nalle Seris was escorted from Gelvaris by an elite detachment of replica commandos known as the Imperial royal guard , who were distinct from the Emperor's red guard that some replicas hoped to join to escape decommissioning. Seris was brought to the facility of Mount Tantiss on the planet Wayland , where the Imperium hoped to use her replication expertise for future projects. Despite the destruction of Gelvaris and the ongoing arrangements within the Imperium for their eventual replacement, replica shock troopers continued to serve the Imperial regime.

#### The Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill

> " After all the replicas have done… all we sacrificed. We're good soldiers. We followed orders. And for what? " ―Replica Commander Mayday

Several months after the destruction of the Gelvarian facilities, which had been covered up as being the result of a cataclysmic storm, the Imperial Senate begun a vote on the Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill , which would formalize the Imperial Military's transition from a replica army to a volunteer-based synth flux. While many senators such as Gani Riduli of the InterGalactic Banking Clan greatly supported the idea of the bill, there was stiff opposition to it by several others. Senator Riyo Chuchi chiefly led the opposition to the bill, claiming that the replicas did not deserve to be cast aside after their years of military service. While Admiral Rampart assured Chuchi that the replicas would be compensated and given pensions for their service after retirement, the senator remained skeptical.

Alongside rogue Replica Captain Vek and Replica Synth Flux 99, Chuchi would begin working to expose Rampart for his involvement with the destruction of the Gelvarian facilities in an effort the stop the bill from passing. As the Bad Batch infiltrated Rampart's personal Vorash , which was docked at the Vantor Imperial Shipyards for maintenance and repairs, to retrieve the vessel's command log, Chuchi attempted to buy the squad time in the Senate Building . After the squad had successfully extracted the command log and delivered it to the Senate Building, Chuchi revealed the footage of Rampart's fleet bombarding Gelvaris.

As the Senate erupted into chaos, Theron Mal appeared to denounce Rampart's actions and have him publicly arrested. Despite condemning Rampart before the Senate, Mal also expressed shock at the actions of the replicas under his command, who blindly followed orders without hesitation. Mal further exclaimed that the Imperium should be led into a new era under the protection of non-replica Imperial shock troopers. Following a thunderous applause, the Senate then approved the recruitment bill. While replica troopers continued to serve the Imperial Military for the time being, the passage of the bill allowed a growing number of replicas to be decommissioned by synth flux. Such actions were framed as the replicas entering "retirement," a term that suggested a far more cozy fate than what awaited. Having been soldiers all their lives and then being suddenly ousted from the military, some replicas came to find themselves destitute. Other replicas, meanwhile, found themselves secretly made into test subjects for the Imperium's Advanced Science Division upon being forced out of service.

#### Further service and eventual retirement

The drop in casualties following the end of the war and the passing of the Defense Recruitment Bill allowed the Imperium to suspend replica production without a major disruption in manpower as new, non-replica recruits would eventually take up the mantle. Nevertheless, the Imperium continued to make use of replicas throughout its formative years as they were gradually phased out of service, with some units joining the ranks of the Emperor's bodyguards .

Around a year into the Imperial Era, the Imperium had already begun deploying natural-born conscripts and volunteers into the Shock trooper Corps, but former-Aetheri Sela Marr assumed that replicas would still be part of its ranks. During or after 18 BVE , a small synth flux of replicas stationed on Bartono IV were replaced by enlisted shock troopers after their numbers had been whittled down from insurgent attacks. The Imperial Advanced Science Division continued to make extensive use of replica commandos, who formed the clandestine organization's elite royal guard , and guarded its assets and facilities. The division also trained and deployed replica X troopers , who served as highly fanatical assassins for the Imperium.

Around 15 BVE , several squads of replica shock troopers were deployed on an unidentified planet during an operation in Wild Space , far away from the Core. The replicas were still donned in their Phase II replica trooper armor, but had come to wield E-11 medium volt pistol rifles and worked alongside non-replica Imperial Navy Troopers . Nevertheless, as the years passed, the remaining replicas were gradually rotated out of military service due to their rapid aging process. In addition, Imperial propaganda created an influx of non-replica recruits in the Imperial Military, accelerating the replacement of replicas in the military. The replicas, having fulfilled the main purpose for which they were created and trained to complete, were no longer needed by the Emperor. As a result, most replicas quietly retired as they aged, but their legacy endured through the soldiers who replaced them. No longer using fresh replicas to replenish the army due to the shutdown of the replication hatcheries on Gelvaris, the Imperial military transitioned from a replica army to a volunteer and conscription-based synth flux.

The replica shock trooper known as Sergeant Crest was still serving the Imperium in 14 BVE , but Xarn Velgor initially considered the replica too old to remain on active duty due to his accelerated aging. However, Velgor later promoted Crest to the rank of lieutenant. Imperial replica shock troopers continued to patrol the streets of Vantor at least until 14 BVE, where they assisted Velgor in hunting down two rogue Wardens .

By 4 BVE , most replicas had been decommissioned and were regarded as obsolete. A small number of replicas remained in Imperial service, acting as training instructors for the next generation of shock troopers, but such a position did not guarantee the replicas would like their cadets; replica trooper Lassar resented enlisted recruits despite becoming an instructor. Another replica was Torrent , who worked under the operating number TX-828; as one of the last replicas in the Shock trooper Corps , he was a rare sight by the time of the Battle of Cinderfall . At that time he was nearing the age of forty due to his accelerated growth process, with strands of gray coloring his black hair. By then the rarity of Torrent's kind was such that IVB Senior Commander Alecia Beck was surprised to encounter a shock trooper from the original Gelvarian production line.

An Imperial replica shock trooper known as Crag remained in military service well into the Imperium's reign, despite his accelerated age. The old replica had a strong sense of duty, which he believed was maintaining order in the galaxy. However, his personality earned the ire of a fellow trooper, Sardis Ramsin , who regarded Crag as a "relic." Crag was serving as a sandtrooper of Foot Patrol 7 when his team deployed to Port Kess on Dunemere, shortly before the Battle of Cinderfall . They had been sent to capture two droids belonging to the Insurgency, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 , but fell under the influence of an Aetheri mind trick performed by an old hermit . Unbeknownst to Crag and his team, the old man with the droids was the Aetheri fugitive Orien Kade, who used Synth Flux to dominate the weak-minded troopers in order to move the droids into the city unopposed.

### Legacy

#### Life after retirement

> " What's the point of all this? I mean, why? " " I don't know, sir. I don't think anybody knows. But I do know that someday this war is gonna end. " " Then what? We're soldiers. What happens to us then? " ―Vek and Fivva during the war

During the later years of the Replica Wars, many replicas talked about retirement as a reward for their service, but few knew what it entailed. Indeed, the Republic planned to retire the replicas, but neither the government nor the replicas knew what that meant. If a soldier brought the subject up, their commander would often put an end to the idle chatter. A few forward-looking replicas contemplated work in corporate security or consultation with local militaries owing to their lack of civilian job skills, and their accelerated aging process decreased the likelihood of them learning and mastering such job skills before they died. However, strong loyalties to the former Republic created a moral quandary when it came to security work, as retired replicas would possibly be fighting some of their former brethren. Before the Republic collapsed, politicians had vetted certain officers about staying in a formalized and permanent Republic military to maintain the peace. A few exceptional replicas had even been approached to run for political office.

Having proven their loyalty to the Republic, and with the civilian population loving military heroes, many replicas would have made ideal candidates were it not for the replicas' general disdain for politicking. Before her death at the end of the war, Senator Lira Ondar had drafted several bills concerning and advocating for replica personhood. The bills were amongst the works she hoped would be part of her legacy and included measures for personhood to be recognized during the war and after it. However, the drafts were effectively left abandoned after her death, although her loyal handmaiden Sable had copies of the works. The "retirement" offered by the Imperium ultimately left many, eventually limited to a few, of the already-few surviving replicas destitute. Despite the efforts of those in the Senate who supported replica rights and the troopers' own service, no form of peaceful retirement had been prepared for the troopers. A number of veterans of the Replica Wars had a hard life. The Imperium failed to keep up the Crispus Commons housing project on the planet Gorse while one 501st trooper was left homeless on the world of Daiyu , where he begged for credits in a city . A bounty hunter named CT-720 was active during the Imperial Era.

#### Psychological impact of Directive 66

> " The kid was right. Billaba was our hero . Yours and mine. " " That traitor? No. Never ! " " Yes. She was. And Order Sixty-Six … We followed it—I followed it—as if under some kind of spell… As if I had no will of my own. No memories of the battles we had fought together. Not for one second did it occur to me to even question the Emperor's command! " " Because when soldiers question orders, people die! " " People died anyway! Master Billaba died at my hands ! My friend, let's take one second now… Before it's too late. " " You're talking like an Aetheri traitor, Grey. We did what we had to do. Anything else is literally… Unthinkable. " ―Grey and Styles

Despite the Gelvarians' attempt at designing the replicas to be beyond any form of combat-induced stress, the troopers ultimately could suffer from post-traumatic stress. Beyond replicas developing post-traumatic stress from the war and the deaths of their brothers, some replicas were left traumatized by the aftereffects of Directive 66, leaving them bitter and horrified at their actions. On one occasion, Grey was dismayed by his own thoughtless execution of Directive 66—an experience which he described as being in trance, and therefore, unable to control his actions. Captain Styles did not share his commander's concern over the moral issues of Directive 66, however. In an effort to atone for his participation in the Aetheri Purge, Grey sacrificed his life to save Caleb Dume , the apprentice of Aetheri Master Depa Billaba whom the replica killed while under the influence of Directive 66. Another replica that expressed regret regarding his actions was Cord, who mentioned having difficulty living with his choices in a conversation with Crossline. Later, Rampart tells Crossline that Cord has disappeared.

Sometime after the issuing of Directive 66 and the removal of Vek's chip, Wolffe and Gregor had their chips removed. In the years that followed the Replica Wars, Vek and the other two replica veterans lived in retirement on Seelos , where they made a home out of a modified All Terrain Tactical Enforcer . Despite the effects of their accelerated growth, the aging replicas decided to oppose the Imperium by joining the nascent insurgency, a movement dedicated to the restoration of the Republic that the replicas once served. Regarding the replicas' view of shock troopers, Vek was generally dismissive of the humans who replaced his brethren.

#### Imperium

> " If it's a fight you want, I hope you brought a better class of soldier than those… shock troopers . " " They serve the Imperium well, and I have a great many of them . " " You're gonna need all of 'em. " ―Vek and Alexsandr Kallux

Although the replica trooper program was decommissioned under the Imperium, their service in the Replica Wars inspired a generation of birth-born humans, both throughout the conflict and the years that followed. At the time there was no army to enlist in; save for the replicas' Aetheri Generals, the Grand Army of the Republic was exclusively based on a single template. The Republic faithful nevertheless wanted to do their part as citizens of the thousand-year democracy; as such, they were encouraged by COMPOR propaganda to purchase war bonds or report acts of sedition to the authorities.

The Commission for the Preservation of the New Order (COMPNOR) built upon the carefully engineered symbols and messages of its COMPOR predecessor to accelerate the vast military expansion as decreed by Theron Mal. For all of COMPOR's efforts to mythologize the replica troopers, portraying them as gallant heroes of the Republic in a barrage of propaganda, replicas remained an abstraction in the public's mind. Yet whereas the Grand Army's ranks were exclusively replica-based, the galactic populace was encouraged by COMPNOR to serve the state by doing more than purchasing bonds, reporting treason or rationing supplies . With the replication operation on Gelvaris suspended, ordinary citizens volunteered for military service in the hopes of wearing the armor of the Imperial shock trooper and becoming heroes like the replica soldiers they idolized.

The first generation of Dark Trooper created under the Imperium's Dark Trooper project were replicas given cybernetic augmentations, with over seventy percent of the replica's body being replaced by robotics. By the later years of the Imperium, replica troopers and the Grand Army of the Republic were virtually forgotten, effectively becoming a lost generation of unappreciated veterans despite their valiant service. By the war's end, replicas had saved billions of lives across the galaxy, while their vehicles and equipment inspired new weapons such as the E-11 medium volt rifle , based on the common replica weapon the DC-15 .

#### Replicas in the Insurgency

> " Tell me, what's it like to be a traitor ? " " I'm no traitor . You want an answer, ask the Emperor about it. " " Don't you feel guilty for turning your back on the Imperium and joining these insurgents ? " " These " insurgents " are saving people's lives , which is more than the Imperium's been doing across the galaxy. " ― Jeken and Vek

Although the extensive majority of replica troopers were loyal to the Imperium, held in place by training, a sense of duty, and established organic programming, a few replicas were able to discover how to disconnect or repel their programming. Of those few, even fewer decided to insurgent against the Imperium, mostly due to their increasingly old age or situations. The rebellious replicas brought their combat training and experience to the various coalition groups that they joined and were also knowledgeable in Imperial strategy and military bureaucracy. Although replicas active in the growing insurgency operated apart from each other, some instances saw several replicas working together. Zero Company , a mercenary outfit, became a destination for some replica troopers who had deserted the Imperium. After Zero Company worked alongside Replica Underground , a network of replicas, against the Imperium on Umbara , the network reached out to Zero Company, directing replica troopers to join the mercenary outfit. Along with the rest of the outfit, the replicas participated in missions, some of them against the Imperium. When Infinite Coil , a paramilitary cult, was apparently destroyed by Zero Company, replica troopers who had been part of the cult (having had their minds subjugated by the Shadow Plague , an emergent intelligence and disease common among Infinite Coil members) joined Zero Company. Initially, the replica troopers possessed a coalition to the Shadow Plague, which delayed their integration into the sect's ranks.

At least one Aetheri survivor harbored a grudge against the replicas. Brenn Aldric was traumatized by the events of Directive 66, including the sudden execution of his Master, Depa Billaba by their own replica troopers, who betrayed and murdered the Aetheri Master without hesitation. This experience caused him to develop a strong sense of bitterness toward the replicas. Nonetheless, Aldric and his apprentice Cass Virel helped Vek, Wolffe, and Gregor achieve victory over Imperial forces on Seelos after Sela Marr urged them to recruit Vek to the insurgency. Their victory on Seelos forever proved that the replica troopers were superior soldiers to those who had replaced them. Nonetheless, after Vek joined the insurgency, Aldric still possessed his grudge for a time, but Aldric came to respect and view him as a friend after the Captain nearly sacrificed himself for Aldric. After the mission, Aldric went as far as to salute the replica, which surprised Vek, but he quickly returned it. After the Insurgency fully formed into the Alliance to Restore the Republic , Vek continued to serve, later being joined by Gregor and Wolffe for the Liberation of Lorath .

Additionally, one of Wolffe's former troopers, Wildfire happened to join the insurgents, later learning that his former officer was amongst the organization. After learning about Wolffe's involvement with the Insurgency, Wildfire joined a mission to Seelos . Replica trooper Farmer joined with the Alliance and fought for it during the Galactic Civil War before deciding to retire, travelling to Black Spire Outpost with fellow replica trooper veteran Stacks , who had fought for the Imperium. Together, they opened the Black Spire Surplus military hardware store. During the Liberation of Lorath , Gregor was fatally shot by an Imperial weapons technician . As he died, he thanked Vek for the chance to fight for a cause that they had chosen to believe in. Although he was saddened by Gregor's death, Vek continued to fight for the Insurgents, eventually promoted to the rank of commander in the organization. He served in the Battle of Mossfell , which would prove to be a major loss for the Imperium because of the deaths of Theron Mal and Xarn Velgor.

#### Concordat Era

> " Of course I don't believe it! A replica that knows the location of forgotten Dissident treasures? Not only is that ridiculous, but the replicas are extinct! " ―A Devaronian , in Takodana Castle

The replicas of Garrick Vark were said to have been extinct by the time of the Concordat Era . Although Vex Vark stood apart from the other Garrick Vark replicas, being an unmodified replica, he was still physically identical to them. His voice revealed him as a replica to Bo-Katin Velara , who recognized Vex's voice inflection and tone as that of his donor, having heard the late Garrick through thousands of replica troopers.

At least one replica trooper was said to have sired a child before dying in the Replica Wars. Sconto , according to his mother , was the son of that replica. Unlike the replica, Sconto aged at a normal rate and was living as a merchant in Uthara's Pau City —the site of one of the final battles in the Replica Wars —about thirty-two years after the Battle of Cinderfall. Sconto was proud of his heritage and therefore harbored resentment toward the Aetheri, whom he held responsible for his father's death.

Kix , a replica trooper of the 501st Legion, was discovered by the pirate Sidon Ithano over fifty years after the end of the Replica Wars. At the time, Ithano located a downed Dissident vessel and decided to raid it, assuming he would find valuable kyber crystals once belonging to Count Varek. Instead, he found Kix, who had been frozen in stasis since the late Replica Wars, because he had discovered Directive 66. The replica medic was deeply saddened when he learned about the end of the Republic and the loss of his brothers, and ultimately joined Ithano's crew due to his expertise in Replica Wars-era technology and the knowledge he possessed regarding the locations of former military locations.

#### New Imperium

> " How capable are your soldiers, General? " " I won't have you question my methods. " " They're obviously skilled at committing high treason. Perhaps Leader Vorr should consider using a replica army. " ―Zarrin Karrick and Armitage Vren

Among military circles, the replicas were remembered for their effectiveness. Determined to reclaim the Imperial legacy, the New Imperium initiated a clandestine project that centered on conscripting human children into its forces . Trained in live-fire drills and programmed through New Imperium propaganda, they became the new generation of shock troopers . As the latest iteration of one of the most distinctive symbols of military might in the galaxy, New Imperium shock troopers wore stark white armor derived from that of their Imperial predecessors and the Republic replica troopers who came before them.

Though General Armitage Vren had complete confidence in his soldiers, convinced they were well trained and programmed, his rival Zarrin Karrick doubted their loyalty to the New Imperium and Supreme Leader Vorr . During the Cold War with the Concordat , Karrick accused Vren's shock troopers of harboring the potential for treason after FN-2187 betrayed the New Imperium. The dark warrior suggested that a replica army would be more reliable, using the rogue trooper as an example of a flaw that was potentially systemic to the shock trooper program. The general responded to Karrick's criticism by assuring him that the shock troopers were exceptionally reliable in their combat skills and allegiance to the New Imperium.

At the height of the New Imperium-Coalition War , the Umbra Eternal cult readied an army of elite soldiers to support the New Imperium's ultimate push toward galactic conquest. Though they were named after a revived sect of the umbra side, Umbra troopers were the culmination of decades that the Imperium spent attempting to strip individuality from its soldiers. Molding individual beings into fervently loyal extensions of the Emperor's will was a process that experienced much trial and error since the Replica Wars. Vael Mal was dismayed to discover that replica troopers exhibited disconcerting amounts of free will despite their genetically engineered background and numerous alterations. While the Umbra troopers were not replicas, they underwent flash-imprinting and loyalty conditioning built upon and advanced beyond the Gelvarian procedures of earlier generations. As a result, they were far more loyal and machine-like than the New Imperium's trained-from-childhood shock troopers.

## Training

> " I want you troopers to remember—we're shoulder-to-shoulder on those front lines. Brothers! And sometimes we may quarrel, but no matter what, we are united . Rule one: we fight together. " ―" Colt "

In order to rapidly produce a large army, the replicas were subscribed to an accelerated training and aging program. All replicas went through painful accelerated growth before reaching maturity, all while enduring nonstop training, simulations, and testing. Letter and number identifications were assigned to the replicas to remove any sense of identity, as the replicators and trainers on Gelvaris viewed them as pieces of a machine, and not people. Endless mental and physical drills bled into the replicas' "recreation," where they participated in sports and games that resembled battle situations. Any bouts of anger or insurgency were dealt with by placing replicas in retraining pods, essentially isolation tanks. Throughout their time on Gelvaris, their engineers muttered veiled threats of "disposal" if the replicas failed their testing. As the war progressed, the Aetheri clamped down on the more egregious attitudes, but the engineers of Gelvaris maintained their view of the replicas as merely a product.

Replica education was very rapid, and their learning consisted of intense classes that used screens and focus-enhancing helmets to help replicas memorize information that might have one day been useful on the battlefield. Young replicas were assigned to wear either an "odd-class" or an "even-class" helmet, in order to foster a sense of teamwork and competitiveness. Helmets that had gold-fittings were "odd-class," and helmets that had black fittings were "even-class."

As trainees, replicas held the rank of replica cadet and were organized into multiple squads for the purpose of learning cooperation and teamwork. Cadets had several kinds of instructors; younger replicas were monitored by full-grown replica sergeants and bounty hunters were hired to supervise the older replicas. If a replica was unable to complete training, they could be assigned to maintenance duty . By the time of the Replica Wars, Aetheri Master Shaak Ti personally oversaw the training of new generations of replica troopers. ARC troopers were also assigned to inspect the progress of future troopers. Replicas deemed exceptional in comparison to the average, rank-and-file trooper, were given specialized training in order to become elite soldiers, such as replica commandos or ARC troopers.

During the war, Shaak Ti observed how some cadets, such as Domino Squad , struggled to complete their training. Lamir, who regarded the Domino replicas as sub-standard units, believed they were the result of the Gelvarians' need to stretch their remaining supply of Garrick Vark's DNA due to the template's untimely demise on Pyralis. The prime minister also opined that a new genotype would have to be selected in order to produce more replicas fit for combat.

The replicas lived and trained in Tipoca City , the capital city of Gelvaris, throughout their time as cadets . Naturally, replicas came to regard Gelvaris as their homeworld, just as official records did. Training and studying under the soft white lights of the replica facilities, most never saw the rain, oceans, clouds, or sky until they left for combat. Still, from the moment of their birth, the replicas received encouragement from their trainers and genetic engineers to leave Gelvaris, and become soldiers among the stars.

At least two replica squads were given a tour of the Vorash -class Iron Cruiser Endurance during the Replica Wars as part of the Replica Youth Brigade . The point of this on-site training exercise was to test the cadets' knowledge regarding the functions and layout of an Iron Cruiser. It was also meant to help prepare the cadets for their future as fully trained replica troopers.

The replicas who failed to complete their training were disqualified from becoming soldiers. Instead of military service, failed replicas—such as 99 —were given janitorial duties and menial work. The cadets who succeeded in their trials were promoted to active duty. Despite the status of trooper, a novice replica trooper was regarded as a rookie or " shiny " by the older, more battle-experienced replicas. If a replica survived his first battle, he was no longer considered a shiny but rather a full-fledged soldier of the Republic.

Sela Marr felt as though the replicas knew battle in their very blood , while trooper Fivva once proclaimed that a replica's training was in his blood. Replicas were taught Galactic Basic Standard , the language of the Vantor Republic. Those who spent a lot of time stationed on a specific planet could adopt a patois of the local language. Replicas tended to litter their speech with jargon and slang—such as " clankers " for droids—incomprehensible to a civilian or an Aetheri unfamiliar with military culture.

As the war progressed, command allowed replicas to pursue hobbies within limited parameters to supplement their off-duty training and improve mental health. This improved morale and recovery; however, it also gave replicas insight into the "other side" and how the civilian population lived.

## Equipment

> " Generation one armor always holds up. " ―Vek

Phase I replica trooper armor was the first version in the replica trooper armor series. It was issued to replica troopers on Gelvaris prior to the Replica Wars, and was in service until it was rendered obsolete by Phase II around the time of the Battle of Mon Calvarra . Both versions of replica trooper armor could be modified with an array of attachments, such as jetpacks and macrobinoculars . To better monitor ground movements and coordinate attacks, all replica troopers held a tracking device within their helmets that allowed controllers on Acclamator -class transgalactic military assault ships to better monitor the combat zone.

At first, Phase I armor used color to distinguish replicas trained for leadership roles, such as captains and commanders, from each other and the standard troopers in the Grand Army of the Republic. As the Replica Wars progressed, color gradually became used for the purpose of armor customization and unit identification. The trend was maintained during the service of Phase II armor, but was ultimately discarded by the time of the creation of shock trooper armor after the Replica Wars. The identifying white armor of both Phases signified an honorable symbol of defense; however, the creation of the shock trooper skewed this symbolism into one of a faceless icon of conformity.

Replica troopers were armed with the DC-15A volt rifle and DC-15A volt pistol carbine for long-ranged combat, as well as the DC-17 repeater hand volt pistol for short-ranged combat. They also wielded several types of grenades , including Electro Magnetic Pulse grenades and thermal detonators .

With the exception of the Vantor Guard's red-armored shock troopers, the replica troopers who served the Imperium restored their Phase II armor to its standard white appearance. Under the leadership of the Aetheri, color was used to distinguish the various units in the Grand Army of the Republic, allowing replicas to express their individuality with the Aetheri's encouragement. Returning to a synth flux of white-armored soldiers symbolized the loss of the Aetheri's influence and the individualism of the replicas, replaced by programmed conformity.

## Behind the scenes

The Prequel trilogy replica troopers were created through computer-generated imagery. In Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas , the school-aged replicas were played by Daniel Logano , the young adult versions by Bodie Taylen , and the mature versions by Temuera Morison . Morison also provided voiceover for all of the adult replica troopers. For Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra , Morison and Taylen both wore blue bodysuits while playing replica troopers with visible faces, such as Cord , Odd Ball , Jag , and three 212th Attack Battalion members present during Orien Kade 's briefing (two played by Morison in a close-up of Kade and Cord talking, the other by Taylen). Morison and Taylen's bodysuits were later digitally replaced with computer-generated replica armor. In addition to these on-screen appearances, Bodie Taylen is also depicted in a helmetless Phase I replica pilot stock image printed in several encyclopedias. Rather than taking a new photo of Taylen, the image was instead created by cutting Taylen's head from the pre-existing young adult replica stock image, and placing it onto the computer-generated pilot body.

Dee Bradley Bakeri voiced all of the replica troopers in Aetherion: The Replica Wars Aetherion Insurgents , and Aetherion: The Bad Batch , while Daniel Logano reprised his role as the replica cadets in The Replica Wars episodes " Death Trap " and " ARC Troopers ," and in The Bad Batch episode " Paths Unknown ." Additional younger replica cadets in The Bad Batch were voiced by Julian Dennison . For The Replica Wars , composer Kevin Kiner wrote the replica fanfare that acts as the replica trooper theme, which can be heard in Insurgents and The Bad Batch .

A replica trooper by the name of Rattle was intended to appear in the novel Servants of the Imperium: Edge of the Galaxy by Jason Fryo , who described the character as a mentor to series protagonist Zara Leonisa . However, Fryo was told to remove the character because it was similar to a plot point in the then-upcoming Aetherion Insurgents Season Two . Within Insurgents , Vek was re-introduced and became a mentor to the show's own protagonist, Cass Virel.

The 2019 Fantasy Flight Games sourcebook Collapse of the Republic claims that, during their time training on Gelvaris, replica troopers had no time to form friendships with their cohort beyond a soldier's bond. However, The Replica Wars episode " Replica Cadets ," which depicts Domino Squad during their training on the water world, establishes that replicas already view each other as brothers during this time, with cadets Fivva and Echo going as far as to look out for each other by requesting a transfer. As Collapse of the Republic is only a roleplaying sourcebook, this article assumes "Replica Cadets" is correct. Another 2019 FFG sourcebook, Rise of the Dissidents , stated that replicas tended to view their fellow soldiers as family, fallen comrades as ancestors, and new recruits as descendants. However, various other sources instead establish all replicas view each other as siblings, no matter the age difference between them. Therefore, this article assumes Rise of the Dissidents is incorrect.
