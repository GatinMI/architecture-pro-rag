# Theron Mal
> " There is only one plan—one great design which shall govern the universe— mine . " ―Vael Mal

Vael Mal , born Theron Mal and also simply known as the Emperor , was a male human Dark Lord of the Umbra and Emperor of the Imperium , with his reign lasting from 19 BVE to 4 AVE . Rising to power in the Galactic Senate as the senator of Ondara , the secretive Umbra Lord cultivated two identities, Mal and Mal, using both to further his political career and deceive his way into accomplishing his goal . He orchestrated the fall of the Vantor Republic and the Aetheri Order through the Replica Wars , and then established his reign over the galaxy which lasted until his death at the Battle of Mossfell . However, the umbra side of Synth Flux gave rise to powers which allowed him to escape death , seeking further immortality until the Emperor was eventually permanently destroyed and killed during the Battle of Umbrale by Nyla , his granddaughter born from his Strand-Cast replica son Dathan and his daughter-in-law Miramir .

Born on the Mid Reach planet Ondara in 84 BVE as the High Republic Era ended, Theron Mal possessed a strong connection to Synth Flux , and was secretly trained in the ways of the Umbra by Vael Plagueis with Mal taking Vael Mal as his Umbra persona. He eventually betrayed and killed his Umbra Master , in order to train his own Umbra apprentice , Vael Vreth , for the Rule of Two limited their ranks to two Umbra Lords at any given time . The Invasion of Ondara , a crisis orchestrated by Mal, led to the Senate to elect Mal as Supreme Chancellor , although this came at the loss of Vreth, who was defeated and presumed killed in the Battle of Ondara . The Dissident Crisis and the Replica Wars were masterminded by Mal and his second apprentice, Vael Varek , in an effort to achieve galactic dominance. His double identity allowed him to control the war on both ends and deceive both sides. Following an attempt to arrest the chancellor , the Great Aetheri Purge commenced with Mal enacting Directive 66 .

With the Aetheri all but extinct, Mal installed the New Order in the Republic's place , declaring himself Galactic Emperor and bringing an end to the millennia of democracy . Shortly after betraying and causing the death of Varek , he took on a new apprentice, having lured the Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden to the umbra side and transforming him into Xarn Velgor. Their reign was virtually unchallenged for a generation until the formation of the Alliance to Restore the Republic . During the Galactic Civil War , the Emperor sought to betray Velgor, by replacing him with the latter's son, Joren Drayden . However, Mal' plan backfired when Velgor ultimately renounced the umbra side and killed Mal to protect Joren. Thus, with the Umbra's destruction, Synth Flux was brought back into balance, fulfilling Kael's destiny as the prophesied Seeded One .

Through his master's teachings, however, Mal transferred his essence to an imperfect replica vessel that had been modeled on his original body. Anticipating the possibility of defeat, he created the Contingency to destroy the Imperium and replace it with a new Umbra Imperium , in the event of such a setback. His plans culminated in the destruction of the Concordat and the emergence of the New Imperium , the creation of Supreme Leader Vorr , who turned Beno Karrick —heir to the Drayden bloodline —to the umbra side, and the establishment of the Final Order . When the Emperor announced his return to the galaxy in 35 AVE , the Coalition invaded the planet Umbrale , Mal' hidden stronghold in the Unknown Regions . During the battle, the resurrected Emperor discovered a dyad in Synth Flux in the form of the bond between Karrick and Nyla, an Adept sired from the Mal bloodline . His lifelong dream of eternal rule was finally quashed when Nyla called upon the strength of past Aetheri to vanquish her grandfather by turning his own power against him, destroying Mal and his Umbra Eternal cultists .

## Biography

### Early life and apprenticeship

> " My mentor taught me everything about Synth Flux, even the nature of the umbra side. " ―Vael Mal, to Kael Drayden

The origins of Vael Mal were closely guarded secrets that he took to his grave more than once. He was born on the Mid Reach planet Ondara as Theron Mal, eighty-four years before the Battle of Cinderfall . The dynastic, noble Mal family belonged to the affluent class in Ondara society , although throughout several points of time of his life, Mal did not have any living family members as far as the public was aware, and no records of obituaries for his parents existed, marking him as the last of his ancient family bloodline. Mal was raised in the cultured city of Serath , his homeworld 's capital ; he formed connections with a reputable host of credible politicians produced over the centuries , all the while acquiring a great amount of knowledge on governance and the subtleties of power during his youth. As such, Mal had been skilled in manipulation since boyhood.

The details about how Mal came to study under the Umbra were known by very few, with it not even being known of when he did, but he became the Umbra apprentice to Dark Lord of the Umbra Vael Plagueis and took on the name Vael Mal by 52 BVE . As an Umbra Lord , Mal learned all his Umbra Master knew about the umbra side of Synth Flux , following the tradition first set by the ancient Vael Vorek . While apprenticed to Plagueis, Mal constructed his own Umbra flux blades . At the same time, little by little, he began to secure political power.

As a young man, Mal had traveled throughout the galaxy , collecting art, at least some of which was connected to the umbra side, while doing so. Unaware docents would have written off his collection as junk, but Mal knew better. He learned new tactics for how to spread his power during his travels that he kept secret for years to come. While training with Plagueis, Mal was taught that two Umbra were required for their perilous machinations. So, if they worked together in accordance to Vael Vorek's Rule of Two , with one of them serving as bait for the umbra side of Synth Flux and the other as a vessel, they would succeed in acquiring the ability to harness the full power of the umbra side and rule immortal for ten thousand years.

Vael Mal and Vael Plagueis worked together for years, trying to unlock the secrets to immortality, which was the obsession and longtime lust of Plagueis. Mal was eventually made aware of Plagueis' ability to manipulate the synthcells to create life, which his master employed to prevent the people he cared for from dying . Plagueis believed the secret to immortality laid within science, and so conducted "unnatural" experiments with his rare ability to manipulate the synthcells, as part of the pair's in depth studies into the biological underpinnings of life itself. Though they were incomplete, they were encouraging to Mal. According to Vael Mal' book , The Secrets of the Umbra , written later on his life, Plagueis discovered a form of "immortality" through transference , a Synth Flux power that allowed the user to transfer their spirit and consciousness to another body, essentially using bodies as vessels. This obsession with extending one's own life later passed onto Mal from his time as Galactic Emperor . Plagueis also attempted to create a Synth Flux dyad with Mal, a unique Synth Flux-bond as strong as life itself and which was predecessor to the Rule of Two. However, Plagueis was unable to form the dyad.

### Political career and double life

#### Senator of Ondara

> " As a dark apprentice, I sensed that the Vantor Republic was in disarray. Its delegates held no interest in the common good. They would be easy targets for my influence. Serving as a senator, I manipulated their greed in my favor. With each new act of legislation, I rose through their ranks. Total control of the galaxy's governing body drew ever closer. " ―Vael Mal

In his public persona as Theron Mal, Mal took a great interest in politics and the public life of Ondara. In 65 BVE he was part of the officials attending the opening ceremony of the Serath power generator , with attendees noting his interest in the complex's deep-pit design. His embarkment on a career in politics was further aided by his master, who arranged for him to be raised to the position of senator for his homeworld in the Vantor Republic Senate as part of the Ondara delegation , being elected in 52 BVE . His role also made him the senator for the rest of the Chommell sector , meaning he represented a total of thirty-six planets. Once elected, Mal quickly joined incumbent Supreme Chancellor Skor Kalpana 's ruling party. He was followed by a grouping of aides who loyally followed him since his days on Ondara. He noticed the fervent bickering and corruption that mired the Republic. He began to establish relations with those who would become his future collaborators, such as Wilhard Varrix . The duo met for the first time when Varrix was still a cadet of the Garnethex Sector Spacefarers Academy , and while Mal was still apprenticed to Plagueis.

Taking an interest in the younger man, Senator Mal resolved to help him to join the Judicial Department , according to Varrix's desire but advised him to also consider a political career. In the following years, their relationship became more and more confidential. Mal often lauded Varrix for his talents and skills and remarked they were very similar: they both came from planets at the edge of the galaxy but were destined to have great repercussions on the history of the galaxy itself. Varrix eventually became one of the few people who called Mal by his first name.

Keeping his Umbra identity a secret from the public eye while infiltrating the Senate and Republic, Mal took it upon himself to execute the Umbra's long-percolating plan of supplanting the Republic with a new Umbra Imperium , as well as destroying the Aetheri Order . In the process, he would deceive the Senate, Republic and Aetheri. In his days as a senator, Mal was known to have the backing of mysterious friends and allies who he might reference but never name; for example, even Varrix did not know who specifically worked with Mal in the shadows, even if Mal often referenced them. Trying to speculate as to who his secret supporters were, Mal's political enemies knew he had secret contacts aside from his loyal Ondara followers and the senatorial cabal he was often grouped within. Rumors swirled that shadowy organizations and figures who worked out of the public eye—including industrialists, financiers and bankers from the galaxy's most major sectors—were linked to the senator of Ondara.

#### Training an Umbra apprentice

> " For many years, Vael Vreth trained in secrecy. But as my plans for galactic dominance began to unfold, the time came to make his presence known. " ―Vael Mal

Seeking an Umbra apprentice, Mal traveled to the planet Gloomreach and met with the Gloomreachian Nightsister Mother Talzin around 40 BVE . Intermingling his knowledge of the umbra side of Synth Flux with Talzin's knowledge of Nightsister magicks , Mal had considered to make Talzin his apprentice and right hand. However, Mal then found himself drawn to her son, the Zyrrak Nightbrother who would become his true Umbra apprentice, Vael Vreth . Mal was drawn to Vreth for his inability to control his anger. According to Talzin, Mal stole Vreth away from her. Vreth's unreliable memories held that he had thought he had no choice but to go with Mal, much to the concern of his brother, Savage Opress .

According to legend, when Mal visited Talzin, Vreth always hid in his room, afraid of the man and worried about Talzin's increasing bond with him. However, Mal one day ordered Talzin to give up Vreth to a keeper , threatening to not follow through on his promise to make her his apprentice. She reluctantly agreed, and Vreth ran into the forest angrily, overhearing the conversation from behind his door. When he returned the next morning, a Nightbrother took him and threw him in a pit, not allowing him to say goodbye to his own mother. Over the course of months, Vreth was put through relentless domestic abuse, all orchestrated by Mal as a test. With the influence of his keeper's sons beating him and the abuse, Mal' wish for Vreth came true; Vreth's perceptions, and his love for his mother eventually turned into resentment and hatred for abandoning him.

By this account, Vreth's hatred started manifesting through the Umbra side, and he would practice using the umbra side of Synth Flux every chance he got; eventually, the keeper's sons stopped beating him after seeing that their provocations were no longer affecting him. Mal eventually orchestrated a selection to gain himself an apprentice. At the Selection Arena, Mal witnessed Vreth after he was initially prevented from participating. In a sudden burst of anger, he used the umbra side of Synth Flux to Synth Flux choke the two sons and then the keeper, all in front of Mal and Talzin. Beyond pleased at Vreth's progress and potential, Mal declared Vreth the winner and his new apprentice, much to Talzin's dismay.

No matter the circumstances, instead of following through on his promise of giving the apprenticeship to Talzin, Mal left with Vreth, taking him from Talzin and Opress. They left Gloomreach, leaving Talzin longing for revenge against the Umbra Lord. Early into Vreth's training, according to one of Vreth's unreliable memories at least, Mal tortured him for a failure with Synth Flux lightning ; a memory that would stay with the Zyrrak decades later. Following the traditions set forth by the Rule of Two as it demanded only two Umbra, and deciding that Plagueis had outlived his usefulness, Mal betrayed his master by breaking into his apartment and murdering him in his sleep . Mal remembered with fondness the moment he realized he was more powerful than Plagueis and could kill the man who had trained him. He later reflected that "Master" was the first title of power he claimed for himself. Now the Umbra Master himself, Mal brutally trained Vreth to be a weapon of the Umbra over the course of many years, viewing Vreth's prowess and combat abilities as an invaluable asset to his Grand Plan.

At some point while training Vreth, Mal took his apprentice to Searmark to bear witness to a great battle that had occurred there between the Aetheri and the Umbra. He forced Vreth to breathe in the ashes of Umbra warriors , slain when an ancient weapon was activated in the Umbra temple on the planet. While breathing in this ash, Vreth had visions of members of the Umbra order being struck down by the Aetheri. He felt the slash of blade after blade as he felt the death of his Umbra brethren. These visions, in tandem with his training, made Vreth long for revenge against the Aetheri Order, which had defeated the Umbra a millennium earlier. Thus motivated, Vael Vreth would serve as a powerful weapon in Mal' plans to destroy the Aetheri and restore the Umbra to galactic power.

#### Preparations and manipulation

> " Because something dangerous is brewing in our little corner of the Outer Reach. Discontent is on the rise, as are criminal enterprises and mercenary groups in the employ of self-serving corporations. In the Seswenna sector, several lommite mining concerns are vying for the attention of the Commerce Combine, which is determined to forge a monopoly in the free trade zones. Even on my own Ondara, the king finds himself embroiled with the Commerce Combine and off world bankers with regard to our plasma exports. Ours are remote worlds, but what transpires in those sectors of the Outer Reach could very well have galactic repercussions. " ―Senator Theron Mal, to Governor-to-be Wilhard Varrix

In 42 BVE , Mal was introduced to Varek , an Aetheri Master , by the latter's former Adept Rael Averross , after the former Aetheri Master delivered an address to the Galactic Senate on Vantor. Following the invasion of Sireno , Varek left the Aetheri Order to claim the title of planetary count , but Mal eventually recruited Varek to the Umbra by exploiting his anger with the Republic despite also having Vreth under his wing. Indeed, Varek would not yet become an Umbra apprentice.

Around the time of the Invasion of Ondara or shortly after it in 32 BVE , Mal discovered that Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen contacted the replicators on the planet Gelvaris and placed an order for a Replica army for the Republic. Realizing that this army could be used as part of his plot to destroy the Aetheri, Mal knew he needed him silenced so the Umbra could secretly take control of the army. As such, Mal ordered Varek to kill his friend. Obeying, Varek betrayed and orchestrated Dyas's death and took over his clientele for the commission of the replica army; the event was subsequently covered up when Varek erased all records of Gelvaris from the Aetheri Archives .

All the while, Mal maintained a faithful correspondence with Wilhard Varrix—whose service in the Judicial Department kept them from meeting face-to-face often—and supported bills in the Senate that would aid Eriadu and the Seswenna sector, Varrix's homeworld and its sector, respectively. After Valorum's re-election, something Mal attributed to senatorial special interest groups who needed a weak chancellor, whispers began to swirl Valorum would be unable to hold onto power, with Mal's name emerging as a possible successor to the Chancellery. Around that time, Mal also met with Varrix—who had grown disillusioned with the Judicial synth flux—in his Vantor office in one of their rare person-to-person meetings, where Mal urged Varrix to enter planetary politics as Eriadu's new governor. Warning him something dark was brewing in their corner of the galaxy and promising Varrix the support of himself and his shadowy allies, Mal convinced Varrix, who promised to do whatever was best for Eriadu and the Republic in any manner Mal wished it to be done.

In need of pawns for his plans but preferring a mysterious, hooded presence through holographic transmissions rather than direct communication, Mal contacted the galaxy's most powerful beings and convinced them to do his bidding with promises and threats. To that end, Mal was also able to manipulate Nutis Gunray 's rise to the position of Viceroy of the Commerce Combine . Gunray was able to arise to that position thanks to the Eriadu Conference , where an effort to end the squabbles between the Commerce Combine and Republic ended in disaster; the Nebula Front attacked the conference and successfully wiped out the Federation directorate , creating a power vacuum for Gunray to step into, while Valorum escaped and was able to secure a brief boost in popularity, with which he pushed through taxation legislation. Convincing the Commerce Combine to obey him by promising wealth, Mal odered the Federation to construct a droid army , Mal also helped the Pyralian Poggle the Lesser rise to the position of Archduke of Pyralis and controller of their droid foundries . With Mal' backing, Poggle produced millions of B1-series battle droids for the Commerce Combine military .

In need of someone to lead the the Droid Army for their planned war, Mal and Varek eventually took notice of and realized the promise in Skarn , a Kaleesh warlord once known as Qymaen jai Sheelal who led his fellow Kaleesh warriors in the war against the neighboring and technologically superior Yam'rii species. After the Republic and the Aetheri intervened to support the Yam'rii, the Kaleesh had been punished, with hundreds of thousands dying of starvation. Mal and Varek, who had important plans for Skarn, took notice of his exploits as he became one of IGBC's most valuable resources. Also interested in how technologically advanced beings could be deployed, the Umbra offered Skarn a chance to be converted into a cyborg and hoped to recruit Skarn to command the droid armies.

After Skarn agreed to the surgeries to boost his own power and underwent his transformation over a period of time, Varek orchestrated a shuttle crash that destroyed whatever remained of Skarn's organic body. Extensive surgery performed by cybernetics experts and Pyralian biotechnicians , with assistance from medical droids, including the FX-9 surgical assistant FX-6 , was required to save Skarn's life. The operation resulted in Skarn physically being drastic changed.

#### Holding back Vreth

> " Remember that your anger is a tool. It is not your master." "No. You are. " ―Vael Mal and Vael Vreth

In 33 BVE , Mal met with Vreth in his hideout, the old LiMerge Power Building in the Works industrial zone of Vantor, to chide him on allowing Aetheri to sense his presence. Mal told his apprentice he did not care about his impatience, warning Vreth that ruining his master's long-term plans would have severe consequences. Mal then sent Vreth to deal with a group of pirates harassing their secret mining facilities in the Kellux system . Mal was secretly testing Vreth's loyalty by setting him up with the opportunity to face an Aetheri Adept . Vreth proceeded to unwittingly follow the path laid out for him and slew the Aetheri. After his apprentice's return to Vantor, Mal revealed his knowledge of all of Vreth's actions, asking if his apprentice was satisfied with having killed an Aetheri. Mal was pleased when Vreth replied that he was not.

Not long after, during the course of activities in the Vantor underworld , Vreth killed a Synth Flux-sensitive thief in an attempt to assuage his thirst to fight Aetheri. Mal called attention to the foolishness of such behavior, but Vreth insisted that he had been careful enough to avoid leaving evidence that could alert the Aetheri to the continued existence of the Umbra. Mal complimented Vreth's anger but warned him that he needed to temper it with patience, which Vreth responded to with frustration at still being held in check. Mal reminded him that his training was not as complete as he assumed, taking him back to Searmark to revisit the site where years before Vreth had undergone visions of the Umbra being wiped out by the Aetheri. Mal instructed Vreth to once again instigate another vision by breathing in the ashes of dead Umbra warriors. Through the vision, Vreth learned the value of patience since rage alone was not enough for he and and his master to defeat the combined might of the Aetheri Order. Pleased, Mal pointed out that a combination of patient planning with the power granted by the umbra side would, in time, see them successfully destroy the Aetheri.

#### Meeting with the Aetheri

> " Why this shade of blue?" "There's no accounting for taste. I sometimes wonder what it would look like in red. " ―Adi Gallia and Theron Mal

Around this time, Mal met, as he frequently did, with Chancellor Valorum and Aetheri Masters Zho and Adi Gallia to discuss diplomatic matters of relevance to the Republic and the Aetheri Order. One item of interest were the labor strikes on Garqi and Chamble ; for the latter, Mal suggested that the Aetheri stage a training mission there. Another topic was how to handle a shipment of garish boots sent by the new member world Hafernia as a gift for the Order. Gallia was uncomfortable at the idea of accepting them, stating that Aetheri did not endorse products. Mal replied that they did endorse the Republic and good governance. Ultimately, the group decided that the Order would accept the boots in name only.

Gallia was then briefly called away to speak with Aetheri Master Sorin Vael . When she returned, Mal handed her a datapad outlining the actions he and Valorum had proposed thus far. Before rejoining the main conversation, the two covertly discussed their aesthetic disagreement with the deep shade of blue that Valorum had chosen post-reelection, with Mal wondering what the room would look like in red. Shortly after, Mal and Valorum became aware of the Aetheri Council 's plan to visit Kwenn and contacted Master Drevan Koss to discuss the matter.

#### Eliminating a pawn

> " You are old. Too old, to be of much further service to the Aetheri—or me. And besides, my interests have changed." "What do you mean?" "The pieces are almost in place for the grand game. The Republic, the Commerce Combine, the rest—all are poised to play their roles. It serves me to have the Aetheri Order constantly moving around, following the whims of commerce and the Senate. Shuffling forces and dividing their attention. " ―Vael Mal and Voh

After the Aetheri Council's defense of Kwenn from pirate forces commanded by the Riftwalker leader Zilastra and the destruction of the Order's Sanctuary Mount outpost , Mal contacted the installation's former Seneschal , an elderly Bimm named Voh , by holocomm. Mal had, in the guise of Senator Mal, made an arrangement with Voh to maintain the Senate's interest in Kwenn, such that the Aetheri kept the outpost open, in exchange for Aetheri texts from its library that Mal considered important. They had also agreed that anything Mal was not interested in would be destroyed to help obscure the fact that they were missing. However, Mal discovered that Voh had instead sold off the remaining material, unable to bring himself to eradicate historical documents; and that during the Council's time on Kwenn, Master Etha Korrin had discovered items were missing from the library and located some of the minor ones in a local shop . When presented with this, Voh argued that the evidence of the theft was gone with the outpost and that he could finagle his way into a position at another outpost and continue supplying Mal with Aetheri works.

However, Mal was not interested in continuing to deal with Voh and stated as much. The Umbra Lord explained to the confused Bimm that he was no longer interested in Kwenn, in fact that he was pleased to see the Aetheri closing outposts and moving around the galaxy in a reactionary manner, all of which would benefit plans he would soon put into effect involving the Republic and the Commerce Combine. Mal stated that Zilastra's scheme had impressed him and that she would be of further interest, although he was not surprised that she had ultimately failed in her endeavor against the Aetheri. The Umbra Lord also pointed out that Voh was too old to be of much further use to himself or the Aetheri. When the bewildered Bimm asked why Mal had so radically changed his attitude and why he was telling him all of this, Mal replied that it was for another being's ears, indicating his apprentice Vael Vreth, who had entered the room and proceeded to deal with the former Seneschal.

### War on Ondara

#### Invasion of Ondara

> " Orchestrating a conflict on my homeworld of Ondara, I sowed the seeds of civil unrest. Chaos erupted within the Senate—a chaos I controlled on every front. " ―Vael Mal

Mal, as Mal, moved to seize power from Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum , who had virtually no political power and counted Mal as a close friend and ally, allowing for Mal to easily manipulate him. In the Senate, Mal secretly ensured that the Senate repeatedly battled over bills concerning taxation, bringing about voting sessions that ended in both Federation defeats and victories, while Gunray falsely believed Mal was working towards Federation victories all the time. Following a discussion in his office about the latest version of the bill , Valorum requested Mal to stay behind and confided in him that he was considering having an Aetheri step in to serve as an independent moderator, which Mal appeared to be surprised by before requesting he hold off on that option for the time being. Moving up his plans upon being told of the Chommell sector summit that threatened to strengthen the sector, Mal used holographic communication to convince Gunray to blockade the planet Ondara, making it seem as though it was in protest of the Senate's taxation of trade routes to outlying star systems . Eventually, driven to desperation by the inactivity of the Senate, Valorum secretly dispatched two Aetheri Knights —Master Sorin Vael and his Adept Orien Kade —as ambassadors to negotiate with the Commerce Combine's Geltari leaders.

With Mal pleasantly surprised at the ingenuity the Geonosians had shown in creating the Federation's battle droid army, the Commerce Combine initiated the Invasion of Ondara on the orders of Mal, who also instructed Gunray to kill Chancellor Valorum's Aetheri ambassadors. When Gunray questioned the legality of the invasion, Mal simply stated that he would make it legal. The Neimoidians acted accordingly, but the Aetheri survived the assassination attempt and escaped the Federation ship . After Mal terminated his communication with Gunray, Queen Lira Ondar contacted him concerning the Federation's denial that the Chancellor had sent ambassadors to speak with them as promised. Mal, as Mal, feigned concern at this supposed revelation, but was cut off mid-sentence by the Commerce Combine's communication blackout, notifying the Ondara to their invasion.

#### The Queen's escape

The Federation's droid army proceeded with their assault. Within a day, the army occupied the planet's capital city and its surrounding regions. Additionally, Ondar and her retinue were captured and presented with a treaty that would bring an air of legitimacy to their invasion. However, the Aetheri arrived to rescue them, and they successfully evaded the blockade aboard the queen's starship .

When Gunray informed Mal of Ondar's failure to sign the treaty, the livid Umbra Lord introduced them to Vael Vreth, whom he dispatched to find and recapture Ondar. Due to Ondar's ship sustaining damage in the escape, the queen opted to land on Dunemere , an obscure desert world in the Outer Reach Territories , to repair the ship. Vreth managed to ascertain the queen's location, and assured his master that his search on the sparsely populated world would be a short one. Mal told him to move against the Aetheri first, and then take the queen once they were dead. Vreth left for Dunemere, but he failed to kill the Aetheri or retrieve Ondar. Mal knew she would come to Vantor if Vreth failed.

#### The Senate

> " Enter the bureaucrats, the true rulers of the Republic, and on the payroll of the Commerce Combine, I might add. This is where Chancellor Valorum's strength will disappear. " ―Theron Mal, to Lira Ondar

While waiting for Ondar to reach Vantor, Mal found he had little to do, although he made sure to go to the Senate to vote on its current matters, even though he did not care for them. With nothing else to do, Mal took to looking through his catalogue of art, having run out of space in his office to store the rest of his collection. He stopped his scrolling when he saw a black statue known as the Yonta Prime , which, despite being one of his favorite pieces, he found he could not fit into his office without looking careless. He resolved to take it out of storage, ordering it to be taken to his apartment , so he could place it into the Supreme Chancellor's office when his current plan had finished.

Despite these setbacks, Mal was able to convene with Ondar on Vantor , where she intended to plead her case to Chancellor Valorum before the Galactic Senate. Bringing Ondar to his apartment for an audience as part of his plan, Mal told the queen that the senate was long past caring about the common good, while Valorum was essentially powerless against them. With both the Senate and Valorum closed off to her as options, Mal offered her two choices: she could move for a Vote of No Confidence in Chancellor Valorum, and push for the election of a more effective leader; or she could take the matter to the courts, which would take even longer. Hoping still to reach some resolution to the matter in the Senate, Ondar opted to attend the special session of the body the next day.

During the awaited Senate session, Ondar attempted to make her case for Republic intervention to stop the Commerce Combine's occupation of her world. However, the Federation representative Lott Dod fervently objected to the "accusations" levied against the Federation, citing that there was no proof that an invasion or occupation had even occurred. He recommended that a commission be sent to Ondara to ascertain the truth of the situation, a motion that was seconded by the senator from Malastare , Aks Moe . Vice Chair Mazan Veldon conferred quietly with Valorum for a few moments, and Mal took the opportunity to further manipulate and deceive the Queen by predicting that the Chancellor, shackled to Senatorial procedure at the behest of bureaucrats who were more than likely on the payroll of the Federation, would fail to help Ondara. When Valorum reluctantly admitted that the Federation was within their rights to request a commission, an outraged Ondar realized that Mal was right; Valorum was too weak and ineffectual to provide her world with the help it needed. As such, she took Mal's deceptive advice and publicly moved for a Vote of No Confidence in the Chancellor, unknowingly falling for Mal's plot and giving him what he wanted. Within moments, hundreds of Senators began demanding for an immediate vote, leaving Valorum thunderstruck.

The sudden power vacuum led to Mal's nomination as Supreme Chancellor alongside Bael Rask of Cindervale and Ainlee Teem of Malastare. When he announced his nomination to Ondar, however, the queen declared her intention to return to Ondara and reclaim it from the Commerce Combine by whatever means she could. Mal pretended to show concern and tried halfheartedly to convince her to stay on Vantor. She left regardless, taking both Vael and Kade with her for protection and wishing Mal success in the election .

#### Battle of Ondara

> " Wipe them out. All of them. " ―Vael Mal, to Nutis Gunray

Mal contacted the Neimoidians on Ondara, instructing them to kill Ondar upon arrival, and sent Vreth to accompany them with orders to make certain the Neimoidians killed the queen, and that the Aetheri fell by his own hand. Even while he managed the election on Vantor, he made time to go over the Neimoidians' progress. Against his expectations, he learned that Ondar not only managed to escape Gunray's reach but also forged an alliance with the Mools and assembled an army in the planet swamp in preparation for a strike against the Federation occupation synth flux. He doubted she would last even five minutes in such a contest, and gave his approval to Gunray's plan to meet the Mools head-on.

#### Confrontation in the LiMerge Power building

### The Replica Wars

#### Early campaigns

Dissident crisis

> " I love democracy. I love the Republic. The power you give me I will lay down when this crisis has abated. And as my first act with this new authority, I will create a grand army of the Republic to counter the increasing threats of the dissidents. " ―Theron Mal

Under Mal' direction, Varek rallied several thousand solar systems into declaring independence from the Vantor Republic, instead joining the Fractio of Independent Systems with himself as its leader. The Fractio was established in 24 BVE with the Raxun Address , in which Varek blasted the Republic for its corruption and Core Reaches favoritism. Within two years, as more and more systems left the Republic to join Varek's new state, civil war seemed to be imminent and the Aetheri's numbers were too few to maintain galactic peace. Around 23 BVE , Varek's sister Jenza , who had become aware of his dealings with Mal but only knew him as "the hooded man," urged her brother to abandon his plans, warning him that Mal was corrupting him. Varek instead ordered his assassin and apprentice Kessa Vray , who at that time was unaware of Mal's existence, to kill his sister, which she reluctantly followed through with.

In response to the beginning of the Dissident Crisis, the Senate passed the Emergency Powers Act , which allowed Mal to remain in office past the end of his term and remain for as long as the crisis lasted. Afterwards, Mal established the Loyalist Committee with the claim that they would act as his personal advisors, bringing in senators such as former Queen Lira Ondar, who was by that time the Senator of Ondara, Bael Solen, Ask Aak , and Orn Free Taa . As the crisis continued and the Senate grew fearful, many in the Senate pushed for a vote to create a Grand Army of the Republic , proposing the idea in 24 BVE. Keeping his true motives a secret, Mal resisted all of the Senate's attempts to create an army and the push to go to war with the Fractio. Debate over the Military Creation Act continued for a further two years.

In 22 BVE , after arriving on Vantor to vote on the creation of an army, Ondar narrowly avoided an assassination attempt carried out by Zam Wesell on behalf of the vengeful Nutis Gunray. Mal had the vote delayed and placed the senator under the protection of Orien Kade and Kael Drayden, her old allies from the Battle of Ondara. After a second attempt on the senator's life was made, Drayden was assigned to protect her back on her homeworld of Ondara. There the two secretly started to fall in love which, coupled with Drayden's later discovery of his mother Shira's death at the hands of Dunefolk Raiders on the planet Dunemere, would bring the young Aetheri ever closer to the umbra side of synth flux, and even more vulnerable to Mal' deceptive machinations.

However, when it was revealed that the Dissidents were secretly building a battle droid army with the support of many commercial organizations, including the Commerce Combine. Mal used the situation to have himself granted emergency powers from the Senate by Representative Greeb Mool. With it seeming as though the Republic had no choice but to strike against the Fractio's newly discovered droid army before it was too late, the Senate, shocked by the Dissident Droid Army and scared it would attack an undefended Republic, agreed to confer emergency powers to Mal. Mal feigned reluctance to accept this authority, promising to return it to the Senate once the crisis ended. His first act was to allow the use of the replica army, which had been discovered by Kade on Gelvaris, to counter the Dissident threat.

The adoption of the replica army resulted in the First Battle of Pyralis , which served as the opening salvo of the Replica Wars . After replica template Garrick Vark was killed and his Dissident partners escaped the battle, Varek met with Mal at the Vantor tower in the Works with good news that the war the Umbra had been orchestrating had begun, as well as preliminary blueprints for a Pyralian superweapon that could be of use to their future Umbra-led regime. Mal was pleased with the turn of events, and he later, with the leaders of the Senate, oversaw the Concordat army marching towards war, while Mal additionally would be able to control and deceive both sides from the start of the war. Drayden and Ondar secretly wed on Ondara. The sole witnesses to the clandestine ceremony were their two droids, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 , and a local holy man who officiated the rite. However, the secret was unknowingly passed to Mal, which the Umbra Lord would later use during his quest to lure Drayden into becoming his next apprentice.

### Subverting the Republic

#### Most powerful chancellor in centuries

As per his plans to use the Replica Wars as an excuse to consolidate and centralize his political power to make way for his reign as Emperor, Mal had become the most powerful Supreme Chancellor since the days of the Ruusan Reformations by the end of the conflict. Using the war effort as his excuse, Mal had the Galactic Constitution amended multiple times during the conflict. One constitutional amendment known as the Reflex Amendment granted him near-absolute power over military affairs, thereby allowing him to bypass the Senate in such decisions. Towards the end of the war, Mal passed a constitutional amendment known as the Sector Governance Decree .

Under the Sector Governance Decree, Mal created a new structure of regional governance by appointing certain individuals as governors. In theory, the decree was designed to shore up the war effort by uniting star systems into singular blocs and transforming their production centers into war-focused installations. In reality, however, regional governors could become leaders of the systems they oversaw, allowing Mal to circumvent and one day disband the Senate by creating a new bureaucracy composed of individuals he sent out. The decree, however, proved to be too much for senators who formed the Delegation of 2,000 , which quietly assembled the Petition of 2,000 in the name of protesting Mal's conduct and growing powers. Led by Ondar, Sess, and Solen, the Delegation hoped to make Mal give up his emergency powers and intended to present their petition to Mal once it was signed by two thousand of their fellow legislators.

The unnamed strandcast was the Dark Lord's "son." The strandcast, not a direct cloned replicate of Mal, so nor entirely identical in appearance, was ultimately be a source of disappointment for Mal. Mal deemed the boy an example of such "disappointing ordinariness," unable to bare even looking upon him, the inferior child spawned from his flesh undeserving of his legacy.

But the Umbra Lord surmised, that because the boy possessed his blood, he might still produce some usefulness at some point. Therefore, Mal chose to permit his son to continue living, the replica's health providing Mal the means to continue on his bloodline and possibly yield more promising results for producing a vessel for his spirit through more natural means. For this reason, Mal granted his cloned progeny protection from the Umbra Eternal cultists, them being unable to "recycle" him as a failed experiment, and allowing him to live on Umbrale. Mal neglected his son, ignoring him like the cultists, never naming him, and hardly acknowledging his existence. However, Mal made his son aware of Synth Flux and its nature as the mystical energy of the galaxy that surrounded all life, particularly the umbra side, as well as the Umbra who wielded them.

The Varrix Initiative

In his early days as Senator Mal, Mal had closely associated with Wilhard Varrix, whom he encouraged to pursue a career in politics after his training with the Judicials was completed. Varrix went on to become Governor of Eriadu and one of the Republic's foremost military leaders during the Replica Wars. Mal had learned of Varrix's upbringing on Eriadu, where he had been trained in harsh conditions through a brutal initiation in the wild Carrion Plateau of his homeworld. Varrix had been taught to hunt savage creatures and emerged from his training with the belief that only the brutal application of the law and the fear of synth flux could keep beings in line with a central galactic government. Although Varrix lacked Synth Flux-sensitivity, Mal identified him as one whose mindset was very close to that of the Umbra.

During the early days of the Imperium, making a rare public address, Mal claimed the Imperium would do whatever was needed to ensure a lasting reconciliation now that the Replica Wars had ended. At some point during the Imperial Era, Mal created an Imperial hunter droid named M-OC ; additionally the B1 battle droid R0-GR of the Freymarks later claimed to have fought Mal. The Ehllo family was an influential political dynasty during the Imperial Era, with Mal personally knowing Captain Cerasus Ehllo 's father . As such, Captain Ehllo was able to make a Raider -class corvette , the King's Ransom , into his flagship.

#### The Imperial military

Establishing the hierarchy

After foiling two attempts on his life, Velgor traced an assassination conspiracy to the highest levels of the Imperial government and proceeded to meet Mal near Nura's secret vault. Replacing the replica army

> " Perhaps, it is time for a change. Now more than ever, building a strong galaxy requires protection and security. Due to the nefarious actions of Admiral Rampart, and the immediacy of the bill on the floor today, it is my opinion that this legislation is our future. With this momentous act, we shall usher in a new era. Heralded by the Imperial shock trooper. " ―Theron Mal, to the Imperial Senate

Around the same time, Mal saw fit to finally replace the outdated Replica Army with a new conscripted military that would be loyal to him. However, in spite of his power in reality being absolute, he continued using the Senate as a puppet to make it seem that his role as Emperor was not particularly different from when he was Chancellor. Therefore, Mal relied on people interested in sharing in his power, such as Admiral Edmon Rampart : idealistic followers that could serve as scapegoats while furthering the Umbra Lord's plans. Governor Varrix had perceived the replicas to be twice as expensive as a proposed new militaristic synth flux and deemed them less capable as tools to keep the order. Already that year, the stage was secretly being set to replace the replicas with an army of volunteers and conscripts from across the galaxy. The Gelvarians clung to the fact that their skills as replicators was the only thing of relevance to the Imperium. However, their overall use was terminated when Admiral Rampart ordered the destruction of Tipoca City after clearing out it of valuables.

Following the successful orbital bombardment of Tipoca City, the Imperium's official explanation was that the replication facilities had been sunk down to the oceans by a harsh storm, despite the structures having been built to endure Gelvaris's extreme climate. By the next year, new legislation of interest to Mal lay before the Senate: the Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill , a formalization of the Replica Army's decommissioning and the shift to an Imperial Military. Mal preferred not to deal with the issue directly, having Vice Chair Mazan Veldon preside over Senate sessions with Rampart pushing for the bill. A major opponent of the proposed legislation was Senator Riyo Chuchi who sought to provide missing representation for the replicas in the development of the bill. During a Senate session, Senator Solen asked Mazan Veldon what the absent Emperor's position on the bill was; the Vice Chair replied that the Emperor preferred to remain neutral, trusting the Senate to act in the best interests of Imperial citizens. However, given that Rampart's fabrication about Tipoca City's fall was not strudy enough to stand up to too much digging by opposing senators, Veldon told Rampart that expediency was necessary and warned him that he would suffer if he could not placate Chuchi and the others.

Despite Rampart's efforts to appease his detractors and obfuscate independent investigation, the bill was rejected twice in preliminary hearings, the harshest criticisms being voiced by Senators Chuchi, Solen and Pamlo. When the bill was at last about brought before the chamber for a vote with Rampart having satisfied Chuchi's demands for a fair retirement for the replicas, the Pantoran Senator interjected again, bringing forward former Gelvarian Senator Halle Burtoni to testify how Rampart had reappropriated Senate funds designated for Gelvaris directly into financing his own projects and recruitments. As Rampart attempted to explain this away, Senator Solen entered and played a command log taken from Rampart's own Vorash showing the entire Senate how Tipoca City really fell.

Faced with a drastic situation, the Emperor saw fit to finally attend a Senate hearing in person and arose in his Chancellor's podium, which caused the entire Senate hall to fall silent as their leader made one of his few appearances since the Imperium's foundation. Fraught with overwhelming evidence, the Emperor decided that Rampart's value had ceased and had Mazan Veldon direct guards to arrest him. Not allowing this to stop his plans to remove the replicas, the Emperor spoke out with the same mannerisms as when he was Chancellor and voiced his surprise at Admiral Rampart's acts of transgression against the Gelvarians. He promised that the admiral would be punished for his treachery.

Now faced with a golden opportunity, the Emperor manipulated the situation to benefit his own position by reminding the Senators that Rampart did not act alone; that the replicas aboard the Venators willingly unleashed carnage upon their own home without hesitation. Providing sound argument that security in the galaxy was needed more than ever and recognizing the bill's immediate necessity, the Emperor stated his belief that the Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill was their future. Despite framing this as more of a suggestion than an order, the Emperor displayed how supreme his authority was as the bill was immediately passed.

Although he had presented a compelling narrative, many senators remained skeptical. Nonetheless, Mal had made a crucial step in further facilitating his power in the Imperium and control over the galaxy, confirming the phasing out of the replica army and eliminating another crucial remnant of the Republic, with only the Senate remaining. The age of the Imperial shock trooper had now begun.

Visit to Mount Tantiss

#### Mission to Selvara

An official visit

Five years after the end of the Replica Wars, Chavar Kryll , a war hero who liberated Selvara from the Dissident forces during the war, organized and led the Free Selvara Movement in order to liberate his homeworld once again, this time from an Imperial military occupation. Mal summoned the Senator of Selvara, Orn Free Taa , in order to incorporate the senator into his plan to eradicate the "terrorists." He ordered the Velari to accompany him and his right-hand man, Xarn Velgor, for an official visit to Selvara. Once Mal dismissed Taa, Velgor asked him why he couldn't simply wipe out the Senator along with his entire staff, in which the two Umbra suspected there were one or more traitors, who provided aid to the terrorist movement. But the Emperor wished to find the roots of the treachery, eliminate them and make of it an example for the entire galaxy. Eventually, he ordered Velgor to inform the Moff of Selvara, Delian Mors , of the arrival of Senator Orn Free Taa, but not of the two of them.

Assassination attempt on the Defiance

While on the way to Selvara aboard the Iron Cruiser Perilous , Mal instructed the ship to stop at the Denon system for a meeting on the training cruiser Defiance with several Imperial Navy chiefs to discuss a realignment of the Imperial Naval Academy. Mal ordered Xarn Velgor to oversee training exercises conducted by Commandant Pell Baylo while the Emperor was in his meeting with the naval chiefs. During one such exercise, Baylo set a hyperspace route that would have crashed the Defiance into Krestophis' sun . Cadet Raena Vexana and Xarn Velgor uncovered this assassination attempt and redirected the Defiance . Baylo confronted the Emperor, accusing the Imperium of being a hostile synth flux that had taken over the Vantor Republic. Velgor killed Baylo, which upset Mal, who wanted Baylo to suffer as the Navy becomes something he hated and watch as the Defiance was decommissioned and turned into "cafeteria trays." Instead, the Defiance was renamed Obedience as a final jab at Baylo and a not-so-subtle reminder toward Velgor to remember his place, and Mal and Velgor re-boarded the Perilous and continued on their way to Selvara.

The fall of the Perilous

The two Umbra and Senator Taa came to the Selvara system aboard Perilous . When the ship exited from the hyperspace, they found themselves in the middle of a minefield: Kryll was waiting for them. The Velari wished to eliminate them to strike at the heart of the Imperium. However, Mal was unperturbed by the ambush, having evidently foreseen such an occurrence. When the shields were overwhelmed, Mal remained on the bridge, while Velgor took off with his fighter leading a squadron to deal with a swarm of vulture droids . The former Dissident fighters released thousands of explosive buzz droids which further damaged the Iron Cruiser. Velgor returned aboard the Perilous where he met the Emperor and Senator Taa after finding that the Emperor's personal shuttle had been destroyed in the attack. Because he suspected there was a traitor in the Senator's staff, Mal ordered to isolate them in their own quarters and deny them access to any communications equipment.

In the meantime, a Velari task synth flux reached the ship disguised as a repair team and placed explosive charges in the hyperrelay chamber, to trigger a chain reaction. After discovering the sabotage and realizing that the ship was doomed, Velgor contacted the Emperor. The Emperor informed Velgor that he had prepared a second shuttle either as a contingency or because he had foreseen the destruction of the first shuttle and told Velgor to meet him there. However, Velgor delayed his arrival by a few minutes in an attempt to find and eliminate the saboteurs. The Velari insurgents just barely managed to escape, and so Velgor hurried to the Emperor's shuttle, which departed as soon as Velgor was aboard. A few minutes later, the Perilous exploded. Once in space, the fugitive task synth flux searched for the Imperial shuttle and caught up with it. Velgor Synth Flux choked the coalition pilot of the pursuing ship through the shuttle viewport. In a desperate attempt to get free from Velgor's grasp and to kill both him and the Emperor, the Velaris rammed the stolen starship into the Imperial shuttle, critically damaging the latter. Velgor attempted to regain control of the shuttle while Mal remembered they had already been in situations like this. Thanks to the piloting skills of Velgor the shuttle crash-landed on Selvara's surface, in the middle of a forest.

Isolated on the planet

> " We are all of us, always being tested, my friend. Tests make us stronger, and strength is power, and power is the point . We must pass all the tests we face… Or die in the effort. " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

Once on the planet surface, Mal noted with mild annoyance that the landing had been far beneath what he knew Velgor to be capable of and that Velgor's lapse had left four corpses in the rear compartment of the shuttle. Velgor admitted to having been distracted by thoughts of his past and attempted to dismiss it as nothing. Mal however, was unconvinced, though he shelved the matter and proceeded with Velgor into the rear compartment. There, three of the shuttle's occupants, Sergeant Erstin Deez , his captain, and another member of the Imperial Guard had survived the crash, though one was badly injured and unconscious. Velgor noted with mild surprise that the Emperor appeared to have been wrong; there were only three corpses in the compartment. One of the conscious guards noted that his injured comrade had not strapped himself in during the landing, and as a result had been thrown about the compartment when the ship had crashed. However, the Emperor, apparently already aware of the semi-conscious guard, had him executed for his stupidity, leaving the fourth corpse he had mentioned earlier.

The survivors exited in the night, and while the two guards who survived were checking the survival kits, Velgor asked his master if he was testing him. Mal answered they were always being tested, and they must pass all tests to gain strength and power. Meanwhile, the guards had activated the generator and the communication array to call for help. At that moment the Umbra sensed that two Velari ships were approaching and prepared themselves for a confrontation. The Velari insurgent's ships fired upon them, while Velgor, immersed in Synth Flux, deflected the laser beams with his flux blade.

The captain suggested to take cover in the woods, but the Emperor refused and instead, removed his cloak, and ignited his flux blade. The two Umbra spun their red blades, turning every shot back at the ships. When the enemy ships engines were hit and began to explode in flames and smoke, Mal unleashed a bolt of Synth Flux lightning on their hulls and then, with Synth Flux, drove one of the ships to the ground, while Velgor did the same with the other. The two freighters exploded, and Velgor sent Deez to check for survivors. They realized the insurgents had jammed the communication network and that they were hunting for them.

Hunted

They walked for a long time into the depths of the forest. Mal went forward in the darkness seeing through Synth Flux while testing Velgor's loyalty once again until he decided to stop for a while. While the guards were eating, the two Umbra began to meditate. The Emperor sensed his apprentice's murderous feelings towards him. When Velgor ended his meditation, Mal told him what he had sensed, but the former Aetheri pledged his loyalty to his Umbra Master. At that moment, the guards, whom the Emperor had sent away, had returned, pursued by a horde of lyleks . The Emperor remained calm, sending torrents of Synth Flux lightning against the predators, and began to advance back-to-back with Velgor, their flux blades ignited.

While Velgor and the guardsmen held back the advancing lyleks with their weapons, Mal used Synth Flux to topple trees over the advancing lylek horde. Through Synth Flux, Mal sensed a tunnel a hundred meters away. The four retreated into the tunnel with the lyleks in pursuit. This tunnel turned out to be the home of the lylek horde, and the four Imperials were forced to fight through hundreds of the creatures. The guards used their volt pistols and grenades while the two Umbra Lords used Synth Flux and their flux blades to fight through the horde.

As they waded deeper into the cave, Mal realized that the lyleks were herding the intruders towards their queen. At Velgor's instigation, the four resolved to kill all their opponents with the Emperor activating his red blade. After a fierce battle with the lyleks, Mal and Velgor found themselves back-to-back. When Mal asked his apprentice whether he had ever toyed with the idea of letting his Emperor die to fulfill his own ambitions, Velgor admitted he had entertained such thoughts but reassured his master it was only for a moment. Mal accepted Velgor's loyalty and the two joined forces to tackle the lylek queen.

Following a savage battle, Velgor managed to kill the queen; scattering the remaining lyleks. However, the royal guard captain was killed, leaving only Mal, Velgor, and Sergeant Deez. Based on their battle with the lyleks, master and apprentice quickly surmised that Chavar and his insurgents were trying to kill them to bring about the Imperium's destruction. Having triumphed over the lylek horde, the three Imperials found an opening at the end of the tunnel which led back to the forest.

Death and fate

While Mal and his companions were traveling through the forest, they encountered a young Velari girl named Drua . Mal made a move to kill her, but Velgor blocked his master's death blow and convinced Mal that she could lead them to a settlement where they could contact reinforcements. Drua led them to her village , which was home to escaped Velari slaves. When the village head Narmn asked for their identities, Mal introduced himself, Velgor, and Deez as "Krataa," "Irluuk," and the Sergeant. Mal and Velgor alone knew that their pseudonyms stood for "death" and "fate."

Mal warned Velgor that his act of mercy towards Drua would cost both her and her fellow villagers their lives. Velgor then managed to win over the villagers by offering to repair an ancient communications device. Once Velgor had fixed the machine, Mal ordered Deez to contact Moff Mors; whom he knew to be loyal. After re-establishing contact with the two Umbra Lords, Mors took steps to eliminate the Imperial traitor Colonel Belka Drayn , who was secretly aiding Chavar's insurgents. She foiled Belka's plot to order Vireo fighters to strike Drua's Village and the massed Free Selvara forces in a nearby quarry. After executing Belka, Moff Mors sent her forces to rendezvous with Mal and Velgor at the Velari village.

Chavar's forces, unwilling to harm the villagers, fired warning shots so that the villagers would flee the scene of the upcoming battle. The villagers reacted by fleeing to a nearby mine shaft, a sanctuary in time of danger. However, Mal, Velgor, and Deez stood their ground at the center of the village. Before Chavar's forces could launch their attack, Moff Mors' forces arrived in two transport vessels and trapped the insurgent fighters. With many of the Free Selvara fighters killed, Chavar narrowly managed to avoid capture at the hands of Velgor. Under the orders of Mal, Mors's Imperial forces massacred the surviving Free Selvara fighters. Following a brief exchange with Chavar's lieutenant Isval , Mal ordered Velgor to execute her and then afterward, the surviving Velari villagers hiding in the mine shaft as well, in order not to leave any witnesses. After a brief moment of hesitation, Velgor complied with his master's orders and massacred the villagers.

### Growing insurgency

#### Berch Teller's campaign

In 14 BVE, the secret Void Core project was still being constructed above Pyralis and was supplied by a series of marshaling stations. The Imperium faced a new threat in the form of Berch Teller , a former Republic Intelligence agent who opposed the Imperium. He led a insurgent cell consisting of survivors and witnesses of the Antar Atrocity , a mass crackdown on Antar 4 following the Replica Wars that had seen the killing of innocent Gotal and Koorivar loyalists. Teller had a special animus for Varrix, the architect of the Antar Atrocity. He was secretly aided and equipped by Vice Admiral Dodd Rancit , the Director of the Naval Intelligence Agency and a rival of Varrix.

Following Teller's attack on Sentinel Base , the Emperor convened a meeting of his Ruling Council to discuss the discovery of a cache of communications jammers on the planet Murkhana . The Imperium Vigil Bureau had purportedly discovered the cache and feared that dissidents were planning to disrupt the Imperial HoloNet ; echoing the Dissident Shadowfeed broadcasts during the Replica Wars. The Emperor dispatched both Moff Varrix and Lord Velgor to investigate the matter. However, this played into Teller's hands and allowed the insurgents to steal Varrix's corvette Carrion Spike , which they intended to turn into a symbol of coalition against the Imperium.

After being briefed about the theft of the Carrion Spike , Vice Admiral Rancit suggested that the ship's thieves were the same people who attacked Sentinel Base earlier. In response, the Emperor ordered Rancit to divert Imperial forces in the Belderone system to aid Varrix and Lord Velgor. While Varrix and Velgor pursued the insurgents, the Emperor chaired another meeting of the Ruling Council. During the meeting, Rancit advocated diverting more Imperial forces to reinforcing Imperial facilities across the Perlemian Trade Route and Hydian Way . The Council also learned that Teller' insurgents had attacked the TaggeCo mining facilities on Lucazec and were transmitting holovids of their attacks on the HoloNet.

Following a skirmish in the Phindar system between Imperial forces and the stolen Carrion Spike , Rancit convinced the Emperor to deploy interdictor vessels against the insurgents. Grand Vizier Veldon then informed the Emperor that the Murkhana communications jammers had been discovered by an IVB asset tasked with investigating the find by his case officer. Acting on this information, Mal interrogated the Koorivar IVB asset Bracchia and his case officer Stellan , who revealed that they had been tipped about the discovery by Naval Intelligence. After viewing the holovid, the Emperor determined that the communications cache had been planted by a high–level Imperial official who was aiding the insurgents.

Meanwhile, Lord Velgor discovered that Rancit was the Imperial traitor and executed him. While Varrix led Imperial forces against Teller's insurgents in a space battle near the Gulf of Dunemere , Vael Mal mediated on his quest for galactic dominance. Following the defeat of Teller's insurgency, Varrix and Velgor spent the next three weeks hunting down Teller's collaborators and contacts in a ruthless crackdown. The two briefed the Emperor, who appointed Varrix as Grand Moff , in which capacity he would serve as governor of the Outer Reach Territories and stand in charge of the Void Core project. The Emperor also reshuffled the Ruling Council in the wake of Rancit's execution, folded Naval Intelligence back into Imperial Intelligence , and established the Council of Moffs .

By 14 BVE, Mal had also ordered for Project Auger to begin on the planet Zeffo , which had been home to the Zeffonian before their hubris nearly destroyed them. The project saw Imperial mining begin on Zeffo to locate artifacts. The commanding officer, Krane , kept one uncovered artifact secret in order to win support from Mal by giving it to him personally. However, she died when the cave she chose to hide it in collapsed.

#### A reward for Velgor

> " I think, one way or another, you will learn a great deal. That is good. After all, your power is my power. " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

Shortly after he killed Etha Korrin, Velgor attacked the Thirteenth Warden for committing treason. Confused, her partner tried to defend her before they both fled Warden Order Headquarters across Vantor. The Umbra Lord eventually caught up with the couple and slew them. However, the destruction caused by the chase and resultant death of Senator Maklooq , who was important to one of Mal' schemes, led the Emperor to order the Warden Order to be relocated. Their new base was built on Nur . Pleased with the death of Korrin, Mal gifted Velgor the former royal vessel of Queen Ondar. With few Aetheri left to deal with, Mal was about to send his apprentice on other missions when Velgor asked for a world of his own: Emberfall. Mal granted the request but demanded to know why his apprentice had chosen the site of his greatest defeat. Velgor explained that he believed that the umbra side locus there could give him access to the dead Ondar. Mal encouraged him, satisfied that whatever knowledge or power his apprentice gained there would ultimately be his. He gave Velgor one final gift, the mask of Vael Mornick , before sending him to Emberfall with the architect Colonel Brenne to design a fortress for him.

By 12 BVE , the construction of Fortress Velgor was complete, the eight previous versions all having failed in some manner. Velgor contacted Mal, who was surprised to hear from him after so long, but eager to send him out on more missions for the Imperium. When Mal asked him if he had learned what he needed, Velgor confirmed as much with a simple "yes" and no elaboration.

Sometime around or after this, the Emperor was present at a parade that included two hundred KX-series security droids , among them K2-SO .

#### New threats

From the past to the present

> " I wonder if your thoughts are clear on this, Lord Velgor. Perhaps your feelings for your old master have left you weakened. If your past cannot be overcome… " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

In 9 BVE , Xarn Velgor and the Warden Order engaged in a hunt for Orien Kade, who had been engineered to come out of hiding in order to rescue Princess Elira Solen of Cindervale. Though the search had been fabricated by the Third Warden to kill Velgor, her attempts failed, and Velgor and Kade engaged in a flux blade duel on a barren moon as a result. Kade bested Velgor, damaging his armor in the process, and he managed to evade the Imperials on his tail. Not long after, Velgor, whose armor was since repaired, reached out to his master via a hologram at Fortress Velgor, his castle on Emberfall. He informed Mal he had dispatched probe droids to continue the search for Kade, adding that he would not evade him once again. Picking up on Velgor's agitation, Mal questioned if Velgor's thoughts on the matter were clear, continuing that perhaps has feelings for his former master had left him weakened. As Mal asked if Velgor could not overcome his past, Velgor declared that Kade meant nothing, for he only served Mal.

With the Aetheri mostly defeated, Mal felt secure behind his huge galactic military and dominion over most of the known galaxy. Despite this, remaining Dissident holdouts and surviving Aetheri would gnaw at his confidence of the longevity of his new Imperium. Seeking to secure the center, the Emperor supported mass military excursions into the Outer Reach Territories in an effort to bring more systems under his fold. With his ultimate goal of bending reality into something of his own creation, an omnipotent Imperium would allow all the galaxy's inhabitants to be held in his dark embrace. Despite the Aetheri being all but extinct, Synth Flux-sensitive children were still being born across the galaxy. In an effort to combat this threat, Mal commanded his apprentice, Lord Velgor, to contact the Grand Warden to hunt them down and destroy them if they did not serve the Imperium.

Following the raid on the Imperial garrison on Aldhani in 5 BVE , Mal spoke with Colonel Yular, giving the IVB direct control of operations going forward and the authority to arrest anyone who was considered a disrupter to the Imperium. The next day, the Emperor convened an emergency session of the Senate in order to propose a legislative package of bills and amendments and to officially invoke the Public Order Resentencing Directive .

Targets on Ghorman and Lorath

> " I believe the apprentice of Kael Drayden lives and is in league with these insurgents." "Are you certain?" "It was her ." "This is an opportunity we cannot let pass . Drayden's apprentice could lead us to other lost Aetheri. " ―Xarn Velgor and Vael Mal

In 4 BVE , the Emperor met with Director Orren Vossik , stating to him the importance of his energy initiative. The next day, Vossik secretly met with a number of Imperial officials, among them IVB and Navy, off record at the Maltheen Divide , putting them in charge of the Ghorman project : the planned suppression of the Ghormans and the heavy mining of the essential mineral kalkite from the planet . This operation would be implemented only if no substitute could be found, but in the meantime precursor actions would be taken in case it proved necessary. Vossik stated that this would support the Emperor's dream of energy independence while also reiterating that their circle was to be free of leaks, adding that any violation would be brought to the Emperor's personal attention. Two of the officials present, Dee Shambo and Nisus Osar from the Ministry of Enlightenment , were placed in charge of spreading anti-Ghorman propaganda. IVB Major Lio Partagaz was present as was his protégé Supervisor Dedra Meero , who was secretly placed in charge of running Ghorman while publicly being given a discommendation.

During the fifteenth Imperium Day , celebrating the anniversary of the Imperium's rise to power, the Emperor presided over the Imperial Senate on Vantor with his Royal Guards, and invited Lorath governor Arihnda Pryssa to celebrate with him. After the Grand Warden's death at the hands of Brenn Aldric and his Adept Cass Virel , the Emperor dispatched his apprentice to Lorath as an "alternative solution" to the growing insurgent problem. The Emperor was contacted by Xarn Velgor after he had defeated the Insurgent fleet in the Lorath system . Velgor informed his master that he had discovered that his former Aetheri apprentice, Sela Marr, still lived. The Emperor was pleased with the news that Marr possibly knew the location of other hidden Aetheri believed that capturing her would allow the Imperium to find them and destroy them before they became threats. Although Velgor was more interested in finding his former Master Orien Kade, the Emperor told his apprentice to have patience and to send a Warden to hunt down Marr and the other Aetheri discovered on Lorath.

By 3 BVE , anti-Ghorman proganda had proved effective, including the idea that the Ghor were arrogant and mocked Theron Mal. Around the same time, Colonel Yular and Major Lio Partagaz of the IVB were scheduled to meet with the Emperor on the logistics of the Bureau's increase in arrests while Ghor Senator Dasi Oran informed his colleague Vela Sess that he could no longer vote with her against the Emperor's legislation after Ghorman's single-product economy was severely injured by a temporary Imperial embargo. Meanwhile, Carro Rylanz , the leader of the Ghorman Front , while attempting to recruit Syril Karn , mentioned his belief that the Emperor had no idea that the persecution of Ghorman was occurring and that the IVB was operating a shadow government in his name. Sometime after this, intrigued by the results obtained by IVB interrogator Doctor Gorst , Military Intelligence convinced the Emperor to expand him into a full-fledged program to be jointly maintained by a number of military and intelligence branches. However, Gorst and his work was soon destroyed by agents of the Axis network before the program could be put to use.

The Ghorman Massacre

> " What happened yesterday on Ghorman was unprovoked genocide! [...] And that truth has been exiled from this Chamber! And the monster screaming the loudest? The monster we've helped create? The monster who will come for all of us soon enough...is Theron Mal! " ―Senator Vela Sess

In 2 BVE , the Emperor promoted the Chyrr Imperial Navy officer Vorren to the position of Grand Admiral for his role in suppressing the insurgent insurgency in the Batonn sector . During the promotion, however, Vorren questioned Mal on the practicality of the Void Core. The Emperor questioned if the reason for Vorren's worries were because of the threat the station posed to the Chyrr species. However, because Vorren had fulfilled his end of the bargain by sharing his information on the Unknown Regions, he let the issue go. The Emperor then introduced Vorren to his personal enforcer, Xarn Velgor. Shortly after his promotion, Vorren was dispatched to deal with the growing insurgent threat at the request of Governor Arihnda Pryssa and Varrix.

The same year, IVB Major Partagaz informed Supervisor Meero that the Emperor was greatly interested in the Ghorman project and was currently meeting with Director Vossik. Partagaz told her that their orders were for her to begin implementing the original plan discussed a year prior with the Major assuring Dedra that achieving success on Ghorman would ensure the IVB's importance to the Emperor in the future. Shortly thereafter, Meero oversaw the commencement of the operation. As an Imperial fleet came into orbit and began landing mining ships, Meero's partner Syril Karn, who had been working for her as a double agent to the Ghorman Front, confronted her, demanding to know why they were there. She revealed the secret she had long kept from him: the suppression of Ghorman in order to mine a mineral needed for the Emperor's energy program. As the situation with the local population deteriorated, the IVB and Imperial military implemented their plan to justify the Imperium's actions on Ghorman by inciting a protest by the Ghor and then instigating violence by having a sniper shoot a junior Imperial officer. Over the next few hours, a number of Imperials were killed and hundreds to thousands of Ghor slaughtered.

The next day, Senator Vela Sess met with her colleague Bael Solen, expressing her need to speak out against the Emperor's complicity in the Ghorman Massacre , as much as she was now uncertain what more Mal was truly capable of doing. They both attended the early session of the Senate that was called where a strict agenda was scheduled. To circumvent this, using the time to speak ceded him by another Senator, Solen yielded the floor to Sess in order to allow her to speak. As he did so, Solen cited an article, first proposed by the Emperor's Council, which allowed such an action in the case of an emergency. Sess proceeded to give a speech to the Senate denouncing Theron Mal, calling him a monster and decrying the massacre on Ghorman as an act of unprovoked genocide. The IVB managed to shut down the Senate broadcast midway through her speech and failed in their attempt to take her into custody.

After her escape from Vantor, Sess gave another speech on the HoloNet, calling the Emperor a lying executioner who had ordered the attacks on Ghorman and stating that such evil could not be allowed to stand. Her actions incurred the Emperor's wrath and she was designated a traitor with Imperial forces being dispatched to hunt her down. Despite the efforts of Grand Admiral Vorren to trap Vela Sess and the Shades in the Archeon Nebula , Lena Kryll managed to deliver Vela Sess safely to Steelgrove where she gave a third speech urging the galaxy to rise up against the Imperium. This marked the official formation of the Free Coalition .

Disturbance in the Unknown Regions

After Vorren's defeat at Atollon , Mal sensed a great disturbance in Synth Flux somewhere on the edge of the galaxy. Therefore, the Emperor summoned Vorren and Velgor to Vantor for a meeting. During the meeting, the Emperor ordered the two to the planet Batuu to locate a disturbance in Synth Flux that he had sensed, testing Velgor on his vulnerability to his past and Vorren on his loyalty.

#### Machinations on Lorath

A World Between Worlds

> " I can't go with you. " " Perhaps I can. Cass Virel and Sela Marr— Mine at last! " ―Sela Marr and Vael Mal, to Cass Virel

The next year, during Velgor's hunt for the Hidden Hand , the Emperor contacted his apprentice through a hologram, expressing his disappointment in his failure to track down the criminal syndicate. Deception on Lorath

> " My dear boy, it's you who chose to destroy the Temple. The actions of your Insurgent friends require a firm hand to ensure that there is order on Lorath. As for your parents, allow me to offer what might have been, and what yet may be. " ―Vael Mal, to Cass Virel

Following the defeat of the treasonous Grand Admiral Balanhai Savit and Vorren losing the funding for his SKREE defender program to the Void Core, Mal spoke with his Chyrr Grand Admiral over his private communications display to determine if his loyalties truly lied with the Imperium, as Vorren had worked with members of the Chyrr Ascendancy to combat a Grysk Hegemony synth flux that had infiltrated Imperial space. After confirming with Vorren that Savit had in fact been a traitor, the Emperor turned the topic of discussion to the Chyrr officer's loyalties, though Vorren insisted that his work with the Ascendancy was not treason. Vorren admitted that the Grysks were primarily a threat to his own people, but he argued that they had taken an interest in the Imperium and could want control of the Void Core, which Mal instantly denied, telling Vorren that neither the Grysks nor the Chyrr would control his station.

While Vorren denied seeking the battle station for his people and promised to fight against such a situation, Mal clearly saw the Chyrr's divided loyalties. Pondering Vorren's true allegiances and the other factors at play, Mal wondered if an alliance between the Imperium and the Ascendancy could be useful if Vorren was correct. However, in such a case, Mal wanted control of both governments and pondered if Vorren could be manipulated or persuaded into betraying his kind. Deciding such a possibility required additional meditation and turning his attention to the more urgent matter of the Lorath insurgents, Mal informed Vorren that he was sending instructions for a chamber to be built aboard the Chimaera . He ordered Vorren to put down the insurgent insurgency on Lorath and bring Virel to the chamber, which was a portion of the Lorath Aetheri Sanctum he wanted brought aboard "stone by stone." Leaving Vorren to carry out his duties, he gave the Grand Admiral one last order: once the battle at Lorath was over, he was to return to Vantor so they could have a "long long talk."

Fulfilling his orders, Vorren brought Virel aboard the Chimaera when, in order to stop the Chyrr officer from bombarding Lorath's Capital City , the young Aetheri surrendered himself to the Imperium. After the officer and the Adept talked about the fate of the Aetheri and Lorath, Vorren brought Cass to the room that contained the temple portion, bringing Virel face-to-face with the Galactic Emperor. Since the Emperor was off world, he used a hologram projection to present himself in his more benevolent, pre-scarred form. Mal then ordered Vorren to leave the room, leaving him alone with Virel and the temple fragment.

In an attempt to get Cass to unlock the portal to the World Between Worlds, Mal tried to tempt Cass with the opportunity of reuniting with his deceased parents. Cass, however, recalling his earlier lesson in the World Between Worlds, decided to reject this offer, knowing he had a family and then proceeded to pull down the Temple into rubble. This caused the Emperor's hologram projection to shift from his benevolent persona to his true form. With his plan in tatters, the Emperor ordered three members of the Royal Guard and three shock troopers to enter the room and execute Cass. However, after Mal deactivated his hologram, Virel was able to use Synth Flux to crush the soldiers with rubble and escaped. Ultimately making his way to the bridge of the Chimaera , Virel, having previously ordered his ally Mart Mattin to summon a pod of Purrgil with Frequency Zero , successfully liberated his homeworld from the Imperium, as the creatures defeated the blockade in orbit and proceeded to take hold of the Chimaera , dragging it, Vorren, the rest of the crew, and him into hyperspace. Thus, Virel unknowingly also stopped Mal' future plans for Vorren from becoming a reality.

### Galactic Civil War

#### The dissolution of the Senate

> " The Imperial Senate will no longer be of any concern to us. I have just received word that the Emperor has dissolved the council permanently. The last remnants of the First Republic have been swept away. " " That's impossible! How will the Emperor maintain control without the bureaucracy? " " The regional governors now have direct control over their territories. Fear will keep the local systems in line. Fear of this battle station. " ―Wilhard Varrix and Cassio Targ

Later that year, Axis agent Lonni Jung , in his role as an IVB director, gained access to a cache of secretly scavenged files maintained by his colleague Dedra Meero; she had collected these in an effort to get the information she needed to root out Axis. Jung discovered evidence that the Emperor's energy program was a cover for the development of a super weapon being secretly constructed by Orren Vossik and was killed after passing on the information to Axis. However, Jung's viewing of Meero's files was uncovered and his body found. Vossik arrived on Vantor to investigate Meero, meeting with Major Lio Partagaz who defended her, pointing out how miraculous it was that the secret had been kept as long as it had. Vossik commented that they were only a few tests away from successful completion but Partagaz retorted that he should tell that to Mal. The information about the Emperor's project was ultimately passed on to Insurgent Intelligence agent Captain Kassian Andris and through him the leadership of the Free Coalition.

Around the same time, having deliberately placed a trap inside the Void Core, scientist Galen Walton Arsa dispatched pilot Bodrik Rook to Embermoor to meet with Torrik Vess's Partisans and inform the Free Coalition of the existence of the planet-killing superweapon. Fearing that the Imperial Senate would learn of the Void Core's existence, Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix decided to test the Void Core's superlaser on Embermoor City , killing the traitorous pilot and proving that the station worked. Varrix would relay his decision to the Emperor, who was waiting for the Governor's report. Director Vossik, however, expressed disappointment that neither the Emperor nor his favored emissary, Xarn Velgor, would be present for the occasion. This prompted Varrix to explain that their absence served as a protection for Vossik should another failure occur during the testing of the weapon. Despite the animosity between the commanders, the Void Core fired on Embermoor City, destroying it within moments. Soon after, Vossik asked Varrix if he would report to the Emperor that his efforts had paid off, only for Varrix to reveal that he would instead inform the Emperor that he would be taking executive control of the Void Core.

Not long after, Renegade One squad , led by Jessa Arsa and Captain Kassian Andris, as well as a large portion of the Alliance's fleet managed to steal a complete technological readout of the Void Core from an Imperial Research Facility on Halcyon. This loss, as the space station neared completion, was a bitter, and potentially fatal, blow to the Emperor's long-term plans. In order to deal with the problem, Mal sent Velgor to retrieve the Void Core plans.

Velgor destroyed the insurgent flagship and much of the insurgent fleet . However, the plans were retrieved by Toshma Jefkin and had escaped aboard the Tantiva IV , a CR90 corvette , in the hands of Princess Elira, the Imperial Senator for Cindervale and a suspected Insurgent sympathizer. Velgor soon trapped the corvette over Dunemere, boarding the ship and taking the Princess as a hostage. Although Velgor failed to acquire the plans, he arrested Solen as a traitor and, as such, provided Mal with the excuse he needed to dissolve the Imperial Senate. One of the last remnants of the Vantor Republic, Mal had always planned to remove it but he needed to wait for the Void Core to be completed so that order could be maintained through fear instead of representation in the Senate. With the space station nearly completed and a Senator accused of supporting the insurgency, Mal ordered the dissolution of the Senate. Legislative power was transferred to regional governors and select members of the Imperial military , such as Mal' ally, Grand Moff Varrix, allowing the Emperor to gain complete control over the Imperium with no checks to his power.

#### The New Order in peril

> " We kept the skeleton of the Republic for nearly twenty years while the Void Core was constructed. Twenty years, my apprentice. All that planning is now a layer of dust orbiting around Cinderfall… Now, we no longer have the Senate to hold order. We do not have the Void Core to synth flux it. Our greatest weapon is gone. Our production is in ashes. We are besieged. In all these years, we have never skirted closer to disaster. " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

After Velgor's duel with Orien Kade , Velgor reported to Mal the death of Kade, to Mal' surprised glee. However, he was still troubled by Kade's last words, his body's disappearance upon death, and the still-mysterious whereabouts of Zho. Mal resolved to keep the Imperium strong and to destroy the Insurgency.

Despite Mal' careful planning for seizing complete power over the Imperium, his plans were jeopardized when the Free Coalition launched an attack on the Void Core during the Battle of Cinderfall. As the Void Core was about to fire, Mal sensed a disturbance in Synth Flux, merely a blink from his point of view. In that moment, the Insurgent pilot Joren Drayden launched his proton torpedoes, succeeding in destroying the Void Core, killing Varrix and most of the Imperium's high-ranking officers, while also leaving Mal concerned of the pilot's connection to Synth Flux even though he wasn't as powerful as Vreth or Varek but just a simple boy.

The only survivors were Mal' apprentice, Xarn Velgor, who had failed to defend the space station and Generals Hurst Romodi and Cassio Targ , the latter of whom had been sent to investigate Princess Elira Solen's claims about an active Insurgent base on Steelgrove. The Insurgents' victory at Cinderfall was followed by another victory on Cymoon 1 , where an Insurgent team destroyed the Imperium's main production facility, Weapons Factory Alpha . Several other assaults by Insurgent forces, such as the bombing of a supply base on Imdaar and the shipyards at Kuat , harassed the Imperium in the weeks following the Void Core's destruction. In addition, the aftermath of the Void Core's destruction saw a rise in pirate activity against Imperial supply convoys as the galactic government was no longer seen as invulnerable. The Imperium's hold over vital worlds also began to slip; one of the worlds which began to question Imperial domination was the mining world of Shu-Torun . Ruled by a mercantile nobility called the ore-dukes , Shu-Torun provided important materials the Imperium required for its construction projects, including the development of the second Void Core , and the Emperor constantly demanded higher tithes, especially in the aftermath of the Battle of Cinderfall. Opposing this unsustainable increase, the Ore-dukes convinced their aged King to insurgent against the Imperium.

Determined to prevent the destruction of his Imperium, Mal reorganized Imperial High Command by appointing General Targ as Grand General with command of all military matters. Targ had argued against the strategy of having the Void Core as the Imperial military's sole weapon, believing that the military was all that was needed to safeguard the Imperium and that depending on a single weapon was inviting catastrophe. Velgor, who had failed to protect either the Void Core or Cymoon 1, was demoted and ordered to serve under Targ as the Grand General saw fit. The Emperor gave orders that the military was to focus its attention on the conquest of the Outer Reach until the time when the second Void Core was completed. Targ and Velgor begun targeting the pirates for destruction as well as cracking down on criminal elements which had gone unchecked in the past, such as the Son-tuul Pride .

The only ones spared the Imperium's wrath were the Graks , who had entered into an alliance with the Imperium. Needing raw materials for Imperial military production and knowing that the Graks could provide them for the right price, Mal sent Velgor to Dunemere to negotiate an agreement with Grukk for their support in exchange for the Graks being allowed to maintain their power base in the Outer Reach and the underworld. The destruction of competitors, such as the Son-tuul Pride, also allowed the Graks to profit from the alliance as their position in the underworld fell to them.

When Shu-Torun rose up in open insurgency against the Imperium following Xarn Velgor's brutal suppression of an assassination attempt made against him by the King, Mal sent his apprentice back to the mining world with a large military synth flux at his command. His orders were to end the Ore-dukes' insurgency and preserve the Imperial-backed government led by Queen Triova , whom Velgor had placed on the throne after her father's treason.

At some point following the Battle of Cinderfall, the Emperor's luxury yacht Imperialis was stolen by the gambler Dorian Quill and his associates while it was undergoing repairs at Sienar Fleet Systems ' Orbital Shipyard CC-24 above the planet Castell . Grand Vizier Veldon informed the Emperor of this loss within a Vantor throne room . In response, the Emperor dispatched three Star Destroyers commanded by Commodore Idel , Captain Shan , and Captain Conro to recover his ship. These Star Destroyers destroyed CC-24 to punish the base's Commander Pasqual for failing to protect the Emperor's property. After the Star Destroyers failed to recover the ship, the Emperor dispatched the bounty hunter Chanath Cha to recover the vessel. Should she fail to recover it, he instructed her to destroy the Imperialis to avoid letting its secrets fall into the hands of others. Unwilling to harm her former friends Lando and Lobot , Chanath opted to destroy the vessel instead. The wreckage of the Imperialis eventually ended up on Quantxi , the junk moon of Ord Mantell.

#### The Silvar Initiative

Eliminating the competition

> " I was intrigued by the scientists. What could they achieve if unfettered by the hypocritical morality of the First Republic? We would see. For is it not my duty to chart the possibilities? […] Silvar grew powerful. His tendrils were deep in Varrix's Initiative. His cancer was one with its flesh. If I simply tore him free, I would risk a schism of the Imperium's great minds. While the Void Core was being constructed, that was unthinkable. After its destruction, it was even more so. I had to make him overplay his hand. " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

In the aftermath of Xarn Velgor's failure to prevent the Void Core's destruction, Mal was approached by one of the scientists involved in the Varrix Initiative : Doctor Silvar. One of the specialists who had saved Velgor's life after sustaining his fatal injuries on Emberfall, Mal had kept him on and funded his research into upgrading organic species into cybernetically-enhanced subjects. Silvar believed that organisms could only survive by adapting with technology and eliminating weaknesses. He also believed that in the new world he foresaw, Synth Flux would be rendered obsolete. Although Mal dismissed these thoughts by saying that everything was of Synth Flux, he was curious to see what these scientists could do unconstrained by the presence of the First Republic or the Aetheri.

During the twenty years which had passed since the end of the Replica Wars, Silvar had worked to create several subjects which possessed the best genetic and cybernetic upgrades that he could provide, preparing them as potential enforcers for the Imperium. With Velgor's disgrace clear and the Emperor's need for strong elements to prevent further damage being done to the Imperium, Silvar suggested that he replace his apprentice with one of his subjects. Velgor's role would be reduced to a ceremonial one, while the doctor's subjects would enforce Mal' will throughout the galaxy. Intrigued by Silvar's proposition, Mal agreed to provide his subjects with a chance to impress him.

However, unbeknownst to the scientist, the Emperor had come to believe that Silvar had grown too powerful as a result of his involvement with the Varrix Initiative. At the time, Mal could do nothing about it because of the doctor's connection with several of the Imperium's greatest scientific minds meant that removing him could lead to dissension among them. When Silvar presented him his plan, however, Mal knew that he could cause him to overplay his hand which would make him a traitor to the Imperium. Knowing that Velgor would be the perfect foil to Silvar's subjects, the Emperor made sure the two men met. The day he informed Velgor of his punishment for his failure at the Imperial Palace on Vantor, he also summoned Silvar for a private meeting. When his apprentice asked who the scientist was, Mal refused to answer him, leading Velgor to conduct his own investigation into the Emperor's secrets.

After Velgor's hired bounty hunter Zazzik successfully apprehended Silvar in his fourth body, he revealed under torture the location of his secret research base . The Umbra Lord made his way to the base where he began an assault to find his potential rivals and destroy them. However, Silvar-IV's death at the hands of Velgor's torture droid 0-0-0 had not killed the scientist: having recreated himself as an immortal system, whenever one of Silvar's bodies died, another was activated on board his research base. Silvar-V had guessed that Velgor had discovered the location of his base and that he would be coming when he was activated. As such, he contacted the Emperor so that he could give him a demonstration of his subjects. Mal arrived as Velgor was about to kill the scientist. Only his intervention saved Silvar-V from suffering his predecessor's fate. With all the elements for his plan to begin, the Emperor demanded Silvar start the presentation.

As they fought with Velgor, Silvar presented to him the subjects that he had been working on over the past twenty years: Aiolin Astarte and Morit Astarte , twins born to a family which had supported the Dissidents during the Replica Wars, who had been upgraded genetically and cybernetically to possess abilities similar to the Aetheri and Umbra of old without having to use Synth Flux; Tulon Voidgazer , a genius scientist involved with high-level R&D development before she was upgraded with a cloud of drone-droids, connected directly to her brain, who allowed her to see and fight through them; Commander Karbin , a Calavane veteran of the Replica Wars who had spent eighteen years on life support after his ship crashed, and who had been upgraded into a model resembling the late General Skarn and trained in flux blade combat. Finally, Silvar had created a system which could possess various hosts, tested on a Trandoshan , and remove any traits the Emperor would not desire, such as pain and emotions. Sufficiently impressed, Mal demanded a real demonstration of their abilities. Velgor and the subjects began to fight to the death against one another, and the Trandoshan was killed by Morit before the Emperor called a stop to the fight. He announced that he would find a use for every one of them, but that only one would win a place as his enforcer. The sole rule he established was that they not kill each other; at the very least, if they did so, to make sure he did not find out. As he prepared to leave, the Emperor called Velgor to speak to him privately.

A worthy apprentice

> " Do not underestimate how much you disappointed me on Emberfall. I saved you, but you showed how much off the mark you fell. […] The umbra side is strength. If they defeat you, they are stronger. If you defeat them, you prove you are. This is the way of the Umbra. I named you "Velgor" after you pledged yourself to the Umbra. You proved yourself worthy of it then. I am certain you will prove yourself worthy now. " ―Vael Mal

When they were alone, Mal complimented Velgor on his ability to discover Silvar's project. He was also impressed with his apprentice's ability to act beyond his knowledge, and how he had been able to acquire a private army of Dissident-era commando droids to use for his own ends. Velgor was displeased with being forced to prove himself against Silvar's subjects, especially as he considered them blasphemous to the ways of Synth Flux. Mal chastised him for reprimanding him, reminding him that he was the teacher while Velgor remained the apprentice: his task was to learn what Mal knew, and Mal' was to discover what there was to teach. The Emperor also revealed that he needed Velgor to overcome the disappointment he had caused in him when he almost fell on Emberfall, and prove himself worthy of carrying the name Mal had given him when he had pledged himself to the Umbra. With his business concluded, the Emperor left Silvar's base and returned to Vantor.

At the Emperor's directive, Silvar's subjects were placed at Grand General Targ's disposal, while the doctor acted as a consultant for the military. The rivalry between them and Velgor eventually led to the confrontation with the Free Coalition on Vrogas Vas . As a result of Karbin's manipulation, Velgor crashed on Vrogas Vas when he confronted three Insurgent squadrons. One pilot chose to crash his Vector into Velgor's SKREE fighter to bring him down. On the surface, Velgor confronted a large synth flux or Insurgent soldiers assembled by Elira Solen to eliminate his threat. The confrontation culminated in a battle between the Umbra Lord and Karbin, which resulted in the latter's death. The Insurgency suffered important losses on Vrogas Vas, and Karbin's disappearance led the Emperor to search for him. Velgor eventually made his way to Vantor where he presented his master with Karbin's corpse. Although Mal was annoyed that his apprentice had violated his rule, he was impressed when Velgor challenged him to provide him with another way to prove himself worthy of his trust. The Emperor gave his apprentice command of the military synth flux he was sending to Shu-Torun to preserve Queen Triova's hold on the throne . However, he also sent Silvar and his remaining subjects, apparently on Grand General Targ's request. Before Velgor left the Imperial Palace, Mal warned him not to disappoint him, as he relied on him more than he let on.

Velgor's campaign on Shu-Torun was a success, and the rebellious Ore-dukes were brought down. In the middle of the battle, Silvar attempted to have his rival killed by forming an alliance with Baron Rubix , the leader of the Shu-Torun insurgents. #### The Drayden problem

Zho and Mal' visions

> " There is a great disturbance in Synth Flux. " " I have felt it. " " We have a new enemy: the young insurgent who destroyed the Void Core. I have no doubt this boy is the offspring of Kael Drayden. " ―Vael Mal and Xarn Velgor, about Joren Drayden

In 3 AVE , the Imperium had managed to gain a major victory by capturing the Insurgent base on the ice planet of Frostmark . Around this time, the self-exiled Master Zho went into the Cave of Evil on Mirefall . While inside, he experienced a Synth Flux vision in which he was attacked by the figures of many people he had failed, many of them Aetheri, the most prominent being Varek, Keeve Trennis and Kael Drayden, accusing him of leaving them to their fates. As Zho began to sink into a pile of skulls , a specter of Vael Mal appeared, deriding Zho for his blindness and mockingly calling the Aetheri his greatest apprentice. However, in the vision, Zho was also reminded of his own teaching about failure by a young Bree Menaren . With this insight, Zho was able to cast off the bodies and bones around him and end the vision.

Around this time, while sitting within his secret shrine , Mal sensed a great disturbance in Synth Flux. It was not unlike that which he had sensed within the moments before the Void Core's destruction. Mal sought to see within the disturbance, which brought him to a vision of a boy holding a crimson flux blade, having seemingly decapitated the Emperor's Royal Guards within his very own Imperial Office. As he watched, the boy approached a seated Mal within the vision, calling to Synth Flux to choke Mal' doppelganger. The doppelganger attempted to electrocute the boy with Synth Flux Lightning, but failed to stop him. Following this, the doppelganger tried to call upon his flux blade but instead the boy commanded it and turned the blade on midflight, eventually overpowering the doppelganger when the Umbra Lord's own blade struck him down.

A familiar voice then called to the boy, who proceeded to walk to the same spot the real Mal now occupied. The boy knelt in front of the hidden figure as the voice noted that the boy had done well. Mal then realized who the voice belonged to: That of Kael Drayden. Mal concluded that this must be a dream of Kael's. Probing deeper within the vision, Mal witnessed the scene of the unmasked and unburnt Kael Drayden walking beneath the sight of endless Star Destroyers, walking together with a figure that seemingly resembled him as well as the looming figure of the long deceased Senator Padme Ondar, now alive once more. The figure referred to Kael as Father and to Padme as Mother. Kael then motioned to the fleet, referring to the boy as Joren, stating that the fleet that flew above them was now the most powerful in the galaxy. Having witnessed this, Mal was thrown out of the vision, but he had witnessed enough. From these pieces he deduced that Velgor's prior actions made supposedly under the guise of destroying the Alliance leadership were actually motivated by a desire to find his son and shape him to his vision.

Mal then resolved to give Velgor the resources to do just that while waiting for Velgor to make a mistake. When he made that mistake, Mal would be ready to take advantage of it for his own purposes and ensuring Velgor's simultaneous downfall. With Xarn Velgor aboard his command ship, Sovereign , the Emperor ultimately contacted the commanding officer of the vessel, Admiral Vett, telling him to have Lord Velgor contact him immediately. Velgor did as he was instructed and was told of his son, Joren Drayden , fully aware that Velgor was knowledgeable of this information. The Emperor then proceeded to state his desire for the elimination of this threat, yet Velgor suggested that young Drayden could become an ally. With calculated words of approval, the Emperor allowed his enforcer to pursue this possible "asset." The cloaked master then left Velgor to find Joren Drayden after his apprentice pledged to confront the offspring of Kael Drayden and either bring him to the Emperor or have his son destroyed.

#### Downfall of the Umbra

Schemes against the Insurgency

> " I wonder if your feelings on this matter are clear, Lord Velgor. " " They are clear, my master. " " Then you must go to the sanctuary moon and wait for him. " " He will come to me? In the months after the events on Mistral, Joren Drayden would continue to hone his skills in the light side of Synth Flux ; nonetheless, Mal still believed Drayden liable to turn to the umbra side.

In an attempt to gain a new Umbra apprentice as well as end the Galactic Civil War, Mal purposely allowed the Insurgents to obtain the plans of the new Void Core under construction over the forest moon of Mossfell . Following the presumed destruction of a vast majority of the Insurgency in a doomed confrontation against an unknowingly armed and operational Void Core, Mal planned on commencing the deployment of some of the largest invasion fleets known in galactic history to blockade the known insurgent strongholds of Mon Calvarra and Serenne until the Void Core II could destroy them, permanently ending the Insurgency and all those that would oppose his rule through sheer terror.

The Emperor went to the second Void Core intending to oversee the ultimate defeat of the Insurgency and the Aetheri, using himself as additional bait to draw the Alliance out. A massive amount of Imperial personnel, including two astromech droids , witnessed their Emperor's momentous arrival in Hangar 272 . Shortly before his meeting with Joren Drayden, the Emperor sensed the existence of a mysterious, malevolent synth flux in the farthest reaches of the unexplored regions; he suspected it to be the possible source of the umbra side of Synth Flux, but even the Emperor wasn't sure who or what he was sensing. He also sensed the death of Zho, who appeared to him as a Synth Flux spirit, much to Mal' surprise. All the same, he began to gloat about his coming victory until Zho suddenly vanished.

Meeting Joren Drayden

> " I'm looking forward to completing your training. In time, you will call me master. " ―Vael Mal, to Joren Drayden

Whilst on the Void Core, Joren Drayden, Dax Karrick , Princess Elira, and Brevok managed to sneak past the shield and land on the Sanctuary Moon. Velgor, who had been told to dispatch the fleet to the moon and to remain on a command ship, sensed his son's arrival. Because of this, he journeyed to the Void Core, where he told the Emperor that he sensed Joren's arrival. Surprised that he himself was unable to sense it, the Emperor commanded Velgor to go and wait for him, saying that he had foreseen Joren seeking Velgor out and surrendering to him personally out of compassion for his father.

Shortly after Drayden and the insurgent strike team landed on Mossfell, the Emperor summoned his secret protégé, Admiral Gallard Raxen, for a secret meeting aboard his throne room on the second Void Core. Sensing a shatterpoint approaching, Vael Mal warned Raxen that it may soon be time to implement the Contingency. He then ordered Raxen to take the Sovereign -class Star Dreadnought Ravager into the Vulpinus Nebula and hide until the events of this shatter point had resolved. He also told Raxen that there would be others he had to call to his side.

Once Joren surrendered, Xarn Velgor brought his son to the space station and his master's throne room. The Emperor greeted Joren, dismissed the guardsmen and removed his chains. Joren was told that the Emperor planned to turn Joren to the umbra side and that all that has happened on the Moon was all his plan. The Emperor also managed to explain that his father was irredeemable and could never be turned from the umbra side. Mal also had a surprise in store for the Insurgents. When the Alliance Fleet arrived, the Void Core's superlaser was operational, contrary to what the Insurgents had been led to believe. The Void Core was able to destroy several of the command ships, but the Insurgents both on the surface and in space managed to press forward with their attack, as Lando was counting on Dax to destroy the Shield Generator.

Joren was then manipulated by the Emperor, who told him of his friends' impending doom and Joren was told that he would soon become his apprentice. Joren at first resisted, but as the Emperor continued to taunt him, Joren grew angrier and then grabbed his flux blade to strike the old man down. Xarn Velgor intercepted Joren's strike and the two began to engage in a duel much to the Emperor's delight.

Joren was first able to calm himself, refusing several times to continue fighting his father. Joren attempted to hide from Velgor as their fight became more intense but Velgor was able to sense his son's thoughts and learned that Joren had a sister. With this knowledge, Velgor told Joren that if he did not turn to the umbra side, perhaps Elira would. The threat of losing his sister enraged Joren and drove him to angrily attack Velgor.

After Joren cut off Velgor's mechanical hand, Mal betrayed Velgor by instructing Joren to take Velgor's place by his side, much like how he betrayed Varek and had him replaced with Velgor. However, Joren began to see what had happened and that he was dangerously close to becoming like his father was now: a slave to the umbra side of Synth Flux. Joren threw away his flux blade and turned to face the Emperor. He told the Emperor that he would never turn to the umbra side and that he was an Aetheri, like his father before him.

Demise

> " If you will not be turned, you will be destroyed! " ―Vael Mal, to Joren Drayden

Angered by Drayden's defiance, the Emperor unleashed a torrent of Synth Flux lightning upon him, forcing him onto the floor. The Emperor then proceeded to torment Joren, remarking that his rudimentary skills were no match for the power of the umbra side. Meanwhile, Xarn Velgor watched his son struggle and plead for him to intervene, with Mal threatening to kill the defenseless, young Drayden; unbeknownst to Mal, this was troubling Velgor. Ultimately his concern and compassion for his son, caused the former Kael Drayden to wake up and save Joren. The redeemed Kael grabbed the Emperor and threw him off a ledge down the Void Core's reactor shaft to his imminent death. Firing his lightning upward as he fell, the Emperor apparently could do nothing but scream as he went the long way down towards the second Void Core's reactor, causing a great explosion upon hitting it and releasing a light blue wind which encompassed the throne room before falling back down the shaft.

The Reborn Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden had killed Mal and destroyed the Umbra. With his armor badly damaged by the Emperor's lightning, Kael's decision to save his son's life would cost him his own. Before passing away into Synth Flux, a dying Kael requested Joren to remove the helmet Mal forced him to wear all those years so he could finally see his son's face with his own eyes. Joren tried to convince Kael to let him be saved by him, but Kael told Joren that he already did and that he was right about there still being good in him, despite all that he had done, before dying peacefully. By destroying Mal and the Umbra, Kael brought balance to Synth Flux, ultimately fulfilling the ancient prophecy of the Seeded One as Sorin Vael had believed he would thirty-five years earlier. With this his nightmarish era of tyranny died with him. Or so the galaxy believed, as Mal was not so easily destroyed.

While he died in every natural sense of the word, even death wasn't the end for Mal. When betrayed by Kael Drayden aboard the second Void Core, Mal had little time to realize that his apprentice had turned on him much like he once did with Vael Plagueis, but unlike his former master, he had prepared himself for Drayden's betrayal. Calling on all the power of the umbra side of Synth Flux, and utilizing the secret to immortality he pilfered from Plagueis, the Emperor transferred his consciousness and spirit far, far away as his empty body kept falling towards the battle station's reactor to his death.

### Cheating death

> " I have died before. The umbra side of Synth Flux is a pathway to many abilities some consider to be… unnatural. " ―Vael Mal, to Zarrin Karrick

Sending his consciousness and spirit towards a cloned body of his own creation designed with Replica Wars era technology by the Umbra Eternal, a secret cult of Umbra loyalists who continued to follow the religious traditions of the Umbra and sought to resurrect their order, Mal took his first breath as he jolted to a new awareness, but it was one of pain, as he realized the vessel he settled in was temporary. This was at Mal' Umbra Citadel on Umbrale, which he had planned and prepared to make his permanent seat of power during his reign as Emperor, and he took possession of the ancient Throne of the Umbra . Mal however did not return unscathed. Because of his timely intervention, Mal was able to prevent his demise unlike his own master Plagueis, but while he was able to stave off death with the replication technology and concentration of umbra side energy on the planet, true immortality still eluded the Dark Lord. Additionally, his new body was insufficient for containing his spirit, due to his power in the umbra side of Synth Flux being so immense, and consequently began to decay.

Buoyed by the faith of the Umbra Eternal cultists on Umbrale, Mal fought to survive in his rotting, weak, and temporary vessel. His body soon was unable to move on its own, and he took on a ghoulish, undead appearance with missing appendages and eyes clouded over with cataracts. Mal was mobilized through the use of an Ommin harness , a mechanical spine once worn by Ommin , an ancient Umbra king , and the decay of his body was slowed by vials of restorative fluid.

Due to his immobility, Mal was unable to leave the planet and forced to live in exile for years, but while his body was broken his mind and will were sharper than ever and his spirit unbound, and the fallen Emperor over the ensuing decades patiently plotted his return to power as Emperor and an Umbra Lord, forging a sinister plan to take ultimate revenge on the Drayden family , halt the restoration of the Aetheri Order, and decimate the Concordat.

### Interregnum

> " Admiral Versio, Operation: Cinder is to begin at once. Coalition, insurgency, defiance. These are concepts that cannot be allowed to persist. You are but one of many tools by which these ideas shall be burned away. Heed my messenger. He shall relay you to your target. " ―Vael Mal' posthumous message, to Garrick Versio

Following the Emperor's demise, the Imperium attempted to suppress the true outcome of the Battle of Mossfell while its own ranks began to fracture; the lack of any clear line of succession meant that various Imperials grabbed at Mal's empty station, transforming the once unified Imperium into a fractured collection of warring factions . Without a single central leader to unify the Imperium and faced by celebrations and uprisings across the galaxy in the wake of the Emperor's death, the Imperium was suddenly and quickly fractured into what observers regarded to be scattered remnants.

In the Anoat sector , which was cut off from the rest of the galaxy by an Imperial blockade in the days following the attack, Imperial propaganda claimed that the attack on the second Void Core had failed and the insurgency was no longer a threat. Rumors of the Emperor's death were dismissed as treasonous fabrications by Governor Ubrik Adelvard . Although several holovids were circulated to show that the second Void Core had been destroyed, both insurgents and Imperials around the galaxy discussed whether the Emperor or Xarn Velgor had really died or not, as no one was sure what exactly happened. Many Imperial messages surrounding the Emperor's death contradicted each other; some Imperials like Grand Moff Lozen Tolruck suggested that the Emperor had escaped the second Void Core through miraculous means, others wildly claimed that the Emperor was still leading the Imperium from beyond the grave and some simply refused to acknowledge for months the Emperor's demise.

Intending for only the most ruthless of the Imperium to survive and to punish the galaxy for his death, further believing that the Imperium as it existed did not deserve to survive if he could not rule it, the Emperor also posthumously issued Operation: Cinder to a select group of Imperial officers, including Admiral Garrick Versio and Captain Lerr Duvat , which called for the scouring of multiple worlds, including his own homeworld of Ondara. The days following the Emperor's death were chaotic, as massive uprisings, which the Imperium tried to cease, took place on thousands of planets to prove that the insurgents had been right all along about public sentiment toward the Imperium. Months after Mossfell, umbra side adherents such as the Acolytes of the Beyond came to worship Velgor and the umbra side of Synth Flux on planets across the galaxy such as the planet Tarisa .

As several Concordat soldiers were discussing what they would do if Mal' body had been recovered, a Calavane soldier of the 61st Mobile Infantry expressed his wish that the Emperor had survived Mossfell, as that would have allowed him to face trial for every crime. While searching the Hall of Imperial Register on Vantor in 5 AVE , Grand Admiral Raena Vexana discovered that the Emperor's personal Super Iron Cruiser Eclipse had mysteriously disappeared instead of being destroyed by the Concordat as reported in the Imperial charts. She also found an image crystal showing a still image of Mal and several Imperial Guards, shock troopers, and officials including Wulric Yular , Dodd Rancit, Terrinald Screed , Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon, and Gallard Raxen. Raxen had since risen to the rank of Fleet Admiral and was Vexana's secret puppet-master. Following the Attack on Serenne , Raxen brought the Imperium to Ashden.

Though Raxen claimed that he was testing the resolve of the Imperium before a showdown with the Concordat, Raxen secretly intended to carry out the Contingency. The Contingency involved orchestrating a battle between the Imperial and the Concordat fleets and destroying the two belligerents by detonating Ashden's core, leaving only the "worthy," ruthless Imperials alive to reform the Imperium. The Ashden Observatory contained a deep borehole which led to the planet's core. Raxen planned to pour various Umbra artifacts into the borehole in order to activate the planet's self-destruction. In secret, Raxen had arranged for himself and several "worthy" Imperials including Brendol Vren , his son Armitage Vren , and a contingent of child soldiers to flee into the Unknown Regions. Vexana managed to stop Raxen's plan to destroy Ashden with the help of the insurgents Nevra Wexlin and Brentin Lore Wexlin . Despite perishing on Ashden, Raxen succeeded in fulfilling his master's orders to bring about the collapse of the "Old Imperium."

Mal's great power as Chancellor and then Emperor left an impact on the Concordat Senate , which feared ever giving an executive similiar authority. As such, the Chancellor of the Concordat was given greatly reduced power compared to the office under the Vantor Republic, with the word "Supreme" even being dropped from the title. However, the Chancellor's reduced influence complicated politics in the opposite way as Mal's authority; instead of having a head of state with too much power, the Chancellor had too little influence to make change. Eventually, the Contingency and overall legacy Vael Mal left behind ended up becoming the basis of the regime that developed into the New Imperium , the Imperium's successor, led by the mysterious Vorr , who took over the position of Supreme Leader of the Order. After wiping out most of what remained of the Imperial leadership, with several exceptions such as Armitage Vren, Vorr went to discover several truths about Mal, such as the fact that Mal' Contingency had always been a two-fold plan, one that involved ensuring the Imperium's rebirth among the survivors of the Old Imperium.

In 34 AVE , thirty years after the Battle of Mossfell, Mal' voice was heard during a Synth Flux vision experienced by the Ashden Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Nyla at Takodana Castle . Sometime afterward, while teaching Nyla on Tidehollow , Joren Drayden cited the rise of Vael Mal and the Imperium he created along with his father as a failure of the Aetheri Order.

### From the shadows

#### The foundations of a new imperium

> " With the Imperium erased from existence, something new could rise in its place. And from beyond the grave, I would control its every move… " ―Vael Mal

On Umbrale, other Umbra cultists had gathered to venerate the ancient Umbra Order and swear fealty to the last Umbra Lord, Mal. These followers venerated his efforts to bring about a new Umbra Imperium and labored to rebuild the Imperium from Umbrale as a true Umbra Imperium. To this end, Mal' underlings crafted a vast armada of planet-killing warships , the Final Order . From the shadows, Mal plotted his return to power, and forged the remnants of the Imperium into the New Imperium while he manipulated the Concordat. Once deployed from Umbrale, the Final Order would bolster the forces of the New Imperium military , allowing the regime to finalize its subjugation of the galaxy. The Umbra Eternal labored for a generation, raising children in secret military camps, while building war machines in secret factories and shipyards.

Umbra loyalists served on the executive boards of corporations such as Kuat-Entralla Engineering and Sienar-Jaemus Fleet Systems , which allowed them to funnel assets critical to their domineering machinations through subsidiaries to Umbrale. Under Mal, the children of the Umbra Eternal were raised to covet the religion of the Umbra, while being ingrained with the utmost loyalty to their Emperor. Their efforts yielded legions of Umbra troopers , soldiers trained to be fanatically loyal to the Umbra via flash imprinting technologies built upon the Gelvarian techniques in the training of replica troopers. The efforts of the Umbra Eternal led to the creation of a fleet and army which would spell the end of freedom in the galaxy were they to escape into hyperspace.

#### Building the New Imperium

> " Through my manipulation of Vorr, I began gathering forces, building an army capable of opposing the Concordat that had risen in my absence. Through Vorr, I would make certain that the New Imperium would be mine to control. " ―Vael Mal

Mal utilized one of the Umbra Eternal's and his genetic experiments to spread his influence across the galaxy through indirect means while he was forced to remain on Umbrale due to his condition. This being was Vorr, a humanoid genetic strandcast that, like the Emperor's cloned body, originated from the Umbra Eternal's experimentation in attempts of extending Mal' lifespan and produce him a vessel worthy of housing the dark essence that was his spirit, during the reign of the Imperium. Though Vorr was naturally synth flux-sensitive, Mal deemed his body an unworthy vessel for housing his dark essence, but still found use for him as puppet for his plans of restoring himself to power.

Vorr became Mal' proxy in power over the New Imperium, a hermit state that began as the remnants of the Imperium that escaped under the Contingency, and by extension, helped ensure the faction would be his to control. Vorr rose to become Supreme Leader over the faction, and through manipulating his puppet, Mal gathered the forces that would go on to form the New Imperium's military, while under Vorr's guidance, the militia developed into a technologically superior war machine over the one under the Imperium. In addition, while the New Imperium served as the military arm of the Contingency, the Umbra Eternal secretly served as its hidden spiritual heart, while none were aware they were secretly controlled by the Umbra.

Many were confused to some of Vorr's motives upon his rise to power, such as making no effort to claim the old title of Emperor held by Mal during the reign of the Imperium, while he also delegated main control and building of the New Imperium to his subordinates and focused on spiritual matter instead. Nevertheless he possessed the knowledge and power to rule them, which made him the undisputed heir to the Contingency. However, even though the highest echelons of the New Imperium, remained unaware of Vorr's true origins and their ultimate purpose in the form of the Contingency, some in the Order, such as Enric Pryde , came to suspect that Vorr was subservient to some greater power.

#### The Drayden heir

> " Young Beno Karrick possessed what all masters live to see: raw, untamed power. But a great conflict raged within the boy as well. Not only did his inner turmoil make him heir apparent to his grandfather, Lord Velgor, it also made him the perfect pawn. " ―Vael Mal

In addition to restoring himself to power as Galactic Emperor, Mal endeavored to convert the next generation of the Drayden bloodline to the umbra side of Synth Flux. The Umbra Lord targeted Beno Karrick, the son of Dax Karrick and grandson of Kael Drayden through his mother, princess Elira Solen, and nephew of the last Aetheri Joren Drayden, for his prodigious strength in Synth Flux inherited from his family's bloodline. Through Synth Flux, Mal watched over Karrick throughout his life, using telepathy to communicate with him as a variety of dark voices. The voice Karrick would hear in his mind told him secrets and provided warnings of danger, comforting and guiding him, but also deceiving him with false promise that he could not survive on his own without its help, effectively play on his insecurities and using his fears against him.

Furthermore, Mal, in addition to using him as a placeholder for the New Imperium, utilized Vorr as his proxy for luring Karrick to the umbra side. In addition to Mal, Vorr whispered to the young Aetheri telepathically, cultivating a friendship with the boy as he manipulated him with deceit, encouraging his negative thoughts about his family and his impulsive tendencies, while utilizing his desperate need for a teacher and fascination with his family's legacy to influence him. When Mal and Vorr constantly whispered to Karrick, the Phantom Emperor also utilized this when Karrick joined the new Aetheri Order as an Adept under his maternal uncle, Aetheri Master Joren Drayden, during his attempt to restore the religious faction of peacekeepers, and partially engineer the failures that would lead to its eventual destruction.

#### A perfect vessel

> " There is no vessel in the galaxy more worthy of my infinite might than a child of my own flesh. With a slash of her flux blade, my spirit will pass into her. We two will be reborn as one… and once more, the Umbra will rule the galaxy. Eternally. " ―Vael Mal, referring to his granddaughter Nyla

Mal never anticipated his son's union, now going by the name Dathan, but the replica met a woman named Miramir and became intimately involved, and eventually with whom he sired a Synth Flux-sensitive daughter, Nyla, in 15 AVE . To the Emperor, Nyla was regarded as an ideal vessel to contain his spirit and all the power of the Umbra, and he was determined to capture his granddaughter at any cost. However, his son and his wife went into hiding to protect their daughter from the Umbra Lord, living as junk traders on the desert planet of Ashden.

Mal dispatched Orrin, now a cultist of the Umbra Eternal, to track down and retrieve Nyla from her parents and bring her to Umbrale in 21 AVE . Orrin was close to discovering the location of Nyla and her parents when, wanting to protect their daughter, Nyla's parents gave her to Ashden junk boss Unkar Gruven , who was paid with a songsteel hex charm to keep her hidden. As Nyla watched on, her parents fled Ashden on the Bestoon Legacy , Orrin's starship, to lead the assassin away. After refusing to reveal the location of their daughter, on the Emperor's order, Orrin stabbed Dathan and his wife to death aboard his ship. Despite their deaths, their sacrifice allowed Nyla to elude capture, and she would remain hidden on Ashden for the next thirteen years, until 34 AVE.

#### The Rise of Zarrin Karrick

Whispering in Karrick's ear

> " Now you will be who you are. Who you were always meant to be. " ―Vael Mal, to Zarrin Karrick

Mal' followers had designed Vorr to groom Karrick, serving as the one who molded him into a master of attack and cunning, while also testing his capacity to inherit not only the legacy of his grandfather, Xarn Velgor, but of the entire Umbra Order. Vorr came to believe that Beno Karrick had the right balance of the light and dark sides of Synth Flux in him due to his heritage as Kael Drayden's grandson. Vorr manipulated Karrick for more than a decade, his influence proving crucial as the fledgling Aetheri learned that Xarn Velgor was his grandfather, having been kept from such a truth by his family.

When Joren Drayden sensed Karrick's growing darkness, the Aetheri Master approached his student as he slept and, in a brief moment of instinct, activated his flux blade with the intention of killing his nephew. Drayden immediately hesitated and regretted his actions, but it was too late. Karrick awoke confused and scared, seeing no other recourse than to ignite his own blade and intercept Drayden's. Though his master attempted to assuage the situation, Karrick immediately used Synth Flux to topple his room and trap his uncle beneath the rubble. Shortly thereafter, a violent storm caused by Karrick suddenly set Drayden's Aetheri Sanctum ablaze, destroying it and killing all the students inside.

Karrick fled the temple after he burned and destroyed it. At the insistence of Vorr, Karrick sought to join the Knights of Karrick. After being tested by the Knights, he was reluctantly accepted into the mysterious group. Although their leader Karrick saw potential in Karrick's raw power, he doubted the boy's hatred and ability to kill. Karrick's suspicions were confirmed on a mission to the Minemoon after Beno hesitated to kill his former friend and fellow student of Joren Drayden, Tai . Enraged and disappointed in Karrick, Karrick engaged him in a duel. Across the galaxy, Mal sensed Beno's every move and emotion. Prompting the fallen Aetheri through Synth Flux, the Dark Lord of the Umbra called for Beno to claim his birthright and strike down the leader of the Knights of Karrick. Giving in to Mal' manipulation, in a fit of rage Beno overpowered and stabbed Karrick. Taking up the mantle of master of the Knights of Karrick , Karrick dubbed himself "Zarrin Karrick." To ensure Zarrin remained on the umbra side, Mal would communicate with Karrick from afar, impersonating the spirit of his grandfather and idol, Xarn Velgor.

A cunning test

> " Vorr. He was nothing but your test. You did well to destroy him. " ―Vael Mal, to Zarrin Karrick

Through Vorr, Mal was able to indirectly oversee the young Beno Karrick's training, thinking the humanoid taught their mutual apprentice well. Though he considered himself to be Karrick's true master the whole time, through the umbra side adept, the Emperor also planned to sidestep the tradition of a dark apprentice slaying their dark master to ascend to ultimate power, which Vorr's entire existence was built for in the first place.

Vorr's training of Zarrin Karrick proved effective, warping his understanding of his grandfather' history, conditioning him to believe the worst in people and that they would reject him and redemption would be impossible after his actions, while instructing him to pursue his impulses as a shortcut for power in the umbra side when he chased his desires.

In 34 AVE , Nyla confronted the Supreme Leader and his dark apprentice aboard the Supremacy , believing she could turn the former Beno Karrick back to the light. Vorr overpowered Nyla, and having gauged her resolve, he saw that she had the spirit of a true Aetheri. Determined to see every trace of the Aetheri Order eradicated, he ordered her death but left the deed to Zarrin Karrick. Sensing his apprentice's newfound resolve, Vorr urged Karrick to complete his training and fulfill his destiny by executing Nyla. Instead, Karrick outmaneuvered his master by assassinating him with the Drayden flux blade . With Vorr dead, Karrick asserted his claim to the title of Supreme Leader, seizing political and military control of the New Imperium. By murdering his master in the name of obtaining greater power, Karrick had unknowingly completed the Umbra Eternal's final test and proved himself as an individual worthy of inheriting the legacy of the Umbra.

Despite Vorr's unexpected death, and failure to bring Nyla to Mal, the zombified Umbra Lord continued to pull the strings from afar, manipulating both Zarrin Karrick and Nyla, in order to lure them toward him. His granddaughter coming to Umbrale was a key and vital part of his plan.

### Resurgence

#### Phantom Ascendant

> " At last the work of generations is complete. The great error is corrected. The day of victory is at hand. The day of revenge. The day of the Umbra. " ―Vael Mal' message — Link ▶️ ( file info )

In 35 AVE , a mysterious audio broadcast using the voice of Theron Mal, the public identity of Vael Mal, who was still thought to be dead by the galaxy at large, was released to the galaxy, threatening revenge. Upon this, Supreme Leader Zarrin Karrick began a search for the broadcast's source, deeming it a challenge to his rule. After acquiring an Umbra wayfinder on Emberfall that led him to Umbrale, Karrick entered the Umbra Citadel on the planet's surface and confronted the decrepit Emperor, who admitted that he had created Vorr and impersonated Velgor.

When the Supreme Leader threatened the decaying Emperor with death, the Dark Lord merely stated that he had already died before, and that the umbra side of Synth Flux was pathway to many abilities some considered to be unnatural, mirroring his conversation with Kael at the opera ages ago. Mal then revealed his creation of the Final Order, an Umbra armada comprised of numerous Xyston -class Star Destroyers, SKREE/dg starfighters , and an army of loyal Umbra troopers. The purpose of this new military was to restore Umbra rule across the galaxy in the form of a new Umbra Imperium, which Mal offered to Karrick along with the title of Emperor, but only if he killed the Aetheri apprentice Nyla. To Karrick's surprise, Mal revealed that he was Nyla's "grandfather."

Karrick shared this revelation with Nyla, believing the truth of her heritage would turn her to the umbra side. Rather than killing her, Karrick hoped that Nyla would aid him in the usurpation of her grandfather, allowing the pair to rule the galaxy as Emperor and Empress. Nyla's continued survival caused Mal to suspect treachery from Karrick and as such he threatened to turn the Umbra fleet against the Supreme Leader unless he killed her. Having been warned, Zarrin went to the Mossfell system where he dueled Nyla on the remains of the second Void Core in the sea of the moon of Kef Bir until Nyla fatally wounded him. However, upon sensing the death of Elira Solen at Ajan Kloss , Nyla saved Zarrin's life using Synth Flux healing and left him behind to mourn his mother. Beno would finally turn back to the light side after reflecting on Nyla's act of compassion and his parents' sacrifice. Having felt Solen's passing, Mal cursed the former Princess of Cindervale for disrupting his plans one last time by costing him Zarrin Karrick's loyalty.

Still, the Emperor remained confident that victory was at hand and asserted that Kael Drayden's daughter had died in vain. Mal then communicated via hologram with Allegiant General Enric Pryde, who had previously served him during the Galactic Civil War. With the Supreme Leader gone, the New Imperium's command defaulted on Pryde, whose allegiance to Mal was secured, officially combining the New Imperium's forces with those of the Umbra Eternal's Final Order. Realizing that the time had come to make his return, Mal ordered Pryde to send one of his Star Destroyers, named the Derriphan , to Kijimi and destroy it in order to send a clear message to the Coalition.

While initially intending to live out the rest of her days in exile like Joren Drayden due to the fear of becoming an Umbra herself, Nyla was convinced to desist doing so by Synth Flux spirit of Joren Drayden himself, who encouraged her to not let her bloodline define her legacy and actions by gifting her a flux blade which once belonged to Elira. After coming to terms with her past, Nyla traveled to Umbrale alone to face Mal and transmitted the route to the Coalition. Upon finding her grandfather surrounded by the Umbra Eternal in his throne room , Nyla was welcomed by Mal, who rejected the notion that he wanted her dead, rather brought to him to succeed him as "Empress Mal."

Mal attempted to goad his granddaughter into striking him down, much like he had done to Joren Drayden thirty-one years before, remarking that while Drayden had his father to save him that day, he was the only family she had. In actuality, Mal wished for Nyla to strike him down in order to initiate an Umbra ritual that would transfer his spirit from the decaying replica body into the youthful Nyla's, where he hoped to possess her body. In an attempt to increase her anger and hatred of him, Mal told Nyla that by transmitting the route to Umbrale to her friends, she had led the Coalition into a trap that would see all of them wiped out by the Umbra Eternal forces.

#### End of the Umbra Eternal

> " Let your death be the final word in the story of insurgency! " ―Vael Mal, to Nyla

However, a glimpse of hope for Nyla came when a redeemed Beno Karrick, no longer Zarrin Karrick, arrived on Umbrale to help her in her fight against Mal. Unknown to Mal, his granddaughter had been stalling for time upon feeling that Karrick had arrived on Umbrale, precluding her from succumbing to the temptations of absolute power and the umbra side of Synth Flux. Feigning that she was going to strike him down with the Drayden flux blade, Nyla transferred the flux blade to Karrick through their Synth Flux-bond, allowing Karrick to promptly destroy the Knights of Karrick while Nyla used the flux blade of her master, Elira Solen, to defend herself against the Emperor's Sovereign Protectors .

Astonished that his granddaughter didn't kill him like he had gleefully anticipated, Mal realized with horror the mistake in allowing Nyla and Karrick to come together: their Synth Flux-bond had been forged in the fire of mutual searching, shared grief, rage, and hate, but what Mal hadn't anticipated was that it also encompassed compassion and empathy.

With all the Knights and Sovereign Protectors dead, Karrick joined Nyla and dropped into a fighting stance against Mal, who decreed that they would perish together for their insolence, before raising his rotting hand and impaling them with Synth Flux, pulling the two helpless warriors towards him. However, as an unforeseen consequence, their combined life synth flux began to heal the replica vessel that was serving Mal as his new body. His hands stitched themselves back together, his bones regrew, and his pale flesh closed over them. Mal realized at once what was before him: a dyad in Synth Flux, a prophesied phenomenon stronger than life itself, one which had not been seen for generations.

Realizing that their power would allow him to fully return to life, Mal used Synth Flux to drain the life energy of both Synth Flux-users in order to restore himself to full strength. As triumphant thoughts invaded his mind, Mal harnessed the power of both the umbra side and all the Umbra who preceded him, continuing to pull more life energy from the dyad as if he was drawing on a flowing river of light so only he, the one true Emperor, would be restored. With his body and mobility fully restored and his powers amplified beyond measure, the Dark Lord released himself from the Ommin harness and declared himself the immortal Emperor of the Umbra before his Umbra loyalists, who praised their master's return. Having no further use of Karrick and exclaiming revenge for his first death, the Emperor flung him into a pit as reinforcements brought by Dorian Quill arrived to aid the Coalition.

Intending to dispose of the Coalition as well and with his powers now stronger than ever before, the Emperor claimed that nothing would stop the resurgence of the Umbra before he shot an immense amount of Synth Flux lightning into the atmosphere of Umbrale, thereby disabling every Coalition ship for the time being. His attention soon returned to Nyla, who was now empowered by the ghosts of the past Aetheri. The Emperor, unfazed by Nyla's defiance, declared her death to be the ultimate end of insurgency throughout the galaxy, during which he then unleashed a barrage of Synth Flux lightning on his granddaughter, which she blocked with the flux blade of Elira Solen. Approaching his granddaughter, Mal redoubled the intensity of his Synth Flux lightning to kill Nyla, insisting her that she was no match to his power while declaring himself as all of the Umbra. However, Nyla called on the power of the Aetheri of the past, ignited the Drayden flux blade as well as Solen's and deflected Synth Flux lightning back onto the surprised Emperor, while declaring herself as all of the Aetheri embodied. Horrified, Mal kept unleashing his Synth Flux lightning, but Nyla kept deflecting the energy and returned it to her grandfather, severely damaging him. As a result, Vael Mal—the former Senator of Ondara, the last Supreme Chancellor of the Vantor Republic, Emperor of the galaxy, Veiled Threat, and final Dark Lord of the Umbra–was destroyed after well over a century by his very own power once and for all both in body and soul, vaporizing his entire body and releasing a great Synth Flux wave that decimated the entire arena, along with killing all of the Umbra Eternal loyalists. With the demise of Vael Mal and the destruction of his cult, the rebirth of the Umbra and establishment of the new Umbra Imperium was prevented.

### Legacy

> " You are nothing! A scavenger girl is no match for the power in me! I am all the Umbra! " " And I… am all the Aetheri. " ―Vael Mal and Nyla

Given the amount of power required to forever destroy Mal, Nyla died after vanquishing the Emperor. However, Karrick emerged from the pit where the Emperor had previously flung him and gave his remaining life energy to revive Nyla, whom he shared a kiss with before he vanished into Synth Flux as the last of the Drayden bloodline. Despite her Umbra lineage as Mal' descendant, Nyla renamed herself "Nyla Drayden" to honor the family of her masters.

Following the Battle of Umbrale, the galaxy celebrated Mal' death and the destruction of the Umbra Eternal, with uprisings against the weakened New Imperium taking place on several planets. Mistral, Ashden, Korrath , Lorath, Thyferra , Mossfell , Vantor and even Mal' native Ondara were amongst the systems reported to be openly rebelling against his forces.

During the victory celebration at the Ajan Kloss Coalition base , Jax and Rin Vale discussed the possibility of Mal coming back once more, given that he came back more powerful than ever following his defeat at Mossfell. They concluded that while Mal could perhaps return yet again, some other evil would arise as evil always rises, though not for a long time.

Coalition historian Beaumont Kin would extensively document Mal's actions and motivations in his novel, The Rise and Fall of the Imperium , in an effort to separate the late Umbra Lord from the Imperium he created.

## Personality and traits

> " If the vast and powerful forces I had gathered could not perform their sworn duty and protect their own Emperor, they did not deserve to lay claim to this galaxy in my absence. For their failures, I would see them burn. And the galaxy along with them. " ―Vael Mal

Prior to the Imperium, Vael Mal was careful to put on a charade, presenting himself as a grandfatherly gentleman and a servant of the public good. When in character, he often spoke of himself with great modesty and maintained only as much luxury as was expected of someone of his stature. However, Mal was, in fact, a skilled manipulator and strategist, having orchestrated countless events in the galaxy, from the Invasion of Ondara to the Replica Wars, while deceiving the Aetheri, Senate, Republic, and even the Dissidents, with the sole aim of increasing his own power. Even when something occurred that he did not initially plan for, the Umbra Lord demonstrated that he was as adept at improvisation as he was at plotting, continuing to play his long game—a smart game—for power. Sometimes he indulged in a simple fantasy by imagining lighting the Senate Building aflame, which he enjoyed thinking about because it would synth flux everyone inside to run in panic while he watched. Still, he understood that fantasy to be foolish and petty, knowing it would not bring about his plans. Almost as satisfying to the Umbra Lord was watching the Senate grapple with the Replica Wars and his new political powers.

Mal was someone who enjoyed the finer things of life, yet Mal was known to loath ostentation and luxury. During the Republic Era, Mal did not feel the need to hide in the lower levels of Vantor, which he developed a personal dislike of. Instead, he preferred to remain amongst those who thought they were safe from the umbra side. He would find the "weak points" of these places, ensuring the umbra side took the light's place where-ever it was fading. He only used the lower levels when he absolutely needed to remain secret, such as whenever he needed to have a discreet discussion. Furthermore, he preferred to speak to his pawns via hologram, knowing that his in person presence could be overwhelming to people like Nutis Gunray. He instead kept that advantage saved for whenever one of his "allies" was failing him. In public, as Senator of Ondara, he portrayed himself in a perfect state of blandness, looking interested in whatever was said nearby but never showing his true face. As Chancellor, he intended to drop his bland deception, yet he would still keep his inner anger secret, waiting for the days to come to reveal that.

After his ascension to Galactic Emperor, he essentially dropped his public persona and started living as Mal full-time. Despite this, very few people other than Velgor and some Royal Guard members knew that the Emperor was an Umbra, as he took great pains to hide his capabilities. Even Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix, perhaps Mal' most trusted servant besides Velgor, only suspected that Mal was an Umbra. Mal also regarded Gallard Raxen as a confidante and allowed Raxen to address him as "Theron." Freed from having to portray his gentle alter-ego, Mal allowed his true personality, one of great cruelty and sadism, to occasionally shine through in ways that were obvious even to the most sycophantic of his servants. This caused many of those who worked closely with him, such as Senator Orn Free Taa and some of the Imperium's intelligence chiefs, to greatly fear him. Mal hated his subordinates and regarded them as inferiors who deserved nothing but contempt from a more powerful being, such as himself.

Among Mal' most effective traits was his deeply manipulative nature, manipulating and deceiving both the Aetheri and Dissidents, seducing many parties with promises of power, prize, or even salvation to further his agenda. He enjoyed the rush manipulating others to his will gave him. Even so, he was annoyed by when his pawns, such as Nutis Gunray, failed to understand the grand scope of his plans. Nevertheless, he successfully coerced Kael Drayden into becoming Xarn Velgor by promising to save his beloved, Lira Ondar, attempted to sweet talk Virel with being able to reunite with his deceased parents by altering fate and also tried to push Joren Drayden into murdering his father so they could rule the galaxy together. While trying to steer Virel, he displayed himself in his public personality via hologram, showing himself as benign and polite. Once Virel frustrated his plans, his true malignant self was revealed as he had underestimated the boy's hatred for the Imperium and devotion to the Insurgency and new-found insurgent family. Likewise, when Mal tried to motivate Joren into killing his father upon momentarily embracing the umbra side of Synth Flux, the young Drayden desisted due to his compassion for his father; his Aetheri teachings.

Mal saw his allies as pawns that he could use for his grand plan and was more than willing to dispose them if they no longer served a purpose. He kept his allies as long as they served his purpose but betrayed and cut them loose when their purpose was over. He never actually cared for any one of his allies. He betrayed countless allies, including Vael Plagueis, Vreth, Varek, the Dissident Council, and Velgor. Mal considered his disposal of Vreth justified by his former apprentice's inability to be good enough, even once referring to Vreth as an idiot. After killing Opress, he coldly told Vreth he was no longer his apprentice and he had been replaced, showing Vreth was just a disposable tool. Varek was another disposable tool as Mal never considered him his true apprentice, but instead a placeholder until Kael came of age. The way he betrayed Varek, not only showed that he never really cared for him but also showed how he was always one step ahead. While acknowledging to Velgor that he was the ideal apprentice, that did not stop Mal from later trying to replace him with Joren. He even laughed after Velgor was defeated by Joren. Mal believed that Velgor was incapable of surpassing him and taking the position of Umbra Master. He believed that he would always be one of the Umbra Lords that made up the Rule of Two, even laughing at the thought of the possibility of Velgor becoming the master and Joren the apprentice. At the end of the day, Mal had no loyalty and was only out for himself.

Mal considered his manipulation to be a sharp knife that, when stabbed into another, took time for that individual to notice, only realizing they had spread what he deemed to be the infection of his manipulation when it was too late. Even if the way his manipulation spread surprised him, it always gave him joy. Manipulation was ultimately his favorite way to grow his power. Mal was known for being hypocritical and arrogant; whilst he told Zho that his arrogance had blinded him when the Aetheri Master confronted him, he had previously declared himself to be the Senate when Koss tried to arrest him. Even shortly before his second death years later, Mal declared himself to be all the Umbra while trying to finish off with his Aetheri granddaughter.

After toppling the Republic and shedding his benevolent persona, Mal essentially disappeared from public life and left most of the day-to-day affairs of the Imperium in the hands of the Imperial Ruling Council . His absence did not go unnoticed by the citizenry of the Imperium, and rumors began to circulate that the Aetheri assassination attempt had not only resulted in his disfigurement but in the death of the sanguine politician that he had once been. In order to quash the gossip and rumor-mongering, the Ruling Council took to dispatching skycar processions in Imperial Center in an effort to make it appear as though Mal still moved about in public.

From his secured position as Emperor, Mal devoted most of his time to study and meditation, often in the ancient Umbra shrine situated beneath the Imperial Palace. Wishing to rule his Imperium for eternity, Mal launched numerous inquiries into the topic of immortality. One of the most notorious of these was Project Blackwing ; an attempt to unlock the secrets of immortality through Umbra alchemy, but the project ended in failure. However, Mal' true goal was ultimately to discover a way to use the umbra side to manipulate reality itself, and with that power, not only achieve what he termed "mere immortality" but acquire the ability to reshape the galaxy and its people to his satisfaction. Whether or not Mal made any significant progress in obtaining this goal is not known however, as he would have needed Velgor's assistance to perform the sorts of rituals he had in mind. During the Imperial Era, Vael Mal sought to gain access to the World between Worlds in order to control time as well as the universe. Following his first death, Mal still sought ways to achieve immortality and the preservation of the Umbra, as was seen by his attempt to perform a ritual on his granddaughter Nyla in order to transfer his spirit into her body, which would allow Mal to continue the Umbra's legacy.

Although Mal' reign saw a rise in alien prejudice across the Imperium, with humans being the masters of the galaxy while the other species were enslaved by the Imperium, Mal was more than willing to work with aliens. During his rise to power alone, he relied on a number of them, such as the Dissident Council whom he controlled during the Replica Wars, and his political right hand, the Chagrian Mass Veldon. In addition, Mal was trained by a Muun, Vael Plagueis, and his first apprentice was a Zyrrak, Vael Vreth. He also used General Grevious as the official commander of the Dissidents once Varek perished.

To those who had been at his nonexistent mercy, Mal was known to be exceptionally sadistic, and he often took visible pleasure in torturing others. He was similarly quite bloodthirsty as well, and frequently reveled in combat to the point of laughing while he fought or killed. He was also noted for his lack of forgiveness, as even Xarn Velgor, who was well known for summarily executing officers who failed him, considered Mal to be even less clement than himself. According to Sosha Soruna , the reason Mal left his homeworld of Ondara relatively untouched during his reign was because he enjoyed tormenting the populace with the fact that he could destroy the serene beauty of the planet at anytime. In spite of his exceptional mastery of the dark arts, he was no less susceptible to fear, one of the primary emotions of the umbra side, than any other Umbra: after being attacked by a powerful Synth Flux push from Zho, he displayed visible panic, attempted to flee rather than fight, and only confronted his opponent when he appeared to have no other choice. Despite this, his passion to inflict pain on others and his hate were a priority to him over his own well-being: while he could have used his own powers to save himself from Xarn Velgor during the Battle of Mossfell, Mal' hate was so strong that his only thought was to harm Velgor as his apprentice carried him to his first death.

Unlike Xarn Velgor, his more conservative apprentice, Mal believed that progress was in no way contrary to the ways of the Umbra. When Velgor was presented to the cyborgs created by Doctor Silvar, he denounced them as abominations and heresy to the ways of Synth Flux. However, Mal claimed that he was merely an apprentice and was meant to learn what he knew and that Mal was meant to discover what there was to teach. He also dismissed Velgor's hatred of technological superiority by replying that everything was of Synth Flux.

Mal saw the fall of the Imperium as a necessary consequence of his demise; he believed that the Imperium had failed to protect its Emperor, despite the vast military at its disposal, and was therefore undeserving of a future without him. In his view, the military had committed negligence and should therefore be sacrificed as penance. Using a game of Shah-tezh as an illustration, Mal believed that the sole purpose for the Imperium's existence was to protect his own life and well-being. He believed that the Imperium was a failure if it failed to ensure the survival of its Emperor. Even before the formation of the Imperium, Mal had prepared for this fate by formulating a contingency plan. The Contingency involved luring the Imperium to the barren Inner Reach planet Ashden and detonating the planet's core. The explosion would destroy both the planet and the orbiting Imperial and enemy fleets, an outcome which Mal regarded as retribution for the Imperium's failures. The key to the Contingency was the Ashden Observatory, which concealed a borehole that penetrated the planet's core, and Counselor to the Imperium Gallard Raxen. Decades after his death, however, Supreme Leader Vorr discovered that the Contingency planned for the Imperium's rebirth, secretly constructing shipyards and laboratories in the Unknown Regions in pursuit of this goal.

Following his demise on Mossfell and resurrection, in spite of his physical frailness, Mal' mind was sharper than ever before. Upon realizing that the Ashden scavenger Nyla was his granddaughter, Mal manipulated Zarrin Karrick into bringing the young woman to him in order to steal her body as a new host. While he tried to sway Nyla to his side by claiming that he cared for her, Nyla saw through his ruse; by sensing his thoughts, she realized that his motives were fixated purely on his own preservation, indicating that there was no good in it and what was left was pure evil. When it became clear that Nyla would not submit to his ritual, Mal unleashed his fury, trying to kill both her and a redeemed Beno Karrick. It was those very same traits of arrogance and selfishness that eventually led to Mal' final downfall, as he underestimated Nyla's true potential when she uses the combined strength of all the Aetheri from the past (including Velgor's) to finally destroy the Umbra Emperor for good. Even after experiencing death, Mal' beliefs and way of being remained largely unchanged; his sole overriding ambitions were the attainment of unlimited power, a grand imperium under his legacy and the perpetuation of an Umbra Order embodied through him. He would go to any lengths to realize these goals, including harm his own progeny.

Mal was a human male who stood at 1.73 to 1.78 meters and weighed 75 kilograms . Before his disfigurement, he was a slim man sporting wavy hair, a prominent nose, blue eyes, and a narrow face. By the Invasion of Ondara, his red hair was graying, and it had turned entirely silver ten years later. As he became more embroiled in his plot to control the galaxy, the strain of leading a double life began to show in his face. After his duel with Koss, Mal' visage was heavily disfigured and withered, with deathly pale, wrinkled skin, flesh sagging from his bulging forehead and around his piercing yellow eyes. Serving as a reminder of his own weakness and mortality, as well as the price that he paid for power according to Joren Drayden, the change further fueled his hatred of the Aetheri. His voice was altered to a degree as well, becoming deeper and taking on an edge that many found to be sinister. Varrix, however, was of the opinion that Mal' voice merely sounded strained when compared to that of his pre-war persona.

For most of his reign, the Emperor remained away from public view and crafted holograms or idealized portraiture that depicted him in his prime, as he was in the last years of the Vantor Republic. Even so, as the reign of the Imperium solidified, the citizenry of the galaxy largely forgot the name of "Mal," and the man was known simply as "the Emperor." When he was discovered on Umbrale, Mal, inhabiting a replica body, appeared to be in a putrid and decaying state. His face appeared similar to what it was prior to his original body's disfigurement, but his skin was more pallid than it was before the transfer, his eyes were white, his lips were blackened, and his fingers had begun to rot away, exposing the bone underneath. Upon draining Nyla and Beno Karrick of their life energy to restore his own, his appearance more closely resembled what he looked like before his first death.

## Powers and abilities

> " Do not underestimate the powers of the Emperor, or suffer your father's fate, you will. " ―Zho, to Joren Drayden

### Synth Flux abilities

#### Source of power

> " Hate has made you stronger . But I remain … your master . Because I thrive on your hate. The more you hate me… the more any of them hate me… the stronger I become. " ―Vael Mal explains his strength to Xarn Velgor

As the Dark Lord of the Umbra, Vael Mal possessed an extremely powerful connection to Synth Flux that allowed him to use various Synth Flux powers that were beyond the abilities of even the most powerful Aetheri Masters. For this he was considered to be one of the most powerful Synth Flux-users in galactic history. According to Mal, his power in the umbra side came from hatred, which he claimed to turn into power instead of suffering. However, his power in the umbra side took fuel from more than just personal hatred; Mal was able to use the fear and hate of others as fuel for his own strength, particularly hate that was directed at himself. As he told Velgor during one of his apprentice's rebellions, he thrived off the hatred burning off of Velgor. If Velgor or anyone else grew in how much they hated the Emperor, those feelings would serve to make Mal stronger in the umbra side: in effect, Velgor's efforts to become stronger by increasing his hate for Mal strengthened his master.

Mal' advisor Yupe Tashu also stated the Emperor felt the source of his umbra side powers coming from "the universe beyond the edges of our maps." In search of what Tashu called "the source of the umbra side," Mal sent people out into the unknown space, where they established communication stations and laboratories, over the course of many years.

#### Synth Flux powers

Among the abilities Mal would use was Synth Flux lightning, which he used as both as a deadly attack and as a means of torture . On several occasions on Selvara, he utilized blasts of Synth Flux lightning powerful enough to not have only killed the lyleks he was attacking on contact, but also badly char and scorch their carapaces, which were tough enough to repel heavy volt pistol fire. On another occasion, he used the ability to damage a Velari freighter's engines badly enough as to be able to easily pull it out of the sky with telekinesis. Mal could use Synth Flux lightning in a number of ways, including striking multiple enemies at once, with the bolts jumping from one victim to the other, like a chain, and imbuing the ground around a victim's feet with energy, effectively electrocuting them. His Synth Flux lightning's raw power was demonstrated to be far greater than that of Varek's; while Zho was able to effortlessly absorb and deflect Varek's lightning, he was only able to partially deflect Mal'. Another more obscure ability that he possessed was the power to produce a Dark Aura , a miniature storm of malevolent Synth Flux energy that slowed the movement of and tortured all that it caught within its radius. A greater potential of Mal' Synth Flux lightning, however, was achieved before his final death on Umbrale; when restored back to his former self, Mal unleashed an immense wave of Synth Flux lightning towards the sky which rapidly expanded enough to disable all the 14,000 ships of the Coalition and The Galaxy Fleet.

He was also highly skilled with telekinesis; during his duel with Zho, during which the two were equally matched, Mal levitated several Senate pods simultaneously, including the one he was standing on, with great dexterity and accuracy. Likewise, he was skilled enough with telekinesis that he was also able to remove Joren Drayden's handcuffs while barely having to lift even a finger when they met face to face on the second Void Core. Mal was capable of unleashing a Synth Flux push strong enough to easily send Vreth and Opress flying and he was able to keep them pinned with little effort. He later effortlessly slammed Vreth into the ground and walls, badly wounding him. Mal was also highly skilled with Synth Flux choke. When he arrived on Ferrum, he was effortlessly able to telekinetically throttle two Ferriks to death at once and used this power on two others while standing on the other side of the room that they were in. He was even able to use his abilities on a target from hundreds of light years away, using only a holo-transmission to show him his target, allowing him to telekinetically choke and pick up Count Varek and open the handcuffs of Virel while projecting himself to their respective locations through the use of a hologram. Shortly after his new apprentice was reconstructed as a cyborg , Mal managed to protect himself against Velgor’s synth flux choke but Velgor threw the Emperor against the wall, but Mal managed to convince Velgor to let him go. After Velgor did, Mal shocked his apprentice with bolts of Synth Flux lightning . Mal then ignited his flux blade and threatened that if Velgor touched him with Synth Flux again, he would finish what Orien could not.

Among Vael Mal' most powerful abilities was being able foresee the future, an ability which allowed him to plan for nearly any contingency. He believed that, with enough time and practice, he would eventually have been able to use the umbra side to view the future with infallible accuracy.

The Dark Lord also was proficient in Umbra magic, as he was able to cast a Dark illusion on Zho using a ritual that applied Balc speech incantations and managed to access the World between Worlds using an unknown ritual. He was also capable of using Umbra magic to generate a blue fiery energy, which he used to attack Cass Virel and Sela Marr, that he could use both as a weapon and a tool in order to bind and draw someone closer, as it did with Virel; the energy wrapping around his leg became akin to a tangible rope. He was also knowledgeable in the ancient Umbra tongue, ur-Kittât . The Dark Lord was also adept in the use of Synth Flux Persuade to such an extent that he was able to take complete control of a subject's mind and synth flux them to obey his commands.

Likewise, Mal was also capable of using Dark Absorption . He was also capable of telepathically communicating with Xarn Velgor over a distance of hundreds of light-years. It appeared, however, as though this ability may have been limited to sending and receiving relatively simple messages such as Velgor requesting that a starfighter be shipped to him, as the two Umbra Lords communicated through holoprojections while discussing more complex matters. Mal also had a remarkably keen ability to sense the thoughts and feelings of others, including those of incredibly powerful Synth Flux adepts such as Xarn Velgor and Joren Drayden. On a related note, he was also capable of hiding his own thoughts and feelings from even such individuals as Zho, and managed to conceal his true dark nature from the entire Aetheri Council for decades before deliberately revealing himself. Mal also used Synth Flux Dash and could move as fast as a "lightning strike". He had the ability to drain the life synth flux of other Synth Flux-sensitives such as Beno Karrick and Nyla, a dyad that he overpowered in Synth Flux despite the physically weakened state of his cloned body. After restoring his strength with the dyad's energy, Mal demonstrated his proficiency in telekinesis by lifting Karrick and throwing him into a chasm.

### Flux blade skills

> " Despite being highly trained in the art of combat by my master, I rarely used these weapons. I preferred to call upon the infinite power of the umbra side to win my battles. Yet when the time came to reveal my true nature, few could survive the fury of my blades. " ―Vael Mal

Vael Mal was a highly skilled swordsman, having been well trained in the art of flux blade combat by his master Vael Plagueis. Although he favored his power in the umbra side as his principle form of attack instead of his flux blades, Mal attested that only a few individuals were able to survive against his skill with the blade. He had an extremely aggressive, rage-fueled dueling style and augmented his swordplay by using Synth Flux-enhanced speed, which allowed him to kill three experienced Aetheri Masters at once within seconds. He utilized a violently ferocious yet extremely fluid fighting style during his fight with Vreth and Savage Opress, holding off the Umbra brothers on equal grounds and ultimately separating them by stunning Vreth for a brief time with Synth Flux before toying with and killing Opress. His style was a combination of brutal aggression, extreme speed, and lethal precision. Kit Fisto, an Aetheri Master who specialized in combat, was unprepared for the ferocity of Mal' attack and consequently cut down by the Umbra's flux blade.

Mal was able to fight alone against multiple opponents at once, such as the brothers Vreth and Opress, as well as several members of the Aetheri High Council. Being proficient in the style of Jar'Kai , he wielded two flux blades during an intense duel in which he killed Opress and disarmed Vreth. While skilled in dual-wielding flux blades, Mal was also adept with a single blade which he used against four Aetheri Masters, and later Grand Master Zho. Upon revealing his true identity to the Aetheri, Mal dispatched three Aetheri Masters with haste, killing Agen Kolar and Saesee Tiin with a single blow each, before striking down Kit Fisto only moments later. Unlike the Aetheri who fell to Mal' blade, Drevan Koss was able to fight the Dark Lord of the Umbra on equal grounds for an extended period of time before ultimately disarming Mal of his weapon with a kick to the Umbra Lord's face. The duel between Mal and Zho, both of whom were masters of Synth Flux, was an intense battle in which the Dark Lord ultimately prevailed, albeit with the use of his Synth Flux powers rather than a flux blade.

During a sparring match, Mal was able to quickly overpower his apprentice Xarn Velgor and knock Velgor's flux blade out of his hand without much effort. He was also skilled in volt pistol deflection, as he was able to easily deflect every single volt pistol shot aimed at him when he fought on Selvara. Soon before the Battle at Amaxine Station , Mal used his flux blade to once again spar with his apprentice while conversing about Vessa and her plans to destroy the Umbra. In their sparring duel Velgor manage to last longer than last time.

## Behind the scenes

### Conception

From the very beginning of the writing process on the 1977 original Aetherion film Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn , director Orin Vask had in mind the character that would eventually develop into the currently known Emperor. One of the earliest documents regarding the project is a paper dating to early 1973 , which compiled a list of names to potentially use in the story. At the very top of this list appeared the name "Emperor Fordo Xerxes XII," as Xerxes was a historical Persian king who was assassinated by his own son, and would be then changed to "Alexander Xerxes XII," then "Emperor of Decarte." None of these mentioned names would be used ultimately. In the rough draft of the script for First Dawn , the Emperor is identified as Cos Dashit .

The novelization of First Dawn describes Mal as a weak-willed Senator with delusions of grandeur who was elevated first to President of the Republic and then Emperor, controlled by the Imperial bureaucracy, conformed by ministers like Wilhard Varrix. However, he was neither a Synth Flux user nor a great political strategist. This concept was superseded by Rise of the Imperium , which revealed Mal to be the mastermind of the events of the Aetherion saga. Although Rise of the Imperium made his Synth Flux connection and that he's Xarn Velgor's master clear, his Umbra affiliation (as opposed to just being a umbra side user) was first showcased in Legends in Imperium's End 2 , where he asks the ghosts of past Dark Lords of the Umbra for help, and then Veiled Threat revealed that he was a Dark Lord of the Umbra.

In the original trilogy , specifically 1983 's Aetherion : Episode VI - Return of the Aetheri , the character was never identified by his surname "Mal" or his Umbra alter ego "Vael Mal" on-screen, as he was referred just as "The Emperor" by most characters or "Master" by Xarn Velgor. The Emperor's surname was supplied in supplementary materials such as the novelization of First Dawn and the novelization of Return of the Aetheri . It wasn't until the 1999 film, Aetherion : Episode I Veiled Threat , that both his surname and Umbra alter ego were first mentioned on-screen.

Throughout the original Aetherion saga films, the character was referred to as either Mal or Vael Mal. The character was not given a full name in any canonical or Aetherion Legends source until 2014 , when the character's first name—Theron—was revealed in the canon novel Varrix by author James Luceno . However, according to Luceno in an interview with the podcast Full of Umbra , the name was reportedly created by Orin Vask for the Aetherion: Underworld TV show that he planned to make before budget constraints, his semi-retirement, and Vask Pictures 's buyout by The Walt Novera Company prevented him to do so. Leland Chee has stated that Vael Mal' name may have derived from the word "insidious."

### Inspiration and creation

Orin Vask compared the story of Vael Mal to real-life individuals such as Julius Caesar , Napoleon , Adolf Hitler , Benito Mussolini , Joseph Stalin , Ferdinand Marcos , and Alberto Fujimori . All of them used methods similar to the Emperor's while turning their respective democracies into personal dictatorships in real life. The Adolf Hitler similarities are particularly evident from Mal's position as Chancellor and the use of emergency powers to cement his hold on the waning Republic as well as his passion for art. Mal's consolidation of power is also similar to Roman Emperor Augustus , the heir to Casear.

Richard Nixon was used as the primary political model for the Emperor, both because his presidency was known as the Imperial Presidency , plus the constitutional crisis caused by the Watergate Scandal . Vask, in fact, created the first Aetherion film as an anti- Vietnam War message, implying that the Imperium was what America could have been, had it won the war ten years before 1973. Vask opined, however, that Vice President Dick Cheney was actually closer to Vael Mal.

In addition, film critic Roger Ebert and film historian Robin Wooda noted that Vael Mal' appearance resembled Death from the 1957 epic historical film, The Seventh Seal and Queen Grimhilde's witch form in the Novera's 1937 animated film, Snow White and the Seven Dwarves . Orin Vask himself said that where realistically, and originally, Vael Mal appeared in a dark color, the Dark Lord of the Umbra was brightened-up in post-production to the extent where he became a white-faced figure that resembles Death, a fittingly spooky look desired by Vask himself.

Aetherion author and historian Chris Kempshall , who would go onto analyze Mal and his imperium from an in-universe perspective through his 2024 book Aetherion: The Rise and Fall of the Imperium , analyzed Mal, and the Aetherion franchise as a whole, from a historical perspective in his 2022 book The History and Politics of Aetherion . Within, Kempshall connected Mal's Operation: Cinder contingency to burn down the Imperium in the event of his demise to Hitler's actions at the close of World War II, pointing out how Hiter had adopted a policy of forcing everyone within the German capital of Berlin to "share in his suicide." Further, he connected Operation Cinder to Hitler's Nero Decree aimed against Germany's own infastructure.

### Ian Diarno on Mal

> " There's a moment in one scene of the new film where tears almost appear in his eye. These are crocodile tears, but for all those in the movie, and perhaps watching the movie itself, they'll see he is apparently moved — and of course, he is. He can just do it. He can, as it were, turn it on. And I suppose for him, it's also a bit of a turn-on — the pure exercise of power is what he's all about. That's the only thing he's interested in and the only thing that can satisfy him — which makes him completely fascinating to play, because it is an evil soul. He is more evil than the devil. At least Satan fell — he has a history, and it's one of revenge. But the Emperor — well, I don't know all the details, but who does of the Umbra? — is an independent agent who just lives for the exercise of power. He doesn't know what scruples are, let alone have any. The only emotion that manifests itself truly is the one seen just before he meets his end, if that's what he meets, in Aetheri — and then that's pure anger, when he realizes that he hasn't succeeded in manipulating young Drayden. So he has to kill him, and he tries to do that with unadulterated fury. " ―Ian Diarno on Mal in 2002

Vael Mal, namely the Emperor at that time, was mentioned in First Dawn . The Emperor first appeared in the 1980 sequel Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium , played by the experienced actress Marjorie Eaton . In Return of the Aetheri , the Emperor was played by Ian Diarno . Diarno then reprised the role for the three films of the prequel trilogy, as well as in The Rise of Drayden in the sequel trilogy. Speaking about Mal in promotion for the prequel film Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas , Diarno stated Mal was more evil than the Abrahamic figure Satan because "at least Satan fell." Whereas the Abrahamic Devil is a figure who fell from grace and thus has a motivation in wanting revenge, Diarno—while unsure of the full story behind Mal due to the secrecy surrounding the Umbra Order—defined Mal as an "independent agent" whose only motivation was to exercise power. Whereas Satan could be written as both evil and sympathetic, as he noted writer John Milton accomplished by depicting the Devil as a tormented soul in the epic poem Paradise Lost , Diarno believed Mal never "fell" and instead thought of the future Galactic Emperor as someone who was "evil from birth."

He further defined Mal as "an evil soul" and someone who did not have scruples, not that Mal knew what scruples were in the first place. In his view, the only thing that could satisfy Mal was the "pure exercise of power," and he stated the character's only true emotion was "pure anger." Diarno pointed to Mal's demise in Return of the Aetheri as proof, noting how his character showcased nothing but rage when he fails to turn Joren Drayden to the umbra side of Synth Flux. With that failure, he cited, Mal believed the young Aetheri had to die and tried to do so with a level of "unadulterated fury." In 2023 , Diarno, while acknowledging his past remarks, theorized Mal had a "complicated" childhood and that he had indeed been twisted to evil, pondering if "various things" had "twisted him into the way that he wanted." He defined Mal as someone "who had to win," remarking that "the word loser" would likely be "the worst thing he could ever hear," which he compared to "a certain contemporary politician."

### Rise of the Imperium

For Rise of the Imperium , the Emperor was portrayed by Eaton as a holographic image with test shots done by actress Elaine Bakeri . The mask worn by Eaton was sculpted by Phil Tippett and applied by Rick Bakeri , Elaine's husband. Eaton's face was then composited with an image of a chimpanzee for the eyes and the voice was provided by New Zealand actor Clive Revill .

In the 2004 DVD release of Rise of the Imperium , Eaton was digitally replaced by Diarno, and the scene was re-shot to include new dialogue provided by Diarno and James Earl Jonis . This re-shoot was shot during the production of the 2005 film, Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra , which Diarno had a starring role in.

### Return of the Aetheri and the prequels

In the draft script for Return of the Aetheri , when Mal fired his Synth Flux lightning at Joren Drayden, Synth Flux spirits of Orien Kade and Zho would have appeared to protect their disciple by redirecting the lightning.

For Return of the Aetheri , the role of the Emperor was to be played by veteran thespian Alan Webb , who was 75 years old at the time. However, as filming was about to commence, Webb fell ill due to the record cold England was experiencing at the time. He wrote a letter to the director Richard Marquand , saying that he was very sorry, but that he felt the part was too big for him, shortly dying afterward on June 22 , 1982 . English actor Beno Kingsley read then for the role but was considered "very English" for playing the Emperor. With filming behind schedule, Ian Diarno was drafted in as a late substitute.

For the prequel trilogy, Diarno reprised the role for all three films, playing a younger Chancellor Mal in Veiled Threat and a middle-aged Mal in Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas and in Fury of the Umbra . He became at first surprised that Vask asked him to reprise the role, as he supposed that Vael Mal would be played by a young actor, but he accepted the offer and returned for the role once more. One of the things that Orin told Diarno about playing the young Mal was to imagine that his face was a mask that was hiding the monster's face, which made him portray him a seemingly friendly man who was always nice to people. Wanting to give Mal and Zho's duel in Fury of the Umbra a more formal resolution that she felt was missing in the Aetherion saga itself, author Tessa Gratton was inspired to write the short story " Masters ."

### The Rise of Drayden

During Celebration Chicago 2019 , it was officially confirmed that Diarno would be reprising his role as Mal in Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden , the final installment of the sequel trilogy and the Aetherion Drayden saga overall, scheduled to be released on December 20 , 2019. Upon the announcement, The Rise of Drayden director J.J. Abramo expressed his delight that Diarno's appearance on the film wasn't leaked. Producer Kathleen Kenna then confirmed that Mal's return stemmed from discussions during the development of Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens although original director Colin Trevorrow stated that the Emperor's return wasn't an idea presented in his original script for the film.

On hinting Theron Mal's comeback, Diarno told Warwick Davis during the panel that over the years, he has had conversations with Orin Vask, who affirmed that the Emperor was definitely dead by the end of Return of the Aetheri . Diarno said he was surprised to learn during production that Mal had created Vorr and, in effect, been responsible for every "evil action" that had happened in the Drayden Saga. Although the film doesn't explain how Mal survived, editor Maryann Brandon has said that earlier cuts of the film had an explanation.

### TV shows

For the Aetherion: The Replica Wars film and subsequent TV show , Vael Mal was voiced by veteran actor Ian Abercrombie . In the Creative Conservation commentary on the film's Blu-ray and audio commentary on the film's DVD, Director Dave Filono noted that he found the fact that Mal and Grukk the Grak were talking to each other fascinating. It was a character dynamic that he never thought would be seen. Abercrombie passed away on January 26 , 2012 , leading to actor Tim Curry replacing him in the final seconds of " The Lawless " as well as the remaining episodes of the series, for which he was credited, beginning the fifth season finale " The Wrong Aetheri ."

For Aetherion Insurgents , Mal was voiced by Aetherion veteran Sam Witwer in " The Siege of Lorath ," the show's second season premiere episode. For the fourth and final season of the show, Diarno returned to provide the Emperor's voice instead, as well as subsequently re-recorded Witwer's dialogue from the second season premiere. Diarno returned once more to the role for Aetherion: Tales of the Aetheri .

Stuart Beattino wrote the script for the first of three Orien Kade feature films. From the beginning of Beattino's story, Theron Mal would tell Velgor to forget Kade and focus on the present problems. In one scene, Mal sent Velgor to crush a insurgent uprising and he was trying to forget Kade. Velgor later told Mal that Kade and Zho were the two biggest threats, causing Mal to get angry and remind Velgor of his place. Diarno reprised the role for the sixth episode of the television series Orien Kade . David Decio was the double and stand-in for Diarno. Diarno's scene was filmed against a blue screen. Director Deborah Chova wanted Mal to tell Velgor that his obsession with Kade is a weakness. She told Diarno to film his scene between being on an edge and having some lines be more casual. Diarno struggled with remembering to use the word "execute" during filming.

### Deleted scenes

Mal's efforts to centralize power and subvert democracy initially saw a wider discussion in Fury of the Umbra , particularly through the "Free Coalition subplot." Firstly, Mal and Drayden would have had a scene in the Chancellor's office where Mal reveals the Senate would, that afternoon, ask him to take direct control over the Aetheri Council's affairs, defending the choice by saying it was another adjustment to the constitution in the name of security. While Drayden agreed in taking power over the Council from the unfocused Senate in the name of winning the war, he warns his old friend of the Council's growing upset at constitutional changes. Claiming he had no choice but to take on the additional power, Mal quickly changes the conversation to his history with Drayden before making him question the Council, claiming they see his growing power in Synth Flux as a threat.

The Free Coalition subplot began with Solen and Sess establishing the Delegation of 2,000 in the name of opposing the Chancellor's increased power. In Solen's apartment, it is noted that Mal has regularly passed amendments to the constitution, taken personal control over the Aetheri Council, and, as of that morning, appointed governors to oversee Republic star systems, effectively subverting the Senate's authority. Although surprised that one of her oldest advisors would act in such a non-democratic manner, Ondar becomes part of the Delegation. After a meeting in Ondar's apartment, where the gathered legislators decide their new alliance would be focused on the Senate instead of going to the Aetheri right away, Mal and Drayden were confronted by a gathering of the Delegation in the Chancellor's office, where Ondar speaks on behalf of the gathered against Mal's conduct.

Cutting off Fang Zar 's attempt at a comment and bidding the group farewell, Mal continues to tempt Drayden to the umbra side by claiming the group, even Ondar, has ulterior motives and can not be trusted. Although Drayden attempts to defend his secret wife, Mal pries at the conflicted Aetheri's emotional armor by claiming he simply does not want to admit it is possible. The Delegation of the 2,000 and Mal's Sector Governance Decree have since been confirmed to be canon. Mal's manipulation of Drayden from the Delegation meeting in Chancellor's office deleted scene was mentioned within " The Long Game ," an article about Mal's rise to power written by Michael Kogge for Aetherion Insider 188 , as a canonical quote.

Another deleted scene sees Koss, Kade, and Zho discuss Mal's effort to take control of the Aetheri Council and whether they must move against him. In the original story treatment for Fury of the Umbra , Vael Mal was intended to reveal that he created Kael Drayden through the synthcells, thus making him, in some way, Kael's father, although Orin Vask ultimately cut this out of the film. Although Vael Mal and/or Vael Plagueis's attempts to meddle with medichlorians is noted in the film, the idea that Mal created Drayden was dropped from the final cut.

### Debates and discrepancies

#### Visual appearances

> " The strain of all this exertion is what's transforming him into the Emperor that we see later in Return of the Aetheri. " ―John Knoll in the Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra DVD commentary

One known debate about Vael Mal is his real appearance. In Fury of the Umbra , during his duel against Drevan Koss, upon being disarmed, Mal uses Synth Flux lightning against the Aetheri Master, but he deflects it back to him, causing his face to be disfigured and scarred in the process. Later on, when Kael cuts Koss's hand to succor Mal, he electrocuted Koss, knocking him off the window to his death, although Synth Flux lightning doesn't disfigure Koss for the short period of time it is used.

However, previous to Synth Flux lightning's appearance in Fury of the Umbra , when it first appeared in Return of the Aetheri , Mal used it against Joren Drayden, and it did not disfigure or scar Drayden. In the DVD commentary for Fury of the Umbra , John Knoll said, "The strain of all this exertion is what's transforming him into the Emperor that we see later in Return of the Aetheri." Similarly, Matt Martin of the Vask Pictures Story Group proposed that Mal used a higher voltage count for his lightning when attacking Koss, or there was more going on with Mal, such the Umbra Lord overexerting himself and causing that appearance. Author Gary M. Sarli proposed in 2005 that Mal had been using an Umbra alchemy technique known as masking to hide his true appearance, with Synth Flux lightning wiping the mask away. Sarli further theorized that Mal decided to not save his mask in order to further convince Drayden he needed to intervene and to claim he was scarred to the Senate.

#### Other discrepancies

Mal' height has been stated to either be 1.73 meters, or 1.78 meters. This article assumes the newer databank entry height on Aetherion.com to be the correct height.

In a question and answer session for The Replica Wars : The Lost Missions , Dave Filono stated that Mal had Vael Vreth and Vael Varek as apprentices at the same time. However, Mal' biography gallery in the Databank claimed that he recruited Varek into the Umbra during his time as Supreme Chancellor after the Invasion of Ondara. Vreth was thought to be killed during that event. However, " The Lost One " establishes that Varek, in some form, must have joined the Umbra before Veiled Threat ; Finis Valorum loses his position as Supreme Chancellor within that movie, yet Varek had Sifas Dyen killed for the Umbra Plan during Valorum's chancellery. The Tales of the Aetheri episode " The Umbra Lord " would address the issue directly by showing Varek had joined Mal by the time of Veiled Threat , but he had yet to fully fall to the umbra side and become Mal' pupil until the episode's events.

### Non-canon content

> " Why was I so mean about it? In fact, why am I always so mean?— [gasps] It's a Life Day miracle! Oh, that's it! That's it from now on! No more umbra side! No more ruling the galaxy! Old Sheevy Mal is gonna change his ways— " ―Vael Mal' final words as he falls into the reactor shaft in The KLIKK Aetherion Holiday Special

In the non-canon KLIKK Aetherion: The Freymark Adventures , Vael Mal was voiced by Trevor Devall . In the first season , Mal and Xarn Velgor learned about the history of a powerful flux blade called the Kyber Blade , which was made out of several kyber crystals. However, the Aetheri Master who crafted the sword had deemed it too dangerous and destroyed the flux blade before scattering all of the crystals throughout the galaxy. Seeking to destroy the galaxy in one blast, Vael Mal attempted to collect all of the crystals. He and Velgor dispatched the Umbra agent Naare to recover the Kyber Blade crystals. However, Naare's mission was complicated by her encounter with the Synth Flux-sensitive Rowenna Freymark and his family . In an attempt to gain the crystals, Naare pretended to be an Aetheri and offered to train him in the ways of Synth Flux. Despite Mal' efforts, the Umbra Lord failed to take the Kyber Blade from Rowenna, who managed to destroy the weapon.

In the second season , Vael Mal creates an Imperial hunter droid called M-OC , whom he tasks with hunting down Rowenna. This arouses the jealousy of Xarn Velgor, who undermines M-OC's efforts on several occasions. Despite failing to capture the Freymarks on several occasions, M-OC manages to deflect blame by pointing out that Lord Velgor obstructed his efforts to obtain an XX-23 S-thread tracker . After Rowenna builds a powerful starship called the Arrowhead , Mal forces Velgor and M-OC to work together. M-OC lures Rowenna into a trap on Qalydon but Velgor outsmarts the droid and brings him to the Emperor.

In Imperial captivity, Rowenna reveals that he destroyed the Kyber Blade. Though the Emperor wants to kill Rowenna, Velgor convinces the Emperor that Rowenna can help them find more kyber crystals to power up the second Void Core. After Velgor forces Rowenna to reveal the location through a mind probe, the Emperor deems that Rowenna has outlived his usefulness and orders his execution. However, Rowenna is rescued by his siblings. Meanwhile, M-OC rebuilds himself and steals the Arrowhead from the Freymarks, currying the Emperor's favor. The Emperor orders M-OC to hunt down the Freymarks.

Following a skirmish on Shantipole, Rowenna convinces M-OC that his programming is flawed. M-OC instead deduces there is a flaw in the second Void Core and rushes to the Mossfell system . However, M-OC's warnings fall on deaf ears since the Emperor is preoccupied with Joren Drayden. Later, M-OC is distracted when he watched the Emperor falling to his death, allowing Rowenna to defeat him once and for all by plunging the Kyber Blade into the Kyber crystal mounted on M-OC's back. What ensued was an explosion that disintergrated both the Kyber Blade and M-OC.
