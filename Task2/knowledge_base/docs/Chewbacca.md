# Brevok
> " I saved him, at least that's what he says, the big fuzzy fool, but really, he saved me. I was on a bad path, and Brev, he put me straight. Saved my shanks more than once too. " ―Dax Karrick, on Brevok

Brevok , known affectionately to his friends as Brev , was a Vorak male warrior , smuggler , mechanic , pilot , and coalition fighter who fought in the Replica Wars , the Galactic Civil War , the Cold War , and the subsequent New Imperium-Coalition War . He hailed from the planet Thornwood where he was born during the High Republic Era and became a Vorak military leader. During the Replica Wars, he was captured by Trandoshan slavers and held captive on Wasskah , but he worked with a fellow captive, Aetheri Commander Sela Marr , to escape. He later commanded Vorak forces of the Kachirho Vorak Militia during the Battle of Thornwood alongside the Grand Army of the Republic , led by Aetheri General Zho . During the battle, one of the last ones of the war, Zho's replica troopers received Directive 66 from the Umbra Lord Vael Mal and, with the help of Brevok and his fellow Vorak Chieftain and General Tarfful , Zho escaped Thornwood and the destruction of the Aetheri Order .

In the years that followed, during the rule of the Imperium , Brevok was enslaved by the Imperium until he was rescued by Corporal Dax Karrick , becoming his co-pilot aboard the Corsair Wing during a raid on Cindral for Carmine Dawn with Tobias Karren and Vessa . Winning the Corsair from Landonis Balthazar Quill , they later worked in the employ of crime lord Grukk the Grak until Karrick was forced to dump a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Imperium, at which point a bounty was placed on their heads. Soon thereafter, Brevok and Karrick were hired by Aetheri Master Orien Kade to ferry him, Joren Drayden , Astra-9 , and Bolt-3 to Cindervale on a mission vital to the survival of the Alliance to Restore the Republic . After finding Cindervale was destroyed , the group was brought aboard the Void Core , where they helped Princess Elira Solen escape from captivity and return to the Insurgent base on Cinderfall 4 . Though Brevok and Karrick initially chose to leave Cinderfall 4 and not join the Insurgency, they ended up fighting in the Battle of Cinderfall and were instrumental in Drayden's destruction of the Void Core, a major victory for the Insurgency.

Throughout the war, Brevok and Karrick fought the Imperium in a number of engagements, including an assault on Cymoon 1 and the battle on Vrogas Vas . Along with Princess Solen and Astra-9, they escaped from Icehold on the planet Frostmark after the Imperium tracked the Free Coalition there. The group sought refuge with Quill on Aether Spire , but Quill betrayed them and turned them over to Xarn Velgor , the Emperor 's chief enforcer, after his city was threatened. Karrick was frozen in carbonite and Quill helped the others escape, but they were too late to save the frozen Karrick from being taken by Vex Vark to Grukk the Grak. Brevok and Quill set off to find Karrick and located him in Grukk's Palace on Dunemere , where Brevok and his friends saved Karrick and destroyed Grukk. Shortly thereafter, they fought in the Battle of Mossfell , where Brevok helped destroy the shield generator and allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station . Mal was killed for the first time aboard the battle station , delivering a crippling blow to the Imperium.

After Mossfell and the rise of the Concordat , Brevok and Karrick fought to liberate Thornwood from the Imperium. When the war finally came to a close, Brevok returned home to Thornwood with his family . After Karrick's son Beno , turned to the umbra side and destroyed Drayden's new generation of Aetheri, Brevok reunited with his old friend and the two spent many years as smugglers once more. They lost the Corsair Wing and ended up smuggling aboard a ship called the Eravana , but they eventually found the Corsair and, with it, stowaways named Nyla , Jax , and the droid Orio-6 . The droid had part of a map that led to the exiled Joren Drayden, so the group brought the map to the Coalition that opposed the New Imperium, which had risen from the ashes of the Imperium. Brevok soon fought in the assault to destroy Voidfang Base , a devastating New Imperium superweapon, a conflict in which Karrick lost his life at the hands of his son. After the base was destroyed, Brevok joined Nyla in traveling to the planet Tidehollow on the Corsair , where they found Joren Drayden. After Karrick's death, Brevok often served as the sole pilot of the Corsair , including piloting the ship to aid the Coalition during the Battle of Saltmark .

Roughly a year after the battle of Saltmark, Brevok was one of the last of the Coalition. Having learned of the mysterious return of Vael Mal, Brevok and the crew of the Corsair , now including Rin Vale, traveled to the planets Pasaana and Kijimi to find a clue to how to find where the Emperor was hiding , being reunited with his friend Quill in the process. The quest nearly ended in Brevok's apparent death, as Nyla, having been revealed to be the Emperor's granddaughter through his son, lashed out with dark power during a fight with Zarrin Karrick and destroyed a New Imperium transport that she had thought Brevok had been loaded onto, seemingly killing him.

However, Brevok survived and was rescued by the Coalition as they continued the quest. Nyla eventually found Umbrale during a failed self-exile attempt to protect the galaxy from her becoming an Umbra, and led the charge during the battle that ensued later on . Nyla finally killed her malevolent grandfather with the help of the returned Beno Karrick in the climax of the battle, while the Coalition, joined by the Citizens' Fleet , progressively overpowered the Umbra Eternal 's fleet , the Final Order , that Mal planned to unleash.

Having ultimately destroyed the Umbra once and for all, the Coalition reveled in their victory, but also mourned their losses, including Elira, who had died of natural causes shortly after reaching out to her son and helping him turn back towards the light.

## Biography

### Early life

> " Hey, you're the married one. Find me a human girl with Malla's brains and silky hair and maybe I'll consider it. " ―Dax Karrick, to Brevok

A Vorak male , Brevok, nicknamed "Brev," hailed from Thornwood , a peaceful planet where he was born in the city of Rwookrrorro , one hundred and eighty years before the Replica Wars to Attichitcuk and a female Vorak . during the High Republic Era . He was married to a female named Mallatobuck , and had a family to support, including a son named Lumpawaroo . During his life, he explored Thornwood, including the wildest reaches of its forests, from one pole to the other. His exploration hardened his footpads.

### The Replica Wars

#### Meeting Sela Marr

> " It's a Vorak! " ―Sela Marr on first seeing Brevok

At some point during the Replica Wars, a galaxy-spanning conflict between the Vantor Republic and the Dissidents , Brevok famously shielded his allies. Fighting with courage to defend both Thornwood and the Republic, he served as a combat engineer while other Vorak soldiers led the charge in battle. In 20 BVE , he was captured by a Trandoshan hunting guild led by Garnac ; taken to Island Four on Wasskah to be their prey, his transport was attacked by a trio of Aetheri apprentices—the Adept Sela Marr and the younglings Jinx and O-Mer , also prey of the Trandoshans.

Brevok escaped from the wreckage and followed the trio, with whom he communicated with thanks to Marr's understanding of Vorak tongue , to their hideout. Using his technical expertise and parts from the crashed transport, he constructed a transmitter to contact his people on his nearby homeworld; however, it seemingly failed and an impatient Jinx, supported by O-Mer, decided to lure down and hijack a hunting pod and take the base ship. As persuaded by Marr, Brevok joined in their endeavor and raided the Trandoshan base, only to be joined by a Vorak relief synth flux led by his old friend General Tarfful —who had received Brevok's transmission—as well as bounty hunters Sulie and Seripas . Once all the Trandoshans had been killed, Brevok and the Voraks accompanied the young Aetheri to the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor , where they parted ways.

#### Battle of Thornwood

During the last days of the war, Lieutenant Brevok was a major player in the Battle of Thornwood , overseeing the confrontation between the Grand Army of the Republic and the invading Dissident Droid Army alongside Tarfful and Aetheri General Zho , as well as Aetheri Master Luminara Undula . Following the implementation of Directive 66 , Brevok and Tarfful witnessed the assassination attempt on Zho by Commander Grev and his aide; he helped the Aetheri Grand Master elude the replica troopers and escape from the planet in a Vorak escape pod .

### Rise of the Imperium

#### Capture

Following the reorganization of the Republic into the Imperium , Brevok saw his people sent to work as slaves , or as miners in places such as Cindral , or made to serve as science experiments. A year into the Imperium's reign , former Adept Sela Marr, the very same Synth Flux-sensitive Brevok had met on Wasskah, pondered what had happened to him after reading updates on the state of Thornwood, starting to lose her hope that he remained free.

At some point, while on the run from Imperial forces, Brevok and several other Voraks were captured and enslaved by several Trandoshan slavers. Like them, he was forced into slavery, working to cut the wroshyr trees in order to feed the Imperial Army . Brevok temporarily escaped but was turned over to the Imperium by a back-stabbing and greedy Kowakian monkey-lizard bounty hunter. Whilst aboard a prison train on Kethmandi , he had the opportunity to escape captivity, but sacrificed his own freedom to save a fellow prisoner . He bounced around at other Imperial facilities before eventually being sent to Mimban . Whilst on Mimban, he came to be referred to as "the Beast." Brevok also learned that the Imperials were trying to sell him off as a pit fighter.

#### Secret battle of pretend

> " What's your name, anyway? " « Brevok, son of Attichitcuk. » " All right. Well, you're gonna need a nickname, 'cause I ain't saying that every time. " ―Dax Karrick and Brevok

In 10 BVE , after Dax Karrick was denounced as a deserter by Tobias Karren , a criminal impersonating an Imperial officer, his commanding officer decided to execute him by giving him to "the beast." While imprisoned in an underground holding cell, Dax used his knowledge of Vorak tongue to convince Brevok to stage a fight with him after realizing that one of the supports in the cell was weak, and that destroying it would allow them to escape imprisonment . Following their escape, the pair managed to join a reluctant gang . Brevok, grateful for Karrick's intervention, proclaimed a life debt to the outlaw. Brevok thereby became Karrick's lifelong friend.

#### Meeting Dorian Quill

> " Oh, come on, Val. He looks so sincere. Plus, have you ever tried to disinvite a Vorak to anything? Not a good idea. " ―Rio Durant, in response to Val's distrust of Dax Karrick and Brevok

Brevok and Dax began their outlaw lives working as part of pirate Tobias Karren's crew . The group were hired by Carmine Dawn to steal a train car of refined coaxium on Vandor . However, they failed due to the intervention of Enfys Nest and the Cloud-Riders , and Karrick's decision to drop the cargo. Karrick and Brevok promised Dryden Vossa of Carmine Dawn an equivalent shipment that they would obtain unrefined by making the notorious Cindral Run . Vossa sent Vessa , who had become his top lieutenant, with them.

Knowing they would need a fast ship to transport the coaxium before it degraded and exploded, Vessa introduced the pair to Dorian Quill . Karrick played Quill in a game of " Korrathi Spike " sabacc , with the stakes being Quill's YT-1300 light freighter , the Corsair Wing . Karrick lost the game, regardless, Quill agreed to personally pilot the Corsair for the job in return for a cut of the profits.

#### The Cindral Run

> "Since when do you know how to fly?" [Brevok growls] "190 years old?" [Brevok growls] "You look great!" ―Dax Karrick and Brevok

Brevok, Karrick, Vessa, Quill, and Karren stole the unrefined coaxium from Cindral during a slave riot instigated by Quill's navigational droid, L3-37 , which included several wookies enslaved in the mines whom Brev helped free from their abusive handlers. During the escape, Karrick flew the Corsair , with Brevok as his copilot, for the first of many times. Because the crew stole the coaxium fuel as unrefined, it was necessary to make the escape from Cindral to the refinery on Savareen in record time, prompting Karrick to plot a shorter, and much more dangerous course through the maelstrom that surrounded Cindral and near a gravity well called the Maw , reducing the typical distance required from 20 to 12 parsecs.

#### Savareen

> " Big stuff, you're coming with me. " ―Tobias Karren commandeers Brevok

Upon reaching Savareen and refining the coaxium, the crew was confronted by Enfys Nest, who explained to them that the Cloud-Riders were freedom fighters stirred to action by the atrocities committed by Carmine Dawn and other crime syndicates, not pirates. Karrick devised a plan to give the coaxium to the Cloud-Riders and give Vossa fake coaxium, but Vossa was prepared for their betrayal because of information given to him by his double-agent in the crew, Karren.

However, Karrick had double-crossed Karren and Vossa: the containers he left with the Cloud-Raiders were empty, and Nest dispatched the agents Vossa sent to retrieve it. The coaxium Karrick, Vessa, and Brevok brought to Vossa was, in fact, genuine. Karren managed to escape with the coaxium and Brevok as a hostage. After Vessa betrayed and killed Vossa, Karrick found and killed Karren, but could only watch as he and Brevok were left behind, while Vessa left Savareen in Vossa' yacht to meet Carmine Dawn leader Vreth . Karrick and Brevok entrusted the coaxium to Nest, who intended to use it to form a insurgency against the Imperium.

#### The Corsair

Karrick and Brevok tracked down Quill to Numidian Prime . Karrick swiped the spare cards Lando held up his sleeve, and challenged him to a rematch of sabacc for the Corsair , a game which Karrick won fairly. The Corsair instantly became Karrick's most prized possession. With their new-found ship and confidence, Karrick and Brevok began engaging in much more dangerous and risky criminal operations, eventually becoming smugglers. Around the same time, Brevok likewise hung a pair of chance cube dice above the freighter's piloting console.

With the Corsair as their ship, the two smugglers took on many jobs over the ensuring years. They also came to meet the Tonnika sisters , though their relationship with the siblings soured over the course of their work together, and fellow smuggler Dash Rendar . At some point, Brevok also told Tivoche Bilure about what happened to Thornwood after the Replica Wars.

#### The Cordaxian Sea

> " We light enough now , fuzzball?! " ―Dax to Brevok, after the latter dumps Frax out of the Corsair

At one point, Dax Karrick and Brevok were transporting two tons of Cordaxian ore on Odona , with the help of Frax , a Kajain'sa'Niktal . They were being pursued by the Imperium, and Frax, who was worried about being put in an Imperial prison, pulled a volt pistol on Dax. Brevok did a barrel roll with the Corsair Wing , knocking Frax out of the ship entirely. They successfully escaped the Imperium and Odona, but left Frax behind.

#### Bounty hunted

Dropping Grukk's cargo

Karrick and Brevok were later smuggling some Imperial cargo for Grukk the Grak when they were stopped by an Imperial blockade . Several SKREE fighters gave chase and they were faced with a choice: surrender, or drop the Imperial cargo. Karrick chose to drop the cargo, and the cargo was dropped right in the path of the pursuing SKREE fighters, destroying both. Their pursuit thrown off, Karrick and Brevok flew down to the nearby planet , the Corsair heavily damaged.

Making repairs

Once landing, they began making repairs, until they found out that the planet was inhabited by the crime lord Nodo . Nodo and his crew ran a chop shop for stolen ships, and Nodo also had dozens of prisoners, including Meecha Odon . Karrick pretended to be Grukk, and convinced Nodo to have his crew fix the Corsair . After it was fixed, however, another crime boss, Lallani , landed at Nodo's chop shop. Lallani had heard Grukk was there, and wanted to capture him.

Freeing the prisoners

A battle broke loose, and in the chaos, Karrick and Brevok freed Odon and the other prisoners. They went back to the Corsair , and on the way they found several barrels of rhydonium , enough to replace the cargo they'd lost. However, Brevok convinced Karrick that Odon and the prisoners needed the fuel more, and Karrick relented. They gave up the fuel they desperately needed, so that Odon could use the fuel to pay for her and the other prisoners' way home.

Versus the Mist Hunter

In 9 BVE , as they were unloading the rhydonium from the Corsair at Valtos space port , Zuckusso and 4-VOX attacked them. They revealed that Dax and Brevok had a bounty on their head, put there by Nodo himself. Zuckusso managed to put Dax into the Mist Hunter 's hold, but Brevok attacked Zuckusso in order to rescue Karrick and Berisa Fordo , Zuckusso's other prisoner, from the bounty hunters. He placed Berisa into a barrel, despite his protests before he and Karrick took a speeder back to the Corsair . However, Zuckusso and 4-VOX caught up with, but Brevok and Karrick were able to use several barrels to knock them down. As they flew Corsair away from Valtos , Brevok and Karrick ejected Berisa' barrel into space with a homing beacon to notify his family. However, they were pursued by Zuckusso, 4-VOX, and their ship, the Mist Hunter .

Brevok manned one of the Corsair ' s laser cannons to fight off the Mist Hunter . Eventually they crash-landed in a ocean on a planet in Wild Space and they were forced to abandon the Corsair to make it to shore. After the Mist Hunter crashed, Brevok and Dax then Zuckusso behind. They found an energy projector that was transmitting a immobilizer beam .

Working together

Karrick and Brevok climbed the projector, but were confronted by Zuckusso who tossed spears at them. When Brevok go to the top, he was able to turn off the beam by destroying it's power source. However, small droids were activated and wanted to destroy them and all organic life in the galaxy.

The two opposing sides decided that it would be best to work together to escape the droids, and they escaped to the Corsair. Dax pretended that the Corsair lost power again, and tricked Zuckusso and 4-VOX into fleeing the Corsair , jumping into the water below. Karrick and Brevok left the planet, leaving the two bounty hunters behind.

#### Mission to Ushruu

False pretenses

At one point, while on Vantor after a smuggling job, Brevok and Karrick were approached by Alinka Aloo , the daughter of Sim Aloo . Aloo, wanted to hire them for a job, and requested that they go to her tower . A week later, they went to see her, and she told them that she had a tooka -sitting job for Brevok, and Karrick was to remain with her. They reluctantly accepted, and Brevok went on a cargo ship with the librarian Mayvlin Trillick . But the ship, on autopilot , pulled them into hyperspace . During the unwilling flight, Brevok and Trillick were told by Aloo that the real job was to steal an unknown artifact from a temple on the planet Ushruu . Aloo revealed that she had had taken Karrick prisoner, and would not release him unless the job was completed. With no choice but to continue, Brevok continued the job, along with Trillick. Before the ship reached Ushruu, the two bonded, as Trillick told Brevok about how her planet had been ravaged by the Imperium, and she was also being blackmailed by Aloo, who had one of her peoples' sacred texts. The two dealt with the tookas and the cargo ship's cargo droid, K-2SB, who was actually Q-2VO on an undercover mission for Kassian Andris . Eventually, the ship pulled out of hyperspace.

Acquiring the artifact

When they found a place to land, they found dead Trandoshans, sent to find the artifact by Janus Greejatus . Trillick and Brevok proceeded to travel towards the location of the temple, bringing Q-2VO along. Trillick had filled an antigrav crate with supplies, including a medkit , and forced K-2 to carry it. The group faced several Korrathi hounds , which they accidentally trapped within their cargo ship. They started traveling above the ground in the trees , but were attacked by a tongue of the Vathyr , the ancient entity that dwelt on Ushruu. It dragged Trillick to the Vathyr itself, but Brevok managed to free her. Eventually, they reached the temple, and searched it. They found the artifact, which was an ancient book of umbra side teachings, in the temple's library. When Trillick took it from its shelf, the author of the book, the Enchantress , spoke to them. The Enchantress's voice asked who the book was for, and seemed satisfied when she was told it was for Theron Mal .

Saving Dax

Q-2VO stored the book in his chest compartment as they traveled back to the cargo ship. But when they reached the ship, Andris had arrived. Kassian and K-2 flew away with the book, which Brevok and Trillick needed to rescue Karrick and the ancient text. Brevok came up with a plan, and he and Trillick flew back to Vantor. They arrived at Alinka's tower, and set free the Korrathi hounds that were trapped in the ship's cargo hold free. In the chaos that ensued, Karrick escaped, and the trio flew away in the cargo ship. They went back to the Corsair , and fought many SKREE fighters to escape Vantor. Karrick reluctantly agreed to take Trillick back to her home planet of Oktaro .

### Galactic Civil War

#### Void Core rescue

> « It's okay to care about them. They need our help. » ―Brevok, to Dax Karrick

During one of their smuggling attempts at the time of the Galactic Civil War, the Corsair Wing was boarded and the two lost the cargo that they were hauling for their usual employer, Grukk the Grak. Arriving at Port Kess on Dunemere to speak with Grukk about the lost cargo, they sat down in Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina . At the bar, Brevok was approached by Beno Kade , who was searching for passage for himself, Joren Drayden and the droids Bolt-3 and Astra-9 to Cindervale . Promised 2,000 credits in advance and an additional 15,000 upon arrival to Cindervale, Karrick and Brevok agreed to the commission.

In Docking Bay 94 , Karrick and Brevok found Grukk waiting for them with a crew of hired help. One of them, the famous bounty hunter Vex Vark , kept an eye on Brevok, noting the Vorak getting ready for a fight as Karrick spoke with Grukk. Thus, he moved his arm enough to ensure the Vorak braids he wore were visible to Brevok, which the Vorak decided to show no reaction to. Ultimately, Karrick managed to convince Grukk to give him more time. After a brief firefight with shock troopers, the Corsair left Dunemere with its new passengers, escaping from Imperial vessels before entering hyperspace.

During the hyperspace journey, while Kade trained Drayden in the use of Synth Flux , Brevok played a game of dejarik with Bolt-3, who made a move that Brevok disliked. Astra-9 advised that the move was technically fair, and even chided Brevok, at which point Karrick advised them not to anger the Vorak because of his species' tendency to pull arms out of their sockets, prompting the two droids to surrender.

Upon the Corsair ' s exit from hyperspace, the group found themselves in a meteor storm produced by the destruction of the planet by the Imperial Void Core superweapon . The group were soon caught by the grapple beam of the battle station which was still looming in the system, but managed to evade capture by hiding in the Corsair ' s smuggling compartments. While Kade set out to disable the station's grapple beam in order to allow them to escape, Drayden was told by Bolt-3 that Princess Elira Solen was being held in Detention Block AA-23 , her execution scheduled. They set out to rescue her, with Brevok posing as a prisoner of the shock trooper -disguised Drayden and Karrick. Despite encountering trouble, Brevok and the others managed to escape from the Void Core—except for Kade, who had died in a duel with Xarn Velgor . The Corsair soon arrived at Cinderfall 4 , landing on Insurgency's secret base , just as the Void Core closed in on the moon .

#### Cinderfall 4

> " What're you looking at? I know what I'm doing. " ―Dax Karrick to Brevok

Drayden was hopeful that Brevok and Karrick would join the Insurgency, but Karrick was more concerned with receiving his payment in order to pay his debt to Grukk. Though Brevok growled in dissatisfaction at this course of action, the two departed, seemingly leaving the Free Coalition on its own to deal with the Void Core. At Brevok's urging, with Karrick also feeling that he needed to help Drayden, they would return just in time to protect Drayden during the Battle of Cinderfall from Velgor's SKREE Advanced x1 , allowing the young pilot to destroy the massive battle station by firing proton torpedo s into its exhaust port.

Back on Cinderfall 4, Joren Drayden and Dax Karrick were both awarded medals by Solen, while Brevok howled in delight during the ceremony . The time for celebration was soon over, for Solen asked the Insurgents to take a moment of silence to honor and remember all those who had died when Cindervale was destroyed, whereas General Dodonna announced their need to evacuate Cinderfall since the Imperium was aware of their location. Brevok would be also awarded a medal, and shared a moment of banter between Solen and Karrick, just before they parted ways.

#### Cyrkon

> " You two play fast and loose. " " It's worked so far. " ―Caluan Ematt, on Brevok and Dax Karrick's tactics

However, before they could depart for Dunemere to pay Grukk, Solen would ask Karrick to run an errand for the Insurgency, which Brevok convinced him to accept—to rescue Lieutenant Caluan Ematt from Cyrkon . On the Corsair , the two made their way to the planet, only to find themselves confronted by the Vehement , which they avoided with a false IFF transponder , and landed in Motok . There, they were immediately attacked by some of Grukk's bounty hunters, including Captivator , which prompted Karrick to accuse them of being "Insurgents" and set them against IVB Commander Alecia Beck 's garrison.

Brevok slipped away with Karrick to the Miss Fortune cantina , where they found their old friend Delia Leighton , to whom they asked about Ematt's location. After she told them their destination and the password, Brevok and Karrick rented a V-40 and went into a hotel , where they met Ematt, but were attacked by the bounty hunters again, as Leighton had revealed their location to save Curtis 's life. However, they escaped and made their way to the Corsair , only to be ambushed by Beck and more than forty shock troopers.

Forced to surrender, Brevok, Karrick and Ematt were taken into custody, with Beck focusing on Ematt and leaving Brevok and Karrick to the shock troopers. Unbeknownst to the Imperials, Karrick noticed the Miss Fortune making their way to save them, but with the magnetic field activated, it would be pointless. Once aware of Karrick's intentions, Brevok pretended to be angry at Karrick and shoved him, sending him flying into a trooper to grab his E-11 medium volt rifle and destroy the field generators, flooding the hangar with Cyrkon's toxic gases. Soon enough, the Miss Fortune opened fire at the Imperials, and Brevok made his way to the Corsair to start the engines while Karrick helped Ematt and recovered their weapons.

Soon in Cyrkon's orbit along with the Miss Fortune , they found themselves against the Vehement and eight SKREE fighters. They engaged the starfighters , defeating several of them, until the Miss Fortune ' s took a hit that damaged its navicomputer; Brevok saw Karrick's nobler side when he decided to cover the 1550-LEX space yacht to make a run for Cyrkon's atmosphere just as the Vehement ' s grapple beam advanced towards them. They resisted the beam by entering the planet's atmosphere themselves, while Leighton fled, and approached Motok in a dead-engine glide, daring Beck into catching the Corsair at the price of destroying the city with the grapple beam and turning Cyrkon against the Imperium. As she desisted, the Corsair began its fall to the ground and the two pilots manually restarted the engines, just in time to avoid crashing into the city and escape.

#### Andelm IV

Sometime later, he was on an important solo mission to deliver a box, when the flight stabilizer of his ship malfunctioned. Forced to crash-land on Andelm IV , Brevok went to a city to obtain a replacement and engaged a Besalisk vendor. Their deal was interrupted by a fugitive young girl, Zarro , who tried to sell her stolen speeder bike . The Besalisk, however, refused and tried to betray her to the planet's crime lord, Jaum . Brevok stopped him, though, by having the Besalisk sell him the stabilizer. However, he did not have enough credits to buy it. Subsequently, he went to a cantina and played sabacc, successfully doubling his credits. Brevok then noticed the girl from before, and helped her hide from Jaum's men.

Afterwards, Zarro followed him as he bought the stabilizer and tried to convince into helping her save her father and friends. Unable to delay his mission, Brevok repaired his ship, and changed his mind to leave the planet when Zarro mentioned her people had been enslaved by Jaum. Unknown to Brevok and Zarro, Jaum was planning to export the Andelm beetle larvae to the Imperium to be processed into Dedlanite , a substance used in the manufacture of volt pistols . To maximize profits, Jaum also planned to sell his slave workforce to the Imperium.

The following day, Zarro devised a plan to infiltrate Jaum's mine . She got Brevok to search for an open air vent for the slave miners to escape. Meanwhile, Zarro infiltrated the mine by allowing herself to be recaptured. Her plan was to locate a hollowed part of the mine. Keeping in touch by transmitter, Zarro told Brevok to find an open air vent. While struggling through the tunnel, the Vorak was haunted by memories of his enslavement at the hands of the Trandoshans. Zarro's escape plan encountered a problem when she was caught by Jaum and his men. After the miners including Zarro's father Arrax objected to Jaum's plan, the gangster ordered his men to execute the miners.

Before Jaum's guards could carry out their execution, Brevok snuck up behind them and overpowered them with his bowcaster . After meeting Zarro's father, Brevok joined forces with the miners to fight their way out of the mine. He refashioned a rod from a broken-down GNK-series power droid and used it to disable a wheeled battle droid . Shortly later, the Vorak knocked out one of Jaum's henchmen with the derelict GNK power droid. Brevok eventually found his way to the mine's loading bay, which was full of crates of Andelm beetle larvae. Dax was joined by a team of insurgents consisting of the Concordat starfighter pilot Nevra Wexlin , her son Temmin Wexlin , his battle droid Mister Bones , the former Imperial loyalty officer Sinjir Rath Velus , the Zyrrak bounty hunter Jas Emari , and the Concordat soldier Jom Barell . Karrick managed to free Brevok and a hundred other prisoners including Nevra's husband and Temmin's father Brentin Lore Wexlin . Following an emotional reunion, Brevok stayed behind with Karrick, Sinjir, Jas, and Jom while Nevra, Temmin, and Mister Bones returned with the other liberated prisoners to the Concordat capital of Serenne .

Liberation of Thornwood

For the next month, Dax and Brevok organized a campaign to hunt down Grand Moff Lozen Tolruck , the governor of Thornwood. Lozen had fitted all of the Voraks living on Thornwood with inhibitor chips which he controlled using a control module that he kept on his person. In order to defeat Grand Moff Tolruck, Brevok and his insurgent allies devoted their time to finding Tolruck's island fortress . Jas also recruited more Vorak exiles and smugglers for their planned uprising. During this time, Jom was captured and imprisoned by Tolruck.

While Brevok, Dax, and the rest of the team infiltrated Camp Sardo , Sinjir infiltrated Tolruck's palace disguised as an Imperial officer. Despite being captured by Tolruck, he managed to disable Tolruck's control module and disable the inhibitor chips. This set off a mass Vorak uprising all over Thornwood. Meanwhile, Brevok and his team fought off a Low-altitude Imperial transport while Jas destroyed Camp Sardo's deflector shield generator. With the aid of Dax's insurgent forces, the Voraks managed to overwhelm the meager Imperial garrison on Thornwood.

After Grand Moff Tolruck summoned the three orbiting Imperial Star Destroyers to bombard Thornwood, Brevok and his fellow insurgents helped usher the liberated Voraks into underground shelters. In response, Brevok along with Dax, Sinjir, and Jom Barell traveled aboard the Iron Cruiser Dominion . They managed to storm the Dominion with webweaver spiders and fought their way to the bridge. However, the insurgents were captured by shock troopers and hauled before Vice Admiral Domm Korgale .

Before Korgale could deal with the prisoners, Concordat reinforcements arrived in the form of Princess Elira aboard the Corsair Wing , Captain Torb Rask and his Phantom Squadron , and Fleet Admiral Vorian's flagship Home One . Brevok and his allies managed to break free from their captors and seize control of the Dominion . They then used the Dominion ' s cannons to destroy the nearby Vitiator . As a result, the remaining Imperial Iron Cruiser Neutralizer capitulated to the Concordat. Following the liberation of Thornwood, Brevok stayed behind to reunite with his family at the insistence of Dax Karrick. Karrick then left on the Corsair Wing to look after his wife and his unborn son Beno Karrick.

Reunion with Waroo

### Cold war

#### Recovering the Corsair Wing

> " Brev, we're home. " ―Dax Karrick upon entering the Corsair Wing with Brevok after recapturing the freighter

In 31 AVE the Corsair Wing was stolen from Dax and Brevok and they were using a Baleen -class heavy freighter , the Eravana , for their smuggling operations. They eventually recovered the Corsair using their freighter's grapple beam after it was found floating in space not far from the planet Ashden . Upon boarding their old vessel, Dax and Brevok went about inspecting their ship and found the Ashden scavenger Nyla and the defecting shock trooper Jax hiding, whom Dax assumed were the thieves that had stolen the Corsair . When Nyla and Jax explained that they were going to take a droid named Orio-6 to the Coalition as he had a map that would reveal Joren's location, Dax and Brevok agreed to help them.

Unfortunately, their freighter was boarded by two notorious criminal factions, the Guavian Death Gang and Kanjiklub , who had hired Karrick on previous jobs, which he had failed to complete. Not wanting Nyla and Jax to be involved, Dax placed both of them in the lower corridors of the freighter while he and Brevok went to meet with the criminals, with Orio-6 accompanying them. The smuggler tried but failed to convince both parties that he would fix things eventually, with the Guavian Death Gang negotiator, Bala-Tik , recognizing the BB-series astromech droid from a New Imperium bounty placed on it. They demanded that Dax hand over the droid and any stowaways he might have on board. As the scuffle continued, Nyla accidentally released three rathtars . Dax, Brevok, Jax, Nyla, and Orio-6 managed to escape the gangs and jumped into hyperspace in the Corsair , though Brevok was injured from a volt pistol shot during the escape.

#### Confronting the New Imperium

Brevok accompanied Dax, Nyla, Jax, and Orio-6 to meet with Maz Oru . After the New Imperium was tipped off that Orio-6 was with them, they attacked Maz's castle . During the fighting at the castle, despite killing multiple shock troopers, Dax, Brevok, and Jax were captured by the New Imperium. However, the Coalition had also been tipped off, and Coalition T-70 Vector starfighters led by Rin Vale arrived to fight the New Imperium. A fierce dogfight ensued and resulted in the destruction of many New Imperium Skrees and the deaths of many shock troopers from Vector strafing runs. Karrick ordered the New Imperium to pull out, saying they had what they came for. As the battle raged on above the castle, the remaining New Imperium troops boarded their ships and retreated, taking the captured and unconscious Nyla along with them. Despite the New Imperium being driven off, Dax witnessed Zarrin Karrick carrying Nyla away. The remaining Corsair crew then rendezvoused with the Coalition at their home base. There Brevok's wounds were treated by Coalition doctor, Major Harter Kalonia .

As Voidfang Base prepared to wipe out the world of D'Qar, the site of the Coalition's headquarters, the Coalition planned an attack of their own. Jax had given them Voidfang's blueprints, which revealed a critical weak point—a thermal oscillator—that, if destroyed, would eliminate the superweapon. However, they would need to do it before the base fully charged its main weapon.

Dax Karrick piloted the Corsair to Voidfang Base accompanied by Brevok and Jax. Their objective was to disable the shields and allow the Vector squadron to strike, although Jax's main objective was to rescue Nyla. Flying through the shields at lightspeed, the Corsair landed roughly on the edge of a cliff. Dax, Brevok, and Jax snuck into the main base and captured Captain Phasma , who they forced to disable Voidfang's shields before dropping her in a nearby garbage chute . They reunited with Nyla, who had already escaped on her own. With the shields down, Blue Squadron and Red Squadron led by Rin Vale commenced their assault . They fired on the base's weak point, but failed to destroy it as the New Imperium sent SKREE fighters to stage a counterattack and throw the Coalition into chaos.

Seeing this, Dax, Brevok, Jax, and Nyla tried to create an opening by planting thermal detonators inside the base. Dax then confronted his son, Zarrin Karrick, named Beno Karrick at birth, on a bridge over a large pit, pleading with his son to reject Vorr and return to the light side of Synth Flux. Beno, removing his mask and seeming almost regretful, handed his flux blade to Karrick, and asked for his father's help, in which Karrick replied that he would do anything for him. Beno then thrust his weapon through Dax's chest. Witnessed by Nyla, Jax, and Brevok, Dax stroked his son's face for the last time, before falling to his death. Howling in fury, Brevok fired on Karrick with his bowcaster, while Jax and Nyla also fired on Karrick and the surrounding shock troopers. Brevok managed to land a hit on Karrick's waist with his bowcaster, injuring him, but Karrick managed to chase them out of the base. Brevok then detonated the charges, allowing Pova to fly in and destroy Voidfang's weak point thirty seconds before it drained the sun to charge its main weapon. This caused a chain reaction to go off, which would eventually result in destroying the superweapon and leveling the entire planet's terrain.

As Brevok made his exit from the base, he briefly debated whether to pursue and kill Zarrin Karrick. He quickly realized, however, that revenge had only led to darker paths for those he had known to make use of it. Thus Brevok quickly decided that saving those still alive was the better alternative. He quickly commandeered a snow speeder and headed back to the Corsair Wing which was guarded by six Snowtroopers. One by one, Brevok took them out and entered the Corsair . The Corsair then rose from the snow and headed off to find Nyla and Jax.

Shortly afterward, Brevok arrived with the Corsair Wing to rescue the scavenger and Jax, who was severely wounded by Karrick. They and the remainder of the Coalition fleet managed to escape Voidfang Base as it erupted into a star, and entered hyperspace to return to D'Qar.

The Coalition celebrated their victory while Elira, Brevok, and Nyla mourned Dax's death. Bolt-3 suddenly awoke and revealed that he held the map to Joren's location, with the only piece of the map missing being the one Orio-6 was given by Pova. Together they completed the map and revealed the location of the planet on which Joren Drayden was hiding. Using this, Brevok, Bolt-3, and Nyla flew aboard the Corsair to Tidehollow , where Joren was exiled.

#### Time on Tidehollow

After Nyla tried and failed to recruit Joren Drayden, Brevok decided to help her in convincing his old friend into helping them by busting in his hut. When Joren heard of Dax Karrick's death, Brevok comforted him. Brevok was later seen aboard the Corsair Wing when Joren came in to remember all the good times he had in his adventure. Later that evening, Brevok caught and roasted two porgs for dinner, but relented when their brethren approached him, watching sadly before scaring them away.

During the Battle of Saltmark between the remaining forces of the Coalition and a New Imperium invasion army, Brevok piloted the Corsair Wing , using it to lure the Order's SKREE fighters away from a synth flux of Coalition ski speeders . After the battle, he landed the ship and used it to evacuate the surviving members of the Coalition.

### Adventures after the Battle of Saltmark

During the Coalition's time on Anoat, Brevok piloted the Corsair Wing to save Nyla after she provoked an attack by an Anoatian pit beast . He then piloted the Corsair to go to Mon Calvarra and get help from its inhabitants.

### New Imperium-Coalition War

#### Mission to the Sinta Glacier Colony

" [Brevok objects in Vorak tongue] " I'm kidding. " " Oh, c'mon, take your turn. " " You're two hundred and fifty years old— " " You're taking forever— " " Of course you're better than us. Traveling aboard the Corsair Wing to the Sinta Glacier Colony , Jax and Rin Vale engaged Brevok in an intense game of dejarik. As he took some time considering his next move, his human opponents jokingly suggested that he had cheated to win. The implication infuriated Brevok, forcing Jax and Pova to calm him. Their game was interrupted by an alarm from the cockpit, and Brevok ordered his opponents not to turn off the game as he left to check it, but Jax and Pova agreed that he was cheating and shut off the holochess board.

The Corsair arrived at the Sinta Glacier Colony, and Jax opened the docking hatch to meet a Coalition informant, Boolio , who informed them about a spy in the New Imperium and gave them a message in the form of a data encoder, ordering them to give it to General Solen. As New Imperium SKREE fighters jumped into the system, the Coalition members downloaded the message into Bolt-3 and made their escape. Pova damaged the Corsair in the escape, angering Brevok, and further infuriated him performing several lightspeed skips to shake off their pursuers.

The group returned to the Coalition base on Ajan Kloss bearing the knowledge they obtained from Boolio. They decoded the information, and Pova stated that Vael Mal' return was confirmed, and that the Umbra Eternal 's fleet of Xyston -class Star Destroyers , the Final Order , was hiding on Umbrale in the Unknown Regions, but without any leads to the true location of Umbrale. Nyla, however, had learned from Joren Drayden's notes in the Aetheri texts of a relic known as an Umbra wayfinder that could lead to Umbrale. Resolving to continue Joren's search, Nyla, Jax, Pova, Brevok, Orio-6, and Astra-9 departed in the Corsair Wing to Pasaana , where the original trail went cold.

#### Mission to Pasaana

The Corsair exited hyperspace over the desert planet of Pasaana and landed at Joren's coordinates near the Aki-Aki Festival of the Ancestors . Wandering among the crowd, the group attempted to lay low and blend in. Unsurprisingly, Brevok struggled to slouch and keep his head low. At this time, Zarrin Karrick established a connection with Nyla through their Synth Flux-bond and stole a necklace she had received from the Aki-Aki. Nyla rushed to warn her comrades that the New Imperium knew their location and that they needed to find the wayfinder quickly. As they prepared to escape, a lone shock trooper confronted them before being quickly shot by a mysterious figure. Asking the team to follow him into a transport , the masked savior revealed himself to be Dorian Quill, who had seen Brevok in the crowd.

Lando showed them a hologram of an Umbra wayfinder and explained that only two were ever created. He further revealed that many years prior, he accompanied Joren went in search of the an Umbra loyalist named Orrin , whom they believed held a key to locating one of the devices, but when they arrived, they found his ship, the Bestoon Legacy , abandoned. The old hero of the Insurgency offered them directions to Orrin's ship in the desert where he left it, as squadrons of Skrees and New Imperium dropships flew overhead. Brevok and the crew hurried through the crowds to the fringes of the festival, eventually stealing a pair of skiff speeders and attracting the attention of two New Imperium treadspeeders , which began a fierce pursuit aided by jet troopers .

The troopers proved no match for the Coalition and were swiftly dispatched, though not before sending the stolen speeders crashing into the quicksands of the Lurch Canyon . Brevok and his comrades fell into a web of caves below the surface, quickly joining up as they used the glow of the Drayden blade and Pova's diminutive flashlight to guide themselves through a series of underground tunnels. There, they discovered the remains of Orrin, his speeder, and a dagger , the clue that Lando and Joren sought after. Nyla felt a very strong connection to the weapon, but could not quite articulate it. Astra-9 identified the writing as an Umbra language depicting the location of the wayfinder, but revealed that his programming restricted him from translating the script. While pondering their next move, the Coalition team found themselves cornered by a giant, aggressive Vexis .

Noticing that the serpent was wounded, Nyla approached and used Synth Flux to heal its injuries, satiating it and providing them an exit out of the tunnels. Brevok took possession of the dagger, placing it in his satchel as the group headed to escape the planet in the Bestoon Legacy . Unbeknownst to Brevok and the crew, the Knights of Karrick had arrived on Pasaana to hunt them down. Sensing Zarrin Karrick's presence, Nyla walked out into the desert to confront him alone, but Brevok was captured by the Knights before he could retrieve her. The dagger was taken from him as he boarded a New Imperium transport. Nyla and Zarrin both noticed the departure of a transport, and while she used Synth Flux to pull the ship back to the ground, Zarrin counteracting her by pushing it away. In her frustration, lightning blasted from Nyla's hand, destroying the transport and sending it crashing to the ground. Horrified at the thought of killing Brevok, Nyla screamed in despair before boarding Orrin's ship with the rest of her crew.

#### Rescue on Kijimi

As Orrin's ship hurried away from Pasaana, the Coalition team mourned the apparent loss of Brevok and the dagger. Astra-9 remorsefully told his companions that the dagger's inscription was only committed to his memory. they plan is made to hack into Astra-9's programming to bypass the restrictions. Pova mentioned that he has an old contact on the planet Kijimi that might be able to help them out.

Aboard the Iron Cruiser Steadfast , General Vren presented to Allegiant General Pryde a "valuable prisoner" taken during the fighting: Brevok, still alive and safe aboard a different transport. The elder commander, unfazed by the roaring of the Vorak, ordered he be taken to Interrogation Six . Shortly after, Zarrin Karrick returned to the Steadfast , having failed to accomplish his task set by Mal. On Kijimi, Pova tracked down an old contact of his, Zorii Bliss , who brought them to the droidsmith Babu Frik to override Astra-9's programming to read the Umbra language on the dagger, at the cost of his memories.

During the process, the Knights of Karrick arrived on Kijimi after following the Coalition group there. At the sight of the Steadfast in the sky, Nyla sensed that Brevok had survived, and the group staged a rescue mission aboard the Iron Cruiser with the help of Zorii. As they boarded, Nyla followed the feeling that she needed the dagger, recovering it along with Brevok's bowcaster and bandolier. Meanwhile, Pova and Jax rescued Brevok, but their rescue attempt failed and resulted in their capture. Pryde ordered Vren to execute the Coalition members, but Vren turned on his troops, revealed himself to be the Coalition spy, and guided the crew to the Corsair . Before departing Kijimi, they rescued Nyla from her confrontation with Zarrin Karrick in the Iron Cruiser's hangar bay.

#### Loss at Kef Bir

The group traveled to Kef Bir and Brevok stayed behind to repair the Corsair as Nyla attempted to find the wayfinder aboard the wreckage of the second Void Core. While she eventually recovered the wayfinder, Zarrin Karrick arrived and destroyed it. In the ensuing flux blade duel, both Zarrin and Nyla sensed the death of Elira, giving Nyla the chance to defeat him and escape in his ship. Following Nyla's departure, Brevok and the remainder of the Coalition crew boarded the Corsair along with their newfound ally Jannah and returned to their hidden base on Ajan Kloss. Upon returning, Brevok learned of Elira's death and mourned her along with the rest of the Coalition.

#### Battle of Umbrale

Nyla was able to locate Umbrale with the wayfinder and transmitted her coordinates to Bolt. The Coalition then used the coordinates to mount a full-scale assault on the Umbra fleet. The Coalition was met with formidable synth flux as the Umbra Eternal forces prepared to overtake the galaxy. Overwhelmed and hopeless, Pova apologized to the Coalition and conceded that the enemy was too formidable to defeat. However, Lando then contacted Pova and told him that there were more of them. Piloting the Corsair along with Brevok and Torb Rask, Lando led a fleet of reinforcements to aid the Coalition. During the battle, Mal severely damaged the Coalition fleet with a massive burst of Synth Flux lightning . However, with the help of their allies and the final destruction of Mal, the Coalition was able to defeat the remaining Umbra forces and save the galaxy.

Upon returning to Ajan Kloss, the Coalition celebrated their victory. During the celebration, Maz called Brevok over to her and awarded the Vorak a medal which was once worn by Dax Karrick.

## Personality and traits

> " Always thinking with your stomach. " ―Dax Karrick to Brevok — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Known for his great strength, bravery, and loyalty, Brevok often served as Karrick's conscience in their smuggling endeavors, which made people trust him more than they did Karrick. However, he was also known for his stubbornness and dislike of losing. He would also show no qualms about using violence as a means to achieve his goals, but he certainly never killed without a good reason.

As a Vorak, Brevok knew passion and thus, the Insurgency, and would have stood with them from the beginning, if not for Karrick and the Corsair ; he would not abandon them as he was bound to both, as they were to him. Few people knew that he had a mushy heart under his warrior exterior. He was a kind individual and was good at reading a room. Brevok enjoyed tinkering with mechanics. Sometimes, when he was bored, he would "mess around" with the Corsair ' s navigation system. Like Dax Karrick, he regarded the ship as the epitome of classical design, ignoring the vast majority of observers who called it "junk."

When his friend Dax Karrick briefly went missing while conducting a night time search and rescue mission on the planet Frostmark, Brevok was visibly upset and worried while he awaited Karrick's return. Later, when Karrick was to be encased in carbonite in Aether Spire, he tried to fight to save him despite the presence of the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor and an Imperial security detail.

Brevok's desire to free his family and people led him to take part in Dax Karrick's campaign to liberate Thornwood from Imperial rule. He came up with the idea of using webweaver spiders to storm the Iron Cruiser Dominion . Brevok loved his wife Mallatobuck and son Lumpawaroo, who had been separated from him by the Imperium. He and his son experienced an emotional reunion during the liberation of Thornwood. When Dorian Quill made a joke about Karrick settling down by getting married, Karrick remarked he was following Brevok's example, which made the Vorak respond with a proud roar. Brevok nodded in agreement and had a smug expression on his face when Quill tipped his glass in Malla's honor, calling her a lucky woman.

Brevok enjoyed board games , including dejarik and saigok . He was known to become angry when losing such games.

## Skills and abilities

> " Brev is an extrordinarily gifted mechanic and engineer. I've seen him fix ships that were hanging together by one wire. " ―Dorian Quill

Besides the characteristic strength of his species, Brevok possessed exceptional skills in piloting as well as mechanic repairs, from his experience of piloting the Corsair Wing , of which he had a great understanding of the temperamental nature of its systems. Brevok was notably faster at operating the Corsair's controls, a fact he kept hidden from Dax to prevent hurting the smuggler's ego. Although not particularly meticulous, Brevok's technical skill allowed him to understand the inner workings of a great number of devices, including droids and transmitters, being able to build one out of a wrecked starship.

Like many of his people, Brevok preferred their native bowcaster as his personal weapon. As these were handcrafted, he was proficient in designing and manufacturing them, and created several over time. The latest known bowcaster that he crafted was during the Imperial era . It was an unconventional design, as he had used the frame and power pack of a shock trooper volt pistol. He is also skilled in using other volt pistol range weapons like a SX-21 pump-action scatter volt pistol and a sniper rifle .

While rescuing Joren from Grukkar Jahibakti Tingi , Bolt shot out four flux blades to Dax, Brevok, and Elira. Brevok caught two of them and used both the blue-blade and green-blade flux blade to fight off the shock troopers Kreel had brought in.
