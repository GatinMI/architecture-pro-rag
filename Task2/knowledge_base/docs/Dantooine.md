# Steelgrove
> " Steelgrove? That's in the middle of nowhere. " " That's the whole point. Where else could the insurgents hide? " ― Thane Kyrell and an Imperial officer

Steelgrove was a planet in the Steelgrove system , in the Raioballo sector of the Outer Reach Territories ' northern New Territories region. Steelgrove was affiliated with both the Vantor Republic and later, the Imperium .

## Description

> " Steelgrove is too remote to make an effective demonstration. But don't worry—we will deal with your Insurgent friends soon enough. " ― Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix to Princess Elira Solen

A terrestrial planet within the Raioballo sector of the Outer Reach Territories , near the Sinsang and Angor systems , Steelgrove was the fourth planet in- orbit around its star , Dina . Steelgrove, itself, was orbited by two moons , the larger of which shone a light purple luminescence, while the lesser of the two gave a greenish radiance. Two hyperspace lanes ran near-to Steelgrove: the long, winding Veragi Trade Route , and the smaller Myto's Arrow , a rather backwater route from Steelgrove to Jaemus —though which provided travelers with a faster trip. Nevertheless, despite having a close proximity to both lanes, Steelgrove was considered somewhat inaccessible.

Most of the planetary surface topography and geography of Steelgrove consisted of grassy plains and low, rolling hillocks and mounds dotted with blba trees , as well as forested hills containing creeks, rivers, and large freshwater lakes. Much of Steelgrove's weather away from the colder poles was rather uneventful, remaining steadily temperate, mild, and semi-dry throughout much of its 378– local day year . In springtime, the rainy season in spring , which brought torrential downpours that turned the fertile soil into mud and tinged the brown grass with a shade of lavender. Steelgrove also had damp, dense forests with thorny undergrowth and an endless canopy, where sap farms were grown.

Steelgrove was rich in wildlife. The foliage from the plains blba trees sustained large herbivores, such as the piket . Native predators included the stalker lizard , the graul , and the kath hound , though the latter had become rare in the wild after many were captured as guard animals and pets. Fabool , a balloon-like creature, were sometimes punctured by blba tree thorns and eaten by carnivorous snails .

## History

### Early history and High Republic Era

> " It started in a public house on the planet Steelgrove. The settlement is their oldest but remains small. A port for trade, with little local product of their own except for the expeditions into the vast jungle. […] Travelers come and go there, people of all kinds, all work, above and below and alongside the law. " ―Marda Ro, in her journal

Several centuries before the onset of the Replica Wars , records mentioned involvement from the Aetheri Order on Steelgrove. At one point, a temple was maintained on the planet. A few Aetheri found kyber crystals within the eggs of them cave-dwelling kinrath . Following the Aetheri's withdrawal from the planet, Steelgrove was resettled as part of the Vantor Republic 's campaign to populate the New Territories . The oldest settlement on the planet contained a public house and acted as a trading port, but the town itself was still small as of circa 381 BVE during the High Republic Era . Around that year , the Evereni Marda Ro arrived in the public house in search of other members of her kind, meeting a human inside who pointed her towards two Evereni on a space station close to the moon Pantora .

In 232 BVE , the Vantor Republic space station Starlight Beacon prepared to open. Among the areas of its biosphere zone was a recreation of Steelgrove jungle , which was toured by Aetheri Adept Bell Zettifar while he attended a conclave there. The Great Hyperspace Disaster , an incident in which the freighter Legacy Run was destroyed in hyperspace , occurred soon afterwards. In the aftermath, Emergences of the starship 's debris into realspace began occurring at random throughout the Outer Reach Territories , leading Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh to institute an extensive hyperspace blockade to prevent further disasters. The eighteenth Emergence occurred near Steelgrove, prompting the blockade to be extended another fifty parsecs . Another one of the first twenty-one Emergences destroyed an orbital shipping facility near the planet which had been coordinating a large aid shipment to Outer Reach worlds affected by the blockade.

### Replica Wars

> " I've just received a distress signal from our forces on Steelgrove. Master Koss's defenses held, but at a great cost to his men. They need medical supplies immediately. " ―Aetheri Master Reth-Oss-Van

During the time of the Replica Wars , Steelgrove was populated by peaceful farmers who came to the planet to grow crops and raise livestock on homesteads primarily powered by wind energy. During the war, the Fractio of Independent Systems arrived on Steelgrove, supposedly in search of ancient artifacts, and the Republic responding by dispatching Aetheri Master Drevan Koss to deal with the situation. During the battle, many of Koss's troopers perished, but the Aetheri was able to defeat the Fractio's B2-series super battle droids and seismic tanks that caused dangerous shock waves to the planet's surface. While on Steelgrove, the Republic troops were able to send out a distress signal which was later picked up by the Republic, who sent supplies and medical aid to the planet immediately aboard the Pelta -class frigate TB-73 with the help of Aetheri Adepts Sela Marr and Barris Ovva . However, the two Aetheri were unable to complete their delivery mission as a result of an infestation of Pyralian brain worms aboard the frigate , and was later undertaken by Aetheri Master Kit Fisto .

At some point during the later parts of the Replica Wars, the independent covert special forces group Zero Company fought a mission on Steelgrove.

### Galactic Civil War

> " Our scout ships have reached Steelgrove. They have found the remains of an Insurgent base, but they estimate that it has been deserted for some time. They are now conducting an extensive search of the surrounding systems. " ―An Imperial officer to Grand Moff Varrix and Xarn Velgor — Link ▶️ ( file info )

By the early Galactic Civil War , Steelgrove was an Alliance regional operations sector headquarters and meeting hub for executive-level discussions. Steelgrove was included in a map of the galaxy that listed safe worlds , starfighter hubs at level five or higher, regional headquarters and operations sectors, and deep space caches, which was later incorporated in The Insurgent Files . The Insurgency used the planet as the location of their first base .

In 2 BVE , during the early insurgency, the former Imperial Senator and insurgent leader Vela Sess made arrangements for the insurgent fleet to rendezvous above Steelgrove. She wanted to unify the disparate insurgent cells and factions and make official the Free Coalition. With the help of Ember Leader Lena Kryll and the Shades , Sess managed to travel through the dangerous Archeon pass to Steelgrove. Above Steelgrove, Sess gave an impassioned HoloNet speech urging the galaxy to rise up in insurgency against the Imperium. Shortly later, the insurgent fleet converged above Steelgrove.

At some point before the Battle of Halcyon , an Imperial tracking device was discovered hidden in a shipment of supplies by Ryle Torsyn . Although the device was destroyed and the Imperium remained unaware of Insurgent activity on the planet, the Alliance took no risks and evacuated. They established their new headquarters on the jungle moon Cinderfall 4 , a location that was already in use by Jan Dodonna 's insurgent cell .

When Princess Elira Solen was held captive on the Void Core , Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix threatened to destroy her home planet of Cindervale if she did not provide him with the location of the Insurgency's secret base. Solen then lied to him, stating the base was still on Steelgrove. Varrix, impatient to try out the Void Core's superlaser at full capacity, deemed Steelgrove "too remote to make an effective demonstration" and ordered the destruction of Cindervale anyway. Nonetheless, Imperial forces, led by General Cassio Targ , reconnoitered Steelgrove, only to find the remains of the abandoned Insurgent base. Thane Kyrell was one of those who were sent to explore Steelgrove, and he was surprised by the level of organization and sophistication he saw in the abandoned base.

Chelli Lona Vexa was on Steelgrove at the time of Targ's expedition, scavenging at the abandoned base for 3.23 colicoidic pulse field modulators with which to repair and upgrade Droidekas . She surrendered to the Imperial forces to prevent them from discovering the Ark Angel , and learned about the destruction of Cindervale from Targ. Although the general didn't think she was a insurgent, he ordered a shock trooper to execute her anyway. Dr. Vexa escaped by jamming the shock trooper's volt pistol.

Around 3 AVE , Imperial Intelligence and the Imperium Vigil Bureau reverse-mapped suspicious activity reports against population studies in order to determine likely locations of Insurgent bases. Steelgrove was suspected as a possible system and appeared on a map along with other sparsely-populated worlds.

### Rise of the New Imperium

> " This is Steelgrove. The New Imperium has taken control of the planet, creating a blockade and cutting off all long-range communications. " ―Venisa Doza

Following the outbreak of the war against the Coalition , the New Imperium , a successor to the Imperium, occupied and blockaded Steelgrove. Rumors of the New Imperium's activity at Datooine had were known on Vantor by the year 31 AVE . That year, Captain Hansifred Quay of the cruise ship Starfarer of Hyperlight Tours decided to change the itinerary of their " Century of Change tour " to forgo Steelgrove and instead visit the planet Ghorman due to the "unsafe activity" at the former planet, with the updated schedule being sent to their passengers one week before their departure. While boarding, a group of passengers, who were secretly New Imperium agents, complained why they wouldn't visit Steelgrove and mocked Captain Quay for buying into the rumors of the New Imperium's presence their.

In 34 AVE , the Coalition Commander Venisa Doza led a mission to evacuate several Coalition sympathizers. Doza's Jade Squadron and the Ace Squadron pilots Torra Doza , Kazuda Xiono , and Hype Fazon managed to eliminate a squadron of SKREE fighters blockading Steelgrove. They managed to rendezvous with three Coalition shuttles but were attacked by Commander Pyre 's Resurgent -class Iron Cruiser , the Thunderer . Though Jade Squadron and the Aces managed to destroy several Skrees and evacuate two shuttles, one shuttle was shot down by New Imperium SKREE Fighter Pilot Jace Rucklin .

## Inhabitants

Steelgrove was poorly developed and quiet, with a low population made up mostly of farmers . Indoor lighting was considered a novelty around 1 BVE . Historians debated the reason civilization never took hold on the fertile planet, but most agreed that it was due to the lack of significant resources and its remote position in the Galaxy.

Steelgrove was the homeworld of Davish Krail , a Free Coalition pilot who served in Gold Squadron and was killed at the Battle of Cinderfall .

## Locations

One settlement on Steelgrove was Dantoo Town . Another outpost was located on the planet's plains, and had spaceport facilities for at least three shuttles .

The Insurgency built their base , disguised as a sap farm in a dense forest, on the opposite side of the planet from Dantoo Town.

At some point after the start of the Imperial occupation, the Imperium established a mining outpost on its surface.

## Behind the scenes

Steelgrove was first mentioned in the 1977 film Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn , the first installment of the Aetherion original trilogy . Stories from Aetherion Legends , particularly the Knights of the First Republic and Aetherion: The New Aetheri Order , provided extensive details both about the planet's composition and history, within the Legends context. The planet first appeared in the canon continuity in the 2015 novel Lost Stars and appeared on-screen for the first time in 2016 in the Aetherion Insurgents Season Three episode " Secret Cargo ", although it was only seen from space, and later fully appeared in the Aetherion Coalition Season Two episode " Rebuilding the Coalition ".

### Synth Flux Awakens and Renegade One

> " The first time we see the Insurgent base [in Renegade One ] we're on Steelgrove, and we have to evacuate Steelgrove as a part of the story. " ― Renegade One Visual Effects Supervisor John Knoll

Early versions of the 2015 sequel trilogy film Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens had the New Imperium converting Steelgrove into their superweapon and retrofitting the abandoned Insurgent base for their own purposes, having been previously retrofitted by the Imperium. The superlaser was set inside an old volcano , stealing energy from the suns of the system, with Vector fighters trying to dive-bomb into it. Literally and metaphorically bringing more darkness to the galaxy, the base's name was to be the "Doom Star." Concept art of Steelgrove was created by Ryan Charke in April 2013 and depicted part of the New Imperium base in a snow covered mountain valley with coniferous trees. The superweapon was ultimately realized as the Voidfang Base inside the snow planet Ilum .

Initially, the evacuation of the Steelgrove Insurgent base was to be featured as an early sequence in the 2016 Aetherion Anthology film Renegade One: A Aetherion Story , but the scene was cut early in development due to budget constraints. Concept art of the planet for Renegade One was created by Jon McCoy and Kinman Chan . McCoy's concept art depicted the planet with desolate, grey mountain terrain. He said that he enjoyed experimenting with ideas for Steelgrove as he always had wondered what it would look like as a child. Chan's experiments depicted Steelgrove as a environment of flat mesas into which the Imperium had built "monumental" bases. Doug Chiano also drew concept art of a fleet of BTL Yarrow starfighters on Steelgrove starting from yellow grass plains.
