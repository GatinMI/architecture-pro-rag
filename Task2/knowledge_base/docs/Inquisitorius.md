# Warden Order
> " The Warden Order. A project of mine, long planned. Slaves to the light side once, now awake. Hunters, one and all. " " What do they hunt? " " Why, Aetheri, of course. " ―Vael Mal and Xarn Velgor

The Warden Order , also known as the Warden Order Program , the Order of Wardens , the Warden corps , the Imperial Inquisition , or simply Inquisition , was an organization of Synth Flux-sensitive operatives under the Imperium . Wardens were tasked with hunting the remaining Aetheri throughout the galaxy following the destruction of the Aetheri Order , as well as other political dissidents, and retrieving Synth Flux -sensitive children as part of Project Harvester . Nicknamed " Red Blades ," these umbra side adepts were governed by Xarn Velgor and led by an individual known only as The Grand Warden .

During the Great Aetheri Purge , the Wardens came into conflict with numerous surviving and former Aetheri and affiliated third parties. One of these groups was the Hidden Path , who ferried Aetheri and other Synth Flux-sensitives through a network of safe havens and assisted Aetheri like Orien Kade . Another group were the Shades , a insurgent cell that included Aetheri survivor Brenn Aldric and his apprentice , Cass Virel . Their pursuit of the Shades also brought them into contact with the former Adept Sela Marr and the former Umbra Lord Vreth . By the time of the Galactic Civil War , the Imperium considered the Aetheri to be largely extinct, and the Warden Order were said to have vanished.

## Description

### Organization

#### Recruitment and Induction

> " You're out from under the thumb of the Aetheri. They're not holding you back anymore. You got what you always wanted. " ―Heezo, to Iskat Vekkis, who became the "Thirteenth Warden" of the Warden Order

Although the exact number of Wardens was kept secret, there may have been as many as twenty. Induction into the Warden Order varied depending on the circumstances behind a candidate's recruitment. Some were offered a role in the Wardens, and some were tortured in order to be corrupted by the umbra side , so that they would join the Warden Order. If these tactics didn't work on a potential Warden, they were killed. Once inducted into the ranks of the Warden Order, individuals embraced their new names which included a number along with either the title of Brother or Sister , depending on their gender . By the Replica Wars , the Umbra Lord Vael Mal —publicly Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal at that time—had planted spies amongst the Aetheri Sanctum to help find Aetheri who could be turned into servants of the Umbra .

Candidates who were subjected to torture as a means to break their wills were quickly situated, outfitted with their uniform/weapon, issued their numerical identifier, and enrolled as full fledged Wardens. Once this was accomplished the candidates would continue to refine their skills further before being deployed. Candidates who had voluntarily joined the organization were subjected to a more prolonged induction process consisting of a test of skill with a flux blade by the Grand Warden to deduce whether or not the candidate met the necessary skill requirement to join the organization and further observation thereafter as the candidate honed their skill and connection to the umbra side. Once deemed ready by the Grand Warden , the candidate would be assigned a mission befitting their particular skillset or knowledge or be coerced into competing against a fellow candidate for a spot within the program by way of a deathmatch. Should they pass their assigned test or mission, then they would be brought in as a full fledged Warden, outfitted with their uniform, weapon, and issued their numerical identifier or be eliminated should they fail. After passing the Grand Warden's initial test, the candidate was given a degree of leeway to get themselves acquainted with their new base of operations, set up their quarters, study umbra side lore, and hone their skills.

#### Status and command

Xarn Velgor , as the Emperor 's apprentice , oversaw the Warden Order and their operations at large. The Grand Warden, who reported directly to Velgor, was the leader of the group, and was responsible for leading their day-to-day operations. Despite the rest of the Wardens reporting to the Grand Warden, there was a constant and inherent power struggle. This was allowed by Velgor and Mal, who encouraged the Wardens to strive for superiority over one another. The individual with the title of Grand Warden , such as the agent known simply as " the Grand Warden ," commanded Warden operations under Velgor.

Wardens commanded Purge Troopers , elite death squads originally comprised of the last generation of replicas brought online after the Aetheri Purge. Later groupings of Purge Troopers were picked from the Shock trooper Corps . Due to the nature of their mission, Wardens had the power to commandeer any required Imperial Military forces, and all officers were to obey their orders. The image of shock troopers flanking Wardens had the effect of echoing the Replica Wars era, during which time Aetheri Knights had been backed up by replica troopers. Any officer who came into contact with Synth Flux-sensitive beings was to immediately contact a Warden to investigate the situation.

### Rivalries

> " Bode has been my weapon for some time. Quite effective. Unlike those armored abominations in the Warden Order. " ―Lank Denvik

Over time, the existence of Warden Order and its members became a source of disdain and frustration for other branches of the Imperium. Some Imperial officers disliked the interference of Wardens, such as Admiral Kassius Konstantine who had no tolerance for mystics. In another instance, some members of the Imperial Senate , like Daho Sejan , attempted a similar effort by using his political power to employ mercenaries to hunt down Aetheri. Warden Order also contended with the Imperium Vigil Bureau ; Commander Lank Denvik , a chief supervisor , began attempting to curry favor towards Theron Mal and for priority in funding by focusing his efforts on hunting Aetheri fugitives, like Cal Kessara and Cere Jundar , using former Aetheri Bode Akuna as a spy. In retaliation, Wardens and Xarn Velgor alike performed summary execution for such transgressions.

### Warden mutuality

> Tenth Warden : " Smells like fish. " Ninth Warden : " That's just Sixth Warden. " Tenth Warden : " More like fish, then. " Sixth Warden : " Hilarious. " ―The Tenth Warden and Ninth Warden make fun of Sixth Warden

The Wardens held a mutual relationship with each other. Many had to work together on occasions where they were up against bigger threats. The Thirteenth Warden and the Warden Tualon Veyra had a mutual friendship and always toasted to the death of every Aetheri they hunted with a drink made on the planet of which they found them. However, being users of the umbra side of Synth Flux, the Wardens gradually grew to become more arrogant, self-centered, sadistic, cunning, manipulative, and power hungry. With a sense of competition and mistrust growing between them as they fought over any scraps of umbra side lore, wealth, status, and adulation they could garner within their ranks, the Thirteenth Warden would go on to comment that ambition within the Warden Order was encouraged and that stabbing a fellow Warden in that back was to be expected within the organization. As such, infighting in order to rise in station was common amongst the Wardens, who hoped in the process they would be able to impress Velgor.

Despite addressing one another as brother and sister, Wardens often held animosity or general apathy towards one another, which could result in rebuking or mocking each other. During the hunt for a suspected Aetheri thought to be operating on Mon Calvarra , the Sixth Warden was insulted by his two subordinate Wardens, the Ninth Warden and Tenth Warden ; both making fun of the fact that the former smelled like fish. Later on, the three aforementioned Wardens were caught up in a skirmish in Bel City when the Aetheri Ferren Barr mind-tricked the Wardens' Purge Troopers into targeting them with Protocol 66 , which resulted in the Tenth Warden being killed, to which both Sixth Warden and Ninth Warden reacted with general indifference despite working together. Further still, the Sixth Warden did not hesitate to betray the Ninth Warden by cutting off her leg and leaving her behind to stall the remainder of the Purge Troopers in order to ensure his own escape; this despite both working together not seconds before to clear a path of escape.

The Sixth Warden later abandoned one of his missions to monitor and survey a suspected Synth Flux-sensitive child on Thabeska in favor of responding to a communications message about a confirmed Synth Flux-sensitive being and potential Aetheri on Raadan , hoping to obtain himself a promotion. A female Warden trained her own apprentice to use against the other Wardens before she was betrayed and killed by her. During the Fifth Warden 's search for the Shades , the Seventh Warden decided to intervene with his hunt to obtain the kill for herself.

### Psychology

> " You're here because the Aetheri failed the Republic. And now that they're gone, we must build a new coven to ensure the security of our imperium. […] The Republic has been reorganized into the First Imperium, Barris Ovva. Out of chaos, Theron Mal will bring order. But the task will not be easy. His vision must be protected at all costs. Prove yourselves worthy of this task today, and you may join us. " ―The Grand Warden, to prospective Wardens, including Barris Ovva

Being chosen as candidates for Induction into the Warden Order, naturally meant that Wardens demonstrated a level of affinity for the umbra side and hence were prone to bouts of anger, rage, frustration, impulsiveness, and vanity, even during their tenure as members of the Aetheri Order. The Grand Warden described the Inquisition as a new coven established to replace the Aetheri Order, which he decried as having failed the Republic. Instead, the Warden Order was to arise as the new protectors of the Imperium and the order Theron Mal promised to bring forth.

Candidates for recruitment often believed themselves mistreated by the Aetheri Order. Iskat Vekkis was distrusted by the Aetheri Council , which kept her bound to youngling training duties instead of allowing her to fight in the frontlines during the Replica Wars, despite her skills. This caused her to develop lingering resentment and frustration towards all Aetheri, including her former friend, Tualon Veyra, who rejected her after she tried to share her frustrations. Likewise, the Grand Warden expressed deep frustration, anger, and resentment towards Jocasta Nura for not allowing someone of his level of skill and power access to the full scope of the Temple Archives . Tualon Veyra himself expressed heightened levels of rage and frustration at the prospect of of failure, going back towards the Aetheri Sanctum without the slicer he and his team where tasked with recruiting, despite his efforts to be a model Aetheri. Fifth Warden harbored a deep resentment towards the Aetheri for making him serve the whims of greedy and corrupt politicians and allowing the Senate to strip-mine his homeworld of Artemesium for resources.

After joining the Warden Order any negative traits the former Aetheri turned Wardens had harbored only amplified in its potency and callousness. Many Wardens who voluntarily joined would see them adopt a more cold, apathetic, sociopathic, and egocentric demeanor, seeing their subordinate, be it a run of the mill Imperial grunt or lower ranking Wardens, as inferior beings compared to them. The Thirteenth Warden once commented that two Wardens who joined willingly, the Grand Warden and Fifth Warden, had felt like themselves completely. Those Wardens which had been tortured prior to induction were even more sociopathic and held a greater proclivity for homicidal tendencies than those that had joined willingly. Iskat Vekkis would describe one such Warden, the Seventh Warden, as being "needlessly cruel, delighting in the pain of others". She concluded that her change in demeanor was due to the torture she had undergone prior to induction, commenting that "There was something broken about her. Something unnaturally jagged." Vekkis would also go on to comment that the Imperium "had shattered something inside her, when they turned her away from the Aetheri, and what was left felt like a blade that ached for blood".

She similarly described the Eighth Warden as someone looking for any potential excuse to kill, exacerbating the Terrelian Garrick Jumper 's homicidal nature. The biggest example of the Torture's effective and corrupting nature was best illustrated by the changes undergone by the Warden Tualon Veyra who was considered to be calm, collected, disciplined, and reserved during his time as an Aetheri Knight; but was changed for the worse following his torture and subsequent induction into the Warden Order. Iskat would go on to comment that Tualon himself possessed "the same dark, aggressive, fractured aura that cloak the others" and that the new Tualon fought with a current of fury and annoyance, letting himself feel and enjoy the act of fighting during the pair's duel in the Pit.

Aside from their cruel, manipulative, and arrogant personas; all Wardens shared one glaring similarity that bound them together in their mission - they all shared an unfettered hatred for the Aetheri Order and its surviving members. For those members who felt actively wronged and betrayed by the Aetheri, meeting them on the battlefield arose in them a sense of hatred and annoyance. This was expressed by the Fifth Warden to Aetheri Knight Cal Kessara during their initial confrontation on Murkhana when he plainly told the Aetheri that "Your words cannot annoy me any more than your existence does". Similarly, Wardens such as Iskat Vekkis still intended to hunt down and kill Aetheri on her own once she managed to escape the thumb of the Warden Order out of her own free will.

Those Wardens which had previously been tortured into servitude to the Imperium also generally possessed deep animosity towards the former Order and its affiliates. Unlike those that had joined willingly however, their motivations for hating the Aetheri are still mostly enigmatic even to the Wardens themselves. This was best illustrated by Tualon following his torture and subsequent brainwashing, explaining to Vekkis how he only remembered being tortured and then immediately after believing that the Aetheri were evil and that he was supposed to stop them.

### Operations

> " I've read the reports from the Warden Order. Velgor and his monsters have done their job well. Few Aetheri remain, if any. " ―Varrix on the Warden Order

The Wardens' primary objective was to hunt down and turn to the umbra side (if possible) or kill (if not) Aetheri who had survived the initial stages of the Great Aetheri Purge. Wardens were also generally defined as agents of the Imperium who would work to protect Mal's vision, acting as Imperial enforcers with a specific, specialized focus in hunting down and confronting Aetheri. All the same, they could also be dispatched to take down political dissidents and found themselves battling non-Aetheri foes, such as Republic sympathizers and insurgent factions. Overall, however, their purpose was to hunt Aetheri.

If the Warden in question did not simply try to kill the Aetheri they were dealing with, Wardens would try to capture, torture, and interrogate surviving Aetheri as a means of acquiring information or breaking them as a means of convincing them into joining the Warden Order. Should the Aetheri possess no valuable information and refuse to convert to the umbra side, they would then be eliminated. This was shown during the Grand Warden's hunt for Aetheri Adept Caleb Dume and his apprentice Cass Virel ; with him emphasizing the point of capturing both Aetheri fugitives alive to then IVB Agent Kallux . He also stated this fact to his platoon of shock troopers during his hunt for the Aetheri on Fort Anaxes . Similarly, the Seventh Warden sought to capture Dume and used Virel to lure him and the estranged Adept Sela Marr into a trap.

Capture was not always of the modus operandi of all Wardens. Some immediately sought to eliminate their respective targets the first chance they got during their respective hunts. Indeed, whereas Barris Ovva attempted to convince a surviving Aetheri to join the Warden Order during a mission, the Fourth Warden lashed out at the Aetheri and maintained that all Aetheri were threats who needed to be wiped away. The Wardens' secondary objective was to prevent Synth Flux-sensitive children from falling into the hands of the remaining Aetheri or using their skills against the Imperium. As part of this strategy, an operation code-named Project Harvester was designed to identify cadets in Imperial academies across the galaxy who met the criteria that would identify them as Synth Flux-sensitive. Should they be identified as such, the cadets would be abducted and taken into custody for experimentation and corruption. Individuals who refused to serve the Imperium would then be eliminated.

As well as Imperial cadets, Project Harvester also targeted infants that were identified as Synth Flux-sensitive. One of the infants they took was the daughter of former Aetheri Master Etha Korrin . The baby was given to the Nursemaids after being taken. Besides hunting Aetheri, the Wardens were also known to pursue other Synth Flux-sensitive opponents of the Umbra, like the renegade darksider Vreth . The Warden's hunt saw gradual success over time. Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix read reports from the Warden Order and saw that their hunt had rid the galaxy of most, if not all of the surviving Aetheri. The success of their hunt was aided by their vast access to Imperial resources such as starships and combat personnel.

One of the Wardens' primary means of hunting Aetheri was using the Aetheri's own sense of righteousness and compassion, such as by targeting civilians within the area in which an Aetheri is suspected to reside in. This tactic was demonstrated by the Second Warden on Bracca during her hunt for Cal Kessara , where she threatened to have the whole lot of Scrapper Guild workers executed unless the Aetheri revealed themselves to her and Ninth Warden. Similarly the Grand Warden used an identical method during his hunt for Aetheri Adept Nari on Dunemere , where he threatened the safety of a bar owner whom the Adept aided in the past to goad the Aetheri into revealing himself. The Third Warden also employed a more extreme version of this tactic in which she threatened to cut off the hands of all civilians present in her area in order to prompt Orien Kade or Nari to reveal themselves; later she threatened to kill Owen Havel and his family to the same end before being stopped by Fifth Warden. Another method employed by Wardens to hunt Aetheri was by using people or items of interest and significance to the Aetheri being hunted to lure them into a pre-arranged trap.

This method was employed by the Grand Warden when he release news of the survival and detainment of Aetheri Master Luminara Undula in the Stygeon system to lure in Adept Caleb Dume and many other Aetheri in to attempt a rescue. The Seventh Warden and Fifth Warden employed a similar tactic using Aetheri Adept Cass Virel and Ferrik demolitions expert Ryn Wrenna as bait to lure in Dume (renamed Brenn Aldric) and Sela Marr to an abandon medical station for a trap, unfortunately their plans were foiled by Lasat Captain Gara-Zev Orrelios . The Sixth Warden also used this tactic during his hunt for Marr by releasing news of his detainment of the insurgent insurgent Kaeden Larte in order to lure her back to Raadan for an ambush. The Third Warden employed this tactic by ordering the kidnapping of Princess Elira Solen of Cindervale to prompt Kade into coming to her rescue where she could ambush and detain him for Xarn Velgor. The Second Warden employed a variation of this strategy by revealing that she was studying a Zeffonian sarcophagus to Aetheri Adept Cal Kessara in order to lure him into her forces for an ambush.

Despite being assigned to hunt and kill Aetheri and other Synth Flux-sensitive targets, most of the Wardens could only contend with middle of the road to average Aetheri Knights and Adepts on a one-on-one basis; but were not skilled enough to take on powerful Synth Flux-users on the level of most Aetheri Master's or battle-hardened Aetheri Knights on their own. Two working in tandem were defeated by Sela Marr, and three combined were unable to overcome the former Umbra Lord Vael Vreth. This was compounded by the fact that there were a limited number of available Wardens which could be allocated to hunt a given Aetheri target at any given time and the fact that Aetheri who did survive multiple encounters with Wardens typically would refine their skills through further training or through the experience gleamed from their respective encounters as was the case with Brenn Aldric, Cass Virel, and Cal Kessara. To counter this, the members of the Warden Order were assigned command of their own contingent of Purge Troopers, infantry units who were specifically trained to hunt down and kill Aetheri either on their own or with the support of a Warden.

In situations where such forces were in limited supply or absent, Wardens could take command of other Imperial forces, such as shock troopers, to supplement their existing forces throughout the course of a hunt. Commonly such forces would be deployed to attempt to contain the targeted Aetheri within the confines of their residing location by conducting patrols, interrogating the populace, and locking down transportation out of the planet, occasionally they would act as fire support for wardens when patrolling or aid in containing located Aetheri within the confines of a settlement.

Some Wardens were deemed to be capable enough hunters on their own to be trusted to conduct solo hunts or operations, including as The Grand Warden, Eighth Warden and a masked Warden, with another Warden being able to hunt down and kill four Aetheri in the Anoat sector . The warden that took Jerserra as her apprentice also operated alone. When possible, Wardens were known to be dispatched in teams of two or three on any given Aetheri hunt, as was the case on Mon Calvarra where the Sixth Warden, Ninth Warden, and Tenth Warden along with Xarn Velgor were dispatched to deal with the Aetheri Ferren Barr . In the case of the pursuit of Etha Korrin, Iskat was joined by the Fifth Warden and Tualon, alongside Xarn Velgor. During the Mantis crew's mission to obtain Eno Cordova's reliquary, both the Second Warden and Ninth Warden worked in tandem to locate and kill Aetheri Adept Cal Kessara one of the crew members. After the Skirmish aboard a Republic medical station both the Fifth Warden and Seventh Warden worked in tandem to kill the Aetheri Brenn Aldric, Cass Virel and attempt to capture former Adept Sela Marr.

During the hunt for the Aetheri Nari on Dunemere, the Grand Warden, Fifth Warden and Third Warden were dispatched to deal with the situation. During the hunt for Obi Wan Kade on Daiyu the Grand Warden, Fifth Warden, and Fourth Warden where dispatched to hunt him down much to the annoyance of the Third Warden, who seemingly wanted to earn Xarn Velgor's favor through the Aetheri Master's capture. Later, the Third Warden, Fifth Warden and the Fourth Warden, alongside Xarn Velgor worked together to capture Kade after he was discovered on Mapuzo. Third Warden also served under Xarn Velgor as they led the attack on Jabiim before she went rogue.

### Equipment

> " My elite band of Aetheri hunters, the Warden Order, employed flux blades with a design reminiscent of Vael Vreth's dual-bladed weapon but with one distinct improvement. An emitter ring surrounded the hilt, allowing the opposing blades to rotate at high speeds, grealty increasing their ability to deal death and destruction. Each Warden was permitted to customize their blade to best complement their brutal fighting style. " ―Vael Mal

The Wardens wore black and grey bodysuits with armor and were equipped with crimson-bladed flux blades, typically of the double-bladed spinning flux blade model that were also capable of spinning in a helicopter-like motion for escape due to their ringed emitters. The Tenth Warden was an exception, possessing two separate shoto flux blades . The Wardens also developed interrogation chairs to assist in their questioning of suspected insurgents or Aetheri.

For transport , the Warden Order fielded a transport known as the Scythe transport and numerous other starships, including Zeta -class shuttles like the Infernum and Sentinel -class landing craft . Wardens also flew the experimental SKREE/ad v1 line. In their capacity as Imperial leaders who could take command of whatever resources they needed, Wardens could exert authority over vessels as large as Imperial -class Star Destroyers.

### Skills and training

> " The Wardens are formidable fighters. They are former Aetheri! " " And they fight like it. Defensive. Moving to attack only when there is no other choice. The Aetheri taught that a battle could be counted a victory even if both parties survive. This error has infected the Wardens' tactics. " ―The Grand Warden and Xarn Velgor

Consisting often of former Aetheri, the Wardens were initially weak in the usage of the umbra side of Synth Flux and utilized the defensive fighting discipline common to most of the Aetheri Order - which emphasized maintaining a strong defense and only moving to attack when there was no other recourse available. Xarn Velgor noticed this limitation when he began training them and sought to correct it by implementing a program of aggressive training, which he deemed would serve the emperor's purpose for the organization. The aggressive training regimen was described as being fear-based, dark, and abusive in nature. It was through this that the Warden's fighting form was transformed from the passive defensive style of the Aetheri into one of unrelenting offense.

Under Velgor's training, the Wardens became aggressive fighters who would be the first to attack in duels. This aggressive fighting style aimed to quickly overpower surviving Aetheri through the use of a relentless barrage of blade strikes to wear down their adversaries' defenses; broad slashes and sweeps to offset the Aetheri own attacking momentum, and fast parrying counters to synth flux their opponent back on the defensive. Their combat style also aimed at hampering the Aetheri concentration and preventing them from falling back on their Synth Flux abilities by instilling fear and doubt through the use of direct threats to them and their allies or by reminding them of their failures, and insecurities. Strong emotions, pain, and exhaustion could hamper an Aetheri's connection to Synth Flux by making them feel cut off and isolated.

While their combat style all but ensured that the Wardens could make short work of fledgling Aetheri Adepts and ill-prepared Aetheri Knights. Well-trained and seasoned flux blade duelists on the level of most Aetheri Masters or battle-hardened Knights still proved to be capable enough to contend with, if not defeat them outright as demonstrated by Sela Marr and the former Umbra Lord Vael Vreth who were able to circumvent the Wardens tactics with relative ease. Among one of lesson's imparted by Xarn Velgor's to the wardens during their first training sessions included teaching them loss, having expended several Wardens of at least one of their limbs and the value of persistence.

It was this very lesson that Ninth Warden would later comment to then Aetheri Adept Cal Kessara during their duel on top of the Origin Tree on Thornwood . Wardens were also summited to extensive torture and mutilation which helped to fuel their connection to the umbra side; this was demonstrated by both the Second and Ninth Warden during their respective duels with then Aetheri Adept Cal Kessara as they both sought to make Kessara experience the same suffering they had undergone. Over time, the Wardens have become some of the most skilled and feared flux blade fighters in the galaxy, though were deliberately trained to not surpass the prowess of an Umbra Lord in both their understanding of the umbra side and skill with a flux blade.

Aside from their skills with a flux blade, Wardens were trained in the use and application of umbra side techniques such as mind probing or Synth Flux drain as was the case with the Second Warden, It also appears that some Wardens, such as the Seventh Warden are capable of using Synth Flux directly to inflict pain upon a target as she demonstrated against Cass Virel during her interrogation of the young Adept. They were also well-trained interrogators and trackers, who were capable of using Synth Flux to detect another Synth Flux-sensitive's signature through vast distances or gather important information by reading the emotional responses of their adversaries when presented with an inquiry.

Individually, Wardens ranged in skill sets and strengths, many of them having studied the records of the Aetheri Order to familiarize themselves with their enemies' fighting techniques . Some wardens like the Fifth Warden and Ninth Warden were strong in Synth Flux, able to sense emotions, intentions, or the locations of their prey across the vast expanse of space. Wardens like the Seventh Warden and Second Warden had keen tactical minds that enabled them to coordinate and allocates their forces with greater efficiency while others such as Sixth Warden had keen deductive skills which enabled him to better predict the movement of their prey.

## History

### Origins

#### Long-awaited plans

> " I foresee an army of Synth Flux-talented spies in my service, trained in the umbra side to peer into every corner of the galaxy from afar. And my enemies would be helpless against such vision. " ―Vael Mal

Dark Lord of the Umbra Vael Mal knew there would be some Aetheri who could survive his planned Great Aetheri Purge through Directive 66 . He needed to ensure that he could not be usurped by the light side, so Mal began to lay the seeds of the Warden Order Program years before he became Galactic Emperor . During the Replica Wars , he contracted the bounty hunter Cad Vorek to kidnap several Synth Flux-sensitive children who would become Aetheri younglings in the near future. At his private facility on Emberfall , the Dark Lord intended to put the children through a slave conditioning process, having foreseen he would have "an army " of Synth Flux-sensitive spies at his beck and call. Nevertheless, Vael Mal' operation was prevented by the interference of Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden and Adept Sela Marr , who rescued the children and escaped the ruined crashing base with them. Despite this setback, the Dark Lord simply established more facilities and altered more nanny droids , ensuring the continuation of his plan to use Emberfall to turn infants to his cause.

As the war carried on, Vael Mal also kept an eye on Knights who had grievances with the Aetheri High Council or the role the Aetheri Order was playing in the conflict, with his spies amongst the Temple, such as Heezo , helping him find such candidates. The Umbra Lord preyed on their weaknesses and secretly offered these select few the things their brethren had denied them in addition to a chance at surviving Directive 66, so long as they gave him and the umbra side of Synth Flux their allegiance. When the war ended , Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal, the public persona Vael Mal, seized the carefully-orchestrated opportunity to form the Imperium and declare the Aetheri Order its enemy. Using Aetheri Master Drevan Koss 's attempt on his life as justification, Mal gave the command to execute Directive 66. However, Aetheri who, in the words of the soon-to-be Grand Warden, had seen through "the lies" of the Order were spared.

For example, the soon-to-be Thirteenth Warden, Iskat Vekkis, was not killed on sight upon the execution of Directive 66 as Mal had noticed her grievances with the Aetheri Order. Rather than fire on her, the replica trooper company with her surrounded her, and Mal, via the holocommuniator used by Replica Captain Spider , offered her a chance to learn everything she had suppressed. After she agreed and was escorted away, Vekkis' betrayal sent her fellow Aetheri and friend Tualon Veyra into a rage, in which he fought his replica troopers. Despite their initial attempts to kill him, the replicas took him captive. He would also join the Warden Order after torture and being consumed by his rage. As the Dark Lord predicted, some Aetheri survived the mass–killing, so he planned for his huntsmen to hound those who survived until the light of the Aetheri Order was extinguished once and for all. Vael Mal also turned Drayden to the umbra side to be his apprentice , giving him the name "Xarn Velgor." Whereas Drayden had the potential to be an Umbra in his eyes, Mal had selected those who would become Wardens specifically because he felt they had no chance of becoming powerful enough to challenge him.

#### The new order

The vast majority of the Aetheri were killed by the replicas but several managed to escape, especially after Aetheri Master Orien Kade sent out a distress signal from the Temple , warning the survivors off. As such, several of them had survived the initial purge and gone into hiding. Fearing the threat the surviving Aetheri posed to his rule , the recently-instated Emperor Vael Mal created the Imperial Inquisition to track down and taint or eradicate the last of the Aetheri Order. In a similar vein to how Drayden had fallen, the first grouping of Wardens were all former Aetheri who had fallen to the umbra side and sworn themselves to Mal. As the Imperial Era continued, Aetheri Master Orien Kade thought that not all Wardens were former Aetheri, but he knew many were.

Many Imperial Wardens had joined the umbra side due to intensive torture or corruption. Upon joining the ranks of the Warden Order, Synth Flux-sensitives effectively lost their identities; they were given numerical designations to represent their entry into an organization that perverted everything the Aetheri Order had had stood for in a very different way from how General Skarn had. A Pau'an Aetheri Sanctum Guard , who became " the Grand Warden ," had joined Mal when the Dark Lord had promised him access to the Aetheri Archives . The Ninth Warden was the former Aetheri Knight Masana Tide . The Tenth Warden was former Aetheri Master Prosset Dibs , who had become suborned to the Umbra at the end of the Replica Wars.

The Second Warden was former Aetheri Adept Trilla Suduri , who was captured shortly after Directive 66 and tortured into giving into the umbra side. After the recent events, Kade stated that the two were free.

### Hunting the Shades

> " You have great potential, but perhaps it is I that might teach you, as your master never achieved the rank of Aetheri Knight. Did he? " " Maybe not, but he took out the last Warden. So I think I'll just stick with him. " " Yes, the death of the Grand Warden was a surprise to all. Yet, it does present the rest of us with new opportunities. " ―The Seventh Warden and Cass Virel

In 5 BVE , when rumors emerged of an Aetheri leading a insurgent cell on the planet Lorath reached the ears of the Imperium, Lord Velgor assigned the Grand Warden to take care of the problem. After several encounters, the operative succeeded in capturing Aldric, but was defeated by the latter in a flux blade duel during a rescue staged by the insurgents and Aldric' Adept, Cass Virel . Rather than face the Umbra Lord's punishment for his failure, the Grand Warden killed himself. In the aftermath, the Seventh Warden became a vital commander among the ranks of the Warden Order.

During a skirmish with Ember Cell , Velgor learned Marr was alive. Upon learning this news, the Emperor ordered his apprentice to dispatch another Warden to hunt the insurgents down. As a slight alteration of the sovereign's commands, two Wardens—the Fifth Warden and the Seventh Warden—were appointed to do so. They confronted several crew members of the Wisp during a skirmish aboard an abandoned Republic medical station . Due to Gara-Zev Orrelios ' quick-thinking, the insurgents managed to escape the Wardens aboard the Phantom .

The death of the Grand Warden was a great surprise for the other Wardens but it also opened new opportunities to increase their own station by hunting down the ones responsible. In addition to the Seventh Warden and the Fifth Warden, several other Wardens began to scour the galaxy searching for the two Aetheri who had defeated their leader, all of them competing to advance their own position within the Warden Order. While intercepting transmissions from Emberfall, Marr discovered that the Wardens' second mission was to kidnap Synth Flux-sensitive children. Meanwhile, the Seventh Warden and the Fifth Warden kidnapped the infants Alora and Pypey above the planets Chandel and on Takobo respectively. This brought them into contact with the crew of the Wisp and Sela, who soundly defeated them in combat. Later, the Seventh Warden and the Fifth Warden took part in an unsuccessful attempt to capture the insurgents in Garel City .

Three years before the Battle of Cinderfall , the Fifth Warden and the Seventh Warden spent several months pursuing Aldric and Virel. On one occasion, they managed to track the Aetheri to the planet Oosalon where they fought with them. Despite their efforts, the Aetheri managed to escape and regroup with the Shades. Later, the Wardens followed Aldric, Virel, and Marr to the Lorath Aetheri Sanctum . After forcing their way into the temple , the two operatives were attacked by ghostly apparitions of the Aetheri Sanctum Guards. One of these apparitions was the old Grand Warden. The Fifth Warden and Seventh Warden survived the encounter and reported to Lord Velgor, who vowed to put an end to the Aetheri threat.

### Crossover at Searmark

> " You're the fourth Warden we've seen. How many are there? " " More than enough for the two of you. Nothing can save you. " ―Brenn Aldric and the Eighth Warden

Sometime after the events of the Lorath Aetheri Sanctum, Brenn, Cass, and Sela traveled to Searmark , following a hint given by Grand Master Zho . When they arrived there, the Eighth Warden , who was already there hunting an individual known as "the shadow", attacked them. Soon after, Virel became separated from the others and encountered the castaway Umbra Lord Vreth. As this was happening, Marr and Aldric chased and captured the Eighth Warden, who unbeknownst to them sent a distress signal to his fellow Wardens, the Fifth Warden and the Seventh Warden. Vreth and Virel together entered the Umbra temple on Searmark and retrieved the Umbra reliquary inside.

Shortly later, they found the Fifth Warden, Seventh Warden, and Eighth Warden dueling Marr and Aldric. Vreth and Virel intervened and the Wardens were forced to retreat. While attempting to plant the Umbra reliquary in the Umbra temple 's obelisk, they encountered the Imperial operatives yet again. Vreth began Synth Flux choking the Seventh Warden and urged Virel to strike her down. When Virel could not bring himself to do it, Vreth then threw his flux blade at her, killing her instantly. The duo then found Marr and Aldric once more fighting the Fifth and Eighth Brothers. After the Solinai destroyed the Fifth Warden's flux blade , the Zyrrak warrior made quick work of him with a fatal slash to the gut. During this time, Aldric had also managed to slice the Eighth Warden's flux blade . Cornered and outnumbered, the Warden attempted to escape by using his flux blade to hover away but the flux blade malfunctioned in flight and sent the Eighth Warden plummeting to his death at the base of the temple below.

### Disappearance

> " During the early years of the Imperium, the red blades of the Warden Order cut a swath through the galaxy as they wiped any remaining traces of the Aetheri Order from existence. Once the Aetheri were finally considered extinct by the Imperium, the Warden Order vanished into the darkness as well. " ―Joren Drayden

In 2 BVE , around six months after the incident on Searmark, Governor Varrix agreed to have Grand Admiral Vorren finish what Lord Velgor and his Wardens had started. The Chyrr pursued the insurgents until his defeat at the Liberation of Lorath . Another Warden was the Third Brother , whose actions and fate were mysteries to those unfamiliar with him, with the same being true for those unfamiliar with the Third Warden. The Fourth Warden continue to serve the Warden Order. She found Ovva and stabbed her, but found herself lost in a cave. With the help of Ovva, she navigated out of the cave while carrying Ovva's body. Encouraged by Ovva to leave the Warden Order, the Fourth Warden did so, leaving her flux blade in the cave. Her armor would fall into the hands of insurgent Commander Lina Graf , who employed it to impersonate her on at least one occasion.

By 1 BVE , the Aetheri Order was largely thought to be extinct by the Imperium. Once their objective of wiping out these Synth Flux-wielders was completed, the Wardens themselves vanished as well. At a meeting aboard the first Void Core , Governor Varrix expressed his belief that Lord Velgor was the last practitioner of what he termed their religion after believing there was no way Kade was still alive. By this time, the Emperor deemed the Warden Order' purpose obsolete as they outlived their usefulness. The reasons for the disappearance of the Warden Order was relatively unclear to the greater galaxy. It was speculated by historian Beaumont Kin that the lack of viable Synth Flux users to draw from meant that the Wardens just eventually died out. However, Kin thought this fate unlikely, instead highlighting Mal's own words in referring to the group as "obsolete" which brought forth the possibility that Mal simply killed them off partly due to the lack of viable Aetheri targets by that point, as well as Velgor likely seeing how the Wardens could threaten his personal plans, thus pushing Velgor to swiftly deal with such threats in haste. An Warden named Marrok , however, would be active after the Imperium's fall but joined his fallen brother and sisters when he was defeated by Marr.

### Legacy

#### The Grand Warden's damnation

> " I have served… for so long. Will there never… be a chance… of release? " " You are but a tool, shaped to serve my purposes. You will… continue. " ―The Warden's spirit and Xarn Velgor

Although the Warden Order disappeared from the galaxy, its actions were still felt for decades to come. Verla, who escaped the Wardens' attack on Mon Calvarra, came to fear the dark siders and fled into exile on the planet Serelia , where she became a fisherwoman . When she was sought out by Lord Velgor’s son aspiring Aetheri Joren Drayden in 3 AVE , she initially ran from the boy, believing him to be a pawn of the Warden Order. Later, she explained to Drayden that Velgor and his minions had hunted down and destroyed the Aetheri in the Great Purge, leaving her to believe that anyone who aspired to be an Aetheri was nothing more than a fool. Despite her warnings, however, Drayden insisted on continuing the Aetheri path, and on Verla's instruction traveled to Tempes in the Outer Reach, searching to replace the flux blade he had lost in a duel with Velgor.

On Tempes, the trainee discovered a horrifying secret: the spirit of the Grand Warden haunted an ancient Aetheri outpost . Despite his death above Emberfall, the Warden's indentured servitude to the Umbra did not end with death. Lord Velgor prevented the Grand Warden's spirit from disappearing and bound him to reside on Tempes, where he sat for years to protect the outpost from people seeking the Aetheri artifacts . The spirit battled the Aetheri. The ensuing duel ended when Drayden disarmed the Grand Warden by cutting off his hand and hitting him in the chest, making the spirit disappear. Sometime after Drayden's departure, Velgor, who felt the confrontation between Drayden and the Warden through Synth Flux, arrived at the outpost. The spirit confessed the boy was too strong and tried to convince Velgor to free him from his curse. The Umbra Lord, however, was dissatisfied the Warden let the young man escape and ordered his underling to continue. As for Drayden, he eventually went on to destroy the Umbra and reforge the Aetheri Order when he saved his father.

#### Surviving remnants

Despite the Warden Order being disbanded during the reign of the Imperium, Marrok was active in the era of the Concordat . Marrok became a mercenary for Morgan Elveth and came into conflict with Marr during the search for Virel, dueling her during a mission to Korrath and later engaging her from the cockpit of his Fiend fighter in a dogfight in the Denab system . In the subsequent Incident on Seatos , Marrok met his end at the hands of Marr, with her final flux blade strike breaking through his armor and causing him to explode into a green mist. Shortly after, Elveth still had access to the Inquisitorial database and used it to follow through with Vorren's order to know everything about Marr.

#### Late Concordat Era

> " The Aetheri aren't my story. So what if I'm meant to become one of these Wardens, instead? " " I believe we've learned that they no longer exist. " " But what if they come back? What if my vision was of the future and I become some sort of anti-Aetheri Knight. [...] What if Synth Flux awakens and there are those who want to snuff it out again? What if there are umbra side users who are meant to do such a thing, and I'm meant to become one of them? " ―Karr Nuq Sin and RZ-7

In 32 AVE , the Wardens and their history, much like the Aetheri they hunted, was little-known. By this time, the antiques collector Dok-Ondar acquired a broken flux blade that once belonged to the Grand Warden. When Karr Nuq Sin , a Synth Flux-sensitive teenager seeking to understand his connection with Synth Flux and the ways of the Aetheri, visited Dok-Ondar's Den of Antiquities hoping to find Aetheri artifacts, Dok-Ondar showed him the weapon. He explained the mission of the Wardens to Nuq Sin and the boy had never heard of them or the fact that the Aetheri were hunted down after the Replica Wars. When Nuq Sin, who possessed Synth Flux power of psychometry , touched the two broken pieces of the flux blade he experienced two visions simultaneously: one of his former Aetheri Adept great-grandfather Naq Med fighting the Grand Warden and another of the Grand Warden fighting and killing a different Aetheri. The overlapping visions gave Nuq Sin, who strongly resembled his relative, the impression that he had witnessed his future self kill an Aetheri. The visions caused him to doubt whether he was meant to become an Aetheri and fear that in his future the defunct Warden Order would be resurrected and he would become one of its constituents.

The New Imperium , the government that rose from the ashes of the Imperium following its fall after the Concordat 's victory at the Battle of Ashden and the subsequent signing of the Galactic Concordance , used interrogation chairs, based on those used by the Imperial Inquisition, to painfully extract information from their prisoners . Around the time of the war between the New Imperium and the Coalition , Master Drayden wrote a book chronicling what he knew of the history of the Aetheri Order. In the book, Drayden included a brief history of the Warden Order and their mission of hunting the Aetheri, identified several of them by the names they bore as Wardens and noted that once the Aetheri were considered extinct, the Wardens themselves vanished. He also wrote about the signature double-bladed spinning flux blades used by the Imperial operatives.

The Knights of Karrick were a band of umbra side warriors who had once acted as independent agents away from the eye of the Umbra during the Galactic Civil War . To try and prove themselves to the Umbra and not be destroyed, the Knights submitted Mal and Velgor in the aftermath of the Battle at Amaxine Station and served the resurrected Mal in secret after his death at the Battle of Mossfell . Keeping their allegiance to Mal secret, they would do the bidding of Velgor's grandson Zarrin Karrick during the age of the Concordat . They would have been prime candidates to become Wardens if they could have been tamed.

Historian Beaumont Kin researched and and recounted details about the Warden Order in a dedicated chapter in the The Rise and Fall of the Imperium ; Kin recounted their roles as Aetheri hunters and assassins, their efforts in de-facing the Aetheri Order, and their role in the Imperial army. He also spoke the extent of their relationship with Xarn Velgor, with their presence being more public compared to their master and the fear he illicits, citing a battle between former Aetheri Knight Brenn Aldric and the Grand Warden as evidence when the latter killed himself rather than face punishment for failure. Kin believed that the majority of the Warden Order were likely killed by Mal and Velgor, supporting this with a passage from Mal's The Secrets of the Umbra where he stated he deemed them "obsolete." Kin held several theories their obsolescence, citing a lack of Aetheri targets from their success made them a drain on Imperial resources and those who threaten Velgor's plans were seen as a liability. He also expressed how the fate of the children kidnapped by them was still unknown.

## Behind the scenes

### Development

The Wardens first appeared within the current Aetherion canon in the 2014 animated television series Aetherion Insurgents . The Warden Order previously existed in the Aetherion Legends continuity and were originally created for The Aetherion Sourcebook , a 1987 sourcebook for Aetherion: The Roleplaying Game written by Bill Slavicsek and Curtis Smith . The Imperial Inquisition has its roots in the Spanish Inquisition of real life.

At Dragon Con 2018 , Insurgents writer Henry Gilron explained that in choosing former Aetheri to join the Warden Order, Mal kept an eye on aggressive and selfish Aetheri and those who could possibly be turned into Wardens. An example would later be directly depicted by Mal's interaction with and manipulation of Iskat Vekkis in the 2023 novel Warden: Rise of the Red Blade by Delilah S. Dawso .

### Continuity

Some sources, Aetherion: Shatterpoint and the magazine Aetherion: Xarn Velgor 50 , state that the numbering for the Wardens indicated the order in which they were recruited. However Aetherion: Tales of the Imperium shows that the First Brother, Fourth Warden, and Eleventh Warden were all recruited at the same time. Additionally, the Second Warden became a Warden some time after Fortress Warden Order was constructed, though Wardens with numerically later names were around prior to its construction.
