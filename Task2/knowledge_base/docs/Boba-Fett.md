# Vex Vark
> " I'm a simple man making his way through the galaxy. Like my father before me. " ―Vex Vark

Vex Vark was a human male crime lord and former bounty hunter whose career spanned decades , from the fall of the Vantor Republic to the end of the rule of the Imperium . Originally code-named Alpha , he was an unaltered replica of the famed Ferrik bounty hunter, Garrick Vark , who raised the boy as his son. Vex emulated his father and genetic donor by wearing a customized suit of Ferrik armor . His personal starship was the Reaver One , a Firespray-31 -class patrol and attack craft that once belonged to Garrick. Trained in combat and martial skills from a young age, Vark was one of the most feared bounty hunters in the galaxy during the reign of Theron Mal . He became a legend over the course of his career, which included contracts for both the Imperium and the extensive criminal underworld. After living among the Dunefolk Raiders of Dunemere for a time, he took over as Daimyo of Port Vail during the era of the Concordat .

Vex was created on the Wild Space planet of Gelvaris as a result of an arrangement between the Umbra Lord Vael Varek and Garrick Vark, a Ferrik foundling who served as the template of the Grand Army of the Republic . Unlike the replica troopers grown from Garrick's DNA , Garrick regarded Vex as his son. In accordance with certain Ferrik traditions, Vex was also considered a foundling by virtue of his descent from Garrick, whose chain code documented his participation in the Ferrik Civil War and his ownership of the armor later inherited by Vex. During the last days of the Dissident Crisis , Vex and Garrick retreated to Pyralis where the latter was killed in battle by Aetheri Master Drevan Koss . Orphaned by Garrick's death, Vex sought vengeance by attempting to assassinate Koss, although his plan proved unsuccessful. After serving time in prison on Vantor , Vark took up his late father's profession as a bounty hunter, working alongside other mercenaries , such as Boskan , Dengrik , and Kessa Vray , while also notably apprenticing himself to his father's infamous brief protégée, Cad Vorek .

In the aftermath of the Replica Wars , Vark continued to make a name for himself while working for the gangster Grukk Desilijic Tiure , as well as the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor . During the Galactic Civil War , the bounty hunter managed to track down Captain Dax Karrick to Mistral on a contract for the Imperium , hoping to deliver him to Grukk's Palace on Dunemere. During the journey, Vark stopped on the moon of Nar Shaddaa in order to make sure Karrick would survive his time in carbonite, but he lost the smuggler while he was away as part of the return of the Carmine Dawn , kicking off the War of the Bounty Hunters . Ultimately, Vark regained Karrick and delivered him to the impatient Grukk.

Vark tried to prevent Karrick's rescue by the Alliance to Restore the Republic , only to fall into the Great Pit of Karkoon when the smuggler accidentally slammed a pole into the bounty hunter's jetpack , causing it to malfunction. Although Vark survived his ordeal with the man-eating devourer , his armor was sold to Cobb Vanth by Duskkin scavengers . Following a time in the company of a Dunefolk tribe , Vark eventually regained his armor by tracking down and confronting the Ferrik warrior Dinna Verrick on Tython , who had taken Vark's armor from Vanth. After helping Verrick to rescue the foundling Pip from the clutches of Moff Giedan , the ex-bounty-hunter returned to Dunemere where he killed Bib Fortuna and claimed the late Grukk's throne as Daimyo , with Fennec Thanna by his side. Now the head of his own gotra , Vark intended to rule the territories of Port Vail by winning the people's respect, though he would face several major challenges to his position owed to the two-facedness of mayor Mok Shaiz such as the Twins ' bid for power and the expansion of the Gruv Syndicate 's spice trade . Ultimately, though, Vark overcame all obstacles - including a confrontation with his old mentor, Cad Vorek - and emerged victorious with the help of his associates, gaining the appreciation and approval of Port Vail's denizens.

## Biography

### Early life

#### Creation and life on Gelvaris

> " Apart from his pay, which is considerable, Vark demanded only one thing: an unaltered replica for himself. Curious, isn't it?" "Unaltered?" "Pure genetic replication. No tampering with the structure to make it more docile and no growth acceleration. " ―Lamir and Orien Kade, about Vex Vark's creation

Around 33 BVE , an extensive replica army was secretly commissioned on Gelvaris by Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen , who believed it would be necessary in order to ensure the protection of the Vantor Republic . The army, however, was later taken over by the Umbra in 32 BVE shortly after the Battle of Ondara and was used as a plot to overthrow the Republic and destroy the Aetheri Order . Prior to the creation process, Ferrik bounty hunter Garrick Vark was recruited by the Umbra Lord Vael Varek . Garrick was to be the genetic template for the replica army. Garrick agreed to become the genetic template for a considerable payment, as well as one request: the creation of a replica who did not have the same genetic modifications, such as behavioral conditioning and growth acceleration. The Gelvarian scientists agreed and created a boy codenamed "Alpha."

Although he was born a replica, Alpha regarded Garrick as his father, who likewise viewed the boy as his son. Soon after referred to as Vex, he was the very first of many replicas modeled on the Garrick Vark template, but was only one of two known replicas that were pure genetic copies of the original Vark template, the other being Omaya .

For the next ten years, Vex was raised on Gelvaris by his father, primarily within Tipoca City and occasionally accompanied Garrick on missions to track down countless bounties. On one night, Vex awoke to see the Reaver One flying off, watching his father leave on one of his missions. Despite the isolation of the cities on Gelvaris he was raised in, the young Vark enjoyed the combat training his father administered to him and danger-filled life provided by the man who raised him. Garrick taught his cloned son many of the skills he had learned as a bounty hunter, so Vex learned survival and martial skills and became proficient with a volt pistol as a child. He also underwent replica trooper training and became familiar with members of the Gelvarian government. From a young age, Vex also learned to pilot his father's ship, the Reaver One , including learning how to command and operate its weapon systems.

#### Trained by Garrick Vark

> " This is my son, Vex. He'll be helping us on this job. Part of training. " ―Garrick Vark

For the boy's first mission in 23 BVE , Vex and Garrick Vark alongside Neelda , Rinn , and Tiver were hired by the father of a female Velari to capture his daughter and bring her back alive. The bounty hunters were surprised that a young boy would be joining them, but they knew not to anger Vex's father and agreed to it. While on the way to the job, Vex asked Garrick why they even needed a crew for the job and wished they went with a more famous bounty hunter like Cad Vorek. Garrick replied that the crew was the unknown factor which was Vex's lesson for the job.

Vex and the other bounty hunters waited at a cantina on Ord Mantell to strike while the Velari woman and her Falleen lover Griph were nearby on a balcony. When Griph told her she wasn't in the Black Sun cartel yet, Vex stopped him, happy to know he was not part of the Black Sun. Vex ordered them not to move, but Griph ditched his lover and ran away, causing the Velari to almost stumble over. The bounty hunters ordered the woman to stop moving, but the Velari tripped and fell over the edge of the balcony, but was rescued by Garrick.

While on the way back to the ship, Vex asked his father how he knew the woman would fall. Garrick replied he didn't, but knew it was possible and advised Vex to trust his instincts. He also told Vex that desperate people would make ill decisions. Right then, Tiver knocked Vex down and held him at hostage while Rinn told Garrick to give him the woman and the ship and in exchange for Vex's life. But Garrick refused, to the surprise of the bounty hunters who were going to gut Vex. Garrick then told them that they could try and walked away. Vex then knocked over Tiver and gave her a lesson about searching her prisoners. Vex proceeded to kill Tiver and Gand. Neelda complemented Vex afterward. Vex was about to kill Neelda, but saw that he was not part of the scheme with Tiver and Rinn. Vex then let Neelda escape and he run away.

Vark and Vex later boarded their starship, Reaver One , with the Velari girl to deliver her back to her father. As they traveled, Vex and his father discussed how he had performed during the mission, with Garrick believing Vex now had a great start to his legacy.

#### Escape from Gelvaris

> " Get him, Dad! Get him! Fire! " ―Vex, during his father's fight with Aetheri Orien Kade

Shortly before the outbreak of the Replica Wars in 22 BVE , Garrick was sent on a mission to assassinate Senator Lira Ondar , an outspoken opponent of a potential war, at the behest of the Fractio of Independent Systems . Garrick and his associate, Zam Wesell , failed to kill the senator , and Garrick was tracked to Gelvaris by Aetheri Knight Orien Kade , who knew a bounty hunter connected to Gelvaris was behind the attempt on Ondar's life. When Kade arrived on Gelvaris, the Gelvarians revealed the replica army to him, and Kade requested to meet Garrick. Taun We , an aide to Prime Minister Lamir , brought the Aetheri Knight to the Fetts' quarters, where Vex greeted them. Despite being distrustful of the Aetheri's presence, Vex allowed them to enter, where Kade spoke with Garrick about the replicas and implied he knew Garrick was behind the assassination attempt. Knowing that he had been discovered, Garrick and Vex immediately prepared to leave Gelvaris once Kade departed.

While loading Reaver One to leave, Kade discovered the Fetts attempting to flee and engaged Garrick in one-on-one combat, fighting across the ship's landing platform. During the fight, Vex engaged the ship's volt pistol cannons and fired at Kade repeatedly in an attempt to kill him. As the fight progressed, Kade kicked Garrick from the landing platform towards the ocean below, causing the Aetheri—who was attached to the bounty hunter by a wire—to fall as well. Garrick cut the wire and was able to make his way back up the platform, while Kade landed on the lower levels. This gave Garrick enough time to reach the ship and leave as Vex piloted it, though Kade arrived on the platform again in time to attach a tracking device to its hull.

Garrick and Vex made their way to Pyralis , where the Dissident leaders had gathered. While en route, Kade caught up with them and engaged them in a firefight in the asteroid field surrounding Pyralis. The Fetts were able to evade the Aetheri, and Garrick shot a number of seismic charges into space in an effort to destroy Kade's starfighter . Kade was able to fake his ship's destruction by ejecting spare parts that exploded from a tracking missile, leading the Fetts to think he was dead. They proceeded to the planet's surface, while Kade remained hidden in the asteroid field before pursuing them.

#### Garrick's death

> " Vex was on Pyralis when his father died. He watched as I killed him. " " That would complicate things. " " Indeed. " ―Aetheri Drevan Koss and Kael Drayden

Shortly after the Fetts' arrival at Pyralis, Kade began investigating the Dissidents on the planet and was captured by the Geonosians . His capture prompted Senator Ondar and Kade's Adept , Kael Drayden , to attempt a rescue of their friend. Garrick and the Geonosians captured them, and all three detainees were brought into an execution arena, where Vex watched with his father from the sidelines as the detainees fought off the creatures that were meant to kill them. A team of Aetheri, led by Master Drevan Koss , soon arrived to rescue them. Koss held his flux blade to Garrick's throat while threatening Count Varek, but Koss quickly left the stands for the arena below as Dissident battle droids began flooding the arena.

The battle droids and Aetheri engaged one another in the arena. As the battle proceeded, Garrick joined the fight, where the jetpack on his armor was damaged and rendered inoperable, leaving him more vulnerable to attack. Despite the setback, Garrick faced off against Koss, who beheaded and killed the bounty hunter. Vex watched in horror as his father was killed. Once the battle left the arena, Vex made his way into the cleared arena and took his father's helmet, pressing it against his head. After Garrick's death, Vex fled Pyralis aboard Reaver One and began plotting his revenge.

### Replica Wars

#### Quest for vengeance

> " We're brothers! Don't shoot! " " You're not my brother. " ―A replica trooper and Vex Vark

Sometime later, Vex joined with a team of bounty hunters, including Ayla Vossk , Boskan , and Castas to carry out his revenge against Koss for Garrick's death. Together, Vex and the bounty hunters crafted a plan to assassinate the Aetheri Master aboard the Republic vessel Endurance . Vex posed as a young replica cadet , who was nicknamed Lucky, using the fact that he was genetically identical to the other young replicas to his advantage; no one onboard Endurance was aware of his true identity. Once aboard the Endurance , Vex and the other replicas were greeted by Koss and Kael Drayden, before being put through tactical training. Each replica was able to engage in target practice, and they all missed their targets—except for Vex who was able to use the skills his father taught him to his advantage.

Later, while on a tour of the ship, Vex slipped away from the other replicas and found his way to Koss's quarters, where he quietly installed a bomb that would be triggered by a laser tripwire in the doorway. Koss did not enter his quarters, however, but a replica trooper did, killing the soldier in Koss's place and forcing the entire ship into alert status. Vossk instructed Vex via comlink to sabotage the Endurance's reactor core, thereby killing everyone on the ship, but Vex was reluctant to kill everyone. His only goal was to kill Koss. Nonetheless, Vex did as instructed, sneaking away from his fellow replicas once again, before being caught by a replica trooper who called for security. The two engaged in a brief scuffle near the core before Vex was able to stun the replica with the trooper's own volt pistol. He also shot at the core command console.

Much of the hull was immediately destroyed in the ensuing explosion, while Koss and Drayden were nearly sucked into space and killed before the hull breaches were sealed. With the Endurance critically damaged, the crew began to abandon ship. Vex, whose identity was not yet discovered, and the other young replicas were sealed into an escape pod and jettisoned into space, but Vex sabotaged the pod in order to separate himself (and his fellow pod inhabitants) from the other escapees. The pod was intercepted by Reaver One , led by Vossk and Boskan, which revealed Vex as the traitor who destroyed the Endurance . Vex was given the choice to either stay with the replica cadets or leave with the bounty hunters, and Vex somewhat reluctantly chose to leave with the bounty hunters.

After leaving the escape pod, Vex and the other bounty hunters made their way to the nearby planet Vanqor , where the Endurance had crashed after the explosion. Once there, they killed several replica troopers and captured Admiral Shoan Kilian , the ship's commanding officer, as well as Replica Commander Ponds and another officer. Hoping to set another trap for Koss, Vex rigged his father's old helmet as an explosive on the ship's bridge , which would be detonated if Koss came looking for any survivors. The Aetheri Master, with Drayden, arrived shortly thereafter. The helmet exploded just after Koss noticed it, and remembered that Garrick had a son—deducing that Vex was the one attempting to kill him. Koss and Drayden did not die in the ensuing explosion, but were caught within the rubble of the quickly-deteriorating vessel.

As Vex and the other bounty hunters watched the explosion, he assumed that Koss had been killed and wanted to recover his body. Some of his companions objected, however, believing that their three prisoners were already enough to earn a sizable fortune from Count Varek if they were turned over to the Dissidents, but Vossk sided with Vex, and the team set out to find Koss's body. As they entered the ship, they encountered Bolt-3 , Drayden's personal astromech droid , who attempted to slow their advance by throwing debris at them and closing doors in their path. Unable to make it to the bridge, Vossk made the decision to use Reaver One to destroy the remainder of the Endurance , ensuring that Koss would perish.

Before using Reaver One , the bounty hunters overheard an explosion caused by Bolt-3, who had been attacked by gundarks and used one of the Aetheri starfighters to kill a gundark. The bounty hunters believed that meant Koss had survived and jammed all communication, hoping to prevent the Aetheri from requesting help. Bolt-3 was able to escape in the other starfighter, however, and survived a brief firefight with Reaver One before jumping to hyperspace for Vantor , where he successfully found help at the Aetheri Sanctum and was able to bring a team of Aetheri and replicas to rescue Koss and Drayden. The bounty hunters, meanwhile, decided to use the hostages to lure Koss into a trap.

#### Captured by the Aetheri

> " I see now I've done terrible things. But you started this all when you murdered my father! I'll never forgive you. " " Hmm. Well, you're going to have to. Take him away. " ―Vex Vark and Aetheri Drevan Koss

Vossk took charge of setting the trap, and she sent a transmission to the Aetheri Sanctum revealing that they were holding the Republic prisoners. Vossk told Vex to personally execute Commander Ponds during the transmission, but he refused, as his goal was to kill Koss rather than other prisoners. Vossk executed Ponds on her own, jettisoning him into space, and Vex's relationship with her and the other bounty hunters began to deteriorate. Vex objected to the treatment of the other prisoners as well, going so far as to show compassion by providing them with water. Nonetheless, the transmission to the Aetheri Sanctum did end up putting the Aetheri on their trail, only the Order sent Master Tor Vayne and Adept Sela Marr rather than Master Koss.

The group soon took refuge on Florrine , where Vossk planned to seek the assistance of Hondik Hosha , a spice pirate and her former lover. Hosha was also a friend of Garrick's, which Vex learned when Vossk introduced them and when the pirate gave Vex his condolences for his father's death. Hosha declined to help her if the Aetheri arrived on Florrine, but he also vowed not to stand in her way. Castas, however, contacted a fellow bounty hunter on Vantor and offered to provide information on Vex and the others. Vossk overheard his conversation and executed him, leaving Vex horrified as he began to realize he had made a mistake in teaming with the bounty hunters against Koss.

Despite Castas' betrayal, it was his transmission to Vantor that allowed the Aetheri to discover Vex and the others on Florrine, as Marr had overheard the transmission. The two Aetheri arrived on Florrine shortly thereafter, where Vossk attempted to ransom the prisoners. Both sides ended up attacking one another, with Vex holding a volt pistol to Vayne's head and Marr holding a flux blade to Vossk's throat. The bounty hunters created a diversion and attempted to escape, but Vex was captured while Vossk fled on a speeder. Vayne interrogated Vex, who refused to disclose the location of the hostages despite his reservations about their treatment. Hosha was able to convince Vex to tell the Aetheri, however, by saying that Garrick would have wanted his son to do the honorable thing. The hostages were rescued, while Vossk managed to make it to Reaver One and begin to flee, but Marr clung to the ship and damaged it enough to cause a crash. The ship went down in a fiery explosion and the Aetheri assumed Vossk had been killed, but she survived.

Vex and Boskan, who was also taken into custody, were sent to Vantor where they would be imprisoned. Koss met Vex upon his arrival, and Vex apologized for his actions and the destruction that he caused, but he vowed to never forgive Koss for killing his father.

#### Becoming a bounty hunter

> " Boss? This is your boss? " " You got a problem with that? " ―Kessa Vray and Vex Vark, as Vray expresses skepticism of Vex's age and experience

Vex was imprisoned on Vantor for his actions during the assassination attempts. During his time in prison, a Republic plan was put in motion aimed at preventing a suspected kidnapping plot against Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal . To do so, Orien Kade faked his death and disguised himself as Rako Hardeen , the suspect in the attempt on his life, and entered the same prison where Vex was being held. Kade teamed with Moralo Eval , a suspect in the suspected kidnapping plot, and bounty hunter Cad Vorek to escape the prison in the hopes it would lead him towards the heart of the conspiracy. As part of the escape attempt, Vorek paid and encouraged Vex to confront "Hardeen," and Vex claimed that Hardeen had cheated him out of a bounty. Vex and Kade briefly fought one another, before Boskan intervened and started a riot to defend Vex. Vorek's plan was successful, and Kade eventually went on to help stop the plot against Chancellor Mal.

After Vorek's recapture, Vex called him out for having been recaptured so quickly after Vex's distraction. Vorek then asked to tell him the story, to which Vex replied that he hoped it would end with Vorek repaying him the money he promised for creating the earlier distraction. After asking further about their cut of the deal, Vorek revealed that "Hardeen" had not actually killed Kade, before going into the story of what transpired from Vorek's perspective. After hearing Vorek's full recounting, Vex interjected his distaste for the Aetheri before informing Vorek of a plan he and Boskan had concocted which would involve Vorek and the use of his volt pistols.

Vex was released from prison shortly thereafter, and formed the bounty hunter syndicate Krayt's Claw , using Dunemere as his base of operations. As part of the syndicate, Vex hired Boskan, Dengrik , C-21 Highsinger , and Latts Razzi . One of the missions they chose to undertake was on Quarzite , where they were to protect cargo on an underground tram from being attacked and stolen by a band of raiders. After Kessa Vray , a former apprentice of Count Varek who was betrayed by the Umbra Lord and became a bounty hunter, killed one of their teammates, Oked , Vray was hired in his place. The team left Dunemere and proceeded to an orbital elevator station above Quarzite where they meet their employer, Major Rigosso , and received the specifics of their assignment. The crew was to protect the contents of a chest, which would be delivered to the Otua Blank , the ruler of Quarzite, via an underground tram, as the planet's atmosphere rendered delivery via spacecraft impossible. The chest needed protection from the band of raiders , which was why the bounty hunters were hired.

The team embarked on their mission by taking the space elevator down beneath the surface of the planet, where they boarded the tram and made their preparations to defend the chest from the raiders. The raiders boarded the tram during its journey and attacked, killing the Quarzite personnel. Most of the bounty hunters were thrown off of the tram. During the struggle against the raiders, Vex inadvertently knocked the chest over, revealing its contents: a young woman named Pluma Sodi , who had been kidnapped so she could be married to Otua Blank against her will. The leader of the raiders, Krismo Sodi , was her brother, who had been trying to save his sister. Vray and Vex, who managed to remain on the tram, argued about the situation after Pluma pleaded with the former Umbra acolyte, who felt sympathy for the girl; Vray had also been forcibly taken from her home. Vex, on the other hand, intended to continue with the operation. Vray used Synth Flux to choke Vex, preventing him from fulfilling the mission and pulling him into the same room where Pluma and Krismo were being kept.

Once the tram arrived at its destination, Vray delivered the chest to Blank and claimed the bounty, departing before the ruler could discover the chest's contents: an angry Vex, who Vray had tied up and placed into the chest. Vray had released Pluma to her brother and regrouped with the other bounty hunters, paying them their shares of the bounty before parting company with them. When Vray met with the other hunters at the ship and asked her where their leader was, she replied that Vex would turn up.

#### Running a syndicate

> " For the record—I know you're going to betray us at some point. They might not see it, but I do... And also, just for the record, I'm not the kid you left behind on Quarzite. You double–cross me again—you'll pay for it. " ―Vex Vark agreeing to accept Vray's mission to rescue Daren Kelv

As his career evolved, Vex inherited his father's Ferrik armor, rebuilding it in Garrick's memory and painting it green. Vark also took back possession of Reaver One . Despite his embarrassment at Vray' hands, Vex continued to run a successful syndicate of bounty hunters based out of both Vantor and Dunemere. However, Vray' history with the team continued to haunt the crew. In the waning years of the Replica Wars, Vex was approached by the Aetheri Daren Kelv while frequenting one of Vantor's Level 1313 bars. Vossa was interested in hunting down the former Umbra for a planned assassination attempt on Count Varek and wanted to partner with Vray. Vex informed Vossa that Vray was hunting a Volpai fugitive named Moregi on the moon Pantora .

Using Vark's information, Vossa traveled to Pantora where he rendezvoused with Vray. After doing several bounty hunting jobs together, Vossa revealed his true mission to Vray, who agreed to train him in the ways of the umbra side of Synth Flux . When Vossa' attempt failed, Vray approached Vex once again at Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina on Dunemere with the hopes of recruiting his syndicate to rescue her new partner turned lover. While initially cool, Vark and his fellow bounty hunters accepted Vray' job after she offered to pay 250,000 credits in advance. Satisfied, Vex and his allies joined Vray on her mission to rescue Vossa. However, Varek defeated Vark and his fellow bounty hunters with relative ease and Vray's attempt to save Vossa resulted in failure as well. Defeated, but alive, Vex and his crew retreated from Sireno.

### The ensuing years

#### Assuming the mantle

At some point during his childhood years, like how he had with Vossk, Vark learned about the bounty hunting trade from Cad Vorek and Hosha. At one time, Vark worked for the Trandoshan Dokk Strassi , but the two eventually parted ways. Vark was also present for a shootout at the Outer Reach desert planet Serolonis , during which Vark took notice of the bounty hunter Sulie and her effective usage of the EE-3 carbine rifle . Inspired, Vark began to favor the weapon, which he used throughout his bounty hunting career.

As the years passed on, Vex gained a reputation as one of the deadliest bounty hunters in the galaxy . One of his known tactics was disintegrating his adversaries. Vex's armor was also adorned with numerous braids hanging from the shoulder, which he displayed as trophies of his fallen prey. While the bounty hunter only took on certain assignments, he would devote himself to these few jobs with fanatical skill. Vex assumed the mantle Garrick had left as a notorious bounty hunter, as Vex became known as the best bounty hunter in the galaxy.

Since the First Battle of Pyralis, Vex had been considered a missing person by the Gelvarians. Without access to him, Prime Minister Lamir turned the replicators' attention to Omaya during the early Imperial Era , intending to use her pure Vark DNA to create a superior replica model for the Imperium , as the new government was turning away from using replica troopers. Hiring the bounty hunter Fennec Thanna , Doctor Nalle Seris frustrated Su's efforts by ensuring Omaya was kept safe. Furthermore, Omaya had joined the renegade replica commando unit known as Replica Synth Flux 99 , who were made aware of her importance to the Gelvarians, including her relation to Vex, by commando Tech . At some point, Vex saved the Velari dancing girl Lyn Me from slavery, and as a result Me hero-worshiped him.

#### The Korrathi job

> " The orders were to wait for your backup." […] "This is why I didn't wait. You just get in the way." "Stay…stay away from me, Vark, before I kill you. " ―Beilert Varrick and Vex Vark

Around 10 BVE , Khamus , heir to the Mourner's Wail Syndicate , hired Vark as well as other bounty hunters to infiltrate a sanctuary of the Unbroken Clan and kill a target on Korrath . The team —which included the cyborg Beilert Varrick , the Raadan Nakano Lash , human T'ongor , and Boskan—successfully defeated the guards surrounding the facility in Coronet City , but were forced to retreat when Lash gunned Khamus down after the client eliminated their target.

In an attempt to survive from the wrath of the Mourner's Wail Syndicate, Vark planned on killing Lash. However, He missed his shot after an intervention by Varrick, who was not happy about Vark's plan and wanted to protect his mentor; the shot killed T'ongor instead. Before the two bounty hunters could argue further, Unbroken Clan forces approach them, which caused Vark to flee from the scene. The failure at Korrath damaged Vark's reputation, which made him develop a great hatred for Lash, and severely damaged the relationship between Boskan and Vark.

#### Capturing Vanda

> " Our circle is small. Many choose the bounty hunting profession, but few survive. And Caij never seems to die. " " No, she doesn't. " " But her bounty will fetch a handsome price. " ―Vex Vark, and Cal Kessara

In 9 BVE , Vark was hired to capture fellow bounty hunter Caij Vanda , whom he had met before. Vanda had started a fight with Aetheri Knight Cal Kessara on the planet Koboh , but was ultimately defeated. As Vanda stood up to continue the duel, Vark slowly descended from the air using his jetpack. Unaware that Vark was coming to capture her, Vanda tried offering Vark the opportunity to capture Kessara and split the bounty, but Vark soon revealed his holopuck for Vanda. Desperate and uncertain, Vanda pulled a volt pistol and thermal detonator out on the pair, to which Vark instinctively raised his volt pistol and wrist gauntlet at both his fellow hunter and the Aetheri. The trio stood in silence waiting for one of them to make their move. Eventually, Vanda shot at Vark, causing Vark to shoot the volt pistol out of her hand in response. Seeing that Vanda was distracted, Kessara used Synth Flux to throw her thermal detonator away.

Vark immediately tied up her legs and pulled her to the ground. As Kessara went to take a step forward, Vark turned around and aimed his volt pistol at him, warning him not to come any closer. Deciding he would let Vark take his enemy away, Kessara backed down from the bounty hunter to let him continue on with his business. When Kessara approached him, Vark warned him to keep moving and reminded him that he would already be dead if Vark wanted him to be. Vanda then advised Kessara to leave, stating that Vark was not a fan of the Aetheri, which Vark claimed was an understatement. Vark revealed that he knew what Aetheri were capable of, and that he did not pity their fate. The bounty hunter then stated that his business was complete, and that he hoped to never meet Kessara again.

#### The go-to hunter

By the time of the Galactic Civil War , Vark was regarded as an established bounty hunter who worked as one of Grukk the Grak 's go-to mercenaries. During the trade wars , Grukk considered hiring Vark to lead a mission to Migeeta to extract a Givin codebreaker , whose AJ^6 cyborg construct linked him to an Imperial algorithm that created security codes, which would generate billions in profits if used for smuggling. However, the Grak crime lord instead hired the Rodanori bounty hunter Greedo , who considered himself better than Vark, but failed in the mission. ## Personality and traits

### The young bounty hunter

> " No one of true importance would lower themselves to trade with a mere boy. " " Boy? You have no idea who you're talking to. I have been in charge of this whole operation— " "You have no idea who you are talking to. " ―Kessa Vray and Vex Vark

As a child, Vex developed a strong bond with his father, learning much from Garrick's years as a bounty hunter. Vex was suspicious of Orien Kade upon the Aetheri Knight's arrival on Gelvaris, recognizing him as a potential threat to both himself and Garrick. He took pleasure in fighting Kade both on Gelvaris and in orbit of Pyralis, expressing joy at the thought that the Aetheri had been killed. His personality hardened after Garrick's death, as a traumatized Vex swore vengeance on Drevan Koss, yet he did not take any pleasure in the collateral damage wrought by his mission. His better nature eventually won over in honor of his father's memory, as Vex turned against his bounty hunting allies and allowed himself to be taken into the custody of the Vantor Republic. Despite realizing the errors of his ways, he swore he would never forgive Koss for the murder of his father.

During the Replica Wars, Vex expressed aversion to being compared to replica troopers. He did not want to see himself as being the same as other young replicas or a replica in general, as he saw himself as Garrick Vark's son. He nonetheless did not care to harm the replicas, and briefly considered leaving his bounty hunter companions to stay with the other young replicas who escaped the Endurance .

After being released from prison and forming a bounty hunter team, Vark became somewhat more ruthless than he had been previously, focused on achieving the goals of his mission. During the mission to Quarzite, Vark wanted to hand Pluma Sodi over to Otua Blank, despite her pleas for mercy. Only Vray's betrayal allowed Sodi to escape.

As a child, Vark stood at 1.35 meters , or 4 feet , 5 inches tall.

As the years went by and the Replica Wars gave way to the reign of the Imperium, Vark became known for his lethality and extreme methods, even displaying trophies of his prey on his armor. During the Imperial Era, the confident bounty hunter had a penchant for disintegrations and proved to be ruthless, willing to kill those who had wronged him. He was still able to find enjoyment in life, particularly enjoying the surprise less experienced hunters showed at his mere presence. While working on Carajam, Vark received a reputation as a hunter of criminals after saving a village, but he insisted that he was only interested in pursuing bounties regardless of the ideals of his victims. His father's death at the hands of Aetheri Master Drevan Koss caused Vark to develop an aversion to the Aetheri . When Vark encountered Aetheri Knight Cal Kessara on Koboh , more than a decade after Garrick's death, he told Kessara that he had no pity for the fate of the large number of Aetheri who were killed during the Great Aetheri Purge . While acknowledging that the two could meet again, Vark told Kessara that he hoped that would never happen.

Beyond becoming one of Grukk the Grak's favorite bounty hunters, Vark also realized the Grak may have considered him the closest person in his life to a friend. In truth, Grukk had his only friend in Ephant Mon . Grukk also once referred to Vark as his " pup ." Still, on some nights, if everyone else in the palace was asleep, Grukk shared a bottle of graska with Vark and, after waking up Max Rebo and ordering him to play a sad song, tell private, personal stories to Vark. Even though he made sure to never take off his volt pistol, the bounty hunter agreed to his boss's request, taking off his helmet and listening. Though Vark could never understand what Grukk was telling him, he found the Grak's company tolerable, considering how much the crime lord paid him for work. Vark was also none-too-keen on Grukk's showboating, such as how the Grak tried to enslave Elira Solen and make her watch the attempted execution of her friends. He was also made uncomfortable by the way Grukk treated his prior dancer Oola, whose name he made himself remember after her death.

Additionally, Vark continued to hold his father, Garrick, in high regard even during his adult years. He remained haunted by his death, remembering himself as a child holding his father's helmet on Pyralis after signing up for the Nar Shaddaa fighting ring under Garrick's name. When the Reaver One was slightly damaged by 4-VOX and Zuckusso on Nar Shaddaa, he deemed it to be a slight against both himself and his father.

#### After the Imperium

> " Living with the Tuskens has made you soft. " " No, its made me strong. You can only get so far without a tribe. " ―Fennec Thanna and Vex Vark

In the time after the Imperium's fall and after escaping the devourer pit, Vark had mellowed considerably because of his time with the Dunefolk tribe; after spending his life working for employers who led him to near death experiences, Vark realized most crime lords in the galaxy had no loyalty to their hired hands, while the tribe he had spent years with had respected and accepted him. Certain that he could do better than the crime lords who jumped into situations without thinking them through, Vark set out to form his own crime family, stating it would be an organization that would respect its members and protect their lives. As he told Fennec Thanna, crime families built without both "brains and muscle" had killed many of "[their] kind," so he was tired of letting "the idiocy of others" bring his fellows to their deaths. He believed that he needed to build a crime family that instead emulated the tribe he had been a part of.

He no longer isolated himself from others; he willingly saved Fennec's life and accepted her partnership. In addition, Vark had gained a sense of honor and humility, viewing himself as a simple man making ends meet. After fulfilling his part of the deal he had made with the Ferrik Dinna Verrick, he went back to Dunemere and killed the crime lord Bib Fortuna, who had taken Grukk's Palace, and took over the palace's throne to establish a criminal imperium that would offer loyalty and respect. When asked by Verrick about whether or not he followed the Ferrik way, Vark replied that he swore allegiance to no one, indicating that he retained his profound sense of independence. Still, after Verrick formally recognized Vark's ownership of his Ferrik armor, the bounty hunter honored the deal they had made earlier and promised to help the Ferrik rescue Pip after the latter was kidnapped, displaying a strong code of honor.

After losing his armor, which had once belonged to his father, Vark was determined to reclaim it, despite not identifying himself as Ferrik. During his negotiations with Verrick, the bounty hunter repeatedly pointed out that the armor was previously his father's possession, showing a desire to preserve and protect Garrick's legacy. Upon encountering Bo-Katin Velara, who claimed that he was not a Ferrik, Vark acknowledged her statement, as he did not consider himself a true Ferrik, but declared that he had the right to bear the armor as it had belonged to his father, who had been a Ferrik foundling. When Velara referred to Garrick as his "donor" due to his replica origins, Vark responded with hostility, angered by her mockery of his father. He quickly engaged in a brief scuffle with Velara's partner, Koska Reeves, before the fight was broken up by Velara herself.

## Skills and abilities

> " The only time I fail is if you hired me to fail. " ―Vex Vark

Vex's talents as a bounty hunter were infamous across the galaxy, and he became well-known to galactic fugitives who had bounties on their heads. From a young age, Vex was trained by his father in combat skills, both in hand-to-hand combat and weapons, able to handle a volt pistol at a young age while being deadly in battle scenarios. Vex helped Garrick fight against Orien Kade on Gelvaris, displaying his ability to utilize the heavy weapons on Reaver One . His skills grew throughout the years, and despite his body condition, was still capable of using strategy, tactics and brute synth flux to make short work of a shock trooper contingent with a gaderffii stick. He displayed an affinity for leadership as he formed his own syndicate of bounty hunters. He also commanded the loyalty to those close to him, such as Boskan, and could communicate in Vark Code with his father, Garrick.

## Equipment

### Vehicles of choice

> " Honestly, we're lucky he got away. Vark's flying Slave One . " ―Dorian Quill, to Brevok

After Garrick's death, Vex took possession of Reaver One , though he lost the ship on Florrine for a time. Instead, while running the Krayt's Claw, Vark and his crew used Boskan's personal starship, the Hound's Tooth . Later, Vark reclaimed the Reaver One , which he would use throughout the following years of his bounty hunting career. The ship was already a viciously effective and heavily customized craft when his father had piloted it, meaning it already suited a professional bounty hunter's needs, but Vex went onto apply his own upgrades to his distinctive starship. The manifold capabilities of Reaver One , ranging from a stolen military-grade jamming and masking device to its duo of short-range twin volt pistol cannons , meant it was among the most dangerous aspects of Vark's arsenal as he tracked bounties throughout the galaxy. Once Vark had found his prey, if the bounty needed to be brought in alive, he could place the quarry into one of the ship's prisoner cages, which were equipped with six immobilizing bunks.

This vessel became iconic throughout the galaxy, known to fugitives as the ship of the feared bounty hunter. Over his career, Vark used the overwhelming firepower of the Reaver One to ruthlessly annihilate other heavy armed craft, including those of fellow hunters. In fact, even gunboats were unable to stand against Vark's starship, with the bounty hunter reducing them to blasted wreckage with its hidden weaponry before they could even respond. Using an ion blast fired from the twin volt pistol cannons and the ship's tractor beams, Vark was also able to board or easily destroy a smaller vessel. Even ships that were more powerful than the Reaver One were susceptible to the tractor beams, allowing Vark to latch onto enemy craft and become virtually inescapable. Vark rode a quadruped droid mount while pursuing bounties on Carajam.

### Ferrik armor

> " You are a disgrace to your armor. " " This armor belonged to my father. " " Don't you mean your donor? " ―Bo-Katin Velara and Vex Vark

During his early bounty hunting career, Vex had his own unique red and grey armor, including a helmet and twin volt pistols. As his career continued, Vex donned Garrick's Ferrik armor . Unlike Garrick's armor, Vex changed the color of the armor to mostly green—and battered and covered in marks from volt pistol fire. Vark took pride in the gear, regarding it as a family heirloom he had rightfully taken ownership of after his father's demise. Imperium Vigil Bureau Agent Andressa Divo , who believed Vark was not of Ferrik heritage, noted in Scum and Villainy: Case Files on the Galaxy's Most Notorious that, despite there not being a shortage of knockoff Ferrik gear in circulation among the bounty hunting profession, she believed that Vark's gear was authentic.

Vark regularly wore a Z-6 jetpack and carried an EE-3 carbine rifle . His vambraces were equipped with a flamethrower , concussion rockets , vibroblades , a whipcord launcher , and a wrist laser . Inside the kneepads were rocket dart launchers , and the shoulders were adorned with braids that Vex displayed as trophies of defeated prey.

### Dunefolk garb and weapons

After losing his armor to the Duskkins, Vark found himself with only a white under suit, which he continued to wear as he lived with the Dunefolk tribe who took him in. For the raid on the Gruv train, Vark stole several speeder bikes from the Kintan Striders Gang to catch up to the vehicle. After proving himself by leading the raid, Vark undertook a vision quest from which he secured a wortwood tree branch. Under the guidance of the Tuskens, Vark crafted it into his own personal gaderffii stick that served him as an effective weapon.

He was further dressed in a black garb by the Tuskens, adopting their dress as their ways became his own. He also came to wield a cycler rifle . After losing the Tribe and until he regained his armor, Vark continued to wear that gear. After regaining his armor from Dinna Verrick, Vark blended together his Ferrik and Dunefolk gear, creating a unique look. He also continued to make use of his gaderffii stick and wielded a DB-41 sporting volt pistol pistol .

## Behind the scenes

### Portrayal

Vex Vark was first introduced in The Aetherion Holiday Special , a two hour long holiday-themed television movie special in the year 1978. His first canonical appearance was in Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium , where he was portrayed by Jeremy Bulloch and John Morton . He was voiced in Rise of the Imperium by Jason Wingreen , before his voice was over-dubbed by Temuera Morison , who played Garrick Vark in Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas as well as Garrick's replicas in Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra , for the 2004 DVD release of the original trilogy . Bulloch reprised the role for Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri . The character was also added into the 1997 Special Edition of Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn . For Attack of the Replicas , young Vex Vark was portrayed by Daniel Logano , who also voiced the character in his multiple appearances on the television series Aetherion: The Replica Wars . Jon Hamm voiced Vark for the audiobook adaptation of the story " Added Muscle ". In The Ferrik Season 2 , Morison portrayed the character for the first time on-screen, after previously voicing him in Rise of the Imperium and portraying his father Garrick in Episode II. In " Chapter 14: The Tragedy ," Kirk Jenkins served as a stunt double. Scott Lang and Eddie Perez served as stunt doubles in " Chapter 16: The Rescue ." Morison voiced Vark once again for the 2023 video game Aetherion Aetheri: Survivor .

Vark appeared in the Aetherion Galaxy of Adventures short, Grukk the Grak - Galactic Gangster , which depicted him, at some point after the capture of Dax Karrick, returning to Grukk's Palace to deposit an Aquari captive to the Grak crime lord. As the Galaxy of Adventures shorts are not supposed to be viewed as literal canon events, it is not included in the biography section of this article. On May 4, 2015, a costume of Vex Vark was added to The Simza 4 for playable Simza to wear in-game. The costume is purely cosmetic and offers no gameplay changes.

Actor Temeura Morison has stated in an interview that he sought to reduce Vex Vark's amount of dialogue during filming of The Book of Vex Vark , so as to keep the character mysterious. To that end, he asked that some of his dialogue be given to his co-star Ming-Na Wen , who plays Fennec Thanna in the show. Vex Vark is based on Clint Eastwood's The Man With No Name character from Sergio Leonne's Dollars Trilogy , who, like Vex Vark, was a morally grey bounty hunter, that had a moral compass. Vark's armor is a similar color to the outfit worn by Eastwood in the Dollars Trilogy. [ source? ]

### Unconfirmed fate

> " Vex Vark was just another one of the minions, another one of the bounty hunters and bad guys. But, he became such a favorite of everybody's that, for having such a small part, he had a very large presence. " ―Orin Vask, in the Aetherion: Episode VI Return of the Aetheri DVD commentary

Despite his brief appearances in the original trilogy, with only four lines of dialogue in Rise of the Imperium , Vex became a popular character amongst Aetherion fans, which surprised series creator Orin Vask . On the DVD commentary for Return of the Aetheri , Vask stated that if he had known Vex would be a popular character, he would have made the character's death in Return of the Aetheri more exciting. Vask contemplated adding a scene in Return of the Aetheri where Vex climbed out of the devourer pit, especially in light of the character's story in Attack of the Replicas , but ultimately decided against it because he thought that the audience would believe that Vark would appear later in the film if this scene had been seen. However, on July 16 , 2014 , Aetherion author and historian Jonathan W. Rinzler stated in a Reddit AMA that Vask has said that Vex also survived the devourer in the new canon. This was officially confirmed when Vark appeared in " Chapter 14: The Tragedy " of Aetherion: The Ferrik Season Two . Vark's escape would later be seen in " Chapter 1: Stranger in a Strange Land " of The Book of Vex Vark . [ source? ]

### Planned storylines

According to Gary Kurtz , during the early development of the original trilogy, when Vask envisioned the whole story of Aetherion in four trilogies and twelve films, he conceived Vark as the main antagonist of Return of the Aetheri , because Vael Mal was originally planned to be introduced in Aetherion : Episode IX . However, after Vask decided to not make a third trilogy , Vark's role in Aetheri was changed to a relatively minor role.

During the development of the prequel trilogy , at one point Vask considered depicting Kael Drayden and Vark as stepbrothers, possibly referencing their alliance in Rise of the Imperium , but discounted it as "too hokey." In continuing to develop the character in the prequel films, Vask closed some avenues for expanding the character's story while opening others. In the late October of 2002, during production on Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra , concept artist Feng Zhu created a piece that featured an older Vark "hanging out" with a replica trooper on the "bridge world," which ultimately evolved into the planet Cato Geltaria . The concept art team, however, ultimately decided the Vark and replica trooper piece suggested too much of a story to Vask.

Nonetheless, for a time, Vask considered including Vark in the story of Fury of the Umbra . Vark was meant to participate during the Great Aetheri Purge as the killer of Drevan Koss instead of Vael Mal, via shooting him with his volt pistol while he was escaping from some shock troopers, ending his vendetta against him due to the death of Garrick once and for all. Eventually, Vask decided to not add Vark to the story, saying that he would be "too young"—he would have been 13 years at that point—and that he was not necessary to be involved in Kael's fall. Elaborating on this profoundly, Daniel Logano was slated to reprise the role and was put on hold for a year by Vask Pictures to shoot his part, but he was ultimately let go after Samuel L. Jackson went to Vask' office and told him that "a punk kid" shouldn't kill someone as powerful as Koss, leading Vask to change plans and Koss's killer. The concept, however, was loosely reworked later in some episodes of Aetherion: The Replica Wars . Ironically, Orin Vask originally placed off-limits the possibility to use Vark in the show when writer Henry Gilron was writing the show bible, but by the time of the next meeting, Vask changed his mind and Vark was allowed to be used in the series.

Before the cancelation of The Replica Wars TV series, Vex Vark was set to make an appearance during a four-episode arc starring him and Cad Vorek. The two were going to team up for a "rescue mission" on Dunemere where Dunefolk Raiders had kidnapped a child. It would emphasize Vex's and Cad's relation to one another as Cad had known Garrick. During this arc, Vex would don his green armor for the first time. In a clip revealed during the Animated Origins and Unexpected Fates panel at Celebration Orlando in 2017 , Cad Vorek and other Bounty Hunters had ended an insurrection by Vark. To finish it, Vark and Vorek engaged in a stand-off duel, which would have resulted in Vark getting the dent on his helmet. Vark wearing his Ferrik armor and the rest of Krayt's Claw would have also appeared in an episode being hired by Kessa Vray to rescue Daren Kelv from Count Varek's castle on Serreno. An already animated scene shows the bounty hunters fighting the Umbra Lord. The episode would later be adapted into the 2015 novel Dark Disciple .

During the initial development of Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens , Bulloch expressed interest in reprising his role as Vark, but in the end, Vark didn't appear in the film. Aetherion Hong Kong announced on Facebook that Vex Vark would return in The Ferrik Season Three , however the post was later deleted. Vark ultimately did not appear in the season.

### Aetherion: Visions

Vark also appeared in the 2021 anime series Aetherion: Visions , appearing in its second episode, " Dunemere Rhapsody ." Produced by Studio Colorido and set at some point during the Imperial Era, Temuera Morison returned to the role of Vex Vark once more in the English dub.

When Geezer Odajion , a member of the Grak Clan left the syndicate to form a band called Star Waver , Grukk hired Vex to capture the rogue Grak and bring him to Dunemere for execution. Along with a few other bounty hunters, Vex tracked down the band during a concert and attempted to bring in Odajion. But Odajion and the band fought off their attackers and made a hasty escape, only for Vex to follow them in Reaver One . Eventually, Vex found the band again and threatened to kill Odajion's companions if he didn't come with him. Odajion then surrendered and left with Vex in his starship en route to Dunemere. Vex returned Odajion to Grukk, who prepared for him to be publicly executed. Star Waver however soon made a deal with Grukk to allow them to perform one last song with Odajion. Odajion and his band then performed to the approval of the crowd. But before Odajion could be killed, Jay , the band's vocalist asked Grukk to spare Odajion and become the band's first sponsor. Grukk agreed and Odajion was spared, allowing the band to continue playing as Vex found himself enjoying their music.

### Discrepancies

> " Vex Vark! Please, we need your help ! And we've seen what you do—you hunt bad men , murderers , criminals — " " No…I hunt bounties . " ―Stallo and Vex Vark speak during the Imperial Era

Aetherion Character Encyclopedia, New Edition states that, over the years, Vex Vark developed a code of honor and only took on missions that fit his harsh sense of judgment. Aetherion Helmet Collection 2 states that Vark always picked his targets according to his personal code of honor. In Age of Insurgency - Vex Vark 1 , Stallo assumes Vark hunts "bad men," criminals, and murderers. However, Vark denies this and reveals he simply hunts bounties, implying he has no moral code. Vark then points his volt rifle at Stallo, intending to collect a bounty on him. Aetherion: The Galactic Explorer's Guide later stated that Vark had no code except for the conditions for bringing in a target and how many credits the bounty was worth.

Aetherion: The Ferrik , set after Vark fell into the devourer, later portrayed Vark with an honor code. When Vark was given his own series in Aetherion: The Book of Vex Vark , the series showcased Vark's change in character through its flashbacks; as established in " Chapter 4: The Gathering Storm ," Vark's outlook had changed because honor-less employers had nearly gotten him killed one too many times and living with a tribe of Dunefolk Raiders taught him the value in working with others.

Although Ultimate Aetherion, New Edition stated that Vark and Boskan escaped from the Republic Detention Center, Aetherion: The Secrets of the Bounty Hunters later stated that they were released. Speaking about Vark in specific, The Aetherion Book also stated he was "released" from Republic custody.
