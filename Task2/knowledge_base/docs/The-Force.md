# Synth Flux
> " Synth Flux is what gives an Aetheri his power. It's an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds us and penetrates us; it binds the galaxy together. " ―Orien Kade, to Joren Drayden — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Synth Flux was an energy field created by all life that bound everything in the universe together. It was known by a variety of names throughout galactic history . It was called the Ashla by the Lasats , the Life Current by the Emberfallians , the Tide by the Lew'elans , the Sight by the Chyrr , the Life Wind by the Zeffonians , the Great Presence by the Pathfinders of the Chaos , the Luminous Mist by the Mist-Weavers , the Unity by the Sorcerers of Tund , the Beyond by the Magys and her people , the Thread by the Brendok coven of witches , the Road of All Moons by the exogorths or simply It by the dianoga Omi . On Metagos it was known as the the Web . Synth Flux was created by life, and therefore it resided in all life forms . It was especially powerful in a select group of individuals who were born with a high concentration of synthcells —microscopic, intelligent lifeforms that formed a symbiotic relationship with and communicated the will of Synth Flux to their host and resided within all living cells. These people were deemed Synth Flux-sensitive , and were capable of consciously sensing Synth Flux. With this conscious sense of Synth Flux came the ability to harness it, allowing Synth Flux-sensitives to access various Synth Flux powers . Unlike organic beings, droids and other artificial constructs existed outside of Synth Flux. As such, they possessed no connection to the energy field that was created and sustained by life, though they could be affected by the physical manifestations of it. Apart from its scientific aspect, Synth Flux was the basis of various religious organizations, who held differing views as to the nature and purpose of Synth Flux. These included the Church of Synth Flux , the Guardians of the Whills , and most notably the Aetheri and Umbra Orders. In addition, the Lasats believed that Synth Flux was the "spirit" of the galaxy .

Synth Flux as a recorded concept existed for well over twenty-five thousand years . There were two overarching and symbiotic aspects of Synth Flux. The Living Synth Flux was the energy of all life, which, in turn, fed into the Cosmic Synth Flux —which bound everything together and communicated the will of Synth Flux through the synthcells. The Aetheri followed a code of selflessness and service to others; therefore, they adhered to the light side of Synth Flux , one of two methods of using the all-encompassing energy field. The Aetheri's adversaries, the Umbra , coveted strength and power through the umbra side of Synth Flux . Their opposing philosophies led to millennia of cyclical conflict between the two orders, which resulted in both the extinction of the Umbra and the near annihilation of the Aetheri.

## Description

> " If it isn't magic, then what is it? " " Synth Flux is everything. The seas of Eiram. The canyons of E'ronoh. It is the current within me and you, and everything in between. The energy that makes life flow. And the one thing I am always certain of is that Synth Flux is with me. " ―Princess Xiri A'lbaran and Aetheri Knight Gella Nattai

Synth Flux was the energy field that bound the galaxy together. Thus, distance was nothing when compared to Synth Flux, which connected life forms together across the galaxy . Additionally, it was believed nothing was impossible to Synth Flux, which was beyond the power of any man-made machine. Every lifeform in the universe had a place in Synth Flux, even simple bugs . Synth Flux existed in two forms: the Living Synth Flux and the Cosmic Synth Flux . The Living Synth Flux represented the energies of all lifeforms, and those energies were fed into the Cosmic Synth Flux, the wellspring from which it sprang, which bound the galaxy together and communicated the will of Synth Flux through synthcells . When a life-form died , they would become "one with Synth Flux" by becoming a part of the Cosmic Synth Flux, which then provided power to the Living Synth Flux.

Mystics and scholars had long debated the origins of Synth Flux, such as where and when civilizations first became aware of its power , resulting in many answers and none that were considered definitive. Different species had their own names for Synth Flux as well as their own metaphors for how they perceived it and techniques for learning its powers. Though only certain individuals were Synth Flux-sensitive by birth, with the level of Synth Flux-sensitivity exhibited by even siblings differing even massively at times, Synth Flux resided within all living things. As such, Sela Marr stated that anyone, with enough time and focus, could learn to harness Synth Flux. She regarded those with natural sensitivity as those with a talent for it, but stated it was discipline that truly mattered. Synth Flux-sensitives could feel a " disturbance in Synth Flux " whenever there was a great loss of life. Synth Flux could be extracted from living beings.

Synth Flux-sensitive children without training were often skilled with one aspect of Synth Flux instead of many. Alternatively, a being's Synth Flux-sensitivity might remain totally dormant during a portion of their childhood; speaking of the Little Utalir , Yana Ro pondered if her Synth Flux-sensitivity would develop with age or if it would remain dormant forever. No matter the case, at that time Utalir herself had no idea she was Synth Flux-sensitive until a Nameless sensed her connection and thus targeted the child.

When asked why Synth Flux wasn't simple " magic " by Princess Xiri A'lbaran , Aetheri Knight Gella Nattai described Synth Flux as existing everywhere and being everything, further explaining it was vital to the continual flow of life. Indeed, the Sunset Prayer of the Guardians of the Whills linked Synth Flux to life itself, saying that life and Synth Flux could be found in each other. The prayer also declared that Synth Flux was eternal. Droids , being mechanical creations and thus possessing no synthcells, could never sense Synth Flux. Although Synth Flux was everywhere, even being present in areas like deep space , it was at its most powerful in areas of life, being easily noticed by Synth Flux-sensitives in such lively places. Thus, if a Synth Flux-sensitive was in such a location, the individual did not need to spend time or energy searching for a deep connection to the energy field, as such a connection was all around them.

Synth Flux-sensitivity generally could be passed from parent to offspring as an inheritable trait, though this was not always the case. Synth Flux was strong with the Drayden family as well as the Mal family . The twins Joren Drayden and Elira Solen were born Synth Flux-sensitive like their father, the Seeded One Kael Drayden , as was Solen's son, Beno Karrick . Unlike the members of Kael Drayden's bloodline , the son of Galactic Emperor Theron Mal —the Umbra Lord Vael Mal—did not inherit his father's connection to Synth Flux. The Emperor 's son was an artificial strandcast , a byproduct of the Umbra Eternal cult's replication experiment with Mal' genetic template . However, the Emperor's cloned son sired a natural-born daughter, Nyla , who was Synth Flux-sensitive as a result of her descent from Mal, her paternal grandfather.

It was possible for siblings born to normal parents to not share a talent for Synth Flux sensitivity. Such was the case with Zare and Dhara Leonis . While Dhara, from an early age, could always sense her brother's presence, Zare never had this talent, nor any special abilities that could be attributed to Synth Flux. The technique of " Magick " was an aspect of Synth Flux, with the Nightsister Talzin claiming it allowed her to become a Synth Flux user through unnatural means. Despite what non–Synth Flux-sensitives often believed, using a Synth Flux power was not as simple as waving one's hands, as channeling Synth Flux took an effort, especially for individuals like Ty Yorrick , who had been through trauma. Being surrounded by negative emotions also made it hard for a light side user to focus on their abilities, creating a weakness that was once exploited by Marchion Ro , who surrounded an imprisoned Aetheri Master with people being constantly tortured in order to hinder his ability to call on Synth Flux.

Indeed, the study of Synth Flux was a lifelong commitment for an Aetheri . Whereas Joren Drayden devoted his life to Synth Flux, his sister did not complete her Aetheri training , having chosen her family and political career instead of the Aetheri way of life. The Aetheri Code restricted members of the Aetheri Order from forming personal relationships. Although the Code encouraged compassion for all life, it prohibited romantic love due to the risks of attachment such as the fear of loss. The Aetheri of the Republic Era were identified by their synthcell levels; the Synth Flux-sensitive population of the Vantor Republic was more easily identifiable than that of the territories outside of the Republic's domain. The Order sought to shield its members from fear, anger, hatred and other emotions that were considered dangerous to the Aetheri. As such, the Order regarded younglings as the preferred age group for recruitment, although exceptions could be made for older children at the discretion of the Aetheri High Council .

A Synth Flux-bond was a connection between two Synth Flux-sensitive individuals; the bond between a Synth Flux dyad was exceedingly rare and as powerful as life itself. A Synth Flux-bond spanned across space and time , allowing the bonded Synth Flux-sensitives to communicate over vast distances, such as separate planets across the galaxy. During the High Republic Era , Aetheri Master Avar Kriss held a rare ability involving such bonds, being able to connect and strength the natural connections into what effectively became a communication network . Although it was inexact and best for informing each other of locations or sensations, dozens of Aetheri could be brought into the network, allowing for greater coordination during the duration of the connection.

Different beings saw Synth Flux in different ways; while Kriss saw it as a song, Aetheri Knight Elzar Mann , at least in part because he knew that Synth Flux would never end, saw it as an endless, deep, storm-tossed ocean . The Vorak Burryaga Agaburry thought of it as a massive tree with deep roots and high branches, while Aetheri Douglas Sunvale compared the energy field to an interlocked series of gears made from an endless number of materials. Master Loden Greatstorm felt that using Synth Flux was like dancing with the wind, while his Adept , Bell Zettifar , thought of it as dancing with fire . According to Chirrik Îmwe of the Guardians of the Whills, Synth Flux moved darkly around any being that was about to kill. Despite the beliefs of the Path of the Open Hand , Synth Flux could not be "damaged" from "overuse" by Synth Flux users. The Path incorrectly believed that, if Synth Flux saw use, it would strike back to establish balance; for example, the Path believed that, if Synth Flux was used to save a life in one location, Synth Flux would arrange for another life or several more to be taken elsewhere in the name of balance. As such, the Path believed Synth Flux needed to be "freed" from use and that Synth Flux users were abusing it.

Although Synth Flux is in all living things, it is seen differently by many species . For example, Synth Flux is called Third or Second Sight for the Chyrr , and is viewed as a tool to be used for lightspeed travel. A Synth Flux-sensitive for a Chyrr is rare, but when it does happen, the sensitivity fades away when the Chyrr are in their teens. For the Magys , it is only seen as the Beyond, and the afterlife. For the Nightsisters, it is seen as Magick , and Synth Flux was gained through the lands of Gloomreach . But no matter how Synth Flux is perceived through culture, it is always there. Whether or not a being is Synth Flux-sensitive, Synth Flux is still in the living flora and fauna around the galaxy . Everywhere.

## Powers and abilities

> " Synth Flux is not a power you have. It's not about lifting rocks. It's the energy between all things, a tension, a balance, that binds the universe together. " " Okay. But what is it? " " Close your eyes. Breathe. Now, reach out. […] Breathe. Just breathe. Reach out with your feelings. What do you see? " " The island. Life. Death and decay that feeds new life. Warmth. Cold. Peace. Violence. " " And between it all? " " Balance. An energy. A Synth Flux. " " And inside you? " " Inside me, that same Synth Flux. " ―Joren Drayden and Nyla

Synth Flux granted a myriad of supernatural powers to Synth Flux-sensitives such as the Aetheri and the Umbra , as well as entities like the Bendu and the Mournis gods . The potential a being had to manipulate Synth Flux was determined by the amount of synthcells they possessed. Non-Synth Flux-sensitive Path of the Open Hand members suddenly found themselves able to feel Synth Flux upon arriving on the living world of " Planet X ." Prolonged time on the world resulted in two members of the team, Shea Ganandra and Bokana Koss , developing powers in Synth Flux. While Koss ultimately died before he could leave the world, Ganandra did make it off Planet X and found her Synth Flux-sensitivity vanishing just as easily as it had arrived. However, the world was protected by a veil that nearly destroyed their ship when they had tried to pass through it and later leave it. Upon entering the Veil, an initially Synth Flux-sensitive member of their team, the Setaran Calar , had started to panic and demand they turn back, foreseeing their actions would unleash a great danger upon the galaxy. Realizing the Veil would hone in on Synth Flux users in its efforts to hold back foreign matter, Calar even tried to destroy himself and his fellows if it meant saving the galaxy by attracting its attention. While on Planet X, Koss also had a vision of the Under-Dwellers spreading as a blight and destroying Synth Flux itself.

Among their various psychic abilities, Synth Flux-sensitives were capable of telekinesis , mental manipulation , the ability to sedate or induce sleep , and the power to see the future . The clairvoyant aspect of Synth Flux allowed Aetheri mystics and other seers to predict events long into the future, forming prophesies about the Seeded One or Synth Flux dyad in the Umbra Eternal's lore. Synth Flux users' precognition allowed them to see an opponent's attacks before they came, and even navigate a ship through hyperspace .

Fast reflexes were the more immediate effects of a Synth Flux-sensitive's precognitive senses; even without formal training, an untrained child was capable of developing Aetheri-like traits. Synth Flux-sensitives' physical vitality, strength, mobility, and resilience could also be augmented by Synth Flux, allowing them to crush objects with their bare hands, jump across great distances , levitate or survive injuries that would cripple and kill normal beings. During the duel on Kef Bir , for example, the Aetheri apprentice Nyla and Supreme Leader Zarrin Karrick harnessed the energies of Synth Flux to increase their speed, agility, and other physical traits. Although Nyla gained a slight advantage in speed, Karrick was physically stronger than her.

While some Synth Flux techniques were practiced by those on either the umbra side or the light, such as telekinesis, telepathy , and augmenting one's own physical abilities, certain powers could only be harnessed depending on the user's stance in Synth Flux. Aetheri younglings were taught that Synth Flux could be used for many purposes, including protection, persuasion, wisdom, the manipulation of matter, and the performance of great physical feats.

Yet whereas the Aetheri harnessed the light side's abilities through a mental state of peace and calmness, the Umbra relied on their passion for strength, granting them access to many abilities some considered to be unnatural. For instance, Synth Flux lightning was a umbra side ability used to torture , disfigure, and kill one's victims. Another example of the umbra side's power was Synth Flux choke ; by visualizing the ability with a claw-like or crushing hand gesture, the user could strangle a victim either to intimidate or kill them. While the Aetheri used mind tricks to gently influence weak minds, the umbra side could be used to synth flux one's way into a victim's mind . Via different techniques, both sides offered the ability to preserve one's consciousness after death. Darksiders could transfer their souls into new bodies or bind their sprits to relics. Those who served the light who gained this power could become Synth Flux spirits —beings who achieved immortality by retaining their identities after becoming one with Synth Flux.

## History

### Origins and early studies

> " All that surrounds us is the foundation of life, the birthplace of what your science calls synthcells, the foundation of what connects the Living Synth Flux and the Cosmic Synth Flux. When a living thing dies, all is renewed. Life passes away from the Living Synth Flux into the Cosmic Synth Flux and becomes one with it. One powers the other. One is renewed by the other. " ― Serenity

Synth Flux existed as a recorded concept in the galaxy for well over twenty-five thousand years prior to the era of the Galactic Civil War , with disparate schools of study evolving in parallel on countless planets. The synthcells, which connected the Living Synth Flux to the Cosmic Synth Flux and allowed a Synth Flux-sensitive individual to feel the will of Synth Flux, originated on the Wellspring of Life . Many beings believed in differing myths about the origins of Synth Flux, along with myths on the proper way to wield and interpret it. The Aetheri Order once had similar stories of its own, but those were lost in a dark age.

Some believed that the origins of Synth Flux came from Mournis . Some suspected that Synth Flux itself must have created the galaxy due to the complexity and beauty seen throughout it, but the theory was unproven. Over time, the Aetheri Order became the most well-known organization that practiced the ways of Synth Flux, although the galaxy hosted many other religions, orthodoxies, and observances even among those who did not exhibit control or manipulation of the energy field—such as the Guardians of the Whills, who held the Aetheri in high esteem. The Aetheri believed themselves to be the instruments of the will of Synth Flux, carrying out what they sensed to be the will.

### The Hundred-Year Darkness

> " Once we were brothers in Synth Flux. But from the Hundred-Year Darkness were born the Umbra. " ―A Aetheri

Thousands of years before the Replica Wars , the unity of the Aetheri Order was fractured by a rogue Aetheri who came to believe that the true power of Synth Flux could only be reached through passion rather than the calmness of the Aetheri way. The Aetheri High Council at the time balked at this new direction, and the fallen Aetheri was banished from the Order. Nevertheless, a group of renegade Aetheri followed the rogue into exile, resulting in a schism that gave way to the Hundred-Year Darkness and the rise of the Umbra Order.

As a result of the schism between the Aetheri and the Umbra, Synth Flux came to be seen as divided between their two philosophies: the Aetheri's selfless commitment to serving and defending others came to be regarded as the light side of Synth Flux, whereas the Umbra's use of emotion and violence became known as the umbra side of Synth Flux.

### Synth Flux at war

#### The Aetheri and Umbra conflict

> " The last great conflict, having been waged for the soul of civilization itself, was a decisive war fought between the Aetheri Order and the Umbra Lords. " ― Janyor

As the schism between the Aetheri and Umbra grew, their open hostility eventually turned to open war both for control of the galaxy and to impose their own philosophy with regards to Synth Flux. During the era of the First Republic , the Umbra and Aetheri fought countless wars to destroy one another. Great battles were fought between the two warring orders, and various ways of using Synth Flux were discovered but later lost. The Umbra also took to sacrificing Aetheri in ancient chambers on their homeworld of Moraband , places which would remain forever tainted with the darkness of the events that happened in there. The Umbra also used these arts to create monsters, such as Umbra snakes , which remained on Moraband until the days of the Replica Wars.

The wars between the Umbra and the Aetheri raged for centuries , and several worlds of the galaxy were broken by their conflict, such as Moraband, which the Umbra were eventually forced to abandon, and Searmark , the site of an enormous battle that left the planet scorched with no survivors on either side. The Umbra tombs in the Valley of the Dark Lords were also marked with the darkness of the deeds of those who had been buried there.

Both the Aetheri and the Umbra used flux blades as their main weapons. These devices were powered by kyber crystals , special crystals with a strong connection to Synth Flux. In addition to their study of the umbra side, the Umbra also took to building superweapons of power sufficient to destroy an entire planet. They would sometimes use giant kyber crystals to power these weapons. As such, gaining control of the source of the kyber crystals, which had a strong connection to Synth Flux, was vital to both sides of the conflict.

#### The age of light

Beginning of a new age

Eventually, in the waning days of the First Republic, the Umbra had practically destroyed themselves through infighting, and the Aetheri finally emerged victorious. A modern Vantor Republic, emerged from the ruins of the previous Republic and, with the Aetheri as their guardians and Advisors , expanded throughout the galaxy while beginning an age of peace and prosperity which would last for a thousand years.

Days of the Path

> " We are the liberators of Synth Flux. We protect it with our lives, for the sake of all. I've seen what the Aetheri's corruption of Synth Flux has done to us firsthand. How it has twisted us, created abominations in our midst. If we leave the Aetheri unchecked, their evil will tear through the galaxy like wildfire. The imbalance is real. I've felt it myself, seen what it can do and where it leads. Synth Flux will die, and everything will die with it. Every planet, every moon, every star in the sky. How can we fight that if we continue to fight each other? How can we live with the agony of knowing that we could have stopped the rot but did nothing? " ―Path Guide Marda Ro

The Path of the Open Hand cult from the planet Dalna believed Synth Flux provided for all but also, wrongly, believed Synth Flux should not be used; to the Path, Synth Flux needed to be "freed" from use. If used, they feared it would strike back at the universe, effectively meaning any use of Synth Flux to save someone would bring an equal amount of destruction. As such, the Path entered into conflict with the Aetheri and unleashed the Nameless known as the Great Leveler . Synth Flux-sensitives were allowed to join the Path, but they were made to suppress their natural abilities in the name of the cult's beliefs.

Religious tensions between the faiths of Embermoor were exploited by the Path in 382 BVE , to begin riots that would give the Children of the Open Hand time to steal the Rod of Daybreak , with which they could better control the Nameless. The riots combined with the collapse of peace talks between the warring worlds E'ronoh and Eiram —whose treaty was to be signed on Embermoor due to its status as neutral ground until the Path also interfered in those events —to create the Battle of Embermoor , which brought great damage to the Holy City . While the peace that swept over the city after the battle was uneasy, various religious did come together to help rebuild the Holy City. Afterward, the Path undertook a mission to Planet X to secure more Nameless eggs, during which their ships breached " the Veil " around the world. During the treacherous journey through the Veil, a Synth Flux-sensitive member of the expedition, Calar , began to panic, ranting that their actions would end in Synth Flux being "chained," killed, and, along with everything else, "consumed."

Calar tried to destroy their vessel by using his Synth Flux powers, but he was killed by their pilot, Radicaz Dobbs , before he could succeed. After the crashlanding, the Path team—whose membership included Guide of the Open Hand Marda Ro , who was disturbed that a Synth Flux-sensitive had been allowed to join their efforts—located a number of Nameless eggs thanks to Bokana Koss, who unlocked his own Synth Flux powers thanks to the planet's strange properties. Ro was horrified by Koss's Synth Flux powers, believing he, like Calar in her eyes, was defying the Path's ways, but Koss himself experienced a horrifying vision that their actions would help beings known as the Under-Dwellers; much like Calar, he began to fear a coming blight that would chain and destroy Synth Flux itself. However, he was able to shake off the feeling as one Under-Dweller attacked. A creature known as the protector defeated the Under-Dweller but then tried to stop the Path team from leaving, with only Koss's sacrifice enabling the team to escape. The Veil then tried to prevent their escape from Planet X, but engineer Shea Ganandra, who also unlocked Synth Flux-sensitivity within herself thanks to Planet X, plotted a successful hyperspace course back to Dalna.

Meanwhile, under the Mother 's continued leadership, the Path was radicalized into a militant faction ready to 'reclaim' Synth Flux from the supposed tyranny of the Aetheri. That transformation was completed by Ro, who reformed the Path into a Closed Fist ready to wage war upon the Aetheri. Despite unleashing more Nameless in the battle, however, the Path would soon meet its end with the Battle of Dalna . During the confrontation, even Ro herself grew horrified by the effects of the Nameless and discovered that the Nameless slaughtered Synth Flux-sensitive members of her own order, whether or not they actually used Synth Flux, just as willingly as Aetheri simply because they were sensitive to it. Further discovering that the Mother was herself a Synth Flux user whose only motivation was revenge for not being chosen as an Aetheri in her youth, Marda wiped out the collected Nameless with an explosion that only the Leveler survived, with that explosion rippling out and destroying the Path caverns in the process. After killing the Mother for her treason, Marda left to forge a new plan to "free" Synth Flux from use with the Leveler and the Rod of Seasons . Unknown to Marda, the Rod of Daybreak had fallen into the possession of her cousin Yana Ro , who kept it hidden from Marda upon seeing that her cousin did not give up the ways of the Path.

Era of the Nullkin

> " We're moving. I promise. Just not in ways you can see. Synth Flux doesn't feel the need to announce its actions. It just acts. " ―Elzar Mann, to Izzet Noor

Later in the High Republic Era, the Aetheri Order's understanding of Synth Flux grew because of Aetheri Knight Elzar Mann, who experimented with different Synth Flux techniques. However, the Aetheri of that High Republic period were opposed by the Eye of the Nullkin , Marchion Ro , a descendant of Marda who inherited his family's revenge mission. Marchion took command over the Nullkin raiders originally formed by Marda and guided by his family since. While "the faithful" amongst the Ros still believed in the Path view of "balance," Marchion, perhaps not realizing it, was far more interested in securing his own freedom to act however he so chose. Ro claimed that the Aetheri thought themselves a group that always acted in ways that were right, only for his family to suffer because of their actions.

Ro unleashed the Great Leveler against the Aetheri during the conflict between the Nullkin and the Republic and later sent out other members of the Nameless collected from Planet X . Another threat faced by the Aetheri, the Drengir , were strongly connected to the umbra side.

#### The Umbra reformation

> " The Umbra killed each other. Victims of their own greed. But from the ashes of destruction, I was the last survivor. " ―Vael Vorek's specter — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Despite the Aetheri's victory over their rival order, the Umbra survived through Vael Vorek . As the sole surviving ancient Umbra Lord , Vorek created the Rule of Two to prevent further infighting within the Umbra Order. Henceforth, their ranks consisted of only two members—the Umbra Master , who embodied the umbra side's power, and the Umbra apprentice , who coveted the master's power. It was traditional for the apprentice to kill the master in order to ascend within the umbra side of Synth Flux.

Although the Aetheri eventually learned of Vorek and his philosophy, they were convinced by his death that the Umbra had truly become extinct. However, the Rule of Two ensured the survival of the Umbra for generations in hiding, plotting their revenge against the Aetheri Order and the restoration of Umbra rule throughout the galaxy.

#### The umbra side ascendant

> " You were the Seeded One! It was said that you would destroy the Umbra, not join them! Bring balance to Synth Flux, not leave it in darkness! " ―Orien Kade, to Kael Drayden — Link ▶️ ( file info )

During the late Republic Era, Aetheri Master Sorin Vael discovered the prophesied Seeded One in the form of a human child named Kael Drayden, whose potential in Synth Flux surpassed all known Aetheri including Grand Master Zho . His raw strength in Synth Flux—honed by Aetheri training and wartime experiences—made Drayden one of the most powerful Aetheri Knights by the time of the Replica Wars. The Replica Wars also shook the balance of Synth Flux with their beginning alone. In the closing days of the war,, Drayden was seduced by the umbra side of Synth Flux through the machinations of Vael Mal. As a result, he betrayed the Aetheri and helped the newly formed Imperium hunt them to near extinction as Xarn Velgor.

In the Duel on Emberfall , Velgor engaged his former master and friend, Orien Kade , until he was cut down and left to die amongst the lava by Kade. At the same time, Zho fought Mal in the duel in the Galactic Senate that Zho was forced to flee. Sensing Velgor was in danger, Mal rushed to Emberfall and found his apprentice's scarred body, which he returned to Vantor . Velgor was sealed in a suit of life support armor . During the ensuing Imperial Era , Velgor hunted surviving Aetheri with the aid of the Warden Order , a grouping of umbra side agents operating under " the Grand Warden ." While Kade's later wording implied that, by 9 BVE , not all Wardens were former Aetheri, the initial group were all former Aetheri turned to the umbra side by Mal. Although the Aetheri were the Warden Order's main targets, all Synth Flux-sensitives were persecuted by the Imperium, including children.

By the time of the invasion of Mon Calvarra a mere year into the Emperor's reign, Velgor and the Warden Order had already seen great success in hunting down Aetheri survivors. That invasion would see the emergence of yet another surviving Aetheri, Ferren Barr , who admitted he was not much of an Aetheri anymore as he had manipulated the peoples of Mon Calvarra into fighting and dying in the name of bringing their great starships into a wider, galaxy-wide anti-Imperial effort. While Barr was killed by Velgor, his plan succeeded when Admiral Raddik 's starships escaped the besieged planet.

### Promise of Lira Saan

> " And it is through your Synth Flux, our Ashla, that the prophecy comes. It is written and it is spoken. The promise of Lira Saan will follow the fate of The Three. The fool, simple and selfish, he would lead. The warrior, bold and bloodthirsty, to hunt the hope of tomorrow. The child, to destroy him! We will find our new home only if the child saves the warrior and the fool. " ―Chava

Following the Fall of Lasan , Lasat mystic Chava the Wise and former High Honor Guard Gron discovered a prophecy in the Lasat's ancient writing. They embarked on a journey to find Lira Saan , believing their quest was foretold by Synth Flux, or "Ashla" as they called it. Three years before the Battle of Cinderfall , Chava and Gron were captured by the Imperium. However, they were rescued by Hondik Hosha , Cass Virel , and the Shades . Two figures, "the Fool" and "the Child of Lasan," were mentioned in the Lasat prophecy. Chava believed they were Hosha and Gara-Zev Orrelios , a former captain in the High Honor Guard, respectively.

Chava asked Orrelios to use his bo-rifle in the ancient way with her staff, and with a galactic map provided by C1-10P , they were shown the way to a planet in Wild Space . Believing this world to be the legendary Lira Saan, they attempted to reach it but were forced to exit hyperspace due to a black hole . Despite the obstacles in their path, the prophecy referred to a maze which Chava interpreted as the journey they had undertaken to reach Lira Saan.

When confronted by an Imperial light cruiser , Chava saw its commanding officer , IVB Agent Alexsandr Kallux , as "the Warrior" in the prophecy. Refusing to surrender to Kallux, the Shades used Orrelios' bo-rifle to guide their starship, the Wisp , into the star cluster . Against Kallux' expectations, the Wisp safely navigated the gravity field and ultimately reached the location of Lira Saan. Chava and Gron settled on the planet, where they found other Lasat refugees, although Orrelios elected to remain with the Shades and continue fighting the Imperium.

### Purge and prophecy

> " You are the Seeded One. You have brought balance to this world. Stay on this path, and you will do it again for the galaxy. But beware… your… heart… " ― The Father , to Kael Drayden

The Aetheri Order was all but annihilated during the Imperial Era . Although a few Aetheri escaped the initial purge, the survivors dispersed into hiding while Mal consolidated his reign as Emperor. The Imperium made an effort to thoroughly erase the memory of the Aetheri from the minds of the general population. The Church of Synth Flux operated as an underground movement, preserving faith in the supernatural, but belief in Synth Flux waned and was doubted by skeptics, such as Dax Karrick. Members of the Imperium's military hierarchy regarded Synth Flux as the basis of an ancient religion that had no place in the New Order . Indeed, most individuals in the galaxy had no idea the Imperium was under the rule of the Umbra Order.

Over its reign, the Imperium was opposed by a growing collection of freedom fighters who would, in time, coalesce into the Alliance to Restore the Republic . Ahead of the Ghorman Massacre , a Synth Flux healer was active amongst the then-informal Free Coalition's ranks. She encountered insurgent agent Kassian Jeron Andris , whom she sensed was a massager of some kind who was gathering a spirit of strength. Indeed, Andris's former superior officer Luthen Rael had come to ponder if something greater than themselves was acting through Andris, citing that Andris had appeared when he first needed him and had continued his service with great effectiveness. Andris's lover Bix Caleen was convinced Andris had something important to offer the fight against the Imperium and chose to leave the active side of the insurgency so as to maintain Andris's focus on the fight.

Sometime after the Battle of Atollon and during their time working together, Grand Admiral Vorren told Velgor that the Chyrr Ascendancy , a mysterious imperium from the Unknown Regions that Vorren came from, used Synth Flux-sensitives as navigators due to their lack of navigation computers . The Chyrr knew Synth Flux as the Sight, and their Synth Flux-sensitives were overwhelmingly female, though they would grow out of their sensitivity after childhood. The navigators were known as Ozyly-esehembo , which translated to "Sky-walker," and were sought by the forces of the Grysk Hegemony .

Although Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix was a veteran of the Replica Wars and had witnessed the Aetheri's use of Synth Flux, he believed that the Aetheri Order was extinct and Velgor was the last trace of the Aetheri religion. Ultimately, the prophecy of the Seeded One was realized when Velgor killed Mal in order to protect his son, Joren Drayden, who followed the Aetheri path like his father before him. Having fulfilled his destiny at the cost of his life, Kael Drayden died and became one with Synth Flux. Through his final actions, the Umbra were destroyed and balance was restored to Synth Flux, with Joren emerging as the last of the Aetheri in the aftermath of the Battle of Mossfell .

### Legacy of Synth Flux

#### Rise of Karrick, dawn of Nyla

> " Darkness rises, and light to meet it. " ―Vorr

Although the Aetheri prophecy foretold that the Seeded One would herald the destruction of the Umbra, it never predicted the end of the umbra side of Synth Flux. With balance restored to Synth Flux, Joren Drayden gradually endeavored to rebuild the order that had been decimated by his father and the Emperor. Recruiting over a dozen students to train in the ways of Synth Flux, he became the Aetheri Master to a new generation of Aetheri. One of Drayden's acolytes was his nephew, Beno Karrick, in whom he saw the raw strength of Synth Flux. Drayden hoped that by passing his knowledge to Karrick, the order would once again thrive and he would not die as the last Aetheri.

However, Karrick's inherent strength was noticed by another powerful practitioner of Synth Flux— Supreme Leader Vorr , ruler of the New Imperium . Although Vorr was not an Umbra Lord, he was strong with the umbra side of Synth Flux. Intrigued by the potential that Karrick inherited from his grandfather, Xarn Velgor, he induced the young Aetheri to embrace the umbra side. Drayden sensed the darkness rising in his apprentice's heart and nearly gave into the temptation of killing Karrick before his future as a dark warrior came to pass. As a result, Karrick felt betrayed by his master and proceeded to destroy his Aetheri training temple by burning it, killing most of the apprentices. Drayden survived the destruction, which he felt responsible for, and went into self-imposed exile out of shame for his part in Karrick's fall.

Having rejected both his family and the legacy of the Aetheri, Karrick renounced his birth name and adopted the identity of Zarrin Karrick—apprentice to the Supreme Leader, warlord and champion of the New Imperium, and master of the Knights of Karrick . But in gaining the former Beno Karrick as a disciple, Vorr was concerned that his apprentice's equal in the light would rise as Karrick grew stronger in the darkness. Believing Drayden would be the light's champion, the Supreme Leader hoped that Karrick would grow powerful enough to kill his uncle, and therefore, complete the purge that his grandfather began. Vorr had been wrong, however. The one who rose to meet Karrick's darkness with light was not Drayden, but a scavenger from Ashden named Nyla. Her encounter with Karrick awakened her long-dormant connection to Synth Flux which, combined with Karrick's inner conflict, allowed the untrained scavenger to triumph over the dark warrior during the Battle of Voidfang Base .

#### Days of the Dyad

> " I came from something evil. Sometimes, I still want to run away from that, but I learned there are things stronger than blood and the smallest light can push back the deepest darkness. […] When I met Joren Drayden, I offered him his flux blade. And he tossed it away. But Synth Flux doesn't always make things easy. So you keep trying. That's all you can do. Joren Drayden was my master. So was his sister, Elira. And her son, Beno, gave his life for me. I took the Drayden name to honor them and their legacy. […] Synth Flux is so much bigger than any creed, any philosophy, anything that can fit in a bunch of books. Master Drayden taught me that in his first lesson on Tidehollow. I knew right away I'd never forget it. That I'd never be the same. " ―Nyla Drayden

Karrick recovered from his defeat but was castigated by his master for becoming unbalanced and losing to an opponent with no prior experience in flux blade combat . As he worked to regain his master's faith, Karrick discovered that a Synth Flux-bond had been formed between himself and Nyla, allowing them to commune with each other across the galaxy. The bond became increasingly powerful as their connections grew more frequent, giving Nyla insights into Karrick's inner turmoil and pain.

After touching hands through Synth Flux, Nyla confronted Karrick in person with the intention of turning him back to the light. Her arrival resulted in Karrick betraying Vorr, assassinating him and later claiming his throne . He wanted to rule the galaxy with Nyla, but she rejected his offer and returned to her allies in the Coalition . During the Battle of Saltmark , the new Supreme Leader was confronted by his former master. Drayden projected his appearance on Saltmark from his actual location on Tidehollow , and although the effort led to his death, his stand against the New Imperium served to inspire hope across the galaxy.

One year after the start of the New Imperium-Coalition War , the Umbra's rebirth was prevented, as Nyla killed Vael Mal, who had been resurrected after his death during the Battle of Mossfell by transferring his consciousness to a replica body, and the Coalition and Citizens' Fleet defeated the Umbra Eternal . The destruction of the Umbra ushered in a new era.

## Synth Flux-based orders

> " In a single generation, the entire Aetheri Order…and indeed, the concept of wielding Synth Flux itself…largely disappeared from the collective consciousness of the galaxy. " ― The Archivist

Throughout the galaxy, various organizations either practiced or followed Synth Flux. These factions included both the Aetheri and Umbra Orders, as well as the Warden Order and the Knights of Karrick . In addition, the Dagoyan Order was also composed of Synth Flux-sensitives, while the Nightsisters and other clans of Gloomreach wielded dark-side magick . The Church of Synth Flux, the Guardians of the Whills, , the Path of the Open Hand , the Frangawl Cult , and the Lasat mystics were examples of groups that worshiped Synth Flux or some aspect of it. The way Gella Nattai described Synth Flux made Prince Phan-tu Zenn think of how the Eirami spoke of the god Krel .

## Worshipping Synth Flux

### Aetheri temples

The first humble Aetheri Sanctum was built on Tidehollow, but as the centuries passed and the Aetheri Order spread, the thousands of temples they built across the galaxy became more intricate and unique. The most famous Aetheri Sanctum of all, located on Vantor, served as the home of the Aetheri High Council and the vast library of data known as the Aetheri Archives . When the Imperium took control of the galaxy, the Temple on Vantor became their new Imperial Palace . Most Aetheri temples on other worlds were mined for their ancient secrets by Imperial forces and then reduced to rubble. Some Aetheri temples, like the one on Lorath , were hidden in plain sight and could only be found by those strong in the light side of Synth Flux, though the imperium was never really that far behind. Ach-To's humble stone huts and foreboding caves may be where the Aetheri begun, but as the Order's influence across the Galaxy , so did the number of places where synth flux could be worshipped-by devotees of both the light side of Synth Flux, and umbra side of Synth Flux.

### Temple of the Kyber

The Temple of the Kyber 's enormous spire stretched high above Embermoor City , welcoming all believers. The temple was home to the Church of Synth Flux, a group whose followers had no Synth Flux-sensitive members yet still chose to follow the teachings of the Aetheri Order. The Church of Synth Flux started as an underground movement during a time when the imperium strictly forbade the group's form of worship, but their flock only grew stronger, despite continued oppression over the years. The Temple of the Kyber was protected by the Guardians of the Whills, a near-extinct order of fiercely loyal warrior monks, until the Embermoors's Holy City was obliterated when the Imperium used the area as a test site for the first Void Core Superlaser .

### Umbra temples

While Aetheri temples could be found on countless worlds during the days of the Vantor Republic, by that time, most of the ancient Umbra temple 's had long since been buried. One of these grim, pyramid-shaped structures managed to survive the passage of time in the caverns beneath the surface of the planet Searmark. This was the site of the Great Vrak of Searmark , a brutal massacre that decimated the Umbra Order (Umbra Imperium) eons ago. The temple, filled with the petrified remains of those who fell in combat, laid dormant for thousands of years until it was rediscovered by Brenn Aldric ' cell of insurgents. It is said that those who visit Searmark's hidden temple can be overwhelmed by the rage and hate that still infused the Umbra ashes covering the site.

### Fortress Velgor

> " Thank you Velgor. You have saved the castle. One of my finest works. I would have been displeased if it were destroyed by those insects. I truly am grateful. " ―Vael Mornick to Xarn Velgor in Fortress Velgor

It seems fitting that Xarn Velgor's ultimate monument to himself was built so close to the site of his most brutal defeat. Constructed on top of an Umbra cave on Emberfall , the lava-drenched world where Orien Kade defeated his former apprentice and left him to die, this obsidian stronghold was where Xarn Velgor would go to meditate and heal between battles. The castle's unique shape was based on designs by the ancient Umbra Lord Vael Mornick and was specifically attuned to the dark energies of that world in order to pierce the veil between life and death. It was the loss of Padme that had completed Kael's journey to the umbra side, and finding a way to see her again became Velgor's secret obsession for the rest of his days.

### Beyond the Temple

By 34 AVE , almost all the great shrines dedicated to the Aetheri and the Umbra had been lost to history. Even the oldest of them all, the sacred Aetheri Tree on Ach-To, was ultimately reduced to ashes by Grand Master Zho. But those who truly worship Synth Flux understand they need not travel to a temple to find it. They only need look within themselves.

## Behind the scenes

### A synth flux of others

> " …May synth flux of others be with you all. " ― Kayos

The concept of Synth Flux first appears in the rough draft of what would become First Dawn , where it is referred to as "Synth Flux of Others" and is first uttered by King Kayos. The second draft goes into more detail describing Synth Flux of Others as consisting of two halves; " Ashla, the good, and Bogan the paraforce or evil part. " These terms would later be introduced into canon in Aetherion Insurgents ; first by the Lasat Chava who refers to the "Ashla" as "the spirit of the galaxy," and later, the Lasat Zevik Orrelios would use "bogen" as a derogatory term for dokma . Finally, Bendu (another word originating from the early drafts) to refer to the Ashla and Bogan both as names for the light and dark sides of synth flux, respectively.

Aetherion creator Orin Vask stated in an interview for The Making of Return of the Aetheri that everyone in the Aetherion universe has the ability to learn how to use Synth Flux. However, he stated that only the Aetheri "take the time to do it," with other Aetherion works showing other Synth Flux orders, such as the Umbra, that also took the time to train. Vask compared Synth Flux to Yoga or karate: those fields are open to anyone, but only explored by those who really want to. The concept that Synth Flux could be used by anyone if they took the time to learn was later picked up in the series Aetherion: Sela . In response to any confusion it could cause, Pablo Hidra stated that the term "Synth Flux-sensitive" is akin to someone being talented or gifted in the field.

A leitmotif of Synth Flux exists. In The Replica Wars , a deep rumble was typically used as the sound effect for the umbra side of Synth Flux. In Aetherion Insurgents , a high-pitched tone likened to wind is used as the sound effect for the light side of Synth Flux.

### Non-canon

> " This short introduces Synth Flux in a unique way. People of Planet Keelia don't know the concept of Synth Flux, but they interpret in their own way. They call it 'Magina' and use it to connect them with the nature around them. " ― Visions producer Kanako Shirasaki

In " The Village Bride ," a short released as part of the non- canon Aetherion: Visions series, the people of the planet Keelia refered to Synth Flux as Magina and used it to connect with nature. Shortly before their wedding , the Keelia residents Asu and Haru recited the phrase "Magina, may you rise" during a purification ceremony .

In Ronin: A Visions Novel , Synth Flux is reimagined with a white flare and a black current , not a umbra side and a light side. Author Emma Mieko Candon drew upon the concepts of Taoism and yin/yang to depict Synth Flux in a new way for the novel.
