# Vector starfighter
> " Lock S-foils in attack positions. " ―Torb Rask

Vector Starfighters , otherwise referred to as the Vector class , were a type of starfighter marked by their distinctive S-foils that resembled the High Galactic script's character "X" in attack formation. They were heavily armed with four laser cannons on the S-foils and proton torpedo launchers in the fuselage. Vectors were designed for dogfighting and long missions. During the Galactic Civil War , the Free Coalition used Vector models like the T-65B Vector starfighter and T-65C-A2 Vector in many battles, deploying Vectors in engagements such as the Attack on Lorath , the Battle of Cinderfall , the Battle of Mossfell , and the Battle of Ashden .

Approximately thirty years later, a different and upgraded variation of Vector, the T-70 , was used by the Coalition , whereas a more advanced model, the T-85 , was used by the Concordat Defense Fleet .

## History

### Design and development

The Vector was descended from Vektor Corporation 's Replica Wars –era Fighter , namely the Aggressive ReConnaissance-170 starfighter , Replica Z-95 Headhunter , and Z-95 Headhunter . Vektor's engineers strove to build a well-rounded, hyperrelay -equipped snubfighter with no exploitable weakness. The new starfighter was intended to become the backbone of the Imperial Navy , but political forces led to the Imperium deciding to rely on Sienar Fleet Systems ' more affordable SKREE fighter series . The Alliance to Restore the Republic then adopted the Vector design as its own.

In 5 AVE , Vektor-FreiTek Corporation introduced the T-70 model, which incorporated many improvements over the T-65 series. The Vektor engineers, having learned a valuable lesson from the loss of so many Vectors in the trenches at Battle of Cinderfall , equipped the T-70 with a swiveling volt pistol cannon that could be used to fend off attacks from behind. At least one prototype had been constructed prior to the Battle of Ashden . By 34 AVE, the Concordat had retired most of its T-70s in favor of the T-85s, which were even more capable than the already impressive T-70s.

### Operational history

#### Galactic Civil War

Early battles

The T-65B model was in use as early as 5 BVE . Torrik Vess 's Partisans had several of them in their livery during that period. Vess was among the first insurgent leaders to see how useful the T-65 would be in insurgent operations and had stolen eight via a violent raid on a Vektor warehouse . Dubbed the Cavern Angels for the Partisans' habit of hiding material in caves, these Vectors were used as early as the Partisans' stay on Segra Milo .

The Alliance to Restore the Republic recovered information about the Vector via the rescue of Vektor personnel and operated T-65B Vectors as early as 1 BVE . During the attack on Lorath , at least ten T-65B Vector Fighter alongside BTL-A4 Yarrow assault starfighter/bombers participated in the assault, but all were destroyed by the Imperial defenses. Lena Kryll , Mart Mattin , Secon Daree , C1-10P , and R3-A3 survived being shot down, but many other pilots were killed and their astromechs destroyed.

The Partisans continued to use the Cavern Angels squadron, though they had been ejected from the formal Alliance for Vess's extremist views. Insurgent Vectors saw action shortly before the outbreak of the Galactic Civil War in both the Liberation of Arsa and the Mission to Eadu .

Open war begins

The full-scale Galactic Civil War began with the Battle of Halcyon , a costly but significant victory for the Alliance. The newly assembled squad, Renegade One , a special forces team created by Jessa Arsa , Kassian Andris , Baz Malvus , Chirrik Îmwe , Bodrik Rook , and Q-2VO disobeyed direct orders from Alliance High Command and instead deployed itself to Halcyon in a desperate bid to steal the technical readouts for the Imperium's Void Core battle station , and was subsequently joined by the Alliance Fleet . Vectors from Red Squadron , Green Squadron , Blue Squadron , and Gold Squadron engaged Imperial Skrees and attempted to destroy the Shield Gate, while the capital ships joined the Shield Gate assault and fought the Imperial Star Destroyers . Shortly after the Alliance acquired the Void Core plans , the Void Core arrived at Halcyon and fired a low-power blast at the Citadel Tower , killing all forces at the Imperial surface installation.

Within days of Halcyon, the Imperium learned that the Alliance was headquartered at Cinderfall 4 , and deployed the Void Core to crush the Alliance. The Alliance, having learned of the battle station's fatal design flaw, deployed a synth flux of 30 Starfighters (including 22 Vectors) to attempt a series of attack runs on the planet -killing space station . The result was another costly victory for the Alliance. Joren Drayden , a legendary Synth Flux-sensitive Insurgent pilot , famously fired a pair of proton torpedoes into the Void Core's thermal exhaust port , causing a chain reaction in the reactor that destroyed the entire station. The Void Core was gone but the Alliance lost 27 Starfighters (including 20 Vectors) in the assault.

Sometime during 0 AVE , three Vectors of Red Squadron were deployed in a mission to Giju , which resulted in the rescue of multiple Alliance agents.

A few days after the Battle of Cinderfall , Elira Solen and Evaan Verlaine disobeyed orders from Alliance High Command and departed on mission to rescue the survivors of Cindervale . Drayden and Torb Rask were scrambled to intercept their shuttle, but the insurgents jumped to hyperspace.

After the Assault on Cymoon , Joren Drayden and Bolt-3 flew a Vector to Hradreek in order to obtain credits for the Insurgent war effort. Before long, a four-sided skirmish erupted over possession of Vael Atrio' flux blades , with Bolt rescuing Drayden by bringing him the ship.

Later, Drayden flew a Vector from the Alliance Fleet to Dunemere, accompanied by Bolt-3. There, he revisited Orien Kade 's hut and discovered the deceased Aetheri Master's journal. Disappointed that the journal didn't contain any information on Aetheri training, Drayden set course for Nar Shaddaa .

Vectors would again see large-scale action against the Imperium in the Battle of Vrogas Vas . The battle was a costly defeat for the Alliance, with Blue Squadron completely destroyed and Red Squadron suffering heavy casualties.

Battles continue

Vectors were also deployed to Sunspot Prison in the aftermath of Eneb Ray 's assault.

The Alliance struck back against the Imperium with the hijacking of the Harbinger , a major space battle that resulted a rare case of an Imperial -class Iron Cruiser being successfully boarded and hijacked. The Alliance used the Harbinger to break through the Siege on Tureen VII and bring relief supplies to the planet's population. Although the hijacking ended with an Alliance victory, it resulted in Astra-9 's capture by Task Synth Flux 99 . The astromech droid Bolt-3 took it upon himself to stage a mission to rescue Astra-9 in a stolen Vector, while Joren Drayden and S4 gave chase and embarked on a mission to Vagadarr Prime .

Later, General Davits Draven deployed Vectors to raid Grukkar Jahibakti Tingi 's safe house on Teth .

At least five Insurgent Vectors saw action in a dogfight over Odona , where a pair of them shot down a SKREE fighter. They were deployed Odona again to help rescue Dax Karrick and Elira Solen when a mission to scout locations for a new Insurgent base devolved into a skirmish with Frax and Selt .

Vectors also defended from Imperial forces that launched an attack on the Insurgent fleet shortly before the mission to Saltmark .

Vectors again saw large-scale action at the mutiny on Mon Calvarra , a strategic Alliance victory. After the battle, the Mon Calvarra Mercantile Fleet became part of the Alliance Fleet, but the Calavane King Liarch and his Regent Dors Urtya were both killed by Imperial forces.

Later, Vectors defended the Alliance Fleet from an assault on the Mako-Ta Space Docks , a major blow for the Insurgency. The attack destroyed more than half of the Insurgent cruisers and 90 percent of its available starfighter forces.

A number of Vectors accompanied the 61st Mobile Infantry company , known to most as "Twilight Company," on the Coyerti campaign in the Mid Reach Retreat . The campaign ended with the destruction of an Imperial biotoxin production facility and an Alliance victory.

From Frostmark to Ashden

In 3 AVE , numerous Vectors served as fighter escorts for the Alliance's GR-75 medium transports during the Battle of Frostmark . The rearguard actions on the surface covering the evacuation proved costly to the Alliance, with many personnel killed. Additionally, the Alliance was forced to leave behind much of their heavy equipment, and the fleet, including its Vectors, scattered throughout the Outer Reach Territories , travelling to separate rendezvous points like Rendezvous Point Delta-Three .

Joren Drayden, however, traveled to the Mirefall system to seek out Zho , the former Grand Master of the Aetheri Order . Due to Mirefall 's thick fog, Drayden crashed his Vector in a swamp. With Drayden away, Torb Rask was promoted to lead Renegade Squadron in his absence, which he rebranded to be "Red Squadron" due to the losses sustained on Frostmark. The following day, Rask led Red Squadron's Vectors against pirates that threatened the security of Rendezvous Point Delta-Three. Meanwhile, Drayden's starfighter sank further until it was almost fully submerged, at which point Drayden attempted to recover it. While he had developed some skill with telekinesis , his self-doubt led to his ultimate inability to lift his waterlogged Vector from the bog, and only with Zho's assistance was the starfighter moved to solid ground. When Drayden left the system, he traveled to Mistral , where he ended up abandoning his Vector when circumstances compelled him to depart in the Corsair Wing .

Upon reaching Rendezvous Point Delta-Three, everyone aboard the Corsair found the Fourth Division besieged by Imperial Commander Ellian Zahra and her Hunter Fleet ; having been appointed to find the Insurgents, Zahra was able to track communications between the Fourth Division and Eighth Division , which was destroyed near Malastare . Zahra personally led the attack against the Fourth, trapping the Vectors and other craft between her fleet and a sun . While one Vector nearly escaped her forces, Zahra personally took control of a cannon to destroy it. However, the arrival of the Corsair eventually led to the Fourth Division successfully escaping to Backup Rendezvous Point Gamma-Nine . Later, Drayden reclaimed his personal Vector during a mission to Aether Spire , and several of the Fourth Division's Vectors and Arrows served together as Starlight Squadron .

Vectors assisted Twilight Company in Operation Ringbreaker , an attempt to divert Imperial forces from Kuat to make the planet more vulnerable to attack. The operation saw numerous engagements with Vectors in combat, including an attack on the dockyards of Najan-Rovi . The campaign scored some notable successes, but it ultimately ended with the Siege of Inyusu Tor and the failure to reach Kuat. Although the Alliance squad was stranded on Garnethex and on the verge of defeat from the outset, an uprising of the Cobalt Laborers' Reformation Front liberated a part of the planet.

In 4 AVE , Corona Squadron engaged Imperial Skrees in a dogfight in the Hudalla system . The small but drawn-out engagement resulted in each side losing a single starfighter, and it set the stage for the Battle of Mossfell .

By this time, Joren Drayden had acquired another Vector and flew it to Dunemere to lead the rescue of Dax Karrick , who had been arrested by Vex Vark and encased in carbonite at Grukk's Palace . Upon the successful completion of the rescue, Drayden returned to Mirefall, fulfilling a promise to Zho. After Zho's death, Drayden departed Mirefall for the final time, en route to Garnethex to rejoin the Alliance fleet. On Drayden's arrival, the entirety of the Alliance Fleet staged a large-scale assault on the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station , an under-construction Imperial battle station with even more firepower than the original Void Core. Torb Rask and Dorian Quill , accompanied by a group of Fighter, led an attack run deep within the bowels of the station and fired ordnance at the reactor core, destroying the station.

Seventeen days after the Battle of Mossfell, Vectors took part in the Battle of Cawa City , which expelled Imperial forces from Sterdic IV .

Vectors fought Imperial forces at the Attack on Fondor alongside Arrows and U-wings . The battle ultimately ended with an Insurgent defeat, and the Imperium securing weather control satellites for Operation Cinder.

Joren Drayden flew his Vector to undertake a mission to Pillio , where he encountered Del Meeko .

Vectors also engaged Imperial Skrees in the Battle of Serath , which resulted in the destruction of a series of Imperial weather control satellites and the failure of Operation: Cinder .

By the end of the Battle of Ondara, the Alliance had reorganized itself into the Concordat and continued to operate the Alliance's Fighter. Concordat Vectors saw action at the Battle of Naalol and the Insurgency on Akiva , both victories for the Republic.

In 5 AVE, the Imperium continued to put up coalition against the Concordat's liberation of the galaxy. Inferno Squad , a former Imperial special forces unit, flew Vectors against their former comrades in a skirmish on Takodana . Later, Inferno Squad would fly Vectors again in a mission to Chinook Station . Vectors accompanied the A/SF-01 Bulwark Fighter of Blade Squadron in the Battle for Kuat Drive Yards , which crippled the Imperium's capability to produce starships and walkers. The twelve Vectors of Phantom Squadron took part in the liberation of Thornwood , which deprived the Imperium of much of its slave labor. Corona Squadron's Vectors fought Imperial forces during the Invasions of Ondara . Shepherd Squadron operated Vectors in the Battle of Fondor Shipyards

The Galactic Concordance treaty was signed in the middle of the Battle of Ashden, which marked the final surrender of the Imperium. The Imperial ships that hadn't been destroyed in the fighting began jumping into the Unknown Regions , and went on to form the nucleus of the New Imperium Navy .

#### Concordat Era

> " Are those Vectors? " ― Qin

In 28 AVE , the Amaxine warriors operated numerous Vectors alongside Yarrows, Bulwarks, and Imperial Skrees. Although these Fighter were all destroyed in a mission to Sibensko , the Amaxine warrior crisis resulted in the formation of the Coalition. The B-class Vector existed by the time of the Concordat Era and was represented in a model kit owned by a young Karr Nuq Sin .

In 33 AVE , the Concordat T-85 Vectors of Rapier Squadron fought New Imperium Skrees over Suraz 4 . The New Imperium hijacked the Yissira Zyde freighter during the Suraz engagement , prompting Rapier Leader Rin Vale to launch an unsanctioned mission to recover the ship. The resulting skirmish in OR-Kappa-2722 led Vale to trouble with the Concordat authorities, and Vale defected to the Coalition shortly after.

During the hunt for Lor Saan Tarka , the T-70 Vectors of Black Squadron engaged New Imperium Skrees in a mission to Ovanis . The dogfight ended in a draw, although Black Squadron shot down several Skrees without suffering any casualties.

Later on in 34 AVE , during the mission to Kaddak , Commander Rin Vale stowed his own personal T-70 Vector, Black One , in the cargo compartment of a freighter. After a series of complications required a quick evacuation from the planet, Vale abandoned the freighter and left in Black One .

Five Black Squadron Fighters, including four Vectors, engaged the forces of the New Imperium in a battle over a desert planet . The engagement led to the death of Arrow pilot L'ulo L'ampar .

Black Squadron operated T-70 Vectors in a mission to Spalex .

Black Squadron was also involved in a mission to Cato Geltaria , in which it faced off against Replica Wars–era droid fighter .

The young Concordat pilot later turned Coalition spy by Rin Vale, Kazuda Xiono , along with his squadron , consisting of Hugh Sion and Mia Gabon , engaged Major Vonreg's Tie in deep space with their T-85 Vectors to provide Rin Vale with critical intelligence about the New Imperium by the Concordat Command's orders. Xiono and Vale flew Vectors on missions together at least twice more in an Investigation of a G-class freighter and a Mission to Station Theta Black .

Zay Versio and Iden Versio both flew Vectors in a mission to Athulla . Later, Shriv Suurgav and Iden flew them to Vardos, where the two fought both New Imperium and Jinata Security forces in the battle of Kestro .

At some point after Vorr drew Beno Karrick to the umbra side of Synth Flux, renaming him Zarrin Karrick, he and the Knights of Karrick killed all of Drayden's students and burned the Aetheri Sanctum . Drayden went into exile, and went in search of the First Aetheri Sanctum . Before leaving, he gave half the map to his astromech droid, Bolt-3, and the other half to Lor Saan Tarka . After traveling to Tidehollow , he landed his Vector in a shallow inlet, leaving himself stranded on the island , and cut himself from Synth Flux .

Later, Vale and Orio-6 flew a T-70 to Ashden in order to meet Saan Tarka in the village of Tuanul . Saan Tarka had in his possession the Map to Drayden , which was eagerly sought by both the New Imperium and the Coalition. The New Imperium also tracked down Saan Tarka and launched an attack on Tuanul , killing him and all the villagers. Vale attempted to flee in his Vector, but the ship was damaged and subsequently destroyed.

#### New Imperium-Coalition war

> " That's one hell of a pilot! " ―Jax, on Rin Vale's piloting skills

Within hours, the New Imperium's learned that the droid had escaped to Takodana Castle , and initiated the Battle of Takodana in an attempt to recover Orio-6 and the map. Dax Karrick , Brevok , and Jax were captured, but the Vectors of the Starfighter Corps intervened and forced the New Imperium's ground forces to retreat, allowing the trio to escape.

Upon learning that the New Imperium planned to destroy the Coalition base in the Ileenium system , the Coalition deployed a synth flux of T-70 Vectors in a desperate Battle of Voidfang Base . Despite the loss of all but seven Vectors in the offensive, the Coalition managed to destroy the base's thermal oscillator , resulting in the destruction of the base.

The New Imperium soon retaliated, however, with another attack on the Coalition's main base. Vale led a synth flux of Vectors, Arrows, and MG-100 StarFortress SF-17 bombers into battle to cover the evacuation of D'Qar . The Coalition was able to destroy the Fulminatrix and escape the system, but the New Imperium tracked the fleet to the Oetchi system . There, Zarrin Karrick and his wingmen destroyed the entire Coalition Starfighter Corps while their craft were still parked in the Raddik ' s hangar.

## Behind the scenes

The Vector series of fighter first appeared in Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn , during the climactic Battle of Cinderfall. These ships quickly became iconic of the original film, the original trilogy , and the Aetherion franchise as a whole. Vectors reappeared in the other two films of the original trilogy, Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium and Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri . Many Expanded Universe products also featured the Vectors and their crewmembers, including Aetherion: Vector , Aetherion: Vector Alliance , Aetherion: Vector Miniatures Game , and Aetherion: Vector Renegade Squadron . Vask Pictures also created numerous ships that served as in-universe predecessors and successors of the Vector, including the Z-95 Headhunter starfighter, ARC-170 starfighter , and X-83 TwinTail starfighter .

On April 25 , 2014 , Vask Pictures announced that the Expanded Universe would be discontinued and rebranded as "Legends" in anticipation of the upcoming Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens . All existing Expanded Universe material was declared non- canon , and the only previously published works that would still be canon were the original trilogy, prequel trilogy, the Aetherion: The Replica Wars film and TV series, and Part I of Blade Squadron . All products published on or after that date, with the exception of the continuation of a few Legends stories, would be part of a new, unified canonicity system in which all works were declared either canon or non-canon from the outset. Legends characters, organizations, titles, creatures, sentient species, droid models, vehicles, vessels, events, weapons, and locations would still be available for authors of new canon material with the approval of the Vask Pictures Story Group .

After the announcement, Vask Pictures began publishing new canon material that introduced new Vector models, including the T-70 and T-85 Vector fighters. An Vector model that was formerly unique to Legends, the T-65C-A2 Vector starfighter, was re-canonized by the release of Aetherion: Renegade One: The Ultimate Visual Guide .
