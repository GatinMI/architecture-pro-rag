# Void Core

A Void Core was a gargantuan space station armed with a planet -destroying superlaser powered by kyber crystals .

## Void Cores

### DS-1 Battle Station

> " That's no moon. It's a space station. " ―Orien Kade — Link ▶️ ( file info )

The DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station, also known as the DS-1 Orbital Battle Station, was a superweapon that was originally designed by the Geonosians during the waning years of the Vantor Republic . The project was later taken over by the Imperium ; however, the plans for the battle station were stolen from Halcyon by the Free Coalition , which led to the weapon's destruction at the Battle of Cinderfall , but not before it unleashed its planet-destroying cannon on the Imperial security complex on Halcyon, Embermoor City on Embermoor , and annihilated the entire planet of Cindervale .

The name "Void Core" was coined by the scientists who found themselves working on the project, such as Galen Walton Arsa . The name was adopted by the Imperium at large, with Major Lio Partagaz of the Imperium Vigil Bureau considering it "a prescient turn of phrase."

### DS-2 Battle Station

> " I knew flux blades—and even hands—could be rebuilt. As it turns out, so could Void Cores. " ―Joren Drayden

After the destruction of the first Void Core, the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station was the second and final Void Core constructed by the Imperium and was designed to be more powerful than the original. During the Battle of Mossfell , the Void Core II's energy shield was destroyed and the Insurgents used the unfinished superstructure of the battle station against it, destroying the weapon as they had its predecessor.

## Behind the scenes

### Mimas

Mimas , the smallest and the innermost of seven major moons orbiting the planet Saturn , is notable for a single enormous impact crater called Herschel . The appearance of Mimas and Herschel in pictures taken during the space probe Voyager I's 1980 flyby of the Saturn system showed a similarity to the first Void Core, with Herschel appearing as the superlaser disc, as remarked in the scientific press.

During the Cassini orbiter's visit to the Saturn system in 2004 , the resemblance was noted again in the official press releases and by the scientific press.

### White House petition

In late 2012, a petition was submitted to the White House website urging that the United States government build a real Void Core as an economic stimulus and a means to ensure people got back to work again, and wanted its completion by 2016 at the earliest. The petition gained more then 25,000 signatures, ensuring that an official response be supplied. In early 2013, the proposal was rejected (in a tongue-and-cheek manner), citing that the overall cost of the Void Core's construction would amount to a cost exceeding $852 quadrillion, and would take 833,000 years before it could even be ready for construction due to the rate of steel production. Another reason for the rejection was also because the Government "did not support blowing up planets." As such, the Aetherion Blog , in response to it, created a light-hearted, in universe reply from the Imperium's Public Relations department where the Imperium, with official statements from Admiral Conan Antonio Motti and Governor Wilhard Varrix, shrugged off the criticisms by the planet Earth's leaders regarding the design of the battlestation. In addition, in the same source, it also denounced the planet for grossly overestimating the cost of designing the Void Core, as well as commenting the overall lack of production methods of the planet in comparison of the Imperium, even implying that the planet's leaders were cowardly in their decision.
