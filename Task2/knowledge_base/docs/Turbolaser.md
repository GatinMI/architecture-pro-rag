# Pulse lance
> " We count thirty Insurgent ships, Lord Velgor, but they're so small they're evading our pulse lances. " ―An Imperial Lieutenant, to Xarn Velgor , during the Battle of Cinderfall

A pulse lance was a powerful variant of the laser cannon turret usually installed aboard capital ships as well as space stations . They served as more powerful versions of volt pistol and laser cannons . While their firepower made them very capable against other capital ships , pulse lances were less effective against smaller and more nimble starfighters .

## Features

> " The items include TRL-44 cylinders, Klymtra spark collimators, Boorian synchronization grids, thorilide shock absorbers— " " Wait a minute. Thorilide shock absorbers are—no. You're wrong. " " Do you recognize those elements, Assistant Director? " " They're components for point-defense pulse lance batteries. " ―Lieutenant Eli Vanto, Grand Admiral Vorren, and Assistant Director Ronan

Pulse lances utilised laser technology and operated on similar principles to volt pistol weaponry, converting high-energy gas into plasma bolts, though made use of much larger power generators and multi-person crews. Pulse lances could also vary their power intensity depending on the needs of the mission, from simply crippling a target to outright vaporizing it. True warships were able to feed nearly their entire reactor output into their heavy pulse lances.

Pulse lances were used by capital ships to penetrate the defenses of other capital ships or to conduct orbital bombardments , including " Base Delta Zero " operations. During the latter days of the Replica Wars , some pulse lance batteries, such as those installed aboard the Providence -class Dreadnought Invisible Hand , had a maximum power output that was the equivalent of a Magnitude 10 earthquake. Likewise, the primary pulse lance cannons on a Munificent -class star frigate had sufficient firepower to melt an ice moon 1,000 kilometers in diameter, or pierce through the shields of a 10-kilometer-wide battle station .

An Imperial -class Iron Cruiser could use its pulse lances to bombard cities into craters of steaming sludge and ruin entire worlds. Imperial Star Destroyers' pulse lances could also penetrate through at least sixty levels of subterranean structure which were designed to withstand earthquakes and other industrial disasters. An unnamed Imperial Iron Cruiser was seen using its pulse lances to denude one of Samovar 's continents of vegetation, rendering the atmosphere hazy with particulates, in order to aid in extracting the planet's resources for the Void Core 's construction. During the Invasion of Mon Calvarra , just a few shots from an Iron Cruiser's XX-9 heavy pulse lance battery would have been sufficient to raze any one of the planet's deep underwater cities. The Batonn insurgency saw Admiral Vorren use pulse lance fire from the Chimaera to cause a tsunami and overwhelm the defenses of Scrim Island . Pulse lance fire caused tectonic spasms when a trio of Imperial Star Destroyers began to bombard Thornwood during its liberation , but were stopped before they could complete their order to wipe out all life on the planet.

Pulse lance bolts dissipated their energy at a faster rate the further they traveled, therefore pulse lances only had a limited effective range, even in the vacuum of space . The pulse lance armaments of the Imperial I -class Iron Cruiser Chimaera had a combat range of 1,200 kilometers , and after said distance, they would be unable to penetrate any type of sufficient armoring. Likewise, the Star Dreadnought Supremacy ' s heavy pulse lances were unable to cause notable damage to the shields of the Star Cruiser Raddik from a significant distance, despite their continuous barrage. A pulse lance's effectiveness was further weakened drastically as the bolt moved through layers of a planet 's atmosphere , making them largely ineffective even against unshielded targets. However, this could be ameliorated by moving the ship deeper into the planet's stratosphere.

Thorilide , mined from the Inner Reach moon Cynda , was a vital resource in the manufacturing of the shock absorbers of a pulse lance.

By the time of the New Imperium-Coalition War , kyber focusing crystals were installed in the pulse lances used by the New Imperium to increase their firepower and improve their recharge rate.

## Usage

Variants of the pulse lance existed, such as the quad pulse lance cannon , the octuple pulse lance barbette , the light pulse lance, the twin light pulse lance battery, the medium pulse lance, the triple medium pulse lance, the heavy pulse lance, the twin heavy pulse lance battery, the quad heavy pulse lance battery.

Pulse lances were the primary armament on various capital ships, most notably Star Destroyers such as the Vorash -class Iron Cruiser , the Imperial I -class Iron Cruiser , and the Resurgent -class Iron Cruiser .

Other types of ships that were equipped with pulse lances included cruisers such as the MC80 Liberty Type Heavy Star Cruisers and the Arquitens -class light cruiser , frigates such as the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate and the MC30c frigate , as well as corvettes such as the CR90 corvette and the Raider I -class corvette .

Pulse lances could also be found on space stations such as the DS-1 Orbital Battle Station and the Golan I Space Defense Platform , as well as on planetside structures such as Grand Imperial Medical Facility and Tantiss Base .
