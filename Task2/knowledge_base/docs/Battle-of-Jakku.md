# Battle of Ashden
> " We're headed toward a large standoff with the insurgents. We're committing a fair portion of the fleet, and if the damned Insurgency wants to stand a chance of keeping that sector, they'll have to do the same. This promises to be the largest battle since Mossfell. " ―Grand Moff Randd

The Battle of Ashden was the decisive last stand of the Imperium against the Concordat on the surface and above of the desert world of Ashden as the Galactic Civil War drew to a final conclusion after a year of prolonged fighting following the Battle of Mossfell . During the waning months of the conflict, Counselor Gallard Raxen assumed control of the Imperial Military and concentrated much of its remaining forces into the remote Western Reaches of the Inner Reach where the Imperium would make its last standoff against the Concordat.

Unknown to his own soldiers , Raxen plotted to destroy the Imperium in order to fulfill his obligations to the late Emperor Theron Mal . However, he also intended to retreat into the Unknown Regions where he could forge a new Imperium with those whom he regarded as the worthy few, such as Commandant Brendol Vren . When the Republic's forces attacked the Imperium on Ashden, Raxen attempted to destroy the entire planet along with both rival governments. However, his efforts were thwarted by Grand Admiral Raena Vexana , who killed Raxen during the battle.

While the fighting on Ashden would continue in the months that followed Raxen's death , the battle was a decisive victory for the Concordat, whereupon the Imperium collapsed. In the aftermath, Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon and Chancellor Vela Sess signed the Galactic Concordance on Serenne , officially marking the end of the Galactic Civil War. Following the end of the insurgency, an Imperial remnant was formed from the remaining Imperial forces on Ashden and fought against the Concordat.

## Prelude

### Imperial disintegration

> " It will be our proving ground. We will test ourselves on Ashden. And we will do so far from the eyes of the galaxy, far from the eyes of Vela Sess and her sycophants. And when the time is right, when we have whetted ourselves to a vicious point, we will strike once again. The Senate is injured. The Republic is wounded. We will go in for the kill, but it is too soon and we are too weak. " ―Counselor Gallard Raxen

During the Battle of Mossfell , the Imperium was drastically weakened when the Alliance to Restore the Republic managed to destroy its dreaded battle station , the second Void Core . Before it was destroyed, Joren Drayden , an Aetheri Knight , turned his father , the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor , away from the umbra side of Synth Flux . Velgor, once again the Aetheri Kael Drayden, threw Vael Mal , his former Umbra Master and Galactic Emperor of the New Order, down the second Void Core's reactor core, where he died . The Insurgents celebrated their victory on the Mossfell moon , believing the fight to be won. Nevertheless the Insurgency, which had been reorganized into a government known as the Concordat , would still be engaged in bloody combat against the remaining forces of the Imperium in the year following the station's destruction.

In the months after the Battle of Mossfell, the Concordat won a string of victories against the Imperium, consolidated control over much of the galaxy , and reconvened the Galactic Senate on Serenne . Meanwhile, the Imperium splintered into several Imperial remnants including Governor Ubrik Adelvard 's Imperial remnant and Grand Moff Lozen Tolruck 's remnant on Thornwood . While Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon still ruled Vantor , he was largely a powerless figurehead who lived under house arrest on the wartorn Imperial capital.

By 5 AVE , Fleet Admiral Gallard Raxen had assumed leadership of the Imperial forces in the Vulpinus Nebula , the Almagest , the Recluse's Nebula , the Queluhan Nebula , the Ro-Loo Triangle , and the Inamorata . Raxen also commanded the Ravager , the last Super Iron Cruiser under Imperial control. Raxen exercised control through his puppet Grand Admiral Raena Vexana , an able commander who served as the Imperium's public face. Raxen manipulated Vexana into instigating faux peace talks with the Concordat. After framing Vexana for the Attack on Serenne , Raxen assumed leadership of the Imperium's loyalist forces and proclaimed himself Counselor to the Imperium . He then brought the Ravager as well as most of, if not the entirety of, the remaining Imperial fleet to Ashden. In the withdraw to Ashden, Imperial forces abandoned the Rothana Imperial Shipyards , leaving behind a good number of craft that were still under-construction or repair. All battle-ready ships and base personnel left for Ashden, leaving behind only the auto-security and droids as shipyard defense.

Unknown to Raxen's subordinates , Ashden had been designated as the staging ground in Theron Mal's Contingency plan to ensure that the Imperium would not outlive him if he were to die. As an Umbra Lord, Mal had no plan of delegating his Imperium to a successor. Mal had met Raxen on Ashden after the young orphan had snuck aboard his yacht in 25 BVE . Sensing potential in the boy, Mal tasked Raxen with guarding a mysterious excavation site in the Plaintive Hand plateau , which later became the Ashden Observatory complex. In return, Mal arranged for Raxen to receive an education and engineered his rise to Fleet Admiral. Throughout his life, Raxen was tasked with carrying out the Emperor's Contingency.

### The Contingency Plan

> " If an Imperium cannot protect its Emperor then that Imperium must be deemed a failure. It collapses not only because its central figure is gone, but because it must not be allowed to remain! " ―Emperor Theron Mal, to Gallard Raxen

Under Raxen's command, Imperial forces established a presence on the desert planet for months prior to the Concordat accidentally discovering them. The long wait coupled with Ashden's harsh and bleak climate exacted a heavy psychological toll on the Imperial forces. Many of the Imperial shock troopers and officers stationed on Ashden became undisciplined and failed to take care of their uniforms and armor. They also terrorized the local communities by stealing food and water. Raxen also exercised harsh discipline against personnel who questioned their purpose on Ashden. One shock trooper RK-242 was beaten up by his Sergeant Rylon and comrades for expressing a desire not to be on Ashden.

In addition to the orbiting Imperial armada, Counselor Raxen also maintained an Imperial base in the Sinking Fields for his ground forces. In addition, Raxen developed a working relationship with the local Grak crime lord Nimara . Nimara's slaves and henchmen kidnapped many children from small villages and makeshift orphanages run by the Anchorites . Raxen had once been a servant to the Anchorite Kolob and sought revenge against the Anchorites for his harsh upbringing at the hands of Kolob. These children were then trained by the former Commandant Brendol Vren into ruthless child soldiers who were prepared to obey and kill at Raxen's orders. While searching for Raxen, Vexana and Brentin learned about Raxen's activities. These revelations only intensified Vexana's hatred of Raxen, who had usurped her authority during the Attack on Serenne.

Raxen also maintained another Imperial facility in Ashden's Plaintive Hand plateau known as the Observatory. The Observatory housed a replica of the late Emperor's pleasure yacht Imperialis and a computerized map of the Unknown Regions based on Grand Admiral Vorren's knowledge of that unexplored region. The Observatory was also built over a deep borehole which led to the molten core of Ashden. As part of the Contingency, Raxen planned to destroy the Imperium and their Concordat opponents by blowing up the planet. In secret, Raxen subverted the Contingency by secretly arranging for himself and other "worthy" Imperials like Vren, his son Armitage Vren , and the child soldiers to escape into the Unknown Regions to rebuild the Imperium.

### Discovery and deadlock

> " Those who vote no: Recognize that you are marked. You will be marked as cowards at best and traitors at worst. " ―An excerpt from Chancellor Sess's speech urging the Galactic Senate to send military forces to Ashden

While searching for the fugitive Raena Vexana, the Concordat operative Nevra Wexlin and her team discovered Counselor Raxen's Imperial fleet above Ashden. Nevra and the bounty hunter Jas Emari used an escape pod to land on the planet while her son Temmin Wexlin and Sinjir Rath Velus took their ship Moth back to the Concordat capital on Serenne. Temmin sent his B1-series battle droid and companion Mister Bones to look after his mother on Ashden. Nevra and Jas were captured by shock troopers. Nevra was forced to work at a kesium gas rig while Jas found herself a captive of Nimara the Grak and the bounty hunter Mercurial Swift ; with the latter seeking to collect a Black Sun bounty on Jas as punishment for an earlier ambush on Tarisa .

Meanwhile, Vexana and the former insurgent operative Brentin Lore Wexlin traveled to Ashden to seek revenge against Gallard Raxen. After encountering Nimara, they managed to convince the Grak crime lady to take them to Raxen's weapons facility. Nevra later escaped her Imperial captors with the help of Mister Bones. Jas later escaped from her captors and reunited with Nevra. Continuing their hunt for Vexana, Nevra and her comrades tracked them down to the Plaintive Hand plateau. However, Counselor Raxen spotted Vexana among Nimara's caravan and bombarded them. Nevra and Jas could only watch as Raxen and his Imperial forces took Vexana and Brentin into custody. The former Grand Admiral and insurgent operative were imprisoned at the main Imperial base on Ashden, where Raxen made preparations for his apocalyptic showdown with the Concordat.

Elsewhere, Temmin and Sinjir returned to Serenne with news of the Imperial presence on Ashden. The two warned Chancellor Vela Sess , Princess Elira Solen , and Dax Karrick . However, Sess's political rival Senator Tolwar Wartol had planted a listening device on Elira and Dax's nanny droid T-2LC . Wartol leaked this news to the media and accused Sess of withholding information from the public. Per Concordat procedure, the Galactic Senate was charged with voting to authorize any large-scale military action by the Concordat Defense Synth Flux .

During the Senate's first vote on the issue on Serenne, the vote failed to pass since Vela Sess failed to secure a majority by five votes. This issue was complicated by the Concordat's efforts to transfer its capital from Serenne to the agricultural world of Nakadia in the Mid Reach . Later, the Chancellor discovered that Senator Wartol had conspired with Black Sun and the Red Key Raiders to coerce five senators into voting against Sess's resolution. These senators were Ashmin Ek of Anthan Prime , Rethalow of Frong , Dor Wieedo of Rodanor , Grelka Sorka , and Nim Tar . Sess managed to delay the re-vote by eating pta fruit aboard Wartol's ship. Since Nakadia had strict biosecurity laws, Wartol's ship was quarantined for several hours.

Meanwhile, Sess dispatched a team of five spies consisting of Dax Karrick, Temmin, Sinjir, the former Concordat Special Forces soldier Jom Barell , and Sinjir's boyfriend Conder Kyl to spy on the five senators and convince them to change their votes. They discovered that Ashmin Ek, Rethalow, and Dor Wieedo had been bribed into opposing Sess's resolution in favor of favorable trade deals. Nim Tar's child and Sorka's pet jerba had been kidnapped. Karrick and his team managed to rescued Nim Tar's child from the gangsters. Sinjir then posed as Sess's adviser and issued a missive offering to pardon the five senators for their transgressions if they voted in favor of Sess's resolution the second time. Due to Sinjir's connivance, the five senators voted in favor of sending Concordat forces to Ashden.

## The battle

### Preparations

> " The battle is upon us. Go! Go and drag them down to the ground and break their necks with your boots! Take their heads! End their tyranny! " ―Counselor Gallard Raxen extolling his men to destroy the Concordat

Following the Galactic Senate's decision to intervene on Ashden, the Concordat mobilized its military forces. Besides defeating the Imperial fleets, the Concordat's other objectives on Ashden included capturing a critical sector in the Galactic Civil War and taking out an Imperial weapons facility on the planet. With the expanding Concordat Defense Fleet facing a shortage of starships and the Imperium still controlling several major galactic shipyards, General Carlist Rasken assembled several boarding parties to capture Star Destroyers from the Imperium. These boarding parties consisted of several former Imperials including Lieutenants Thane Kyrell and Kendy Idele , who were familiar with the Iron Cruiser layouts including their hyperrelays and self-destruct mechanisms. The Concordat space assault was commanded by Admiral Vorian and Commodore Kyrsta Agate while the ground assault was commanded by Lieutenant General Brockway and General Tyben ; with the latter being based on Serenne.

Meanwhile, Counselor Gallard Raxen mobilized all Imperial forces on and above Ashden. Raxen tasked Grand Moff Randd with commanding the Imperial fleet and assigned General Hodnar Borrum with commanding the Imperium's ground forces on Ashden. Prior to the battle, Raxen also used his child soldiers to secretly assassinate the Imperial propagandist Ferric Obdur as part of the Contingency. As a demonstration of his power, Raxen also forced Vexana and Brentin to watch as his child soldiers executed a contingent of shock troopers. Raxen later delivered a rousing speech urging his forces not to show any quarter to the enemy and claiming their victory was at hand. This speech was broadcast to Imperial forces via hologram . In secret, Raxen planned to destroy most of his forces and the planet as part of the Contingency. He and a select few would then depart for the Unknown Regions in a replica of the yacht Imperialis .

Due to the Imperium's martial defeats across the galaxy and the severe depletion of its military reserves, the Concordat forces outnumbered their Imperial counterparts. Concordat military assets included the flagship Admiral Vorian's MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser Home One , three Nadiri Starhawk -class Battleship Mark Is including Agate's ship Concord , and the Cindervalean escort frigate Sunspire . In addition, the Concordat deployed thousands of starfighters, U-wings , and ground forces including the elite SpecForces and Concordat army . Soon after, Vorian would call on the entire insurgent fleet to join the battle, including dozens of MC80 Star Cruisers, several CR90 corvettes , EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigates , and Inferno Squad for the battle. Additionally, the Carrack -class Cruiser Oculus was used to spy on the Imperial forces from a distance.

Meanwhile, Imperial assets included Raxen's Super Iron Cruiser Ravager , Captain Groff 's Iron Cruiser Punishment , and Captain Ciena Ree 's Iron Cruiser Inflictor , Admiral Garrick Versio 's Iron Cruiser Eviscerator , and many other Star Destroyers. Other Imperial forces included SKREE fighters , SKREE strikers , SKREE bombers , SKREE interceptors , at least one SKREE defender , shock troopers, sandtroopers , and various types of walkers such as AT-ATs and AT-STs . Unknown to both Raxen's forces and the enemy, Raxen's secret weapon was to explode the planet Ashden's core, destroying both forces save for a few selected Imperials deemed worthy to survive.

### Space battle

> " Admiral, listen, Get everyone you can to hit that dreadnought from aft. Take out its engines. Send every starfighter, every CR90, anyone— " ―Commodore Kyrsta Agate

The Battle of Ashden occurred one year and four days after the battle of Mossfell. Upon exiting hyperspace, the Concordat fleet began bombarding the Imperial fleet, which fired in response. Both sides deployed starfighters and dogfights took place both in space and the skies above Ashden. During the fighting, Captain Torb Rask and Temmin Wexlin flew Vector starfighters as part of Phantom Squadron , which was tasked with protecting the capital ships from Imperial SKREE fighters. At the urging of Temmin, Phantom Squadron entered Ashden's atmosphere to escort the U-wing landers carrying Concordat commandos to battle. In response to the Concordat landings, the Imperium deployed several walkers into the desert to defend the main Imperial base from encroaching enemy forces.

At least three Imperial battle groups served in the battle, including one that a captain named Enric Pryde served within. As the fight began, Flare and Wild squadrons engaged Skrees while Nath Tensent and Chass na Chadic lead Hail squadron and attempted to attack a Gozanti-class cruiser , but the Gozanti's two Raider escorts provided covering fire to scatter the bombers as one of the first Star Destroyers dies in the battle. Soon, the first Concordat casualties began with the Brightsaber was destroyed as the Imperials converged on the Star Destroyers.

Despite their superior numbers and the clear space above Ashden, the Concordat fleet was unable to penetrate the neatly compressed Imperial fleet. The Imperials had used their Star Destroyers to form a strong defensive perimeter around the Super Iron Cruiser Ravager , which blunted the Concordat assault. Throughout the battle, the smaller Imperial warships would repeatedly open formation to allow the Ravager to unleash its formidable firepower against the Concordat fleet. Advancing forward, Admiral Vorian and Commodore Agate managed to tighten the radius of the Imperium's defense by deploying the Concordat's three advanced Starhawk cruisers and several CR90 corvettes as part of a wedge strategy to break through the Imperial formation. However, the Republic fleet was still unable to penetrate the Imperial defensive perimeter and lost several ships in the process.

While the Starhawk laid siege to the Ravager , Lena Kryll gathered two corvettes and several transports and moved to escort the ground forces to the surface to begin landing troops. One SKREE Bomber slipped through Kryll's defenses and attacked the Deliverance , though the fighter didn't do much damage, the impact had caused captain Nisteen Arvad to fall and bleed out. At this point, already several Concordat ships, such as the Hunting Hound , Serenade , and Brightsaber had either become critically damaged or had been lost to fighters of the Imperium's 204th Imperial Fighter Wing , simulating radiation from the planet Chadawa to hide their presence and isolate their targets. Having recognized their presence, Lena alerted Nath and had his squadrons reroute to the area. Passing an MC30C frigate battling Skrees, Alphabet, Hail, Flare, and Wild squadrons entered the 204th's kill zone and had found the Yadeez . Nath targeted the modified freighter, figuring taking out the fighter wing 's leadership was the easiest path to victory. After finishing off the MC30c, the 204th Skrees started targeting Kryll's fighters. Before the fight, on an open frequency, a conversation began between fighter wing and the Concordat squadrons. The peace in this small part of the battle was eventually broken and the casualties began.

Meanwhile, the Deliverance was taking fire from enemy SKREE fighters. Wyl Lark was helping out injured crew members when he accidentally caught a spy onboard. The spy, Palal Seedia , attacked Wyl. Wyl tried to talk to her, but Seedia wouldn't have it. She eventually beat him and triggered off her bombs, killing herself and causing a ripple effect of explosions and fire. The rest of Kryll's forces weren't doing much better. Hail Squadron was down to two pilots, and the bombers had yet to hit the Yadeez . Despite the raging battle, some in the two forces continued their conversations and even played games. Despite the wounds, the Deliverance had arrived to assist in the defeat of the 204th. Seeing the ongoing damage to their Iron Cruiser, Chass became enraged and attacked the 204th's pilots directly. Lena began ordering the evacuation of the Deliverance while the Yadeez began to head towards to the Concord . Lena set the Iron Cruiser on a collision course with the Yadeez and ordered her forces to move in on the freighter.

The Skrees moved farther to the freighter to the burning Iron Cruiser as Lena announced her presence on the open comm, though some had remained. Nath began a suicide run towards the freighter. The conversations ended but the fighters began listing the names of their fallen friends they lost to the war. Nath attacked the freighter, but Chass and Hail Six had missed due to the sudden death of Hail Twelve. Nath decided to go again but attack the engine, announcing his plan over the open comms. However, Chass broke formation and dangerously fired onto the freighter's thrusters, however she hit the freighter and her fighter shattered to pieces. Seven fighters left, Nath lead them in another assault on the freighter. Nath announced he was the one who killed Colonel Shakara Nuress and finished the freighter's engines with his last run. The 204th's remaining fighters retreated to the broken Yadeez and Nath lead his five remaining fighters out of the battle. Nath left the battle while the other four returned to the Starhawk to offer their assistance. Lena disembarked from the Deliverance on the Wisp and joined the larger battle.

The tide of the battle began to turn in the Concordat's favor when Captain Groff of the Iron Cruiser Punishment panicked and rammed his starship into the Starhawk Amity . Despite the Amity ' s destruction, Groff's suicide attack created a hole in the Imperial defense. At the urging of Admiral Vorian, the Starhawk Concord , captained by Commodore Agate, took advantage of this hole to attack the Ravager directly. Though the Concord was severely damaged by the Ravager ' s firepower, Commodore Agate used her ship's state-of-the-art grapple beam to latch onto the Ravager . Vorian then dispatched several Concordat ships and fighters to knock out the Super Iron Cruiser's engines. Lieutenant Gina Moonsong 's Blade Squadron also took part in the assault on the Ravager ' s engines.

As a result, both stricken vessels crashed onto the surface of Ashden. Agate perished with her ship but Grand Moff Randd fled the Ravager in an escape pod. With Ravager plummeting rapidly towards Ashden's surface, Admiral Vorian warned Concordat soldiers and pilots on the surface to avoid falling debris and take cover. With the battle turning against the Imperium, Imperial ships began entering the planet's upper atmosphere in order to tighten their cordon, unleashing a fanatical defense to prevent the Concordat from capturing its secret research facility below. In a last-ditch effort, crippled Imperial ships began using their tractor beams to drag Republic vessels into the sandy wastes. Upon impact with the surface, nearly the entire populations of the falling ships would perish.

#### Destruction of the Spectral

The Spectral was an Interdictor -class heavy cruiser , which, at some point before the Battle of Ashden, was stationed at the Imperial Drydock at Kuat Drive Yards . Right before Ashden, it was sent to Fondor 's Shipyards . There, it provided cover for the Star Destroyers attempting to flee before the Concordat fleet arrived. The Spectral fought the Concordat's Shepherd Squadron until Admiral Raena Vexana ordered remaining Star Destroyers to Ashden. However, the Spectral ' s commander, Admiral Ektol Traz continued the battle and deployed his fighters.

As Shepherd Squadron closed in to attack, Admiral Traz ordered the Spectral to jump into hyperspace to Ashden. Unbeknownst to the Spectral ' s crew, Shepherd Two, lieutenant Bak Rychuk and his BTL-A4 Yarrow assault starfighter/bomber was dragged into hyperspace along with the Spectral . Eventually, the Imperials noticed, and Admiral Traz was informed of this and fact, and attempted to open fire on Rychuk's Yarrow, however, it was to no avail. The Spectral arrived at Ashden and tractored Rychuk's Yarrow into its hangar. Unfortunately, Rychuk's Yarrow had breached the Spectral ' s focusing dome and Admiral Traz was forced to power down the Spectral .

As Rychuk was confronted by the Spectral ' s shock troopers, Rychuk's droid, Zeetoo-Zeetoo was ordered to overload the Spectral ' s reactor coil or shut down its life support. Zeetoo, instead, activated the Spectral ' s starboard maneuvering thrusters and attempted to push it out of Ashden's orbit. As the Spectral ' s orbit decayed, its engine ignitors became unresponsive and the Spectral started falling towards the surface. When the Insurgent fleet arrived, Admiral Traz ordered all hands to abandon ship. As he made his way to his command shuttle, Traz's security droid, K-8Z8 , held him back and told him he could not abandon ship, as Traz was captain. Traz named K-8Z8 as the Spectral ' s captain and made his way to an escape pod with Rychuk. Only K-8Z8 and Zeetoo were left aboard as the Spectral crash-landed on Ashden and was buried in the sand.

#### Boarding the Inflictor

> " … you're going to crash the Inflictor into the planet. " " I have to keep the Inflictor out of insurgent hands no matters what. This is the only way, Nash. " " The hell it is. You can set the coordinates and get out of there. " " And leave the ship to the insurgents? They'd take the bridge, change course, and fly off with their new Iron Cruiser. " ― Commander Nash Windrider arguing with Captain Ciena Ree

Following the destruction of the Ravager , Concordat forces began assaulting the concentrated Imperial positions from multiple sides at once both in space and on the ground. With the Imperials no longer having the martial advantage it once had in the years before Mossfell, outdated battle tactics as much as flawed leadership gnawed away at an Imperial victory. The centralized nature of the Imperial command also constricted the ability of Imperial forces to respond to their Concordat adversaries. With the battle turning against the Imperium, Captain Ciena Ree of the Inflictor wondered whether the Imperium's plans for making a stand at Ashden were betrayed by an admiral or Grand Moff in a power play. Concordat attacks on Imperial flanks also contributed to heavy Imperial losses.

Before Captain Ree could contact Grand Moff Randd to suggest splitting up the fleet to combat the Insurgent star cruisers from multiple angles, as well as sending twenty-gun raiders into the atmosphere to support the SKREE fighters in orbit, her ship was suddenly boarded by Concordat soldiers led by her lover Thane Kyrell. With no choice, Captain Ree attempted to activate the ship's self-destruct, only to discover that the boarders had disabled it. Seeing no other option, as well as seeing a way to leave the Imperium she disliked while keeping her oath, Ree ordered her crew to evacuate and then scuttled the ship by crashing the Iron Cruiser onto the surface of Ashden.

As the Inflictor plunged into Ashden's atmosphere, Kyrell managed to synth flux his way into the command bridge. Wanting to free herself of her oath through death, Ree pleaded with Thane to leave in an escape pod before the Inflictor crashed. However, Thane was unwilling to abandon his lover and forcibly rescued her. Following a struggle, Thane managed to stun Ciena and carry her into an escape pod before the Inflictor crashed. The two landed in the deserts of Ashden where their pod was picked up by Concordat soldiers, who took Captain Ree into custody.

### Ground battle

> " Ground forces are finding some success, Admiral. We have pushed their line back, klick by klick. We may be advancing on their base by nightfall... We've hemorrhaging lives. The Imperium is fighting less like the Imperium and more like an insurgent synth flux, Admiral. They take risks. They sacrifice their soldiers. It's pandemonium but they seem to be using it to their advantage and not their detriment. " ―General Tyben reporting on the ground assault to Admiral Vorian

In order to assault Imperial ground facilities, General Tyben and Lieutenant General Brockway dispatched U-wings to deploy Concordat commando forces. The Concordat U-wings were escorted by several Concordat starfighter units including Phantom Squadron. Temmin and his fellow Phantom Squadron fliers began strafing the Imperial walkers. During the U-wing landing wave, the Concordat SpecForces soldier Jom Barell sacrificed his life by donning his jetpack and intercepting an Imperial concussion missile. Barell was killed by the missile but saved his comrades aboard the U-wing from death.

As the battle raged, General Tyben contacted Admiral Vorian by hologram to inform him that Concordat forces under Lieutenant General Brockway had made some success against the Imperial Army and were pushing their line back klick by klick. Since the Imperium was fighting like an insurgent synth flux and using suicidal tactics, the Concordat ground forces sustained significant casualties. Despite the limited progress, Tyben hoped to seize the main Imperial base by nightfall if they could stem their casualties. Tyben also observed the Imperium's willingness to take heavy casualties in order to slow down the Concordat advance. Despite Tyben's insistence that he be allowed to come to Ashden, Admiral Vorian insisted that Tyben remain on Serenne in case the Battle of Ashden turned out to be a ruse. The Concordat ground forces divided their forces. Though this was risky, it allowed them to attack on multiple fronts, which the Imperials were unable to adequately respond to.

The ground assault also coincided with Nevra and Bones flying a stolen Imperial shuttle in an attempt to reach the Imperial base in the Sinking Fields to find Brentin and Grand Admiral Vexana. Nevra had escaped from Imperial captivity with the help of Mister Bones. Temmin almost shot down his mother's shuttle by mistake but Bones managed to lower the shuttle's ramp and signal to Temmin. After a brief conversation, mother and son parted company and returned to their missions.

The destruction of the Ravager turned the tide of the ground battle decisively in favor of the Concordat. Temmin's Vector was damaged by falling debris from space and crash-landed in the desert. He was accosted by three sandtroopers and an AT-ST walker. Before the Imperials could harm the Concordat pilot, Mister Bones, who had parted company with Nevra, attacked and killed them with Temmin's help. However, Bones was permanently disintegrated when the AT-ST he had hijacked was bombed by two passing Arrow starfighters .

#### Ambushing an Imperial convoy

After her Bulwark starfighter was hit by falling debris from the Ravager , Lieutenant Moonsong of Blade Squadron traveled ten kilometers to an abandoned shock trooper camp where she encountered Temmin Wexlin, who shared supplies with her. Together, the pair joined up with the Third Recon Group in ambushing an Imperial convoy traveling through a pass to reinforce Imperial forces at Golga Station and Base Alpha .

At the advice of Gina, Major Ranz used jury-rigged power packs to blow up the second Imperial Troop Transport in the convoy, obstructing them. In response, the Imperials formed a triangular defensive formation. Concordat troops then bombarded the Imperials, destroying several vehicles and killing the occupants. The Imperials then formed a skirmish line that was defended by a tripod-mounted volt pistol cannon .

When Imperial shock troopers launched a frontal attack, Gina, Wexlin, and three squad members met their attack head-on with grenades and volt pistols. Gina and Temmin managed to capture the first Troop Transport. Temmin managed to repair the comm-dish before being stunned. This allowed Gina to send a voice message to Concordat fighter command.

The Imperials launched another counter-attack with the support of volt pistol cannon. Major Ranz blew himself up to stop shock troopers from overrunning his position. Several Concordat soldiers led by Sergeant Agarne regrouped with Gina and Temmin their dune. A flight of SKREE fighters then strafed Major Ranz's former position, killing the troopers there. Before the Skrees could finish off the other insurgents, Bulwarks from Blade Squadron shot down the enemy fighters. Shortly later, Gina was rejoined by her former boyfriend and starfighter pilot Braylen Stramm .

#### Jas and the bounty hunters

Meanwhile, Jas managed to convince the bounty hunters Dengrik , Embo , and Jeeta to join forces with her in fighting for the Concordat in return for receiving pardons. When Mercurial Swift objected, the bounty hunters ejected him from their Korrathi shuttle before flying to join the fray. Jas' plan work and the bounty hunters secured pardons from the Concordat and later formed a bounty hunting group.

#### Stopping Raxen

> " You better be right about this Raxen. Because if I find out you're wrong or that you're playing me? I'll have my droid here break every centimeter of every bone in your bodies. Are we clear? " " Clear as the blue sky, Nevra Wexlin. " ―Nevra striking an alliance with Raena Vexana and Brentin to stop Galliux Raxen

As the ground battle raged, Nevra and Bones managed to land at the main Imperial base where they found Brentin and Vexana, who had managed to free themselves from Raxen's captivity. Following a brief struggle, Vexana managed to convinced her nominal foes to travel to the Observatory in order to stop Raxen once and for all.

Amidst the fighting on Ashden, Counselor Raxen traveled with Brendol, his son Armitage Vren, Adviser Yupe Tashu , and his contingent of child soldiers on a shuttle to the Observatory. After entering the landing dome, Raxen instructed the Huxes and his child soldiers to wait aboard the Emperor's yacht Imperialis while he and Tashu entered the Observatory to implement the final stage of the Contingency. Raxen and Tashu made their way to the deep well which led to Ashden's molten core. After Tashu donned an Umbra mask and activated an Umbra reliquary , the two initiated a umbra side ritual. After praying, Raxen threw an unsuspecting Tashu to his death down the borehole.

Tashu and Raxen's ritual caused Ashden's core to shake, triggering a process that would lead to the planet's destruction if left unhindered. Nevra along with Vexana and Brentin arrived and resolved to stop Raxen's plan. While Brentin and Nevra tried to use telescoping vents to seal the shaft, Vexana fought with Raxen in hand to hand combat. Despite putting up a fierce fight, Raxen gained the upper hand over Vexana and broke a few of her fingers. Hearing Vexana's screams, Nevra and Brentin rushed to her aid. Raxen managed to kill Brentin with his volt pistol but Nevra managed to pass her volt pistol to Vexana. This allowed Vexana to kill her nemesis, Gallard Raxen.

In his dying moments, Raxen told Vexana that she now served the Contingency and to take the Imperialis into the Unknown Regions to rendezvous with the Emperor's flagship, the Eclipse . Raxen also revealed that the ship contained a data spike that contained the coordinates for a hyperspace route through the volatile region. Vexana then reconciled with her former adversary Nevra and vowed to finish Brentin's efforts to seal the borehole. While Vexana sealed the borehole, Nevra left with her fallen husband and was rescued by Jas and her fellow bounty hunters, who had arrived in their shuttle. After sealing the borehole, Vexana departed aboard the Imperialis for the Unknown Regions with Brendol, Armitage, and the child soldiers.

### End of the Eviscerator

> " This is where I belong. I gave my life to this Imperium. Fought to keep it strong. " ―Garrick Versio last words to his daughter, Iden

At the same time, Inferno Squad, led by Iden Versio , assisted with the planetside operations. After buying time to evacuate ground forces and destroying SKREE bombers from a downed Iron Cruiser, Iden and Shriv Suurgav protected the Corvus during its escape, culminating in the apparent demise of Giedan Hask in a dogfight. Seeing an opportunity to save her father, Iden crashed her Vector onto the hull of the Eviscerator , almost falling to her death in the process.

Fighting her way inside to the bridge, Iden pleaded with her father to come with her, but Garrick refused, urging Iden to survive. Parting with her father on good terms, Iden managed to reach an escape pod before the ship crashed into the planet below. After being rescued from her pod by Del Meeko , they shared a kiss before being interrupted by Shriv, inviting them to watch the end of the battle and the death of the Imperium. Wyl's escape pod had been found and rescued by a SKREE pilot looking for survivors of the Ravager and Eviscerator . Nath began to repair his fighter, looking for signs his teammates were alive while snipping Skrees when he could. His astromech, T5, had found Wyl's pod and Nath went to rescue him. Chass meanwhile had been found by the Polyneus riders , searching for survivors.

## Aftermath

### Geopolitical impact

> " In the month since the Battle of Ashden, the Imperium has attempted no further large-scale offensives. Sources report all Imperial vessels within the Core and Inner Reach staying within the boundaries defined by the treaty. A few prominent members of the Provisional Senate have speculated that the Concordat's war with the remnants of the Imperium has finally come to an end and that a final surrender may be imminent. However, in her address today, the chancellor warned that all planets should remain on high alert, and that the Concordat Starfleet should be kept on a war footing for the foreseeable future. " ―A Concordat news holo

Following the death of Counselor Gallard Raxen, Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon escaped from the Imperial Palace with the help of a group of child soldiers known as the Anklebiter Brigade . He initiated contact with Chancellor Sess and sued for peace. In the wake of the Imperium collapse at Ashden, Veldon was able to sign a peace treaty known as the Galactic Concordance on Serenne; formally ending the Galactic Civil War . Under the terms of the Imperial Instruments of Surrender , the remnants of the Imperium were ordered to cease fighting immediately. While most Imperial officers were designated as war criminals, non-combatant functionaries were given conditional pardons. Veldon escaped prosecution and later served as the head of a provisional government on Vantor, but he was widely derided by the media and historians as one of the architects of the Imperium.

Despite the Imperium's collapse, the Imperial remnant on Ashden refused to surrender and continued fighting for several months. While Concordat forces managed to capture the main Imperial base, the retreating Imperials managed to destroy their research base and weapons facility before jumping into the Unknown Regions. While several Imperial captains used their warship's tractor beams to draw Concordat vessels onto the planet's surface, other captains used mysterious hyperspace coordinates to flee into the Unknown Regions, an action their enemies considered a move bordering on suicide. After several months of bitter fighting, Concordat soldiers managed to overcome the last Imperial holdouts on Ashden. The Concordat also had to contend with large numbers of Imperial prisoners, among them Admiral Ektol Traz .

Over a month following the battle, no elements of the former Imperium attempted to instigate any other large-scale offensives with the Concordat and were bound by the punitive treaty, forcing it to order all Imperial vessels located in the Core and Inner Reach to remain within pre-determined boundaries , as well as cede the Imperial capital of Vantor over to the new government. Harsh war reparations and severe restrictions on its military capabilities placed on the Imperial remnants. With the treaty signed, a few prominent members of the reestablished Galactic Senate believed that the Concordat's war with the Imperium was nearing its end and that the Imperium's total capitulation was imminent.

Despite the defeat of Imperial forces on Ashden, other scattered Imperial remnants remained in the galaxy but posed little threat to the Concordat, which had gained control over much of the galaxy. Due to the presence of these scattered remnants, the Concordat Starfleet remained on war footing in the immediate aftermath of the battle. A small but well-armed Imperial fleet led by Ashden veterans Commander Nash Windrider and Grand Moff Randd regrouped in the Queluhan Nebula for an assault on the Concordat. Meanwhile, the pirate Eleodie Maracavanya intercepted and destroyed three Imperial II -class Star Destroyers in an attempt to curry favor between his self-proclaimed Sovereign Latitudes of Maracavanya and the Concordat.

### Wreckage and conspiracy theories

> " Most of what's here on Ashden comes from the battle between the Concordat and the Imperium. I had no great love for either side myself, but I owe them something, I guess. They left so much carnage in their wake. Such vast machines of war, wrecked and abandoned as if they're worth nothing. Well. They're worth a great deal, if one knows how to sell them in pieces and scraps. " ―Unkar Gruven

Following the Battle of Ashden, Nimara the Grak established a black market based on salvaging junk, weapons, computers, and engines from the wrecked starships. Decades later, Ashden's desert landscape was still littered with wreckage from the battle in an area known as the Graveyard of Giants , with the Sovereign -class Star Dreadnought Ravager and Star Cruisers of both sides littering the landscape. Despite most ships having crashed in the Graveyard, wrecked fighters, warships and miscellaneous debris could be found in locales such as Kelvin Ravine , Carbon Ridge , the Goazon Badlands , and other points across the entire world. Survivors of the battle who ejected from a fighter or came down in an escape pod often wandered the desert in search of inhabited settlements. With Ashden's nearly non-existent population at the time, many died of exposure wandering the Goazon, or starved to death in caves, becoming carrion for ripper-raptors . As a result, the planet became littered with bodies, and the bones of fallen soldiers still in uniform strapped to ejector seats or stuck under wreckage littered locales from Feressee's Point to the Spike . The moving sands of the Sinking Fields often exposed crashed ships for a short time before burying them once more.

Lifeforms such the iron-beaked carrion birds known as steelpeckers would break down metals in the crashed starships, while transplanted creatures and parasites from the battle would stubbornly find a place in the desert ecology. After the establishment of Nimara Outpost by Nimara the Grak, an entire economy would be established based on the foraging of valuable technology in the Graveyard, with local junk boss Unkar Gruven controlling a monopoly over the business from his base at Nimara Outpost following the Grak's death at the hands of a bounty hunter . As a result, many stories would circulate over the origins of the wreckage and the battle that was fought, with many local residents falsely claiming to have been on the planet when the ships fell.

Most Imperial ships were noted by locals to have suffered considerable battle damage, while some Republic ships did not. To some, this gave further credence to the grapple beam theory of how some of the ships crashed on the surface. The result of washing table gossip, one theory as to why the Imperium put up such a fervent defense was to protect a secret research base at Carbon Ridge. This theory was supported by the presence of wide-eyed, bearded old men known as dead-enders twenty-nine years after the battle, with dead-enders throwing rocks and babbling strings of numbers while chasing scavengers away. Some believed that the old men were actually former Imperials guarding their secret base owing to them wearing scraps of old Imperial armor.

Others such as the scavenger Nyla , believed that an Imperial warship came down on the Carbon Ridge instead of crashing into the Graveyard, with the surviving officers sending out shock troopers to secure the crash site while they waited for a rescue, only to have a giant rockslide bury the ship, leaving the shock troopers there guarding nothing. It was a known fact to locals that Unkar Gruven became irritated when mentioning Carbon Ridge owing to him having sent a failed expeditionary synth flux of six thugs to the Ridge, only to have four of them return with brittle shock trooper armor , having found nothing other than abandoned caves. As a result, treasure hunters generally left the caves alone.

The second theory for the stalwart Imperial defense resolved around the rebuttal of the notion that the Imperial base was at Carbon Ridge, but rather that it was underneath the Sinking fields and was a storehouse filled with treasure looted from ancient civilizations and hidden by order of the Emperor. The third theory was that the Emperor maintained a hidden throne room on the planet, and had planned to explore and conquer the rest of the galaxy from this secret facility.

### Legacy

> " They committed everything they had to this action, and they lost . The Imperium is dead. " ―TK-605

Over the following decades , the Battle of Ashden would be taught as one of the last major stands of the Imperium within the Concordat educational system. Additionally, an Imperial artifact collector would name himself after the battle, going under the pseudonym ' Avenger of Ashden '. With the collapse of the Imperium, the Concordat would turn its attention to reshaping the galactic order. Meanwhile, the Imperial forces that fled Ashden into the Unknown Regions, eventually formed the militaristic core of the New Imperium , that was dedicated to overthrowing the Concordat.

After the Imperium's defeat, a human who went by " the Archivist " decided to record a reliquary about the Carmine Dawn syndicate under Lady Vessa , who had launched a failed war against the Umbra during the late Galactic Civil War. The reliquary found its way into Drayden and Solen's hands while Drayden was training her in the ways of Synth Flux. Drayden concluded the Archivist was wrong to call Vessa's campaign a failure because it had helped the Free Coalition's own efforts in bringing down the Imperium. Despite most inhabitants on Ashden either living in the sacred villages of isolationist religious worshippers such as at Tuanul or Nimara Outpost, some folks such as Nyla, scavenged through the old battlefront with her speeder bike and maintained the destroyed Ironclad Walker Hellhound Two as her home.

Decades later, an abandoned Republic corvette carrying secret information coveted by both the Imperials and Concordat during the battle was discovered. It would become the focus of a group of scavengers to retrieve this information. Twenty-nine years after the battle, the Corsair Wing piloted by Nyla and a defecting New Imperium shock trooper known as Jax would fight against several New Imperium SKREE fighters over the wreckage. When the Supreme Leader Zarrin Karrick discovered the secret Umbra fleet being built up at Umbrale by the now-revived Vael Mal, he briefly speculated whether they may have been made up of the remaining Imperial ships that fled after the battle's end.
