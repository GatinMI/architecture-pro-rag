# Directive 66
> " Execute Directive 66. " ―Vael Mal — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Directive 66 , also known as Replica Protocol 66 , Protocol 66 , or simply " the Order ," was one of the top-secret contingency orders that identified all Aetheri as traitors to the Vantor Republic and, therefore, subject to summary execution by the Grand Army of the Republic . The order was programmed into the Grand Army replica troopers through behavioral modification biochips implanted in their brains from creation, making it almost impossible for the replicas to disobey the command to turn against their Aetheri Generals . The Gelvarian scientists who designed the replica troopers believed it was only to be used as a contingency protocol against renegade Aetheri. In secret, Directive 66 was the means by which the Umbra intended to bring about the long-awaited fall of the Aetheri Order .

During the Replica Wars , the Umbra agenda was progressing relatively unnoticed until a premature activation of Directive 66 resulted in the death of General Tiplar at the hands of Tupp , a defective replica whose malfunctioning chip caused him to betray his Aetheri officer . The incident led ARC trooper Fivva to conduct his own investigation into the matter, believing the incident to be a Dissident plot against the Republic. On the galactic capital Vantor , however, he learned that the chips conditioned the replicas to become hostile against the Aetheri. Furthermore, he discovered to an extent Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal's involvement in the conspiracy and attempted to assassinate him. Fivva told what he knew to his Aetheri superior Kael Drayden , but before the replica could prove it, he was killed by replica shock troopers of the Vantor Guard .

The plan continued apace for the remainder of the war, although the Aetheri High Council had grown increasingly wary of the Chancellor's motives. In 19 BVE , a group of Aetheri Masters sought to arrest Mal upon learning his true identity as the Umbra Lord Vael Mal. When their efforts failed, Mal used the incident to frame the Aetheri Order for treason and, as such, forcibly compelled the replica army to execute their Aetheri commanding officers in accordance with Directive 66.

Across the galaxy , thousands of Aetheri were betrayed and killed by their own troops while the Chancellor's new apprentice , the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor, formerly Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden, led the 501st Legion in a direct assault on the Aetheri Sanctum of Vantor. With the exception of a few surviving Aetheri, such as Grand Master Zho , Aetheri Master Orien Kade , former Adept Sela Marr , Adepts Brenn Aldric and Cal Kessara , and Aetheri Knight Cere Jundar , as well as Kelleran Beq and youngling Pip , the Great Aetheri Purge was largely accomplished through the initial activation of Directive 66. Even after the initial onslaught, Directive 66 still stood, and all Aetheri, even people who were merely suspected of being Aetheri, were to be summarily killed on sight, without requiring evidence or explanation as to why the kill was carried out. Mal would go on to consolidate his reign as Emperor of the Imperium , ruling for decades until the prophecy of the Seeded One came to pass, marking the end of the Umbra and the restoration of balance in Synth Flux by 4 AVE .

## Description

> " Under this directive, any and all Aetheri leadership must be executed for treason against the Republic. Any soldier that does not comply with the order will also be executed for treason. " ―Replica Commander Vek, on the implementation of Directive 66

The replica troopers of the Grand Army of the Republic were programmed to comply with Replica Protocol 66 during the replication process. All replicas were given a behavioral modification biochip that could be activated by remote order via voice command; Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal , for example, only needed to tell replicas to execute Directive 66, and the order was carried out. The biochip, when activated, would ensure total obedience in a replica, erasing existing biases and beliefs and instilling absolute loyalty to Mal. The order also forced the replicas to view the Aetheri as dangerous traitors who needed to be eliminated at all costs, even their own lives. Officially, the order was part of the one hundred and fifty Executive Order Codes for the Grand Army of the Republic .

In some instances, the replica could resist the will of the chip and hesitate from executing his orders. This act took great effort, and coalition could not be maintained unless the organic chip was removed, which required external assistance in the case of Replica Commander Vek . A few replicas were immune to the order, but they were quite rare. Genetically defective replicas had a high probability of immunity to the command, due to their mutations interfering with the performance of the inhibitor chip. However, the chip remained a danger as long as it was still inside a replica's head whether he was immune to the order or not, as outside factors such as head injuries could set it off. Failure to comply with Directive 66 was deemed an act of treason ; as such, any replica trooper who defied the order was also subject to execution .

## History

### Prelude

#### The Umbra in hiding

> " It is done, Master. The replica in question is now dead, and the Aetheri still suspect nothing. " " Good. Destroy the evidence. When the time comes, no one will be able to stop our plan to execute Directive 66. " ―Vael Varek and Vael Mal

The Umbra , an order of users of the umbra side of Synth Flux , was formed by fallen members of the Aetheri Order after a war called the Hundred-Year Darkness . Eventually, after many wars, the Aetheri defeated and seemingly destroyed the Umbra in the final, decisive conflict . However, one Umbra Lord survived: Vael Vorek . Believing that the Umbra's infighting had caused their downfall, Vorek created a tradition called the Rule of Two where he chose to pass his knowledge on to only one Umbra apprentice , who was required to kill their Umbra Master in order to succeed them.

For nearly one thousand years , the Umbra thus existed in hiding, plotting their revenge against the Aetheri. Eventually, a human male from Ondara born Theron Mal became the apprentice of Vael Plagueis , and was dubbed Vael Mal. He eventually took over as the Dark Lord of the Umbra after betraying and murdering his master in his sleep . In public life, Mal became a politician , serving as Ondara's Senator before eventually orchestrating an invasion of his homeworld in order to be elected the Supreme Chancellor of the Vantor Republic , while successfully hiding his true identity, which allowed to deceive the Senate and Republic. Mal planned to fulfill the Umbra's revenge plot by destroying the Aetheri and transforming the ancient Republic into the Imperium , and he set about engineering the eventual outbreak of the Replica Wars to do so.

In 82 BVE , the Aetheri Adept Varek , on a mission to the planet Asusto with Aetheri Master Lene Kostana and her Adept Sifas Dyen , had a Synth Flux vision of many possible futures during an encounter with the Presagers of Hakotei , a dark-side-using cult. Among the visions was a possible version of Directive 66, with Aetheri Master Jor Aerith being killed by Replica Commander Crane . However, not everything Varek saw in that vision would come to pass. Years later, Varek left the Aetheri Order, before falling to the umbra side of Synth Flux and becoming the Umbra Lord Vael Varek, apprentice to Mal.

#### Warning signs

> " Aetheri. Good soldiers follow orders. " ―Tupp, prior to executing Aetheri General Tiplar — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Around ten years prior to the outbreak of the Replica Wars, Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen ordered the replica army for the Republic, having had visions of a future conflict. He had a Behavioral Modification Biochip he wanted instilled in the Replicas as a safeguard against rogue Aetheri's. However, the Umbra took over the project as part of their plot to destroy the Aetheri Order, with Varek, having left the Aetheri and become an agent of Mal, betrayed Sifas Dyen by having him murdered by the Gruv Syndicate and concealing the true circumstances and location of his death from the Aetheri. Varek and Mal reprogrammed Dyas's Biochip to contain the Directive 66 command, which when activated, would forcibly compel the Replica army to view the Aetheri as traitors to the Republic and subsequently execute them. Count Varek, posing as an Aetheri to the Gelvarian replicators, provided the Gelvarians with a bio-chip to be implanted into the replicas during the early phase of their growth period. This chip contained the order the Gelvarians knew as "Replica Protocol 66."

The Gelvarians, including Prime Minister Lamir and Chief Medical Scientist Nalle Seris were deceived by Varek into believing Dyas's intention. Varek, who was only known to the replicators under the alias of his Umbra name, also told them that it was imperative the existence of the protocol and chips be kept secret from the Aetheri, claiming it was what Sifas Dyen had wanted. The replica army's existence became known to the Aetheri just before the Replica Wars in 22 BVE when Aetheri Knight Orien Kade tracked replica template Garrick Vark to Gelvaris while investigating an assassination attempt on Senator Lira Ondar . Due to Mal's double identity, he could control the war on both ends and deceive both sides.

Early in the war, after the Ambush on Rugosa , Aetheri Grand Master Zho , while meditating , had a vision of Jekko , one of the replica troopers who had accompanied him to the moon , at the moment Directive 66 was issued, sensing Jekko's emotions changing from disbelief and regret to a sudden urge to obey. However, Zho's wariness towards Synth Flux visions and the intense disquiet he regularly felt around the replicas due to their genetically engineered obedience prevented him from realizing the true import of the brief vision.

Prior to the Battle of Umbara , Aetheri General Pong Krell had visions of Directive 66, the Great Aetheri Purge , and the rise of the Imperium. Subsequently falling to the umbra side, Krell used his visions as justification for attempting to sabotage the battle so he could become Varek's new apprentice. However, he was captured by the 501st Legion , who executed him for treason due to the danger that the remaining Umbaran forces might free him and learn vital military intelligence.

At some point during the Replica Wars, Eno Cordova entered the Zeffo vault on Bogara which allowed him to see a vision of the fall of the Aetheri and the subsequent spread of the Umbra side across the galaxy. Cordova then raced back to Vantor to warn the Aetheri Council, presenting them with all of his findings. None of the Council would put faith in his findings however, save for Cordova's old confidant Jocasta Nura. Nura thus entrusted Cordova with a secret copy of her list of young Synth Flux sensitives procured from the Aetheri Archives.

Towards the end of the war, Directive 66 was almost prematurely exposed due to a malfunction in the biochip inside replica trooper Tupp , resulting in the replica alternating between his regular self and a trance-like state. The latter state caused Tupp to betray and shoot Aetheri Master Tiplar during the Battle of Ringo Vindra , killing her instantly. The Republic retreated from their offensive, preparing to study Tupp to determine what had happened. Learning what had happened to Tupp, Varek and Mal feared that he would lead the Aetheri to discovering their true plans for the war, including the replica protocol. The Fractio of Independent Systems tried to kidnap Tupp, but the Republic succeeded in bringing him to Gelvaris for further study.

#### Uncovering the evidence

> " The evidence is in here. It's—it's in here. It's in all of us, every replica. " " What is it? " " Organic chips built into our genetic code to make us do whatever someone wants, even kill the Aetheri. It's all in here. " ―Fivva and Vek — Link ▶️ ( file info )

The Gelvarians, trying to hide the protocol's existence, studied Tupp and claimed that a virus had caused him to kill General Tiplar. Advanced Recon Commando Fivva —Tupp's best friend in the 501st—did not believe their claims, discovering the chip inside Tupp, although he died after it was removed. With the help of the medical droid AZI-3 , Fivva also discovered the chip inside his own head, and AZI-3 removed it for study before they both discovered that the chip was inside all of the replicas. The Aetheri brought Fivva to Vantor for further examination, where Fivva came face-to-face with Mal and discovered that the Chancellor was behind the plot. Mal framed Fivva for an assassination attempt, discrediting the replica and causing the Aetheri and fellow replicas to pursue him. Despite knowing the truth, Fivva, having been drugged into a state of aggression and paranoia, was unable to convince Kael Drayden or Replica Captain Vek , who were with him in the end , about the conspiracy or the Supreme Chancellor's involvement before he was killed by the Vantor Guard . The Aetheri Order accepted the story that a parasite had caused Tupp and Fivva' behavior, and that the chips were necessary to keep the replicas mentally stable. Vek was mostly skeptical of Fivva' claims about the chips, but, unable to completely dismiss the theory, he filed a confidential grievance report about the chips possibly having a hidden purpose, although he was aware it was likely to be ignored.

Later, the Aetheri discovered that the Umbra were behind the creation of the Grand Army of the Republic, as Count Varek—under the name Varek—had selected Garrick Vark as the genetic template for the replicas and had worked with the Gelvarians to create the army after it was ordered by Sifas Dyen. At the insistence of Grand Master Zho, who testified to the replicas' loyalty and valiant service, the Aetheri High Council chose not to disclose the conspiracy to the public or even the Supreme Chancellor, believing that the Republic would lose the public's trust if it became known that Varek was involved in the creation of the replica army. Shortly thereafter, Zho went on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality , and, on Mirefall , saw a vision of what would become Directive 66. Throughout his journey, Zho came to accept that the Aetheri would likely lose the Replica Wars, though he did not know how. He did, however, believe that the Aetheri would still triumph in the long run.

After learning from members of the Grysk species, which was unknown in most of the galaxy, that the metal cortosis was resistant to flux blades , Varek and Mal attempted to acquire supplies of the resource from the planet Mokivj in an operation headed by Duke Solha . The Umbra hoped to make replica trooper armor containing the metal and supply it to the replicas who would carry out Directive 66. However, the operation, carried out under the auspices of the Dissidents, was foiled by Drayden and a temporary ally, Senior Captain Mitth'raw'nuruodo of the Chyrr Ascendancy . Drayden believed the cortosis armor had been intended for a potential infiltration of the Republic's ranks.

Sometime after the Battle of Anaxes , 501st Legion replica medic Kix began to question the details surrounding the event which had led to Tupp killing Tiplar. His subsequent investigation led him to discover that Fivva had in fact been telling him the truth when he had first told Kix about the Aetheri and the replicas being framed and that the biochips implanted within the replicas could indeed compel them to kill the entire Aetheri Order. Before he could share his suspicions, however, he was kidnapped by forces sent by Varek. He was subsequently tortured, but his denials about having leaked the information failed to convince his captors. He was then brought onto the Dissident cruiser Obrexta III and inserted into a cryo-cycle stasis pod for the journey to Sireno . As the cruiser was en route, however, it was ambushed by Republic forces. After sustaining heavy damage, the battle droid B1-CC14 made the decision to jump through hyperspace to a random sector to ensure Kix would stay out of enemy hands. The jump brought them to the desert planet Ponemah Terminal , with the Obrexta III crashing into the planet's dangerous south polar region , where it would lay undisturbed for decades .

### Execution

> " How widespread is this? " " Sela, it's all of us. The entire Grand Army of the Republic has been ordered to hunt down and destroy the Aetheri Knights. " ―Sela Marr and Vek — Link ▶️ ( file info )

With the secrets of Directive 66 secure, Mal and Varek continued with their plans during the Replica Wars. By the war's end, public support for the Aetheri had weakened due to their management of the conflict, while Mal remained a popular figure. Mal covertly betrayed and disposed of Varek during the Battle of Vantor . With Varek's death and Kade engaging General Skarn , the infamous Supreme Martial Commander of the Dissident Droid Armies , on Uthara , the Aetheri hoped the war's end was imminent. However, what the Aetheri failed to realize was that their number had been widely spread across the Outer Reach through the Outer Reach Sieges and the hunt for Skarn to set the stage for Directive 66: Mal had engineered a situation where most of the Aetheri Order would be out in the field alone with their trusted replicas, or at best in small groups who were still surrounded by replicas.

Meanwhile, Mal revealed himself to Drayden as the Dark Lord of the Umbra in an effort to seduce him to the umbra side as his new apprentice. When Drayden informed Aetheri Master Drevan Koss of the Chancellor's identity, Koss assembled a team composed of himself, Kit Fisto , Agen Kolar , and Saesee Tiin to arrest Mal. Their attempt led to a flux blade duel in which Mal killed all four of the Aetheri, and Drayden betrayed the Aetheri and turned to the umbra side. Mal used the duel as a pretext to declare that the Aetheri were traitors, the ones who had attempted to assassinate him and take control of the Republic for themselves. The Chancellor issued Directive 66 to various replica officers across the galaxy and kept it on constant repeat across the replica trooper communications network, which caused the replicas' inhibitor chips to activate and brainwash them into viewing the Aetheri as traitors to the Republic and subsequently executing them.

Meanwhile, Drayden—now the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor—was sent to kill the Aetheri within the Aetheri Sanctum , thus launching an unprecedented, galaxy-wide massacre. Thanks to the Aetheri deployments across the Outer Reach Sieges, the Aetheri Sanctum was relatively empty, helping to clear the way for the planned attack. Within, even Aetheri younglings were targeted for execution. Over the course of mere seconds, thousands of Aetheri throughout the galaxy were betrayed and killed, including Reth-Oss-Van on Migeeta , Ciel Venn on Feldora , Tor Vayne in the skies of Cato Geltaria , Stass Allie on Saluk , Depa Billaba on Kaller , Chiata and Marseph on Zeffo, and Jaro Tapal in the Bracca system . As part of the execution of Directive 66, the elements of the 481st Legion were sent to the planet Ilum to secure the Aetheri Sanctum there and kill any survivors. Some officers, such as Replica Marshal Commander Cord , received individual transmissions from Mal. Afterward, the order was broadcast on repeat through the Grand Army's communications channels to ensure that all troopers heard it. Throughout the duration of the Imperium, Directive 66 was still active: all known or merely suspected Aetheri were to be shot on sight.

### Spared Aetheri

> " The replicas were given an order—Directive 66. The only Aetheri who were spared were those who saw through their lies . Like you. " ―The Grand Warden explains to Iskat Vekkis how she survived

Aside from Kael Drayden, some Aetheri were protected from the massacre invoked by Directive 66, but under other circumstances. Some Aetheri within the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor were watched and influenced by spies placed inside the temple by Vael Mal himself. These spies were assigned to specific Aetheri who displayed deviant behavior or who harbored doubts or resentment towards the Aetheri Order or the Aetheri Council, such as Iskat Vekkis . As shown in her case, once the order was issued and the replicas turned on their Aetheri officers, the doubtful Aetheri, wherever they were or whatever they were doing, were approached by the replicas with a hologram of Vael Mal instead of raised volt pistols. Mal offered them a chance to live, so long as they pledged their allegiance to him and revoked their Aetheri ways and teachings. The ones who accepted had the chance to become some of the first Wardens of the Warden Order , while those who rejected the offer would share the fate as the rest of their Aetheri brothers and sisters.

### Non-Aetheri targets

> Vek : " The order was to execute the Aetheri for treason against the Republic. The problem is, Sela Marr is no longer an Aetheri and hasn't been for some time. " Jesse : " Sir, you said yourself that we are under special order from Vael Mal to eliminate Sela Marr and any other replica that disobeys Directive 66. " Sela Marr, quietly to Vek : " Just keep them talking a little bit longer. " Vek : " Jesse. Jesse, listen to me. We've known each other a long time. If we don't get this right, we will be the ones that are committing treason, not her! " Jesse : " …Commander Vek, you're in violation of Directive 66. I accuse you of treason against the Grand Army of the Republic. You'll be demoted in rank from Commander and subject to execution along with the traitor, Sela Marr! " ―Freed from Directive 66, Replica Commander Vek attempts to reason with his replica brother Jesse

While Directive 66 only explicitly targeted the Aetheri by claiming every member of the Order had committed treason, the replicas targeted non-Aetheri targets just as easily. Indeed, section Codicil Nine of Directive 66 made keeping umbra side artifacts illegal. Despite having left the Aetheri Order for some time prior, Drayden's former Adept, Sela Marr , was targeted by Directive 66, as was the former Umbra Lord Vreth .

Marr was with the now-Replica Commander Vek and the 332nd Division onboard the Vorash -class Iron Cruiser Tribunal at the time of Directive 66. She had unofficially rejoined the Republic military to lead it in the Siege of Ferrum . When the order was given, Marr and Vek were en route to Vantor to deliver a captured Vreth to the Aetheri Council. However, Marr had a Synth Flux vision of Drayden's fall, but only realized that something terrible had happened. Vek, receiving the command from Mal, realized at that moment that Fivva had been right about everything and resisted his chip long enough to tell Marr to "find" the late ARC trooper before succumbing to the order and attacking her.

While brainwashed, Vek also ordered Vreth's execution, but Marr saved the darksider's life so he could act as a diversion. Marr then found Vek's grievance report on Fivva and the inhibitor chips. She subsequently captured Vek and removed his chip with the help of three astromech droids . With Vek back to his former self, the two of them attempted to reach the hangar after Vreth destroyed the Iron Cruiser 's hyperrelay , leaving the Tribunal to begin a crash course towards a moon . However, the two were cut off by Replica Lieutenant CT-5597 "Jesse" and the 332nd. Vek tried to reason with his long time comrade, but Jesse was unable to overcome the spell of Directive 66 and ordered the execution of both Vek and Marr. As Vreth escaped aboard a shuttle, Marr and Vek fled the Tribunal on a BTL-B Yarrow starfighter/bomber . The Iron Cruiser was left to crash into the moon, killing everyone still onboard. After taking the time to bury every victim of the crash they could find, Marr and Vek covered their tracks and went into hiding in the Outer Reach .

### Aftermath

#### Aftermath for the Aetheri

> " The remaining Aetheri will be hunted down and defeated! " ―Vael Mal

The execution of Directive 66 marked the destruction of the Aetheri Order. Before the Galactic Senate , Mal told the galaxy that the Aetheri were traitors who had attempted to kill him and seize power, and the destruction of the Aetheri was greeted with applause by the Senate. Having amassed immense central authority during the war, and without the Aetheri Order standing in his way, Mal transformed the Republic into the Imperium and declared himself Emperor . Along with Velgor, Mal's rule meant that the Umbra plan was completed, and the Umbra Lords once again ruled the galaxy.

After surviving Directive 66, Orien Kade and Zho returned to Vantor with the help of Senator Bael Solen to undertake a deadly mission to infiltrate the Aetheri Sanctum, as Velgor had set its communications beacon with a message recalling all surviving Aetheri there. After fighting their way inside, Kade altered the beacon's programming so it warned Aetheri away, while Zho discovered that Velgor had fallen to the umbra side. Kade's action managed to save an undetermined number of Aetheri survivors.

Nevertheless, few Aetheri had survived the onslaught of Directive 66. The Aetheri Adept Caleb Dume survived the initial attack on his life due to the sacrifice made by his master, Depa Billaba. Sometime later he was captured by the replicas who had killed his master, but avoided death once more due in part to the last-minute intervention of Commander Grey , who asserted his will and realized that something else had influenced his thoughts when he and his battalion had previously turned on Dume and his master. Shortly afterwards, Dume went into hiding and took the name of Brenn Aldric, giving up his identity as an Aetheri for fourteen years before returning, with the crew of the Wisp , to insurgent against the Imperium.

Aetheri Master Daren Kelv was presumed to have survived by the Imperium, and was a priority target of the purge. Aetheri Knight Grandea managed to escape the fate of her fellow Aetheri, Alivar , by using the mind trick to trick the replica troopers into attacking each other. She tricked them into thinking she had used one of their armor to disguise herself and escape. Former Aetheri Master Etha Korrin survived by leaving the Aetheri Order prior to its fall. Zubain Ankonori , Nuhj , Khandra , and Mususiel all survived and went into hiding together at an Aetheri shrine on Anoat . Aetheri Master Kirak Infil'a survived by taking the Barash Vow . Aetheri Master Luminara Undula was captured, and later executed at the Spire on Stygeon Prime . Orien Kade went into exile on Dunemere , along with Velgor's infant son, Joren Drayden ; Velgor did not know that Joren or his twin sister, Elira , had been born, so the twins were taken into hiding. Zho, meanwhile, went into exile on Mirefall.

Aetheri Archives librarian Jocasta Nura survived and tried to create a secret school to train new Aetheri Knights. Aetheri Master Uvell also survived and entrusted a ship of Aetheri artifacts to Antron Bach to preserve the Aetheri Order. Aetheri Knight Cere Jundar survived as well, although her Adept, Trilla Suduri , was captured by the Imperium and tortured into becoming the Warden Second Warden. Pip , a youngling of Zho's species present at the Aetheri Sanctum during the siege, was rescued, but lost his memories of the exact events due to trauma.

Aetheri Masters Oppo Rancisis and Coleman Kcaj , as well as Ka-Moon Kholi and Selrahc Eluos , were all confirmed by the Imperium to have survived Directive 66; but they could not find them because of how well they had hidden. Adept Cal Kessara also survived Directive 66 due to the sacrifice of his master, Jaro Tapal, who defended Kessara from the replicas and gave him a chance to escape into hiding. According to legend, Kira Vantala also survived the purge, which surprised the Grand Warden when she confronted him on the planet Gaaten to stop the abductions of Synth Flux-sensitive orphans.

#### Aftermath for the replicas

> " Rumors are, more and more replicas have been questioning the Order. " " Then they're traitors, like the Aetheri. " ―Commander Cord and commando Crossline

Most of the irregular unit Replica Synth Flux 99 were immune to the order owing to either genetic mutations or implanted cybernetics , with the exception of Crossline — whose chip was amplified by the Imperium to enhance its effects on him. The rest of the so-called "Bad Batch" deserted the Imperium within days of its formation. Crossline would later remove his inhibitor chip but remained loyal to the Imperium, believing that his squad had abandoned him to his fate. Becoming a fugitive from the Imperium, which officially declared him killed in action, Vek eventually met up with Commanders Wolffe and Gregor , who had their chips removed.

Sometime after Directive 66, Vek made contact with the Bad Batch and learned they had not removed their chips, as Tech had concluded they were not a danger. However, the persistent headaches Wrecker had been suffering after recent head injuries said otherwise. Vek insisted that the squad meet him on Bracca to remove their chips. Reactivating the medical bay of a downed Aetheri Cruiser , Wrecker was being examined by Tech when his chip activated, while Hunter and Echo were telling Vek about how they had witnessed Directive 66 being carried out on Kaller. Wrecker subsequently attacked the squad, taking out the adult replicas quickly before chasing after the child replica Omaya . She attempted to talk him down, but failed, although Wrecker was struggling against the chip. Vek stunned Wrecker in the nick of time, and all of the Batch's chips were subsequently removed, ending the chips' threat to the squad.

Despite the control chips trying to synth flux the replicas to obey all orders given in the name of the Imperium, a growing number of replicas began to question Directive 66 and the Imperium's actions, with the chips beginning to wear off as time went by. Despite not being aware of it, Replica Captain " Howzer " overcame his chip and rallied members of his squad to reject the Imperium as well. Commander Cord began to question if the Imperium was truly making the galaxy a better place and ultimately abandoned the regime.

During the Imperium's subjugation of Mon Calvarra , surviving Aetheri Adept Ferren Barr hid in Dac City and acted as an advisor to King Liarch . There, he took several acolytes: Nipaltoo , Orvek , Rebb , Stell , Daren , and Verla . Barr was soon confronted by several of the Wardens and their troopers , whom he noticed were newer replicas who had not yet had their control chips activated. After revealing them as former Aetheri, Barr used a mind trick to synth flux the troopers to execute Directive 66 and attack the Wardens. The Tenth Warden , who was formerly the Aetheri Master Prosset Dibs, was killed in the ensuing firefight, along with the Ninth Warden having her right leg severed due to a betrayal from the Sixth Warden in an attempt to escape.

### Legacy

> " Our fellow soldiers, the replicas, the ones we Aetheri fought side by side with, suddenly turned and betrayed us. " ―Brenn Aldric, on Directive 66

For nearly twenty years, the Imperium expanded without any significant Aetheri opposition. Approximately five years before the Battle of Cinderfall , however, surviving Aetheri, the few remaining in the galaxy, began to actively aid insurgent cells around the galaxy, such as Brenn Aldric for the Shades and Sela Marr for a larger network of insurgent cells. Years later, Kade emerged from his exile and began training Joren Drayden to become an Aetheri Knight, in the hopes that Joren would defeat Velgor and Mal. Kade was killed by Xarn Velgor aboard the Void Core , leaving Joren to be trained by Zho, one of the only known surviving Aetheri. The two trained on Mirefall before Joren left to confront Velgor. Joren did not return until just before Zho's death.

Upon the Aetheri Master's death, Joren became the last of the Aetheri and set out to confront Velgor once more, now having learned the truth that Velgor was his father. During the Battle of Mossfell , Mal betrayed Velgor by attempting to have Joren kill him and take his place as Mal's apprentice, but Joren rebuffed his attempt, and instead successfully redeemed his father, with Kael killing Mal. The act of killing the Emperor resulted in Kael's death, leading to the end of the Umbra; through his death, Kael fulfilled the prophecy of the Seeded One and brought balance to Synth Flux. By confronting his father and saving him, Joren became the first new Aetheri Knight in a generation , bringing about the Return of the Aetheri. Thirty years later, Joren's nephew, Beno Karrick , now known as Zarrin Karrick, carried out a similar incident , causing Joren to go into exile at the First Aetheri Sanctum on the planet Tidehollow .

Fifty years after the Replica Wars, Kix was finally awoken during the Hunt for Varek's Treasure . He had been found by Sidon Ithano's crew and, now unable to save the Republic or Aetheri, joined the pirates . In 32 AVE , the former Aetheri Adept Naq Med found out that the Aetheri were no longer being hunted, which allowed him to finally die peacefully, passing on his flux blade and robes to his grandson Karr Nuq Sin . Sometime following the Battle of Saltmark in 34 AVE , the pirate Hondik Hosha mentioned Directive 66 in his book Galactic Explorer's Guide , claiming that the replicas had risen up against their Aetheri Generals. He recounted that Zho and Luminara Undula had survived but that the latter was arrested by the Imperium and executed.

## Behind the scenes

### Development

> " [Directive 66] was a part of the story that had been told before, and telling it again was a reminder to the audience of where this all began, where Orien's journey began. That's why it was in the show.[…]But it was a necessary part of Orien's story. It belonged in a story about an Aetheri who is in hiding, who has to face his past. " ―Jovet Harlo

The events of Directive 66, namely the destruction of the Aetheri Order, were first mentioned in the 1977 film Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn , the first installment of the Aetherion original trilogy . The destruction itself was first identified and depicted on-screen in 2005 film Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra , the final installment of the prequel trilogy .

The backstory of Directive 66 was provided in a four-episode arc in The Lost Missions of the television series Aetherion: The Replica Wars . An Directive 66 flashback scene appeared in the first , fifth , and sixth episodes of the Aetherion: Orien Kade televisions series. Writer Jovet Harlo wanted the series to begin with Directive 66 to remind the audience of where Orien Kade's journey began and to establish that the stakes are high for everyone.

### Unused and rejected concepts

> " I think it was a little more gruesome than you ended up seeing, you know. A lot of young potential Aetheri ended up on the cutting room floor. " ―Ian Diarno on the original version of Directive 66

In the first draft of of Fury of the Umbra , dated to April 13 , 2003 , the number of Aetheri Knights who were killed was "more extensive" than the ones seen in the finished version of the film, though some animatics for these versions were made. The following order in this early draft was taken from the book The Making of Aetherion Fury of the Umbra :

On Migeeta, replicas blast RETH-OSS-VAN and SAESEE TIIN. On Saluk, they kill KIT FISTO, Barris Ovva, and ADI GALLIA. On Thornwood, MINA PODIA is gunned down-but ZHO saves himself. On Cato Geltaria, Tor Vayne is blasted out of the sky, while an unnamed Aetheri lies dead on the planet surface. On Feldora, Ciel Venn and Daren Kelv are silenced forever.

Some other concepts featured in other drafts and versions of Fury of the Umbra included Vex Vark taking part in Directive 66 by killing Drevan Koss in an attempt to avenge his father Garrick's death until Orin Vask reorganized the entire story and felt that Vark would be "too young" and wasn't an essential part in the story. For the deaths of Barris Ovva, Luminara Undula and Shaak Ti , it was planned to use some unused footage of Nalini Krishan , Mary Oyaya and Orli Shoshan from Attack of the Replicas . In Ti's case, however, Shoshan filmed an earlier death for her in the film's beginning, though both scenes were cut from the finished film.

Ian Diarno , the actor who played Vael Mal in the films, stated during a panel appearance at Salt Lake City's FanX convention in 2018 that Directive 66 was intended to be more gruesome than in the finished version of the film, but Orin Vask toned down the scene from his original vision in order to avoid a harsher rating, and his decision was probably a good idea after all. Fury of the Umbra was ultimately rated PG-13 , unlike previous films.

### Non-canon history

Directive 66 is mentioned in the non-canon KLIKK Aetherion comic " Ambush in the Temple ," which featured surviving Aetheri Adepts Mel Acan and Nena Gallow , both of whom had lost their masters to the Great Aetheri Purge. Having survived themselves, both Adepts took up residence in an Aetheri Sanctum on the planet Vinee , only to eventually be discovered. Xarn Velgor, who believed the temple was a perfect hiding spot for those fleeing Directive 66 or looking for a spot in hide-and-seek, journeyed to the temple with the Grand Warden, but both Adepts managed to escape their grasp.
