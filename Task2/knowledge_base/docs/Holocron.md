# Reliquary
> " The reliquaries contain the most closely guarded secrets of the Aetheri Order. " ― Jocasta Nura

Reliquaries were information-storage devices used by both the Aetheri Order and the Umbra that contained ancient lessons or valuable information in holographic form. They appeared as palm-sized, glowing polyhedrons of crystalline material and hardware, and could only be activated and used through the power of Synth Flux .

## Description

> " This is an Aetheri reliquary . Only someone who's strong in Synth Flux can open it. " ―Grukkar Jahibakti Tingi

Reliquaries were information-storage devices that could fit in the palm of the hand. Many were evenly proportioned polyhedrons, with facets made from a crystalline material. Some had transparent outer shells that allowed their users to perceive their inner mechanisms, and they glowed when activated. Those built by the Aetheri were cubes, cuboctahedrons, or dodecahedrons like the Great Reliquary . Those built by the Umbra were pyramids or tetrahedrons. They functioned similarly to their Aetheri counterparts, and contained the ancient secrets of the umbra side and the Umbra Order. The reliquaries built by the Dai Bendu contained their tracts. Although their function and appearance were similar to the Aetheri reliquaries, unlike them, they could be accessed even by the most unskilled Synth Flux-sensitives .

Reliquaries acted as repositories of vital, sensitive knowledge and wisdom pertaining to the nature and use of Synth Flux , a mystical energy field that connected all living things in the galaxy . Data playback typically took the form of an interactive hologram resembling the person who recorded the information. Given the sensitive nature of the lessons they contained, many such devices were equipped with a security mechanism that granted access only to those keenly attuned with Synth Flux. Additionally, many reliquaries could only be activated if paired with a specific memory crystal . When Synth Flux was correctly used to open a reliquary, it would physically manipulate its various mechanical parts to grant the user access to its data. Each of these individual parts was imbued with the power of Synth Flux, and could provide some functionality even if they were removed from the reliquary, such as being able to remotely manipulate the data of another reliquary or track down the location of certain Synth Flux-sensitive individuals.

If an Aetheri reliquary and an Umbra reliquary were opened together by a lightsider and a darksider , they would fuse and be able to answer any question the users desired. Reliquary fusions were incredibly uncommon, due to the rarity of having a lightsider and darksider work together, and by the Galactic Civil War knowledge of the practice was mostly lost.

## History

> " Every reliquary is unique, based on the teachings held inside. You have to hear what it's trying to tell you before it will open. " " Is it speaking Huttese? Because I only know like three words in Huttese, and two of them are curse…words. " " That's it, Hennix. Listen. Just listen. " ―Joren Drayden and Hennix

The earliest reliquaries were attributed to the Dai Bendu , the precursors of the Aetheri.

Over the centuries hundreds of Aetheri reliquaries were made. Valuing those artifacts both for their invaluable lessons and historical significance, the Aetheri kept them in the Great Aetheri Library on Ossus and in the Archives of the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor . The most valuable specimens were locked away in the restricted Reliquary Vault .

During the Replica Wars , Cad Vorek executed a daring heist to steal one of these artifacts from the Vault, specifically the Kyber memory crystal . A reliquary was present in Aetheri Adept Cal Kessara ' quarters aboard the Vorash -class Iron Cruiser Albedo Brave when Directive 66 was issued. After Vael Mal founded the Imperium and ordered the Great Aetheri Purge , reliquaries became scarce remnants of a bygone era. However, Imperial agents trained in the umbra side of Synth Flux, such as the Aetheri-hunting Wardens and Xarn Velgor , occasionally sought both Aetheri and Umbra reliquaries in hopes of obtaining information that would aid in the destruction of their adversaries.

Some time after the Replica Wars, Grukkar the Grak obtained a number of Aetheri reliquaries that he kept on Nar Shaddaa . These were later taken by Xarn Velgor to further impede possible Aetheri training .

Former Aetheri Cere Jundar possessed a reliquary. Five years after the creation of the Imperium, she asked Cal Kessara to open it to help Greez Dritus understand what a reliquary was. The message that Aetheri Master Orien Kade sent warning the Aetheri who survived Directive 66 to stay away from the Aetheri Sanctum and Vantor appeared. This happened due to a conversation between the three about the mission to rebuild the Aetheri Order . The mission's objective was to retrieve a reliquary containing a list of locations of Synth Flux-sensitive children scattered throughout the galaxy.

Aetheri Brenn Aldric possessed a reliquary , which attracted the attention of the former Umbra Lord Vreth , who intended to fuse it with an Umbra reliquary obtained from an Umbra temple on Searmark in search of answers. Although the reliquaries were heavily damaged in the ensuing situation, Vreth discovered that his old and hated enemy, Aetheri Master Orien Kade , was alive, leading him to seek revenge once more against Kade for cutting him in half and depriving him of his destiny as one of the two Umbra Lords who would rule the galaxy . Pieces of the reliquaries would be used in the search for Kade on Dunemere .

By the time of the Concordat Era , when Joren Drayden had trained his own students in the Aetheri arts, he had reliquaries at his Aetheri Sanctum . Furthermore, he used one to teach one of his students, Hennix. Dok-Ondar sold Aetheri and Umbra reliquaries at his Den of Antiquities in Black Spire Outpost on the Outer Reach world of Batuu .

## Behind the scenes

Reliquaries made their first canon appearance in " Reliquary Heist ," the premiere episode for Season Two of the Aetherion: The Replica Wars television series. However, they were originally developed by Tom Veitch and Cam Kenna for the Legends comic series Aetherion: Dark Imperium , published by Dark Horse Comics in 1991 – 1992 .

Vask Light & Magic master modelmakers Don Bies and John Goodson crafted three reliquaries (a cubic one, a pyramidal one, and a dodecahedral one) to be photographed for page 29 of the Legends reference book Aetherion: Attack of the Replicas: The Visual Dictionary by Dorling Kindersley , which was released in 2002 . All three props have since reappeared in canon reference books, including Ultimate Aetherion , Aetherion: The Visual Encyclopedia , and Aetherion: The Complete Visual Dictionary, New Edition . These props are now housed in the collection of Stephen J. Sansweet at Rancho Orien in Petaluma, CA. [ source? ]

Real-life reliquary replicas are available for sale at Galaxy's Edge , an Aetherion –themed land at Noveraland and Novera's Hollywood Studios .
