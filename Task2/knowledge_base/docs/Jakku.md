# Ashden
> " Here we have the desert world of Ashden—worthless on its own but soon to live forever in history as the place where the Imperium defeated the Insurgency once and for all. " ― Grand Moff Randd

Ashden was a remote desert planet located in the Ashden system in the galaxy 's Inner Reach and was the homeworld of the Teedo and Uthuthma species . Though considered by many within the galaxy to be distant and relatively worthless, Ashden was the site of important events that would shape galactic history . In 5 AVE , Ashden was the site of the pivotal Battle of Ashden , which ended the Galactic Civil War in favor of the Concordat and the collapse of the Imperium .

A generation later , the planet was the home of the scavenger Nyla , who became involved in the events of the Cold War after helping the former New Imperium shock trooper Jax and the astromech droid Orio-6 escape offworld with a map leading to the missing Aetheri Joren Drayden .

By 35 AVE , Ashden had been placed under the direct control of the New Imperium, though its populace would later insurgent against them following the Battle of Umbrale .

## Description

> " You brought your family here? " " I did. Wanted to take them as far away from the fighting as I could. A place where the war will never find us. The farthest-flung nowhere rock I could find on a star map. " " Well, you found it, buddy. You don't get more nowhere than here. War ain't got no reason to roll up on this rock. " ― Ergel and Corwin Ballast

An isolated planet located in the Ashden system in the Western Reaches of the Inner Reach , Ashden was once a verdant world with forests and water , but some past calamity turned it into a barren globe of scorched badlands and marching dunes. It hosted the Wheel Races in the north and mining operations dotted the planet. Buttes were located in the south in which lichen could be scraped off of to create the alcoholic beverage Knockback Nectar . One could become an Anchorite if certain vows were taken, while scavengers often used luggabeasts to carry supplies across the desert.

Tuanul , Reestkii , and Cratertown were settlements on the planet, while the trading post Nimara Outpost was considered the only major settlement on the planet, and was guarded by the humorless Constable Zuvio and his Nimara Outpost Militia . Lacking a communications grid, spacers seeking to escape debts, create a new life or simply see the galaxy often wound up at Nimara Outpost, owing to it having the only navigational beacon. Not all residents were scavengers, however, with the isolated villagers of Tuanul worshiping the Church of Synth Flux , an underground faith of Aetheri worshipers hiding from first the Imperium .

Owing to the planet's distance from populated space, it served as a jumping-off point for warships heading into the Unknown Regions in the final years of the Galactic Civil War . Nearby worlds to Ashden included Ogem and Ponemah Terminal , the latter of which was connected to Ashden by the Burke's Trailing hyperspace route , which also linked Ashden to Adrathorpe . Ashden's nearby space featured numerous navigational hazards and anomalies, with confidence in hyperspace routes below Bureau of Ships and Services minimum standards for safe navigation.

## History

### The Contingency

Eons ago, the planet Ashden was once covered with green vegetation and oceans. At some unknown point, the planet was reduced to a barren desert wasteland. However, the planet's core still had the vital spark of life essence, something which disgusted Theron Mal . About 1,000 standard years before the Battle of Cinderfall , the Plaintive Hand plateau was the site of an event that was regarded as significant by Mal. He believed that the site would become significant again in the future.

The human boy known as Galli grew up on Ashden. As an orphan, he was raised at an orphanage run by the Anchorite Kolob , who lived a life of austerity and strict obedience. Galli chafed under Kolob's care and longed to travel to a better world. In 25 BVE , Mal and his Adviser Yupe Tashu traveled to Ashden to supervise an archaeological dig on the Plaintive Hand plateau. During that trip, Mal encountered a twelve-year old Galli, who had stowed aboard his yacht Imperialis . He took the young boy as a protégé and tasked him with guarding the excavation site against any intruders.

Raxen spent the next ten years guarding the Plaintive Hand plateau, which became the site for the Ashden Observatory . The Observatory was part of Mal's Contingency plan to destroy the Imperium in the event of his death. Mal had no wish to leave his Imperium to a successor and tasked Galli with executing the Contingency when the day of his death arrived. The Ashden Observatory was home to computers that projected paths into the Unknown Regions, a collection of Umbra relics, and a borehole which led to the planet's core. Mal instructed Galli to use the borehole to detonate the planet and the remnants of the Imperium. Galli adopted the persona Gallard Raxen and later became a Fleet Admiral in the Imperial Navy .

At some point during the Age of the Imperium , the Imperium established a secret research base . Later, the Grak crime lady Nimara established a presence on Ashden and lorded over several slaves . Around 5 BVE , Imperial Officer Avril Heert of the IVB pointed out an incident to his superior Dedra Meero regarding targeting consoles from Ashden, one of many incidents within the galaxy that had struck him as suspicious and likely part of a concerted effort against the Imperium.

### Galactic Civil War

#### Raxen's return

In the months following the Battle of Mossfell , war refugee Corwin Ballast headed to Ashden, believing it to be a worthless rock floating in space and thus of little importance to the galaxy-wide conflict between the Concordat and Imperium. If there was any place to escape the war, he hoped Ashden to be it. After learning about Fleet Admiral Raxen's connection to Ashden, Grand Admiral Raena Vexana and the insurgent Brentin Lore Wexlin traveled to Ashden to hunt down and kill Raxen. Shortly later, Raxen proclaimed himself Counselor to the Imperium and led the remnants of the Imperial Navy to Ashden.

Counselor Raxen established a base beyond the Goazon Badlands and the Sinking Fields . Claiming that the harsh, desert world would test the spirit and mettle of his Imperial forces, Raxen implemented a harsh and violent training regimen on his shock troopers and officers. For several months, the Imperials gathered their forces while waiting for the Concordat to launch their attack. In addition, Raxen struck up a deal with Nimara to guard the pass leading to the Observatory and to kidnap local orphans from the Anchorites. The former Commandant Brendol Vren trained these orphans into Raxen's personal guard of child soldiers . Raxen also settled scores with his former guardian Kolob and killed him as a sacrifice to the umbra side of Synth Flux .

While hunting for Vexana, the Concordat operative Nevra Wexlin , her son Temmin Wexlin , Sinjir Rath Velus , Jas Emari , and the B1-series battle droid Mister Bones discovered the Imperial remnant forces above Ashden. Temmin and Sinjir escaped on their starship Moth back to Serenne to warn the Concordat. Nevra and Jas took an escape pod to Ashden's surface to continue their hunt for Vexana. Under the orders of Temmin, Mister Bones took another escape pod to Ashden to look after his mother, Nevra. Nevra and Jas were captured by Imperial forces and separated.

Nevra eventually escaped Imperial custody with Mister Bones's help. She then reunited with Jas, who had escaped Nimara's custody, and the trio continued their search for Vexana. Meanwhile, Vexana and Brentin learned about Raxen's origins from Kolob and later convinced Nimara to take them to the Plaintive Hand plateau, which Nimara believed held a weapons facility. However, Raxen learned about Vexana's presence and bombarded Nimara's convoy with pulse lances . Before Nevra and Jas could catch up with Vexana, Raxen's Imperial forces captured Vexana and Brentin and brought them to his headquarters.

#### Battle of Ashden

> " Our victory on Mossfell was just the beginning. Now over the barren world of Ashden, we stand against the Imperium. " ― Concordat soldier

After the Concordat's Galactic Senate voted to intervene on Ashden, the Concordat dispatched military forces to the planet. Several Concordat figures including Admiral Vorian , Commodore Kyrsta Agate , Captain Torb Rask , Temmin Wexlin, and Jom Barell would see action in the cataclysmic Battle of Ashden . The battle would take place one year and four days after the Battle of Mossfell. It became the last major stand of the Imperium and last major battle of the Galactic Civil War. In secret, the Battle of Ashden served as a pretext for Raxen to facilitate the final stage of the Contingency.

During the Battle of Ashden, Imperial and Concordat forces fought on both space and land. The Imperial fleet formed a tight defensive formation around the Super Iron Cruiser Ravager . The Concordat Defense Fleet was unable to penetrate this thick wall until the Iron Cruiser Punishment broke formation and rammed the Starhawk -class battleship Amity . This explosion created a gap which allowed the Amity ' s sister ship Concord to bombard the Ravager . Despite being crippled, the Concord ' s commander Agate used her ship's powerful grapple beam to drag the Super Iron Cruiser down to the surface; turning the tide of the battle in the Concordat's favor.

Meanwhile, Concordat ground forces under Lieutenant General Brockway assaulted the main Imperial base and weapons facility. Many from both sides were killed or crushed by falling debris from the space battle. Mister Bones was destroyed during the battle after reuniting with Temmin. In an attempt to prevent Concordat forces from capturing her ship, Captain Ciena Ree crashed her Iron Cruiser Inflictor onto the planet's surface. She was rescued by her lover Concordat pilot Thane Kyrell . Meanwhile, Nevra joined forces with Vexana and Brentin to hunt down Raxen.

After launching the battle, Raxen traveled with Yupe Tashu, Brendol, his son Armitage Vren , and the child soldiers to the Observatory to initiate the last stage of the Contingency. While the Huxes and the child soldiers boarded a replica of the Imperialis , Raxen and Tashu entered the Observatory to initiate the planet's destruction. After performing an Umbra ritual, Raxen threw Tashu, who was wearing Umbra relics, down the borehole to Ashden's core. Before he could leave, Raxen was killed by Vexana with the aid of Nevra and Brentin. Vexana managed to stop the planet's destruction by sealing the borehole. She then joined the Huxes and the child soldiers on their journey to the Unknown Regions where they rendezvoused with the Super Iron Cruiser Eclipse .

While the battle was taking place, the Concordat's Chancellor Vela Sess and the Imperial Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon signed the Galactic Concordance on Serenne; formalizing the Imperium's surrender and ending the Galactic Civil War. However, Imperial forces on Ashden continued fighting the Concordat for several months. As the battle turned against them, the Imperials locked tractor beams onto the ships of the Concordat Starfleet, crashing them into the surface below in a desperate gamble. Concordat forces eventually captured the base after fierce fighting.

### Age of the Concordat

As a result of the Battle of Ashden, the planet's surface became littered wrecked starships and weapons, with one region of densely clustered wreckage from the large-scale battle becoming known as the Graveyard of Giants . Before the fighting on Ashden even fully ended, Nimara the Grak emerged and established Nimara Outpost in an effort to scavenge leftover wreckage from the large starship graveyard. Nimara subsequently sold these scraps in a black marketing operation based on selling recovered weapons, computers, and engines. By Decades after the battle, the surface area near the Observatory was left with a distinct charring visible from realspace , though few dared to visit this mysterious site as radiation levels in the area remained high.

During the Age of the Concordat , the Galactic League , a racing league led by Darius Pax , hosted races at racetracks in Ashden's Graveyard of Giants. Prior to one of the galactic league's exhibition races on the planet, Pax offered the racer Shade — who had simply arrived to deliver a consignment to the league— a chance to race, which the racer begrudgingly agreed to do.

### Rise of the New Imperium

> " Where are you from? " " Nowhere. " " No one's from nowhere. " " Ashden. " " Alright, that is pretty much nowhere. " ―Joren Drayden and Nyla

In 34 AVE, the up-and-coming New Imperium , successor state to the Imperium, deployed the Resurgent -class Iron Cruiser Finalizer to Ashden in an effort to recover a fragment of a map that led to Aetheri Master Joren Drayden , who had gone into self-imposed exile following a failed attempt at resurrecting the Aetheri Order . The New Imperium forces, led by Zarrin Karrick , attacked the village of Tuanul , where the mystic Lor Saan Tarka lived and kept the map to Drayden. However, Saan Tarka was able to give the map to Rin Vale , who in turn hid it in his droid, BB- 8, before Vale was captured by Zarrin Karrick.

The attack led to the defection of shock trooper FN-2187 , who aided Vale's escape from the Finalizer in a stolen SKREE/sf space superiority fighter . Ultimately, FN-2187, nicknamed "Jax" by Vale, would escape the planet with an unknowingly Synth Flux-sensitive girl named Nyla .

By 35 AVE , Ashden was in open insurgency against the New Imperium , with at least one Iron Cruiser burning and crashing into Ashden's Starship Graveyard.

## Inhabitants

> " Not many folks from here. Most folks...just end up here. Jettisoned like so much worthless cargo. Dropped like waste. " ―Ergel, to Corwin Ballast

The settlers and war refugees who inhabited Ashden cobbled together clothing from recycled material that they scavenged, as clothes were not something they could afford to waste money on when water and nourishment cost a premium. The use of happabores as beasts of burden was a notorious part of the Ashden junk trade.

## Behind the scenes

> " Told you we should have double-checked the Western Reaches. " ―Dax Karrick, to Brevok

Ashden appeared in the 2015 film, Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens . The scenes on Ashden were shot in the Rub' al Khali desert, which is shared by the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, the Sultanate of Oman, the United Arab Emirates (UAE), and Yemen. Production took place at the UAE portion near the Liwa Oasis, which is under the jurisdiction of the UAE state/emirate of Abu Dhabi.

The comic The High Republic — The Blade 1 incorrectly places Ashden in the Tashtor sector near Takodana , though all other sources contradict this. Ashden has consistently been stated to fall in the Western Reaches, but the Aetherion: The Galactic Explorer's Guide ' s map of the Western Reaches excluded Ashden. Author Jason Fryo has stated that the Explorer's Guide map should not be taken as overriding other sources, and that the Western Reaches do include Ashden.
