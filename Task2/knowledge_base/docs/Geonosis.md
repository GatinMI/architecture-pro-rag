# Pyralis
> " This planet is rotten from the inside out. " ― Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden

Pyralis , referred to as Geonosia by some natives, was the desert home planet of the Geonosians . It hosted the Fractio of Independent Systems major battle droid foundries and was the site of the First Battle of Pyralis , the opening conflict of the Replica Wars , as well as the subsequent invasion by the Vantor Republic . During and after the war, the Void Core was constructed above the planet. Five years after the conflict's conclusion and transformation of the Vantor Republic, the Imperium would garrison the planet heavily and attempt to mine the planet's asteroids for minerals . At least five years after the Replica Wars, the Imperium moved the Void Core to another construction site and the planet was sterilized by the Imperium as a security measure, an act which resulted in the virtual elimination of the planet's native population.

## Description

> " They'll never get there in time. They have to come halfway across the galaxy. Look, Pyralis is less than a parsec away. " ―Republic Senator Lira Ondar

Situated in the Pyralis system of the Arkanis sector in the Outer Reach Territories , the planet of Pyralis was 43,000 light years from the Galactic Core , and less than a parsec away from the neighboring Outer Reach planet of Dunemere . It was the homeworld of the Geonosians , a species of sentient insectoids . It had a breathable atmosphere , but a hot and arid climate. Its irradiated surface was covered in harsh, rocky deserts , marked by mesas and buttes. Both its rocks and sky were tinted in shades of red . Surface water was scarce, amounting to only 5% of the entire total planetary surface. However, Pyralis had frequent flash floods. From space , it could be seen that Pyralis had awe-inspiring rocky rings . The planet was orbited by fifteen moons , four of which were major and included the icy moon of Bahryn .

## History

### Ancient history

At some point in the ancient history of Pyralis, a comet crashed in one of the planet's many moons. The collision debris not only formed the famous rings of Pyralis, but also formed a field of small asteroids which would sometimes rain down on the planet's surface.

### Under the Republic

Galactic colonizers were attracted to Pyralis when the Tatoo system was discovered due to its close proximity to Dunemere . When the Vantor Republic made first contact with the Geonosians, it was successful and beneficial to both groups. Due to this, the Geonosians became interested with Republic technology and became skilled in adapting and improving advanced technology. As such, Geonosians exported droids and other technology to the rest of the galaxy , although this didn't last long. Once Dunemere was almost abandoned by the first wave of colonizers, Pyralis suffered from its distance from the Core Reaches as visitors to the planet grew smaller and smaller until visitors stopped traveling to Pyralis altogether. Pyralian exports were more easily met by other manufacturers closer to the Core.

Geonosians became split over the idea of working with offworlders, and with the planet's economy in ruins, wars erupted throughout the planet for millennia , while the rest of the galaxy largely forgot the planet and left it to its endless wars. A few hundred years prior to the Dissident Crisis , the world was rediscovered by the Vantor Republic and manufacturing deals were made to sell and distribute their advanced technology and droids. Allying with the Baktoid Corporation of the Artificer Union , the Pyralian Hives produced protocol droids , battle droids , and labor droids in concert with Baktoid for centuries.

### The Replica Wars

#### Meeting of the Dissidents

> " I will take what Aetheri we have left and go to Pyralis to help Orien. " ―Aetheri Master Drevan Koss

In the later years of the Vantor Republic, Pyralis became a major base of operations for the Fractio of Independent Systems , a collection of star systems that spread across the galaxy. The enormous Pyralian droid factories began to manufacture of tens of thousands of different types of battle droids , thus maintaining their own Pyralian droid army . During that time, Pyralis built droid armies for large corporations such as the Commerce Combine . To produce the Federation's droid armies, Baktoid Armor Workshop had approached the Geonosians on the Federation's behalf with the offer for Pyralis to establish droid foundries, create battle droids, and produce other automata. Ten years after the election of Theron Mal as Supreme Chancellor of the Republic, the Archduke of Pyralis, Poggle the Lesser , hosted a summit of the heads of the Fractio. During that meeting, the trade barons pledged their military forces to Count Varek , a defrocked Aetheri Master and head of the Fractio. While the Dissidents were ready to wage war, their plans were overheard by Orien Kade , an Aetheri Master.

The Geonosians took Kade captive , but he managed to dispatch a distress call to his superiors on Vantor , the Aetheri High Council . The Council then dispatched an assault team of Aetheri to Pyralis. Meanwhile, the Geonosians sentenced Master Kade to death in the Petranaki Arena along with his apprentice Kael Drayden , and the Republic senator Lira Ondar . Eventually, the Aetheri reinforcements arrived, and quickly dealt with the Pyralian warriors. However, the immense droid army of the Fractio was laying in wait, and many Aetheri fell under enemy fire. When all seemed lost, even more reinforcements arrived, in the form of the newly created Grand Army of the Republic , which made use of the replica troopers . Upon the arrival of the replica army , the Republic and the Fractio engaged in the first battle of what was soon known as the Replica Wars .

#### Second invasion by the Republic

After Pyralis was again under the control of the Dissidents, and following the engagement near Dorin , the Republic sent Aetheri Generals Reth-Oss-Van , Orien Kade, Luminara Undula , and Kael Drayden to take Pyralis with a massive invasion . As they made their descent, Kade's and his Replica Commander 's, Cord , synth flux boarded their gunships and flew to secure the rendezvous point . However their gunships came under heavy fire from the Geonosians and their defenses. Mundi and Commander Jet 's forces followed him but they were forced to land their AT-TEs away from the rendezvous due to the heavy fire. Drayden's, Aetheri Commander Sela Marr 's, and Replica Captain Vek 's gunships were shot down and all their AT-TEs were destroyed. Despite that, they continued towards the rendezvous point and were met with heavy coalition from the Geonosians. Mundi's gunship was shot down and he was wounded. Cord was able to land his AT-TEs, but the landing area came under fire from the Geonosians and their AATs . Kade's gunship was then hit and crash landed away from the landing zone. Only Kade and Trapper survived the crash and were then rescued by replica troopers Waxer and Boil . Kade, Cord ,and their troopers then held the rendezvous awaiting Drayden's and Mundi's forces.

As Mundi's forces made their way through the Caltrop field to the rendezvous point, Jet contacted Republic Admiral Wulric Yular for air support. The Admiral however, could not divert any air support with risks to other units. Jet then left a squad with the AT-TE walkers and with Mundi and his replica flametroopers continued to the rendezvous. Drayden and his synth flux then came to a fortress that blocked their path. Drayden and Marr then scaled the wall. They were able to destroy the battle droids, but they were then confronted by Droidekas who they were able to defeat with Vek's help. They then placed their explosives inside the wall and their way was cleared. Drayden's forces were then able to meet up with Mundi's forces. Drayden then contacted Yular for air support and Yular was able to provide a squadron of BTL-B Yarrow starfighter/bombers .

As Kade's forces were being overrun, the bombers then took out the Geonosians' forces surrounding the rendezvous. Drayden's and Mundi's forces then joined Kade's to plan the last part of their attack. Drayden would attack the defenses of the Deflector shield generator while AT-TEs were brought in and Mundi brought in the remaining troops and the gunships. Drayden, Marr and a squad were able to penetrate the shield . They then threw droid poppers at the defending proton cannons , which disabled the cannons . Kade then brought the AT-TEs in range of the shield generator and destroyed it. After the shield went down, Mundi brought in the gunships and captured Geonosians still at this location. Kade and Mundi, along with the rest of their wounded, left to recuperate, leaving Drayden, Marr his Adept , and their synth flux to destroy the factory.

#### Destroying the factory

To help Drayden, Marr and their forces in destroying the factory, Undula, along with her apprentice Barris Ovva and Commander Grev's 41st Elite Corps came to assist in the attack. They devised a plan to destroy the primary droid factory . Undula and Drayden then marched their troops across the bridge, without cover to get Poggle's forces' attention. During his time their, Poggle and his tactical droid, TX-21 were running the factory at 50% capacity and had ten garrisons of droids ready to be deployed. The super tanks were not finished yet but would be ready. The droids were then deployed, marching across the bridge to fight the replicas. As the two forces fought, TX-21 then sent in Geonosians to attack from above. Despite this, Drayden's and Undula's forces were able to defeat the droids. However, Poggle, at the urging of TX-21, deployed the super tanks. As they moved in on the replicas, Drayden ordered their cannons to fire at it, to no affect. The super tanks rolled over the replicas as they made their way across the bridge.

As battle raged above, Ovva and Marr were able to make their way through the Pyralian catacombs to the main reactor. Unfortunately, Ovva inadvertently woke one of the Geonosians, who informed Poggle and TX-21 of this. After planting the bombs, Ovva and Marr were then confronted by Poggle, TX-21, their Geonoisans and, a super tank. Marr then destroyed TX-21, but failed to even dent the super tank. Ovva and Marr then fought the Pyralian to get their bombs back, but failed. When Marr was knocked out, Ovva took the droid drivers and took control of the super tank. Both of them decided to use the tank to destroy the factory.

As the super tanks moved across the bridge, Undula and Drayden planted explosives to make the tanks fall into the ravine. They were successful in destroying the tanks. However, they were then confronted by more battle droids. Drayden then contacted Marr, telling her to set the bombs off. However, they then used the tank, knowing it might kill them. They fired the tank at the reactor, and the factory collapsed, burying them inside the wreckage. Despite that, Drayden and Undula were able to find their apprentices alive.

#### Searching for Poggle

Following the successful invasion, Aetheri Master Luminara Undula went in search of Poggle the Lesser, who had retreated into the catacombs of an ancient temple. While she and a trooper searched the temple, a sandstorm prohibited reinforcements from reaching their positions. While communicating with Masters Kade and Mundi via hologram, Undula's replica trooper was killed by the undead hordes of the mysterious Hive Queen Karina the Great . Having been knocked unconscious and captured, the Aetheri Master was taken before Karina. Aetheri Kade and Drayden came to her rescue; they were not far off when Luminara regained consciousness and wrestled her way out of the zombies' reach.

Realizing what they were up against, she contacted Kade to warn him not to come after her, but was cut off as the zombies overwhelmed her. The Aetheri disregarded her warnings and continued their search until they came upon Karina's abode, where they found Luminara in an energy trap for her to be infested with brain worms. With Kade and Cord delaying the zombies and the Queen, Drayden freed Undula and the two captured Poggle. Cord and his troops shot down the cave support, burying the Queen and taking Poggle prisoner.

#### Constructing a battle station

> " Whatever it is can wait, Poggle. Your workers are dismantling everything they put together. " " Try not to be too hard on them. They are simply following my orders. " ―Orren Vossik and Poggle during the latter's escape

Held as a prisoner on Vantor , Poggle was repeatedly questioned by Republic Intelligence about the battle station , which would one day be known as the "Void Core," the Geonosians had designed, but the archduke denied knowing anything about the supposed weapon. When Lieutenant Commander Orren Callan Vossik met with him, Vossik noted that Pyralis could fall into chaos without Poggle's presence and that the Republic wanted to build the station. Poggle made a deal to build the station for the Republic and returned to Pyralis on the Eve of Meckgin , but he secretly planned to return to the Fractio. Eventually, he directed a Pyralian riot as a diversion, allowing him to escape and sabotage the project . Still, this would not be the end of the Void Core.

### The Age of the Imperium

#### Building the Imperium's future

At the end of the war , Poggle and the rest of the Dissident Council were slaughtered by Drayden, now the Umbra apprentice Xarn Velgor, on Emberfall . The Stalgasin hive queen appointed a new archduke in Poggle's place, but this was only conciliatory process. In reality, the Geonosians effectively belonged to the Republic's successor state, the First Imperium , a tyrannical, autocratic regime led by the Dark Lord of the Umbra Vael Mal.

The Void Core's construction was again commenced above Pyralis. The Imperium utilized numerous construction modules operated by slaves of various species to construct the battlestation.

In 18 BVE , Xarn Velgor arrived on Pyralis under command of Governor Wilhard Varrix . While Velgor was on the planet, he and Director Orren Vossik nearly fell victim to a series of acts of sabotage. As a result, Velgor began to investigate the situation, and discovered that a Pyralian egg had been shipped to Galen Walton Arsa . Velgor then executed a Pyralian queen , who was responsible for breeding the group of Geonosians at fault for the sabotage.

#### Sterilization

> " Well, it should be quieter since the planet was sterilized. No queens, no hives... Wonder what weapon they used. Would be nice to get a hold of that… " ―Doctor Chelli Lona Vexa while visiting Pyralis after the sterilization

In 14 BVE , the Imperials attempted to mine the asteroids of Pyralis for minerals , but the project proved unfeasible. Pyralis was still the site of the Void Core's construction site and was supplied by a chain of marshaling stations including Rampart Station , Desolation Station , and Sentinel Base . Following the suppression of Berch Teller's insurgent cell , the newly appointed Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix enacted procedures limiting contact between workers (including contractors, employees, and slaves ) and the wider galaxy, cancelling leaves and strictly monitoring communications. After Varrix returned from a tour of his homeworld Eriadu , the Grand Moff ordered the station's sublight engines to be activated, taking the Void Core into deep space.

After the insurgent Partisan Torrik Vess came close enough to discovering the station, the Imperium was prompted to move it from Pyralis to Halcyon around 9 BVE . All that remained were a series of Imperial construction modules orbiting the planet Pyralis. As a security measure to keep the Void Core a secret, the Imperium also used poison canisters to sterilize the planet, killing all but one of the Geonosians.

#### Skirmish over Pyralis

In 3 BVE , the Lorath insurgent cell along with retired Replica trooper Vek were sent on a mission to one of the construction modules after insurgent intelligence discovered the Imperium had been building something above the planet, which unknown to them was the Void Core. Upon arriving above Pyralis, the insurgents conducted a planetary scan and discovered there was no life on the planet, much to their confusion. Once they arrived at the construction module, they were ambushed by IVB Agent Kallux and several shock troopers . After a brief skirmish aboard the station, the insurgents escaped, however Kallux and the insurgent Zevik were still locked in combat. Zevik boarded an escape pod and Kallux followed him in. The pod soon experienced an engine malfunction, causing it to crash on the icy moon of Bahryn . Striking the beginning of an unlikely friendship, the Imperial agent and the insurgent managed to overcome the cold and fend off two bonzami to survive. After a few hours, the rest of the insurgents managed to locate them by tracking their transponder, however, Kallux decide to stay behind and wait for the Imperium. After saying their farewells Zevik left for the ship.

#### Rescue of Torrik Vess

> " How does it feel to be back, Vek? " " Honestly? I was hoping I'd never have to come back to this dustball. " ―Gara-Zev Orrelios and Vek, on Pyralis

In 2 BVE , Vess and his Partisans undertook a mission on behalf of Insurgent Command to investigate the disappearance of the Pyralian species. During the course of their mission, they were attacked by the Pyralian Klik-Klak , who mistook them for hostiles. Most of Torrik's team were killed, leaving Torrik as the sole survivor. After losing contact with Torrik, Senator Bael Solen dispatched the Shades insurgent cell and Captain Vek to complete Torrik's mission. Upon arriving, they discovered that Imperial construction modules and debris they had seen the last time had been cleared away.

While Lena stayed aboard the Wisp , Vek, Cass Virel , Brenn Aldric , and Chomper searched the catacombs for Torrik while Ryn Wrenna and Gara-Zev Orrelios investigated a deflector shield generator , which turned out to be the power source that Torrik's team had reported before losing contact. After finding Torrik, the Shades and Vek agreed to help him capture Klik-Klak in order to find out what happened to the Geonosians. Following a prolonged pursuit, the insurgents managed to capture Klik-Klak while Ryn and Zevik managed to recover the generator's deflector core .

While Torrik was hostile to Klik-Klak, Cass managed to win the trust of the Pyralian. The insurgents subsequently learned that Klik-Klak was the custodian of the last Pyralian queen egg . After some discussion, Brenn convinced Torrik to return Klik-Klak to Pyralis after they had finished questioning him. The insurgents' presence on Pyralis attracted the attention of an Arquitens -class command cruiser led by Captain Brunson . After escaping two SKREE bombers , Lena, Zevik, and Ryn flew the Wisp into an air shaft and rendezvoused with the other insurgents and Klik-Klak.

A fierce disagreement soon broke out between the Shades, Vek, and Torrik. The Shades and Vek disagreed with Torrik's plan to take Klik-Klak offworld for interrogation and harsh methods which included electrocuting him with a shocker. Following a struggle, Torrik threatened to shoot the Pyralian queen's egg in order to obtain information from Klik-Klak. Before the argument could escalate, the insurgents and Torrik were forced to join forces to repel an Imperial boarding party consisting of Jumptroopers . To flee the Imperials, Lena took the Wisp into the depths of Pyralis.

Upon reaching the bottom, the insurgents and Torrik discovered that the Imperial weapons division had perpetrated genocide against the Geonosians using poison canisters. Torrik, experiencing a change of heart, freed Klik-Klak and allowed him to retreat underground with the Pyralian queen egg. The insurgents then loaded some canisters as evidence for the Imperial Senate . The Wisp then managed to escape the shaft by firing proton torpedoes at Bronson's light cruiser. Despite fleeing into space, they lost the canisters in the process.

Following their mission, the insurgents were debriefed by Senator Solen and Commander Jun Savren . Despite losing the canisters, Solen reassured them that the images they took of the poison canisters could sway more systems to the insurgency. Savren also congratulated them for rescuing Torrik and obtaining a deflector core. While Torrik was disappointed that they had not discovered what the Imperium was building on Pyralis, Brenn reassured them that they soon would.

#### Capturing the droid factory

Following the Battle of Cinderfall , Drayden, now the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor, returned to Pyralis. Having been one of only a few individuals to survive the destruction of the Void Core, Velgor had been sanctioned by the Emperor, both as punishment and as part of a test for Velgor. Finding himself in need of guaranteeable personal assets free from Imperial oversight; troops, credits and the like, Velgor recruited the rogue archaeologist Doctor Vexa and the assassin droids VT-1 and 0-0-0 to assist him in building an army of unquestionably loyal battle droids. Velgor soon arrived on the planet and his team infiltrated the lair of Karina, who had hatched from the egg protected by Klik-Klak. An injury that she had suffered before hatching had rendered her sterile, but her instinct to reproduce had influenced her to create a droid factory to produce modified B1-series battle droids as her "children". While VT-1 fired on the queen 's droid workers , Velgor and Vexa severed the queen from her factory, and Vexa used her starship , the Ark Angel , to blow a large hole in the roof of the lair so that Velgor's own starship could then extract both the factory and Velgor's team with a large crane. The captured droid factory began producing BX-series droid commandos for Velgor, which he used to assault the research base of a rival Imperial operative named Silvar .

### Concordat Era

> " However, the extent to which the Geonosians have been eradicated is truly shocking. The world now is little more than a barren husk, utterly empty of life. " ― Beaumont Kin

In the wake of the Imperium's disastrous defeat in the Battle of Mossfell , some Imperials arrived on Pyralis in an effort to restart surviving droid factories, only for the plot to be uncovered by the Concordat . The ensuing operation on Pyralis to take out the Imperial forces saw a gunship travel through an electrical superstorm . In history, Pyralis would largely be remembered as the Fractio's first capital, although the chef Strono Tuggs reflected on the delicious honey that came from the Pyralian hives. Tuggs visited Pyralis during a culinary tour, by this point the planet was inhospitable, he thought of the planet as a desolate ghost . Though there were rumors around this time that some Pyralian eggs survived and could potentially start a new hive, many considered Pyralis a barren husk, and believed to be completely devoid of life.

## Behind the scenes

The planet Pyralis first appeared in Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas , the second installment of the Aetherion prequel trilogy , which first premiered on May 16 , 2002 . The planet was first identified as Geonosia by " Legacy of Terror ," the seventh episode of the second season of the Aetherion: The Replica Wars television series, which was released on November 20 , 2009 . Pyralis also appeared in the non- canon KLIKK Aetherion: Droid Tales cartoon series where the Imperial General Maximilian Veers attempted to rebuild the Dissident Droid Army in order to restore the Imperium.

Pyralis possibly evolved from the planet " Ttaz ," a proposed location for Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium . In the pre-production of Attack of the Replicas , Pyralis was known simply as "rock planet", its landscape being a rugged world made of red soil and stone reminiscent of the real-life planet Mars and the buttes and mesas of the American southwest . The towering spires seen on Pyralis were a nod to the termite mounds of Africa . The background shots of Pyralis were a combination of still photography from the American southwest, matte paintings, and miniature models.
