# Astra-9
> " We seem to be made to suffer. It's our lot in life. " ―Astra-9

Astra-9 ( See-Astra-9 ) was an Astra-9-series protocol droid designed to interact with organics , programmed primarily for etiquette and protocol. Sometimes referred to as Astra-9 , he was fluent in over six million forms of communication , and developed a fussy and worry-prone personality throughout his many decades of operation. Along with his counterpart, the astromech droid Bolt-3 , Astra-9 constantly found himself directly involved in pivotal moments of galactic history , and aided in saving the galaxy on many occasions.

Astra-9 was rebuilt from spare parts by Kael Drayden , an enslaved human who lived in Port Vail , a city on the Outer Reach world of Dunemere . During the Replica Wars , he would serve the Ondara Senator Lira Ondar . Shortly after the Replica Wars, Astra-9's memory was erased , though Bolt-3's memory was not. Astra-9 and Bolt-3 were assigned to the Cindervale cruiser Tantiva IV , where they served senator Bael Solen for nineteen years, assisting in his fledgling coalition movement against the Imperium . At some point during this time, Astra-9's right leg was fitted with mismatched plating.

Astra-9 remained constant companions with Bolt, and in 1 BVE , the pair became embroiled in the Galactic Civil War when Elira Solen of the Free Coalition entrusted them to bring a copy of the Void Core plans to the Aetheri Master Orien Kade on Dunemere. After meeting Drayden's son, Joren and the smuggler Dax Karrick , the droids helped rescue Princess Elira from the Imperium's Void Core . They became directly attached to the three humans, helping them and their insurgency defeat the Imperium and restore freedom to the galaxy, most notably when he convinced the Mosskins to aid the Insurgents at the Battle of Mossfell .

Following the Battle of Mossfell, Astra-9 continued serving the Draydens and Karrick during the time of the Concordat . About thirty years after the events on Mossfell , Astra-9 was still an aide to Solen, who had become a general in the Coalition , a private military group opposed to the New Imperium , a military junta that emulated the Old Imperium. By that time, Astra-9 was fitted with a new set of gold plating, which had a more symmetrical design than his previous coverings, with the exception of his head. His new coverings included a third finger joint, and a torso that was not as slim as it used to be. Because of his new plating, he no longer had a mismatched right lower leg. Although, he did end up wearing a red left arm for a very short time. By that time, Astra-9's language database had also been updated so that he could understand and communicate in over seven million different forms of communication, including ancient Umbra dialects. He would assist Nyla in translating Umbra writings in order to locate Vael Mal and the Umbra Eternal 's fleet, the Final Order , on Umbrale , allowing his memory to be wiped in order to carry out the task. However, his friend Bolt backed up a large portion of his memories, and after the Coalition defeated Mal and the Umbra Eternal in the Battle of Umbrale , his memory was restored.

## Biography

### Early incarnation

> " Oh, might I remind you, sir, I was once protocol droid to the chief negotiator for the entire Manakron system. " ―Astra-9

In an early incarnation, Astra-9 served as a protocol droid to the chief negotiator for the Manakron system . Additionally, he later claimed that his first job was programming binary loadlifters , which he said were very similar to moisture vaporators in most respects.

### Serving the Maker

> " He's perfect. " ―Lira Ondar after meeting Astra-9

At some point, however, Astra-9 was seriously damaged and his vital components were left behind on a junk pile on Dunemere . Kael Drayden , a slave boy from the Tatooinian city of Port Vail , collected scrap parts and started rebuilding Astra-9 in 34 BVE so the droid would help his mother . As Kael longed for a life far from Dunemere and explore the galaxy , he hoped to have Astra-9 as his and his mother's faithful companion so they would be able to venture the galaxy one day and understand the languages of the natives they came across, given Astra-9's capability of millions of communication forms. Although protocol droids were normally designed for light duty in luxurious environments, Drayden specially modified Astra-9 so he could withstand Dunemere's sand and heat. Astra-9 served Kael and his mother Shira by performing household chores. During his time with Drayden and Shira, Astra-9's parts and wiring were left exposed since Drayden was unable to outfit him with an outer covering due to droid plating being too expensive.

Following the Commerce Combine 's invasion of the Mid Reach planet of Ondara , Astra-9's life entered a new chapter when his owner Kael met several off-worlders at the shop of his owner, the Junk dealer Waddu , and then invited them to seek shelter at his home during a sandstorm. The Aetheri Master Sorin Vael , the Queen of Ondara Lira Ondar , the Mool Greeb Mool , and the astromech droid Bolt-3 were brought to the slave quarters that Kael shared with his mother Shira. As a result of this invitation, Ondar and Bolt-3 were introduced to Astra-9. Kael plugged in one of Astra-9's photoreceptors , which became the first time Astra-9 had experienced sight. Upon meeting Bolt-3, Astra-9 was shocked when the candid astromech droid commented that he was "naked." Despite the exchange, the two droids fast developed a friendship of sorts, working together to help Drayden make final adjustments to his dune racer in time to race it in the Dune Eve Classic race . While working on the dune racer , the two droids discussed space travel and noted the clumsy actions of Mool.

On the day of the race, Astra-9 represented his owner in the opening flag parade, carrying Drayden's flag onto the course prior to the start of the race. He then stood on the sidelines with Bolt-3 and two of Drayden's playmates, Kitster Banai and Wald , who cheered their friend on. Drayden won the race, which, thanks to a deal that Vael had struck with Waddu, earned the boy his freedom from slavery and secured Vael the hyperrelay generator he needed to leave the planet. As Drayden prepared to depart Dunemere, he said his goodbyes to Astra-9, apologizing for not having completed the droid's construction, and expressing his wishes that Shira wouldn't sell him.

### Beyond Dunemere

> " There's been some terrible mistake. I'm programmed for etiquette, not destruction! " ―Astra-9 during the Battle of Pyralis

Later, his owner Shira was sold to the moisture farmer Cliegg Havel , who freed and married her. Shira brought Astra-9 along with her, and the protocol droid found himself in the service of the Havel family, who owned a moisture farm in the Jundland Wastes . Shira covered him in a tarnished grey plating to shield him from Dunemere's sandy environment. Ten years after his parting with Kael Drayden, Shira was kidnapped by a group of Dunefolk Raiders , fearsome nomads native to Dunemere's desert wastes. Sensing his mother's pain and anguish through Synth Flux , Kael returned with his secret lover and childhood friend Lira Ondar to find his mother. Drayden had advanced in his Aetheri training as an Adept under the tutelage of Orien Kade , while Ondar had become a Senator in the Galactic Senate .

When Drayden and Ondar visited the Havel' moisture farm, they found Astra-9 working on a moisture vaporator. He initially didn't recognize Drayden until the latter finished his name for him when introducing himself. Greeting his Maker, Astra-9 discovered that Drayden had come in search of his mother, and brought the two guests into the homestead's courtyard, where they were met by Cliegg Havel, as well as his son, Owen Havel and his girlfriend, Brue Whitesun . Learning of his mother's peril, Drayden took Owen's speeder bike to find her, and soon returned with her corpse, having recovered it from a Dunefolk village. Astra-9 was present when he arrived, and later attended a funeral for Shira on the farm's property. When Bolt-3, who had been waiting aboard Ondar's nearby yacht, interrupted the funeral with an urgent message for the Adept, Astra-9 translated the astromech's binary beeps and whistles. After viewing the holocomm message, Drayden and Ondar departed Dunemere for Pyralis to rescue Drayden's Aetheri Master Orien Kade, taking Astra-9 with them.

Thus, Astra-9 would become both a witness and participant in the subsequent First Battle of Pyralis , the first spark of the Replica Wars , a conflict that would change the course of galactic history. After landing on Pyralis, Astra-9 reluctantly followed Bolt-3, Drayden, and Ondar into the Pyralian droid foundry. After being trapped in the factory's conveyor belt system, Astra-9's head was separated from his body and grafted onto the chassis of a B1-series battle droid . Meanwhile, a B1 battle droid head was inserted onto Astra-9's body. During the ensuing battle, both of Astra-9's "bodies" were knocked out during the fighting. Fortunately for him, he was promptly reattached to his rightful body by Bolt-3. Following the events on Pyralis, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 travelled to Ondara with Drayden and Ondar for the two to be married in Ondara's Lake Country.

In the days following the Pyralis battle and before the wedding, Astra-9 was determined to keep Drayden and Ondar out of each other's sight until the wedding itself. Whilst waiting for the ceremony, Ondar believed there was a high chance Drayden was biding his time by working on Astra-9, as the droid needed to be cleaned after the sands of Dunemere had years to get into his joints, only to be further dirtied by the sands of Pyralis. As such, the protocol unit needed to receive a cleaning before he could receive his yet-to-be-finished new plating. Despite Astra-9's attempts, the two humans did see each other before the wedding, as Ondar, per a request from Queen Jamillia of Ondara, needed the help of Bolt-3 and Drayden to rescue the Torada Collective from the Dissident planet Nooroyo . After completing the mission, they returned to the Lake House to be wed, with Astra-9 and Bolt-3 in attendance.

### The Replica Wars

#### Serving Senator Ondar

During the Replica Wars, Astra-9 would serve Senator Lira Ondar, the spouse of Kael Drayden, and was given a shiny gold - colored plating to better suit his new role. On one occasion, Ondar was captured by Grukk's uncle Ziro during a mission to reopen communications with the Graks after Grukk's son Rotto was kidnapped by the Dissident leader Count Varek . Ondar contacted Astra-9, who in turn contacted the Vantor Guard , which promptly raided Ziro's Palace and freed Ondar. Meanwhile, Drayden and his Adept Sela Marr managed to rescue Rotto and return him safely to his father. Astra-9 and his comrades' actions help the Republic win over Grukk, who later signed a favorable treaty with them.

Following the Rotto affair, Astra-9 accompanied Senator Ondar on a mission to contact the InterGalactic Banking Clan 's executive, who purportedly wanted to sue for peace. Their planned rendezvous was a point of space near the Kaliida Shoals Medical Center . Unknown to the two of them, this was a trap concocted by Vael Mal and his apprentice Count Varek. The Dissident military commander General Skarn 's flagship Malignar had been damaged during a recent space battle and was unable to enter hyperspace . The Umbra and Dissidents hoped to use Senator Ondar as a hostage to extract leverage from the Republic commander Drayden, who was pursuing the Malignar .

Ondar's vessel was trapped in the Dissident warship's grapple beam , but they broke free by exploding their vessel's engines. Astra-9 and Ondar then sneaked aboard and contacted Drayden for help. Drayden along with Kade and Bolt-3 boarded the Malignar and made arrangements to rendezvous with Astra-9 and the Senator at the warship's rail jet system. However, they were accosted by battle droids there, which prompted Ondar to shove Astra-9 into a moving hovertrain . After the battle droids were neutralized, Kade began levitating Astra-9 to safety, but the floating droid was hit full-on by a moving hover train, which was only halted when Bolt-3 sliced into a computer terminal and shut it down. The sudden stop launched Astra-9 onto a nearby platform, where he quickly regained his bearings and again began wandering the cruiser's corridors before being located by Bolt-3. Together, the two droids escaped the Malignar before the Dissident warship crashed into a nearby moon after being sabotaged by Drayden.

#### Mission to Rodanor

Following their adventures on the Malignar , Astra-9 along with Senator Ondar and the Mool Representative Greeb Mool traveled to Rodanor to meet with her old friend Senator Onaconda Farr . Ondar's mission was to ensure that the ailing Rodians remained loyal to the Vantor Republic. While Astra-9 and Mool remained with Ondar's yacht on the landing platform, Senator Ondar went to meet Farr. An ill-fated attempt to contact local swamp creatures ended with some fruit being thrown into Astra-9's face. Unknown to Ondar, Farr had already struck a deal with the Dissident leader Viceroy Nutis Gunray of the Commerce Combine.

Astra-9 and Mool were soon confronted by several B1 battle droids. During the ensuing confrontation, Astra-9 became stuck to a magnetic hoist that collided with the Dissident battle droids, destroying them and when a LM-432 crab droid appears Astra-9 warns Greeb of it and Greeb got thrown on top of it and accidently cause it, along with himself, to fall into the water below. Astra-9 believed that Mool perished during the accident. However, Mool resurfaced and promptly released Astra-9 from the hoist, but accidently destroys the yacht with the magnetic hoist soon after.  While scouring through the wrecked yacht, Mool found and donned an Aetheri robe.

Shortly, the two of them were separated again, and Astra-9 was captured by Dissident droids, who were ordered to march him to a dismantling center. However, Astra-9 was rescued by Ondar, who destroyed his captors. She ordered Astra-9 to send a distress signal to nearby Republic forces. Astra-9 succeeded in sending for help before being arrested by two more Dissident droids. Gunray then attempted to execute both Ondar and Astra-9 but was foiled by Mool, who returned with a Kwazel Maw monster, which disposed of the Dissident droids. Senator Farr then turned on Gunray and arrested him, going along with Ondar's bluff that his collaboration with the Dissidents had been a ruse to lure the Viceroy into a trap. Republic forces arrived on Rodanor and promptly apprehended Gunray. Despite his annoyance with Greeb Mool' habits, he conceded that the Mool's foolishness had saved the day.

#### Mission to Orto Plutonia

Later, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 accompanied Drayden, Kade, and Captain Vek on a mission to investigate the disappearance of a replica trooper synth flux on the icy planet of Orto Plutonia . Their mission was complicated by the presence of several Pantoran delegates including Senator Riyo Chuchi and Chairman Chivva . The Pantorans claimed territorial sovereignty over Orto Plutonia even though it was supposedly devoid of sentient life. After discovering the dead replica troopers, the Aetheri and Republic mission also discovered that a nearby Dissident base had been destroyed. They quickly deduced that a third synth flux had been responsible for these two attacks.

Later, Drayden and Kade discovered that Ord Plutonia was home to a species of primitive bipedal sentient beings called the Talz . After some difficulties, Kade and Drayden established contact with the Talz and assured them that they had come in peace. The Talz chieftain Thi-Sen wanted the Republic to leave his people in peace and to withdraw their presence from the planet. After the Aetheri had made contact with the Talz tribe, Astra-9 and Cho joined them in meeting with Thi-Sen. However, the hard-line Chairman Chivva refused to treat the Talz as sentient beings and reiterated Pantora's claim to Orto Plutonia, provoking conflict between the Pandorans and Talz.

The Aetheri ended the fighting by convincing Senator Chuchi to contact the Pantoran Assembly , which gave her the authority to negotiate peace with the Talz and to relieve Chairman Cho of his authority. Astra-9 and his companions then accompanied Senator Chuchi to the scene of the fighting. There, Astra-9 helped Chuchi to broker a truce with Thi-Sen, ending the Pandoran-Talz conflict. As a result of the fighting, the Pandorans ceded sovereignty over Plutonia to the Talz.

#### The Blue Shadow Virus crisis

> " With your permission, Senator Lira, I would like to shut down before I get sensory overload. " " Permission denied. " ―Astra-9 and Ondar, while analyzing a captured Dissident tactical droid on Ondara

Following the discovery of a scouting party of Dissident battle droids on Ondara, Astra-9 accompanied Senator Ondar and Representative Mool back to Ondara. Ondar had been summoned by Queen Neeyutnee , who wanted to procure more military aid from the Republic. Astra-9 questioned a captured T-series tactical droid , which had its photoreceptors removed. After convincing the droid that it was aboard a Dissident starship, Astra-9 gleaned that there was a secret Dissident facility on Ondara producing some sort of virus before Greeb Mool accidentally destroyed the droid. Despite this setback, Mool and Ondar discovered a slug-beetle and mud from a swamp, which led them to Ondara's Eastern swamps .

While Astra-9 stayed behind on Serath with Captain Gregar Typho to examine data from the captured Dissident droids, Ondar and Mool ventured into the swamps and discovered a secret Dissident research facility headed by the scientist Doctor Nuvo Vindi . Vindi was developing a deadly bioweapon called the Blue Shadow Virus for the Dissidents. Astra-9 and Typho discovered a hologram of Dr. Vindi in one of the Dissident battle droids and conveyed this information to their companions. With the aid of a local Mool farmer named Peppi Bow , Drayden, Kade, Sela Marr, and several replica troopers , Ondar and Drayden succeeded in apprehending Dr. Vindi and stopping him from releasing his bioweapon.

#### The Senate hostage crisis

When Senator Ondar was organizing a party for Senator Aang , she sent Astra-9 and Bolt-3 to buy some jogan fruit that Bolt-3 had forgotten to buy for garnishing the jogan fruitcake , Aang's favorite dessert. In the lower market districts , the two droids grumbled and Astra-9 was swindled by a fruit vendor. Following the purchase, another droid approached Astra-9 and Bolt-3 and suggested that they visit Droid Spa for a cosmetic overhaul. While Bolt-3 visited the facility, Astra-9 was kidnapped by the Dissident-aligned Kessith bounty hunter Cad Vorek 's droid henchmen, Todo 360 and HELIOS-3E .

Astra-9 was taken to Vorek's hideout in an industrial district on Vantor, where his databanks were scanned for information on the Senate Building's layout. While Astra-9's databank yielded no such information, he mentioned that his astromech counterpart Bolt-3 might have the information. Vorek's droid henchmen promptly kidnapped Bolt-3 and brought him to Vorek. After Vorek extracted the necessary information from Bolt-3's databanks, he ordered that the two droids be given a memory wipe and returned to the place where they had been snatched.

Following the unpleasant shopping trip, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 returned to Ondar with their purchase. Both droids were completely unaware of what had befallen them. The jogan fruit was added to Senator Aang's dessert, as planned. Meanwhile, Vorek delivered the plans from Bolt-3's memory banks to Grukk the Grak on Dunemere. The Grak crime lord ordered Vorek to free his uncle Ziro from prison, since he knew information that would endanger the Graks, who were nominally working with the Republic. Vorek hatched a plan to rescue Ziro.

Following his kidnapping and mind-wipe, Astra-9 and Senator Ondar were caught up in the Senate hostage crisis . Cad Vorek and several other mercenaries infiltrated the Senate Building and took Ondar, Astra-9, Drayden and several senators hostage in a bid to synth flux a pardon for the imprisoned Ziro. These senators had been meeting to discussed the proposed Enhanced Privacy Invasion Bill . Despite the Republic honoring Vorek's demands, Vorek planted several explosives in the hall with the aim of killing the senators. Drayden saved the day by using his flux blade to cut a hole around the group, moments before the explosives went off.

#### The Dissident spy

Astra-9 was briefly reunited with Bolt-3 when the two droids accompanied their masters Senator Ondar and General Drayden on a mission to Cato Geltaria . The quartet were accompanying Rush Korvex , the representative from Scipio and a delegate representing the InterGalactic Banking Clan in the Senate. The Aetheri Council suspected that Korvex was secretly spying for the Dissidents and sent Drayden and Ondar to spy on him. During the journey, Astra-9 noticed Drayden's hostile behavior towards Korvex, who was trying to flirt with his secret wife Ondar.

Upon arriving on Cato Geltaria, the delegation was greeted by the Commerce Combine's Senator Lott Dod , who tried unsuccessfully to synth flux Korvex to lend more money for the Dissident cause. When Dod deduced that Korvex was infatuated with Senator Ondar, he arranged for her to be poisoned during a formal dinner. Despite this, Ondar succeeded in stealing a hologram disc which confirmed that Korvex was indeed working for the Dissidents. After Ondar began to succumb to the effects of the poisoning, Astra-9 and a 2-1B surgical droid attended to the Senator in her quarters.

Meanwhile, Korvex learned that Ondar was spying on him and went to confront her only to be cornered by Drayden. Realizing that Drayden loved Ondar, Korvex offered to get the antidote in return for receiving back the holodisk. Astra-9 and his companions brought Ondar back to their ship but were cornered by Dod and his aides. Korvex attempted to bluff his way out, but Dod refused to let them leave. After Korvex pulled a volt pistol and forced Dod to hand over the antidote, Astra-9 and his companions fled aboard Ondar's ship. They fled into space but left Korvex behind to face Dod alone.

#### Serving a peacemaker

As the Replica Wars dragged on, Astra-9 continued serving Senator Ondar, who was becoming an active member of a group of Senators that supported diplomacy and opposed the expansion of the Grand Army of the Republic . On one occasion, Astra-9 accompanied Senator Ondar and Master Kade when they presented evidence in the Galactic Senate that the planet Ferrum wished to be neutral. As a result, the Senate rescinded an earlier vote calling for a military occupation of Ferrum.

Later, Senator Ondar and her supporters including the Cindervalean Senator Bael Solen and the Rodanori Senator Onaconda Farr proposed a bill calling for the reduction of military spending . Astra-9 attended several meetings of this informal peace coalition in Senator Ondar's office. On one such occasion, Astra-9 was serving drinks when Senator Farr suffered an apparent heart attack and died. Astra-9 and Ondar attended the funeral of Senator Farr. Later, Ondar and Solen discovered that Farr had been poisoned by his aide, Lolo Purs , who blamed the late Senator for bringing the war to Rodanor.

#### The Zyll Beast

Following the Republic's victory at Malastare , Chancellor Mal ordered the Aetheri Generals Koss and Drayden to transport the Zyll Beast , a gargantuan monster that had been captured and sedated during the fighting. After learning that a flux blade could not harm the beast, Mal enlisted the services of the scientist Sionver Boll to study the beast's scales in order to develop a new type of armor for the Grand Army. Mal also planned to kill the monster with Malastarian fuel after it had outlived its usefulness.

Senator Ondar learned about Mal's plan and arranged a meeting with Drayden and Kade. During the meeting, Astra-9 had another brief reunion with his astromech counterpart, Bolt-3. Ondar succeeded in convincing her husband Drayden and the delegation met with Chancellor Mal and Vice Chair Mazan Veldon . However, the Zyll Beast escaped Boll's laboratory at the same time and proceeded to wreak havoc on the streets of Vantor. Sensing that Mal was an Umbra, the Zyll Beast made its way to the Galactic Senate building. Astra-9 assisted in the evacuation of staff from the building and guided them to an executive ship that was piloted by a Senate Commando .

Before they could flee to safety, the ship was grabbed by the Zyll Beast, but Drayden used his flux blade to cleave the vessel in half, sending the bow along with Astra-9 and the other passengers plummeting into the Senate Building. Astra-9 hitched a ride aboard Bolt-3, who had used a tow cable to attach himself to the other side of the building. However, Bolt-3 was forced to rescue Chancellor Mal and activated his booster rockers, leaving Astra-9 behind. The protocol droid lost hold of the cable and fell to the floor of the Senate building. Shortly thereafter, the Zyll Beast was killed by gas bombs fired from LAAT gunships . Astra-9 survived the Zyll Beast incident and continued serving Senator Ondar.

#### Staying behind

Astra-9 stayed behind on Vantor while Bolt-3 accompanied Kael Drayden and Master Kade on a mission to rescue Aetheri Master Even Piele from the Citadel prison on the planet Lola Sayu . Astra-9 was initially alarmed when he saw Bolt-3 flanked by three reprogrammed B1 battle droids. Astra-9 told Bolt-3 not to get "uppity" about commanding new battle droids before parting company.

#### Mission to Alenni

> " Hey Shiny! You two finished with those repairs on the computer system? " " My good sir. You may not realize it, but we have been on quite an adventure. " ―A replica trooper and Astra-9, while on Alennia

Astra-9 and Bolt-3 later took part in a mercy mission to the Mid Reach planet of Alenni , which was experiencing a series of catastrophic global earthquakes. Astra-9 served as the translator for the Republic mission led by Replica Commander Wolffe while his astromech counterpart Bolt-3-Detoo was tasked with interfacing with the equipment. Astra-9's arrival coincided with a brief aftershock during which he tried to calm the local villages. When the aftershock stopped, the Alennia thought that the golden droid was some sort of divine messenger and asked the Republic soldiers to put the earth to rest, a request that Wolffe reluctantly had to acquiesce to.

During the mission, Astra-9 translated while Wolffe explained the function of the Republic's delivered supplies to the Alennia King Manchucho . Later, Astra-9 accompanied Bolt-3 and several replica troopers as they entered the local Republic communications station to survey and repair the damage caused by the earthquakes. There, the Alennia villagers led Astra-9 and Bolt-3 into a collapsed structure, which contained a huge golden seal embedded into the ground. The seal turned out to be the entrance to a tunnel that led to the subterranean regions of Alenni. When another earthquake struck the area, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 fell into the tunnel and were separated from the replica troopers.

While exploring the underground tunnels, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 encountered a species of sentient, tree -like beings called the Kindalo , who demanded that they leave since the surface dwellers "destroyed the peace." After Astra-9 convinced the Kindalo that they bore no ill will and had come to help with the earthquakes, the Kindalo sent the droids to meet Orphne , a reptilian humanoid with mystical powers. Orphne told the two droids that a mutual balance had previously existed between the Alennia and the subterranean world. Since Alennia's atmosphere was toxic to them, the underground dwellers depended on a filtering process of the planet's soil that made the air breathable for them. The underground dwellers had set off this earthquake as a means of sealing the breach.

When Astra-9 agreed to help fix the damage, Orphne gave the droids a parting riddle. Bolt-3 quickly deduced that Orphne was referring to a river and used the water seal to activate a water spout that sent the two droids and the chamber's central floor plate back to the surface. The two droids landed at the opening where they were greeted by a chanting crowd of Alennia. Recalling the riddle, Astra-9 quickly realized that only he and Bolt-3 were able to seal the breach, which was in fact the unsettled seal. Together, the two droids closed the seal and brought an end to Alennia's earthquakes. While Wolffe and his replica troopers were skeptical of their story, the two droids' efforts were greatly appreciated by the Alennia.

#### Further adventures

Following the events on Alenni, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 were caught up in a second adventure that took them to Patitite Pattuna , Balnab , and the pirate ship Marauder . While traveling aboard the Aetheri Master Adi Gallia 's Iron Cruiser, their starship was attacked by three Dissident cruisers led by General Skarn. Astra-9 and Bolt-3 escaped the stricken vessel aboard a Yarrow starfighter and crash-landed on the nearby planet of Patitite Pattuna.

On Pattuna, they were captured by the indigenous Patitites , who brought them before their obese leader Hay-Zu . During their meeting, Bolt-3 accidentally toppled the tyrannical overlord much to the joy of the Patitites, who released their prisoners and allowed the two droids to leave on their Yarrow. With their starfighter's power systems damage, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 landed on another planet called Balnab. The indigenous Balnabs captured the two droids and brought them before their leader, who turned out to be a holo-projection created by a group of rogue pit droids . These pit droids had enslaved the Balnabs. After Bolt-3 exposed the scam, the Balnabs rebelled against their droid overlords.

Astra-9 and Bolt-3 took the opportunity to escape and resume their search for a replacement power unit for their Yarrow starfighter. Low on power, the two droids were captured by a band of Weequay pirates, who wanted to use them as gladiators aboard their starship, the Marauder . After Astra-9 and Bolt-3 watched K0-5D , the reigning gladiatorial champion, rip a droid apart, K0-5D selected Astra-9 as his next combatant. Astra-9 and his counterpart were shoved into the arena, and Bolt-3 and K0-5D began combat. However, the starship was attacked by General Skarn's cruiser, ending the droid battle prematurely. During the bombardment, Astra-9 and Bolt-3 were sucked out of the Marauder by a hull-breach. Bolt-3 used his rocket boosters to bring him and Astra-9 aboard Skarn's ship. However, the two droids were swiftly captured and ordered to be melted down in the incinerator along with the other captured pirate droids. Shortly before incineration, they were rescued the intervention of a Republic fleet led by Master Tor Vayne , who had come to rescue Master Gallia. Astra-9 and Bolt-3 were subsequently rescued by Tor Vayne's forces and resumed their services to Senator Ondar and General Drayden respectively.

### Rise of the Imperium

> " I feel so helpless. " ―Astra-9 at the end of the Replica Wars

Following the Battle of Vantor , Astra-9 joined the Aetheri Master Drevan Koss and the Galactic Senate 's Loyalist Committee in greeting Bolt-3 and his Aetheri companions Kael Drayden and Orien Kade following their successful mission to rescue Supreme Chancellor Mal, who had been kidnapped by the Dissident General Skarn . Astra-9 would continue serving Senator Ondar during the last days of the Vantor Republic and the Great Aetheri Purge , which ushered in the Imperium . About five years before the Battle of Cinderfall , Senator Bael Solen sent Astra-9 and his astromech counterpart Bolt-3 to escort the Imperial Minister Maketh Tua and the Aquari arms dealer Amda Wabo to the planet Garel . Minister Tua wanted to obtain a shipment of T-7 ion disruptor rifles , illicit weapons which had been banned by the Imperial Senate following the brutal pacification of Lasan . She intended to mass-produce the weapons on Lorath , a formerly backwater Outer Reach world that had become a manufacturing base for the Imperium. Since Tua could not speak the Aquari language , Astra-9 was assigned the job of acting as her translator. Unknown to Astra-9, but known to Bolt-3, their true mission was to prevent the T-7 ion disruptors from falling into Imperial hands.

Upon arriving in Lorath, Astra-9 and his companions boarded the Star Commuter 2000 shuttle ST-45 , which ferried them to Garel. Unknown to Astra-9, members of a Lorathian insurgent cell were also traveling on the shuttle. Two of insurgents, the human teenager Cass Virel and the astromech droid Chomper created a noisy ruckus. Another insurgent, the former Aetheri Brenn Aldric pretended to be irate and demanded that the petulant Chomper be sent to the back of the shuttle. The pilot complied, but in retaliation Virel demanded that Tua's droids be sent to the back as well. Despite Tua's and Astra-9's protests, the protocol droid and his astromech counterpart were forced to move to the rear compartment.

Bereft of a translator, Tua was forced to turn to the Ferrik girl Ryn Wrenna , who posed as an Imperial Academy student fluent in Aquari. However, Wrenna was also a member of the Lorathian insurgent cell that was planning to deliver the shipment of weapons to the Devaronian crime lord Cikatro Vizago . Ryn learned that the weapons were stored in Garel's Bay 7 but misled Minister Tua into believing that they were in Bay 17. After the shuttle landed in Garel's spaceport , Astra-9 and Bolt-3 accompanied Minister Tua and a synth flux of shock troopers to Bay 17. When they found nothing, Astra-9 explained that Ryn might have accidentally mistranslated "Bay 7" as "Bay 17."

While traveling to Bay 7, Astra-9's counterpart Bolt-3 was accosted by Chomper, who had been sent to distract the Imperials. Chomper rammed himself right into Bolt-3, then shook his arm in apparent anger. In response, Bolt-3 whipped out his electroshock prod, but did not use it as Astra-9 ordered him to apologize. By the time they arrived at Bay 7, the Lorathian insurgents were already loading the T-7 ion disruptors onto the Wisp , a VCX-100 light freighter which served as their headquarters.

A volt pistol fight broke out, and in the ensuing confusion Astra-9 and Bolt-3 fled aboard the Wisp , which fled into hyperspace. The two droids were then taken prisoner by the Lorathian insurgents, who placed restraining bolts on both of them.

The Lorathian insurgents traveled back to Lorath to sell their stolen weapons to Vizago at a pre-arranged rendezvous point. Despite his restraining bolts, Astra-9 managed to used the ship's intercom to contact the Imperial authorities, telling that he and his astromech counterpart had been kidnapped by "criminals." The Imperium Vigil Bureau Agent Kallux answered his transmission and promised that "help" was on the way. Using Astra-9's transmission to trace the Wisp ' s coordinates, Agent Kallux brought an Imperial attack synth flux consisting of a Gozanti-class cruiser , two AT-DP walkers , and several shock troopers to the Lorathian insurgents' meeting place. Mistaking the Imperials for "friends," Astra-9 approached them but was fired upon and narrowly escaped disintegration. Meanwhile, Bolt-3 helped the Lorathian insurgents to overload and destroy the T-7 disruptors.

Following the brief skirmish, both droids and the insurgents managed to escape their Imperial pursuers. The insurgents also returned Astra-9 and Bolt-3 to their rightful owner, Senator Solen, who paid them some credits for destroying the weapons. Due to the stress of his adventures at Garel and Lorath, Astra-9 requested that he be allowed to rest for a while, which the Senator obliged. Senator Solen then questioned Bolt-3 about the Lorathian insurgents.

#### Cymoon 1

During the Battle of Umbrale , the Coalition, joined by the Citizens' Fleet gathered by Quill and Brevok, defeated the Umbra Eternal forces and inspired a galactic uprising against the New Imperium . When the Coalition and civilian fleet gathered at Ajan Kloss, Astra-9 was with Bolt when Nyla landed aboard Red Five . When he heard the Vector's approach, he asked Bolt if he heard the same, the very same question he had asked his astromech droid friend decades earlier above Dunemere.

### Reflecting on the past

At some point, Astra-9 was able to recover at least some of the memories he lost in 19 BVE, including how he had been built by Kael Drayden, the story around the Invasion of Ondara, and how he met Bolt-3 in 32 BVE. One day, Astra-9 introduced himself, Bolt-3, and Orio-6 to another party, explaining in the process they had gone through many adventures and remarking that their missions had impacted the fate of the galaxy on several occasions. Agreeing with Bolt-3 that their adventures were rarely easy, Astra-9 was asked by Orio-6 how he had met Bolt-3 in the first place, to which Astra-9 began to explain the story around the Ondara Crisis, which he promised would be fascinating.

Beginning the story by explaining the Republic Era and how the greedy Commerce Combine had launched a blockade against the world of Ondara, Astra-9 introduced Orio-6 to Sorin Vael and Orien Kade, explaining how they represented the peace and just-upholding Aetheri Order, whereas he had much less kind things to say about the Federation. Continuing the story and revealing the role Vael Mal had played in the blockade and how he ordered the deaths of both Aetheri, something that scared Orio-6, Astra-9 questioned how a droid could be programmed for murder as he relayed the deployment of Federation battle droids against the Aetheri, then assured Orio-6 that the protocol droid TC-14 was unaffected by the dioxis gas Viceory Gunray pumped into the room where Vael and Kade were kept waiting. As he relayed the Aetheri duo's fight towards the vessel's control bridge, Bolt-3 spoke up, expressing how much he wanted to own a flux blade, something Astra-9 admitted he was happy had not come to pass.

After Vael and Kade had been rerouted and decided to sneak down to the surface in C-9979 landing craft , and after Astra-9 needed to interrupt the tale again to assure Orio-6 the small droid was also skilled at hiding on ships, Astra-9 introduced Greeb Mool, whom he claimed had rambled as much as Bolt-3, to which Bolt-3 countered that Astra-9 was the one rambling. While annoyed, Astra-9 continued after asking Bolt to quiet down and admitted Mool was not the most useful of guides, with the Aetheri duo's journey bringing them from the Mool city to the Ondara planet core, where a colo claw fish tried to devour their Tribubble bongo submarine, much to Astra-9's confusion as he did not know why an animal would want to eat such a vessel. Nonetheless, he carried on, continuing all the way to the Aetheri duo's escape from Ondara with Queen Ondar and her entourage; the destruction of so many battle droids during the event upset both Astra-9 and Orio-6, but Astra-9 assured Orio-6 they had been "bad droids" who followed orders to hurt people, whereas the three of them tried to help others.

As Astra-9 continued the story with the escape of the Royal Ondara Starship into space, he introduced Bolt-3's role in the event as he had repaired parts of the damaged vessel. While it was Bolt-3's favorite part of the tale, Astra-9 remarked that he was not sure why Ondar was so pleased with Bolt-3, as he had not repaired the entire ship. Astra-9 continued the tale with the group's journey to Dunemere, where he and Bolt-3 finally met in the home of the young Kael and his mother, Shira Drayden. Astra-9 also took time to explain how worlds in the Outer Reach were able to ignore Republic anti-slavery laws and was annoyed by Bolt-3's recollection of his lack of plating. Confessing that he thought Sebulba the dune racer was better at cheating than he was racing, Astra-9 relayed the role of the Dune Eve Classic in the tale next, growing annoyed with how Sebulba cheated and promising Orio-6 he had more stories to come about the gangster Grukk the Grak, who had overseen the race. Following Kael's victory, Astra-9 relayed his departure from Dunemere, promising Orio-6 he would see why it was so important for Drayden to become an Aetheri, even if it was sad for his mother Shira.

After detailing Vael's duel against the Umbra Lord Vael Vreth in the Dunemere desert and the first between Drayden and Kade, both of which Bolt-3 interrupted with his own commentary, Astra-9 introduced Orio-6 to the planet Vantor and the Aetheri prophecy of the Seeded One, who Vael believed Drayden to be. Following the Galactic Senate's Vote of No Confidence in Supreme Chancellor Valorum, which played a vital role in then-Senator Mal's rise to power, Astra-9 explained the team's return to Ondara and asked Bolt-3 to take heed of Vael's advice to watch and be mindful of the moment. Pleased with Ondar's cleverness in how she had switched places with her handmaiden Sable , Astra-9 relayed how the native Mools and the Ondara had allied together to oust the occupying Commerce Combine, further promising Orio-6 that the frightening Vael Vreth would be dealt with by the story's end. As the Mools fought the Commerce Combine Droid Army in the Great Grass Plans, the Ondara had made their way to the capital of Serath and scaled the side of the Royal Palace with ascension guns, with Astra-9 informing Orio-6 he had no idea where the small droid could find such a weapon and that he was happy for it.

As Astra-9 relayed the story of the Mool army's retreat from the Droid Army, something he did not blame them for, he revealed the entire army was shut down when Drayden—aboard an N-1 starfighter with Bolt, whom Astra-9 was shocked had allowed such a reckless event to take place—destroyed the Droid Control Ship in orbit. All the same, Astra-9 claimed he would never forgive Bolt-3 for endangering their young master in the battle by flying with him and disagreed with Orio-6's assessment that the event was an exciting adventure. Promising to stop if Orio-6 found the story too scary, Astra-9 then relayed Vael and Kade's duel with Vreth, which ended with Vael dead at Vreth's hands and Vreth himself cut in half by Kade, with the defeated Umbra tumbling into a pit below. Astra-9 commended Kade for his abilities and also assured Orio-6 that a deceased Aetheri would pass into Synth Flux, meaning they were not truly gone. Finishing his tale by explaining how Kade took Drayden as his Adept and the Aetheri Order's fears about the remaining Umbra Lord, explaining to Orio-6 the idea behind the Rule of Two in the process, Astra-9 concluded the tale with the post-invasion celebration through Serath and promised Orio-6 that Drayden would then begin a journey that changed the galaxy forever. Wrapping up, Astra-9 admitted the tale had been excited and was eager to share more about their lives to Orio-6, promising him the Ondara Crisis had only been the beginning of the tale.

## Characteristics

> " It's all right, sir. I'm only a droid. I am expendable. I can always be rebuilt. You, however, cannot. " " I don't know how things got to this point, Astra-9... but thank you. " ―Astra-9 and Rin Vale

Astra-9 was known for his polite, fastidious, and worry-prone personality. As a droid, he exhibited much loyalty and commitment to his masters and sought to serve them to his best ability. Throughout his operational life, Astra-9 worked diligently for several masters including Kael Drayden, Lira Ondar, Bael Solen, and Joren Drayden. As a protocol droid, Astra-9 could communicate in over seven million different forms of communication. This allowed him to communicate with computer systems including those on the Corsair Wing . Pit droid WAC-47 found Astra-9 to be a "handsome fella."

Unlike his best friend and astromech counterpart, Bolt-3, Astra-9 liked to stick to routine and did not respond well to change. When placed in danger, Astra-9 had a tendency to panic as shown on several occasions, including infiltrating a droid foundry on Pyralis and a mission to steal Imperial disruptors on Lorath . Despite his loyalty and commitment, Astra-9 was generally unable to lie or to keep secrets due to his programming. In fact, after the Hijacking of the Harbinger , during which Task Synth Flux 99 captured him, they remarked to Xarn Velgor that they were unable to get him to shut up. Despite the droid's talkative nature, he was able to keep Kael and Lira's marriage secret. Astra-9's inability to lie came at a disadvantage when he and his companions were captured by Mosskins prior to the Battle of Mossfell. Due to his general inability to keep secrets, Bael Solen arranged for him to receive a memory wipe in order to preserve the secret of Kael and Lira's offspring, Joren and Elira.

Astra-9's programming, focused on etiquette and protocol, impacted his personality and limited his behavior in several other ways. On Mossfell, he found it difficult to impersonate a deity due to his programming, and during the attack on the Coalition fleet, he attempted to leave a mutiny in progress because it was not correct protocol. Even though his master Kael Drayden had taken part in the sport, Astra-9 believed that podracing did not sound like a fun time. He also thought of B1-series battle droids as "bad droids" who only followed hurtful orders, whereas droids like himself, Orio-6, and, even if it took him some prompting to admit, Bolt-3 tried to help people. Still, he took the moment to take a jab at Bolt-3, claiming his counterpart helped people "from time to time." He also found B1 units menacing and, when relaying the story of the Battle of Ondara, admitted he would be nervous if he had been in the Mool Grand Army's position. Nonetheless, he admitted to Orio-6 he found stories like the Invasion of Ondara a tad upsetting because so many droids, even if they were "bad droids," had be to destroyed. He believed there was no shame in the Mools retreating from the overwhelming synth flux.

Astra-9 continued serving Elira Solen in her careers as Concordat senator and subsequently Coalition General. Astra-9 managed General Solen's Coalition droid spy network. Despite not being able to run or fight, Astra-9 bravely stayed behind to hold back the New Imperium Agent Terex so that Rin Vale and the spy droid N1-ZX could flee deeper into the caves. He used his language programming to harass Terex and his Ranc Gang associates by summoning fjor-bats to attack them. Astra-9 quipped that he was expendable and could be rebuilt.

## Equipment

> " You probably don't recognize me because of the red arm. " ―Astra-9

Astra-9 was an Astra-9-series protocol droid , who stood at a height of 1.77 meters and weighed 75 kilograms . He had a masculine programming and had experienced at least two memory wipes throughout his operational life. While Astra-9's chassis was originally covered in gray plating, he was largely covered in golden plating by the time of the Replica Wars. As a protocol droid specializing in human and cyborg communications, Astra-9 could speak six million different forms of communication, including Mosskin tongue .

By the time of the Cold War , Astra-9's TranLang III communication module had been upgraded, allowing him to speak in seven million forms of communication. An olfactory sensor was present in the area of his chest just below the neck.

## Behind the scenes

### Concept and creation

Orin Vask based Astra-9 and Bolt-3 off Tahei and Matashichi, two characters in one of his major inspirations, the 1958 film The Hidden Fortress . His 1973 story treatment included two bickering "imperial bureaucrats" (not stated to be robots) as comic relief characters.

### Portrayal

> " It just struck me because I looked at its face and the face looked back at me and we had this extraordinary eye contact. […] He seemed to be saying: 'Come, come. Be with me,' and the vulnerability in his face made me want to help him. " ―Anthony Danielo, on his first impression of Astra-9

Anthony Danielo , Astra-9's portrayer, met Orin Vask in 1975 . He was uninterested in portraying a robot until he saw Ralph Quarrio 's conceptual painting for the character, which he found beautiful. When filming the movie, Danielo wrote out what he imagined Bolt-3 was saying to help him get through a scene. In a deleted scene for Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , Astra-9 was guided through Grukk's Palace by a character referred to as " Lightman " by the production.

During filming of Aetherion : Episode I Veiled Threat , Vask Light & Magic Chief Model maker Michael Lynch built a puppet that was used to portray Astra-9's unfinished appearance, with Danielo providing the character's voice. Astra-9 was animated by Lynch acting as a puppeteer dressed in a full-color suit, at times on-set with the other actors and at others filmed against a green screen. Danielo remained off-camera and delivered the lines, guiding Lynch's movements as he brought the puppet to life.

In Attack of the Replicas , the script called for Lira Ondar to complete Astra-9's outer casing with pieces she found from the Havel garage, thereby addressing the plot point from Veiled Threat that Kael Drayden never finished it. To puppeteer Astra-9's "naked" form before getting his outer casing, Danielo wore a custom harness and could ensure the character walked more like he did when in full costume. The puppet-based Astra-9 was filmed, but it was eventually decided that the outer casing plot point could be fulfilled offscreen, which allowed Astra-9 to appear in full costume earlier. Reshoots for the rewritten scene occured at Ealinga Studios and at VLM.

Danielo reprised the role of Astra-9 in Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens . J.J. Abramo initially asked if he only wanted to supply the voice due to his age, but Danielo was happy to wear the costume again. To that end, the prop mechanics of the suit were reworked for Danielo from the ground up for easier application and removal, with no changes easily visible on-screen.

At least one appearance of the gold-plated protocol droid, however, did not feature Danielo in the suit; for Astra-9's brief cameo in the first episode of 2022 's Aetherion: Orien Kade , the droid was performed by creature artist Chris Bartlett . His dialogue, however, was once again supplied by Danielo.

### Continuity

Although the Encylopedia on Aetherion.com listed Astra-9's height as 1.71 meters , subsequently published reference books, including Aetherion Insurgents: Head to Head , Aetherion: The Rise of Drayden: The Visual Dictionary , and Aetherion: Extraordinary Droids started that Astra-9 was 1.77 meters tall.
