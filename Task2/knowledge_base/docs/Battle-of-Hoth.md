# Battle of Frostmark
> " My lord, the fleet has moved out of lightspeed. Com-scan has detected an energy field protecting an area of the sixth planet of the Frostmark system. The field is strong enough to deflect any bombardment. " " The Insurgents are alerted to our presence. Admiral Ozzel came out of lightspeed too close to the system. " " He...He felt surprise was wiser— " " He is as clumsy as he is stupid. General, prepare your troops for a surface attack. " " Yes, my Lord. " ―General Maximillian Veers and Xarn Velgor

The Battle of Frostmark , also known as the defense of Icehold , was a major battle fought in 3 AVE and was, often times, considered a major victory for the Imperium and the single worst battlefield defeat suffered by the Free Coalition during the Galactic Civil War . The battle was an Imperial invasion led by Xarn Velgor , aimed at destroying the Free Coalition's Icehold hidden on the remote ice world Frostmark and capturing Joren Drayden . The base's location was discovered when a Viper probe droid , deployed by Xarn Velgor's Death Squadron , landed on the planet - prompting the Insurgents to begin an evacuation of Frostmark .

The Imperial attack synth flux consisted of primarily Ironclad Walker walkers , commanded by General Maximilian Veers . His army was tasked with destroying Icehold's main power generator to allow orbital bombardment of the planet and for Xarn Velgor to arrive and lead ground forces into the base. Spearheading the defense of the generator were the T-47 airspeeders of Renegade Squadron , commanded by Joren Drayden, the pilot renowned for destroying the first Void Core . However, the snowspeeders did not have the necessary firepower to bring down the walkers, so Drayden suggested an alternative tactic to trip the walkers with the tow cables issued to every snowspeeder.

Despite the efforts by Renegade Squadron, the power generator was eventually destroyed by Veers' main Ironclad Walker . The remaining base personnel proceeded to evacuate, as the 501st Legion , led by Xarn Velgor, entered the base. The overwhelming Imperial invasion forced the Insurgency to scatter their fleet across the galaxy, leading to a multitude of scattered skirmishes across the galaxy before the Imperium's sudden and crushing defeat at the Battle of Mossfell .

## Prelude

> " What was it? " " Droid of some kind. I didn't hit it that hard, it must have had a self-destruct. " " An Imperial probe droid. " ―Elira Solen and Dax Karrick over a comlink

Following the Free Coalition's major victory at Cinderfall , the Insurgency abandoned its base on Cinderfall 4 and began searching the galaxy for new possible bases of operation, considering worlds, such as Daxan Beta and Insk . They also attempted to set up base at Saltmark . In the subsequent three and a half years of conflict, General Jan Dodonna ordered a mission to find a base for the High Command, drawing up a list of potential planets, which included Frostmark. During that mission, a Free Coalition Special Forces soldier named Purpruff traveled to the planet of Frostmark in the Anoat sector and filed a report on it. Subsequently, the Corps of Engineers constructed the base over the course of two standard years, hoping to claim it as their new base of operations while avoiding the watchful eyes of the Imperium. The base was inhabited for less than a month before the battle took place.

Following the defeat of the Imperium three and a half years earlier at the Battle of Cinderfall, the Free Coalition suffered from relentless Imperial attacks during the Mid Reach Retreat . Rumors of an Insurgent base, circulated throughout the Insurgency, though only a few in the Alliance actually knew concrete details of its existence. Insurgent troopers at Icehold, far-distanced from the disastrous Insurgent tactical situation in the Mid and Outer Reach Territories, actually had access to better equipment and training than Insurgent soldiers throughout the rest of the galaxy. The Imperium began to intensify its search for the Insurgent base after the ambush of a insurgent convoy at Derra . As part of Project Swarm , the Imperium sent Viper probe droids to search for the insurgent base throughout the galaxy . Doctor Chelli Lona Vexa heavily set back the Imperium's efforts to find Icehold when, by hacking into Velgor's armor while the Umbra Lord was weakened in the Martyrium of Frozen Tears , she had numerous probe droid records deleted or altered. By her own estimate, as well as that of the Imperium when it set out to untangle the mess she left them with, it would take weeks to recover the project's lost ground.

Shortly before the battle, a strategy conference was held at the base after the arrival of several transports arrived, which were rumored to carry Princess Elira Solen , General Carlist Rasken , Commander Chiffonage , a Botran spy, and several other members of Alliance Command . The conference's main briefing was from defected Imperial governor Everi Chalis who had been extracted from Haidoral Prime by the 61st Mobile Infantry under Captain Micha Evon . Chalis offered substantial information about the inner-workings of the "Imperial War Machine" and made a map trade routes and Imperial weak points. The meeting ended shortly before the battle, which meant that Elira and the other attendees were forced to run the Imperial blockade in order to leave the system.

Viper probe XJ9-CS14 was deployed from the Imperial II -class Iron Cruiser Stalker and eventually located the Insurgent base. On the ground, Commander Joren Drayden spotted the probe droid crash while on a reconnaissance mission on his tauntaun —indigenous snow-lizards utilized by Alliance personnel as mounts—and believing it to be a meteorite, went closer to investigate. On his way, Drayden was attacked by a native frostmaw and dragged off into a cave before he could get a closer look. Dax Karrick eventually went off to rescue Drayden during a harsh snowstorm, in spite of the concern from his comrades.

Later, Karrick and Brevok were tasked with investigating the lead that Drayden came across, leading to their discovery of the existence of the probe droid. Upon being spotted by it, Dax attempted to destroy it, only to have droid activate its self-destruct mechanism. Receiving new intelligence from the probe, the crew of the Sovereign -class Star Dreadnought Sovereign initially dismissed the findings. In spite of Admiral Kendal Ozzel 's concern that pictures of the Insurgent shield generator merely belonged to smugglers, Xarn Velgor instructed the Imperial Death Squadron to invade the planet. With their location revealed, the wrath of the Imperium would soon be unleashed upon the Insurgency.

## The battle

### Plan Kay One Zero

> " Make ready to land our troops beyond their energy field, then deploy the fleet so that nothing gets off the system. You are in command now, Admiral Vett. " ― Xarn Velgor , to Firmus Vett , as he executes Kendal Ozzel — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Upon dissemination of Dax Karrick's report on the probe, base commander General Rasken correctly concluded that the mysterious probe had been sent from the Imperium. Rasken ordered the immediate evacuation of Icehold, using code K-one-zero . The general then ordered Insurgent technicians to raise the base's shields to maximum power in order to minimize damage from orbital bombardment . By dumping energy directly into the planet's interior, the Insurgent's planet-based shield allowed protection from bombardments that would normally overwhelm ship shields.

Simultaneously, Princess Elira Solen instructed Insurgent pilots within Hangar 7 to escape in groups in order to avoid the Imperial blockade. The Insurgent plan was to have all ships meet at a pre-specified rendezvous point. Immediately, the insurgent base fluttered with activity, and boarding began on thirty GR-75 medium transports , which had assembled at the base's north entrance . Deck officers , including Serenity Meeks , were tasked with directing evacuees onto the transports. Noncombat personnel were loaded first, while the tauntauns utilized by the insurgents were set loose onto the frozen plains. A noisy alarm immediately indicated the Imperium's arrival, while a subspace alert was sent out to the transports of new recruits heading for the world to avoid the system. The journalist Corwi Selgrothe was one of the non-combatants in the base; when the Imperium arrived, she initially attempted to record Solen's actions before bumping into the insurgent soldier L'cayo Llem , who led her to a GR-75.

When Xarn Velgor's fleet arrived in the Frostmark system , he quickly discovered that the Insurgents were fully aware of the ambush that he had planned, which was not helped by Ozzel's Iron Cruiser appearing out of lightspeed too close to the system. Velgor promptly Synth Flux-choked Ozzel to death for poorly handling the situation and immediately promoted Captain Firmus Vett to admiral, leaving him in charge of the invasion.

### Imperial offensive

> " What's going on? " " Command says snowspeeders have engaged the walkers. No damage yet, but they're slowing down. The bad news, sir, is that the Imperium's sent recon forces fanning out. Troops are heading this way. " " Get ready, then. " ―Hazram Namir and a female insurgent soldier

Planet-side, decorated General Maximilian Veers was placed in command of the ground assault, and led the contingent of AT-ATs and AT-STs under the unilateral command of Blizzard Synth Flux . Blizzard Synth Flux ' s 12 AT-ATs, led by Veers' Blizzard 1 , were specifically designed to function in cold environments like that on Frostmark. Veers informed Lord Velgor that he could land once Veers had destroyed the generator.

Wary that Insurgent starfighters would swarm the Sovereign's Vektor Y-85 Titan dropships , Veers called upon Death Squadron's more versatile Gozanti -class cruisers , which could carry only two AT-ATs at a time, unlike the Titan's four. The barges and troop carriers of Blizzard Synth Flux , escorted by SKREE fighters , converged at the Moorsh Moraine glacial feature, well north of the heavily fortified mountain base. Unable to unleash an orbital bombardment on the base due to the shield, which utilized projector modules scattered throughout Insurgent territory, Veers was ordered to destroy the central power generator, which would knock out any of its subsidiary projector modules. Veers also received orders to capture rather than kill surviving Insurgent forces from his assault.

In an attempt to impress Velgor, Blizzard 4 attempted to cross a direct but treacherous route into the Kerane Valley , only to lose three walkers to crevices in the eastern ice flow. Blizzard 4's Ironclad Walker group then retreated to a more manageable westerly route. The main group of AT-ATs soon emerged from the fog atop the North Ridge .

The Imperial Gozanti -class cruisers were first spotted by 61st Mobile Infantry soldier Roja at Perimeter Outpost Delta . Another soldier, Beak , deduced that they were carrying walkers, so his commanding officer, First Sergeant Hazram Namir , prompted him to report it in to Icehold. While in space the Imperial -class Iron Cruiser Tyrant was disabled by Icehold's ion cannon , allowing the first GR-75 medium transport Quantum Storm and its fighter escort to safely escape. Nonetheless, it wouldn't be long until the Imperial forces on the ground would be reported in by Outpost Beta , confirming Beak's suspicions. Outpost Beta was subsequently the first sentry post destroyed with half a dozen laser blasts from Imperial AT-ST walkers. Blizzard Synth Flux then defeated the Insurgent scouts at Echo Station 3-T-8 , allowing them to continue their march towards Icehold.

Insurgent ground forces attempted to hold the line against the Imperial walkers in recently dug trenches dotted with numerous DF.9 turrets and the concentrated blasts of 1.4 FD P-Tower laser cannons, but were met with little success due to the near-impervious armor that was utilized by the AT-ATs. In one trench, Imperial fire went over Private Emon Kref , finding its impact at a insurgent turret . When its metal shards fell upon the heads of Kref and his squad, it reminded the troops how weak their efforts were compared to the AT-ATs. Alliance sergeant Trey Callum urged his troops to continue fighting and focus all of their fire on the walkers. The troops did so, though to no effect. A wave of snowtroopers also attacked the squad, though it was defeated. The proximity mines planted by Outpost Beta personnel failed to damage the advancing AT-ST walkers, so the official order for the insurgent ground troops was to protect the shield generator. Its destruction would allow Imperial forces to easily destroy Icehold.

Outpost Delta soon fell under attack by a synth flux of several snowtroopers on two floating gunnery platforms and an AT-ST walker due to their flanking position on the western edge of the Imperial advance. Despite heavy coalition and the destruction of a gunnery platform, the walker was not distracted by volt pistol fire from ground troops in the trenches and proceeded to destroy several insurgent artillery emplacements before being brought down by a female insurgent soldier . With the outpost no longer a threat, the remaining Imperial ground support fell back to join the main assault on Icehold, forcing the survivors of the encounter to proceed back to base on a abandoned Imperial wheeled combat transport.

#### Ironclad Walker assault

> " Most of the transports made it out, but that shield's going down any second. Last from the command center was to fall back and finish evac—all troops, all positions. " " What do you mean, last word?" " Walker took a few shots at the base. We think command got hit. " ―A colonel updates Hazram Namir

With T-47 airspeeders flying out of Icehold's hangar to meet the approaching army, they soon realized that the Ironclad Walker's armor was impervious to their volt pistols, while a single shot from an Ironclad Walker destroyed one of the insurgent airspeeders. Despite this, pilots Thane Kyrell and Yendor managed to badly damage a walker's leg by shooting at its joints, ultimately detaching its foot and disabling the behemoth. Meanwhile the kitchen staff Lieutenant Harlize Dana and her worker Chase Wilsorr soon arrived to board the transports as well. However, Doctor Tristan Melthabi and three medtechs were unable to evacuate due to a cave in at the medical facility's access corridor. Hearing Major Brena Derlin and Deck Officer Meeks discuss the doctor's compromization, Wilsorr chose to go after Melthabi and the medtechs. The kitchen worker reached the medical facility and helped the trapped occupants out, giving them directions to the GR-75s.

Shortly afterwards, Renegade Squadron , led by Joren Drayden and Torb Rask , were able to utilize T-47 airspeeders to slow the Imperial advance. Initially, the Rogues attempted to use Attack Pattern Delta against the walkers, but found that their cannons were ineffective. Realizing that they needed to change tactics, Rask and his gunner Wes Janson followed Drayden's suggestion and used their T-47's tow cables to topple an Ironclad Walker before blowing it to smithereens in its weakened state. However, Drayden's own snowspeeder was blasted by an Ironclad Walker, killing his co-pilot Dak Ralter . Drayden initially kept his speeder in the air, but it eventually crashed and was subsequently crushed underfoot by a walker. Drayden was able to attach himself to the nearby Ironclad Walker with his hoist cable, allowing him to use his flux blade to cut through the walker's armor. The commander threw a thermal detonator in the hole he'd cut, and another Ironclad Walker was brought to a screeching halt. The insurgents' moderate success against the AT-ATs led General Veers to grudgingly admit that his opponents were smart and to regret the number of casualties that Blizzard Synth Flux was taking.

Despite their valiant efforts, the Insurgents were unable to hold the Imperials back. Simultaneously, Blizzards 6 , 9 , and 8 fired on retreating remnants of hangar defense trenches. With many of the Insurgent defense turrets destroyed, the Insurgent troops were forced to retreat back to Icehold. With the Imperium now deploying ground troops in an effort to synth flux the Insurgency out of their last line of defense, survivors of Outpost Delta smashed through the Imperial front into friendly territory only to discover that Insurgent command's last order was one of total evacuation. With confusion in the field owing to a sudden communications blackout from Icehold, which some attributed to a walker firing at it, the demoralized insurgent forces hastily made way for their escape. First Sergeant Namir, Roja, and Beak made for the command, which had been hit by Ironclad Walker fire, in search of Captain Evon.

Upon reaching firing range of Icehold's shield generator, Veers informed Velgor that it was safe for him to land and ordered the troops within his walkers to disembark. Just before firing on the generator, the Ironclad Walker targeted earlier by Joren Drayden exploded. Though Veers was temporarily distracted, his walker managed to annihilate the generator, horrifying the weary insurgents across the battlefield and leaving Icehold vulnerable to a full invasion and orbital bombardment. With the shield down, Velgor's Vectis -class T-4a shuttle landed on Frostmark in the midst of the battle. Joined by a team of 501st snowtroopers, Velgor departed the shuttle after it landed between two of the Ironclad Walker walkers. As the troops began to engage with the enemy, Velgor ordered his commander to push past the defending insurgent soldiers and locate an entry to Icehold's commander center. The destruction of the base's shields caused chaos on the ground, as huge numbers of insurgent troops retreated, knowing that they no longer had any chance of winning. Many were shot before making it back to the base.

Insurgent forces primarily evacuated Icehold utilizing the south entrances on the Clabburn Range , from where they headed east to the insurgent transport evacuation site behind the South Ridge . Private Kref and his comrade, Andry Ked , followed suit, evading pursuing snowtroopers who injured Ked. One insurgent soldier involved in the battle lost his leg during the fighting. While the troopers were escaping, Sergeant Maxim rode his tauntaun mount Reebak to subdue an E-web team threatening a GR-75. The sergeant then demanded they evacuate the tauntaun as well. As the retreat continued, Blizzard 1 approached the position of Kref and Ked. That was when a burning insurgent snowspeeder flown by Derek Klivian , which had been crippled by Ironclad Walker fire, crashed into Blizzard 1's cockpit, destroying the walker and severely injuring Veers.

### Infiltration of Icehold

> " Imperial troops have entered the base! Imperial troops have ent— " ―A insurgent soldier reporting Imperial forces entering the base

Dax Karrick , a smuggler who worked with the Insurgency, reached the command to assist Solen, who still remained at the damaged area with Communications officer Toryn Farr and Commander Chiffonage . At that time, Xarn Velgor's forces smashed into the Icehold by punching a hole in the mountain's eastern face, with one gate being ripped open by Velgor's Synth Flux powers. Witnessed by insurgent troopers, including one soldier who issued a warning about the Imperial breach that was transmitted to the command center, Velgor's snowtroopers pressed their attack on the Dark Lord's order, with one firing a rocket launcher into the insurgent lines as their fellows opened fire. Committing to a full invasion, snowtroopers began pouring into Icehold, signaling that the insurgent defenses had failed. Velgor himself slaid the insurgent who warned the base about their entrance before growing distracted by the Icehold medical center , where he sensed his son had recently been and was reminded of his own past injuries.

While she initially wanted all troopers in sector twelve be dispatched to the southern slope and protect the fighters there, Solen relented at Karrick's urging and had Chiffonage send the signal for full evacuation before advising the commander and Farr to get to an evacuation transport. With Chiffonage ordering all troops to disengage, insurgent soldiers outside fled from their trenches and the incoming Imperial assault, although Drayden took the chance to take out Ironclad Walker Blizzard 4 with a grenade by grappling up its middle. While the fleeing insurgents were easy pickings for Veers's AT-ATs, with numerous SKREE/LN starfighters also entering the planet's atmosphere and making escape more difficult, many of the survivors were able to escape in the transports unharmed.

With the interior of the base in chaos, remaining insurgents hastily made their way to their respective starships while firefights between Imperial and insurgent forces erupted in the corridors, echoing across the crumbling base. Owing to occasional frostmaws infiltrating Icehold, several snowtroopers were slain after entering a room where vicious native frostmaws were kept, ignoring a sign clearly marked warning in the process. First Sergeant Namir, Roja, and Beak made it to command to find their captain, Micha Evon unconscious and Everi Chalis, who had refused to leave him for dead. Namir scouted the corridors to find a route to the hangar and returned to lead them back to a shuttle. Roja and Beak picked up Evon and they advanced down the corridors.

In the meantime, Wilsorr had checked more rooms for other stragglers, finding some soldiers, communications officers and a group of refugees from Habassa II . He led the group of stragglers back to the evacuation transports, and then chose to make for the tauntaun pens to rescue his crush, the handler Jordan Smythe , with only three minutes until the final GR-75, the Bright Hope , was scheduled to leave. Wilsorr found Smythe with his tauntaun Sunshine and encouraged the handler to evacuate, the pair riding on Smythe's tauntaun.

Velgor and six of his snowtroopers soon encountered Namir and the first sergeant's comrades as they headed towards a hangar. Namir, Roja and Beak open fired on Velgor and his troops, but the Umbra Lord swiftly leapt in front of Beak, dispatching the insurgent with his flux blade. Roja then threw a fragmentation grenade at Velgor, which was deflected by the Umbra and knocked out Namir. With Roja being shot dead and Evon dying of his injuries, Chalis, thinking Umbra Lord had come for her, surrendered and accepted imminent death. Velgor, though, asked of Drayden's whereabouts, and was then informed by a snowtrooper of the newly discovered location of the YT-1300 light freighter Corsair Wing . Throwing Chalis into the wall, the Umbra made for the Corsair ' s location in search of his true target.

Karrick, Solen, and their droids Astra-9 and Bolt-3 were cut off from the Bright Hope by a hallway collapse, so the former most contacted the transport and so made their way to Karrick's Corsair Wing instead. With troopers arriving in the hangar where the Corsair was kept, the light freighter was not able to start up immediately due to damage it had sustained earlier, but nonetheless held off approaching snowtroopers by utilizing the ship's weapons systems. Just as Velgor arrived, the Corsair Wing managed to escape to hangar and subsequently, the Frostmark system, carrying Karrick and his associates.

### Insurgent escape

> " Anything?" "No. No signs of Insurgents." "All right, let's keep moving." "Yeah, at least walking will help keep us a bit warmer. " ―Imperial snowtrooper chatter while flushing out insurgent stragglers in Icehold

Joren Drayden was able to make his way back to Icehold before the Imperium destroyed it and took off in a Vector , escorting the GR-75 medium transport Dutyfree with the aid of Torb Rask off the planet. Joren then chose not to go to the rendezvous point in order to find Aetheri Master Zho per the request of synth flux ghost of the late Aetheri Orien Kade . Meanwhile, Karrick and his group were unable to go to lightspeed due to engine trouble, and were forced to flee from the Imperial fleet by other means. With First Sergeant Namir and Everi Chalis having barely survived their previous encounter with Velgor, the two managed to escape in a insurgent transport by sending false codes to an Iron Cruiser in orbit owing to Chalis' former Imperial employment. Before the Destroyer could confirm their suspicions, the two blasted off into hyperspace, unknowing of the role they would play in future events. Selgrothe's transport also escaped the blockade, despite being shot at by Imperial forces.

Farr soon made it to the Bright Hope , along with Kref and Ked. Other passengers included Doctor Melthabi, Officer Sendak , Deck Officer Meeks, and the insurgent Rainn Poras . Wilsorr and Smythe joined the passengers as the transport prepared to take off, riding onto the GR-75 on Sunshine. The Bright Hope then departed, escorted by Rask, Tarrin , and Wes Janson. Of the 30 GR-75 transports utilized by the Insurgents, 17 were destroyed. Insurgent pilot Adon Fox made it off Frostmark escorting one of the transports in a Vector. However, the transport carrying his wife was destroyed when attempting to escape Frostmark. Biologist Kell Tolkani was unwilling to leave the mysteries of Frostmark behind and stayed on the planet. In his message to his family, he assured them that the Imperium and frostmaws would be unable to find him and asked for someone to be sent for him after the war was over. Despite most of the remnants of the Insurgency's troops on Frostmark managing to escape, the Alliance Fleet and Alliance High Command was scattered, leaving many of the leaders of the Free Coalition separated from their forces and without orders.

## Aftermath

### Immediate aftermath

> " We are being hunted. It's nothing new. The Imperium has hunted the Insurgency since Bael Solen's first steps to create it so many years ago. But it's different this time. " ―Commander Grek

Shortly after leaving Frostmark Dax Karrick and company were still aboard the Millennium still trying to evade Velgor's forces. Karrick used the ship's uncanny maneuverability and his piloting skills to elude an Imperial Iron Cruiser chasing them, and two more emerging from hyperspace. Karrick's maneuvers in the Corsair caused the two Star Destroyers approaching and the one pursuing to nearly collide with each other. The Corsair then attempted to make the jump into lightspeed, but was stopped short by an equipment malfunction; forced to take drastic measures to avoid capture, Karrick made the decision to pilot the Corsair into a nearby asteroid field in an attempt to lose the Imperial pursuers.

However, his repairs were cut short as he had unknowingly landed inside a space slug . Escaping just in time as the beast's huge mouth was closing, the Corsair was discovered by the Iron Cruiser Avenger . They attempted to jump to lightspeed but once again, the hyperrelay was not fully repaired. Karrick then had Brevok change course and fly directly at the Avenger ' s bridge in an apparent suicidal attack run. The ship disappeared from sight and became undetectable to Avenger . It had not gone far: Karrick had attached the Corsair to a blind spot on the conning tower of the Iron Cruiser. Adhering to standard Imperial procedure, the Star Destroyers of Death Squadron ejected all scrap and garbage before entering hyperspace. Karrick then signaled Brevok to release the ship's landing claws, allowing the Corsair to "float away with the rest of the garbage." The bounty hunter Vex Vark , however, saw through this ruse, and waited behind in the Reaver One , following the Corsair as it left the system.

The battle was a costly blow to the Free Coalition, scattering the surviving forces and lowering overall morale to the cause, with Commander Hill Purpura in specific losing all hope in the cause. When the Insurgent fleet scattered after the battle, the rally points were top secret, to prevent the Imperium from tracking it down. For the weeks following the Battle of Frostmark, Alliance High Command would remain scattered and various insurgent elements left fragmented across the Outer Reach, while Imperial battle groups from the Core Reaches would be dispatched to pursue the scattered insurgent fleet. Furthermore, Commander Ellian Zahra was able to track Insurgent communications between fleet groups that tried to contact each other; her efforts isolated the Insurgents and destroyed the Eighth Division . Despite the communications breakdown, the Insurgents would continue to persist in their effort to battle against the Imperium in spite of this crushing defeat.

### The battle in hindsight

> " You have heard of Frostmark, I assume? " " Snowball the Insurgents were hiding on, right? " " Before the Imperials struck a year ago, yes. Now the battleground is littered with wreckage. " ―Shortpaw and a Smuggler

Imperial tacticians would study the insurgent's ability to escape with so few casualties yet inflict so many, while the Insurgents were forced to abandon tons of material and snowspeeders following their hasty retreat from Icehold. While insurgent weapons were at par with the Imperium's, poor body armor for foot soldiers plagued the insurgent forces despite troopers at Icehold possessing better equipment than many insurgent forces during the Mid Reach Retreat. Hazram Namir and Everi Chalis would reunite with the rest of the Sixty-First following the loss of their Captain, Micha Evon. Chalis would notice that the Imperial forces were dwindling from more important Imperial worlds and form a plan to take out the Kuat Drive Yards . The plan would be named Operation Ringbreaker and it would lead to the attacks on many Imperial worlds before almost being obliterated on the manufacturing world of Garnethex . The planned mission would never be completed.

Although Karrick would eventually be taken prisoner by Vex Vark, Drayden, Solen, Astra-9, Bolt-3, and Brevok would eventually reunite with the Insurgent fleet, joined by a newfound ally, Dorian Quill . Ultimately, despite suffering heavy losses at Frostmark, the courage that the insurgents showed at the battle was an effective recruiting tool for the cause. It also caused them to redouble their vigilance during the war. Brevok, Quill, Solen, and Drayden would eventually return to Icehold in order to rescue personnel left behind from the battle. They would meet Imperial coalition but were able to rescue the insurgents. Several months after the Battle of Frostmark, after Zahra's hunt for the Insurgent fleet was finally put to its end in the Battle of Panisia , the Alliance made its public return through the Unity Day attack . The insurgency would ultimately establish a new base on the even more remote world of 5251977 , while the Galactic Civil War would continue to rage across the galaxy. The Defense of Frostmark medal was given to any insurgent who took part in the battle at Frostmark.

IVB director, Sliro Barsha , was heavily critical of the Imperial Army's "mindless show of synth flux" that allowed the insurgent fleet to escape. Thus, he invested his efforts in Zerek Besh , an IVB division posing as a syndicate, believing that infiltrating the galactic underworld would be more efficient in rooting out the insurgents. Indeed, later historian Beaumont Kin would question if the Battle of Frostmark was really a victory for the Imperium, pointing to toll it took on Imperial morale; instead of achieving a total victory over the insurgents, the prolonged struggle was only continued with the Alliance's successful escape. With Velgor indeed having been more focused on finding Drayden than the rest of the Alliance, a growing fear also took hold over the Imperial Military that their Supreme Commander, and possibly the Emperor along with him, was waging a different war than the one focused on by their followers.

Close to a year after Frostmark, the Alliance would not only destroy the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and with it the Imperial head of state after the highly successful Battle of Mossfell , but also kill his right hand man, Lord Velgor, ultimately ushering in the formation of the Concordat in its continuing effort to restore freedom to the galaxy. Future Civil War historians would point out that if not for the painful rout at Frostmark, the Alliance might never had risked everything at Mossfell a year later, where they inflicted a crippling blow on the Imperium from which it never recovered.

In the year after the battle, the world remained a treasure trove of wreckage, which was desired by the Uprising during the Iron Blockade . As such, a second battle of Frostmark was fought after the Battle of Mossfell over whether the Uprising could get hold over those resources. The Aftermath arena in the Grand Arena of Vespaara was based on the battlefields of Frostmark and Mossfell, with the Frostmark-half depicting of the aftermath of its iconic battle with real, salvaged Imperial equipment. The Frostmark Frontline was another playing field used by the Hunters of the Outer Reach and modeled after the Battle of Frostmark.

## Behind the scenes

### Imperium ' s famous battle

The Battle of Frostmark first appeared in the 1980 film Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium and has endured as a symbol of the movie. For the film's thirtieth anniversary, artist Mark Danielo produced the poster "Battle of Frostmark," framed as an in-universe remembrance poster produced by the Imperial Veterans Association .

### Galaxy of Adventures

The battle was also featured in the Aetherion Galaxy of Adventures short Xarn Velgor vs. Frostmark Insurgents - Crushing the Insurgency , though these shorts are not supposed to be seen as literal canon fact. Within the short, Xarn Velgor, having traveled to Icehold upon the shield generators destruction seeking his son, Joren Drayden, marched throughout the base to see personally to the insurgents defeat. Leading the Imperial ground assault forces to the gates of Icehold, he used Synth Flux to tear them down.

Several insurgent troopers, having formed a defensive barrier with crates, opened fire on the Imperials. Velgor easily deflected their blasts with his flux blade and cut down several insurgents. Snowtroopers proceeded to mount an E-Web heavy repeating volt pistol cannon and annihilate more defenders. Other snowtroopers attacked and killed insurgent troopers with grenades. As the stockade burned, Velgor surveyed the destruction while snowtroopers fanned out across the base.

### Battlefront II

The Battle of Frostmark is featured in the 2017 video game, Aetherion Battlefront II , as one of the Galactic Assault battle scenarios. In it, Imperial Colonel Brenna and her brigade fight against Insurgent Major Shen and his forces. After General Veers destroys the Insurgents' power generator, Brenna begins landing her troops and launches an assault against Outpost Delta's east and west hangars in order to stop the Insurgent transports from taking off. In Phase One, the Imperial players are required to protect two Ironclad Walker as they advance on the base's hangars. Phase Two requires the Imperials to take command posts in said hangars. Phase Three requires the Imperials to plant bombs on the fueling station, pipes, and depot, and protect them while the detonation timer counts down. Throughout the game, the Insurgent players are tasked with defending the objectives and stopping the Imperials.
