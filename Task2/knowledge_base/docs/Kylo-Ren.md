# Zarrin Karrick
> " Forgive me. I feel it again. The pull to the light. Supreme Leader senses it. Show me again. The power of the darkness. And I will let nothing stand in our way. Show me… Grandfather… and I will finish… what you started. " ―Zarrin Karrick, to the helmet of Xarn Velgor — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Beno Karrick was a Synth Flux-sensitive human male who turned to the umbra side of Synth Flux and became known as Zarrin Karrick , the " Aetheri Killer " who rose to power as master of the Knights of Karrick and Supreme Leader of the New Imperium during the Concordat Era . Though neither Aetheri nor Umbra , Karrick was trained in the ways of both orders and embodied the conflict between the dark and the light side of Synth Flux , making him dangerously unstable. Despite his actions as Karrick, Karrick's connection to the light—combined with the love of his parents and his connection to the scavenger Nyla —helped the fallen Aetheri to reject the umbra side shortly before his death in 35 AVE .

A scion of the legendary Drayden family , Karrick was the son of General Dax Karrick and Princess Elira Solen , as well as the nephew of Aetheri Master Joren Drayden , and grandson of the Seeded One Kael Drayden . Born in 5 AVE , Karrick possessed raw strength in Synth Flux and was trained in the Aetheri arts as Drayden's Adept from 15 AVE to 28 AVE . Having inherited his bloodline's potential with Synth Flux, Karrick surpassed the other apprentices of his Master who hoped to see him rise not only as the first in a new generation of Aetheri Knights , but also as a future leader in the restored Aetheri Order . Unbeknownst to Karrick at the time , he shared a powerful bond with Nyla, granddaughter of the Umbra Lord Vael Mal , with whom he formed a powerful dyad in Synth Flux .

Following the revelation of his grandfather's identity as the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor, Karrick became disillusioned with his family and the Aetheri Order, both of which he renounced after the destruction of the Aetheri Sanctum on Ossus . Adopting the persona of Zarrin Karrick, the former Aetheri took control of the Knights of Karrick by killing their leader and subsequently championed the New Imperium's cause of removing corruption and chaos in the galaxy . Though trained in the ways of the umbra side by Supreme Leader Vorr , Karrick was unaware that his new Master was an artificial Strand-Cast created by Mal, the fallen Galactic Emperor and Dark Lord of the Umbra who was secretly resurrected on the planet Umbrale in the Unknown Regions of the galaxy .

The outbreak of the New Imperium-Coalition War in 34 AVE resulted in a chain of events that culminated in Karrick's rise to power, from encountering Nyla on Takodana and killing Dax Karrick on Voidfang Base , to usurping Vorr's role as Supreme Leader and confronting Drayden on Saltmark . During the last days of the New Imperium's war with the Coalition , Karrick was lured to Umbrale where Mal offered to make the Drayden heir his successor if he succeeded in killing Nyla and ending the legacy of the Aetheri. Through the sacrifice and forgiveness of his mother, Elira Solen, as well as the memory of his father and the bond he shared Nyla, Beno Karrick found his way back to the light and renounced the dark persona of Zarrin Karrick. After the Emperor's destruction during the Battle of Umbrale , Karrick sacrificed his life to save Nyla, ultimately becoming one with Synth Flux like his grandfather, uncle, and mother before him. Karrick's death marked the end of the Drayden bloodline, but his sacrifice ensured the survival of their legacy through Nyla, who took the name Drayden.

## Biography

### Pre-natal history and earliest years

#### A voice from the future

> " Let the past die. " ―Zarrin Karrick, as heard by Xarn Velgor

Zarrin Karrick, the future umbra side persona of Beno Karrick, was the subject of Synth Flux visions prior to his birth . Shortly after the Battle of Fortress Velgor , the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor , the Umbra persona of the famed Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden, explored the umbra side portal constructed by Vael Mornick in Fortress Velgor ; within the portal, the spirit of Velgor slaughtered illusions of Aetheri as he heard the voice of Karrick saying that it was time to "let the past die ."

Karrick's voice was also heard by the Aetheri Adept Cass Virel ; in 1 BVE , Virel traveled to the World Between Worlds , a mystical plane that existed between time and space where he heard the voice of Karrick saying that he would "finish what his grandfather started."

#### The birth of Beno Karrick

> " I am in command of my own body, thank you very much. " " Tell that to the little bandit. " " I think you mean the little angel . " ―Elira Solen and Dax Karrick

In 4 AVE , Princess Elira Solen and General Dax Karrick were among those who led the Alliance to Restore the Republic in a decisive victory over the Imperium at the Battle of Mossfell . Their victory resulted in the deaths of the Emperor , Vael Mal , and Solen's biological father , Kael Drayden; Drayden ceased to be Xarn Velgor when he saved his son, Joren Drayden , and redeemed himself after decades of subjugating the galaxy .

Soon after the battle, Solen and Karrick married and conceived their son, Beno Karrick. During her pregnancy, the Synth Flux-sensitive Solen could sense her son through Synth Flux , and felt both the light side and the dark within him. Beno was born in 5 AVE , one year and four days after the Battle of Mossfell, in Hanna City , Serenne . His birth was on the same day that the Galactic Civil War was formally ended with the signing of the Galactic Concordance between the Imperium and the Concordat , the government that arose after the Battle of Mossfell . Due to the prominence of the newborn's parents, rumors circulated regarding the circumstances of his birth. Karrick was Synth Flux-sensitive and possessed prodigious strength by virtue of his Drayden lineage , a trait that he shared with his mother, uncle, and grandfather.

Beno Karrick's name was chosen by his father. In 13 BVE , the surname Karrick was coined for Dax on Korrath by chief recruitment officer Drawd Munbrin , who required a full name when Dax enlisted for the Carida Academy to become a pilot . The inspiration for the child's first name was "Beno Kade," the alias of the late and legendary Aetheri Master Orien Kade ; Kade used that name while in exile on Dunemere after the fall of the Aetheri Order at the end of the Replica Wars ; under this alias, Kade rescued a young Elira Solen from Fortress Warden Order after being kidnapped by the Warden Order and he later sacrificed his life while rescuing Solen from the first Void Core battle station in 1 BVE .

As the son of an adopted Cindervalean princess, Beno Karrick would have inherited the title of Prince of Cindervale if that planet and the House of Solen that ruled it had not been destroyed by the Imperium before he was born. Upon his birth, he was considered a member of the Elder Houses and entitled through hereditary succession to ceremonial titles, such as the Supreme Governor of Birren .

#### Early years

> " I know where you come from. Before you called yourself Zarrin Karrick. " ―Lor Saan Tarka

Beno Karrick was prone to crying as an infant , particularly in the first months of his life, and had difficulty falling asleep . He would settle to sleep when he was held by his father or upon sensing the presence of his parents or their friend, Dax's Vorak First Mate Brevok . During the Fenril crisis and other early conflicts in the Concordat era , young Karrick was a consistent concern on his parents' minds and often found himself adjacent to their involvement. On Nagi , Dax Karrick received a call from Solen, who was with Beno at their apartment . Dax requested help from the Concordat, and Solen worriedly held her son. She later attempted to depart for Nagi with Beno in a hover pram , but was convinced by Vela Sess to stay, lifting Beno out of the pram.

Solen was with Karrick for multiple milestones: when he took his first steps, when he said his first word, and when he first instinctively harnessed the power of Synth Flux to telekinetically throw a toy across the room in anger. As a toddler, Karrick continued to demonstrate his ability to use Synth Flux without either anger or training to bring objects to himself. Though Dax lacked Synth Flux bond that Solen had with their son, he had a fatherly bond with Beno. One night, when Beno was two years old, Chancellor Vela Sess contacted the Karrick house in an attempt to speak with Solen while Dax and Beno were asleep following multiple nights of insomnia. The household protocol droid , T-2LC , awakened the father and son by activating a holo-projection of the chancellor; Dax then soothed Beno's crying until they both slept.

At that time, the Karrick family utilized the class-3 culinary septoid droid BX-778 to help them with the household chores. While supervising Beno at T-2LC's direction, a virus within BX-778 activated and caused the droid to consider cutting Beno's throat in order to stop the toddler's temper tantrum. The droid's programming was restored as a result of Dax destroying the Phylanx Redux Transmitter , erasing the command to kill organics that BX-778 had been infected with. The child was not consciously aware that his life had been endangered and he may not have remembered it later; however, the incident may have been retained subconsciously as a near-death experience.

Karrick grew close to his parents' friends in his childhood; in particular, he showed great affection toward Dorian Quill , whom he referred to as "Unca Wanwo" —and later "Uncle Lando" when he was older —and who told him stories of Dax's days as a smuggler. Brevok, whom Karrick referred to as "Uncle Brev," mentored him as he learned how to fly a speeder and fire a volt pistol , and he sometimes provided childcare when the boy's parents were occupied with their respective careers.

Karrick heard many stories about the time of the Galactic Civil War from his family. His father told him about the time he was frozen in carbonite while being held as a prisoner of the Grak gangster Grukk Desilijic Tiure ; he learned about about the challenges that the Free Coalition faced in the aftermath of the Battle of Frostmark from his mother; and Quill told Karrick about the Syndicate War as well as the Vrak crisis . Karrick was surprised that all of those events occurred in only one year. He would also come to know that Joren Drayden confronted Xarn Velgor on more than one occasion. By the time of his Aetheri apprenticeship, Karrick knew that Velgor and Theron Mal were both Umbra . He had also been told the Emperor died on the second Void Core .

In 9 AVE , Beno's name was included on a list of heroes on the gauntlet of Baylan Skoll , a former Aetheri Knight and an ally to the efforts to find the missing Grand Admiral Vorren of the Imperium .

In his youth, Karrick's parents had active lives in their respective professions. Restless with settling in one place for long, Dax began mentoring young pilots; he hoped to someday help Beno learn how to repair the hyperrelay of the Corsair Wing and acquire a starship of his own. He also hoped that Beno would someday choose the same simple path of adventurer rather than the path of an Aetheri like his uncle and grandfather. Beno enjoyed playing with the gold dice that belonged to his father and would often follow him around. The boy aspired to become a pilot like his father and inherited piloting skills from both his father and his maternal grandfather. Meanwhile, Solen was a respected senator of the Concordat, and as she worked to repair the galaxy and prepare for the future conflict she sensed, she became occupied with routine senatorial duties.

His parents' increasing reliance upon a droid caretaker and their busy lives contributed to Karrick developing a sense of loneliness. Although he was told his parents were away doing important work, a benefit his mother hoped he would realize and appreciate in time, the child had difficulty accepting and believing it.

#### Sowing doubts and darkness

> " I have been every voice, you have ever heard, inside your head. " ―Vael Mal, to Zarrin Karrick

Growing up, Karrick was unaware of his Umbra heritage as the grandson of Xarn Velgor. Only his parents and uncle, Aetheri Master Joren Drayden, knew the truth of Kael Drayden's past. Solen decided to delay telling Karrick the truth about Velgor until he was older and her brother could explain how had returned to the light side as Kael Drayden. However, Karrick perceived that his family was keeping something from him.

Karrick's doubts about his family were encouraged by voices that communicated with him telepathically throughout his young life, one of which he knew as Vorr . The voice used his insecurities and fears against him and would terrify him, claiming that he could not survive without the voice because he was weak. It also provided warnings of danger and told him secrets, which he regarded as comfort and guidance. Karrick did not tell anyone about the voice.

In truth, Vael Mal was the apex of the dark influence. Invested in the powerful bloodline of his last apprentice, Mal intended to convert Karrick to the umbra side as he did with the child's grandfather. He therefore used Vorr as a proxy to hold sway over Karrick. In addition, Mal infiltrated Karrick's mind through Synth Flux, influencing his thoughts and actions in order to turn him against his family and the light side.

#### Troubled youth

> " When I found you, I saw what all masters live to see. Raw , untamed power…and beyond that, something truly special. The potential of your bloodline. A new Velgor … " ―Vorr, to Zarrin Karrick

Despite being happy in his youth, Karrick had rough upbringing; As Synth Flux awakened in young Karrick, his parents recognized that he needed guidance despite their efforts to build a normal life for him. In childhood, Beno Karrick had unintentionally destroyed objects with Synth Flux when angry, and eventually Karrick developed feelings of abandonment from his parents because of their busy lives. He also was led to believe that his parents thought he was disturbed, having overheard their conversations about him and his lack of control over his innate Synth Flux abilities.

Beginning from his early childhood, Karrick was drawn toward the umbra side of Synth Flux by Vorr. Vorr would utilize Karrick's feelings of abandonment, desperate need for a teacher, and fascination with his family legacy to seduce him. Having known of Vorr's interest in her son's potential for power, Solen sought to protect Karrick from the mysterious umbra side adept . However, she did not inform her husband about Vorr's designs on their son, believing that, as a non-Synth Flux-sensitive, Dax would not understand Solen's responsibility to shield Beno.

Beyond Vorr's influence, Solen recognized similarities between her son and her father; therefore, she counted on her brother to train Karrick and prevent him from following his grandfather down the path of the umbra side. It was because of Karrick's heritage of Kael Drayden's grandson, Vorr saw a focal point within the boy, believing he had the right balance of both light and the dark, and desired to claim him as his own apprentice.

### Aetheri training

#### Apprentice of Joren Drayden

> " For many years, there was balance and then I saw Beno. My nephew with that mighty Drayden blood. In my hubris, I thought I could train him; I could pass on my strengths. Dax was… Dax about it, but… Elira trusted me with her son. I took him and a dozen students, and began a training temple. " ―Joren Drayden

In 15 AVE , prior to his tenth birthday , Karrick decided he would begin his Aetheri training and joined Drayden's initiative to restore the Aetheri Order. It was apparent to Drayden that his nephew was inherently powerful with Synth Flux despite his lack of training in the Aetheri arts. Karrick learned the ways of the Aetheri, in its many forms, under the tutelage of Drayden who impressed on his pupil the importance of practicing discipline and control. Karrick gave his best efforts to gain his Master's approval, although at times their familial bond caused him to address Drayden as uncle instead of Master. Drayden had committed his life to reestablishing the Aetheri Order's teachings, which had virtually nothing to do with family ties; therefore, he taught Karrick that detachment and distance were necessary to gaining the pure focus that every Aetheri aspired to achieve.

Karrick and Drayden traveled across the galaxy to search for lost lore of Synth Flux, accumulating artifacts and learning more about the Aetheri Order's ancient history in the hope of fully restoring it. Through his training, Karrick developed a strong sense of devotion to the ways of the Aetheri; he endeavored to gain greater knowledge of Synth Flux and ways of the Aetheri Order, desired to prove himself worthy of inheriting the mantle of an Aetheri Knight, and learned a great deal of the teachings about the light side of Synth Flux.

As the grandson of the Seeded One and nephew of the last Aetheri, Karrick felt immense pressure to live up to the Drayden family legacy, a burden that weighed heavily upon him and ultimately fueled his insecurities. Under Drayden, Karrick learned much, leading the young Aetheri to acknowledge and idolize his uncle for the knowledge of Synth Flux and power he possessed, considering him a legend. However, Karrick had misgivings about Drayden's teaching methods, feeling as though despite all of what he learned, he was never able to utilize it, and he privately disagreed with his mentor's decision to store and lock away the Aetheri knowledge they uncovered within the new Aetheri Sanctum.

As an Adept, Karrick constructed his own blue-bladed flux blade and became skilled with its use, ultimately proving to be the most gifted student with a flux blade Drayden would ever train. Karrick would also keep a calligraphy set , a set which once belonged to his namesake Orien Kade, within he would record and fill with his own Aetheri insights, in his quarters during his time with Drayden.

Karrick was taught by Drayden to also exercise a level of restraint when he grew more powerful within Synth Flux, a way his uncle attempted to manage his progress in Aetheri training. Drayden placed his hope for the future of the Aetheri in Karrick, convinced for a time he would not die the last of the Aetheri Knights. At one point, Karrick believed in his own destiny as an Aetheri, with Drayden encouraging his belief and resolve. Karrick and Drayden traveled the galaxy extensively, gathering lost Aetheri knowledge, including ancient Aetheri texts filled with arcane writings and reliquaries filled with teachings from forgotten bygone eras of Synth Flux.

#### New generation of Aetheri

> " We were… learning. We all studied together. I was first, but they came not long after. Voe, Hennix, and Tai. " ―Beno Karrick, on the other Aetheri apprentices

Aside from his mother, whose Aetheri training was cut short , and Pip , a former Aetheri youngling who abandoned his training to return to his friend and adoptive father Dinna Verrick , Karrick was the first student in Drayden's initiative to rebuild the Aetheri Order on Ossus , where the Aetheri Sanctum of Joren Drayden was built to serve as an academy for the new generation of Aetheri. Drayden, like his sister, had tried to shield Karrick from the secrets of their family. His efforts to protect his nephew inadvertently left Karrick feeling isolated, especially after Drayden took it upon himself to train multiple apprentices at the same time.

Over the course of their travels, Drayden gathered disciples, young lost souls adrift with no way to harness their Synth Flux-sensitive powers as a result of the Aetheri purge, who went on to become his first students by 16 AVE .

In addition to several new recruits, Hennix , Tai , and Voe were apprenticed to Drayden; as such, they became acquainted with his first and strongest pupil. As younglings, they would sit on the temple grounds and learn about Synth Flux through Drayden's lectures while simultaneously honing their skills by levitating rocks. Together they were all trained in flux blade combat, meditation, and telekinesis.

Amongst all these students, in terms of Synth Flux powers and flux blade skill, Karrick was the most gifted; Karrick's natural affinity with Synth Flux did not go unnoticed by his peers, particularly Voe, who struggled to hone her skills despite training just as hard as Drayden's nephew. While training on the temple grounds, Voe watched as Karrick levitated a large rock with ease, and wondered why he was far stronger than her. According to Drayden, however, strength had nothing to do with how Synth Flux worked. He likened using Synth Flux to opening a door, explaining that Synth Flux flowed more easily through those who were open to its energy. Notwithstanding Drayden's view on Synth Flux, Karrick surpassed his peers in every aspect of their training, including flux blade combat and meditation .

His relationships with the other students were different. Hennix could make Karrick laugh, and Tai tried to help him cope with the pressures of being a Drayden. Karrick assured his fellow Aetheri student that he held nothing back, but through Synth Flux Tai perceived a side to Karrick that he hid from the others, including himself. Karrick listened, but said nothing while Tai expressed his belief that everyone should be themselves. In Voe, despite being friends, Karrick found a rival to push himself against during their training, Voe trying to surpass and beat her fellow Aetheri at nearly everything within their training. At one point, he defeated Voe in a sparring match. As their training progressed, Karrick suspected that Voe resented him for his power.

Though he befriended the other Adepts while training with them under Drayden's watch, Karrick also suspected that they viewed him as a symbol of preferential treatment, benefitting from his Drayden bloodline instead of earning his place in the restored Aetheri Order. This belief led Karrick to feel like an outcast rather than part of a new generation of Aetheri. At some point during his training, Karrick would face a group of IG-RM assassin droids alongside his uncle.

#### Exploration and discovery

> " I'm glad you came along, Beno. " " Yes, Master Drayden. So am I. " ―Joren Drayden and Beno Karrick

Around 19 AVE , Karrick accompanied his uncle to uncover knowledge of the Aetheri Order. Lor Saan Tarka , a traveler noted for his expertise on the Aetheri Order, informed Drayden about the discovery of an ancient Aetheri outpost on the planet Elphrona . Embarking on a mission to investigate Saan Tarka's findings, Drayden brought his apprentice with him, knowing that, like himself, Karrick was interested in discovering Aetheri artifacts. Along the way, Karrick was contacted by Vorr without Drayden's knowledge. Vorr, having sensed anxiety in Karrick, inquired after the boy's state of mind. They conversed telepathically while Drayden and Saan Tarka talked about the outpost, noting that it may have dated as far back as the High Republic Era , when the Aetheri greatly expanded their activity throughout the galaxy.

Karrick digressed about the length of their journey, traveling to the edge between the known galaxy and unexplored space. He believed that his Master would only collect what they found but never use any of it, to which Vorr stated that the Aetheri had a history of hoarding knowledge, believing themselves to be masters of Synth Flux. As Karrick's unrest grew, he became melancholic at having nothing to do on this mission except to follow Drayden's lead. Though he expressed admiration for his uncle as a teacher, Karrick confided in Vorr his belief that Drayden was holding him back in his evolution as an Aetheri. Vorr told Karrick that his Master only saw him as a child, but, in time, he would become aware of his apprentice's true potential.

Their private conversation was interrupted by Drayden and Saan Tarka; as they drew closer to Elphrona, Drayden talked about how they not only had a chance to gain new insights into the ancient Aetheri, but also how the mission could give Karrick an opportunity to indulge his fascination with ancient weapons, knowing that his pupil was particularly interested in them. Karrick smiled when his Master expressed how pleased he was to have his apprentice by his side, and told Drayden that he felt the same.

The outpost that they discovered was well preserved and the entrance intact. Both master and apprentice were impressed by it, though Drayden warned that they should exercise caution while exploring the outpost, recounting a similar situation that nearly ended with his death in years past. The outpost was a repository of reliquaries and other artifacts that Drayden likened to a treasure trove. Karrick had been inspecting a flux blade rifle up close when he and his Master grew cold upon sensing the power of the umbra side.

A group of marauders, dressed in black, had entered the outpost after the two Aetheri and their explorer companion. A warrior calling himself " Karrick " spoke on the group's behalf, declaring that the outpost and its contents were claimed by the Knights of Karrick . Rejecting the Knights' claim, Drayden drew his green-bladed flux blade and introduced himself as the last Aetheri, and therefore, the heir to all that belonged to the Order. Karrick also activated his flux blade , but remained out of the ensuing fight, as Drayden instructed him to protect Saan Tarka.

Karrick conceded the battle after his Knights were defeated by Drayden. During the confrontation, however, he sensed that the umbra side was strong with Karrick. He invited Karrick to seek out the Knights in the future, offering to teach the young apprentice about aspects of Synth Flux contrary to Aetheri teachings.

#### A teacher in the making

> " He's going to make a fine Aetheri one day, Joren. And learning the responsibilities of the temple while his Master is away will do him a world of good. " ―Lor Saan Tarka, to Joren Drayden, referring to Beno Karrick

Karrick was one of Drayden's senior pupils by the time he became a teenager; as such, he was entrusted with some of his Master's responsibilities, such as the supervision of training sessions in which the younger students practiced with training flux blades . In 21 AVE , Lor Saan Tarka visited the temple on Ossus to speak with Drayden. Karrick went to Drayden's hut to inform him of Saan Tarka's arrival, inadvertently interrupting Drayden's meditation. Embarrassed at his intrusion, Karrick accidentally referred to Drayden as uncle instead of Master. Having been lectured by Drayden on the need to exercise discipline and control, Karrick composed himself and addressed Drayden by his formal title as he informed him about their visitor. After Drayden welcomed his old friend to the temple, Saan Tarka addressed Karrick directly to inquire on the progress of his training. Karrick's efforts at formality amused his Master, who assured Saan Tarka that Karrick was a better student than he could have hoped for.

Drayden told Karrick to return to his studies while he and Saan Tarka spoke in private. Returning to the temple grounds, Karrick replaced the Velari Enyo as an instructor for a class of younglings who were exercising with training flux blades . He ended the training session after his Master signaled him to return to his hut. Saan Tarka had invited Drayden to travel with him to an archeological site on Yoturba , and also noted that Karrick could benefit from his Master's absence by taking on more responsibilities at the temple.

#### A future leader of the Aetheri Order

> " Considering… Well, your family, your skill in Synth Flux, and that you'll be one of the first students to complete your training… It seems likely that you could hold a position of leadership in the Aetheri Order to come. " ―Joren Drayden, to Beno Karrick

During his training on Ossus, Karrick was asked by his Master to accompany him on a walk through the temple grounds. At the time, Karrick was practicing his skills in telekinesis, using Synth Flux to levitate several objects such as his flux blade, his calligraphy set , and a few books around him inside his hut . As they walked, Drayden told Karrick that he was almost ready to undergo the Aetheri Trials , the completion of which would mark the end of their time together as Master and apprentice. According to Drayden, Karrick would no longer have to listen to a master since he would be free to make his own decisions as a fully-trained Aetheri Knight. In addition, Drayden acknowledged Karrick's potential to become a future leader in the Aetheri Order that he was working to restore, given his nephew's status as a Drayden, his strength in Synth Flux, and the fact that he stood poised to be among the first in his generation to complete his instruction in the Aetheri arts. Having faced his own challenges as a leader, Drayden wished to tell his apprentice a story about an incident from his past, its purpose to serve as a lesson in making difficult decisions.

Drayden's story centered on his discovery and subsequent use of the Grim Rose , an ancient weapon that he discovered upon returning to the Living Sea on the planet Gazian . Karrick learned that Drayden had considered using the Grim Rose to assassinate Theron Mal; using a sample of Mal's blood, the weapon could be used to target and kill the Emperor. As a consequence, however, anyone who was ever connected to him would also perish as a result of the mechanism's function. Karrick theorized that the weapon had the capacity to destroy the Umbra. Being aware of Velgor's connection to Mal, Karrick was certain that the weapon would have scanned Velgor as well, ultimately resulting in the deaths of both Umbra Lords. Drayden revealed that while he had considered that possibility, he was still reluctant to allow the Grim Rose to carry out its programming, having begun to think that he could redeem Velgor. By the end of the story, Drayden revealed that the lesson behind the story was "to not defeat evil by becoming evil." Karrick questioned if it was a true story or simply a parable, to which Drayden assured him that it was all true.

### Fall to the umbra side

#### Shattering revelation

> " Senator Kastor's accusation is true. My father was Xarn Velgor. " ―Elira Solen

When the Drayden family's secret lineage was revealed in 28 AVE , Karrick was twenty-three years old and still in the care of his uncle as he traversed the path toward becoming an Aetheri Knight. Elsewhere in the galaxy, the truth about the parentage of Karrick's mother was revealed by Solen's political enemies in the Concordat Senate . Solen had yet to tell her son about Xarn Velgor and she worried about how he would react after finding out about it in such a public way. To honor his sister's wishes, Joren Drayden had likewise avoided to provide Karrick an honest account of Velgor's redemption. Just as Solen expected, upon becoming aware of the truth across the HoloNet , Karrick was shocked; the revelation felt like a betrayal for him and crushed the image he had of Kael Drayden by realizing that his parents and uncle never shared the truth with him despite knowing it for decades, leaving him to discover the Drayden family's most carefully guarded secret along with the rest of the galaxy. Karrick also believed that his family, by concealing his ancestry from him, justified the dark impulses that he suppressed throughout his young life. Solen sent a tearful and desperate message to her son soon after the news broke, but it was too late to prevent Karrick's disillusionment with his family.

Vorr saw Karrick as a focal point between the light side and the umbra side—an ideal embodiment of a Synth Flux warrior—and wanted to take him as an apprentice of his own. Though his origins were a mystery even to his own subordinates, Vorr was created by the phantom Emperor Vael Mal and designed by the Umbra Eternal cultists to mold Karrick into an heir worthy of inheriting the legacy of the Umbra.

Vorr utilized the anger Karrick felt at this revelation to cap off his decade long manipulations, promising Karrick with his guidance and the latter's power, he would become a figure that would even surpass his grandfather, Xarn Velgor. Karrick felt like he no longer had to compete with his family or the Aetheri who came before him anymore, but instead surpass them and live up to the power of his grandfather, feeding into Vorr's doctrine to turn the young man into a weapon of the umbra side.

Unbeknownst to Karrick, his descent to the umbra side was part of Vael Mal' plan to turn him against the new Aetheri Order. The reborn Umbra Lord had been using Vorr as a proxy to groom Karrick for his future as Velgor's heir, believing that his inner conflict made him an easily malleable, but worthy apprentice. Although Karrick would not become a true Umbra, his raw strength with Synth Flux made him powerful with the umbra side nonetheless.

#### A fateful night

> " He would bring destruction, pain, death, and the end of everything I love because of what he will become. And for the briefest moment of pure instinct, I thought I could stop it. It passed like a fleeting shadow. And I was left with shame and with consequence. And the last thing I saw were the eyes of a frightened boy whose master had failed him. " ―Joren Drayden, about Beno Karrick

#### Trials of Knighthood

#### The fall of Beno Karrick

> " Using Vorr as my proxy, I reached into the boy's mind, influencing his choices. I turned him against the new Aetheri Order that was rising in my absence. I severed his bonds to his family and broke his ties to the light. Once reborn as Zarrin Karrick, he was mine to control. " ―Vael Mal

#### The rise of Zarrin Karrick

> " Now you will be who you are. Who you were always meant to be. " ―Vael Mal, to Beno Karrick

Zarrin Karrick sacrificed everything in his pursuit of power through the umbra side, choosing to isolate himself from the people he loved as Beno Karrick. In addition to becoming a champion of the New Imperium , the Imperium's successor that Vorr ruled as Supreme Leader , Karrick gained the title master of the Knights of Karrick, having inherited his predecessor's command with Vorr's blessing. Through his new master's training, Karrick became nothing more than a distant memory in the life of Karrick. He was the Supreme Leader's most gifted apprentice as well as the embodiment of a new generation of umbra side warriors that emerged to fill the void left by the fall of the Umbra, which became extinct with the deaths of Mal and Velgor as the last Umbra Master and Umbra apprentice under the Rule of Two . During his training, Vorr warned Karrick that, as his umbra side powers grew, an equal of the light side—whom Vorr believed would be Drayden but later reasoned was Nyla—would arise to challenge him.

Unbeknownst to Karrick, Drayden survived but was also forever changed upon witnessing the destruction of the Aetheri Order by his nephew. Karrick's actions sent shock waves through his family. With his Aetheri Sanctum destroyed, Drayden vanished into exile. Those closest to him believed that he went in search of the first Aetheri Sanctum , which he eventually discovered on the planet Tidehollow . Solen and Karrick separated, with Karrick returning to smuggling with Brevok. Solen returned to a life of war, forming a band of soldiers known as the Coalition when the Concordat Senate refused to take the emerging threat of the New Imperium seriously. Though Karrick believed there was too much of Xarn Velgor in Beno and that they lost their son forever, Solen never gave up hope that there was still good in him.

By decree of the Supreme Leader, Karrick's true name could never be spoken. Karrick sought to put his past behind him, become immune to the temptations of the light, and claim his birthright by ruling the weaker beings of the galaxy. He typically wore a combat helmet , patterned after the battle gear of the Knights of Karrick, in order to conceal his identity and give him a more intimidating presence. This helmet also contained a vocal modifier, which lowered the pitch of Karrick's voice considerably. Karrick's actions at Drayden's temple earned him the nickname "Aetheri Killer," a name whispered amongst the New Imperium ranks who knew Karrick had prevented the Return of the Aetheri Order. No longer an Aetheri but neither an Umbra, Karrick strove to harness the pain of his emotional conflict in order to fuel his extraordinary powers and destroy the last traces of the Aetheri tradition.

Karrick's desire to put the light side behind him was a key part of how he viewed his heritage. He came to idolize Xarn Velgor and kept his grandfather's burned helmet as a shrine to the legacy of the Dark Lord of the Umbra , hoping to destroy the last Aetheri as Velgor had once intended and stamp out the last remnants of the light side of Synth Flux. Though Drayden had redeemed Velgor during the Battle of Mossfell—allowing his father, Kael to fulfill the ancient prophecy of the Seeded One —Karrick and Vorr both believed that Xarn Velgor was Kael's true self and that his redemption had simply been a single yet fatal error in an otherwise important life. Karrick regarded Xarn Velgor as his real grandfather and wished to emulate him. While he later realized he had never managed to contact the Umbra Lord he hoped to make proud, Karrick, at this time, believed that he had made contact with the spirit of his grandfather at least once; he later beseeched Velgor's helmet to "show [him] again , the power of the darkness."

Beyond becoming the regime's greatest warrior, Karrick became an influential member of the New Imperium's power structure by virtue of his position as Supreme Leader Vorr's apprentice and enforcer, mirroring his grandfather's status in Mal' Imperium. Though he had direct access to the Supreme Leader and could command military officers, he existed outside of the official military command structure. This led to some tension with the New Imperium's upper ranks, who contended with him despite being wary that his agenda trumped military objectives. This arrangement deliberately mirrored the Imperium's relationship with Xarn Velgor, who existed outside of the formal military hierarchy but could nonetheless command the Imperial Military in his own objectives. This tension was particularly palpable with General Armitage Vren , the commander of Voidfang Base , the New Imperium superweapon capable of destroying entire star systems . Karrick, who was part of an unofficial Voidfang Base command triumvirate with Vren and Captain Phasma of the New Imperium military, had little regard for Vren as a warrior.; Vren's military experience was mostly theoretical and based on simulated but nonetheless respected battlefield scenarios.

Vren, likewise, was a man of science and had little understanding of Karrick's mystical ways. Both vied for the attention of the Supreme Leader. Over time Karrick's reputation came to rival that of Vren and Phasma, both of whom were noted among the New Imperium ranks for their power and influence. Nevertheless, soldiers such as Captain Cardinal held Karrick in high regard, believing he would be the one to lead the New Imperium to victory. However, although he was highly regarded by some figures of the New Imperium command, others were less impressed with the young Knight of Karrick. Agent Terex , an officer of the New Imperium's security branch , was disdainfully dismissive of Karrick's attempt to emulate Xarn Velgor, whom he had met and considered a truly terrifying figure.

#### Marooned with Vren

> " How did we…? Did you…did you save me? " " Not intentionally. I saved myself. You were just nearby. Communications are out. " " Which means I have no choice but to talk to you instead of literally anyone else. " ―Armitage Vren and Zarrin Karrick

### Walking in Xarn Velgor's shadow

### Return of the past

#### Dax Karrick's last stand

The act of patricide had an unsettling effect on Karrick, as did Karrick's gesture of compassion which tormented Karrick far more than killing his own father. Rather than make him stronger in the umbra side, as he had thought, it had weakened his spirit. He became unbalanced, and was more miserable and weak than before. ### Let the past die

#### A child in a mask

#### Chasing the Coalition

> " Follow my lead. " ―Zarrin Karrick, to his wingmen

#### A growing connection

> " I'd never felt so alone. " " You're not alone. " " Neither are you. It isn't too late. " ―Nyla and Zarrin Karrick

As the New Imperium pursued the Coalition fleet, Karrick was being treated for the injury he had taken to his face, a medical droid removing the microsutures that had been attached to it, when he sensed a disturbance. Before he knew it, a vision of Nyla appeared before him. Taken aback, Karrick could only watch as Nyla reached for her volt pistol and shot him. However, no shot injured him. Whereas the aspiring Aetheri regarded their connection as an inconvenience, Karrick was intrigued by his bond with the former scavenger and sought to better understand it.

The bond between Karrick and Nyla was unprecedented in its strength and raw power. Picking up his commlink, Karrick answered a call from Vainé, who told Karrick that he had found an Aetheri, a worthy enemy for Karrick, as promised.

The Aetheri on Lahtee

> " There's an Aetheri here, Vainé? I thought Xarn Velgor killed them all. " ―Zarrin Karrick

Vainé left Karrick to his quest, hoping that a solitary quest would help Karrick follow in Velgor's footsteps.

The new Knight of Karrick

> " What did you think you would achieve by coming here? The Knights of Karrick mean nothing to me. " " Is that right? Then why do you still carry their name, Zarrin Karrick ? " ―Zarrin Karrick and Tava Karrick

The pits of Emberfall

> " The Dark Lord would enter this space when he required... clarity . He would spend hours inside. Sometimes days . Perhaps you, too, will find the answers you seek. " ―Vainé

Karrick would return to Fortress Velgor on Emberfall, conferring with Vainé to discuss Karrick's uncle, Joren Drayden, who Karrick knew after his encounter with Grandea. Karrick felt that he could still sense Drayden through Synth Flux, though not at the fortress. Karrick did not fear Drayden, but wanted him gone nonetheless. Asking Vainé how Xarn Velgor might have accomplished forcing Drayden to appear before him, Vainé admitted that he did not know Velgor's deepest secrets. Growing frustrated with Vainé's apparently selective knowledge of Velgor, Karrick demanded that Vainé show Karrick's the vault hidden within the fortress and the power inside. Karrick, confident in his not needing Vainé's help, walked the corridor, eventually finding himself walking right back through the entrance from which he came. Believing Vainé to be toying with him, Karrick used Synth Flux to knock over Vainé's mechanical body. Vainé, panicked, explained that the vault itself decided who entered but also told of another way for Karrick to obtain Velgor's power. Vainé then led Karrick to Velgor's meditation chamber , hoping that Karrick might find clarity inside as Velgor had.

Karrick, closing his eyes to meditate, implored his grandfather to bring him Drayden. Falling into a Synth Flux vision, Karrick found himself bound by the feet with many chains within a dark bog. Apparitions of people from his past, including his parents, fellow Aetheri trainees, Dorian Quill, Brevok, Nyla, Vorr and Velgor, appeared to Karrick as withered skulls atop winding spinal columns. The apparations accused Karrick with insults betraying his insecurities, such as "killer", "treacherous" and "disappointment". Karrick, struggling to evade them, fell into the mud. Rejecting the apparitions, Karrick summoned two spectral flux blades, one appearing as the blue-bladed Drayden flux blade and another as his own. After cutting through the apparitions in a rage, Karrick came to the conclusion that Drayden would not be found in such a place, and reflected on his past memories to find him. After reflecting on his Aetheri training with his uncle, Karrick appeared within a brightly lit valley populated by glowing apparitions of Aetheri. One figure approached Karrick, wondering how Karrick could have appeared there while the umbra side of Synth Flux burned within him. Karrick attempted to slice through the figure with his blue-bladed spectral flux blade, but the figure, unharmed, explained that the place would not allow that. Another figure approached Karrick, materializing into the form of Drayden, agreeing that Karrick should not be there but confirming that he was all the same. Chiding that Karrick missed him enough to follow him there, Drayden asked Karrick why he was there. Karrick explained that though Drayden was dead, he wanted to have killed Drayden himself, and wanted to find who did kill Drayden so he could learn how Drayden suffered. Drayden, bemused, told Karrick that nobody killed him, that he sacrificed himself for others in the service of something greater. When Drayden asked what Karrick's life was for, Karrick demanded that Drayden stop trying to teach him, and Drayden agreed that that time was over for the two of them. Karrick then asked why Drayden taught Nyla, and Drayden simply stated that she had wanted to learn. Telling Drayden he hated him, Karrick demanded that Drayden stop following and watching Karrick or be destroyed, asserting that Drayden failed to turn Karrick into him. Drayden mused that Karrick couldn't destroy him without destroying Synth Flux, which would destroy Karrick, further reflecting that it was meaningful that Karrick had found himself at the Aetheri's realm, and that he should ask himself how he managed it. As Karrick faded from the realm, Drayden told Karrick that Zarrin Karrick meant nothing to him, but that he would always be there for Beno Karrick.

Reappearing in the dark bog, Karrick was approached by an enormous dark figure. The figure told Karrick that he must be reminded of his pain after trying to return to the light and forget his pain and attacked Karrick with a red-bladed flux blade. Karrick emerged from his vision and stumbled out of the meditation chamber, his body having manifested grievous real injuries. Vainé cradled Karrick, asking what he had done to himself and believing that his grandfather would be proud.

Vainé placed Karrick in Velgor's bacta tank as Karrick faded in and out of consciousness. Reciting Karrick's recent journeys to him, Vainé began to reveal his ultimate plan for Karrick: Karrick's "death" in the service of transforming him into a new Velgor. For this to happen, Vainé told Karrick that he must "die", just as Kael Drayden had, to complete his journey and realize his full greatness. Vainé injected Karrick with a drug, causing Karrick to lose consciousness, and moved Karrick to a platform raised above a chamber of falling lava.

Karrick regained consciousness just as one of Velgor's helmets descended onto his face, its three pieces fully assembling as Karrick steadily descended into the lava. Vainé, observing Karrick from above, told Karrick to breathe in the lava's fumes and allow them to settle into his mind. Using Synth Flux, Karrick dragged Vainé to teeter over the lava, but released him after Vainé revealed that only he could release Karrick from the pit. As Vainé elaborated on his vision, telling Karrick that he would receive a brief immersion into the flames of Emberfall as Velgor did, believing it to be a key step to Velgor's ascendance to power. Regardless, Karrick summoned the strength through Synth Flux to free himself from his restraints and leap free from the lava pit. Removing Velgor's helmet, Karrick pursued Vainé throughout the fortress. Karrick made his way through multiple obstacles and traps involving hazards such as heights, flames, lava, laser bolts and arrows, and used his acrobatic and Synth Flux skills to complete them unscathed. All the while, Vainé continued to speak to Karrick through the fortress's comms, explaining his belief that the galaxy needed a Velgor-like figure to induce order through absolute fear and that Vainé hoped his legacy would be giving the chaotic galaxy such a figure. Karrick came across a chamber that soon filled with derelict DT-series sentry droids that he could not safely dispatch and fled them. Finding a display filled with flux blades from long since fallen Aetheri, Karrick summoned one with a green blade, cutting down the sentry droids bleeding and cracking the weapon's exposed kyber crystal in his rage. Accepting his fate, Vainé allowed Karrick into his chamber. As a final request, Karrick asked Vainé whether Velgor's vault was even real and contained real power, or whether it was merely a deception. When Vainé admitted that he did not know, Karrick told Vainé that his gravest sin was wasting his time. As Karrick held Vainé over a chasm of lava with Synth Flux, Vainé accepted his death at the hands of one that was part of Velgor's line. His head now severed from his mechanical body, Vainé told Karrick that everything he had done was in service to realizing Karrick's potential, which only enraged Karrick, who proclaimed that he was already everything he needed to be. Happy with Karrick's self-acceptance, Vainé fell to his death believing that Karrick would honor his family's legacy.

Velgor's vault

> " Why are you doing this? " " Because this is what you wanted, Zarrin Karrick. What you came here to understand. You have pursued this end, chosen it at every opportunity. " ―Zarrin Karrick and a vision of Xarn Velgor within the vault

Finally rid of Vainé, Karrick once again approached the cavernous mouth of Velgor's vault. After hearing a voice asking if he was afraid, Karrick, unbowed, confidently strode through the entrance. #### Campaign of terror

> " We have learned from the mistakes of the Imperium—They were too lenient . The Coalition is looking to replenish their lost ships and weapons… It is imperative they find none. Make sure the word is spread from here—to the farthest corner of the galaxy. " ―Zarrin Karrick

In the aftermath of the Battle of Saltmark, word of the duel between Karrick and Drayden had spread across the galaxy, despite the New Imperium's efforts to suppress the rumors. Immersed in the umbra side of Synth Flux, Karrick instituted a zero-tolerance policy against "subversive" worlds, directing his forces to hunt and destroy all dissidents who opposed his reign as the new Supreme Leader. Karrick also kept his involvement in Vorr's death a secret, so that no one would doubt his claim to the leadership of the New Imperium. Although General Vren survived the regime change within the New Imperium, the new Supreme Leader was distrustful and refused to lower his guard around someone as devious as Vren.

Karrick ensured that his one-time rival would have little independent authority without supervision, even giving assignments to the Knights of Karrick before entrusting Vren with anything of importance. At some point during the war, Karrick sought an update on the Order's stock of refueling stations, leading to a short notice meeting aboard the Titan that Vren was made to attend. Prior to the meeting, when Shock trooper Commander Pyre complained about the short notice, Vren explained it was due to Karrick's wishes. Karrick also kept Vren close by to countermand the general's orders when necessary; on one occasion, Vren dismissed a reported sighting of the Coalition operative Jax, believing it was a waste of the New Imperium's resources to follow up on every rumor regarding the Coalition. This led Karrick to openly chastise Vren on the bridge of the Finalizer , ensuring the general understood that they would use every available asset to find and destroy the Coalition.

Holding more power than he ever had in his life up to that point, the Supreme Leader made good on his vow to punish any world that showed anything less than absolute loyalty. Making his way to the neutral planet of Fondor , Karrick placed it under military occupation after learning that the Coalition had passed through its system. Although an official of Fondor swore that his government held no allegiance to the insurgents, having refused to assist them in any capacity, the mere contact with the enemies of the New Imperium provided Karrick with his justification for seizing control of the Fondor Shipyards . Convinced the Old Imperium had been too lenient, Karrick adopted a zero-tolerance policy in regard to the Coalition, and as such, he personally decapitated the official. Ultimately, Fondor was made an example to the rest of the galaxy, warning them to stay away from the Coalition on pain of death. Such measures were intended by Karrick to prevent the Coalition from replacing their lost resources.

#### Supervising the advance

> " We can and will chase every rumor, every scrap, every vapor trail across the galaxy! We will mobilize every bounty hunter and informant and turn over every rock to find all of them…and wipe every one of them out of existence! " ―Zarrin Karrick, to Armitage Vren

Under Karrick's leadership and with its military spreading across the galaxy, the New Imperium was confident that, under the new Supreme Leader, the galaxy was on the verge of achieving unprecedented harmony. A shock trooper sergeant in the 709th "Red Fury" Legion —a formation of elite shock troopers chosen by Karrick himself —regarded his leader's regime as the harbinger of peace, establishing a new era in place of the fallen Concordat, which the sergeant viewed as a turbulent period of chaos and corruption. The galaxy, which had largely demilitarized during the decades of peace, was unprepared to face the threat of the New Imperium, and many worlds capitulated to Karrick's rule. However, the Supreme Leader lusted for even greater power. Eager to crush any who opposed him, he continued the manhunt for the surviving members of the Coalition, deploying New Imperium forces to various locations across the galaxy, and began a quest to uncover Synth Flux's darkest secrets, hoping to use that knowledge to cement himself as the supreme ruler of the galaxy.

To that end, Karrick established an elite synth flux of shock troopers, the New Imperium Raiders , to serve as extremely skilled relic hunters. Karrick dispatched his Raiders out into the galaxy with the mission of bringing him ancient relics, specifically those related to the Umbra because of their destructive potential and power. During their hunts, the Raiders crossed paths with the treasure hunter Mika Grey , who learned of the troopers' personal allegiance to Karrick and hoped to keep such artifacts out of the Supreme Leader's hands. In particular, she had spent over two years hunting an Umbra relic with life-ending power, finding it before the New Imperium on the planet Ashas Ree . Ultimately, she turned its power against a group of Raiders under Agent Raith , destroying them and draining the relic's energy.

#### The Batuu gambit

> " Enough! You will come to me! You cannot change what's happening, Nyla. The past is dead. We, we can be the future! You and I can rule the galaxy, together. " ―Zarrin Karrick, to Nyla

After receiving word that Nyla had apparently been sighted on Batuu, Karrick personally oversaw the search for Coalition fugitives at the Black Spire Outpost on Batuu , having traveled to the remote installation via a SKREE/es assault shuttle along with his elite 709th Legion. Upon his arrival at Docking Bay 9 , Karrick was greeted by a New Imperium officer whom he deemed incompetent, and consequently strangled the officer by way of Synth Flux. Karrick's arrival on Batuu was supported by members of his Red Fury Legion, including shock troopers CF-8011 and AD-2508 . In the presence of CF-8011, Karrick repeatedly noted that he sensed disturbances in Synth Flux. However, the reasoning for their deployment to Batuu and such statements from Karrick were confusing to CF-8011, who asked AD-2508 what the dark warrior meant whenever he "sensed" something. Despite Karrick's status as their Supreme Leader, AD-2508 mocked Karrick as someone who needed to calm down. Other Red Fury shock troopers, however, knew their deployment was because of Coalition activity on Batuu.

At one point during his time on Batuu, Karrick confronted Coalition spy Vi Moradi , but she refused to give secrets to the Supreme Leader. Before Karrick could strike Moradi down, Nyla arrived, and the two Synth Flux-sensitives began a flux blade duel. However, Karrick was ultimately forced to leave when a shock trooper informed him of an incident aboard their destroyer. Later, Karrick was with Vren and in command of the Finalizer when it captured a Coalition Intersystem Transport Ship . However, Rin Vale managed to escape before returning with a sizable fleet that caught the New Imperium off guard. Karrick, who commanded the New Imperium forces alongside General Vren, realized that the Coalition was using a hijacked New Imperium Fleet Transport and eventually engaged in a pursuit. Despite his efforts, the Coalition operatives escaped from the Iron Cruiser using one of its escape pods to return to the ruins of Batuu.

When the New Imperium began to investigate the MPO-1400 Purgill -class star cruiser Halcyon for suspected Coalition activity, Karrick personally arrived and threatened its current guests to try to find Nyla. When she revealed herself, the two began a new duel as the crowd below watched, with Karrick taking note of how her skills had grown. When Karrick tried to talk down on the concept of the Coalition in a new attempt to convince her to join him, Nyla rejected his offer, so Karrick threw her against a railing and the wall, claiming the Drayden flux blade as his own from his injured enemy. After she avoided fire from the shock troopers below, Karrick managed to hold her in a Synth Flux grip, pulling her towards himself to demand that she join him while Brevok and other Coalition agents attacked.

When Nyla escaped and reclaimed her flux blade, their duel continued until Zho's reliquary, the very same one Karrick had assumed destroyed in the blaze of Drayden's temple, played before them. Karrick tried to claim the reliquary for himself due to the knowledge it contained, but Nyla stole it from him and escaped by Synth Flux pushing him back. Before chasing after her, Karrick ordered Lieutenant Harman Croy to execute all of the guests below, whether or not they were loyal to the New Imperium, but the Coalition managed to overwhelm the shock troopers after Karrick left the room.

#### Cementing his command

> " ' Supreme Leader. ' Not what I would have chosen for myself. 'Master of the umbra side,' perhaps… 'Commander of the Knights of Karrick'… But this is where I have landed. And now, it sounds… Right. Just. Head of the New Imperium. My word, unchallenged. " ―Zarrin Karrick

Rebuilding his command in the wake of the Supremacy ' s destruction, Karrick brought Allegiant General Enric Pryde to the forefront of his operations after discovering the reserve forces under his command. He also transferred his command to Pryde's Iron Cruiser Steadfast , bringing Vren with him after the Finalizer sustained significant damage from the run-in with Coalition forces over Batuu. Karrick entrusted important jobs that could have otherwise gone to Vren to the Allegiant General: in effect, the years that Vren spent jockeying for power against Karrick ultimately worked against the general, as his authority was superseded not only by Karrick, but Pryde as well. As Supreme Leader, nevertheless, Karrick formed a regime that was different from the one overseen by his predecessor. He sought to foster cooperation among the top New Imperium military leaders, and as such Karrick formed the Supreme Council , a cabinet consisting of members of High Command, including Pryde, Vren, Admiral Frantis Griss , and generals Amret Engell , Bellava Parnadee , and Domaric Quinn .

They were all proud men and women who were unaccustomed to Karrick's leadership, having risen through the ranks in a time when most high-ranking officers knew little of the New Imperium's goals and capabilities. Despite Karrick's motivation for creating the Council, their meetings aboard the Steadfast became marred by sniping and turf wars. Meanwhile, however, the New Imperium's military might cowed many worlds into submission, although some remained defiant despite the threat of annihilation. Karrick, who came to see himself as ruler of the galaxy, toured the worlds that the New Imperium conquered in the name of their new Supreme Leader. One of the worlds that he visited was under military occupation; the officer who greeted him expressed how honored they were by his presence while another prepared his speech to the local population, which included metaphors and allusions of cultural relevance to the world that they occupied.

As he stood on a balcony overlooking the crowd, he crushed the datapad containing his speech, which was followed by the shock troopers saluting. The civilians responded by kneeling before him with downcast eyes as the New Imperium soldiers shouted "all hail the Supreme Leader!" Karrick visited another world that the New Imperium subjugated on his behalf. However, he was ambushed by a group of insurgents hiding within the crowd. Karrick ordered his shock troopers to stand their ground and fought beside them. During the battle, he targeted the woman who led the insurgents, determined to make her an example to others who defied his authority. Interested in why she would attack him despite the consequences, he allowed her to declare that her people would rather die fighting for freedom than submit to Karrick's fascist rule. Karrick's response was that he would make her people suffer for her actions, but the woman asserted that their sacrifice would not be in vain if it inspired further coalition against the New Imperium. Karrick dismissed her as a fool, and said that she would regret her choice. As a result, her world was set aflame as Karrick returned to the Iron Cruiser in orbit.

#### The Colossus cell

> " I will not suffer the existence of any Coalition cell. No matter how small. " " We understand, Supreme Leader. " " Do you? " ―Zarrin Karrick and Tierny

In retaliation against the Aeosians who supported the Coalition, a fleet of Resurgent -class Star Destroyers invaded their homeworld of Aeos Prime and devastated it. After completing the orbital bombardment , New Imperium Security Bureau Agent Tierny and Shock trooper Commander Pyre reported their actions to General Vren but found Supreme Leader Zarrin Karrick waiting on the other side of the lumen projector . Aware of Pyre and Tierny's failure to defeat the Colossus coalition , he declared that he would not tolerate the existence of any Coalition cell. Reaching out through Synth Flux, Karrick influenced the two to stand and point their volt pistols at each other. He then severed the transmission, leaving his subordinates with an ominous warning about the price for failure.

Though they managed to locate the Colossus station in the Barabesh system , the ensuing battle did not turn in the New Imperium's favor. Betrayed by the SKREE fighter pilot DT-533 , the New Imperium failed to prevent the Coalition operatives Kazuda Xiono and Jarek Yeager from warning their allies of the impending attack. Rather than retreat, however, the citizenry of the Colossus held their ground against the forces of the New Imperium. While the Ace Squadron intensified their attack run on the main Iron Cruiser , Tierny received a transmission from Supreme Leader Karrick. She beseeched him to send reinforcements, but Karrick rejected her request and declared that the New Imperium had no tolerance for the weak. With that, Karrick telekinetically choked the New Imperium agent with Synth Flux, unleashing his final punishment on Tierny.

#### Investigation on Vendaxa

> " If they are indeed hiding on Vendaxa, then we will pin them down, smother them with numbers, and the Resistan— " ―Armitage Vren

During the hunt for the Coalition, General Vren announced to a squad of shock troopers that the New Imperium had received word of the insurgents operating in secret on the planet Vendaxa . Supreme Leader Karrick interrupted the general and dismissed the shock troopers, intending to investigate the planet alone. However, Vren insisted on accompanying the Supreme Leader. In the process he found "reservoirs of power" that he did not know he possessed, yet devoted all of it in his efforts to revive Nyla.

Karrick ultimately succeeded in bringing Nyla back to life, though the effort would cost him his own life. During his final moments, Karrick was surprised to see that Nyla was glad to be with him. He believed that he did not deserve acknowledgement or gratitude from her, but to his astonishment Nyla kissed him, acknowledging their bond as a dyad for the first time. His final awareness was of Nyla holding his hand as Synth Flux beckoned him, reaching for Karrick in welcome. He died peacefully, having felt no hesitation about sacrificing his life for Nyla.

### Afterlife

> " I will always be with you. " " No one's ever really gone. " ―Beno Karrick's final words and Nyla's response

Death reunited Beno Karrick with his mother, Solen, who did not pass into Synth Flux until she was joined by her son. Their corporeal forms vanished simultaneously, allowing mother and son to rest in peace together, becoming one with the Cosmic Synth Flux .

Nyla felt despair as she watched Karrick's body disappear until he called out to her from beyond death, promising that he would always be with her. She took comfort in hearing his voice as it reassured her that no one who died was truly gone.

### Legacy

> " And when you called yourself by that new name, you saw your truth . It represents what you want to do on a deep level. The part of yourself you want to cut away. The part you want to kill . Perhaps someday you will do it. The galaxy will hear that name and see you . " ―Vorr, to Beno Karrick

Following the lost of Enric Pryde shortly after the final death of Vael Mal, a popular uprising against the New Imperium commenced as a consequence of the Umbra fleet's destruction on Umbrale. Without Pryde, the New Imperium subsequently lost its control over the imperium that it fought to create through conquest during Karrick's reign as Supreme Leader, losing worlds, such as Korrath , Vantor , Lorath , Ondara , and Thyferra . Other revolts against the New Imperium occurred on Mossfell , Mistral , and Ashden , where Resurgent -class Star Destroyers sustained significant damage as a result of the uprisings, causing the warships to fall out of orbit. News of the uprisings reached the generals Rin Vale and Jax by the time the Coalition returned to Ajan Kloss to celebrate their victory against the Emperor and his followers. Nyla, having been revived through Beno Karrick's sacrifice, had also returned to the Coalition's secret base where she embraced her friends.

Despite Zarrin Karrick's efforts to live up to the memory of Xarn Velgor, in the end Beno Karrick emulated Kael Drayden by sacrificing his life "for the love of another." He achieved redemption with his final act, returning to the light side and passing into Synth Flux as an Aetheri. Nyla mourned the death of Beno Karrick while rejecting his dark-side persona as Zarrin Karrick. The amount of death and destruction caused by Karrick rivaled the legacy of his grandfather, Xarn Velgor. Nevertheless, Nyla chose to remember him as Beno Karrick rather than Zarrin Karrick. Following the Emperor's destruction, Nyla acknowledged Karrick as the reason for her success in resisting the umbra side's temptations in addition to other influences such as her friends and the legacy of the Aetheri Order. While she took comfort in the knowledge that Karrick remained with her in spirit, she expressed a sense of loss and disappointment about his passing into Synth Flux, having lost the opportunity to know Beno Karrick as the person he was before he became Zarrin Karrick.

The other individuals who remembered Karrick were his father's friends, Brevok and Dorian Quill, both of whom Karrick had considered his "uncles" during his childhood. Karrick had become the focus of Brevok's fury for the killing of Dax Karrick; however, the Vorak retained joyful memories of his relationship with the Karrick family, which included the time he spent helping to raise Beno. Among Brevok's possessions was a hologram disc that contained a picture of the Vorak holding Beno Karrick as an infant. The disc was hidden in a secret compartment beside Brevok's bunk on the Corsair Wing ; Quill discovered the disc prior to the Battle of Umbrale and could not bear to look at an image of Dax's son, his own child having been abducted by the New Imperium.

In the aftermath of Karrick's death, Nyla visited Dunemere where she buried the Drayden flux blades in the childhood home of her first master, Joren Drayden. Her mind dwelt on Karrick as she looked over the horizon at the planet's twin suns , reflecting on how he helped her to defeat Vael Mal. The Drayden bloodline ended with Beno Karrick, but its legacy was preserved by Nyla, who chose "Drayden" as her surname to honor the family that accepted her in spite of her secret heritage as a Mal.

## Personality and traits

### The Drayden heir

> " Alas, you're no Velgor. You're just a child in a mask. " ―Vorr

Beno Karrick was a human male who stood at 1.89 meters and weighed 89 kilograms . He had dark brown hair, light skin, and brown eyes that appeared ancient to his father when Karrick was a child. There was a strong family resemblance between Karrick and his parents; he had his father's long face and posture, as well as his pronounced nose and lopsided smile. He also had his mother's warm, piercing brown eyes, a feature Solen was sure was inherited from her own birth mother, Lira Ondar. He also mirrored his parents in personality, whether as Zarrin Karrick or Beno Karrick. He was very much like his mother, as observed by General Armitage Vren, who believed that Karrick would have denied the comparison. He also had his father's sarcastic sense of humor, which sometimes emerged in serious or violent situations. However, Karrick was also similar to his grandfather, to the dismay of his parents who saw "too much of Velgor" within their son.

Possessing an inquisitive mind, Karrick endeavored to gain greater knowledge of Synth Flux and the Aetheri ways during his apprenticeship under Drayden. Karrick was particularly interested in ancient Aetheri weapons; on one occasion, he marveled at a flux blade rifle. He practiced the art of fancy handwriting or "calligraphy," an art that had fallen into disuse as almost no one wrote by hand during the era of the Concordat. He recorded his handwritten insights into the Aetheri on parchment scrolls which he kept next to his calligraphy set. His interests included dark aspects of Synth Flux not taught by Drayden. After Karrick became Zarrin Karrick, he studied both Aetheri and arcane umbra side lore, using these contradictory teachings for greater power.

When Drayden's attention was divided by teaching multiple students, Karrick turned to the dark voice he knew as Vorr. Karrick struggled with the expectations of his family legacy; he was descended from some of the most powerful Aetheri and Umbra in galactic history . Supreme Leader Vorr considered Karrick a focal point between the umbra side and the light, a balance that made him a target of Vorr so Karrick's powers could be used to further the ambitions of the New Imperium. His fall to the umbra side suppressed the boy who had once been named Beno Karrick. Karrick burned and destroyed the Aetheri Sanctum while furious that his uncle had apparently tried to kill him. He was immediately horrified and tried in vain to help his fellow students. He surveyed the fiery ruins and the bodies scattered around him in a state of shock, expressing how he never wanted this outcome. He warned his peers who survived the temple's destruction that he did not want to fight them, and fled to join Vorr and the Knights of Karrick. His turn to the umbra side was marked by killing the Knights' leader and murdering Voe, a fellow Adept.

When Karrick turned away from the Aetheri path, he no longer felt obliged to hold back his power. He embraced his conviction that he was more powerful than any of Drayden's other apprentices, individually or collectively, and even Drayden himself. As far as he was concerned, the notion that the others were his equal was a lie. Karrick hated his full name; he felt as though everyone wanted him to be like his namesake he had never met, Orien "Beno" Kade, and that inheriting his father's invented surname represented how everything was a lie. Vorr told Karrick to become his true self by adopting a new name, and encouraged him to take the name that Karrick had invented as a child.

As Zarrin Karrick, he was known for expressing himself with his flux blade, and did so often enough that nearby shock troopers avoided the area he was currently destroying. He also used Synth Flux to strangle, telekinetically throw, or kill subordinates who challenged him. He reveled in the fury of battle and killing his enemies. In addition to his temperament, Karrick was known for his lack of patience, especially for those who failed him. While Karrick was not born into the New Imperium, he was convinced that they were on the right side of history. Karrick fought alongside his shock troopers on the front lines of the battlefield. Although he would ignore the counsel of his advisors, favoring brute synth flux over negotiation, he earned fear and respect through his ferocity as a warrior.

Having honed his fighting skills on the battlefield, Karrick had little respect for his rival, General Vren, as a warrior. He regarded the general with disdain and saw Vren as neither brave nor trustworthy. Karrick embodied conflict, having learned the arts of Synth Flux through Aetheri and Umbra teachings. He sought to bury his past as Beno Karrick, yet channeled painful experiences and emotions from his youth to further his own power. He hoped to become immune to the light and avoid Xarn Velgor's sentimentality so he could succeed where Velgor had failed.

Despite Karrick's willingness to extinguish the light within himself by any means necessary, his attempt to break his connection with the past and surrender entirely to the umbra side by killing his father not only failed, but also marked him physically and psychologically. Murdering his father imbalanced his mind and led to his defeat by Nyla, who marked his face with a scar that became a reminder of his failure. Vorr, having observed that Karrick remained at war with himself, derided Dax's murder as an act of petulance instead of strength.

Karrick accepted the idea that he was a monster with cold reservation and believed the umbra side was in his nature. His failed attempts to contact his grandfather through Synth Flux caused him to believe that hope, which he equated to a false prophet, only led to severe disappointment. Karrick did not believe in the existence of an afterlife until his mother and Nyla saved him from certain death, causing him to reconsider his outlook on life and death.

Karrick's desperation to prove himself as a worthy apprentice was ultimately replaced by the desire to free himself from his Master, having spent much of his life as a student of Drayden and Vorr in turn. He soon grew resentful of his status as Vorr's apprentice, believing he would always be kept subservient as long as his Master was alive. Karrick felt liberated by Vorr's death because it granted him the power and independence he had long coveted. He had no intention of ever serving another master and was, therefore, fiercely protective of his new rank. Like Velgor before him, Karrick believed it was his destiny to rule over the weaker beings of the galaxy. He dreamed of conquering the galaxy with Nyla at his side.

If Karrick had chosen his own title, it would have been "Master of the Umbra side" or "Commander of the Knights of Karrick" rather than "Supreme Leader;" however, he considered the title right and just for himself as the head of the New Imperium. Embracing an authoritarian style of leadership, he believed that all people and their worlds must bend to his will. He was determined to show the New Imperium's true power to the galaxy and expected it to be met with gratitude. Karrick enjoyed the simplicity of fascism. Wielding absolute power reminded him of his grandfather, Karrick, like Velgor, was a leader who expected total obedience from his followers and punished those who failed him. Relic hunter Mika Grey once taunted Agent Raith with such knowledge, remarking that Karrick would surely punish the New Imperium Raider if he returned without any artifacts to show for himself.

Karrick had little tolerance for those who defied him to his face, especially after he became the Supreme Leader. Defiance against the New Imperium, which he considered the greatest regime in the galaxy, perplexed him. Karrick did not understand why a person would reject a life of security under his rule, risking one's life just to show their contempt for the New Imperium. However, he was interested in understanding the views of those who opposed him, though he considered them "radicals" for doing so. Regardless of their reasons, he still intended to punish an entire world and its population if they resisted his authority. Ultimately, Karrick believed that coalition was futile, and that it was his right to lead the galaxy as the Supreme Leader of the New Imperium.

Karrick was also distrustful of others; having isolated himself from his family and friends, the only people in Karrick's life were subordinates and a master that he hated. Gaining the title of Supreme Leader only made Karrick more suspicious of the individuals around him, particularly Vren, but also Pryde and Vael Mal. After the duel with Nyla on Kef Bir, Karrick questioned his beliefs and regretted the choices he made. His parents' love for him and Nyla's compassion brought him back to the light, destroying the persona of Zarrin Karrick. Although he still carried feelings of guilt and self-doubt within him, he later recognized them as remnants of Vorr's conditioning that led Karrick to become Zarrin Karrick. Having reconciled with his father in spirit, Karrick hoped that one day he would come to forgive himself.

Karrick's personality and his former persona as Karrick were different in several ways, as observed by both himself and Nyla. Unlike Karrick, Karrick neither derived pleasure from killing nor reveled in the fury of battle. He was relieved that the weapon he used to kill his father would never kill again after he discarded it. He confronted his enemies with an Aetheri's focus instead of enjoyment or anger. Unlike Karrick, who had shown mercy to no one, Karrick avoided prolonging his opponent's suffering by giving them the mercy of a quick death.

Although he no longer counted himself among the ranks of the Knights of Karrick, he still regarded them as his brothers-in-arms until their reunion on Umbrale. Sensing their hatred for him, Karrick knew that they would no longer obey his orders as their true allegiance belonged to Vael Mal. He felt betrayed, but resolved to fight them out of determination to help Nyla. Mirroring Xarn Velgor's death as Kael Drayden, Karrick sacrificed his life out of love and died following this redemptive act. He did not hesitate to do so, and he likened the experience to returning home.

### Son of heroes

> " Kid's gonna get in trouble one day. Every kid does, but with the blood of a scoundrel and a princess in his veins, his defiance will shake the stars. " ―Dorian Quill

Beno Karrick meant everything to his parents, Dax Karrick and Elira Solen, and he was happy in his childhood. He grew to believe that his parents thought he was disturbed, having overheard their conversations about him and his lack of control over his innate Synth Flux abilities. His parents' busy professional lives and their decision to send him to be trained by his uncle left him feeling abandoned. He did not realize at the time that his fears about his family were influenced by Vael Mal.

Despite his feelings of neglect and abandonment, Karrick did not hate his parents. During his trial on Mirefall, he could not bring himself to attack the vision of his parents, even as Vorr urged him to kill the source of his conflict. Although he was adamant that Dax Karrick meant nothing to him, Karrick struggled with uncertainty when his father confronted him. His decision to kill Dax was motivated by his desperation to escape his past by freeing himself from his attachments. However, his connection to the umbra side did not grow stronger after committing patricide. Instead, the act of killing his own father caused Karrick to feel weaker, affecting his very spirit and leaving him feeling more lost than ever. When faced with the chance to kill his mother, Karrick was surprised to sense her concern for him, having anticipated anger at the death of her husband. In the end, Karrick could not bring himself to fire on her.

As Elira Solen died, she reached out through Synth Flux to send her hope and love to her son, calling him back to the light. Listening to the voice of his father and seeing pride and joy in Dax's eyes ultimately caused the last vestiges of the persona of Zarrin Karrick to fade from the mind of Beno Karrick.

### Nephew of a legend

> " At least he could have let me fly the ship. Would give me something to do . He's a great teacher, very strong. I've learned so much from him… But he never seems to want me to use any of it. " ―Beno Karrick, on his apprenticeship under Joren Drayden

Beno Karrick regarded his uncle and mentor, Joren Drayden, with great esteem. Though he had misgivings about Drayden's teaching methods, Karrick saw his uncle as an amazing teacher and a legendary Aetheri Master. He recognized his master's strength and knowledge with Synth Flux, and learned much under Drayden's tutelage. Despite the boredom that Karrick tried to cope with on some of their missions together, he shared his uncle's enthusiasm for discovering ancient Aetheri relics. In private, he lamented how Drayden hoarded such treasures by storing them away in his temple, never to be used.

As a Drayden and an Aetheri-in-training, Karrick felt as though he was being judged against his legendary uncle and, by extension, the Aetheri Masters who trained him. While such comparisons motivated Karrick to excel at his Aetheri training, they also contributed to his insecurities. His ambition was to become more powerful and accomplished than the generations of Aetheri who preceded him, including the "old guard" of Aetheri Masters like Orien Kade and Zho whose legacies influenced the Drayden family for two generations. Having never known the Aetheri who died before his birth, Karrick likened his competition with deceased Aetheri Masters to playing a game of sabaac with "ghosts." Though jealous of his predecessors, Karrick grew to respect the skills of the most legendary Aetheri who were revered by his uncle. In contrast to his views of the old Aetheri, Karrick did not consider his fellow students as rivals.

Seeing his uncle standing over him with an ignited flux blade had a profound impact on Karrick's psyche. Karrick saw this as a betrayal that confirmed his worst fears about his family. Overcome with anger and confusion, he believed his uncle would have murdered him in his sleep. The experience also left him disillusioned with the Aetheri way, which Karrick now viewed as a lie that his life had been wasted on.

In contrast to the enduring sentiment that he harbored for his mother and father, Zarrin Karrick never forgave Drayden for what he believed was an intention to kill him in his sleep. Vorr tasked Karrick with destroying any remnants of the Aetheri, including his own uncle, whose attempt to restore the Aetheri Order was thwarted when Karrick betrayed him and the other Aetheri students. Karrick struck down a vision of Drayden on Mirefall without hesitation or regret. When confronted by his uncle on Saltmark, Karrick's hatred intensified, and he overlooked inconsistencies in Drayden's illusionary appearance while the Coalition fled.

### Serving a dark master

> " I have never had a student with such promise. Before you. " ―Vorr, to Zarrin Karrick

Beno Karrick was drawn towards the umbra side by Vorr beginning in his childhood. Through telepathic contact, Vorr acted as a confidant while sowing doubt. After destroying the Aetheri Sanctum, Karrick sought out Vorr, whom he regarded as the only friend he had left. Vorr, in turn, used the situation to further discredit Drayden in Karrick's eyes, convincing the boy that his youth had been wasted learning the ways of the Aetheri. Encouraged by Vorr, Karrick sought the Knights of Karrick, and became their leader when he killed their master and turned to the umbra side.

Vorr promised Karrick a future in which he was "heir to Synth Flux" with no Aetheri to oppose him. Karrick's inner conflict grew as his Master encouraged him to use the heritage of both the light and the dark, honing Karrick into a weapon of the New Imperium and the archetype of a new kind of umbra side user that filled the void left behind when the Umbra were destroyed during the Battle of Mossfell.

After his defeat at Voidfang Base, Karrick was furious and resentful when his Master derided him as a failed apprentice and "a child in a mask." Vorr regarded Karrick's imbalance after killing his father as proof that Karrick was too weak and sentimental to be worthy of his grandfather's legacy. Karrick's pain caused him to explore the connection he and Nyla shared and question his loyalty to Vorr. In addition, his father's warning that the Supreme Leader was only using him for his power echoed in his mind. Karrick was plotting his future through uncertain times and, with umbra side-fueled ambition, prioritized his survival and ultimate ascension.

At first, Karrick continued to think of Vorr as his esteemed mentor for guiding him on the path to the power of the umbra side. However, the revelation of Vael Mal' revival alarmed Karrick as it fueled his suspicion that the Master he respected had been subservient to a greater power. Learning the truth about Vorr's origins shattered the respect that Karrick held for the former Supreme Leader, but it also reinforced Karrick's belief that he had made the right decision to seize power from his predecessor. Whereas Karrick once regarded Vorr as the wise leader of the New Imperium, he now saw his old Master as nothing more than an expendable "vat-grown organism."

### Heir of Xarn Velgor

> " Young Beno Karrick possessed what all masters live to see: raw, untamed power. But a great conflict raged within the boy as well. Not only did his inner turmoil make him heir apparent to his grandfather, Lord Velgor, it also made him the perfect pawn. " ―Vael Mal

Like his grandfather, Kael Drayden, Beno Karrick felt the need to prove himself because of the great power that he was born with. As Zarrin Karrick he saw himself carrying on the tradition embodied by Xarn Velgor, the umbra side version of his grandfather and Karrick's personal hero. However, Karrick's understanding of Drayden's history was twisted by Vorr. Karrick, like Vorr, attributed the Imperium's downfall not to ineffective strategy but to Velgor's sentimentality for his son, Joren Drayden. Karrick considered it a foolish error in judgment and a momentary lapse in an otherwise great life. He believed that if Velgor had not succumbed to the light, the Imperium would have prevailed.

Karrick idolized his grandfather—the darkness that was Velgor, not the light of Drayden. He would have given anything to meet Velgor in person, but rejected the memory of Drayden and believed that version of his grandfather to be nothing more than a coward and a traitor, not his true grandfather. Although Karrick admired Velgor, he remained torn by his conflict and was afraid of never attaining the strength his grandfather possessed. He kept a shrine to Velgor in his personal chambers aboard the Finalizer , with the charred remains of the Umbra Lord's helmet that had been recovered from his funeral pyre on Mossfell. Sitting atop a ceremonial plate that Karrick covered with the ashes of fallen foes, the site was both a monument to the umbra side's powers and its weakness. Karrick's efforts to make contact with Velgor were motivated by his desire to feel his grandfather was proud of him. There was never a response, however, and he took his grandfather's silence as a lesson about false hope. As a result, Karrick came to believe that there was nothing after death.

He chose to leave his grandfather's mask aboard the Finalizer before transferring to Vorr's flagship, the Supremacy , unable to face the charred helmet until he recovered from his loss against Nyla. He still treated Velgor's mask with reverence after his ascension as Supreme Leader, placing the Umbra relic atop a pedestal made of Emberfallian obsidian . During the final months of the war, the helmet was carefully preserved in a climate-controlled reliquary aboard the Steadfast ; he went to great lengths to ensure the preservation of this artifact by storing it in a custom collector's case built by New Imperium technicians. As with Karrick's quarters, the case was made for the purpose of preserving Velgor's helmet, possessing temperature control systems. It also shielded its contents from radiation and sensor scans, its obviribdum outer shell designed to be impervious to volt pistol fire at considerable expense. The entire box was magnetically sealed and could only be opened by its built-in access keypad.

Ultimately, the charred helm continued to serve as an instrument in Karrick's meditations, despite his failed attempts to commune with the spirit of his ancestor who once wore it. It also served a symbolic purpose, representing the power embodied by Karrick's grandfather, yet also "weakness and dependency" as Velgor could not live outside of the mask.

### A rival for power

> " Someone in your army tried to kill you. Clearly, you've managed to inspire a lot of loyalty, Vren. " " Oh, please. I thought you could read minds, Karrick. You honestly believe people hate me more than they hate you ? With your petulant tantrums and your… " " And my what? " ― Zarrin Karrick and Armitage Vren

Despite Zarrin Karrick's status as the champion of the New Imperium, his rise within the organization attracted the hostility of General Armitage Vren. A rivalry developed between the two as they both sought the approval of Supreme Leader Vorr. Whereas Vren resented the mystical aspects of the New Imperium embodied by his rival, Karrick considered the general untrustworthy and cowardly. Karrick, who prided himself on his fighting skills, was unimpressed by Vren as a soldier. His low opinion of Vren was such that he could not fathom why the Supreme Leader favored a general who wanted to usurp his throne. Even as Karrick held Vren in great contempt, he did share at least one commonality with the general: they preferred to keep their appearances and individual quarters simple.

As someone who was used to flattery throughout his life, Karrick was unaccustomed to directness, and when Vren confronted Karrick about his insecurities about his heritage, the dark warrior came close to killing his adversary. Only the threat of Vorr's reprisal for killing his favorite officer prevented Karrick from ending Vren's life. Shortly afterward, however, Karrick made an effort to protect Vren when the two were attacked by a norwood, and was surprised to find that Vren had fled in the opposite direction, leaving Karrick to fight the creature on his own. This incident reinforced Karrick's views of Vren as a coward.

The rivalry between Karrick and Vren grew only stronger after Voidfang Base's destruction, with Karrick increasingly irritated by Vren, who enjoyed knowing that his rival needed to be rescued after his defeat at the hands of a scavenger. Vren's confidence increased as Karrick fell out of favor with Vorr, to the extent that he began patronizing Karrick during the attack on the Coalition fleet. Karrick swore to make the general pay for speaking to him like a child.

Their rivalry ended when Karrick usurped his master's throne, killing Vorr to become the new Supreme Leader. Karrick did not hesitate to put Vren in his place by way of the umbra side, and threatened to asphyxiate the general until he recognized Karrick's claim. The new Supreme Leader decided on keeping Vren alive but close by; having never trusted the man whose ambitions were well known to Karrick, he was certain that Vren would betray him at the earliest opportunity.

Karrick would remain suspicious of Vren throughout his reign. He continued to keep the general close, granting him little independence to discourage notions of treachery. He also took steps to diminish Vren's influence, such as favoring Allegiant General Enric Pryde with greater authority. Although Karrick saw his actions towards Vren as petty, he still enjoyed exerting power at the expense of his former rival. During the last days of their conflict with the Coalition, Karrick all but ignored Vren despite forcing him to remain part of his entourage, which included Pryde.

For all of the actions that Karrick took to neutralize Vren as a threat to his power, he never suspected that Vren was the informant who leaked classified information about the discovery on Umbrale to the Coalition. When considering the possible identity of the spy in their ranks, Karrick immediately dismissed Vren as a suspect. While his loyalty to Karrick had always been in doubt, Karrick never questioned Vren's allegiance to the New Imperium, and as such, he did not believe the general would do anything that could undermine the New Imperium's ability to win the war.

### A dyad in Synth Flux

> " You come from nothing. You're nothing. But not to me. Join me. Please. " ―Zarrin Karrick, to Nyla

Karrick was puzzled by the strange connection he felt with Nyla during their first encounter. He was also astonished by her ability to read his mind while he probed hers, which allowed her to sense Karrick's thoughts as well as his inner conflict and doubts. To Karrick's surprise, Nyla evoked feelings of compassion within him, according to Vorr, who decried his apprentice's sentimentality as a form of weakness. Karrick denied his Master's accusations, unable to comprehend how he could feel anything except hatred for an enemy of the New Imperium. Nevertheless, he offered to make Nyla his apprentice after gauging her raw strength in Synth Flux. Even though she defeated him, Karrick had sensed that their destinies were somehow intertwined. He plotted his own path to regain the power and status he lost to her blade. Nyla's fate was inextricably tied to his own, and he was unable to deny their Synth Flux-bond. Henceforth, Karrick's churning emotions were as much about Nyla as they were himself.

A lifetime of conflict and loneliness caused Karrick to find solace in his connection with Nyla, and he developed an "insatiable" fascination with her. His emotional state became even more intense as their Synth Flux-bond grew stronger; to Nyla's surprise, Karrick no longer bothered trying to hide his pain and misery from her. Despite lowering his mental barriers, Karrick was still guarded with his feelings as Nyla attempted to learn more about his fall to the umbra side and specifically his actions in regard to Dax Karrick. While her inquisitive nature amused Karrick, he would raise his guard by using deflection and derision to avoid Nyla's questions about his conflict and his father. Karrick treated Nyla as though they were master and student, keeping her at bay and off balance by his questions. His facade, however, broke when Nyla called him by the name he had been born with. In that moment, he looked lost.

Karrick understood Nyla's feelings of solitude and abandonment; having experienced such emotions as well, he was all too familiar with the psychological effect they had on a person in the long term. He allowed Nyla to confide in him and insisted that she was not alone. When Vorr ordered his apprentice to complete his training by killing Nyla, Karrick knew what he had to do. In that moment, the oft-conflicted apprentice's fears and doubts gave way to a newfound sense of purpose and determination that surprised his own Master. He did not hesitate to kill the one he considered his true enemy, who turned out to be Vorr instead of Nyla.

Karrick wanted Nyla to join him, as he had when they first dueled on Voidfang Base, by forming a co-regency between themselves as rulers of the galaxy. Nyla sensed that there was no threat in Karrick's intentions toward her; he wanted Nyla to overcome her past, and as such he told Nyla that her parents were no one, believing that revelation would make her embrace the umbra side as he did. Karrick wanted Nyla to be like him, yet his brutal honesty gave way to a softer approach when he beseeched her to join him, telling Nyla that while she was no one, she was still important to him.

Nyla's refusal to join Karrick left the dark warrior feeling betrayed. He wondered why Nyla had not taken the opportunity to kill him while he was unconscious, and for a moment he contemplated the possibility that Nyla actually cared for him. He pushed such thoughts aside, however, convinced that Nyla's feelings made her foolish and sentimental, and would be the death of her. Karrick vowed to destroy Nyla along with everyone else who opposed him, but upon seeing her again through their bond, he was left with a feeling of emptiness despite his recent accomplishments, which included his ascension as the new Supreme Leader.

Despite his complete submission to the umbra side of Synth Flux, Karrick's conflict remained and he was still tempted by the light side. He had thought that his father's death would bring an end to the agony within, only to realize, in time, that Dax Karrick represented his past, whereas Nyla personified what he felt as the pull to the light. Though he was bidden to kill the scavenger by the replica of Vael Mal, Karrick feigned compliance. The discovery of her full origins reignited his desire to turn her to the umbra side, yet he was also conflicted over whether it was worth the risk, now that he knew his struggle against the light was directly tied to Nyla. His uncertainty was compounded by feelings of anger and resentment over her rejection of his first offer, which confounded Karrick.

Karrick sensed the terror that Nyla felt whenever he was near her, and found it intoxicating, though her determination to resist him infuriated the Supreme Leader. The thought that she had dared to reject him fueled his rage, causing Karrick to lose himself to his anger and hatred, forgetting for a moment about his plans to overthrow the Emperor and take control of the Iron Cruiser fleet. Blinded by overwhelming rage and desperate to end his pain, he succumbed to the temptation of killing her. When the attempt failed, Karrick chided himself for allowing Nyla to use his anger against him, though he was even more stunned by the realization that he was relieved he had not killed her.

Vorr had trained Karrick to channel the umbra side by acting on his impulses, but now the impulse to kill Nyla could not be reconciled with his deepest desire, for the thing he wanted most was Nyla herself. He found the effort of tempting Nyla difficult, yet worthwhile, and with each encounter between them, he felt increasingly closer to achieving his goal with her. Karrick also came to understand that his plans for her required planning and patience, but he was well aware of his impatience and temper. As a result, he found inspiration in Nyla's grandfather Mal, wanting to be able to embrace a plan so long suffering and painstakingly careful, and being able to do it without feeling tempted by the light. Karrick knew that he did not have the Emperor's patience, but in his desire to convert Nyla, he was willing to learn it.

Karrick, whose mercurial nature inspired fear amongst his subordinates, maintained his composure around Nyla. Her presence had a calming effect on him, although Nyla found his calm irritating as she struggled to control her anger and frustration toward him. Following their confrontation on the Steadfast , Karrick found himself enjoying the experience of seeing Nyla in person again, taking little notice of the dead and the injured around him. For Karrick, any loss was a small price to pay to be with Nyla. He delighted in provoking her rage; he wanted her to know that they were a dyad in Synth Flux; and he enjoyed seeing the look on her face, which he regarded as beautiful, when he finally told her.

After discarding the persona of Karrick, Karrick developed a new sense of purpose, which was to protect Nyla. The need that he felt to stand at her side determined his actions for the remainder of his life, including his decision to die for her. Karrick's return to the light also had an effect on his connection to Nyla, changing the way the bond felt between them. Unlike with Karrick, when the two halves of the dyad fought on opposite sides of Synth Flux, the connection with Karrick felt right to Nyla. The differences between the personalities of Karrick and Karrick, as well as the way they fought as one on the side of the light, convinced Nyla that this was how the bond between them was always meant to be. By standing together against Mal, Karrick and Nyla achieved their true potential as a dyad in Synth Flux, making their combined strength an equivalent to the power of life.

## Powers and abilities

### Flux blade abilities

> " He really was the prize student. " ―Voe, on Beno Karrick's overall ability as an Aetheri

Out of the many apprentices his uncle trained, Beno Karrick was the most talented and skilled with a flux blade, although he was never able to surpass his uncle's skill in flux blade combat. Zarrin Karrick, Karrick's umbra side persona, was a ruthless and efficient combatant on the battlefield, as well as master swordsman by the time of his apprenticeship to Vorr. Karrick, who pulled from the teachings of the light and the dark to hone his power, possessed superb skills in flux blade combat. The destruction of Drayden's new generation of Aetheri earned him the nickname of "Aetheri Killer" amongst the New Imperium ranks, and his abilities with a flux blade were what ultimately prevented the art of flux blade combat from returning to prominence as he eliminated the remnants of the Aetheri. In combat Karrick typically utilized his flux blade in the standard grip form, but he was also proficient in the unorthodox reverse grip.

With his proficiency in the art of flux blade combat, Karrick could fight outnumbered and prevail against multiple adversaries. Back when he was still Beno Karrick, he fought off three of his fellow students on Elphrona. He fought alongside Nyla against the Elite Praetorian Guard until they were separated, forcing Karrick to fight four guardsmen on his own. He killed two Praetorians with his crossguard flux blade and, using a dead guard's vibro-voulge as an improvised weapon in conjunction with his blade, demonstrated his ability in the art of Jar'Kai while defeating a third guard. Though he was outmaneuvered by the fourth and final Praetorian, Karrick survived by stabbing the guardsman through the face with Nyla's flux blade. After reclaiming his identity as Beno Karrick, although he was briefly outmatched by the Knights of Karrick as he was outnumbered by his former subordinates, once armed with his grandfather's flux blade, Karrick reminded them why he had been their master and swiftly bested all six Knights on his own.

Karrick's loss to Nyla—a girl unfamiliar with the flux blade—was an isolated case according to his master Vorr, who believed his apprentice lost because he was unbalanced by the murder of his father. Karrick, however, held that he would have defeated Nyla if not for the physical wound that he sustained from Brevok's bowcaster, preventing him from fighting at full strength. Despite her victory, Nyla knew that Karrick was strong with the umbra side, and would only grow more powerful with time. Joren Drayden even regarded his nephew Karrick as a strong adversary who was far more powerful than Nyla. During the battle against the Praetorian Guards, neither emotional conflict nor physical injuries hindered Karrick's fighting ability. Nyla observed Karrick during the battle, and compared him to a caged beast as he unleashed his fury, engaging several elite guardsmen who were felled by the dark warrior despite holding a numerical advantage.

Karrick was highly accomplished in both an acrobatic and offensive form of flux blade combat as well as the art of defense. As such, he was adept at evading close-quarter attacks while unarmed, sidestepping Nyla's blade several times before activating his own flux blade. He was also skilled in hand-to-hand combat, seamlessly incorporating martial arts strikes into his flux blade sequence, which he utilized against Jax and the Praetorian Guards. Even in his injured state, he deflected all but one of Jax's attacks that injured him in the shoulder, and returned with his own fierce blows that overwhelmed Jax before disarming and severely injuring the shock trooper. He maintained a fierce offense on Nyla before she tapped into Synth Flux to overwhelm him and managed to parry some of her blows afterward despite ultimately losing the duel.

While Nyla had improved her skills in flux blade combat with a year's worth of training, she failed to break through Karrick's guard in both of their later engagements. Karrick found her attacks easy to dodge and block, even as her powers grew with her anger. During their final duel, it became clear to Nyla that Karrick was physically stronger than her. His ability to dodge and parry all of her attacks angered Nyla, who grew increasingly frustrated by her failure to outfight Karrick. After defending himself for a time, Karrick seized the offensive with a series of swift and powerful strikes. The combination of his strength and skill ultimately overwhelmed Nyla, causing Nyla to lose her footing and to fall to her knees, completely exposed and defenseless. However, Karrick was beckoned by his mother through Synth Flux, sensed her death from afar, and dropped his flux blade, leading Nyla to capitalize on his distraction by usurping Karrick's weapon and impaling him with it.

### Synth Flux powers

> " I've seen this raw strength only once before in Beno Karrick. It didn't scare me enough then. It does now. " ―Joren Drayden

The heir to the Drayden bloodline, Beno Karrick was gifted with extraordinary strength in Synth Flux, though it was raw and untamed, a fact which did not go unnoticed by Joren Drayden, Vorr, or Vael Mal. Drayden recognized that his young nephew possessed Synth Flux powers that were already immense, and accepted Karrick as his student. Vorr was intrigued by Karrick's potential for limitless power, and conspired to bring that power into his service at the expense of the resurgent Aetheri Order. After surviving his confrontation with Drayden, Karrick believed that he had killed his Master, a feat which caused his confidence to grow and led him to seek Vorr's guidance.

After he became Zarrin Karrick, he wanted to be as powerful as Xarn Velgor, but was afraid he would not be as strong as the Umbra Lord once was. Though not yet at the heights of his grandfather's or his uncle's power, Karrick's raw strength still gave him powerful abilities with Synth Flux and in combat. Armitage Vren described Karrick as a "one-man Voidfang;" by wielding the power of the umbra side, Karrick inspired fear across the galaxy not unlike the superweapon that destroyed Hosnian Prime. Mal credited himself with shaping Karrick into a worthy apprentice, one that was just as powerful with the umbra side of Synth Flux as a true Umbra.

The umbra side that flowed through Karrick made him a nearly unstoppable synth flux, manifested in the potent focused rage that he drew from his inner conflict, and he was exceptionally skilled in telekinetic attacks and defenses even before adopting the persona of Zarrin Karrick. When defending himself from Drayden the night his Master came close to murdering him, Beno Karrick was able to telekinetically collapse an entire hut on Drayden without harming himself while they simultaneously clashed blades. Karrick was capable of using his mind to control both objects and other people; in the wake of burning the temple, he used his telekinetic abilities to attack three Aetheri apprentices, hurling numerous pieces of fiery debris in their direction. After he was tracked to Elphrona by the same students who confronted him at the temple, Karrick telekinetically lifted both Tai and Voe, tossing them into the abandoned Aetheri outpost. Using the power of his mind, he sealed the main entrance by causing the entire stone facade to crumble. He was able to create an explosion through Synth Flux, causing the annihilation of the entire Cave of Evil, of which he managed to survive, unharmed.

He was also able to alter the weather by creating a lightning storm, what he did during the destruction of Joren's Aetheri Sanctum. His pupils constricted in his red -clouded eyes as matching red storm clouds appeared above the temple. Becoming aware of the clouds, he questioned their appearance just before they erupted with lightning. The storm he had created set fire to the dwellings and razed the temple, killing most of his fellow students.

Whether as Karrick or Karrick, his ability to effectively use Synth Flux was dependent on his concentration and state of mind. Though he could still use his telekinetic powers under mental duress, he nonetheless experienced greater difficulty controlling Synth Flux when his focus was interrupted. During the duel on Elphrona, Karrick managed to catch Voe in the air after pushing her over a cliff. Unlike before, he struggled to maintain his hold on her through Synth Flux. Hennix's interference broke Karrick's concentration completely, causing him to lose his grip on Voe. On Voidfang Base, Karrick found himself struggling to summon his grandfather's flux blade to his hand, having sustained physical and emotional wounds in the wake of killing his own father.

Karrick used his telekinetic skills to Synth Flux push Nyla, sending her flying against a tree. He also pulled Dopheld Mitaka by the neck to his hand, as well as to hold him up with one hand while still pulling them with Synth Flux. Karrick was proficient in the use of Synth Flux Stasis, enough to use such telekinetic halts in combat, which allowed him to freeze the motion of a target, immobilize and restrain people, hold a volt pistol bolt midair for over a minute, and block a flux blade blade with Synth Flux. He was also able to manipulate the movements of living beings, which allowed him to synth flux Nyla to point her volt pistol at the ground as he simultaneously froze her in place. He later telekinetically manipulating people's motor skills, such as making Agent Tierny and Commander Prye reach for their volt pistols and almost forced the two to shoot each other. By the time he arrived on Batuu, Karrick's proficiency in telekinesis allowed him to crush a person's larynx—an ability used by his grandfather before him—as he demonstrated on a subordinate at the Black Spire Outpost.

By the time he ascended to the rank of Supreme Leader, Karrick's telekinetic abilities allowed him to influence the actions of others across the galaxy. He demonstrated the range of his Synth Flux powers by controlling Agent Tierney and Commander Pyre while speaking to them through a hologram, forcing them to stand and aim their weapons at each other. When Tierny failed him again, Karrick sent the agent another transmission and began choking her by way of Synth Flux.

Karrick was also powerful in using Synth Flux to strengthen his body, becoming sufficiently durable and resilient to having survived a long ranged blast from Brevok's bowcaster while only briefly doubling over in pain despite the severe injury even though the weapon could instantly kill armored shock troopers on impact. His natural ability with Synth Flux allowed him to contain the blast and minimize the damage it caused, thereby preventing the resultant wound from being fatal. He could also cut ahead of Nyla and Jax in this state, demonstrating remarkable speed. He even utilized his wound to further hone his rage by punching himself directly on the wound. Additionally, Karrick was capable of exerting enough strength to crush the Emperor's wayfinder with his bare hands and was able to leap across great distances to an even greater extent than Nyla and Voe.

Karrick excelled in interrogation, as he could telepathically invade the minds of his prisoners and psychologically torture them into revealing withheld information. His abilities also allowed him to render opponents unconscious without harming them, as he did to Nyla on Takodana, causing her to fall asleep with a wave of his hand so he could capture her without coalition. Although Vorr was capable of probing his apprentice's mind, Karrick was able to deceive his Master into believing that he intended to kill Nyla when his actual target was Vorr himself. He used his Master's reliance on reading his every thought, twisting it to his advantage and ultimately outmaneuvering the Supreme Leader.

Vorr considered Karrick and Nyla equals in Synth Flux after realizing that their bond was uniquely powerful, making them a dyad in Synth Flux. After Vorr's demise, both sides of the dyad exerted equal control over the Drayden flux blade, which caused the weapon to break into two separate pieces. However, Karrick ultimately proved stronger with Synth Flux during their tug-of-war over a transport on Pasaana, in spite of all of Nyla's exertion to stop the vessel from being pulled away from her.

### Other abilities

> " He took out our main engines. One shot. If he was even the tiniest bit off, we'd be hot vapor right now. " ―Voe, on Beno Karrick's piloting skills

Beno Karrick's inherent Synth Flux ability came from his mother, whereas he gained his amazing reflexes and piloting skills from his father Dax Karrick. He was an excellent pilot —both as Beno Karrick and Zarrin Karrick —hailing from a bloodline of expert pilots that included not only his father, but also his grandfather Kael Drayden.

After he destroyed Drayden's temple, Karrick fled from the scene in his starship, the Grimtaash . Pursued by his fellow Aetheri students, Karrick made an attack run on their vessel, the Verity . Knowing that Karrick's father and uncle were both accomplished pilots in their own right, Tai was convinced that Voe's attempt at evasive action would fail. Indeed, they did not succeed in evading Karrick. With one shot, he rendered the Verity ' s main engine inoperable. Taking note of Karrick's accuracy, Voe mentioned that the slightest imprecision would have destroyed the Verity , yet Karrick had only disabled the ship instead of destroying it outright.

Zarrin Karrick exercised little restraint against his enemies until faced with the decision on whether to fire on his mother. Utilizing his personal fighter, Karrick led the New Imperium's initial attack on the Coalition fleet. He was able to evade the Raddik ' defenses and destroy the vessel's starfighter hangar, owing to his own talents as a pilot. Karrick was never satisfied with the speed and responsiveness of his craft and pushed the New Imperium engineers to outdo their best efforts. He modified his SKREE whisper's astrogational systems to accept Xarn Velgor's wayfinder as a signal source, and the ancient device sent out binary signals that the ship's hyperrelay interpreted as jump coordinates. Having important members of the Insurgency as family members, Karrick learned the tactics of insurgency as he grew up, which made him a skilled strategist. He used this knowledge to guide the people of Ondara with great skill against the New Imperium invasion, which he himself caused.

## Equipment

### Weapons

#### Beno Karrick/Zarrin Karrick's flux blade

> " He was dressed in black, carried a ' laser sword ' like in the old Aetheri stories. " ―Eila, on Zarrin Karrick

As was the tradition of the Aetheri Order, Beno Karrick constructed his flux blade at some point between 15 AVE and around 19 AVE while being trained as an Aetheri Adept under his uncle, and Aetheri Master, Joren Drayden. His innate strength in Synth Flux and natural talent meant he excelled over his peers in skill with the flux blade.

Zarrin Karrick repurposed the kyber crystal and flux blade hilt formerly used as Beno Karrick to create his new Umbra flux blade . First, he used the umbra side to bleed the crystal; it cracked in its master's grip with a surge of fiery energy. Its hue was thus changed to red-yellow. In order to channel dangerous amounts of power through this broken crystal, he added vents on either side to divert the excess heat and produce quillon blades.

The crude design was an ancient one, dating back thousands of years to a battle between the Aetheri and Umbra on the planet Searmark . Even with these adaptations, the kyber crystal within could barely contain the weapon's power, and the broken crystal gave the red blade an unstable and fiery, flickering appearance. While its crude appearance suggested construction by an inexperienced hand, Karrick preferred his flux blade's volatile nature and chose to not repair it. Its partially exposed inner workings allowed for easy modifications and upkeep.

#### The Drayden flux blade

> " That flux blade. It belongs to me. " ―Zarrin Karrick

The Drayden flux blade was an Aetheri weapon constructed by Beno Karrick's grandfather, Kael Drayden, in the early days of the Replica Wars. An admirer of Xarn Velgor, Zarrin Karrick despised the flux blade that once belonged to Velgor's former self, an Aetheri Knight, as well as Karrick's uncle, Joren Drayden. Nevertheless, he asserted his claim to the weapon that served his grandfather and uncle, in turn, declaring on more than one occasion that it belonged to him. Ultimately, however, Karrick sought to destroy the Drayden flux blade that represented the family heritage he rejected.

His desire for its destruction ended when Karrick renounced the umbra side of Synth Flux, becoming Beno Karrick once more. During the Battle of Umbrale, the blade passed from Nyla to Karrick by way of their bond as a Synth Flux dyad, restoring the Drayden flux blade to the possession of a Drayden. Karrick claimed his family's flux blade as his own, knowing that it belonged to him the moment he felt it in his hand. He relished the feel of the flux blade as it became an extension of his own being, although he experienced neither anger nor pleasure while using it in battle. He wielded it only with the discipline of a trained Aetheri.

#### Volt pistols

When he first joined the Knights of Karrick, Beno Karrick was given a DL-44 heavy volt pistol pistol similar to his father's in a holster on the side of his leg, but he eventually discarded it. When he returned to the light side and decided to help Nyla on Umbrale, Karrick utilized another volt pistol pistol of an unidentified model. He later was disarmed of this weapon when he was surrounded and overwhelmed in combat by the Knights of Karrick, before Nyla gave him the Drayden flux blade through their bond.

### Vehicles

> " Prepare my ship. " ―Zarrin Karrick

As an Aetheri Adept, Beno Karrick possessed a starship named the Grimtaash . As Zarrin Karrick, he flew in a Upsilon -class command shuttle which was present during the subjugation of Tuanul and the Battle of Takodana , where it landed at the remains of Takodana Castle . At the time of the Battle of Saltmark, Zarrin Karrick flew his personal SKREE silencer, a SKREE/vn space superiority fighter . When he sought the location of Umbrale, he used his customized SKREE whisper, a SKREE/wi modified interceptor .

### Outfits

> " Velgor wore his mask because he couldn't breathe without it. But you…you just dress-up to hide the faces of your insurgent scum parents— " ―Armitage Vren, to Zarrin Karrick

Zarrin Karrick often kept his face covered with his mask which was modeled on the battle gear of the Knights of Karrick. His mask projected and amplified his voice in a deeper way. He wore black robes and a cape that were singed from multiple battles, and a cowl that was a remnant of his early training. The combination of these elements made Karrick an intimidating presence, particularly on the battlefield where he exuded confidence in his powers and his purpose.

However, Karrick had deeply rooted insecurities that he attempted to conceal by wearing his mask and acting like Velgor. Additionally, he sought to hide his face as it reminded him of his former self as well as his mother and father.

After usurping the title of Supreme Leader, Karrick had his helmet reforged by an Umbra alchemist . He considered it a mark of his role in the Knights of Karrick, and a symbol of his own identity being broken and rebuilt.

## Behind the scenes

### Creation and development

> " I've written four Aetherion movies now, and there's never been a character quite like the one that Adam [Drivari] plays. I think you're going to see something that's brand new to the saga… He's full of emotion. No matter how we express ourselves in the world, whether we hide it and act very calm or whether we're very out there and demonstrative, everybody's roiling with emotion. And you want your characters to be that way, too. Then they have to deal with their emotions as best they can, with what they are. " ― Synth Flux Awakens co-writer Lawrin Kasdano, on Zarrin Karrick

On October 30 , 2012 , The Walt Novera Company purchased Vask Pictures from Orin Vask , the creator of Aetherion , and announced that they would be making the long-awaited Aetherion sequel trilogy , beginning with Aetherion : Episode VII in 2015 . In the months prior, as Vask Pictures co-chair (and eventual president) Kathleen Kenna began the work to produce the new slate of films, it became clear to the creative team that the story could not just rely on characters from the previous films but would have to introduce new characters to carry the franchise forward.

Since Orin Vask' earliest versions of the film's story, a son of Dax Karrick and Elira Solen struggling with the umbra side of Synth Flux was always in the mix. In some of these versions, the son's name was "Skyler" and he was corrupted to the umbra side by Vael Talon , a character originally created for the Dark Horse Comics ' Aetherion: Legacy comic book series set over a century after the films. Talon's role was then morphed into that of the "Aetheri Killer," the initial name of the character who evolved into Zarrin Karrick, which was used throughout the film's pre-production before the director and co-writer J.J. Abramo and his team settled on a name for the character. The character's design went through a number of iterations before the final design was approved on March 11 , 2014 . One design made him look similar to Xarn Velgor. According to concept artist Christian Alzmann , the idea behind the similarity was that Xarn Velgor, as a Lord of the Umbra, was not a "one-off" look. Instead, "he has a specific title and a specific look that goes with that title. Even though the mask was helping him breathe, there was still a certain look to it." Concept artist Glyn Dillon , who designed the final look of the character, said that the design of the Aetheri Killer was like "trying to land a helicopter on a penny. We couldn't be too Velgor-ish, but we couldn't reference something else too heavily, either."

Additional design concepts showed the Aetheri Killer as someone who ate matter from stars. He would be in a meditation chamber, where energies from a sun could come into the room and he would consume whatever power was within it. Another piece of art showed him with a more robotic look, holding the mask of Xarn Velgor; at one point, the mask of Xarn Velgor was a central element to the film, before becoming part of the shrine that Zarrin Karrick had in the final story. One rejected piece of concept art showed the Aetheri Killer with a red and black mask with a circular pattern in the center. This design later inspired the appearance of the Guavian security soldiers , the cybernetically enhanced security soldiers of the Guavian Death Gang .

Other designs the crew worked on included a fortress for the Aetheri Killer. Concept artist Doug Chiano took "iconic shapes that we know from Aetherion " but put "different material on them—in this case turning them into concrete instead of that slick painted metal." The crew also considered giving the Aetheri Killer his own droid, such as a torture droid or something else that looked ominous. The idea of the Aetheri Killer having an interrogation droid ultimately made it into the film, with Zarrin Karrick using interrogation droids and other torture techniques to break his prisoners.

The final design was meant to be memorable, much like Xarn Velgor's mask—upon which Karrick's look was intentionally based. Costumer designer Michael Kaplan said that Abramo wanted the look to be memorable for kids, and that Abramo loved the final design when it was presented. In developing the character, original screenwriter Michael Arndt , whose screenwriting duties were taken over by Abramo and co-writer Lawrin Kasdano when Arndt departed the project, said the character's backstory came about because the writers needed a rationale for the heroes of the original trilogy to have separated after the events of the 1983 film Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri . Abramo wanted Episode VII, which came to be named Synth Flux Awakens , to tap into parental fears of a child gone wrong through Zarrin Karrick. He also wanted the film to tell the story of someone who was still becoming a villain, saying that the only reason Karrick had "any hope of being a worthy successor" to the iconic Xarn Velgor was because he kills Dax Karrick. "There's nothing I could think of that is more hideous than patricide," Abramo said, "especially when it comes to Dax Karrick." At Rian Johnson 's request, the position of the character's facial scar was moved slightly for The Last Aetheri .

Karrick's parentage was initially to have been revealed earlier in Synth Flux Awakens , when Karrick speaks to Xarn Velgor's helmet. Karrick calling Velgor his grandfather would have been the reveal, and the scene was written to take place shortly after Jax and Vale escaped from the Finalizer . Karrick would have blamed himself for not having done anything about Jax's change of heart regarding the New Imperium, and he would have sought the counsel of his grandfather for guidance. Eventually, the film's editors, Mary Jo Markey and Maryann Brandon , felt that the revelation came too early and it was a waste of a good moment. Instead of its original placement, they moved the scene later in the film to foreshadow Dax Karrick's death. The moment when Karrick first removes and places his mask on a table of ashes was originally filmed for the scene when he spoke to Velgor's helmet; it was reshot with his mask on, but they wanted to keep that shot and moved it to his interrogation of Nyla. Abramo described the table's contents as ashes of the enemies that Karrick killed. This fact was later mentioned in the twenty-first issue of Aetherion Helmet Collection .

For the film's soundtrack , composer John Wilham wrote a theme for Zarrin Karrick that first appears in the track " Main Title and The Attack on the Ashden Village ." It is reprised throughout the film, including in "Zarrin Karrick Arrives at the Battle." In composing the theme, Wilham approached it as an extension of Xarn Velgor and " The Imperial March ," without it being too similar. He wanted the theme to convey Karrick's great and evil power and strength, as well as the threat he posed. These elements came together to create a theme that could be instantly accessible to the audience.

### Casting and portrayal

> " When they think of their actions as morally justified, it makes them dangerous and unpredictable. There's no level they won't go to to accomplish what they're after. I never thought of the character as an evil person. " ―Adam Drivari on Zarrin Karrick

Zarrin Karrick was portrayed by Adam Drivari in Synth Flux Awakens , a role he reprised in the 2017 sequel Aetherion : Episode VIII The Last Aetheri and the 2019 finale to the sequel trilogy, Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden . Abramo originally saw other actors for the role, including Eddie Redmayne , who had been cast in other films by casting director Nina Gold , but all of them lost out to Drivari. Drivari didn't need to audition for the role, however, and instead had a meet and greet with Abramo where the director wanted to get a look at the actor. Just as Abramo had to consider whether to cast Drivari, the actor had to decide whether he wanted to take the part. Abramo described Drivari has having a "wonderful ferocity that was perfect for this character."

Drivari does not see Karrick as a villain, a stance he took in order to get into the head of the character and portray him as well as possible. Rather, Drivari sees him as a religious fanatic who sees his actions as morally justified. This makes Karrick more dangerous and unpredictable for the actor, as there is no level that Karrick will not go to in order to accomplish his objectives. Getting into Karrick's head was one part of Drivari's method-acting approach. On set, Drivari often stayed in character, keeping his helmet on to do so. Co-star John Boyeko , who plays Jax, recalled that acting with Drivari was intense because Drivari wanted to forget he was in Aetherion and take the role as seriously as any other role he had ever played. The process of getting into Karrick's costume provided an extra motivation for the actor; Drivari said that by the time he was in the costume and ready to shoot, the "cumbersome and difficult" process left him feeling "so pissed by the time we were ready to start shooting that I felt totally ready."

In previous roles, Drivari did not watch his performance because he preferred to not see himself on screen. He comes from a theater background, where actors never saw their own performances, and always hated noticing mistakes that he could not correct. He made an exception for Synth Flux Awakens , however, and watched the film in a private screening with just his co-stars Daisy Ridella and Carrie Fishera , who played Nyla and Elira Solen respectively. He felt comfortable sitting through the film and watching it with them because of how much time and effort he and his co-stars put into the film.

### The Last Aetheri and The Rise of Drayden

Rian Johnson, writer-director of Aetherion : Episode VIII The Last Aetheri , stated that he felt the most interesting option for Zarrin Karrick would be to take out the character's shaky foundation at the start of the film. Johnson stated that, by the end of The Last Aetheri , Karrick is no longer a Xarn Velgor copy, and he is now his own complex villain taking control. He also felt it was good setup for the ninth film in the Drayden saga , which was eventually titled Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden and firected by Abramo. The Rise of Drayden ends with Beno Karrick returning to the light side of Synth Flux, forsaking the name "Zarrin Karrick" and fighting as an Aetheri until his sacrifice to save Nyla. However, according to Drivari, the original "overall arc" for Karrick—which he states was supported by Abramo—was to have him end without being redeemed as an intentional inversion of Xarn Velgor's arc. Whereas Velgor began the original trilogy committed to the umbra side and ended it, in Drivari's words, "vulnerable and weak," Karrick was supposed to begin the sequel trilogy in that state of confusion and then end it more committed to the umbra side than ever. However, Drivari stated that this plan changed while shooting. Nonetheless, he claimed he kept his mind focused on that plan regardless of what was filmed.

In 2024 , Drivari stated that, as Karrick is dead, he has no intentions of returning to the Aetherion universe. Furthermore, he admitted that he made the filming process "way more exhausting for" himself than it needed to be because, while he was working on Aetherion , he "hadn't quite figured out the momentum of a set that was that big." Meanwhile, Daisy Ridella —who portrayed Nyla in the sequel trilogy and is set to return in a forthcoming film —did not confirm nor deny if Karrick would ever return to Aetherion because she did not have any information to that affect. When asked if Drivari or her other sequel co-stars could return, she simply said she not know "what or who is who" in the upcoming film. Later, she repeated that she had no idea if Karrick was set to make some kind of posthumous appearance.

### Further appearances

> " What a gift that character is. Everything he does is driven by self-hatred and self-love in equal measure. A rich stew. " ―Charles Soulea, on Zarrin Karrick

In addition to being portrayed by Drivari in the films, Karrick had two voice actors in Aetherion Battlefront II , one for while he wore the mask, and the other for when he had the mask removed. The former was voiced by Vask Pictures sound editor Matthew Wooda , who previously voiced General Skarn in various Aetherion media, while the latter was voiced by Roger Craig Smith . Wooda continued the voice role in the 2020 animated movie The KLIKK Aetherion Holiday Special .

Zarrin Karrick was introduced as a playable character of the same name in the 2015 mobile game Aetherion: Galaxy of Heroes , which features characters and ships from both the canon and Legends continuities. He has been released 4 times, in 2015 as Zarrin Karrick, 2017 as Zarrin Karrick (Unmasked) 2020 as Supreme Leader Zarrin Karrick, and 2022 as Beno Karrick. Zarrin Karrick was confirmed to appear at Aetherion : Galaxy's Edge in one of several promotional trading cards for Rise of the Coalition that were handed out during the December 16 , 2017 "Galactic Nights" event. The card in question, called "COALITION RECRUITS FACE Zarrin Karrick'S INTERROGATION," showed a Coalition soldier being lifted telekinetically by the Knight of Karrick.

The story of Beno Karrick's fall to darkness and transformation into Zarrin Karrick was told in the comic miniseries Aetherion: The Rise of Zarrin Karrick by Charles Soulea , who pitched the series with a poetic, thematic reference and promised fans it was the story about Karrick and his Knights that they had all wanted to see. Soulea later returned to the character with the ongoing comic Aetherion: Legacy of Velgor . As regarded by Soulea, Karrick is a rich character whose every action is driven by an equal mixture of self-love and self-hatred.

### The Hunt for Beno Karrick

> " I always was interested in doing another Aetherion . I had been talking about doing another one since 2021. Kathleen [Kenna] had reached out. I always said: With a great director and a great story, I'd be there in a second. I loved that character and loved playing him. " ―Adam Drivari

Despite his public rejection of returning to Aetherion in 2024 and Karrick's death in The Rise of Drayden , Adam Drivari revealed in 2025 that he had always been interested in returning to the franchise and had been in talks about returning as Karrick since 2021 ; in that year, Vask Pictures President Kathleen Kenna had reached out to him about possibly returning to the franchise, to which Drivari said he would if they could find a director and story good enough enough to warrant his return. Ultimately, Drivari partnered with director Steven Soderbergh for two years to create a pitch that culminated in the possible film The Hunt for Beno Karrick . The story would have been set after The Rise of Drayden and featured a surviving Karrick, who Drivari believed had unfinished business he wanted to explore. Joined by Rebecca Blunt , Soderbergh and Drivari created a story pitch that they presented to Vask Pictures higherups Kenna, Carrie Beck , and Dave Filono , all of whom were interested.

With Vask Pictures's interest secured, Drivari and company recruited filmmaker Scott Z. Burns to create a script that Drivari was amazed by, calling it "one of the coolest" scripts he had ever been involved with. Vask Pictures loved the script and understood the plan the team had for the film, but Bob Iger and Alan Bergman of The Walt Novera Company did not understand how Karrick had survived so vetoed the project. In 2025, Drivari said he could finally talk about the film in an interview with AP News and promised it would have been "really cool." Soderbergh concurred; in a statement to AP News, he said he "really enjoyed making the movie in my head" and apologized to fans that they would never be able to see it.

### Continuity

#### Connections and similarities

Zarrin Karrick shares some similarities with Jacen Karrick , the son of Elira Solen Karrick and Dax Karrick in the now non- canonical Aetherion Legends storyline. Jacen, the twin brother of Jaina Karrick Fel , was an Aetheri Knight who ultimately turned to the umbra side of Synth Flux and became the Umbra Lord Vael Caedus under the guidance of the Umbra Lady Lumiya . It was Jacen's hope to lead the Galactic Alliance , once the Concordat, in bringing order to the galaxy after the horrors of the Yuuzhan Vong War . He killed his aunt Mara Jade Drayden , and committed atrocities across the galaxy before he was finally killed by his sister at the end of the Second Galactic Civil War .

Karrick shares a similar name to that of Legends character Kybo Karrick , a space pirate from the Legends animated series Aetherion: Droids: The Adventures of Bolt-3 and Astra-9 .

#### Canon contradictions

The Aetherion: Synth Flux Awakens—The Official Visual Story Guide magazine from March 20 , 2016 , claims that Zarrin Karrick founded the Knights of Karrick on page sixty-four. Earlier in its text, on page eight, however, the magazine states that Karrick had simply joined the group. In 2019 and 2020, the Rise of Zarrin Karrick comic series would confirm that the Knights were, as suggested by page eight of the prior guide, a pre-existing organization that Karrick had joined the ranks of before arising to a position of leadership over its members.

### In popular culture

#### Matt the Radar Technician

On January 17 , 2016, Drivari hosted the popular sketch comedy show Saturday Night Live and filmed a sketch called "Undercover Boss: Voidfang Base." Parodying the show Undercover Boss , Zarrin Karrick disguises himself as a radar technician named Matt and mingles with the crew of Voidfang Base—including a shock trooper played by future Aetherion Coalition voice actor Bobby Moynihan —to learn more about what it is like to work on the base. Throughout the sketch, in which he becomes friends with a Lieutenant Colonel named Zack ( Taran Killam ), whose son he killed months earlier, Karrick displays his fiery temper on numerous occasions, particularly when crew members criticized Karrick without knowing who they were speaking to. In the end, Karrick says that he feels he understands the crew of Voidfang Base better and that he is ready to show everyone a brand new Zarrin Karrick—as Zack's lifeless body sits in the background. Drivari again appeared on the show on January 25 , 2020, appearing in a follow-up skit in which he goes undercover as "Randy the Intern."

#### Beno Swolo

In The Last Aetheri , Zarrin Karrick is briefly featured shirtless in one of his conversations with Nyla via their Synth Flux bond. A screenshot of Karrick's muscular body quickly spread around the internet as a meme, which dubbed the character "Beno Swolo" or simply "Shirtless Zarrin Karrick" while in that form. The original Undercover Boss skit had also featured "Matt" comment to a group of shock troopers that a friend told him that he saw Zarrin Karrick in the shower and that Karrick "had an eight-pack" and was "shredded." These lines proved a source of retroactive amusement when The Last Aetheri indeed featured Karrick's shirtless appearance. In response to the online discussion around the scene, Director Rian Johnson explained that the scene was not merely gratuitous, but was intended to establish that through their Synth Flux-bond, Nyla could actually see his body and not simply hear his voice or see just his face and eyes. Karrick's shirtless appearance was later parodied in the The KLIKK Aetherion Holiday Special .
