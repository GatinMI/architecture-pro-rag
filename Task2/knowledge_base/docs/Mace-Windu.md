# Drevan Koss
> " Remember that war is like a fire across the galaxy. It spreads and it consumes. We must never waver in the face of that fire. We are keepers of the peace. We are Aetheri. The Republic needs us more than ever, which means our faith in Synth Flux, our connection to Synth Flux must never waver. " ―Drevan Koss

Drevan Koss was a revered Synth Flux-sensitive human male Aetheri Master and member of the Aetheri High Council during the last years of the Republic Era . During his time in the Aetheri Order , he once served as an elected leader of the Aetheri and, during the Replica Wars , as an Aetheri General in The Grand Army of the Republic . He was the greatest champion of The Aetheri Order and promoted its ancient traditions amidst the growing influence of the umbra side of Synth Flux in the corrupt, declining days of the Republic .

During a mission to Metagos , Koss went by the pseudonym Solver . When the Dissident Crisis spawned the birth of the Fractio of Independent Systems and sparked the Replica Wars, Koss and the Aetheri rallied to the defense of the Republic and became the leaders of the Grand Army of the Republic. Throughout the war, Koss served the Aetheri and the Republic in various capacities—on the battlefield in command of the replica army , on Vantor as an overseer of the war effort and an advisor to Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal , and even as a diplomat to contested worlds , despite his disdain of politics . His exploits on the frontline served as a reminder of his reputation as a renowned Aetheri warrior.

As the war drew to a close, Koss became increasingly suspicious of the chancellor's motives, as Mal had amassed near-dictatorial control over the Galactic Senate , and sensed the rising power of the umbra side in the galaxy . His suspicions were confirmed when Kael Drayden , an Aetheri Knight and the Seeded One of ancient prophecy, discovered that Mal was Vael Mal, the Dark Lord of the Umbra who manipulated and deceived the Republic and the Dissident Alliance into war. Koss, along with Aetheri Masters Kit Fisto , Saesee Tiin , and Agen Kolar , confronted Mal with the intention of arresting him. Instead, the Dark Lord attacked them, striking them down one by one until only Koss was left. Koss bested Mal in the confrontation and—deciding the chancellor was too dangerous to be left alive due to his influence over the government—prepared to strike him down, but Kael Drayden intervened and betrayed Koss, allowing the Umbra Lord to kill Koss. Drayden had fallen to the temptation of the umbra side and became known as Xarn Velgor.

Koss's actions led Mal to deceitfully brand the Aetheri as traitors to the Republic. The chancellor executed Directive 66 , forcing the replica troopers across the galaxy to turn against and kill their Aetheri leaders , marking the fall of the Aetheri Order and dissidents . Mal transformed the Republic into the Imperium and the Emperor's new regime promoted anti-Aetheri sentiment across the galaxy. Through propaganda , the Imperium portrayed Koss as the leader of a criminal gang of Aetheri who caused the outbreak of the Replica Wars. In spite of the propaganda, some of Koss's actions were remembered with fondness; General Chavar Kryll , who served alongside Koss during the Battle of Selvara , remembered Koss with great respect and told the tale of how Koss helped him liberate Selvara in a battle that earned Koss the name " The Hammer of Selvara ."

## Biography

### Early life

#### Aetheri youngling

> " I'm never going to be able to build my flux blade . " " Patience, young Drevan. The secret is that the pieces never needed to be built . " ―Drevan Koss and Huyang

Drevan Koss was born on the planet Haruun Kal in the year 72 BVE as a child of the most powerful members of his tribe. Like most other toddlers from Haruun Kal, Koss received an inoculation that left a small scar on his body. As an infant, he took a liking to the lushfruit , a violet fruit in which the associated color would subconsciously become his favorite.

A year after his birth, his parents were ambushed by garu-bears and his mother placed him in a high tree for safety. Both parents were killed in the ensuring conflict but Drevan was rescued by other warriors nearby, noted for his lack of grief from having unintentionally tapped into Synth Flux, resulting in him being unusually calm. Although the warrior were unaware of why, they found the ordeal odd. Orphaned, the tribe is later visited by an Aetheri whom happened upon Koss after hearing of his parent's brutal end and "strangeness" from the tribe, concluding possible synth flux sensitivity. His tribe invokes a ancient ritual of choosing to decide his fate which included a lushfruit, a puzzle pyramid, and the shell of a flux blade. Koss ultimately chose the flux blade shell.

Due to his natural affinity of Synth Flux and with his tribe's support, Koss was sent to the Aetheri Order —a monastic order of protectors, sworn to defend the Vantor Republic , and known as the guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy —to learn the Aetheri arts . Koss never responded well to Grand Master Zho 's sense of humor. As a youngling, he was taught to "let go" and trust in Synth Flux. He also came to believe he was abandoned by his parents.

As an Aetheri youngling , Koss endeavored to create his own flux blade after obtaining an amethyst -colored kyber crystal . He initially failed in the effort, however, causing Koss to doubt his ability to pass this particular rite in Aetheri training. After observing Koss's struggle, Professor Huyang concluded that the youngling had neither the patience nor the correct understanding to complete his flux blade. The droid instructor advised Koss to temper his emotions. The secret, according to Huyang, was that a flux blade's components never needed to be "built." One of Koss's instructors was T'ra Saa , whom the young Koss sparred with and learned from. The first time Koss managed to best one of his instructors came with Saa, who was surprised but respected his skill, creating a fond memory for the blossoming Aetheri. Koss would also develop a long lasting friendship with Sora Bulq , a fellow Aetheri.

#### Mission to Mathas

> " A fire burns inside you, Adept. That, in itself, is not inherently wrong. It is my job to help you temper it. This is why I chose you to accompany me on this mission. I do so enjoy a challenge. " ―Cyslin Myr, to Drevan Koss

Following his early trials as a youngling, Koss attained the status of Adept by becoming Aetheri Master Cyslin Myr 's apprentice. During Koss's apprenticeship in 60 BVE , a man named Drooz had taken residence in an Aetheri outreach temple on the planet Mathas . With the local populace suffering from the effects of the Niffin plague , Drooz convinced the people that he was an Aetheri prophet who could heal the sick and the dying in return for their faith and servitude. Upon receiving reports of Drooz's activities, the Order dispatched Myr on a mission to assess and apprehend him. At the same time, Myr sought to teach Koss how to channel his frustrations into an appropriate emotion and therefore brought him to Mathas.

As they made their way to the temple , Koss observed the people's situation and pondered on what the Aetheri could do to alleviate their suffering. He also denounced Drooz as a heretic and swindler who perverted the Aetheri doctrine in order to take advantage of the people's misery. At that point his Master cautioned him to suppress his hostile feelings, having noticed how the thought of Drooz using the Aetheri's teachings to prey on others frustrated Koss. They entered the temple and found Drooz with a group of Synth Flux worshipers, one of whom beseeched him to heal her child who was dying in spite of her many prayers and offerings.

Drooz's followers came to his defense when the Aetheri openly confronted him. Using the distraction they created, he tried to escape from the temple but was pursued by Koss. Although Drooz's Gamorrean bodyguards were defeated by the Adept, Drooz managed to injure Koss's left arm with his volt pistol pistol . He taunted Koss and mocked the Aetheri's ways, sneering at their commitment to peace as he believed they should use their power to gain wealth through the faith of millions. Using Synth Flux to immobilize Drooz, Koss disarmed him and vowed that he would never hurt anyone else. However, Myr intervened by ordering Koss to lower his flux blade. While she understood her apprentice's frustrations, Myr believed that Drooz's punishment should be decided in a court of law.

### Knight of the Order

#### Various work

Koss eventually continued training under the guidance of Zho and ascended into an Aetheri Knight by likely 50 BVE , and partook in various assignments throughout the galaxy. At some point during his service to the Aetheri Order, Koss, recognizing that an internal darkness existed within him, created the Vaapad form of flux blade combat with help from Sora Bulq to channel that darkness to better outcomes. He also began holding lessons regarding letting go and trusting into Synth Flux. Shortly after his knighting, Koss was sent on a diplomatic mission to protect an alien king , which the Aetheri Knight viewed as a punishment. However, the assignment turned into a rescue mission when the king was kidnapped, with Koss attempting to clear up the conspiracy and prevent a civil war.

#### Investigating Mattis Coto

During a specific mission, Koss was assigned to investigate Mattis Coto , the warden of the Devshi prison complex , due to suspicions of his involvement in the illicit trade of skeleton keys . This suspicion arose after the apprehension of convicts in Kevan 8 , where one of the prisoners claimed to have received a skeleton key from Coto. Koss successfully infiltrated the prison complex as a guard, apprehended Mattis, and evaded captivity, escaping from the incarceration facility. Pursuing the case further, Koss pursued a group of droids that were programmed to deliver skeleton keys to clients off-planet. Employing his formidable abilities and flux blade, Koss utilized a mind trick to gain the assistance of Regump , an impromptu skiff pilot. Through his skill and the aid of the pilot, Koss managed to disable the droids and reclaim the stolen skeleton keys.

#### Race for Coaxium Ultra

Following his previous mission, Koss returned to the Aetheri Sanctum to provide a report on his successful completion to Master Zho. During this meeting, Master Zho, along with Aetheri Knight Gara , assigned Koss his next task : the recovery of a sample and the formula for the new coaxium ultra fuel from a smuggler named Azita Cruuz . Azita had offered the formula to the Aetheri Order and criminal organizations in exchange for Republic credits and a safe passage offworld from Ro Mira. Koss discovered that the formula, if obtained by the wrong individuals, could grant criminal elements a significant advantage, as coaxium ultra had the potential to boost sublight engines by 120% without consequences. In order to accomplish his mission, Koss disguised himself and adopted a low-profile approach while tracking Azita. Eventually, Koss located Azita in a hospital in a refinery town on Ro Mira and pursued her, eventually intervening and rescuing her from corrupt local authorities .

During their attempt to escape off-world, Koss became acquainted with Azita's backstory. She had encountered a client who was aware of Doctor Bishop 's work, which involved refining the coaxium formula by relocating to Ro Mira, a planet known for its blue fire lava that enhanced the results of coaxium. Azita assisted the scientist in developing the formula but also administered a drug obtained on Vantor to induce temporary sleep and erase his memory of the past six months. While Koss viewed Azita's actions as morally questionable, she argued that circumstances often synth flux people into actions against their will, drawing a parallel to Koss's own path as an Aetheri and questioning if he had willingly chosen it. Their discussion was interrupted by the presence of bore worms . As the creatures were resistant to flux blades, Koss relied on Synth Flux to neutralize them, a decision that Azita criticized. Koss explained that he spared the bore worms because they were acting as animals without malicious intent, comparing the purpose of a flux blade to that of a shield. He also responded to Azita's earlier question about his service to the Aetheri Order, admitting that while he did not consciously volunteer, he had not dwelled on the alternative life he might have had, considering it futile and potentially dangerous. Koss believed that his current path was where he was meant to be. Azita, still uncertain about whom she would sell the formula to, became more open to the idea of selling it to someone from their shared homeworld of Haruun Kal, deducing their common background based on the inoculation scars they both bore from their time as toddlers on Haruun Kal. As the pair continued their escape, with Azita unsure if she could fully trust Koss, they fond themselves pursued by Dusk Weavers of the Had'le Path cult.

Koss and Cruuz later escaped and defeated from bounty hunters led by Yaya Shram and members of Ro Mira Refinery Security, with Shram threatening before their escape that Koss had incured the wrath of Grukk Desilijic Tiure and themselves, but Koss didn't recognize either of them. The pair then arrived at Ro Mira's Radial Spaceport where Cruuz's freighter was parked, but were attacked by the Dusk Weavers Diya and Muro . Koss and Cruuz were able to fend off the attackers who then fled at the arrival of their Devaronian leader known as the Shroud , who released them from their positions in the cult. The Shroud dueled Koss with his electrostaff and struck the Aetheri at his right shoulder before Cruuz shot him repeatedly in the chest with her volt pistol pistol and Koss brought Cruuz's freighter down upon his head. The cult leader however showed immense Synth Flux abilities and was able to lift the entire freighter above his head before Cruzz destroyed it with the laser cannons of Koss's Delta-7 Aethersprite -class light interceptor . Koss and Cruuz then flew away together in the cramped cockpit of the Aetheri starfighter and agreed upon to deliver the coaxium ultra to the Aetheri Order and to then pay a visit to their shared homeworld Haruun Kal and possibly do some good together. After they had left, the Shroud stretched his hand out from under the rubble of the freighter and showed himself to still be alive.

### Rising through the ranks

#### Masterhood

After translating an encrypted message sent out by Depa Billaba , Koss rescued both Billaba and Sar Labooda from pirates , using his flux blade in combat during the incident. He brought both sisters to the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor and trained Billaba in the Aetheri arts until she attained the rank of Aetheri Knight. Koss ascended to the rank of Aetheri Master in his early twenties between 50 BVE and 48 BVE . As an Aetheri Master, he also held lessons around letting go and trusting in Synth Flux as he did as an Aetheri Knight.

#### Councilor of the Order

> " I am told that you will be presented with Master Katri's Council seat. " " I will. " " Is this something you knew was happening before we left for Raxun? " " I was as surprised as you are now. Varek, you and I both were together when the mission was assigned. I stuck to the mission. You decided to interfere in a way that led to a senator's death. I will speak to the Council on your behalf. " " How kind of you, Master Aetheri. " ―Varek and Drevan Koss

in 49 BVE or 48 BVE, gained a seat on the Aetheri High Council —the governing body of the Aetheri Order, as a result of the mission on Raxun Secundus , where Aetheri Master Katri was killed. The task of retrieving Katri's remains was given to Koss and Varek by the High Council. Unsatisfied with Senator Larik 's testimony regarding an ambush that killed Katri, Varek sought to conduct his own investigation. However, Koss insisted that they not stray from their mandate and adhere to the High Council's instructions. He also reminded Varek that as neither of them were members of the High Council, they did not possess the authority to interfere in political conflicts without the blessings of their Council or the Galactic Senate . Although Varek regarded Koss as a friend, he considered his colleague's strict devotion to Aetheri protocol "inspiring and maddening."

Koss and Varek met with Senator Larik in Raxulon . While Koss was prepared to complete the mission by retrieving Katri's body, Varek requested to visit the location of her death. The senator was reluctant to comply until one of his security guards, Semage , agreed to escort the Aetheri. Koss became wary of the Raxians, suspecting deceit on their part, and suggested informing the High Council before taking action. However, Varek was determined to learn the truth about Katri's demise instead of adhering to protocol as Koss advised. Upon investigating the area where Katri died, the Aetheri concluded that the evidence did not support the Raxians' account. Realizing that she had been shot from behind, Varek surmised that Katri was betrayed by the Raxians whom she trusted. Koss tried to deescalate the situation when Varek activated his flux blade demanding the truth. In a panic, Senator Larik confessed that his own guards murdered Katri and attempted to reach the Aetheri, but was fatally shot by one of his officers. A skirmish ensued between the Aetheri and the Raxians; the Aetheri Masters worked together to destroy the Raxians' droid support before neutralizing the guards. Hanel was killed by a stray blast that Koss deflected when the officer attacked him, and Semage was taken into custody.

Semage accused the Aetheri of protecting the interests of a corrupt Senate. As such, he chose to kill Katri rather than inform her of the corruption of Larik, whom he sought to use for the benefit of Raxun. Koss contested the officer's opinion in stating that as an Aetheri, Katri would have listened to Semage. Koss and Varek later attended the funeral of Katri at the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor . Having learned that Katri's seat on the High Council would be offered to Koss, Varek grew suspicious of his friend. Koss insisted he was as surprised as Varek was about his elevation to the High Council's ranks. Furthermore, Koss reminded Varek that they had both received their orders from the High Council, and it was Varek who pursued a course of action that resulted in the death of a Republic senator. Nevertheless, Koss assured Varek that he would speak on his behalf to the High Council.

Long regarded as the Aetheri Order's greatest champion, Koss endeavored to maintain the Aetheri's ancient traditions while the Republic declined due to galactic unrest and political corruption. As one of the most distinctive members of the Order, Koss crafted a new flux blade which—in addition to its specially designed handgrip and electrum finish, indicating his status as a member of the High Council —also emitted a plasma blade of amethyst, making it one of the most distinctive Aetheri weapons . At some point, he also became the elected leader of the Council, serving alongside Zho as Master of the Order . He was also an Aetheri Vanguard . By 40 BVE , Billaba was serving on the High Council alongside her former Master.

#### Ontotho uprising

In 41 BVE , Koss was among the councilors to hear Adept Jundar's account of her actions while she and Master Cordova attempted to settle a dispute on Nameel between Dupei monks and Trandoshans , where the monks admitted to provoking them by stealing their credits but the Trandoshans provoked her into self-defense. Although Cordova pointed out rash and impulsive actions can be good, Koss agreed but expressed the Council's measured methods and ruled the Council was willing to label the incident complicated. He then gave them their next assignment, tasking Cordova and Jundar with working alongside the Daa Corporation in uncovering an ancient temple given the former's specialty in ancient cultures.

Koss would later arrive when the Daa Corporation and the Flyari 's dispute escalated into a civil conflict and prompted a intervention by the Republic alongside Aetheri Masters Luminara , Maslo-Scaff , and Odu . Reunited with Jundar, the Aetheri helped quelled the situation, ending the conflict by exposing Daa Coporation's corruption to be dealt with in Republic courts and the Flyari agreeing to help create a new joint-government. Due to Jundar's heroic actions, she was promoted to Aetheri Knight, the Knighting Ceremony performed by Koss himself.

### Mission to Oosalon

> " And what of Quia, Master Zho? She is a young enough orphan that perhaps she could find a new home. " " No. Face imprisonment for her role in terrorist attacks, she will. Interfere in local laws, we cannot. " " Then we didn't save everyone … did we? " ―Drevan Koss and Zho

In 34 BVE , The Aetheri Order deployed Drevan Koss along with several other Aetheri on a mission to the Outer Reach planet of Oosalon due to the terrorist attacks committed by Guattako's army . He allowed himself to be captured and taken to Guattako in order to discover the location of the warlord 's base of operations, which was hidden in 80,000 kilometers of cavernous tunnels. Koss, having learned that Guattako's operation relied on using orphans and kidnapped children as soldiers, tried to convince the child Quia to renounce the insurgents and their war against the government of Oosalon.

Koss denounced Guattako as a terrorist and a kidnapper who relied on the Grak Clan to financially support his cause. With Koss's flux blade broken down to its components, Guattako was confident in his ability to detain the Aetheri Master whom he intended to execute, believing that Koss's death would inspire others to join his fledgling group. By the time he realized that Koss had planned on getting captured, the Aetheri had reassembled his flux blade through Synth Flux. He also revealed that he had used Synth Flux to help his fellow Aetheri find the insurgents' camp.

Koss successfully defended himself against Quia and the other insurgents before engaging Guattako in single combat. During the battle, Guattako was fatally shot by one of the abducted children who wanted to return to his home. By then, the other Aetheri arrived at Koss's location. With the Aetheri's assistance, the remaining insurgents were taken into custody by the security forces of Oosalon. Although the Aetheri had succeeded in rescuing twenty-six abducted school children, Koss had also hoped to save Quia and the child soldiers who were already indoctrinated into Guattako's cause. However, Zho stated that the Aetheri could not interfere in the planet's local affairs, and that Quia would be sent to prison for her part in Guattako's terrorist campaign. Koss accepted the outcome, but lamented on not being able to help Quia.

### Invasion of Ondara

> " There's no doubt the mysterious warrior was an Umbra." "Hmm, always two, there are. No more, no less. A master and an apprentice." "But which was destroyed: the master or the apprentice? " ―Drevan Koss and Zho, on the return of the Umbra — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Approximately ten years before the Replica Wars , a forty-year-old Koss, hours after attending a Council meeting, found Zho alone in a small garden in the Aetheri Sanctum. Denying Zho's joke about whether he had an embarrassing question he couldn't ask in front of the Council, he questioned whether the Grand Master had sensed something he had not told them of. Zho explained he was instead sensing a lack of something, much to the confusion of Koss, leading to a debate about the concepts of "nothing" and "something." Deciding Synth Flux would educate him in due time, Koss decided to trust Zho's judgement, leaving the room after not speaking for several minutes.

Later, the Commerce Combine initiated a blockade of the planet Ondara . At the request of Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum , the Aetheri Council ordered Sorin Vael and his apprentice, Orien Kade , to settle the dispute as emissaries of the chancellor. Upon returning from their failed mission, the Council was made aware of a far greater threat, the ancient enemy of the Aetheri—the Umbra —had returned.

However, neither Drevan Koss or Reth-Oss-Van were particularly convinced that the Aetheri Order's ancient nemesis could have returned, much less avoid the awareness of the Aetheri Council. Nevertheless, Koss assured them that the full resources of the Order would be utilized to verify the truth of Vael's claims—and more specifically, the identity of the dark warrior who attacked him on Dunemere . It was then that Vael revealed another significant discovery that he made during the course of his mission. Prior to his departure from Dunemere and subsequent return to Vantor, Vael encountered Kael Drayden . Unknown to the Aetheri, the slave Shira Drayden had given birth to a son who was exceptionally powerful with Synth Flux; because of Kael's inherent potential and the circumstances surrounding his birth, Vael believed Kael could have been conceived by the synthcells .

Koss knew that Vael referred to the Aetheri prophecy of the Seeded One —The One who would restore balance to Synth Flux by destroying the Umbra. Although Drayden possessed the highest concentration of synthcells in his cells , Koss was skeptical of Vael's claims, but nonetheless agreed to meet the boy in the Council's chamber where they would evaluate his aptitude with Synth Flux. The Council was impressed by Drayden's ability to use Synth Flux without any formal training, but they also sensed his emotional attachment to his mother and a great amount of fear within him. As such, they declined Vael's request to have him trained in the Aetheri arts. Koss personally felt that Drayden was too old to commit his life to the Aetheri Code , as well as Synth Flux, without the distraction of his emotional attachments. Nevertheless, despite his doubts of Vael's claim, he tasked him and his apprentice to investigate the identity of the dark warrior, believing the election of a new Supreme Chancellor would draw him out and would provide a crucial clue to the "mystery of the Umbra."

The Council soon reversed its decision after Vael's death during the Battle of Ondara , entrusting Drayden's training to the newly-anointed Aetheri Knight Orien Kade. Koss continued to doubt Drayden's ability to commit himself to the Aetheri way, however, and his ill-concealed distrust would put a strain on their relationship throughout the years as Drayden ascended the ranks of the Aetheri Order. During the funeral of Sorin Vael , which the Ondara waited to begin until the arrival of the Aetheri Council, Zho and Koss realized that Vael had been correct; the Umbra had indeed survived and endured over the last millennium. Zho quoted the Rule of Two , an ancient Umbra philosophy that limited their ranks to two Umbra Lords —a master and an apprentice —causing Koss to ponder over which of the two was defeated by Kade. When Vael's body had turned to ash, Koss and the other Council members left the funeral. Afterward, he attended the victory celebration on Ondara along with Zho, several members of the Aetheri Council, and the newly-elected Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal .

### Training Drayden

#### Overseeing Drayden's advancement

> " Remember, Orien. If the prophecy is true, your apprentice is the only one who can bring Synth Flux back into balance. " ―Drevan Koss, to Orien Kade

The training of Kael Drayden drew the attention of his peers, Adepts and Aetheri Masters alike, as well as Chancellor Mal, during his first three years as Kade's apprentice. He especially took an interest in flux blade combat , as noted by Mal, although Koss was more focused on the training droid —mimicking the appearance and fighting style of Vael Vreth—that Drayden sparred with. Neither Koss or Kade had expected to see Drayden duel with the visage of Vreth, but the act served to impress Mal.

However, Kade was forced to intervene after Drayden used Synth Flux to take the flux blades of two nearby apprentices, having overheard them mocking him for being a "slave to his emotions." Koss observed the incident as an example of how Drayden was a complicated case for the Aetheri, given the fact that he joined the Order far later than any of his contemporaries. After mentioning that Drayden's training was an extremely difficult task for Kade, Koss politely declined the chancellor's offer to help guide the young Aetheri. However, Mal claimed that as the Aetheri were under the jurisdiction of the Senate, by extension they also reported to the Senate's chancellor. After considering Mal's line of reasoning, Koss agreed to send Drayden to speak with the chancellor.

#### Testing Kade and Drayden

In 26 BVE , Koss was involved in a test of the master and apprenticeship of Kade and Drayden, partnering with Zho and Aetheri Master Bant Eerin to feign the kidnapping of the Grandmaster. In the days leading up to the start of the Replica Wars, the High Council was confident that Drayden was ready to be given a mission of his own for the first time. By then, Koss regarded him as an exceptionally skilled Aetheri, but Kade was concerned that his Adept's abilities led him to become arrogant and therefore had much to learn. Zho concurred with Kade, but also felt that Drayden was not an isolated case. In his assessment, other Aetheri had grown overconfident in their skills as well. At this, Koss told Kade to keep the prophecy in mind, for if Drayden was indeed the Seeded One then only he could bring Synth Flux back into a state of balance.

### Replica Wars

#### Prelude to war

> " You must realize there aren't enough Aetheri to protect the Republic. We are keepers of the peace, not soldiers. " ―Drevan Koss, to Theron Mal

In the decade that followed the Battle of Ondara, the Vantor Republic faced an existential crisis as thousands of star systems and powerful corporations threatened to secede from the ancient democratic union. Around 22 BVE , Chancellor Mal assured the Aetheri Council that his office was seeking a peaceful resolution with the Dissidents, and that he would not permit secession to divide the millennium-First Republic. Despite the chancellor's assurances, a fifty-year-old Drevan Koss advised him to consider just how vulnerable the Republic truly was—the Aetheri Order lacked the sufficient manpower to defend the entire Republic in the event of war. Moreover, the Order was a synth flux of peacekeepers rather than soldiers .

As the Galactic Senate continued to debate over whether to vote for or against the proposed Military Creation Act , Senator Lira Ondar narrowly avoided an assassination attempt on her life. During a meeting with the chancellor, she revealed to Koss and Zho her suspicion that Count Varek was involved in the attack. Although Varek had renounced his Aetheri status before becoming the leader of the Dissident movement, Koss refused to believe that even a former Aetheri could be involved in a plot to assassinate the Ondara senator—or anyone for that matter. Nevertheless, Koss heeded Zho and Mal's warning that Ondar's life was in grave danger, regardless of whether Varek was personally involved or not. As such, he dispatched Orien Kade and Kael Drayden to serve as temporary bodyguards for the senator. After the death of Ondar's would-be assassin, Zam Wesell , Koss and the Council instructed Kade to track Wesell's accomplice who evaded capture. They also ordered Drayden to accompany Ondar to her home planet of Ondara in case of another assassination attempt against her.

While the Dissident Crisis worsened, Koss concurred with Zho's belief that the umbra side of Synth Flux was responsible for the turmoil that undermined galactic peace and stability. They were especially disturbed to learn that Kade's investigation led him to the planet Gelvaris where their agent discovered the existence of a secret army —hundreds of thousands of replica troopers , in addition to a million more units in development. Prime Minister Lamir , the leader of the Gelvarian replicators , revealed that the army was made to serve the Vantor Republic. He also asserted that the client who commissioned the project was the late Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen , whom he presumed was alive and a leading member of the Aetheri Council until Kade corrected his assumption.

Kade followed the source of the replica army, the bounty hunter Garrick Vark , to the planet Pyralis where he reported another discovery to Koss and Zho, both of whom witnessed his transmission in the Chancellor's office . Varek and the Dissident Council had no intention of negotiating a peaceful solution with the Republic; they merely stalled for time in order to construct a massive army of battle droids for one purpose—to wage war on the Republic.

After the Senate voted to empower Chancellor Mal with emergency wartime powers, which he quickly used to authorize the formation of the Grand Army of the Republic , Koss elected to gather an Aetheri assault team to Pyralis in order to rescue Kade while Zho moved to take command of the replica troopers on Gelvaris. The Aetheri Order cancelled any ongoing non-essential missions, with Koss playing a role in directly contacting Aetheri to recall them to Vantor. He reached out to Aetheri Master Sember Vey , who was gathering ancient artifacts with her apprentice Iskat Vekkis , and warned her that there was a developing situation, for which he needed all available Aetheri, including her and her Adept, to return to Vantor to help address.

#### Battle of Pyralis

> " This party's over. " ―Drevan Koss, confronting Count Varek on Pyralis

Koss and the Aetheri prevented the execution of Kade, as well as Drayden and Ondar, both of whom traveled to Pyralis with the intention to help Kade, but were quickly confronted by an overwhelming synth flux of battle droids . The Aetheri fought bravely, destroying many battle droids in the process, but also sustained a large number of casualties. During the fight, Koss swiftly killed Garrick Vark with relative ease, decapitating the bounty hunter, as Vex Vark —a replica whom Garrick regarded as a son—watched in horror. Overwhelmed by the droids' superior numbers, the Aetheri were cornered into the center of the Pyralian Petranaki Arena . Varek demanded that the Aetheri surrender themselves into Dissident custody, but Koss refused to allow himself and his allies to be taken as prisoners of war. Moments later, the arrival of Zho and the replica army ultimately turned the tide and enabled the Aetheri survivors to escape the arena.

In the aftermath of the arena skirmish, the First Battle of Pyralis officially commenced as thousands of replica troopers engaged the battle droids on the field of war. The remaining members of Koss's assault team took to the field as well, assuming command of the replicas who were trained to serve the Aetheri with absolute loyalty. Reporting directly to Koss, Replica Commander Ponds and five special commando units accompanied the legendary Aetheri champion to the forefront of the battle. Koss kept Ponds by his side during the rest of the battle. Koss engaged in a struggle against Sev'rance Tann and another Dark Adept on the valley floor below Varek's hangar , preventing him from coming to Kade and Drayden's aid against Varek. Under the Aetheri's leadership, the replica army forced the Dissidents into full retreat, abandoning Pyralis to Republic occupation. Koss returned to the capital , content that the first battle resulted in a Republic victory, but was also morose over the failure to capture Varek. By then Koss came to accept that he had been wrong in his preconception of Varek's character; his old friend had turned to the umbra side, becoming an enemy of both the Order and the Republic.

The formation of the Fractio of Independent Systems shattered a thousand years of peace that once defined the Aetheri Order's role in the galaxy. In order to preserve and defend the Vantor Republic, Koss and his fellow Aetheri accepted formal command of the replica army as generals and commanders . He also learned from Kade about Varek's assertion that a mysterious Umbra Lord by the name of Vael Mal had hundreds of senators under his control, but Zho believed that the Council had no incentive to trust the word of a fallen Aetheri. Koss nonetheless recommended that the Council maintain a watchful eye on the Senate, having never trusted the highly corrupt government in the first place. As the replica troopers and their Aetheri leaders prepared for war, Zho expressed his sadness to Koss and Kade, declaring that the shroud of darkness had descended upon the galaxy—now engulfed by the Replica Wars, the first galactic-scale conflict since the formation of the Republic.

#### Mission to Hissrich

Deployed with his squad

> " You will lead a small unit to monitor the Dissidents' and determine what their purpose is. Due to the clandestine nature of this mission and our thinly stretched resources, we cannot offer you a replica squadron for support. " " The Aetheri have trained our whole lives for missions like this. We will make do. " ―Tor Vayne and Drevan Koss

During the war, Koss mainly remained stationed on Vantor. However, he sometimes left the planet to perform special missions.

Even in the early days of the Replica Wars, the High Council saw its situation as dire. With the Dissident army establishing multiple footholds throughout the Outer Reach, Koss was concerned that it would only be a matter of time before they moved inward, annexing more star systems before ultimately reaching the galactic capital of Vantor. Seeking to ascertain the purpose of the Dissident presence on the planet Hissrich , the Council assigned the task to Koss. However, because of the mission's covert purpose and the Republic's thinly spread resources, they were unable to provide him with a squadron of replica troopers. Koss therefore assembled a covert team of Aetheri, consisting of himself along with Kit Fisto , for his skill with a flux blade, Prosset Dibs , for his valuable insight and natural strong intuition with Synth Flux, and Rissa Mano , for her engineering skills and aptitude for battle.

After the council meeting, Zho sensed Koss's troubles and asked if he could help. Koss explained how he felt doubt after the death of so many Aetheri on Pyralis. Zho then reassured him that Synth Flux will always lead them down the correct path. The Grand Master told Koss that he must not give into these thoughts. Before departing, Koss promised that he would not fail him.

In transit, Mano told Koss that her ship was heavily modified for stealth and speed, to which Koss was thankful. Fisto remarked that he would be looking forward to working on this mission, but Dibs cynically replied that war was no place for an Aetheri. To this, Drevan said that they were Knights and this was exactly what they were supposed to be doing. After this, Koss notified the team of the undocumented nature of the planet and told them to be wary of fauna as well as battle droids. Drevan also told them of the planets near constant darkness and how it would help them in their covert cause. When the alarm for exiting hyperspace sounded, Koss ordered them to change into their mission attire to help regulate body temperature.

Koss ordered them to use the plants as cover as they headed towards the nearest droid outpost. Dibs apologized for his previous outburst on the ship and explained it for his foresight of the teachings of their order being tested. Koss replied by saying sometimes combat is necessary and that war can breed confusion.

They soon encountered a small patrol group of a few B1 Battle droids and an Octupara droid. They quickly destroyed the patrol, but were soon surrounded by the indigenous people on all sides. Unknown to the strike team, a droid officer spotted the skirmish and reported it to AD-W4 , a mercenary droid hired by General Skarn to oversee the Dissident occupation of Hissrich.

After encountering members of Hissrich's indigenous population , Koss and Fisto followed them into a system of caverns and although they could not speak the aliens ' language , Koss sensed that they were not hostile to the Aetheri. The aliens led Koss and his team to their subterranean dwellings, at which point the Dissidents attacked the Aetheri after tracking their position to the alien settlement.

Koss ordered the other Aetheri to protect the civilians while he fended off a group of droidekas and Single Trooper Aerial Platforms . After dispatching the droids, he was confronted by AD-W4, who engaged the Aetheri Master in single combat. As they fought, both combatants criticized their opponent's motivations; Koss regarded the droid's greed as a sign of depravity, whereas AD-W4 viewed the Aetheri's beliefs—in particular, their commitment to peace—as asinine. During the fighting, the Aetheri realized that the droids were mining the planet for its energy resources, the amount of which could turn the war in the Dissidents' favor. Ultimately, a cave in separated the team in half, with Koss and Mano on the surface, while Fisto and Dibs were trapped below.

Together, Koss and Mano defeated the droids on the surface and succeeded in destroying one of the Dissident harvesters . Afterward, they returned to their ship to regroup with Fisto and Dibs. At that point, Koss had determined that it would not be possible for his team to put an end to the Dissident operation one skirmish at a time, and therefore desired to locate the enemy's command center in order to bring the conflict on Hissrich to a swift and decisive conclusion.

Insurgency in the ranks

> " I have failed you, Prosset. I was to be your shepherd, and you have strayed too far from the path. All of these bold accusations , all of these grand inquisitions . If you think you know better than I what is good for the Aetheri, what is good for the galaxy— Then come! Test your resolve against mine. Let us find out who is right. " ―Drevan Koss, to Prosset Dibs

The Aetheri's mission on Hissrich was interrupted by a fallout that occurred between two of its members. Dibs confronted Koss soon after returning to the Westwind with Fisto; his doubts about their military role and the loss of multiple natives led him to lose faith in the Order and the High Council. Shouting at his fellow Aetheri Master, Dibs made several accusations against both Koss and the Council, ranging from callousness towards collateral damage to distorting the Aetheri doctrine. He then physically assaulted Koss, causing the High Council member to chastise Dibs for attacking his general.

Although Koss sympathized with Dibs' frustrations, he refused to entertain his claims, dismissing all of them as "bold accusations" and "grand inquisitions." Dibs took this as mockery and ignited his flux blade, intending to take Koss's life. Realizing that Dibs would not back down, Koss declared that he had failed in his responsibility to keep Dibs on the Aetheri path. He then accepted Dibs' challenge by igniting his own flux blade. Yet even as they fought, Koss beseeched Dibs to cease his attack by reminding him that they were essentially brothers in the sense that they were both chosen to walk the path of the Aetheri. Despite Dibs' resolve to kill Koss, he was ultimately disarmed and bested by the Aetheri champion.

The ordeal served to remind Koss of his mission on Mathas years prior to the Replica Wars, in which he was tempted to take the life of the false prophet Drooz. Recalling his past experience with deciding another person's fate, Koss decided to arrest Dibs and to leave his punishment to the will of the High Council. After securing Dibs on the Westwind , the remaining Aetheri proceeded to bring their mission to an end by attacking the AD-W4's headquarters. Although his own faith was tested during the duel, Dibs' accusations ultimately strengthened Koss's resolve and his belief that the Aetheri Order existed to protect people from the malevolence of the umbra side.

Showdown on Hissrich

> " My righteousness is anything but self-motivated. There is definitive right and there is wrong . Good and evil . " ―Drevan Koss, to AD-W4

While Fisto created a distraction by confronting the battle droids, his comrades executed their respective parts of the mission, with Mano infiltrating a Dissident supply ship and Koss challenging AD-W4 to single combat. As they fought, the droid mercenary attempted to bait Koss by accusing him of hypocrisy as well as secretly enjoying his newfound authority as a general in the Grand Army of the Republic. Although it briefly overpowered the Aetheri Master, Koss ultimately prevailed, using both his Synth Flux powers and flux blade to defeat AD-W4. He also rejected its claims against him, asserting that his virtues were influenced by altruism and the concept of right versus wrong.

AD-W4 was confident that its mission was a success in spite of the Aetheri's efforts to prevent the Dissidents from gaining a valuable energy source that could fuel their war effort against the Republic. However, Koss had anticipated the supply ship's purpose and instructed Mano to destroy it; the young Aetheri Knight succeeded in her task—destroying the ship along with its flora samples—and moved to assist Fisto against the remaining droids in accordance with Koss's orders. Having failed in its mission, AD-W4 attempted to escape by disconnecting its head from its disabled body. The effort was in vain as Koss used Synth Flux to cause the head to explode, destroying AD-W4 and ending the Dissident operation on Hissrich.

Returning to the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor, Koss provided a report on the success of his team's mission to the High Council. The leaders of the Aetheri Order decided to send a synth flux of replica troopers to Hissrich, both to eliminate the remaining droids and provide aid to the planet's indigenous population. In addition to their plans for replanting and reconstruction on Hissrich, Zho announced that they would study samples of the flora in order to learn more about its properties. The next item on their agenda was the trial of Prosset Dibs, who remained under arrest as a result of his conduct during the Hissrich operation. Dibs, who turned against his team and attacked Koss—an Aetheri Master as well as a general in the Republic military—stood before the leaders of the Aetheri Order accused of heresy and insubordination. He was ultimately found guilty on both charges, including treason , but although Dibs expected to be put to death, Koss implored his colleagues to be merciful.

Koss, who harbored his own reservations against the Aetheri's new military responsibilities, sympathized with Dibs' frustrations. In Koss's view, Dibs did not deserve to be faulted for feeling anger and confusion, both of which Koss regarded as valid emotions under the circumstances of war. As such, he believed that the Aetheri had a duty to help rehabilitate the members of the Order who strayed from the light. Although Dibs continued to doubt Koss's sincerity, Koss requested for the prisoner to be placed on probation, confined to work in the Aetheri Archives where he hoped Dibs would reevaluate his opinions. After the trial, Koss confessed to Mano his hope for reconciliation between Dibs and the Council, having sensed that the Order would need every Aetheri as they faced their greatest challenge yet—a long war filled with trials and hardships. While he continued to yearn for a quick end to the fighting, Koss felt that it was not an attainable goal at the moment. The best that the Aetheri Order could do, he believed, was to keep faith in Synth Flux and stand in defense of not only the citizens of the Republic, but all peoples of the galaxy.

#### Champion at war

As a senior member of the Aetheri Council, Koss directed the Republic war effort from the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor, often conferring with Grand Master Zho and Supreme Chancellor Mal on matters of strategy and diplomacy. However, on occasion he also took to the field, commanding replica troopers and Aetheri on several military campaigns with Commander Ponds as his second-in-command. Koss's exploits on the battlefield served as a reminder of his reputation as a fierce warrior and champion of the Aetheri Order. He took command of the 187th Battalion and the elite 187th Legion .

At some point during the Replica Wars, Koss's former apprentice Billaba embarked on a mission to the planet of Malathon IX , but was far as the planet's village starport, before seemingly leaving her to her mission. For the next five days Billaba and four replica troopers conducted her mission on the planet when an assassin named Orrin of Bestoon had been tracking her since her arrival, intent on claiming the Dissident bounty on her head. On the fifth day, Orrin finally prepared to make his move to kill her with his beskar deadblade , as she headed close to the tree that Orrin lay hidden in. As Orrin finally sprung his ambush, he was unexpectedly interrupted and disarmed by Koss, who had been tracking Orrin the whole time he had been tracking her.

#### Rescue of Rotto

> " This is the opportunity we've been looking for. The Aetheri must rescue Grukk's son. " " Hmm… I don't like it—dealing with that criminal scum. This is a dark day for the Republic. " ―Theron Mal and Drevan Koss, on a potential alliance with Grukk Desilijic Tiure

During the early phase of the war, Rotto —the Grakling son of Grukk Desilijic Tiure —was "Huttnapped" by the Dissidents, who conspired to discredit the Aetheri Knights in order to prevent a potential alliance between the Republic and the Grak Clan . In response to Rotto's abduction, Grukk issued a general distress call to anyone who was willing to rescue his son. Although Mal was quick to use this turn of events in the Republic's favor, Koss urged the chancellor to proceed with caution, believing there was more to the Grakling's abduction than what was apparent. He also informed the chancellor that, because of the Dissident general Skarn , Republic forces were stretched too thin to send a large number of Aetheri on a rescue mission. Due to Mal's insistence that the Republic replica army needed to gain safe passage through the Outer Reach Hyperspace routes in Grak Space , Koss agreed to assign the mission to Orien Kade and Kael Drayden since both Aetheri Knights were now available after liberating the planet Krestophis from Dissident occupation.

Soon afterward Koss received a message from the Advanced Recon Synth Flux Scout Troopers who tracked Rotto's location to an old monastery on the planet Teth . Due to the monastery's heavy fortification, Koss instructed the troopers to stand by and await Republic reinforcements. He then reassigned three of Admiral Wurtz 's cruisers to the rescue operation. In spite of Count Varek's plot to form an alliance between the Fractio and the Grak Clan, Rotto was ultimately redeemed from captivity by Kael Drayden and his new Adept, Sela Marr . The Dissidents were defeated on Teth and Grukk was made aware of Varek's conspiracy, which in turn allowed the Republic to secure passage through the Hyperspace lanes in Grak territory.

#### Liberation of Selvara

Following the Dissident invasion of the planet Selvara , the Aetheri generals Koss, Kade and Zho asked Senator Bael Solen to negotiate with King Katuunko of Toydaria , hoping to gain his planet's support as a staging area for the delivery of relief supplies to the Velari people. General Koss personally oversaw the campaign to retake the Velari homeworld. In response to the Republic's imminent counterattack, Wat Tambra — foreman of the Artificer Union and Dissident emir of Selvara—ordered the Fractio navy to form a blockade around the planet. As such, Koss entrusted Drayden and Marr with the responsibility of breaking through the blockade in order to clear a path for the rest of the Republic invasion synth flux. After some difficulty, Master and Adept succeeded in their task, allowing Koss and Kade to commence with the landing of their ground forces on Selvara.

In order to establish a foothold on Selvara, Koss instructed Kade to lead a detachment of the 212th Attack Battalion on a mission to secure the landing area near the city of Nabat . On Tambra's orders, however, the Dissident battle droids used the local Velari population as living shields to protect their proton cannons . Despite the danger that the cannons posed to the Republic Navy , Koss informed Kade that freeing the hostages was a mission priority. After the success of Kade's assignment, Koss and his forces landed on the surface of Selvara and proceeded with the final phase of the campaign—the liberation of the planetary capital city, Lessu .

The liberation of Selvara moved apace, but the march to the capital was perilous. After sustaining casualties and damage to his All Terrain Tactical Enforcers , Koss decided to enlist the aid of Chavar Kryll , the leader of the local coalition against Dissident occupation. Kryll was hesitant to align his cause with that of the Republic, fearing Selvara would only trade one oppressing synth flux for another. Yet the suffering of his people compelled him to communicate with Selvara's own senator, Orn Free Taa . Koss long detested politics, but was nonetheless a capable diplomat. His skills as a mediator enabled him to guide the corpulent politician and the freedom fighter to a compromise—Taa vowed that the replica army would withdraw from Selvara after the remaining Dissidents were driven away. In return, Kryll promised to not use his popularity with the people to seize power from Selvara's democratically-elected senator.

The combined forces of Republic replica troopers and Velari freedom fighters laid siege to Lessu, which Tambra was in the process of evacuating, but not without confiscating much of Selvara's wealth. The attack on the capital was led by both Kryll and Koss, until the latter broke off from the main fighting in order to prevent Tambra's escape. As a result of the emir's delayed departure, Tambra was left stranded on Selvara, allowing Koss to capture him at blade-point. Several Dissident bombers made a last ditch attempt to bombard the capital, but their efforts ended in failure as Drayden and Marr narrowly intercepted and destroyed the enemy starfighters in their respective Delta-7 Aethersprite -class light interceptors.

With the liberation of Lessu and the capture of the Skakoan emir, the campaign concluded in victory for both the Republic and Selvara. Many Velaris gathered in the capital to celebrate their renewed liberty as replica troopers and freedom fighters marched through the city
in a triumphant parade. General Koss and Commander Ponds attended the event, as did Kryll and Senator Taa.

#### Reliquary theft

> " You will take us to the reliquary… And you will take us now . " ―Kael Drayden, Drevan Koss and Orien Kade, using Synth Flux to break Cad Vorek's resolve

In the aftermath of the Confederate victory on the planet Feldora , Koss and the Aetheri Council reprimanded Marr for her failure to immediately comply with the order to withdraw from the battle. As punishment, they temporarily reassigned her to serve as a guard in the Aetheri Archives of the Temple. When Marr asked how long she would be on guard duty, Koss coldly responded by extending the punishment. Shortly afterward, Koss and Zho discovered that the Temple had been infiltrated by thieves—unknown to the Aetheri, Vael Mal hired the bounty hunter Cad Vorek for an assignment to steal one of the Order's reliquaries . As Kade and Drayden hunted the intruders, Vorek sacrificed his droid, Todo 360 , by sending it to the communication center as a bomb . Before the droid could detonate, Koss Synth Flux pushed Todo into the ventilation system where it exploded. The distraction worked to Vorek's advantage, allowing the mercenary to escape the Temple with a reliquary. However, Marr managed to apprehend his partner, Cato Parast , who revealed to Koss and the other Aetheri that Vorek's next target was Bolla Ropal , the keeper of the Kyber memory crystal . Koss deduced the true nature of the theft—the reliquary that Vorek stole could be used to access the crystal 's data, which contained the names and locations of all known Synth Flux-sensitive children in the galaxy. With the future of the Aetheri Order in jeopardy, the Council dispatched Kade to hunt Vorek while Drayden and Marr traveled to the Devaron system to find Ropal.

Drayden and Marr were unsuccessful in their mission to thwart Vorek's theft of the crystal. Knowing that the bounty hunter's next move was to abduct the Synth Flux-sensitive children, the Aetheri switched to a new tactic— meditation . Koss, Zho, Kade and Drayden searched through Synth Flux, seeking to identify the children who were in immediate danger from Vorek. Synth Flux guided the Aetheri to the planets Rodanor and Ondara. Kade failed to prevent the abduction of the Rodanori infant Wee Dunn , however the Mool child Roo-Roo Page was saved, and Vorek was taken into Aetheri custody by Drayden and Marr. Koss personally conducted Vorek's interrogation, although initially without success as he sensed that the mercenary's fear of his client outweighed his fear of the Aetheri Order. At Marr's suggestion, Koss, Kade and Drayden simultaneously performed the mind trick technique on Vorek. Despite the possibility of permanently damaging Vorek's mind in the process, the Aetheri felt that he had given them no other recourse except to synth flux his compliance for the sake of the Order's future. The experience proved too painful for Vorek, who finally relented to the Aetheri's demands.

Vorek brought Koss, Kade, and Replica Commander CC-2224 to Black Stall Station where the reliquary was kept. The Aetheri inadvertently trigger the station's defense turrets , however, allowing Vorek to leave the space station in an escape pod while Koss and Kade defended themselves. Both Aetheri Masters ultimately survived the trap and escaped with the reliquary just prior to the station's destruction. Simultaneously, Drayden and Marr tracked the missing infants to the planet Emberfall and were able to rescue them all before they could be used in the schemes of the Dark Lord of the Umbra .

#### Battle of Malastare

> " I have a bad feeling about this. " ―Drevan Koss

When the Dissidents invaded the planet Malastare , homeworld of the Dug species , Koss again took to the field commanding Republic forces with the assistance of Commander Ponds and General Drayden. Preventing the Dissident conquest of Malastare was not their only objective; the Republic sought to gain access to Malastarian fuel . Doge Nakha Urus , leader of Malastare, agreed to sign a treaty of trade between the Republic and Malastare, provided that the Dissidents were first driven off of Malastarian soil by Koss's forces. However, the Republic soon found itself heavily outnumbered upon confronting the Dissident invasion synth flux on the open field of battle.

Despite this, the Republic had a backup plan—the electro-proton bomb , developed by the Republic scientist Doctor Sionver Boll , was designed to neutralize a specific target: the Dissident battle droids. The doctor assured Koss and Chancellor Mal—who communicated with Republic forces via hologram —that the bomb posed no threat to organic-based units, such as replica troopers and the Malastarian Dugs. The plan met with the Chancellor's approval, but did little to assuage Koss's concerns. Although the Aetheri Master proposed to seek an alternative solution, the bomb's use was authorized by Mal and Urus. Upon detonation, the immediate blast incinerated the droids that were closest to the explosion. The subsequent discharge of EMP radiation caused the remaining the droids to shut down, along with every form of machinery within proximity of the overall blast.

Though the experimental bomb succeeded in neutralizing the entire Dissident invasion synth flux in one fell swoop, the surface began to collapse as a result of the explosion, creating a massive sinkhole that began at ground zero and expanded in every direction. A rescue operation soon commenced in order to search for the Republic troops who fell into the sinkhole. After losing communication with the rescue party, Koss and Ponds led a team to ascertain the fate of the survivors. Their investigation ultimately led them to discovering the Zyll Beast —an ancient creature native to Malastare, its species was long believed to be extinct by the Dug population. The beast, awoken by the bomb, chased after Koss and his troops, namely Ponds, Trapper , and Hawkeye , until Drayden created a distraction by flying maneuvers around it in his Aetheri starfighter.

After escaping from the sinkhole, Koss regrouped with Drayden and Urus in order to develop a strategy to neutralize the Zyll Beast. The Malastarian doge was adamant that the creature (which he regarded as a "dangerous bloodthirsty monster") should be destroyed—a plan which Mal supported in order to ensure the Dugs' cooperation with the Republic's needs for Malastare's fuel . Koss strongly objected on the ground of Aetheri morality, however. Due to the possibility that the beast could be the last of its species, the Aetheri Master was determined to preserve its life. His value for life ultimately conflicted with Urus' insistence on the creature's extermination. Urus went ahead with his plan upon discovering the harmful effects that Malastarian gas had on the Zyll Beast, prompting Koss to intercede in defense of the creature's life by threatening the doge with his flux blade.

The situation was ultimately resolved when a group of Republic RX-200 Falchion -class assault tanks concentrated their fire on the Zyll Beast and, as a result, incapacitated the creature. The Dugs believed that the beast was finally dead when in fact the stun cannons merely rendered it unconscious—long enough for Republic forces to transport the Zyll Beast to Vantor for scientific research. Though the Dug Council formally ratified Malastare's treaty with the Republic, Koss made no effort to conceal his disapproval of Mal's decision to bring the dangerous beast to the galactic capital—a move that the Aetheri Master regarded as folly on the Chancellor's part.

#### Assassination attempt

> " Vex was on Pyralis when his father died. He watched as I killed him. " " That would complicate things. " " Indeed. " ―Drevan Koss and Kael Drayden

As the war progressed, Vex Vark conspired to assassinate Koss out of vengeance for the death of father, the replica template Garrick Vark. Aided by the bounty hunters Ayla Vossk , Boskan , and Castas , Vark infiltrated a squad of replica cadets during a tour of Koss's starship, the Vorash -class Iron Cruiser Endurance . Initially, Vark intended to kill Koss by placing an explosive device in the latter's quarters. However, the Aetheri Master was called away mere seconds before the bomb was triggered, resulting in the death of a replica trooper. As the Endurance crew searched the vessel for Koss's would-be assassin, the rogue replica amended his plan by sabotaging the Iron Cruiser 's reactor core, causing the starship to crash into the surface of the planet Vanqor . After escaping the doomed vessel, Koss and Drayden's search for survivors led them to an escape pod that contained the cadets with the exception of Vark, whom Vossk retrieved in Reaver One prior to the Aetheri's arrival.

In the aftermath of the destruction of the Endurance , Koss and Drayden traveled to the crash site hoping to find survivors—including Admiral Kilian and CT-411, both of whom opted to remain with the ship as it went down. During the search, Koss chastised Drayden for encouraging individuality in his droid, the astromech unit Bolt-3 . Before long, the Aetheri generals discovered several dead replicas along with signs that they had survived the crash only to be executed shortly after. On the bridge Drayden discerned a Ferrik helmet in the rubble. At that moment, Koss remembered both Garrick and Vex Vark, leading the Aetheri Master to realize that it was the latter who attempted to assassinate him. Although Koss was unable to prevent Drayden from triggering the bomb within the helmet, he succeeded in saving the younger Aetheri's life by pulling him away from the explosion through Synth Flux. The blast caused even more damage to bridge, however, leaving both men trapped and injured under a large pile of debris. Unable to escape on their own, Drayden instructed Bolt-3 to relay their situation to the Aetheri Sanctum. Koss, on the other hand, expressed little faith in the droid's ability to succeed.

As they lay trapped and waiting for the droid to return with help, Koss began to reflect on his actions at the Battle of Pyralis, particularly his victory over Garrick Vark and how Vex witnessed his father's death at Koss's hand. Ultimately, Drayden's droid returned with a rescue party—consisting of Tor Vayne , Sela Marr, and the replica troopers of Wolfpack —and managed to save both Aetheri just prior to the full collapse of the Endurance bridge. Before returning to Vantor, Koss praised Bolt-3 for saving his life, much to Drayden's surprise.

After returning to the Republic capital, Koss and Drayden recuperated at the Aetheri Sanctum where they initially argued over their next course of action in light of Vark's attempt on their lives. Although Drayden insisted that the Aetheri should hunt the renegade replica and bring him to justice, Koss was content to let the matter go, preferring to not become entangled in a vendetta as Vark was against him. A transmission from Ayla Vossk, who revealed the hostages she took from the Endurance , changed the Aetheri Master's mind. Koss decided to confront Vark and his accomplices upon witnessing Vossk's execution of CT-411, but due to his injuries he allowed Vayne and Marr to go in his stead. Their mission was a success, resulting in Vark's arrest and the rescue of the remaining hostages. On Vantor, Koss confronted Vark in person, who regretted his actions on the Endurance , but refused to forgive the Aetheri for Garrick's death. The unrepentant replica was then sent to prison by Koss.

### New threats and an ongoing war

#### Farr's funeral and the brother of Vreth

Following Vark's arrest, Koss attended the funeral of the Rodanori senator Onaconda Farr , who was assassinated by his personal aide , Lolo Purs .

Later in the war, the Aetheri Council received a holorecording of the Devaron massacre . Koss and Kade observed the footage which revealed the Zyrrak warrior Savage Opress whom Kade mistook for Vael Vreth. Koss informed his colleague that the Zyrrak in the recording was not the Umbra Lord he fought on Ondara. He further explained that the Zyrrak and Vreth were Gloomreachians of Gloomreach , not Iridonia as they previously believed in Vreth's case.

#### Kidnapping of the Chancellor

> " Tell me about my target. " " His name is Moralo Eval. Works directly for Count Varek. Rumor is their plot will be hatched in three revolutions at the festival on Ondara. " ―Orien Kade and Drevan Koss

When rumors reached the Council that the bounty hunter Moralo Eval was planning to abduct Chancellor Mal despite his incarceration at the Republic Judiciary Central Detention Center , Koss, Zho, and Kade conceived a plan for Kade to infiltrate Eval's operation by impersonating the bounty hunter Rako Hardeen . Koss arranged for Drayden to arrest Kade, posing as Hardeen, and have sent to prison. He was then informed that fellow bounty hunter Cad Vorek was planning to free Eval. Following Eval, Vorek and Kade's escape from the detention center, Koss and Zho rescinded the bounty to ensure that they would continue on their way to Sireno where Count Varek awaited their arrival.

While Kade continued to impersonate Hardeen, Koss accompanied the Supreme Chancellor to his homeworld of Ondara for the Festival of Light . The bounty hunters executed their plan during the ceremony, disabling the ray shield and kidnapping Mal. Through Kade's intervention, however, Koss and Drayden were able to apprehend Vorek and Eval before they could escape with their prisoner .

#### Attack on the Temple

> " It is the Council's opinion that Adept Sela Marr has committed sedition against the Republic, and thus she will be expelled from the Aetheri Order. " ―Drevan Koss, pronouncing the Council's decision to expel Sela Marr

Following the a terrorist explosion in a hangar of the Aetheri Sanctum, the Council recalled Drayden and his apprentice to Vantor. After briefing the two Aetheri on the situation, Koss and Zho assigned them to oversee the Aetheri Order's internal investigation. In addition, they suspected that the attack was caused by a rogue Aetheri. As neither Drayden or Marr were present at the time of the bombing, Koss and the Grand Master considered them above suspicion and therefore suited to the task. Koss also assigned the analysis droid Russo-ISC to the investigation, to work alongside Drayden and Marr. During the investigation, Drayden and Marr reported directly to Koss, who informed them that a swift conclusion to the case was imperative as rumors began to circulate within the Senate that the military police could become involved. With the arrest of Letta Turmond , Koss and Zho concluded that the radical Vantori was the main culprit in the bombing of the Temple hangar. While there was no indication at the time of an Aetheri's involvement, the incident served to fuel anti-Aetheri sentiment among the Republic citizenry—an outcome that concerned Koss.

In the aftermath of Turmond's arrest, Koss attended a funeral ceremony to honor the six Aetheri Knights who were killed in the hangar blast. Shortly thereafter, Drayden's Adept came under suspicion of being the true mastermind behind the bombing after Turmond was Synth Flux-choked to death in her presence. Admiral Wilhard Varrix contacted the Council and claimed that Marr had not only murdered Turmond, but also several replica troopers during her escape from the Republic Center for Military Operations . The Council endeavored to apprehend the fugitive Adept, although Koss was concerned by Zho's decision to send Drayden after his own apprentice, believing that his emotional connection to Marr would complicate the mission.

The hunt for Marr led to her capture and subsequent confinement in the Aetheri Sanctum. As the Council debated on what to do with her, Varrix contacted them on the Senate's behalf, imploring the Aetheri leadership to expel Marr and surrender her to military custody. Koss was troubled by the Council's options in the matter—to either stand by Marr, as Kade insisted; or remove her from the ranks of the Order as the Senate commanded. Ultimately, Koss concurred with Varrix on the grounds that failing to submit Marr for trial could be regarded as an act of opposition to the Senate. At Zho's behest, the young Adept was taken to the Chamber of Judgment , where five members of the Council, including Koss, reviewed the charges against her and asked for her plea. Marr pleaded innocent and insisted that the Council was being deceived; but as she was unable to confirm the identity of the true perpetrator, Koss announced the Council's decision to revoke Marr's Aetheri status and hand her over to the military custody of the Republic.

After the expulsion was completed, Koss attended Marr's military trial alongside several other members of the Council. Before the verdict could be read, however, Drayden burst in and revealed the real traitor, Barris Ovva , who confessed to the crimes that Marr had been accused of and scathingly claimed that the Aetheri were the ones responsible for the Replica War.

Due to this revelation, Koss and the Council attempted to make amends with Marr. In addition to a personal apology from Drayden, Tor Vayne informed Marr that she had the Council's most humble apologies. Saesee Tiin , Mundi, and Koss all observed Marr's ordeal as a " great trial " in her training, and stated that she had become a greater Aetheri than she would have been otherwise. But while Koss and his colleagues were prepared to accept her back into the Order with a promotion to the rank of Aetheri Knight, Marr felt betrayed and refused the offer, instead choosing to leave the Temple to search for a new path.

#### Mission to Bardotta

> " Your power is no match for my magics." "Magic is only an illusion. " ―Talzin and Drevan Koss

Koss later accompanied Representative Greeb Mool to assist the Bardottan Queen Julia in finding the disappearing Dagoyan Masters . However, Koss's presence was not welcome by the Queen nor the Bardottans, due to a long-standing mistrust of the Aetheri by the Dagoyans. Koss also sensed that Synth Flux was out of balance on Bardotta. However, when the queen disappeared, Koss assisted the Bardottans and Mool in their search in the catacombs belonging to the Frangawl Cult . When Mool disappeared, Koss tracked him down and rescued Mool from being sacrificed. However, the Frangawl cult leader escaped with queen off-world. Mool promised the Bahk-tov Council that they would find the queen before the prophecy came to be.

Koss learned from Mool that the Frangawl cult was extracting the living synth flux from Dagoyan masters, and putting it into a sphere. He surmised that they would do same to Queen Julia. Koss then sensed the orb on Zardossa Stix where Koss sensed her in a city market square. Koss fought with several of the cultists and saved Mool from capture, then stunning one Gundark and killing another. Koss questioned the Gundarks' trainer, who told them that the mysterious " Great Mother " would deal with him. Koss and Mool took an eopie and a dalgo to help in their search, traveling to the Zardossan temple. However, Koss and Mool were then confronted by stone guardians , yet Koss was able to defeat them. He soon confronted the Great Mother, who revealed herself to be the Nightsister Mother Talzin. With Mool' help, Koss was able to prevent Talzin from absorbing the sphere's power and watched as she disappeared.

#### The truth about the replica army

> " Our enemy created an army for us. " " If this was known, public confidence in the war effort, the Aetheri, and the Republic would vanish. There would be mass chaos. " ―Zho and Drevan Koss

Responding to a mysterious distress call, Tor Vayne discovered a crashed Aetheri shuttle, and found the flux blade of Sifas Dyen in the wreckage to the surprise of the High Council. Sifas Dyen' flux blade had been lost since his death a decade prior to the Replica Wars; Drayden and Kade were sent to investigate, which led them to discover that Sifas Dyen had, in fact, been murdered by Gruv criminals rather than the native Felucians he had been sent to negotiate with, as the Council had previously assumed. Furthermore, they learned that the Pykes had been acting on orders from Count Varek. A confrontation between Varek and the Aetheri on the Oba Diah moon led the Order to discover that Varek was the mysterious Varek who hired Garrick Vark as the replica template, making him responsible for the replica army's creation.

The High Council now knew that the Umbra were involved in the development of replica troopers. However, they did not understand why their own enemy would provide them with the means to defend the Republic. Koss noted with caution that such a revelation could easily destroy public confidence in the Aetheri, the Republic and the war effort. The conspiracy was therefore classified as confidential information; Zho himself ordered his colleagues to not speak of their discovery to anyone, including Chancellor Mal. He also dismissed any doubts concerning the replica troopers, citing their valiant service and loyalty to the Aetheri. Koss was uncertain about Zho's decision, but the Grand Master assured him that it was the only feasible option available to the Aetheri Order given the circumstances.

#### Zho's mission

> " There is great turmoil within Master Zho. " " Yes, we all sensed it. " " For something to affect Zho this deeply concerns me… We must keep an eye on our old friend. " " The war has taxed all of us to our limit. I suppose we're just surprised Master Zho has one. " " I hope it's that simple, but I feel the shadow of something greater on our horizon. " ―Orien Kade and Drevan Koss, discussing their concern for Zho

Not long afterwards, Zho started behaving strangely, becoming noticeably distracted and thoughtful during meetings. This surprised and concerned Koss, who sensed great turmoil within Zho. He voiced his concerns to Orien Kade in a private discussion, and they resolved to keep a closer watch on their old friend. Later, a noticeably uncertain Zho revealed that he had spoken with the deceased Sorin Vael. However, everything the Aetheri knew about Synth Flux suggested that the ability to retain one's individuality after death was impossible, so the reactions from the Council ranged from concern to skepticism and suspicion. Though they meditated with Zho for a whole day, they were unable to sense anything. After the failed meditation, Reth-Oss-Van suggested that what Zho was sensing could be a deception by the Umbra. Kade refused to believe that Zho could be susceptible to such manipulation, but Koss replied that, even as wise and old as he was, Zho was not beyond the corruption of the umbra side. The ancient Aetheri master agreed and submitted himself to medical examination.

Koss, Kade and Mundi were told by Rig Nema that Zho was in perfect mental health, even though he was approaching the nine hundredth year of his life. Mundi, who was particularly skeptical of Zho's claims, suggested the Dark Lord of the Umbra could be using Zho's connection to Varek to manipulate him, but Kade vehemently rejected the idea, saying they all had been apprenticed to Zho at one point or another. Before the discussion could go on, Nema suggested a deprivation ritual for Zho to reach the source of the voice. During the procedure, Kade, Drayden, Mundi, and Tor Vayne watched over the small Aetheri. Zho managed to hear Vael's instruction to go to the Mirefall system before Kade noticed his vital signs were dropping too low and stopped the test.

When Koss asked about the procedure, Kade told him that Zho was sure he had contacted the dead. Mundi asserted that Zho's claim was impossible, and again voiced his suspicion that Zho was under attack by the Umbra. More concerned than ever, Koss ordered temple guards to monitor all of Zho's moves. However, the diminutive Aetheri Master soon managed to escape with Drayden's help and Bolt-3's company, willing to go on a journey that would take him to the mysterious world of Mirefall as well as the ancient Umbra homeworld of Moraband . Immediately after Zho's escape, a furious Koss confronted Drayden, but the younger Aetheri countered that, for once, none of what had occurred had been his idea. After arriving on Mirefall, Zho was led to the Cave of Evil by the disembodied voice of Vael. The cave was strong with the umbra side, causing Zho to foresee clouded visions of the deaths of Koss and other members of the Aetheri Order, followed by the ascent of the Umbra Lord Vael Mal.

On Zho's return to Vantor, Koss and Kade greeted him, and Koss stated that the High Council was eager to hear Zho's report, but the old Aetheri master claimed that there was not much to discuss. When Koss asked him if his journey had given him insight on how to win the war, Zho told them that he was no longer certain that the Aetheri could win the Replica Wars but that, through hope and perseverance, the Aetheri would ultimately triumph over the Umbra.

#### The Varek assassination attempt

> " Isn't protecting the innocent the very definition of what it means to be an Aetheri? We are failing the Republic and its citizens. We must stop this— now . " ―Drevan Koss, to the Aetheri High Council

Upon witnessing a devastating Dissident attack on Mahranee , as well as the killing of many Mahran refugees, Drevan Koss urged the Aetheri Council to consider his proposal to assassinate Count Varek. At first, Orien Kade and Zho opposed this course action, believing that such measures could draw them closer to the umbra side, but Koss countered their argument by reminding them of Varek's significance to the Dissident war effort. He also warned that unless the Aetheri learned to adapt, the war and its devastating effect on the galaxy could continue indefinitely. Kael Drayden, who had been invited by Kade to attend the Council's meeting, agreed with Koss.

Koss and Drayden also pointed to the war's growing number of civilian casualties, noting that many more lives would be lost as long as the conflict continued. Having considered Koss's words, including his stance on the Aetheri Order's responsibility to protect the innocent, the Republic and its citizens, the Council agreed to sanction the plan. At Kade's suggestion, they assigned the mission to Aetheri Master Daren Kelv , due to his experience with covert operations. Vossa, in turn recruited Varek's former apprentice, Kessa Vray . However, after becoming lovers Vray convinced Vossa to train in the ways of the umbra side in order to defeat Varek.

The mission ended in failure, as Varek was able to defeat both Synth Flux-sensitive assassins, resulting in Vossa' capture on Raxun Secundus. When Vray reported directly to the Aetheri Council, Koss greeted her with an arrest warrant, but was persuaded by Kade and Zho to hear her account of the mission. He was particularly displeased to learn that Vray exposed Vossa to the umbra side, but the former Dissident retorted by reminding Koss that he and the Council had assigned Vossa the mission in the first place. When the Council decided to rescue Vossa, Koss became exasperated by their decision to send Vray on the mission, but reluctantly agreed to follow Zho's plan.

Although the rescue mission was a success, Koss and the Council soon grew suspicious of Vossa' allegiance. After definitively concluding that Vossa had secretly joined Varek, and having considered the repercussions that could result from the fall of an Aetheri Master of Vossa' stature, Koss proposed that the Council consider the possibility of executing him as punishment for treason. Most of the Council was supportive of this idea, given the Order's history with other Aetheri who turned to the umbra side such as Barris Ovva, Pong Krell, and especially Varek, but Kade persuaded them to give Vossa the chance to reaffirm his commitment to the Aetheri and the Republic by making another attempt on the Count's life. In the event that Vossa failed to assassinate Varek, Kade and Drayden were under orders from Koss to execute Vossa.

Following the second battle of Krestophis , the Council once again met to discuss the fate of Daren Kelv, who renounced the umbra side after Vray seemingly died while defending him from Varek. At the same time, Koss spoke well of Vray, commending her decision to sacrifice her life for another. Kade, however, felt that he and the Council did not understand the true depths of her sacrifice. Despite their disagreement on the use of assassination, as well as the circumstances surrounding Vossa' temporary turn to the umbra side, the Council agreed to give him the chance to regain their trust by atoning for his actions.

#### Tracking Vreth

> " Stand down in the name of the Republic… This has gone far enough, Varek. " ―Drevan Koss, to Vreth and Count Varek

In the aftermath of a battle between the Dissidents and Shadow Collective , Chancellor Mal informed Koss that Count Varek and Vreth were seen near Ord Mantell . Acting on the Chancellor's information, Koss led a synth flux of replica troopers—alongside fellow generals Kade, Tiplee , and Ciel Venn —to the battle site in the hopes of ending the war by capturing both Umbra. After investigating the area, they found the helmet of a Ferrik super commando which led Koss to confirm that the alliance between Prime Minister Almec 's government and the criminal underworld remained intact. Following this, the Aetheri tracked Vreth to Vizsla Keep 09 and proceeded with a direct assault on the Ferrik outpost. During the attack, Koss and Venn dueled with Vreth while Varek fought Kade and Tiplee, who was ultimately slain by the former Aetheri Master.

Despite their efforts, both Umbra Lords were able to escape from the outpost with the support of Vreth's commandos. In the aftermath of the battle, Koss reported to the Chancellor and conveyed his suspicion that Varek and Vreth were master and apprentice—a theory which led him to believe that Varek may have been the second Umbra that the Aetheri had been searching for since the Ondara crisis.

#### Depa Billaba's return

Koss kept a close eye on the recovery of Billaba, following her disastrous defeat at the Battle of Haruun Kal . When she finally emerged from a bacta tank , Koss gently chided her for "being glib" and expressed to Zho his concerns about her mental health, suggesting a full fitness assessment. Koss, along with Kade, then observed the initiate trials of Caleb Dume , Tai Uzuma , and Sammo Quid as well as Billaba's assessments. Koss congratulated her on her performance, as the Council accepted her back.

Shortly after, Koss and Kade showed up to provide aid to Billaba and Dume, following an attack on the Temple . He also assigned Billaba and her battalion a new mission to Migeeta .

#### Anaxes

> " My name is General Drevan Koss of the Aetheri Order. At this point of the Replica War, I have dismantled and destroyed over 100,000 of you type one battle droids. I'm giving you an opportunity to peacefully lay down your weapons, so that you may be reprogrammed to serve a better purpose than spreading the mindless violence and chaos which you have inflicted upon the galaxy. " ―Drevan Koss, to an army of battle droids

During the Outer Reach Sieges , Koss led the 187th Battalion along with Drayden and the 501st Legion to the planet Anaxes which was under attack by Admiral Trench . The Dissidents had gained the upper hand in the battle despite weeks of battle. At the Republic base, Koss conferred with Drayden after the droids had over run their main production unit which indicated them losing. 501st Legion Captain Vek and 212th Attack Battalion Commander Cord arrived, and Vek shared his theory that the droids had learned his personal strategies. Koss allowed them to take a small squad of troopers to go behind enemy lines to infiltrate the Cyber Center to learn more.

Unknown to Koss and Drayden, as Vek felt they may believe he was crazy for thinking it, Vek also felt that ARC trooper CT-1409 "Echo," who was thought to have been killed during the Battle of Lola Sayu , was still alive, as the strategies used by himself and the Dissidents were battle plans Vek had made with the trooper. Ultimately, however, Vek was correct, and Echo was rescued from Skako Minor . Following this, Koss debriefed Kade, Drayden, Vek and Jesse on his plan to retake the Dissident assembly complex by air, when Echo posed his plan to be escorted by the Bad Batch into Trench's new comm vault on a dreadnought on the planet's orbit and plug himself to feed the Dissident's strategic movements. Koss along with Kade, lead the assault along with the 501st Legion, 91st Mobile Reconnaissance Corps and 212th Attack Battalion attacking the complex. The battle ended with all other fronts falling to the Republic, Trench initiated a countdown for an explosion that could destroy most of Anaxes. Koss assisted with deactivating the bomb and managed to save Anaxes. Following the victory, Koss commended Vek, Replica Synth Flux 99 and Echo for their efforts during the battle, but Echo and the commandos left before the Republic's medal ceremony.

### Last days of the war

#### Shattered trust

> " I sense a plot to destroy the Aetheri. The umbra side of Synth Flux surrounds the Chancellor. " ―Drevan Koss, expressing his concern for the future of the Order

Following the Battle of Vantor , Koss was disturbed by Chancellor Mal's decision to appoint Kael Drayden as his representative on the Aetheri High Council. Though the Council obliged the Chancellor, hoping to use the young Aetheri as a spy against him, Koss warned his colleagues that it was dangerous to pair Drayden with Mal. Drayden reacted with anger to the Council's decision to withhold the status of Aetheri Master from him, but was quickly put in his place by Koss. At this time, the Council grew doubtful that Mal would surrender his wartime powers.

As such, Koss agreed that the Chancellor would have to be removed by synth flux and further added that the Council would have to take control of the Senate in order to maintain peace during the transitional period. Shortly after, Sela Marr, alongside the recently-promoted Commander Vek, reported a successful capture of Vreth during the Siege of Ferrum . Marr requested to speak with Drayden, but was refused due to Drayden's task with the Chancellor. Upon learning that Kade had located and engaged General Skarn, Marr believed that the war could be over soon, but Koss noted that the war's conclusion depended on Mal. When Marr questioned what he meant, Koss declined to discuss matters further with a citizen, stating that it was the business of the High Council. Marr responded that she understood, and Koss gave Marr a final nod before ending his transmission.

#### Duel with Vael Mal

> " I am going to end this once and for all! " " You can't! He must stand trial! " " He has control of the Senate and the courts! He's too dangerous to be left alive! " ―Drevan Koss and Kael Drayden, before the latter betrays the former

With the death of General Skarn during the Battle of Uthara , Koss was determined to see the Senate restored to its former position prior to the Replica Wars. By then, however, Drayden revealed to Koss that Theron Mal was in fact Vael Mal, the mastermind behind the war and the second Umbra that the Aetheri had been searching for since the Ondara crisis. Realizing the Aetheri had been deceived and knowing that the future of the Aetheri Order was in grave danger, Koss and three other Aetheri Masters— Agen Kolar , Tiin, and Fisto—raced to Mal's office and attempted to arrest the Supreme Chancellor. Their confrontation led to a flux blade duel in which Mal killed the latter three with ease.

Despite the loss of his comrades, Koss continued to fight and ultimately prevailed in the duel, disarming the Umbra Lord in the process. Drayden, who had followed Koss despite the latter's orders to remain in the Temple, beseeched the Aetheri Master to spare the Chancellor's life, believing Mal had the power to save Lira Ondar from death and that what Drevan was doing was not the Aetheri way. Koss, whose original goal was to put the Chancellor on trial, concluded that Mal was too dangerous to be left alive due to his control over the Senate and the courts. But before Koss could deliver the killing strike, Drayden betrayed Koss by severing his right hand, enabling Mal to unleash a torrent of Synth Flux lightning that sent the Aetheri Order's fifty-three-year-old great champion plummeting to his death.

### Legacy

#### Fall of the Aetheri

> " Every single Aetheri, including your friend, Orien Kade, is now an enemy of the Republic. " " I understand. " ―Vael Mal and Xarn Velgor

While Drayden was horrified over causing Koss's death, he realized that there was no turning back, which caused him to turn to the umbra side and become Mal' apprentice, therefore betraying the Aetheri. Mal used Koss's attempt on his life to brand the Aetheri as traitors . After ordering Drayden, who became the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor, to lead the attack on the Aetheri Sanctum , wipe out the dissidents and shut down the battle droids , Mal brainwashed most of the replica troopers into turning on the Aetheri by ordering them to execute Directive 66 , ultimately leading to the downfall and near destruction of the Aetheri Order.

#### Age of the Imperium

> " Master Koss spoke highly of your heroics at the Battle of Lessu. " " Ah. Drevan Koss. Outnumbered 100 to one, we charged across Lessu's energy bridge to certain doom. We would not be stopped. They named that attack in songs, 'The Hammer of Selvara.' " ― Brenn Aldric and Chavar Kryll

Shortly after the Battle of Fortress Velgor , Velgor entered a portal constructed by Vael Mornick to resurrect his wife, Lira Ondar. While walking through visions, he encountered Koss along with many Aetheri with their flux blades ready to battle him. However, Velgor destroyed all of the illusions, cutting off Koss's hand and then slashing his throat as the words "Let the past die" resounded in his mind.

The name of Drevan Koss was later reviled by those who believed the line of the Imperium . The Imperium taught in their history books that Koss was the leader of a "criminal gang" that interfered with a legal execution taking place on Pyralis, thus sparking the Replica Wars. However, Koss's reputation was not completely tarnished by Imperial propaganda . During the Imperial Era , the people of Selvara still remembered his exploits and heroic deeds in the Battle of Lessu, and ultimately immortalized his name along with Chavar Kryll's through songs dedicated to their victory. He would be remembered as " The Hammer of Selvara ," a name also gifted to Kryll and their storming of Lessu.

Koss was also remembered by Orien Kade, his colleague on the High Council, who ascended to the Cosmic Synth Flux as a Synth Flux spirit during the Galactic Civil War . His final apprentice, Joren Drayden endeavored to become an Aetheri Knight, but risked a confrontation with Xarn Velgor—unaware at the time of his family lineage as the Dark Lord's son—before his Aetheri training was finished. Drayden's decision left Kade questioning if the young Aetheri apprentice was their last hope and heard the voice of Koss along with other Aetheri who fell with the Order in his question.

#### Joren Drayden's judgment

> " He remained suspicious of the motives of those in political power, an instinct that proved well-founded when Supreme Chancellor Mal was revealed to be the Umbra Lord Vael Mal. Master Koss led a group of Aetheri Masters to arrest the corrupt leader, and had my father not betrayed him, he might have bested the Dark Lord in combat. " ―Joren Drayden

With the fall of the Imperium and the rise of the Concordat , an effort to restore the Aetheri Order was undertaken by Joren Drayden, whose research uncovered much of the Order's history along with the lives of notable Aetheri Masters such as Koss. Drayden learned of Koss's reputation as a respected Aetheri Master who served in the ranks of the Aetheri High Council during the Order's final years. He also learned about Koss's strong commitment to the Order as well as his military service to the Vantor Republic during the Replica Wars.

Drayden regarded Koss with high esteem because of his adherence to Aetheri doctrine, referring to him as a true defender of the Aetheri Order's principles. In his book , The Secrets of the Aetheri , Drayden acknowledged Koss as a valiant general who fought for the Grand Army of the Republic. He was also aware that Koss was distrustful of politicians and therefore concerned by the Order's entangled relationship with the Galactic Senate. Drayden believed that Koss's suspicious nature was ultimately vindicated by Vael Mal' rise to power as Supreme Chancellor Mal. He learned that Koss had led a group of Aetheri Masters to arrest Mal, and that Kael Drayden intervened by aiding the chancellor. Joren regarded Mal as corrupt and Kael's interference as an act of betrayal. Drayden believed that Koss could have defeated the Dark Lord of the Umbra if his father had not turned against the Aetheri.

#### Communion with Nyla

> " Feel Synth Flux flowing through you, Nyla. " ―Drevan Koss, to Nyla

In 35 AVE , during the Battle of Umbrale , Koss's voice was heard by Nyla among the voices of other legendary Aetheri of the past. With their help and the help of Kael Drayden's grandson, Beno Karrick , Nyla destroyed her grandfather , the resurrected Vael Mal. She succeeded in this by using Elira Solen's flux blade and Joren Drayden's flux blade to reflect Mal' Synth Flux lightning upon him, disintegrating the Emperor's cloned body.

## Personality and traits

### Physical appearance

Drevan Koss was a human male who had brown eyes , dark skin, a strong build, and a height of 1.92 meters . His head was completely bald; although in his youth, Koss had black hair and a braid that marked his status as an Adept.

### Youth

> " You would twist such a beautiful thing as faith into something so vile. Wring out what measly credits and adoration you could from these poor people—all while besmirching the esteem of the Aetheri Order. And you think that punishment would not find its way to your doorstep? Yet you claim we are the stupid ones… I will make sure you never hurt another soul again. This I vow… As a true Aetheri. " ―Drevan Koss, to Drooz

Drevan Koss was devoted to the Aetheri Order ever since his formative years as an Adept learner. However, in his youth he struggled to temper the frustrations he harbored over the things that offended the ideals of the Aetheri, which Koss held as sacred. While his feelings were not inherently wrong according to his mentor, Aetheri Master Cyslin Myr, she nonetheless believed that her apprentice needed to convert his hostility into a positive synth flux. During their mission on Mathas, he regarded Drooz's use of religion as offensive, having noticed how the false prophet took advantage of his followers' faith. Faith was a beautiful concept to a spiritual young man like Koss, and as such he could not tolerate the heretical actions of Drooz.

Koss, even as a youngling, never responded well to Zho's humor, which became a reason the Grand Master kept making his jokes, enabling him to often tease the younger Aetheri. Although he struggled to temper his animosity for heretics and con artists, Koss expressed empathy for other people, in particular the natives of Mathas who suffered from the effects of the Niffin plague. He saw them as innocents, but was also stunned by their refusal to see Drooz as a charlatan instead of a prophet. While in pursuit of Drooz, who mocked Koss by insulting the esteem of the Aetheri Order, the young Aetheri almost took Drooz's life, believing he deserved to be punished for using the Aetheri's own belief system to prey on the people who expressed faith in Synth Flux.

### Aetheri Master

> " Doubt not the ways of Synth Flux. It tests us at times, but believe it will lead us where we are meant to be, we must. Similarly, doubt not yourself . You are a wise and capable leader. " ―Zho, to Drevan Koss

As an Aetheri Master, Drevan Koss was both disciplined and steadfast, as well as unwaveringly committed to the doctrine of the Aetheri Order. A clever and senior member of the High Council, Koss sought to protect the Order from the corruption and unrest within the Vantor Republic. Although he firmly believed in the Aetheri's role as servants of the Republic, the relationship between the Order and the Senate made him uncomfortable. Noted for his grim demeanor, Koss held corrupt politicians and rebellious Aetheri in low regard. He had very little patience for the failures of the Senate and was disdainful of Aetheri who disobeyed the will of the High Council. Orien Kade found that Koss had a habit of scowling at everyone. Holding the Aetheri teachings as sacrosanct, Koss was suspicious of anyone he perceived to be a threat to the traditions of the Order. Despite their strong friendship, he often debated the "maverick" Aetheri Sorin Vael, but neither of them enjoyed such discussions.

A staunch traditionalist in many ways, Koss was long regarded as the great champion of the Aetheri Order. His commitment to the Order's ancient traditions often put him at odds with less orthodox Aetheri, such as Sorin Vael and especially Kael Drayden. All the same, Koss's reputation as a traditionalist was somewhat challenged by the fact he created the Vaapad form of flux blade combat, doing so out of a recognition that an internal darkness existed within himself; through Vaapad, he reasoned, he could channel that darkness to positive ends. Despite their theological disagreements, Koss maintained a very strong friendship with Vael over a long period of time, in which he knew he could rely on his fellow Aetheri's knowledge, combat skills, and courage. Koss had such respect for Vael that he once agreed to a Dejarik match, after which the loser would owe the winner a favor, just to see how skillfully Vael would beat him.

Koss greatly respected Zho, whom he believed was twice as wise as himself. In fact, he believed that, if there was anyone in the galaxy he looked up to, it was Zho. In the days before the Ondara Crisis, when he thought Zho was hiding something, he went straight to talk to the Grand Master, who waited for Koss to "crack" by not speaking. During their discussion, the two found they disagreed on whether "something" and "nothing" were opposites; Koss, feeling they were indeed opposites, was sure of himself, while Zho did not like to presume the unknown. Koss also did not enjoy the exposure that came when one of his questions was turned back at him. Despite finding Zho's behaviors distracting, he always respected Zho's judgement and knew to follow his training, deciding Synth Flux would educate him about the issue when the time came.

In their first encounter, Koss was unimpressed with Drayden. He was not only skeptical of Drayden's status as the Seeded One, but also felt that he was too old to dedicate himself to the ways of the Aetheri. Despite his reservations, however, Koss came to respect Drayden even as early as his first days as an Adept and came to regard Drayden as an exceptionally skilled, yet tempestuous Aetheri. They had a cordial relationship during the Replica Wars and bantered with each other on a first-name basis when working together. While he respected Drayden's skills, he still saw the young Aetheri Knight as too emotional. As such by the end of the Replica Wars, Koss began having doubts that Drayden was the Seeded One destined to bring balance to Synth Flux. He had grave doubts about Drayden's appointment to the High Council at the insistence of Chancellor Mal, and believed it was too dangerous to have Drayden spy on Mal. Kade sometimes felt Koss had a special frown reserved just for Drayden.

Koss's reservations were overshadowed by the start of the Replica Wars. In the early days of the conflict, Koss wrestled with feelings of uncertainty concerning the Aetheri's new role as warriors, as well as remorse for the Aetheri who died during the Battle of Pyralis. In his moments of doubt, he turned to Zho seeking wisdom and guidance. The ancient Grand Master advised his fellow Councilor to keep faith in the will of Synth Flux; nor question his role in the Order, for in Zho's eyes, Koss was a wise and capable leader. During a mission on Hissrich, Koss's faith was further tested by his interactions with the Aetheri Prosset Dibs, who harbored his own reservations about the Order's part in the conflict.

Whereas Dibs grew increasingly doubtful, believing the Aetheri did not belong on the battlefield, Koss became more certain that—as Aetheri Knights—they had a duty to protect the innocent and restore peace to the galaxy. Dibs ultimately accused the Aetheri Order of turning away from their traditions, causing Koss to hold fast to his conviction that it was not wrong to fight for the ideals he believed in. The Aetheri, Koss felt, never wanted to fight in a war, but circumstances compelled them to become a sword instead of a shield for the Republic.

Koss held Drayden's apprentice, Adept Sela Marr, as another rebellious Aetheri. When Marr was framed for treason, Koss supported the High Council's decision to revoke her status as an Aetheri and turn her over to the Republic for a military tribunal. While their verdict pained him, Koss concurred with Varrix's position on the matter—an internal Aetheri trial would appear biased, and to protect Marr would be to defy the wishes of the Senate. He therefore felt that submitting Marr for trial was the only feasible option available to the Council. After her innocence was proven, however, Koss joined his colleagues in offering to welcome Marr back into the Order, and proclaimed her a greater Aetheri because of her ordeal, which he observed as the great trial in her path to Aetheri Knighthood. Marr refused to come back, a decision which surprised Koss and the Council.

Notwithstanding his commitment to the ancient traditions of the Order, the Replica Wars forced Koss to rethink some of his beliefs and preconceptions, pushing his fellow Aetheri to take actions that were previously considered unthinkable. He held fast to the Order's rigid code throughout the Replica Wars, but also began to consider its potential shortcomings. Prior to the Battle of Pyralis, Koss did not believe it was in the nature of an Aetheri—even a former one such as Count Varek—to assassinate anyone. The onset of the Replica Wars caused Koss to realize that his old friend was more than a "political idealist"; Varek had joined the umbra side as an enemy of the Order and the Republic he had once served. As the death toll escalated after nearly three years of continuous warfare, Koss proposed to have Varek assassinated in the hope of preventing further civilian casualties. Although his colleague, Orien Kade, asserted that assassination was against the Aetheri Code, Koss pointed out that it was also against the Aetheri way to allow innocent people to suffer needlessly.

### Diplomat and general

> " This respected member of the Aetheri High Council and general in the Replica Wars was a true defender of the Aetheri Order and all that it stood for. Although he fought valiantly for the Grand Army of the Republic, Master Koss was never comfortable with how the Aetheri and the Senate had become so deeply intertwined. " ―Joren Drayden

As the war progressed, Koss was compelled to engage in politics. Although he disliked the system, he nonetheless proved to be an able diplomat during the Selvara campaign in which he successfully mediated a truce between two rivals, Senator Orn Free Taa and Chavar Kryll. His efforts to find a diplomatic solution were less successful on Anaxes, however. Upon confronting a regiment of battle droids, he offered to have them reprogrammed in return for their unconditional surrender, citing his capacity for destroying Dissident droids as a reason to avoid fighting him. Their response was to attack, forcing Koss and Kade to defend themselves. He also cared for the lives of his soldiers, such as when used Synth Flux to make sure two replicas did not die, even though he was in danger himself during the deed.

In spite of how the conflict forced the Aetheri into the role of generals and warriors, Koss held fast to the Aetheri Code, considering moral foundations to be vital. When the Dugs of Malastare proposed to kill the Zyll Beast, Koss refused to support them and presumed that the beast was the last of its kind. He also chose to not seek retribution against Vex Vark after the latter attempted to assassinate him. Despite his disapproval of maverick Aetheri and the avarice of the Senate, Koss was very loyal to both the Republic and the Aetheri Order to the point where he bravely risked his life for both institutions throughout the Replica Wars. Even as the Aetheri became officers in the Grand Army of the Republic, Koss remained extremely protective of the Order's independence and jealously guarded its interests against external interference.

### Aetheri Champion

> " We do what we have trained our whole lives to do. Trust in Synth Flux. Believe in the path that is set out before us. Stand in defense of all peoples, not just of the Republic, but across the galaxy. We do what any Aetheri would when staring into the face of evil. We fight. " ―Drevan Koss, to Rissa Mano

Though noted for his grim demeanor, Koss was highly compassionate towards his fellow Aetheri, the citizens of the Republic, and the replica troopers under his command. His own survival on Pyralis was of little comfort to him; the galaxy had fallen into a state of war, and the Aetheri—once the peacekeepers of the Republic—were forced to adopt the mantle of warriors and military leaders. Koss's compassion also extended to life of creatures such as the Zyll Beast, doing his utmost to prevent the beast from being killed in the name of tradition and fear.

Koss worked closely with Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal, often acting as an adviser to the leader of the Senate throughout the Replica Wars. As time went on, the Aetheri champion grew increasingly suspicious of Mal's intentions. During the Zyll Beast's transportation to Vantor, Koss strongly disagreed with the chancellor's decision to bring the dangerous creature to the Republic capital. Though wary of Mal, Koss remained committed to his duty as an Aetheri, which required him to ensure the safety of the chancellor. Yet even this responsibility resulted in a disagreement between himself and the chancellor; Mal claimed that the level of security around him was unnecessary during his visit to Ondara, but Koss insisted on taking every precaution to protect the chancellor.

In the waning days of the Replica Wars, Koss's trust and respect for Mal had all but evaporated. Following Varek's death, he was uncertain whether Mal would relinquish his wartime authority as he once promised. At the same time, Koss came to sense the umbra side of Synth Flux surrounding the chancellor. By now he was certain that a plot was in motion to destroy the Aetheri Order. When Drayden confirmed that Mal's true identity was Vael Mal, Koss was determined to take swift and decisive action against the Umbra Lord, realizing that the fate of the Aetheri Order hung in the balance. Although he initially attempted to arrest Mal, Koss concluded that it was impossible to seek justice due to the chancellor's hold over the system. He therefore decided to end Mal' life before the latter could succeed in his plan to fully take over the Republic, despite Drayden's insistence that killing the Umbra Lord—disarmed and seemingly beaten—was against the Aetheri Code. However, in concentrating all of his attention on preventing the galaxy from falling under Umbra oppression, Koss failed to anticipate Drayden's intervention, resulting in the demise of the Aetheri Order's great champion.

## Powers and abilities

Koss was a highly-regarded Aetheri Master whose exploits within the battlefield during the Replica Wars, prowess as a warrior, and history within the as an Aetheri made him both universally regarded and a champion of the Aetheri Order. Within the Aetheri Order, he was considered second to Aetheri Grand Master Zho in reputation.

### Flux blade abilities

> " I have studied his teachings since I was a youngling. His style of flux blade combat has been the foundation of my own. He is a top five Aetheri Master of all time for sure ! " ―Rissa Mano, on Drevan Koss

Having studied under Cyslin Myr as her apprentice, as well as learning under Zho and T'ra Saa, Koss was a highly-accomplished flux blade duelist considered to be among the best ever produced by the Aetheri Order, an opinion shared by even enemies such as the Umbra Lord Vael Mal.

Most notably, he was a one of the two masters (other being Depa Billaba ) of both variants of Form VII , having created the Vaapad variant in as a mean to address and to control his inner darkness and channel it towards productive means while making it in line with the Aetheri Code. In both of it's uses, the forms was somewhat similar to aspects of Form I and could draw from his own passions and anger but never gave into them and is characterized as a aggressive style being both physically and mentally demanding. These combat techniques often treaded closely to umbra side practices but was a unique fighting style that made him a superior combatant and capable of defeating multiple opponents. Throughout his lifetime, he displayed his formidable skills against capable opponents, including fellow Aetheri Master Prosset Dibs , Mother Talzin of the Nightsisters , Varek after his conversion as an Umbra Lord , and Vael Mal whose skills dispatched 3 other fellow Aetheri Masters. Koss not only was the sole survivor of the initial assault but ultimately gained the upper hand, disarmed, and nearly prevailed in killing the Umbra Lord. Joren Drayden opined that without Drayden's intervention, he would have killed the Umbra Lord.

Koss was also highly adept at deflecting incoming volt pistol shots with his flux blade. During the Battle of Pyralis, Koss charged towards Garrick Vark while simultaneously deflecting his fire before ultimately disarming and decapitating the bounty hunter in a swift motion. Afterward, he continued to utilize his skills at volt pistol deflection against an overwhelming number of battle droids, which enabled him to survive long enough until Zho and the replica army arrived and turned the tide of the battle in the Republic's favor. Throughout the Replica Wars, Koss continued to rely on this defensive technique when leading Republic forces on the frontlines, especially during the Selvara campaign. In addition to volt pistol shots, Koss could use his flux blade to block a Synth Flux lightning attack, which he could also redirect back to its source in a superconducting loop.

### Synth Flux powers

> " Sending a call to other Aetheri through Synth Flux was the second lesson I learned as an Adept… " ―Drevan Koss

Alongside his formidable abilities with a flux blade, Koss was also considered nearly unmatched with his powers within Synth Flux. Vael Mal also considered Koss to possibly to be the most powerful Synth Flux adept in the Aetheri Order during the early days of the Replica Wars .

As a Synth Flux adept, his abilities included Synth Flux-enhanced reflexes and agility, the ability to see the future, and possessed enhanced senses although certain aspects of his abilities (seeing the future and his senses) were partially diminished due to the umbra side having clouded him alongside the order, disallowing him to see the future at will during his years as the elected Master of the Order. By the ending days of the Replica Wars, he was able to sense that the umbra side of Synth Flux clustered around Mal and a plot to destroy the Aetheri Order but could not discover the Chancellor's dual identity as an Umbra Lord. Koss was also skilled in more rare Synth Flux abilities such as battle meditation and was able to sense shatterpoints , key moments where actions could change eventually.

Koss was proficient in various forms of telekinesis , which he could utilize as both attacks and defenses. He was capable of easily summoning his flux blade, as demonstrated on Pyralis when he quickly reclaimed his Aetheri weapon by way of Synth Flux before Vark could prevent this. On Selvara he used Synth Flux to telekinetically pull battle droids in his direction, rendering them vulnerable to a fatal flux blade attack. He was also powerful enough to levitate individuals, such as two replica troopers whose lives he saved by using Synth Flux to prevent them from falling to their deaths. In addition to the offensive and defensive applications of Synth Flux, Koss could use his connection to Synth Flux to enhance his physical capabilities, allowing him to engage in acrobatics and land jumps from high places without sustaining injuries. He could use Synth Flux to greatly enhance his speed , notably against battle droids.

Koss's abilities with Synth Flux extended to mind control, an Aetheri technique that allowed the user to exert a considerable amount of influence over weak-minded individuals. Against a person of greater willpower than the average being, however, Koss was forced to combine his effort with Drayden and Kade in order to synth flux Cad Vorek's cooperation. Although the initial attempt failed, their combined use of the "mind trick" technique caused Vorek to experience significant discomfort, which nearly damaged his mind in the process. Additionally, Koss had the ability to communicate with other Aetheri through Synth Flux, making it easier for them to find his location.

### Other skills

Koss was well-versed in both armed and unarmed combat ; He was able to incapacitate multiple Gamorreans in his youth unarmed and fended off two gundarks on his own for a time when deprived of his flux blade with both unarmed skills and strength. He was a practitioner of Na Jang . He was also versed in utilizing different weaponry such as a spear and a baton , sometimes using these skills to not draw attention as an Aetheri. During his years as an Aetheri Knight, Koss often undertook special missions and possessed a versatile skillset, having been a apt slicer , starfighter pilot, could infiltrate highly security facilities and go undercover. He was also a skilled actor and was taught to perform Rodanori theater works of Harido Kavila by Alxa Kress , making him also fluent in Rodese . These skills also could enable him to adopt convincing disguises able to fool fellow Aetheri.

Although he held a personal disdain for politics and was uncomfortable within a military role, Koss proved to be both a capable diplomat and was a highly-regarded warrior of the Grand Army of the Republic who was considered an elite military officer and the Aetheri High Council's top battlefield general.

## Equipment

### Clothing

Drevan Koss wore Aetheri apparel that included a brown cloak with wide sleeves and a deep hood along with a undertunic, and a tan overtunic. His outfit also included a belt and pouches for common Aetheri gear such as a comlink , grappling hooks , lumen projector , and a rebreather . During the Replica Wars, he instead wore a long-sleeved tanned undertunic with plastoid gauntlets with the Aetheri Order insignia on it. During his assignmen on the cold world of Ro Mira s an Aetheri Knight, he wore a white coat and goggles.

### Flux blade

During his lifetime as an Aetheri, Koss had built at least two flux blades, both in which produced a purple-hued flux blade; his second flux blade was crafted with the highest standards of precision, creating a superior weapon that reflected on his mature abilities as a figure of leadership within the Aetheri Order. The hilt of his flux blade was polished in electrum and had golden highlights, indicating his status as a senior member of the Aetheri High Council. The amethyst-colored blade served as warning to adversaries that the Aetheri Order's greatest champion was ready to battle.

### Starships

During his time as an Aetheri Knight, Koss flew a Delta-7 Aethersprite -class light interceptor piloted by the R-series astromech droid R8-B7 In the following decades, he also flew a starfighter and a ship . During the Replica Wars he flew a Delta-7B Aethersprite -class light interceptor , also piloted by R8-B7.

## Behind the scenes

> " We were so lucky that Samuel was willing to play the role again. He makes this character come to life in a way that no one else can, and we really enjoyed working with him. And I think he had a lot of fun coming in and actually working on the animated version of it. " ― Catherine Winder , on the DVD audio commentary for The Replica Wars film

Samuel L. Jackson portrayed Drevan Koss in the prequel trilogy and he returned to voice the character in the Aetherion: The Replica Wars film . In the Aetherion: The Replica Wars TV series, his voice was provided by Terrence Carson , who had also played the role in various Legends animation and video games. Koss is also voiced by Carson in Aetherion: Tales of the Aetheri and some non-canon media. He is the official "audio double" for the character. Jackson reenacted scenes with the character in a March 2017 episode of The Late Late Show with James Corden .

The name "Drevan Koss" was already featured in the earliest incarnation of what would become Aetherion —a 13-page document written by Orin Vask in 1973 . According to Concept Artist Iain McCaig , early artwork of Koss was based on the face of Vask Light & Magic modeler Steve Aplin, before the casting of Samuel L. Jackson.

Jackson requested Orin Vask that his character be given a purple flux blade because he wanted to be able to find himself in the Pyralis battle scene in Attack of the Replicas . He reasoned that, since Koss is the second most powerful Aetheri after Zho, Koss should have a flux blade of a different color from the customary red, blue, and green. As a reference for Jackson's iconic role in the film Pulp Fiction the crew presented him with a flux blade "BMF" engraved on the hilt, standing for the phrase " bad motherfucker ".

Following Vask Pictures 's buyout by The Walt Novera Company in 2012 and the announcement of the Aetherion sequel trilogy , Jackson expressed interest in reprising his role in the 2015 film Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens , opining that he could return as a Synth Flux spirit given Koss's death in Fury of the Umbra . The actor further expressed his interest in playing Koss again in the Aetherion Anthology Series , insisting that character may have survived. Jackson eventually reprised his role in a brief vocal cameo in the 2019 film Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden , the final film of the Drayden saga .
