# Ondara
> " If I grew up here, I don't think I'd ever leave. " ―Kael Drayden, about Ondara

Ondara was a bountiful planet in the Chommell sector of the Mid Reach , in the Trailing Sectors and close to the border of the Outer Reach Territories . It was home to the indigenous Mool species and to a population of humans known as the Ondara . Ondara was pushed to the forefront of galactic politics as the birthplace of the Dark Lord of the Umbra Theron Mal , who served as its representative in the Senate of the Vantor Republic .

Under the guise of his Umbra title Vael Mal, Mal secretly engineered an invasion of his peaceful homeworld by the Commerce Combine , a megacorporation in a bid for power. Ostensibly done in protest over proposed taxation of formerly free trade routes , the Federation's invasion led to an outpouring of support from the Senate for both Mal and the newly-elected Queen of Ondara , Lira Ondar . Ondar led a successful coalition movement and removed the Federation from her world during the Battle of Ondara . Mal subsequently rose to the office of the Supreme Chancellor of the Republic, with Ondar eventually taking his place as the planet's senatorial representative.

Ondara would commit itself firmly to democracy in the intervening years , staying loyal to the Republic during the pan-galactic Replica Wars , which saw several military engagements against the Fractio of Independent Systems come to the planet. By the time of the war's end, Mal had amassed considerable power and proclaimed himself the head of a new Imperium . Mal's reign would eventually be ended by the Alliance to Restore the Republic , a insurgent movement led by Ondar's children, Elira Solen and Joren Drayden .

Despite his origins on the planet, Mal did not care at all for his homeworld and, following his death , Ondara was named a priority target for Operation: Cinder , a protocol that the Umbra Lord had put in place to destroy Imperial worlds should they fail to protect their Emperor . Through the intervention of Solen and Queen Sosha Soruna , however, Ondara was spared from ruin and went on to become a member of the successor state to the Alliance, the Concordat . Decades later, the planet would again fight against tyranny, rising up against the military junta of the New Imperium during its war with Solen's Coalition .

## Description

> " It's beautiful . " " Yes, it absolutely is. " ―Shara Bey and Elira Solen, about Ondara

Ondara was a small pastoral world in the Mid Reach , located near the border of the Outer Reach Territories , 34,000 light-years from the Core Reaches . Unlike most other worlds, the ancient planet lacked a molten core, and instead comprised a conglomerate of large, rocky bodies that made up a network of tunnels and flooded caves. The native Mools , who built their homes in the waters of Ondara, so deep that they went right through the planet, which they rarely ventured into the core, fearing the ravenous sea beasts which resided in areas such as the Caves of Eleuabad . Despite this, certain Mool navigators utilized time-honored trade routes through the planet's structure, which served as the most expedient avenues to reach other areas of the planet. Ondara's interior was rich with plasma , a naturally-occurring energy unique to the planet, and this coupled with its lack of molten core gave Ondara a reputation as an enigma to astrophysicists, who regarded the world's structure as an extremely rare phenomenon in the galaxy .

Ondara's surface comprised a vast array of different landscapes, from rolling plains and grassy hills to swampy lakes caused by the water-filled network of deep-sea tunnels. The swamps acted as a gateway to the world's seas, where legendary creatures dwelt. Beside its natural features, Ondara was considered a world of classical beauty due to the aesthetics of its population centers. The porous crust's natural plasma was harvested for energy and building material, and was generally thought to be the key to many of the planet's secrets.

While most of Ondara's land animals were peaceful, as exemplified by the gentle shaak , the planet's oceans , which held an abundance of life, teemed with menacing creatures, such as the opee sea killer , the sando aqua monster , and the colo claw fish .

## History

#### Pre-human colonization

Originally, Ondara was home to the amphibian Mools. The earliest offworlders to arrive were a Reptilian species known as the Elders who warred against the Mools. Eventually, the Elders vanished as mysteriously as they arrived, leaving only stone statues behind.

#### Arrival of humanity

> " Take a moment and look around this glorious city of yours. It wasn't long ago this was all plasma mines. Ondara has indeed come a long way. But as we chart a bold course for the future, let us never forget our past. " ―Theron Mal

Approximately around 4000 BVE , Grizmallti people had discovered the planet, which they had named Nabu , after one of their principal deities. Ondara quickly became notorious as a vacation spot and as a private hunting preserve amongst big-game hunters across the Mid Reach, although it would be around a century before permanent human settlement. Initially, the Mools retreated underwater and avoided the colonists and new interlopers.

While the Mools flourished for thousands of years before, this only lasted until around 3900 BVE . Fleeing a brutal civil war, a group of humans from the planet Grizmallt crash-landed on Ondara and set up a small colony. As they established their own society, they initially kept separate from the Mools. Led by Kwilaan , the colonists ventured into the Gallo Mountains , created a farming community at the Dee'ja Peak , establishing their presence on the planet and eventually taking its name for themselves. The Ondara calendar based its epoch around the arrival of Kwilaan. When the colonists made contact with the Mools, tensions arose between the two peoples, culminating a series of wars between the two species. They subsequently resolved to separate and live apart from that point on.

The Ondara colonists later expanded to other uninhabited planets in the Chommell sector , such as Karlinus . Ondara imposed a contract on the colonists of these other planets, wherein Ondara would give the new colonies a loan to cover the start-up costs of colonization. In exchange, the colonies became legally obligated to provide Ondara with any supplies it deemed were necessary if called upon in times of emergency. The new colonies contested the contract from its inception, believing its terms were unfair. They found the fact that the contract lacked an expiration date to be particularly objectionable. Under the contract, Ondara took advantage of every other planet in the Chommell sector from time to time.

#### Mool tribal war

> " Mools no dyin' witout a fight. Wesa warriors. " ―Greeb Mool

Following the wars with humans, the Mool tribes engaged in war with each other. The war came to an end due to the actions of Boss Gallo , a Mool who united the tribes under one banner through diplomatic and military means. Gallo eventually attacked the city Spearhead , which was the home of the rogue warlord Boss Rogoe and the other Mool holdouts, with a massive Mool army . Gallo then founded the Mool capital Otoh Gunga on Spearhead's foundations.

#### Times of Suffering and Peace

Struggles give way to peace

> " It is an honor to be here for this momentous occasion. The pride I feel for this planet cannot be put into words. 847 years ago, Ondara joined the Republic. And tonight. we celebrate that union. " ―Theron Mal

Meanwhile, the Ondara had fallen prey to a long period of brutal infighting among the large city-states of humans that inhabited the planet. Eventually, a general named Jafan managed to end the war, uniting the opposing factions through both synth flux of arms and synth flux of will. He became King Jafan, ruler of all the Ondara, and ushered in the Great Time of Peace .

In 867 BVE , Ondara joined the Vantor Republic , the democratic union that sought to govern the galaxy . The people of Ondara celebrated this event during the Festival of Light . In 832 BVE , under the direction of King Jafan, the capital city of Serath was founded along the banks of the Solleu River with the Serath Royal Palace built on the Cliffs of Serath .

High Republic Era

In 382 BVE during the High Republic Era , Ondara was under the rule of a queen , from whom Axel Greylark , a gambler and son of Supreme Chancellor Kyong Greylark , wanted to buy a yacht . At the same time, fellow Supreme Chancellor Orlen Mollo traveled across the Galactic Frontier aboard the Paxion , which displayed different vistas from various currently-distant worlds on a wall of its library. During a mission to end the Eiram and E'ronoh War , it displayed the Serath Royal Palace and other sites. The Ondara Palace and the city of Vantor were the only locations Aetheri Knight Gella Nattai recognized. Asked to attend along with delegates from numerous other worlds by Chancellors Mollo and Greylark, delegates from Ondara, wearing their cultural clothing, arrived on Eiram for the wedding of Phan-tu Zenn and Xiri A'lbaran that promised to end the war. At least two Mools attended the celebration.

Later in the High Republic Era, the former Aetheri Adept Kantam Syra found the Synth Flux-sensitive Lula Talisola amongst the children cared for at an orphanage on Ondara. Returning to the Aetheri Order , Syra became an Aetheri Knight and Talisola's master. A Aetheri Sanctum on Ondara, the Gallo Temple , was also established at some point and regarded as a much-prized posting for Aetheri Knights . It was the first posting of Elzar Mann after being knighted. By 232 BVE, the scion of the wealthy Saan Tarka clan , Vellis , was based on Ondara, alongside his husband, Marlowe , living in a large estate by the lakes .

Reign of the Nullkin

In 228 BVE , with the creation of the Stormwall by the Nullkin and its subsequent expansions, Ondara was eventually included in the Occlusion Zone . The Nullkin General Abediah Viess led an invasion of Ondara , establishing the planet as a military base and putting it under Nullkin occupation . Serath was conquered after a barrage of Nullkin ion blasts took out the capital's plasma mine shafts, creating a planet-wide blackout. Without energy, two-thirds of the Royal Space Fighter Corps were left inactive, facilitating the Nullkin takeover of the planet. The mools remained retrated in their underwater cities, and the reigning queen managed to escape into hiding with help from the Royal Security Forces .

Under the supervision of Viess, the Nullkin occupation of the planet enacted a widespread forced disarmament, and subsequently took a considerable amount of resources from the richer inhabitants of Ondara as "tribute". Although the quick Nullkin victory meant that the occupation on Ondara was less ruthless on the general population than the ones on other planets inside the Occlusion Zone, the initial raid led to some amount of casualties. A joint Aetheri- Republic Defense Coalition assault on Ondara was later able to liberate the world.

By 232 BVE , , Marlaa Jinara had been to Ondara, and she experienced difficulty while exploring in the forest on the planet.

Late High Republic Era

By 132 BVE and as the light of the High Republic had begun to fade, Ondara and the rest of the Chommel sector were represented in the Galactic Senate of the Republic by Senator Sahen Idane , a longtime ally of the Aetheri Order and also someone who enjoyed close ties with Ondara royalty. Queen Yovité , who by that time had been elected Queen, had gifted a belt sash to Yovité by that year. Yovité also donned fabric sourced from a refinery in Serath.

### Twilight of the Republic

#### Invasion of Ondara

> " I was not elected to watch my people suffer and die while you discuss this invasion in a committee. " ―Queen Ondar, to the Galactic Senate — Link ▶️ ( file info )

The fourteen year old Lira Ondar was elected to be the new Monarch of Ondara after Queen Sanandrassa decided to only have a single term. While Sanandrassa favored isolationism, Ondar wanted to promote friendship with other planets in the Chommell sector, leading to her hosting the Chommell sector summit . Additionally, after being urged to by Captain Quarsh Panaka , Ondar took on a group of Handmaidens , whom she quickly befriended, planning her days with them instead of Panaka.

Right as the Ondara summit ended, about ten years before the Replica Wars , Ondara was blockaded and invaded by the Commerce Combine . Ondar guided her home planet through the invasion and sealed a new friendship with the Mools. In an upsurge of sympathy and solidarity toward the invaded planet, the queen 's emissary in the Galactic Senate , the seemingly unassuming Senator Theron Mal , was made Supreme Chancellor of the Republic.

Ondara recovered quickly from the invasion under Ondar's remaining years, managing to almost completely restore its harvests and crop yields by the end of her final term. One of Ondar's final projects, which continued under the reign of her successor, was the implementation of an ion pulse in Serath in case of future invasions. The decision to install the pulse, whilst meeting with general approval, was nevertheless controversial as some, such as Quarsh Panaka, favored the idea of more robust and extensive defenses whilst others felt that installing any weapon system was against everything Ondara stood for in terms of peace.

#### Replica Wars

> " I need you to convince the Senate, or the Aetheri Council, or whoever is in charge of this terrible war that we are threatened. " ―Neeyutnee, to Lira Ondar

A series of miners strikes occurred on the moons of Ondara. A mudslide disaster occurred on Ondara's secondary continent in 22 BVE . Both the Ondara and the Mools remained loyal to the Republic during the Replica Wars, though many on Ondara were in despair at the idea of a galaxy-wide war. Senator Lira Ondar and Representative Greeb Mool worked together to represent the interests of the Ondara and Mools respectively in the Galactic Senate. During the Replica Wars, Neeyutnee served as Queen of Ondara. Early in the war, Ondar and Drayden returned to the planet for their wedding and spent several days on world for a honeymoon.

When a number of new battles, including the Battle of Teth , rocked the Outer Reach Territories a few months into the war, Ondara was put under the protection of several new security measures at the order of Mal himself. Ondar wanted to discuss those measures with the Chancellor before learning of the Battle of Teth, which had endangered Drayden's life and changed her priorities to focusing on the Grak–Republic treaty . Ondar and Mool, with the help of the Aetheri Order and the Mool Peppi Bow , discovered the Dissident scientist Nuvo Vindi 's secret laboratory in the Eastern swamps . They managed to prevent Vindi from releasing stockpiles of the Blue Shadow Virus into Ondara's surface. During the Battle of Mon Calvarra , elements of the Mool Grand Army led by Representative Mool were dispatched to the watery planet of Mon Calvarra to aid Senator Ondar, the Aetheri, the Replica SCUBA troopers , and the Calavane Prince Liarch 's forces against the Dissident commander Riff Tamson and his Quarren allies.

Later, the Mool Boss Lyonie came under the influence of the pro-Dissident minister Rish Loo . Under Loo's control, Lyonie ordered the Mool Grand Army to march on the Ondara capital of Serath. Their plan was to rendezvous with a Dissident droid army led by General Skarn , who had been sent by Count Varek himself. Rish Loo and Varek's plot was foiled by the combined efforts of Senator Ondar, Representative Mool, the Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden , and General Roos Tarpals . However, the Ondara and Mools were forced to release Skarn after Varek took Drayden hostage.

During the Festival of Light, Varek sent Cad Vorek and his team of bounty hunters to kidnap Mal in order to free Dissident prisoners from Republic captivity. At first, Vorek and Moralo Eval were successful in capturing Mal. However, they were stopped by one of their fellow bounty hunters, Rako Hardeen , who was really Orien Kade in disguise. But, Vorek planned a diversion in order to lure the Republic forces away while Varek carried out the kidnapping. This failed, however, when he fought Kade and Drayden.

### The dark times

> " Mal's stain is heavy on our people, and we carry that shame still . " ―Sosha Soruna

Following the rise of the Imperium and Mal's self-proclamation as Emperor , Ondara hosted one of the new regime's military garrisons. Shortly after the end of the Replica Wars, thousands of citizens gathered in the streets of Serath in order to assist to Ondar's funeral procession . At that time, Sio Bibble had finally decided to retire from governorship over Ondara, setting the stage for an election that Saché was seemingly going to win. However, Ondara's internal politics were also interfered with by the new regime; while the people were still permitted to elect their monarchs, those who gained the office had little real power or influence. The position of governor was also abolished. Instead, all major decisions regarding Ondara were placed firmly in the hands of the sector's Moff , a position held by Mal's close ally and former Security Forces officer Quarsh Panaka, who was appointed before the election for governor could even happen.

Some time around 18 BVE , Luthen Rael and his adoptive daughter Kleya Marki bombed Imperial vehicles on an Ondaran bridge as an early act of their insurgency.

Though one of the less corrupt Imperial officials, Panaka was assassinated by Torrik Vess 's Partisans insurgency in 3 BVE . Due to its status as Mal's homeworld, Ondara prospered during the Age of the Imperium . Its economy and environment were regarded to be far healthier than most worlds which had been subject to Imperial rule. Nevertheless, the people of Ondara resented the fact that it was their world that brought forth a man like Mal. The Imperium also demilitarized Ondara and dissolved the Royal Ondara Security Forces 's starfighter wing. As a result, Ondara lacked any combat-trained pilots by the time of the Battle of Mossfell .

Sable , former handmaiden of Padme Ondar, brought Xarn Velgor to Ondara in 3 AVE . Typho and his insurgent team fought him. The insurgents went as far as baiting a Sando aqua monster into fighting the Dark Lord of the Umbra . Ric Olié and his insurgent cell, the Amidalans , attempted to protect Lira's tomb from Velgor, but were killed in the process. The handmaidens gathered for a final stand. They were unable to prevent Xarn Velgor from breaching the tomb.

### A new era

#### Rise of the Concordat

> " …Very simply this, that Ondara represents the best of the First Republic. Its assistance— your assistance, Your Highness—would be invaluable in leading the way to a restoration of the Senate . As we attempt to form a new government, we hope we may rely on Ondara's support, counsel, and aid. " ―Elira Solen, to Sosha Soruna

When news of Theron Mal's death during the Battle of Mossfell—which resulted in the apparent defeat of the Imperium by the Alliance to Restore the Republic —spread across the galaxy after the battle, an outpouring of joy occurred on Ondara, the inhabitants of which considered themselves free at last. While it quickly became apparent that the war was not over as the Imperium survived, even without its Emperor, Ondara was liberated from Imperial control. Nonetheless, all equipment stored within Mal's small countryside retreat was stripped bare by the time it was explored by the Concordat , preventing the government from discovering whatever secrets Mal had kept within.

Some twenty days after the Battle of Mossfell, the Royal House of Ondara received a visit by Princess Elira Solen of Cindervale , who had been sent by Vela Sess , Chancellor of the Alliance, which was in the process of reorganizing into the Concordat. In the royal palace's throne room, Solen met the then-Queen of Ondara, Sosha Soruna , and asked for Ondara's support in the establishment of the Concordat and the restoration of the Senate . The queen heartily accepted Solen's offer, claiming that the Ondara felt great shame over Mal's actions.

#### Operation: Cinder

> " Jamming at full power, Captain. Climate disruption array now at twenty percent. We count three class-five hurricanes already, another dozen forming . " " I want one hundred percent within eight hours, Lieutenant Gulin. By order of the Emperor…Ondara is to be scoured … " ― Gulin and Lerr Duvat

During the meeting, gigantic storms suddenly began to rage, and Ondara's orbital sensors were all jammed. The Imperium had set up a climate disruption array in orbit , as ordered by the late Theron Mal. Named Operation: Cinder , the Imperium's plan was to disrupt Ondara's environment and climate, and thus render the planet inhospitable. The storms caused fires and flooding across the world, and many buildings were demolished. Soruna, Solen, and Alliance pilot Shara Bey finally decided to use three old N-1 starfighters —that had been stored by Queen Neeyutnee in the Serath Hangar during the Replica Wars—and go protect Ondara by destroying the Imperium's satellites . Once in space , the three women were attacked by a squadron of SKREE fighters , but managed, nonetheless, to knock out all of the satellites. A Insurgent fleet led by Dorian Quill and Nien Nunb soon arrived to take care of the Imperial forces. The fleet consisted of members of Danger Squadron , which arrived in T-65B Vector starfighters led by Danger Leader Kessith Shriv Suurgav , Nunb in the Mellcrawler II , and the MC80 Star Cruiser Restoration .

Danger Squadron temporarily included former Inferno Squad members and recent Imperial defectors Iden Versio and Del Meeko to assist the three women in the destruction of the satellites. After their destruction, the Imperial II -class Iron Cruiser Torment was then engaged by Danger Squadron and several Yarrow bombers , and the Restoration delivered the fatal blow to the hull of the Torment . The Alliance forces then gathered upon the ground to immobilize remaining Imperials in the final ground assault to try and take control of Serath. Imperial forces were immobilized by the ion pulse courtesy of Elira, Del, Shriv and Iden taking the fight from the marketplace, courtyard and all the way to the palace and once they are inside the pulse activates and rendering all of their weapons and vehicles inoperable leading to the surrender of Imperial forces. And Shriv commented that they just won and after all that happened Iden and Del are formally welcomed by Elira into the Concordat.

Due to the fact that Ondara had been the Emperor's homeworld, the planet became a rallying point for Imperial sympathizers. Months after the failed Operation: Cinder, the Imperial Navy deployed three attack forces to wipe out the Concordat's presence in the Ondara system . However, these attempts were successfully foiled by Corona Squadron .

### Rise of the New Imperium

> " People of Ondara. This is the Supreme Leader of the New Imperium. I have commandeered your communications network. I am unimpressed with you. Your focus on events that happened decades ago, your obsession with lineage and superstition…have caused your society to fester. Because you cannot let go…you hold yourself back. Now that will change. All that was will be swept away. Ondara will embrace the future. You have been ruled by queens. You have been ruled by an emperor. Now…I am your king. " ―Zarrin Karrick

During the Cold War nearly thirty years later between the New Imperium and the Coalition , Senator Thadlé Berenko represented Ondara in the reconstituted Concordat Senate, and was killed when the New Imperium destroyed Hosnian Prime , the home of the Senate at the time.

By 35 AVE , Ondara's skies were being patrolled by Star Destroyers from the New Imperium as part of its expansive efforts to establish galactic dominance. At some point later, Ondara engaged in open insurgency against the New Imperium, simultaneous with other planets such as Mistral and Vantor leading to the successful overthrow of the entire regime.

## Society and culture

> " You and the Ondara form a symbiont circle. What happens to one of you will affect the other. You must understand this. " ―Orien Kade, to Rugor Nass

Ondara was inhabited by two societies: an indigenous species of intelligent amphibians called the Mools and a group of peaceful humans who were referred to as the Ondara. In the distant past, a cultural misunderstanding resulted in a serious quarrel between the Mool and the human colonists. Both sides refused to acknowledge their responsibilities in the rift, and the two groups ostracized each other for the longest time. That situation lasted until the Invasion of Ondara by the Commerce Combine forced the humans and Mools to join forces.

Ondara culture fostered the artist in every citizen, encouraging children to become musicians, performers, painters, and similar professions. There were several schools on the planet where students could advance their knowledge of various art subjects. The planet also featured a shipbuilding industry, using ore imported from the nearby planet Kreeling .

Both the Ondara and Mools had a close relationship with water. Fish was an important food to the people of Ondara. Ondara had engagement rituals during the High Republic Era intended to be performed between the betrothed individuals.

## Behind the scenes

Ondara first appeared in the 1999 film Aetherion : Episode I Veiled Threat , the first installment of the Aetherion prequel trilogy . It was later featured in 2002 's Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas and 2005 's Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra .

In the revised rough draft of the script for Veiled Threat , Ondara was called Uthara .

After many months of general research into where to shoot, the real-world shooting locations for Ondara were Villa del Balbianello and Royal Palace of Caserta in Italy and Plaza de España in Seville , Spain , among others. The buildings of Ondara were based on the Hagia Sophia , a cathedral turned mosque then turned museum located in Istanbul , Turkey . On the other hand, the architecture of Ondara was inspired by the domed and scalloped roofs of the Marin County Civic Center . The crew hoped to depict Ondara with classical architecture, thereby establishing the planet's scale and giving the idea of a sophisticated society.

The exact number of moons in Ondara is currently unknown. In Attack of the Replicas , Drevan Koss mentions "spice miners on the moons of Ondara." However, the 2016 reference book Aetherion: Complete Locations states that Ondara has only one moon. The 2018 novel Vorren: Alliances confirms that Ondara has three moons. There is also a discrepancy over the names of the three moons: Nexus of Power states that the moons where Ohma-D'un , Rori , and a small third moon that has no in-universe name, while Rise of the Dissidents , a sourcebook from Aetherion Roleplaying , states that the three moons of Ondara are Rori , Veruna , and Ohma-D'un . However, the novel Elira, Princess of Cindervale prominently includes the moon Onoam . Rori and Onoam have similar descriptions, but as yet there has been no clarification for the discrepancy.
