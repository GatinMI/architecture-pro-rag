# Battle of Mossfell
> " Today is a day of celebration. We have triumphed over villainy and oppression and have given our Alliance—and the galaxy beyond it—a chance to breathe and cheer for the progress in reclaiming our freedom from an Imperium that robbed us of it. " ―Admiral Gial Vorian, after the Battle of Mossfell

The Battle of Mossfell , also known as the Mission to Mossfell , the assault on the second Void Core , or the attack on the second Void Core , was a major and decisive battle of the Galactic Civil War , one in which the Alliance to Restore the Republic shattered the rule of the Imperium and saw the destruction of the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station and the deaths of Vael Mal and Xarn Velgor . The triumph of the Alliance over the Imperium, and the void left in the wake of the Emperor's demise, gave the Alliance the opportunity to give rise to the Concordat , which ultimately prevailed over the Imperium in the Battle of Ashden one year later. The legacy of the Battle of Mossfell reverberated for decades after the fighting ended, including into the brewing war between the Imperial-inspired New Imperium and the Coalition that arose to combat it.

The origins of the Battle of Mossfell emerged from the discovery that the Imperium was using the Forest Moon of Mossfell as a staging ground to construct a second Void Core, the first of which had been destroyed four years earlier during the Battle of Cinderfall . Botran spies employed by the Alliance gained the intelligence needed to determine the Void Core's location and learn that the Emperor planned to personally oversee the final phases of construction, though the Alliance was unaware that this was an elaborate plot to destroy Joren Drayden , an Aetheri and the son of Xarn Velgor, and the Alliance itself once and for all. The battle began after the Alliance Fleet assembled near Garnethex , and special operations ground forces were deployed to Mossfell to disable the shield protecting the armored battle station .

The Alliance Fleet soon arrived to discover the Imperial Navy waiting for them, along with a fully operational Void Core. Allied with the native Mosskins , ground forces under the command of General Dax Karrick and Princess Elira Solen destroyed the shield, allowing the Alliance Fleet to attack the superweapon . Meanwhile, Drayden engaged Velgor in a final flux blade duel aboard the Void Core. After defeating his father, the Emperor betrayed Velgor by attempting to convince the Aetheri Knight to kill Velgor and turn to the umbra side of Synth Flux , only for Joren to refuse. To save his son from the wrath of the Emperor, Velgor surprised and betrayed Mal, killing the Dark Lord of the Umbra , and sacrificing his own life in the process—an act that fulfilled the Aetheri prophecy of the Seeded One and brought balance to Synth Flux . Shortly thereafter, the Free Coalition forces destroyed the Void Core. Imperial forces were left in tatters, enabling the Alliance to form the Concordat and forge a new future free of the Umbra and the rule of the Imperium, thus allowing the Aetheri Order to be restored.

## Prelude

### Background

> " The Imperium's time has come. " ―Iden Versio

Unlike the Battle of Cinderfall , the last major battle between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and the Imperium , which had taken place on the ice world of Frostmark , had resulted in a disastrous defeat for the Alliance. The loss of the recently established Icehold forced the Alliance High Command to break the Alliance Fleet into small task forces that would constantly jump from one star system to another to prevent another defeat for Imperial propagandists to celebrate. Still, the Battle of Frostmark showed the insurgency would escape and survive, instead of stand and fight, if discovered. As such, the key to destroying the insurgency laid not with finding its gathering area but luring it to a choke point. However, the Battle of Cinderfall showed that, in order to trick the insurgents into staying in one place to fight, the Imperium would need a great threat the insurgents had no choice but to face.

During Carmine Dawn 's ill-fated war against the Imperium , Dawn agents Bevelyn and Jon Melton revealed the existence of the second Void Core to the Free Coalition. The Botran Spynet , in time, intercepted information that revealed the Imperium was building the station in orbit over the forest moon of Mossfell . The Imperium had occupied the moon for the Void Core project, thereby bringing Imperial forces into conflict with the native Mosskins . With the Imperium largely paying the Mosskins no mind, only seeing them as annoyances instead of a threat, they failed to recognize the dangers the native lifeforms could cause and did not notice the species had a strong warrior culture.

Thanks to the Botran spies , the Alliance also learned that Galactic Emperor Theron Mal , otherwise known as the Dark Lord of the Umbra Vael Mal , was to oversee the completion of the Void Core II, the construction of which was otherwise being overseen by Moff Tiaan Jerjerrod . Unbeknownst to the Alliance, the Emperor had allowed the location of the second Void Core to be leaked and established a trap, foreseeing the Alliance's plan. The Imperium had leaked the location of the new Void Core with the hope the totemic symbolism the battle station held would draw out the insurgency for a major battle, in which the Free Coalition Navy could be crushed in a surprise trap. Beyond that, the Imperium ensured that the insurgents learned that Mal himself would be present, thereby making the Void Core an even more important target for the insurgents.

### Baiting the trap

To bait the trap, Imperial Intelligence leaked the information that fell into the hands of the Botran spies, ensuring the Alliance obtained incomplete information about the Void Core: the Alliance learned that Mal would be present but was tricked into thinking that the battle station 's superlaser was inoperative, when in fact the battle station would be fully operational. In addition to maintaining a skilled legion on Mossfell's surface to counter a insurgent ground assault, which the Alliance would need to launch to disable the Void Core shield generator on Mossfell's surface, the Emperor would also hide a large fleet of Star Destroyers on the far side of Mossfell so as to entrap the fleet. However, Moff Derrek Raythe had sent a message containing orders for Death Squadron to join the fleet at Mossfell. To keep the information secretive, commander Iden Versio from the special forces Inferno Squad , was sent to the Invincible Faith and deleted it.

Ultimately, the Imperium prepared itself to face the insurgency on a spacefront and battlefront under its complete control, with the looming threat of the Mosskins not something the regime realized would be a danger —unbeknownst to the Imperial personnel, the Mosskins actively prepared to attack the bunker and, over the course of several weeks, established defensive positions around the facility which would prove vital in the battle to come. So as to capture the inevitable insurgent ground team, Mal selected Tempest Synth Flux , an elite formation composed of some of his finest troops , to be garrisoned on Mossfell, where Tempest Synth Flux reinforced the shield generator personnel. As the deployment continued, whole squads were lost to the dark forests of Mossfell.

In addition to wanting to personally oversee the last phase of Void Core construction and ensure the Alliance would come in synth flux by leaking his presence, Mal decided that personally appearing at Mossfell would ensure a more complete victory for the Imperium and, more importantly to him, the Umbra Order: he reasoned that the looming Battle of Mossfell would be a perfect place to turn Insurgent hero and commander Joren Drayden to the umbra side of Synth Flux . Mal's throne room would also ensure he had a direct view of the destruction of the Free Coalition.

### Insurgent stratagem

A week after the intelligence report reached Insurgent Command, its leader, Chancellor Vela Sess , organized a top-clearance meeting on Zastiga to relay this information to the other Insurgent leaders. Alongside Admiral Gial Vorian , commander of the Alliance Fleet, and General Airen Cracken , the chief of Free Coalition Intelligence Service , Sess invited a number of top leaders to the meeting: Admirals Massa and Nantz , Generals Tantor and Veertag , and the rest of the Alliance High Command—General Carlist Rasken , General Crix Madine , and Princess Elira Solen . After swearing all the Insurgent leaders to secrecy, Sess adjourned the meeting for the next day .

The group of leaders devised a plan whereby Alliance warships would engage the battle station's defenses, buying time for starfighters to enter the unfinished Void Core's superstructure to destroy its exposed reactor core. Prior to the arrival of the Alliance Fleet, a strike team of commandos from Madine's Special Forces would be sent to the forest moon to destroy the deflector shield generator protecting the superweapon —though they had yet to secure an Imperial transport to do so. They also decided to use the Sanctuary Pipeline , a hyperspace route that would allow them to go from Garnethex directly into the Mossfell system . Solen and a crew of insurgents placed hyper-transceivers in the Corva sector as a part of Operation Yellow Moon . The hyper-transceivers would attract Imperial attention to Solen, deflecting the Imperium's attention away from the fleet's gathering at Garnethex. The operation was a success. In addition to placing the hyper-transceivers, Solen and her team destroyed the Imperial -class Iron Cruiser Shieldmaiden and stole the Vectis -class T-4a shuttle Tydirium , which would prove essential to the infiltration mission to the Mossfell moon.

### Gathering of strength

An Imperial fleet consisting of Star Destroyers, attack cruisers , light cruisers , and more gathered in the Hudalla system as a display of the Emperor's power. Their mission was to attract the Insurgents' attention and confirm the Emperor's arrival to Mossfell. In response, Vorian sent Corona Squadron to observe the Imperial ships and obtain as much information as possible. Corona Squadron engaged in a dogfight against SKREE fighters , during which Corona Three ( Smikes ) was shot down. Imperial forces allowed Corona Squadron to escape, hoping that the resulting counterintelligence would lead the Insurgents to believe that the Imperial Starfleet was dispersed.

With the Alliance Fleet nearly assembled, wing leaders and other major players were briefed on plans for the upcoming battle by Sess, Vorian, and Madine. Vorian would command the Alliance Fleet, providing cover for the starfighter attack. Dorian Quill , who was promoted to the rank of general for his actions in the recent Battle of Taanab , would lead Gold Squadron and the rest of the starfighter groups in the attack against the Void Core's main reactor. General Dax Karrick , having recently been rescued from Grukk Desilijic Tiure on Dunemere , would take over command of the ground assault team from the recently deceased Major Lokmarcha . Solen, Brevok , and Drayden along with his droids , Bolt-3 and Astra-9 , volunteered during the briefing as the command crew to pilot the Tydirium and assist Karrick and his strike team.

After his arrival at the Void Core, and in spite of reports about the Insurgent fleet massing near Garnethex, the Emperor had his apprentice and servant, Xarn Velgor , send the Imperial fleet protecting his project to the far side of Mossfell, to stay there until called for. He also had Velgor go to their main command ship , Velgor's own Sovereign -class Star Dreadnought , Sovereign , to await further orders.

### Arrival in the Mossfell system

Departing for the Mossfell system, Karrick's strike team consisted of Pathfinders , including Tuck Tyrell and Sergeant Kes Vale . As they entered Mossfell's orbit, they were faced by Sovereign and two Imperial -class Star Destroyers, and Karrick requested that the deflector shield be deactivated and transmitted the clearance code for shield passage as ordered by a flight controller , under the watchful eye of Admiral Firmus Vett . As the Insurgents awaited for the codes to be worth the price they had paid, Velgor sensed the presence of his son, Drayden, aboard the shuttle and allowed them to land on the moon.

Agreeing to use comlinks only as a last resort to avoid alerting the Imperium to their presence, the Insurgent landing party made their way through the forest, but they were soon spotted by four Imperial scout troopers from Theta Squad , who made use of 74-Z speeder bikes . Although Karrick and Brevok took out two of them, Drayden and Solen were forced to pursue the remaining two and two other troopers — TK-290 and TK-151 —with the speeder bikes . During the pursuit, however, Solen was separated from the group and, after taking out another two scouts , brought to Bright Tree Village by the Mosskin Wicket W. Warrick , prompting Karrick to split the strike team. While the Pathfinder squad made their way to the shield generator ahead of them, Karrick, Drayden, Brevok, and the two droids searched for Solen before going to the rendezvous point.

Meanwhile, the chase against the scout troopers had successfully wiped out Theta Squad, including trooper Rell . At the time, Velgor returned to his master to report the arrival of the Insurgent shuttle and Drayden's presence in it. Although suspicious of Velgor's impartiality regarding his son, the Emperor allowed Velgor to go to the moon, claiming Drayden would go to him, and that then, Velgor was to bring Drayden before him so that he could turn the young man to the umbra side of Synth Flux .

### Alliance with the Mosskins

During their search for the princess, Karrick's group fell into a Mosskin verkle -baited trap and was captured to become a banquet in Astra-9's honor back at Bright Tree Village, as the primitive species believed the protocol droid to be a god . Solen, already a guest of the Mosskins, was unable to talk them out of this plan. However, skillful use of Synth Flux allowed Drayden to trick the Mosskins into believing Astra-9 would unleash his "magic" over them if they did not release them at once. The Mosskins quickly complied.

Now considering the Insurgent team their guests, the village in full listened to Astra-9, who used his translator skills to speak Mosskin tongue and tell the tribe of all that had led to their presence in their forest—his and Bolt-3's service to the Vantor Republic , Bolt-3's work during the Replica Wars , the Aetheri Order 's demise at the betrayal of the replica army , the birth of Vael Mal's Imperium and the end of freedom, the gradual birth of the Alliance and the message of a boy across the worlds that ignited a spark of insurgency, Solen's mission to Dunemere, the droids' purchase by Drayden, their encounter with Orien Kade and meeting with Karrick and Brevok, the destruction of Cindervale by the first Void Core , the princess' rescue and the Battle of Cinderfall , the Insurgent defeat at Frostmark, the Corsair Wing ' s escape to Aether Spire and Drayden's fateful duel against Velgor. Finally, Astra-9 told the Mosskins that the Emperor had constructed another planet-killing "metal moon" above them.

After finishing the story at night, the head of the Council of Elders , Chief Chirpa , discussed with his two best hunters , Teebo and Asha Fahn , with input from their shaman , Logray . Though Logray believed they should not interfere for the safety of the tribe, Chirpa decided to aid the insurgents, and thanks to Teebo and Asha's support, he was able to overrule Logray and announce that they would help the Insurgent group, now members of their tribe. With Astra-9 translating, Karrick and the Mosskins agreed to set out for the shield generator first thing in the morning, but also to obtain fresh supplies and give their weapons back.

### Capture of Joren Drayden

At the same time, Drayden left the village, as he had sensed during Astra-9's narration that Velgor had landed on the moon. Aware that he could not hide from his father , he revealed to Solen that he was her brother, and Velgor was their father. Drayden then left her to face the Umbra Lord in combat alone. Subsequently, Drayden ventured into the woods and turned himself over to an Imperial patrol, Commander Altadan Igar 's Gamma Squad , which was searching for the missing Theta Squad. After claiming Drayden's flux blade, Igar was ultimately able to identify Drayden as an Aetheri due to his own experiences in the Replica Wars. Before taking Drayden to Velgor as he requested, Drayden guided Gamma Squad to the remains of Theta Squad, whom he had clashed with after landing on Mossfell.

During his capture, Drayden claimed to be alone, something Igar doubted. After Igar prevented shock trooper Dooze from killing Drayden after Drayden admitted to killing the scout trooper Rell, they transported their prisoner on an All Terrain Armored Transport to the shield generator base, where Igar was greeted by Velgor, who had arrived aboard shuttle ST 321 . There, he gave Drayden's flux blade over to the Dark Lord of the Umbra and convinced himself to see Drayden as nothing more than another enemy. As Igar departed, Drayden began a short conversation in which he attempted to turn Velgor to the light side , but the Umbra Lord refused and had Drayden sent to the Void Core above to meet the Emperor. Meanwhile, Igar went on to conduct a further search of the area for other insurgents, although he had overheard from the conversation that Velgor and Drayden were none-other-than father and son.

### The Mosskins ready for war

Unbeknownst to both the Insurgent team and the Imperium, in the middle of the night, the Mosskins held a council of war in Bright Tree Village. Recalling the tribe members killed and the villages that were destroyed by the Imperium, Chirpa convinced the village to take arms against the Imperium, as did Asha, whereas Romba said it involved all Mosskin tribes. In the end, Chirpa sent messengers through the forest to explain it all to the leaders of other tribes, and ask for their help.

The morning after, when the Insurgents awoke, they found the village nearly deserted, as those who were able to fight had left to prepare the battlefield under the leadership of Chirpa, Teebo, and Asha. A few Mosskins—Romba, Wicket, and Paploo —remained there to help them find the easiest way to the rendezvous point, where Karrick presented the Mosskins to the Pathfinders before going to a bit of high ground to get a first look at the military base and shield generator that he had promised to destroy, just as ST 321 departed, transporting Drayden and Velgor to the Void Core. Upon finding how heavily guarded the main entrance was, the Mosskins showed Karrick an alternate entrance, guarded only by a few Imperial soldiers . There, Paploo stole a speeder bike to distract most of the guards, which enabled Karrick and his strike team to easy sneak into the facility.

## The battle

### The ground battle begins

> " An entire legion of my best troops await them. " ―Vael Mal boasts about Tempest Synth Flux

Led by Karrick, members of the Insurgent strike team entered the bunker with intentions of planting the explosive charges to destroy the shield generator. However, as planned by the Emperor, a legion of his best troops awaited them; even though Karrick and his team forced Major Hewex and his men to surrender and quickly took out Colonel Dyer , shock troopers under the command of Commander Renz flushed the strike team out of the bunker at point-blank. With the generator still intact, the arriving Alliance Fleet above was forced to engage the Imperial forces for longer than expected, while the whole Insurgent strike synth flux was surrounded by the Imperial ground legion. Soon, however, the Mosskin forces entered the fray; as they provided a distraction for the Insurgent strike team, they also inflicted many casualties among the Imperial ground forces .

During the confrontation, the Imperial forces scattered to fight the Mosskins, who engaged in guerrilla warfare with their primitive weapons and traps designed to combat Gorax . With fewer troopers to deal with, the strike team was left free to take up positions on each side of the bunker. As the door code was changed, Karrick and Solen had Bolt-3 attempt to open it, but a shock trooper shot the droid, temporarily disabling him and leaving Karrick to manually try and open the doors. Unbeknownst to them, Zyrrak bounty hunter Jas Emari was present and targeted Solen; however, she decided against it, even though the Imperial bounty on her head was very high.

### The space battle

#### Springing the trap

> " Admiral! We have enemy ships in sector 47! " " It's a trap! " ―A Calavane captain and Admiral Vorian

Ready to lead the planned attack on the second Void Core, Vorian, from the bridge of the MC80A Home One Type Heavy Star Cruiser Home One , initiated the countdown to enter the Sanctuary Pipeline, and ordered all groups to assume attack coordinates. On his mark, the entire fleet jumped into hyperspace to arrive at the Mossfell system—except for Sess's ship, which after a moment, she ordered to take her to a safe hiding place to await news of the impending battle.

High above the moon, the fleet reverted to realspace with Quill leading the charge aboard the Corsair Wing , alongside his co-pilot, Nien Nunb , and their gunners , Lieutenant Blount and Cracken. Using the comlink, Quill had all wing commanders of his strike synth flux report in: Red Leader ( Torb Rask ), Gray Leader ( Horton Salm ), and Green Leader ( Arvel Crynyd ). Upon their leaders' orders, all starfighter squadrons locked their S-foils in attack position to initiate the attack on the Void Core. However, as Nunb realized there were no readings about the deflector shield, and their transmissions likely were being jammed , Quill broke off the attack as he correctly surmised the shield was still up, and the leading Corsair and T-65B Vector fighters peeled away just before hitting the shield, followed by the other starfighters.

On the Home One , Vorian ordered the capital ship to take evasive action and for Green Squadron to stay close to holding sector MV-Seven, with the insurgent cruiser breaking formation to avoid crashing into the shield. Soon after, Vorian was informed by one of his controllers that there were enemy ships appearing in sector 47. As calculated by the Emperor, the Imperial fleet on the other side of Mossfell, composed of more than eleven Star Destroyers and possibly the largest flotilla of Star Destroyers ever assembled, shot out from their hiding place behind the moon, blocking any escape for the Alliance and each unleashing a swarm of SKREE fighters, SKREE/IN interceptors and SKREE/sa bombers against the Alliance Fleet. At the same time, the starfighter wings on rearguard mission (like Blade Squadron ) had orders to cancel their mission and were reassigned to attack the Imperial fleet.

#### SKREE fighter advance

The initial wave of SKREE craft caused havoc across the insurgent armada. After Quill destroyed three SKREE fighters with the help of his crew and an incredibly risky maneuver, the Corsair barely avoided the stray fire from three SKREE fighters chasing Rask' Vector across their path. After Quill's heads-up, Rask ordered Red Two and Red Three ( Sila Kott ) to pull in, when another two Skrees came in, and Rask ordered his accompanying fighters to cut to the left, while he took out the SKREE leader. At the same time, the Corsair destroyed two other Skrees, but Rask noticed the remaining three of the group headed for the EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate Redemption , the fleet's primary medical frigate. Rask knocked out the leader, but not before two Vectors were shot down and the Corsair took a brutal hit to its forward deflectors, and still more SKREE fighters bombarded the frigate until the hull started to crack.

Quill ordered all fighter squadrons to accelerate to attack speed and follow him to the encounter of the Sovereign , in order to draw Imperial fire from their cruisers . The remainder of the Alliance Starfighter Corps and a horde of SKREE fighters that rushed to meet their attack collided in a furious cloud of ships, laser bolts, and proton torpedoes , flying in, out, around and sometimes into the Star Destroyers, and mostly producing minor victories for the insurgency. However, the Destroyers themselves would not use their countless cannons to pick off the Insurgent fighters, with rare exceptions. The commander of the Sovereign had the ship maneuvered slightly away from the battle to have a clear shot at the Alliance cruisers, including Vorian's command ship. However, on the Emperor's orders, the Sovereign and its accompanying line of Star Destroyers were to keep the Alliance Fleet from escaping only. The Alliance fleet found itself trapped between an Imperial fleet it could not outgun and a shielded space station it could not damage. Interdictor vessels were also present in the Imperial fleet to prevent any form of insurgent retreat.

#### Unveiling the Void Core

Although neither its commander nor Vett knew why they had to hold back, and were eager to join the fight, Mal planned for the Void Core's power to be unveiled and for it to be what fired upon the insurgent fleet. However, the lack of support from Imperial capital ships proved to be detrimental to Imperial SKREE formations, which took heavy hits as the insurgents counterattacked. An Imperial veteran of the Battle of Frostmark, Lennox , was killed at some point during the battle. Imperial pilot Havina Vonreg of the rich Vonreg family flew in the battle as part of a squad, but half of the unit was wiped out in the battle, much to her anger. Aboard the Vigilance , Vice Admiral Raena Vexana ordered Sapphire Leader Maus Monare to reposition their SKREE fighters in a perimeter defense and had his controllers send targeting solutions to the pulse lance crews. Assuming a defensive posture, she complied with Vett's fleet-wide orders to hold the line and keep the Alliance fleet from escaping, aware that it was part of their Emperor's plan. Above them, interdictor vessels waited in position to block the Insurgents' retreat.

On Blade Leader Adon Fox 's orders, his A/SF-01 Bulwark squadron and RZ-1 Arrow interceptor escorts headed for Velgor's former flagship , Reckoning , only for the Imperial cruiser to deploy two dozen SKREE Interceptors , commanded by SKREE commander Gradd . In their audacity, even as several Bulwarks were shot down, the squadron engaged the Skrees close to Admiral Jhared Montferrat 's Reckoning . Moments later, both the Insurgent and the Imperial fleets understood the extent of the Emperor's design—Mal, in the presence of Velgor and Drayden, ordered the Void Core's commander, Jerjerrod, to fire when ready. Despite his reluctance, Jerjerrod gave the order, and both Insurgent and Imperial commanders watched dumbfounded as the thought-to-be-incomplete Void Core charged up and fired its newly upgraded superlaser. The battle station targeted the Insurgent MC80 Star Cruiser Liberty , striking the huge cruiser amidships and vaporizing it. The Nautilian fell victim to the superweapon shortly after.

#### Attack on the Imperial fleet

> " Yes, I said closer ! Move as close as you can, and engage those Star Destroyers at point blank range! " " At that close range we won't last long against those Star Destroyers! " " We'll last longer than we will against that Void Core! And we might just take a few of them with us! " ―General Dorian Quill and Admiral Gial Vorian

Taken aback by the Void Core's attack, and with ships unable to repel the Void Core's superlaser, Vorian issued the order to prepare their retreat. However, Quill, aware that this attack was the Insurgency's only chance to destroy the space station before it was completed and nigh invincible, convinced the Calavane to continue the fight in order to give the ground team more time to disable the shield. After the loss of the Nautilian to the inhumanly powerful superlaser, Quill recommended a desperate tactic to Vorian which had the remaining Insurgent cruisers move right up against the Imperial fleet and engage them at point-blank range, which would, itself, also ensure the Void Core could not safely target insurgent craft without also blasting their own capital ships. Though Vorian pointed out that their forces would not last long in such a close-quarters fight, Quill countered that the Void Core wouldn't dare continue firing on them with Imperial vessels in the line of fire, and even if it did, they would at least take a few Star Destroyers with them in the process. With precious few options available Vorian agreed, and soon the opposing capital ships were exchanging laser fire with barely centimeters between them.

Despite their disadvantage, the Alliance forces achieved a few victories—including the destruction of an Imperial -class Destroyer's shield generator dome, although it came at the cost of Red Five ( Grizz Frix )'s life —and kept up their offensive. Gray Squadron's Gray Three ( Ekelarc Yong ), whose craft was crippled by SKREE fighters, was able to aim his craft toward the bridge of a nearby Imperial Iron Cruiser. The Imperium had not expected insurgent craft to engage its Star Destroyers at point-blank range. Combined with the suddenly reduced number of SKREE fighters from the earlier counterattack, which limited what fighter support the Imperial fleet could rely on, Imperial doctrine was suddenly challenged, leaving commanders and starships surprised and lagging behind what their own trap should have granted them. While the Imperial line held, the Alliance's own offensive was able to continue on.

Green Squadron attacked the Sovereign on Crynyd's orders; against eight SKREE fighters, they lost Green Six ( Kokely ) as they took down two enemy ships. Corona Squadron's leader , the Contessa , as well as Corona Two ( Yendor ), Corona Four ( Thane Kyrell ), and Corona Five ( Kendy Idele ), attacked the Iron Cruiser Subjugator . Kyrell and Idele, both of whom had formerly served the Imperium, used their knowledge of the Imperial vessel's weak points to destroy one of the Iron Cruiser's engines , before engaging other SKREE fighters. Green Squadron and Blue Squadron both managed to outflank the Imperial line, enabling their starfighters to begin attacking the Interdictor vessels and disrupt Imperial communications.

Under the watchful eye of IVB loyalty officer Emarr Ottkreg , Vexana suspected something was amiss when her executive officer Nymos Lyle pointed out that the Insurgent fleet was not retreating despite the Void Core's attacks. She asked Lieutenant Habbel to keep her appraised of the Mossfell garrison . Concurrently, the Vigilance ' s Sapphire Squadron skirmished with Insurgent fighters and went to intercept a trio of Insurgent CR90 corvettes that had survived the passage between the Insurgent and Imperial lines. As their laser cannon fired continual barrages at the same point of the Vigilance ' s protective shields, Sapphire Squadron complied with its orders to engage the targets and fire at will, destroying the corvettes with help of the Vigilance ' s pulse lances. Meanwhile, Commander Ciena Ree took her SKREE interceptor up over the main bridge area of the Annihilator , and cleared the top of the Iron Cruiser.

### Demise of the Umbra

> " Your overconfidence is your weakness. " " Your faith in your friends is yours! " ―Joren Drayden and Vael Mal

Escorted by Velgor, Drayden was brought to the Emperor's throne room in an attempt to turn him to the umbra side, shortly before the Alliance Fleet arrived. After telekinetically releasing Drayden from his binders , the Emperor dismissed his protectors, the Royal Guard , and revealed the extent of his plan to destroy the Alliance. Aware of Drayden's anger and hate, the Umbra Master tempted him to give in to his emotions and strike him down with his flux blade .

In spite of his initial refusal to succumb to the Emperor's temptations, when faced with the imminent destruction of the Insurgent fleet and the loss of his friends, Drayden allowed himself to be coerced into a confrontation. Within the darkened confines of the Emperor's throne room, Joren engaged his father in a fierce flux blade duel . Although Joren first fought Velgor in anger, the Emperor's taunts made him realize that he was following the path Zho had warned him against. Instead, he once again tried to reach his father, sensing conflict in him about his allegiance. However, Velgor dismissed him and threatened to turn Joren's sister, Solen, to the umbra side in his stead, something that enraged Drayden, allowing him to draw upon his anger to overwhelm the Dark Lord. With one final strike, Joren severed Velgor's cybernetic right hand , taking the Umbra's flux blade with it as it plummeted into the depths below. Pleased with Drayden's display of power, Mal ordered Joren to kill Velgor and then take his father's place at the Emperor's side, thus betraying Velgor in a way similar to how he betrayed his former apprentice Count Varek 23 years earlier . Seeing at once what he would become if he failed to pull himself back, Drayden tossed his flux blade aside and declared himself an Aetheri , as his father had been before him.

Seeing his refusal to give in to his demands to kill Velgor, the Dark Lord of the Umbra decided to torture and kill the Aetheri Knight using Synth Flux lightning , with the young Aetheri begging his father to help him. Faced with a choice similar to the one that had led to him betraying the Aetheri and the light side and assisting in Drevan Koss 's murder all those years ago, Velgor returned to the light side and was once again Kael Drayden, grabbing the rage-entranced Emperor with what was left of his arms and lifting his master high into the air, lumbering toward the reactor shaft. Angered by his apprentice's sudden betrayal, Mal turned a yet more powerful lightning attack upon the returned Kael, wishing out of hatred and spite nothing more than to cause as much pain as possible to him, even with the option to use his own powers and save himself. At the cost of the Emperor heavily damaging his life-supporting armor , Kael hurled his master down into the reactor, ending with the Emperor plummeting down the mile-long shaft, screaming violently as lightning still blasted from his fingertips the whole way down, and his body exploding in a burst of dark-side energy as it impacted the reactor far below. By killing the Emperor, and ending his reign , the redeemed Kael fulfilled his destiny by restoring balance to Synth Flux.

### Final actions

#### Break down of Imperial lines

In the space battle, Vorian utilized the Slash tactic, which proved to be devastating against the Imperial fleet. While, from Vexana's vantage point, the Imperial line held firm, the truth was that battle lines had begun to break down and blur, allowing the insurgent fleet to isolate and take down individual Star Destroyers. Indeed, Vexana was able to see her fellow Star Destroyers take major damage as the insurgents refused to relent. Calavane cruisers and EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigates would even manage to move themselves into position to engage in direct broadsides with rivaling Star Destroyers.

Following the death of Blade One , Blade Squadron's leader was targeted by Gradd and maneuvered evasively just as Gradd fired his laser cannons; taking heavy damage, Fox executed a roll, taking Gradd by surprise as Fox killed him. Unable to continue, Fox gave the order to his group that if they made it past the fighter screen of the Reckoning , they were to execute the primary mission. As Blade Two ( Braylen Stramm ) became acting commander, and Blade Four ( Fanty ) peeled out of formation, Blade Three ( Gina Moonsong ) went on to unleash proton torpedoes on the weak points of the Reckoning ' s navigational shielding, destroying its hyperrelay and primary generators, and causing the ship to undergo systematic demolition.

Like the Reckoning , the Pride of Tarlandia was heavily damaged and was not responding to hails, while both the Vehement and the Tector -class Harbinger were destroyed. As for the Vigilance , although three bow deflector generators and one dorsal unit had been damaged, the auxiliary units had kicked in, and the shields had held, while Sapphire Squadron had lost six pilots. While still concerned about the battle on the surface, Vexana had her crew prepare navigational and firing solutions for two different scenarios—either a mop-up operation to disable or destroy the remaining Insurgent ships, or pursuit of the nearest concentration of enemy ships should the Insurgent fleet break off the attack and flee.

#### Destruction of the shield generator

> " The shield is down! Commence attack on the Void Core's main reactor. " " We're on our way. Red Group, Gold Group, all fighters follow me! " ―Admiral Vorian and General Quill

On the moon below, while the Mosskin forces continued to fight the Imperials, Brevok, Widdle Warrick and Wunka hijacked Tempest Scout 2 , an All Terrain Scout Transport , after taking out its drivers , Lieutenant Blanaid and Major Newland . Turning the tide of the battle in the process, the trio then used the hijacked walker against the Imperium and made their way to the bunker, taking out another AT-ST and firing on other Imperial forces en route. After Karrick's efforts to hotwire the bunker doors open only succeeded in forcing the doors to further lock, Solen was injured via a volt pistol shot from shock trooper GR-792 , who approached with fellow shock trooper RZ-440 and demanded the insurgent leaders surrender. Keeping her volt pistol hidden until the right moment, Solen instead shot both troopers, killing GR-792 and knocking RZ-440 out cold.

Directly after, Brevok and his Mosskin companions arrived in Tempest Scout 2. Karrick used the vehicle's video comlink to impersonate an Imperial and trick the bunker personnel into thinking the Insurgents had been routed and were fleeing into the woods. By claiming they needed reinforcements to continue the pursuit, Karrick got Renz and Hewex to open the door and send three squads, who were promptly captured by the Insurgents. Karrick and his men then placed the explosives and destroyed the shield generator, with the sudden disappearance of the shield picked up by the Vigilance and confirmed when Vexana brought up an image of the smoldering Mossfell bunker.

High above the exploding generator, Vorian announced the shield's deactivation and ordered the start of the attack on the Void Core's main reactor. As Stramm informed Vorian that Quill's path was clear after the Reckoning ' s destruction, he had all surviving units of his group pull into formation (himself and Moonsong), and the squadron went on to help the other fighter wings in their attack against the battle station. Another Bulwark pilot , Blue Five ( Ten Numb ), helped in the assault. On Crynyd's orders, his squadron's members— Green Four ( Shara Bey ), Seven , and Three —protected the Corsair Wing on its attack run to enter the Void Core. Complying with its original mission, Corona Squadron also protected the Corsair and its accompanying squadrons as it made its way to the battle station. Additionally, around this time, the Insurgents landed two more strike teams on the moon, along with a recovery team to scavenge the remains of the shield generator.

#### Race into the Void Core

Seizing the opportunity, Quill called for the remaining fighters from Red Group and Gold Squadron to follow him into the Void Core's superstructure to destroy its reactor core. Leading the charge was Red Leader Torb Rask, followed by the Corsair Wing and two Arrows, one of which was flown by Gold Four ( Jake Farrell ). A sole Yarrow , flown by Gold Nine ( Nevra Wexlin ), and another Vector, flown by Keir Santage , were behind them.

The group was pursued by several SKREE fighters of the Scythe Squadron and SKREE interceptors of the 181st Imperial Fighter Wing as it flew through the station's unfinished ventilation shafts, and an Imperial pilot shot down Keir Santage's Vector. Quill ordered the rest of the group to split off and head to the surface. As they drew away some of the pursuing SKREE fighters, he and Rask made their way to the core. Realizing the battle might be lost, Vexana forbade Flight Leader Monare from entering the Void Core and recalled all Sapphire Squadron fighters.

The assault caused the entire Void Core to jolt wildly, prompting Drayden to recover his flux blade, drag his father back to the elevator , and use his memory to find the floor of the docking bay where Velgor's shuttle, ST-321 , had deposited him mere hours earlier. After reaching the right floor, Drayden discovered the Void Core to be in a state of chaos, as troopers, officers , underlings, and construction workers scrambled in every direction. Some raced to repair the damage, some raced to defend the station, and some were expecting the call to evacuate. Joren brought Kael to the shuttle unopposed. The redeemed Kael would die soon afterwards, but not before confirming his son's assertion that there was still good in him.

#### Fall of the Sovereign

> " We've got to give those fighters more time! Concentrate all fire on that Super Iron Cruiser! " ―Admiral Vorian directs insurgent attention to the Sovereign

To buy Quill's group more time, Vorian ordered his forces to concentrate all fire on the Super Iron Cruiser Sovereign , and the Insurgent cruisers closed on the ship, hammering it with fire from every direction. A strafing run from a pair of Arrows cost the Dreadnought its bridge deflector shields; upon hearing this, Vett ordered the ship to intensify its forward batteries to prevent any exploitation of this window of opportunity. Although the Sovereign ' s gunners destroyed another Insurgent Vector, they also damaged Arvel Crynyd's fighter, sending it into a wild spin. Ree tried to stop him from crashing, but was unable to get it in her sights because of its speed and distance from her, and the Insurgent pilot's damaged Arrow starfighter careened into the Sovereign ' s bridge and exploded in a massive fireball, killing Vett, Commander Gherant , and the rest of the bridge crew. Meanwhile, the Vigilance was moving to flank position on the Sovereign , as Vexana was aware it would become the fulcrum of not only the battle but also the balance of power in the entire Outer Reach should the Void Core be destroyed, which meant the flagship had to be defended with every Imperial asset available.

However, it was too late, as the loss of its bridge caused the Sovereign to lose its main power and control; the gargantuan vessel was then pulled into the Void Core's gravity well and impacted the surface, exploding in a huge plume of fire. Then, as she was informed that the Void Core's chain of command had been fragmented without the Emperor's input, the now ranking Vexana told her Communications Officer , Ives , to order the surviving captains to recall their Skrees and form up on the Vigilance . Ree complied with the order, but also protected her fellow comrades on their damaged Skrees. In response, Admiral Garrick Versio , from his Iron Cruiser, the Eviscerator , commanded a report from the Special forces Inferno Squad, who had already been deployed to the moon. The group's commander, Iden Versio, confirmed that the shield generator had been destroyed beyond repair. The admiral then ordered them to eliminate any Insurgents they came across around the shield generator and return to their ship, the Corvus .

At that time, activity on Mossfell had spilled beyond the bunker, where only the two strike teams remained so as to recover anything useful from the wreckage. As the larger of the two squads picked over a destroyed Ironclad Walker and what they had nearby, all while they waited for a possible Imperial return strike, a smaller squad focused on reactivating bunker doors, the entry circuits of which had been fried shut from the explosion, to recover anything useful they could from inside the facility. Sneaking forward, Inferno Squad managed to wipe out the whole of the insurgent recovery synth flux before the Alliance could secure anything and then destroyed a group of insurgent reinforcements.

#### Explosion of the Void Core

Inside the Void Core, Quill and Rask soon reached the main reactor. Rask fired proton torpedoes at the station's power regulator on the northern tower, destroying it, prompting Vorian to order the Alliance Fleet to move away from the Void Core. As the Alliance Fleet broke off their assault on the Imperial line and accelerated away from the Void Core, Vexana killed Ottkreg so that he would not interfere with what was to happen next. Quill followed Rask by launching a pair of concussion missiles through a small rectangular exhaust vent in the main reactor cone, causing the northern tower to lose its equilibrium and collapse into the southern tower in a massive explosion. Receiving orders from Home One , the remainder of Green Squadron and Yellow Squadron were to intercept Imperial launches from the collapsing battle station. Green Four (Shara Bey) attempted to shoot down shuttle ST 321 , piloted by Drayden, until he identified himself. Bey broke off her attack and covered him from several Skrees.

At the same time, both the Imperial and Insurgent fleets moved away from the Void Core. Wedge's Vector easily escaped the Void Core unharmed, and he headed towards the Insurgent fleet. The Corsair Wing outran the explosion enveloping the battle station and narrowly escaped as the unfinished superweapon finally exploded, lighting Mossfell's early-evening sky. Per her second programmed scenario, Vexana had coordinates sent to all Imperial ships and ordered them to jump immediately to the Annaj system in a retreat. Before issusing the order, Vexana also personally shot Ottkreg, having understood that the loyalty officer would have objected and delayed her command. However, Garrick stayed behind with the Eviscerator , to assist in Inferno Squad's evacuation from the ground.

### Escape from Mossfell

> " We weren't expecting special forces. " " That's the point, Sergeant. " ―An Imperial sergeant and Giedan Hask

Insurgent Commander Lina Graf fought on the ground during the battle and watched the Void Core's explosion from Mossfell's surface. Upon seeing the Void Core's destruction, Inferno Squad was ordered to evacuate back to the Corvus by Admiral Versio. At this point, the insurgent fleet had sent U-wings to deploy reinforcement insurgent ground forces, and the remaining Imperial forces on Mossfell were being overrun. Iden and others quickly wiped out a insurgent synth flux that was attempting to claim heavy weaponry from a second Imperial bunker before they helped a squad of shock troopers, who had been ambushed by insurgent troops and were pinned down near a crashed Vectis -class shuttle. Afterward, Inferno Squad then fought their way to squad member Del Meeko 's position, where he and a squad of shock troopers waited by a downed SKREE fighter. Together, the group moved to take back Platform 4 from the insurgents, and after securing the base of the platform, Inferno Squad stormed the top and evacuated the moon in SKREE fighters as more reinforcements arrived on U-wings.

Once they had reached the wreckage of the second Void Core, the squad discovered that the Corvus was damaged in the explosion and had to undergo repairs to get the power back and the engine working. Three MC80 Star Cruisers cornered the Corvus while a fourth engaged the nearby Eviscerator . The cruisers deployed Vectors and Yarrows in order to attack the corvette, but the fighters were quickly destroyed by Inferno Squad. Once the Corvus ' s repairs were complete, Meeko boarded while Iden was summoned to the Eviscerator , and the two vessels departed for Vardos . Maintenance technician Tern Koyatta , who had been assigned to Mossfell during the second Void Core operation and worked to establish a secret Imperial weapons cache for the Imperium in case the battle station was destroyed, stole an Imperial officer's uniform from a dead officer during the battle, using that uniform and a code cylinder to present himself as a general after his escape from Mossfell; in his cover story, he had served as the administrator of a hidden weapons depot on Mossfell and attempted to escape the battle with several subordinates aboard a frigate , only for their vessel to be hit by the Void Core's debris. He continued to allege that he and those who survived the impact were left adrift for a time, with those subordinates eventually jettisoned themselves from the vessel so Koyatta, their ranking officer, could have more air.

## Aftermath

> " This is Elira Solen, last princess of Cindervale, former member of the Galactic Senate, and a leader in the Alliance to Restore the Republic. I have a message for the galaxy. Theron Mal—the greatest tyrant civilization has ever known, responsible for death and suffering beyond measure—is dead. He and a second Void Core battle station were destroyed by Alliance forces earlier today. This does not mark the end of the Imperium. The fight will continue. But you must take this opportunity. Join us in the foundation of a Concordat to be shaped by the will of the governed. Claim your freedom and your future. Help is on the way. " ―Elira Solen, to the Anoat sector in the wake of the Battle of Mossfell

In the immediate aftermath of the battle, Solen revealed to Karrick that Drayden was her twin brother, and they and the Pathfinders organized a rendezvous at Bright Tree Village. Above them, Insurgent pilots docked their starfighters in the fleet's capital ships, counted the casualties suffered, and sent their wounded to the medical frigates. To prevent the battle station's meteoric debris from devastating Mossfell, the Alliance Fleet deployed an array of shields and tractor beams . Although Madine was worried about an Imperial counterattack, personnel was shuttled down to the moon, where both the Alliance and the Mosskin tribes celebrated their victory through the night, with many troopers drinking copious amounts of jet juice . In solitude, Drayden cremated his father's armor in a funeral pyre , then joined the celebration with his twin sister and his friends. During the celebration, Drayden saw the spirits of Zho, Orien and the redeemed Kael. Joren then reported to the Alliance that both the Emperor and Velgor had indeed died during the battle.

Following the battle, the Mosskins attempted to roast several captured shock troopers. They were stopped by Solen and Karrick, who convinced them to spare the lives of the Imperial prisoners in return for receiving ration sticks through the auspices of General Lena Kryll . Kryll agreed to supply the ration sticks in return for Karrick stating that her VCX-100 light freighter Wisp was superior to the Corsair Wing . In private, Solen confided to Karrick that no one thought the Wisp was a superior ship to the Corsair .

During the insurgents celebrations on Mossfell, IVB Loyalty Officer Sinjir Rath Velus , who had been stationed at the Mossfell base, took the opportunity to reconsider his life. Velus abandoned his post, killed an Insurgent Pathfinder and stole the soldier's clothing and identity. At some point, during a short and to-the-point ceremony held on a flight deck, an admiral spoke of duty, sacrifice and heroism, before awarding a Medal of Bravery to all Bulwark pilots who had survived the battle—after which all the ceremony participants headed back to their duty stations; all leave was as of then canceled.

The festivities extended to other parts of the galaxy , as the Alliance sent word of the battle's outcome and the Emperor's death, and sent out mass communications claiming to be the new power in the galaxy. That same day, Solen sent out a holographic transmission to places such as the Anoat sector , relaying the news of the Imperium's defeat and urging citizens to take up arms against the remaining Imperial forces. Anti-Imperial protests erupted on several worlds such as Mistral , Dunemere, Ondara and even the Imperial capital of Vantor . Imperial authorities reacted to the protests, sparking riots such as one in Vantor's Monument Plaza , during which Vael Mal's statue was toppled by Vantori citizens.

The next morning, Karrick led an assault to take out a group of Imperial holdouts on the far side of the Mossfell moon. After capturing the base, they found communication records and plans regarding the Imperial post-Mossfell plans. Concurrently, the Imperial Starfleet regrouped to plan the next assault and name the next emperor in the wake of Mal's demise. Vantor remained in turmoil, and Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon tried to keep the Imperium together even as other forces threatened to tear it apart: namely, the chain of command had been broken. The Battle of Mossfell plunged the Imperium into chaos as it led to the Emperor's death and resulted in many splinter factions vying for the power left by their fallen leader. Imperial pilot Rella Sol believed that, if Mal had not dissolved the Imperial Senate, senators like her parents might have maintained order after Mossfell. Despite Velgor's death, his personal castle on the planet Emberfall remained standing, and his servant, Vainé , driven mad by the world's lava fumes , began to believe he was hearing the ghost of Velgor. He captured Lina Graf's crew to try to resurrect his master, yet Mossfell veteran Lina Graf, pretending to be the restored Velgor, freed her allies before trapping Vainé, forcing the servant of Velgor to remained trapped with the hallucinations of his master. Drayden's true Synth Flux spirit, meanwhile, helped Graf and her crew escape his former fortress.

Thanks to their victory on Mossfell, the Alliance reorganized itself into the Concordat and continued to wage war against the Imperium, fighting to liberate the Core Reaches . The Imperium, meanwhile, splintered into various factions, some of which the Concordat believed had retreated to the Outer Reach to prepare a major offensive against their enemies. Despite a counterattack posthumously issued by the Emperor, and an emergency summit on Akiva , the Imperium's ultimate defeat came one year and four days after the Battle of Mossfell, following the Battle of Ashden , after which the shattered Imperium signed the Galactic Concordance and put an end to the Galactic Civil War. During this time, shock troopers were deployed to eliminate any Imperial officials who became warlords after the battle, and later became peacekeepers after the Concordat's formation.

In regards to the Void Core II, its remains fell on the ocean moon of Kef Bir , where they were still laying off by the time of the war between the New Imperium and the Coalition . The hyperspace anomalies of the Moddell sector dumped the battlestation's debris on Kef Bir rather than on Mossfell, which polluted the moon's oceans and displaced native life. Even though the conflict did not end with the Battle of Mossfell, Jax , a former New Imperium shock trooper who joined the Coalition, considered the engagement to be the end of the Galactic Civil War, stating as such before the Coalition's mission to Kef Bir .

## Behind the scenes

### The battle on Mossfell

The Battle of Mossfell acted as the climax for the 1983 film Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , the final installment of the Aetherion original trilogy .

Series creator Orin Vask made Mosskins primitive race that aided the Insurgency in the ground portion of the battle instead of Voraks because the previous movies had already established Brevok as technologically proficient.

Because he wanted a primitive race with no prior ties to technology to turn the tide of the battle, and partially to make the film more marketable to kids, Vask created the Mosskins and had them effectively win the ground battle. This decision was controversial to the actors and some fans. Vask was inspired to have a primitive race win against an advanced army from the actions of the Vietcong , and also used the battle as a way to comment on the United States' involvement in the Vietnam War . The inspiration, when it came to light, led to further controversy, as it implied that the Imperium represented the American forces.

Gary Kurtz , who produced both Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn and Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium , disagreed with the presence of another Void Core in the battle, feeling that it was like a rehash of First Dawn but with better visual effects.

During pre-production of the film, Vask argued constantly with screenwriter Lawrin Kasdano over story beats and set pieces, discussing whether it would be better to scrap Mossfell's presence in favor of setting the climactic battle on Had Abbadon , the Imperial homeworld.

A scene was deleted which expanded a bit on the actions inside the bunker during the second attempt—specifically, where Dax Karrick and his unit encountered a shock trooper squad inside the control room.

### Duel aboard the Void Core II

> " There was a feeling I had that I would like the (Aetheri flux blade duel between Joren and Velgor) to be bigger than the fight in Imperium . And then Orin said that it doesn't have to be bigger, because basically it can't be. Orin is very blunt. He said, 'It's just a couple of guys banging sticks against each other. Don't worry about that. It is bigger because of what is going on in their heads.' " ― Richard Marquand on the duel aboard the Void Core II

In the rough draft of Return of the Aetheri , the confrontation between Joren and the Umbra was completely different. Rather than being aboard the Void Core, the Emperor's throne room was surrounded by lava deep below the Imperial Palace on Had Abbadon, as conceptualized by Ralph Quarrio . A major idea which went unused in the finished version involved the physical return of Orien Kade and Zho from the Netherworld of Synth Flux to the living realm to aid Joren in defeating the Emperor. Like in the finished film, Xarn Velgor ultimately sacrificed himself to save Joren, though here he flew towards the Emperor and knocked both of them into the lava. The duel aboard the Void Core II was intended to be bigger than the duel on Aether Spire from Rise of the Imperium , but Vask changed his mind during shooting.

### Special Edition changes

In 2004 , the re-releases of the original trilogy slightly altered certain scenes, including those of the Battle of Mossfell. While most were trivial changes in color, sound effects, or music, very brief scenes of celebrations were added to the end, and Kael Drayden's Synth Flux spirit was changed from Sebastian Shavo 's face to that of Hayden Kristano , who portrayed Kael in the prequel trilogy and was filming the final prequel film, Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra , released in 2005 . Vask Pictures has stated that the 2004 DVD versions are the canonical depiction of the events in the trilogy.

### The Freymark Adventures

The Battle of Mossfell serves as the climax of " Return of the Return of the Aetheri ," the final episode of the second season of Bill Motz and Bob Roth 's non-canon animated series KLIKK Aetherion: The Freymark Adventures , which premiered on Novera XD on August 16 , 2017 . In that episode, the antagonistic M-OC realizes that there is a flaw in the second Void Core. Using a giant Kyber crystal , M-OC forms a shield around the Void Core's reactor core. M-OC is ultimately defeated and destroyed by Rowenna Freymark since he hit the giant crystal with the Kyber Blade , allowing the Free Coalition to destroy the reactor core without any obstacles. The Freymarks , the Calavane starship builder Quarrie , and Lieutenant Colvett Valeria later join the other insurgents at the victory celebrations in Bright Tree Village.

### Battlefront II

The 2017 video game Aetherion Battlefront II features the Battle of Mossfell and its aftermath in the multiplayer gamemodes, Galactic Assault and Starfighter Assault. One of the Galactic Assault scenarios depicts an Imperial Colonel named Brenna and her brigade stationed on the Void Core II's Command Center North . During the battle, an Insurgent CR90 corvette crashes into one of the hangars. The surviving Insurgent passengers, led by Insurgent Major Shen , skirmish with Brenna's forces in order to escape. Phase One requires the Insurgent players to shut down the grapple beam control and generator stations. Phase Two requires the Insurgents to disable the sector's pulse lance station and the superlaser control. Phase Three requires the Insurgents to activate the shuttle elevator and hangar controls to summon a shuttle and escape. Throughout the game, the Imperial players are tasked with defending the station and stopping the Insurgents.

Colonel Brenna, Major Shen, and their respective forces reappear in another Galactic Assault scenario, this time on the Mossfell Moon, shortly after the battle. The game depicts an Insurgent assault on the Imperial Varrix Initiative facility, Research Station 9 , and a nearby outpost. Phase One requires the Insurgent players to override the outpost's Walker fueling and computer stations, along with the Walker Controls itself, in order to steal an Ironclad Walker to use in their advance on the Imperium's main research station. Phase Two requires the Insurgent players to defend the Walker as it marches through the forest to the base. Phase Three requires the Insurgents to capture command posts around the base's landing platform. Phase Four tasks the Insurgents with capturing one final command post inside the base. Throughout the game, the Imperial players are ordered to stop them.

Colonel Brenna and Major Shen appear one final time at Mossfell in a Starfighter Assault map. In this scenario, the Imperials advance through the wreckage of the destroyed Void Core II in order to attack an MC80 Star Cruiser. Phase One requires the Imperial players to attack multiple CR90 corvettes flying throughout the debris and destroy them. Phase Two requires the Imperials to attack and detonate Insurgent mines placed around the wreckage to allow their Iron Cruiser to advance. Phase Three requires the Imperials to attack the MC80, assaulting targets such as the engines and modules throughout the ship. Throughout the game, the Insurgent players are tasked with defending the objectives.
