# Zho
> " Size matters not. Look at me. Judge me by my size, do you? Hmm? Hmm. And well you should not. For my ally is Synth Flux, and a powerful ally it is. Life creates it, makes it grow. Its energy surrounds us and binds us. Luminous beings are we, not this crude matter. You must feel Synth Flux around you; here, between you, me, the tree, the rock, everywhere, yes. Even between the land and the ship. " ―Zho, to Joren Drayden — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Zho was a legendary Aetheri Grand Master who led the Aetheri Order through the time of the High Republic, in the years leading up to its destruction by the Umbra , and during the transformation of the Vantor Republic into the Imperium . Small in stature but revered for his wisdom and power, Zho trained generations of Aetheri , ultimately serving as the Aetheri Order's Grand Master . He played an integral roles in defending the Republic during the Replica Wars , survived Directive 66, lived to pass on the Aetheri tradition to Joren Drayden , and unlocked the path to immortality .

Born in 896 BVE , Zho served the Aetheri Order for centuries , becoming Aetheri Grand Master and helping to teach many younglings . During the High Republic Era , in 382 BVE , Zho helped bring an end to a devastating battle between the Aetheri and the anti-Aetheri Path of the Open Hand cult . After the battle, which became known as the Night of Sorrow , he decided to keep the living weapons against Synth Flux-sensitives the Path had used, the Nameless , out of the Aetheri Archives to protect the order. Over a century later, he took a sabbatical from the Aetheri High Council to train Adepts aboard the Star Hopper , leading them and other Aetheri in fighting the Nullkin pirate organization in 232 BVE . After disappearing without explanation for over a year, Zho reappeared in 230 BVE on Korrath , where he helped his former Adept Kantam Syra and other Aetheri battle the Nullkin, accompanied by a former Aetheri who fought in the Night of Sorrow named Azlin Rell . As the Nullkin had also weaponized the Nameless, Zho hoped that Rell's knowledge he had accrued about the creatures since the battle would help the Aetheri find a way to fight them.

Zho was almost nine hundred years old in the latter days of the Republic. A leading member of the Aetheri High Council, his contemporaries included other legendary Masters, such as Drevan Koss and Reth-Oss-Van . During the Invasion of Ondara , in 32 BVE , the maverick Sorin Vael introduced Kael Drayden to the High Council, believing with absolute certainty that he had discovered the prophesied Seeded One . The Aetheri elders sensed that the boy was full of fear and anger and declined to train him, deeming Drayden too old and emotionally compromised to commit himself to the Aetheri Code . After Vael's death during the liberation of Ondara , the High Council reversed their decision in spite of Zho's continued opposition to Drayden's apprenticeship, having sensed grave danger in his training. At the same time , the Aetheri discovered that their ancient nemesis , the Umbra, had returned after a millennium in hiding. Aware of the Rule of Two , Zho was convinced that at least one more Umbra Lord remained active following Orien Kade 's victory over the Umbra apprentice Vael Vreth .

Over the next decade, the galaxy was on the verge of civil war with entire star systems —led by Count Varek , Zho's one-time Adept—threatening to secede from the Republic by 22 BVE . Unable to defend the entire Republic against this betrayal on their own, the Aetheri took command of the newly-formed Grand Army of the Republic , with Zho himself leading an army of replica troopers against the Dissident Droid Army of the Fractio of Independent Systems in the first battle of the Replica Wars, rescuing the Aetheri and Senator from execution. For three years Zho led the Republic military's war effort as an Aetheri General , determined to bring a swift and decisive end to the conflict. During this time he was approached by the spirit of Sorin Vael, who helped Zho learn how to retain his identity as a spirit after death. Zho's efforts against the Dissidents were undermined by Vael Mal , the Dark Lord of the Umbra who conspired to destroy the Aetheri and restore the Umbra to power. After the Great Aetheri Purge commenced in 19 BVE , killing thousands of Aetheri both on Vantor and across the galaxy, Zho confronted the self-declared Galactic Emperor but failed to cut short Mal' reign and consequently retreated into exile , leaving the Umbra Master to consolidate his power with a new apprentice, the former Kael Drayden-turned- Xarn Velgor, at his side.

Zho's remaining years were spent living in isolation on the remote world of Mirefall . After the death of Orien Kade, he was able to commune with his spirit, who wished for Zho to train Kael's son Joren Drayden . Zho initially reluctantly agreed, but later believed that the galaxy as better off without him and refused Kade's request. After being convinced by Kade to enter the Cave of Evil , in which he confronted many figures from his past and made peace with his failures, he agreed to teach Joren Drayden, who soon after sought out the legendary Grand Master in the hope of becoming an Aetheri Knight . He trained Drayden for a time, until despite Zho's warnings Drayden hurriedly abandoned his training to save his friends after a having a vision that they were in danger. A year later, in 4 AVE , with his health rapidly declining, Zho died of old age when Drayden returned to see him. He became one with Synth Flux , shortly before Joren redeemed his father Kael, who fulfilled his destiny as the Seeded One by killing Mal.

Years later, Joren Drayden's attempt to reform the Aetheri Order would be foiled when his nephew Beno Karrick turned to the umbra side and destroyed his Aetheri Sanctum . Believing the galaxy was better without him and the Aetheri, Drayden went into exile, just as Zho had. After his student he reluctantly trained, the scavenger Nyla , also abandoned her training, Zho appeared as a ghost to Drayden. Zho taught Drayden to learn from his failures and helped him forgive himself.

## Biography

### Early life

> " For 800 years have I trained Aetheri. " ―Zho, to Joren Drayden

Born nine-hundred years before the Battle of Mossfell , Zho belonged to an ancient and mysterious species and little was known of Zho's early life; he never revealed his homeworld . As he was keenly attuned to Synth Flux , he joined the Aetheri Order as a youngling . As an Aetheri Initiate , Zho made use of a training flux blade . After undergoing the ritual known as the Gathering , he received his own personal kyber crystal , and Professor Huyang helped him construct his first flux blade aboard the Crucible . The circumstances surrounding Zho and the construction of his first flux blade were exciting, according to Huyang. At some point during his time as a youngling, Zho skipped across the lily pads in the Aetheri Sanctum's meditation pond .

Although Zho spoke little of his early days, it was rumored that one of his early instructors was the Hysalrian N'Kata Del Gormo . Zho was paired up with a personal mentor at a younger age than usual. During their time together, Zho's Aetheri Master told him the tale of K'ungfu and Chuang , two great Aetheri of the past. As an Adept , Zho visited multiple planets and during his first years he could have named them all, but in the following centuries, he couldn't remember how many he'd seen and later estimated the number to be in the tens of thousands. Around the age of one hundred, in 796 BVE , Zho was ready to pass on what he had learned. Having attained the rank of Master, he spent the next eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Aetheri. It was estimated that over his lifetime, he trained around twenty thousand Aetheri.

### High Republic Era

#### Early High Republic Era

> " You must choose Synth Flux. One does not fall into being an Aetheri Knight by mistake, hm? Or because it is convenient! You must choose Synth Flux, with your whole heart. To do this, you must learn, again, to listen. To hear the world, the world outside of your own emotions. Even when they are very, very loud, heh, [like] a hurricane. " ―Zho

By the High Republic Era , Zho was considered a great Aetheri Master. He had also gained a seat on the Aetheri High Council and the rank of Grand Master . Two other Aetheri— Xo Lahru and Pra-Tre Veter —shared the Grand Master title with Zho. Eventually the human Aetheri Ry Ki-Sakka also attained the position. As the Aetheri who taught younglings, Zho one day met the human youngling Gella Nattai , who was scared of the training remotes and ran from the room. Finding Nattai in the Temple garden and sitting with her, Zho and Nattai watched a frog in the garden repeatedly try to jump into a waterfall behind a stone wall. Zho used the frog as a lesson, teaching her to face her fear and always get back up to try again.

#### An uncertain Path

> " No one, no galaxy, no Republic, no Aetheri Order, no family, is perfect. " " You are right. […] Perhaps we are doing too much, too fast. " " You must continue. The great good is a good vision. Fight for this good as well, the Aetheri do. " ―Zho and Chancellor Kyong Greylark

In 382 BVE , after learning of the Battle of Embermoor and the failure of a peace treaty between the warring planets Eiram and E'ronoh there, Zho went to the Republic Executive Building on the galactic capital Vantor to meet with with Supreme Chancellor Kyong Greylark while they waited for more information from Embermoor . They spoke to Aetheri Master Creighton Sun and Aetheri Knight Aida Forte , who were on the ground on Embermoor and confirmed that the E'roni ambassador was dead and the Eirami had deployed a droid army. They requested to perform an investigation on the Outer Reach Territories planet Dalna of the Path of the Open Hand cult, considering their involvement in the failure of the peace talks, the fact that Aetheri Master Silandra Sho felt a disturbance that made the Aetheri ill at the Path's almshouse , and that Aetheri Knight Gella Nattai believed the Chancellor's criminal son Axel Greylark was working with the Path. Zho admitted that he had sensed a disturbance beyond Embermoor, and emphasized that they need to be careful, reminding them that there was always those who thrived in chaos. After much deliberation, Zho and Chancellor Greylark eventually agreed to a fact-finding mission accompanied by two of her guards and to send Aetheri Master Char-Ryl-Roy and Adept Enya Keen to Eiram and E'ronoh to try and keep the peace and discover what was causing communications buoys to be down, Zho explaining to them that Werth Plouth , the Path's Herald , was responsible for the riots on Embermoor.

After the communication ended, Zho expressed his concern as they received another message from Aetheri Master Yaddle , who was accompanied by an Aetheri Initiate named Cippa Tarko , though the connection of the message was poor. Tarko asked about Zho, and Yaddle explained that he was an Aetheri Master and not her brother. Zho told Yaddle that they were sending help to Embermoor and two Aetheri to Dalna and that she was to return to Vantor, but the message was so choppy they were not sure if she understood. Zho had an unsettling feeling in Synth Flux that reminded him of the feeling he got whenever a battle was coming. Master Yaddle did misunderstand Zho's message to mean she was needed on Dalna and joined Sun and Forte there, who sent a message to Zho confirming they were there shortly before Yaddle arrived.

Later, Chancellor Greylark had her guards summon Zho to her to reveal that her son Axel had escaped from prison with the help of the Path and someone named Binnot Ullo . She told Zho that she did not know who Ullo was and did not think Axel had many friends. He assured her that no one, no galaxy, and no republic was perfect, and that she should continue to fight for the greater good like the Aetheri did. She explained that she felt like they have all been moving through a fog that has corrupted everything lately and Zho insisted that Axel still had good in him and that the 'fog' was just corrupting him too. However, the Chancellor resolved to make Axel truly feel the consequences of his actions.

#### Chancellor no more

> " We will go together. Chancellor you may not be anymore. Friend, you still are. " ―Zho to Greylark, on going to Dalna

Eventually, Greylark received a message from her son on Dalna and immediately called Zho to have him listen to it with her via hologram. After hearing Axel ask for her to go to Dalna to speak with the Path and with him, Zho asked her what she would do, the Chancellor deciding to go to Dalna. She then recorded a message for Axel that included Zho in it, wearing a blank expression, so that he could bear witness to her stepping down as Chancellor. After she sent the message, Zho stayed with her wordlessly for a while, and she appreciated his kindness.

Before Greylark could leave for Dalna, Zho approached her and said that since he had not heard back from Masters Sun or Ela Sutan , who had also been sent to Dalna, that he would go with her because she was still his friend even though she was no longer Chancellor. Zho had an Aetheri Longbeam cruiser follow them with a contingent of Aetheri. While in hyperspace, they received a distress call from Princess Xiri A'lbaran of E'ronoh and Prince Phan-tu Zenn of Eiram. They then received a message from Chancellor Orlen Mollo , who said that the leaders of Eiram and E'ronoh were still not cooperating. Zho volunteered to rescue the heirs due to their proximity to them and left, taking other Aetheri with him.

#### The Night of Sorrow

> " Speak to the Aetheri Council soon, you should. We must explain the happenings on Dalna. All that went wrong. " […] "But we must also speak to all the things that went right. We could not have defeated the Path without the Aetheri. " " And the Aetheri could not have defeated the Path without the help of our Republic allies. " ―Zho and Greylark

Eventually, Zho arrived at the battle that was now raging on Dalna between the Path and the Aetheri, which came to be known as the Night of Sorrow . The Path, much of it reorganized into the Path of the Closed Fist , was led by Marda Ro of the Ro family . He was joined by Princess A'lbaran and Prince Zenn, the three announcing that the combined forces of Eiram and E'ronoh were there to fight together with the Aetheri. When Zho arrived on the ground at the Path compound , Aetheri Gella Nattai told him to direct people to go to the cave system as there was potentially many more Path members there. On Dalna, he met Path Little Tromak . Zho eventually came to forget Tromak but always remembered the importance of his visit to Dalna.

As the battle started to come to a close with it turning in the Aetheri's favor, Zho and Yaddle stood together, the latter watching Cippa Tarko sleep. However, A'lbaran and Zenn contacted them and informed them that explosions have been set off underground. As Yaddle suggested getting help, the ground shook and Zho spotted sections of the caves collapse into sinkholes. Zho and Yaddle then sensed an Aetheri in distress and went to help. Finding a door in the ground, Zho and Yaddle connected to synth flux and sensed Gella Nattai and Axel Greylark. Yaddle lifted up the ground, rocks, and soil while Zho lifted the water to try and rescue them. Enya Keen then ran toward them, seeing Nattai, and pulled her and Axel from the mud-like water, having been brought to the surface by the two Aetheri Masters. Zho and Yaddle then set the water and mud down far away. In the sinkhole, E'roni and Eirami soldiers began recovering survivors. Yaddle noticed that the sounds of battle have faded and the sun was rising. She asks Zho if the battle was done, and he says it was.

Zho met with Kyong Greylark and Chancellor Mollo on a medical frigate above Vantor, the three discussing now only having one Chancellor instead of two and what it means. Zho suggested they speak to the Aetheri Council and tell them everything that went wrong, but Greylark also wanted to focus all the things that went right, including the help of the Republic and the Aetheri. Mollo revealed that the people of Dalna were calling the event the Night of Sorrow. While he imagined the future, Greylark assured Zho that she would meet him on Vantor soon, Zho adding that there was much to celebrate and remember.

#### Return to the Temple

> " What do you know about these nameless creatures? " "I, too, know of them. Powerful beings, they are. Dangerous, they are. But in pain, they could be. Only a few understand them. You, and I. And still, much to learn is there. " ―Zho and Creighton Sun

Zho then traveled to the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor, confirming first that the royal families of Eiram and E'ronoh had already gathered, who he met with when he arrived. He met with several Aetheri, including Yaddle, who was standing with Tarko. Zho raised his arm to silence the room and Master Sun rose from his chair, walking to the center of the room using a walking stick before beginning a funeral for Aetheri Aida Forte, Orin Darhga , and Char-Ryl-Roy. Zho allowed Roy's former Adept, Keen, to keep his flux blade and use it alongside hers.

Later, in the Aetheri Archives , Creighton spoke with Zho about Nameless creatures that the Path deployed on Dalna and the horror they wrought upon the Aetheri, causing them to be weakened, hallucinate, and eventually be turned to dust. Zho confirmed that there is a larger danger connected to them, which caused Creighton to wonder what Zho knew. Realizing how catastrophic to the Order it could be if people found out about the creatures, and since they had heard no other reports of their usage, they decided to keep quiet about them and not include them in the Aetheri Archive. They agreed not to speak of it again and each of them left.

#### Master of Kantam Syra

> " The lesson of Younglings we take on is one of the hardest ones for most Aetheri to learn. Indeed, some never do." "Detachment." "To let go. Elusive it is, but also, always available to us, always there. And then, when we grasp, gone again. Takes practice, it does. Repetition. To have to do it again does not mean we have failed, only that we must do it again. And again. " ―Zho and Kantam Syra

At some point, Zho took on a human named Kantam Syra as his Adept. The two were stationed at the Aetheri Sanctum on the planet Endovar . Syra was a very talented Adept and was far ahead of their peers and even some of their elders, with Zho even admitting that they were the most disciplined Adept he had ever taught and one of the most connected to Synth Flux. Despite this, Zho held off knighting Syra, for a reason that the Adept did not understand until they began a romantic relationship with an acrobat named Aytar and realized that both Zho and them had been waiting for something like that to happen. Eventually, Syra decided to leave the Aetheri Order, a decision partially incited by their newfound relationship with Aytar.

Zho and Syra loved to spar with each other, and it was how the two often found understanding and how Syra often found inner harmony while troubled or when they had a problem. Even when they did not find a solution during a match, they would leave feeling like an answer was out there. When Syra traveled to the meditation room in the Endovar Aetheri Sanctum in order to tell Zho about their decision, Zho challenged them to a sparring match, remembering that they had dueled many times in that room and saying that this duel could be one final lesson. Syra agreed to the duel, as it was custom to not refuse a duel with Master Zho, but also realized that this meant Zho knew about their decision. Zho dodged Syra's question by engaging in combat, before saying that the time had come to make a choice, but not the one that Syra thought. Zho explained that Synth Flux chose Syra just as it had chosen all other Aetheri, and asked when Syra would choose Synth Flux. Syra insisted that they chose Synth Flux every day when they took part in their Aetheri training, but after knocking Syra to the ground, Zho asked if we really make a choice when we do not believe there are any other options. Zho told Syra that there was a reason he had not knighted them yet, before ending the duel, helping Syra up, and sitting down with Syra to meditate.

Eventually Syra asked Zho if they should leave the Aetheri Order, but Zho insisted that there was no 'should' and that the right thing to do is an idea we invent to make ourselves feel better. Zho also suggested that Syra had stopped listening, in the literal sense, and in a larger general sense. Zho explained that feelings are like wind, we can feel it, we can experience it, but it passes, though sometimes it can feel like a hurricane which can make it easy to give in to anger and fear. Zho told Syra that in order for them to continue to be an Aetheri they must actively choose the Aetheri Order and Synth Flux and learn to listen to the world outside of their emotions even when they were as loud as a hurricane. Eventually, after a moment, Syra set down their flux blade in front of Zho. Zho nodded at them, and then his Adept left him, and the Aetheri Order, behind. Zho was saddened but supported Syra through their departure. Syra would reflect on the conversation they had that day throughout the rest of their life.

Over a year later, Syra returned to the Aetheri, and met with Zho on the Endovar. Zho was in the process of helping a pet Happabore named Monsalmo at the time, after being awoken from a nap by the creature's squealing. Zho was used to dealing with her complicated behaviors, including finding her inside the temple underneath the younglings' bunks. This time, Monsalmo was pregnant and had climbed onto the roof of her pen to avoid giving birth in a fuel spill. However, the roof could not hold the creature's weight and it collapsed. Zho caught Monsalmo with Synth Flux, but at that same time she started to give birth to her pups, so Zho had to carry them with Synth Flux too. Just then, Syra and a Synth Flux-sensitive girl they had rescued on the planet Ondara named Lula Talisola arrived and both used Synth Flux to help Zho carry Monsalmo and her pups. Zho welcomed Syra back, telling them that they had left an Adept and returned a Knight. A few hours later, Zho knighted Syra in a modest ceremony so that they could take on Talisola as their Adept learner.

After the ceremony, Syra and Zho sat around a fire near the happabore enclosure and discussed the events that had occurred. Syra expressed that they did not understand how one second they felt so sure in their path separate from the Order but was then pulled back to it when they saved Talisola. Zho explained that the young are the greatest teachers and the greatest lesson that can be learned from them is necessity of detachment and letting go, that being an Aetheri was to let go. Syra realized that they had been the one that taught Zho that lesson. Zho revealed that he had struggled with Syra's departure and had been unsure of the future. The two then shared Sweetpuff jamwiches with Talisola.

Sometime later, Zho and Syra met with Talisola in the Endovar Aetheri Sanctum. Syra told Talisola that the lesson she taught the two masters was the hardest one of all. Talisola hoped that it was front flips with a flux blade, which made Syra retract their statement and say that it was the second hardest lesson to learn. Zho concurred, saying that the front flip was nothing simple. But the lesson that Syra wanted to teach Talisola was the same one that Syra had learned from her and Zho had learned from Syra: Detachment.

Sometime before 232 BVE , Zho traveled to the Aetheri Sanctum on Takodana to pick up a Synth Flux-sensitive Aloxian child named Qort from Maz Oru and Aetheri Sav Malagán and bring him into the Aetheri Order. Adept Reath Silas briefly met Zho during some events both attended together.

#### Time on the Star Hopper

> " Chaos on the surface, there will be. Remember that Synth Flux moves through you, through all living things, even in chaos. Move with Synth Flux, we must. Never against it. " ―Zho during the Trymant IV disaster

By 233 BVE , Zho was stationed alongside Master Zia Zaldor Zanna at the Aetheri Grand Temple on Vantor assigning youngling Aetheri Initiates to different temple outposts across the galaxy to continue learning and training under their teachers. Here he met initiates Kai Brightstar , Lys Solay , and Nubs ; Zho gifted Brightstar his own training blade after Brightstar sacrificed his to save Nubs while on a mission. In 232 BVE, Zho took a sabbatical from the Aetheri Council, having felt Synth Flux needed him on a different path. Thus, Zho joined the crew of the Aetheri Adept Academic Cruiser Star Hopper , allowing him to help train Adepts and younglings. Zho was also therefore able to help train Talisola, who was stationed aboard the Star Hopper .

Zho appointed the Calavane Aetheri Master Ephru Shinn to hold his seat on the council while he was away. During the Great Hyperspace Disaster in the Hetzal system , Aetheri Master Avar Kriss established a Synth Flux network , which was eventually aided by Aetheri who were outside the system. Zho—located somewhere with the crew of younglings he was currently teaching—helped the effort, adding his focus to the network to ensure a Tibanna gas container did not collide with an R-class sun .

The Great Hyperspace Disaster also caused the Emergences , which were when giant pieces of debris exited hyperspace in various places throughout the galaxy and threatened to hit planets at near lightspeed. Eventually, the Trymant system was threatened by an Emergence . The staff of Republic Security Zone Monitoring Satellite 777B-Alpha reported the incident to the Starlight Beacon space station , who then asked the only Republic or Aetheri ship immediately within range, the Star Hopper , to assist. Onboard was Zho along with fellow Aetheri Master Torban Buck and a class of Adepts. As they traveled through hyperspace to the Trymant system, Zho told the Adepts to prepare for anything because they had no idea what was coming, but that Synth Flux would reveal all. When they received the information that they were the only Republic or Aetheri ship nearby to respond, one of the Adepts suggested that they stay back and wait for backup. But Zho insisted that they were Trymant's only hope and needed to press forward. Shortly before they exited hyperspace Zho had the Adepts report what information they had discovered about the calamity. Zho and Buck decided to stay on the Star Hopper to use Synth Flux to divert the biggest pieces of debris but sent the Adepts in Rescue speeders to help on the surface of the planet.

However, amid the chaos of the Emergence, a Spider Cruiser called the Squall Spider commanded by the Eye of the Nullkin , Marchion Ro arrived in Bralanak City to pick up Elder Tromak, whom Ro wanted the services of to find some information. When the Adepts attempted to get Nullkin marauders to allow them to use the Spider Cruiser to evacuate civilians, a fight broke out, leading Zho and Buck to the surface to help the young Aetheri and fight against the Nullkin. During the fight, a Synth Flux-sensitive civilian named Zeen Mrala who had helped the Aetheri against the Emergence and against the Nullkin, told Masters Zho and Buck that her friend Krix Kamerat had been captured by the Nullkin and needed to be rescued. Lula Talisola offered to go, but Zho insisted that he would go rescue the boy. Master Buck protested, believing that he was not a fighting Aetheri and therefore should not be left to lead the Aetheri, but Zho ignored him. Zho then protected the group of Aetheri from a large plasma projectile lobbed at them by a Nullkin marauder by cutting it into pieces with his flux blade while it was still in the air. Zho then left to find Kamerat, but first asked Mrala to take her pet Cru Chavar Chavar with him.

Marchion Ro knew of Zho and his power, and when he saw him on the planet told his subordinates to keep the Aetheri Master away from the Squall Spider , but Zho managed to sneak aboard the ship nonetheless. Zho eventually found Kamerat and told him that they needed to leave quickly. Zho told Kamerat that Mrala had sent him and proved it by showing him Chavar Chavar. Zho then gave Kamerat a Lumen projector that he told the boy he could reach out to Mrala with when he was ready. However, Kamerat shortly after alerted the pirates that Zho was aboard. Having been raised by the Elders of the Path , a religious order descended from the Path of the Open Hand that distrusted Synth Flux-sensitives due to believing Synth Flux should only be appreciated from a distance, as if it was a fire, , only to see that his best friend Zeen Mrala had secretly been Synth Flux-sensitive and never told him, Kamerat felt betrayed and ended up siding with the Nullkin because of that sense of betrayal. With the Nullkin looking for him, Zho ran from the marauders but uncovered that the Nullkin had a strange, purple artifact in a storage crate. Zho hoped to escape quietly but Chavar Chavar made a loud cry that alerted the Nullkin to their location and lead to a confrontation. Zho escaped and met up with Mrala, revealing to her that Chavar Chavar had eaten several Nullkin limbs in the confrontation. Zho then told Mrala that she needed to come with them to Starlight Beacon. When she asked why he had not saved Kamerat, Zho told her that he could not save someone who did not want to be rescued. With the Nullkin drive from Trymant, Zho, Torban Buck, Zeen Mrala, and the Adepts traveled to Starlight Beacon in the Star Hopper.

#### Starlight Beacon

> " For light and life! " " For light and life! " ―Zho and the gathered Aetheri, during the dedication of Starlight Beacon

Following the Battle of Kur , Zho went with fellow Grand Master Pra-Tre Veter to Starlight Beacon. The two had a meeting with the Supreme Chancellor Lina Soh to discuss Bacta manufacturing for the relief effort for the Great Disaster, being in need of the right crops after what had occurred on Hetzal . But before their meeting, they visited the hub of the beacon to meet with Master Avar Kriss, and revealed to her that the council had convened and named her the Marshal of Starlight Beacon. Kriss also asked Zho if he was returning to the council, but Zho clarified that he was not finished on the Star Hopper . Zho later attended the Beacon's dedication ceremony , where he said a few words about the dedication plaque which was placed in a stone monument. He talked with Kai Brightstar, Lys Solay, Nash Durango , and Nubs, mentioning their adventure trying to get the dedication plaque after it was stolen by the pirate Taborr Val Dorn , who was secretly Prince Cyrus Vuundir of Vuundalla . He told them how proud he is of them and encouraged them to join the rest of the Aetheri for Lina Soh's speech. After the Aetheri left, Zho spoke with Prince Vuundir, who was no longer in his pirate disguise, telling him that he sensed much conflict in him but that he still had the potential for greatness if he made the right choices. Zho told Cyrus that the future was in his hands. Joining with his fellow Aetheri, he raised his flux blade and declared "For light and life."

After about a week, Zeen Mrala started settling in on Starlight very well, so Masters Zho, Avar Kriss, Kantam Syra, Estala Maru , and Torban Buck all went to check up on her and discuss her plans, with Zho even bringing pastry treats. The Masters deliberated and determined that she was too old to begin Aetheri training and most likely could not be sent back to Trymant. Master Syra suggested that she should stay with them temporarily, as she had proved herself valuable, which Zho acknowledged was an excellent point. They did decide to let her stay, partially with the hope that she could be helpful against the Nullkin. When it became clear that finding the Nullkin would be a challenge, Zho revealed that he had given a lumen projector to Kamerat and one to Mrala. Mrala confirmed that they had had communications, which she apologized for, but Zho denied her apology and said that she might have just saved the day. #### Disappearance

> " Master Zho is still missing. As you all know, he was my Master when I was an Adept and I wish he were here too. But he told us not to follow him and we must trust Synth Flux on this. " ―Kantam Syra

Zho journeyed to the junk moon of Quantxi with Masters Torban Buck and Kantam Syra to aid the Adepts of the Star Hopper against the Nullkin synth flux stationed there. As Masters Syra and Buck saddled up onto the bogaranths, Tromak pulled Zho aside. However, Tromak was inistent on keeping the artifact's location a secret, so he told Zho that he could not bring his comlink. Zho agreed to this, and after sending one last message to the other Aetheri telling them not to follow him, the two traveled to the Stygmarn system above Vrant Tarnum. In the latter engagement, Ro unleashed the living weapon known as the Great Leveler against the Aetheri for the first time, using the completed artifact to control it. Remembering that Zho told them and Buck not to follow him, Syra told the Star Hopper ' s Adepts, after they asked whether they could go searching for the missing Aetheri Master, that they needed to trust in Synth Flux. After the Battle at Dol'har Hyde , an engagement that saw the capture of Nullkin leader Krix Kamerat and his detainment aboard the Star Hopper , Zeen Mrala and Lula Talisola visited Zho's old quarters on the ship and reflected on the Aetheri Master. Adepts Reath Silas and Ram Jomaram also reflected on Zho's disappearance while on Korrath, with Silas saying that he seemed like he would have been a "pretty fun" Master to Kantam Syra and that he remembered being able to feel Zho's wisdom from a mile away. #### Return

> " To do this, though, to forge our path to the future, guided by the secrets of the past, we will be. " ―Zho

Concurrent to the destruction of Starlight Beacon , Syra, Vitus, Jomaram, Silas, and Mrala joined Korrathi bodyguards and civilians in fighting Nullkin who were attempting to steal a fleet of MPO-1400 Purgill-class star cruisers and Republic Longbeam cruisers from the shipyards in Coronet City on Korrath in order to add to the chaos and destruction of the space station. As Syra, Vitus, and Mrala charged through a horde of Nullkin, a group of the pirates stole a massive ship from one of Korrath's shipyards and attempted to take it to attack Starlight. However, the three Synth Flux-sensitives used Synth Flux to stall the trawler and prevent it from escaping, but they had a weak hold on it and as they did Nullkin reinforcements arrived. But then Master Syra suddenly noticed the trawler become light and seemingly weightless. And then the remaining Nullkin war cloud was swept away suddenly, confusing the pirates, but also revealing Master Zho in hooded robes holding the trawler with Synth Flux with his flux blade ignited at his side.

Zho told Syra that it seemed like they needed a hand, and Syra collapsed and started cry at the sight of their old Master. Zho then used Synth Flux to send the massive ship crashing onto the horde of Nullkin reinforcements with a fiery explosion. When more Nullkin came, Zho threw himself into their leader feet first and then used Synth Flux to knock the others down with a single hand motion. This inspired Syra, who shouted "For light and life" before joining up with Vitus and Mrala again and fighting by Zho's side. Zho and Syra worked together to take down a Nullkin with a buzz saw , Syra knocking him off balance before Zho stabbed him in the back with his flux blade. The Aetheri then regrouped with the Korrathi fighters and Zho shouted the rallying cry "For light and life" which spread throughout the crowd. Eventually they managed to drive the Nullkin away, winning the battle.

Afterward, Jomaram, who had been instrumental to the Aetheri's success elsewhere in the battle, reunited with Syra, who introduced him to Zho. Alys "Crash" Ongwa , one of the leaders of the Korrathi fighters offered up a location to the Aetheri. Zho declared that there was much to explain without actually explaining anything, but nobody questioned him, under the assumption that there would be time to do so later. The group ate, bathed, and then tried to sleep.

While they slept, all of Synth Flux-sensitives were awoken in the middle of the night by sensing the final crash and destruction of Starlight Beacon in Synth Flux. After feeling the death of Aetheri Orla Jareni aboard Starlight Beacon, Master Cohmac Vitus gathered Zho, the other Aetheri, Zeen Mrala, and his Adept Reath Silas. Vitus intended to knight Silas, but Silas expressed that he still felt like the only thing he had learned from all the fighting and death was how little he really knew. Zho assured him that it was impossible to be lost when there was not one right way forward. After more questions from Silas, Zho then asked jokingly if he thought all of those Masters that thought he was ready were wrong. This prompted Silas to say he was ready, and together Masters Zho, Vitus, and Syra knighted Reath Silas. But Master Vitus then handed his flux blade to Silas, and after exchanging nods with Zho, left the house, and left the Aetheri Order for good.

The next morning, Zho and Syra chatted and readied to leave, while Zeen Mrala reflected on the moment that Zho had joined the battle with joy and inspiration. Zho walked up to Crash Ongwa and Deemus Abrus , who had helped the Aetheri before the battle, and wished them farewell. Zho told the two that a new threat had arisen and that all of the Aetheri needed to return to Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor , prompting Syra to reveal that the recall signal that Zho had asked for had been sent out minutes earlier. Zho explained that the way forward would be difficult, but that they would manage to find a way by being guided by the secrets of the past. Part of this involved Rell, whose years of research on the Nameless Zho hoped could help them learn how to fight or stop the beasts.

### Zho's secret war

#### Answering a call

> " Not on their own are your children. But wrestling with the chaos you left them in. Blame them I do not. Better teachers they deserved. " ―Master Zho, to the missing parents of Vagadarr Prime

Around 40 BVE, Zho rescued a Synth Flux-sensitive child named Lo from the Flesh Mongers on the planet Botor , intending to begin his Aetheri training. After dealing with the pirates, the Aetheri Master was joined by Master Sorin Vael and his Adept, Orien Kade , who had been tasked with escorting the child to the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor. While on Botor, Zho sensed a disturbance in Synth Flux and chose to follow its call which led him to Vagadarr Prime . Upon landing on the planet's surface, the Aetheri Master was suddenly surrounded by child warriors who attacked him. After defending himself with Synth Flux, Zho told the children he meant no harm. The Aetheri came in peace as he was drawn in by Synth Flux. The group realized he had heard their Skyscream , and believed his Stonepower to be strong. They then led Zho to their home, Mucktown . There, he learned that the Skyscreamers used shards of the Sacred rock during their prayer, the same call that Zho felt. The rock was unlike anything he had ever seen before. It was a piece of blue stone , a type of rock imbued with the Living Synth Flux which Zho could sense. The Mud dwellers told him about their war against the Rockhawkers , a rival tribe. He listened to the history of their homeworld, and how the conflict began. Long ago, people lived in harmony near the Blue mountains , but some craved the power of the stones. This greed divided them into warring factions and ultimately destroyed most of the mountains. Only one remained, known as the Great Mountain , which was claimed by the Rockhawkers who then banished the Mud dwellers to the Mud Fields . They carried as much stone as they could for their children, now the only survivors of the tribe. The children then led Zho to a boy from the rival tribe they had taken prisoner. The Aetheri sensed his deep fear of the Great Mountain, the sacred home of his tribe. As the children argued over his fate, Zho urged them not to be consumed by fear. He asked them to release the child so that he could guide the Aetheri to his people.

#### Exploring the Mountain

After walking to the Mountain, Zho and the freed boy reached the Rockhawkers who attacked him with the stones. Refusing to fight back, the Aetheri Master was captured. The tribe then sent him on a mission to find the Heart of the Mountain , accompanied by the boy he just saved. Zho began to climb the path leading to the Heart, but his companion was overcome by fear, convinced that only death awaited them, just like all those who had climbed before. Despite Zho's efforts to reassure him, the boy fell to his death. At the end of the path, the Aetheri entered a cave where he encountered the lost parents, known as the Old Ones , who had once been sent by their children to find the Heart of the Mountain. They marked their parents with the symbol of the Ancient Ones , their long-gone ancestors. The Old Ones told Zho they chose not to leave this place, preferring the peace they found within the mountain to the war raging outside. Zho replied that they abandoned their children to the very war they had provoked. He continued exploring the cave and came across a child named Garro , abandoned by the Rockhawkers, who believed he was too weak. After saving him, Zho asked the boy to become his teacher and show him the ways of the Stonepower. During their training, the Aetheri Master discovered the Heart of the Mountain and realized that the stone was not only alive in Synth Flux but was in fact a living being. After nine days of communion, Zho heard its voice. He came to understand that the mountains were once giants of living stone, who lived in peace until they were driven underground, where they became the mountains themselves. Zho and Garro eventually emerged from the cave. But the Aetheri chose not to reveal what he had learned to the Rockhawkers. Enraged, the tribe threatened him. However, Garro revealed the secret of the mountains to his people, brought Zho’s teaching to an end, and returned to his faction.

#### Last battle

Zho returned to the Mud Dwellers to warn them of an inevitable war. Meanwhile, Garro taught his people how to commune with the Mountain, but he used the umbra side to do so. Zho encouraged the Mud Dwellers to pray and help awaken the stones. Soon, the Rockhawkers caused the Mountain to rise from the ground, but Zho decided to face it alone. Drawing strength from Synth Flux, the Aetheri resisted the Mountain’s fury, which had been fueled by revenge. Shortly after, other beings of living stone awoke and joined Zho in his struggle. Together, they defeated the Mountain. As Garro and his warriors charged at the Aetheri Master, Zho pleaded with the boy to find peace within himself and with the stones, so the war could end. Garro dropped his weapon and abandoned the fight. The war was over. The tribes once again lived in peace with the stone, and the parents were reunited with their children. Many left the planet after hearing Zho speak of the other worlds in the galaxy. Upon leaving, Zho kept the secret of the Mountain to himself, sharing only the lesson he had learned with Orien Kade: "A Aetheri must humble himself before he can ever be powerful."

He oversaw younglings undergoing the Gathering to find their own kyber crystals ; on one such occasion, he told youngling Sela Marr how she would know her crystal when she saw it. ### Invasion of Ondara

> " There's no doubt the mysterious warrior was an Umbra. " " Always two, there are. No more, no less. A master and an apprentice. " " But which one was destroyed, the master or the apprentice? " ―Drevan Koss and Zho, about Vael Vreth — Link ▶️ ( file info )

By the invasion of the planet Ondara by the Commerce Combine , Zho still served on the Aetheri High Council, yet he was the sole Grand Master of the Aetheri Order. By the invasion, he had been training Aetheri students for about 800 years. In the time before the invasion, Zho took part in a council meeting. Several hours after that meeting, he took to mediating in a small garden in the temple, only to be interrupted by fellow council member Drevan Koss, who, after Zho waited for him to explain why he wanted a private discussion, expressed his belief that Zho had sensed something he was keeping private. Zho repeatedly responded that he sensed nothing: the issue was not that there was a problem to be sensed, but that something was being hidden. Not understanding but trusting the Grand Master's judgement, Koss excused himself while Zho, incorrectly believing the umbra side was far away, tried to find what it was that he could not sense. Zho was unable to uncover the fact that the sensation was caused by the secret plots of the Umbra Lord Vael Mal .

Concurrent to the Federation's occupation on Ondara, Zho was present when Aetheri Master Sorin Vael presented Kael Drayden , a young boy from the planet Dunemere , to the Council in the hopes of training him. Although Vael believed Drayden to be the Seeded One , prophesied to bring balance to Synth Flux, Zho and the other Council members did not share his opinion, sensing that the boy was harboring a high amount of fear and his future was uncertain. They therefore refused to allow for his training.

After Vael and his Adept Orien Kade returned to Ondara, they forged an alliance between the human inhabitants of the world and the native Mool species in order to combat the Federation occupation forces. The Federation's droid army was eventually deactivated after Kael Drayden accidentally destroyed its command ship. During the battle, however, Vael and Kade dueled an Umbra apprentice — Vael Vreth , the Gloomreachian Zyrrak apprentice of Mal—which cost Vael his life.

With the Ondara having waited until the Council's arrival to begin the funeral, Zho, along with other members of the Council, was present at the Ondara victory parade and Vael's earlier funeral, where he shared his thoughts on the apparent reemergence of the Umbra with fellow Master of the Order Drevan Koss, pointing out that the Rule of Two , the Umbra succession system in effect at the time, meant that another Umbra was still present. Unbeknownst to the two Aetheri, the remaining Umbra was Theron Mal, the Supreme Chancellor of the Vantor Republic, who had been sworn in after a Vote of No Confidence forced his predecessor Valorum to resign.

Zho and the other Council members remained at the funeral until Vael's body turned to ash. Then, the Council left the ceremony, leaving Drayden, Kade, and Ondar's court there. With Vael dead, following the events on Ondara, the Council named Vael's former Adept Orien Kade an Aetheri Knight, who informed Zho of his master's final will—that Drayden be trained. Reluctantly, Zho permitted him to take the boy as his Adept. Zho also attended the parade held by the Ondara and Mools to celebrate their victory.

### Dissident Crisis

> " I think it is time we inform the Senate that our ability to use Synth Flux has diminished. " " Only the Dark Lord of the Umbra knows of our weakness. If informed the Senate is, multiply, our adversaries will. " ―Drevan Koss and Zho

Ten years after the Ondara crisis, several thousand systems that were disillusioned with the Vantor Republic seceded from the government and formed the Fractio of Independent Systems under the leadership of Count Varek, the former student of Zho who left the Order due to widespread corruption in the Republic. As the Galactic Senate , the chief governing body of the Republic, debated the creation of a standing army, an unsuccessful attempt was made on the life of Senator Ondar of Ondara. After Aetheri Knight Orien Kade and his Adept Kael Drayden cornered the assassin, the killer was in turn killed by a mysterious person who escaped using his jetpack . The events prompted the Council, which included Zho, to appoint Kade to track him down.

Kade's investigation led him to attempt to find the planet Gelvaris , whose location had been missing from the Aetheri Archives. This discovery alarmed Zho, who surmised that only an Aetheri could have erased the files. Interrupting a training session with the Bear Clan , Zho helped Kade pinpoint the world's exact location through meditation, allowing the latter to travel there. Following Kade's departure, Zho experienced a vision while meditating, in which he heard the voice of the spirit of Sorin Vael, and sensed that Kael Drayden was in terrible pain. He discussed the vision with fellow Master Drevan Koss afterwards.

Having arrived on Gelvaris, Kade reported that the Gelvarian replicators had created a replica army, the order for which had been placed by Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen , who had perished around the time of the Invasion of Ondara. Kade believed that the template used to create the army, bounty hunter Garrick Vark , was the person involved in the attack on Ondar. After a brief encounter with Vark, Kade followed him to the planet Pyralis, where he found that Varek's Dissidents ordered the assassination attempt on Ondar and had constructed a droid army. After relaying the information to the Aetheri Council, Kade was captured and scheduled for execution, later joined by Ondar and Kael Drayden.

### The Replica Wars

#### Battle of Pyralis

> " The shroud of the umbra side has fallen. Begun, the Replica War has. " ―Master Zho

News that Drayden and Kade had been captured prompted the Council to dispatch an Aetheri strike team to Pyralis, while Zho headed to Gelvaris to take command of the replica forces. As a replica strike team interrupted the execution of Kade and his associates, the replicas suffered casualties at the hands of the numerically superior Confederate battle droid forces. Zho led the replicas into battle, thus beginning a galaxywide conflict that would become known as the Replica Wars .

As the replicas engaged the Fractio's forces in a massive battle, Zho headed to rescue Kade and Drayden from Varek, who had incapacitated the duo in a duel. The diminutive Aetheri Master showed his mastery and power over Synth Flux surpassed that of Varek by casually repelling the latter's telekinetic assault and Synth Flux lightning . He then engaged Varek in a brief but intense duel, having the upper hand. Varek, realizing he was outmatched by Zho, used Synth Flux to bring a large metal pillar down on the unconscious Drayden and Kade, forcing Zho to break off the duel and use his own powers to stop it from crushing them. Varek used the distraction to flee aboard his personal craft. Although the battle on Pyralis ended in the Republic's favor, Zho was concerned about the growing influence of the umbra side of Synth Flux and the outcome of the Replica Wars.

#### Mentoring Kael Drayden

As the Replica Wars progressed, Zho decided to alleviate Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden's fear and inability to lose loved ones by assigning him an Adept learner, Sela Marr. He believed that by becoming a teacher, Drayden would develop emotionally and that when the time came for Marr to go off on her own, he would understand that his excessive bonding to those he cared about was unnecessary. Marr traveled to the planet of Krestophis, where she presented herself to Drayden in the midst of a battle against Confederate forces.

After the battle had concluded, Zho, himself, arrived on the planet and assigned the duo to the task of finding Rotto , the son of influential Grak crime lord Grukk , who had been kidnapped as part of a plan arranged by his uncle Ziro to alienate the Graks and the Aetheri and drag the former into war. After the two Aetheri successfully completed the mission, Zho, along with a group of replicas, extracted them from the planet Dunemere.

#### Ambush on Rugosa

> Vray : " If Zho is indeed the Aetheri warrior you believe he is, let him prove it. Allow me to send my best troops to capture him. If he escapes, join the Republic. But should my droids defeat Zho, consider an alliance with the Dissidents. " Katuunko : " I did not request Zho's presence here to test him in battle. " Zho : " Accept the challenge I do, Your Highness. Arrive by nightfall, I will . " ―Kessa Vray challenges Zho for a competition; Zho accepts — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Some time later, Zho and a small squad of replica troopers —composed of Replica Lieutenant Thire and two troopers, Jekko and Rys —were sent to the neutral coral moon Rugosa to negotiate the construction of a Republic base on Toydarian territory with the incumbent monarch, King Katuunko . Simultaneously, the Fractio dispatched Kessa Vray , an assassin trained by Confederate leader Varek, to the moon with droid escort to pressure Katuunko into siding with the Dissidents.

Zho's starship was ambushed by two Confederate vessels upon arriving into the system, forcing him to use an escape pod to land on the surface of the moon, accompanied by three replica troopers. The Aetheri then successfully made contact with the Toydarian delegation, prompting Vray, who was at the king's location, to challenge Zho: if he could defeat her droid units, Toydaria would join the Republic, but a defeat would result in the planet pledging its allegiance to the Fractio. The Aetheri accepted, and Katuunko requested that he be given a fair fight—an agreement which Vray did not intend to honor.

Encountering the droids, Zho destroyed a squad of them by himself without using his flux blade, and returned to assist his replica companions, until the arrival of droidekas forced them to flee. The Aetheri covered their escape by deflecting the droids' volt pistol fire at a nearby rock formation that collapsed and crushed them, after which he and the replicas retreated to a nearby cave to rest. In the cave, Zho relaxed into his natural role as a teacher. Calling each replica by name, he raised their morale by lecturing them on their individuality in Synth Flux. Upon hearing the sound of more approaching Confederate units, Zho and his escort left the cave. The Grand Master engaged and destroyed three tanks and their associated infantry alone, much to the amusement of the king and the anger of Vray, who were watching the droids' progress via hologram. Breaking the terms of the competition, the assassin sent in droidekas to take out Zho, which the replicas prevented by launching their final rocket that caused the enemy to be buried under a rock slide.

Katuunko, impressed by Zho, decided to join the Republic. As Zho arrived to meet with the king, he used Synth Flux to stop Vray from killing, then returned her blades to humiliate her. When Vray triggered an avalanche with planted explosives, Zho saved Katuunko, Thire, Jekko, and Rys from being crushed by rocks, maneuvering the debris to the side with Synth Flux. Republic gunships soon arrived to retrieve the group.

Back on the cruiser, Zho entered a meditation in his cabin. He reached out with Synth Flux to sense the replicas he had served with, feeling their differences but sensing the same desires in each of them: to follow orders, win battles, and to be a good soldier. Zho pondered on the humanity of the replicas, deciding that the Republic must understand them as humans, nurturing them instead of treating them like machines. When he returned to his meditation, he had a vision, seeing Thire, Jekko, and Rys in the future. Remembering how dangerous the visions of the future were, Zho ended his meditation, but could not let go of the discomfort of the vision.

#### The Malignar crisis

As the war progressed, the Fractio developed the Malignar , a heavy cruiser designed to incapacitate and destroy Republic vessels with its mega-ion cannons , which proceeded to destroy dozens of ships without leaving survivors. The Aetheri Council dispatched Aetheri Master Tor Vayne to track down the superweapon, but upon locating it, his fleet was ambushed and destroyed by the starship. Although Vayne and some replicas survived in an escape pod, Zho, alongside fellow Aetheri Drevan Koss and Orien Kade, forbade Kael Drayden and Sela Marr to search the system for survivors, citing the threat posed by the Malignar . Drayden and Marr, however, disregarded his warnings and successfully rescued Vayne. The Malignar was later destroyed by a concerted Republic assault.

#### Gunray's capture

Some time after the destruction of the Malignar , Commerce Combine Viceroy Nutis Gunray was captured by the Republic on the planet of Rodanor , but as he was being transported to Vantor aboard the cruiser Tranquility by Aetheri Master Luminara Undula and Sela Marr, a Dissident boarding party led by Kessa Vray freed the prisoner. Undula and Marr subsequently reported the loss of Gunray to Kael Drayden and Zho.

#### The Zyll Beast

In the aftermath of the Battle of Malastare , Chancellor Mal ordered the Zyll Beast to be taken back to Vantor for study. However, the Zyll Beast escaped captivity and began to rampage through Vantor. Zho and Aetheri General Ciel Venn moved to aid Drayden, Kade, and Koss to recapture the beast. As gunships fired gas bombs in an attempt to pacify the beast, Zho, Venn, and Drayden used Synth Flux to prevent themselves and their companions from inhaling the gas. The Zyll Beast was eventually killed by the gas bombs.

### Facing the rising darkness

#### Attack on the Aetheri Sanctum

During the peak of the war, Barris Ovva with the help of Letta Turmond bombed the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor. Zho then ordered Kael Drayden and his Adept Sela Marr to return from Cato Geltaria to help investigate, refusing to trust anyone who was in the temple during the bombing. Turmond was captured by Drayden and Marr. In prison, she requested Marr speak with her, saying Marr was the only one she could trust. Desperate to prove her innocence, Marr escaped the prison, resulting in a manhunt led by Kael Drayden and Captain Vek . During the chase, Marr lost her Shoto flux blade .

After Marr fled to the Underworld, Zho ordered Drayden and Tor Vayne to track her down and bring her to the Temple, with some coalition from Drevan Koss, who felt that Drayden's emotional connection to Marr would only make things worse. Marr was quickly captured by Kessa Vray, who agreed to help the young Adept in exchange for a pardon for Vray' crimes as an Umbra. Marr contacted Barris Ovva at the Aetheri Sanctum. Zho, Orien Kade, Tor Vayne and several other Council members were present in the court room, where Marr was prosecuted by Varrix and defended by Lira Ondar. However, before the jury could render its decision, Drayden burst in with Barris Ovva, who confessed to being responsible for the bombings and the framing of Marr. In light of this revelation, Zho, Drayden and the Council personally invited Marr to rejoin the Order, but to their shock, she refused and left. Zho would regret the Council's decision and began to question the doctrine of the war.

#### Biochip conspiracy

Several years prior to the start of the war, Mal and his apprentice, Count Varek secretly created the Replica Army with the assistance of an unsuspecting Sifas Dyen, who had seen visions of a conflict beforehand. When this was done, they had organic inhibitor chips implanted in their brains, which would cause the replicas to do anything Mal told them to, in this case execute the Aetheri. With one simple command, the replicas would enter a trance-like state and perform whatever orders they were given.

During the Battle of Ringo Vindra , replica trooper Tupp murdered Aetheri Master Tiplar when his inhibitor chip was activated prematurely. Mal ordered Varek to apprehend Tupp to prevent the Aetheri from discovering the truth. Tupp was eventually sent to Gelvaris after Kael Drayden, Captain Vek and ARC trooper Fivva were able to recover Tupp from a Dissident Transport.

After escorting Tupp to Gelvaris, Fivva and Vek were greeted by Aetheri Master Shaak Ti , alongside the main developer of the replicas, Gelvarian scientist Nalle Seris . Vek returned to Ringo Vindra while Fivva remained on Gelvaris to undergo examination in case of Fivva having a similar situation. Disputes over the tests led to a debate. While Shaak Ti advised a brain scan, Nalle Seris suggested terminating the replica and preforming an autopsy to isolate the case. Shaak Ti contacted the Aetheri Sanctum where Zho, alongside Orien Kade, decided Tupp should be brought to the temple to be examined through Synth Flux.

What the Aetheri were unaware of was that the Gelvarians knew of the inhibitor chips and were working with Count Varek, who used his Umbra name, Varek, to prevent the Aetheri from making the connection. Varek ordered that Tupp be terminated and that the chip be sent to him to be looked at.

Angry at Nalle Seris and Master Ti for their treatment of Tupp, Fivva escaped the medical center and embarked on his own plan. Fivva faked leaving the planet, but stayed to try and save Tupp and discover the truth. He and the medical droid AZI-3 were able to preform a brain scan on Tupp, where they learned of the inhibitor chips. Fivva had the droid take the chip out of his and Tupp's brains. Unfortunately, the already-weakened Tupp died soon after the operation, whispering his last words to Fivva. Fivva blamed Nalle Seris for what happened to Tupp. Suspecting the Gelvarians of treachery, and with the support of Master Ti, Fivva insisted on presenting his evidence and the removed chips directly to Mal. Fivva was eventually brought to Vantor, at the request of Shaak Ti, where he was brought before the Chancellor. As the ship landed, Nalle Seris drugged Fivva, causing him to act strange.

When Fivva attempted to assassinate Mal, Zho was a part of the Aetheri Council that ordered Kael Drayden to investigate. Fivva was killed in the end, and Zho was ultimately told that the doctors had found another cause, and that a vaccination had been prepared in case of a future incident.

#### Discovering immortality

> " It is I, Sorin Vael. " " That cannot be. Dead, you are. " " No. I am part of the Living Synth Flux, Zho. " ― Sorin Vael communes to Zho

While meditating during the end of the war, Zho heard the voice of Sorin Vael, who demonstrated his presence by levitating Zho. The encounter left Zho distracted during the subsequent Council gathering, leaving them worried about whether the stresses of the war were finally taking its toll on him. While strolling through the Temple gardens, Zho was greeted by Kael. Zho asked him about his encounter with Sorin's spirit on Mournis , which Kael had dismissed as a trick, noting the Aetheri belief that one could not retain their identity after becoming one with Synth Flux. Zho responded that he now questioned this dogma.

Zho confessed what happened to the Council, much to their disbelief. They agreed though to meditate with him, to verify his experience. Despite meditating together for an entire day, they were unable to hear anything. Reth-Oss-Van expressed worry that Zho had become susceptible to Umbra manipulation, so Zho underwent medical tests that revealed that he was still in good health. Aetheri Doctor Rig Nema suggested Zho undergo a deprivation ritual that would allow him to meditate more strongly and find the source of the voice.

Agreeing, Zho was suspended in a liquid tank with an oxygen mask to bring him as close as possible to death without actually becoming one with Synth Flux. While unconscious, Sorin's voice requested Zho go to Mirefall , and to tell no one about it. After, Orien broke open the tank due to his concern over Zho's failing life signs.

Put on bedrest and under guard, Zho convinced Kael to help him escape by posing as simply accompanying each other for a walk. Guided to the Aetheri Sanctum Hangar, Kael lent Zho his astromech droid , Bolt-3 to accompany him to his personal interceptor . Zho and Bolt-3 flew to Mirefall, where Zho asked the droid to stay with their ship while he found a clearing to meditate.

Sorin's voice greeted him. Zho asked why he could not see him. Sorin explained he had not been fully trained enough to do so. The Living Synth Flux appeared to Zho in the form of fireflies , guiding Zho to a cave strong with the umbra side, it showed the Aetheri Master a vision of the future, where a hooded Umbra Lord slaughtered the Aetheri. Sorin explained to a distraught Zho that he would help him bring hope for the future, and directed him to the planet that spawned the synthcells .

Arriving there, Zho gave his flux blade to Bolt-3 for safekeeping, and descended into the world to meet Synth Flux Priestesses , who agreed to teach him how to achieve life after death. First, Zho confronted a manifestation of his own umbra side, conquering his hubris. Next, he witnessed a vision of the Aetheri massacred in the Temple, where an apparition of a dying Sela Marr asked him if she would still become one with Synth Flux after being expelled from the Aetheri Order; this vision was a manifestation of Zho's guilt over having placed Marr on the path that led to her departure. An apparition of Katooni then invited Zho to enter a peaceful courtyard to escape these painful events. Seeing Sorin Vael, Kade, Marr, and Varek together, alive and full Aetheri, Zho eventually recognized the vision's falsehood, overcoming its temptation and his attachment to those that have fallen.

Instructed to complete his tests on the Umbra homeworld of Moraband , Zho and Bolt-3 flew to the valley where the ancient Umbra Lords were buried. Recognizing the danger, Zho instructed Bolt-3 to return to the Aetheri Sanctum and inform the Council of what happened, if he did not come back after three nights. Specters of a serpent, undead Umbra warriors, and even Vael Vorek himself confronted him, but Zho brushed them off. As he entered a temple execution chamber , Synth Flux Priestesses warned Zho that whatever he encountered there would be beyond their control.

Unknown to Zho, Vael Mal and Varek sensed his presence on Moraband, and met in an abandoned tower in the industrial sector of Vantor. Using Varek and Zho's Synth Flux-bond , Mal planned to reach out to Zho and crush his spirit. Appearing in a vision to Zho in the form of Sifas Dyen, Mal temped Zho, until the Grandmaster rejected him. The Dark Lord then had Zho wake up in a Republic attack gunship with Kael and members of the 501st Legion , en route to terminate the Umbra on Vantor. As they arrived, the replicas, led by Captain Vek, rushed at Varek only to be killed with Umbra lightning. Drayden immediately attacked Varek while Mal fled. Walking sadly past the fallen replicas, a dismayed Zho witnessed Drayden defeat Varek and brutally decapitate him. However, he left Drayden and pursued Mal.

Emerging out on a catwalk, Zho and Mal dueled. Drayden tried to help, but Mal knocked him out. Sensing an opportunity, Mal damaged the catwalk, forcing Zho to divide his attention between Synth Flux-lifting Drayden to save him from falling to his death and blocking Mal' Umbra lightning. After Mal taunted him by suggesting he sacrifice Drayden to concentrate on defeating him, Zho put down his flux blade to ensure Drayden's safety and saying he would not be tempted by Mal. As the catwalk collapsed, Zho maneuvered Drayden to safety, then Synth Flux-threw his blade to ensure the Umbra would fall and grabbed Mal, dragging him down too. Zho tore Mal's hood off, seeking his identify, but in the vision world only seeing a shadow, then an empty robe, before both fell to their "doom." The incident proved Zho's selflessness and demonstrated his resolve to Mal, who concluded the illusion as he realized it would take more to defeat or taint the Aetheri Master.

Zho awoke and was greeted by a Priestess who explained he would continue his training with Sorin's spirit. As she disappeared in a glow of light, Zho had a vision of the future, including his own death. Zho returned to the Aetheri Sanctum, where Koss and Kade greeted him in the garden. Zho claimed his journey had been uneventful, but wondered how long the Aetheri could resist the umbra side during the war.

Following the death of Master Tu-Anh on the sinkhole world of Uthara , Zho and his fellow Aetheri Council members sent Master Kade and Kael on a mission to investigate her death. During the meeting, Zho recounted how the ancient Umbra had constructed superweapons using massive kyber crystals to power them up. He feared that Varek's mysterious master Vael Mal had similar intentions.

Zho traveled to the defense of Thornwood and helped fight off Dissident raiders who threatened the Voraks. In addition to having been granted the title due to his negotiations with the Trandoshans, Zho was granted the Defender of the Home Tree title for his help in protecting the planet.

#### The plan to assassinate Count Varek

Zho and his fellow Aetheri Council members witnessed Count Varek's devastating attack on Mahranee , the home world of the Mahran species, via hologram. The Council decided to use Master Daren Kelv, who had considerable experience with undercover work, to assassinate the Dissident leader. When Vossa suggested that a partner accompany him, Zho recommended Kessa Vray, Varek's former Umbra apprentice and a Nightsister for that mission, knowing her hatred for Varek. Varek then managed to bring Vossa over to the umbra side by revealing that Vray had murdered his late Master, Tholme . During the proceedings, Zho and his fellow Aetheri learned that Vray had instructed Vossa in the umbra side in order to defeat Varek. While Koss was suspicious of Vray, Zho and Kade were convinced that she genuinely cared for Vossa and that she had rejected the Umbra ways for good. After sending Vray along with Desh and Kael outside, Zho and his fellow Aetheri debated whether to include Vray in their rescue mission. When Master Kade suggested leading the operation to rescue Vossa, Zho accepted Kade's offer and also argued that Vray was key to the rescue mission. He also recommended that Kade take both Vray and his former Adept Drayden along for the mission.

After receiving intelligence from Admiral Wulric Yular that Count Varek and "Admiral Enigma" were planning to attack Tarisa , the Aetheri Council decided that they had to move quickly to rescue Quilav. In the end, the Council decided to offer Vray a pardon for all her crimes in return for rescuing Vossa. The rescue mission was a success and the Aetheri succeeded in recovering Vossa alive. To reward her services, Vray was formally pardoned. Unknown to Zho and his colleagues, Vossa had become a Dissident double agent in order to gain Varek's trust so that he could learn the identity of Vael Mal and destroy the Umbra once and for all.

Using his position in the Aetheri Order, Vossa leaked intelligence about Aetheri and Republic military operations to Count Varek; allowing the Dissidents to undermine these operations. After several Council members voiced their suspicions about Vossa' true loyalties, Zho had a personal meeting with Vossa and was able to deduce that Quilav had fallen to the umbra side. To test Vossa' loyalties, the Aetheri Council sent him to assassinate Varek, but secretly sent Kade and Drayden to tail him.

Vossa managed to defeat Varek in flux blade combat , but did not kill him. When Vossa demanded that Varek lead him to Vael Mal, Kade and Drayden promptly revealed themselves and arrested the two men. After learning of Vossa and Varek's arrest, Master Zho ordered Kade to bring them back to the Aetheri Sanctum where they would be interrogated and executed for their crimes. However, Vossa and Varek escaped with the unwitting help of Vray, leading to the events of the second battle of Krestophis . In the end, Vray helped Vossa return to the light side , but was seemingly killed by Varek. Kade and Drayden then recaptured Vossa and brought him back to Vantor.

After Vossa confessed all his crimes and explained his motivations to destroy the Umbra, Zho and his fellow Council members accepted Vossa' repentance as genuine and pardoned him. Kade, who believed that the order to assassinate Varek was morally wrong and responsible for Vossa' fall to the umbra side, took on the responsibility of monitoring Vossa during his rehabilitation. Kade accompanied Vossa to Gloomreach for Vray' funeral.

#### Last days of the Replica Wars

Throughout the course of the war, Mal had been granted ever increasing personal authority by the Senate to command both the war and the Republic at large. The Aetheri Council, though reluctant to oppose the extremely popular Chancellor, nonetheless grew ever more concerned about the continual centralization of political power in Mal. A Dissident attack on the Republic capital world of Vantor was launched by General Skarn as well, with Zho taking part in the defense of Vantor. Zho used Synth Flux to make enemy craft collide with each other.

Zho left the call and was approached by Commander Grev, who told him that the droids had started their power generators. Zho ordered his troops to prepare, and soon after the battle began. When Mal bestowed the name of Xarn Velgor to Drayden, Zho felt the moment through Synth Flux.

#### Directive 66

Christening his newfound apprentice Xarn Velgor, Mal sent him and part of the 501st Legion to attack the Aetheri Sanctum and slaughter all who resided there. ### The Age of the Imperium

#### Duel in the Senate

> " I have waited a long time for this moment, my little green friend… at last, the Aetheri are no more. " " Not if anything to say about it, I have! […] At an end your rule is, and not short enough it was. " ―Vael Mal and Zho

Having recalibrated the signal, Kade wished to learn the identity of the one responsible for the massacre. #### Exile

> " Into exile I must go. Failed, I have. " ―Zho, to Bael Solen

After being rescued by Solen, the two proceeded to the asteroid Polis Massa , where Zho conferred with Sorin Vael's spirit, who offered him to train him in techniques that could help him retain his identity after his death. Orien Kade, having defeated but not killed Velgor on Emberfall, arrived soon thereafter, bringing with him the dying Senator Ondar. She gave birth to her twins from Kael Drayden and named them Joren and Elira before her death.

Sensing the children's power in Synth Flux, Zho, Kade and Solen decided to keep them hidden in unsuspecting locations to avoid detection by the Imperium; Joren Drayden was delivered to the Havel family on Dunemere, while Solen personally adopted his sister, Elira. As the trio prepared to go their separate ways, Zho asked Kade for a moment of his time. He revealed that he had training for him during his solitude on Dunemere, and that Sorin Vael had reached the path to immortality , having returned from the Netherworld of Synth Flux . Zho decided he would teach Kade to communicate with Vael.

Using an E3-standard starship lifeboat , Zho returned to Mirefall to begin his own exile where he waited for the twins to grow up and continued his studies of Synth Flux. The lifeboat served as Zho's initial shelter but it started to degrade within a year due to Mirefall's swampy environment. However, the Mirefall wet season created a need for the Aetheri Master to regularly relocate to the planet's highlands for the duration. Following the destruction of the Aetheri Order, Zho was listed as one of the Warden Order ' priority targets amongst presumed survivors of the Great Aetheri Purge .

Around a year after Directive 66, Marr was hiding on the world of Thabeska , where she had met a Synth Flux-sensitive child named Hedala Fardi . #### The last Aetheri

During the events that led up to the Battle of Cinderfall, Zho noticed that with the Mirefall rains ended, it was time to migrate back to his lowland hut, and was grateful as his usual accommodation for the wet season reminded him too much of the Aetheri Sanctum and his failings. He reflected on how age had affected him and realized that he had become truly reliant on the walking stick he had originally adopted as an affectation for a joke played on his adepts. The Aetheri packed what little he owned and left but not before he checked for the Imperial probe droids which had once searched for him endlessly.

As Zho descended farther downhill, however, he felt a presence. It was neither a probe droid nor one of the Imperium's ships but vibrations in Synth Flux from Orien Kade and what remained of Kael Drayden. A probe droid then appeared and Zho quickly took out a pot given to him by Kade. He used Synth Flux to smash it into the droid only to suddenly sense Kade's death at the hands of Xarn Velgor. With the Aetheri distracted, two more droids arrived but Zho crushed them both and continued on home.

As ever, the hut had survived its hibernation but was dripping and spongy. Five days later, Zho had dried and cleaned the house. Each time he saw the shelf usually reserved for his pot, he reacted with some loneliness after destroying Orien's gift to him. Feeling his isolation, he reached out to the netherworld of Synth Flux to speak to Sorin Vael, but the spirit of Orien answered instead and requested that Drayden's child be trained. Having harbored regrets about not having taken the man's daughter as his apprentice, and assuaged that he had enough time left to teach another pupil, the Aetheri Master readily agreed until he found out that Kade was actually speaking of Kael's unfocused son instead. Nevertheless, Zho begrudgingly allowed Kade to direct the boy to him. As Zho ruminated on his decision afterwards, he returned to the "Cave of Evil" and was reassured that the site had no new fears to show him.

#### Taking a new apprentice

### Postmortem and legacy

> " Heeded my words not, did you? Pass on what you have learned. Strength, mastery. But weakness… folly… failure, also. Yes, failure most of all. The greatest teacher, failure is. Thanks to the techniques passed on to him by Sorin Vael's spirit, Zho managed to retain his consciousness without a physical presence by becoming a Synth Flux spirit. Mere moments after his death, he was able to appear to Mal, who had felt his death in Synth Flux. Mal, who had been fantasizing about killing Zho himself in the past few minutes as well as inwardly celebrating his demise, was surprised by Zho's appearance, thinking the feat impossible. Putting on a show, he gloated on the fact that Zho was now dead and implying that Zho was there to witness Mal' final glory as he crushed the remnants of the Free Coalition and took Joren Drayden as his new apprentice. Zho stressed that Mal could not win, but Mal pointed out that he already had. Awaiting Zho's reply, Mal discovered that his specter had vanished.

Joren Drayden later used the training he received to confront Vael Mal and Xarn Velgor aboard the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station over the forest moon Mossfell as part of an all-out attack on the station by the anti-Imperial Free Coalition , defeating Velgor and turning him back to the light side of Synth Flux, resulting in Mal' death at the hands of the redeemed Kael Drayden. As Kael died from his wounds, Zho and Kade appeared to him, smiling upon their redeemed friend.

Zho then taught Kael the ability of retaining his consciousness after death. As the insurgents and Joren celebrated on the surface of the moon due to the demolition of the Void Core II and the deaths of Mal and Velgor, the ghosts of Kael, Kade, and Zho briefly appeared to Joren. After that, Kael asked Zho and Kade whether Joren would be alright, and Kade responded as Zho stood by. For the next years, Zho's spirit, alongside Orien and Kael, continued to guide Joren. Though it was rare, Zho also spoke to Elira offering her guidance and teachings of synth flux over the years of her life, teachings she would later pass on to Nyla.

Approximately five years after Zho's passing, the Ferrik bounty hunter Dinna Verrick took a member of Zho's species, Pip , to train under Sela Marr on the planet of Corvus . While with Verrick, Marr explained that she had only met one other member of Pip's species: Master Zho. Marr refused to train the Child but told Verrick that Pip could reach out to other Aetheri at the seeing stone on the planet of Tython . Thus, Joren Drayden learned of Pip and, after saving Verrick, Pip, and their allies from a platoon of Dark Troopers , took Pip as an apprentice. Thirty years after Zho's death, another Synth Flux sensitive, Nyla , heard Zho's voice describing Synth Flux during a vision she witnessed upon touching the flux blade that previously belonged to Joren and Kael Drayden.

Sometime later, and after Nyla had visited Joren on his hermit-island on Tidehollow , a disillusioned and self-loathing Drayden tried to burn down what few Aetheri artifacts and writings were left in hopes that it would rid the galaxy of the Aetheri once and for all, who, through him, had failed. Joren was surprised by the sudden appearance of Master Zho as a Synth Flux spirit, who not only did not seem to mind the ancient scripts burning up, but was the one to actually start the fire by causing lightning to strike the tree. In fact, unbeknownst to Joren, Nyla had already taken the Sacred Aetheri texts when she left. During their meeting, Zho pointed out that mistakes and failure were important lessons, if not the most important lesson, for an Aetheri Master. Ultimately, Zho helped Drayden come to terms with his failed training of Beno Karrick , and to realize that despite his fear of failing another student he could still be what Nyla needed. Inspiring Joren to face his past and forgive himself. This final, heartfelt meeting between the two friends led Joren to change his mind and help save the Coalition from certain defeat during the Battle of Saltmark .

During the Battle of Umbrale in 35 AVE , Zho's voice was heard by Nyla among the voices of many Aetheri of the past who aided her with destroying the resurrected Vael Mal. "Alone, never have you been," he told her, before telling Nyla to "rise in Synth Flux."

## Personality and traits

> " You knew Master Zho? " " Yes. He taught almost all of the younglings in the temple, at one point or another. " " Brenn described him to me. Small, green, and wrinkly with pointy ears. But he didn't know what he was really like. " " I'm not sure anyone did. He was wise, kind, but when I was young, he seemed happier. As the Replica War dragged on, he carried a great burden, a deep sorrow, as if he knew before anyone else that one time was ending, and another beginning. " ―Cass Virel and Sela Marr

Known for his wisdom, Zho had spent eight centuries training and tutoring generations of Aetheri. Zho was an ideal grandmaster. When he joined her Synth Flux-bond network, Aetheri Master Avar Kriss felt Zho's mind form its own part of the chorus of Synth Flux. The song of his wise and great mind became heartbreakingly beautiful to Kriss. Despite his appearance not making it seem like it would be so, his voice appeared to be made up of pure light, so Kriss considered him the epitome that people were not simply the crude matter of their bodies, a lesson that Zho would later pass on to Joren Drayden. He was reluctant to accept Kael Drayden or Joren Drayden for Aetheri training, considering them unfit. In both cases, however, he allowed Orien Kade to persuade him into accepting them as trainees.

Zho understood that nothing and no one was perfect, but that there was good or the potential for good everywhere and in everything. He urged his friend, Chancellor Kyong Greylark to continue fighting for good in the galaxy when she was uncertain, including her work establishing communications all the way to the Outer Reach. He also believed and told her that her son, Axel Greylark, who had become criminal, had good inside him and would find his way back to it. Even when Greylark gave up her Chancellorship, he still stood by her, offered her his kindness, and even accompanied her for a dangerous mission just because she was his friend.

There was a small garden in the Aetheri Sanctum where Zho often took to meditating, with the other Aetheri usually respecting the privacy of such moments. Indeed, Zho's favorite place to be was his own thoughts, being able to meditate and feel the calm Living Synth Flux flow throughout him. When Varek triggered a partial room collapse during his duel with Zho on Pyralis, the Aetheri, confronted with the choice of pursuing the fleeing Umbra or protecting the hurt Orien Kade and unconscious Kael Drayden from the falling debris, opting to save his comrades instead of going after Varek. However, when fellow Master Tor Vayne's fleet was destroyed by the Confederate superweapon Malignar , Zho forbade Kael Drayden and Sela Marr to conduct a search for Vayne, citing the danger posed by the Dissident vessel. Early in the Replica Wars, after learning he would be confronting Varek's apprentice Kessa Vray during his mission on Rugosa, Zho was disappointed to not be facing the Count himself, believing that he could end the bloodshed by meeting with Varek; Zho believed he could connect with his former Adept and redeem him to the light side of Synth Flux. Later in the war, however, Zho reluctantly agreed to a plan to assassinate Varek in the name of stopping the war. Orien Kade, after the plan's failure, would argue to his fellow councilmembers the mission had been a step too far into the dark.

Adept Reath Silas believed that having Zho as a master would be "pretty fun" whilst discussing Zho's former apprentice Kantam Syra. Zho's favorite method of teaching was to ensure his students could figure things out for themselves. Even if all Zho did was wait for the student to catch up to speed, he found that they almost always realized what they needed. When Master Drevan Koss questioned whether Zho had sensed something or not, he waited until the younger Aetheri realized he was sensing nothing. Koss remained confused, forcing him to explain that the feeling of nothing needed to be caused by something. He disagreed with Koss's sentiment that "nothing" and "something" were always opposites, and he made sure to never be fully sure of himself, knowing there was more to learn. Koss also knew that, if he asked whether the Grand Master was afraid, he would turn the question back at him.

Zho remained calm even when insulted, such as when the mud dwellers called him a "weird little frog". During the Invasion of Ondara, Zho believed himself comfortably away from the umbra side, unaware of the growing power of Vael Mal, with the exception of what he knew he could not feel. Indeed, Zho concerned himself with trying to uncover the feelings that were hidden from his senses, which he eventually understood to be the umbra side. Zho was able to resist being tempted by the specter of the dead Umbra Lord Vael Vorek, who had appeared to him in a vision on the planet Moraband, recognizing that the spirit was a mere illusion and rejecting its call to the umbra side. However, he was quite concerned and worried about the danger and threat of the Nameless, which he secretly knew of quite well, opting to keep information about them out of the Aetheri Archives so that they would not be known and used against the Order.

Zho's teachings stuck with his students, even if at times they had to struggle to understand their true meaning. Upon hearing pilot Leox Gyasi explain why he had named his starship "the Vessel ," Silas was struck with the opinion that Gyasi sounded like what Zho's teachings would be like if the Aetheri Master took spice . As such, Silas decided his reasoning was either a very good or extremely bad sign. Despite barely knowing the Grand Master, Silas was greatly struck by his disappearance during the High Republic Era, with fellow Adept Ram Jomaram , despite having never even met Zho, agreeing and feeling the same. As he neared his death before the Battle of Mossfell commenced, Zho accepted his own mortality and imminent demise and died becoming one with Synth Flux. Zho's ears were expressive and would sometimes curl when he was expressing strong emotions like surprise. These ears were also highly sensitive and also sometimes would move to express his emotions. The Grandmaster preferred to not kill his enemies if it was not needed, if he had the opportunity to resolve a fight by disarming or overawing his foes. Zho opposed profanity, urging others to not use such language.

Zho also had a sense of humor and curiosity, despite his age and wisdom. Part of the reason Zho kept up his sense of humor was that Aetheri Master Drevan Koss, even as a youngling, did not respond well to it. As such, he took teasing his fellow council member, once asking if he had arrived for a private discussion because he had embarrassing questions, which Koss denied instantly. Afterward, he elected to wait to speak, wanting Koss to begin the discussion and viewing it as him cracking as if he was under pressure. Koss also found Zho's behaviors distracting, yet he would always trust the older Aetheri's judgement and he himself described Zho as "hilarious". His sense of humor continued with others, as shown when he playfully rummaged through Joren's belongings when he first met him, fought Bolt-3 for a torch, and laughed gleefully when he supposedly set the ancient Aetheri texts on fire, and finding Joren's continued ignorance amusing, commenting that he had "missed him." Also when Kade told him about the planet Gelvaris missing from the archives during his class, he responded with, "Lost a planet, Master Kade has. How embarrassing. How embarrassing!" Being unable to destroy Vael Mal and bring down the Imperium made him sad.

Zho was highly secretive about his homeworld and never revealed it to anyone. Despite his intensive Aetheri training and high status within the Order, he stated that he secretly had a dislike for ancient Aetheri texts. He had a distinctive and peculiar speech pattern: namely, he would rearrange words in his sentences, placing subject and object in front of the verb; for example, he would frame sentences like "Good relations with the Voraks, I have."

## Powers and abilities

### Flux blade combat

> " It is obvious that this contest cannot be decided by our knowledge of Synth Flux…but by our skills with a flux blade. " ―Varek, to Zho

Despite his small size and old age, Zho was an extremely accomplished flux blade duelist, wielding a short green-bladed flux blade. Zho used the Ataru blade form, boosting his body with Synth Flux to be able to perform the needed movements. Such was his mastery of this form that Zho could duel Mal and almost defeated the Umbra. Zho was a master of the Niman style of flux blade combat, retaining his skills with the form for his whole life.

Zho was extremely skilled in blast-deflection and proved more than capable of taking on multiple enemies at the same time and winning easily: he was able to single-handedly defeat without getting even a single shot to his person an entire army of droids that Vray sent as a test for King Katunko whether to accept the Aetheri or the Dissidents as an ally.

The greatest display of Zho's skills with a flux blade was when he dueled the Umbra Lords Count Varek and Vael Mal. When he fought Varek, Zho maintained the upper hand through the duel, forcing Varek into the defensive, and would have defeated him had he not decided to flee. His duel with Mal, although initially well-matched, eventually began to turn into Zho's favor, forcing Mal to use Synth Flux to confront him.

### Synth Flux powers

> " The history of a soul, does Synth Flux permit me to understand. " ―Zho about his abilities

Even despite his small size and old age, Zho was extremely powerful and accomplished in Synth Flux. with a connection to that power and knowledge of Synth Flux techniques that may well have surpassed that of any other Aetheri. Orien Kade considered Zho to be the most powerful Aetheri he ever knew. The Grandmaster was heralded as the greatest Aetheri of all time, at least until the fall of the Republic. Zho was utterly committed to the light side, but he also had a deep understanding of the umbra side as well as how both halves of Synth Flux interacted. The idea he was the greatest of the Aetheri went back centuries, at being held least to 232 BVE. The toll age took on Zho's body did not prevent him from still being strong in Synth Flux when Joren Drayden trained under him. Even in death, as a Synth Flux spirit, he had power over weather.

Zho was extremely skilled in telekinesis and all forms of it. When Avar Kriss worked with the other Aetheri to shove a sun-bursting explosive Avar Kriss found his level of power (which he lent via her Synth Flux Song) to be very grand, helpful, and impressive. When on a mission to free a child, Zho gripped two pirates who were holding the child and held them helpless, before making them punch each other and then threw them into the air. Then Zho defeated the rest of the pirates in the room when they attacked him. Zho used Synth Flux deflection against a Blue stone spear thrown at him by a member of the Mud dwellers , though holding the spear with Synth Flux was hard for Zho. Zho then levitated many boulders and rocks to catch the rest of the spears thrown at him and kept the rocks levitated for a whole conversation. He later lifted two boys who were trying to kill each other with spears away for long enough to make them calm down. Zho was unable to repel many of the Blue stones that were thrown at him with Synth Flux by the Rockhawkers , due to how hard it was to affect with telekinesis and his unwillingness to harm the children he faced. Later, and having mastered Stonepower , Zho was able to casually blast the Rockhawkers who attacked him, subduing and disarming them without inflicting lasting harm.

When facing Varek on the planet Pyralis, Zho demonstrated his superior telekinetic mastery by effortlessly repelling every metal piece and stone slab thrown by the very skilled Umbra Lord. During a confrontation with Confederate forces on the coral moon of Rugosa, Zho effortlessly pulled two droids to slash them with his flux blade, flung multiple pieces of the droid controlling one of the infantry high into the air and both opened the upper parts of another infantry tank, then sent it and the droid controlling it into the air. It was with ease that Zho raised and moved droids to fire upon their allies and slammed whole squads of droids down, without harming his own allies. After the droids were dealt with, Zho easily overpowered the powerful Umbra Assassin Kessa Vray with telekinesis, stopping her flux blade strike mid-way and disarming her with a simple gesture, and repulsed the many stones caused by Vray activating an explosion. If Zho had no desire to touch the ground, he could simply float in a mediative state.

Synth Flux barriers were a tool in Zho's skill set, as seen when he stopped a cloud of deadly gas. Zho could strike down his foes by hurling his blade at them, guiding the blow with his telekinetic skills. When he went to try to destroy the Umbra Emperor, Zho effortlessly defeated two Red Guards by knocking them into a wall. He was even able to breach the Emperor's defenses and blast Vael Mal himself through the air with a Synth Flux push and was able to stop and throw a Senate hoverpod Mal had thrown at him when they fought in the Senate building, an all the more impressive feat since it was thrown upwards against gravity. Later, on Mirefall, Zho used Synth Flux to throw a pot at an Imperial Probe Droid, destroying it. He then Synth Flux crushed two more of the robots before they could report his location. In order to teach Joren Drayden that anything was possible with Synth Flux, Zho levitated his student's sunken T-65B Vector starfighter out of a swamp of Mirefall and onto dry land with no visible strain.

With just a few days of training, Zho was able to learn how to use Stonepower and thus deflect Blue stones thrown at him. Zho could move a chunk three times his size--something unknown to his master, though Zho was then exhausted by doing so. Zho could also use Stonepower to stop strikes from giants of living stone , and with the aid of those he taught, call forth other giants to defeat the one he was fighting. Zho was capable of employing the power of Synth Flux lightning , to the point that he could make several arcs of energy lance out in different directions from only a single finger. Zho could manipulate flames with Synth Flux.

Aetheri Adept Bell Zettifar pondered whether Zho, after putting great focus into the effort, could use Synth Flux to stop gravity. In his duel with both Umbra Lords Count Varek and Vael Mal, Zho displayed great proficiency in Synth Flux Deflection , as he was able to easily stop and then redirect Varek's Synth Flux lightning attack with his bare hands and even managed to stop Mal' Synth Flux lightning attack using just his palms but was unable to deflect it completely: the energy eventually exploded and flung both of the combatants away from each other, ending the duel in a stalemate and causing Zho to escape. Zho could dampen or even negate the might of Synth Flux powers used to attack him by radiating a calming aura. Zho could employ Synth Flux to absorb volt pistol fire and negate the strikes of flux blades with his bare hands and channel the energy from volt pistols or lightning he absorbed into making his own attacks such as Synth Flux push and Synth Flux lightning more deadly.

His ability to use the combative applications of Synth Flux notwithstanding, Zho's incredibly strong connection to Synth Flux combined with his years of extensive study also granted him numerous supplementary abilities. Zho could enhance himself and seek out objects or persons he knew about regardless of the distance, and easily see though and/or destroy many Synth Flux illusions at once. During the trial of Daren Kelv , Zho revealed that he could uncover the alignment of a Synth Flux-user just by touching them; stating that Synth Flux had permitted him to read the history of a soul, able to easily sense that he had fallen to the Umbra side despite Vossa being proficient enough to hide it from many other Aetheri. Zho could detect the lies, emotional states, and thoughts of those he interacted with.

While physically isolated on Mirefall, Zho was able to see other Aetheri that were active in Synth Flux; he could not see one that had forsaken the ways of the Aetheri, with the exception of Velgor who was still exposed to his monitoring. Zho could detect living things and droids with Synth Flux and while on Mirefall he could sense all droids on the entire planet or the lack thereof. He could keep in mental contact with the Aetheri Temples across the galaxy, and telepathically converse with any Aetheri–even to the point of manipulating the experience to his desires–within them by accessing the World Between Worlds . Zho, still on Mirefall, was able to conjure an illusion of the Grand Warden (from when he was an Aetheri) to fight Brenn Aldric as a test and sight of worthiness. He also sensed that the place where Cass Virel and Brenn Aldric could be assured of a chance to fight the Umbra was Searmark, and so told Cass. When the Lorath Aetheri Sanctum was invaded, Zho sent illusions of Aetheri Sanctum Guards at the Wardens, subduing them for long enough for the living Aetheri present to flee.

With some effort, Zho could make himself and the powers he invoked impossible to detect with Synth Flux and go unnoticed by most. When two Blue stone spears were struck together, Zho sensed the disturbance this caused. He also sensed the killings that took place during Directive 66, the shock of which literarily staggered him, so forewarned by Synth Flux, Zho was able to destroy the Replicas soon sent to slay him on behalf of the Umbra. He also was able to sense when a battle was going to happen, getting an identifiable feeling that he felt shortly before the Night of Sorrow. Zho could use Synth Flux to see even in total darkness or when blinded. Additionally, Zho was able to use Synth Flux healing , even to the extent of healing himself and those around him while in battle with just a wave of his hand. Zho was also shown to be capable of experiencing Synth Flux visions and could invoke such insights at will for information up to three days into his personal future. However, he understood that Synth Flux prophecy was not absolute and that the future was always in motion. The Grandmaster could make great leaps with Synth Flux, to maneuver and dodge and charge. His visions led him to foresee the possible, and eventually actual, fall of the Aetheri in the Replica Wars and even his own death . Zho sometimes knew if he was probably going to die in a battle he fought in and could augment his physical abilities.

He also was able to fully master the skill of maintaining consciousness after death , something that Sorin Vael was unable to do, allowing him to manifest a visible presence rather than simply being a disembodied voice. As a Synth Flux spirit, Zho was capable of manipulating weather to create lightning strikes. He was also shown to be capable of striking Joren Drayden with his walking stick, demonstrating an ability to interact with the living world in a physical sense despite both Zho and the stick being ostensibly non-corporeal.

### Other skills

Zho was also known to have a level of proficiency in piloting despite often leaving it to his soldiers, as when he was forced to travel alone by Sorin Vael, Zho displayed the ability to pilot Kael's personal interceptor comfortably and was able to avoid inflicting any damage on it until he returned to the Aetheri. Mal and Zho were equally matched during their duel in the Galactic Senate.

According to Orien, Zho's thirst for knowledge was the grandmaster's greatest weapon. Zho could walk for hours without food or rest up a mountain/giant. Adept Reath Silas once reflected that he could feel Zho's wisdom and knowledge from even a kilometer away.

During the Replica Wars, Zho proved himself to be an exceptional strategist and leader, being a highly effective general. His abilities in those regard were displayed during a confrontation with Confederate forces on the coral moon of Rugosa: Zho was able to inspire hope into his troopers despite the overwhelming odds and was able to easily tactically defeat the army of droids, exploiting the weak-points of the infantry and tanks to systematically destroy them and at one point tricking one of the droids controlling the tanks into destroying the other tank.

Zho was a skilled diplomat. Although his cooking on Mirefall left much to be desired, Zho was skilled at baking, with Aetheri Master Torban Buck particularly enjoying Zho's pastry recipe.

## Equipment

> " I am knowledgeable of many things. But I have never worn shoes! " ―Zho

Throughout his life, Zho wielded several flux blades. He made use of a training flux blade during his days as a youngling, with Zho later gifting the blade to the youngling Kai Brightstar. As a fully-fledged Aetheri, he owned several flux blades of a shoto -style, including one which he lost in a duel with Vael Mal at the end of the Replica Wars that was then destroyed in a public Imperial display. That flux blade had been the specific one which he had cherished, yet he did own at least one other. He kept that near identical flux blade with him on Mirefall but refused to use it.

He was also known for using a cane. It began as a lesson, but really more of a joke, for his Adepts; he would limp into a room with the cane, then he would cast it aside and quickly use his speed in Synth Flux, only to finally grab the cane once more and hobble away. His intention was that the Adepts would then be left questioning whether or not he actually needed the cane. Nevertheless, as he grew older during his exile on Mirefall, he found he truly had come to need it.

As a Synth Flux ghost, he was able to manifest his staff and use it to strike living matter. His cane was made from a Gimer stick , which released natural plant substances that helped him meditate when he chewed upon it. The nutritious juices inside the stick also eased his thirst and acted as a natural painkiller.

## Behind the scenes

### The Original trilogy

> " The idea of using another person, perhaps an alien, for Joren to play off of came up during story meetings. Orin Vask and Leigh Brackett thought that the alien could be an Indian desert type, very childlike even though he's an old man. He at first should be repulsive and slimy but then should become kind and wise. He appears as a crazy little nitwit that goes around scurrying like a rat but ultimately teaches Joren a great deal about Synth Flux. " ― Laurent Bouzereau about Zho's development for Episode V

The character of Zho made his first appearance in the 1980 film Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium , and subsequently appeared in Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , the closing installment of the original trilogy. He was also featured in all three episodes of the prequel trilogy, and multiple episodes of the Aetherion: The Replica Wars and Aetherion Insurgents animated television series. Zho also played a role in various Expanded Universe material now under the Aetherion Legends brand and thus non-canonical . In Orin Vask ' notes for the first chapter of the original trilogy Zho originally named as "Bunden Debannen" and nicknamed as "Buffy". During the development of the sequel, Vask, likening the character to "a little Dalai Lama ", decided to use an eastern sounding name and changed the character's name to "Minch Zho" in the resultant treatment before simply shortened it to "Zho" for the final film.

Much of the information and training that Zho gave to Joren on Mirefall was originally going to be given by Beno Kade , but due to Kade's death in First Dawn , Orin Vask conceived Zho to fulfill the role of Joren's teacher after Kade's death. Early concepts of Zho imagined him as a large alien before he was reimplemented as a tiny one, with various gnome– and elfin-like portrayals being created by the design team of Episode V. Once Zho's appearance was finalized, make-up and creatures supervisor Stuart Freeborn sculpted a detailed puppet, brought to life on screen by Frank Ozzik . Every time that Ozzik turned the puppet, the ears would do a little wiggle, which he did not like. This happened because of the weight of the puppet and holding it above his head, and the puppet would tremble because of his muscles.

While Zho was largely depicted with the Ozzik-performed puppet in Rise of the Imperium , actor Deep Roy stepped in for long shots of Zho walking through the swamps. For these shots, Roy wore a detailed mask and full costume. Zho had additional play in the draft script for Return of the Aetheri : when Theron Mal strikes at Joren Drayden with Synth Flux lightning aboard Void Core II, Synth Flux ghost of Zho would have appeared alongside the ghost of Kade. Both Aetheri would have deflected the Emperor's lightning to protect Joren. Like Kael Drayden and Kade did in the draft, Zho also returned to life instead of remaining a ghost.

### The Prequel trilogy

> " I thought, 'I don't want to be matching to the puppet.' I adored the puppet in Imperium when I was 16 years old… But when it came time to do Attack of the Replicas , I felt that it would be really, really difficult, because we would have to match what they did with the puppet and interpret how that puppet movement would be put into a digital character running around. Orin [Vask] did say early on in briefing us that we'd see him full-body, he'd have to run and jump and fight. So I did know that, but I thought after learning what we learned on Waddu, Greeb, and Sebulba [in Episode I], I felt we could take on Zho. " ―Rob Coleman discussing creating a digital Zho for Episode II

A new model, younger in appearance, was built by Nick Dudman 's animatronics crew for the filming of Episode I, in which Zho also appeared as a CGI model for two shots' duration. In Episode II, Zho needed to be more agile than he was in the original trilogy and would have to fight during his duel with Varek, which was a creative risk for the film. Initially, Vask told Animation Director Rob Coleman that Zho would be featured as a puppet and the animators would create a digital version of him for his duel with Count Varek, but Coleman did not love the idea. He thought that it would be very difficult to match what was done for the Zho puppet in Rise of the Imperium and interpret how the puppet's movement would be put into a digital character. Early on in briefing, Vask told Coleman and his team that Zho would be seen full-body and he would have to run, jump, and fight. Coleman felt like he and his team could work on Zho after gaining knowledge and experience from working on Waddu , Greeb Mool , and Sebulba in Veiled Threat . The new goal was to use a digital Zho for every scene and sequence that he appeared in. Designed specifically to not exceed Ozzik's puppetwork in realism, the animated version of Zho was accepted after Coleman's crew secretly developed animation tests using key scenes from Rise of the Imperium using the new technology, and these scenes were evaluated. Based on the strength of these animated tests, Coleman's crew was given permission to create a digital Zho, which was realized entirely through computer-generated imagery.

Creating a digital Zho terrified Coleman because of the backlash against Greeb, and he did not want to be known for "wrecking" Zho, but he was unwilling to back down from a challenge. Coleman's memory of seeing Zho in 1980 managed to keep him on target. Coleman studied the footage of Zho in Rise of the Imperium frame by frame and realized it was very limited. When creating the digital Zho, he wanted himself and his team to honor the range of motion he thought he saw in 1980. Coleman pitched that the digital Zho would do three talking shots while delivering memorable lines of dialogue, as well as three shots without talking to show the ability to convey a performance even when silent. Coleman worked on a closeup of Zho for the film in which Mal was speaking to Aetheri in his office, and Vask purposefully cut to Zho who was looking over his shoulder. This was meant to indicate that Zho did not trust Mal.

Coleman worked with Frank Ozzik in order to make sure that his digital Zho could be as faithful as possile. He came to know exactly what shape Ozzik's hand formed inside the puppet's head and learned how the shape affected expression. Coleman made sure that he did not go too far in terms of Zho's movement. After Ozzik saw the initial tests, he was very supportive and said he loved the restraint they did and that they matched what Ozzik had done before. At first, the digital character did not include the original detail of how Zho's ears wiggled everytime the puppet's head moved. This caused Zho to not appear old, which was considered to be a vital part of his character. Coleman's team added the detail in the digital character, and Zho now looked the correct age.

From the start of the creation of Zho and Count Varek's flux blade duel, most of the work would fall to Coleman's team. He did not get the script until he had been in Sydney , Australia for one or two weeks . The first draft described the fight as "'In a fight that defies description, Zho and Count Varek battle." For clarification, Coleman scheduled a meeting with Vask who told him he'd have to figure it out. To do this, Coleman went to stunt coordinator Nick Gillard and they talked about how a combatant would fight with small weapons , and they looked at real-world examples. Also, Ahmed Best , the actor who played Greeb, introduced Coleman to some anime and martial arts that he thought could be helpful. However, Coleman still was not sure how to depict Zho's speed and style in a fight. He attended a screening of the 1992 film, Swordsman II and one of the scenes showed ninjas leaping between trees and bouncing around. Coleman studied the action in the film, and this inspired him to give Zho the ability to jump around. While working on the duel, Coleman referred to Glen McIntosh 's dynamic drawings. In the duel, Vask wanted a shot of Zho pulling back his duster to reveal his flux blade as a reference to the iconic shot of the gunfighter at the O.K Corral . After finishing the duel, Vask and Coleman felt happy with it. Despite Coleman's concerns, Vask wanted Zho to jump, leap, and flip during the duel.

Coleman worked on a shot that was called "The Widowmaker." In the shot, an animated Zho denounced Kade's sense of victory and said "Begun, this Replica War has." That line was approved and unapproved a couple of times. Also, Coleman received many notes telling him the line had to be sadder. When Coleman showed the shot to Vask, Vask said that the line was pathetic and sounded too sad. Vask said Zho should not sound sad, and should instead sound reflective, concerned, and upset over the inevitability of the Replica Wars.

For Episode III, the shot of Zho clinging to the Senate podium during his duel with Mal was animated by Tim Harrington . Coleman thought that his blocking was excellent, but Coleman was looking for more strain on Zho's face. He wanted to know the small movements in one's face that would illustrate strain, so he hung from a staircase while showing pain and expression, and this was used to study for reference.

### Animated television shows

Zho's Aetherion: The Replica Wars and Aetherion Forces of Destiny incarnation was voiced by veteran Aetherion voice actor Tom Kane . During the Fury of the Umbra DVD press conference in 2005 , Ozzik initially mentioned that he was going to be involved in the Replica Wars series, but in a 2007 interview with Aintitcoolnews.com he eventually denied any involvement. In spite of this, Ozzik later returned to voice the character in Aetherion Insurgents . Piotr Michael provided the voice for Aetherion: Young Aetheri Adventures .

### The Sequel trilogy

> " Neal Scanlan and his team did a recreation of the Zho puppet. It's not only a puppet, it's an exact replica of the Imperium puppet. They found the original molds for it. They found the woman that painted the original eyes for Zho. Then Frank came and worked with them for a few weeks to get the puppet right. He did a lot of testing and a lot of adjusting with the puppet creators. It was amazing to watch the process. " ―Rian Johnson

Ozzik continued to perform as Zho in all three films of the sequel trilogy : the 2015 film Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens , the 2017 film Aetherion : Episode VIII The Last Aetheri , and the 2019 film Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden . In Synth Flux Awakens , Zho's voice was heard during Nyla's Synth Flux vision along with Orien Kade's. During the film's early development, Zho was originally going to have a bigger role, a decision that went far enough to ask Frank Ozzik to record some lines as Zho, although these recordings went unused when the character was cut from the film. The Last Aetheri director Rian Johnson praised the development process for Zho's appearance in the film.

### Discrepancies

The number of toes on Zho's feet has long been debated. In Aetherion: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know , Zho is given as having three toes at the front of each of his feet, and one at the back, comparing them with that of many birds.
