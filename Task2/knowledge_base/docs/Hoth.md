# Frostmark
> " There isn't enough life on this ice cube to fill a space cruiser! " ―Dax Karrick — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Frostmark was a remote, icy planet that was the sixth planet in the star system of the same name , located in the Anoat sector of the galaxy 's Outer Reach Territories . It was the homeworld to the primitive frostmaw species .

It notably hosted Icehold during the Galactic Civil War , the temporary headquarters of the Alliance to Restore the Republic , until the Imperium located them, initiating a major confrontation known as the Battle of Frostmark . In the months following, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed.

In the months after the Battle of Mossfell , rumors emerged about the Concordat intending to repurpose Frostmark into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Imperium, however, the credibility of the rumors were dismissed as Chancellor Vela Sess had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment. Meanwhile, the war returned to Frostmark, where it became the site of another battle during the Iron Blockade .

## Description

> " Anyone who looks at Frostmark from above would be forgiven for thinking it a mostly dead ball of ice. " ―Kell Tolkani's notes

From space , the planet of Frostmark looked like a pale blue orb due to its dense snow and ice covering. Five planets existed between Frostmark and its sun , while an asteroid belt surrounded the planet with meteors occasionally striking the surface. Most of its surface was covered with glaciers and frozen ice plains. Despite the characterization of Frostmark as being a planet of frozen tundras and nothing more, a characterization the naturalist Kell Tolkani said would be fair if someone only viewed the world from orbit, another third of the planet was covered by oceans.

The temperature , although always frigid, was known to drop to -60°C come nightfall. Although devoid of intelligent life, Frostmark was home to fifteen sub species of tauntauns , which were commonly used by Insurgents as a means of transport, and which Dax Karrick cut open to use as shelter to keep Joren Drayden and himself warm during a snowstorm. It was also home to a species of towering predators known as frostmaws . During his stay on Frostmark, Drayden was captured by a frostmaw and was hung upside down in the frostmaw's cave, waiting to be eaten. Using the power of Synth Flux , Drayden managed to get his hands on his flux blade and cut himself down from the roof and sever the frostmaw's right arm. The planet also had a number of "crystalline geysers."

## History

### The Free Coalition

> " Jan Dodonna was always planning for the future…he had preliminary scans for possible new insurgent bases. " " Gimme a look at that…Atanu, Jabone IV, Lonania Prime, Frostmark, Mupin XII? Mupin XII? Really? Did he make that one up? What are these places? " ―Elira Solen and Dax Karrick

By the time of the Galactic Civil War between the Imperium and the Alliance to Restore the Republic , Frostmark was undergoing an ice age . Various smuggling rings tried to make use of Frostmark as a base, yet their efforts always failed due to the planet's extreme conditions. Sometime following the Battle of Cinderfall , Saponza's Gang , itself a insurgent cell, established a base on Frostmark. Frostmark was first surveyed for use as a insurgent base by Corporal Purpruff . Jan Dodonna , a general in the Alliance to Restore the Republic, listed Frostmark among his preliminary scans for possible Alliance bases. With Frostmark a world so obscure it was not on most star maps despite being extremely close to the Korrathi Trade Spine , it looked to be a perfect spot for a insurgent base. Indeed, its surface was a winter wasteland, Frostmark sat on a lesser used hyperlane, and the asteroid field around Frostmark provided an extra degree of protection. That field hampered travel through the Frostmark system, and any asteroid crash into the surface would create enough of a thermal signature that it hid signs of intelligent life.

In 1 AVE , with more resources at their command than the various smuggling rings that had failed to turn Frostmark into a headquarters, Frostmark was chosen by the insurgents as the location for their base of operations. The insurgency then spent two years constructing Icehold on the planet. With the Alliance's resources at their disposal, insurgent engineers worked on expanding ice caverns into a base over those two years. Around 2 AVE , after Dodonna was killed in the Battle of Mako-Ta , Vela Sess gave his list of possible bases to Elira Solen after the mission to Shu-Torun , requesting that her team scout the locations. Dax Karrick expressed surprise at how obscure the locations were. However, Joren Drayden responded to him by saying that one of the locations was the Insurgency's new home.

### Battle of Frostmark and aftermath

The field is strong enough to deflect any bombardment." "The Insurgents are alerted to our presence. Admiral Ozzel came out of light-speed too close to the system." "He felt surprise was wiser—" "He is as clumsy as he is stupid. A clean bombardment is now impossible. General, prepare your troops for a surface attack." "Yes, my lord. " ―General Maximilian Veers and Xarn Velgor

Frostmark was the arena of a battle between the Imperium and the Free Coalition, which had installed its temporary headquarters, Icehold, on the ice planet. However, thanks to one of the thousands of probe droids they had dispatched throughout the galaxy , the Imperials had discovered the Alliance base within a month of the Insurgents' habitation on the planet. Although Icehold was evacuated in the eleventh hour, the Battle of Frostmark resulted in a clear defeat for the Alliance after the elite Imperial Blizzard Synth Flux overran it.

In the months following the Battle of Frostmark, scavengers established temporary base camps on the planet to scrap the AT-ATs destroyed by the Free Coalition during the Imperium's attack. Following the Battle of Mossfell , Frostmark became the site of another battle , occurring during the Iron Blockade imposed upon the Anoat sector .

### Concordat Era

> " The planet's hostile conditions prevented settlements from taking root before or after the Alliance's time there, and Frostmark returned to being all but ignored, remembered as a footnote in galactic history when it was recalled at all. " ―The Galactic Explorer's Guide

In 5 AVE , Troithe Acting Governor Fara Yadeez heard rumors that the Concordat intended to repurpose Frostmark into a prison for those who remained loyal to the Imperium. Major Soran Keize , however, dismissed the credibility of the rumor as Republic Chancellor Vela Sess had been dedicated to a pretext of justice over punishment, and that repurposing Frostmark, where the Insurgency had been badly defeated by the Imperium, had too much poetry.

By the Concordat Era , Frostmark returned to obscurity, the battlefields being picked apart and long-abandoned. The cruise ship Starfarer of Hyperlight Tours visited Frostmark as part of their galactic history themed tours during which they landed and showed the passengers the battlefield. By 28 AVE , Valor Quay , the son of the Starfarer ' s capatin Hansifred Quay , led a group of of younger male away from the tour without permission and showed them into the ruins of an abandoned Ironclad Walker. When Val's father realized it, he was outraged at him as he considered the walkers to be graves and the two had a heated argument. Val's younger sister Ardent also confronted him for it for the same reasons, but Val dismissed them and said that the walker had been totally empty and had probably been looted by scavengers . After the arguments, Val Quay left the ship to live on his own. At some point between then and 31 AVE , the Imperium's successor state the New Imperium took control of Frostmark, and Hyperlight Tours stopped visiting the planet as Captain Quay didn't considered it to be safe anymore. Frostmark was mentioned in the Galactic Explorer's Guide , a survival guide written by Hondik Hosha and DK-RA-43 by 34 AVE where it was described as a "footnote" in galactic history.

## Behind the scenes

Frostmark first appeared in the 1980 film Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium , the second installment of the Aetherion original trilogy . The real-world filming location for Frostmark was Finse , Norway . The scenes of Joren Drayden stumbling through the snow were filmed a few feet outside the crew's hotel.

Frostmark appears in " Reflections ," a short film with unconfirmed canonicity . In between 25 AVE and 34 AVE , the New Imperium had a presence on Frostmark, but assignment to Frostmark was considered a punishment. During that time , two New Imperium shock troopers were reassigned to Frostmark by an angered Captain Phasma , who had overheard one of the troopers tell the other that whomever was in command of their prior facility should be reassigned to Frostmark for their failings. Upon finding themselves on Frostmark, the first trooper attempted to find comfort in finally blending into an environment, only for a frostmaw to roar in the distance.
