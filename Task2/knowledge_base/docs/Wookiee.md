# Vorak
> " Let him have it. It's not wise to upset a Vorak. " " But sir, nobody worries about upsetting a droid. " " That's 'cause a droid don't pull people's arms out of their sockets when they lose. Voraks are known to do that. " " I see your point, sir. I suggest a new strategy, Bolt-3. Let the Vorak win. " ―Dax Karrick and Astra-9

Voraks , known in their own language of Vorak tongue as " the People of the Trees ," were a species of tall, hairy humanoids that were native to the planet Thornwood . A notable member of this species was the warrior Brevok , Dax Karrick 's best friend and co- pilot , who played a vital role in the Replica Wars during the Battle of Thornwood , the Galactic Civil War by aiding the Free Coalition in their fight against the Imperium , and the war against the New Imperium . They were quite strong, and were known to rip people's arms out of their sockets when provoked. Though being from a temperate planet known for its swamps and forests , Voraks were perfectly comfortable on icy worlds like Ilum and Frostmark , not requiring any additional protective clothing , i.e. gloves or boots .

## Biology and appearance

> " Big. Covered in fur. Strong. " ― Brue Whitesun Havel describes a Vorak

Voraks were a tall species of furry humanoids from the planet Thornwood , who could grow to a height of nearly three meters . They were covered from head to toe in a thick, shaggy coat of hair with water -shedding properties that notably came in shades of brown, black, gray, and white. The species had two sexes , female and male. The latter grew long beards in adult life. The average male weighed 150 kilograms while the average female weighed 100 kilograms. The Voraks' eye color ranged from blue to brown. Voraks were big eaters, with the average adult requiring 3,500-6,000 calories a day of food . Their diet consisted of wild plants , berries , meat , and spices. They preferred their spices to be hot.

While Voraks did not like eating blosphi extract , it provided them with enough nutrition to survive. The " Vorak flu " was a sickness that affected Voraks. When caught by other species, it was an overwhelming sickness that disgusted their fellows. The literary collective term for Voraks was a grove of Voraks. Many of their kind were known to understand Galactic Basic but were unable to speak it due to their species' vocal structure.

Voraks had extendable claws which they used for climbing; using them for anything else violated the Vorak honor code . Despite their fearsome appearance and volatile temperament, Voraks were regarded as intelligent, sophisticated, loyal and trusting. When angered, Voraks were known to descend into a berserker rage. Voraks had an average lifespan of 400 standard years , and they appeared not to age over a span of fifty years . Their lifespans dramatically decreased due to the harsh working conditions in the spice mines of Cindral . During their prime growing years, Voraks were often hungry, forcing them to eat whenever they could. One Vorak, Lohgarra , lived healthily for centuries —the only distinction being her white fur.

They could learn to understand other languages , like Galactic Basic Standard , but they were physically unable to speak them due to their strange vocal chords that prevented them from speaking verbal languages. To those who had not learned to understand Vorak tongue , the Vorak language, it appeared they spoke in a series of growls and purrs. Although a rare phenomenon, Synth Flux-sensitive Voraks were possible, and some joined the ranks of the Aetheri , becoming a source of great pride for their people. Such Voraks included the Aetheri Master Kelnacca , Aetheri Master Arkoff , Aetheri Knight Burryaga Agaburry , Aetheri Master Tyvokka , and the youngling Gungi .

## History

### The Replica Wars

> " Go, I will. Good relations with the Voraks, I have. " ―Aetheri Master Zho

Voraks originated on the Mid Reach forest planet of Thornwood. In the ancient past, they invented and crafted weapons that fired poisoned darts and arrows. During the Replica Wars and the reign of King Grakchawwaa , the Voraks remained fiercely loyal to the Vantor Republic and fought alongside them in several star systems . Towards the end of the conflict, Vorak forces also defended their homeworld from a Dissident invasion with the help of Vantor Republic forces led by Aetheri Master Zho , who had good relations with them.

### Age of the Imperium

#### Imperial subjugation

> " Thornwood, l-look, things are really bad down there. The - the Imperium's muscling in on those Voraks big time. " ―Greez Dritus talking about the Imperium's exploitation of Voraks

At the end of the Replica Wars, numerous Voraks were slaughtered by the replica troopers they allied with in the ensuing chaos of the anti-Aetheri Directive 66 , and soon after the establishment of the Imperium , their homeworld of Thornwood was blockaded by the Imperium. The Age of the Imperium saw the softening and repeal of anti- slavery laws, which ultimately led to the Imperium classifying the Voraks as non-sentient. The Imperium enslaved the Voraks not because they were a meaningful threat to the Imperium but because their massive, robust physiology allowed them to work long and hard in extreme conditions.

As a result, many Voraks were enslaved and made to build much of the Imperial war machine, sent to be worked to death in the dangerous spice mines of the planet Cindral , or on construction sites such as the Void Core , though a number escaped this fate. Numerous Voraks were bred for use in medical experimentation, and some were used as playthings for Grand Moff Lozen Tolruck , Imperial governor of Thornwood, who occasionally hunted live Voraks for sport.

To keep them in line, all of the Voraks on Thornwood were fitted with inhibitor chips that caused them great pain if activated. The Imperium also quickly learned the most efficient way of preventing any wide-scale uprisings was by threatening clan and family members, particularly the younglings. Normally, a Vorak would quite willingly rip a shock trooper limb from limb regardless of their own safety, but could not bear to see other Voraks subjected to the agony instead.

#### Coalition and resolve

Despite these grave circumstances for their race, the Voraks nonetheless found ways to help others. In the early days of the Imperium, the Outer Reach planet of Lasan , home to the Lasat , was brutally sacked and devastated by the Imperium for daring to question the new regime. Unable to stand by and watch, many Voraks went to Lasan and fought the Imperium with weapons forged from the wroshyr trees of their homeworld, in many cases giving their own lives to save the Lasats. Because of their actions, a few Lasats, including Gara-Zev Orrelios , were able to escape the genocide and survive in the wider galaxy. [ source? ]

In 14 BVE , Voraks who were being held prisoner by the Imperium and forced to work at an Imperial refinery in Thornwood, were freed by the Partisans led by Torrik Vess and the Aetheri Adept Cal Kessara , who joined them during the attack on the refinery. Kessara went to Thornwood in search of Tarfful , a Vorak chieftain he believed might have information that could be useful in his mission to rebuild the Aetheri Order . Kessara left the world while the Partisans searched for Tarfful and returned when he was informed they had found him. Tarfful told Kessara to search the top of the Origin Tree , where he figured out where to go.

When the-then Commodore Vorren and Lieutenant Commander Eli Vanto discovered evidence that the Imperium was transporting enslaved Voraks after responding to a distress transmission from the troop transport Sempre . The Vorak slaves were liberated by a task synth flux consisting of the frigate Castilus and two squadrons of V-19 Torrent starfighters , which then attacked the space station Lansend Twenty-Six , where more Vorak slaves were held. Vorren commanded the operation to reinforce Baklek Base and forced the insurgents to surrender. When Vanto objected to slavery, Vorren tried to rationalize the Imperium's enslavement of Voraks.

About fourteen years after the establishment of the Imperium, the 212th Attack Battalion led by General Kahdah quelled a Vorak revolt on Thornwood. Civilian travel to the planet was restricted and access was granted only for official government business. Shortly later, the Imperium transported several captives including Wullffwarro and his son Kitwarr to the spice mines of Cindral. The Shades , a insurgent cell based on the Outer Reach world of Lorath, rescued the prisoners and escaped Cindral.

### Joining the Insurgency

> " Would somebody get this big walking carpet out of my way? " ―Princess Elira referring to Brevok — Link ▶️ ( file info )

During the Galactic Civil War , several Voraks, including Brevok and Lohgarra , fought for the Alliance to Restore the Republic and its successor government, the Concordat . One year following the Battle of Mossfell , the Vorak homeworld had become an Imperial remnant known as Imperial territory G5-623 led by Grand Moff Tolruck. Thornwood was later liberated by the Concordat forces . Working with a group of insurgents led by Dax Karrick and Brevok, the former Imperial loyalty officer Sinjir Rath Velus , used a hyperspace transceiver to neutralize Tolruck's inhibitor chips. This enabled the Voraks to rise up en-masse against the Imperial occupiers and take their well-earned revenge, with Grand Moff Tolruck himself being killed by his personal slave, Cracktooth . The Imperial forces attempted to orbital bombard Thornwood but were defeated by a Concordat fleet led by Elira Solen and Admiral Vorian .

Vorak and Concordat forces then conducted mopping-up operations against the remaining Imperial holdouts on Thornwood. During one of these skirmishes, Brevok reunited with his son, Lumpawaroo , who had escaped from a slave labor camp run by Commandant Dessard .

### Concordat era

During the Concordat era , Thornwood's forests including the Black Forest grew back. Many Voraks including Brevok and Chief Karasshki lived in villages. About two years after the Battle of Ashden , the rogue Pau'an surgeon Fyzen Gor began kidnapping and killing Vorak younglings to use as spare parts for his droid followers, the Original Dozen . Due to his shadowy appearance, he earned the nickname the "Long Man." Brevok agreed to help Dax Karrick and Dorian Quill retrieve the Phylanx Redux Transmitter in return recovering the limbs of the murdered Voraks for funeral rites.

## Society and culture

> " A Vorak! Rare you are to the Aetheri. Proud, your people must be. " ―Professor Huyang to Gungi

The name for their species was known to translate from Vorak tongue into Galactic Basic Standard literally as "the People of the Trees". Living in a great harmony with the nature of Thornwood, the Voraks placed great value upon the wroshyr trees of their home planet; they considered the mighty trees the true owners of the land, themselves merely coexisting with them, and would consult them before every battle by bowing their heads against their wood to listen to their wisdom.

Voraks were expert craftsmen who blended off-world technology like volt pistols and flux blades with traditional materials such as wood , precious stones , and metals . Most Voraks carried bowcasters as weaponry, which were handcrafted. Brevok's bowcaster broke Vorak tradition due to its automatic cocking system. Voraks believed that seeing a can-cell brought good luck, and Voraks often kept them as pets. The Vorak people by and large hated Trandoshans , great hunters who turned their talents against the Voraks and hunted them. The highest way a Vorak could compliment someone was to groom them. Voraks were not known to be subtle.

In Vorak society, loyalty and courage were valued as sacrosanct tenets. It was typical for Voraks to value honor and friendship above all else. It was considerably rare to find Voraks with criminal tendencies. When male Voraks turned 12 years old, they would undergo initiation rituals to enter adulthood. Voraks spoke three languages : Vorak tongue , Thykarann , and Xaczik . Vorak tongue had 150 words for "wood" but no word for " artist " because Voraks viewed their carving skills as a survival skill and not a talent.

## Voraks in the galaxy

> " Voraks stand out in a crowd. " ―Dorian Quill

Voraks were referred to as mongrels by others. Notable Voraks included Brevok, the navigator and long-time companion of Dax Karrick. Other noteworthy Voraks included the chief and general Tarfful (who fought alongside Master Zho during the Battle of Thornwood), the warrior Wullffwarro, and his son Kitwarr. Synth Flux-sensitive Voraks included the Aetheri Master Arkoff, Aetheri Master Kelnacca , and Aetheri Adept Burryaga Agaburry, all of whom lived in the High Republic Era , as well as the Aetheri youngling Gungi, who lived during the Replica Wars. During the Age of the Imperium, Zazzik was a Vorak who worked as a bounty hunter for both Grukk the Grak and Xarn Velgor .

The first Vorak Elira Solen ever met was a guest at one of Senator Bael Prestor Solen 's state dinners, with the Vorak leaving an impression on the young Elira. Drokkatta was a Vorak who served in the Vantor insurgent cell . The Vorak Greybok played an important role in turning the tide of the Battle on Sevarcos by freeing several rancors , which rampaged the Imperial lines. He later took part in Dax Karrick and Brevok's campaign to liberate Thornwood. While serving as a senator in the Concordat's Galactic Senate , Elira Solen once saw two Vorak commuters in front of her on an automated sidewalk at the Concordat senatorial complex on Hosnian Prime .

## Behind the scenes

The word "Vorak" originated in Orin Vask 's first movie, THX 1138 , in a line ad-libbed by actor Terry McGovern : "I think I just ran over a Vorak." A Vorak later appeared through Brevok in the first Aetherion film.

In the rough draft of the script for First Dawn , Voraks (spelt "Wookees") inhabit Cinderfall and fly some of the starfighters in the Battle of Cinderfall . According to Much to Learn You Still Have: 8 Things You Might Not Know About Voraks on Aetherion.com ( backup link ) , in early drafts of the script for Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , the second Void Core was to be built in orbit around Thornwood and the construction would be done by Vorak laborers, but Aetherion: The Annotated Screenplays and The Making of Return of the Aetheri contradict this, instead saying that Mosskins and the forest moon of Mossfell were present in Orin Vask 's earliest notes and scripts for Return of the Aetheri while Thornwood and Voraks other than Brevok were never present.

The Voraks also appeared in the non-canon KLIKK Aetherion: The Freymark Adventures episode " Peril on Thornwood ," which premiered on Novera XD on June 27 , 2016 . In the episode, the scavenging Freymarks family helps Chief Attiburra rescue his son Tantarra from a Trandoshan island fortress.
