# Nyla Drayden
> " I had to continue the fight. To stop Mal from rising again and regaining power. To redeem Beno Karrick once and for all. And to proudly wear my master's name while doing so, in honor of his life and sacrifice. " ―Nyla Drayden after the Battle of Umbrale

Nyla Drayden , also known simply as Nyla , was an Aetheri member of the Coalition during the war against the New Imperium . The human woman was a scavenger on the planet Ashden , but her life was changed by tumultuous events in the last days of the Concordat Era . Nyla's decision to help the droid Orio-6 set her on a course to discovering Synth Flux awakening within her, leading her to seek out Aetheri Master Joren Drayden for training . Despite her commitment to the Aetheri Order and the light side of Synth Flux , Nyla felt the pull to the umbra side due to her anger as well as the adversarial relationship she had with Beno Karrick , a fallen Aetheri who joined the New Imperium as a warrior named Zarrin Karrick; they shared a unique bond as a dyad in Synth Flux .

Nyla was born on Hyperkarn in 15 AVE during the rise of the Concordat . Her father , Dathan , was a bioengineered Strand-Cast cloned from the genetic template of Vael Mal , the Umbra Lord who ruled the Imperium as Theron Mal until his death and resurrection following the Battle of Mossfell . The replica and his wife, Miramir , chose to live as simple junk traders in order to hide their daughter, who inherited Mal' connection to Synth Flux, from his followers . Even after the deaths of her parents, Nyla was unaware of her Mal bloodline and was forced to survive on her own in the Ashden desert , and became a skilled mechanic , pilot , and combatant in the process. Her isolated life was disrupted shortly before war broke out across the galaxy in 34 AVE between the New Imperium and the Coalition. Determined to help the Coalition, Nyla gained several new allies including Jax , a renegade shock trooper , as well as the veterans Dax Karrick and Brevok aboard the Corsair Wing . In the course of their journey, she discovered her latent Synth Flux powers and sought guidance under Joren Drayden, who taught her the art of Synth Flux despite his decision to end the Aetheri legacy. After Drayden sacrificed himself to save the Coalition, Nyla assumed his mantle as the last Aetheri and became the apprentice of General Elira Solen .

With the return of Vael Mal in 35 AVE , Nyla set out to find the dark world of Umbrale and prevent the Umbra from being reborn through the resurrected Dark Lord . It was through Zarrin Karrick that Nyla discovered her origins as Mal's granddaughter, and upon confronting the resurrected Emperor in person, she was nearly swayed to the umbra side of Synth Flux, thus falling into his trap of transferring his spirit into her, which will allow him to possess her body. However, Nyla was saved by Beno Karrick after he renounced his dark alter ego. Aided by the spirits of all the Aetheri, Nyla died vanquishing her grandfather, causing Karrick to make the ultimate sacrifice in order to bring Nyla back to life. The two embraced and shared a kiss as Karrick peacefully vanished in Nyla's arms. Although Karrick was the last of the Drayden bloodline , Nyla assumed the name "Drayden" to honor their memory and in recognition of her chosen family. Sometime later, the now self-proclaimed Nyla Drayden became an Aetheri Master.

## Biography

### Pre-birth

> " We will see each other again. I believe that. " ―Nyla's voice, as heard by Cass Virel

In 1 BVE , during the Galactic Civil War and more than a decade before Nyla's birth , her voice was heard by Adept Cass Virel during his brief visit to the World Between Worlds —a place that existed beyond time and space .

Nyla and Beno Karrick were the culmination of a prophesied Synth Flux dyad as well as the Rule of Two . Inspired by the Doctrine of the Dyad , the Rule of Two mandated that only two Umbra could exist at a given time , an Umbra Master and an Umbra apprentice , though its ultimate goal was to create a dyad between two Umbra Lords . Vael Plagueis tried to create a dyad with his apprentice, Vael Mal , who, in turn, tried to create one with Karrick's grandfather, Kael Drayden . Despite years of searching, the Umbra never found a way to form a dyad on their own. Mal regarded the Rule of Two as a pale imitation and an unworthy but necessary successor to the older, purer doctrine of the dyad that would be ultimately fulfilled by Karrick and Nyla.

### Early life

#### Hunted by the Umbra

> " They were nobody. " " They were filthy junk traders who sold you off for drinking money. They're dead in a pauper's grave in the Ashden desert. You have no place in this story. You come from nothing. You're nothing. " ―Nyla and Zarrin Karrick, on the former's parents

Born on the planet Hyperkarn in 15 AVE , Nyla was the daughter of Dathan and Miramir . While their names were lost to history , Nyla's father was the cloned son—an artificial genetic strandcast —of the resurrected Vael Mal, making her the granddaughter of the fallen Galactic Emperor and Dark Lord of the Umbra . Although Mal had not anticipated his son's marriage with Miramir, he sought to claim their child who, unlike his son, had a strong connection to Synth Flux . As such, Nyla was a receptacle strong enough to hold her grandfather's spirit, which was trapped in an imperfect cloned body. To protect Nyla from her grandfather, her parents escaped into hiding, choosing to become ordinary junk traders to keep their daughter safe.

Nyla's parents had a difficult life; they struggled to survive on the desert planet Ashden while raising the daughter whom they loved. Since the Emperor's son and his wife absconded with their child, the Umbra cultist Orrin , on orders from Mal himself, endeavored to find the strandcast and his daughter. Nyla's grandfather had foreseen what she had the capability of becoming, recognizing her immense strength in Synth Flux, and that such power was not mere ordinary power. It was his own power. Because of this, Mal was determined to make certain that one day , Nyla would embrace her destiny as a Mal, and of course, the heir of his Umbra legacy and successor as Galactic Emperor. At the same time, two Insurgent heroes, Aetheri Master Joren Drayden and former General Dorian Quill , began investigating a disturbance in Synth Flux after Quill had overheard a drunk Orrin discussing his orders to kidnap the young Nyla. However, the two never learned the girl's name nor saw the family face-to-face.

Ultimately, Nyla's parents returned to Ashden in Orrin's own ship, the Bestoon Legacy , and, trusting that he would look out for his self-interest, hired Unkar Gruven to hide her within Nimara Outpost until they returned for her. Above the planet, they were captured by Orrin; refusing to give up their daughter's location, they were both killed by the hunter on the Emperor's order. Despite telling herself they lived, Nyla, deep down, was left with the belief that their bodies were buried in the Ashden desert, in a paupers' grave, and a suspicion that they had sold her for drinking money. In truth, they were buried by Quill and Drayden on the planet Neftali . Tricked into traveling to the planet Pasaana because Miramir took some Aki-Aki beads from Gruven in case they were caught, Orrin never succeeded in finding Nyla and died within Pasaana's Forbidden Valley .

Since Nyla's parents never returned to end their arrangement with Gruven, he considered Nyla in debt to him and required her to work for him to pay it back. With Drayden and Quill unaware of her hiding place, Nyla was forced to grow up alone as a result of her parents' deaths . She endured years of isolation while waiting to be reunited with her parents, having subconsciously suppressed the memory of what she knew to be true—they were no one, and they were dead. Nevertheless, Nyla remained hopeful that she would see them again. She recalled voices from her childhood, referring to her as "sweetheart" and promising to return for her one day. Nyla had believed that they were her parents, though she was not certain if the promise was made by her mother, her father, or both.

Throughout her life on Ashden, she would sometimes see off-worlders in Nimara Outpost, a chief trade outpost on the planet, who she thought might have arrived on Ashden accidentally, unaware of the harsh realities of the desert world. Nyla wondered if she was left behind under similar circumstances, if her family had come to Ashden by accident and she was left behind as a result of their misfortune. Whatever the reasons were, she held out hope that her family would return for her one day, though she began to realize that they would never come back. She proved unwilling to admit this even to herself for a long time.

#### The scavenger

> " I'm Nyla, by the way. I'm a scavenger. Have been since I was kid. " ―Nyla

In order to survive on Ashden, Nyla became a scavenger in the employ of Unkar Gruven, trawling through the junk fields of the Starship Graveyard which was littered with the remains of starships and other military technology from the Battle of Ashden . At first, she worked for other scavengers, bringing them the loot she found in crashed Concordat and Imperium vessels that were downed during the battle. She heard local stories about what happened during the battle: that it was the last stand of the once-powerful Imperium during the Galactic Civil War, that the Imperium locked onto Republic ships during the fighting and dragged them down to the surface together, and more. The battle had left the ships trapped in the sand in varying conditions, and finding working parts became essential in order to earn food portions, the only thing that was up for trade in Nimara Outpost. The trades were made by Gruven, who traded leftover Republic and Imperial rations in exchange for whatever Nyla and other scavengers were able to recover. Nyla learned that parts that could be repurposed were the most important; anything that could not be put into another ship was worthless to Gruven.

Some of the scavengers that Nyla worked for were kind to her, such as Ivano Troade and Mashra . Others, whose names she refused to speak, were not kind, and even viewed her as their property. As Nyla's skills as a scavenger grew, she came to realize that while other scavengers needed her, she did not need them. She struck out on her own, utilizing the skills she had to scavenge and survive, but no one took her seriously at first. She was aided by Gruven himself, who told other scavengers to leave Nyla alone and would send his thugs after those who did not. Nyla did not want his help, and the help was not out of generosity. Rather, Nyla was the best scavenger Gruven had and he wanted to continue utilizing her as a source of acquisitions. Nyla gained a reputation in Nimara Outpost for using her quarterstaff to defend herself, which also kept most people from bothering her—save for the occasional new scavenger who did not know about her, or were so desperate for supplies that they did not care.

#### Life on a backwater world

> " I live in a toppled Ironclad Walker walker in the Goazon. It's a strange place but it's mine. " ―Nyla

At some point in her youth, Nyla made her home in a downed Imperial All Terrain Armored Transport , Hellhound Two , left over from the Battle of Ashden. When she first found it, Nyla did not believe that she could live there, as the Goazon Badlands where the Ironclad Walker was located was a barren wasteland in which nothing seemed to grow. When she inspected the walker, though, she found a small green spinebarrel flower growing out of a few millimeters of sand that had poured into the walker. Nyla realized that if such a tiny flower could survive there, so could she, and that life always found a way to survive on Ashden. Nyla would go on to collect flowers like that, as a reminder that beauty could be found anywhere, even on Ashden.

Once she settled into the walker, Nyla salvaged working parts of the Ironclad Walker for trade. She mostly just used one room in the walker, for a variety of reasons. She would often bring her salvages to the walker before bringing them to Gruven, as Gruven deducted portions one could receive from a salvage if they used his washing and repair stations. The walker also contained scratches she made to mark the passage of time, to remind her how far she had come and to count down the days until her family returned for her. Outside of the walker was where she would go on to keep the speeder she built for herself, which gave her a degree of independence as she made her way back and forth from the Starship Graveyard.

By the time Nyla was ten years old, she had acquired a Free Coalition flight helmet that once belonged to Captain Dosmit Ræh of the starfighter squadron Tierfon Yellow Aces , which she found while scavenging in the Starship Graveyard. She enjoyed making up stories about Ræh's adventures during the war, which was why Nyla made a doll out of a flight uniform she found in a cargo container. She often enjoyed wearing the helmet and playing with the doll, pretending she was helping Ræh explore the Ironclad Walker and the sands outside. Together, their pretend adventures saw them find lost Insurgents and return them to their ships. When Nyla was older, she no longer played with the doll, but kept it and the helmet, which she sometimes wore anyway, to remind her how far she had come on Ashden.

Inside the walker, Nyla also had a computer display from an old BTL-A4 Yarrow assault starfighter/bomber that she used to learn alien languages, study the schematics of Republic and Imperial starships, and run flight simulations in order to hone her skills as a pilot . Her ability to understand alien languages, including the binary language of droids , helped her when off-worlders came to Nimara Outpost. Two such off-worlders were Voraks , who regaled her with the stories of the famed Vorak smuggler -turned-Insurgent fighter Brevok . She learned of Brevok's exploits, as well as those of his friend and fellow smuggler Dax Karrick . Studying schematics to learn how ships worked was also an important part of her survival on Ashden. She recognized almost all of the Republic and Imperial vessels that could be found in the Graveyard, including what roles they played in combat, the types of weapons they were armed with, their models and classes, and how many crew members each one had. She learned this not just through studying schematics on her computer, but also by climbing through and exploring the ships and tinkering with their systems. This let her know what each part was, what it could do, whether it worked, and, most importantly, whether it would carry any value in Nimara Outpost.

Growing up on Ashden meant Nyla not only learned how the ships that littered its wastelands worked, but also about the dangers that lurked on Ashden itself. Nyla saw firsthand the hardship it could inflict. Rumors of an Imperial base on Carbon Ridge once compelled Gruven to mount an expedition there. Knowing her skills as a mechanic, Gruven brought Nyla a flyer to fix. She did so by unclogging one of its turbojets and rebuilding the other out of scrap from the front of an air speeder. Gruven and six of his thugs used it to try to find the rumored base, but he only returned with a collection of shock trooper armor —and two of his men were killed. That led other scavengers to avoid Carbon Ridge. The creatures of Ashden were also dangerous. A fellow scavenger named Teng Malar once accidentally broke open a hatch on a ship beneath a colony of steelpeckers , ferocious avian creatures with razor-sharp beaks and strong talons. Within ten minutes, Teng was ripped to shreds and Nyla watched helplessly as she was unable to do anything to stop the creatures. The Graveyard itself was also a reminder of the realities of war, with bodies and skeletons littering the sands. Nyla once discovered a deceased SKREE fighter pilot still in the cockpit of his SKREE fighter ejector seat. Nyla scavenged his helmet, sidearm, and comlink before giving the pilot a proper burial.

Aetheri Adept Beno Karrick turned to the umbra side of Synth Flux , in 28 AVE , culminating in his battle with the dark warrior known as " Karrick " on the Minemoon . Karrick's immersion in the umbra side was sensed across the galaxy by his mother, Princess Elira Solen , the umbra side adept Vorr , Mal, and Nyla, who still resided on Ashden. Nyla was in the desert with Gruven when she felt a cold sensation within her body; unaware of her connection with Karrick or Synth Flux, she asked Gruven if he felt it too. As Karrick's training in the umbra side under Vorr began and the man took on the name "Zarrin Karrick," Vorr believed that his apprentice's growing power in the umbra side would be matched by someone from the light, with Vorr later stating that Nyla filled that role.

#### The happabore hazard

> " I can't move this ship with you here. And Unkar knew that, didn't he? " ―Nyla, to a Happabore

One day between 28 AVE and 34 AVE , Nyla traveled to Nimara Outpost, where she took the job of hauling a pile of scrap heap to Gruven, who offered her ten portions. Gruven reluctantly gave her the coordinates but placed a collateral on Nyla's speeder if she were to fail the errand. Nyla responded by placing a bet of twenty portions since she was risking her speeder.

Nyla traveled on her speeder to the site, which turned out to be a wrecked quadjumper . She found a happabore lying beside the spacetug. Realizing that Gruven had set her up to fail, Nyla tried to get the creature to move. After trying to push it, Nyla realized that the happabore was having trouble breathing because something was blocking its nose. Steeling herself, Nyla dug into the creature's nose and retrieved a piece of machinery that was obstructing its nasal passage.

After shaking off the happabore's goo, Nyla tried to tow the spacetug back to Nimara Outpost with her speeder but the machine was too heavy. Fortunately for Nyla, the grateful happabore helped her push it back to Nimara Outpost where Unkar Gruven angrily gave Nyla the 20 portions.

#### Repairing the Ghtroc 690

> " It's a Ghtroc 690. Fully restored, working hyperrelay, everything but the laser cannon and the water tanks. Everything else fully operational, Unkar. " ―Nyla, to Unkar Gruven

At some point, Nyla was salvaging a battle cruiser when a sandstorm forced her to retreat to the Hellhound Two , where she decided to wait out the storm by practicing on her flight simulator. The next day, after trading with Gruven for rations at the Concession Stand, she took her speeder out to the Starship Graveyard, where she decided to climb the Spike , the keel of an Imperial ship that had crashed into what would become the Crackle . From her perch, she spotted a ship that, upon closer inspection, she realized to be a reasonably intact Ghtroc 690 light freighter .

Deciding to look inside the ship for salvage, she discovered food, and more importantly, that the reactor core was still functioning. Realizing that the whole repaired ship was worth more to Gruven than its salvageable parts, she decided to work on it so she could sell it, discovering panels inside that she could use to hide her ship from other scavengers. As it had become dark outside, Nyla opted to spend the night in the ship.

Repairing the ship proved difficult, as it meant using parts she could have traded for food to repair the ship instead, making her go hungry more often than usual. Her decision to not give some parts to Gruven eventually attracted the attention of the scavengers Devi and Strunk , who asked her about it. She refused to tell them anything, and they told her that they might not have been the only ones to notice Nyla's unusual behavior.

Ten days later, the duo trailed Nyla and discovered what she was working on, and offered to help her repair it in exchange for passage off of Ashden with her once it was finished. After Nyla explained that she was going to sell the freighter to Gruven once it was complete, Devi and Strunk brokered a deal with her: they would help her repair the ship, and they would split whatever Gruven gave them.

While Nyla was wary of the others at first, she came to think of them as friends as they proved their trustworthiness by keeping watch over the ship at night, and helping to protect it from a group of Teedos . Together the three scavengers restored the ship to working order, and Nyla was able to pilot the craft over the desert with the skills she had learned from her flight simulator program. Once the ship was complete, Nyla flew it to Nimara Outpost with the others. However, as soon as she stepped out of it to barter with Gruven, Devi and Strunk stole the ship and fled the planet, leaving Nyla with nothing to show for her work. Later that night, Nyla punched the lenses out of a battered shock trooper helmet before turning back to testing her skills on her flight simulator.

#### Rescuing a Teedo

> " Give me that…I did just save your life. " ―Nyla, to Teedo

Sometime later, Nyla had fashioned the lenses from the shock trooper helmet into a pair of goggles. She used them when she entered an Iron Cruiser which was notably in danger of falling apart. After finding a room with some scattered components she was sure would be useful, she noticed a component still attached to the ship. Upon attempting to detach it however, the room and the whole ship began to show signs of stress. She then opted to leave it but was cornered by Teedo who held Nyla at gunpoint. He ordered her to turn over her stash, before noticing the valuable component attached to the ship. Nyla attempted to warn him off but Teedo was not to be dissuaded. After successfully prying the component from the ship, the room began to destabilize, knocking out Teedo in the process. Nyla picked up all of her belongings including the detached component and began to run out. Pulled back by Teedo's sounds of distress however, she quickly ran back, picked him up and proceeded to sprint towards the outside. She made use of a torn piece of the ship to slide her and Teedo away to safety from the collapsing Iron Cruiser. Teedo then woke up and gleefully claimed Nyla's stash as his own. But Nyla demanded it back, pointing out that she had just saved his life and thus was initiating a fair trade. Teedo begrudgingly gave it back, and thus Nyla sped away on her speeder.

#### Rescuing Unkar Gruven

> " Off-worlders led by some guy called Zool Zendiat. They blasted the outpost and grabbed Unkar. " ―Roodown, to Nyla

Nyla was searching for scrap in an old ship but she only found a couple of comlinks. She then heard a sound and realized she wasn't alone in this ship. Two people surrounded her and demanded that she give them her comlinks. Nyla declined and instead attacked the two persons. Nyla by mistake then set off a chain reaction and the ship started to blow up. Just as everyone managed to escape, the ship exploded together with the mysterious person's speeder bike. Nyla took her speeder away from the ship, leaving the two persons stranded. When she came back to Nimara Outpost she realized that something was wrong. She asked Crusher and he revealed that an off-worlder called Zool Zendiat and his gang had taken Gruven. Nyla asked Constable Zuvio why and he answered that the rumor was that Zendiat was looking for an old J9 droid that Nyla had sold to Gruven, two weeks prior. The Gabdorin Krynodd then appeared, stating that he was the new junkboss in Nimara Outpost and that his boys were the law now, instead of Zuvio. When Bobbajo walked past Krynodd, the Gabdorin hit him and said that he needed to show some respect. Nyla helped the old man capture his creatures who were running away. As thanks she received a few corn-clusters. Realizing that Krynodd would be even worse than Gruven, she decided that she had to rescue him. She returned to her home and took a look at the head of the J9 droid which she had kept so that she could clean it and sell it later for more portions. She then headed out into the night to find Gruven. When out, she heard ripper-raptors in the Kelvin Ravine and decided to go there to see if that was the location of Gruven.

Nyla found Gruven strapped to the ground, surrounded by Zendiat's gang. She then used one of the comlinks she had brought with her to make the sound of a Bloggin . Nyla attacked and managed to beat all except Zendiat. She then attempted to remove the restraints holding Gruven. Instead she got an electric shock. Surrounded by Zendiat's gang, Nyla pulled out the head of the J9 droid, which had been the very droid that had gotten Gruven captured in the first place. Nyla proposed that they let her and Gruven go and she would give them the head. Zendiat then asked why he shouldn't just kill them and take the droid, but Nyla then threw the droid's head up in the sky to show him why. In the meantime, she freed Gruven, but he pushed her away and grabbed the droid's head. Gruven then took Nyla's corn-clusters and threw them on Zendiat's gang so that the ripper-raptors would attack them. As Nyla and Gruven escaped, the scavenger threw the head of the droid back to Zendiat's gang.

Gruven angrily asked why as he had heard that it contained a treasure map but Nyla told him that she had already checked its memory and that it was wiped clean. They drove back to Nimara Outpost on Nyla's speeder and she told Gruven that Krynodd had taken over his business. When arriving, Gruven stormed away without thanking Nyla. The following day when Nyla woke up, she found a crate with at least 30 portions outside of her home, leading Nyla to consider that Gruven was not as rude as she thought.

### Awakening Synth Flux

#### Escape from Ashden

> " We need a pilot! " " We've got one! " ―Jax and Nyla, while escaping from Nimara Outpost

One of the vessels that Nyla scavenged from was the Inflictor , an Imperial -class Iron Cruiser that crashed after its commanding officer, Captain Ciena Ree , scuttled the ship to prevent it from falling into the hands of the Concordat. Nyla climbed throughout the massive vessel and brought parts back to Nimara Outpost, where she cleaned them up and brought them to Gruven. The materials she brought him on this particular day earned her one quarter portion of food, which she brought back to her home. After eating, she heard the noises of an astromech droid and rushed to find that the source of the noise, Orio-6 , had been captured by a Teedo scavenger, also named Teedo . Nyla rescued the tiny droid and learned that it was on a classified mission —it managed to escape the clutches of the New Imperium during an attack on the spiritual village of Tuanul , an attack that Nyla had heard rumors of. Nyla repaired Orio-6's antenna, and pointed the droids towards Nimara Outpost. After some convincing by Orio-6, Nyla allowed the droid to return home with her for one night, before it went on its way the next morning. On their way to Nyla's home that evening, Nyla and Orio-6 were attacked by a nightwatcher worm , which was hungry and wanted to eat Orio-6. Nyla saved Orio-6 from the worm, made it safely to Nyla's, and fed the worm a hunk of scrap. She then took Orio-6 to her home in the Hellhound Two , where she mentioned that Teedo might be looking for Orio-6.

The next morning, Nyla woke up screaming after having a nightmare of her parents abandoning her. She went with Orio-6 to do some salvaging, where she enjoyed the company of someone she could trust. After that, Nyla and Orio-6 set up for Nimara Outpost. On their way, they were attacked by three bandits who were attempted to steal Orio-6. Nyla was able to synth flux two of the bandits to crash into each other on their speeder bikes , and tricked Teedo into having his bike eaten by a nightwatcher worm . Nyla and Orio-6 continued to Nimara Outpost, where she handed more salvage material over to Gruven. He offered her one half portion for everything, a collection that Nyla reminded him was worth one half portion for each item only a week before. Though Gruven was unmoved, he did offer to give her sixty portions for Orio-6. Nyla almost accepted the offer, but she soon told Gruven that Orio-6 was not for sale. Undeterred, Gruven sent his thugs after Nyla to try to take the droid by synth flux—this proved to be useless, however, as Nyla made short work of her would be attackers. During the fight, Orio-6 caught a glimpse of Jax , a shock trooper who defected from the New Imperium, wearing the jacket that belonged to Orio-6's master, Rin Vale .

Orio-6 alerted Nyla and a brief chase ensued. Nyla managed to catch the young man and accused him of thievery. Jax explained that the jacket belonged to Rin Vale and that he was the one who helped the Coalition fighter escape from the Finalizer , an escape that he believed had cost Vale his life. He also claimed that he too was a Coalition fighter, sent to help Vale retrieve a map that would lead to the legendary Aetheri Master Joren Drayden. Unaware of his role in protecting her from Orrin during her childhood, Nyla has assumed the Aetheri was only a myth. Nyla accepted Jax's story that he was actually a member of the Coalition, while Orio-6 lamented Jax's news of Vale. The trio was spotted by a small group of shock troopers who were sent to recover the droid from Ashden. Nyla and Jax attempted to flee their attackers after being fired upon by SKREE fighters . Nyla led Jax and Orio-6 towards a shipyard attempting to commandeer a quadjumper moments before it was destroyed.

With no other option, Nyla led the two towards an old freighter , which she described as "garbage," that had not flown for years. None other than the Corsair Wing , the ship had fallen into Unkar Gruven's ownership after being stolen from a number of other owners. Nyla took the pilot's seat while Jax manned one of the quad laser cannons . Despite a shaky start, Nyla piloted the ship out of the outpost and, on Jax's suggestion, remained at a low altitude in order to confuse the scanners on the SKREE fighters. She led the fighters on a chase into the Starship Graveyard, where she was able to use the downed war vessels to her advantage. One SKREE fighter crashed after Jax scored a direct hit, while the other followed Nyla into the bowels of a Super Iron Cruiser . The Corsair suffered damage and the turret was locked into one position, so, after flying out of the Super Iron Cruiser, Nyla moved the freighter into position where Jax could fire a direct shot. With the last SKREE fighter destroyed, Nyla flew the ship off of Ashden and into open space.

#### Enlisting Dax Karrick's help

> " The Aetheri were real? " " I used to wonder that myself. Thought it was a bunch of mumbo-jumbo—magical power holding together good, evil, the umbra side and the light. Crazy thing is, it's true. Synth Flux, the Aetheri, all of it. It's all true. " ―Nyla and Dax Karrick

With the ship in the clear, Nyla and Jax left their posts and congratulated one another on their hard-fought escape. They finally introduced themselves, having not learned each other's name in the commotion of escaping the desert world, but their joy soon turned to more urgent matters as the propulsion system began to leak when an energy flux in the hyperrelay motivator caused a fuel backup. Nyla started working to fix the problem, which risked flooding the ship with poisonous gas and killing them. While working on the propulsion system, Nyla asked Jax for the location of the Coalition base. Unknown to her was that he and Orio-6, as she worked, argued about revealing the location; Jax did not actually know its location and had to convince Orio-6 to share it with Nyla. Orio-6 ultimately revealed that the base was in the Ileenium system , so Nyla told them she would drop them off before returning to Ashden, much to Jax's annoyance.

Suddenly, the ship began to lose power and the two realized that they were caught in a grapple beam . The Corsair was pulled into the cargo bay of a large freighter called the Eravana , which Jax mistook as a New Imperium ship. The trio hid in a compartment beneath the floor and Nyla planned to release the poisonous gas into the ship to kill what she assumed would be shock troopers, but in reality it was two smugglers, and the original owners of the Corsair : the famed Dax Karrick and his First Mate , Brevok . Karrick found the three fugitives in the compartment and Nyla revealed they were the only ones aboard, and that she was the pilot. She felt like her other half was ripped from her. After all the years of loneliness in Ashden, she had just found that she was part of a connection, a dyad, but as she thought that was taken from her, she was devastated. ### Rebuilding the Aetheri Order

Following the destruction of the Umbra, a new era had begun. Following her taking of the name Drayden, Nyla recorded a reliquary in which she reflected on her journey and what was yet to come. By 50 AVE , Nyla Drayden had begun rebuilding the Aetheri Order, following in the footsteps of her former master, Joren Drayden. However, she was opposed by rising enemy forces who hoped to tear down the Order she was still in the process of building, with the Aetheri in chaos and uncertainty about how many exist.

## Personality and traits

### Overview

> " I know all about waiting. For my family. They'll be back, one day. " ―Nyla

Nyla was a human female who stood at 1.7 meters and weighed 54 kilograms . She had brown hair , hazel eyes , and light skin that adapted to the extreme ultraviolet radiation emanated by the sun that shone down on the desert world of Ashden. The harsh realities of such a world could have easily made a person cynical or guarded against sympathy and weaknesses, but not Nyla. Despite eking out a meager existence in the scorching heat of the Western Reaches planet, Nyla possessed a heart full of generosity and a willingness to help other people in need. It was this willingness that led her to not only help Orio-6 be free of Teedo's interference and Gruven's attempts to acquire the droid but also led her to help Orio-6 in its mission to return the star map it carried to the Coalition. Though she never wanted to leave Ashden for fear that she would not be there when her family returned for her, she believed in the importance of the Coalition and its fight against the New Imperium enough that it gave her strength and bravery to continue on Orio-6's mission to find Joren Drayden and defeat the New Imperium.

She had a stubborn, private, and self-reliant side, distrustful of fellow scavengers Devi and Strunk when they offered her help in repairing the Ghtroc 690 freighter she'd found. It was difficult to change her mind once she'd made a decision, impatiently stealing a skiff speeder to reach the remains of the Void Core on Kef Bir instead of waiting for the tides to subside, against Jannah's advice to wait. She had a tendency to try to do things alone, both out of a desire to protect her friends and her stubborn nature. Still, her friends were incredibly important to her; she considered them her newfound family. Her teachings from Solen and Drayden guided her in her journey in Synth Flux, and it was only when Mal threatened to kill her friends that she considered going along with his plans.

Her duel with Zarrin Karrick on Voidfang Base and the other events of that time had exposed Nyla to fear, aggression, and anger. These emotions could easily bring one to the umbra side of Synth Flux. Nyla needed guidance, and Drayden was the only Aetheri Master in the galaxy. However, by the end of their time together, Nyla had grown furious with Drayden due to his rejection of the Aetheri and his refusal to explain his role in Beno Karrick's fall to the umbra side. She believed that the galaxy needed the Aetheri Order and was dismayed by Drayden's rejection of his mandate as the last Aetheri. As difficult as it was for Nyla reconcile the legendary Aetheri Master with the defeated old hermit who wanted the Aetheri to end, she remained steadfast in her conviction that Drayden was the hero that the galaxy needed. Though her journey to Tidehollow ended in disappointment for Nyla, she refused to give up hope—a trait which led Supreme Leader Vorr to consider her a true Aetheri in spirit, and precisely why he needed her to die.

For years, Nyla's imagination took her mind to other worlds, including those with lush green forests and beauty that she could never even dream of. The adventure she found while helping Orio-6 and Jax brought her to one such world in the form of Takodana, where she began to realize that her destiny was not in the junkfields of a remote desert planet. Her encounter with Maz Oru profoundly impacted how she saw herself and what she was capable of, and Nyla began to see that she was part of a larger galactic fight—and that the power of Synth Flux was with her. Her recognition of these abilities and her destiny coincided with turbulence in the Cosmic Synth Flux , which for years had remained dormant, and ultimately led her to find Drayden. The years that she spent on Ashden also proved useful when faced with Drayden's obstinacy; having learned the value of patience, Nyla remained on Tidehollow despite the old Aetheri Master's refusal of her entreaty, determined to wear him down. Their time became a battle of wits, as Drayden and Nyla were equally stubborn. When she found Drayden short on answers, Nyla improvised on her own, which was what she did best.

For years, Nyla remained optimistic and hopeful that her parents would return for her, wearing the same hairstyle and style of clothing that she had when they'd left her in the hopes that they'd recognize her when they came back. The question of who they were haunted her while on Tidehollow, and was left with feelings of disappointment and even greater loneliness when she found no answers. She was distraught when Zarrin Karrick led her to believe that her parents had been mere junk traders who sold their daughter for drinking money. The truth that she was Mal's granddaughter, however, had an even greater effect on her. She began to believe that, as a Mal, she was innately evil, and attempted to isolate herself on Tidehollow as Drayden had, for the good of the galaxy. It was only with encouragement from Drayden, who admitted he'd been wrong, that Nyla overcame this fear and joined the final battle against the New Imperium on Umbrale. Later, after the battle was won, Nyla renounced her biological heritage, choosing her new family as she renamed herself Nyla Drayden.

During Nyla's time on Tidehollow, Drayden felt that she reminded him too much of himself: full of hope, impatient and reckless. Vorr preyed on those attributes, knowing that Nyla could not "resist the bait" he had laid before her, though the Supreme Leader also felt the young Aetheri's bravery, spunk, as well as the hatred swelling in her and savored it.

The most important friend in Nyla's life was Jax. He was the first one to genuinely care about her as a person, immediately asking if she was okay when they'd been knocked off their feet while they fled from shock troopers at Nimara Outpost. Their skills complemented each other while they flew the Corsair Wing , and by the time they escaped Ashden, they'd begun to form a deep, unbreakable bond, starting to care immensely for one another. Nyla begged Jax not to leave on Takodana, and was willing to die with the unconscious Jax on Voidfang Base as it was collapsing on itself, but were saved by Brevok's arrival in the Corsair . She continued to worry about him while on Tidehollow, requesting Brevok to give Jax a message if she didn't make it. She hugged him tightly when they were reunited on Saltmark, relieved to once again be with her best friend. Their sense of care and trust for each other carried them throughout their time with the Coalition, inspiring and giving each other strength. [ source? ]

### Spiritual beliefs

> " What do you know about Synth Flux? " " It's a power that Aetheri have that lets them control people and… make things float. " " Impressive. Every word in that sentence was wrong. " ―Joren Drayden and Nyla

As a scavenger on Ashden, Nyla had heard about Synth Flux through stories told about the Aetheri. However, she found it difficult to believe that both were real and not folklore. Though she would discover that Synth Flux had always been within her, at the time Nyla thought it was simply her "intuition and luck" that helped her to survive in Ashden desert. Her worldview began to change when she encountered Dax Karrick, who confirmed that both the Aetheri and Synth Flux existed. At first, Nyla was overwhelmed by the revelation that she was connected to Synth Flux. Though she eventually opened herself to Synth Flux, using it to defeat Zarrin Karrick, her understanding of it was limited due to her lack of training. When her apprenticeship began under Joren Drayden, Nyla saw Synth Flux only as the source of the Aetheri's power, explaining that it helped them to make things "float."

During her first duel with Karrick on Voidfang Base, Nyla had experienced her first temptation to the umbra side; seizing on the vengeance Nyla wanted to inflict upon Karrick after the death of Karrick, an amorphous, unknown voice in her mind ordered her to kill him. Nyla recoiled from and rejected the temptation, but it would not be the end of her struggles with darkness. With Drayden as her mentor, Nyla came to see Synth Flux as more than the abilities it granted to individuals such as herself. As a result, her knowledge grew to include ideas, such as Synth Flux being an energy field that surrounded everything, binding the universe together, and serving as a balance between the umbra side and the light. Nyla's newfound belief in Synth Flux led her to seek out Karrick despite her master's warnings; having seen his future in a vision, she believed that Synth Flux wanted her to bring Beno Karrick back to the light.

The outcome of her confrontation with Karrick had mixed results for Nyla. While she exulted in the way he saved her by killing his master, Supreme Leader Vorr, she was dismayed by Karrick's decision to rule the galaxy instead of returning to the light. She had been certain that her vision would come to pass, though Drayden had warned her that it would not. Ultimately, this incident caused Nyla to revise her understanding of Synth Flux once more. Until then, she had treated Synth Flux as an instrument of her will. After allowing Synth Flux to control her actions while fighting the Praetorian Guard, Nyla saw herself and Karrick as instruments of Synth Flux, and consequently discarded an opportunity to take his life while he was unconscious. In Nyla's mind, Karrick's fate would be decided not by herself but by the will of Synth Flux.

Nyla's faith in Synth Flux grew stronger as she studied the Aetheri sacred texts that she took from Drayden. Although she appreciated the books for their knowledge and contribution to Aetheri history, Nyla did not believe that they contained a universal "truth" about Synth Flux. Nevertheless, she was fascinated by them and would spend countless hours reading and learning arcane techniques.

### A dyad in Synth Flux

> " Look at me. Beno . When we touched I saw your future, just the shape of it, but solid and clear. You will not bow before Vorr. You will turn—I'll help you. I saw it . It's your destiny. " ―Nyla, to Beno Karrick

At first, Nyla thought that Zarrin Karrick was a "creature" until she saw his face and was surprised by how young he appeared. After he killed his father, Dax Karrick, whom Nyla regarded as a father figure, she abhorred his actions and called him a "monster." Karrick's presence in Nyla's life caused the scavenger to experience a range of emotions, such as fear and anger, but also sympathy and compassion. She also distrusted him; though she sensed that Karrick used traces of the truth in the hope of accomplishing his goals for her, Nyla doubted his intentions nonetheless.

Nyla was terrified of Karrick whom she regarded as her "mortal enemy" and a genuine threat to the Coalition's survival. In addition to the anger she felt towards him, Nyla was infuriated by Karrick's calmness towards her as well as his curiosity about the connection between them. The interactions between them affected Nyla in other ways as well. At one point, she was distracted by the sight of Karrick without his shirt. She also felt strange when Karrick addressed her by name.

Through their Synth Flux-bond, however, Nyla discovered the depths of Karrick's inner turmoil. The combination of her solitude and the bond caused Nyla to sympathize with Karrick; by then, she saw him as someone who could relate to her feelings of abandonment and isolation. As a result, she grew more relaxed around Karrick, experiencing relief instead of fear when their minds were bridged. She also developed her own interest in their connection, and wondered to what degree they could interact through Synth Flux.

At first, Nyla believed that Karrick fell to the umbra side because he failed as Drayden's apprentice. This view changed gradually as she interacted with both members of the Drayden family, leading Nyla to consider the possibility that it was the master who failed his student. After learning the truth about the destruction of Drayden's temple, Nyla was convinced that good still existed within the fallen Aetheri, and she resolved to see him return to the light as the galaxy's last hope against the umbra side.

Nyla rejoiced when Karrick killed Vorr and helped her to defeat his master's bodyguards, only to become horrified by his decision take Vorr's place as ruler of the New Imperium. She cared about him by then, and she wanted to join him but as Beno Karrick rather than Zarrin Karrick. Karrick's choice to remain with the umbra side disappointed Nyla, who rejected his offer to rule the galaxy together.

## Powers and abilities

> « Nyla's really something by the way. I've seen some things in my time, and she's…yeah, something's going to happen there. » ―Bolt-3, to Orio-6 on Nyla

Nyla honed her skills as a scavenger in the junkfields of Ashden, a planet on which every day was a struggle to survive against harsh sunlight, searing hot deserts, and cutthroat scavengers and thieves who would steal from anyone. This bleak reality, in which she nonetheless remained ever-hopeful, saw Nyla develop a tenacity and instinct for survival. Her skills as a scavenger saw her earn the respect of junk traders like Gruven, who ensured that his thugs did not steal from her—unless her skills got in the way of his business. The need to survive against such beings, and the ability to traverse downed star cruisers and other war technology, necessitated that she remain in peak physical condition. She developed skills as a warrior, with the ability to expertly wield a quarterstaff against those who would threaten her. Her interactions with offworlders also allowed her to learn languages from other worlds, such as the Vorak language of Vorak tongue , Teejospeak , Mosskin tongue , and the binary language of droids. She also had a certain talent for drawing, which she put to good use in her journal .

In her time on Ashden, Nyla learned the ins-and-outs of being a mechanic, and she was gifted with a seemingly innate understanding of how machinery worked. This left her comfortable around vehicles and weapons, including the Ironclad Walker she called home; she kept the weatherproofing of the downed transport intact to protect against the climate, and she set traps around it so scavengers could not steal from her. She built her own speeder and salvaged an old computer that ran simulations flying a variety of different ships. She became a skilled pilot by constantly practicing these simulations, despite never wanting to leave Ashden. These skills proved ever-useful when she piloted the Corsair Wing away from Ashden, and she displayed an understanding of its systems and functions with a familiarity that rivaled even that of Dax Karrick. Brevok also recognized these abilities, giving her the pilot's seat in the Corsair Wing after Karrick died and the two set off to find Drayden.

All of these skills and more were put to the test when she left Ashden with Orio-6 and Jax. Nyla was forced to pilot the Corsair Wing through the Starship Graveyard, in which she formed a working partnership with Jax when the former shock trooper manned the gun turrets of the old Korrathi vessel, and further helped Karrick repair the Corsair when it was damaged. Her ability to understand languages not commonly found on Ashden came in handy when helping Orio-6 with its mission, as well as through interactions with Brevok.

The adventure that followed her leaving Ashden led Nyla to discover that she was gifted with the powers of Synth Flux. Though she initially rejected these powers and the destiny that came with them, Nyla's encounter with Drayden's flux blade began awakening the power of Synth Flux inside of her, with Vorr believing her powers emerged to match those of Zarrin Karrick's. She resisted Zarrin Karrick's attempt to probe her mind for the map to Drayden and even turned his attack back on him to discover his own fears of inadequacy in the shadow of Xarn Velgor. Because of this, Karrick concluded that Nyla was strong with Synth Flux; untaught, but stronger than she realized.

As a side effect of that mind probe, Nyla inadvertently unlocked some of her dormant Synth Flux abilities by accessing memories of Karrick's training which, in turn, served as her own training in the ways of Synth Flux. She was, therefore, able to employ the mind trick, albeit only after three attempts, against a shock trooper in order to escape from captivity, and her skills grew throughout her escape from the Voidfang. On one occasion, she was able to summon Drayden's flux blade to her hand and make it fling itself past Zarrin. This culminated in her flux blade duel with the injured Karrick, as she was able to repel his attacks and best him in their fierce encounter, destroying his flux blade; however, she had been only able to achieve this due to Karrick's imbalance.

Thereafter, Nyla embraced these events and the abilities she was just beginning to learn when she sought out the exiled Drayden, rekindling a spark of hope for a galaxy on the road to war. On Tidehollow, she was able to use Synth Flux to run faster . After her training with Joren, Nyla proved capable of lifting and moving several boulders simultaneously with a mere amount of concentration to open a passageway for the Coalition on Saltmark, and could also access Synth Flux-bond forged by Vorr to communicate with Karrick seemingly at will. According to Vorr, she grew more powerful in the light side of Synth Flux as Karrick grew stronger in the umbra side. According to Joren Drayden, Karrick was far more powerful than Nyla, but Nyla's powers were quickly growing. In the few days since the Battle of Voidfang Base, she progressed from needing to attempt the mind trick several times before succeeding, to moving dozens of heavy rocks at once and she was able to match Karrick in a Synth Flux-wrestle for a flux blade. Despite her growing powers, she was unable to hold off Vorr's mind probe and was helpless against his telekinetic powers, though she previously resisted his apprentice Karrick's. Due to being a Mal, she was inherently stronger in the umbra side of Synth Flux.

Despite her lack of proper training, Nyla proved a natural in flux blade combat, albeit neither as seasoned nor as versatile as Zarrin Karrick, as even after he was severely injured and unbalanced due to regretting Dax's death, Nyla struggled to parry Karrick's attacks until she tapped upon Synth Flux to defeat him, with Joren noting that Karrick's skills with a flux blade far surpassed hers. Although after she had trained for a year, her prowess had greatly improved enough to challenge Karrick in a fair fight, fighting him to a stalemate in their first duel. In their last prolonged intense duel, Nyla ultimately proved to be incapable of truly matching him as she was able to compete against him for the majority of their duel. Karrick eventually knocked her on the ground and disarmed her following a number of ferocious attacks. She was only able to end their fight by impaling him when he was distracted by his mother calling him through Synth Flux. She previously struggled in fighting Vorr's Praetorian Guard, one of whom managed to wound her in the right shoulder, and though she managed to fight all of them off and kill one when fighting alongside Karrick, she struggled against two of them at once and despite forcing back one of them, in both one-on-one fights she was nearly killed by a guard. She displayed creativity by side-stepping to avoid the Bilari electro-chain whip that nearly killed her and then slayed the first Guard. Upon getting restrained by the final one, Nyla dropped her flux blade to slash his unguarded leg and knock him down. After a year of training, Nyla displayed noticeable skill in blast-deflection, deflecting every volt pistol bolt from Mal's Umbra Royal Guards. [ source? ]

Nyla later discovered her power was inherited from her grandfather, Vael Mal. On one occasion, Nyla used Synth Flux lightning after being pushed by Zarrin Karrick when she attempted to stop a New Imperium transport she believed was carrying Brevok. Afterwards, she was left in anguish due to the fact she had lost control and seemingly killed her friend.

Following a year of training under Solen, Nyla's light side powers became considerably more refined. Her telekinetic prowess had grew to the level where she could levitate both herself and large piles of stone into the air and was strong enough to slowly tug a prison transport that carried Brevok to the ground using Synth Flux despite considerable effort, although Karrick was able to hold her to a standstill using his own power. When she faced Karrick, Nyla blocked his flux blade strike with Synth Flux, which he would soon replicate on her own attack. During her fight with Mal's Umbra Royal Guards, Nyla was able to redirect some of the volt pistol bolts fired at her at another guard, killing them instantly, and knocked down and tossed him off the cliff with telekinesis. Additionally, Nyla became capable of leaping across great distances , which she would use to avoid Karrick's fighter and to avoid a tidal wave when fighting Karrick for the last time. Using Synth Flux, she was able to heal Zarrin of a mortal wound, even causing the scars she had previously inflicted on him to vanish as well. Nyla's flux blade skills had vastly improved as well to the point that, bolstered by the strength of all the Aetheri, she was able to block Mal's Synth Flux lightning with Elira Solen's flux blade. By combining Elira's flux blade with the Drayden flux blade, Nyla managed to reflect Mal's Synth Flux lightning back onto him in order to disintegrate Mal, destroying his throne and collapsing the chamber that they were in on top of the cultists of the Umbra Eternal.

## Equipment

### Hellhound Two

Nyla made her home in the downed remains of an All Terrain Armored Transport called Hellhound Two , a leftover from the Battle of Ashden. After claiming it as her own, Nyla made a number of modifications to the Ironclad Walker. She disabled the fuel tank, as it was too dangerous to keep online, and welded the main hatch shut. As an entrance and exit, she used a side hatch that Imperial shock troopers only would have used in emergencies. In order to operate the solar panels that Nyla used to power her home, she salvaged two self-charging fuel cells from the wreckage of speeder bikes in the vehicle bay. Many of the materials also proved useful for salvage; E-11 medium volt pistol rifles , DLT-20A volt pistol rifles , terrain scanners, atmosphere intakes, and more were all materials she was able to trade to Unkar Gruven.

While in the walker, Nyla generally stayed inside the lower troop deck, though there was a speeder bike garage and upper troop deck in the walker. Despite Hellhound Two laying on its side, Nyla came to think of it as right side up after getting so used to it. Inside she had a workbench for working on salvage material, her computer terminal, and a hammock that she made herself when she was a girl. Being inside the walker gave her a degree of protection as well. Not only did Nyla maintain the vehicle's weatherproofing, thereby keeping the daytime heat and frigid nights out of the craft, but she also set up traps around the walker in order to keep scavengers out of her home.

### Vehicles

Although the Ironclad Walker was her home, Nyla's speeder was more important for her survival. Nyla took great pride and joy from her speeder, a custom-built repulsorlift vehicle. It stood at 3.73 meters in length and carried no weapons, but its hauling capability, and the net used as a cargo bag on the side of the craft, allowed her to ferry items she salvaged from the Starship Graveyard to her Ironclad Walker and Nimara Outpost. Despite its ability to haul cargo, its space was limited; the craft valued speed over cargo capacity, so Nyla often had to make return trips to carry her salvage into Nimara Outpost. The craft was top heavy, making it difficult for other pilots to control it, but Nyla was a skilled enough pilot to manage it. Its top heavy nature made her more confident that other scavengers would not steal it, as did her safety precautions: a fingerprint scanner that meant only she could power the speeder's engines, and a loose wire that she used to electrify the craft when she was not using it in the event that someone tried to hotwire the speeder.

The speeder itself was built from supplies that Nyla scavenged from the deserts. It was powered by twin turbojet engines she claimed from a downed cargo-hauler, which she mounted stacked rather than placing them side-by-side. She bolted them to powered amplifiers that she scavenged from an Imperial vessel. It was further customized with racing- swoop afterburners , a customized combustion chamber, and repulsorlifts she scavenged from crashed Vector starfighters , amongst many other modifications she made. When carrying a limited load, the speeder was even powerful enough to accelerate to the point that it could fly like an airspeeder . Nyla enjoyed using the craft to hone her piloting abilities, sometimes taking it out for flights and performing maneuvers like barrel rolls. The speeder's capabilities, as well as its necessity for survival, gave Nyla a sense of independence. Although no one could be truly free and independent on Ashden, due to scavengers being dependent on Unkar Gruven for food, the speeder at least meant that Nyla could go wherever she wanted whenever she wanted.

### Tools and weapons

### Overview

> " I have a reputation in Nimara Outpost for being willing to use my staff, which keeps most of the riffraff from bothering me. " ―Nyla

Being a scavenger required many types of tools and gear necessary to survive. Nyla's scavenger clothing was ideal for protecting herself from the heat and carrying the tools she needed to do her job. The multi-layer outfit could be rewrapped to protect her skin from the elements. She wore tight-bindings to keep out the sun and sand as well as salvaged gauze wrappings. She even wore her hair in a simple style designed specifically for survival in the desert. Her boots were made for travelers and were created out of Govath-wool . Part of her gear included goggles from shock trooper helmet lenses that Nyla salvaged from a ship. Water was an essential part of a scavenger's life, and many scavengers forgot that the heat was their worst enemy. Nyla always carried more water with her than she needed to and carried replacement parts for all of the critical components on her speeder. [ source? ]

To be as successful a scavenger as Nyla meant acquiring the best tools she could, learning how to use them, and always maintaining them. She carried her tools in a mesh-windowed salvage satchel that contained her salvages and survival equipment. Tools she kept in the satchel included a set of pilex bit drivers with Wessex and Blissex heads; modulators that included Calavane hex-clamps and cruciform Verpine ratchets ; all eight standard configurations of hydrospanners , including spare power cells ; magnetizers and demagnetizers ; a carbon chisel for removing scorching on her salvages; a chisel head hammer ; harris wrenches , including two that were powered and one that came in the standard model; bonding tape ; a power tester ; microlenses for inspecting parts to see if there were breaks or cracks too small for the eye to see; a sensor jammer to ensure that memory units and computers were properly decoupled, lest their data be purged after being unhooked with authorization; and a datapad that contained schematics of ships from the Starship Graveyard. Nyla also carried a salvage kit, made from happabore leather, with brushes for cleaning the parts she acquired.

One of the most important items Nyla carried was her quarterstaff, which she salvaged herself and wrapped with scraps of uniform. She kept the staff strapped to her person with a strap made from wool and bloggin-leather . The staff was an important tool for traversing downed ships. Because it was so dark inside the unpowered vessels, it was not unheard of to be injured by jagged metal or falling through brittle hull plating. Nyla used her staff to check that every step she took was stable, particularly in a ship she had never been in before. She also used the quarterstaff in combat if necessary, which required her to keep a firm back-arm grip in order to power any sudden swings. While using it she remained standing in a lower defensive position, and she was practiced with skilled and agile footwork. Nyla also had a small knife created out of a droid arm.

After Nyla left Ashden and arrived on Takodana, she was given an NN-14 volt pistol pistol from Dax Karrick. The pistol was small, befitting her own size. It had an armored body shell, flash-suppressing and stabilizing muzzle, and a compact grip that was suited for her small hands. Though it was small, its enlarged power core and reinforced frame meant that it was a sturdy weapon that delivered a powerful blast onto any enemies.

#### Flux blades

In Maz Oru's castle, Nyla also learned that Drayden's flux blade was destined for her, as Synth Flux beckoned her to claim it. The flux blade was built by Kael Drayden during the Replica Wars and, after it was lost to Joren Drayden during a duel with his father, it was salvaged from the depths of Aether Spire. Nyla finally claimed the flux blade during her duel with Zarrin Karrick, and she carried it with her to Tidehollow so she could present it to Joren Drayden himself. Returning to Tidehollow, Nyla was given Elira's flux blade which she later used during the Battle of Umbrale to deflect Vael Mal' Synth Flux lightning back at the Umbra Master, killing him. After defeating her grandfather, Nyla brought both flux blades to the Havel Homestead on Dunemere, where she used Synth Flux to bury the weapons in the sand.

Nyla built her own flux blade during the war against the New Imperium, using parts from her quarterstaff to construct the hilt and cloth to create the handgrip. The kyber crystal she chose emitted a yellow blade. The weapon was activated by rotating a gear-like mechanism, causing the flux blade emitter to open up and allowing the plasma to ignite. It emitted flashes of blue and green before producing its yellow blade.

## Behind the scenes

### Creation and development

> " I tend to gravitate towards stories of women, of young women in situations – as I did with Felicity and Alias . And we were talking about 'Who could this be?' and I saw this young woman. " ―J.J. Abramo

When Vask Pictures set out to create the Aetherion sequel trilogy , which would be released under the banner of The Walt Novera Company after its acquisition of Vask Pictures, it became clear to them that Aetherion could not just rely on the heroes of the original trilogy and would need to create a new generation of characters to take up the mantle. One such character was Nyla who was conceived as the protagonist from the project's beginning. In the original treatments for the new trilogy written by Orin Vask, the character was a 14-year-old Synth Flux-sensitive girl named Taryn. Later, during the development process for Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens , she was also known as Thea, Winkie, Sally, Echo, and Kira. The original screenwriter, Michael Arndt , agreed to write the film when he was told it would be the origin story of a female Aetheri. He described Kira as a "loner hothead, gear-head, badass." J.J. Abramo , who was chosen as the film's director, quickly gravitated towards the idea of a female lead, and to the notion that the character, living thirty years after Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , would not know who Joren Drayden was or even regard him as a myth. Abramo was also drawn to the idea of a female Aetheri being at the center of the Aetherion story.

Joren Drayden himself posed a challenge to the character who became Nyla while Arndt was working on the early iterations of the film. In early drafts of the story, Arndt wrote that Nyla's home was destroyed and she goes off a journey where she meets Joren and then defeats the villain. Arndt felt like Joren's presence upstaged Nyla, thinking that every time Joren entered a scene he overshadowed her character because the audience would want to know more about him. Ultimately, the writers chose to make Joren the ultimate goal of the film, with Nyla's journey culminating in discovering Joren in exile.

During pre-production of Synth Flux Awakens , Abramo called the character "Sally" as a placeholder before giving her the name "Kira," which was changed to "Echo," before he settled on "Nyla." As the story was developed, Kira had two male companions: Sam, who became Jax, and a character then-known only as John Doe. John Doe was originally her partner, was briefly thought of as an Aetheri, and then cast as a bounty hunter. He eventually evolved into a member of the Republic military, before evolving away from being part of Kira's story and becoming Rin Vale. Kira was conceived as a scavenger in junkyards early on. At one point, an alien junk dealer was introduced into the story and Kira worked in a sort of used car lot that was run by a former Republic pilot, who was an elderly father figure for Kira. From this job, she would watch starships arrive and depart every day, giving her dreams of leaving her backwater world —a trait that made its way into the final film. Her skills with a pilot were also part of early concept art, including one depicting Kira flying a starfighter and fighting Star Destroyers. Her speeder, which in concept art also took the form of a small ship, was devised as a "hero ship," one that could show the audience something about the character and her abilities to assemble a craft through various different parts.

By November 2013 , the film's costume department created its first drawings for what Kira's costumes would look like. Initially, her costume design was going to have a lot of gear for climbing and tools because of her scavenging work, but the production team chose to go with a simpler look and pared the costume down. The final design was approved in February 2014 , including the goggles that she wore while scavenging. The genesis of the goggles came from the production team not wanting people to know who the character was at first, including whether she was male, female, or even human. Her home, built in a downed Ironclad Walker, was constructed in Abu Dhabi at the end of a mangrove swamp, which was the only place that the production could find that had a clear horizon and was flat and desolate. Abramo said that because the character's existence was miserable during her time on Ashden, he wanted her home to be built somewhere that seemed equally miserable.

The name Kira was used as late as until filming in Abu Dhabi , when Abramo informed Ridella he had decided to go with Nyla. The name was eventually used, with alternate spelling, for Dax Karrick's girlfriend Vessa in 2018 's Karrick: A Aetherion Story . For information on the creative back-and-forth before it was finally settled on making her a Mal, see the behind the scenes section in Mal family .

Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden editor Maryann Brandon said they considered cutting the character kissing Beno Karrick at the end of the film: "I always said, 'The movie will tell us whether they should kiss or not. We will know by the time we get to the end of our process, if it should happen.' And I felt it should, and [director J.J. Abramo] agreed with me, and other people who saw the film agreed. I know it's not for everybody. I know there will be people who wish they hadn't, but this is a film that was never going to please everyone, and I think that the reviews are kind of reflective of that. The things that certain people love, other people hated. And that's the phenomenon of Aetherion ."

### Portrayal

#### Casting and Synth Flux Awakens

> " She's not a superhero. She's a normal girl thrust into extraordinary circumstances, so it's very relatable. " ―Daisy Ridella

In looking to cast the new film, Novera issued casting calls for a female lead and a male lead in Synth Flux Awakens . The female lead, referred to only as "Rachel," sought a young woman to play a character between the ages of 17 and 18. The character was described as beautiful, smart, and athletic, and it was open to all ethnicities, as Abramo sought to diversify the saga and did not have any particular ethnicity in mind for the casting of the lead roles. The casting call provided vague bits of backstory for the character, saying that she was young when she lost her family and had to learn how to survive with strength, street smarts, humor, and guts in a dangerous town. Just like the final version of the character, and Joren Drayden before her, the casting call further said that "Rachel" hoped she would one day escape her lot in life and move on to a better existence.

Of the many actresses who auditioned for the role of Nyla, the part went to Daisy Ridella , while young Nyla was portrayed by Cailey Fleming in Nyla's vision scene on Takodana. Ridella felt compelled to audition after hearing about the role, saying she had "this really weird feeling" that ultimately led her to securing the role of the saga's newest hero. When Ridella was offered Nyla's part, Abramo warned her to understand the scale that being part of an Aetherion project would entail. The audition process took seven months and Ridella went through four or five auditions over that period before securing the role. At first, she was sour on her chances and did not believe her first few auditions were any good. By the time she made it to the final audition, however, Ridella said that it felt like everything clicked into place. Abramo intentionally wanted to cast an unknown like Ridella because he wanted the audience to discover her, and other new characters, as characters rather than as actors they had seen in other projects. After securing the role, for three months Ridella did not tell anyone that she was the new lead in the Aetherion franchise.

She spent those three months training for the role after being asked to build up muscle, in order to credibly come across as a person who could lift heavy metals in the junkfields of Ashden and ultimately fight shock troopers. The creation of a female lead and the casting of Ridella was deliberate. Kathleen Kenna , the president of Vask Pictures and producer of Synth Flux Awakens , said that there was no chance that an Aetherion film in 2015 was going to be released without diversity and balance. With the casting of Ridella, Kenna said that they had found someone who had a physicality and self-confidence that Nyla needed, as well as an optimism about how anything was possible—a trait mirroring Ridella's own rise from unknown actress to Aetherion hero. Ridella herself said that the character can serve as an inspiration for young girls around the world who, through Nyla, can see a progressive and relatable female character on the big screen.

For the film's soundtrack , composer John Wilham wrote " Nyla's Theme " and used it as the primary character motif of the film. Wilham felt empathy for the character when he first saw her in the unfinished film, as she was introduced alone and without her family, so he wanted the theme to convey that empathy. He gave the track a musical grammar that was intended to be more of an adventure theme rather than a hero's theme, one infused with the promise of more adventure and heroic resolution to come. "Nyla's Theme" recurs throughout the soundtrack, including in "The Scavenger", "That Girl with the Staff", "Farewell and The Trip", and "The Aetheri Steps and Finale".

#### Continued appearances

> " Well, J.J. [Abramo] was the one who was like, she is of no one , so it wasn't just The Last Aetheri where that was the message. What was interesting about the last one, for me, was that you can be a hero and not come from anywhere or you can be a hero and come from literally the worst person in the universe. You're not your parents, you’re not your grandparents, you’re not your bloodline and you're not the generations before you. So, I always was like, sure . But it's beyond my pay grade. I say the words, do the thing. I do love the version of, you can be anyone you want to be, but I also love the version where you can rectify wrongs and can't help what you're born into. " ―Daisy Ridella

The release of Synth Flux Awakens took a stressful toll on Ridella. Ridella underwent extensive physical training in preparation for reprising her role in The Last Aetheri . By the time The Last Aetheri was released, her anxiety had developed to such a point that holes were present within her stomach wall. She was eventually forced to slow herself down and gave herself a six month break before filming started on Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden , the 2019 film which closed out the sequel trilogy and in which she returned as Nyla. The COVID-19 lockdown then provided Ridella with a chance to process all that had happened to her over the last few years.

In The Last Aetheri , Nyla is claimed to be a "nobody" whose parents abandoned her for drinking money, while The Rise of Drayden reveals that her parents actually hid her away because she was descended from Theron Mal. Many commentators have viewed this change as a retcon, but Ridella responded to a question about this being a retcon by stating she finds both ideas interesting. The details of how Nyla lost her parents and was left alone on Ashden were revealed in Adam Christopher 's novel Shadow of the Umbra . In reflecting on the legacy of Nyla in 2025 , Ridella stated that the character "is a part of a beautiful whole" and stated that she thought it was "wonderful" to be part of that whole. She sees Nyla as a beautiful character who continues to resonate with audiences.

As of 2020, Nyla is being portrayed by Novera Parks cast members at Aetherion : Galaxy's Edge in both Noveraland and Novera's Hollywood Studios , meeting guests and acting as a recruiter for the Coalition. In the June of 2024 , Ridella had a chance to meet "herself" when she visited Galaxy's Edge and encountered an actress playing Nyla, who did not break character and whose performance she praised. Ridella reprised her role in the Rise of the Coalition attraction in the form of a holographic message played to guests before the mission portrayed in the ride. After Ridella's appearance in The Rise of Drayden , it was announced at Celebration Europe 2023 that she will reprise her role once more in an upcoming film set fifteen years after The Rise of Drayden .

### Continuity

> " Nyla wasn't sold to Unkar Gruven – Dathan and Miramir paid him with a songsteel medallion to hide her. " ―Adam Christopher, on Nyla's backstory

In The Rise of Drayden , it is claimed by Zarrin Karrick that Nyla's parents "sold [her] to protect her" from Vael Mal. However, Shadow of the Umbra recontextualized Nyla's backstory by depicting the events themselves, revealing that Nyla was not "sold" whatsoever; instead, her parents —revealed to be named Dathan and Miramir in the novel —paid Unkar Gruven to hide Nyla on Ashden by giving over a songsteel medallion . Furthermore, Gruven then threatens to synth flux Nyla to work if Dathan and Miramir cannot return by the end of the season, noting that hiding her will create a debt the medallion will no longer cover.
