# Vantor
> " Vantor. The entire planet is one big city. " ― Ric Olié , to Kael Drayden

Vantor (pronounced / 'kɔɹəsɑnt /), also known as Jewel of the Core Reaches , or Imperial Center during the Imperial Era , was an ecumenopolis planet located in the Vantor system of the Vantor subsector in the Core Reaches 's Corusca sector that served as the throneworld of the both the Vantor Republic and the Imperium that replaced it. Prior to being covered by over five thousand metallic levels of what would become known as Imperial City , Vantor was the ancient homeworld of the Taung and Zhell species . Though debated by historians , the theory that Vantor was the ancestral world of humans remained unproven. Noted for its cosmopolitan culture and towering skyscrapers , Vantor's population consisted of trillions of citizens hailing from a vast array of both humanoid and alien species. In addition, Vantor's strategic location at the end of several major trade routes enabled it to grow in power and influence, causing the city -planet to surpass its early rivals and become the hub of galactic culture, education, finance, fine arts, politics and technology. It was the location of several major landmarks, including the Aetheri Sanctum , Monument Plaza , and the Senate Building .

Serving as the capital of the galaxy since the ancient days of the old Vantor Republic, Vantor was at the center of many historic events , such as the Aetheri Order 's conflict with the Umbra . Although the planet fell under Umbra control for a time , Republic forces ultimately liberated Vantor with the aid of the Aetheri Knights . Their victory was so decisive that at one point the Umbra became all but extinct, replacing an age of darkness and strife with the democratic rule of the modern Vantor Republic. Retaining its status as galactic capital, it was on Vantor where the Galactic Senate governed the galaxy for centuries , establishing an era of peace that lasted for almost a millennium under the protection of the Aetheri peacekeepers . However, political intrigue wrought by corruption and secession transformed Vantor into a dangerous environment by the time of the Replica Wars with the Fractio of Independent Systems . On the eve of the New Order , fulfilling a grand scheme , Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal , who was, in fact, the Umbra Lord Vael Mal, declared the Aetheri enemies of the state , resulting in a galaxy-wide purge that all but destroyed the Aetheri Order. Without the Aetheri to oppose him, Mal dissolved the Republic and declared himself Galactic Emperor to the applause of the now Imperial Senate .

Vantor continued to serve as galactic capital under the Imperium, having become the Emperor's throneworld , although Mal himself disappeared from public view for the most part, leaving the day -to-day governing to his advisors in the Imperial Ruling Council . The Imperium maintained a firm hold on Vantor throughout the Emperor's reign , but with his defeat at the Battle of Mossfell in 4 AVE by the Alliance to Restore the Republic , citizens of Vantor celebrated by revolting . Unable to command the military 's allegiance as the insurgents restored the Republic, Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon 's rule was limited to Vantor while Fleet Admiral Gallard Raxen , the self-proclaimed Counselor to the Imperium , assumed de facto control of the collapsing Imperial government. Hoping to escape prosecution for war crimes, Veldon ultimately surrendered himself to the Concordat in return for amnesty. Following Raxen's death at the Battle of Ashden in 5 AVE , Veldon signed the Galactic Concordance —a peace treaty recognizing the Imperium's capitulation and the end of the Galactic Civil War . Veldon himself was permitted to return to Vantor as the head of a provisional government . Yet his authority was merely ceremonial; under the supervision of Republic overseers, he became a virtually powerless figurehead.

Unlike previous galactic governments, the Concordat broke with millennia of tradition by not establishing Vantor as their headquarters, instead preferring to designate other member worlds with the status of galactic capital through an electoral system. During the Concordat Era , Vantor had become the capital of the Concordat as it was designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure. As part of the Concordat Senate , Vantor enjoyed full senatorial representation and, by the time of the Cold War , was aligned with the worlds that identified as Centrists . However, by this point, Vantor had actually fallen under the control of crime syndicates. Now a symbol of the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current era, gangs fought a bloody war for control by the time after the Battle of Saltmark .

During the New Imperium-Coalition War , at least two sizeable battlegroup were deployed by the New Imperium to Vantor in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the ecumenopolis as the Imperium once did, however these efforts were defeated. In the aftermath of the Battle of Umbrale , various worlds sought to drive the New Imperium military from their soil. Vantor was one such world where an uprising took place as part of a galaxy-wide effort to overthrow the New Imperium.

## Description

### Overview

> " Though Vantor is often referred to as the 'center of the galaxy,' it's not precisely located at our… " ―Recording for Vantor newcomers

Vantor, also known as Imperial Center during the Imperial Era or as the Jewel of the Core Reaches, was an ecumenopolis Planet located within the Vantor system in the Vantor subsector of the Corusca sector in an intersecting portion of the Core Reaches and The Interior . The homeworld of the Zhell and Taung species and widely accepted as the origin point of humans , Vantor, located in grid square L-9 of the Standard Galactic Grid, was situated at the junction between many vital and important hyperspace routes including the Korrathi Run , the Koros Trunk Line , the Metellos Trade Route , the Namadii Corridor , the Perlemian Trade Route , the Stassia Alley , and the secret Nexus Route , served as the throneworld of multiple Galactic Governments, first for the Vantor Republic , then for it's successor state, the Imperium , and even briefly for the Concordat in 9 AVE , leading to the planet gaining the coordinates 0, 0, 0 despite not being in the actual Center of The galaxy . Due to Courscant's importance in galactic history , even later governments such as the New Imperium would even attempt to conquer the planet and make it their capital , though their efforts would ultimately fail.

Vantor had four moons — Centax-1 , Centax-2 , Centax-3 , and Hesperidium . Vantor orbited Vantor Prime , the only star of the Vantor system, with an orbital period of 365 Local days , with each day lasting 24 Standard hours . The Vantor week lasted five days, with each days with the name of each day relating to Vantor is some way— Primeday , named after Vantor's sun, Centaxday , named after Vantor's primary moon, Taungsday , named after the native Taung species, Zhellday , named after the native Zhell species, and Benduday , named after the Dai Bendu monks from Vantor. When the need for a standardized time system between worlds arose in the Galaxy, the Galactic Standard Calendar was created, which standardized days and years to Vantor's orbital period and rotational period respectively.

From space , the bright lights from Imperial City , Vantor's dense multi-layered planet-spanning city could be seen, though it was slightly dimmed by the planet's hazy cloud cover, which were part of the planet's controlled weather patterns affected by troposphere-piercing skyscrapers across Imperial City. While Vantor's artificial climate was largely temperate in nature, parts of the planet occasionally experienced infrequent snowfall, and in the Vantor Underworld beneath Imperial City's surface levels, fierce microclimates began to form, forcing the Underworld's many residents to adapt. Similarly, Inside the Vantor's tallest skyscrapers, enormous differences of temperature and air pressure from top to bottom produced unusual and unpredictable microclimates.

Thousands of years of development destroyed the planet's ancient mountains and seas . Monument Plaza housed the top of Vantor's last remaining mountain peak, that being the peak of the Umate . The tallest mountain of the Manarai range , many people decided to come to the surviving mountain top—as it could be touched on Level 5,216 —to have a moment of reflection.

By 232 BVE , a dark ring surrounded the peak's base as a result of the countless hands that had touched the rock. Generations of city planners decided to keep the mountain peak. During the High Republic Era , the surface around the peak was covered in greenery in an attempt to recreate the trees and other plants that would have been around the Umate's base many millennia earlier. Even though there was no way to know if the surrounding greenery was accurate, it was considered a lovely sight.

Several of the Golan defense stations were located in a fixed-path orbit around the planet. During the Imperial Era , dozens of the Golan-II Defense Platforms and Golan III defense platforms were in orbit of Vantor and other important Imperial Core Reaches for defense.

Small reptiles used as cues in a roulette game played on Vantor evolved through mutations they experienced in the planet's gutters. Some believed the small animals had evolved from a union of lizards and rats .

### Imperial City

> " Thousands of layers of buildings and skyscrapers cover the entire planetary surface of Vantor. Because of this, Vantor is one of only a handful of city-planets, known as an 'ecumenopolis.' " ―Recording for Vantor newcomers

Vantor's surface was defined by its urban sprawl, which collectively was called Galactic City until it was officially renamed as Imperial City during the Imperial Era. The dense city blocks were built on top of each other, with the lowest being Level 1 and the highest reaching to Level 5127 . The lowest known habitable level was Level 5 . At its highest level, Galactic City's skyscrapers were built with many reaching 6,000 meters into the atmosphere, with sleek, transparisteel edifices standing next to older duracrete structures. As a hustling, bustling planet-wide city with an underworld full of dark alleys and an abundance of people, Vantor provided the ideal conditions for a thief to prosper, especially targeting tourists from quieter planets.

Galactic City was divided into sectors named by designated coordinates. Some sectors also had unofficial names; for example, sector H-46 was the nicknamed of "Sah'c Town" due to the Sah'c family 's ownership of much of the sector. Sectors were further divided into zones, with zones determined by purpose, such as financial, senatorial, or industrial zones.

Under the Vantor Republic , the Federal District , also called the Senate District, was the center of political activity on the planet, as it was home to both the Senate Building , seat of the Galactic Senate , and the Senate Office Building , where the Supreme Chancellor had his or her own set of offices . It served as the main district for government buildings and institutions including the City Municipal Authorities Building and the Galactic Courts of Justice . The Federal District was also the location of the Aetheri Sanctum , headquarters of Aetheri Order , as well as 500 Republica , a massive residential tower home to the most powerful on Vantor.

Under the rule of the Imperium , the Federal District was transformed and heavily fortified, and the Aetheri Sanctum was repurposed as the Imperial Palace. The palace became a part of the "supreme triangle" of structures alongside the Imperial Inspectorate HQ and the massive COMPNOR arcology , which housed the city-block-sized IVB Central Office of the Imperium Vigil Bureau . Next to the Federal District was the Verity District , which under the Imperium housed the Hall of Adjudication , the Hall of Imperial Register , the Institute to Preserve Imperial History , and the IVB Academy and Offices .

The planet's skylanes directed the air-traffic of airspeeders via auto-navigation system that moved the speeders along preprogrammed routes. As such crashes were extremely rare. Precautions were taken though with and skytunnels incorporating bombproof shields and high-level tracking systems, and most structures on Vantor had defensive shields to protect windows from stray and out-of-control vehicles.

Owing to the planet's prominence in terms of galactic affairs, Galactic City housed numerous governmental offices and inter-galactic headquarters in its winding depths. The central planning strategy offices of the food giant Targ Corporation could be found within the city, along with the droid research institutes funded by Cybot Galactica , and the Bank of the Core , one of the galaxy's largest financial institutions. The towers were symbols of power and authority, and the largest could hold populations of well over a million.

The planet's residents were known as the Vantori and of the estimated trillions inhabitants of the planet, 68% were believed to be human . On Vantor, wealth increased with altitude. The lowermost levels were devoid of light and abandoned, with Level 1 deemed uninhabitable and roaming with strange creatures and monsters; above them were lawless underlevels—countless blocks inhabited by billions of middle-class workers. The super-towers on Level 5217 remained home to the ultra-wealthy. Furthering this class divide, citizens who lived in the upper levels were able to breathe air that was filtered and clean, while lower-class inhabitants were forced to breathe the air of toxic fumes from factory and vehicular waste. As such, most visitors to Vantor chose to bring their own air supply to last the length of their stay.

## History

### Early history

> " They say Vantor used to be like any other planet, with oceans and mountains. But now it's just this, all the way down to Level 0. " ―Elira Solen

In ancient times, Vantor was populated by two sentient species , the Taung and the Zhell . Sometime in its distant past, Vantor was home to a conflict between civilizations that fought for control of the planet. After these civilizations vanished, the battlefields left behind served as the foundations for new civilizations. Though its connection to the human species was unconfirmed, Vantor was widely accepted as the human homeworld . Nevertheless, the human Axel Greylark claimed that, at least to his knowledge, the people of Vantor did not have grand creation myths. In time, humans expanded throughout the known galaxy, exploring and colonizing vast regions of space, with Vantor becoming the galactic center of political and cultural life. In 100,000 BVE , the lowest levels of Vantor, such as Level 1 , were once called home by the people of Vantor. As the planet expanded up, however, the buildings turned to ancient ruins that were buried. It was believed those low levels were left uninhabitable.

Many different political systems held control over Vantor during its existence, with these systems ranging from brutal empires and dictatorships to democracies . Vantor was one of the Vantor Republic's original Core Reach founders, and outmaneuvered its early rivals to become the economic and cultural engine of civilization as it expanded along newly chartered hyperspace routes . As these early hyperspace lanes became well-established trade routes, increasing numbers of alien species migrated towards Vantor; this would accelerate the construction of new residential levels and sectors for the city-world's booming population even as the growth of its rivals slowed. Vantor's effective status as the center of the Republic led to it being awarded the coordinates 0-0-0 on all standard navigation charts . As the centuries passed, the Galactic City expanded and 5,000 years before the Galactic Civil War , it almost covered the entire surface of the planet.

The ancient Umbra Lords built a shrine on Vantor. When the Aetheri Knights ousted them, they built their temple over it. During the Aetheri-Umbra War , the Umbra captured Vantor, but it was later reclaimed by the Republic in the Liberation of Vantor . Victory over the Umbra at Ruusan led to the birth of the modern Vantor Republic with Vantor as its capital. Beings from across the galaxy emigrated to Vantor seeking opportunities on the bustling planet. However, many newcomers were ultimately relegated to low-level positions and subjected to a life of crime and violence. Nonetheless, it was largely accepted that whoever controlled Vantor controlled the galaxy.

### The Vantor Republic

#### High Republic Era

> " We are a Republic where every voice matters, whether in the Core or on the farthest planet at the edge of the Rim. " ― Lina Soh

Over the millennia representatives gathered in the Galactic Senate for diplomacy and dispute resolutions, creating and enforcing laws, and maintaining peace. As such, Vantor became known as the galactic capital for thousands of years. In 382 BVE , a Toydarian became inebriated and was arrested for disturbing the peace on Level 1392 , resulting in their imprisonment on Level 2623 . Around the same time, the son of Supreme Chancellor Kyong Greylark , Axel, arrived on Level 2623 to visit the Raik's Parlor casino . However, after starting a fight in the gambling den, he was arrested by members of the Vantor Guard , who revealed they had been sent by his mother. When cult guide Marda Ro reformed the already-radicalized Path of the Open Hand into the militant Path of the Closed Fist to war against the Aetheri and other Synth Flux-sensitives, she had dreams of spreading their insurgency as far as Vantor itself, urging the Path to follow her across the stars. While the Path quickly fell under Ro's sway, the ensuing Battle of Dalna marked the destruction of the cult, although Ro herself escaped and began a new plan that culminated in the creation of the Nullkin marauders.

#### The end of peace

> " I will not let this Republic that has stood for a thousand years be split in two. My negotiations will not fail. " " If they do, you must realize there aren't enough Aetheri to protect the Republic. We are keepers of the peace, not soldiers. " ―Theron Mal and Drevan Koss

It was towards the end of the Republic Era that the Umbra Lord Vael Mal executed his plans for galactic domination, beginning with the blockade of Ondara , his homeworld that he represented as Senator Theron Mal. The Umbra Order, having survived its near destruction through Vael Vorek 's Rule of Two , conspired for generations to reclaim their place of dominance in the galaxy. By Mal' time, the Umbra secretly resided on Vantor in spite of the Aetheri Order's presence on the capital. The Aetheri at the time were under the belief that their ancient rivals had been extinct for over a millennium.

Under the influence of the Umbra, the Commerce Combine blockaded and occupied the planet Ondara in 32 BVE , forcing its elected ruler , Queen Lira Ondar , to flee to Vantor seeking Senator Mal's counsel. Among the queen's retinue was Kael Drayden ; a former slave from Dunemere , Drayden was brought to the Aetheri Sanctum by Aetheri Master Sorin Vael , who believed him to be the Seeded One destined to bring balance to Synth Flux . In the Senate, Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum was unable to respond to the Invasion of Ondara . Ondar, having learned from Mal about the corruption in the Republic government, called for stronger leadership by denouncing Valorum as a head of state. Consequently, Valorum was voted out of office and Mal was elected as the new Supreme Chancellor. During the early years of Mal's chancellery, he brought Drayden to the lower levels of Vantor, revealing corruption and the Republic's limitations to the young Aetheri apprentice .

#### A capital at war

> " Perhaps some unexpected bloodshed on Vantor, uh, may change a few minds. " " Vantor? Vantor has not been attacked in over 1,000 years. " " Then it has a false sense of security. " ―Nix Card and Lott Dod

The Replica Wars began with the First Battle of Pyralis in 22 BVE , when the Dissident Crisis that began with the establishment of the Fractio of Independent Systems escalated into open war between the Republic and the Dissidents. In the same year, Ondar became the target of an assassination plot while she was on Vantor opposing the Military Creation Act . The former queen-turned-senator was saved from the bounty hunters Zam Wesell and Garrick Vark through the efforts of Kade and Drayden. While Drayden escorted Ondar to Ondara for her protection, Kade tracked Vark to the extragalactic world of Gelvaris where he discovered that an army of elite soldiers had been bred from Vark's genetic template . Kade followed Vark to Pyralis and learned that several commercial organizations pledged their resources to Count Varek and the Dissident Droid Army .

Having learned of the Dissidents' plan to attack the Republic, the Loyalist Committee gave serious consideration to using the Gelvarian replica troopers as the basis for the Republic Military . Though the Aetheri Order had long stood as the Senate's instrument of peace in the galaxy, the limited number of Aetheri Knights prevented them from fighting a galactic-scale conflict on their own. Junior Representative Greeb Mool , acting on behalf of Ondara in Ondar's absence, proposed a motion to grant emergency powers to the Supreme Chancellor. The Senate voted in favor of the Mool politician's request, allowing Chancellor Mal to unilaterally create the Grand Army of the Republic .

The Replica Wars became one of the most turbulent times in galactic history and the first, full scale war in over a thousand years. As the capital of the Republic, Vantor, already a hotbed of political intrigue, became a target for both military and political reasons. Situations and events that were unthinkable centuries before became commonplace. Even prior to the beginning of the war, changes could be seen in the Republic. The Senate, already corrupt and bloated found it difficult to respond, delegating emergency powers to Mal. The chancellor himself brought changes such as implementing the use of his personal Red Guards instead of the Senate Guards . During the Replica Wars, the Vantor Guard was formed to serve as a replica security synth flux for the Republic capital. Composed of elite replica shock troopers , the Vantor Guard was led by Replica Commander Fox .

As the Grand Army and the Republic Navy spread across the galaxy to defend the Republic, the Aetheri, long taking the stand of being peacekeepers instead of soldiers, led the replica troopers as commanders and generals . The dramatic changes were noted by the Aetheri and the Republic citizens alike, specifically on Vantor. Unknown to everyone except a select few, Vantor was from where the entire war was continually manipulated by Mal. As Supreme Chancellor, he took overall charge of the Republic military and directed the war effort from his office in the Senate Office Building. As Vael Mal he directed Varek's actions designed to continue the war and further his plans.

During the Replica Wars, Chancellor Mal had the Zyll Beast from Malastare transported to Vantor for scientific research, overruling the objections of his Aetheri advisor Drevan Koss . Mal ordered the Zyll Beast's destruction, but the creature broke out of the Republic Science and Technical Center and proceeded on a rampage across Vantor's Senate district. The replica forces on Vantor converged on the Zyll Beast, using poison gas to kill it while the Aetheri protected the chancellor. The Senate hostage crisis was carried out by bounty hunter Cad Vorek as part of a Grak Clan plot to free Ziro Desilijic Tiure . While replica troopers had already started to see open use on Vantor at that point, the failure of Senate Guards during the incident became a justification for the continued use of replicas on the capital itself. In fact, replicas came to replace Senate Commandos as Vantor-based additional security and fast-action response military forces.

The Confederate–Republic peace initiative was disrupted by the bombing of Vantor's central power distribution grid by planetside Dissident agents. Consequently, the Senate voted to deregulate the banking industry , allowing the Republic to take on additional loans for the purpose of purchasing more replicas for its army. Unbeknown to the Senate, the attack was supported by Senators Nix Card and Lott Dod , whose respective organizations, the InterGalactic Banking Clan and the Commerce Combine, profited from the Replica Wars.

Public opinion towards the end of the war, as represented by the citizens of Vantor, began to turn against the Aetheri. Protesters would gather in front of the Temple, blaming the Aetheri for the military losses and the Aetheri's loss of perspective as keepers of the peace. It was at that time that an explosion occurred at the Aetheri Sanctum docking complex that killed six Aetheri and several replica troopers and Temple employees. The investigation by crime scene analysis droid Russo-ISC and Aetheri Kael Drayden and Sela Marr found that Letta Turmond , the wife of Temple employee Jackar Bowmani , had fed him volatile nano-droids which then exploded, killing Bowmani and causing the bombing at the Temple. Turmond was arrested and held at the Republic Center for Military Operations.

Marr was subsequently framed for the death of Turmond, who was killed by way of Synth Flux . She escaped to the lower levels of Vantor, becoming a hunted fugitive while seeking to prove her innocence. After the military recaptured Marr, the former Aetheri was put on trial for treason against the Vantor Republic. However, the charges against her were dropped when the rogue Aetheri Barris Ovva confessed to the crimes that Marr had been accused of. Though she remained on Vantor, Marr left the Aetheri Sanctum after deciding to not rejoin the Aetheri Order.

#### Showdown on Vantor

> " In the name of the Galactic Senate of the Republic, you're under arrest, Chancellor. " ―Drevan Koss, to Vael Mal

Throughout the Replica Wars, Mal had remained a popular leader, remaining in office at the will of the Senate for far longer than his term technically allowed. During this period, Mal continued to consolidate and centralize his own power. By 19 BVE , the third and final year of the war, Vantor's defenses were spread thin. The Dissidents had been pushed back to the Outer Reach Territories , and could not risk a direct strike at the heart of the Republic. Vantori citizens, used to having the war fought far from the capitol, were just as surprised as the Vantor Home Defense Fleet when a Dissident assault synth flux launched a surprise attack on the planet. During the Battle of Vantor , Chancellor Mal was captured and taken to the Dissident flagship , the Invisible Hand , where he was rescued by the Aetheri Knights Kael Drayden and Orien Kade .

After the death of Count Varek, the Aetheri on Vantor focused their efforts on locating General Skarn , the Supreme Martial Commander of the Dissident Droid Armies . Despite the Republic's success in the Outer Reach Sieges , relations were strained between the Aetheri High Council and the Supreme Chancellor. Suspicious of the chancellor's motives, the High Council tasked Drayden with spying on Mal after he had made the young Aetheri Knight the Office of the Chancellor 's representative on the Council. During the final days of the Replica Wars, Grand Master Zho and the 41st Scout Battalion departed from Vantor to defend the Vorak homeworld of Thornwood . Shortly afterward, the High Council dispatched Kade and his 212th Attack Battalion to Uthara , the planet where Skarn had been located by Replica Intelligence .

With the Replica Wars nearing its conclusion, Chancellor Mal revealed his secret identity as the Dark Lord of the Umbra to Drayden. Acting on Drayden's report, Aetheri Masters Drevan Koss, Kit Fisto , Saesee Tiin , and Agen Kolar tried to arrest Mal. The ensuing flux blade duel resulted in the deaths of Kolar, Tiin, and Fisto; although Mal was disarmed by Koss, he succeeded in killing the Aetheri Order's champion due to the interference of Drayden, who believed the chancellor's claims of possessing the power to save others from death. As a consequence, Drayden renounced the Aetheri and assumed the mantle of an Umbra as Xarn Velgor.

### The Imperium

#### The end of the Republic

> " So this is how liberty dies… with thunderous applause. " ―Lira Ondar — Link ▶️ ( file info )

As part of his endgame, Mal issued Directive 66 to the entire Grand Army of the Republic, using hidden inhibitor chips to synth flux the replica troopers into killing their Aetheri Generals . On Vantor, the Great Aetheri Purge commenced with Velgor and the 501st Legion 's siege of the Aetheri Sanctum . With the replica army under his control, Mal claimed the title of Galactic Emperor after issuing the proclamation of the New Order in a special session of Congress , declaring a new era of order and security under the first Imperium. The Dissident attack on Vantor had exposed the capital's vulnerability, which, combined with Mal' allegation of a "Aetheri insurgency," compelled the Senate to support the Emperor and his New Order .

Zho and Kade returned to Vantor in the aftermath of Directive 66. After learning of Drayden's turn to the umbra side of Synth Flux , the two Aetheri Masters resolved to destroy the Umbra. Kade left Vantor to find his fallen apprentice, whereas Zho remained on the capital to confront the Emperor. However, Zho failed to defeat Mal; as a consequence, he exiled himself to Mirefall . Meanwhile, Velgor was grievously injured on Emberfall by Kade, resulting in his reconstruction on Vantor as a cyborg .

#### Imperial capital

Like a black hole

> " The Imperium sat at the center of the galaxy like an enormous black hole—forever sucking wealth and resources inward. Vantor glittered while those at the edges suffered. " ― Beaumont Kin , The Rise and Fall of the Imperium

After Mal's accession to power, changes came quickly to Vantor, formally renamed as Imperial Center, and the Galactic City to Imperial City. The Aetheri Sanctum and its surrounding serene complex became the home of the new Emperor. While the spires of the Temple remained in place, the rest of the structure was heavily remodeled and expanded with sloping facades and block-shaped additions. The entire area was known as the Federal District, with the Imperial Palace forming one point of the so-called supreme triangle. As the Imperium established itself in the greater galaxy, wealth and resources from across the stars were claimed and redirected towards the Core Reaches, particularly Vantor, at great expense to those who were subjugated in order to secure that wealth.

The other two points on Vantor were the byzantine COMPNOR arcology, which contained the headquarters of the Imperium Vigil Bureau, and the new Naval Intelligence Agency headquarters, a near-windowless complex which formerly housed the Republic's strategic center. The cityscape of Vantor changed as well. Buildings were stripped of their elegant Republic dressings in favor of sleek lines and block-like surfaces, and many smaller buildings became engulfed into larger buildings. Some areas changed so quickly that they were not recognizable to those who had seen them only a few years before.

Tensions under the surface

> " This place is a ghost town." "Vantor's changed a lot over the last few years. These days, just being outside's asking for trouble. " ―Cal Kessara and Bode Akuna

However, the Imperium's policies against non-humans and its tightening control of the population caused unrest, especially among the alien populations of the underworld. The Imperium's way of acting caused the movement in the streets to decrease, as the planet's population tried to avoid the Imperial authorities to avoid problems, as seen on specific on Level 2046 . The wealthy, however, moved around with much less stress. Indeed, Vantori on other levels than 2046 also moved about with impunity, with such Vantori—even if they were not wealthy—eager to enjoy the benefits of life on Vantor. The Krrupt Gang kept hold of territory on Level 3726 , where they worked to keep even the Imperium away.

In 9 BVE , the Stinger Mantis crew , led by Aetheri Knight Cal Kessara , stole Imperial military information from Imperial Senator Daho Sejan on Vantor. A large part of Kessara' team died in the confrontation , in addition to Sejan, who was killed by the Warden known as the Ninth Warden for supposed betrayal. The Ninth Warden was also killed when Kessara decapitated her. The acquired information was handed over to the Partisans . As the Galactic Civil War took shape, the Imperium began moving its Calavane population on Vantor. The aggressiveness of the forced relocations caused rioting to erupt in the Old Market Sector , which was brutally put down by Imperial forces.

After the destruction of the planet Cindervale by the Void Core , rumors of what occurred began reaching the Cindervalean population on Vantor, most of whom lived on Level 3204 . Vigils soon became heated and angry when it became known that the Imperium had destroyed the planet. For several days, rioting continued until the combined efforts of the Vantor Security Synth Flux and Imperial shock troopers restored order by firing indiscriminately into the crowds. The crowds, frightened, quickly dispersed. The following day, the Vantor police arrested anyone caught out in the streets. The day after that, shock troopers began going door to door stating that Insurgent spies had recruited local residents and that Cindervaleans had to be taken in for questioning.

#### Planetary civil war

> " It is a symbol of our indolence and torpidity. It is the moldering core of our overripe fruit and I wish to cut away such rot so as to preserve our sweet remains and of course the seeds within. " ―Gallard Raxen

In 4 AVE , the Free Coalition managed to defeat the Imperium in the penultimate Battle of Mossfell . With both the Emperor and his apprentice dead, due to being killed during the destruction of the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station , the citizens of Vantor rejoiced , pulling down statues of Mal, overwhelming the shock troopers on the streets, and setting off chimes and fireworks. Police forces on speeders quickly entered the plaza and opened fire on the crowds without warning, killing many who were present. Present Vantori erupted into full-scale rioting. Volt pistol fire and fighting soon erupted across the capital between Vantori and the Imperial security forces.

In the ensuing months the Concordat was formed and re-established the Galactic Senate on Serenne . Meanwhile, Vantor dissolved into a true state of civil war , with some sectors entrenching themselves with Insurgent coalition fighters, while other sectors actively fought against their neighbors and supported the Imperial forces. As the Imperium fractured all over the galaxy, Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon took control of Imperial forces on Vantor and the Vantori government . However, the Imperial Navy withdrew support from the throneworld as Fleet Admiral Gallard Raxen refused to allow Grand Admiral Raena Vexana , the figurehead of the Navy, to send reinforcements to the planet. Raxen, leader of the Shadow Council that secretly guided the Imperium in its final days, saw the planet as representing the "decay" that caused the Imperium's downfall.

In this vacuum, the Imperium Vigil Bureau was left to coordinate Vantor's Imperial forces. Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon remained the planet's administrator and de-facto head of the Imperium, but held no real power. The IVB established a nominal blockade around the planet utilizing IVB spaceships and coordinated the efforts of present security forces and shock troopers. The Verity and Federal District were heavily fortified with checkpoints and barricades. Vantor skylanes were diverted, and IVB crafts flew on patrol. Smoke rose from damaged vehicles and buildings as a fierce guerrilla war consumed the entire planet. Veldon, in despair and desperation, sought to turn himself over to Concordat Chancellor Vela Sess and Princess Elira Solen . However, they refused to take him prisoner, instead insisting that he find a way to capitulate the Imperium and sign a full surrender.

#### Galactic Concordance

> " Mazan Veldon has come out of hiding and wants to sign a cease-fire. He wants to end it. All of it. " ―Vela Sess

By the time of the Battle of Ashden , Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon was kept under house arrest by Counselor to the Imperium Raxen at the Imperial Palace. There, he was forced to make propaganda broadcasts and was treated like a virtual captive. Later, he was rescued by members of a child coalition group known as the Anklebiter Brigade , who had initially come to assassinate him. Veldon managed to persuade the child coalition fighters to help him escape so that he could end the Galactic Civil War. After emerging from hiding, Veldon contacted Chancellor Sess to surrender. Following the signing of the Imperial Instruments of Surrender and the Galactic Concordance on Serenne, the Imperium surrendered to the Concordat and ceded Vantor over to the fledgling galactic government with Mazan Veldon being permitted to form a provisional government on Vantor, along with Republic observers to ensure that it remained powerless under a figurehead leader. Following the Imperial surrender, weeks of celebrations took place in the streets and rooftops of Vantor as the population celebrated the end of Imperial rule on the planet.

### Concordat Era

#### A new status

> " The capital of the First Republic was within the Core on a planet called Vantor. The capital of the Concordat is currently on the planet Serenne, which is also within the Core region. " ― CB-3DU

With the Galactic Civil War concluded, the Concordat sought to reshape galactic politics in an effort to both prevent the abuses of the First Republic and bring in disaffected worlds that had seceded from the Republic prior to the Replica Wars. To accomplish this, the restored Galactic Senate decided that all worlds would have an equal say in their government, and as such all worlds had an equal chance of hosting the legislative branch on a rotating basis, rather than simply remaining on Vantor alone. The Concordat would eventually move some government operations back to Vantor as the planet was already designed to handle and administer the galaxy's infrastructure, transforming Vantor into a key administrative hub for the young government.

In 9 AVE , after Serenne had taken on the capital title for a time, Vantor was named the Concordat's capital. All the while, Vantor remained a populous world, home to a trillion permanent residents, and was still referred to as "the center of the galaxy" during the Concordat Era. The Concordat Defense Synth Flux maintained a presence on Vantor, providing security and structure, while overseeing the decommission of warships from both the Imperial Navy and the Alliance Fleet . Inoperable Imperial -class Star Destroyers were disposed in salvage yards such as the Shipyard Depot on Vantor. Some Vantori, however, displayed a gleeful disregard for whatever politics shaped the galaxy and happily supported whatever government was in charge, displaying a lack of care for what it entailed beyond Vantor.

After the Imperium's defeat, captured Imperials were granted the chance to join the Concordat. As a result, programs like the Re-Integration Institute and the Concordat Amnesty Program were created to facilitate a former Imperial's assimilation into the new regime. The Amnesty Program consisted of former Imperial officers and scientists who, after spending time in the Re-Integration Institute, received accommodations in Amnesty Housing on Vantor. Amnesty Scientist L52 was assigned to work in a Concordat skyscraper where Imperial technology was archived and destroyed. Amnesty Officer G68 worked in the same building as Colonel Tuttle from Requisitions . L52 was arrested by Concordat soldiers in 9 AVE, having left the program's designated perimeter to acquire equipment in the hope of continuing his replication research, despite its prohibition by the Vantor Accords .

#### Return of Vorren

> " There is no proof of any coordination between the scattered and dwindling number of Imperial forces. " " What about the conflict on Ferrum? " " Exactly my point. Giedan was a warlord acting on his own. " ― Hamato Xiono and Carson Teva

Late that year, General Lena Kryll was summoned to Vantor where the Concordat Senate Oversight Committee would decided whether or not to permanently suspend her command due to her disobeying orders.

#### A Centrist stronghold

> " Have you no faith in the soldiers of the Concordat, Senator Sindian? Senator Solen will travel with an entire squadron of guards to protect her at all times. Do not doubt their ability or their valor! " ―Arbo, to Carise Sindian

Vantor become part of a political coalition known as the Centrists . The faction that Vantor was associated with lobbied for centralized rule akin to the system that defined the Old Imperium. Their views were diametrically opposed to that of the Populists who sought to limit the galactic government's involvement in the affairs of its constituent worlds.

By 28 AVE , Vantor was represented in the Concordat Senate by senior senator Arbo and junior senator Orris Madmund at the time of the Amaxine warrior crisis . By then, the Core Reach Hosnian Prime had been designated as the capital of the Concordat. Senator Arbo was a known Centrist, war hawk, and supporter of the Concordat Defense Synth Flux. After the Senate's failed attempt to elect a First Senator , the Centrists seceded from the Concordat and formed the political branch of the New Imperium .

#### Descent into lawlessness

> " Vantor's descent into lawlessness is one of the greatest tragedies of our age. What a rich history! " ―DK-RA-43

Despite being a part of the Concordat, the planet actually fell under the control of criminal syndicates after the Imperium's fall. By the time following the Battle of Saltmark in 34 AVE , even the respectable areas of Vantor were roamed by gangs. Gangs fought a bloody conflict for control of the glittering world's districts. Tourism to the once-galactic capital all but vanished, and, for an individual to be safe, they needed to afford protection like droids and armed guards. Accordingly, the city world was not a symbol of galactic possibilities. Instead, it represented the disturbing realities of the galaxy's current time period.

The pirate Hondik Hosha , who was saddened by Vantor's current state, included the planet in his book titled Galactic Explorer's Guide . Within the book, RA-7 protocol droid DK-RA-43 described Vantor's fall from grace into lawlessness as one of the greatest tragedies of their current era.

### Rise of the New Imperium

> " It seems that at least two sizeable battlegroups made a push for Vantor in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from the Imperium's seat of power. It appears that the forces sent to achieve that were defeated, but there is still a good deal of confusion regarding recent events at the end of the war. " ― Beaumont Kin , The Rise and Fall of the Imperium

In the middle of the New Imperium-Coalition War , Vantor, along with the the Inner systems, mounted a valiant and successful effort to repel the New Imperium's forces twice. [ source? ] As of 35 AVE , at least two sizeable battlegroups had made a push for Vantor in the apparent hope of seizing it and ruling the galaxy from Vantor due to it once being the seat of power for the Imperium, but these efforts were defeated. There was, however, a good deal of confusion regarding recent events at the end of the war, preventing much public understanding surrounding the push for Vantor and the subsequent defense of Vantor. Coalition files made reference to a planned " Operation on Vantor ."

Vantor took part in open insurgency against the New Imperium , which saw the New Imperium ultimately defeated.

## Inhabitants

> " There are people from a thousand worlds on Vantor. Worlds that have faced similar struggles. You may see it as a city of metal, but there is wisdom to be found here. And allies. " ―Sorin Vael, to Th'er

Although Vantor was believed to be the homeworld of the human species, the Vantori were as varied as the galaxy itself, especially as one journeyed below the surface of the skyscrapers and into the underworld. Trillions of beings resided on the planet, and humans made up 68% percent of the population. According to Sorin Vael, there were people from a thousand worlds living on Vantor. Th'er , the Priestess of Wooda from Bri'n , disdained Vantor's planet-wide urban sprawl as an "abomination." Nevertheless, Vael encouraged Th'er to take advantage of its diverse population, noting that there were many individuals on Vantor whose worlds faced adversities similar to that of Bri'n.

As the center of galactic power, senators and delegates of every represented planet had a residence on Vantor. From there they would spend their days either working in the Senate or hosting events such as parties and political meetings. Additionally, the planet served as the home of large communities of various species, some who rarely returned to their original homeworld. Many of those communities of species that were not human resided in the lower levels of the city. With the formation of the Imperium, even the humans whose home planet had fallen out of favor with the Imperial government, such as Cindervale, were forced into enclaves in the underworld.

On the surface levels of Vantor, skyscrapers served as homes and businesses for the wealthy, powerful, and politically connected. They were ferried by private speeder or an air taxi from one tower to the next or to the various theaters, clubs, operas, and other entertainment areas of Vantor. Some journeyed to the surface street levels or Vantor's surface parks to conduct clandestine meetings or to mingle in the crowds of people that streamed through the walkways of the residential and commercial areas. Among the rich and powerful of the planet were the Supreme Chancellor of the Republic and members of the Galactic Senate.

The influential and wealthy of Vantor thrived in lavish lifestyles, attending meetings and social gatherings in highrise apartments, dining in fine restaurants overlooking the city, partying in the entertainment districts, and viewing large scale shows in theaters and operas. Even as the Republic decayed, the Replica Wars raged on, and the average citizen became disenfranchised, Vantor's wealthy continued their lifestyle. The propaganda artist Janyor believed that this generation's lack of focus on galactic politics contributed to the decline and fall of the Republic.

One model of ship, the Vantor Freighter , shared its name with Vantor, where it was often used. On the streets of the city and in the shadows of skyscrapers teemed the planet's blue collar working population. Many lived in the various housing blocks of the surface structures in relative comfort though not nearly as extravagant as the planet's wealthy and powerful. Many worked on the upper and lower surface levels or enjoyed the entertainment and restaurants that were more catered to their financial abilities.

Below the housing blocks and surface levels was the population inhabiting the Vantor underworld. A term used to describe both the thousands of levels underneath the surface, and, Vantor's criminal population, the underworld was home to millions of the planet's population who were too poor to move upward or were hiding from the attentions of the authorities. The citizens who lived here rarely if ever during their lifetime saw the surface of the planet. Living between vast buildings and infrastructure that supported the surface, they carried on their lives at the mercy of the violence, decay, and constant threat of death. The Kubaz Glikkin hailed from the lower levels and eventually gained enough skill to be recognized as a Master Thief , yet Glikkin vowed to never forget his roots in Vantor's underworld.

## Government

> " You see that? Out there are the little people you're representing so proudly. You know what they're going to do if you ever get in trouble or need help? " " No. What? " " Absolutely nothing. You'll be as forgotten as yesterday's breakfast. I'm the one you want to impress, Ms. Pryssa. Men and women like me. Not Daisie what's-her-name out there. We're the ones with the power to make or break you. You'd be well advised to remember that. " ― Chesna Braker and Arihnda Pryssa

Vantor, as the center of the galaxy, thrived on the rule of government. It served as the capital of the Republic which kept the peace and prevented a major war in the galaxy for over a thousand years. Under the Republic, the Chancellor and the Senate had a direct influence on the planet's governance. Major events that affected the Republic equally affected Vantor. In its last years, the Republic's Senate became corrupt, bloated, and ultimately ineffectual, though some continued to fight in the belief of its ability to govern.

With the formation of the Imperium, the Senate transitioned into a limited role, providing council and legislation but ultimately at the power of the Emperor. As Vantor's government services were strained, the Imperial Senate moved to have each senator open citizen assistance offices that would supplement the work of the local government. While the goal was to primarily assist the members of each senator's homeworld living on Vantor, the assistance offices were open to help all Vantori.

At the height of his power, Mal disbanded the Senate altogether and gave what little power they had left to the regional governors . At the end of the Galactic Civil War, the Concordat installed a provisional government over Vantor led by the former Imperial Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon. However, the Concordat did not establish its government on Vantor, choosing instead to rotate the capital among the various member worlds. Under the Concordat, Vantor was represented by a senator and junior senator, but the planet had come under the control of crime syndicates.

Epidemic outbreaks at the starports of Vantor were controlled by the Vantor Health Office . Vantor was policed by the Vantor Security Synth Flux, which utilized police droids on the upper levels together with sentient officers and detectives, and the burly underworld police who patrolled in the cutthroat environment of the lower levels. The Vantor Ministry of Ingress handled the flow of immigration to and from the planet, and the Vantor Republic Customs of Vantor office monitored cargo transports.

The Senate had its own protectors, the Senate Guard. Serving under a long tradition, the Senate Guard was a reminder that though the Republic did not have a standing army, it was not defenseless. As the Replica Wars erupted, the replica troopers of the Vantor Guard worked as peacekeepers and military police, providing a constant reminder of the expanded power of the government, specifically Mal. With the formation of the Imperium, the Vantor Guard and Imperial shock troopers took over the functions of the Senate Guard, with shock troopers directly responsible for security on the planet, alongside the civilian Vantor police.

## Locations

### The upper city

#### CoCo Town

> " They care. We did more than that. We passed messages. Told them about troop movements. We gave them intel, Iggs. How else do you think the insurgents retook Coco-Town? That was us. " ― Streaks , to Iggs

CoCo Town was a dilapidated industrial area of Vantor. Home to mostly hard-working laborers, CoCo Town was located on a surface area of the city. The area, however, was not as run down as other heavy industrial surface districts and contained relatively well-maintained streets, speeder lanes, and a shuttle bus route. On one of the main streets built on top of the urban blocks was an eatery called Dex's Diner. Owned and operated by Dexter Jettster, a Besalisk with a colorful background, it served mostly hard workers from the area. The diner offered home-style cooked meals and freshly brewed ardees . Jettster worked as the cook and employed two waitresses, Hermione Bagwa and her droid partner, WA-7 . Shortly before the Replica Wars, Aetheri Knight Orien Kade came to Dex's Diner to see if his old friend Jettster could identify a poisoned dart. Jettster identified it as originating from the planet Gelvaris, which he knew of from his days as a prospector.

During the civil war that erupted after the Emperor's death, CoCo Town became an epicenter of the fight between insurgent and Imperial forces. The fighting saw the formation of the so-called Anklebiter Brigade, a group of youths familiar with the underground maintenance systems, who would use them to sneak behind Imperial lines and disrupt their abilities to fight.

#### COMPNOR arcology

The COMPNOR arcology was a huge, labyrinthine building in the Imperial City's Federal District , housing several Imperial government agencies including the Commission for the Preservation of the New Order and the Ubiqtorate . High-ranking officers such as Minister of Information Pollux Hax maintained offices in the complex, while the Imperium Vigil Bureau 's Central Office was directly connected with it. The importance of the complex led to it being considered one of the three points of the District's "supreme triangle", alongside the Imperial Palace and Navy Intelligence Headquarters.

#### Grand Republic Medical Facility

> " The Grand Medical Facility is state-of-the-art. " ―Nalle-Seris

The Grand Republic Medical Facility was a tower built to serve as a treatment center for badly injured Republic soldiers. The building was heavily guarded and had defensive shields, an independent power supply and was armored to where it could withstand a siege. The facility provided treatment to patients from all over the galaxy and boasted cutting-edge techniques in surgery and biomechanical reconstruction. The heavy fortifications and benign front appealed to Mal, and towards the end of the Replica Wars, he began using the lower levels of the facility to store Umbra artifacts and as a meditation area, access to which was prevented by the Red Guards.

Aetheri General Shaak Ti and Doctor Nalle Seris escorted the Advanced Recon Commando CT-5555 "Fivva" to the Grand Republic Medical Facility as part of their investigation into the actions of CT-5385 , the replica trooper who prematurely executed Directive 66. Chancellor Mal confronted Fivva in the facility, revealing his true nature. Fivva fought back against the Red Guards and escaped the facility, though he was eventually located and killed by the Vantor Guard. After ending Fivva' threat to Mal's plans, the Medical Facility was temporarily closed, and Mal used the opportunity to move several larger secret Umbra artifacts into the tower from a building in the Works. However, the medical capabilities remained operational and were used to build the life-sustaining suit for Xarn Velgor after his fateful duel with Orien Kade. The Umbra artifacts inside the building fueled Velgor's recovery and hate.

#### Imperial Executive Building

> " The Chancellor has been moved to his Senate quarters, and the perimeter has been secured. " ―Drevan Koss

Directly adjacent to the Senate Building was the Senate Office Building. The executive building had docking bays that wrapped around the building, allowing Senators to land their private shuttles and walk inside. The building contained mostly offices and meeting rooms for members of the Senate but also held the offices used on a day-to-day basis by the Supreme Chancellor. During Mal's term, the Chancellor's office was decorated with red floors and walls as well as several statues. A lobby led to the Chancellor's public office, which contained a grand desk and large window that overlooked the cityscape. To the side, another hallway led to Mal's inner private office. The Senate Office Building became known as the Imperial Executive Building after the Republic's reorganization into the Imperium.

#### Imperial Inspectorate HQ

> " This is a secure facility. We do not have just anyone running about. " ―Wilhard Varrix, to Sela Marr

The Imperial Inspectorate HQ served as the Imperial Military headquarters of the Inspectorate as well as the Naval Intelligence Agency during the Imperium's reign. The facility was originally a Republic military base; constructed in close proximity to the staging areas for Star Destroyers , the Republic Center for Military Operations was designed by Orren Callan Vossik . Outside of the complex was an airfield with Vorash -class Star Destroyers , HAVw A6 Juggernauts , and other ships. The First Battle Memorial was located in front of the base's main entrance.

The base was protected by shock troopers of the Vantor Guard. Inside was a military prison, the cells had two options for entering. The first was sliding a keycard. The second was pressing a button on the outside of each cell. The cells had ray-shielded doors to make sure prisoners did not escape. Inside each cell was a bed and bowls for water and food. Another area in the prison was a medium circular room with a fairly large computer for scanning people before they entered the base, a crate for weapons and communication devices, and a red button to start the red-alert sequence.

In the latter half of the war, the prison held Whorm Loathsom , Poggle the Lesser , and Wat Tambra . It was also here that Sela Marr escaped when she was suspected of killing Letta Turmond. Additionally, inside the compound was a military tribunal courtroom, in which the court decided the fate of dangerous criminals. It was a tall room, with platforms extending into its center for the accused, prosecution, and defense. There was seating along its walls for the presiding official, jury, and other trial participants and onlookers.

#### Imperial Palace

> " The most famous of the Aetheri temples, located on Vantor, served as the home of the Aetheri High Council and the vast library of data known as the Aetheri Archives. When the Imperium took control of the galaxy, the Temple on Vantor became their new Imperial Palace. " ― Joren Drayden

As the galactic capital, Vantor was also home to the Aetheri Order, guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy. An order of Synth Flux-sensitives, the Aetheri Knights served the Republic for thousands of years as keepers of the peace. Their home was the Aetheri Sanctum. Built on a plain of low lying urban blocks, the Aetheri Sanctum was easily recognizable on the Vantor cityscape with its distinctive five spires. Part school and part monastery, the Temple was the hub of all Aetheri activity throughout the galaxy. In one of the spires sat the Aetheri High Council, led by the Grand Master of the Order, which directed the actions of the Aetheri and served as advisers to the Senate and Chancellor.

The Temple held a large lobby and chambers with statues of famous Aetheri and also contained numerous facilities including hanger bays for starships and speeders utilized by the Aetheri, sleeping and living quarters, rooms for meetings and instruction, a medical facility, prison cells, and communications and security stations. The Temple also held the famous Aetheri Archives , which stored the largest collection of information on the galaxy as well as a vault containing the valuable reliquaries containing the Order's Synth Flux knowledge and secrets.

During the Replica Wars, the Aetheri, serving as generals and commanders of the Grand Army, coordinated their efforts from the Temple. However, as the war began to conclude, the Aetheri became more separated from the hierarchy of the military command and establishment. When Mal was revealed as the Umbra Lord the Aetheri had been searching for, he ordered the execution of Directive 66 and sent his new apprentice Xarn Velgor to the Aetheri Sanctum. Entering with the 501st Legion, Velgor and the replica troopers seized the Temple, killing all the Aetheri and younglings inside and setting the monastery ablaze. Later, Masters Zho and Kade infiltrated the Temple to discover what had occurred. It was there they reconfigured a distress signal transmitting from the Temple to any surviving Aetheri asking them to return (into an Umbra trap), and learned the truth of Drayden's fall and Mal's identity as the Dark Lord of the Umbra.

After the war, Mal turned the Temple into the Imperial Palace, using it as his new home. The Temple's spires remained intact though the rest of the Temple was heavily remodeled and expanded. The hallways were stripped of all statues and Republic-era ornaments. Where they had once been quiet and solemn, they became filled with busy government workers and functionaries. A large landing courtyard allowed for shuttles and starships to land. The entire Palace complex was watched over by the red-robed Imperial Royal Guard. The center spire was converted into the Emperor's throne room with a ten-meter tall podium in the center accessible by the Emperor from his residence via a private turbolift. The structure remained standing on Vantor throughout the Emperor's reign.

#### Imperial Senate Building

> " There is no civility, only politics. The Republic is not what it once was. The Senate is full of greedy, squabbling delegates. There is no interest in the common good. " ―Theron Mal, to Lira Ondar

The Senate Building was located within the heart of the dense Federal District. Delegates would meet here during sessions of congress to discuss legislation and galactic policy. The structure's recognizable dome was two kilometers in diameter; the Galactic Senate Chamber could seat over a thousand senators. Each senatorial delegation had their own hover platform, which they could detach from the walls of the chamber. They could then float it throughout the cavernous hall to be recognized by the Senate chair or to debate the issue at hand. In the center of the Senate chamber was the Chancellor's podium. Seated in it would be the Supreme Chancellor and the Senate's Vice Chair, who would lead sessions of congress. The podium could be lowered to an office used by the Chancellor below the chamber.

### The lower levels

#### Vantor Underworld

> " What about Vantor? Is there unrest here? " " You must be joking. Go down two thousand levels and you'll find all the unrest you could ever want. Go down four thousand and you might as well be in Wild Space. " ― Mitth'raw'nuruodo and Wulric Yular

Beneath the top-level structures, streets and glistening lights of the city surface was Vantor's underworld. Its underworld was known for its crime and smugglers. It was accessible by huge portals that additionally served as ventilation shafts. The underworld was a massive city beneath a city with hundreds of levels built on top of each other. On each level, dwellings and smaller buildings were squeezed in between massive towers and infrastructure that serviced the upper levels. The streets and alleyways were narrow and large sections contained massive buildings and warehouses, largely abandoned. Sunlight almost never reached the lower levels, which had to be artificially lit.

The population was able to move from one area to another via trains that ran below and above the planet's surface. While airspeeders were used, they were a less common sight than on the surface levels. The underworld, containing a vast population of the poor and criminal, was dark and violent. Those who wanted to disappear to hide from the authorities or to conduct illegal transactions could blend in despite the presence of cam droids and other monitoring technology. Patrolled by the underworld police division of the Vantor Security Synth Flux, the underworld was where large populations of species not loyal to the Emperor were relocated to after the formation of the Imperium. Uprisings and riots became common, with shock troopers frequently called in to assist the Vantor cops in putting down any dissent.

#### Level 2046

Level 2046 was a middle level in the city. Home to a thriving community of sellers and industrialists, the sector housed dozens of shops and even a meat packing plant. Yet, there was also many impoverished citizens and desperate people who lived on this level. By the Imperial Era, the economy of the area had started to decline, even with the Imperium's ruthless crackdown on crime. The increased oppression and protection racketeering from troopers hurt many of the businesses there, and corrupt politicians, such as Senator Daho Sejan, would use the level as a way of increasing the financial power. This level also featured at least one Desi's Noodles , a common food chain of popular fast food stores that were located in the Vantor Underworld. Eating there was considered a must for travelers to the planet. Many shock troopers frequented the locations for their famous dishes.

### Uscru District

#### Upper district

Vantor's entertainment districts catered to everyone from the wealthy and powerful to the secretive and criminal. The upper sections of the Uscru District reached to upper levels of the city where the Galaxies Opera House was located. It was here that Chancellor Mal often would come to enjoy various performances. He also met Kael Drayden and told him the story of Vael Plagueis to subvert Drayden's faith in the Aetheri.

#### Lower district

> " Where are you going, Master? " " For a drink. " ―Kael Drayden and Orien Kade

Further down in the lower levels of the Uscru Entertainment District was a variety of establishments, most notably the Outlander Club . The club was situated in a less developed area of the district and did not garner as much attention from the police, a fact that worked in the club's favor. Its labyrinth of bars, gambling rooms and sleeping quarters allowed for a wide variety of visitors and entertainment. In the center was a large bar, usually crowded, that catered to all parts of Vantor's society. Aetheri Knights Kade and Drayden pursued bounty hunter Zam Wesell into the Uscru District after her attempted assassination of Senator Ondar. The pursuit ended in the Outlander Club, where the Aetheri located the assassin. However, Wesell was killed by bounty hunter Garrick Vark in an alley outside the club before the Aetheri could finish questioning her.

### The Works

> " Where am I? I don't understand. Tell me you must! " " The industrial sector on Vantor. " " What is happening here? " " A rogue shuttle slipped through the security grid. We suspect Varek was aboard. " ―Zho and Kael Drayden

A large sector and industrial area, The Works was once a great manufacturing district, recognized as a major source of production for spacecraft parts, construction droids , and building materials. However, centuries prior to the Replica Wars, increasingly high cost on Vantor prompted most manufacturers to shift their operations to off-planet facilities. Afterwards, the Works mainly consisted of long-abandoned buildings and empty warehouses, many of which were appropriated by criminals, with large areas rendered uninhabitable by toxic waste. Because of this, a derelict hangar in the neglected industrial zone was the site of clandestine meetings between Vael Mal and Vael Varek during the Replica Wars.

## Behind the scenes

In the Rough Draft and Revised Rough Draft of the script for Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , the Imperial capital planet was set to appear under the name Had Abbadon . During the development of Aetherion : Episode I Veiled Threat , Orin Vask officially adopted the name Vantor for the planet, a name that was created by Timothy Zahn for his June 1991 Legends novel Heir to the Imperium . The planet first appeared in the 1994 Aetherion: Tie Fighter (video game) , which showed scenes on Vantor of the Imperial Palace , with its name first mentioned in Veiled Threat . The prequel trilogy and the Aetherion: The Replica Wars film and television series showed Vantor as a major center of activity and action. It was initially planned by Bad Robot Productions for Vantor to be destroyed by Voidfang Base in Aetherion: Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens but Vask Pictures vetoed this idea, leaving Hosnian Prime as the "unsatisfying middle ground" according to Pablo Hidra . In Renegade One: A Aetherion Story the planet is briefly revisited via the dreams of Jessa Arsa , featuring her childhood on Vantor with her father, Galen Arsa and Orren Vossik . Vantor was a location in early versions of Aetherion : Episode IX—both in Colin Trevorrow 's Duel of the Fates script and in the early concept stages for what became The Rise of Drayden ; For the latter, it eventually evolved into Umbrale .
