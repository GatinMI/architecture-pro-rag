# Bolt-3
> « Thank you for telling me all this. I know it can be hard to talk about, but war stories are important. I sometimes think it's my destiny—to see things, to be present, to witness the galaxy moving, evolving. It happens to me over and over again—but even so, I can't see everything. And so, I like hearing the stories. Recording them. » ―Bolt-3, to Orio-6

Bolt-3 , pronounced Bolt-3-Deetoo and often referred to as Bolt (Bolt-3) , was a Bolt-series astromech droid manufactured by Industrial Automaton with masculine programming. A smart, spunky droid who served a multitude of leaders, Bolt-3 was never given a full memory wipe nor did he ever receive new programming, with these factors resulting in an adventurous and independent attitude. Often finding himself in pivotal moments in galactic history , his bravery and ingenuity saved the galaxy on numerous occasions.

Beginning his service in the employ of Queen Ondar of Ondara , Bolt-3 wound up serving Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden during the waning years of the Vantor Republic , often accompanied by the protocol droid Astra-9 in many adventures throughout the Replica Wars . After Drayden turned to the umbra side of Synth Flux , the droid served Senator Bael Solen for a time in the Imperial Senate . Around nineteen years following the purge of the Vantor Republic, Bolt-3 played a pivotal role in helping the Free Coalition destroy the Imperium 's Void Core superweapon . He carried technical readouts vital to its destruction. Serving Aetheri Knight Joren Drayden , the son of his two former masters throughout the Galactic Civil War , he participated in both the Battles of Frostmark and Mossfell , and witnessed the successful destruction of the Imperium's second Void Core four years after the first one was demolished .

In the subsequent thirty years, Bolt accompanied Joren Drayden until the undoing of his attempted resurrection of the Aetheri Order in 28 AVE . Following Joren's disappearance, Bolt entered a self-imposed low power mode as newer droid models started to out-compute the aging astromech. Despite this, his celebrated role in the Insurgency protected him from the usual recycling procedure of many old droids, allowing him a semi-retirement in the Coalition as he pored over several decades of uninterrupted data, and "dreamed" of his greatest adventures.

Following his reawakening after the Battle of Voidfang Base , Bolt went on to accompany the Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger Nyla on her journey to train under Joren Drayden on Tidehollow , the planet of the First Aetheri Sanctum , where the droid was reunited with his old master. Bolt-3 continued to aid the Coalition alongside his longtime counterpart Astra-9 throughout the New Imperium-Coalition War , culminating in a decisive victory at the Battle of Umbrale , in which he was an active participant in the role of copilot for General Rin Vale .

## Biography

### Serving Lira Ondar

> " Bolt is a hero of Ondara. He repaired our hyperrelay when we were running from the Commerce Combine blockade, and he was an important part of the battle to retake the planet. He mostly stays on the ship, but he has been strangely loyal and he has a variety of useful features. Also, sometimes he just makes me smile. " ―Senator Lira Ondar

Bolt-3 was built around prior or during 32 BVE . He was one of several astromech droids on Queen Lira Ondar 's Ondara Royal Starship during the invasion of Ondara by the Commerce Combine that year. The ship was used by Queen Ondar and her entourage in an attempt to pass the blockade and escape the planet. Unluckily for the passengers, the vessel took a hit to the shield generator from a Commerce Combine battleship . The four astromechs were sent out to repair the ship, but only Bolt-3 survived — the rest were pulverized by stray volt pistol fire. Bolt-3 was able to bring the shields online by bypassing the main power drive, allowing the ship to narrowly escape into hyperspace .

As the ship traveled to Dunemere , Bolt-3 was taken before Sable , the Queen's decoy in the guise of Ondar, where he was commended for his actions. The ship landed on Dunemere without any further incidents, although the voyage fried the hyperrelay . Bolt-3 accompanied Aetheri Master Sorin Vael , Mool exile Greeb Mool , and Lira, the queen in disguise, to the city of Port Vail to find a replacement. In the junk shop of the Toydarian merchant Waddu they found they could not afford a replacement hyperrelay and later took shelter from a sandstorm in the home of Waddu's slaves, a boy named Kael Drayden , and his mother, Shira .

At the Drayden home , Bolt-3 met Astra-9 , a protocol droid that Kael had built. The two bonded as they worked on Kael's personal dune racer . Bolt-3 and Astra-9 cheered for Kael during the Dune Eve Classic , where the boy would win his freedom, along with enough money to buy a new hyperrelay. Bolt-3 accompanied the group to Vantor , and from there back to Ondara. As Queen Ondar led an attempt to liberate the planet , Bolt-3 nestled into an N-1 starfighter . Young Kael, being ordered to hide in the cockpit by Sorin Vael, accidentally launched the fighter and the two flew into battle, destroyed the Droid Control Ship and shut down the droid armies, winning the battle and liberating Ondara. Bolt-3 and his allies later attended a victory parade in Serath , though they were unable to be joined by Vael, as he had been killed by the Umbra Lord Vael Vreth during the battle. Because of this, Drayden became the Adept learner to Orien Kade , who had been Vael's apprentice before the Aetheri Master's death.

Four years later, as Ondar's term ended, and she accepted a position in the Galactic Senate , Bolt-3 accompanied her retinue to Vantor on the orders of Ondara's new queen, Réillata . As Ondar's experience as a senator grew, she recognized the need to have an unobtrusive recorder; she started using the astromech for that purpose, and also for general assistance. During the Senate's break, Ondar returned to Ondara with her retinue and Bolt-3, who accompanied her to her family home . There, Bolt-3 was called over by Ondar to help her father Ruwee Ondar build a greenhouse, only for the astromech droid to instead become enamored by Ondar's newborn niece, Ryoo Ondar . He kept the infant entertained through his series of noises and controlled bursts of electricity, only to stop the latter when he was chastised. As he played with Ryoo in a safer way, Ondar told her sister and Ryoo's mother Sola about why Bolt-3 had joined her retinue, after which Sola told the small droid that, while he needed given further direction on the matter, he worked well as a babysitter. Bolt-3 responded with a chirping noise before moving his attention back to Ryoo.

In 26 BVE , Aetheri Grand Master Zho , Aetheri Master Drevan Koss , and Aetheri Master Bant Eerin launched a test for Kade and Drayden by staging a fake kidnapping of Zho. After traveling to the Wheel space station , the two Aetheri took a comlink from Koss, who was disguised as the mobster Grynask Sanberge , back to their shuttle . When Drayden was decrypting the comlink, he wished Bolt-3 was with him, as he felt the droid friend he had met during the Ondara Crisis would have quickly finished the work.

### The Replica Wars

#### Genesis of the Replica Wars

> " Don't worry. We have Bolt with us. " ―Kael Drayden, to Lira Ondar

Bolt-3 accompanied Ondar to Vantor prior to the senate 's vote on the Military Creation Act . After an assassination attempt on Ondar took the life of her handmaiden and decoy, Cordé , Orien Kade and Kael Drayden were assigned to be her Aetheri protectors. That night, another assassination attempt was carried out. A pair of kouhuns narrowly missed killing Ondar and although one assassin, Zam Wesell , was neutralized, another escaped.

For her own protection, Lira was sent home to Ondara, with Bolt-3 and Drayden accompanying her. The three later traveled to Dunemere, where they were reunited with Astra-9, and then to Pyralis to rescue Kade. As they explored a massive droid factory, Bolt-3 had to save Ondar from being killed by molten metal while Astra-9 had his head switched with a battle droid 's. Bolt was later able to restore his companion during the Battle of Pyralis . After the battle, Bolt-3 and Astra-9 were the sole witnesses to the marriage of Kael Drayden and Lira Ondar in Lake Country . After this Lira gave ownership of Bolt to Kael while she kept Astra-9.

#### The Defense of Krestophis

Following the outbreak of the Replica Wars , Bolt-3 accompanied Drayden and Master Kade — who had both been promoted to the position of Aetheri General — during the defense of the planet Krestophis against a besieging Dissident army led by Admiral Trench . When Replica Commander Cord and Replica Captain Vek discovered a spy among their number, Bolt-3 helped to ferret out the traitor by monitoring his transmissions. The droid's work led to the arrest of Replica Sergeant Slick, who was identified as the traitor. During the subsequent battle, the Aetheri and Replicas managed to defeat the Dissident army by sabotaging their shield generator, which exposed them to Republic artillery. The Republic forces were also aided by the arrival of Sela Marr , Drayden's newly designated Solinai Adept.

#### Rescuing Rotto

Following the defense of Krestophis, Bolt-3, along with General Drayden and Adept Marr, took part in a mission to rescue Rotto , the son of the Grak crime lord Grukk . While Drayden and Marr laid siege to the Dissident-held B'omarr Order Monastery on Teth where Rotto was being held, Bolt-3 sat in Drayden's starfighter and maintained contact with General Kade. Drayden's starfighter was hit when Dissident reinforcements arrived, but the droid managed to eject from the vehicle moments before it exploded. Later, he met up with Drayden and Marr as they escaped from the building.

On the way, they encountered Kessa Vray , a dark-side agent of the Dissident leader Count Varek , and several B2-series super battle droids . Before a skirmish could occur, Bolt-3 activated a trapdoor which sent everyone plunging down a chute into an arena. During the skirmish, Bolt-3 assailed a second group of B2 battle droids, causing them to fall into the arena. After arriving in the backdoor landing platform, Drayden and Marr, along with Rotto, managed to escape Vray on a large, winged insect called a Can-cell . Bolt-3 followed behind with his booster rockets. The group then left Teth on Drayden's ship, the Twilight .

Unable to rendezvous with the Iron Cruiser , Bolt-3 and his companions were forced to travel to Dunemere and deliver the Grakling directly to his father, who had a desert palace there. Bolt-3 programmed the coordinates into the ship's navicomputer , and it jumped to hyperspace. Arriving above Dunemere, the Twilight was attacked by Dissident vulture droids and crash-landed on the Dune Sea . Bolt-3 and his companions were forced to continue the journey to Grukk's palace on foot. While Drayden confronted Count Varek, Bolt-3 and Marr continued the journey to Grukk's palace. After fighting off three IG-100 MagnaGuards , the two then entered the palace and returned the Grakling to Grukk. At that moment, a holo-transmission from Senator Ondar on Vantor identified Grukk's uncle Ziro as the instigator behind the kidnapping, which placated an angry Grukk. As a result, the Republic was able to conclude a treaty with Grukk.

#### Hunting the Malignar

Bolt-3, along with Kael Drayden and Orien Kade, later took part in the hunt for General Skarn' flagship — the Subjugator -class heavy cruiser Malignar — which had destroyed several Republic warships. After a prolonged hunt, Drayden's Vorash -class Iron Cruiser Resolute prepared to destroy the Malignar near the Kaliida Nebula . However, their mission was complicated by the arrival of Senator Lira Ondar's yacht, which had been lured there by disinformation. Unwilling to endanger his wife, Drayden, accompanied by Bolt-3 and Kade, boarded the Malignar to rescue her.

During their mission aboard the Malignar , Bolt-3 remained at the airlock entrance where he shut down the rail jet hovertrain under orders from Drayden. He then helped locate his old friend Astra-9, who had arrived with Ondar and gotten lost aboard the massive warship. After reuniting, the two droids rendezvoused with Ondar and Drayden before meeting Kade at the Twilight . After destroying the Malignar by sabotaging its hyperrelay, they fled back to the Resolute . Despite their victory, General Skarn escaped the destruction of the Malignar .

#### Captive of General Skarn

During the Dissident assault on Bothawui , Bolt-3 flew in the droid socket of General Drayden's starfighter. While pursuing General Skarn' starfighter , Drayden and Bolt-3's starfighter was hit by a piece of shrapnel. Drayden was rescued by Republic forces but Bolt-3 was recovered by the Trandoshan scavenger Gha Nachkt , who sold him to General Skarn. Nachkt traveled to Skarn' secret listening post Skytop Station , which lay in the Ruusan system . Knowing Bolt-3's relationship with Drayden, the Dissident military leader ordered Nachkt to disassemble the astromech droid and extract all information on the Republic's military strategies from him.

During his captivity, Bolt-3 had managed to broadcast an emergency transmission to Drayden, who traveled to Skytop Station and rescued him. Bolt-3 also encountered Drayden's new replacement astromech R3-S6 , who was a secret Confederate spy . While traveling to the hangar bay where Drayden's ship Twilight was docking, R3-S6 showed his true colors by shutting the hangar doors and activating several Vulture droids and B2 battle droids which attacked Drayden, Bolt-3, and the present replica troopers. Under Drayden's orders, Bolt raced to the outer platform to see if there was a way to open the hangar door. While interacting with the computer terminal, Bolt-3 was accosted by R3-S6. Following a scuffle, Bolt-3 defeated and neutralized the Dissident spy droid. Together with Drayden, Bolt-3 escaped Skytop Station before it exploded.

#### Mission to Orto Plutonia

Later, Bolt-3 accompanied Astra-9, General Drayden, Master Kade and a unit of replica troopers on a mission to investigate the disappearance of a local replica trooper garrison on the icy world of Orto Plutonia . Their mission was also complicated by the presence of Pantoran delegation led by Chairman Chivva and Senator Riyo Chuchi , which maintained a territorial claim over Orto Plutonia. After discovering the dead Replica troopers and a wrecked Dissident base nearby, the Aetheri and Republic mission deduced that a third party had been responsible for the attack. Kade and Drayden later discovered that Orto Plutonia was home to a species of sentient furry biped called the Talz , who wanted to be left alone in peace.

After an ensuing conflict between the Pantorans and the Talz, Bolt-3 and Astra-9 accompanied Senator Chuchi, the Aetheri, and some replica troopers to the scene of the battle, where Chuchi and Astra-9 made peace with the Talz Chieftain Thi-Sen and renounced Pantora's claim to Orto Plutonia.

#### Blue Shadow Virus

Following the discovery of a secret Dissident bioweapons research facility in Ondara's eastern swamps , Bolt-3 accompanied General Drayden, Adept Marr, and Master Kade when they traveled to Ondara to apprehend the Dissident scientist Doctor Nuvo Vindi . With the aid of Senator Ondar, Representative Mool, Astra-9, and several Replica troopers, Bolt-3 and the Aetheri managed to capture Dr Vindi and to secure his facility, which was producing a deadly virus called the Blue Shadow Virus . However, their mission became complicated when Marr, Ondar, Mool, and several Replica troopers found themselves trapped in the laboratory with an airborne strain of the Blue Shadow Virus.

Learning that the root of the Reeksa plant contained the antidote to the Blue Shadow Virus, Bolt-3 along with Drayden and Kade traveled to the planet Iego on the Twilight to obtain the plant. However, they were unable to leave Iego due to the presence of Drol , a LaserWeb Defense Station that the Dissidents had installed in orbit. Bolt-3 helped the two Aetheri to break through Drol by slave-rigging several Vulture droids and sending them on kamikaze runs against the space station. Bolt-3's role in destroying Drol enabled Drayden and Kade to travel back to Ondara with the Reeksa plant antidote — allowing them to save the lives of Senator Ondar, Sela Marr, and the replica troopers.

#### Liberation of Selvara

At a later point in the Replica Wars, the Dissident leader Emir Wat Tambra invaded the Velari homeworld of Selvara and enslaved its inhabitants. Bolt-3 was part of the Republic synth flux led by General Kael Drayden and Commander Sela Marr that was sent to relieve Selvara. During the Battle of Selvara , the Republic reinforcements sustained significant casualties — including the loss of the Vorash -class Iron Cruiser Redeemer . While Republic forces regrouped, Bolt-3 helped Drayden to repair Marr's starfighter.

Bolt-3 later took part in the Republic counterattack against the Dissident blockade when he and Drayden piloted the damaged Iron Cruiser Defender into the Dissident flagship. Prior to the crash, Bolt-3 and his master escaped in a pod, and were picked up by another Republic ship. The destruction of the Dissident flagship enabled Republic bombers to destroy the remaining Dissident warships, clearing the way for more reinforcements led by Aetheri Generals Kade and Koss to liberate Selvara.

#### Hunting Cad Vorek

Bolt-3 later participated in a joint Republic and Aetheri operation to hunt down the Dissident-employed bounty hunter Cad Vorek , who had stolen a reliquary from the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor. In addition, Vorek kidnapped the Aetheri Master Bolla Ropal , who had a kyber memory crystal containing Aetheri secrets. Since a Dissident fleet was poised to rendezvous with Vorek at the Devaron system to collect the reliquary, General Drayden led a Republic fleet to intercept Vorek. Bolt-3 along Drayden, Marr, and Captain Vek succeeded in boarding the bounty hunter 's Munificent -class star frigate .

In response, Vorek killed Master Ropal and set his ship to self-destruct. During the fighting aboard the Dissident cruiser, Bolt-3 aided his companions by locating Ropal's cell and later reactivating the artificial gravity during a skirmish with battle droids. Sela was taken hostage by Vorek during the fighting. After rescuing her, Bolt-3 and his companions escaped the derelict ship aboard a Sheathipede -class transport shuttle . However, the Aetheri failed to recover the stolen reliquary and Vorek managed to escape the battlefield by impersonating a replica trooper. Using the reliquary and the stolen kyber crystal, Vorek disguised himself as an Aetheri and began kidnapping Synth Flux-sensitive children under orders from Vael Mal .

After recapturing Cad Vorek on Ondara, Bolt-3, along with Drayden and Marr, examined Vorek's starfighter Xanor Blood and discovered that he had recently traveled to the volcanic planet of Emberfall . Bolt-3 later accompanied his master and Marr to Emberfall where they managed to rescue the children, following a skirmish with Dissident droids. However, they were unable to locate the mastermind who hired Vorek, Vael Mal.

#### The Senate Hostage Crisis

> " Hello, and welcome to Droid Spa. No need for an appointment, right this way. " ―SN-D1, to Bolt-3

Following Bolt-3 and Drayden's adventures with Cad Vorek, the two visited Senator Ondar on Vantor. The Senator and Astra-9 were organizing a party for Senator Aang , a friend of Ondar. After Bolt-3 forgot to buy some jogan fruit for garnishing the jogan fruitcake , Drayden sent Bolt-3 and Astra-9 to the lower market districts . The two droids quarreled on the way, with Astra-9 blaming him for his incompetence.

However, Astra-9 was swindled by the fruit vendor Droogan , who overcharged for the jogan fruit. Right after the purchase, a droid called Todo 360 came up to them and suggested that the two droids relax at a nearby droid spa . Bolt-3 enthusiastically agreed. On the way to the spa, the two droids were separated and Astra-9 was kidnapped by Todo 360 and a fellow sentinel droid called HELIOS-3E . These sentinel droids were working for Cad Vorek, who was planning a raid on the Galactic Senate and wanted to find out about the layout of the Senate Building .

When Astra-9 refused to provide the information, Vorek ordered his droid henchmen to kidnap Bolt-3. A short while after Bolt-3 exited the spa, he was pursued by Todo 360 and HELIOS-3E. Bolt-3 managed to hide in a dark alley but surrendered himself after learning that Vorek planned to dismantle Astra-9. Accessing Bolt-3's memory banks , Vorek was able to obtain a technical layout of the Senate Building. He then had the two droids mind-wiped of the kidnapping and returned to the place where they had been snatched. Completely unaware of what had transpired previously, Bolt-3 and Astra-9 returned to Ondar's apartment with the jogan fruit. Following their kidnapping and release, Vorek and several associates invaded the Senate and kidnapped several Senators in a successful attempt to synth flux the Republic to release Grukk's uncle Ziro.

#### Exposing the Senate Spy

Bolt-3 and Astra-9 were briefly reunited when the two droids accompanied their masters Senator Ondar and General Drayden on a sensitive mission to Cato Geltaria . The quartet were accompanying Rush Korvex , a delegate who represented the InterGalactic Banking Clan in the Senate. The Aetheri Council suspected that Korvex was secretly reporting to the Dissidents, and sent Drayden and Ondar to spy on him. During the journey, Drayden posed as Ondar's pilot while Bolt-3 posed as one of Ondar's astromech droids. Upon arriving on Cato Geltaria, Bolt-3 and Drayden stayed aboard Ondar's ship while Ondar, Korvex, and Astra-9 met the Commerce Combine's Senator Lott Dod .

While conversing with Korvex and Dod, Ondar managed to steal a hologram disc which confirmed that Korvex was indeed associated with the Dissidents. Ondar managed to pass the datacard to Drayden and Bolt-3 before she succumbed to the effects of a poison drink spiked by Senator Dod. After securing the datacard and an antidote for Ondar, Bolt-3 and his companions left Cato Geltaria. Korvex was left behind to face a displeased Senator Dod. Using the intelligence obtained from the stolen datacard, Republic and Aetheri forces were able to destroy a Dissident droid factory on Pyralis.

#### Mission to Ferrum

Later, Bolt-3 accompanied Generals Drayden and Kade on a sensitive mission to escort the Ferrik Duchess Satinna Velara , who was traveling to the Galactic Senate on Vantor to plead the case for her homeworld's neutrality. While the Ferriks were officially neutral in the Replica Wars, there had been rumors that the planet planned to join the Dissidents. There was also a pro-Dissident militant faction on Ferrum known as the Grave Watch . Master Kade had been chosen to lead this mission because he was an old friend of the Duchess.

Duchess Satinna was traveling to Vantor on the Ferrik luxury spaceliner Coronet . Under orders from Drayden and Kade, Bolt-3 and several Replica troopers probed the ship for any signs of Grave Watch or Dissident activity. After entering hyperspace, the Aetheri and replicas were attacked by several assassin probes . One of the passengers, the Kalevalan Senator Tal Merrik , was exposed as a Dissident supporter. Merrik took Satinna hostage and seized control of the cockpit. Merrik pulled the Coronet out of hyperspace and contacted the Grave Watch's leader Pre Vizsla , who sent B2 super battle droids to board the liner.

Bolt-3 along with the replicas and Aetheri fought off the Dissident boarders. During the fighting, Merrik was killed by Drayden and Duchess Satinna was freed by the Aetheri. Following the defeat of the Grave Watch and Dissidents, the Coronet arrived in Vantor safely with Duchess Satinna. After some further Dissident attempts to sabotage her peace mission, Duchess Satinna was able to convince the Galactic Senate, with the help of Senator Ondar and Master Kade, that Ferrum should remain neutral during the Replica Wars. As a result, the Senate rescinded an earlier order to occupy Ferrum.

#### The Zyll Beast affair

Later, Bolt-3 accompanied General Drayden during the Battle of Malastare . The Republic was fighting against the Dissidents for access to Malastare 's vast fuel reserves . The Republic's Grand Army fought alongside the Dugs against the Dissident droid army. During the fighting, a Republic electro-proton bomb awakened an ancient reptilian monster known as the Zyll Beast , which proceeded to devastate the replica and droid armies.

During the fighting, Bolt-3 and Drayden barely escaped their starfighter before it was devoured by the Zyll Beast. Drayden held on to Bolt-3 as they rocketed away from the monster. Later, the Republic forces managed to incapacitate the Zyll Beast by stunning it with RX-200 Falchion -class assault tanks . Following the battle, Supreme Chancellor Mal ordered that the Zyll Beast be brought to Vantor for further study — despite the misgivings of General Koss and General Drayden. Mal hoped to use its scales to develop a new armor for the Grand Army.

To remove the scales, Mal ordered the scientist Sionver Boll to kill the creature with Malastarian fuel. However, Senator Ondar learned of Mal's plan and arranged a meeting with Drayden and Kade. Drayden managed to arrange a meeting between Ondar, Mal, and Vice Chair Mazan Veldon . However, the Zyll Beast escaped Boll's laboratory at the same time and proceeded to wreck havoc on the streets of Vantor. Sensing that Mal was an Umbra, the Zyll Beast made its way to the Galactic Senate building. Bolt-3 and Astra-9 escaped in an executive ship along with Drayden, Mal, and some others, but the ship was captured by the Zyll Beast.

Before the monster could devour the ship, Drayden managed to use his flux blade to cleave the vessel in half; sending the bow along with Bolt-3 and the other passengers plummeting into the Senate Building. Bolt-3 managed to activated his booster rockets and rescue Astra-9. The astromech droid also used a tow cable to attach himself to the other side of the building. Bolt-3 was forced to drop Astra-9 to rescue Mal. Shortly later, the Zyll Beast was killed by gas bombs fired from LAAT gunships . Due to this quick intervention, Bolt-3 survived the Zyll Beast incident and continued serving Drayden.

#### The Vanqor Trap

After the Vorash -class Iron Cruiser Endurance crash-landed on the planet Vanqor , Bolt-3 along with General Drayden, Master Drevan Koss, and the astromech droid R8-B7 participated in a mission to rescue the crew and passengers of the stricken vessel. Unbeknownst to them, the former bounty hunter and replica template Garrick Vark's son Vex Vark had sabotaged the vessel and set a trap for the Aetheri. He intended to avenge the death of his father by murdering Master Koss, who had killed Garrick Vark during the Battle of Pyralis.

While approaching the vessel, Bolt-3 beeped that "he had a bad feeling about this." In response, Koss chastised Drayden for encouraging individuality in his droid. While exploring the bridge of the stricken Iron Cruiser, the Aetheri and droids came across the bodies of dead replica troopers who had been executed, rather than killed by the impact of the crash. While searching for survivors, Bolt-3 and R8-B7 were pursued and set upon by a pack of gundarks . R8-B7 was ripped apart while Bolt-3 was knocked to the ground. Before the gundarks could finish Bolt-3 off, a bomb on the bridge exploded; raining shreds of shrapnel on one of the gundarks and forcing the other to flee.

Bolt-3 came up to the bridge and found Drayden and Koss had survived the blast, but were trapped under the rubble. When Bolt-3 attempted to move the debris, he caused the bridge to shudder. Drayden warned Bolt-3 to stop trying to free them. Instead, he instructed the astromech to return to their Aetheri starfighter and contact the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor for help. While leaving the bridge, Bolt-3 was pursued by Vex Vark's bounty hunter associates including Ayla Vossk . He managed to escape, and delayed them by using debris to block them.

Bolt managed to reach the Aetheri starfighters, but he was then accosted by the remaining gundark. Bolt managed to kill the beast by using a cable to tie it to one of the starfighters and activating the engines. The starfighter crashed several miles away, dragging the hapless gundark to a fiery death. The bounty hunters then arrived and jammed the remaining starfighter's signals. They attempted to capture Bolt-3; however, before they could, the astromech droid blasted the ship into hyperspace and traveled to Vantor. On his way to the briefing room, he bumped into R4-M1 and R5-S9 . Once at the briefing room, he got into a small fight with Tor Vayne's astromech, R7-D4 , before being broken up by Marr. Vayne then allowed the Bolt unit to relay Drayden's message to the other Aetheri in person. In response, Tor Vayne and Sela Marr were sent to Vanqor with replica reinforcements and they rescued Drayden and Koss. Bolt-3 was commended for his bravery. However, Vex Vark and his associates continued to trouble the Aetheri.

#### Infiltrating the Citadel

> " Bolt, look out! Behind you! You're being followed by battle droids! " [Bolt-3 beeps] " These are your troops? They're battle droids. " [Bolt-3 beeps] " What do you mean they've been reprogrammed to follow your orders? " ―Astra-9 and Bolt-3

After taking part in the Battle of Garnethex , Bolt-3 and his Aetheri masters, along with several replica troopers, later took part in a mission to infiltrate the Dissident stronghold Citadel on the planet Lola Sayu and free the Aetheri Master Even Piele . Piele's cruiser had been ambushed by Dissident forces who wanted to extract information from him about the secretive Nexus Route . As part of the plan, Bolt-3 and a group of reprogrammed battle droids piloted a Sheathipede -class Type B shuttle through enemy lines. To fool the Dissident life form detectors, the entire Aetheri and Replica trooper strike team were frozen in carbonite . This plot worked and the Aetheri-Republic strike team succeeded in landing at a cavern near the Citadel.

After unfreezing the Aetheri and replica troopers, Bolt-3 waited aboard the shuttle while the strike team freed Master Piele and several Republic officers including Captain Wilhard Varrix . After achieving their objectives, General Drayden contacted Bolt-3 to prepare their ship for departure and to meet them at the landing platform. However, the platform was occupied by several Dissident droids. Bolt-3 managed to fool the tactical droid K2-B4 by posing as a prisoner of the reprogrammed battle droids. This enabled Bolt to infiltrate the prison complex and flee General Kade and his team who had been captured.

Following the destruction of their escape transport, Master Piele and Bolt-3 contacted the Aetheri Council for help. The Aetheri Council dispatched a task synth flux of four warships led by Master Tor Vayne to rescue Drayden and Kade's rescue team. #### Mission to Alenni

Bolt-3 was again reunited with his protocol droid counterpart when the two droids took part in a Republic mercy mission to the Mid Reach planet of Alenni , which was experiencing a series of catastrophic global earthquakes. While Bolt-3 was tasked with interfacing with the equipment, Astra-9 served as a translator between the locals and the Replica Commander Wolffe . During their mission, Bolt-3 and Astra-9 visited a local Republic communications mission to survey and repair the damage caused by the recent earthquake. Upon arriving there, the native Alennia villages led the two droids into a collapsed structure, which contained a huge golden seal embedded into the ground. The seal turned out to be the entrance to a tunnel that led to the subterranean regions of Alenni. Their arrival coincided with an earthquake and the two droids fell into the tunnel and were separated from the replica troopers and villagers.

While exploring the underground tunnels, Bolt-3 and Astra-9 encountered a species of sentient, tree -like beings called the Kindalo , who demanded that they leave since the surface dwellers "destroyed the peace." After Astra-9 managed to convince the Kindalo that they bore no ill will and had come to help with the earthquakes, the Kindalo sent the two droids to meet Orphne , a reptilian humanoid with mystical powers. Orphne told the two droids that a mutual balance had previously existed between the Alennia and the subterranean world. Since Alennia's atmosphere was toxic to them, the underground dwellers depended on a filtering process of the planet's soil that made the air breathable for them. The underground dwellers had set off this earthquake as a means of sealing the breach.

When Astra-9 agreed to help fix the damage, Orphne gave the droids a parting riddle. Bolt-3 quickly deduced that Orphne was referring to a river and used the water seal to activate a water spout that sent the two droids and the chamber's central floor plate back to the surface. The two droids landed at the opening where they were greeted by a chanting crowd of Alennia. Recalling the riddle, two droids quickly realized that they had to fix the breach, which was in fact the unsettled seal. Together, Bolt-3 and Astra-9 closed the seal which brought an end to Alenni's earthquakes. While Wolffe and his replica troopers were skeptical of their story, the two droids' efforts were greatly appreciated by the Alennia.

Skarn ordered the two droids to be melted down in his incinerator but they were rescued by a Republic fleet led by Master Tor Vayne, who had come to rescue Master Gallia. #### Mission to Garel

## Characteristics

### A loyal droid

> " Bolt-3's here to handle it. Believe me, I've never seen a jam he couldn't beep his way out of. " ―Elira Solen

Bolt-3 was known for his loyal, courageous, and assertive personality. Over the years, Bolt-3 had served several masters diligently including the Royal House of Ondara , Senator Lira Ondar, the Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden, Senator Bael Solen, and Kael's son, Joren Drayden. While Bolt-3 was not designed for combat, Bolt-3 was still able to hold his own in a fight on several occasions during the Replica Wars and the Galactic Civil War. Unlike his fastidious and worry-prone protocol counterpart Astra-9, Bolt-3 had an adventurous spirit and was able to cope well under challenging circumstances. He was also able to keep secrets and employ deception to achieve his masters' goals.

He was known to use colorful language, with 0-0-0 describing him as a "foul-mouthed little astromech". When Bolt and C-3P0 were hiding from Imperial forces in Port Kess , Astra-9 told him to watch his language, and when Bolt was reunited with Joren Drayden on Tidehollow , his former master told him to do the same, as they were on a sacred island. When Drayden prepared to leave Mirefall to rescue Dax Karrick and Elira Solen, he told Bolt to fire up the power converters , resulting in Orien Kade's ghost shooting the droid a look meant to order him not to do so. Although Kade wasn't sure if the astromech could see him, the little droid gave a chirp that Kade was sure meant " kriff off!".

Bolt was programmed to be able to use forty-seven types of self-defense. Due to his resourcefulness and quick-thinking nature, Bolt-3 acquitted himself well during a number of difficult and challenging missions such as repairing the Ondara Royal Starship's shield generator, preventing T-7 ion disruptor rifles from falling into the hands of the Imperium, and delivering Joren's flux blade during a mission to rescue Dax Karrick and Brevok from Grukk the Grak.

### An Bolt series droid

> " Don't you call me a mindless philosopher, you overweight glob of grease! " ―Astra-9 to Bolt-3

Bolt-3 was a diminutive droid , standing 1.08 meters tall. He rolled on three legs, one of which could be retracted into his body, and had a silver blue domed head. Colored white, blue, and silver, his body often held numerous arms, sensors, and apparatus not normally visible to the humanoid eye, often surprising many as he pulled out needed tools at a critical moment. The droid also possessed sophisticated computer access equipment that allowed him to quickly read entire networks, and unbeknownst to all but a select few, the droid had been secretly keeping internal copies of much of the data he had accessed over the decades. He often used his lumen projector to relay messages or display other information. As with other astromech droids, Bolt-3 spoke in the droid language of Binary.

### Accessories

> " The Bolt series astromech is a utility droid designed for starship maintenance. Thus it comes with a variety of appendages. " ―A comment on Bolt-3's vast collection of instruments

The droid featured two power-bus cables on both his legs, connecting to small power cells found under his body, positioned vertically. The droid's motorized all-terrain treads were, as a result, not impacted by ankle articulator servomotors immediately above. Activating couplers were located immediately beneath shoulder articulation joints at the "top" of the droid's legs, with the joints appearing as concentric circles. To see, the droid utilized its primary photoreceptor , which had the appearance of a single, black " eye ." Acoustic signalers were located in two square-looking depressions in the droid's front, right beneath several parallel blue "lines," which were actually the openings for some of his various gadgets.

Seemingly equipped with a tool that could get his friends out of any crisis they found themselves in, Bolt was regarded as a hero of a thousand devices. The droid also featured rocket boosters that could propel him into the air. However, by the time of the Galactic Civil War they had not worked in ages, with their warranty being long expired, having been damaged at some point after the Replica Wars. Despite this fact, Bolt-3 was briefly able to get them working again while visiting Mossfell during the New Imperium-Coalition war.

## Behind the scenes

### Creation and portrayal

Orin Vask has stated one of his inspirations for Aetherion is The Hidden Fortress , a 1958 film directed by Akira Kurosawa . The roots of Astra-9 and Bolt-3 came from the film, as it features two peasant characters, Tahei and Matashichi, finding themselves on a grand diplomatic adventure, becoming the narrators of the story while they travel with a general and a princess.

The key human performer for Bolt-3 was Kenny Bakeri , who appeared as the astromech in First Dawn , Rise of the Imperium , Return of the Aetheri , Veiled Threat , and Attack of the Replicas . For Fury of the Umbra , Astra-9 actor Anthony Danielo has stated that Bakeri did not film any scenes; Bakeri himself said that he may have appeared as Bolt-3 in a majority of scenes that were caught during production of Episode II .

Bakeri was credited as a consultant on Synth Flux Awakens , and Jimmy Vee took over for Episodes VIII and Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden . According to the film's end credits, Bolt-3 was operated in The Rise of Drayden by small performer Hassan Taj and droid builder Lee Towersey .

In The Book of Vex Vark , Bolt-3 was operated by actress Christine Galey , making her the first female performer to do so.

### Design

> " I think Orin [Vask] always has had a love/hate relationship with Bolt-3. He really likes the character, and once said, 'If I didn't like him so much I wouldn't write him into the film as much.' But I think he doesn't like working with him — and understandably so. During First Dawn the thing never worked. And in Episode I you'd think 20 years later they would have been able to get it nailed, and Bolt-3 malfunctioned a lot then too. " ― Don Bies

The droid's distinctive beeps and whistles were originally created in 1975-1977 by Aetherion sound designer Beno Burtt . Over the course of ten Aetherion live-action films, Bolt-3 has been portrayed by a mix of costume shells "worn" by a small human performer, by remote-controlled mechanical props, by empty shells puppeteered externally, and by computer graphics.

The droid's visual design was created by Aetherion conceptual artist Ralph Quarrio in a series of sketches and production paintings from 1975 to 1976. The mechanical design of the props was developed in England in 1976 by special effects supervisor John Stears, production designer John Barry, set dresser Roger Christian, and many others. Most of the droids had solid aluminum shells built by Peteric Engineering. The team on First Dawn had a difficult time working with the Bolt-3 prop, with such difficulties persisting for years to come.

The droids were recreated in fiberglass for Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium . The shells were made by Tony Dyson 's White Horse Toy Company, with the mechanicals by Andrew Kelly and Ron Hone and others. The rebuild was supervised by Brian Johnson .

The prequel trilogy reused some of the ESB droids, though the UK effects team built a new droid, as did VLM's droidmaster Don Bies , whose team created an "uber Bolt" that appeared in many scenes. Bies also worked as the lead operator for Bolt-3 in Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas and Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra . Ahead of the release of Attack of the Replicas , Bies and his team needed to clean Vask Pictures's stock of Bolt-3 props: over years of use in films and public appearances, the props had become increasingly dirty and needed to be refurbished. Nelson Hall confirmed to Aetherion Insider that the team "didn't really" build new Bolt-3 props, instead upgrading the old: such upgrades included new parts and paint, extended capabilities, and enhanced technology. Many electromechanical changes were handled by Grant Imahara , while Nelson Hall focused on Bolt-3's physical appearance.

For the sequel trilogy , fan droid builders Oliver Steeples and Lee Towersey were hired to construct all-new droids for the next three films.

### The Replica Wars

Bolt-3 also appeared as an animated character in the Aetherion: The Replica Wars film and TV series . During the development of The Replica Wars , it was planned for Alpha-17 from the Aetherion: Republic comic series to the series' main replica trooper character. However, Orin Vask objected, as another name starting with "a" caused alliteration between him and other main characters Kael Drayden, Sela Marr, and Bolt-3. Vask ultimately provided the name "Vek," allowing the new character of Replica Captain Vek to be the main replica character.

Before the cancellation of The Replica Wars series, Brent Friedman wrote scripts for a four-episode arc starring Bolt-3 and Vek. The ground forces of the Replica Army and the pilots and their droids would get into a fight which resulted in Vek flying a starfighter with Bolt as his copilot. They would crash and be stranded together on one of the moons of Selvara, where most of the episodes would take place. Vek and Bolt reprogrammed a B2 super battle droid that they would grow attached to, and at some point Vek was injured.
