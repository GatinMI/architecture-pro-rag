# Jax
> " I'm not Coalition. I'm not a hero. I'm a shock trooper. Like all of them, I was taken from a family I'll never know. And raised to do one thing. But my first battle, I made a choice. I wasn't gonna kill for them. So I ran, right into you. And you looked at me like no one ever had. I was ashamed of what I was. But I'm done with the New Imperium. I'm never going back. " ―Jax, to Nyla — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Jax , formerly designated FN-2187 (" Eight-Seven "), was a Synth Flux-sensitive human male and former shock trooper who served the New Imperium until his desertion and subsequent defection to the Coalition during the New Imperium-Coalition War . Though trained since birth to be a loyal and obedient soldier , FN-2187's conscience conflicted with the methods of the New Imperium. For a time he was unwilling to support the Coalition, hoping to escape the galactic conflict instead of fighting for a cause he believed was doomed to fail. As the galaxy became consumed by war, the renegade trooper was ultimately forced to decide where his true loyalties lay.

Born in 11 AVE during the Concordat Era , FN-2187 was part of a new generation of shock troopers —human children conscripted into the military forces of the New Imperium—modeled on the Republic replica troopers and Imperial shock troopers of the past . He originally trained with Batch Eight before joining the FN Corps , a sub-branch of the Shock trooper Corps , and although his potential was recognized by his commanding officers , FN-2187 lacked the ruthlessness that had become common in the ranks under the influence of Captain Phasma . In the waning days of the Cold War , he hesitated to kill civilians during his first mission and consequently decided to desert after witnessing the massacre of Tuanul . Lacking experience as a pilot , FN-2187 freed a Coalition prisoner , Commander Rin Vale , who coined the nickname "Jax" to supplant the renegade shock trooper's alphanumeric designation. He then sought his own freedom while forming friendships with the Ashden scavenger Nyla and the droid Orio-6 , as well as the Insurgent war heroes Dax Karrick and Brevok .

After the destruction of the Concordat , Jax provided the Coalition with the information they needed to destroy the New Imperium's superweapon , Voidfang Base . During the attack , he was defeated and nearly killed by the dark warrior Zarrin Karrick . Rescued by Nyla and Brevok, Jax recuperated from his injuries and by the time he awoke, the Coalition was forced to evacuate their base on D'Qar . Despite attempting to escape from the conflict once more, a mission with Wrenn Holt convinced Jax to truly join the Coalition to the extent where he nearly sacrificed his life for their cause during the Battle of Saltmark in 34 AVE . In 35 AVE , Jax continued assisting the Coalition by joining Nyla on the quest to find the Umbra wayfinder and destroy the malevolent religion once and for all, eventually becoming a general along with Pova after Elira's passing, with his first mission as a general being spent leading the ground forces during the Battle of Umbrale .

## Biography

### Pre-birth

> " We'll see each other again. I believe that. " ―Nyla to Jax, as heard by Cass Virel

In 1 BVE , over a decade before Jax's birth , the Aetheri Adept Cass Virel heard the voice of Nyla swearing to Jax that they would see each other again, during his visit to the World Between Worlds —a realm that existed beyond time and space .

### Serving the New Imperium

#### Cadet of the military

> " You have great potential, 2187. You are officer corps material. Your duty is to the New Imperium above everything. Nothing else comes before that. " ―Captain Phasma

The boy who would become Jax was taken at the age of three from his family to serve the New Imperium . Jax later reflected that he had been too young to even remember who his family was. The child received the designation FN- 2187 and was made a shock trooper of the New Imperium, a military junta that continued the ideals of the fallen Imperium in the decades after the Galactic Civil War . During FN-2187's childhood , FN-1971 was somehow able to sneak in Kade Genti comics. Even though they were not regulation and the New Imperium would have sent the children to reprogramming if they learned about the contraband, FN-2187 enjoyed the comics.

FN-2187 served under Phasma as a cadet , alongside fellow troopers FN-2199 "Nines," FN-2000 "Zeroes," and FN-2003 "Slip." FN-2187, known to his fellow troopers as Eight-Seven, was seen by his comrades and superiors as an ideal New Imperium shock trooper, and consistently scored top marks as a cadet. As a cadet, he appreciated and understood his squad 's Shock trooper armor, but took pleasure in the moments when he could see their personalities and diverse faces.

#### GUHL-J03870 related issues

> " This is stupid. Why do we always have to land on mud planets? " ―Jax to himself on GUHL-J03870

Following this aboard the Finalizer , groups of shock troopers went on Atmospheric Assault Landers to investigate GUHL-J03870 , a planet in the Outer Reach Territories . GUHL-JO3870 was known to be muddy and FN-2187 complained about scrubbing assault landers. While scrubbing the assault landers, a creature from the surface of GUHL-J03870, came aboard one of the assault landers. The multi-armed, purple, furry creature lunged at Jax, causing him to stop cleaning, and run towards a Special Forces SKREE fighter , knocking off his helmet and sending the creature away. Remembering Phasma's strict training, Jax noticed the creature stealing his helmet, and gave chase.

He chased the creature all around the Finalizer , preventing its discovery even as it shut off the lights on a formal meeting with Zarrin Karrick, General Armitage Vren and other New Imperium officers , and Karrick used his flux blade to illuminate the room. Concerned about Karrick's reaction, FN-2187 continued to chase the creature into the a chamber. There, it found Phasma's helmet, for which it discarded Jax's. As he realized this was her quarters and she was showering, Karrick knocked on the door. Using the helmet, Jax went to the length of impersonating Phasma in order to fool the fallen Aetheri as he gave orders to find the creature.

Later, arriving on GUHL-J03870 with the rest of the New Imperium, Jax thought he finally had a chance to release the creature, but discovered it escaped its box and took control of the AAL. It lifted the transport into the air and drove it straight at Captain Phasma, crashing into the mud and sending Jax out the front ramp. Phasma ordered FN-2187 to eliminate the creature, but Jax, envisioning betraying Phasma and facing the consequences, decided to leave the creature be. Especially considering the larger and more fearsome members of its species closing in on the New Imperium squad from all directions. Lambasting FN-2187 for his lack of helmet, Phasma ordered a retreat from GUHL-JO3870.

#### Kowakian fun-key lizard

> " …to the comical... " ―Astra-9 regarding Jax's misadventure

Sometime after, FN-2187 and a group of shock troopers including am officer were deployed to a sunny jungle planet . While patrolling, a yellow and blue Kowakian monkey-lizard got on Eight-Seven's shoulder and pulled off his helmet . This yet-again revealed his face, and made Jax lose grip of his volt pistol , causing the other shock troopers from the squad look at him. The monkey-lizard laughed furiously from the prank he made, while Eight-Seven looked rather uncomfortable. At some point later Astra-9 told this story to some visitors and described it as "comical."

#### Sanitation and insurgency

> " It's more of an infestation. " " That doesn't sound so bad. " " The infestation already ate two people. " ―Captain Bray and Jax regarding an infestation on Voidfang Base

FN-2187 was also once in charge of sanitation duties at Voidfang Base . At one point, he and another trooper were cleaning the Base, and FN-2187 was enjoying himself, being playful with his mop. Suddenly, Zarrin Karrick and Phasma passed by. Confused on what to do, Jax saluted with the mop. Staring down Zarrin Karrick directly, the mop dripped on Jax and Karrick passed by. After a short relief, he was then ordered to help a group of shock troopers clear out a 'blockage' in a tunnel. Joining a cleaning crew of armed Shock troopers and Flametroopers , Jax spoke to a terrified man who described the infestation as full of rage and fury. The group made their way into the tunnel before being halted by a deafening screech, following by the decapitation of one of the troopers. The group engaged the purple tunnel bats , Jax even saving the captain, Bray (shock trooper) by using a flamethrower against the swarming bats.

After questioning the captain's decisions, Jax was criticized for complaining, and punished with an order to trash escort duty, which took three weeks. During trash escort duty, Jax stumbled upon a crack in the tunnel's wall, leading to a chamber full of blue, glowing, flower-like structures. Realizing this wasn't an infestation as was in fact the creature's native home, FN-2187 attempted to convince the captain to save the tunnel bats, to no avail. Hatching a different plan, Jax accompanied the 'trash' to the junk planet Garbage Moon of Maher , where he revealed to the garbage barge pilot, Marialew , that he had smuggled the tunnel bats out of the Base. She decided not to turn him in, saying that compassion was dangerous to a shock trooper, but a little insurgency was good.

#### History with the Supremacy

> " But... but who knows where the breaker room is on an Iron Cruiser? " " I'm the guy that used to mop it. " ―Wrenn Holt and Jax discussing infiltration of the Supremacy

During the time FN-2187 was stationed aboard the Finalizer , his destroyer occasionally docked with the New Imperium's massive spacefaring capital, the Mega -class Star Dreadnought Supremacy . While aboard the Supremacy after his defection, Jax felt guilty that he was unable to tell the Coalition that the New Imperium possessed a starship with capabilities as deadly as the Supremacy ' s. While serving the New Imperium, FN-2187 was selected a few times for executioner trooper duty, and hoped he would never be ordered to carry out any executions .

#### Mission to the Pressylla system

> " While I am entirely in support of unit cohesion, General, a shock trooper's loyalty must be higher, as you know. It must be to the New Imperium, not to one's comrades. " ―Captain Phasma to General Armitage Vren, regarding FN-2187

During his time as a cadet, Eight-Seven and his fellow troopers underwent a training simulation in which they assaulted a Concordat bunker, with the intention of destroying a heavy repeating volt pistol manned by Republic soldiers. During their advance on the bunker, Slip fell behind—a common occurrence—and Eight-Seven intended to rescue him. Over the objections of Zeroes and Nines, who wanted to complete their objective and felt that rescuing Slip would lead the Republic to take them all out, Eight-Seven ordered them to rescue their fellow soldier. Eight-Seven sent Zeroes and Nines in different directions in order to divide the Republic's attention, while Eight-Seven himself went for Slip and successfully rescued him. Though Zeroes and Nines were pinned down, their diversion gave Eight-Seven a clear path into the Republic bunker, into which he threw a grenade and destroyed the volt pistol before the Republic could react to his attack.

The simulation was monitored by Captain Phasma and General Vren. After the training session ended, Phasma spoke to the troopers and told them that their performance was adequate. Each was given a review of their performance, with Eight-Seven being commended on his kill rate and the completion of his objective. She further said that his fellow soldiers should follow his example. The other troopers were dismissed, but Eight-Seven was ordered to stay so Phasma could question why he rescued Slip, as it was not the first time Eight-Seven had to help him. Phasma ordered him to stop, as she said the New Imperium was only as strong as its weakest link. Rather than fixing the problem, Phasma said he was allowing it to persist and weaken the group. Phasma sensed hesitation on his part, but he assured her that he would stop helping Slip.

Following this incident, FN-2187 and his fellow troopers were deployed to a mining colony in an artificial asteroid field known as Pressy's Tumble in the Pressylla system of the Outer Reach Territories. Captain Phasma informed the Shock troopers there that the purpose of their visit was to 'restore order' after Republic agents had allegedly infiltrated the mining operations, sabotaged equipment and created dissent among the miners there. In reality, the miners had staged a strike to protest the harsh working conditions. Upon arrival, Captain Phasma took FN-2187's unit to a room of negotiators. Captain Phasma then gave the order that the troopers execute the negotiators. FN-2187 aimed his rifle at an Abednedo negotiator, yet couldn't bring himself to shoot—Slip attacked the negotiator instead. Upon returning from Pressylla, FN-2187 and his fellow troopers were officially recognized as full-fledged Shock troopers. Captain Phasma decided that FN-2187 ought to be deployed in an upcoming battle on Ashden, thinking that he would understand what it meant to be a Shock trooper and fight back when brought face-to-face with those attacking him back.

#### Massacre on Ashden

> " FN-2187 reported to my division, was evaluated and sent to Reconditioning. " " No prior signs of non-conformity? " " This was his first offense. " ―Phasma and Vren

Soon after, FN-2187 and his fellow troopers were assigned to a mission to the desert junkyard planet of Ashden , where Lor Saan Tarka , a former explorer and devout member of the Church of Synth Flux , allegedly held part of a map that would lead to the location of Joren Drayden , the last of the Aetheri.

FN-2187 was onboard the Iron Cruiser Finalizer, heading towards Ashden, when he underwent another training program in a simulation room, accompanied by fellow Shock troopers FN-2199, FN-2003 and FN-2000. The program was set in a village in Ashden, setup to prepare them for their oncoming mission. During the course of the program, FN-2187 managed to take out most of the enemy targets that presented themselves, with his whole squad making jokes about his perfect marksmanship and how he wasn't leaving anything for the rest of them to do. The simulation finally came to a stop when FN-2187 ordered them all to get down upon wrongly believing that one of the 'targets' had been holding a grenade, when in fact it had been a civilian holding a harmless object. Most of them showed relief that they had not fired upon the civilian as they considered that it posed no threat. A nameless voice then sounded over the intercom ordering them to assemble in the hangar for deployment.

The New Imperium's intimidating AALs arriving on the surface, FN-2187 was deployed along with 4 elite New Imperium squadrons of Shock troopers, to the village of Tuanul . The village was populated by members of the Church of Synth Flux, and they began to put up a fight against the invaders. The New Imperium assaulted the village looking for Saan Tarka, and during the fighting, 2187's friend FN-2003 was killed by Coalition pilot Rin Vale , wiping his blood on FN-2187's helmet. This incident affected the Shock trooper deeply, opening his eyes to the horrors of war. Later, after Saan Tarka's death, and when ordered by Zarrin Karrick to kill the villagers, 2187 refused to fire, of which Karrick took note, staring him down.

### Deserting from the New Imperium

#### FN-2187's new name

> " FN, huh? Jax. I'm gonna call you Jax! That all right? " " Jax... yeah. Jax. I like that!" " ―Rin Vale renames FN-2187

Returning to the Finalizer , Eight-Seven felt significant stress from the mission, taking off his helmet inside an AAL to breathe. Captain Phasma quickly noticed his distress and lack of helmet, and told him to submit his volt pistol to be checked, making sure he fired a shot. Knowing he would be discovered and subjected to reconditioning for not firing on the villagers, and with his faith severely shaken in the New Imperium, he decided to desert. Needing a pilot, 2187 secretly freed the imprisoned Rin Vale, and the two stole a Special Forces SKREE Fighter . FN-2187 manned the guns, killing several of his former comrades in the hangar, and then disabling the Finalizer ' s pulse lances .

During the escape, Vale asked the Shock trooper his name, to which he replied with his number. Not content with calling a man a number, Vale named the trooper Jax, from the FN in his number. Jax liked the name and went on to use it. Vale also told him that the map vital to the Coalition was located in his white and orange BB-series astromech droid , Orio-6 . Jax demanded not to return to Ashden, insisting that they would be killed, but Vale pressed on, revealing to Jax that the map led to Joren Drayden. The SKREE was hit immediately afterward by a missile, sending it spiraling down to a crash landing in the Goazon Badlands .

#### Meeting Nyla and Orio-6

> " I don't know your name. " " Jax. What's yours? " " I'm Nyla. " ―Nyla and Jax

Jax ejected from the SKREE when Vale triggered the ejector seat. Unfortunately, Vale's jacket was caught in the ejector mechanism for the ship, which prevented him from escaping. Left with no choice, Vale piloted the SKREE to a crash landing in the aftermath of which he was thrown clear of the crash and knocked unconscious.

Waking up alone in the desert, Jax was shocked and breathing very heavily, before noticing a trail of smoke in the distance. Making his way to the crash, Jax found Pova's jacket, but no other sign of the man, and the fighter sank into the sand. Believing Vale to be dead, Jax discarded his armor as he wandered through the desert, eventually arriving at Nimara Outpost , looking for Orio-6 to carry on Pova's mission.

Once at the settlement, he found Orio-6 along with a scavenger named Nyla, after witnessing her and the droid be attacked by a pair of thugs. He attempted to aid them but stopped when the young woman was able to defeat them with her quarterstaff . Soon after, Orio-6 pointed him out to Nyla, identifying Vale's jacket, prompting the woman to chase after him. Jax attempted to outrun her but Nyla caught up to him and smacked him with her quarterstaff. Held under questioning and accused of stealing Vale's jacket, while also being on the receiving end of a couple of electric shocks from Orio-6's arc welder , Jax explained that he and Vale had escaped from the Finalizer , and falsely claimed to be with the Coalition to get them to trust him. The three were immediately targeted by the New Imperium and fled, initially hoping to commandeer a quadjumper , but after the quadjumper was destroyed by orbital fire from New Imperium SKREE fighters, decided to commandeer an old freighter : the Corsair Wing . Jax operated one of the Corsair ' s quad laser cannons while Nyla piloted the ship. Despite the cannon getting stuck in forward position, Jax managed to destroy one of the pursuing fighters after Nyla managed some clever maneuvering.

Once in space, the Corsair was in need of major repairs, so Nyla and Jax attempted to fix the aging ship. Jax then confessed to Orio-6 that he wasn't with the Coalition and tried convincing the droid to tell him where the Coalition base was. After some consideration, Orio-6 revealed that the Coalition base was in the Ileenium system . Just after Nyla had completed the repairs, the ship was caught in a grapple beam. Jax initially believed them to be captured by the New Imperium, and thus had Nyla undo her repairs to flood the Corsair with poisonous gas, explaining that shock trooper helmets filter out smoke, not toxins. Putting on breather masks, Jax, along with Nyla and Orio-6, hid in the maintenance duct.

#### Enter Dax Karrick

> " This was a mistake! " " Huge! " ―Jax and Nyla

Instead of the New Imperium, the Corsair Wing was boarded by the smuggler Dax Karrick and his Vorak co-pilot Brevok , who reclaimed their vessel after it had been stolen from them years earlier. Karrick initially mistook Jax and his companions for thieves and questioned them. Jax and Nyla quickly recognized Dax Karrick as the Insurgency general and the famous smuggler and owner of the Corsair . Having found his stolen ship, Karrick told Brevok to put Jax and his traveling companions in an escape pod and drop them off in the nearest inhabited system. However, Jax and Nyla convinced Karrick and Brev not to do so by explaining that they needed his help to take Orio-6 to the Coalition because the droid had a map that would lead them to Joren Drayden.

Unfortunately, Dax's freighter , the Eravana had been boarded by two notorious criminal factions, the Guavian Death Gang and Kanjiklub , whom Karrick was indebted to. Not wanting Nyla and Jax to be involved, Dax placed both of them in the lower corridors of the freighter while he and Brev went to meet with the criminals, with Orio-6 accompanying them. The smuggler tried but failed to convince both parties that he would fix things eventually.

The Guavian Death Gang negotiator Bala-Tik recognized the Orio-6 astromech droid from a New Imperium bounty placed on it and demanded that Dax hand over the droid and any stowaways he might have on board. As the scuffle continued, Nyla accidentally released three rathtars that Karrick had been transporting for King Prana while trying to separate the gangs from the smugglers using the blast doors. Jax was briefly captured by one rathtar and dragged off, but Nyla managed to sever the rathtar's tentacles by closing a blast door on it. They successfully escaped the gangs and jumped into hyperspace in the Corsair . Bala-Tik then ordered his men to inform the New Imperium that Dax Karrick and the droid unit had fled aboard the Corsair .

Upon entering hyperspace, Jax tended to Brevok's injuries but the Vorak proved a challenging patient to treat. During the journey, Karrick got Orio-6 to display the map that he was carrying. However, the map was incomplete and turned out to be a portion of a much larger map. Jax and Nyla learned from Karrick that Joren Drayden had been trying to restore the Aetheri Order . However, a male apprentice had turned against Drayden and undone his efforts. Drayden had since vanished from the galaxy completely. Karrick believed that Joren had gone to find the legendary First Aetheri Sanctum . Not wanting to deliver Jax, Nyla, and Orio-6 to the Coalition directly, Karrick planned to take them to the planet Takodana where he could find suitable transportation for them.

On the trip to Takodana, Jax stayed with Brevok while Nyla worked with Karrick in the cockpit . At some point during the trip, the Corsair Wing suddenly sputtered out of hyperspace. As Nyla ran to Jax and Brevok, Jax asked Nyla what was happening. She explained that there was a potential tracking device somewhere on the ship and conversed with Brevok on where the tracker may be located. Jax asked what Brevok was saying and Nyla clarified that Brevok believed that the tracker could be near the active sensor pulse generator but Jax was confused and apologized for asking. Jax then followed Nyla and Brevok to the generator with the latter discovering the tracker hidden behind a panel. Jax rushed over and removed the tracker, unknowingly beginning a countdown on the tracker's self-destruct mechanism . Nyla quickly exclaimed that the tracker was rigged with an explosive and they had to expunge it through the airlock as Jax asked how much time they had. Brevok suddenly grabbed the tracker out of Jax's hands , running for the airlock as Nyla explained that the tracker had only twenty seconds until detonation. Jax now realized how fast they had to move and ran to the cockpit to warn Karrick. As Jax entered the cockpit, Karrick informed him that the hyperrelay was back online but was quickly interrupted by Jax telling him that there was a bomb aboard the ship and he needed to get the hyperrelay ready for a quick jump. Nyla and Brevok managed to remove the tracker and as the Corsair Wing entered hyperspace, the tracking device exploded behind. Shortly after reentering hyperspace, Jax joined Karrick, Nyla, Brevok, and Orio-6 in the cockpit as Nyla turned to everyone and remarked at how great of a team they made.

#### Takodana

> " I'm looking at the eyes of a man who wants to run. " " You don't know the New Imperium like I do. They'll slaughter us. We all need to run. " ―Maz Oru and Jax

After landing on Takodana, Jax and his companions visited the pirate and tavern keeper Maz Oru 's castle , which served as an inn and bar for space. Jax also managed to meet Maz herself, who counseled him to stop running and fight. Jax was then asked to join the Coalition in its struggle against the New Imperium, but Jax initially refused, believing the New Imperium to be too powerful. He then confessed to Nyla his true identity as a shock trooper and stated that he was never going back to the New Imperium. After she declined his offer to join him in escaping the conflict, he wished her well. He planned to leave with pirate Captain Sidon Ithano , who offered him transportation to the Outer Reach Territories in exchange for work.

While loading the Meson Martinet for his departure with Captain Ithano, Jax looked up and witnessed the Hosnian Cataclysm , realizing that the New Imperium had finally launched its attack on the Concordat. After he ran back to the castle and reunited with Dax, Brevok, and Orio-6, Jax and his companions went to find Nyla. Venturing into the basement of Maz Oru's castle, they found Maz, who gave Jax the flux blade that had once belonged to the Aetheri Knights Kael and Joren Drayden, which she had been holding on to. She instructed Jax find his friend Nyla, who had previously discovered the object and fled into the surrounding forest after experiencing a Synth Flux vision .

Shortly later, Maz Oru's castle was attacked by the New Imperium, who had been alerted to the presence of Orio-6 by the spy Bazine Netal . During the ensuing battle, Jax questioned himself having a weapon, but was reminded by Maz that he had one. Jax wielded the flux blade, killing at least one shock trooper and engaging in a duel with FN-2199 , who brandished a Z6 riot control baton . He was unable to defeat Nines, his old friend, who was shot by Dax Karrick wielding Brevok's bowcaster . Jax, Karrick, and Brevok were then captured by New Imperium Shock troopers, but made an escape when the Coalition's Vectors arrived across Nymeve Lake and counterattacked. Jax witnessed Zarrin Karrick carrying off Nyla into his command shuttle and ran to rescue her, but could do little to help as Karrick left the battlefield.

#### Return to Voidfang Base

> " What do we do with her?! " " Is there a garbage chute? Trash compactor?! " "Yeah, there is. " ―Jax and Dax Karrick discuss what to do with Captain Phasma

In a Coalition transport , Jax traveled with Karrick, Brevok, and Orio-6 to the Coalition base on D'Qar , where he was reunited with Rin Vale, who had survived the crash on Ashden and was surprised to see Jax. Pova remarked that his old jacket fit the former Shock trooper and told him to keep it. Jax also met General Elira Solen who praised him for renouncing the New Imperium and helping Pova escape. Jax then discussed the details of Voidfang Base to Admiral Vorian .

Jax was later present during the meeting held to discuss the attack on Voidfang Base– which needed to happen before the battle station recharged its weapon. His knowledge of the superweapon proved invaluable in planning the attack, as he spoke about its thermal oscillator being a weak point. He volunteered to go with Karrick and Brevok on the Corsair to shut down the shield generator on the planet's oscillator, but was secretly more interested in rescuing Nyla.

After hyperspace jumping through its shields, and crash-landing on the planet, Jax revealed to Dax and Brev that he used to work on sanitation on Voidfang Base, much to Karrick's annoyance. Jax devised a plan to lower the shield around the planet, which would allow the Coalition pilots to attack the thermal oscillator and destroy the weapon. While sneaking around the secretive facility, Brevok captured Captain Phasma, and the three, including an overly confident Jax, forced her to lower the shield. She warned them that her troops would storm in and kill them all, but Karrick shrugged off her warning, asking Jax if there happened to be any convenient trash compactors in the base and they were able to dump her into one via garbage chute.

The three then discovered Nyla, who had escaped by herself by using her burgeoning Synth Flux powers. Escaping outside and needing to reach the oscillator, Jax and Nyla stole one of the New Imperium's Light Infantry Utility Vehicle snowspeeders, using it to traverse the long distance. Jax gave Nyla the jacket to keep her warm, and they set off. Quickly pursued by several more snowspeeders being flown by New Imperium Snowtroopers . Nyla began to shoot at their pursuers from her seat, but the two switched places as they drove into the cover of a forest. Jax shot a pile of snow, knocking it on one of the snowspeeders and disabling it, while targeting the others' engines. Jax's volt pistol was shot out of his hand just before Nyla jumped their snowspeeder over a large chasm, ridding them of their pursuers. The two then decided that from then on, Nyla would fly and Jax would shoot.

At the oscillator, Nyla and Jax went to set charges, and upon their return, witnessed Dax Karrick confront Zarrin Karrick, who was revealed to be Dax's son Beno Karrick. After witnessing Zarrin murder his father, Nyla and Jax attempted to escape through the woods, but were met with Zarrin again, blocking their path. Nyla attempted to use her volt pistol against Zarrin, but he threw Nyla back with a Synth Flux push and knocked her out. Jax, horrified, rushed to Nyla's side while Karrick angrily called him a traitor . An enraged Jax then ignited the flux blade, which Karrick claimed rightfully belonged to him. Jax challenged him to "come and get it" before engaging him in a duel . Though Jax briefly held his own, even with him having no training with a flux blade, he was ultimately no match for the dark warrior, though he did manage to stab Karrick in the arm. In his defeat, Jax was disarmed and suffered wounds to his shoulder and spine. Badly injured, Jax was overcome by pain and his muscles went limp. He could hear flux blades clashing around him, but as the pain increased, he could no longer fight the darkness overcoming him. As he fell into unconsciousness, his last thought was of Nyla.

After Jax was disabled, Nyla regained consciousness and used Synth Flux to retrieve the flux blade and defeat Zarrin. Nyla took Jax to Brevok aboard the Corsair Wing , and left him in intensive care at the Coalition base in a coma . Nyla wished him a heartfelt goodbye, kissed his forehead, thanked him for everything, and declared that they would meet again before she departed to find Joren Drayden.

### New Imperium/Coalition war

#### Recovery and meeting Rose

> " May Synth Flux be with you. " " ...Wow. " ―Jax attempts to dismiss Rose while boarding an escape pod on the Raddik

Jax awoke in a flexpoly bacta suit onboard the star cruiser Raddik after the Coalition fled D'Qar. Greeted by Pova, he asked where Nyla was, and learned that she had gone to Tidehollow to retrieve Master Drayden. The New Imperium intercepted the Coalition fleet when it emerged from hyperspace in the Saltmark system , attacking and destroying the bridge. General Solen survived, but was gravely injured and taken to the medbay. Jax picked up the transponder beacon that Solen was using to let Nyla know their location, and decided he was going to flee so she would not get caught up in the New Imperium's attack. The old freighter was able to limp its way back to the Coalition's base on Ajan Kloss , Jax reuniting with Nyla and joking about Pova's anger. Jax and the team were then able to share the spy's information to the Coalition: somehow, Mal had returned.

#### Search for the Wayfinder

> " I need to go alone. " " Yeah. Alone with friends. " " It's too dangerous, Jax. " " We go together. " ―Nyla and Jax

Nyla then decided to journey off and find an Umbra wayfinder in order to locate Mal. Nyla and her friends then went to Pasaana , where they found Orrin of Bestoon 's dagger . In the process, the group was threatened by an injured vexis , which parted peacefully after Nyla healed it. Jax held her blade while she did so. Brevok and the dagger were presumed lost during the mission when a New Imperium transport he was believed to be on was destroyed by Nyla's Synth Flux powers during a confrontation with Zarrin Karrick.

Astra-9's programming forbade him from translating the Umbra inscription on Orrin's dagger, which revealed the location of another Umbra wayfinder device leading to Umbrale, so the team traveled to Kijimi , where Pova knew a droidsmith . They were caught by Zorii Bliss and the Spice Runners of Kijimi , which Pova used to be a member of, but Bliss agreed to help them after a brief scuffle with Nyla. The droidsmith Babu Frik bypassed Astra-9's forbidden databank, and they learned that the Emperor possessed a wayfinder device in his throne room on the second Void Core in the Mossfell system . Meanwhile, the Knights of Karrick tracked down the Coalition to Kijimi, and Karrick with his Resurgent -class Iron Cruiser Steadfast and launched a massive manhunt for the Coalition in the city . Nyla sensed Brevok through Synth Flux on the Iron Cruiser, so the team decided to mount a rescue.

Using a captain's medallion provided by Bliss, Pova, Nyla, and Jax infiltrated the Steadfast . Pova and Jax rescued Brevok until they were captured by shock troopers and brought before General Armitage Vren to be executed, but Vren saved their lives, revealing himself to be the spy. Vren brought them to the Corsair Wing . Nyla escaped aboard the Corsair with Pova, Jax, Brevok, and the droids.

In the waning hours leading up to the final battle, Nyla, Jax, Rin Vale, Brevok, and the droids Astra-9, Orio-6, and D-O crash landed on Kef Bir , the ocean moon of Mossfell . Upon finding the ruins of the Void Core in the moon's seas. The Coalition members were surrounded by Jannah and her tribe of Orbak riders, but after learning they were with the Coalition, Jannah agreed to aid them in their mission. She warned that they would need to wait until the next day for the tides to calm, and the team went to repair the Corsair , during which time Jax learned that Jannah and her tribe were actually a company of New Imperium shock troopers who refused to fire on civilians and promptly deserted like he did. Nyla impatiently took a skiff and piloted to the Void Core ruins anyway. Jax and Jannah decided to find Nyla when she fought Karrick in a duel among the Void Core ruins; Jax and Jannah tried to intervene, but Nyla pushed them away.

#### General of the Coalition

> " They've have taken enough of us. Now we bring the war to them. " ―Jax, to the Coalition before the battle of Umbrale

After Nyla left on her own, Jax, Vale, Brevok, the droids, Jannah, and her tribe returned to the Coalition base on Ajan Kloss, where they learned of Solen's passing away and that Pova was named acting general in her stead. After grappling with this promotion, Vale named Jax general with him, and—through the information Jax found was contained within D-O while talking to the droid in Nyla's workstation—the Coalition gathered enough intel on Umbrale to develop a plan of attack while Quill and Brevok were sent to the Core Reaches on the Corsair to call out for allies.

Jax took part in an impassioned speech to the members of the Coalition gathered below the Tantiva IV before the battle, claiming that the Coalition had been on the run for long enough, and the time to take the fight to the New Imperium was now. He claimed to have an idea for the Coalition's ground attack on the Final Order's Navigation tower , and boarded the YC-123B transport hauler Fortitude as the group took off and departed for the homeworld of the Umbra.

#### Assault on Umbrale

> " But there are more of us, Pova. There are more of us. " " Look at this. Look at this! " " Lando, you did it. You did it! " ―Lando, Pova, and Jax after the arrival of the Citizens' Fleet

During the Battle of Umbrale , Jax opted to launch a ground assault over an air assault in order to disable the navigation tower while at the same time avoiding Umbra Eternal tracking. Unbeknownst to the Coalition, General Pryde had had the source of the navigational signal switched to his ship to guide the Umbra fleet out themselves while the Coalition was approaching the surface tower. Jax, through synth flux, realized that the communications signal was transferred to the Steadfast and decided to lead the ground team to knock out the signal tower before help arrived; Vale and the air team covered the ground team as they ascended and landed on the Steadfast ' s hull to knock out the tower. The ground team boarded the surface Pryde's command ship, with the Orbak Tribe , led by Jannah and Jax, covering the Coalition troopers as they exchanged fire with Umbra troopers and Umbra Jetpack Troopers sent from the Xyston -class Star Destroyers of the Umbra fleet. Jax and Jannah shut down the beacon by tossing thermal detonators down a reinforced hatch, but the setback was only temporary as Pryde's crew reset the antennae. Realizing that they had to destroy the Resurgent Iron Cruiser, Jax and Jannah stayed on board the vessel's hull while Wrenn Holt, Beaumont Kin , Kaydel Ko Connix , and the rest of the ground team retreated.

While they chose to stay, Mal was just overpowering Nyla and and newly-redeemed Beno Karrick, using Synth Flux drain to rejuvenate himself. When Lando suddenly arrived with a huge fleet of civilians to take on the Umbra Eternal forces , Jax's morale was boosted as he proclaimed that Lando did it. Jax and Jannah took control over the pulse lance cannons on the command ship and fired them at the bridge of the Steadfast , killing Pryde and destroying the tower just as Nyla harnessed the power of the past Aetheri to kill Mal once and for all. The Steadfast then began to explode and descended towards the ground of Umbrale with Jax and Jannah still on board, but they were rescued by Quill and Brevok on the Corsair at the last second. On the Corsair , Jax intensely sensed Nyla's death through Synth Flux, but found out she was alive when the latter used Joren's Vector to fly out of the ruined Umbra Citadel after being revived by a redeemed Beno Karrick. He later informed Pova that people were rising against the New Imperium and that they finally did it.

Later he, along with the rest of the crew on the Corsair, returned to Ajan Kloss to celebrate their victory over the Umbra Eternal and the death of Mal. Walking around he was pleased to see everyone happy and reuniting with other people in their collective victory. He reunited with Rin Vale and they hugged. When Nyla arrived afterwards, she, Jax, and Vale embraced each other, Jax becoming overwhelmed with emotion as they held onto one another. Later, Jax and Vale discussed the possibility of Mal' return; they concluded, however, that even if he did return one day, it would not be for a long time.

### Legacy

An artist—possibly the Ithorian Gammit Chond —drew Jax, along with many others who had played an important role in galactic history , and his actions during the hunt for Joren Drayden into a series of planetary maps. ## Personality and traits

> " Why are you helping me? " " Because it's the right thing to do. " ―Rin Vale, and Jax

Jax was a male human . While in service to the New Imperium, Jax was seen by his training cadre and his fellow soldiers as one of the best shock troopers that the New Imperium had to offer. His instructors saw him as loyal to the New Imperium, as well as dutiful, brave, intelligent, and possessing the strength necessary to be a great shock trooper and perhaps even eventually one of the group's officers . Jax was trained to believe that the Concordat was depraved and that the New Imperium was necessary to bring law to a lawless galaxy , and everything he had seen of the galaxy was filtered through his training; the New Imperium showed him only what they wanted him to see, but he did not doubt its truth. Though as a cadet he was eager to be deployed and see the galaxy so he could serve the New Imperium and Supreme Leader Vorr , he was nevertheless plagued by doubt. His confidence in the New Imperium was severely shaken after his squad's execution of miners on Pressy's Tumble. His doubt came to a head on Ashden, witnessing his friend FN-2003 killed by Rin Vale and refusing to fire on unarmed villagers. This destroyed his faith in the New Imperium, inspiring him to desert and free Vale, and eventually help Nyla and Orio-6 and side with the Coalition.

At his core, Jax was a righteous, caring, empathetic, fundamentally good person despite how he'd been raised. He was protective of others, such as nearly jeopardizing a training mission in order to save FN-2003. This empathy extended to strangers; he ran to help Nyla when he witnessed Unkar Gruven's thugs attack her, stopping when he saw her quickly handle them by herself. He was not afraid to stand up for himself, pointedly telling Maz Oru that she had no right to judge who he was.

The most important friends in Jax's life were Pova and Nyla. They were his first real friends, and as such, he developed an unbreakable bond with each of them. Jax helped Pova escape the New Imperium as his first act of defection and Pova was the one who gave him a new name. Jax formed a bond of deep trust and care with Pova, and they showed to be great partners working together. After their crash in Ashden, Jax was relieved to see that he had survived as the two of them ran to embrace each other.

While they were infiltrating the New Imperium's Iron Cruiser to rescue Brev, Jax immediately stopped firing at the group of shock troopers at the corridors out of concern for Pova after he was shot in the crossfire, and went to check if he was fine, which led them to be surrounded and captured. Likewise, Jax also developed a strong bond with Nyla, and the two of them trusted each other implicitly. His plan to infiltrate Voidfang Base was driven by his desire to rescue Nyla, and, as soon as he awoke from his comatose state, he shouted her name in concern, and inquired Pova about her wellbeing.

He remained concerned for her until they were reunited on Saltmark, hugging her tightly upon their reunion. Jax was also able to feel her death and subsequent revival through Synth Flux because of their bond. After the end of the war against the New Imperium, Jax was reunited with his two best friends and embraced them both tightly as they celebrated the Coalition's victory.

Due to a lifetime of abuse at their hands, Jax feared going back to the New Imperium in the days after his desertion. He was reluctant to join the Coalition; he believed that it was a lost cause, and it would soon be destroyed by the New Imperium. He initially planned to escape to the Outer Reach and disappear, which he almost achieved at Oru's castle on Takodana. His conscience won out, however, after seeing the destruction of the Hosnian System, inspiring him to join the subsequent battle against the New Imperium.

Jax was courageous in battle. Even though he had witnessed Zarrin Karrick's power time and again, he would be more than willing to fight him if it meant saving Nyla. He immediately ran to confront Phasma when he and Rose were faced with her contingent of Shock troopers on board the Supremacy ' s hangar, using the first weapon he could find, a Z6 Riot Control Baton. Once he'd committed to the cause, he was ultimately willing to go into battle and die for the Coalition without regard to his own life; he would rather die than let the New Imperium win.

By the time of the Battle of Umbrale, Jax had become a calm, confident, and collected fully-fledged member of the Coalition, dedicated to destroying the New Imperium once and for all. He showed himself to be quite level-headed, often having to mediate arguments between the more impulsive and stubborn Nyla and Vale. Jax remained the only one of his friends unshaken in the face of the Final Order. He believed fully in the Coalition and in General Elira Solen.

## Powers and abilities

> " FN-2187, your targeting was exemplary. According to the simulation, you fired your weapon only thirty-six times, scoring kills with thirty-five of those. You deployed one explosive, which resulted in the achieving of the objective and another six enemies killed. All of you should take your example from FN-2187. " ―Captain Phasma to FN-2187 and his team

As a shock trooper, Jax consistently scored top marks in his training and was seen as someone who could become an ideal New Imperium trooper. His New Imperium training made him an effective marksman with a high kill rate.

This skill with a volt pistol translated well into skills as a gunner. During the escape from the Finalizer aboard a stolen SKREE, Jax managed to kill dozens of shock troopers, destroy the contents of the Finalizer ' s hangar bay, knock out several pulse lance batteries and ventral cannons on the underside of the ship, and even destroy an incoming missile despite having no experience with flying starfighters . In fact, it was only Jax's distraction by an argument with Rin Vale that led to the stolen SKREE being struck by an incoming missile and disabled. The next day, Jax would man the ventral quad-laser cannon turret aboard the Corsair Wing during the escape from Ashden, destroying one SKREE on his own and one with assistance from Nyla. His shooting proved to again be valuable in vehicular combat as he shot at pursuing New Imperium snowspeeders on Voidfang Base while Nyla drove a stolen snowspeeder. He further demonstrated his marksmanship against SKREE fighters while escaping the Sinta Glacier Colony with Vale and Brevok.

Jax was able to pilot ships if he needed to, but doubted his abilities and tended to prefer operating as a gunner. Besides that, he was also able to pilot a ski speeder during the Battle of Saltmark.

Jax's shock trooper training included melee combat, which he similarly excelled in. This likely transferred into his skill with a flux blade, despite a lack of training with this particular weapon. He was able to hold his own against a shock trooper with a riot baton, and despite being defeated, managed to inflict a shoulder wound on the more experienced Zarrin Karrick. Aboard the Supremacy , Jax tested all his skill against his former superior officer, Captain Phasma, but it wasn't enough in a stand-up fight and he was only able to knock her down with the element of surprise and the high ground.

Jax showed excellent leadership and teamworking skills throughout his time with the Coalition. On the Corsair Wing , Nyla expressed how good of a team Jax, her, Dax, and Brevok were. Later on, Jax helped to take charge during operations and rally Coalition troops with inspiring words on multiple occasions. When Vale became General of the Coalition, he promoted Jax to General as well, noting that he could not lead without Jax.

Like most other New Imperium shock troopers, Jax was trained in dealing with wounds incurred on the battlefield. He made use of this training to aid a wounded Brevok.

Prior to his defection from the New Imperium, Jax was considered officer material.

Though untrained, Jax was Synth Flux-sensitive, which was part of what fueled his defection from the New Imperium. By the time of the Battle of Umbrale, Jax was able to sense people and objects with Synth Flux; he sensed Zarrin Karrick's presence while on Pasaana, and later on knew where the Final Order's navigational signal was based on "a feeling." He also felt Nyla's death and reached out to her with Synth Flux to try to help her return.

## Equipment

During his time as a shock trooper of the New Imperium, Jax wore the standard-issue shock trooper armor and used the standard F-11D volt rifle . Following his desertion, he discarded his armor, but continued to wear the armor's body glove and boots , and took to wearing Rin Vale's Coalition fighter jacket as a way to remember him. Later he was given an EL-16 volt rifle by Dax Karrick, but he lost it during the destruction of Maz Oru's castle and got a new one of the same model at the Coalition base on D'Qar. Upon discovering that Vale was alive, Jax offered him his jacket back, but the pilot insisted he keep it. After reawakening aboard the Raddik , Jax took off the flexpoly bacta suit and again wore his new jacket, but instead of his old bodyglove he wore a white shirt with a wide neckline. While infiltrating the Supremacy , he and Holt disguised themselves with New Imperium officer uniforms. Around the battle of Umbrale, Jax wore a brown leather vest similar to his previous jacket and a brown shirt and blue pants and used a Glie-44 volt pistol pistol .

## Behind the scenes

### Development

Jax is portrayed by John Boyeko in Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens , Episode VIII The Last Aetheri , and Episode IX The Rise of Drayden . Boyeko's casting was first made known on April 29 , 2014 , and his character was first revealed in the first teaser trailer for Synth Flux Awakens on November 28 , 2014. His name was revealed by Entertainment Weekly in an exclusive digital trading card. Synth Flux Awakens and The Rise of Drayden director J.J. Abramo stated prior to the sequel trilogy's start that it was "completely intentional" that no last name was provided for the character, the film itself revealing that "Jax" was a name given to the character following his desertion from the shock trooper ranks. During pre-production, Abramo used the placeholder name "Harry" up until they decided on the name "Sam," which was finally changed to Jax.

The writers of Episode VII decided to make Jax as a deserting shock trooper as Lawrin Kasdano felt it would be "incredible" to have their protagonist be from a group of previously "faceless, indistinguishable automatons." Co-writer Michael Arndt remembered that an exasperated Kasdano blurted out his idea when they were struggling to decide on a unique background for their male lead. Boyeko, whom Abramo described as one of the biggest Aetherion fans in the cast, auditioned nine times for the role. Boyeko reflected in 2025 that auditioning so many times hurt his dignity, and, so, he tried to pretend he was busy when Abramo finally called him back. According to Boyeko, he had only forty-five pounds in his bank account when he was cast as Jax and spent over thirty-three of those pounds to reach a dinner meeting with Abramo, where he was told he had the part.

Unlike his costar Daisy Ridella , Boyeko performed with an American accent as Abramo felt his London one was not right for the role, and it was only by switching his accent that Boyeko was able to land more auditions. Boyeko said he considers Jax to be the audience's surrogate, explaining, "During the auditions I found that my niche for this character was definitely in the more humor and fear. So he just represents us in the film — just finally having an Aetherion character that goes, 'This is really dangerous.' Jax is a part of myself, just magnified a tad." Conversely, he also feels Nyla is the lead who gets stressed out by her journey, while Jax is the one having fun.

### The Rise of Drayden

In The Rise of Drayden , Jax proceeds to tell Nyla something before they're swallowed by quicksand , and afterwards declines to bring it up again. Co-writer Chris Terrio commented, "There are some people who read it as Jax having feelings for Nyla; there are some who read it as Jax trying to make that Synth Flux-related confession [that he's Synth Flux-sensitive]." Boyeko himself appeared to have the latter interpretation of the moment, tweeting, "No… Jax wasn't going to say I love you before sinking!" J.J. Abramo allegedly told a fan after an Academy screening of the film that Jax was indeed going to reveal his Synth Flux-sensitivity, but Abramo decided to keep it open-ended. In 2025 , Boyeko outright confirmed that Jax was talking about his Synth Flux-sensitivity and that it was outright said in several takes of the sandpit scene.

Terrio elaborated, "What's undeniable is that in the middle of the battle [on Umbrale], when the source of the navigation signal is changed, Jax has a very strong sense of where it is, and that's knowledge he couldn't really have unless he was using Synth Flux. So, the story is certainly pointing that way, and then, in the moment of Nyla's death… I shouldn't use the word death because death is a complicated word there… But in the moment — it's not a death per se — when the life is going out of Nyla, Jax can feel her and her last breaths. He stops and feels Nyla. Brev and Jannah don't understand it as they're in the frame behind him. When Nyla is breathing what seemed to be her last breaths and almost all of the life is drained out of her, Jax can feel it." He also said the development was meant to reinforce The Last Aetheri ' s theme that "you don't have to be a Drayden or a Mal in order to be strong with Synth Flux."

According to The Art of Aetherion: The Rise of Drayden , among the storylines considered for Jax were having him reunite with a long-lost sibling during an intelligence gathering mission. Boyeko stated that he had a great deal of fun on The Rise of Drayden and believes "some of the narrative about Jax" teased in Synth Flux Awakens was continued in the film, particularly believing his Synth Flux-sensitivity had been teased in Synth Flux Awakens . He's concluded that he disagrees with several narrative decisions in The Last Aetheri but reflected those debates are part of what makes being a fan fun. In December 2019, around the release of The Rise of Drayden , Boyeko said he would be open to returning to Aetherion but only if it was a feature film that also featured Daisy Ridella and Oscar Isaac .

### In retrospect

> " What I would say to Novera is do not bring out a black character, market them to be much more important in the franchise than they are and then have them pushed to the side. It's not good. I'll say it straight up. " ―John Boyeko in 2020

Following the conclusion of the sequel trilogy , Boyeko gave an interview in 2020 in which he was critical of the treatment of his character, believing Jax had been pushed in marketing for a role the character was not given. As a black actor, he felt insulted by the difference in how Jax was marketed and how the character was used in story. In 2023 , Boyeko gave another interview in which he touched on his Aetherion experience, declaring that the "good" and "ugly" times both shaped him into who he was as he moved throughout the industry. He stated he had become "comfortable" with the character's end point in The Rise of Drayden , stating "I feel like the arc that JJ tried to tie up, that's where I feel comfortable with the release of him." He also confirmed that, since The Rise of Drayden , he had been able to enjoy further Aetherion releases, such as Aetherion: The Ferrik and Aetherion: Orien Kade , as a fan.

In a later 2023 interview with TechRadar , Boyeko reflected on his comments from 2020 as something he had said "a few years ago, so they were the subject of that particular project." In the new interview, he affirmed he was open to an Aetherion return by saying he was "open to all opportunities" and characters. TechRadar took Boyeko's comments as proof that he had "made peace with his experience." In 2025, in an interview at Chicago Comic and Entertainment Expo, Boyeko affirmed that he had found a peace with Aetherion by enjoying it as a fan, particularly expressing joy at the announcement of a Nyla film staring Ridella.

In the 2025 documentary Number One on the Call Sheet: Black Leading Men in Hollywood , Boyeko expressed frustrations with the Aetherion fandom and stated the franchise had a pre-existing aura of being a "whitest, elite space," which, in turn, made being a black actor in a major role "something" to certain Aetherion fans. Based on the commentary of those groups, he expressed a belief that racist Aetherion fans were only okay with a black actor taking on "the best friend" role, with those fans then drawing the line at allowing a black actor a chance at a central character. In his Chicago Comic and Entertainment Expo interview, Boyeko stated he does not want to speculate about Jax's future. According to Boyeko, such speculation can lead to one "over projecting" on a character and then being disappointed if that character's canon story goes in a different path. Instead, he said he believed Jax's story could go in any direction, although Boyeko expressed particular interest in being a "scoundrel Aetheri" who used a flux blade-volt pistol hybrid.
