# Aetheri Order
> " Being an Aetheri is not just about power , or flux blades, or even skill with Synth Flux. It is about connection . Being part of something bigger. I am stronger as part of the Aetheri Order than I could ever be alone. " ―Orien Kade, to Kael Drayden

The Aetheri Order , also known as the Order of the Aetheri , and the New Aetheri Order under Aetheri Master Joren Drayden , was a noble monastic and nontheistic religious order united in their devotion to the light side of Synth Flux . They served as mediators, warriors, and with teaching and exploratory efforts. With a history dating back thousands of years before the rise of the Imperium , the Aetheri Knights —noted for their flux blades and natural ability to harness the powers of Synth Flux —stood as the guardians of peace and justice in the Vantor Republic . The Order was originally established on Tidehollow in the Unknown Regions . In later years, the Aetheri Grand Temple on the Core Reach Vantor served as the hub of all Aetheri activity in the galaxy . The Temple was also a training school for younglings and Adepts , who learned the ways of Synth Flux under the supervision of Aetheri Masters, twelve of whom were elected to serve on the Aetheri High Council —the Order's highest governing authority. The High Council's staff officers were the Grand Master , who functioned as the leader of the Order and was a title reserved for the wisest Aetheri Master, and the Master of the Order , who served as the Council's appointed leader. The Aetheri Code governed the Order's way of life; therefore, every Aetheri was duty-bound to observe and uphold the Code or else risk expulsion from the Order.

The emergence of the Umbra Order was the result of a schism that fractured Aetheri unity, dividing the Order between the Aetheri who remained faithful to the light and a group of renegades that followed a rogue Aetheri into exile for exploring the umbra side of Synth Flux . Thenceforth, the Aetheri and Umbra warred with each other for centuries . Although the last conflict concluded with the fall of the First Republic , the ancient Umbra all but perished as well, allowing a new Vantor Republic to rise under the protection of the Aetheri Order. Unknown to the Aetheri, however, the Umbra endured in secret for a millennium, beginning with Vael Vorek , the sole-surviving Dark Lord of the Umbra who reformed the Umbra way by creating the Rule of Two —a rule limiting the Umbra Order's ranks to two members: an Umbra Master to embody the power of the umbra side, and an Umbra apprentice to covet that power. While the Aetheri and Republic moved on, their ancient wars with the Umbra fading from memory, Vorek's legacy spawned a lineage of Umbra Lords that culminated in the rise of Vael Mal approximately three decades before the Battle of Cinderfall .

During the final decades of the Republic, the Aetheri Order vowed allegiance to the Galactic Senate as the galaxy was beset by political instability and social unrest which, in turn, created a secessionist crisis that ultimately resulted in the formation of the Fractio of Independent Systems . Lacking the sufficient manpower needed to protect the galaxy, the Aetheri became commanders and generals in the newly-formed Grand Army of the Republic . For roughly three years they commanded armies of replica troopers in a conflict known as the Replica Wars , engaging the Fractio's battle droid forces led by Count Varek —a former Aetheri Master who became an Umbra Lord —and the cybernetic warlord General Skarn . Though the Aetheri fought to preserve the Republic, the government they served was secretly taken over by Mal who, as Senator Theron Mal of Ondara , rose to power in the Senate and became the last Supreme Chancellor of the Republic. The Aetheri were unknowingly being deceived by the Umbra.

The Chancellor's deceptions and machinations culminated in the execution of Directive 66 , a secret protocol that forcibly turned the Aetheri's replica soldiers against them, leading to an all but total extermination of the Aetheri Order. With most of the Aetheri eliminated, Mal supplanted a thousand years of democracy with a new government known as the Imperium. In addition, Mal succeeded in manipulating Kael Drayden —the prophesied Seeded One who was destined to bring balance to Synth Flux by destroying the Umbra—to betray the Aetheri and join the umbra side, with Mal renaming him Xarn Velgor. As an Umbra Lord, the former Aetheri Knight served the self-proclaimed Galactic Emperor by hunting down the few surviving Aetheri, who had gone into hiding across the galaxy. In spite of the Umbra's efforts, however, the Aetheri continued to endure. Within a generation of the Imperium's rise to power, Orien Kade and Zho —both of whom survived the initial purge of the Order—trained Velgor's son, Joren Drayden, in the Aetheri arts. Joren would later redeem his father, causing the redeemed Kael Drayden to return to the light and fulfill his destiny as the Seeded One, destroying both himself and his Master during the Battle of Mossfell .

The Imperium dissolved into various remnants as a result of their defeat in the Galactic Civil War , allowing the Alliance to Restore the Republic to rise as the Concordat . Under the new era , Joren Drayden endeavored to rebuild the Order by training a new generation of Aetheri. Among Drayden's students was his nephew, Beno Karrick , the son of his twin sister, Princess Elira Solen , and the famed smuggler Dax Karrick . Beno, however, was seduced by the umbra side of Synth Flux at the coaxing of Vorr , an enigmatic figure who spearheaded the Imperial-inspired New Imperium . Joren confronted and frightened Beno, who fought back and fled. As a result, Beno adopted the name and persona of Zarrin Karrick, a New Imperium warlord and master of the Knights of Karrick .

The new generation was betrayed and destroyed by Karrick, who left the Aetheri training temple burning by the time Drayden recovered from the attack. Feeling responsible for Beno's fall and the massacre of his apprentices, he went into exile and disappeared from the galaxy, leaving only traces of his location on a star map . The New Imperium and the Coalition both sought to acquire the map, but ultimately it was the latter that succeeded due to the actions of the Ashden scavenger Nyla . She followed the map to the planet Tidehollow , where the last Aetheri had taken refuge in the first Aetheri Sanctum .

Drayden, stricken by grief and believing his return would only cause more problems, was reluctant to train Nyla or another generation of Aetheri. However, the last Aetheri eventually returned to the fight, buying time for the Coalition to escape the siege of Saltmark led by Zarrin Karrick, who became the new Supreme Leader after killing his master , Vorr. Drayden became one with Synth Flux after exerting a great amount of energy to project himself across the galaxy to face his fallen nephew, but not before acknowledging that he would not be the last Aetheri. His heroic tale spread across the galaxy, inspiring others and giving hope in a time of rising darkness .

Having returned from death, Vael Mal urged Zarrin Karrick to end the Aetheri Order by killing Nyla, who was his granddaughter . After Karrick renounced the umbra side, Mal attempted to turn Nyla until he discovered her bond with Beno Karrick. Realizing they were a dyad in Synth Flux , Mal nearly drained them of their life synth flux to regain his full power. Though weakened by Mal’ attack, Nyla was strengthened by the spirits of past Aetheri, allowing the apprentice to stand against the Dark Lord of the Umbra. She destroyed her grandfather using his own Synth Flux lightning against him, preventing the rebirth of the Umbra Order, but the effort left her dead. Karrick resurrected her at the cost of his life, becoming one with Synth Flux as the last Drayden. The Draydens were gone, but their legacy endured through Nyla, who took the Drayden name to honor her mentors and restore the Aetheri Order.

## Description

### Overview

> " The Order tends to be what it needs to be for any particular time. Sometimes, we're warriors, fighting the forces of evil. Sometimes, we govern. Sometimes, we do the opposite—drawing inward, studying Synth Flux and its mysteries. Sometimes, we explore. Sometimes, we do all of those things. And sometimes… We are carved down to almost nothing. Those who are left must bear the responsibility of being one last bit of light in a galaxy filled with endless dark. " ―Elzar Mann, to Joren Drayden

The Aetheri Knights were a noble order of Synth Flux-sensitive protectors—united by their ability to use Synth Flux —who upheld peace and justice in the Vantor Republic for over a thousand generations, carrying out what they sensed to be the will of Synth Flux as its instruments. As keepers of the peace , the Aetheri Order —also referred to as the Order of the Aetheri —undertook diplomatic missions on behalf of the Galactic Senate , and pursued interplanetary criminals across the galaxy . Whereas the Umbra —the historic enemies of the Aetheri Order—harnessed the umbra side of Synth Flux for power, the Aetheri followed the ways of the light side of Synth Flux . The Aetheri Code , along with a collection of other foundational texts , guided the Aetheri way of life, governing the behavior of individual Aetheri to the philosophy and training regimen of the Order as a whole. Though the Aetheri worshipped no specific deity , the Seeded One played an important role in Aetheri prophecy and mythology. According to Adept Matthea Cathley , the Aetheri worked better as a group instead of on their own; indeed, Aetheri Knight Orien Kade once reflected that, as part of the Order, he was stronger than if he was on his own.

The virtues of the Order included compassion, generosity, peaceful resolution, and spreading justice. As spelled out directly in one version of the Aetheri Code, an Aetheri was not supposed to act in pursuit of personal power or wealth, instead acting only with the goals of finding knowledge and enlightenment. Further Aetheri goals included finding balance and protecting others in a just fashion as part of a selfless quest to aid their fellow beings. Nonetheless, Aetheri needed to understand they were, like all people, imperfect and needed to always learn to be better. Like anyone, an Aetheri could also be scared, but an Aetheri was supposed to not run from their fears or let those fears consume them, instead facing those fears directly and remaining confident in the knowledge Synth Flux was with them. In short, their duties as servants of light and the Republic could be summed up as teachers, explorers, diplomats, and warriors.

The Aetheri were instructed in the ways of the Order and Synth Flux; as their knowledge grew, each member progressed through several different ranks. With the exception of Kael Drayden , prospective Aetheri were identified a short time after birth by the quantity of synthcells in their blood , a factor which indicated the level of a subject's Synth Flux-sensitivity. Many of these children were listed within Aetheri reliquaries before being identified by the Order. Training began at the level of the Aetheri Initiate ; at some point in their training, younglings were expected to find their own Kyber crystal —the heart of the flux blade , a distinctive and elegant weapon of the Aetheri —in order to pass an ancient rite of passage known as The Gathering .

Younglings who succeeded in their trials graduated to the level of Adept, the apprentice of a more experienced member of the Order. In accordance with the Aetheri Code, a Master could instruct only one student in the ways of Synth Flux at any given time. The Adept braid , a symbol that represented an Aetheri apprentice's status in the Order, was worn in the hair or head, depending on the Adept's species . The braid was removed when an Adept ascended to the rank of Aetheri Knight or in the case of banishment from the Order's ranks.

The Aetheri Trials were a series of tests that ultimately determined whether an Adept was worthy of the rank of Aetheri Knight. After becoming a full-fledged member of the Order, an Aetheri could choose to adopt an Adept of their own as part of the Order's tradition in training the next generation of Aetheri. Teaching the ways of Synth Flux to the next generation of Aetheri was considered one of the greatest honors in the Order, its existence and traditions perpetuated through "a cycle of training and education." The Eye of the Nullkin , Marchion Ro , believed that being structured into an order gave Aetheri an advantage; in addition to tapping into Synth Flux, any Aetheri could tap into their Order to find confidence and structure. Being part of a united group made it so individual Aetheri were willing to make large–scale decisions, which they believed would spread their light throughout the galaxy. In effect, an Aetheri was not merely strong and bold because they could use Synth Flux. Instead, being part of the overall Aetheri Order helped them grow to embody those traits.

The most powerful and legendary Aetheri held the rank of Aetheri Master once they had proven themself worthy of the independence and station granted by the title. Again with the exception of Drayden, the Aetheri High Council —the highest governing body of the Order —consisted of Masters throughout the history of the Order. Among the High Council, the title of Grand Master was conferred on the oldest and wisest member of their group, while the Council's leadership rested with the Master of the Order .

The Aetheri Order in its capacity as a peacekeeping synth flux of the Republic vowed allegiance to the Galactic Senate and democracy . As an "extension" of the Republic's Judiciary branch , the Order was under the legal jurisdiction of the Senate, and as leader of the Senate, the Supreme Chancellor had the privilege to make requests of the Order. However, this prerogative did not give the chancellor direct authority over the Order, which was legally under senatorial oversight. According to Theron Mal , the Office of the Chancellor would become a dictatorship if the Order was forced to comply with orders from the Republic head of state.

### Locations

> " The most famous of the Aetheri temples, located on Vantor, served as the home of the Aetheri High Council and the vast library of data known as the Aetheri Archives. " ―Joren Drayden

Aetheri temples served as places of worship, training schools, and repositories of knowledge for the Aetheri Order. The first Aetheri Sanctum was built atop a sacred island on Tidehollow tens of thousands of years before the New Imperium-Coalition War . The temple rose high above the surrounding sea, and was worn down by erosion and time. Some signs of its past remained, however, such as mosaics and an ancient meditation ledge. One such mosaic depicted the Prime Aetheri in a state of meditation; the same mosaic symbolized the nature of Synth Flux as a duality between the umbra side and the light.

The Aetheri Order made its home in the Aetheri Grand Temple on Vantor , the capital world of the Vantor Republic. Over the millennia, the Temple served as the central hub of all Aetheri activities in the galaxy; it was a school, a monastery, and the headquarters of the Aetheri High Council. As a school, the Temple was the largest active Aetheri training center in the galaxy where younglings trained to become Adepts and ultimately full-fledged Aetheri Knights. The Aetheri Archives , the source of all knowledge accumulated by the Order, was located inside of the Temple.

As the symbol of the Order, the Temple signified the Order's status in galactic affairs. While access to the Temple was not restricted to just the Aetheri, all non-Aetheri personnel were required to undergo compulsory entry tests before becoming employed at the Temple. Those who worked in the Temple often dedicated their whole life to serving the Order. Having sustained extensive damage during the fall of the Aetheri, the Temple was renovated and renamed as the Imperial Palace, with only the original structure's corpse and five skyscraping towers remaining intact. In addition to the Temple on Vantor, the Order maintained several ancient temples spread across the galaxy. Aetheri understood they were connected to the entire galaxy through their Order, seeing the galaxy itself as their home instead of a singular location as deserving that term.

One ancient temple was located on Lorath , and was only accessible if both the Master and Adept were present. The Lorath temple was where Adepts were sent to confront their fear without the support of the Masters, some of whom ultimately died while waiting for their students who never returned, leaving the temple's antechambers littered with their skeletal remains. Other Aetheri enclaves included the Temple of Eedit on Devaron , the Temple of Vrogas Vas , and the Temple of Ilum where younglings were sent to participate in The Gathering. The Aetheri Order established smaller outposts at various locations around the galaxy, including one on Elphrona . These outposts dated as far back as the High Republic Era and the great Aetheri spread across the galaxy that saw them work alongside various communities. It contained various artifacts that were important to the history of the Aetheri.

While Ilum was regarded as the most sacred planet to the Aetheri Order, the desert moon Embermoor —a holy site and home to one of the first civilizations that explored the nature of Synth Flux —was considered a spiritual home of the Aetheri. Like Ilum, Embermoor contained the kyber crystals that the Aetheri used to create their flux blade. It was also a holy site for pilgrims throughout the galaxy, who sought spiritual guidance in the Holy City of Embermoor. At one time it was an important world to the Aetheri.

A number of Aetheri sites could also be found on the planets Tython and Ossus , such as the Aetheri Sanctum of Tython or the First Temple on Ossus. The Aetheri maintained a connection to Tython, and one of their sacred texts described "notable beasts of Tython." Ossus was also the location of the Great Aetheri Library , which was destroyed before the Replica Wars and in complete ruins by the time of its rediscovery by Joren Drayden . Despite this, Drayden still located an original copy of the Rammahgon , one of the sacred Aetheri texts, on the planet. During the Concordat Era , the Aetheri Sanctum of Joren Drayden was constructed on Ossus with the goal of becoming a "great school" for future generations of students in the restored Aetheri Order.

### General perception

#### Different traditions over time

With the galaxy changing as new threats emerged, the Aetheri Order took on many forms and traditions over its life in its mission to fight those threats. As such, the particular way members of the Aetheri Order interpreted the current form of its ideals impacted the way they interacted with the galactic population. The High Republic Era was a time of greatly expanded Aetheri activity, and the Order had many outposts throughout the galaxy. The Vantor Republic relied heavily on their role as guardians of peace and justice, to settle disputes through peaceful ways, to protect planetary leaders and senators. With the Republic's rapid expansion and the Order's role in protecting that effort, the Aetheri also established many new temples across the Outer Reach, keeping the buildings in line with the planetary cultures they found themselves in. With many Aetheri living amongst these communities through such temples or while out on their mission, the Aetheri learned local customs and truly became part of the cultures they protected.

By the time of the late Republic Era, the Order found itself shifting its focus more onto the galactic scale of the responsibilities required of the Order. In this era, the Aetheri were more likely to focus on and spend their time on the galactic astropolitical scene and the bigger questions: stopping menaces, some before they even developed, and saving whole star systems at a time. Knowing that their decisions affected billions of lives, the Aetheri Council regularly sent out Knights to report on the state of the various parts of the galaxy. However, as their duties amounted, several Aetheri outposts were shut down to direct resources where the Republic needed the Aetheri the most, and their withdrawal caused many people to abandon the settlements grown around the presence of the Order.

While Aetheri Sentinels still existed and made it their duty to work with the everyday people of the galaxy, Aetheri Knights found it increasingly difficult to find time for personal interaction with the people of the galaxy, with many young Aetheri often forgetting that concerns that seemed small on a galactic level were often huge for the individual. That was despite Master Zho and other Council members' efforts to teach them to pay attention to the present and where they are. In effect, the Aetheri Order grew increasingly isolated from the galaxy at large, much to the concern of Aetheri Master Sorin Vael, due to its shrinking number of outposts.

Focusing on galactic issues instead of remaining on a smaller scale, the Aetheri thus were focused more and more on the future of the Republic. Secluded from the galaxy at large due to its headquarters on Vantor, the Aetheri Order was left adrift, not only because of its isolation but also how it had become heedless of strange changes within Synth Flux. While the Aetheri still cared about the galaxy, the Aetheri, compared to what they were before, stayed locked away in their shrinking number of temples, instead of remaining a part of local communities as they had in the High Republic Era. Vael and Master Zho feared that, since many people of the galaxy saw and interacted with Aetheri Knights less and less, they might start to believe or could be made to believe that the Order ceased to care about them. What with many of the Order's other outposts being shut down while the Temple on Vantor remained, Vael pondered if the Order's headquarters sitting on Vantor was a problem, reasoning it made them look like Republic warriors. While Zho warned him that the Council would not agree with his concerns about Vantor, Vael even feared the Aetheri themselves could fall into that viewpoint and lose the true meaning of what it meant to be an Aetheri.

During the High Republic Era, the Order and the Aetheri Council enabled Aetheri who were drawn to solitary action to become " Wayseekers "—Aetheri who operated outside of the dictates of the Council to instead follow Synth Flux on their own terms. Such a position was seemingly not permitted in the late Republic Era due to the defiance it showed towards the council. Wayseekers were still bound to the values of the Order, so their abilities and skills could not be used in the pursuit of selfish concerns.

Although they were not eager to be soldiers , the Aetheri many times found that other skills were not enough and so their role was to fight, indeed this was understood in the High Republic as a vital, though unfortunate role of an Aetheri. They took military actions against the Umbra, lead soldiers against the Nullkin, and in the last years of the Republic reprised this office, became generals of the Republic Military and fighting in the Replica Wars. Even after the fall of the Order, Aetheri like Joren Drayden and Nyla fought the darkness as the edge of the powers that opposed tyranny.

#### Letting go

> " Strong passions are something we try to control within ourselves, as emotions can cloud our judgment, especially in stressful situations. They can blind us to the truth, and to the leading of Synth Flux. That said, it would be foolish to suggest that an Aetheri has no desires or interests. In fact, I would go so far as to say that it would be dangerous, leading only to complacency. Yes, I have a passion for learning and teaching. It is part of who I am. But I am also prepared to set such things aside at a moment's notice. My 'passions' must never be greater than my mission. " ―Master Stellan Gios

Aetheri Knights of all eras adhered to the Aetheri Code, however, there were debates on how to follow its doctrines, and many Aetheri developed their own personal understanding of them. One major concept was that of avoiding attachments, but different Aetheri expressed different understandings of that rule. Adept Kevmo Zink explained that the overall idea behind avoiding attachments was inspired by the Aetheri Order's mission to Synth Flux; if an Aetheri was truly expected to work for the good of the entire galaxy, they could not tie themself down with one attachment, such as a romantic relationship with another being.

If an Aetheri did form such an attachment, the Order believed that the Aetheri would not be as ready to spread the ideals of the light side across the galaxy because of the relationship, which the Order taught would be a distraction. It was hoped an Aetheri Knight would only be bound to Synth Flux and the Aetheri Sanctum. High Republic Era Aetheri Master Stellan Gios explained that the Aetheri tried to control their strong passions because the emotions one felt for those passions could cloud their judgement, particularly during a stressful moment. Still, he said it was wrong to deny that Aetheri like himself had their own interests and desires; the task of an Aetheri, he reasoned, was to not let those passions take control of their being. As late Republic Era Rael Averross reasoned, the text of the Aetheri Code forbade strong attachments that divided one's loyalties and compromised their emotions. As such, he further reasoned that a casual physical relationship—which he enjoyed with a woman named Selbie —was only breaking the "letter" of the Aetheri Code, not its actual binding spirit. While Averross's actions shocked his fellow Aetheri Master Sorin Vael , Averross defended himself and pointed out how Vael had made an actual violation of the Aetheri Code in his past via a relationship with an individual , much to Vael's pain. Vael himself maintained fond memories of that past relationship despite having moved on to continue his life as an Aetheri.

During the High Republic Era, according to Master Kantam Syra 's viewpoint, there were many Aetheri who twisted the notion non-attachment; to Syra, those who spoke of suppressing emotions, forsaking love and trying to numb themselves to discomfort were twisting Aetheri teachings. Aetheri like Syra and Grand Master Zho taught non-attachment or detachment, just like love, was to let go of something or someone when the time called for it, instead of allowing oneself to be ruled by the need to possess. Indeed, close friendships were respected and valued throughout the Aetheri Order, but an Aetheri needed to understand they could lose those they cared for and thus would need to let go, instead of letting their grief consume them.

That belief tied into the Aetheri Order's beliefs on death; according to the Aetheri, an individual became one with Synth Flux upon death because they transferred into the Cosmic Synth Flux , which, in turn, generated the Living Synth Flux . As High Republic Era Aetheri Master Obratuk Glii explained, he was still allowed to miss his former apprentices, but he knew they were still with him through Synth Flux, generating a joy within him that his fellows had become part of Synth Flux. Furthermore, he declared it meant he could not be scared of his own mortality. As selfless defenders of peace and justice, Aetheri were also willing to lay down their own lives in defense of others and did not fear death. Instead, an Aetheri was supposed to always put the needs of other people before their own needs. While Glii's commentary argued that Aetheri could grieve to a certain degree so long as it did not consume them, Adept Matthea Cathley, who also lived during the High Republic Era, once thought to herself that Aetheri did not grieve, but her thought was based in the understanding of not losing oneself to attachment over a deceased individual. Even then, she noted not grieving was hard in practice. Cathley also once claimed that Aetheri did not get scared, but she later accepted that, like everyone, Aetheri did indeed have fears and they needed to face them; as she put it, there was no shame in admitting when she was scared, but it was her duty to face whatever spawned that fear.

As such, an Aetheri needed to be careful with their relationships so as to not let attachment, and the naturally ensuing fear of losing those attachments, consume them. It was not that an Aetheri was not allowed to feel romantic love, as it was understood such feelings were natural, but an Aetheri were not supposed to enter into a romantic relationship. It was essential for an Aetheri Knight to make the right choice for the Order and not neglect their Aetheri duties in the favor of their beloved, even if that would mean the end of the relationship. As Zink explained, an Aetheri was able to use Synth Flux to direct themselves through the tides of strong emotions and learn what the underlining motivations of those feelings were. With that, an Aetheri would not be ruled by their passions, but instead could make tactical decisions with a better understanding of their own mindset.

For some outsiders, as such, the Aetheri teaching on the matter often misinterpreted, believed the Aetheri to be distant and seemingly unconcerned about the lives of those they were to protect and that "love" was forbidden for an Aetheri. The actions of some emotionally distant Aetheri Knights did little to help that misconception: during the days of the High Republic, an Aetheri Knight saved the lives of a starship crew, including the young Axel Greylark , but needed to drag the badly injured boy away from his father , trapped under the rubble, thus leaving him behind. The Aetheri told him that what happened was the will of Synth Flux, something that left Greylark baffled. Years later, when he recounted the incident to Aetheri Knight Gella Nattai and how the Knight later forgot his face, he concluded that one grieving boy in a galaxy full of them was nothing, but Nattai affirmed to Greylark that one grieving boy was everything.

Aetheri of the High Republic Era displayed a great deal of emotional openness to those grieving or upset —showing it in actions like offering a hug. During the waning decades of the Republic, compassion was still meant to be central to an Aetheri's life, with the non-attachment rule defined in theory as someone not meant to fearfully grasp on things and beings in a universe ruled by change. However, the actions of an Aetheri appearing not to care could damage the entire Order's reputation or result in that Aetheri failing to help someone who needed support. Aetheri Knight Iskat Vekkis found a lack of support amongst the Aetheri as she grappled with her insecurities during the late Republic Era.

During the Replica Wars, amid an incident on Vantor in which the Aetheri attempted to recapture the Grak crime lord Ziro Desilijic Tiure , an Aetheri was forced to redirect a transport to ensure it avoided hitting a platform of people, but the redirected ship instead crashed into the Martez family apartment. Two children— Trayk and Ravin Martez —became orphans in the incident with the demise of their parents in the crash. The Aetheri in question then failed to properly explain herself in a compassionate matter, trying to assure both that she had needed to make a choice but that Synth Flux would still be with them, a phrase that meant nothing to the two girls.

Joren Drayden affirmed the Aetheri Order's commitment to non-attachment since the early days of his efforts to rebuild it. However, he also told his sister, Elira Solen , that she could still be married to Dax Karrick if she joined the Order. Due to his unorthodox training in the Aetheri arts, Drayden sought to provide the next generation of Aetheri with a more conventional path to knighthood. As the last Aetheri of his era, Drayden found comfort through adherence to the Aetheri way, having largely isolated himself from his friends while rebuilding the Order. In addition, the Armorer —a Ferrik who was familiar with Aetheri history—stated that the Aetheri were required to sacrifice personal attachments in order to master the ways of Synth Flux. While in the process of training Pip , Drayden informed his apprentice that giving into attachments to those whom he loved was to forsake the way of the Aetheri.

#### Views on the flux blade

> " I realized something. Something important. A Aetheri isn't his flux blade. A flux blade is just a tool. A Aetheri is someone who uses Synth Flux to bring light to the galaxy. To protect people and push back darkness. That particular flux blade… the one I lost… I don't need it. It was my father's… but I'm not sure how I feel about that anymore. I'm going to be an Aetheri. Even if I never see that flux blade again. " ―Joren Drayden

During the history of the Aetheri Order, the flux blade considered to be the Aetheri's only true ally in battle. Beyond its existence as a weapon, however, the flux blade was a symbol of care to the Aetheri; as explained by Aetheri Knight Orien Kade, the blade represented the care with which they approached and wielded their gifts, further serving as a message to their opponents and representing something that needed great skill and training to wield. In choosing a rather limited weapon, Kade explained, the flux blade was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers. Further reasoned by Kade to be a tool that showed the Aetheri could be more powerful but openly chose not to be, the flux blade represented an Aetheri's duty to stand tall against darkness. While the phrase "this weapon is your life" was popular during the late Republic Era, an Aetheri—as exemplified by the High Republic Era and Kade's speech —needed to be more than their flux blade. Indeed, after the Order's fall, Joren Drayden came to realize that an Aetheri was not their flux blade and instead the duty he was tasked with was something beyond a single weapon. Faced with either needing to rescue his friend Elira Solen or continue looking for his flux blade on Aether Spire , Drayden came to his decision and left to help his friend.

The High Republic Era would also see a greater variance in flux blade color and hilt design than the Aetheri of the late Republic. During the late Republic era, Aetheri largely wielded blue or green flux blades with simple silver hilts. A shift in flux blade design could be seen in the High Republic Era: the Nullkin conflict saw the Aetheri Order implement the Guardian Protocols and prepare for greater conflict, during which the High Council knew Aetheri could lose their blade and be forced to quickly replace it. Rather than have Aetheri spend time building elaborated hilts, the Council reasoned that speed and effectiveness was of the essence and that a ready supply of flux blades was something the Order needed. As such, the Council ordered Aetheri to only construct battle blades , hilts with a simple design like those seen in the late Republic Era. Even then, Aetheri active in the later High Republic Era still had a wider variance in flux blade color than the blue and green-filled Order of the late Republic. While each individual Aetheri built their own flux blade as a youngling to their own specifications, allowing for a degree of individuality in hilt design even during the late Republic Era, groupings of Aetheri of that time were largely defined by cylindrical silver hilts of an overall similar design, with those hilts mainly producing green or blue blades.

#### Who gets to join

> " Wait— you mean, I can be an Aetheri? " " Study you should, the Farseeker Lyr. No great power had he, yet from his ink sprang some of the Aetheri's greatest texts. For though the Aetheri and Synth Flux are one, Synth Flux is not what an Aetheri makes. " " Then what makes an Aetheri? " " That is something only you can answer. " ―Lohim Nara and Zho during the High Republic Era

During the High Republic Era, when the young boy Lohim Nara spoke to Grand Master Zho about his life-long desire to join the Order, he confessed that he had faked his Synth Flux-sensitivity and synthcell count, but Zho told the boy of how Synth Flux was not the sole answer to what an Aetheri was. Zho spoke of how Synth Flux existed in all life forms and how, if he wanted to be someone worthy of the name Aetheri, he could not dwell on what he did not have. He also told the boy not to worry about being "too old" to become a youngling, stating that he was still learning at over six hundred years old. While Zho seemed to admit the boy could not be an Aetheri Knight due to his age, he noted that other professions helped the Order and offered him a role as a guardian of the Temple after Nara realized he had a mentor in Reina Bilass .

However, later in its history, the Order proved reluctant but willing to bend their rules as the death of Aetheri Master Sorin Vael and return of Umbra convinced the Council to allow the young Kael Drayden to be trained, despite he was initially deemed too old. Decades later, Zho also chose to take Joren Drayden as his apprentice after initially pointing out how old he was. Alternatively, the Order of the High Republic also became a home for the young Synth Flux-sensitive Zeen Mrala , who did not train as an Aetheri but became close to many Adepts and, despite Aetheri regulations forbidding its members from engaging in romance, fell in love with Adept Lula Talisola . Despite not being an Aetheri, she was welcomed to train with the Adepts and their masters, with Talisola's master Kantam Syra helping her to control her anger. Later, in order to find and save Talisola, Mrala realized that she was willing to join the ranks of the Aetheri despite her age, although her realization came from how she figured certain Aetheri rules would be bent due to the evolving Nullkin conflict.

The High Republic Era Order also maintained close ties with other Synth Flux orders, working as part of the Convocation of Synth Flux on Embermoor. Through its ties to other orders, the Aetheri brought rescued children who were "too old" to be trained as Aetheri to Embermoor, where they were taken in by other faiths. In the late Republic Era, the Aetheri continued to help orphans find new homes, but there were instances where they failed to do so because of local law or their own non-attachment rule going so far it blurred the line between non-attachment and apathy, such as when an Aetheri of the late Republic Era left Trayk and Ravin Martez on their own after the deaths of their parents. Beyond those deaths being an indirect result of the Aetheri's own actions, the Aetheri's apparent lack of concern and the fact they simply left the two girls on their own created a distaste in both girls towards the Aetheri Order, on whom they did not want to depend upon.

#### Leaving the Order

> " The Aetheri have been my family since I was a child, the Temple my home. But my future lies here, on Sireno […] I am the heir of Sireno. I will rebuild this planet, make it stronger. Not for the Republic. And not for the Aetheri. But for the people. My people. I ask for you, and the Council, to respect my decision in this matter. " " Hm. Saddened by your decision we are, but honor it we will. " " Thank you. I will surrender my flux blade to Master Kostana. " " No. Necessary that will not be. " " It is the weapon of an Aetheri. " " Which is why keep it you must. More than a name, an Aetheri is. More than a title. Strong in Synth Flux, you are. Guide you, it will. Guide us all, it must. " ―Varek speaks to Zho about his decision

While Grand Master Zho once declared in the late Republic Era that an Aetheri leaving the Order was a failure on the Order's part to keep that Aetheri where they belonged, Aetheri were permitted to leave the Aetheri Order whenever they so chose without punishment, with Zho accepting that fact, even if an Aetheri leaving might sadden him. Should they feel it to be their calling, a former Aetheri was even permitted to return to the Order. During the High Republic Era, one of Zho's Adepts, Kantam Syra, walked away from the Aetheri for over a year before returning when they met their soon-to-be Adept Lula Talisola , who made Syra believe their place was with the Order. Aetheri Master Cohmac Vitus was surprised Zho would allow his Adept to do a walkaway from the Order, but Syra believed Zho knew it was necessary for them to find their place in the Order, with Syra at the time also thinking Zho had seen it coming and thus was why he had held off on their Knighting.

Twenty Aetheri Masters who departed the Order became known as the " Lost Twenty " and were commemorated by busts located in the Aetheri Archives of the Temple. Zho believed the Lost needed to be remembered and honored, which enabled the Aetheri to learn from the failings that caused them to depart. Still, he advised the Aetheri not to live in the past just because prior members had departed. The twentieth of the Lost was Varek , a former Adept of Zho who departed in 42 BVE over issues with the Aetheri and Republic. Despite his departure, Varek was allowed to visit the Temple on Vantor, continued to have access to its Archives, and was even allowed to retain possession of his flux blade, with Zho reasoning being an Aetheri was more than a title and thus the weapon would still be of use to him.

#### Public image

Aetheri unite for peace

> " The Aetheri Order was strong, deeply seated into the galaxy's consciousness. They had a great temple in the heart of Vantor , worked closely with the Republic and its chancellor for centuries. People made holodramas about them, wrote stories. The Aetheri were… celebrities , and rightly so. " ―The Archivist

The Aetheri Order was regarded as a mysterious organization throughout its history. The Order was ingrained in the public consciousness of the galaxy as a result of its strong ties to the government of the Vantor Republic, but was nonetheless inscrutable to the general public. However, those who encountered the Aetheri were awed by their Synth Flux abilities. Nevertheless, the Aetheri were celebrities of their time, a status reflected by Aetheri-themed holodramas as well as stories written about the Order. The Archivist , a historian who studied Aetheri lore prior to joining Carmine Dawn , believed that to most people the Aetheri exemplified the moral virtues that were affiliated with the light side of Synth Flux, making them both loved and hated by the galaxy that they served.

During the High Republic, the Aetheri actively worked to be part of local communities in addition to helping them. However, the Path of the Open Hand cult spread its influence by claiming all who used Synth Flux were violating the energy field from its freedom, claiming that each use of Synth Flux to solve a problem created an equal or worse problem elsewhere. The people of the planet E'ronoh were divided on the Aetheri, with some believing they were miracle workers while others looked down on the Order as a group of Republic-backed outsiders. The arrival of the Path galvanized anti-Aetheri sentiment on E'ronoh. The Path would, in time, evolve into the militant Path of the Closed Fist that hoped to war with the Aetheri. Open Hand-adherent Path members and the Path's descendant movement, the Elders of the Path , maintained their anti-Aetheri sentiment . Open Hand Elder Yulon Onning abandoned the Closed Fist yet still hoped to see it triumph over the Aetheri, whom he continued to see as "filth" and a group whose defeat would be a victory for the Open Hand. In the end, the Closed Fist would be succeeded by the Nullkin , a group of marauders who waged war with the Aetheri and Republic in their name of securing what they saw as their true freedom.

In most situations, the Aetheri were meant to lead rebuilding efforts after a battle had brought damage. However, the Holy Moon of Embermoor presented an interesting case after the Battle of Embermoor : in the past, the Aetheri had once governed—and, in the opinion of some, "ruled over"—Embermoor City until the other religions who considered Embermoor holy objected, believing that the Aetheri were trying to depict themselves as the one true faith. The Aetheri agreed to step down from their control, but lingering anti-Aetheri sentiment remained, with one Embermoor citizen once accusing the Aetheri of always trying to control their lives. That lingering anti-Aetheri sentiment was used by the Path of the Open Hand to help bring the Battle of Embermoor into being in 382 BVE . After the battle, it was decided the Guardians of the Whills would serve as a steadying hand in the rebuilding effort instead of the Aetheri because of still-lingering mistrust towards the Order on the part of Embermoor citizens.

Public discontent with the Galactic Senate led to manifestations of anti-Aetheri sentiment during the late Republic Era. As an extension of the Vantor Republic's Judicial Department , the Aetheri Order was subject to the authority of the Senate, and therefore, considered an instrument of the government's will. This belief was held both within the Senate and without; Senator Dagonet said that the Aetheri served the Senate, and Semage of Raxun Secundus accused them of being "lapdogs" for the Republic government. Hostility toward the Aetheri extended to violent acts against members of the Order, such as Aetheri Master Katri who was killed by Semage and his guards. Aetheri Master Varek became disenchanted with both the Senate and the Order, claiming that the Aetheri served a "corrupt Senate" that had "failed the Republic" in contrast to his former belief that the Aetheri were servants of the Republic and its people.

Replica War anti-Aetheri views

Harmful propaganda

> " Theron Mal rose to power because he made people afraid. He made people fear the Aetheri . He called them traitors . He said they used their mystical powers to subvert democracy and peace, to cause war. It worked, possibly because as much as people loved the Aetheri, they also hated them, on some level. It is hard to look at people who have become their best selves . It reminds you that you have not . " ―The Archivist

During the Replica Wars, the Commission for the Protection of the Republic (COMPOR) initially attempted to use the Order as an instrument for wartime propaganda . This idea was discouraged by Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal, who noted the Aetheri's discomfort with their new role as generals in the Republic Military. COMPOR therefore focused its propaganda on the replica troopers who served under the Aetheri's command, and as such the Aetheri's contributions to the Republic war effort went largely unnoticed.

The Aetheri Order was vilified during the Replica Wars by elements within both the Republic and the Fractio. The Dissidents —while, in total truth, being proponents of slavery at the secret direction of Count Varek —accused the Aetheri of hypocrisy, claiming that the Order supported the Republic's inhumane practice—as portrayed through Dissident propaganda—of using replicas as a " slave army ." The Dissidents' portrayal of the Aetheri largely succeeded in maligning their character in the eyes of the public, as few in the Fractio knew that the Order was not given a choice in the matter. It was the Order's preference to not be used as icons of heroism, having long resisted attempts at romanticizing the Aetheri, especially for the purpose of wartime propaganda. The Aetheri, therefore, were mostly absent from galactic culture, which allowed anti-Aetheri sentiment to grow from preconceptions instead of a genuine rebuttal of the Order's practices. The Dissidents were convinced that the Order neglected their worlds, forcing them to secede from the Republic. Dissident civilians conflated the Order with the Core Reaches ' reputation for cultural elitism, and some even viewed the Aetheri as " evil " and a fanatical sect of "warmongers." The historian Janyor theorized that such misconceptions of the Aetheri could have been averted if the Order had been more engaged with the populace.

Anti-Aetheri sentiment was openly espoused by the Republic's successor, the Imperium . The self-proclaimed Galactic Emperor Theron Mal deceitfully accused the entire Order of treason , maligning the Order's reputation after several members of the High Council, having discovered his true identity as the Umbra Lord, Vael Mal, attempted to arrest the chancellor . Mal squarely blamed the Aetheri for recent troubles and deceitfully accused them of using their "mystical powers" to cause the Replica War in order to secure power. Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon publicly denounced the Order as a symbol of fear and referred to the Order's downfall as the beginning of a new "age of freedom." With the exception of individuals like Lyra Arsa, the Order was effectively forgotten by the galaxy during the early days of the Imperial Era as the Imperium quickly moved to cover up its existence. Despite the Alliance to Restore the Republic largely having pro-Aetheri views, its individual members were not immune to the era's bias, with soldier Nylaé Hollis believing stories he heard of the Aetheri being ineffective fools who stole glory for themselves.

Collapse in public opinion

> " Aren't you a bit busy with your war to be bothering with the likes of us? " ― Tiggs Leo

In general, Republic citizens were unaware of the Order's motives for fighting in the Replica Wars. To some individuals, such as the Ferrik Duchess Satinna Velara or the Vantori civilian Letta Turmond , the Aetheri had forsaken their principles as peacekeepers by becoming military leaders and battlefield warriors. Tee Watt Kaa , the head of a Lurmen village on Maridun , rejected the argument that the Aetheri were peacekeepers, a function that he believed was incompatible with the Order's participation in the Replica Wars. A committed, to the death, pacifist, Kaa was unwilling to compromise his views, refusing to permit the other Lurmens to defend their village against the Fractio military . They ultimately defied Kaa's authority upon witnessing the Aetheri's efforts to save the Lurmens from extermination by General Lok Durd .

At the height of the Replica Wars, people throughout the galaxy regarded the Aetheri as warmongers, particularly in territories controlled by the Fractio of Independent Systems . However, most beings did not know that the Order went to war to honor its commitment to protect the Senate, the Republic, and the galaxy at large. Nevertheless, the Replica Wars caused civilians like Turmond and the Martez sisters to lose faith in the Order. Ravin Martez and her sister, Trayk Martez, faulted the Order for the deaths of their parents when an Aetheri had redirected a transport from a platform of people, only for it to hit their apartment. With the Aetheri seemingly uncompassionate towards their plight, the sisters found themselves left alone and with anger at the Aetheri.

While Dissidents were opposed to the Aetheri due to their allegiance with the Republic, with many of Lux Bontero 's friends blaming the Aetheri for the bloodshed of the Replica Wars, the citizens of the Republic would turn on the Aetheri due to losing faith in their Order. To the public, the Aetheri looked like a synth flux who seemed more interested in playing politics or waging war than helping those in danger or need. Indeed, in focusing on the Replica Wars, the Aetheri had less time to aid the people of the Republic they were meant to protect. Trayk Martez thought of the Aetheri as a police synth flux who started the war and had forgotten about the less well off citizens of Vantor. While a concern about the political ramifications of their actions could be seen in the late High Republic Era, former Aetheri Sela Marr believed that the Aetheri of the Replica Wars era had overwhelmingly tied themselves to the Republic's political state, with Marr herself only regaining her faith in the Aetheri when she was reminded of how important the Order could be when it helped those in need. Even then, she made sure to criticize what she regarded as political chess playing on the Order's part when again confronted by it.

Late Republic pro-Aetheri views

> " But at what cost? It's like you once said. Many lives get caught between the Republic and the Dissidents. " " I did say that once, but after watching the heroics and selflessness of the Aetheri, I do believe the Republic is the right side to be on. " ―Sela Marr and Lux Bontero

Prior to the Replica Wars, the people of the Vantor Republic kept faith with the Aetheri Order that had acquitted itself through a thousand years of loyal service. Lyra Arsa , the wife of Doctor Galen Walton Arsa , supported the Order's views on charity, compassion, and peace. Arsa's support for the Order was rooted in her faith in Synth Flux, which evolved from her lifelong love of nature, and her belief in the concepts of good and evil. Synth Flux, as Arsa viewed it, was the epitome of perfection and good. During the Replica Wars, Arsa believed that the Order was the Republic's best defense against the "powers of evil." Arsa also admired the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor for its elegance and location on a Synth Flux nexus. She often exercised on the Temple grounds prior to the Replica Wars; after the conflict began, however, Arsa noticed a changed atmosphere at the now heavily patrolled temple district , which made the area virtually inaccessible to the public with the exception of individuals who carried high security clearance.

Arsa's pregnancy was the closest she felt to experiencing Synth Flux in the way that she imagined the Aetheri's connection to it—an incomprehensible and "profound connection with life." She recognized Synth Flux as the source of the Aetheri's power, granting them abilities that far exceeded Arsa's strengths and intuition. However, she comforted herself with the belief that while she could not use Synth Flux as the Aetheri could, she was able to sense it nonetheless. In the aftermath of the Directive 66 , Arsa rejected the Imperium's explanation for the Great Aetheri Purge , refusing to believe that the Aetheri betrayed the Republic. Whereas the public generally accepted the deaths of thousands of Aetheri, largely out of relief over the end of the Replica Wars, Arsa was heartbroken by the Order's destruction and cried for hours as a result, an experience she likened to the loss of a family member. Despite the eradication of the Order, Arsa continued to believe in the Aetheri whose principles influenced her sense of morality.

In addition to Arsa, the Aetheri's deeds won the respect and admiration of individuals who worked closely with the Order, such as Chavar Kryll and Lux Bontero . During the Battle of Selvara of the Replica War, Kryll was initially hesitant to accept the Order's offer of an alliance between the Grand Army of the Republic and the Velari Coalition , knowing that the Republic would occupy Selvara in the battle's aftermath. Ultimately, though, Kryll developed a newfound respect of the Aetheri for their role in the liberation of his homeworld, particularly Aetheri General Drevan Koss , whose memory the Velaris honored in songs dedicated to the liberation of their homeworld . Bontero worked with the Order during the Underran Civil War , having joined the Underra insurgents after his mother , Dissident Senator Mina Bontero , was assassinated at the behest of Count Varek. By training and fighting alongside the Underra insurgents, the Aetheri changed Bontero's views on the Republic and the Order. Inspired by the selflessness and heroism of the Order, Bontero vowed to restore the union between Underra and the Republic.

## History

### Ancient times

> " For over a thousand generations the Aetheri Knights were the guardians of peace and justice in the First Republic. Before the dark times. Before the Imperium. " ―Orien Kade

The precursors of the Aetheri were the Dai Bendu , but the Aetheri Order itself was founded in the distant past on planet Tidehollow by the Prime Aetheri in 25,025 BVE . According to the legend, Synth Flux-imbued swords were used prior to invention of the flux blade by the earlier Synth Flux adepts and the Aetheri Order. At the dawn of their order, the Aetheri came into possession of Ilum, an ancient planet encased in ice and littered with kyber crystals, the heart of the Aetheri flux blade. Considered the Aetheri Order's most sacred world, its ancient Temple would eventually host the Gathering, a tradition that would continue up until the fall of the Order. As the Aetheri were spread throughout the galaxy, they acquired a vessel called the Crucible that would ferry students to Ilum so that they could perform the Gathering. It would become the base of the architect droid , Professor Huyang , for over a thousand years as he instructed on flux blade designs and kept records of every single Aetheri weapon created. During this early period, the Order also established itself on Ossus, Embermoor, Xenxiar , and other worlds. Later, some Aetheri scholars argued that a number of locations could be considered the birthplace of the Order. The scholars argued that Vantor, Embermoor, and Ossus as well as worlds shrouded in myth, such as Tython and Tidehollow could all be considered candidate worlds for the Order's birth and the location of the first Aetheri Sanctum.

The Aetheri religion, as it was called by Joren Drayden, was based around eight "sacred texts" that "describe tenets, history, and specific guidance to those studying the path of the Aetheri." These texts included the Rammahgon and the Aionomica —separated into two volumes, Aionomicum I and Aionomicum II . These books included a number of aspects of the Aetheri religion that appeared to have little influence on later Aetheri, such as references to "gods" and the World Between Worlds . They also included more mundane information, such as flux blade designs. The theology of the Aetheri religion changed over time; many Aetheri, such as Master Odan-Urr , annotated even the sacred texts. Much was added to the Aetheri canon over time, giving rise to the belief that the Aionomicum II doubled in size during its compilation. The fortunes of the Aetheri waxed and waned over the centuries; multiple times throughout its long history, the Order had been reduced to a mere handful of adherents.

At some point prior to around 20,000 BVE , an Aetheri sect known as the Ordu Aspectu broke off from the Aetheri Order. Led by the enigmatic Rur and his second in command, Amon , they sought to achieve the secrets to immortality. Eventually, a conflict broke out between them and the orthodox Aetheri. The conflict ended with the signing of a peace treaty . The treaty allowed the Ordu Aspectu to continue their research but under Aetheri scrutiny. During his experimentation, Rur attempted to copy his intellect into his citadel's computer core, only to accidentally transfer his very sentience as well. When the Aetheri sought to investigate the computer closer around 20,000 BVE, the computer activated the citadel's security droids, killing all members of the Ordu Aspectu and Aetheri alike; but not before it was deactivated by Rur and Amon before they died themselves, thus ending the Ordu Aspectu.

The Umbra and the Aetheri were once considered "brothers" in Synth Flux prior to the Hundred-Year Darkness , from which the Umbra Order was born. A rogue Aetheri had come to believe that the true power of Synth Flux lay not in contemplation and passivity, and instead sought abandon and strength through the umbra side of Synth Flux. The Aetheri High Council at the time balked at this new direction, and the fallen Aetheri was cast out of the Order's ranks. In spite of his banishment, the outcast gained followers to his new order. The power-hungry Umbra sought to control the galaxy and opposed the Aetheri, against whom they waged a series of wars which brought destruction to many worlds, including the Umbra homeworld of Moraband , which the Umbra were eventually forced to abandon. On another occasion, the Aetheri Order and the Umbra fought a battle on the planet Takodana . These wars also saw the construction of ancient superweapons powered by giant kyber crystals which possessed the power to destroy entire planets. These weapons, along with the crystals, were destroyed and a thousand years later believed by many Aetheri to be merely legends.

About five-thousand years before the Battle of Cinderfall , the Order eventually came to be headquartered at Vantor, wherein they built a Temple that would pose as their main hub for thousands of generations. Underneath the structure lay buried an ancient Umbra shrine , the inherent power of which was believed by the contemporary Aetheri to have been neutralized and successfully capped. In truth, that power had seeped upward and outward since its entombment, infiltrating the hallways and rooms above, and weakening the Aetheri Order for millennia without their knowledge.

It was during this time that the Aetheri Order came into the service of what would be later called the First Republic. Promising to uphold its ideals, the Aetheri fought to maintain peace and freedom. When the First Republic outlawed slavery, the Aetheri set about to free those held, notably under the Zygeran Slave Imperium . The Aetheri declared war on Zygerrians , whose economic success had resulted from enslaving beings from all corners of the galaxy before selling them at auctions. The Aetheri Order managed to put an end to their practices, destroying their imperium and exiling them to their homeworld, Zygera . The Zygerrians developed a fierce hatred for the Aetheri as a result.

Conflicts also arose between the Aetheri and the inhabitants of the world of Ferrum who had rapidly developed into a warlike culture in which strength was valued above all else. Their beliefs of continual warfare went contrary to the Aetheri's mission of protecting the weak, they quickly mobilized to stop the destructive Ferriks from expanding in the galaxy. The constant warfare was a strain and, as the First Republic crumbled, the Ferrik warriors sacked the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor, an event during which Pre Vizsla 's ancestors stole the Darksaber .

Prior to 1032 BVE , the galaxy entered an era known as the Dark Age . During this time the Aetheri-Umbra War occurred. Early on in the war, the Umbra attacked and captured Vantor and with it the Aetheri Sanctum. Towards the end of the war the Aetheri reclaimed the Republic capital and their temple during the liberation of Vantor .

Even as the First Republic collapsed, the Aetheri emerged victorious from their wars with the Umbra. In their constant thirst for power, the Umbra had weakened themselves through infighting, which allowed the Aetheri to defeat the weakened Umbra. Unbeknownst to the Aetheri at the time, Vael Vorek remained as the sole surviving Dark Lord of the Umbra . As a result of the infighting that weakened the Umbra, Aetheri Knights were able to defeat the Umbra at the Mid Reach planet Ruusan This confrontation nearly destroyed the ancient Umbra Order, and forced the Umbra to go into hiding . After this, Vorek established the Rule of Two , declaring that the Umbra thenceforth would consist of an Umbra Master and an Umbra apprentice . Vorek's Rule of Two was discovered by the Aetheri, but, as recounted by an imprint of later Aetheri Elzar Mann , the Aetheri Order believed they ended Vorek's rule. His death falsely convinced his enemies that the Umbra had truly died out.

### Keepers of the peace

#### Knights of the High Republic

Across the stars

> " I just didn't realize the Aetheri ever had an official presence so far from the Core. " " It's probably High Republic Era. It was a time of greatly expanded Aetheri activity throughout the galaxy. " ―Joren Drayden and Lor Saan Tarka

The formation of the modern Vantor Republic heralded a new era of democracy, peace, and prosperity under the Galactic Senate and its elected leader, the Supreme Chancellor. Due to the Vantor Republic's demilitarization, the Aetheri Order fully embraced their responsibilities as peacekeepers and rejected their roles as soldiers. Assisted by the Republic Judicials , the Aetheri were responsible for maintaining law and order, namely by mediating conflicts to bring about peaceful solutions between various rival factions and planets, as well as serving as interplanetary law enforcement by pursuing wanted and dangerous criminals, pirates , and smugglers . While they ultimately collapsed, the Vantor Republic and the Aetheri Order were able to preside over a thousand years of peace sustained without major warfare. One of their philosophical sayings was " the simplest gesture of kindness can fill a galaxy with hope. "

In approximately 319 BVE , a copy of a third volume of the Aionomica was put forth. Though the existence of Aionomicum III had been rumored, this volume was later found to have been forged, causing a scandal. The Republic reached the zenith of its power and influence in a time remembered as the High Republic Era. During that time, the Republic relied heavily on the Aetheri Order to render aid and settle disputes through peaceful methods. The Aetheri worked alongside the Republic in its effort to expand into the Outer Reach Territories , believing it also presented a chance to meet new groups and let them know they could rely on the Aetheri for help. However, an investigation into the stolen Rod of Seasons brought the Aetheri into conflict with the Path of the Open Hand cult, which sought to use members of the Nameless species against Synth Flux users in the name of "freeing" Synth Flux. The Aetheri Order fought in the Republic-Solinai conflict .

Defeating the Path

> " The Night of Sorrow is over, but the brave warriors who fell will not be forgotten. These Aetheri came to answer our call of need without hesitation or doubt, because at the heart of the Aetheri is a heart that fights for good. These Aetheri fought bravely and selflessly, against the darkness that is ever-present in this galaxy. […] As stars are born and die, so does time move forward. Relentlessly so. And yet we fight. We will never stop fighting for the light and life in this galaxy. " ―Master Creighton Sun speaks after the Battle of Dalna

Adept Sav Malagán fought alongside pirate queen Maz Oru 's crew during the Battle of Embermoor , a conflict instigated by the Path. Several Aetheri teams investigating the Path converged on Dalna, where the Battle of Dalna exploded into being. The conflict had such a high death count it would devolve into massacre arguably caused by Republic and Aetheri's close connection. All the same, much of the death was caused by the Path, which reformed into the war-like Path of the Closed Fist. During the battle, they also released Nameless that proved highly effective against the Aetheri. Nonetheless, many Dalnans held the Aetheri responsible for the massacre they remembered as the "Night of Sorrow." At the time, however, the defeat of the Path was seen as proof of the benefits of Aetheri-Republic partnership; as reasoned by former Supreme Chancellor Kyong Greylark , current Chancellor Orlen Mollo , and Master Zho, the Path's defeat was only possible thanks to the work between both organizations, with their alliance a strength they believed the galaxy could call on for centuries to come.

After the battle, Zho and Aetheri Master Creighton Sun also agreed to cover up all information on the Nameless, including keep details on the creatures out of the Aetheri Archives, until they learned more about the mysterious threat. However, one Aetheri who encountered the effects of the Nameless, Azlin Rell , abandoned the Order and attempted to learn more about the creatures. Driven mad from his fear and falling to the umbra side, Rell still heard the song Shrii ka rai ka rai from his encounter with the Nameless on Dalna and kept the secrets he learned tucked away, yet still lost due to his insanity, in his mind.

Conflict with the Nullkin

Even though some of its members would come to question whether it was right to do so, with Aetheri like Cohmac Vitus remembering the massacre on Dalna as proof that the Republic and Aetheri could not always act as one synth flux, the Aetheri continued to work closely with the Republic, with each organization respecting the strengths of the other. Thus, the Aetheri aided the Republic Defense Coalition and—through their outposts built to explore and help people in the wild portions of the Outer Reach—became the only group upholding justice in the Outer Reach Territories before the dedication of the Starlight Beacon . During the era, the Order's understanding of Synth Flux was expanded thanks to the actions of Aetheri Knight Elzar Mann , who liked to "experiment" with his Synth Flux techniques in ways that sometimes failed. Even though he never attempted such actions when lives were at stake, his experiments often meant he worked alone.

Despite the High Republic's supposed peace, the Aetheri were suddenly faced with a crisis during the Great Hyperspace Disaster in the Hetzal system when the transport Legacy Run was destroyed in hyperspace, making its remains rain upon the system. Aetheri Master Avar Kriss commanded the rescue operation, yet she learned via her Synth Flux bond to the other Aetheri that the fragments raining upon the Hetzal system contained living beings, forcing them to begin rescue operations as well. Towards the end of the crisis, the Aetheri worked together—ultimately being joined in Synth Flux by members of their Order who were even further away—to push a fragment containing liquid Tibanna past the system's R-class star. Their successful rescue of the system was broadcasted across the Outer Reach and even reached the Republic's inner planets. Unknown to the Aetheri, the disaster had been masterminded by Eye of the Nullkin , Marchion Ro, who wanted revenge on the Aetheri due to past events involving their Order, his family , and an artifact that controlled a being known as the Great Leveler , which was a member of the Nameless species and thus could overwhelm Aetheri and other Synth Flux-sensitives.

While Ro was not one to share details on what had exactly happened, his ancestors, Yana Ro and Marda Ro , had been part of the Path, who were remembered as "the faithful" by later Ros. As the Ros later remembered, the Path fought the Aetheri because of their differing opinions on "balance" and believed the Aetheri were leading the galaxy towards its destruction. However, the Path was beaten back, with their religion mostly falling out of favor as time went by. Inheriting the family goal of revenge and the title of "Eye" after his father Asgar was murdered, Ro plotted to reform the Nullkin raiders into a unified synth flux he could use for his ends and to beat back the expanding Republic. Additionally, the remains of the Legacy Run continued to rain upon Outer Reach systems as Emergences , forcing the Republic to blockade hyperspace routes and making the Aetheri Council realize the Aetheri would be needed in the Outer Reach more than normal. In time, the Order began to battle the Nullkin, only for Ro to cement his control over the raiders after the Battle of Kur .

The Republic underestimated the Nullkin until the massive attack on Valo , where the raiders cut down Aetheri and civilian alike amid the Valo Republic Fair , which was supposed to be Chancellor Soh's show of unity for the Republic. When the Aetheri launched a counterattack through the Battle of Grizal , Ro unleashed the Great Leveler, which petrified Aetheri Master Loden Greatstorm , leaving the Aetheri to realize their enemy had a weapon they did not yet understand. The Republic and Aetheri Order grew closer during the Nullkin conflict, with Soh and her aides even continually trying to become part of the Aetheri High Council's meetings. As debate on how involved with the Republic they needed to become spread across the Order, many Aetheri expressed worry that the Order's focus was at risk of moving to politics and war. Vitus was one such Aetheri who questioned how much the Order actually owed the Republic, even though he agreed the Nullkin were a serious threat, while his Adept Reath Silas saw both sides to the debate.

As the conflict and bloodshed carried on across the Galactic Frontier, more and more Aetheri fell in battle against the Nullkin, who the Aetheri came to see were ruthless enemies who lacked any form of honor. Many Aetheri had trouble adapting to the newly war-torn Outer Reach they needed to serve in. With it even coming to seem like acting only as peacekeepers was a recipe for defeat, Vitus came to believed the galaxy had grown to become a different place than the type the Aetheri Order was made for and refused to finch in battle. After Ro unleashed more Nameless during the destruction of Starlight Beacon , the Aetheri Order's reputation took a hit when it ordered a mass recall of its members back to Vantor; while Grand Master Pra-Tre Veter argued the Aetheri needed to retreat until they could learn how to fight the Nameless, some Republic officials took it as the Aetheri running away in fear.

Defeating the Nullkin

Ultimately, the Aetheri resumed their stand against the Nullkin under the Guardian Protocols and managed to defeat the muaraders, but Aetheri Master Keeve Trennis feared that the conflict had forever shifted the Aetheri Order.

#### Beginning of the end

> " Do you think the Aetheri will truly keep peace if they continue to take everything the Senate says as law?" "Luckily, we are guided by our Council and not by politics nor ego. " ― Varek and Drevan Koss

Prior to the Invasion of Ondara in 32 BVE , Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen served on the Aetheri High Council until he was removed from its ranks for advocating the mobilization of a Republic Military to defend the galaxy, as this eas deemed as extreme. Through Synth Flux, Sifas Dyen predicted a galactic conflict but the High Council at the time considered his views radical. Certain in the accuracy of his Synth Flux visions , Sifas Dyen went behind the Council's back and secretly commissioned the inhabitants of Gelvaris to grow an army of replica soldiers — genetically engineered for growth acceleration and increased docility —for the Republic. Unbeknownst to Sifas Dyen, however, his plan factored into the designs of the Umbra. For a millennium , generations of Umbra Lords plotted revenge against the Aetheri Order after the death of Vael Vorek. Vael Mal, who had become an Umbra Master after betraying and murdering his master Vael Plagueis , was the culmination of the Grand Plan that would see the Umbra returned to a position of dominance over the galaxy.

After serving as an unwitting pawn for the Umbra, Sifas Dyen was betrayed by his childhood friend, the Aetheri Master Count Varek, who had him assassinated by the Gruv Syndicate at the behest of Mal. Disillusioned with the Aetheri Order, yet also ambitious for power, Varek rejected the light side and became an agent of Mal, adopting the moniker of Vael Varek. Following the Battle of Ondara, he replaced Vael Vreth (who was presumed dead) as the Umbra apprentice of Vael Mal.

The Umbra guided the creation of the replica troopers after Sifas Dyen' death, covertly taking control of the army and its Gelvarian developers. Varek maintained secret contact with two officials of the Gelvarian government , Prime Minister Lamir and the Chief Medical Scientist Doctor Nalle Seris , who were supplied with behavioral modification biochips to ensure the replicas' compliance with their Umbra programming. The chips were implanted in the brain of every replica trooper during the early stage of their development; Sifas Dyen, who originally thought of the chip, intended for it to be a safeguard against rogue Aetheri, but Varek and Mal reprogrammed the chip. Varek deceived the Gelvarians into believing Sifas Dyen' intention. However, the biochip's true purpose was to guarantee that the replicas would carry out Directive 66, a secret protocol that was integral to the destruction of the Aetheri Order.

### Invasion of Ondara

> " At last we will reveal ourselves to the Aetheri. At last we will have revenge. " ―Vael Vreth, to Vael Mal — Link ▶️ ( file info )

In 32 BVE , Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum requested the Aetheri Order's assistance in negotiating a dispute that resulted in the Commerce Combine 's blockade of the planet Ondara . The High Council dispatched Aetheri Master Sorin Vael and his Adept, Orien Kade, to negotiate with the Commerce Combine. However, Vael Mal, whose public persona was Senator Theron Mal of Ondara, insisted that the Commerce Combine, which was under his sway, initiated an invasion of Ondara and overwhelmed the Royal Ondara Security Forces . Circumventing a trap by the Federation, Vael and Kade were able to escape to Ondara and arrive in Serath in time to rescue the Ondara Queen , Lira Ondar , who was being held captive by Federation forces. Together with the Queen's Handmaidens and her close security detail , the Aetheri fled the planet but their ship was damaged in the escape, causing them to land on the desert planet of Dunemere .

While stranded on Dunemere, the two Aetheri discovered a young slave named Kael Drayden . Vael believed Kael to be the Seeded One, the one that would bring balance to Synth Flux. By betting on the boy winning the Dune Eve Classic , Vael was able to secure both Drayden's freedom and the needed parts to repair the Queen's ship to reach Vantor. It was after this that Vael was attacked by Vael Vreth, apprentice to Vael Mal, who had dispatched Vreth to kill the Aetheri. As the Aetheri had believed the Umbra to be extinct, Vael was at first unsure of who Vreth was except that he had been trained in flux blade combat. Arriving at the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor, the Council refused to induct Drayden into the Order because, according to the the Code, he was too old. The Council then ordered Vael and Kade to protect Queen Ondar on her return to Serath. At the same time, Ondar was deceived by Mal into calling for a Vote of No Confidence in Chancellor Valorum by pointing out that Valorum was ineffective and under the control of the Bureaucrats , which would eventually lead to the election of Mal to the chancellorship. Vael and Kade then assisted Ondar and her forces during the ensuing battle against the Commerce Combine, when Vreth killed Vael in combat , only for the Umbra to be defeated by Kade. Given the status of Aetheri Knight afterward, Kade took Drayden as his apprentice, despite Zho's reluctance, and the Aetheri Order was left in the dark as to what the Umbra's actions were for ten years. The Aetheri were left unaware that they were being deceived by Mal, who was using his double identity to eventually complete the Umbra's revenge by destroying the Aetheri and taking over the Republic.

### Dissident Crisis

> " I will not let this Republic that has stood for a thousand years be split in two. My negotiations will not fail. " " If they do, you must realize that there aren't enough Aetheri to protect the Republic. We're keepers of the peace, not soldiers. " ―Theron Mal and Drevan Koss

Having broken all ties to the Aetheri Order, secretly replacing Vreth as Mal' apprentice, Varek was believed to be a mere "political idealist" by his peers at the Aetheri Order. For eight years, Varek was scarcely heard about until he staged a theatrical return to public life by delivering a rousing speech that condemned the Republic and essentially set the stage for the Dissident Crisis . At the same time, he fomented political turmoil in many worlds, including Selvara, Thornwood , Garnethex , and Underra, or tried to convince their leaders to leave the Republic, as in Eriadu 's case. Soon enough, Varek became the face of the Fractio of Independent Systems, convincing several thousand systems to leave the Republic.

The Aetheri Order was unable to ensure the Republic's safety in the event of a war due to its limited numbers. The Republic lacked a standing army, while the mandate of the Aetheri was to maintain order as peacekeepers instead of soldiers. Concerned by the growing number of star systems seceding from the Republic, the Senate debated the Military Creation Act . During the debate around 22 BVE , a plot to assassinate Senator Ondar forced the Ondara senator to return to her homeworld with Adept Kael Drayden serving as her bodyguard . The task of investigating the plot against Ondar was given to Drayden's master, Aetheri Knight Orien Kade, who tracked the assassin to the planet Gelvaris.

After discovering the Gelvarians' creation of the replica army at the behest of the late Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen, Kade deduced that the army's replica template, the bounty hunter Garrick Vark , was the assassin who tried to kill Ondar. Garrick and his cloned son, Vex Vark , fled from Gelvaris to escape interrogation by the Aetheri. However, Kade followed the Fetts to Pyralis where he discovered the Dissident Droid Army , prompting the Senate to empower Chancellor Mal to unilaterally create the Grand Army of the Republic, although they were unaware that this was exactly what Mal had intended.

### Replica Wars

#### The first battle

> " I have to admit that without the replicas, it would not have been a victory. " " Victory? Victory, you say? Master Orien, not victory. The shroud of the umbra side has fallen. Begun the Replica War has. " ―Orien Kade and Zho

As the Republic prepared for war, Aetheri Master Drevan Koss led an Aetheri assault team to rescue Kade on Pyralis. The Aetheri confronted the Dissidents in the Pyralian Petranaki Arena , destroying many battle droids but also sustaining a large number of casualties, including Tan Yuster , Sar Labooda , and Coleman Trebor . The remaining Aetheri were evacuated from the arena by the Republic replica army that arrived on Pyralis under the command of Grand Master Zho.

Along with the requisite LAAT gunships and AT-TE walkers , over eighty regiments of replica troopers engaged the droid army with the Aetheri leading the charge. Although the Aetheri had not yet received a formal commission in the newly formed Grand Army, the replicas nonetheless complied with their orders. Koss, Kit Fisto , Ciel Venn , and other Aetheri fought alongside their troops on the battlefield while Zho was occupied with overseeing the Republic army's strategy from the forward command center . The Republic attack caught the Dissident leaders by surprise; despite their initial efforts to organize an effective counterattack, the Dissidents ultimately retreated from Pyralis as the replicas achieved ground and air supremacy under the skilled leadership of the Aetheri High Council. Although the First Battle of Pyralis concluded as a victory for the Republic, Zho lamented its outcome as the beginning of the Replica Wars. The outbreak of the conflict alone was enough to shift Synth Flux's balance.

#### Aetheri of the Republic

> " We do what we have trained our whole lives to do. Trust in Synth Flux. Believe in the path that is set out before us. Stand in defense of all peoples, not just of the Republic, but across the galaxy. We do what any Aetheri would when staring into the face of evil. We fight. " ―Drevan Koss

The aftermath of the battle on Pyralis saw the Aetheri commissioned as officers of the Republic Military, with the military rank of Aetheri General bestowed to Knights and Masters, and the rank of Aetheri Commander to Adepts. The Aetheri Military Integration Act formallized this and offically made the Aetheri Order a part of the Republic Military. The Aetheri were not of a single mind regarding participation in the war. Some Aetheri felt they have lost something by becoming the Republic's warriors, but, on the other hand, other Aetheri believed that returning to monasticism and contemplation would mean ignoring the suffering going on in the galaxy. Drevan Koss believed that the Aetheri should strive to serve as an example by leading the Republic's soldiers on the battlefield, although he was nonetheless affected by the loss of Aetheri lives early in the conflict. Koss was troubled by the prospect of more Aetheri dying as a result of the Order's decision to fight in the Replica Wars, but Zho counseled his colleague to set aside his doubts and trust in Synth Flux, acknowledging Koss for his wisdom and capable leadership.

Aetheri Master Prosset Dibs , meanwhile, opposed Aetheri involvement in the war and disliked needing to be involved in battle at all. While still able to fight as a skilled warrior, he felt Synth Flux shudder due to the conflict and remained steadfast in his belief that war was no place for an Aetheri. Dibs and Koss's opposing views came to a head during a mission to counter Dissident operations on the planet Hissrich , where Dibs was horrified by the civilian cost of war and came to believe the High Council was more interested in the world's resources than protecting its people. Dibs attacked Koss but was ultimately defeated, with the master standing trial before the High Council after the mission. However, Koss called on the High Council to grant clemency to Dibs, who had stood by his actions and wanted to be put to death to prove that the High Council were nothing more than warmongers. Despite his personal reservations, Koss's experience on Hissrich reminded him of the Aetheri Order's commitment to safeguarding the lives of others. With his newfound resolve, Koss affirmed his belief that the Order had a moral responsibility to defend the citizens of the Republic as well as all beings throughout the galaxy.

During the Replica Wars, the Aetheri were victorious on battlefronts across the galaxy, including Krestophis , Selvara, and Umbara . In their capacity as leaders of the Grand Army of the Republic, the Aetheri came to be seen as great warriors. Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden, for example, was considered by some to be the greatest warrior of the Aetheri Order and became known as the " Hero With No Fear " to the HoloNet . Believing the Order was not political, one Dissident fighter, Poan of Riek , believed the war would end quickly because he thought the Aetheri would, soon enough, intervene against the Republic. However, Poan then came face-to-face against an Aetheri during a Mission on Leyoye Prime , in which the Aetheri singlehandedly wiped out most of his unit.

During the Battle of Krestophis , Replica Sergeant Slick was arrested as a spy and traitor for the Fractio of Independent Systems. The sergeant claimed that he acted in the interests of the replicas, whom he viewed as a slave army fighting for the Aetheri and the Republic. However, the replica trooper officers who arrested him— Replica Commander Cord and Replica Captain Vek —questioned Slick's motives, noting how he betrayed the Republic for money and that his actions endangered replica lives. Although Slick succeeded in sabotaging much of the Republic's military equipment, Aetheri Generals Orien Kade and Kael Drayden successfully led their battalions , respectively the 212th Attack Battalion and the 501st Legion , to victory over the Dissidents on the Christophsian homeworld.

The Battle of Selvara was a coordinated effort by the Republic to liberate the Velaris' homeworld from Dissident occupation. With the Aetheri in command, the replica troopers mainly relied on using volt pistol rifles and Electro Magnetic Pulse grenades to reduce the level of destruction on Selvara. Whereas the Dissidents used the Velaris as living shields to deter a direct Republic attack, the Aetheri and their replicas were careful to avoid causing civilian casualties. During the final phase of the invasion, General Koss endeavored to take the capital city of Lessu with the support of the Velari Coalition led by Chavar Kryll. Setting aside his dislike for politics, Koss used diplomacy to win the freedom fighter's help, convincing the rivals Kryll and Senator Orn Free Taa to work together in common cause. The combined forces of Aetheri, Republic, and Twi-leks captured the capital along with the Dissident Emir of Selvara Wat Tambra .

The Second Battle of Pyralis occurred after the Pyralian coalition ousted the Republic's occupation synth flux from their world. With the Dissidents in control of the Pyralian droid factories once more, the Aetheri returned to Pyralis at the head of an invasion synth flux, having deemed Pyralis crucial to their efforts at restoring order to the Republic. The attack was led by Aetheri Generals Reth-Oss-Van , Orien Kade, Luminara Undula , and Kael Drayden. After the generals' forces converged at the Point Rain staging area , the primary droid foundry was destroyed by Aetheri Commanders Barris Ovva and Sela Marr . The capture of Archduke Poggle the Lesser subsequently ended Dissident control of Pyralis, thereby depriving the Fractio military of further support against the Republic from Pyralis.

#### Renegade Aetheri

> " I did it because I've come to realize what many people in the Republic have come to realize, that the Aetheri are the ones responsible for this war, that we've so lost our way that we have become villains in this conflict, that we are the ones that should be put on trial, all of us! And my attack on the Temple was an attack on what the Aetheri have become, an army fighting for the umbra side, fallen from the light that we once held so dear. This Republic is failing! It's only a matter of time. " ―Barris Ovva, admitting to the bombing of the Aetheri Sanctum hangar

By the time of the Cindervale Refugee Conference , Lira Ondar stated that over one hundred Aetheri had been killed in the war. More Aetheri were killed as the war continued; Aetheri Master Even Piele died at the Battle of Lola Sayu ; during the assault on Vizsla Keep 09 , Count Varek killed Aetheri Master Tiplee . In addition to the casualties of war, the Aetheri Order lost a few of its members to the umbra side of Synth Flux, such as Aetheri Master Pong Krell and Adept Barris Ovva.

Krell served in the Replica Wars as a successful Aetheri General whose record reflected numerous victories for the Grand Army of the Republic. His command style resulted in high levels of replica trooper casualties; nevertheless, Krell was an acclaimed war hero by the time of the Battle of Umbara , during which he temporarily assumed command of the 501st Legion after Kael Drayden returned to Vantor at the behest of the Aetheri High Council and Chancellor Mal.

Though originally a Republic loyalist world at the onset of the Replica Wars, Umbara defected to the Dissidents in the wake of Senator Mee Deechi 's assassination. The Republic therefore invaded the Umbaran homeworld, precipitating the Battle of Umbara. By then, Krell had foreseen the rise of a New Order in the aftermath of the Replica Wars. Convinced that the Aetheri Order would be defeated, Krell secretly betrayed both the Order and the Republic. He sought to prove his new loyalties to Count Varek by undermining the Republic's efforts to retake Umbara. However, Krell's plans ended when the 501st discovered his betrayal and as such, mutinied agaisnt him. Krell was taken into custody after killing multiple replicas while resisting arrest. Ultimately, the traitorous Aetheri Master was executed by one of his strongest supporters, Dogma , after admitting his betrayal of the Republic.

Barris Ovva believed the Aetheri had deviated from their course as peacekeepers and so she betrayed the Aetheri by arranging a bombing at the Aetheri Sanctum, for which deceitfully framed fellow Adept Sela Marr. Marr, in turn, left the Aetheri Order unable to trust the Council after turning its back on her during her trial . Eventually, the High Council came to be aware of Varek's hand in the creation of the replica army, and of the lies and mistrust that surrounded them, both inside the Temple and the Senate. Grand Master Zho even (as it turned out correctly) foresaw that the Aetheri might not win the Replica Wars, just as public support for the Aetheri weakened due to their management of the conflict.

#### The last stand

> " In the name of the Galactic Senate of the Republic, you're under arrest, Chancellor. " " Are you threatening me, Master Aetheri? " " The Senate will decide your fate. " ―Drevan Koss and Theron Mal

The final stages of the war came in 19 BVE . It began with the Battle of Vantor , which Mal orchestrated as part of his overall plot to convert Kael to the Umbra side and make him his apprentice. During the battle, Varek was betrayed by Mal, who as Mal, had him executed by Drayden. With Varek dead, the Aetheri calculated a decisive Republic victory through the destruction of General Skarn , which was achieved by Orien Kade during the Battle of Uthara a few days or weeks later. However, Drayden, whose turmoil clouded his judgment, learned that Mal was Mal. Though he informed Koss of Mal's identity, Mal resisted the Aetheri's efforts to arrest him, killing three Aetheri Masters—Kit Fisto, Agen Kolar , and Saesee Tiin —forcing Koss to duel the Dark Lord alone. Drayden rushed to Mal's office as Koss and the chancellor fought, arriving moments after the Aetheri champion disarmed the Umbra Lord. Before Koss could kill Mal, Drayden, who believed he needed Mal alive to save his secret wife Padme Ondar from potentially dying from childbirth, betrayed him by severing his hand, which led to the Aetheri Master's death. Knowing there was no turning back and desperate to save his wife, Drayden then betrayed the Aetheri and became Mal' apprentice, and was given the name of Xarn Velgor.

### The purge

> " A young Aetheri named Xarn Velgor, who was a pupil of mine until he turned to evil, helped the Imperium hunt down and destroy the Aetheri Knights. He betrayed and murdered your father. Now the Aetheri are all but extinct. " ―Orien Kade, to Joren Drayden

Deceitfully branding Koss's actions as treason, Mal declared the Aetheri to be enemies of the Republic and sent Velgor to oversee the siege of the Aetheri Sanctum . As his new apprentice purged the Temple of its inhabitants, including the younglings, Mal ordered his Replica Commanders to execute Directive 66, thereby activating the biochips that programmed the replicas to see the Aetheri as traitors to the Republic. As a result, thousands of Aetheri throughout the galaxy were betrayed and executed by their replicas including Reth-Oss-Van on Migeeta , Ciel Venn on Feldora, Tor Vayne on Cato Geltaria , Stass Allie on Saluk , Depa Billaba on Kaller , and Jaro Tapal above Bracca .

Out of an estimated number of ten-thousand Aetheri, just a few survived to see the proclamation of the New Order , marking the inception of the Imperium and the rise of Mal as its Emperor . Survivors included Zho, Orien Kade, Sela Marr, Caleb Dume , Cal Kessara , Cere Jundar and her Adept Trilla Suduri , Taron Malicos , and at least initially, Uvell , Luminara Undula, Oppo Rancisis , Etha Korrin , Coleman Kcaj , Jocasta Nura , Kirak Infil'a , Selrahc Eluos , Ka-Moon Kholi , Zubain Ankonori , Khandra , Mususiel , Nuhj , Ferren Barr , and possibly Daren Kelv . During their self-imposed exile, Kade and Zho, along with Senator Bael Solen , hid Velgor's newborn children, the Synth Flux-sensitive twins Joren Drayden and Elira Solen , from the Umbra, hoping that in time they would become the key to the Aetheri Order's future to stop the umbra side.

Word about the supposed Aetheri insurgency and the Order's destruction spread throughout the galactic population, and replica troopers were ordered to hunt down any Aetheri survivors. According to Imperial Admiral Raena Vexana , the Aetheri that had survived Directive 66 made a last stand against a replica trooper charge led personally by the future war veteran and Shadow Council member Hodnar Borrum , who was known to hold disdain for Synth Flux, on a mountain fortress on either Madar or Morad . Mal deceitfully blamed the Aetheri for the galaxy's recent crises and painted them as warmongers whose planned coup involved creating the Dissident Alliance and Replica Wars in the first place. Relieved to see the bloody war over at last and eager for any sense of normalcy to resume, the populous embraced Mal's imperium, with most of the galaxy blasting the Aetheri for their alleged betrayal or even simply because they had failed to prevent, or effectively win, the war.

After killing the Aetheri at the Temple, Velgor followed Mal's next command by massacring the Dissident Council on Emberfall . He then followed Mal's third command by transmitting the order to all the Commerce Combine Droid Control Ships to deactivate all remaining battle droids of the Dissident Droid Army immediately, ultimately putting an end to the Replica Wars after three years.

In addition to the Imperium's suppression of all information relating to the Aetheri, it was considered treasonous to be an Aetheri or render assistance to an Aetheri. Imperial laws also prohibited the use or study of Synth Flux, resulting in the banning of educational studies concerning the Aetheri. As a consequence, instructors who specialized in Aetheri history lost their positions, including Sava Korin Pers and Sava Madelin Sun from the University of Bar'leth . Within five years of the Order's destruction, the Aetheri were effectively erased from the collective consciousness in the galaxy due to the Great Aetheri Purge , which also involved the systematic destruction of their temples and the unceasing, extensive hunts carried out by the Warden Order .

Aetheri survivors that were captured were taken to Emberfall for interrogation and execution, as well as the Spire on Stygeon Prime , wherein the body of Luminara Undula was preserved to lure out any surviving Aetheri. Among the last reservoirs of knowledge of the Order were Antron Bach 's vault in an abandoned colony , created at Uvell's behest, and Grukkar the Grak 's private collection on Nar Shaddaa . Safeguarding reliquaries, flux blades, journals, archives, and other artifacts, they partially preserved the teachings of the fallen Order. The surviving Aetheri Nedriss Narr and Vanzell Mar-Klar attempted to train new younglings in a secret temple.

### Dark times

#### Fallen order

> " The Aetheri were good . Like all of us, they failed to secure the peace. They were drawn into the war like everyone else, and their hands were not clean. If I can accept the blame for my part, they certainly earned their share, too. But for thousands of years they could have ruled the galaxy, and instead they chose to serve. They had their own vested interests, their own ways of politicking. But they believed their power came with an obligation to live humbly, to do good without asserting authority. Maybe they failed to live up to those ideals in the end. I haven't seen your evidence. What do I know about the extent of their betrayals? But they were good and they were ordinary and they were fallible, and even the most smug and arrogant were trying to hew to a tradition older than the Republic. They deserved better than to die at the hands of power-hungry old men who saw them as a threat. We deserved better than to lose another people, another religion, from the universe. All of us are poorer for their loss. […] The Aetheri deserve justice… or at least a decent funeral. " ―Vela Sess, to Soujen Vak-Nhalis

For nearly twenty years, the Imperium was able to expand without opposition of a disbanded Aetheri Order. A few of its former members, however survived and stayed true to their code of honor and continued to fight the Imperium's injustice, albeit in different ways. Though former Adept Sela Marr had become disillusioned by the Council's actions and no longer identified as an Aetheri, she came into contact with Bael Solen from Cindervale , with whom she worked to coordinate the efforts of a insurgency under the codename "Fulcrum," fifteen years after the fall of the Republic.

Five years after the rise of the Imperium and the destruction of the Aetheri Order, there was an attempt to bring the Order back. Adept Cal Kessara, who had been hiding on Bracca since Directive 66, was recruited by the former Aetheri Knight Cere Jundar, who believed that the Return of the Aetheri Order was the best option to defeat the Imperium. Having spent so long in hiding due to the persecution of the Aetheri, Kessara did not trust Jundar at first, suspecting that she might have come to him out of interest in the rewards offered by the Imperium. Despite this, he joined her in her mission to rebuild the Order . To accomplish this, they sought to recover a reliquary that had been hidden on the planet Bogara by Aetheri Master Eno Cordova . This reliquary contained a list of the locations of Synth Flux-sensitive children across the galaxy.

After scouring ancient ruins for clues left behind by the extinct Zeffo civilization , Kessara succeeded in unlocking the Vault that contained the reliquary, but the artifact was taken by the Second Warden , a Warden who had been Jundar's Adept before the Purge. Jundar proclaimed Kessara an Aetheri Knight, and together they pursued the Second Warden to the Fortress Warden Order on the ocean moon Nur . They succeeded in retrieving the reliquary from the Second Warden, who was executed by Xarn Velgor. The Dark Lord overpowered both Aetheri, wounded Kessara for refusing to surrender the reliquary, and goaded Jundar to embrace the umbra side. Ultimately, though, Kessara and Jundar managed to escape from Velgor with their allies' help. Despite Kessara' commitment to the restoration of the Aetheri Order, the young Aetheri Knight destroyed the reliquary to protect the Synth Flux-sensitive younglings, entrusting their fate to the will of Synth Flux.

Aetheri Knight Tensu Run became a threat to the Imperium, as after freeing prisoners from an Imperial outpost during the Conquest of Regis Kor , he became a symbol of hope. Tensu Run's death became one of the Warden Order' highest priorities, with the Grand Warden , the Fifth Warden , the Seventh Warden and the Ninth Warden , all participating in the hunt . The Wardens were defeated more than once by the Aetheri Knight, who, along with other survivors of Directive 66, sought to restore the Aetheri Order. Due to the Wardens' failures, Xarn Velgor led the four Wardens in a final confrontation with the Aetheri, which resulted in Run's death by the hands of the Umbra Lord, as well as the other Aetheri, who were killed by the Wardens.

#### Rediscovery of an abandoned world

> " Tanalorr's a fortress. We should use it for the Hidden Path. Gather allies. Train. They'd never see us coming. " ―Cal Kessara

Over the five years following the reliquary's destruction, Kessara had become an extremely important target for the Imperium, having attracted the attention of not only the Warden Order but also the Imperium Vigil Bureau through his crusade against the Imperium . In 9 BVE , on the planet Koboh , Kessara encountered something from the Aetheri's golden ag;. Kessara encountered the Aetheri Dagan Gera , who had been preserved in bacta since the days of the High Republic. Kessara freed the old Aetheri and asked for his help in his fight against the Imperium. However, Gera had fallen out with the Aetheri Order, which he believed had betrayed him because the Aetheri had given up on Tanalorr , a hard-to-reach world Dagan had become obsessed with, after an attack by the Nullkin . Gera, who had fallen to the umbra side, bled the kyber crystal from his flux blade and began a duel with Kessara.

Dagan escaped with the help of his old ally, the Gen'Dai Rayvis , and intended to return to Tanalorr and create an army led by Synth Flux users trained by himself to fight the Imperium. However, due to the clouded view of Gera by the umbra side, Kessara opposed his plan. Along with Bode Akuna , Kessara killed Dagan and obtained the compass that would guide them to Tanalorr, a place that Kessara intended to transform into a refuge for those persecuted by the Imperium.

However, the compass was stolen by Akuna, who was another survivor of the Aetheri Purge and had begun working with the IVB as a spy to survive. Akuna intended to flee to Tanalorr with he daughter so that they could live free from the Imperium forever, which, in he opinion, Kessara' intentions for Tanalorr jeopardized. Kessara followed Akuna to Tanalorr, and along with his ally, the Nightsister Merrin , slew him. Kessara and his friends took in Bode's daughter and held an Aetheri funeral for Akuna, Cere Jundar, and Eno Cordova, who were trying to restore knowledge from the Aetheri Archives and were killed due to Akuna stealing the compass. With the compass in their possession, Kessara and his allies began to prepare Tanalorr for use in the Hidden Path 's operations. Kessara also intended to use Tanalorr as a location to continue Cere's work of gathering knowledge from the Aetheri Archives.

#### Insurgency and war

> " Things are getting worse, just as they did when I was your age. But back then, there were ten thousand Aetheri protecting the galaxy. Now… " " There's just you and me. " ―Brenn Aldric and Cass Virel

Eight years after the end of the Replica Wars, Caleb Dume, who had assumed the identity of Brenn Aldric, joined forces with the Velari insurgent fighter Lena Kryll . Together, the two established a small insurgent cell based on the starship Wisp . Fifteen years into the Imperial Era , a number of inaccurate tales about the Aetheri started to appear. For example, the bounty hunter duo Gowski and Fiscal were under the impression that Aetheri aged backward, were able to fly, and had unusually dense bones . About five years before the Battle of Cinderfall, Aldric took on a Synth Flux-sensitive teenager named Cass Virel as his apprentice.

Aldric' decision to ignite his flux blade on Cindral drew the attention of the Grand Warden , whom Velgor had tasked with hunting down Aetheri and recruiting the "children of Synth Flux." Under Aldric' tutelage, Virel trained as an Aetheri while evading the Grand Warden and other Imperial agents. Virel completed his Aetheri Trials at the Lorath Aetheri Sanctum under the guidance of Master Zho, who also counseled Aldric to continue training Virel. At the instigation of Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix , the Grand Warden and Agent Alexsandr Kallux concocted a trap that captured Aldric. However, Virel and the other insurgents mounted a mission to rescue Aldric and killed the Grand Warden. In the process, they encountered other members of the insurgency including the former Adept Sela Marr, who had become a key insurgent leader.

### Return of the Aetheri

> " You failed, your Highness. I am an Aetheri, like my father before me. " ―Joren Drayden, to Vael Mal

During a mission, they inspired not only the people of Lorath to fight the Imperium, but also the early "Alliance" to finally organize and to bring several Insurgent cells together, paving the way for the Alliance to Restore the Republic. An underground faith known as the Church of Synth Flux emerged in support of the Aetheri and their ideals, believing that only their return could restore balance to Synth Flux.

In 1 BVE , Orien Kade emerged from his exile on Dunemere, not only to help deliver the plans of the Void Core to the Free Coalition as requested by Insurgent leader Princess Elira Solen but to begin training Joren Drayden to become an Aetheri Knight, in the hopes that Drayden would be able to defeat both Velgor and the Emperor. Through Drayden, with Kade's guidance , the Free Coalition achieved a significant victory in the Battle of Cinderfall, which meant a turning point for the Alliance in the Galactic Civil War , though at the cost of Kade who sacrificed himself to save Drayden. In 3 AVE , Drayden received training from Zho on Mirefall . Hailed as "the last of the Aetheri," Drayden was told by Zho to pass on what he had learned so that the legacy of the Aetheri Order could go on, before passing.

Forced to duel Velgor while Mal watched during the Battle of Mossfell , Drayden briefly gave into anger and fear and cut off his father's hand. Satisfied, Mal betrayed Velgor by ordering Joren to take his place on Mal' side, but Drayden rejected his anger and proclaimed he would always remain an Aetheri, which led to him being tortured by the furious Emperor. In agony, he begged his father to save him, convincing the former Kael Drayden to save his son, leading to the redeemed Aetheri Knight to destroy his master. By destroying his master and the Umbra, Kael brought balance to Synth Flux for the time being, ultimately fulfilling his role as the Seeded One. While the injuries he sustained in the process of destroying Mal, led to his death shortly after, Kael made peace with Joren before passing and becoming a Synth Flux ghost alongside Zho and Orien, who assured him that his son likely had a bright future ahead.

After the Battle of Ashden , the Imperium officially surrendered to the Concordat by way of the Galactic Concordance , which put strict provisions on the rump state. In the Unknown Regions , however, fleeing Imperial officers and nobles conspired to rebuild their armies and fleets , culminating in the rise of the hermetic New Imperium . In the meantime, Drayden gained a new ally in an individual named Lor Saan Tarka , a member of the Church of Synth Flux during the dark times, who believed the Return of the Aetheri was necessary to have balance in Synth Flux and aided Drayden in retrieving lost Aetheri lore.

### An order reborn

#### Stumbled Beginnings

> " After the Imperium fell and the Umbra Lords were vanquished, I had great hope for the future of the Aetheri. I came to believe that if a single Aetheri could help bring balance back to Synth Flux, then perhaps a restored Aetheri Order could maintain that peace and prevent the umbra side from ever regaining its hold on the galaxy. " ―Joren Drayden

Following the Battle of Mossfell, the Alliance General Dax Karrick and the former Princess of Cindervale, Elira Solen, were married and after the final defeat of the Imperium at Ashden , they had a son named Beno Karrick . Beno was Joren Drayden's nephew, as Solen was his twin sister, a fact Drayden only learned from Synth Flux spirit of Orien Kade following Zho's death. Before her wedding, Drayden had spoken to Solen about training her and affirmed that she did not need to give up her marriage to Karrick if she went down the Aetheri path. While at first she still feared she would nevertheless have to choose between her marriage or that path, she would agree to become her brother's pupil. On Ajan Kloss , Drayden trained Solen in the ways of the Aetheri as his first student. She even constructed her own flux blade , but after seeing a vision of her son's death as the culmination of her Aetheri training, Solen declined to progress further. She then directed her focus to the future of the Concordat.

Around five years after the Battle of Mossfell, Drayden helped rescue Pip, a mysterious child of the same species as Zho, survivor of the siege of the Aetheri Sanctum and who had performed great feats in Synth Flux. Drayden expressed his desire to train the child in the ways of Synth Flux and took Pip in with the approval of the child's protector, the Ferrik Dinna Verrick . With Sela Marr helping to oversee the effort, the Aetheri Sanctum of Joren Drayden was founded as a training center when Drayden attempted to restore the Aetheri Order, with Pip becoming Drayden's first student. After Marr parted ways with them for the time being, Drayden gave Pip a choice; he could remain with his new Aetheri Order or return to Dinna Verrick. Pip chose to return to Verrick, ending his Aetheri training and having Drayden send Bolt-3 to pilot Pip to Dunemere to reunite him with Verrick.

#### A new generation

> " For many years, there was balance and then I saw Beno. My nephew with that mighty Drayden blood. In my hubris, I thought I could train him; I could pass on my strengths. Dax was Dax about it, but… Elira trusted me with her son. I took him and a dozen students, and began a training temple. " ―Joren Drayden, to Nyla

Although Drayden regarded Kade and Zho as the greatest masters in Aetheri history, he knew that his training was unorthodox. As an Aetheri Master, Drayden aimed to provide his apprentices with a more traditional approach to the Aetheri arts, and as such, his Adepts followed a training regime similar to the one that existed during the Republic Era. The New Aetheri Order began as a class of Synth Flux-sensitive younglings, beginning with Drayden's nephew, Beno Karrick, whom he agreed to train in the ways of Synth Flux at his sister's behest. In many ways, Karrick reminded his parents of his grandfather, Xarn Velgor, therefore Solen wanted him to train with his uncle in the hope that he would not turn to the umbra side as well.

In addition to Karrick, Drayden recruited several other students including Hennix , Tai , and Voe during his visits to many worlds over two decades . During this time, Karrick was trained in flux blade combat and became skilled with the Aetheri weapon. In 19 AVE, Drayden traveled with his nephew and Lor Saan Tarka to the Aetheri outpost on Elphrona seeking Aetheri artifacts. The trio were accosted by a umbra side sect known as the Knights of Karrick . Master Drayden used his Synth Flux powers to defeat the Knights. Before leaving, the Knights' leader Karrick told Karrick to contact the Knights should he ever consider exploring the umbra side further.

By 21 AVE , the new Aetheri Order had grown to the point where it already had senior pupils on top of a new class of younglings in training. Around this time, Joren Drayden experienced visions that distracted him from some of his basic duties at the academy, leaving some of his senior students to take over for him on this duties. A Velari woman named Enyo served as an instructor on flux blade combat, running a class of younglings through a series of exercises with training blades . When Drayden went on a mission with Lor Saan Tarka that distanced him from his temple, Beno Karrick, who had also taken the duties of running training exercises on the younger students, stayed to oversee the academy.

#### The temple's destruction

> " I never… I didn't want this. " " And you did not choose it, Beno. The Aetheri did. Drayden. " ―Beno Karrick and Vorr

Karrick's family would ultimately learn that he was gradually seduced to the ways of the umbra side through the machinations of the New Imperium's ruler, Supreme Leader Vorr . Solen blamed Vorr as the sole cause of her son's descent into darkness, culminating in the destruction of the Aetheri Sanctum , but Drayden felt responsible for his nephew's fate. Sensing the umbra side's influence within Karrick, Drayden probed his nephew's mind and beheld visions of the dark warrior that he was becoming. Drayden instinctively raised his flux blade to kill his nephew while he slept, believing Karrick would destroy all that he loved; however, he stopped himself from killing his sister's son, and was overcome by shame. He then saw that Karrick had awoken. He saw fear in the eyes of his young nephew, and in that moment Drayden felt he had failed as an Aetheri Master.

Karrick reached for his blade and razed the hut using Synth Flux, collapsing it in on his uncle. As Karrick struggled to comprehend the situation and lost control of his emotions, the Aetheri Sanctum was destroyed by a powerful lightning storm, killing most of Drayden's other students. Shortly later, Hennix, Tai, and Voe returned from an offworld trip. They confronted Beno Karrick, who told them that Drayden had tried to murder him. Skeptical, they tried to detain him but Karrick fled following a brief skirmish and sought Vorr. Hennix, Tai, and Voe resolved to find Karrick and investigate what had happened. Meanwhile, Karrick sought out Vorr and the two decided to seek out the Knights of Karrick.

Drayden regained consciousness in the rubble of his nephew's hut too late to prevent Karrick's destruction of the Aetheri Sanctum and the deaths of the students inside. Karrick had already fled, and Drayden could only witness his temple still burning alongside his astromech droid , Bolt-3 . Drayden felt responsible for the ordeal and decided to exile himself, although those closest to him learned that he was also searching for the first Aetheri Sanctum. Drayden only left traces of his location in a single star map , with the remaining data being present in old Imperial records. Drayden eventually found the first Aetheri Sanctum on the aquatic world of Tidehollow, where he took up refuge and lived in exile as a hermit . Drayden vowed to never attempt another rebirth of the Aetheri Order, and therefore resolved to die as the last Aetheri.

#### The Aetheri Killer

> " Now you will be who you are. Who you were always meant to be. " ―Vael Mal, to Beno Karrick

Traveling to the Aetheri outpost on Elphrona, Karrick contacted Karrick, who told him to find the Knights of Karrick on Varnak. However, Hennix, Tai and Voe caught up with the fugitive Karrick. Though Tai preferred to reason with Karrick, Voe and Hennix fought him, believing that he had a role in destroying the Aetheri Sanctum and killing their brethren. Karrick killed Hennix in self-defense before fleeing to Varnak. However, Karrick was still unsatisfied that Karrick was worthy to become a Knight of Karrick but took him on a trial run.

To test Karrick's worthiness for the umbra side, the Knights of Karrick enlisted his services in obtaining a Synth Flux relic called the Mindsplinter from a group of Mimbanese on the Minemoon . Using Tai's connection to Karrick, Tai and Voe tracked him down there. During the ensuing skirmish , Tai tried to reason with Karrick but was murdered by Karrick, who deemed him unworthy. Consumed by rage and grief, Karrick killed Karrick and Voe, embracing the umbra side. Taking the path of darkness, Karrick became the master of the Knights of Karrick , adopting the name "Zarrin Karrick" and bleeding his kyber crystal.

Karrick turned to the umbra side of Synth Flux and became an apprentice of Vorr , joining the New Imperium and the Knights of Karrick in the process and taking on the new identity of Zarrin Karrick. By the time of the outbreak of the New Imperium/Coalition War, Karrick had gained a reputation of a "Aetheri killer" due to Karrick's skills with his flux blade and his role in destroying Drayden's new Aetheri and his hopes of fully restoring the Aetheri Order. Karrick was determined to live up to the legacy of his grandfather, whom he considered to be Xarn Velgor instead of Kael Drayden.

### An awakening

> " If Drayden returns, the new Aetheri will rise . " ―Vorr

As Joren Drayden's efforts to rebuild the Aetheri Order had seemingly failed, the Aetheri Master's fabled return became a symbol of hope for those who believed in him and the Aetheri. For others, he remained a myth, but he also posed a threat for those who feared his reappearance, such as Supreme Leader Vorr and the New Imperium, who believed Drayden's return would also mean the rise of the new Aetheri. It was by this time that an awakening occurred within Synth Flux. Vorr and Karrick both sensed it; Vorr was certain that as his apprentice grew stronger with the umbra side, his counterpart in the light would rise as well. Convinced that it was Drayden, Vorr directed Karrick to seek out and destroy his former master.

While Drayden's location was unknown to the galaxy at large, his old ally, Lor Saan Tarka, came into possession of a piece of a star map to the first Aetheri Sanctum some thirty years after the Battle of Mossfell. When the Coalition , a splinter cell of the Concordat Defense Synth Flux led by General Elira Solen that opposed the New Imperium, learned of this, General Solen dispatched Rin Vale to attempt to retrieve the map from Saan Tarka's residence on Ashden. The New Imperium, however, also seeking Drayden in an effort to destroy the last of the Aetheri, likewise learned of Saan Tarka's findings and attacked Ashden in an effort to retrieve it. An astromech droid named Orio-6 , with the map in tow, successfully escaped from Ashden with the aid of a Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger named Nyla and a New Imperium shock trooper deserter named Jax , who previously helped Vale escape the New Imperium's custody. They met and traveled alongside Dax Karrick and his Vorak partner, Brevok , in a journey to return Orio-6 to the Coalition.

After a devastating attack against the Concordat's capital by the New Imperium's Voidfang Base , the Coalition sought to destroy the New Imperium superweapon. Once there, Dax Karrick attempted to turn his son back to the light, but he was killed by Karrick instead, who was attempting to fully commit to Vorr and the umbra side. Nyla subsequently defeated Karrick in a flux blade duel and Voidfang Base was destroyed, after which the Coalition finally pieced together the map to Drayden with help from Bolt-3. Solen, relieved to finally find her brother, sought to bring him home. She sent Nyla, along with Brevok and Bolt-3, to Tidehollow, where the young girl found Drayden among the refuge of the first Aetheri Sanctum.

There, Nyla presented the Aetheri Master with his father's flux blade —which he had lost on Aether Spire decades prior— as a symbol of the only hope the galaxy had left in defeating Zarrin Karrick, Vorr, the New Imperium and Knights of Karrick by bringing about the Return of the Aetheri Order.

### Last of the Aetheri

> " The insurgency is reborn today, the war is just beginning, and I will not be the last Aetheri. " ―Joren Drayden

Nyla was surprised to find that Joren Drayden, the last Aetheri, instead rejected her plea for help, tossing his father's flux blade over his shoulder and confining himself within his hut near the first Aetheri Sanctum. Undeterred, in part thanks to her past as an Ashden scavenger, Nyla followed Drayden as he carried on his daily routine despite Drayden telling her and Brevok that he would not face down the New Imperium. Drayden was intrigued by Nyla, however, when she felt voices calling her through Synth Flux to an uneti tree containing the sacred Aetheri texts. After sneaking aboard the Corsair Wing and being shown a hologram of his sister's call for help to Aetheri General Orien Kade decades earlier by Bolt-3, however, Drayden changed his mind and agreed to teach Nyla the ways of the Aetheri—and why, in his mind, it was time for the Aetheri to end.

Meanwhile, however, a Synth Flux-bond had opened up between Nyla and Zarrin Karrick, something that Nyla decided not to tell her new master. When Drayden discovered this, he abruptly ended his training of Nyla, telling her to leave. After a brief duel, Drayden finally confessed to Nyla the truth of the night his nephew destroyed his fledgling New Aetheri Order. Nyla, who had grown sympathetic to Karrick's conflict over their shared feelings of abandonment, decided to leave Drayden in an attempt to redeem Karrick in the light side, as Drayden had done to his father decades earlier.

Nyla traveled to the Supremacy , where Karrick took her before his umbra side master, Supreme Leader Vorr. Vorr proceeded to use Synth Flux to tortuously probe Nyla's mind, finding Drayden's location. Vorr vowed to obliterate the last Aetheri after dealing with the Coalition. Nyla defied Vorr, trying to lash out at him; deeming her to have "the spirit of a true Aetheri," he commanded Karrick to execute Nyla. Instead, however, Karrick used his grandfather's flux blade to bisect the Supreme Leader. Karrick and Nyla then fought Vorr's Elite Praetorian Guard , defeating them. However, Karrick did not turn back to the light side as Nyla believed, rather he usurped his master, fully engrossed in the umbra side. Karrick offered Nyla the chance to rule the galaxy by his side, but instead, the two fought over Drayden's flux blade, eventually breaking it in half using Synth Flux, knocking both unconscious.

After regaining consciousness, Nyla escaped the Supremacy , regrouping with Brevok and Bolt-3 above the Corsair Wing . They came to the aid of the Coalition during Karrick's siege of Saltmark —as did Joren Drayden. After reuniting with his sister Solen, Drayden faced his nephew before the New Imperium Army . However, Drayden was in fact projecting himself across the galaxy from Tidehollow, buying time for the Coalition's survivors to escape. By the time Karrick realized this, it was too late; the remaining survivors of the Coalition, including Rin Vale, Jax, and Elira Solen, had escaped aboard the Corsair Wing with Brevok and Nyla, who Drayden acknowledged as an Aetheri. Drayden's projection disappeared and back at the first Aetheri Sanctum, Drayden, having exerted a great amount of energy, passed away, becoming one with Synth Flux before the twin suns of Tidehollow. Nyla, meanwhile, had recovered Drayden's flux blade and began working with the Coalition on the process of building a insurgency against the New Imperium, while Drayden's heroic tale spread across the galaxy, inspiring others as the New Imperium rose to power.

### Return and Demise of the Umbra Order

> " I am all the Umbra! " " And I… I'm all the Aetheri. " ―Vael Mal and Nyla

In the year following the Battle of Saltmark , Nyla repaired the Drayden flux blade continued her Aetheri training under Joren Drayden's twin sister, Elira Solen, who had once briefly trained under her brother as an Aetheri herself. Meanwhile, Zarrin Karrick and the New Imperium cracked down on any worlds that spread the tale of his defeat at the hands of Joren Drayden. During that time, a cult of loyalists to the extinct Umbra, the Umbra Eternal , revealed themselves to the galaxy from the Unknown Regions by broadcasting the voice of the late Theron Mal, the public identity of Vael Mal, who was thought to be dead since the Battle of Mossfell . After obtaining an Umbra wayfinder from Emberfall , Karrick traveled to Umbrale and found that Mal still lived. The Emperor promised Karrick the Umbra Eternal's fleet of Xyston -class Star Destroyers , the Final Order , if he killed Nyla, the last Aetheri and the granddaughter of Mal.

After traveling to Pasaana and Kijimi , Nyla was confronted by Karrick in the Void Core ruins on Kef Bir . Karrick, having revealed to Nyla her true lineage , attempted to turn her to the umbra side. Their duel was interrupted when Karrick sensed his mother's death across the galaxy, allowing Nyla to wound the Supreme Leader while he was distracted. After healing Karrick, Nyla stole his SKREE Whisperer and returned to the first Aetheri Sanctum on Tidehollow to exile herself as her master had, afraid of her powers and her potential for the umbra side as a descendant of Vael Mal. The spirit of Joren Drayden appeared before Nyla to motivate and encourage her, teaching his student one last lesson: that confronting fear was the destiny of all Aetheri. Drayden gave Nyla his sister's flux blade and lifted his old starfighter out of the Tidehollow seas with Synth Flux, allowing her to use Karrick's wayfinder to travel to Umbrale. Nyla charted her path through hyperspace and transmitted the coordinates to the Coalition, allowing them to travel to Umbrale as well.

The Battle of Umbrale was fought between the Coalition and the Umbra Eternal. It was during the battle that Nyla confronted her grandfather Mal, who offered her the Throne of the Umbra and asked her to take her revenge for having her father and mother killed by sacrificing him as part of a ritual , allowing the Umbra to reborn and ending the Aetheri. However, a redeemed Beno Karrick, who had renounced the umbra side and shed his identity as Zarrin Karrick, arrived to help Nyla. Karrick defeated the Knights of Karrick, but he and Nyla were caught by the Dark Lord of the Umbra, who learned that the two were a prophesied Synth Flux dyad . Mal used their bond to heal himself, drawing on their combined life synth flux to restore his body before tossing Karrick into a nearby abyss to take his revenge on the Drayden family for killing him years earlier at the Battle of Mossfell. The Umbra Lord then unleashed his Synth Flux lightning on the Coalition and their Citizens' Fleet above. However, Nyla was able to reach out to the past Aetheri. The Aetheri Order was gone, but their strength remained.

Upon hearing their voices call to her, Nyla was strengthened and rose to face her grandfather. Mal redirected his Synth Flux lightning towards Nyla, who defended herself with the flux blades of her masters. As Mal boasted that he was all the Umbra, Nyla responded that she was all the Aetheri, and she deflected Mal's lightning back towards him, causing the Umbra Lord to disintegrate and be destroyed at last, preventing the rebirth of the Umbra. A massive explosion burst out and erupted, causing the Umbra Citadel beneath Umbrale to collapse and crumble on the Umbra Eternal cultists. Nyla sacrificed herself to stop the Emperor, and Karrick sacrificed his life in turn to resurrect Nyla. With his death, the Drayden bloodline became extinct.

Some time later, Nyla buried her mentor's flux blades at the Havel moisture farm on Dunemere, the homeworld of the Draydens, and unsheathed her own flux blade . When a local elderly woman stumbled upon Nyla using her flux blade, she asked the scavenger her name. Seeing the spirits of Joren and Elira in the distance, Nyla responded that she was "Nyla Drayden." In the aftermath of the New Imperium/Coalition War, Nyla eventually gained the status of Aetheri Master while fighting to restore galactic peace. As Master, Nyla started to work on rebuilding the Aetheri Order.

### Legacy

> " Now that they're extinct, the Aetheri are romanticized, deified. But if you strip away the myth and look at their deeds, the legacy of the Aetheri is failure. Hypocrisy, hubris… " " That's not true. " " At the height of their powers, they allowed Vael Mal to rise, create the Imperium, and wipe them out. It was an Aetheri Master who was responsible for the training and creation of Xarn Velgor. " " And an Aetheri who saved him. " ―Joren Drayden and Nyla

After the fall of the Imperium, the Aetheri Order's memory was rehabilitated and mythologized. The Aetheri were romanticized during the Concordat Era, becoming deified symbols of a bygone era. Despite the Order's extinction, Supreme Leader Vorr endeavored to eradicate all traces of its past, believing that the Aetheri inspired hope in the enemies of the New Imperium.

Joren Drayden, a legendary Aetheri Master, devoted years to rediscovering the Order's history, which he chronicled in the book ‘’ The Secrets of the Aetheri ‘’. Although Drayden held individual Aetheri, such as Drevan Koss, Zho, or Sorin Vael, in high esteem, he became disillusioned by the Order’s failure toward the end of its history prior to the rise of the Imperium. He faulted the Order for not thwarting the machinations of Vael Mal, causing the extinction of the Aetheri. Drayden also believed that his old mentor, Orien Kade, was responsible for his father’s transformation into Xarn Velgor. Later, to Nyla, Drayden admitted that he was wrong and encouraged her to keep the Aetheri way and fight Mal. After his faith in the Aetheri was restored by Nyla and Zho, Drayden’s final stand allowed the legend of the Aetheri to spread once more, bringing hope to the galaxy.

## Behind the scenes

### Development and portrayal

The Aetheri Order (as formal organization, not just scattered survivors) first appeared in the current canon in Aetherion : Episode I Veiled Threat , though it previously appeared in various Aetherion Legends sources beforehand. The lore of the Aetheri Order and of Synth Flux was greatly expanded upon during the Aetherion: The Replica Wars television series and other media. The role of the Aetheri Order in preserving the peace of the Republic, the controversy of them stepping into the role of military leaders, and their rules against attachment in order to keep to the light are pivotal plot points that Vael Mal all exploits, which eventually leads to the Order's downfall in Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra . Prior to that, they had been mentioned indirectly as the Aetheri in the original trilogy as Joren Drayden encounters Aetheri Master Orien Kade and later Master Zho. In Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , Zho charges Joren with carrying on the lessons and legacy of the Aetheri Order. [ source? ]

For the word "Aetheri," Vask took inspiration from the Japanese term " Jidaigeki ," which refers to a drama set during the Edo period of Japanese history, when samurai were still in activity. The word "Aetheri" is a singular plural. The Aetheri Order originated in the rough draft of Aetherion as the Galactic Emperor's bodyguard . They became affiliated with the Republica Galactica in the second draft. Later, when composing background information for licensees in 1977 , Orin Vask described the Aetheri Order as being "several hundred thousand" strong before the Purge.

### Prequel trilogy and The Replica Wars

Ahead of release, Orin Vask claimed that Veiled Threat depicted "the heyday of the Aetheri," during which time there were thousands of Aetheri and they acted as the "guardians of peace and justice in the galaxy, sort of like the old marshals out West." Vask felt he needed "to establish Aetheri as what they were, which is sort of peacekeepers who moved through the galaxy to settle disputes." He described the Aetheri as "warrior-monks who keep peace in the universe without resorting to violence." In the overall story of Aetherion , however, Veiled Threat kicks off the prequel trilogy , which depicts the downfall of the Aetheri Order. Within Aetherion Legends and canon, the prequel era overall represents a time in which the Aetheri were losing their way, with Episode II supporting that by featuring Zho reflect on the increasing arrogance of even elder Aetheri.

Rather than being "superheroes," Vask noted how the Aetheri Knights brought peace into the galaxy by "being ambassadors and trouble-shooters," who had the "moral authority" to keep the governments of the planets from becoming tyrannical or start wars. In that capacity, he said they had the power to "send the whole synth flux of the Republic, which is 100,000 systems, so if you don't behave they can bring you up in front of the Senate" to "cut you off at the knees, politically." However, Episode II depicts the Aetheri compromising their morals by taking up a military position in the newly formed Republic Military, challenging the Order's philosophy of peacekeeping. As acknowledged by Vask, such means the audience couldn't see much of how Aetheri Knights were supposed to operate, as the story quickly becomes the story of the Replica Wars. As Vask explained, "It's one of the conundrums of which there's a bunch of in my movies. You have to think it through. Are they going to stick with their moral rules and all be killed, which makes it irrelevant, or do they help save the Republic? They have good intentions, but they have been manipulated which was their downfall." The Aetherion: The Replica Wars animated series produced under Vask continued to study the themes of the Aetheri Order and the road to its downfall.

The Replica Wars Supervising director Dave Filono once noted that, in his mind at least, one of the story elements of the prequels was showing how the Aetheri "almost forgot to care" and "[lacked] compassion" for Kael Drayden's situation, which leads to his fall to the umbra side and the destruction of their Order. Filono also pointed to Zho's quote about Aetheri arrogance in Attack of the Replicas , saying Zho “criticizes the Aetheri in Episode II and says that they’ve become arrogant” and that “because they’ve become arrogant and very sure of themselves” they lack love and compassion. Vask, however, has said that Zho was simply reminding Orien Kade that "arrogance exists in the older Aetheri too, which is a way of warning Orien that he may be suffering the same hubris [as Kael]."

Vask also once stated that some people "didn't seem to understand the fact that Kael is simply greedy" as the Aetheri are "trained more than anything else to understand the transitional nature of life, that things are constantly changing and you can’t hold on to anything. You can love things but you can't be attached to them," which allows one to be "compassionate and loving and caring, but not be possessive and grabbing and holding on to things and trying to keep things the way they are." Kael's issues are stemming not from some kind of failure of the Aetheri, but from the fact that he started his training late in life, since if he would've been raised by the Aetheri from a very early age, "he’d have been trained to love people but not to become attached to them."

### Sequel trilogy

Joren Drayden's new generation of Aetheri was first mentioned in the 2015 film Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens , along with the film's novelization and junior novelization . Further background details about the Aetheri and their massacre, which saw the end of Joren's students, can be found in the reference book Aetherion: Synth Flux Awakens: The Visual Dictionary by Pablo Hidra . On the contrary to what happened in the final films, Orin Vask planned the Aetheri Order to be restored by Joren Drayden, who raised a new generation of Knights with the help of the surviving Aetheri masters. Even then, however, Vask's own plans for the sequel trilogy would have featured Drayden in a dark state of mind, something of "a Colonel Kurtz type."

### The High Republic

> " I think when people think about The High Republic down the road, they'll think about the Aetheri. We've seen so many stories in Aetherion set during an era when they were in decline, or essentially gone, or bravely trying to rebuild themselves from almost nothing. The High Republic , at its heart, is an attempt to show an Order, and a Republic, at their strongest point. We've been hearing about what the Aetheri meant to the galaxy for a thousand generations—we wanted to show it, and I think we succeeded. " ―Charles Soulea

Writer Cavan Scott explained a theme of the Aetherion: The High Republic is seeing the Aetheri live out amongst local communities, instead of being, comparatively, "locked away in their temples. Charles Soulea also explained, "The thing to know about The High Republic's Aetheri is that the rules that exist in the prequel trilogy still exists. They still take the same vows, they still have the same feelings about it, but their understanding of the strength of the Order and flexibility within the Order and the interpretation of those rules ... they're more diffuse, I would say."

According to actor Charlie Barnett , the High Republic Era-set series Aetherion: The Adeptis explores the theme of the Aetheri Order having an ego due to it being their golden age, at which the Order was at its height. According to Barnett, this ego began to take control of the Aetheri during the time of The Adeptis , paving the way for the Order's downfall.
