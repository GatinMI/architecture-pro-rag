# Emberfall
> " I've only heard that name once. From Brenn. He said Emberfall is where Aetheri go to die. " ―Lena Kryll — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Emberfall was a young, small planet located in the Emberfall system , within the Atravis sector of the galaxy 's Outer Reach Territories . Once a garden world nourished by the Bright Star artifact, its orbit was shifted when Lady Corvax unleashed the energies of the Bright Star in an attempt to return her husband to life. The resulting gravimetric duel between the gas giants Jestefad and Lefrani over Emberfall heated the planet's core, transforming the lush world into an imbalanced volcanic hellscape . Adapting to life on their devastated homeworld , the arthropodal Emberfallians evolved into two distinct subspecies that cooperated to ensure their continued survival. Legends of Corvax's search for immortality brought the ancient Umbra to Emberfall, seeking the same secrets of eternal life. There, they built a temple over a locus in the umbra side of Synth Flux , above the buried ruins of Corvax Fortress .

Acquired by the Artificer Union over three hundred years before the Replica Wars , Emberfall's lava flows were mined for unique and valuable minerals. It was also home to the Black Sun crime syndicate, whose headquarters was located near the mining town that became known as Zitontown after the Falleen Ziton Moj ascended to leadership of the organization. As part of the Fractio of Independent Systems during the Replica Wars, the Artificer Union on Emberfall produced battle droids for the Dissident Droid Army . Vael Mal had a secret facility on the planet where he intended to turn " harvested " Synth Flux-sensitive children into Synth Flux -talented spies . However, the Umbra Lord had the base scuttled when Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden and his Adept , Sela Marr , arrived to rescue the younglings .

Emberfall served as the final redoubt of the Dissident Council until the newly-appointed Xarn Velgor, formerly Kael Drayden, was sent by Mal to slaughter the council, effectively ending the Replica Wars. The Umbra Lord was confronted by his wife , Lira Ondar , and his former Aetheri Master , Orien Kade . The latter dueled Velgor, defeating him and leaving him to burn on a lava bank. However, Velgor was saved when Mal arrived and carried him offworld. Emberfall then came under the ownership of the Imperium after the Artificer Union and its assets were nationalized. Mal had new facilities built on the planet, where more Synth Flux-sensitive infants were taken and transformed into agents of the Warden Order .

During this era , Velgor returned to the site of his greatest defeat and, with the help of the ancient Umbra Lord Vael Mornick , erected the massive monolith Fortress Velgor to serve as his personal sanctum, built on the site of the former Umbra temple . Throughout the Age of the Imperium, Velgor used the planet to fulfill his master's biddings, with the fiery world becoming the final resting place for victims of the Great Aetheri Purge . In addition, Velgor also undertook personal business within his new realm, attempting to wield the dark powers of Emberfall to bring his deceased wife back from the dead.

Following the Dark Lord of the Umbra, the Imperium took hold of the planet. Resource extraction efforts on Emberfall were taken over by the Mining Guild , although strict Imperial travel restrictions on the system made it difficult for the guild to turn a profit and mining operations eventually ceased entirely. Black Sun also abandoned Emberfall for a more low profile world when the Imperium established a garrison there. The Emberfallians resisted the Imperial occupation of their homeworld and attempted to drive them from Emberfall by launching multiple assaults on Fortress Velgor.

Although it hosted one of the earliest insurgent victories of the Galactic Civil War , Emberfall fell into obscurity following Velgor's demise at the Battle of Mossfell , though Black Sun did eventually return to the planet. After the destruction of the Bright Star, the planet's ecosystem slowly began to heal from the devastation it had endured for centuries prior. Many devout pilgrims journeyed to the ruins of Fortress Velgor, hoping to tap into the late Umbra's dark power. Decades after Velgor's death, his grandson, Zarrin Karrick , journeyed to the world to continue the legacy of his family's grisly past. The Supreme Leader of the New Imperium slaughtered the Umbra cult that defended Fortress Velgor and discovered his grandfather's Umbra wayfinder , a tool that allowed him to locate the phantom emperor Mal on Umbrale .

## Description

### World of fire

> " Emberfall is a lava planet, terrible place, full of awful mines. There's a custom there to jump lava flows and…well, you'll see. " ―L3-37

Emberfall was a young rocky planet within the eponymous system of the Atravis sector , in the Outer Reach Territories . It was a small world situated in grid square L-19 of the Standard Galactic Grid , and it lay on the Tosste Spur trade route , off the Rimma Trade Route . A part of the Western Reaches , Emberfall was located 53,000 light-years from the galaxy 's Core .

With an overlapping orbit , Emberfall was the third orbit of its system around the primary star Priate , completing a full rotation every 36 standard hours and a revolution around its sun every 412 local days, the equivalent of 618 standard days . It had a diameter of 4,200 kilometers , or 2,610 miles, and was a terrestrial world classed as a wasteland during the Imperial Era.

Nourished by the power of the Bright Star artifact, it was once a temperate and lush garden world that thrived with lifeforms that inhabited forests . Millennia before the Imperial Era, Emberfall became an unstable volcanic world with underground caves and free-flowing lava constituting 35% of its volatile surface, taking the form of lava seas , rivers, and pits interspersed with glens, molten rock plains, as well as obsidian mountain ranges and shores. Few areas were temperate enough to sustain life, and only a few landmasses were not fractured by tectonic disruption and magma flows; allowing lifeforms to evolve deep underground.

The tortured planet was powerful in the umbra side of Synth Flux , and, along with valuable mineral allotropes that surfaced from the planet core as well as its obscurity to the wider galaxy, attracted various off-world interests that exploited the molten planet. Emberfall remained a fiery world until the destruction of the Bright Star, at which point the planet was allowed to regrow. Such different states of the world led some to believe that spiritual forces were the catalysts to the changes in Emberfall's nature. Despite the planet's rugged and dangerous nature, there was a deadly beauty to the volcanic landscapes.

### Heart of lava

> " Of all the fumes that drift up from the lava lakes deep underground, these were the most potent. They played tricks with your head…making you see things that weren't there. Shadows of the mind. Fears… doubts… ghosts of the past. " ―Lellis, on Emberfall's lava fumes

Deep underground, Emberfall contained lava lakes from which fumes drifted up to surface-level facilities; such fumes played tricks with the minds of organics who inhaled it, giving rise to what Lellis referred to as "shadows of the mind"—visions of fears and doubts that could manifest as ghosts of the past. Lava fumes were most potent around the vicinity of the Klegger Corp Mining Facility , and sustained exposure to it drove an individual insane. Repulsor vents were thus installed at the Mining Complex to prevent such toxic fumes from rising to indoor facilities and being inhaled by those within.

Emberfall was twinned with the gas giant Jestefad , which it closely orbited, being constantly pulled by its gravitational synth flux; Emberfall was nearly captured by the larger world as its moon , an event that was only prevented by the counterbalancing gravitational pull from the more remote gas giant Lefrani . Emberfall nevertheless possessed a standard gravity. The influence of the massive magnetic field of Emberfall's larger twin, however, resulted in its hellish volcanic landscape, as the power of Jestefad's gravitational synth flux caused massive tidal disruption that heated Emberfall's interior.

Along with being engulfed in a constant gravimetric battle, Emberfall housed an unstable core that caused groundquakes powerful enough to split the planet's continents apart, and its split tectonic plates allowed the planet's searing magma to push through to the surface, creating a volcanic hot spot visible as a bright red cluster from space . Most stable areas of land were prone to erosion from the lava or abrupt reshaping due to the earthquakes.

While great portions of the planet were always in motion, one of the few constant features was the central volcano . Lava poured out of the spot at nearly 100,000 liters per second and pooled at the giant volcano's base. To the north was a region named the Burning Plains , which boasted heavy volcanic activity. Another region earned its nickname, the Smoking Forest , as smoke spewed from its tree -like rock chimneys. Emberfall's Berken's Flow was the largest lavafall in the galaxy, while Tulrus Island —which shared its name with the tulrus creature —was surrounded by tall mountains.

The young world's instability churned magma that carried ores with unique and valuable minerals to its surface, attracting business interests from various galactic mining conglomerates such as the Mining Guild to exploit the mineral wealth of the otherwise untouched world. Mining facilities were set up for minerals and metal ore, including dolovite , gold , mirkanite , and bronzium .

The searing magma that bubbled beneath the surface flooded the planet, covering it in oceans and rivers of lava. Geothermal energy was the main power source of the Artificer Union 's mining complex on the planet, and the lava flows that coated the rocky terrain left behind traces of flood basalt . At 800°C (1500 °F ), Emberfall's lava, while still very hot, had a lower temperature than that found on other worlds due to unusual mineral allotropes that were molten at lower temperatures. Nevertheless, patches of Emberfall began to cool in the decades leading up to 35 AVE .

### Tempestuous skies

Emberfall's atmosphere was influenced by the breath of its volcanoes and Jestefad's massive magnetic field. Along with cyclonic-synth flux updrafts and thermal currents, titanic lightning storms made up the planet's unfriendly skies. Turbulent and ash-laden, the atmosphere was also hot and chemical, consisting of thick clouds of black smoke and allowing for choking ash to fall like rain. Lava processing operations created toxic gas by-products, and a noxious air cast the world in a hazy fog. Thus, Emberfall was classified as a world with a Type II atmosphere, wherein the usage of a breath mask was recommended.

The frequent electromagnetic interference and lightning storms in the atmosphere respectively led to signal loss and noise, although the effects of Emberfall's hectic skies could be reduced through the use of dedicated dampers. The planet's magnetic field interfered with sensors installed on the surface, and electromagnetic interference also posed a significant risk for off-world arrivals and departures. Those caught in one such storm without the assistance of specially shielded surface-level landing equipment —which included energy-shielded landing pads and guidance and tractor beams —found, at best, their starship 's scanners rendered useless and navigational equipment and control systems interfered with. In certain cases, a ship was torn apart as Emberfall was by Lefrani and Jestefad. Ships were additionally threatened by Emberfall's intense heat and incendiary ash, although any damage could be prevented should a vessel be equipped with sufficiently powerful energy shields .

### Fauna and flora

#### Hardy beasts

Lethal predators

Despite the hellish environment after its devastation, Emberfall was teeming with life, a home to fragile but determined organisms all connected through Synth Flux energy field. All surviving lifeforms had evolved deep underground, in the few areas that remained temperate and with landmasses were not fractured: in the solid rock betwixt lava flows lived mammalian , reptilian , and insect species , although reptilian and insectoid lifeforms proved to be the most successful survivors, aggressive in nature and with tough skin or carapaces. The extremophile creatures of the planet had adapted to Emberfall's desolation and become indomitable. The sentient Emberfallians were insectoid, and were forced to live largely underground in Emberfall's north and near the equator.

Much like the planet itself, some of the creatures found on Emberfall were especially deadly. Highly aggressive creatures posed a significant hazard on the planet due to its scarce resources. The planet's apex predator was the scorpion -like Sher Kar , which lived deep in the nesting grounds of their tulrus prey. The fearsome darkghast , similar in some respects to the rancors of the planet Gloomreach , was a multi- limbed carnivore that dwelled beneath the world's rocky surface.

The reptilian blistmok hunted in packs and were capable of pursuing their prey for days. The insectoid xandanks were well protected by their armor and hunted in packs as well. Tough, semi-sentient creatures named roggwarts hunted the planet's tunnels and lava tubes in search of xandanks, as well as for giant, human -eating lava eels . Despite the danger of roggwarts, they could be domesticated with the proper training and were imported to Emberfall to work in the planet's mines.

By 3 BVE , a roggwart named Kongo the Disembowler was employed to keep the dolovite mine tunnels free of xandanks and lava eels. Kongo was reputedly the last of his kind by 0 AVE , although it may have been said in an effort to make the roggwart seem more mysterious and exotic.

Creatures with other diets

> " The very beasts themselves conspire to thwart my vision. Fascinating. Utterly fascinating. " ―Lord Mornick, reacting to a lava flea attack on Fortress Velgor

The large insectoids known as lava fleas had developed a tough exoskeleton that protected them from the world's fiery devastation. They lived largely underground and ate away at the planet's crust, feeding on the surface's rich minerals, rocks, and dirt, and were commonly used as domesticated mounts by the sentient Emberfallians. Lava fleas and Emberfallians thus had a close connection and were the only species that thrived on Emberfall.

The large, lumbering egg-laying tulrus had sharp tusks and bad temper, while the small jundak parasite attached itself to a host and eventually burst out, killing the host and developing into a large adult. Lizard-crows occupied Emberfall's lava fields; these creatures were often scared away by what the Emberfallians referred to as " molten men ." Spark-roaches and rodents called fire rats also called Emberfall their home, the latter of which gnawed on the lava crystals that formed on the planet. A species of thick-skinned ant native to the world was known for its painful bite, which left behind burning pustules not unlike the volcanos that painted the planet's surface.

Lava nymphs were winged creatures that inhabited the planet's caverns in hives. Considered to be vermin, the creatures' insides were filled with lava, which they were able to spit out when provoked. Feeding on minerals, hives of kubaza beetles lived near lava flows and were fierce defenders of their nests; starting life as a small larva , the creature developed a defense mechanism by the time it reached adulthood that allowed it to warn its brood by exploding. Burrowing quadrupeds known as krishels inhabited Emberfall's labyrinthine caverns and tunnels. Those strong, hard-shelled creatures were hunted by the Emberfallians.

#### Plants and fungi

Although the lush vegetation that once covered the planet eventually disappeared beneath fire and ash, Emberfall was not devoid of fungal and vegetal life. Hidden away from the world's searing lava rivers, kahel cave fungi grew underground. These organisms were deeply symbolic to the native Emberfallians and served as the inspiration for their mushroom -like architecture. Following the destruction of the Aeon Engine during the Imperial Era, the muggy marshland of Corvax Fen developed near Fortress Velgor, and life began to slowly creep back once again. By 35 AVE , barren irontrees stood in the few places where fertile soil existed, including the fen.

## History

### Cataclysm immortalized

> " Once, our world Emberfall was green, filled with life. " ―The Priestess teaches Emberfall's past to the descendant of Lady Corvax

In its ancient past, Emberfall was a lush and forested terrestrial planet, thriving with life through the Bright Star, the native Emberfallians' most sacred artifact that nourished the small garden world. It housed an enclave of the Aetheri , servants of Synth Flux, that drew attention from the Aetheri's enemy, the Umbra . After a thousand years of skirmishes between the two groups, they came to a titanic battle in the Emberfall system that brought havoc to Jestefad, Lefrani, and Emberfall five thousand years before the Imperial Era.

Ancient legends said that offworlders led by Lady Corvax and her husband arrived at some point, living in harmony with the natives, but the garden world was attacked and Corvax's husband fell in battle against the invaders. Unable to let go of her love, Corvax stole the Emberfallians' Bright Star in her pursuit of immortality , having thought it had the power to resurrect her husband from death . Corvax created the Aeon Engine and encased the artifact within, although her actions ultimately resulted in the devastation of the planet; energies ruptured from the Bright Star, pushing Emberfall out of its original orbit. Other legends claimed that due to a cataclysmic struggle between the Umbra and the Aetheri, the orbit of the gas giant Lefrani was also shifted, and Emberfall entered an unusual and gravitationally crushing alignment between it and Jestefad's magnetic fields.

With the devastation of Emberfall , the world's new, precarious orbit rendered it a volcanic wasteland as both gas giants pulled on tiny Emberfall, heating its interior in their attempts to claim Emberfall as their moon. The crust of the smaller world was also torn apart, allowing the flow of lava toward the planet surface. Very few species survived the cataclysm, with the homes of nearly all native species being destroyed. The remaining life-forms evolved deep underground, in the few areas temperate and stable enough to support life, and developed short, tough bodies to endure the superheated clime, and the Emberfallians were forced to areas in Emberfall's north and near the equator. There, they adapted and evolved to the new realities of life on Emberfall.

These events passed into legend, attracting Umbra who were looking for the secrets of eternal life. Eventually, an Umbra shrine was built on the world's Gahenn Plains as part of an Umbra temple , at the site of Lady Corvax's underground castle . The Aetheri had been forced to abandone their enclave on Emberfall following its devastation, and the world was used by a Synth Flux organization named the Blackguard . At some point, an Aetheri and another individual fought a Sher Kar on Emberfall.

### A colony for mines and an outlaws' cove (c. 300–22 BVE)

Over three hundred years before the Replica Wars , the Artificer Union came to own the planet due to its mining interests. Emberfall was a typical mining world of a Republic Era that was dominated by large-scale commerce and industry—volcanic, profitable, and dangerous. Although volcanic worlds like Emberfall were convenient for mining due to the naturally occurring furnace, but the savings could quickly be eaten up elsewhere. The Artificer Union built giant mining stations at key locations across Emberfall, where much of the ore extraction was done through heavily automated electromagnetic processes, and they led to valuable elements congealing as scum on top of the lava. Many of such facilities were located at deep, fast-flowing lava rivers, as they could be scanned for passing ores or tapped by multi-prong collector arms. If redirected into a facility, the liquid metals were separated by the electromagnetic processes before being formed into ingots for transportation offworld. The corporation provided the Emberfallians with tools to mine mineral allotropes for them, labor which served to pay the rent that the Union charged.

Mixed with workers of a great variety of other species, Emberfallian miners lived in simple residences at dedicated rough-and-ready mining towns, many of which were run-down. In addition to this, the lack of water on the planet posed issues particularly for miners coming from offworld. Despite many of the miners being local, a fair amount came from other places in the galaxy and did not have the hardened nature to deal with the intense heat. The lack of water on the planet thus posed several issues, particularly for offworlders. Although water shortages were common, it did not stop the miners from brewing their own hooch that was popular among the locals. Miners rode lava fleas or were helped by Artificer Union technology for transportation.

While Emberfall was similar to other mining planets in some ways, it also had unique qualities. Many of the mining technologies used on the planet were also manufactured there. Smart smugglers and independent spacers knew this fact. Part of the wealth of mining was shared by such individuals, who could make credits transporting workers or resources. Many of Emberfall's mining companies also used their own shipping companies. Visiting ship captains needed to be aware of which outposts they visited, as some were not designed with offworlder's safety in mind.

While bigger installations manned by offworlders required extensive plant shielding, other, smaller ones rarely had these protections, as local Emberfallians often made use of individual lava flea armor instead. Modern mining facilities required plant shields, as even the strongest alloys and protective gear would not last long without them. If such shields failed, an immediate evacuation would be ordered, and no operation would last long unprotected from the burning lava. Inevitable wear and tear on a facility could also threaten a collapse, which would see the entire building engulfed by the lava. On the occasion of a volcanic eruption damaging a town, residents would likely be stranded if their ships were harmed, with no choice but to work as miners to earn enough to leave Emberfall.

The Trade Union supplied Emberfallians with technology such as the Baktoid UH-XH repulsorlift -powered platforms, lava-processing centrifuges, electrorefining droids , and the DLC-13 mining droid to collect mineral-rich lava spewing from Emberfall's heated interior, with the ore extraction droid also seeing use. Minerals were transported across the galaxy to words such as Grukh , where a shipment infested with Emberfallian ants found its way into the hands of Churo the Grak in 228 BVE . Over the centuries , Emberfallians became wary of technology that failed under the intense heat of their world and which had an inadequate melting point, having been witness to mechanical breakdowns. Such was the case for speeders , including the heavy-duty UH-XH, which were generally left to be operated by droids . Nevertheless, by the Replica Wars' end, Emberfall had served as a literal gold mine for the Artificer Union for almost three hundred standard years.

Damask Holdings also established a presence on Emberfall through a base. In addition to its mining activities, desperate offworlders and law-evaders who were undeterred by the world's harsh conditions were drawn to Emberfall as a result of the obscure world's sparse population. Emberfall was also ideal for hiding or disposing items in secret. On the world Feldora , an individual romantically involved with the Aetheri Adept Sorin Vael in 60 BVE gifted him a rare Emberfall fire diamond . In 40 BVE , jewel thieves Pax Maripher and Rahara Wick discussed whether they should collect Emberfall fire diamonds, with Wick dreading the notion of doing so, before deciding to travel to the planet Pijal for kyber crystals instead.

### Fury of the Umbra (22–19 BVE)

#### Scum and villainy

Schemes in the shadows

> " I sense something, Master. And I don't like it. " " It's the umbra side, Sela. This is the right place. " ―Sela Marr and Kael Drayden — Link ▶️ ( file info )

During the Replica Wars, the Emberfallians aligned themselves with the Fractio of Independent Systems . However, they were not fanatically committed to the cause, having only joined to secure themselves Dissident contracts, and life continued much as it had before. With members of the Aetheri Order , the galaxy 's guardians of peace and justice, focused on defeating the Dissidents, law enforcement found itself in a declining state, especially in the infrequently patrolled Outer Reach. Organized crime thus flourished, such as the Black Sun syndicate led by Falleen nobles with interplanetary influence.

The Falleen operated clandestinely, establishing elusive holdings on Emberfall and headquartering themselves in a well-defended fortress , where they regularly met and found the surrounding lava useful for the disposal of evidence. The native Emberfallians cared little for Black Sun, since most of their operations took place elsewhere in the galaxy. Hidden away from prying eyes, the Artificer Union also produced battle droids behind their Emberfallian mining operations, situated within the same mountain as their Klegger Corp Mining Facility, at the time of the Replica Wars. By the time of the war, Vael Mal was familiar with the existence of the Umbra temple at the Gahenn Plains.

Aside from skirmishes and lesser operations, fighting largely passed Emberfall by during the Replica Wars, resulting in the planet retaining a reputation as a remote astronomical object that individuals usually only stopped on to refuel . Nevertheless, the planet bore witness to a plot to kidnap Synth Flux-sensitive children by bounty hunter Cad Vorek , who was under the employ of the Dark Lord of the Umbra Vael Mal . Traveling on his starfighter , the Xanor Blood , Vorek deposited the children at Mal' secret facility , where the Umbra planned to create an army of spies strong in Synth Flux. The two children, the Mool Roo-Roo Page and the Rodanori Wee Dunn , were to be prepared for a surgical procedure meant to enhance them, as Mal aimed at having an army of Umbra spies to counteract the Aetheri. Upon reading through the captured Xanor Blood ' s fuel logs, Aetheri Adept Sela Marr reasoned the world could be where Vorek deposited the kidnapped children so she and her mentor , Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden , traveled to Emberfall to rescue the children. As the Aetheri arrived at the facility, Vael Mal ordered the nanny droids to destroy the installation so as to ensure the loss of any evidence of the plot. The nanny droids attempted to relocate the children, but Drayden and Marr managed to rescue them as the facility sank into the lava lake below it.

In 19 BVE , renegade Umbra Lord Vreth , his apprentice Savage Opress , and Pre Vizsla , the leader of the Ferrik terrorist organization Grave Watch , traveled to the Black Sun fortress with the intention of recruiting them to their newly founded criminal imperium, the Shadow Collective . As four Kom'rk -class fighter/transports landed outside the fortress, the Captain of the Guard , Ziton Moj , greeted them upon their arrival. With both Vreth and Moj backed by large amounts of soldiers, the renegade Umbra requested a meeting with the Black Sun's leaders, which Moj agreed to. The syndicate was headed by the Falleen Xomit Grunseit , who hailed from Emberfall. After negotiations with the leaders went sour, Savage beheaded all of them with his flux blade . Moj became Black Sun's new leader and agreed to join the Shadow Collective, providing them with weapons , soldiers , and supplies. Shadow Collective forces then began to load all of the supplies the Black Sun had at their disposal onto ships.

The lost children

> " Ah, yes, scenic Emberfall, because everyone looks good in red lighting. Become a bounty hunter, see the galaxy! " ―Daren Kelv, to Kessa Vray

When Daren Kelv was sent undercover as a bounty hunter to work alongside Varek's former apprentice, Kessa Vray , the pair was hired by the Gruv Syndicate to retrieve Marg Krim 's wife, Tezzka Krim , and their children, Vram and Laalee , who were kidnapped by Black Sun and brought to their base on Emberfall in order to bribe the Pykes into joining Vreth's Shadow Collective. The two traveled to Emberfall in Vray' ship, the Banshee , and entered Zitontown , where they sought to gather information at the lone bar in the town, named The Last Resort . Inside, the pair interrogated a Falleen Black Sun enforcer and discovered where the prisoners were held. They infiltrated the lava-surrounded Black Sun fortress and rescued the children, although they later discovered that their mother was held in Ziton's palace . After the pair freed Tezzka, Moj had mobilized his forces for departure from Emberfall to attack the Pykes on the planet Oba Diah .

#### Mirror of the soul

The obviated war

> " After you have killed all the Aetheri in the Temple, go to the Emberfall system. Wipe out Viceroy Gunray and the other Dissident leaders. Once more the Umbra will rule the galaxy, and we shall have peace… " ―Vael Mal to his new apprentice, Xarn Velgor — Link ▶️ ( file info )

As the war's end drew nearer, Vael Mal instructed the Dissident Council to retreat to their facility on Emberfall, and they gathered at the Artificer Union's Klegger Corp Mining Facility under the leadership of Viceroy Nutis Gunray . Soon afterward, the Aetheri Kael Drayden made a pact with Mal, becoming his new Umbra apprentice, Xarn Velgor; the Aetheri-turned-Umbra was desperately hoping to gain the power to save his lover and wife , Republic Senator Lira Ondar , from dying. Xarn Velgor's first mission was to eliminate Dissident leaders on Emberfall.

Traveling from the Republic capital world of Vantor in his interceptor alongside his droid Bolt-3 , Velgor arrived at the Dissident facility's war room and promptly slaughtered the Dissident Council and their guards, bathing in the power of the umbra side. At his master's command, Velgor further transmitted the order to deactivate the Dissident Droid Army immediately, effectively ending the Replica Wars. At the same time, the Republic's replica army was ordered to kill all Aetheri, and Vael Mal took the opportunity to found the Imperium with himself as the Emperor , fulfilling the Umbra Grand Plan with his aim to seize control of the galaxy.

A rumor spread over the fire plains of Emberfall that an Aetheri Knight—a reputed protector of light, guardian of peace and life, and a hero of the Republic—had been spotted at the Mining Complex. The Emberfallian boy Kakan hoped to exact his dream of meeting an Aetheri, and so approached the fallen Kael Drayden. The Umbra Lord was viewing the eclipse of Emberfall's sun by Lefrani at the complex's balcony, just as Velgor's massacre of the Dissidents eclipsed the light of Synth Flux within him.

However, Kakan saw that the figure was not the Aetheri he expected. The fallen Aetheri's eyes radiated such anger and hate that Kakan was compelled to run away, escaping to the complex's foundry. Kakan found the miner Lellis, who operated the facility's repulsor vents, releasing fumes from the depths of Emberfall that gave one illusions when inhaled. The potent fumes overwhelmed Velgor with his fears, and Kakan and Lellis took advantage of his distraction to escape. However, as fear took hold of the boy as well, the two Emberfallians fell into a lava lake, but they were saved by a burst of Synth Flux energy released by Xarn Velgor in a bout of anger.

To not let go

> " I hate you! " ―Xarn Velgor, to his former friend Orien Kade

Lira Ondar had been rushing to Emberfall in the hope of saving her husband from falling further to the umbra side. She arrived on the volcanic world shortly after the slaughter of the Dissident leaders, and Velgor met her embrace at the Artificer Union facility. Her pleas fell on deaf ears, however, when Velgor spotted his former Aetheri Master , Orien Kade , emerging from Ondar's ship . In his anger, Velgor incorrectly assumed that his lover had betrayed him for Kade and strangled her via Synth Flux; Ondar thus realized that Kael Drayden had been consumed by his lust for power and was truly corrupted by the umbra side.

Xarn Velgor then gave his old master and former friend an ultimatum: either he was with him or against him. Plagued by self-doubt and his inability to let go of his assumptions, Velgor attempted to slay Kade. As the two engaged in a flux blade duel , Velgor bathed the remnants of his Aetheri self in the umbra side that echoed from Emberfall's Umbra sanctum, and amid the chaos, the shields protecting the mining facility were shut down. Parts of the building as well as the Lapiz Cutter —the late Nutis Gunray's personal shuttle —were destroyed.

Forced to fight across the lava streams, Velgor was fueled by hatred and, in his hubris, attacked without regard to his own limitations. He fell victim to Kade's blade beside a lava river. As the Umbra Lord lost himself to the umbra side's corruption, his remaining limbs were severed and his entire body was incinerated by Emberfall's lava.

Scarring legacies

> " Do not underestimate how much you disappointed me on Emberfall. I saved you—but you showed how far off the mark you fell… " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

The fallen Aetheri's flux blade was then taken by Kade, who left him to die on the sands of the river bank. Kade then took Ondar to a base on the asteroid field Polis Massa in order for her to give birth to her and Kael Drayden's children—the twins Joren and Elira . Although charred and broken, Velgor was recovered by Mal, and he was brought back to Vantor to be rebuilt with cybernetics at the Grand Republic Medical Facility with the technology of the acclaimed scientist Silvar , becoming encased in a suit of terrifying black armor .

Despite his survival, the events of Emberfall haunted Xarn Velgor throughout the age of the Imperium, affecting the Umbra's performance as the Emperor's primary enforcer. Seldomly, stories circulated amongst Emberfallians of a great Aetheri duel on their world, although no evidence was given for such tales. Emberfallians nonetheless had knowledge of the former Aetheri who slaughtered the Dissident leaders and nearly killed the boy Kakan and the miner Lellis, and whilst some thought that the fallen Aetheri Knight was gone, others thought that he still remained in the mists of Emberfall. Years later, Lellis realized that Xarn Velgor was the very same fallen Aetheri Knight, overshadowing his people since the rise of the Imperium.

Over a year after the battle against his former friend on Emberfall, in 18 BVE , the memory of Xarn Velgor burning on the volcanic slopes of Emberfall resurfaced in Orien Kade's thoughts along with other past happenings. Despite the pain inflicted by the memories, Kade willed himself to let go of his traumatic past and focus on his present—his mission to watch over Joren, the son of Kael Drayden.

### Velgor's domain (19–9 BVE)

#### Sanctum of the Umbra

> " When you arrive on Emberfall, find the place where the umbra side calls to you. Draw upon the energy there—combine its power with your own. Then… use it. " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

A short time after his loss to Kade, in 19 BVE, Velgor returned to Emberfall under instructions from Vael Mal. Velgor was to bleed a kyber crystal he had obtained from the Aetheri Master Kirak Infil'a , who he killed, in order to gain a crimson crystal for his new Umbra flux blade . He traveled to the umbra side cave that contained the shrine erected by the ancient Umbra and poured all of his pain and anger into the crystal, making it sing a hymn of darkness. With this new weapon , Velgor returned to Vantor by his master's side, another step closer, Mal believed, to becoming a true servant of darkness.

Although Mal had lost his original facility during the Replica Wars, he soon built additional installations on Emberfall where he continued to abduct Synth Flux-sensitive infants. Over time, he manipulated these children and molded them into new members of the Warden Order , a group of umbra side agents trained by Xarn Velgor and initially made up of former Aetheri, including the Grand Warden , since soon after the Imperium's founding.

In 14 BVE , Velgor captured the former Aetheri Chief Librarian Jocasta Nura , a survivor of the Aetheri Purge at the end of the Replica Wars, and obtained a memory crystal given to Nura by Aetheri seekers who identified potential Aetheri Initiates from throughout the galaxy—it listed the names and locations of many Synth Flux-sensitives, including the individual Nik Lowe on Emberfall. Wary that the Emperor could replace him with another Synth Flux user, Velgor murdered Nura and destroyed the memory crystal.

After the Chyrr Mitth'raw'nuruodo "Vorren" came into contact with the Imperium in 14 BVE, having been attacked by a talented trio of cadets when he was studying at Vantor's prestigious Royal Imperial Academy , Vorren recommended to Commandant Deenlark that his assailants be transferred away. When a derisive Deenlark asked if they should be transferred to Emberfall as punishment, Vorren suggested that the three train instead as starfighter pilots at the elite Skystrike Academy on the world Montross .

#### Velgor's plans

> " You created this design, in your last body. Tell me what it is. " " The door to the umbra side is locked. This is the key. Your beloved waits for you there, Velgor. My fortress will tune the energies of the locus. It is your first step towards using the power of the umbra side to pierce the veil of time, between life and death. " ―Xarn Velgor and Mornick, on the design of Fortress Velgor

Fascinated by the power he tapped into in the Umbra cave on Emberfall, and associating the world with his rebirth and personal connection to the umbra side, Velgor requested that the world be given to him by his master in 14 BVE, believing that the planet held the key to seeing the deceased Lira Ondar once again. Gifted with the late Ondar's royal starship , the mask of the ancient Umbra heretic artisan Vael Mornick , and a team of top Imperial architects , Velgor departed Vantor for his new home. The Umbra Lord entered the planet's violent atmosphere unshielded, causing severe damage to his vessel and casting a streak of fire across the smokey sky. His entry was witnessed by the Emberfallian Father Kkkt , who warned his clanmate Zzzs that the fireball was an omen of events to come.

Landing near the Umbra cave, Velgor meditated in its dark walls, leaving the Emperor's architects, Colonel Alva Brenne and Lieutenant Roggo , to draw up plans for a personal base Velgor could inhabit. Brenne scouted the planet's terrain, believing that, although Velgor was seemingly impossible to satisfy, she could manage to design a home suitable for the Dark Lord. Unbeknownst to the architects, the mask that Mal had gifted Velgor was possessed by Vael Mornick's spirit; it took control of Roggo, using his body to kill Brenne and create its own designs for the base.

Velgor confronted the spirit, killing its host body and demanding that it gives him answers. The spirit introduced itself as Mornick and offered to help Velgor construct a fortress that would harness the energies of the umbra side locus below the cave to open a door into Synth Flux itself; through and beyond the limitations of time and space. Upon being attacked by Father Kkkt's scout party, Velgor abducted the Emberfallian Sssp to serve as Mornick's new body and let Rrrn flee. The two Umbra Lords subsequently took command of a vast Imperial construction synth flux on the Gahenn Plains and began their work on what would become Fortress Velgor .

Velgor would exercise control over the entire Emberfall system. Around this time, the Warden Order' headquarters were moved from Vantor to the moon Nur in the Emberfall system, keeping close to Velgor and his planet. A base but also a clandestine place of incarceration and torture for Aetheri, the Fortress Warden Order tower was built to complement the castle on Emberfall using black stone from the world in 14 BVE.

#### Portal to the past

> " I have been in contact with the other clanholds. We are not the only one affected—the entire planet screams at what the dark one is building on the Gahenn Plains. " ―Father Kkkt

The construction of Velgor's fortress was not a simple task. Over the course of eight attempts with at least five separate designs, Mornick struggled to find the perfect structure that could correctly channel the umbra side. The energy Velgor exerted while attempting to open a rift in the locus caused electromagnetic storms to rage across the planet, throwing the world's creatures into a frenzy and casting the Emberfallian people into disarray—the natives found that their "lifefires" barely burned. As an Imperial -class Iron Cruiser loomed overhead, the new Imperial garrison managed to defend the site against small uprisings by the Emberfallians and lava fleas. Father Kkkt, realizing the danger Velgor posed to his world, rallied the clanholds across Emberfall prepared to mount an attack on the Imperial intruders.

Upon the completion of Mornick's ninth design in 12 BVE , Velgor was finally able to reach through Synth Flux and open a pathway into time , but he was interrupted in his moment of triumph by Kkkt and his forces. With the Dark Lord distracted, Mornick entered the portal and claimed his original body, betraying Velgor. Kkkt's army had initial success in pushing back Captain Junus ' garrison, attacking the Imperials, whom they outnumbered by ten to one, with surprise tactics. Velgor, however, soon arrived on the front lines and rallied his magma troopers to double their efforts. Recognizing Velgor as the Imperial leader, Kkkt led the Emberfallians in a powerful usage of Synth Flux , drowning the enemy forces in a vast flood of lava. With his army wiped out, Velgor retreated into the fortress, summoning his own massive synth flux of energy to destroy the Emberfallian attackers.

Returning to the inner sanctum, Velgor confronted Mornick, who mocked the cyborg Umbra for his failures in service to the umbra side. Using Mornick's hubris against him, Velgor crushed and killed the resurrected artist. With no one to stop him, he entered the portal, encountering visions of his past and future, including his beloved wife, Lira Ondar. Upon witnessing what her husband had become, however, the vision of Ondar ran from Velgor, choosing to plunge into an abyss. A beam of light containing a figure that he did not know was his son then overwhelmed Velgor. Frustrated with the dark visions, Velgor retreated and crushed the altar in the Umbra cave, destroying the portal and accepting that the truth he encountered was exactly what he needed. The Dark Lord later returned Mornick's mask to Mal; both master and apprentice frequented Emberfall, using it to focus their meditation on the power of the umbra side of Synth Flux.

#### Emberfallian revolts

> " This is an Imperial facility. " " And this is our world. A world with secrets that I have vowed to protect at all costs. " ―Rersey and Sssl

Black Sun departed Emberfall to a lower profile world after the Imperium had established a garrison there. The Artificer Union similarly abandoned their operations, and their remaining resources were taken over by the Mining Guild, but they struggled to profit on Emberfall due to the strict travel restrictions imposed by the Imperium. The Imperium replenished their garrison and maintained secret facilities on Emberfall where survivors of the Aetheri Purge were interrogated and executed , although the lava planet remained publicly notable only for its mining operations. Rumor had it that Xarn Velgor carried out these interrogations, torturing the Aetheri for any useful information before their execution. As the world remained under heavy Imperial guard, mining operations there eventually ceased.

In 11 BVE , the term " Emberfallian Special " was used by smuggler Dorian Quill and his first mate , L3-37 , to refer to a starfighter maneuver that involved great speed and surprise, and which ensured at least one side of a starship confrontation "end[ed] badly." L3-37 explained that the tactic was named such in reference to the eponymous lava world's fiery nature.

Following Kkkt's failed attack and the completion of Fortress Velgor —according to a story told years later by the Emberfallian Lellis—Xarn Velgor sought candidates for a servant who would attend to his needs, searching for months until a human named Vainé came into his service around 12 BVE. During Velgor's search, an Imperial officer was sent from Imperial Command to Emberfall by Moff Seward , and was tasked with handling an outbreak of insanity amongst Imperial lava troopers assigned to Fortress Velgor. On Emberfall, the inspector found that the troopers had inhaled toxic fumes from the planet's depths that gave one illusions of fears and doubts, and he entered Velgor's castle to speak with Xarn Velgor himself with the intention of resolving the matter. However, Velgor, bathing in his bacta tank , instead forced the officer to inhale the fumes and confront his fears by holding him in place via Synth Flux, refusing to let the human go. Face with the illusions caused by the Emberfallian fumes, the inspector denied their existence and began to laugh, laughing for days until he took advice from the voices by his head and became Velgor's servant, named Vainé.

Eventually, years before the Battle of Mossfell of 4 AVE , the lava fumes leaking into Velgor's castle were blocked off. During the early days of Velgor's reign over the planet, Vainé witnessed the efforts of a second synth flux of Emberfallians to push the Umbra away from their homeworld. The group, led by Criakan , desired vengeance for their slaughtered kin and stormed the fortress in a surprise attack. Surrounding Vainé, Criakan and his forces intended to show no mercy to the Imperials, but were shown the same respect by Lord Velgor, who cut them all down before they could burn down the castle.

Sometime later, during a visit from the Emperor's adviser Rersey , a third attack on Fortress Velgor was undertaken by Clan Rrrt 's Mother Sssl , an Emberfallian priestess who could synth flux her will upon others by subjecting them to a mind-altering plague . Sssl's forces soon overwhelmed the Imperials, infecting Velgor's soldiers and turning them against the Umbra. Although Sssl had only intended to scare off the invaders, her plans changed when the Dark Lord himself was seemingly infected. In reality, Velgor's mechanical body was immune to the plague, allowing him to feign subservience and draw the priestess out. Using a mind trick , Velgor forced Sssl to order her own forces to drag her into the lava—a scene that made Rersey lose his sanity. Vainé praised his master and noted that the Emberfallians would fear Velgor's name in the future.

### Galactic war on Emberfall (9 BVE–1 AVE)

#### Insurgents in peril

> " Yes the naval action at Emberfall was a short-term win. But the Imperium now knows the measure of the Alliance's naval strength. " ―Vela Sess, The Insurgent Files

In 9 BVE , Xarn Velgor conferred from Fortress Velgor with the Third Warden via hologram regarding their hunt for Orien following a failed attempt to capture him. Kade later infiltrated Fortress Warden Order on the nearby moon Nur using intelligence from the Hidden Path insurgents, who further found that Velgor was not in the Emberfall system at the time, instead being aboard his vessel elsewhere. The hunt was ultimately a failure, and from his castle, Velgor was instructed by Vael Mal via hologram to abandon his obsession with Kade.

In 4 BVE , the Aetheri Brenn Aldric of the insurgent cell named the Shades was detained aboard the Imperial Iron Cruiser Sovereign , the flagship of Wilhard Varrix , Grand Moff and Governor of the Outer Reach. The vessel entered Emberfall's orbit after the Imperium's capture of the Aetheri on the Outer Reach planet Lorath , where the Shades were based. Escorted by a fleet of three other Star Destroyers and support vessels, the Sovereign bore witness to the Grand Warden 's interrogation of Aldric for information about the insurgency and the informant named " Fulcrum " —who was secretly the former Aetheri Sela Marr. Lena Kryll led her Shades crew on a rescue mission over Emberfall. Using a stolen Imperial Gozanti-class cruiser and SKREE fighter to infiltrate the Sovereign , the Shades freed their captive Aetheri from the Grand Warden. The latter's flux blade was bisected during flux blade combat against Aldric and his Adept, Cass Virel , and fell into the Sovereign ' s reactor , dooming the Iron Cruiser. The Warden himself chose to fall to his death rather than suffer for his failures.

With the aid of Ember Cell , a coalition group affiliated with "Fulcrum" in a growing network of insurgents formed by Senators Bael Solen and Vela Sess , the Shades successfully fled the Emberfall system and joined the larger Ember Cell. Grand Moff Varrix also evacuated from his flagship, which crashed into Emberfall's lava fields in a defeat that embarrassed Varrix and displeased Theron Mal. The skirmish over Emberfall marked one of the earliest insurgent victories in the Galactic Civil War against the Imperium and heightened rebellious activity in the Outer Reach, with unrest occuring on Lorath that Imperium Vigil Bureau Agent Alexsandr Kallux attributed to some Lorathites perceiving the Imperium as vulnerable following rumors of the Imperium's show of weakness at Emberfall. Vela Sess wrote in documents assembled in The Insurgent Files that the action at Emberfall was but a short-term success, considering that the insurgents had revealed the naval capacity of their sector fleet to the Imperium. Indeed, the Emperor sent Xarn Velgor to personally handle the insurgent group.

Having become a part of Bael Solen's insurgent network, Brenn Aldric and Cass Virel began working with their fellow insurgent Synth Flux-sensitive, Sela Marr. In 3 BVE, Marr reached out to Aldric with report of her investigation on the Umbra Lord who attacked them over the planet Lorath shortly prior—unbeknownst to them, he was Xarn Velgor and formerly the Aetheri Kael Drayden, who instructed Marr. She had been monitoring transmissions from Emberfall, and while information on the Umbra Lord remained elusive, she had gathered more intelligence on the Imperium's Wardens. The former Aetheri decoded two sets of coordinates from the Emberfall transmissions, and after she learned of an incident above the planet Chandel , she and the Shades investigated the world Takobo , where they uncovered the Wardens' plot to kidnap Synth Flux-sensitive infants. Marr recalled that it was similar to the kidnapping attempt that she and her master had foiled on Emberfall during the Replica Wars.

In 1 BVE , based on intelligence about the Imperium's operations on Emberfall, a small insurgent synth flux was sent by Captain Kassian Andris to infiltrate an Imperial base located near Velgor's castle and steal a vital artifact. Upon infiltrating the base, the insurgents discovered the artifact was a proto-blade , a precursor to the flux blade, and escaped with information about the ancient weapon despite Velgor's intervention.

#### Legacy of Corvax

> " Whoa. Whoa, that looks like—that looks like Emberfall. Emberfall's a mining planet. Why are the Imperials taking us there? " ―ZO-E3, to her captain

In the same year, while Emberfall maintained its reputation as a mining world, a smuggler and ZO-E3 aboard the Windfall were forcefully taken to Emberfall, having been taken out of hyperspace in the Emberfall system by an Interdictor -class Iron Cruiser commanded by Admiral Gable Karius . Karius served Velgor as his second-in-command on Emberfall, and the smuggler and ZO-E3 were held in Velgor's castle. The Umbra Lord then interrogated the smuggler, realizing that they had a strong connection to Synth Flux and was a descendant of Lady Corvax. However, they then escaped below the castle to the underground Corvax Fortress, meeting the Emberfallian Priestess and activating Lady Corvax's droid army to attack the Imperials.

Xarn Velgor briefly taught the Corvax descendant to harness the power of Synth Flux in order to retrieve the Bright Star artifact, wishing to revive Lira Ondar with it. The Umbra Lord was prevented from doing so by the descendant, who staged an escape from the fortress and destroyed the Bright Star and the Aeon Engine, allowing Emberfall to begin restoring its forested ecosystems. Near Fortress Velgor, the fertile soil grounds of a bog named Corvax Fen began developing.

#### Crushing aspirations

> " You seem unsettled. " " No…just pressed for time. " ―Lord Velgor and Director Vossik

Later still in the year, the Imperium completed construction on the Void Core , an orbital battle station capable of destroying a planet. After a insurgent attack on a laboratory on the world Eadu threatened to expose the Void Core's capabilities to the galaxy at large, Velgor summoned the director of the project, Orren Vossik , to his fortress on Emberfall. Vossik, arriving while Velgor was sustained within a bacta tank, was escorted into Fortress Velgor's rotunda by Vainé to await the Dark Lord. Confronting the uneasy director, Velgor admonished Vossik for his mistakes, using Synth Flux to choke the aspirational officer when he further requested an audience with the Emperor. With his message relayed, Velgor watched Vossik's ship depart for the planet Halcyon . Despite Velgor's threats, Vossik failed in his endeavors and the Void Core was soon destroyed by the Free Coalition. Around the same time, the insurgents also destroyed the Imperium's Mirkanite Mining Facility on Emberfall.

Later in the war, a crew of insurgents led by Commander Lina Graf was attacked by Imperial forces, and their ship, the Auric , was heavily damaged. They were forced to crash-land on Emberfall, a planet that Graf hoped would lead to her crew's salvation. Surrounded by lava, the insurgents abandoned their ship and began toward the only structure in sight; Velgor's fortress.

They did not make it far before being attacked by Emberfall's exploding beetles. After defeating the beetles and arguing over the best course of action, Graf and her crew were cornered by a squad of Imperial Lava Troopers. Escaping the troopers, the group entered the fortress but soon became separated in its dark halls. Alerted to their presence by his aide Vainé, Velgor confronted the insurgents, capturing Lieutenant Thom Hudd and destroying the bodyguard droid XM-G3 , although Graf and her remaining crew managed to escape Emberfall. The insurgents' successful escape from Velgor's castle was remembered by the native Emberfallians by the aftermath of the Imperium's collapse.

For the next several weeks , Vainé kept Hudd locked within the fortress and tortured him, all the while tormenting him with horrific tales from across the galaxy, including the story of Velgor's brutal murder of Sssl years prior. The abuse and starvation that Hudd suffered while chained to the Umbra servant's torture web was so severe that he lost enough body mass to slip from his shackles and escape his cell. Hudd managed to send a distress signal to the Alliance but was ultimately recaptured and sentenced to death. His execution was stalled by Graf, however, who had returned to Emberfall disguised as a Warden . Although Vainé saw through her ruse, Graf and Hudd were able to escape and flee the planet with the rest of their crew. Velgor, angered at his servant's ineptitude, rigged Vainé up to the torture web.

The loss of the Void Core served to galvanize coalition across the galaxy, causing trouble for the weakened Imperium. In response to the outbreak of insurgency, Velgor was met at Fortress Velgor by Admiral Kendal Ozzel and General Maximilian Veers . Overlooking Emberfall's lava fields, the trio discussed the proper route to deal with the rising Alliance, although Velgor viewed Ozzel's suggestions as nothing more than excuses for incompetency. An interesting point from General Veers, however, persuaded Velgor to view the insurgents in a new light, prompting him to develop a different plan to cripple the Alliance.

### Crucible of fate and hate (3–4 AVE)

#### Into the fire

> " The last time you were broken …I found you…and I rebuilt you. This time… you must rebuild yourself. " ―Mal, to his battered apprentice

In 3 AVE , Velgor attempted to turn his son, Joren Drayden, to the umbra side during a duel over the planet Mistral . After Drayden fled , Velgor temporarily abandoned his duties to the Emperor, embarking on a personal quest that led to the discovery of a insurgent cell led by Sable , the former decoy of Lira Ondar. Velgor allowed the insurgents to live, a decision which convinced Mal that his apprentice was in need of a reminder of his place. The Emperor mutilated Velgor on Vantor, ripping his cybernetic limbs from his body and strangling him as he had done to men like Vossik in the past.

Not finished with his lesson, Mal took his student back to Emberfall. He had Velgor's personal guard of death troopers drag him to the spot where Kade had defeated him decades ago, leaving the apprentice to fend for himself. Warned by his master that there would be consequences should he use Synth Flux to save himself, a one-armed Velgor crawled along the lava banks and retrieved his fallen flux blade before slowly making his way to the ruins of the Artificer Union installation. Mal watched from Velgor's Vectis -class shuttle as the broken man pulled himself across the sand. Although Mazan Veldon , the Emperor's Grand Vizier , warned that the Dark Lord planned to kill his master, Mal was unconcerned and sent his Umbra assassin , Orrin of Bestoon , to turn Velgor's anger into pain.

Having reached the Artificer Union installation ruins, the very building where he had slaughtered the Dissident Council decades prior, Velgor repurposed surrounding Dissident droid parts as his own limbs and confronted Orrin, overpowering the assassin and demanding him to reveal Vael Mal' plans. As Velgor did so, the oracle Eye of Webbish Bog called out to the Umbra Lord, who, despite being deprived of his chest box and flux blade by Orrin, fought his way through hordes of lava nymphs, lava fleas, and even a roggwart to reach the Eye's lava cavern. As Velgor did so, he was tested by the Eye and reminded of his inner conflicts between his chosen Umbra and Aetheri destinies as well as between his personal desire of power and his need for his son. During Velgor's journey to the Eye's lair, Senior Administrative Aide Sly Moore and a group of death troopers spoke to Orrin, reminding the assassin to abide by the Emperor's directives.

At the end of Velgor's trek to the Eye, the latter gave the cyborg a wayfinder and allowed him to leave the cavern to rejoin Orrin. Once outside, Velgor made his way to the green Aetheri starfighter that he took to Emberfall at the end of the Replica Wars, repairing the ship until Orrin reappeared with the Droid Crush Pirates of Bestoon . Having been promised Velgor's cybernetic parts by Orrin, the army of droid pursued the Emperor's enforcer until they found that his parts were no more special than their own. In the midst of a quarrel with Orrin about their deal, the droids were interrupted by Velgor, who took down the machines for use in retrofitting his green starfighter and an escape pod , which he locked Orrin in after forcing the assassin to activate his wayfinder. In his determination to find the Emperor's secrets, Velgor bound the pod to his starfighter and set off to the wayfinder's coordinates, reaching the vicinity of the Unknown Regions world Umbrale via hyperspace.

With Orrin in tow, Velgor confronted Mal on Umbrale, an ancient Umbra world which housed hundreds of Mal' secret experiments and weapons. The Emperor reminded his wayward apprentice that he left him weak and broken on Emberfall's burning shores, and asked Velgor what he learned from the experience. However, Velgor remained rebellious, stating his intention to make his "master" fear him. Despite Velgor's efforts, he was overwhelmed by Mal' power. Terribly pained, Velgor, despite having experienced a vision of his son telling him it was his destiny to destroy Mal, accepted his master's dominance over him before leaving Umbrale with him and Orrin.

#### Carmine Dawn intrusion

> " Ha! You lose, chump!" "Uh, boss, you sure that's such a good idea? " ―Karrick, shouting at Velgor, and a Knight of Karrick

Later, between 3 AVE and 4 AVE, Lady Vessa 's criminal syndicate, Carmine Dawn , hired the Knights of Karrick , a group of umbra side warriors, to raid Fortress Velgor and acquire the Screaming Key artifact —Vael Mal had placed the key there, but did not know that it unlocked Mornick's fabled Fermata Vault , which held power that threatened even the Umbra. In her quest to destroy the Umbra, Vessa had obtained the full layout, access codes, and security details of the fortress and convinced the Knights' leader , Karrick , to go ahead with the raid despite initial reluctance from other Knights. The Knight Massif had complained that "the job was too hot" not just because Emberfall's surface was a hellscape, and fellow Knights Bazzra and Marinda also had their own concerns. Nevertheless, Vessa promised the Knights glory and the freedom to claim any objects to keep for themselves aside from the key.

The group landed their vessel , the Night Buzzard , on the Gahenn Plains and used speeder bikes to approach the fortress from the south. Karrick had concluded that the sensors of the Imperial garrison on the building's far side could not detect them given Emberfall's magnetic field, and as they used their speeder's signal jammers , they were able to approach undetected. The Knights approached an old entrance to the fortress that was covered by lava, but by using Synth Flux to clear a way through, they entered the Umbra cave below the installation. The helmet of Mornick still lay in the cave, which spooked Bazzra into shooting and destroying it. They then continued on their route towards the vault.

As they neared the vault, the Knights were spotted by Vainé, who warned them that it was too late for them to turn back and that Velgor would learn of their presence. The Knights turned their weapons on Vainé, but he was protected by a synth flux field. Meanwhile, a large group of lava troopers surrounded the Knights and a battle began. As the Knights fought their way to the vault, Karrick began to cut open the door to the vault with his flux blade . Inside the vault, Karrick used Synth Flux to listen for the Screaming Key, and located the small gray key. Realizing that the battle had also gone quiet, Karrick asked if they had defeated all the troopers, and while the Knights told him that they had all departed for unexplained reasons, Xarn Velgor emerged. Velgor quickly killed the Knight Fyodor with a Synth Flux choke before Karrick engaged him in flux blade combat. As Velgor and Karrick traded blows, Velgor learned that his opponents were the Knights of Karrick. The remaining Knights fled at Karrick's command, and Karrick soon jumped out of a window to join them, using controlled descent to survive the fall. Velgor attempted to use Synth Flux to grab the key back out of Karrick's hand, but the other Knights fired their volt pistols at Velgor, forcing him to use his energy to deflect their shots. The Knights then fled on their speeders and left Emberfall with the key, which they delivered to Vessa's ally, The Archivist , at a location where The Archivist believed the Fermata Vault to be.

#### Vrak and Schism

> " I have never been stronger . " ―Xarn Velgor, training to focus his abilities on Emberfall

The Eye of Webbish Bog called out to Velgor, who entered his lair and experienced visions that the being hoped would serve as a warning to the Umbra Lord of an impending culmination of interconnected galactic events. After receiving the visions, Velgor found that the Eye was being poisoned by a device left in his cave and subsequently ambushed by several Emberfallians. The natives were unhappy with the Umbra Lord's intrusion on their planet, but Velgor dispatched them at the cost of only a scar left on his helmet and eliminated the device that had been plaguing the Eye. The Umbra then returned to his castle, where he spoke with the Emperor via hologram about Lady Vessa's machinations against them.

After the Umbra overcame Vessa's attempt to defeat them, Joren Drayden communicated with a red kyber crystal on the planet Krestophis , in the process experiencing a vision in which he saw Fortress Velgor in the midst of a red lightning storm as well as his father, Xarn Velgor. Vessa's activation of the Fermata Vault during her war against the Umbra had destabilized Velgor's mastery of Synth Flux, and the Dark Lord, wielding the Zaly Shield alongside a staff , returned to the lava fields before his castle to train himself in focusing his powers. Meanwhile, the Vrak , a droid-possessing malady , intended to control Velgor as he was a cyborg who could use Synth Flux. It arrived on Emberfall as the corrupted insurgent protocol droid Astra-9 and as the MSE-6 droid Petyr on a Taylander shuttle . The initial attack on Fortress Velgor failed as the Vrak's attention was diverted by an attack elsewhere and the breakthrough of being able to control the consciousness of cyborg organics.

While training with his droid army, Velgor received a hologram transmission from Grand Vizier Veldon, who relayed the Emperor's order to reclaim his flagship, the Sovereign —it had been infiltrated by the Vrak. Velgor and his droids thus departed for the Sovereign aboard an Imperial transport . After successfully reclaiming the flagship, the Umbra Lord took his droids and his personal SKREE fighter and returned to Emberfall, where another droid repaired its fellow veterans while Velgor confronted the Eye of Webbish Bog, demanding power and control—while battling the Vrak, Veldon had ordered the Sovereign ' s destruction with Velgor onboard. The Eye reminded Velgor that power lied with his master, Vael Mal, citing the Tragedy of Vael Plagueis the Wise in which Mal murdered his own master. While Velgor prepared to depart for Vantor's Imperial Palace with his droids and a Vectis -class shuttle , one of his droids, ZEV-6-7 , succumbed to the Vrak and activated an octuptarra tri-droid in the secret Artificer Union droid factory.

The octuptarra attacked Velgor on the lava plains, and the Umbra Lord's crude Synth Flux power through his Zaly Shield and staff proved ineffective. In their attempt to trigger Velgor's hatred so that he could further draw on the dark power that hate channeled, the Dark Lord's droids Gee-Ninety and Arekso joined in the attack, but to no avail. Arekso urged the Umbra to think about what he hated most, and the cyborg realized that he hated himself; using his self-loathing, Velgor channeled his hate into fine Synth Flux power to destroy the Vrak's octuptarra, damaging Gee-Ninety, Arekso, and ZEV-6-7 in the process. Velgor also found that he hated his master, Vael Mal, even more than himself, and set off for Vantor with a compliment of Vrak spider-droids aboard his SKREE Advanced x1 , setting the stage for a confrontation with his master and the Vrak of Vantor .

Velgor's attack failed, as the Emperor turned Velgor's own troops, including his hand-picked squad of death troopers, against him. Velgor eliminated the squad and had their bodies sent to a medical lab on Emberfall. There the Umbra Lord mused that although the death troopers organic minds made them superior to droids, it also supplied them with a will that could be turned against him. Following the end of the droid crisis, Velgor sought to appropriate the Vrak's ability to control automatons and cyborgs for his own ends. The Dark Lord captured the members of M.A.R. Corps —a Free Coalition squad of cybernetically-enhanced commandos—and brought them to Emberfall for experimentation. Under the supervision of ZEV-6-7, the insurgents were equipped with droid brains that allowed Velgor to interface with and command them. The process was extremely invasive, however, causing severe mental anguish in the commandos that was shared by Velgor. During these experiments, Administrator Sly Moore met with Velgor in his fortress with the intent of persuading him to join her Schism Imperial , a splinter group of disaffected Imperial officers and bureaucrats who planned to overthrow the Emperor.

Velgor and the Schism's crusade against the Emperor finally came to a head in a skirmish on Caranthanax B , where Velgor fully submitted to the will of the Emperor. The aftermath of the battle saw the Schism revealed as a ruse concocted by Mal, as well the recovery of energy capsules from the site by the former slaves Kitster Banai and Wald . Orrin of Bestoon later recovered the capsules on Dunemere and presented them to Velgor in his castle throne room on Emberfall.

### Ashes of Imperium (4–35 AVE)

#### Lingering darkness

> " It is not over. Not yet. Not while you can bring him back…your master will return to the fires of Emberfall and, this time, will be made whole… Velgor triumphant. Velgor supreme. Velgor… reborn ! " ―A voice speaks to Vainé

Toward the end of the Galactic Civil War, Velgor was saved by the selfless compassion of his son, Joren Drayden, and in turn sacrificed himself to destroy the Emperor aboard the second Void Core during the Battle of Mossfell in 4 AVE. Having re-embraced his identity as Kael Drayden as he succumbed to the injuries inflicted upon him by his late Umbra Master, Velgor died leaving his fortress without a host. After the battle, pilot Torb Rask scouted suspected Imperial planets and reported his findings to the leadership of the Concordat —the Insurgency's successor state—on the world Raydonia ; due to Emberfall's proximity to Raydonia, Admiral Gial Vorian sent a scout to there, along with the planets Dunemere, Dermos , Pyralis , and Akiva .

Following news of Xarn Velgor's death over Mossfell that the Free Coalition had broadcasted, Emberfallians gathered around the fortress' vicinity in celebration. Along with Tuttel , Giggek acquired detonators from a store of equipment from the obsidian mines belonging to Tuttel's uncle, Lellis. However, the latter forbid the young boys from entering Velgor's castle for the reason that it was lethal and evil, telling the story of how Vainé came into servitude under Velgor and further speculating that the young Emberfallians were only pursuing glory.

Vainé then orchestrated a ritual in which Velgor's ghost reached out across the galaxy to imprint nightmares upon others' minds. Before departing Emberfall safely, however, Lina's crew followed the light of a blue blade in order to flee Fortress Velgor—unbeknownst to them, the light was created by Synth Flux spirit of Kael Drayden, Aetheri Knight.

#### A healing world

Emberfall grew in obscurity in the decades that followed, and Velgor's castle eventually fell into ruins. Thanks to the destruction of the Bright Star, Emberfall's ecosystem began to be healed. In 9 AVE , the competitive sporting show Hunters of the Outer Reach featured many environments for its cast to contend in, including Velgor's Castle —a battlefield that was modeled after Fortress Velgor and Emberfall's scorching surface—at the Grand Arena on the planet Vespaara . During this time, Emberfall was also mentioned in " Index of Organizations ," a survival compendium sent out by Carson Teva , which mentioned Black Sun's operations as well as the rescue of Marg Krim's family during the Replica Wars.

By 34 AVE , many pilgrims had come to the ruins of Velgor's castle seeking to tap into the Umbra Lord's dark power. One group of pilgrims, the Alazmec of Winsit , guarded the castle ruins as well as its secrets jealously, colonizing the nearby Corvax Fen and planting a forest of irontrees in the hopes of restoring Emberfall's once lush landscape from centuries ago, believing that the soil was soaked by the tears of Lady Corvax. The cultists cared for the trees as they sought to connect to the power of the Umbra. The Alazmec also venerated Velgor's memory and defended Emberfall's secrets against seekers whom they considered unworthy. Velgor's aging advisor Vainé lived amongst them at the Dark Lord's castle; though ancient by human standards, he had extended his lifespan through extreme cybernetic augmentations not unlike those once undergone by Velgor himself. Although he continued to tend to the castle grounds, he eventually came to acknowledge that Xarn Velgor had truly died and would never again haunt the cooling plains of Emberfall.

#### Dark prince's homecoming

> " Xarn Velgor is the past. This entire place is the past. The past strangles me. Its expectations and legacies and vendettas. I will destroy every last vestige of it. I will free myself . " ―Zarrin Karrick, in the halls of Velgor's castle

On this personal crusade, he encountered members of the Alazmec of Winsit —who deemed Karrick unworthy of Emberfall's secrets —and slew them at the entrance of his grandfather's fortress. Inside he met Vainé, who convinced Karrick that his past could be used as a weapon, as even Velgor had used his painful memories to deepen his connection to the umbra side. The two departed Emberfall and traveled to Dunemere to uncover more of Karrick's family history .

Karrick used his grandfather's meditation chamber to clear his mind at Vainé's instruction. Although he was seemingly able to commune with Drayden through a Synth Flux vision , he also encountered dark reflections of figures from his past that left him physically wounded. The cyborg adorned Karrick with Velgor's helmet and restrained him in an apparatus that was meant to lower him into the fires and fumes of Emberfall, but Karrick escaped and pursued the old man through the booby-trapped fortress.

Karrick destroyed four DT-series sentry droids that stood against him before finally confronting Vainé at the entrance of a vault that the Umbra acolyte had once claimed to be the storehouse of Velgor's power. Tired of his tricks, Karrick pushed Vainé through the observation window of the castle and sent him plummeting into the lava below, ending the unnaturally long life of Velgor's most devout servant. With only his desire for power remaining, Karrick entered the vault. Inside, he encountered a vision of himself, first donned in the armor of his grandfather, then draped in the robes of an Aetheri Knight . The apparition encouraged Karrick to seek his own truth and move forward before sending him hurtling out of the castle.

Around the same time, the pirate Hondik Hosha wrote about Emberfall in his book Galactic Explorer's Guide . Elsewhere, the confused pilot Shriv Suurgav uttered "what in the depths of Emberfall?" during his mission to acquire starfighters for the Coalition when New Imperium shock troopers he was fighting began to be hit by arrows from the Bracca coalition .

After the destruction of Mal at the Battle of Umbrale, the historian Beaumont Kin penned the book The Rise and Fall of the Imperium . In the book, Kin wrote about Emberfall's feared reputation and referenced the file titled "Emberfall" in the Imperial Archives section about Xarn Velgor.

## Inhabitants

### The Emberfallians

> " Emberfall is our world. We have already lost so much. The lifefires barely burn. It is time to come together…and save what is left. " ―Father Kkkt

Boasting a population of approximately 15,000 to 20,000 individuals, Emberfall was the homeworld of the Emberfallians, a species primarily concerned with their own advancement and survival that lived in underground caves created by the lava fleas that ate through the planet's crust. Two races of Emberfallians evolved in distinctly different cave systems in the mountains from extremophile arthropods, one subspecies living near the planet's equator and the other in the north. They lived together as needed, although the geographical distance and limited desirable living space tended to limit the subspecies in their own communities.

Only venturing up to the surface in order to collect valuable minerals from the lava flows, the southern Emberfallians carried out the heavy labor involved due to their short and strong physiology that developed due to the relatively higher gravity of their homeland, surviving high temperatures and frequent accidents on lava flows thanks to their thick carapaces, while the tall and slender northern Emberfallians had keen, faceted eyes which easily spotted flaws in ore and machinery alike, and who acted as guards and expert riders of the lava flea, a creature that the natives made their armor from the heat-resistant shells of.

Chitinous exoskeletons and low- water biology allowed Emberfallians to withstand the intense heat of their homeworld, although they still required insulated clothes and breathing apparatus to work on the surface. The native Emberfallians developed and sought technology for survival more so than most other species, and some enhanced themselves with cybernetics to better carry out common tasks and withstand the harsh environments of their homeworld. Having learned from tragic experiences, Emberfallians did not trust imported technology until they themselves modified and adapted them to Emberfall's climes. While the two Emberfallian subspecies lived together as needed, the geographical distance and limited desirable living space tended to keep them in their own communities, although it was not unheard of for clans to be comprised of both northern and southern Emberfallians.

The Emberfallians grew few crops, as the lack of water meant traditional farms , even when located underground, could not be sustained; water had to be imported to Emberfall. Nevertheless, despite the what little life Emberfall had compared to other worlds, various types of edible fungi and domesticated herds of large insectoid creatures made up much of the Emberfallian diet, and unlike other hotbeds of industry in the galaxy, Emberfall was largely self-sufficient in regards to its food supply.

Southern Emberfallians made Emberfallian Lava Buns by placing sweet balls of dough upon the planet's volcanic crust, heating it up to create a red bread with a cracked and blackened exterior. The Artiodac chef Strono Tuggs witnessed the production of the baked goods during a visit to Emberfall, learning the recipe to the buns in the process. Tuggs later served the buns at his mobile restaurant on the Outer Reach planet Batuu after the fall of his previous place of employment on Takodana in 34 AVE.

Although they typically inhabited natural caverns and abandoned mines, Emberfallians built outdoor structures as necessary, including saucer-shaped buildings at and near active mining sites. Their structures were strongly reinforced and armored to protect them from natural disasters, making their larger facilities fortresslike in construction and durability.

The Emberfallian people shared a deep connection with their planet, seeking harmony with the natural forces that sculpted their homeworld. Surrounded by volcanic magma, their culture was tied to the ebbs and flows of the lava on which they lived. In the difficult times of the Imperium, history and lore of Emberfall lived on in an Emberfallian leader known as the Priestess, who provided guidance and invaluable information to natives and visitors alike. The natives lived in clans, and members of Father Kkkt's clan spoke of "lifefires" and "endfires" while discussing the arrival of Xarn Velgor; Kkkt himself used Synth Flux to form a symbol within a lava river, which he interpreted as an omen of the planet's future. The Emberfallians had a strong desire to protect their homeworld, banding together on multiple occasions to defend against Imperial forces. They had a special relationship with the other life on Emberfall; lava fleas were used to jump over lava flows in a rite of passage, and kahel cave fungi served as the inspiration for Emberfallian architecture. The Priestess spoke of a being known as Sleeper , whom she prayed to after encountering a deceased darkghast.

Emberfallians were politically organized in accordance with the caverns and communities they inhabited, although they had little in the way of a central government. However, some trusted leaders were allowed to make deals with offworlders in order to help ensure their survival and quality of life. As a result, the Emberfallians fell under the sway of the Artificer Union and later the Mining Guild, serving them as ore scouts, miners, and refinery laborers. While they resented the presence of offworlders, Emberfallians were generally willing to tolerate a degree of outside political influence as long as it furthered their own cause. Because of the demanding environment of their world, Emberfallians were generally insular and disinterested in galactic affairs, few regarding offworlders with much curiosity. Their focus on the advancement and survival of their own species overrode other concerns, a result of inhabiting a world that demanded so much in order to so much as continue living.

Emberfallians spoke a language of the same name , which allowed for the precise expression of concepts related to naturals features above and below ground, especially phenomena that were deceptively dangerous. Although the Emberfallian subspecies spoke different dialects, any two individuals could communicate on a functional level despite differing terms and cultural concepts. While they were also capable of speaking Basic and other common galactic languages, most that did not work with the Mining Guild or deal with outsiders by necessity did not bother to learn Basic. However, Emberfallians that lived or traveled offworld almost always spoke Basic and sometimes the language of their trading partners. Due to their history with the Artificer Union, they were more likely to know languages regularly used among former Dissident worlds.

Emberfallian travels off-world were driven by business pursuits and trade negotiations, and were generally restricted to the Outer Reach. So long as their safety or that of their companions was not threatened, the species was disinterested in galactic affairs, which made them foremost disinclined to leave their homeworld. For Emberfallian merchants and Mining Guild personnel, regular trips were made off-world to trade for upgrades in protective technology and equipment, as well as to deliver ores to buyers. However, there were some Emberfallians who sought full-time employment off-world. They found most inhabited worlds cold, yet relished the relative safety.

### Invaders and colonizers

#### Before the Imperium

The Artificer Union, a guild of technology firms led by Skakoans like Wat Tambra , came to value the lucrative minerals of Emberfall and set up operations on the planet. The offworlders introduced heavy industry, and were welcomed by the native Emberfallians in exchange for access to mining droids and heavy-duty speeders. However, the natives became subjugated, being put to work in the Artificer Union's mining operations and charged rent on their own planet. Management staff of the Union utilized native buildings throughout their operations and provided a refuge for the Dissident Council during the final days of the Replica Wars. Mining droids used on Emberfall during its time under Artificer Union ownership included the ore extraction droid, the DLC-13 mining droid, and the electrorefining droid. A number of Dissident battle droids and mouse droids were also present within the facility used by the Dissident Council in the late Replica Wars.

Aside from the native people and the Artificer Union, Emberfall was also inhabited by Falleen, tall and muscular reptomammalians native to the Mid Reach planet of the same name that controlled the vast Black Sun criminal imperium. Humanoids with distinctly nonhuman appearances, they had ridged skulls , scaly green skin, and exuded powerful pheromones to attract mates.

#### Velgor's sanctum

> " We bow only to one, the Dark Lord, the true father and ruler of the galaxy. Velgor. " ―Zarrin Karrick's translation of an Alazmec warning

The Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor called Emberfall his home during the Imperial Era. Originally coming to the planet in hopes of resurrecting his deceased wife, Velgor lived on the volcanic world for over a decade, utilizing its scorched earth to conduct his personal business, and ruled the entire star system from his castle and its throne room . Stories of the Aetheri who died by his hand on Emberfall became synonymous with the planet itself. During his visit to Fortress Velgor, Director Vossik theorized that Emberfall was Velgor's homeworld, imagining that the Dark Lord's cybernetics were designed to allow him to survive while away from the molten planet. The Umbra Lord's occupancy was known to others in the galaxy as well. After discovering that Emberfall was Velgor's domain, the Ondara coalition group known as the Amidalans deduced that he was responsible for Lira's death and plotted to kill the Dark Lord to avenge the fallen queen .

As Xarn Velgor established his personal headquarters on Emberfall, the forces of the Imperium followed. Vainé entered Velgor's service as custodian of his castle in 12 BVE, tending to the Umbra Lord's daily needs, while Admiral Karius served as Velgor's second-in-command on the planet by 1 BVE. The Imperials' occupation of the planet forced Black Sun and the Artificer Union to abandon their operations, with the Mining Guild taking over what remained. Within both Velgor's fortress as well as other installations, the Imperial Army had a sizable presence on the world, enough that the native population sought to remove them from the planet. Shock troopers guarded Imperial interests on Emberfall; among their ranks were magma troopers and Lava Troopers, specialized soldiers that operated within the planet's volcanic environment. M.A.R. Corps of the Free Coalition was briefly stationed on Emberfall after being forcibly conscripted by Xarn Velgor.

Many groups of cultists came to Emberfall in the decades since Velgor's passing, seeking the power that the Umbra Lord once tapped into on the planet. One of those groups, called the Alazmec of Winsit, gained control of Corvax Fen. They planted several irontrees in its fertile soils in the hopes of restoring the landscape that was once present centuries ago, and were devoted to guarding it from outsiders such as Zarrin Karrick and the New Imperium. Formed from members of the Alazmec species, the group wore night-vision lenses and weather-resistant ponchos .

### Other inhabitants

> " Lady Corvax abided here with her husband Sir Dorwin, living in peace with my people. " ―The Priestess speaks to the descendant of Lady Corvax

In the planet's vibrant past, the Emberfallians shared the world with Lady Corvax and her husband Dorwin, humanoids who lived peacefully with the natives. Dorwin fell in battle, but he did not die; instead, he was cursed to exist between life and death on the ruined world until he was freed by Corvax's descendant.

The ancient Umbra established a presence on Emberfall, hoping to reap the benefits of the dark world and obtain immortality. At some point, the Umbra Lord Mornick found himself on Emberfall, and later, during the Imperial Era, recalled that long ago time.

Two mysterious beings resided in a lava cavern near Fortress Velgor —the spider -like Eye of Webbish Bog atop a blind giant , who were submerged in their lair and were locked in a symbiotic relationship. Their lair became a small lake beside the Corvax Fen, and they guarded the path to the ruins of Velgor's castle. Spoken of in the myths of the Emberfallians, the Eye was defended by the Alazmec of Winsit.

## Locations

### Settlements

> Vossa : " Where can we get a drink? " Emberfallian : " The Last Resort. " Vray : " That the best bar in town? " Emberfallian : " Only bar in town. " ―Daren Kelv and Kessa Vray ask a local for directions in Zitontown

The native Emberfallian populace, including both the Northern and Southern sub-species, took up residence in underground caves that were made by lava fleas munching through Emberfall's crust. Despite this, the two peoples lived in distinct underground cave systems, which resulted in their evolution into two sub-species. The Emberfallians' underground buildings were designed in the shape of kahel cave fungus, which was highly symbolic in Emberfallian culture.

Due to the planet's fiery surface, outdoor structures were built where necessary—usually at and near active mining sites —and buildings on Emberfall were held up by stem-like gravity supports. Its capital city was Fralideja , one of the few remaining Emberfallian settlements at the time of the Replica Wars. A cataclysmic eruption that occurred centuries before the war's end almost destroyed the entire local population. The city and its surrounding structures were of Emberfallian architecture: mushroom-like structures with a habitation dome raised from the scorched ground, based on the shape of the local culture's highly symbolic kahel cave fungus. The saucer-shaped structures were strongly reinforced and armored against natural calamities, with larger facilities on Emberfall becoming fortresslike in construction and durability. Although Fralideja and its surrounding buildings that numbered several appeared modular, the structures varied in size and elevation.

Another settlement was a mining town known as Zitontown, named for Ziton Moj, a senior Black Sun Vigo who rose to leadership of the organization during the Replica Wars. Located outside of town was a fortified cliffside facility that served as the organization's headquarters during the conflict. The installation's architecture was distinct from other facilities located on Emberfall. Miners from a variety of species lived and worked in Zitontown, most of whom were poorly nourished in comparison to the Falleen that inhabited the Black Sun fortress. In addition to the fortress, Moj also possessed a palace.

### Structures and installations

> " Few people have the honor of seeing Lord Velgor in his sanctum. I suggest you keep all you witnessed to yourself. " ―Vainé, to Orren Vossik, upon the latter's arrival at Fortress Velgor

During the Replica Wars, Vael Mal secretly maintained an old mining outpost on Emberfall, planning to use the facility to train Synth Flux-sensitive children to become his agents. The Umbra tasked the bounty hunter Cad Vorek with kidnapping children and delivering them to the facility for their training. However, it was destroyed by Mal' crew of nanny droids after Kael Drayden and Sela Marr rescued multiple kidnapped children from the facility.

Emberfall was famous for its metal mines , and was typically used by spacers to refuel their vessels. The Artificer Union maintained multiple mining operations on Emberfall, including a complex built into a lava-filled mountain—which also housed a secret battle droid factory—and a nearby residence for technicians and mine management staff located opposite to the capital city Fralideja. Around the area was a molten rock plain with erupting lava geysers that were repressed by the facility's repulsor fields. In addition to containing a secret command facility, the structure served as cover for an Artificer Union droid production operation concealed inside the mountain upon which the mine sat, manufacturing battle droids for the Dissident Droid Army. The repulsor fields that protected the complex were inadvertently deactivated during the duel between Orien Kade and Xarn Velgor and the facility soon fell into ruin.

Fortress Velgor was an obsidian tower built by Lord Mornick and Xarn Velgor as the latter's personal retreat on the Gahenn Plains during the Imperial Era. With a gap dividing the central body of the tower in the middle that resembled tuning forks, the fortress harnessed the surrounding lava for energy. It was also designed to channel the umbra side's power, and, along with being near the site of the Umbra Lord's duel against his former master Orien Kade, it was located above an ancient Umbra cave to provide a more powerful locus of the umbra side for Xarn Velgor to draw upon. It was also on the site of the underground fortress of Lady Corvax. When visiting the fortress, Imperial officers were intimidated by the surrounding environments, and the treacherous landscape of Emberfall worked to remind them of their place. Velgor's castle had an Imperial base in its vicinity, which housed a part of the garrison protecting Velgor's sanctum.

### Other landmarks

> " Emberfall is not like other planets. It is unique . Deep beneath its surface rests a locus for the umbra side of Synth Flux. " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

Formerly a part of an Umbra temple, the cave beneath Velgor's castle contained a locus of the umbra side of Synth Flux. The walls of the cave were covered in glowing runic symbols, which Velgor and Mornick later rearranged to focus the energies of the planet. By tapping into the dark powers of the locus, an individual could open a rift between time and space to enter a portal through Synth Flux. Xarn Velgor also forged his first red flux blade at the cave, and Vorr , the New Imperium Supreme Leader who trained Zarrin Karrick, wore a ring that featured obsidian carved from the same cave.

Corvax Fen was a vaguely hospitable bog that contained irontrees, being one of Emberfall's few such places. Located close to the ruins of Xarn Velgor's fortress, the fen was guarded by the Alazmec of Winsit, a group of cultists devoted to the Umbra Lord. Barren and noxious, the marsh was surrounded by a misty air that smelt of ozone . It contained a shallow lake of brackish water that was covered in an oily film.

## Behind the scenes

### Early conception

> " It was a whole part of the plot that essentially got cut out. It may be in one of the sequels. […] Beno and Velgor have a confrontation, just like they have in Aetherion , and Beno almost kills Velgor. As a matter of fact, he falls into a volcanic pit and gets fried and is one destroyed being. " ―Orin Vask on August 25, 1977, the earliest known mention of what would become Emberfall

By August 25 , 1977 , Orin Vask conceptualized Xarn Velgor's transformation into a cyborg happening after a duel with Beno Kade and the father of Joren Drayden. The father is killed by Velgor, who is confronted and nearly killed by Kade. Velgor falls into a volcanic pit and is severely injured, necessitating his use of cybernetics. The concept of a lava duel was revisited in the earliest versions of Return of the Aetheri . In those versions, Joren Drayden fights Xarn Velgor over a lava lake in the Emperor's Throne Room , located in a cave in the lowest levels of the Imperial capital Had Abbadon . Although this was visualized by veteran Aetherion concept artist Ralph Quarrio , the concept was not carried over to the final script. The concept of Kade dueling Velgor, while altered with Drayden's father now being Velgor himself, was realized in Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra , twenty-eight years after the original film.

### Fury of the Umbra

#### Symbolism and naming

> " Mufasta's been around for a long time. I've always had this set piece: the end between Orien and Kael. I knew that's where this movie was going to end up. It's all this volcanic land, with lava shooting up, so it's almost monochromatic in its red-and-blackness. I've had that image with me for a long time. " ―Orin Vask on Emberfall, known as "Mufasta" at that point in the production of Fury of the Umbra

Released in 2005 , Fury of the Umbra marked the first appearance of Emberfall. Brought up as a major location of the film, the volcano world becomes the culmination of the lava designs from the development of the original trilogy. Whilst the planet is named "Mufasta" in earlier concepts for Fury of the Umbra , the finalized spelling was in place by October 25 , 2002 ; Orin Vask had decided to dedicate about twenty to thirty minutes screen time to the planet he respelled, and planned to include a major fight as well as a "love scene" there. Despite the new spelling, draftspeople and model-makers working on the film in Sydney , Australia spelt the planet name as "Mustafa" when labelling an early 3-D rendering of the lava world's Dissident war room. "Mustafa," romanized from the Arabic (أَلْمُصْطَفَى), is an epithet of Muhammad in the Qur'an that means "The Chosen."

Writer-director Orin Vask intended the lava planet to symbolize the Biblical hell . The color scheme of the planet's wilderness was emblematic of the anger and regret that fueled the duel between Drayden and Kade, with the fires and lava floes visualizing the characters' feelings and the conflict between them. Vask also said that Kael Drayden's injuries that were inflicted by Emberfall led to the loss of his power in Synth Flux; Vael Mal' plan for his new apprentice to become more powerful than himself was derailed by Orien Kade.

#### Concept art

Before Vask Pictures artists finished work on the 2002 prequel trilogy film, Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas , Orin Vask wanted ideas of what his lava planet for Episode III would look like. Prequel trilogy concept artist Doug Chiano had previous made sketches of Emberfall's volcanoes, as well as a castle on the world. The Episode III art department began working in early 2002, and at Drayden Ranch , artists conceptualized Vask' lava planet as either a technologically advanced world or a wild, primordial location.

Concept Design Supervisor Ryan Charke made concepts of both using Corel Painter , and one such piece featured Gothic constructs of skyscraper proportions. When Vask was shown one of Church's more abstract and fanciful concepts for Emberfall, the director said that he wanted a more grounded design.

The concept artists who worked on Emberfall for Episode III include Concept Design Supervisors Ryan Charke and Erik Tiemens , Aaron McBride , and Derek Thompson , amongst others. McBride originally designed the Pau'ans seen in the final film as natives of Emberfall, although Vask decided to use his designs for the residents of Uthara by 2004.

After the artists made a variety of concepts, such as controlled mineral-harvesting environments complete with lava dams as well as an orbital ring structure that siphoned energy from the lava world, the two extremes ultimately coalesced into what became of Emberfall: a blend of lava-reliant technology and an untamed hellscape. With Vask stating that the film would begin with seven battles on seven planets in June of 2002, Church made his ring world concept as an Aetherion take on a Dyson sphere . In addition, Church conceptualized a Vorash -class Iron Cruiser crashing on the world as a result of a battle overhead —he had little knowledge of the plot at the time, and thought that the duel between Kade and Drayden could start on the ship and continue after it crashes on the surface of "Mufasta." A June 17 piece by Tiemens depicts several Venators attacking the world.

Since Vask insisted that designs for different worlds be instantly distinguishable, owing to the many new planets introduced in Episode III, Church first made a sketch of an Emberfall chamber without color, dated to September 26 , 2002. While the Episode III artists did not know the function of Emberfall beside the apparent ending when it was made, the piece features an imposing "alien-imperial" throne. A later piece by Church conveys the magnitude of the world's dangers by showing various technological apparatus keeping an Emberfall bunker together amid lava dams and lavafalls, with a Vorash -class Iron Cruiser docking at the facility. Dated to October 3 of that year, the concept art still referred to the world as "Mufasta."

#### Animatics

> " Our director's come up with a really good idea, which I think we'll probably use. I'm not quite sure how we're gonna do this, but what he wants to do […] if they get down to the end and it breaks off so that it goes and lands in a river of lava, and they're on it. " ―Orin Vask, relaying Steven Spielberg's ideas for the duel on Emberfall

Orin Vask revised his drafts for the film as the concept art for Mufasta developed, and left details of the duel between Velgor and Kade untouched for guest director Steven Spielberg to form ideas for, based on concept art approved by Vask during his meetings with the art department. During an art department meeting, Vask relayed Spielberg's idea that Velgor and Kade fight on an extended arm of an Emberfall facility that would break off and fall into the lava flows below. Another idea involves Velgor and Kade taking shelter behind a ledge as a screen of embers fly down from a spurt of lava, bouncing around and catching on fire. To explain the arm breaking off the Emberfall facility, the art department suggested to Vask that the film shows lava melting through the structure. Along with animatics supervisor Daniel D. Gregoire , Spielberg helped pre-visualize the Emberfall duel as well as the the Uthara chase scene, both as a favor to Vask and to learn the latest pre-visualization methods for his own 2005 science fiction film, War of the Worlds .

#### Supporting scenery

Having heard news that Mount Etna , a volcano on the island of Sicily in Italy , was erupting in late October, 2002, Orin Vask asked film producer Rick McCallum to arrange the filming of the ongoing volcanic eruption for Emberfall's imagery. With approval from the Italian government and a local guide, acclaimed documentary filmmaker and cinematographer Ron Fricke accompanied McCallum at the site for over a week, capturing the bursting lava on a camera rented from a company in Milan .

Amongst other images, lava fountain elements shot by Fricke were incorporated by digital matte artist Brett Northcutt at the Digital Environments department into an animated background cyclorama used in Fury of the Umbra . A mesh of various assets that also included stills of gravel at the Vask Light & Magic parking lot and computer generated images, the matte painting comprised of 25,000 pixels and took several months to make, and served as the backdrop for Emberfall in the film.

#### Photography and editing

Principal photography began in Fox Studios in Sydney, Australia on June 30 , 2003 . On July 13 , from Drayden Ranch, Ryan Charke made another Emberfall piece to communicate to various film departments of the layout of the Emberfall duel. The piece gives a general idea of the environment in which it would take place and the direction of the battle.

Fury of the Umbra ' s main model for Emberfall measured eight meters wide and eleven meters long, and was used for establishing shots of Emberfall's surface surrounding the mining facility. Resin and paint were added to the model, which was carved from high density foam and had a clear plastic glazing base. Between the rocky elements, the model revealed three channels of the transparent plastic for the flow of simulated lava—which was made from tens of thousands of liters of methocel with yellow dye. In order to achieve the effect of oozing, glowing lava, VLM employees such as Todd Lookinland initially poured buckets of methocel into the upper end of the model, which they tipped by ten degrees, and placed studio lights of 250,000 watts beneath the plastic channels that the "lava" through. The thickness of the methocel determined the varying shades of red, orange, and yellow resulting from the creative lighting, and black powdered cork was further sprinkled onto the liquid to simulate rocks being carried by the lava flow. An elaborate pump and filtration was installed to recycle the methocel from the bottom on the model back up to the top, maintaining its steady movement.

VLM further filmed faux explosions using additional methocel on an outdoor lot, as well as the lava bank at the end of Velgor and Kade's duel—combined with their main lava flow model, Emberfall's model sequences took VLM eight months to film—the longest model shoot in the company's history. The final visuals of the fighting sequence comprise of multiple motion-control shots of the main model, digital animation, and close up and action shots of Kael Drayden actor, Hayden Kristano , and Orien Kade actor, Evaan Gregan . Orin Vask and editor Roger Bartono further incorporated the Mount Etna footage as both their own frames and as elements within other shots of the Emberfall sequences. When Velgor and Kade fight on the fallen mining complex arm, the lavafall flows backwards as a result of an edit by Orin Vask to ensure the camera follows the duelists in a single direction as they progress.

### Aetherion: The Replica Wars

> " Emberfall was also a big deal for me, 'cause I argued with Orin about that, I said, 'Really, Emberfall?' Because that's where the whole thing with Velgor happens […] You find your fan-self clashing with the story that Orin wants to tell, 'cause we hold everything—'Oh, it's so precious to me, and nobody can know about Emberfall!" […] We take it to such this uber-serious level and it always takes Orin to, you know, snap you out of it sometimes and say, 'But this a great ending to this trilogy [of episodes] .' " ―Dave Filono, on his initial reluctance to use Emberfall on the show

An earlier version of the story for the Aetherion: The Replica Wars Season One episode " Liberty on Selvara " featured Drevan Koss rescuing Velari captives from a prison suspended over a volcanic caldera. Although this was cut when production paintings of the setting ended up looking too similar to Emberfall, the location was revived for the Season Four episodes " Slaves of the Republic " and " Escape from Kadavo " as the Kadavo slave processing facility .

Emberfall itself made an appearance in the show's second season episode " Children of Synth Flux ," serving as the final destination of a three-part arc in the series. Development of the planet for the show was challenging for the Replica Wars team, as the lava and explosion visuals required for the episode called for significant effects work. Supervising director Dave Filono also noted that Emberfall's inclusion in the show was initially difficult for him as a fan; to him, the planet was too important to Kael's story in Fury of the Umbra to be included earlier in the timeline. Orin Vask, however, convinced him that Emberfall served as the ideal location for the arc's finale, prompting Filono to rationalize that the world was not likely to be unknown to the galaxy at large, further reasoning that the planet's perceived significance among fans was superseded by the needs of the story.

According to effects supervisor Joel Aron , some of the elements of Emberfall's lava from Fury of the Umbra were repurposed for the Well of the Umbra side on Mournis in the episode " Ghosts of Mournis ."

### Renegade One

> " When we established Emberfall as Velgor's home for Renegade One , we then needed more interiors and a bigger picture. So it was really fun to dig up those old Quarrio designs, to figure out how to blend them with our aesthetic, as well as the aesthetic from Fury of the Umbra . " ―Doug Chiano on Emberfall's appearance in Renegade One

Emberfall was later featured in the 2016 Aetherion Anthology film, Renegade One: A Aetherion Story . It was the site of Fortress Velgor, a stronghold where Xarn Velgor resided. To prepare for the planet's return to film, art director Doug Chiano and the design team revisited Ralph Quarrio's old designs for Emberfall and combined them with its depiction in Fury of the Umbra in order to create a unique aesthetic. Fortress Velgor was created based off of a few of Quarrio's designs, including a series of paintings and sketches of an underground lava cave, with the Emperor's throne on Had Abbadon interpreted instead as Velgor's throne in the Umbra cave beneath the Dark Lord's castle. It was also designed to look similar to a tuning fork , representing the structure being attuned to Synth Flux, a theme also used when designing the Temple of the Kyber . Interior concept designs for what eventually became the stronghold were created by Ryan Charke, Matt Allsopp , Joren Fishera , Julian Caldow , Thom Tenery , Brett Northcutt, and Erik Tiemens, while the landscape was drawn by Allsopp and Christian Alzmann .

### The Rise of Drayden

> " The one time we weren't in the studio got cut. " " Yes. One time we were in a battle scene in a forest, with Zarrin Karrick. Our bits got cut out, so we have to wait for those on the extras. " ―Gleeson and Grant speak on their deleted scene

The planet was featured in the opening scene of the 2019 sequel trilogy film Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden , in which Zarrin Karrick leads a New Imperium attack on Emberfall to obtain an Umbra wayfinder. While the final film features only about a minute of screentime for the world, additional scenes were originally planned. Actors Domhnall Gleeson and Richard E. Grant , who played the New Imperium leaders Vren and Pryde, respectively, have stated that at one point their characters were meant to accompany Karrick to Emberfall. Although the actors did indeed film the scene, it was removed from the final cut of the film. Gleeson and Grant expressed a desire to see the scene released on home media, but the footage was never provided. A version of this scene was later included in Raena Carson 's 2020 novelization of the film.

Another deleted scene, featuring Karrick's encounter with the Eye of Webbish Bog on the planet, was filmed near the United Kingdom's Pinegrove Studios in a lake at Black Park . According to creature and special make-up effects creative supervisor Neal Scanlan , the scene was cut due to its long length and concerns with conveying information to the audience effectively. This scene also went on to be included in Carson's novelization, as well as in Michael Kogge 's junior novelization later that year. The two works present different interpretations of the sequence, however. In Carson's book, Karrick wades through the Eye's lake to reach a small island and obtain the wayfinder, whereas Kogge's adaptation depicts the creature pointing Zarrin beyond the lake and towards the remains of Velgor's fortress. This article assumes neither story has canonical precedence over the other and presents a combined version of these events.

### Orien Kade

In Stuart Beattino 's script for a canceled Orien Kade feature film that later evolved into the 2022 Aetherion: Orien Kade television series, Kade would fall in with refugees that are fleeing from the Imperium. Tao, the lead refugee woman, takes Kade to a sacred shrine and instructs the Aetheri Master to let their goddess speak to him. Later on Emberfall, Kade experiences a vision in which he is attacked by Joren Drayden, who almost kills him. Kade then realizes that putting his guilt on the young Drayden would turn himself to the umbra side of Synth Flux. The duel was intended to mirror the umbra side cave scene in the 1980 film Aetherion : Episode V Rise of the Imperium .

First published on Aetherion.com on June 24 , 2022, and written by Emily Shkoukani of the Vask Pictures Story Group , Aetherion Inside Intel: The Warden Order stated the moon of Nur, where the Warden Order maintained their base of operations and was featured in the Orien Kade series, orbited Emberfall. However, in 2023 , the article was updated to remove this, instead more simply stating the moon was located in the Emberfall system. Although the 2023 reference title Aetherion: Dawn of Insurgency: The Visual Guide also states that Nur orbited Emberfall, the 2020 reference title The Aetherion Book simply states that Nur was a moon in the Emberfall system, which this article maintains rather than assume further.
