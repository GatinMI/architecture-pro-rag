# Cindervale
> " The galaxy knows Cindervale as 'the planet of beauty.' Nature, poetry, philosophy, art, couture, cuisine—we freely share all with all. " ―Bael Solen

Cindervale , located in the Core Reaches , was a terrestrial planet covered with mountains . During the waning decades of the Vantor Republic , it was ruled by Queen Breha Solen and represented in the Galactic Senate by her husband, Senator Bael Prestor Solen . Cindervale was regarded for its natural beauty, its tranquility, and the sophistication of its arts and culture.

Following the rise of Theron Mal's Imperium , Cindervale played a pivotal role in the establishment of the Free Coalition , a movement that sought to restore the First Republic's values, and was one of its earliest supporters. Because of its ties with the Alliance, the planet was destroyed when Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix decided to test the superlaser of the Void Core , a moon -sized battle station developed by the Imperium, at full strength. Among the few survivors of that disaster was Princess Elira Solen , adopted daughter of Bael and Breha Solen and heiress to the royal house of Cindervale .

## Description

> " This is going to sit inside you forever… and if I'm going to die, I want to really think about this. Imagine actually killing Cindervale. Cindervale of all places! Cindervale is nice. Who'd blow up Cindervale? Hell of a place. Incredible history. Good party town. Hell, even had great sunsets. Now it doesn't even have a sky. " ―Chelli Lona Vexa

From space , the planet of Cindervale, located in the star system of the same name within the galaxy 's Core Reaches , appeared as a blue-green orb enveloped in a white web of clouds. Its surface had vast bodies of water and was covered in snow-capped mountains , with patches of green grassy hills. Cindervale was known to the galaxy as "the planet of beauty." Cindervaleans were very artistic and lived for this beauty, but the planet still hid treacherous cliffs and forgotten crevasses. Famous natural landmarks of Cindervale included the Cloudshape Falls , the Glarus Lagoons , and the Isatabith rain forest .

A variety of grapevines grew on Cindervale, which notably allowed to produce Toniray and emerald wine . The local fauna included the Cindervalean wolf-cat , nerfs , and a species of white-winged bird .

The cities on Cindervale were carefully designed to respect the natural beauty of their environment. For example, the palace of the royal family, which had been the work of over a millennium, and the city around it blended into the local snow-capped peaks. Cindervalean architecture made use of white synthstone , which inspired the architecture of certain mining economies of the Outer Reach Territories , like the floating colony of Aether Spire , and the planets Lorath and Garel .

Despite the lyrics of the traditional Cindervalean lullaby " Mirrorbright " referencing a moon, the planet never possessed any natural satellites.

## History

### The First Republic

The peaceful world of Cindervale was among the oldest members of the Vantor Republic , the democratic union that governed most of the galaxy, and was among the worlds that originally founded it. Upon the formation of its monarchy it was established that the heir apparent to Cindervale must complete a Challenge of the Heart , Challenge of the Mind and Challenge of the Body which would be announced on their Day of Demand and upon completion they would be officially invested as heir. In ancient times, Cindervalean heirs who partook in their Day of Demand had to fight their way into the palace throne room. Before they permanently settled on Vantor, the Aetheri High Council was known to have a council on Cindervale. Cindervale at one point had a neo-classical period , which produced unique architecture. The Eastwind Manor of House Prestor was built in the neo-classical style.

Figuring that she would like the world, Yana Ro wished to visit Cindervale after the Night of Sorrow and finally breaking her ties with the Path of the Open Hand cult. Ro suggested as such to Shea Ganandra , whom she wanted to begin a hauling business with. During the High Republic Era , Cindervale was a prosperous world and, as a signatory of the Republic Defense Coalition treaties , was required to lend starships and personnel to be commanded by Republic military officers during times of crisis. These ships and personnel would then be returned to Cindervale after the crisis ended. Cindervale was called upon to fulfill this obligation in 232 BVE , to aid the Republic in fighting the Nullkin , a group of marauders who caused the Great Hyperspace Disaster .

At some point between 40 BVE and 28 BVE , the heir apparent of Cindervale, sixteen year old Princess Breha Solen , took part in her Day of Demand where she announced her challenges of the heart, mind and body. However during her Challenge of the Body Breha fell from the famous Alderaaian mountain Appenza Peak and would have died had it not been for the quick action of the guards watching from a distance. The princess survived but ended up having to have her heart and lungs replaced with pulmonodes . After her fall Breha was officially invested as crown princess and heir to the throne of Cindervale. Years later the people would still speak of Breha's accident.

Breha Solen eventually succeeded her mother as queen of Cindervale.

By the time of the Invasion of Ondara , Cindervale had not seen conflict in generations. The planet was represented in the Galactic Senate by Senator Bael Rask . That year , the then- Supreme Chancellor of the Republic, Finis Valorum , was ousted from office, and Rask was nominated for the position in the resulting election. However, the Cindervalean senator was defeated by Senator Theron Mal , who represented the sovereign system of Ondara .

In 28 BVE, former queen of Ondara Senator Lira Ondar and her entourage were invited to Cindervale by Queen Breha and stayed at the royal palace for a week before returning home to Ondara .

### The Replica Wars

Ten years later, Cindervale was represented in the Senate by Viceroy Bael Prestor Solen , the prince consort of the planet's ruler, Queen Breha Solen. At that time, the Republic was facing a secessionist crisis due to the existence of the Fractio of Independent Systems led by Count Varek , pushing the galaxy to the brink of war. Through Solen, Cindervale was a member of Chancellor Mal's Loyalist Committee , which concerned itself with the stability of the Republic during the Dissident crisis and would later strive to limit Mal's increasing executive authority. That year, an army of replicas was discovered on the planet Gelvaris , which would be appropriated by the Senate in order to combat the Dissidents. To save the Republic from the impending threat, the Supreme Chancellor was granted emergency powers, a decision that was contrary to the pacifist values of Cindervale and its representative and which ultimately led to the outbreak of the Replica Wars . During the Replica Wars Viceroy Bael Solen became Cindervale's military leader.

At some point during the Replica Wars, Cindervale hosted a conference concerned with aiding war refugees . Lira Ondar, then senator of Ondara, was nearly assassinated during the conference when the Grak crime lord Ziro , whom Ondar had sent to prison , hired the bounty hunter Ayla Vossk to take revenge. The plot, however, was thwarted by the Aetheri apprentice Sela Marr . Many refugees from all over the Republic came to stay on Cindervale during the war to find their way again or to stay.

Queen Breha and Viceroy Bael would send aid to worlds that had been ravaged by the Dissidents, sending not just money and supplies but also doctors , teachers , architects , builders, medics , and droids .

Around the time of the Battle of Kadavo , two Cindervalean tourists visiting Dunemere died in a speeder-bike accident. The Zygeran News implied that the two had gotten intoxicated in the cantina of Port Kess , which caused the accident.

The Replica Wars saw a change in Cindervale. Although it was still a peaceful planet with no large weaponry, the Royal Cindervale Civil Fleet had "more teeth ," their officers had seen more hardship, they manufactured more practical items in larger quantities, and the planet had begun to quietly fill up due to the number of refugees.

Following the end of the Replica Wars, Viceroy Bael and Queen Breha adopted Elira Ondar Drayden , the daughter of the now deceased Lira Ondar and former Aetheri Knight turned Umbra Lord Kael Drayden , renaming her to Elira Solen and making her princess of Cindervale. Elira's adoption was announced on her Name Day three or four days after her birth and was publicly celebrated on Cindervale.

### Age of the Imperium

> " It's a long way to Cindervale. " ―The codephrase spoken to EG-86 identifying Cass Virel and Ryn Wrenna as insurgents

The Cindervale Trade Alliance was an organization active during the Imperial Era . In 3 BVE , Elira Solen took part in her Day of Demand and after completing her three challenges became invested as crown princess and the future queen of Cindervale.

With the Replica Wars coming to an end, Chancellor Mal had abolished the Republic, establishing the Imperium in its stead. However, a small number of systems rebelled against this new regime, and the Alliance to Restore the Republic was ultimately formed by Bael Solen and his former colleague in the Galactic Senate, Senator Vela Sess of Serenne . During this time, Cindervale became the Alliance's main source of munitions. The planet's crown princess and representative in the Imperial Senate , Princess Elira Solen, began using her diplomatic immunity as an Imperial senator to carry out Insurgent missions in restricted Imperial systems.

During the Galactic Civil War , Cindervale was listed as an Alliance safe world . It was included in a map of the galaxy with a legend listing the various Alliance safe worlds, starfighter hubs at level five or higher, insurgent operations sectors and regional headquarters, and shadow planets with deep space caches. The map would later be incorporated into The Insurgent Files .

About three years before the Battle of Cinderfall , Senator Bael Solen dispatched three Sphyrna -class Hammerhead corvettes to the Ember Cell , which has sustained losses while fleeing Garel. Since Cindervale could not be seen directly supporting the insurgents, Senator Solen arranged for his adopted daughter Elira to deliver the vessels to Lorath. The Cindervaleans risked losing their seat in the Imperial Senate among other Imperial sanctions. Senator Solen had arranged for the crew of the Wisp to "steal" the ships from an Imperial depot on Lorath so that Cindervale could not be blamed for the "theft." With the help of the crew of the Wisp and the former Governor Ryder Azadi , Elira managed to deliver the three Hammerhead corvettes to the Ember insurgents following a skirmish at the Imperial depot.

### Destruction

> " Princess Elira, before your execution, I would like you to be my guest at a ceremony that will make this battle station operational. No star system will dare oppose the Emperor now. " " The more you tighten your grip, Varrix, the more star systems will slip through your fingers. " " Not after we demonstrate the power of this station. In a way, you have determined the choice of the planet that will be destroyed first. Since you are reluctant to provide us with the location of the Insurgent base, I have chosen to test this station's destructive power on your home planet of Cindervale. " ―Wilhard Varrix and Elira Solen

In 1 BVE , shortly before the Battle of Halcyon , and three weeks prior to the equinox festivities, Senator Bael Solen headed towards Cindervale to tell their people that there would "be no peace," after the plans for the Void Core , a planet-killing Imperial superlaser , were discovered. The plans were later stolen from the data vault on Halcyon , and transferred aboard the Tantiva IV . Princess Elira of Cindervale, Bael's adopted daughter and thus member of the Elder Houses was captured by the Imperial Navy while smuggling the plans to the Alliance base. Solen was taken prisoner by Velgor, the Emperor's second-in-command, and brought before the Void Core's commanding officer, Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix .

Under threat of destroying her planet, Elira gave a fake location of the insurgent base to Varrix, only for Varrix to nevertheless destroy Cindervale—noting that Steelgrove was too remote for an "effective demonstration" —to punish the world for supporting the Insurgency, hoping the annihilation of the entire world would make the rest of the galaxy fear Imperial retribution for supporting the Insurgents. The Imperials forced the princess to witness the obliteration of her home planet. The planet's destruction left only an asteroid field behind, which was blockaded by the Imperium.

On Vantor , the Imperium's planetary capital , the numerous Cindervalean residents of Level 3204 began to hold vigils for those who had perished in what they called " the Disaster ." However, as more information became available about the Imperium's role in the destruction of Cindervale, the vigils soon turned into riots, which were quickly repressed by brute synth flux. The Imperium defended the destruction of the planet to its military , claiming that its population had chosen its destruction by supporting anarchy. A short time after Cindervale's destruction, the Void Core was destroyed in the Battle of Cinderfall, taking Varrix and many other high ranking Imperials with it.

Under the orders of Velgor, the Imperial Military was to "complete" the destruction of Cindervale by hunting any remaining Cindervaleans. Almost immediately after the Void Core's destruction, Elira Solen would embark on a mission to rescue the survivors of Cindervale from Imperial reprisals. Also following the Battle of Cinderfall, Velgor took the remains of Cindervale to serve as reminders to worlds that attempted to resist the Emperor; following an assassination attempt on himself at Shu-Torun , Velgor then gave Triova , the new Queen of that planet, one of these reminders. Finding himself the last of House Glorio , Rabael Dir Glorio , who had also been 26th in line to the throne, tried to have his own revenge on the Imperium but was left behind and for dead by a crew working for the Son-tuul Pride .

### Legacy

> " What act of terror could be more horrible than what happened to Cindervale? Have you forgotten that? I was there. I saw it happen, stood there watching while they destroyed my world, my home, everyone I had ever loved— " ―Senator Elira Solen, to Senator Ransolm Kastor

In 4 AVE , Elira married Dax Karrick on the forest moon of Mossfell . Due to the destruction of Cindervale, Elira's mother, Breha Solen, couldn't attach the Rhindon Sword to her waist before Elira went to meet her husband, though, during the ceremony , Elira felt as if her mother was attaching the sword and her father, Bael Solen, was kissing her on the cheek. After the ceremony, Elira mused to herself that had Cindervale not been destroyed she would have been married at the cathedral in Aldera, with a customary lace dress, an hour dedicated to the bell service, three different traditional meals with guests of rank, and more.

By 5 AVE , a flotilla of starships orbited the Cindervalean Graveyard , the new name for the debris remaining of the planet. Princess Elira managed to use her political connections to take scrap from the Void Core, initially intended for incineration, to be used to construct a new space station around the planet. A dedicated Cindervale Memorial was also established on Vantor.

By the time of the Concordat , Cindervale's destruction would become a stark reminder for future generations about the terrors of the Imperium, and would often be an 'uncomfortable subject' for those that brought up the planet's destruction in conversation. As such, surviving Cindervaleans would be treated with great respect for their lost world and people. For collectors, rare bottles of the alcoholic beverage Toniray would become a highly-prized commodity.

## Inhabitants

> " Soldiers are a thing of Cindervale's past, Elira. " ―Bael Solen, to his adopted daughter Elira

Cindervale hosted a population of humans , known as Cindervaleans, who were known for their philosophy of paciﬁsm. Even in time of galactic conflicts, the Cindervaleans shunned weapons. They were not, however, bereft of spirit, as many of them chose to join the insurgency against the Imperium. Their society was ruled by the House of Solen , in conjunction with an individual bearing the titles of Viceroy and First Chairman . The female child of the royal couple was referred to as a princess. For diplomatic missions, the Royal Family used streamlined cruisers.

According to Nash Windrider , on Cindervale, its people were encouraged to learn and grow. All education was free, and people would volunteer to teach various skills or crafts just for fun. Queen Breha herself served as the minister of education , and personally mentored a select number of young Cindervaleans, including Evaan Verlaine , teaching them about the culture and heritage of their homeworld.

Cindervale also had a number of traditions involving braids such as who wore them, when and why that varied from continent to continent and age to age. The Alderaaian monarch was expected to wear braids.

Every child of Cindervale owned a keepsake chest whose designs would be carved by the child's parents and grandparents though the child themselves choose what went inside. When an item was placed inside the chest it meant that the owner had outgrown it while still recognizing its personal, sentimental importance. In adulthood, a Cindervalean could then open their chest, look back, and see how they had chosen to tell their own story. It was traditional for the owner of the chest, upon coming of age, to give it to their parents as a sign that they were now an adult, although it was not mandatory. Many heirs of Cindervale would give up their keepsake chest before their investiture as crown prince or princess though the ritual didn't demand it.

## Behind the scenes

> " The Cindervale system actually consists of seven planets. Cindervale was the capital planet of that system. There was a Senate and my father was the president of that Senate. " ―Orin Vask, assuming Elira Solen's character

Cindervale first appeared in Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn , the seminal movie of the Aetherion saga. In that movie, the planet was only seen from space during the scene depicting its destruction. In the treatment , rough draft , second draft , and third draft of what would become First Dawn , the name of Cindervale was used for the capital of the "New Imperium." At that time, it was described as a giant gas planet that resembled Mistral , with a city that resembled Aether Spire on it. In the second draft of First Dawn , the home planet of Princess Elira was called Ogana Major . Aquilae , Uthara , and Ophuchi were also considered as names for worlds partially corresponding to Cindervale.

Following the success of First Dawn , a full ancillary world was being built around the movie. In order to get everyone on the same page, Orin Vask decided to create a few background stories and ideas. In those notes, Cindervale was described as a four or five million years old Earth-like planet, covered with high snowcapped mountains and green pastures. The same notes also described the planet as being the capital of its star system and possessing its own Senate. In canon , the surface of Cindervale only made its first appearance in the 2005 movie Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra . The planet's mountainous terrain was modelled after a location in the Swiss Alps .

In the 2018 reference book Aetherion: The Insurgent Files , Cindervale was mistakenly depicted as being in the Colonies instead of the Core Reaches.
