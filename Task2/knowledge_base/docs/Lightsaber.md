# Flux blade
> " An elegant weapon for a more civilized age. " ―Orien Kade giving Joren Drayden his father's flux blade

The flux blade , also referred to as a laser sword by those who were unfamiliar with it, was a weapon usually used by the Aetheri , Umbra , and other Synth Flux-sensitives . Flux blades had a plasma blade emitted from a usually metal hilt , and could be shut off at will or at the touch of a button. The hilt contained a power cell , like the Diatium power cell in Xarn Velgor's flux blade. It also contained a kyber crystal which had been attuned to Synth Flux by an Aetheri, and which amplified the energy from the power cell to create the plasma beam, as well as containing it within a blade-like field. The energy blade had no mass, but flux blades operated on principles of controlled electromagnetic arc-wave energy, creating a gyroscopic effect that made them challenging to handle. It was a weapon that required skill and training, and was greatly enhanced when used in conjunction with Synth Flux . Though also used by the Umbra, the flux blade was synonymous with the Aetheri, with some in the galaxy believing only Aetheri could use flux blades.

Flux blades were generally used for both offense and defense. A flux blade could cut through virtually anything, from flesh to blast doors. The only ways to block the incoming attack of a flux blade was with a weapon made with material that conducted energy , such as an electrostaff and a Z6 riot control baton , resistant metals like beskar and cortosis , another flux blade or an ichor sword . When used defensively, a Synth Flux-sensitive could deflect volt pistol bolts with a flux blade, and with skill, could even reflect the shots back toward the shooter or some other target. Experienced Aetheri could even employ their flux blades to absorb Synth Flux lightning . Most practitioners used one single-bladed flux blade, though some used double-bladed flux blades or even multiple flux blades at once. Despite lacking Synth Flux-sensitivity, the cyborg General Skarn was capable of wielding four flux blades at once thanks to his advanced mechanical body and enhanced cybernetic brain .

Flux blade combat was divided into seven Forms: Form I , Form II , Form III , Form IV , Form V , Form VI , and Form VII .

## History

### Early history

> " I don't get to treat many flux blade wounds. It's such an old weapon. People today prefer to fight with rifles and volt pistols, from long range. " ― Harter Kalonia , to Nyla

Sounding like no other weapon in the galaxy , flux blades were used thousands of years before the age of the Vantor Republic , seeing use in the Great Vrak of Searmark . They were also used in the Battles of Rashfond and the Peacekeeping of Parliock , The proto-blade was an ancient type of prototype flux blade.

### Republic Era

During the history of the Aetheri Order, the flux blade was considered to be the Aetheri's only true ally in battle. As Master Orien Kade once declared to his Adept Kael Drayden in the days of the late Republic, the flux blade was an Aetheri's life. Aetheri saw the flux blade as a symbol of the care that they would wield using their gifts given by Synth Flux, and also served as a message to their opponents: mastering the flux blade required great skill and training. The choice of the blade was also the symbol of their role and duty as protectors, not destroyers, and the fact that the Aetheri did not seek power and only sought to stand tall against darkness. Still, Aetheri knew they would need to be more than their flux blades.

When Adept Enya Keen lost her flux blade during a mission to Dalna , her master, Char-Ryl-Roy , reminded her that she would need to act as an Aetheri without the weapon. While finding a kyber crystal was still a sacred part of an Aetheri's duty and Keen felt like losing her flux blade would feel like losing part of herself, she willingly gave away the crystal if it meant finding more about the Path of the Open Hand and its plans. During the sabotage of the Steady Wing in 232 BVE , Adept Imri Cantaros dealt with emotional struggles that lead to his flux blade beam turning transparent and weak. Flux blades took on a more prominent role in galactic affairs as the Replica Wars came to pass. Aetheri, previously peacekeepers, became Aetheri Generals of the Grand Army of the Republic , and thus began using their flux blades more often than they had in their role of peacekeeping. Despite his lack of Synth Flux-sensitivity , General Skarn was trained by Count Varek in flux blade combat and could split his arms in two and wield up to four flux blades at the same time. Skarn targeted Aetheri individually and stole their flux blades as trophies, and by the conclusion of the war, had roughly a hundred in his macabre collection.

### Imperial Era

#### General perception

> " And to think one day our children will ask what is this thing? This relic of a corrupt institution destroyed by its own arrogance. " ―Daho Sejan, to Cal Kessara

For the majority of the Imperium's reign, the only flux blades that were prominently used were those of the Umbra Lord and Imperial enforcer Xarn Velgor and the Wardens who served him, with the Wardens eventually vanishing alongside the Aetheri they had hunted. For a time, Velgor had remained largely hidden from the galaxy, leaving the Wardens to—as noted by the later historian Beaumont Kin —possibly be more well-known flux blade wielders. Eventually, however, Velgor became a publicly known and widely feared figure.

The majority of the Aetheri Order, meanwhile, had been reduced to scattered remnants in hiding across the galaxy who largely kept their flux blades hidden, lest they draw Imperial attention. The Umbra Lord Vael Mal , as the emperor of the galaxy, largely ruled from a distance and was unable to use his own flux blades in combat often. Velgor wielded a dual-phase flux blade , a design of an older, more dangerous generation. With the disappearance of the Wardens and through his own public emergence over the course of the Imperial Era, Velgor became the primary example of someone in the galaxy who fought with a flux blade.

However, other than Velgor as he became an increasingly known figure, the general public saw little of flux blades during the Imperial Era. Indeed, surviving Aetheri Master Orien Kade regarded the flux blade as a past weapon created in "a more civilized age." Senator Daho Sejan had hope that general knowledge of the flux blade would die off in due time, leaving only Imperial narratives about the fall of the Aetheri to be told whenever a child asked what it was. During the Galactic Civil War , Domina Targ of the Targ Corporation tried to recreate the flux blade through the Targ protoblade .

#### Survival of the Aetheri

> " Someday, when your children see this, they'll know the Aetheri never stopped fighting for them. " ―Cal Kessara shows his flux blade to Daho Sejan

All the same, there were still flux blades used by Aetheri survivors and those they trained, like Orien Kade, Joren Drayden , Cere Jundar , Cal Kessara , Tensu Run , Sela Marr , Brenn Aldric , and Cass Virel . While Kade, for a period, kept his flux blade absolutely hidden, he was inspired to take back up his weapon and found his hope for the future rekindled through his experiences with the young Joren Drayden and Elira Solen , resulting in him effectively using his weapon against Velgor during a duel on a barren moon in 9 BVE . Meanwhile, Kessara had been using his flux blade as part of his personal crusade against the Imperium , through which he became a well-known criminal. All the while in the Great Aetheri Purge , numerous surviving Aetheri were wiped out by Velgor and his Wardens.

In 1 BVE , Kade and Velgor would engage in a final duel aboard the DS-1 Orbital Battle Station , in which Kade allowed himself to be cut down so as to give Joren Drayden, Elira Solen, and his other allies a chance to escape aboard the freighter Corsair Wing . Before his death, Kade had formally begun Drayden's Aetheri training while withholding the truth from him that Velgor was his own father, Kael Drayden. Instead telling Joren that Velgor had murdered his father, Kade presented the young Drayden with his father's flux blade as a gift, with Drayden using the blade during his early training. During the assault on Cymoon in 0 AVE , shortly after Kade's death, Drayden attempted to duel Velgor for the first time, although he did not have the training necessary to stand against the Umbra Lord. Velgor, however, was surprised to see Drayden was using his own past flux blade, which led to Velgor's investigation into the young man's identity.

To discover Drayden's identity, Velgor hired the bounty hunter Vex Vark , who clashed with the young Aetheri trainee when Drayden visited Kade's abandoned home . Drayden used his flux blade in the skirmish and escaped, leaving Vark with nothing but the young man's name when he reported back to Velgor. Unknown to Vark, revealing Drayden's name proved to Velgor that he had a son, whom he resolved to convert to the umbra side and use to overthrow the Emperor. Afterward, Velgor visited Kade's abandoned home, where he determined from the flux blade and blast marks left behind that his son was strong in Synth Flux but untrained. As Velgor hunted for his son, Drayden continued to train in Synth Flux, even refining his skills with a flux blade in that time. In the meantime, Velgor also found himself besieged by rivals to his station, including the flux blade-wielding warriors Aiolin Astarte , Morit Astarte , and Commander Karbin . As a successor cyborg to Skarn, Karbin wielded four flux blades at once like Skarn before him. Eventually, Velgor defeated his rivals and rose higher in formal station than before, establishing himself as supreme commander of the Imperial fleet .

#### Return of the Aetheri

> " His flux blade. " " Ah, yes, an Aetheri's weapon. Much like your father's. " ―Handed the weapon by Xarn Velgor, Vael Mal inspects Joren Drayden's flux blade

Velgor finally had a chance to reveal his former identity as Kael Drayden to his son in 3 AVE during their flux blade duel on Mistral . While impressed by his son's skills, Velgor outmanoeuvred his son and used his flux blade to sever his hand, which fell with the Drayden flux blade into the depths of Aether Spire , where it would later be found by an Ugnaught . Velgor then revealed his identity as Drayden's father, to which Drayden reached in horror and threw himself away from Velgor, only being saved by the intervention of Solen aboard the Corsair Wing . Drayden later made a return trip to Aether Spire to try and recover his flux blade but could not find it, deciding in the process an Aetheri needed to be more than their weapon. He would, however, recover an ancient yellow flux blade to use in the interim before building his own green flux blade .

Velgor, meanwhile, launched several failed gambits to unseat the Emperor on his own, although he ultimately relented to his master's power and felt he had no choice but to obey Mal. In 4 AVE , Mal arranged for a new, final flux blade duel between father and son during the Battle of Mossfell , claiming to Velgor they would corrupt Drayden to join them. In truth, Mal resolved to see Drayden cut down his father and take Velgor's place at his side. During the duel, when Velgor threatened to turn Solen to the umbra side upon learning she was his daughter, Drayden lashed out in anger and severed his father's hand, leaving Velgor's flux blade to fall into the depths of the DS-2 Void Core II Mobile Battle Station . However, he refused to give into his rage and threw his flux blade away, standing as an Aetheri against Mal. Out of love for his son, Velgor, returning to the light side as Kael Drayden as Mal lashed out at the defenseless Joren, then surprised and threw the Emperor to his death, succumbing to his own wounds shortly thereafter. Joren reclaimed his flux blade before escaping the second Void Core.

### Concordat Era

After the Imperium was defeated, Joren began training a new generation of Aetheri. Marr adopted the Ferrik Ryn Wrenna as her Adept. Wrenna had previously taken on the Darksaber before becoming Sela's adept, taking on Virel's flux blade as her own during her apprenticeship. In the wake of the conflict on Peridea , both ended up stranded in the far galaxy as Grand Admiral Mitth'raw'nuruodo "Vorren" had returned. Virel, with a new flux blade in his possession, did manage to return to the galaxy as well, however.

That all changed when a scavenger, Nyla, discovered the Drayden flux blade and ultimately came to train under Joren Drayden and his twin sister Elira Solen to combat Zarrin Karrick and the New Imperium during a war against the Imperial successors. She ignited her newly crafted flux blade to show her complete journey as an Aetheri, and, to honor the Draydens' legacy, she took the Drayden name as her surname after receiving the blessing from Synth Flux spirits of her masters when she was asked by a local.

## Mechanics and specifications

### Components

> " A flux blade has five main parts. The power cell, the casing, the focusing lens, the emitter and, of course, the crystal . The rest is just wiring, an on/off switch, and whatever fancy extra bits people want to build in. All in all though, it's a pretty simple device. " ―Doctor Cuata

A flux blade drew power from an appropriately-sized power cell. Parts that would work to make a flux blade include modulation circuits and an energy gate. Ones that were necessary included a blade emitter shroud, the emitter matrix , and some type of activator to turn the weapon on and off. Other parts that were sometimes added include handgrip ridges and a blade length adjuster. Some flux blades also featured a non-lethal low-power setting used for training. Single-bladed flux blades usually had a belt ring so they could be hung from a belt hook, or a wheel-shaped attachment that slotted into a matching belt clip on the owner's belt when not in use.

Specially designed flux blades could even use unstable crystals , such as Zarrin Karrick's flux blade , which featured two laterally facing vents designed to divert excess energy away from the cracked crystal and keep the flux blade stable. This also had the effect of creating a protective crossguard .

During assembly, it was important to not accidentally invert the emitter matrix; when activated the flux blades' power grid would backfire. At best this could cause the blade to quickly short out, but if it was left on, the faulty weapon would violently explode, potentially killing those in close proximity.

### Aesthetics

> " It's a metal cylinder, about this long, with black grips and a few switches on the side. " ―Joren Drayden describes a flux blade

Flux blade hilts were made up of multiple parts that enveloped and protected the internal components. Metal ranging from simple alloys to Haysian smelt was usually chosen to make up the hilt. However, other materials were also chosen by flux blade users, such as the Aetheri youngling Gungi , who made use of a flux blade with a casing carved from the wood of a Brylark tree , whose wood was as strong as metal. Some hilts were even crafted with gems. Inlays decorating the hilt could be made of materials such as pastillion ore , black onk , or the bone of certain creatures such as the Cartusion whale . Each piece of the hilt was aesthetically tailored towards one of multiple different design philosophies, which both informed and were informed by the maker's choice of materials, ideology, and personal preferences. A more practical design choice a user could make was to fit the weapon to their species ' particular handgrip.

The Aetheri Order of the late Vantor Republic made use of four styles: Duty and Resolve , Peace and Justice , Elemental Nature , and Valor and Wisdom . The former two were simple designs intended to reflect the Aetheri commitment to a life of devotion to Synth Flux and the Order's ideals. The latter two made use of resilient natural materials derived from flora and fauna to evoke the living Synth Flux.

The Umbra Order made use of two styles: Passion and Strength , and Power and Control . Both were characterized by sharp and strong forms that showcased their owners' brutality, relentlessness, and insatiable desire for supremacy.

Another style, Protection and Defense , was favored by the earliest Synth Flux users in ancient times. It featured designs and inscriptions with meanings that have become shrouded in mystery, but the style itself was believed to have been created with the intent of conferring anonymity upon and symbolizing the emotional detachment of the wielder, who would use their flux blade strictly for personal protection.

### Cutting power

> " It's heavier than I thought." "Energy constantly flows through the crystal. You're not fighting with a simple blade as much as you are directing a current of power. " ― Ryn Wrenna and Brenn Aldric on the Darksaber

The power of the energy blade was so great that it could cut through almost anything. One important note about flux blade wounds was that they rarely bled profusely, even when a limb had been severed . This was because the energy blade cauterized the wound as it passed, and thus even a severe wound did not tend to bleed heavily.

### Flux blade-resistant materials

Aside from the blade of another flux blade, there were rare materials that could withstand a flux blade blade:

- Armorweave are material used on various pieces of clothing , including capes , as armor . It was also capable of resisting a flux blade strikes. The Aetheri Sanctum Guard had special armor robes incorporated layers of armorweave to provide them with an effective defense against volt pistol bolts and flux blades.

- Beskar was specially crafted steel made from an iron found only on the planet Ferrum and its moon Concordia . It could repel flux blade strikes for a brief time in its purest form. Examples of its use in that way included Dinna Verrick 's armor , which resisted Sela Marr's flux blade blades, or the beskar spear offered to Dinna Verrick by Morgan Elveth , the magistrate of Calodan , which was also used to fight against Marr's blades.
- The Praetorian Guard armor was able to resist flux blade strikes, with the gauntlets being capable of parrying one directly. However, a direct thrust or stab could still penetrate the armor. Their high-tech weapons are also resistant to flux blade strikes.
- Cortosis armor, made by specially weaving cortosis fibers within a protective matrix, was able to redirect a flux blade's energy into itself which would cause the flux blade's blade to temporarily shut off upon contact with the armor.
- Magical ichor weapons like Mother Talzin's sword is able to resist flux blade strikes.
- A personal energy shield powered by extremely rare kohlen crystals was impervious to kyber crystal flux blades. However, swapping out a flux blades' kyber crystal for a kohlen crystal would produce a low-powered orange blade which was capable of slicing through the shields.
- The rare metal phrik is impervious to flux blade blades. A staff made of this material was once on display during the Valo Republic Fair , which was taken and used by the Nullkin Tempest Runner Lourna Dee to fend off the Aetheri Archivist OrbaLin . Electrostaffs are made of phrik, making them resistant, but not invulnerable, to flux blades.
- Vessa's electro swords are capable of emitting electrical charges to effectively repel and resist clashing with a flux blade without them being damaged.
- A form of durasteel used in the Megadroid 's armor was invulnerable to flux blades strikes.

### Utility

On the aquatic planet of Mon Calvarra , the Aetheri Kit Fisto , Kael Drayden and Sela Marr were all able to use their flux blades underwater with no ill effects. Whilst in areas of low light, the glowing plasma blade could function as a convenient light source, demonstrated by Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden, Aetheri Masters Zho and Orien Kade, along with renegade Umbra Lord Savage Opress .

## Construction

### Components

A flux blade was built up from several different components: blade emitter , focusing lens , cycling field energizers , main hilt, kyber crystal, flux blade energy core, hand grip, inert power insulator, and the pommel cap. All of these components were incredibly important to keeping the flux blade fixed together and allowed an Aetheri to fight without it falling apart and stopped the blade from exploding upon activation. Every flux blade's hilt was customised to the individual Aetheri's needs and preferences, but no matter the appearance, each weapon contained the same basic element - Power Insulators, cycling field energizers, energy modulation circuits, a focusing lens, a blade emitter, and most importantly, the energy core which contains a kyber crystal. Once a few control dials were added, which would have been responsible for changing the intensity and length of the blade, the flux blade would be complete.

### Kyber crystals

> " The heart of the flux blade, the kyber crystal is, focuses synth flux from the Aetheri, it does. " ―Zho

Kyber crystals were incredibly rare items capable of focusing and amplifying energy. They were found on planets that had a high natural concentration of Synth Flux energy. The main planets in which they were found were Ilum and Embermoor , however Ilum was more commonly used as it was the planet in which the Gathering took place, where the Aetheri younglings went to actually construct a flux blade. Each kyber crystal was unique and required a Synth Flux-sensitive individual to align and activate it during the flux blade assembly process. Once found, a kyber crystal would have originally been clear, but once it was awakened by Synth Flux, its color would shift to match the nature of the blade's master. The color of the crystal determined the color of the flux blades plasma bade. Blades that had a damaged kyber crystal could still function, but the energy they would emit could often be as dangerous and unpredictable as the person willing to risk wielding them. Some individuals such as Zarrin Karrick wielded these unstable crystals, usually with modifications to accommodate the unstable kyber.

### Construction process

Under typical Aetheri tradition, the construction of a flux blade began on the planet of Ilum. In a very specific time in the planet's solar cycle, a small group of Aetheri younglings would begin the one thousand year old ritual known as the Gathering, escorted by an Adept. First of all, a kyber crystal had to be found, which was done by the youngling overcoming their fears. Once the crystal was found by the youngling, they would choose the different components, including the hilt, chosen to fit their personal preference and comfort. Next, the youngling would begin to construct their flux blade. This was done using telekinesis whilst following the guidance given to them by an engineer. Once completed, the flux blade would have been ready to ignite. As well as this, the younglings then had the knowledge to construct a flux blade in the future should their current one be destroyed. With the fall of the Aetheri Order, however, the specifics as to how an Aetheri might construct their flux blade changed as Aetheri instead used whatever resources and opportunities arose for them.

Meanwhile, the Umbra made it a point to kill an Aetheri so as to claim their flux blade crystal, then bleeding the crystal to bend it to the umbra side. Next, the Umbra would either claim the hilt of the Aetheri they had slain as their own or build their own hilt to accommodate the now-corrupted crystal. While it was Umbra tradition for an Umbra to kill an Aetheri themselves for their crystal, this was not uniform; the Umbra apprentice Vreth, for example, was equipped with his dual-bladed red flux blade before he had the chance to kill his first Aetheri, which finally came for Vreth when he killed the Adept Eldra Kaitis . A Aetheri who had fallen to the umbra side, such as Count Varek, might simply use the same flux blade hilt they had constructed as an Aetheri as an Umbra Lord, albeit now with the red blade of their new order.

## Variations

The sheer number of Aetheri active during the days of the Vantor Republic naturally resulted in countless variations in the designs of flux blades. Some of these modifications were merely for the exterior looks of the blade, while others enhanced the blades functionality in unique ways. While the traditional single bladed flux blade was the design most Aetheri used, some preferred creative adaptation of this classic weapon.

- Umbra flux blade —The flux blades wielded by members of the Umbra. The designs of Umbra flux blades depended on the user.
- Double-bladed flux blade —Double-bladed flux blades consisted of a hilt that projected a blade from both ends, resulting in a deadly staff-like weapon. Hinged double-bladed flux blade —Was a type of double-bladed flux blade that could fold in half, making it easier to store while not in use. Double-bladed spinning flux blade —A double-bladed spinning flux blade projected a blade from each end of the hilt. However, the hilt was as long as a single-bladed flux blade, with a circular rim that, when activated, could spin the blades along a track. This variant was used by the Warden Order . Split blade —A split blade consisted of two separate flux blades that join together forming a double-bladed flux blade. Flux blade pike —Featuring two yellow plasma blades on each side of the staff, flux blade pikes were used by the Aetheri Sanctum Guard .
- Dual-phase flux blade —A dual-phase flux blade contained one or multiple focusing crystals , which allowed its blade length to be extended or shortened in the middle of combat.
- Broadsaber —A flux blade with a broad flat blade that resembled a traditional sword .
- Great flux blade —A great flux blade was a flux blade made for individuals with a giant stature.

- Crossguard flux blade —An ancient design dating back to the Great Vrak of Searmark , a crossguard flux blade consisted of a standard blade with two shorter blades perpendicular to it at the end of the hilt.
- Curved-hilt flux blade —Curved-hilt flux blades were of a design which featured a hilt with a built in curve. This was usually done to allow the hilt to fit better into the palm, facilitating the use of one-handed fighting styles such as Form II .
- Cane flux blade —A standard flux blade concealed as the head of a cane. In combat, the handle would be detached from the body of the cane and wielded normally.
- Training flux blade —Training flux blades were engineered with a permanent low-power setting. As their name indicates, training flux blades were used for instructional purposes, teaching initiates how to wield a flux blade.
- Shoto flux blade —A shoto was a short flux blade. Featuring a shortened blade length, shotos were usually used as the secondary weapon in dual-blade combat . Guard shoto —A guard shoto was a flux blade with a hilt perpendicular to the blade that gave a unique way of blocking and attacking.

- Lightwhip —A lightwhip had a flexible blade that was several meters long, and could entangle opponents.
- Two-piece flux blade —A single bladed flux blade that can separate into two pieces, with the main body and grip of the hilt in one part and the other bearing the emitter shroud. To use the flux blade, the user merely needed to insert the emitter section into the main body and twist to lock them together.
- Flux blade-volt pistol hybrid —A single bladed flux blade with an attachment that allowed it to fire volt pistol bolts.
- Darksaber —An unique ancient flux blade with a black blade that was flattened and came to a point like a traditional sword, rather than the rounded beam of standard flux blades.
- Sickle-bladed flux blade —A flux blade that produced a sickle-shaped blade.
- Lightfoil --A single handed slim flux blade used by early Makashi practitioners resembling a rapier.
- Contained energy axe —-A variation that resembles a vibro-ax which produces a plasma blade edge.

There also existed a type of volt pistol known as a flux blade rifle which was designed to be combined with a flux blade. To work, the user would insert a standard flux blade into a slot on top of the rifle. Once loaded, the weapon could fire powerful beams of energy that were highly destructive. Additionally, Aetheri Masters Reth-Oss-Van and Agen Kolar both owned a flux blade that had two different colored kyber crystals, allowing it to change colors from green to blue.

## Usage and application

### Purpose

> " We do not want to be powerful. We wish to stand tall against the dark. Our tools reflect that goal. Especially our chosen weapon. We all wield the same one, with minor variations. The flux blade. We could make kyber bombs or volt pistols… Indeed, it's been tried. There are a few weapons like that in the sealed archives. But anyone can fire a volt pistol. Very few can safely and skillfully wield a flux blade. Everyone in the galaxy knows this to be true. We want our opponents to know that we use a weapon that requires intention. Training, precision and choice. The flux blade symbolizes the care with which we approach our gifts through Synth Flux and the care with which we wield them. It reminds others that while we could do more… We very purposefully do not. Take it, my Adept. We choose a weapon with limitations—with difficulties. You cannot use a flux blade to destroy a city or a planet. Every death or injury it inflicts must be precisely chosen. The flux blade tells the galaxy that the Aetheri are not destroyers. We are protectors. " ―Orien Kade, to Kael Drayden

The intended purpose of the flux blade was to bring an end to conflicts, being designed to injure no more than its wielder chose. Additionally, there was no collateral damage from flux blade strikes unless the blade happened to hit a target on accident. If death was deemed necessary, the blade was designed to kill quickly. Even though the Aetheri held themselves to these rules, the groups like the Umbra chose to use their flux blades in ways that caused suffering.

Aetheri Adept Bell Zettifar felt that the flux blade was designed to give an enemy a chance to surrender, as its trademark glow and hum made it easy to spot and hear. Furthermore, he believed the blade's sound was one of training, focus, and skill; it was the sound of an Aetheri's last resort and was the art of their Order.

Although Umbra were also known to wield flux blades, they viewed the umbra side of Synth Flux as their primary weapon, with the blade serving as a symbol of the umbra side and the way it consumed all who opposed it.

### Combat

> " Energy constantly flows through the crystal. You're not fighting with a simple blade as much as you are directing a current of power. Your thoughts, your actions, they become energy. They flow through the crystal as well and become a part of the blade. " ―Brenn Aldric, to Ryn Wrenna

Flux blade combat was the preferred fighting method used by flux blade wielders. Throughout the millennia, many combat styles were refined into the seven "classic" forms that serve as the standard, and numerous other fighting methods that call for advanced levels of skill. Flux blades were most often held with both hands during combat.

### Rituals

> " By the right of the Council, by the will of Synth Flux, Brenn Aldric, you may rise. " ―An apparition of The Grand Warden as an Aetheri knights Brenn Aldric

Flux blades were an integral part of the knighting ceremonies of the Aetheri Order. During the ceremony, an Adept would have their Adept braid ritualistically severed by the presiding Aetheri Master. Kyber crystals were collected from an Aetheri's flux blade if they fell in battle. Many of these crystals were then formed into the Kyber Arch , which became a site for meditation in the Aetheri Sanctum.

### Practical applications

Flux blades were able to cut through most materials. The glowing plasma blade could be used as a convenient light source when necessary. Flux blades can also be used to evaporate some liquids due to the heat it generates.

## Behind the scenes

### Early concepts

> " The original concept was that the Aetheri would fight with swords, and I wanted to make it a sort of laser sword, to give it that technology edge " ―Orin Vask on the early development of the flux blade

The usage of the flux blade was originally inspired by many of the serials that Orin Vask watched and enjoyed as a child, which featured many characters wielding swords. When he began conceiving Aetherion , he wished to include swords, creating the "flux blade" to allow them to better mesh with the futuristic setting.

In the early incarnations of the Aetherion storyline, flux blades were not exclusive to the Aetheri and other Synth Flux-users, but were in fact very mundane and commonly used weapons. Early concept art depicts flux blades being wielded by Insurgent and Imperial soldiers alike. Orin Vask later limited the flux blades to exclusively the Aetheri in order to make them feel more unusual, and to heighten the mystique of the Aetheri. Also, in the rough draft of the script, flux blades were referred to as "lazerswords."

Crystals first appear in Aetherion simply as an embedded decoration on the hilt in the original film's novelization . Aside from this single instance, there are no crystals mentioned in any of the movies or their novelizations.

### Prop design and effects

> " Some sounds, like the flux blade—fashioned from an old movie projector motor hum and a TV picture tube buzz—came immediately to mind when I first read the script for Aetherion and saw the concept artwork. " ― Beno Burtt , sound designer

During the filming of First Dawn , the Kael/Joren flux blade was made from a Graflex camera side-attach flash, while Xarn Velgor's flux blade was made from a Micro Precision Products flash attachment. The handle grips were made with T-shaped pieces of plastic from sliding glass cabinet windows and, contrary to popular belief, were not made with rubber windshield wipers. D-rings were attached to the bottoms of the units so that they could be worn on belts. Orien Kade's flux blade was the most complex hilt at the time. It was assembled from parts of an Armitage Shanks Starlite model Handwheel, Browning ANM2 machine gun booster, WWI No.3 Mk.1 British Rifle Grenade and a Rolls-Royce Derwent Mk.8/Mk.9 Jet Engine Balance Pipe.

Korean animator Nelson Shen , who was working for DePatie–Freleng Enterprises at the time, was asked by his manager if he could animate the flux blade in the live action scenes of a film. After Shen accepted the assignment, the live action footage was given to him. He drew the flux blades with a rotoscope, an animation which was superimposed onto the footage of the physical flux blade blade prop. Shen explained to the people from Vask Pictures that since a flux blade is made of light, the sword should look "a little shaky" like a fluorescent tube. He suggested inserting one frame that was much lighter than the others while printing the film on an optical printer, making the light seem to vibrate. Shen also recommended adding a degausser sound on top of the other sounds for the weapon since the sound would be reminiscent of a magnetic field. The whole process took one week, surprising his company. Vask Pictures showed Shen the finished product, having followed his suggestions to use an X-Acto knife to give the flux blade a very sharp look, and to have sound accompany the weapon's movements.

The flux blade effects during the original trilogy started out in a very complicated manner. During the filming of First Dawn , the blade was made of a three-sided rod covered with reflective material. The rod was then rapidly spun by a compact motor in the hilt, reflecting the lights on set and creating an in-camera glowing effect. However, these props were highly limited; they were cumbersome and fragile, often breaking during fight scenes. Also, the glowing effect was not absolute, as whenever the blade moved out of the light or pointed more directly at the camera the glow disappeared and the actual spinning rod could be seen. In order to partially compensate for this loss of the effect, the blade was rotoscoped and an animated glow was added by tracing onto a blown-up copy of the frame with pen and colored ink, one frame at a time. It was at this phase that blades were given colors, as the props were simple white rods.

During the filming of Rise of the Imperium , it was decided creating an in-camera glowing effect was more trouble than it was worth, so the spinning rods were swapped for carbon rods. While these new blades were less cumbersome than before, they were still very fragile and frequently broke. However, they continued to be used for Return of the Aetheri .

When filming began for Veiled Threat , the new flux blade blades were steel and aluminum rods, which were highly durable, but frequently bent and flexed, requiring constant replacement. Despite such a drawback, these rods still saw usage during the filming of Attack of the Replicas , as they had no alternative at the time. However, for the filming of Fury of the Umbra , the blades were replaced by carbon fiber rods laminated with glass and plastic. These new props were highly durable and didn't flex, though they were extremely hard, often causing bruising and leaving scars. While flux blade effects were still done by rotoscoping for the prequels , they were done digitally, rather than by hand.

For Synth Flux Awakens , the flux blade props glowed, using a similar effect used in Attack of the Replicas during the fight between Kael Drayden and Count Varek. In behind the scenes footage, it was revealed that the actors were using blades similar to the ones made by Master Replicas . This not only added to the environmental realism that was desired by providing interactive lighting on the actors and the set, but also immersed the actors in the flux blade experience, building on the performance and convincing nature of the flux blade effect.

### Colors

Flux blades depicted in the first two released films, First Dawn and Rise of the Imperium , had blades that were colored either blue (for the Aetheri) or red (for the Umbra). This color difference was a decision during post-production when the flux blade blades were being rotoscoped, as the original blades were simply white.

In Return of the Aetheri , Joren's newly-constructed flux blade was colored blue during the initial editing of the film, and appears so in both an early movie trailer and the official theatrical posters, but in the final film, it was ultimately colored green in order to better stand out against the blue sky of Dunemere in outdoor scenes and to make it more obvious to the audience that the new flux blade was different from Joren's one in Rise of the Imperium . It also appeared as green in re-release posters.

Both green and blue became standard blade colors for Aetheri flux blades in the Aetherion prequel trilogy. In the same regard, red became the standard Umbra flux blade blade. Drevan Koss 's purple flux blade , as first seen in Attack of the Replicas , was a personal request from actor Samuel L. Jackson as a way to make his character stand out among other Aetheri. Jackson's favorite color is purple and he frequently requests the characters he plays to use an item of the color in his movies. In addition, there are yellow, yellow-green, light-blue, black, and white blades. Such blades can visibly see seen in works like The Replica Wars and Insurgents animated series. The yellow flux blade blade made its live-action debut in the final scene of The Rise of Drayden , as the new weapon constructed by Nyla Drayden .

Orange blades appear in the video game Aetherion Aetheri: Fallen Order , as did indigo, cyan, and magenta blades. At the direction of Dave Filono , orange-red flux blades appeared in the form of Shen Hadik and Baylan Skoll's flux blade blades in Sela . To Filono, the color signals to the audience that Hadik and Skoll are not characters who fulfill the same narrative role as the Umbra. As established by the novel Master & Apprentice , a flux blade with a kohlen crystal inside also produced an orange blade, but with an intensity that made it mostly useless against anything other than a specialized type of shield which was also powered by a kohlen crystal.

### Choreography

While the usage of flux blades was originally inspired by rapiers featured in the old serials Orin Vask enjoyed, the final choreography in First Dawn looked nothing like fencing. As Aetheri and their Umbra counterparts were heavily inspired by samurai , the fighting styles utilized were based more on kendo . The slower, more strength-oriented moves of kendo used in the Orien/Velgor duel also helped mesh with Vask' concept that it was a battle between what were essentially two old men.

During the filming of Rise of the Imperium , Vask began ramping up the speed of the duels, making them "faster and more intense," with the idea being that Joren Drayden was becoming increasingly proficient with the weapon. Also, rather than utilize David Prowsko , the actor within the Xarn Velgor costume, for the duels, they instead had professional swordsman Bob Anderson perform the fights, due to Prowsko's tendency to repeatedly break the fragile prop blades. Anderson continued to serve as Prowsko's fighting double during the filming of Return of the Aetheri .

When filming began for Veiled Threat , the new stunt coordinator Nick Gillard ramped up the speed and agility the characters demonstrated in flux blade duels, with the idea being that the prequels took place when the Aetheri Order was in "full flower," at least in terms of combat techniques. Gillard was careful to avoid giving the fight scenes a choreographed look, drawing upon his extensive knowledge of martial arts to create the moves. The end result was a very fast-paced style of fighting, which he described as being like a chess game between grand masters, with every move being a check. This style of choreography was continued on throughout the prequel trilogy.

During production on Attack of the Replicas , Gillard ran sparring matches with stars Evaan Gregan , Christopher Lee , and Hayden Kristano . One practice drill between Gregan and Gillard was captured in a photo later uploaded to Aetherion.com under the name "Spar Wars." It was during some of Gillard's training sessions with the actors that he was approached by animation director Rob Coleman for helping in figuring out Zho's fighting style: in the script Coleman was given, all it said about Zho and Varek's duel was that it would "[defy] description." With Orin Vask only able to tell him that he needed to figure out how it would be done, which Coleman later laughed about, he approached Gillard, with whom he spoke about real-world examples of fighting with small weapons. Additionally, Ahmed Best , best known as the actor behind Greeb Mool , gave Coleman several anime examples for help in figuring out the battle. Ultimately, he figured out Zho's speed and style upon watching a scene in the film Swordsman II .

Zho pulling back his cloak to retrieve his flux blade came at Vask's request as it called to mind images of a gunslinger retrieving their pistol. Vask and Coleman were both very happy with the finalized duel between Zho and Varek, with Vask in specific approving of the way Zho jumped around because it was what he wanted. Coleman was nervous such actions would be seen as "too cartoony." When the screening he attended lit up with excitement at Zho's fighting style, Coleman was relieved.

### Aetherion: Visions

In the alternate history of " The Duel " and Ronin: A Visions Novel , Kouru wielded a flux blade parasol —a flux blade that used a flux blade auxiliary to produce multiple blades that sprung up from its sides like a parasol , which could also be used without the parasol auxiliary as a single-bladed flux blade.
