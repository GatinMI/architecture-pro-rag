# Sela Marr
> " In my life, when you find people who need your help, you help them, no matter what. " ―Sela Marr — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Sela Marr was a female Solinai who was a Synth Flux-sensitive outcast from the Aetheri Order . After the Replica Wars , she helped establish a network of various insurgent cells against the Imperium . Marr was discovered on her homeworld of Shili by Aetheri Master Tor Vayne , who brought her to the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor to receive Aetheri training . Following the outbreak of the Replica Wars, Aetheri Grand Master Zho assigned the young Marr to be the Adept learner of Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden , who nicknamed her " Snips " when she joined him at the Battle of Krestophis . Whereas Marr was eager to prove herself, Drayden had a reputation for recklessness, and they had a rather difficult start as master and apprentice. Yet they worked together to rescue Rotto , the son of crime lord Grukk Desilijic Tiure , and returned Rotto to his father , thus facilitating a crucial alliance between the Grak Clan and the Vantor Republic .

As a commander in the Grand Army of the Republic , Marr found a mentor in Replica Captain Vek of the 501st Legion , with whom she and Drayden collaborated to lead frontline campaigns against the Fractio of Independent Systems . In her first command position at the Battle of Selvara , Marr disobeyed orders and lost her entire squadron of pilots , but she rebounded and helped secure a victory at Selvara . Her assignments pitted her against a variety of opponents, from General Skarn and Umbra apprentice Kessa Vray to bounty hunters such as Cad Vorek and Ayla Vossk . Over time , she matured into a respected leader, contributing to key Republic victories like the Second Battle of Pyralis and the Battle of Mon Calvarra . Marr even died during a series of events on Mournis , but the Daughter , a Synth Flux wielder who personified the light side , sacrificed herself to resurrect Marr and bring her back to life.

Marr gained much experience on and beyond the war front, leading a group of Aetheri younglings to revolt against their Trandoshan captors, helping rescue a colony of Solinai from enslavement by the Zygeran Slave Imperium , and serving as an advisor to the Underra insurgents —with her close friend, Lux Bontero , among them—as they liberated their world from the Fractio. In a stark turn of events, Marr was framed for the bombing of the Aetheri Sanctum hangar and other homicides, and she escaped into the Vantor Underworld to clear her name. Though she formed an unlikely alliance with Kessa Vray, she was detained by Republic forces and was consequently barred from the Aetheri Order. Ultimately, Drayden uncovered the true culprit, Marr's friend, and fellow Adept Barris Ovva , and prevented his former apprentice from being convicted of sedition . Nevertheless, the ordeal dislodged Marr's faith in the Aetheri. She refused the Aetheri High Council 's offer to rejoin the Order, instead departing in search of a new path. Marr briefly returned to lead Republic forces during the Siege of Ferrum and faced Vreth and his allies. Directive 66 was enacted shortly after the Umbra Lord's capture and Marr alongside Vek, after freeing Vek from the control of Directive 66, escaped the rest of her 501st division . Faking her death, she was forced into hiding.

After the Imperium came to power, Marr went into hiding on Thabeska and Raadan disguised as " Ashla ." Following the evacuation of Raadan , Marr joined Senator Bael Solen's growing insurgent movement. She became the manager of his intelligence network and adopted the codename " Fulcrum ." As Fulcrum , Marr provided intelligence to various insurgent factions including Lena Kryll 's Shades insurgent cell. Marr took a particular interest in Kryll's insurgents because one of their members, an Aetheri named Brenn Aldric , had begun to train his own Adept, Cass Virel . In addition, Marr's efforts against the Imperium brought her into contact with the Dark Lord of the Umbra Xarn Velgor—who, unknown to her, was actually her former master, Kael Drayden—as well as the Imperial Wardens known as the Fifth Warden and the Seventh Warden , but it also gave her the chance to reunite with Vek. Following the mission to Searmark , Marr became lost to the rubble and shadows of the Umbra temple and was believed by many, including Aldric and Virel, to have perished during a duel with Xarn Velgor.

However, Marr was unexpectedly saved from Velgor's wrath by a future Cass Virel, who pulled her through a portal into the World Between Worlds , a mystical plane of Synth Flux connecting all time and space. After a confrontation with Vael Mal , who desired access to the dimension, Marr returned to her time, promising Virel that she would find him once she returned. After the end of the Galactic Civil War , Marr joined Ryn Wrenna in her quest to find the missing Virel, who had disappeared during the Liberation of Lorath , and trained Wrenna in the ways of the Aetheri until parting ways. Marr later journeyed to the city of Calodan on the forest planet Corvus looking for information on the location of Grand Admiral Vorren , who had disappeared alongside Virel, from Magistrate Morgan Elveth . Needing to rescue the city from Elveth's rule, she allied herself with the Ferrik Dinna Verrick , who had been on a quest to bring the Synth Flux-sensitive youngling Pip to the Aetheri. After freeing Calodan, Marr refused to train Pip and directed Verrick to the planet Tython instead. She later visited Joren Drayden at his new Aetheri Sanctum and, when Drayden found Pip in his care following a summons from the seeing stone , oversaw his training of the child temporarily.

Faced with Vorren's return, Marr returned to Lorath and enlisted Wrenna's help once again in finding Virel and the missing Grand Admiral. Decades after Marr began her search, her voice joined a multitude of Aetheri spirits to guide the young Aetheri Nyla , the granddaughter of a resurrected Vael Mal, during the final battle of the New Imperium-Coalition War . Sela inspired Nyla to stand up to Mal and destroy him once and for all.

## Biography

### Childhood

> " It was Master Tor Vayne who found me and brought me to the Temple where I belonged… " ―Sela Marr, to Kael Drayden — Link ▶️ ( file info )

In 36 BVE , Sela Marr was born on the planet Shili to the Togrutas Pav-ti and Nak-il Marr . Early in her life, Marr was taken on a journey by her mother, who taught her about life and death . During that period, the young girl encountered a raxshir feline , which she reached out to without fear. She proceeded to ride atop the animal to return home. Upon her return, the elder Solinai Gantika realized the infant was Synth Flux-sensitive and declared that she would be an Aetheri . When Marr was three years old, she was discovered by Aetheri Master Tor Vayne among her people on Shili after her natural ability to sense the intentions and feelings of others had marked her as Synth Flux-sensitive.

Around the same time, however, Marr was nearly enslaved by an impostor , who intercepted the signal to the Aetheri Order and hoped to use her powers for their own ends. Being unable to properly explain to the village elders the danger she knew the impostor posed, Marr's fellow villagers held scorn towards her when she refused to demonstrate her Synth Flux powers, but the impostor's plan was ultimately thwarted. Although the impostor had scared the young Marr, she trusted Vayne instantly, and he taught her how to breathe slowly to calm herself. Afterward, she was accepted into the Aetheri Order, beginning her new life after Vayne brought her to the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor , where, like all Aetheri of her era, she was raised. Training as an Aetheri Initiate restored her self-confidence after the fear caused by the impostor impacted it, and the life of an Aetheri became the only life she ever knew.

At some point, Marr traveled to the Aetheri Sanctum on the ice planet Ilum to acquire a kyber crystal for a flux blade , feeling the crystals , albeit without knowing that was what she sensed, as soon as the Aetheri starship came out of hyperspace . She entered into the temple's cave after Grand Master Zho told her she would know her crystal when she found it. As the opening to the cave slowly closed as the sun of Asar set, she urgently looked for her crystal until she calmed herself, thinking back on Zho's words and only then seeing its light. Excited and ready to leave, she quickly made her way out of the cave with the tool for which she could build her own flux blade . Although her visit to Ilum had overall been unremarkable, her decision to trust Zho's teaching restored her ability to trust others after it was impacted by the false Aetheri. From there, she would construct said flux blade using the kyber crystal under the tutelage of Aetheri architect droid Huyang , who fussed over her shoulder as she worked on it. At another point, Zho taught her that she could sometimes find something she was not looking for in a situation.

During her time at the Aetheri Sanctum, Professor Huyang would regale her with the History of the Galaxy, Parts One, Two and Three , of which she considered part one the best.

### In the midst of galactic war

#### Becoming Drayden's Adept

> " I'm the new Adept learner. I'm Sela Marr. " ―Sela Marr introduces herself — Link ▶️ ( file info )

At the age of fourteen, mere months after the First Battle of Pyralis and the beginning of the Replica Wars , Marr was assigned to Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden to learn the ways of Synth Flux as his apprentice on the orders of Zho. Her training had been accelerated due to the galactic conflict that overshadowed her apprenticeship. Having been granted the rank of Adept , which made her an Aetheri Commander under the Aetheri Military Integration Act , she demonstrated an eagerness to prove herself worthy to be his apprentice. Zho did this to alleviate Drayden's fear and inability to lose loved ones, as he believed that by becoming a teacher, Drayden would develop emotionally and understand that his excessive bonding to those he cared about was unnecessary once Marr was ready to go off on her own.

The Aetheri Council finally sent Marr on her first mission to the planet Krestophis in order to meet her new mentor and inform Kade and him that they were needed back at the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor. However, after arriving on the planet moments after a wave of Dissident forces pulled back and giving that message to the two, she learned that the Republic had in fact failed to take the planet as the Aetheri on Vantor had believed, leaving the generals and their forces trapped and outnumbered as the battle dragged on. Kade introduced himself, presuming that she was his new Adept learner. She corrected him, insisting that Master Zho specifically assigned her to Drayden, who was shocked to hear the news due to the fact that he had spoken adamantly against the idea of taking on an Adept learner. Despite Kade's confusion, he accepted that having an Adept would be good for Drayden, who vehemently disagreed.

Meanwhile, the droid army set up a deflector shield that would block the Republic artillery bombardment that earlier halted their advancements. Drayden took Marr to check on Replica Captain CT-7567 "Vek," who was also confused by Marr's presence, referencing Drayden's earlier affirmation against taking on an Adept learner. Marr stood up for herself, saying that Zho believed she was capable and that she would prove it. As Drayden went on ahead, Marr asked Vek if she outranked him, to which Vek responded that experience outranked any kind of designated command. However, Marr just responded that she was eager to get some.

#### Liberation of Krestophis

> " You're reckless, little one. You never would have made it as Orien's Adept… but you might make it as mine. " ―Kael Drayden to Sela Marr, following the Battle of Krestophis — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Drayden and Marr eventually made a plan to infiltrate the enemy lines while Kade and his troops held off the enemy forces in order to buy them time. The two Aetheri hid inside a box while the droid tanks moved around them. As they made their way to the source of the shield, Marr tripped on a sensor antenna , accidentally activating a batch of Dissident retail droids . While Drayden was occupied with the droids, Marr began to place charges around the generator. As she was planting charges, a battle droid snuck up behind her, but Marr noticed it and sliced it in half. The battle droid's body rolled over more sensors and activated additional retail droids. With her master pinned down by the horde, Marr noticed a piece of wall behind him with a convenient hole right where Drayden was positioned. She used Synth Flux to push the wall down, crushing the droids around her master.

Meanwhile, in the city, Kade tricked Dissident General Whorm Loathsom , stalling for time until the shield blew up. Then, Kade took the Dissident General hostage. When the battle was over, Drayden noted how reckless his new Adept was, saying that she might not have made it as Kade's Adept. Marr felt disappointment until her master reaffirmed himself, thanking Marr for her help on their mission and noting that her recklessness might serve her well as his student. As Marr and Drayden found themselves settled, Zho wondered if having an Adept would calm Drayden's wild ways.

#### Battle of Teth and rescuing Grukk's son

> " Sela, a very wise Aetheri once said, "Nothing happens by accident." It is the will of Synth Flux that you are at my side. I just want to keep you there in one piece. " ―Drayden, to Marr — Link ▶️ ( file info )

After the Battle of Krestophis , Drayden and his new apprentice were dispatched to find and rescue Grukk the Grak's missing son Rotto , who was captured by Dissident forces and brought to the planet Teth . Rescuing Rotto was critical to ensure trade and shipping routes for the Republic through Grak Space in the Outer Reach Territories.

As Drayden communicated with the scouts who had been sent to Teth, Marr quickly became friendly with the replicas, telling them about her rescue of Drayden on Krestophis. Drayden approached them and told them that it would be impossible to land at the B'omarr Order Monastery . As Vek sent the men to their positions, Drayden told Marr to remain humble, who replied that she was trying to keep the men inspired.

Republic forces were forced to land below the monastery, with Marr riding in a LAAT/i gunship with Drayden, Vek, and several other troopers . After landing, their forces began to scale the mountain with AT-TE walkers and ascension cables where the building was located. Drayden led the charge and Marr stood on top of a walker and deflected oncoming laser fire. The climb proved to be a challenge, but the Aetheri and replicas eventually reached the top of the mountain.

After securing the outer perimeter, they discovered a caretaker droid called 4-A7 , who revealed the location of the captured Grak. Drayden and Marr went to look for him and were forced to fight several enemy battle droids, but eventually found the cell where the Grak was located. Rotto turned out to be just a Grakling. Meanwhile, 4-A7, who actually served the Dissidents and Dark Adept Kessa Vray , recorded the Aetheri's speech. Vray transmitted the recording to her master, Count Varek , who gave the "evidence" to Grukk that the Aetheri were behind the kidnapping. Varek assured Grukk that he would sent his army to "rescue" Rotto, intending to gain Grukk's favor for safe passage through the system.

With the incoming droid army on their way and the little Grakling sick, the Aetheri had to look for another way out to bring the kidnapped Grak back to Dunemere . On their way out, Vray had arrived and eventually located the two and engaged them in battle. After a brief duel , they escaped on a can-cell to a nearby hangar , followed by Drayden's astromech droid , Bolt-3 , to where a ship, the Twilight , was berthed. Once on the other platform, they encountered 4-A7 once again. However, it became obvious the droid was a spy when three battle droids came out of the ship. The Aetheri destroyed the droids and hijacked the ship.

While on the escape, Master Kade and Republic reinforcements had arrived with Kade engaging in a duel with Vray. Now on the ship, Drayden and Marr attempted to land on a Republic Iron Cruiser, but the cruiser 's hangar was destroyed, forcing the Aetheri to make their way to Dunemere on their own. As the two jumped to hyperspace, Vray and Kade sensed their withdrawal. Kade, knowing that Vray had failed her mission and that her master would be displeased to learn that, managed to gain the upper hand over Vray until she boarded a Vulture droid to escape capture.

Meanwhile, Marr and Drayden found medicine for the Grakling, which saved his life as they made their way back to Grukk's palace on Dunemere. However, as they approached Dunemere, they came under attack by two MagnaGuards in Renegade -class Porax-38 starfighters . Despite destroying the fighters, their ship was forced to crash-land in the Dune Sea . Marr, Drayden, and Bolt then took Rotto across the Dune Sea to reach his father's palace. However, she and Drayden sensed Varek's presence.

While Drayden distracted Varek in a duel, Marr and Bolt took Rotto to Grukk's palace. However, they came under attack by several MagnaGuards. Marr fought the MagnaGuards, destroying them all and meeting up with her master at Grukk's palace. However, Grukk ordered their execution until he was contacted by his uncle, Ziro . Senator Lira Ondar then revealed to Grukk that his uncle was working with Varek and was the one who arranged Rotto's kidnapping. Grukk then spared their lives, and Marr and her master were picked up by Master Kade and Zho.

#### The Malignar

> " Wait! Just because there haven't been any survivors before doesn't mean there won't be any this time! " ―Sela Marr, to Chancellor Mal and Aetheri Council — Link ▶️ ( file info )

At some point, Kade told Marr about how his own master, Sorin Vael , found Drayden because of his quick reflexes. During the hunt for the Fractio's new warship , the Malignar , which was a Subjugator -class heavy cruiser armed with two mega-ion cannons powerful enough to disable the power systems of an entire fleet of warships, Marr, along with her master, were sent to protect the staging area in the Bith system . She and Drayden were contacted by Master Tor Vayne, who had tracked the Fractio's secret weapon to the Abregado system . When Vayne's transmission was lost, Marr urged her master to send support to Vayne, but he required the Chancellor's and Aetheri Council's permission. During the meeting, Drayden and the Council believed that there were no survivors and Drayden was ordered to protect the Republic's convoys . Marr then spoke up and urged them to search for survivors, but she was rebuffed by the Council and the Supreme Chancellor for her outburst. Drayden had Admiral Yular deploy their fleet while Drayden and Marr took the Twilight to "scout ahead."

Despite Marr's outburst, her master agreed with her that they must search for survivors. At first, they were unsuccessful and were ordered by both Kade and Mal to give up their search. However, as Drayden piloted the Twilight away, Marr sensed Vayne and took the controls, piloting the Twilight to his escape pod 's location. Marr and Drayden found Vayne with the replicas Commander Wolffe , Sergeant Sinker , and trooper Boost . They learned from Vayne that the Malignar ' s weapon was an ion cannon mounted on an enormous ship that could disable ships. Marr then detected the Malignar approaching them and hurried to disable power so they could blend into the debris field. At first, the ship passed, but it then detected the Twilight ' s medical droid TB-2 , who was caring for the injured Wolffe, and fired the mega-ion cannon . Drayden was able to outmaneuver debris and jump to hyperspace before the blast wave reached them.

Following their return to the Republic fleet and the Resolute , Drayden and Marr were given command of Shadow Squadron and dispatched on their next mission: to destroy both General Skarn and the Malignar . Marr and Vayne were both skeptical of the plan, knowing that Skarn would be well protected by his ship's defenses. Marr and Drayden were then informed Skarn had attacked a medical convoy in the Ryndellia system . Drayden deduced that Skarn's next target was the Republic's secret Outer Reach medical center near the Kaliida Nebula . Marr served as Drayden's gunner aboard his BTL-B Yarrow starfighter/bomber as Shadow Squadron flew to intercept Skarn.

Marr, along with her master, Vayne, and Shadow Squadron, took a shortcut through the nebula. During the journey, Marr detected several Neebray mantas. Despite damage to one of the bombers and an astromech, Marr, Drayden, Vayne, and Shadow Squadron reached the medical station. The Malignar then arrived, and Marr shot down several of the vulture droids despite her master's fancy flying. When Shadow Squadron started to lose more ships, Marr convinced her master to target the ion cannon instead of the bridge. This strategy succeeded in disabling both the Malignar ' s ion cannons and its hyperrelay.

Marr, Drayden, and Vayne then rejoined Kade aboard the Resolute as he continued attacking the Malignar . However, Marr detected an Ondara ship approaching the battlefield. Marr then picked up a comm signal from the ship's pilot, Senator Lira Ondar. When Ondar was captured by a grapple beam, Drayden decided to rescue the senator. Marr remained on the bridge of the Resolute with Vayne and Admiral Yular and guided both her master and Kade through the ship to find Ondar and her protocol droid , Astra-9 , until Skarn jammed their transmissions. Marr watched as Drayden, Kade, and Ondar made their escape, and the the Malignar crashed into the Dead Moon of Antar .

#### Battle of Bothawui and search for Bolt-3

> " They sent a child to destroy my station? The Republic must be running out of Aetheri. " " You must be General Skarn. He's just another tinny, boys. Let's scrap him like the rest. " ―Skarn goads Marr above the Ruusan moon — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Following the Republic's defeat at Falleen , Marr, Drayden and their fleet were stationed at Bothawui to protect it. Marr agreed with Kade that they should retreat, but stood by her master when he refused to do so. Marr was part of Resolute command as Drayden, Bolt, and Gold Squadron engaged Skarn's fleet. As Skarn's fleet hit them, Marr signaled Vek and his AT-TEs to open fire from the asteroids behind Skarn's fleet. Though they destroyed the bulk of Skarn's fleet, Skarn himself escaped.

After Drayden was injured when his starfighter collided with a large piece of shrapnel, he was brought to the Resolute to recover, though Bolt was left behind. Marr later accompanied her master along with Bolt's temporary replacement R3-S6 , secretly a spy for General Skarn, on a subsequent salvage operation to try and recover the droid. Marr was optimistic R3 would be a suitable replacement but failed to convince Drayden that R3 could ever replace Bolt. The pair boarded a nearby salvaging ship piloted by the Trandoshan scavenger Gha Nachkt . R3, secretly in league with the Dissidents, activated several VX-88 assassin droids , which attacked the two Aetheri within the ship's cargo hold. After failing to find Bolt, they left to continue their search, unaware that Nachkt was also in league with Skarn and was secretly hiding Bolt from the Aetheri. After Drayden was caught in an ambush by Skarn as a result of R3's sabotage, Marr and Vek were forced to rescue him, once again escaping the General's clutches. Marr continued to stick up for R3, but this only made Drayden angrier since they were no closer to finding Bolt.

Upon receiving a transmission from Bolt aboard Gha Nachkt's ship , Marr and Drayden followed the transmission coordinates to Skarn's Dissident listening post on one of Ruusan 's moons. To avoid the station's scanners, Marr, Drayden, R3, Vek, Denal , and several other replicas skydived from the Twilight onto the station's surface, gaining entry via flux blade. Once inside, Drayden went to rescue Bolt while Marr and the replicas began to plant explosives all around the station. However, as Marr and the replicas advanced through the station, they were found by a synth flux of droids and were pinned down. Unbeknownst to them, R3 was the one who contacted Skarn to help him locate the infiltration team. After they defeated the enemy, Skarn personally came to attack them. While Marr initially remained confident that they could defeat him like the other droids, Skarn quickly overpowered her before he killed all of the troopers save Vek and Denal. Before he could deal a killing blow to Vek, his strike was parried by Marr's own flux blade, and the two engaged in a duel. Far outmatched by the cyborg General, Marr retreated into another room full of spare droid parts along with R3. As she hid, she contacted Vek and ordered him to proceed with the mission and continue to plant explosives with Denal while she distracted Skarn. Then, she was discovered and ambushed by Skarn thanks to the betrayal of R3. She escaped into the ventilation shaft and rendezvoused with the rest of the crew at the hangar and saw that Bolt had been rescued. They escaped on board the Twilight , this time with Bolt, just as the station exploded.

#### Capture of Viceroy Gunray

> " If it isn't the hairless harpy. " " If it isn't Drayden's filthy, obnoxious little pet. " " How nice of you. Tell you what, I'll give you a merciful death. " ―Sela Marr and Kessa Vray — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Following the Republic's apprehension of Dissident Viceroy Nutis Gunray , thanks to the efforts of Lira Ondar and Senator Onaconda Farr on Rodanor , Sela Marr, and Master Luminara Undula were tasked with overseeing Gunray's voyage to Vantor aboard the Tranquility , where he would be put on trial for his crimes. Several Senate Commandos under Captain Faro Argyus were also dispatched as extra security to accompany the Aetheri. Though Marr was initially skeptical of the need for so much security for just one Geltari , Undula assured the Adept that his allies could easily be conspiring against them and devising a rescue plan. The Mirialan Aetheri Master was indeed correct, as Count Varek had already sent his most trusted assassin , Kessa Vray, to extract the Viceroy and return him to the Dissidents. As Marr and Undula attempted to interrogate Gunray, with the Aetheri Master chastising Marr for threatening him out of impatience, Vray and her small contingent of Super Battle Droids breached the hull of the ship.

Marr was left to guard Gunray as Undula and Commander Grev went to deal with the droids, where she was confronted by Vray, who had slipped inside the ship undetected. Marr was once again confident enough to taunt Vray as they dueled. The two dueled for a short time before Vray managed to free Gunray and tricked Marr, trapping the Adept behind an energy shield in his holding cell. Undula arrived shortly after, freeing Marr so the two of them could duel Vray together. However, instead of surrendering, Vray set off several explosives which she had planted in the engine room, knocking both Aetheri to the deck, and escaped down the elevator shaft. Though Marr wanted to follow and confront the assassin once again, Undula beckoned her to stay put despite her protests. Ultimately deciding to disobey Undula's instructions, Marr arrived just in time to save the Aetheri Master from certain death by Vray' blade. United as a team, the two Aetheri confronted Vray for a second time, though she once again escaped, this time accompanied by Gunray and Republic defector Faro Argyus. Thus, Vray successfully completed, her mission much to the dismay of Marr and Undula.

#### Rescue on Vanqor

> " We had the situation well under control, my little Adept. " " Oh, I see. So which part of the situation did you have under control? The blocked entrance, the poison gas, or that gundark behind you? " ―Drayden and Marr — Link ▶️ ( file info )

After her master and Orien Kade were trapped in a cave on the crystalline planet of Vanqor after crash landing during a confrontation with the leader of the Fractio of Independent Systems Count Varek, Marr and a small extraction team of replica troopers were dispatched to rescue to two Aetheri. Marr and her team blasted apart the collapsed cave opening in order to free Drayden and Kade, who had collapsed from the fumes of a noxious green gas which they had accidentally released. They then re-sealed the cave entrance, trapping a swarm of Gundarks inside.

#### Stranded on Maridun

> " Okay, you and Bly go south. Vek and I will stay here with Kael. " " No, Sela. In order to get help quickly we must work together. " " I can't leave him! Master, I know if I was hurt, he'd never leave me behind. " ―Marr argues with Ciel Venn — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Sela Marr and Kael Drayden, along with a fleet of Republic gunships , were later sent to evacuate Aetheri Knight Ciel Venn from Dissident forces after her fleet had been defeated during the Battle of Quell . The two Aetheri, Vek, and their replica battalion managed to board Venn's ship and rendezvous with the Velari Aetheri and her own battalion, narrowly escaping onto a docked cruiser with an injured Drayden, who had been caught in an explosion from the Dissident attack. Marr and Drayden led Venn and some survivors from her battalion, which were Replica Commander Bly , Lucky , Flash , and Cameron , to a cruiser. On their way, Drayden was gravely injured saving the group from an explosion. They were forced to jump to hyperspace prematurely in order to escape the exploding Resolute in time, much to the alarm of Admiral Yular. As a result, they were forced to crash-land on Maridun in order to avoid colliding with one of its nearby stars.

Stranded on the planet following the explosion of their cruiser, they set up a crude camp using some parts from the wrecked ship. Fearing for her wounded master, Marr volunteered to watch over him with Vek, but Venn insisted that they instead work together to find help from the local population. Marr reluctantly agreed and accompanied Venn and Commander Bly to scout for help while Vek remained with Drayden. Along the way, the young Adept confessed to Venn her feelings of confusion towards the Aetheri Code over the rule of not forming attachments yet also showing compassion. In response, Venn shared similar feelings that she used to have regarding her own master , whom she eventually was able to let go. Not long after, they were ambushed by a pack of mastiff phalones which killed Flash, Lucky, and Cameron. After a long journey, they found the local Lurmen village, encountering the village elder Tee Watt Kaa and his son Wag Too . Initially, Watt Kaa refused to help the Aetheri, not wanting to be involved in the war, but later agreed to send his son with Marr to retrieve Drayden for healing on the condition that Venn remained with the village as insurance. Wag Too and Marr later returned with Drayden and Too healed his wounds using native healing oils.

When Dissident forces arrived under the command of Lok Durd in order to impose their "protection" of the Lurmen people, the Aetheri were forced to leave the village to prevent the villagers from paying the price due to their presence. As the Aetheri hid, they discovered a nearby heavily guarded Dissident base and Durd's superweapon : the Defoliator , which was capable of destroying all organic life. When they realized that Durd meant to test this weapon on the Lurmen village, the Aetheri revealed themselves to protect the villagers. At first, Watt Kaa adamantly opposed getting involved in any way, not even defending his people, but changed his mind after the Aetheri fended off Durd's forces and saved the village from annihilation, thanking them personally for their efforts.

### Age of the Imperium

#### Life on Thabeska

> " We all look like each other, everyone in my family. Our long hair and our brown skin. People don't try to tell us apart, and we fool them. It helped us avoid the shadow, and it keeps us safe from the law. I wish you looked like us, too. " ―Hedala Fardi, to Sela Marr

Sela Marr fled into the Outer Reach , intent on hiding from the Imperium. Eventually, she found the planet Thabeska , a planet that dealt largely in small-scale water and technology trading, and one with a relatively small and unorganized population located far away from major hyperspace routes . Believing the rural world to be of little interest to the Imperium, and a steady stream of newcomers would allow herself to blend into the quiet world, Marr made landing on Thabeska. However, the refugee did not account for the presence of the local Fardi clan , an influential family that either touched or ran most of Thabeska's businesses, including their own smuggling operations, though the family fronted such with several legal businesses. In time, Marr came to hear rumors that some kind of dark lord who was serving the Emperor and hunting Aetheri, yet she was unable to confirm such reports.

In order to hide her identity, Marr took on the name "Ashla," which was also the name of a moon that orbited the scared Aetheri world of Tython , another term for the light side of Synth Flux, and the name of an Aetheri youngling from the Replica Wars-era. Upon arrival on Thabeska, Marr was greeted by several young girls. They showed her where she could find food and a place to stay, and introduced her to Fardi , who bought Ashla's ship for what she thought was a nearly decent price. Followed ceaselessly by the young Fardi sisters, Ashla soon found herself working for the Fardi family once they learned that she was a good mechanic, and the refugee was given her own room in the Fardi compound and paid fair amounts of money for her work. Ashla had secured her place in the Fardi clan, becoming a friend amongst the Fardi girls.

Two months before Imperium Day , the first anniversary of Theron Mal's rise to power, Sela Marr was working on some droids in the shipyard on Thabeska when she noticed some of the Fardi children playing by a stack of crates. Only Hedala Fardi , the youngest of her family, looked at Ashla with fascination. Sometime later, the Solinai outlander discovered that Hedala Fardi was Synth Flux-sensitive, as the Fardi youngling lifted several small stones with subtle hand gestures before the eyes of Sela Marr.

On Imperium Day, Marr was informed by the Fardi girls that their father was having important Imperial guests over for dinner and that they wanted to meet anyone who was new to the planet. Once in orbit, Marr remembered Hedala Fardi and the danger that she was in, being a Synth Flux-sensitive child who would likely be sought out by the Imperium, but Marr knew that she was too late, so she left.

#### Raadan

A pastoral refuge

Marr found herself on the remote agrarian moon of Raadan in the Outer Reach. Marr landed the Fardi ship at the planet's main settlement. Under the alias of Ashla, Marr befriended a local girl named Kaeden Larte , who was part of a farming community. Kaeden allowed Marr to move into an abandoned home formerly owned by Cietra . Marr agreed to work as a mechanic, repairing droids and other machinery in return for ration packs. While adjusting to her new surroundings, Marr reminisced about her droid friend Bolt-3 and having to hide her Synth Flux powers. The following day, Kaeden hired Marr to repair her thresher. While repairing the machinery, Marr learned more about Kaeden's life and work as a farmer and considered settling on the remote moon due to the lack of an Imperial presence. After repairing Kaeden's thresher, Marr explored the town and sighted several nearby hills and caves which she identified as a potential hiding place.

On her third day on Raadan, Marr took the opportunity to explore the hills and caves ringing the settlement. She found a cave which she identified as her hiding place. Marr later met with Kaeden, her sister Miara Larte and several local friends including the farmer Tibbola , the work crew leader Vartan , and the twins Hoban and Neera at Selda's cantina , which had the best food in the settlement. The cantina was owned by the Solinai bartender Selda and run by the Garnethexan woman Malat , who reminded Marr of Aetheri Master Tor Vayne. While socializing with her hosts, Marr learned that Kaeden and Miara were orphans who had lost their parents in an accident. Vartan had adopted the two girls. As time passed, Marr became the settlement's mechanic and repaired numerous broken threshers and other farming equipment. While several Imperial personnel visited the cantina, Marr and her friends avoided contact with the Imperials by playing a game of crokin . When they inquired about Sela's knowledge regarding their new situation, Marr decided not to reveal her past yet.

## Personality and traits

### The fearless Synth Flux-sensitive

Sela Marr was a Solinai female who had blue eyes, dark orange skin, brownish-grey lips, white facial markings, a slender build, and white lekku and montrals with blue stripes. The markings on her montrals were unique, and her montrals continued to grow as she matured. During her early childhood, Marr's trust in others was negatively impacted by an Aetheri impostor who tried to enslave her, and the memory of the scorn her fellow villagers felt towards her remained with her until her trip to Ilum; upon trusting Master Zho, she felt that trust came easier to her because she was again trusting her own instincts. The teachings and values of the Aetheri Order became sacred to her, and she always sought to protect the lives of innocents.

As a teenager, Marr was snarky, cocky, stubborn, reckless, and impulsive, yet also brave, loyal, generous, and kind-hearted. The young Marr's emotions made her a perfect apprentice for Kael Drayden, with Grand Master Zho himself recognizing that the two became "twice the trouble" after being paired together. Like her master, she could at times become aggressive and willing to use terror or threats to get the answers she wanted. She had a tendency to follow her heart, which negatively impacted her path to Aetheri knighthood. In her younger years, Marr's emotions inhibited her ability to make wise decisions, although she eventually gained the confidence to make the right ones, despite the great cost to her feelings. Beyond learning from both Drayden and Orien Kade, the teachings of Sorin Vael were indirectly passed down to Marr.

Over time, Marr became more mature and enlightened, with her experiences during the Replica Wars shaping her into someone who could control her emotions. She had her victories, her failures, and her losses yet strove through them all, as she felt it was her duty as an Aetheri to help restore peace and prosperity to the Republic. Her constant travels and experiences made her more worldly than most people her age, and often she was surprised by the various events and people she came across during the Replica Wars. Throughout her time as an Aetheri, she remained close with Tor Vayne, who had brought her to the Aetheri Sanctum, and saw him as an important mentor in her life. She also developed a friendships with Orien Kade and his second-in-command, Cord, as well as Senator Ondar through her master, Kael Drayden.

She respected Zho despite often not understanding his advice, feeling he was almost always correct. However, when Marr was framed for murder and treason, she was horrified when the Aetheri Order cast her out and all but condemned her to a Republic military tribunal, and came very close to being sentenced to death. When Drayden unveiled the true culprit, Marr was shocked to see it was her friend Barris Ovva. Though conflicted, she ultimately rejected the opportunity to become an Aetheri again, having lost faith in the Order for not standing by her.

Marr formed a strong bond with the replicas, especially Captain Vek, and throughout her time as serving as their commander had learned a lot from them. She respected the replicas as individuals and got along with them very well. Marr quickly began to enjoy their company, telling war stories with them as soon as she had any experience to speak on. When Marr found herself serving with the replicas of the 501st again at the Siege of Ferrum, the replicas continued to salute her out of respect and even repainted their helmets in her likeness to show how much she meant to them. When Directive 66 turned the friends she had served with for years into enemies, Marr stood by her beliefs and told Vek to not kill them, determined to escape without directly killing her former comrades. When the ship crashed into the moon, Marr returned to the crash site to dig graves for each replica.

Marr was a confident and moralistic person, and a firm defender of the innocent. This carried on even after she left the Order, as she joined Bael Solen's insurgency to restore liberty and democracy to the people oppressed by the Imperium. Marr also felt pity and compassion for the Warden known as the Sixth Warden despite slaying him in combat. She regarded the Sixth Warden as a victim whose potential for good had been twisted and turned to the umbra side. After befriending the Synth Flux-sensitive Hedala Fardi, Marr became concerned with protecting Synth Flux-sensitive children from being captured by the Imperium's Wardens. During her time with the Martez sisters shortly before the end of the war, they noted that she still acted like an Aetheri even though she was no longer a member of the order.

During her time running from the Imperium, Marr continued to suffer from grief over the loss of her friends and guilt over the fact that she wasn't there to help them. During her time on Raadan, Marr was unsure about making new friends, largely due to the emotional pain inflicted by Ovva's betrayal. Despite everything Ovva had done, Marr believed that while she was wrong about "a lot of things," she did have a point about the Aetheri and the Republic, and later expressed her belief that if had they truly listened to her, they might have been able to stop Vael Mal before it was too late. When Marr began to help people hurt by the Imperium, she felt better about herself and as though she was fulfilling her training to do justice, feeling even better because her work was hurting the Imperium that hurt her. She felt as though she had been a hero in her past and was one again, although she knew she needed to try to avoid tapping into her reckless nature. She decided to find a middle path to life when she became a insurgent, not becoming an Aetheri again but also not allowing her heart to harden when faced with the death of friends.

In addition to knowing friends would die in war, she knew it could change people, having spent her life as an Adept fighting as a soldier instead of working as a peace keeper. Thus, she came to hate warfare, with her distaste for it making her notice that the fourteen year old Miara Larte had altered her behavior to avoid the Imperium's patrols. In fact, she believed that, just because she had started to fight in the Replica Wars at that age, it was not a good example for Larte. Even as Imperial forces closed in, she found that meditation was easy for her, yet she also recognized she had nothing to say to the Imperium's troops on Raadan, not even using banter or taunts. Upon meeting Senator Solen for the second time, she noticed when he said his daughter took after her mother, yet she failed to answer the test she suspected the words were; Elira was secretly the daughter of Kael Drayden and Lira Ondar, but Marr elected to move past the subject, knowing Solen and her would need to trust each other despite each other's secrets.

As a insurgent Informant code-named "Fulcrum," Marr worked indirectly with various insurgent cells, always with her face hidden and her voice disguised, and became somewhat pragmatic as she advised Lena Kryll to cease searching for Brenn Aldric to protect her crew, especially Cass Virel. This may suggest she developed a more discreet nature and decided overall to put the many before the few. When Marr was saved and reunited with Virel in the World Between Worlds, she was saddened to learn of the fate of Aldric, but disturbed by Virel's idea of saving Aldric from dying. Drawing from her own experience of being unable to save her master, Marr successfully convinced Virel to let Aldric go, and they parted ways, promising to find each other again as Marr went back to Searmark and resumed her journey.

After the Battle of Mossfell, Marr sought to keep her promise to Virel and joined his former companion Ryn Wrenna in her quest to find him and bring him home. At that time, she had adopted a more stoic persona. However, after Synth Flux spirit of Drayden gave her a final lesson and her decision to live, her personality returned to a more lively state.

### Relationship with Kael Drayden

#### Kael's student

> " Thanks for the support. As always. " " That's what friends are for. " ―Marr and Drayden, in their last meeting as friends

Marr's relationship with her master was tenuous to start. Drayden did not want an Adept, particularly one that was inexperienced and argued with him. Drayden had also maintained that he would never take on an Adept learner for fear that he would be "slowed down," so he was originally strongly opposed to Master Zho's assigning of the young Sela to him. Marr was offended by this, and deeply hurt, but she was eager to prove herself to her new teacher. Zho hoped that Marr's apprenticeship would teach Drayden to have better control over his other, more dangerous emotions.

Marr's enthusiasm often came at odds with her brash nature, a trait shared by Drayden himself. In fact, in part due to her youthful impulsiveness and bravery, she was the perfect Adept for Drayden. The two would often butt heads, each believing that they knew better than the other. During their first mission together at the Battle of Krestophis, they developed demeaning nicknames for each other; Marr gave her master the diminutive title "Skyguy," while Drayden returned the favor with the name "Snips," referring to Marr's snippy wit. Marr used the nickname Skyguy only because it annoyed Drayden, although, as the war progressed, these nicknames would remain by changing into more playful terms of friendship. Drayden also quickly came to respect Marr's bravery and determination, and he recognized that Marr was just as reckless as him.

For all their bickering, the two developed a close relationship during the Replica Wars. Drayden reassured his Adept that their meeting was no accident and that it was the will of Synth Flux for her to train under him. Their rapport made it's way onto the battlefield, with the duo competing to see who could destroy the most droids during the Second Battle of Pyralis. The master and apprentice grew increasingly reliant on each other throughout the war, taking turns rescuing each other from Dissident forces and bounty hunters. After situations where she was nearly killed, she found that she could talk to her mentor to come to terms with it. Marr's naive outlook balanced with Drayden's cynical approach. During her time on Mournis, Marr was killed by the Son and only resurrected after Drayden pleaded with the Father to help save her. Later, after being captured by Trandoshan hunters, Marr credited her master's teachings as the one thing that allowed her to survive.

Drayden grew protective of his Adept, which Marr viewed as a lack of trust in her abilities. During the mission to Lola Sayu, Marr directly disobeyed Drayden and joined his team, believing she should be the one to decide when to put her life in danger, not her master. Later, when Marr went on the run after being accused of murder, she realized that Drayden was the only person who would believe in her innocence and begged him to trust that she would make the right decision. Ultimately, however, Marr left the Aetheri Order, knowing that she had to discover more about herself without the help of her master. Drayden confessed to his Adept that he too understood the desire to leave the Order, and Marr, who had become aware of a connection between her master and Senator Ondar, but had never pushed the issue, assured him that she knew.

During her time away from the Order, Marr held her master in respect, regarding him as her own brother and later as a father figure. The lessons she had learned from him stayed with her in her exile; when asked by Trayk Martez where she had learned to fly, the former Adept credited her skills to "Drayden Academy." Even still, when she was approached by the Nite Owls to join in the fight against Vreth, Marr feared that she would become closer to the life and master she had left behind, but knew that she must go with them to help others. As she came face-to-face with Drayden prior to the Siege of Ferrum, she did not indulge an emotional reunion and focused her attention on convincing the Aetheri to assist the Ferriks. In their final meeting as friends, Drayden gifted Marr with her old flux blades, now shining with blue blades, just as his own did. Before Drayden left, Marr allowed her emotions to be shown, thanking him for his help and wishing him luck.

#### Velgor's nemesis

> " Kael Drayden was weak. I destroyed him. " " Then I will avenge his death. " ―Velgor and Marr

During the Siege of Ferrum, Marr learned from Vreth that Drayden had been groomed for years to become the apprentice of Vael Mal, but she refused to trust the Zyrrak, defiantly believing that she knew her master. After Vreth's capture, Marr attempted to speak with Drayden but was unable to make contact with him. Later, she was able to feel his fall to the umbra side through Synth Flux, hearing him call out in anguish after attacking Drevan Koss. She had no time to process these events before the execution of Directive 66, forcing her to go into hiding. Although she did not know the fate of her master, she remembered him fondly, never forgetting the compassion and kindness Kael offered to the galaxy.

In 14 BVE, Vael Mal partnered Moff Wilhard Varrix and Xarn Velgor on a mission to investigate attacks on supply lines for the early Void Core . Mal recalled Varrix's prosecution at Sela's trial as harming the relationship between Kael Drayden and then-Admiral Varrix, and surmised that despite the Aetheri's transformation into a Dark Lord of the Umbra , Xarn Velgor still harbored resentment against Varrix for his treatment of his former Adept.

During her time with the Shades, Marr was stationed aboard the Ember Home when the Insurgent fleet was attacked by the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor. During the battle, Marr sensed the anger and hatred emanating from the umbra sider, and she was met with the realization that the individual behind Velgor's mask was Drayden. The incongruity of the two men being the same caused Marr to lose consciousness. In the aftermath of this discovery, Marr struggled to accept the truth. Although she maintained that she was unaware of the Umbra Lord's identity, she secretly harbored feelings of grief and guilt, haunted by the idea that she could have helped her master in his time of need. During a mission to the Aetheri Sanctum on Lorath, Marr was confronted by a vision of Drayden that preyed upon these fears, blaming her for allowing Xarn Velgor to rise. Marr fought back against the vision, believing that there was still a way to reach her master.

Upon finally coming face-to-face with her former master once again on Searmark, Marr refused to believe that he and Velgor were the same person; she was unable to reconcile the Umbra Lord's vile nature with the kindness of the man she once knew. Although it pained her, Marr viewed Velgor as the murderer of Kael Drayden and vowed to avenge her master's death, going against the Aetheri teachings she had learned as a child. She battled him atop the Umbra Temple, defending the Adept Cass Virel. As the Temple began to collapse, Marr damaged Velgor's mask, revealing the scorched face of Drayden. The sight of her master forced her to pause. Feeling the guilt from leaving him years ago, Marr refused to leave Velgor and continued to duel as her friends escaped. Before the debris of the ceiling could crush them both, Marr destroyed the floor beneath them, causing Velgor to fall.

#### After Kael's death

In 4 AVE , Kael returned to the light side of Synth Flux and sacrificed his own life to defeat Vael Mal at the Battle of Mossfell . Following her master's death, Marr reflected on the impact of Kael's decisions on the galaxy. Despite his flaws, she remembered him as "the best of us," a good man who fell victim to his own fears, and had favorable things to say about him. In 9 AVE, she refused to train the youngling Pip because she recognized that the child's attachment to his father figure Dinna Verrick made him vulnerable to the same fears as Kael. Marr could not bring herself to separate the infant from Verrick, vowing to not set him down the same self-destructive path as her old friend. However, she relented and told Verrick of Tython, a place where Pip could reach out with Synth Flux to contact other Aetheri if he chose to.

Unsurprisingly, given her close relation to the Drayden family, Marr met Kael Drayden's son Joren Drayden at some point. In fact, when asked how she knew of his temple, she only answered that she was an old friend to the Drayden family. Marr and Drayden were on good terms. She even advised him in the training of Pip, such as telling the young Drayden to trust his instincts, and accepted his decision to train the youngling despite her own lack of desire to do so. She would even declare that being by Drayden's side was the safest place in the galaxy for a young Synth Flux-sensitive like Pip. While she still did not consider herself an Aetheri, she had faith that Drayden would create a "great school" and kept Verrick from seeing Pip, as per Aetheri rules on attachment. She favorably referenced Kael to his son by noting they were similar and did not rule out seeing Joren again. In the World Between Worlds, Kael appeared to Marr as his younger self to complete her training.

### Flux blade abilities

> " I admit, Sela's a little rough around the edges, but with a great deal of training… and patience… she might amount to something. " ―Kael Drayden, on Marr — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Sela Marr made use of an unorthodox reverse grip during combat, but, at the insistence of her master, eventually began to utilize the standard grip in addition to her reverse grip. Marr was a particularly acrobatic flux blade duelist who often utilized Synth Flux to jump down on her opponents and to dodge flux blade strikes. Although she was physically smaller than many of her foes, she proved to be a skilled duelist, wielding one and eventually two flux blades.

At the beginning of her apprenticeship under Drayden, Marr's combat skills were already quite considerable given that she defeated three IG-100 MagnaGuards single-handedly during her first mission. Later, Marr temporarily held her ground against the skilled Umbra assassin Kessa Vray and the Aetheri hunter General Skarn after only a very short time training under Drayden, although she was forced to run away from Skarn a short time into their confrontation. Her skills in flux blade combat developed over time, and she began to favor using a variant of Form V in battle like her master, although Ovva was certain she would be skilled at Form VI as well.

During the Replica Wars, Marr became a practitioner of Jar'Kai , wielding a yellowish-green shoto flux blade in addition to her standard flux blade. However, Marr occasionally utilized only her main flux blade, such as when she dueled Drayden on Mournis while under the umbra side influence of the Son. Nevertheless, Marr more commonly utilized Jar'Kai, particularly when facing multiple opponents such as when she engaged the much more experienced Drayden and Kade on Mournis. Whilst fleeing from replicas after being framed for murder, Marr lost her second flux blade and was forced to use only her main flux blade to fight Barris Ovva. Her duel against Ovva showed that she had become too reliant on her second flux blade, something she had in common with Kessa Vray, and though she did put up a considerable fight, Ovva overwhelmed her.

Around two years after her first duel with Skarn, Marr proved during their encounter on Florrine that her skills in flux blade combat had improved vastly as she held her own against all four of Skarn's flux blades, whereas previously she only lasted a short time when he was using only two flux blades. Instead of fleeing after exchanging only a few blows, Marr held her ground and gave the Younglings time to escape before escaping with them. By the time of the Siege of Ferrum, Marr demonstrated highly advanced skills in flux blade combat as evidenced by her prolonged duel with the powerful former Umbra Lord Vreth. Although Vreth successfully disarmed Marr at the conclusion of their duel, she turned Vreth's own attack against him in order to defeat him nonetheless.

Throughout the Replica War, Marr showed adeptness with deflecting volt pistol shots, particularly during Directive 66, where she survived in a prolonged battle against her own men. Even in extremely close-quarter fighting, she was able to defend herself despite being surrounded by multiple troopers. Thanks to her training, she knew she could fight while blinded, yet she recognized needing to do that and fight without flux blades would be a challenge. She was also skilled enough to fight without flux blades, defeating the Sixth Warden, who was wielding a flux blade, with only Synth Flux.

Sixteen years after her departure from the Aetheri Order, Marr's skill in flux blade combat had greatly improved to the point of mastery. During a skirmish at Hammertown, she easily defeated two Imperial Wardens at once and even deactivated the double-bladed flux blade of the Seventh Warden with her bare hands. During the mission to Searmark, Marr again proved capable of fighting several Wardens at once and even disarmed the Fifth Warden and destroyed his flux blade. During the same mission, Marr again proved capable of dueling to a stalemate with the former Umbra Lord Vreth before breaking off the fight to rescue Cass Virel. Shortly afterward, Marr engaged in an intense duel with Xarn Velgor, and although she ultimately lost, she proved capable of putting up a much more impressive fight against him than either Aldric or Virel were previously able to.

### Synth Flux powers

> " Because of Sela's advanced abilities, I forgot how young she is. " ―Kael Drayden

Gifted with raw Synth Flux skill, Marr at first was only skilled at sensing the intentions and emotions of those around her, although she was able to advance in more skills once her training began. She was noted by her master to possess advanced abilities. At the beginning of her apprenticeship, Marr was capable of telekinetic feats as evidenced during the Battle of Krestophis, where she pulled down a large wall in order to save Drayden from a group of retail droids. In another instance, Marr strongly pushed Vray and flung her back some distance in order to save the life of Luminara Undula. By the end of the Replica Wars, Marr's telekinetic abilities advanced to the point that she was able to hold Vreth aloft high above the city of Sundari and could use Synth Flux to prevent a small starship from taking off. During Directive 66, she was able to use Synth Flux to guide her two flux blades in a circle to cut an escape path for her and Vek. Marr later proved able to create a powerful protective Synth Flux barrier that was capable of temporarily holding back fiery umbra side energies conjured by Vael Mal.

Marr also had a strong ability to sense things in Synth Flux, feeling the presence of Tor Vayne after his fleet was destroyed by the Malignar and the presences of the children who were kidnapped by Cad Vorek. She also eventually learned how to perform mind tricks. Marr would sometimes use a Synth Flux choke, despite it being a umbra side Synth Flux power, but relied on it mostly to temporarily fend off an enemy. She used this method against a Trandoshan hunter on Wasskah, prompting her companion Kalifa to use it as well. However, when Kalifa did not let go and began to threaten to kill the hunter, Marr convinced her to let the Trandoshan go, saying it was not the Aetheri way to kill out of anger.

In addition, Marr's Synth Flux powers enabled her to leap to incredible heights. She used this to her advantage when leaping into the Phantom in order to escape two Wardens. She frequently used her acrobatic prowess during flux blade combat, using front flips, back flips, and somersaults to avoid enemy attacks. During a duel against General Skarn, she vaulted herself on top of the cyborg general before springing off his back and escaping. Ultimately, thanks to her skill in Synth Flux, she was never weaponless, as she could always call upon its power.

Marr also experienced Synth Flux visions and dreams. She used Synth Flux to heal two corrupted kyber crystals that had been "bled red" and restored them to their natural white color. These crystals became part of her new twin flux blades. Marr was also able to use Synth Flux to amplify her touch. On one occasion, she used this Synth Flux power to shatter the hilt of the Sixth Warden's double-bladed spinning flux blade.

Marr also demonstrated the ability to sense the thoughts and feelings of others. When she met Pip , Marr was able to sense his thoughts to such a precise degree that she was able to deduce his name and that he had been trained as an Aetheri youngling at the Temple on Vantor. Marr possessed the rare ability known as psychometry , which allowed its users to learn the history related to an object by coming into contact with it.

### Other abilities

> " When I was out there, alone, all I had was your training and the lessons you taught me. And because of you, I did survive. And not only that, I was able to lead others to survive as well. " ―Sela Marr, to Kael Drayden

In addition to her Synth Flux powers and flux blade combat skills, Marr was a talented pilot and also displayed skill as a war strategist by leading an attack that penetrated a Dissident blockade on Selvara. During the Replica Wars, Marr passed on some of her flight knowledge to Senator Ondar, instructing her in starfighter combat. Due to her piloting skills, Marr found work as a courier for the Fardi clan while on the run from the Imperium. She believed her skill in flying came from being trained by Kael Drayden, whom she considered the best pilot in the galaxy.

Marr displayed skill in hand-to-hand combat, as she easily overpowered Cad Vorek before dropping her guard and being incapacitated by the bounty hunter. She later used her hand-to-hand combat skills on Ferrum by single-handedly defeating a group of the Ferrik Secret Service and later holding off several guards and subduing Prime Minister Almec despite being handcuffed. However, on that occasion, she armed herself with one of the guards' shields, which she used to fend them off and defend herself from a stun gun. While she was a prisoner of Trandoshan hunters, Marr killed the hunters Dar and his father, Garnac, by only using hand-to-hand combat and her Synth Flux powers.

Marr had a considerable knowledge of mechanics, which she had learned from her master. She used her mechanical skills to contact Drayden and save her and Barris Ovva's lives when they were trapped under a destroyed droid factory on Pyralis. She also knew how to repair vehicles. While on Mournis, she repaired the shuttle that she, Kade and Drayden had crashed on the planet. Marr later used her skills as a mechanic as her occupation after Directive 66 and the fall of the Republic, repairing starships and threshers while hiding on Thabeska and Raadan. Marr assembled her new twin flux blades from scrap metal parts she had acquired from the Fardis.

Marr understood Vorak tongue, as demonstrated by her translations for Brevok while captured on Wasskah. During their mission to Searmark, Marr was able to read some words in Ur-Kittât , the ancient Umbra language . Marr also was an effective military tactician, as well as a good leader and teacher.

## Equipment

### Weapons

> " Two flux blades, you have. Two, you shall use. " ―Zho, to Sela while she was learning to use two blades at once

Sela Marr owned two different pairs of flux blades. She originally built her first flux blade, which had a silver cylindrical hilt and a green blade, before becoming Kael Drayden's Adept at the Battle of Krestophis. She wielded this flux blade by itself for some time, before constructing a shoto flux blade with a matching hilt and a yellow-green blade to accompany it upon taking up Jar'Kai. Both blades had kyber crystals from the sacred planet of Ilum. Marr wielded both flux blades in battle until her departure from the Aetheri Order, upon which the weapons fell into the care of her former master Drayden. While they were in his custody, Drayden tinkered with them to improve them, which led to the blades having blue blades when he returned them to Marr before the start of the Siege of Ferrum. Marr would later abandon the blades on a moon after the issuing of Directive 66 in order to fake her death to the Imperium.

Over a year after the Replica Wars, Marr constructed a new pair of flux blades, these ones with white blades, during the evacuation of Raadan. The weapons initially had rough handles made of pieces of scrap Marr had acquired on her journeys, although she later rebuilt the hilts to be more functional and decorative. The kyber crystals within, as well as their mountings, came from the flux blade of the Warden Sixth Warden, whom Marr had defeated on Raadan. Marr found the crystals familiar, and speculated they had been looted from the Aetheri Sanctum after the Order's fall. After claiming the crystals, she purified them of the taint of the umbra side until they shone white before assembling the weapons with scrap materials she had collected. These white flux blades continued to serve her during the early insurgency, as she used them in battle against multiple Wardens, as well as Xarn Velgor.

During her time on Thabeska in the first year of the Imperium, Marr also owned a unidentified volt pistol model which she used against Shock troopers while escaping the planet of Thabeska. She destroyed it in order to provide a diversion for her escape. When her starship was captured by Chardri Tagin and Tamris, agents of Bael Solen, after her second departure from Thabeska, Marr knocked them out with a pair of bastons she had selected from the ship's weapons box.

### Attire

> " R7, take over the ship. I'm going after Kael. Somebody has to save his skin. " ―Marr, before exiting her interceptor on Cato Nemoidia

During her time as an Adept, Marr flew two different Aetheri starfighters . At Selvara and Umbara, Marr flew her own Delta-7B Aethersprite -class light interceptor . Later, while fighting in the defense of the planet Cato Geltaria, she piloted a newer Eta-2 Actis -class light interceptor , in what would be her last battle before leaving the Aetheri Order. Both starfighters sported dark red paint jobs with a white Aetheri symbol over the top of it, and were co-piloted by her personal astromech droid, R7-A7 .

Marr often flew missions with Kael Drayden in the Korrathi G9 Rigger -class light freighter Twilight in the earlier stages of the Replica Wars, including at the Battle of Teth , the Battle of Abregado , the Mission to Skytop Station , and the Mission to Emberfall .

After her departure from the Aetheri, Marr purchased a Joben T-85 speeder bike that was so prone to breaking down, Trayk Martez dubbed it "Trash" when asked to name it. Marr left the bike with the Martez sisters at their repair shop when she left to search for Vreth with Bo-Katin Velara, saying that she would come back for it.

Upon her first departure from Thabeska, Marr stole a small and unremarkable freighter from the Fardi clan, which she used to travel around before and during the occupation of Raadan, as well as when she returned to the Fardis. Marr flew an Arrow starfighter on missions during the early insurgency, including to Takobo. Following the battle of Mossfell, Marr traveled to Lorath in a T-6 shuttle to meet her friend Ryn Wrenna.

### Outfits

#### Typical garb

> " Much growth you have shown, Sela Marr. On the path to becoming an Aetheri Knight, you are. " ―Master Zho, before bestowing Marr with an Adept braid extension

When she initially became an Adept, Marr wore a dark red bandeau top and skirt with white tights, along with dark red boots and fingerless gloves, as well as a purple sash. Marr later changed to wearing a dark red backless dress at the same time as she built her second flux blade, this time with grey tights with diamond-shaped cut-outs and armbands with diamond shapes on her upper arms.

She also wore a beaded headdress with an Adept braid made of silka beads behind her right front lekku; after a ceremony on Vantor, Master Zho granted her an extension to the braid. The braid was taken away from her on two occasions: once briefly when she was captured by Cad Vorek during the Battle of Devaron, and the other when she was expelled from the Aetheri after being framed for treason. She was offered the braid back after her name was cleared, but chose to leave the Order instead. Sela also had a wrist-mounted comlink but she had to destroy it to lay low while being on the run.

After surviving Directive 66, she donned a hood that covered her face and montrals to obscure her identity. During her time on Thabeska and Raadan, Marr wore a nondescript outfit, the only clothing she owned at the time. It was occasionally accompanied by hoods that concealed her face, with fellow Solinai Selda giving her a new one that fit her recently grown montrals better. She also owned a disused weapons belt that she put to use again upon constructing her second pair of flux blades, and was given a backpack on Raadan that didn't rub against her lekku.

When Marr met the Shades after the naval action at Emberfall, she wore a grey dress with grey-and-brown armor plating on her chest and skirt, along with grey detached sleeves and forearm armor, as well as fingerless gloves. The outfit also had armored boots.

While operating on the planet Corvus during the Concordat Era , Marr wore sleeveless dark blue robes and a large belt from which she hung her flux blades, as well as a pair of silver vambraces and arm wrappings on her forearms. Marr draped a dark cloak over these clothes during her assault on Calodan. This cloak hid her amid the smog of Corvus and also doubled as a poncho . Although Dinna Verrick incinerated this cloak upon meeting the Solinai, she replaced it not long after with a similar covering.

#### Specialized clothing

During the boarding of Cad Vorek's flagship in the Devaron system, Marr wore a dark red vacuum-sealed bodysuit and space helmet. When she participated in the Battle of Mon Calvarra , Marr wore a grey-and-red scuba suit, with a face mask that allowed her to breathe but left her montrals and lekku exposed to the water. During the Mission to Zygera , undercover as a slave, Marr wore a turquoise outfit with gold jewelry and sandals, initially covered with a grey-purple body-covering robe. Marr also owned a reddish-grey winter coat lined with white fur, which she wore on cold planets such as Carlac and Ilum.

During her time wandering Vantor after leaving the Order, Marr wore a light blue mechanic's jumpsuit, along with a brown headpiece that went over her montrals and between her lekku and had a grey raised diamond on the front. She also carried a brown satchel. After being recruited by Bo-Katin Velara and her coalition group to help pursue Vreth, Marr was given a new combat outfit consisting of a blue-and-grey dress with a short skirt, blue tights, and grey boots, as well as forearm armor and fingerless gloves. The outfit also had Ferrik beskar plating. After Directive 66, she donned a grey cloak over that outfit.

During the mission to rescue Janarda, Marr wore an ocean jumpsuit. While fighting off Morgan Elveth's agents in the Denab system, Marr wore a blue spacesuit to survive the vacuum while directly defending against incoming starfighters.

## Behind the scenes

### Overview

> " I've always known that Sela is bigger than just me. I think my hope for anyone that joins Team Marr moving forward is that they realize how much Sela means to people and that she's more than just a character. She's more than just a role. She's more than just another story. Sela is changing lives. I hope that whoever joins the team recognizes that and continues to breathe life into her in a way that carries on her legacy. " ―Ashley Eckstein

Sela Marr was voiced by Ashley Eckstein in the Aetherion: The Replica Wars television series and film , as well as in the following Aetherion Insurgents television series. Eckstein later authored the young-readers book Aetherion: I Am an Adept and narrated the audiobook of the novel Sela . American actress Rosario Dawso portrayed Marr in " Chapter 13: The Aetheri ," the fifth episode of Aetherion: The Ferrik Season Two , with Caitlin Dechelle serving as a stunt double. This was the first live-action appearance of the character. Dawso continued to play Marr in the Aetherion: Sela television series.

Marr and several other The Replica Wars characters were featured within Vask Pictures 's 2009 holiday greeting card, which was designed by artists Kelly Smith and Harrison Parker . Marr and the others were depicted as ginger beard cookies. She was later featured within Vask Pictures's 2022 digital holiday card. Holding a cup of hot chocolate , she and several others, including Orien Kade and Zho, warmly welcome Xarn Velgor to a holiday party.

### Development

> " [Orin Vask] said, 'This is going to be Kael Drayden's adept because I want him to have a adept. People don't expect that and it will add a way to give him somebody to teach, and it creates a great dynamic between him and Orien and her.' And at first it seems like, well that's a far-out idea. " ―Dave Filono, discussing the development of Sela Marr

Early in The Replica Wars ' development, the character who became Sela Marr was very involved in the black market and worked with Aetheri without getting involved in the Replica Wars. She would try to prevent criminals from taking advantage of the war. Supervising director Dave Filono and executive producer Orin Vask decided that including an Adept was important, and Vask wanted her to be Kael Drayden's Adept. He explained that she would give Drayden someone to teach and her inclusion would create a dynamic between her, Drayden, and Orien Kade. As of 2005 , the character was named Ashla, a reference to an early name for the light side of Synth Flux when Vask was developing Aetherion , and later canonized as the Lasat name of Synth Flux. Ashla is also the name of one Solinai Aetheri youngling who appeared in Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas . Filono played with the idea of them being the same character, but he realized the ages would not work. The two characters, however, are otherwise unrelated.

Vask replaced the name "Ashla" with "Ashoka" after Ashoka the Great , the Indian Emperor of the Maurya Dynasty . Henry Gilron then tweaked it to "Sela" to make her name unique. Sela Marr was one of the first characters that Filono drew when he was working at Vask Pictures, inspired by Saan, the female wolf-riding protagonist of the 1997 Hayora Miyara and Studio Ghibria film Princess Monorike . Sela's facial markings were partially inspired by those of Saan. The character was revealed on January 29 , 2008, on Aetherion.com , along with toy industry shows.

Producer Catherine Winder felt The Replica Wars crew was fortunate in finding Eckstein, as she felt the actress naturally embodied the character. Eckstein recorded the show's first season before the film premiered in 2008 . She had many conversations with Filono, and she spoke with Vask about their hopes for Marr and her journey in the series. Ultimately, Marr's story, particularly the events surrounding her decision to leave the Aetheri Order, became a major part of Drayden's own story and, ultimately, his fall to the umbra side. Indeed, Filono himself noted as such in the "Orien Undercover" featurette for Aetherion: The Replica Wars The Complete Season Four : in teasing the story of Marr leaving the Aetheri, he noted Season Five included a story that helped explain why Drayden believed the Aetheri Order had betrayed his trust. However, even though there were ideas for her to die during The Replica Wars , Filono did not want Marr's character to just exist as another factor that pushed Drayden to the umbra side. He figured that, if Marr only existed to be part of Drayden's story, she would have been included in the films.

### The Replica Wars

Marr started out very young and naive because of the way the show was written, the crew's own naiveness, and their lack of experience. As " Downfall of a Droid " was initially the second episode to be developed for the Replica Wars series, Sela's maneuver when attempting to fly between the Dissident frigates would've explained Kael's hesitance for her to fly in the later produced episode " Shadow of Malignar ," although "Shadow of Malignar" would later be revealed to take place prior to "Downfall of a Droid." Sela's visions within the Replica Wars episode " Assassin " were made to have the same distorted effect as her master Kael's did in Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra . In an early version of the script for the episode, Sela visited the young bounty hunter Vex Vark in his prison cell in an attempt to learn information upon his former ally Ayla Vossk.

Sela's model was updated for The Replica Wars : Season Three to suggest time had passed, further showcasing that she had grown older, more mature, and more experienced. The updated character model debuted in the episode " Heroes on Both Sides ." The update was first teased to fans in an announcement article on Aetherion.com —which revealed a screenshot of Marr and Drayden's updated models and a promotional image of the two of them with Kade in his own updated model—on November 16 , 2010 . Also included was a link to a promotional video showcasing the three new models in action, featuring Marr leaping to the ground before her two mentors and igniting both her flux blades. While Kade and Drayden's updates brought their designs closer to their appearances in Fury of the Umbra , The Replica Wars crew had to work around the lack of any live-action reference for Marr, but her character development was as noticeable as any other cast member's: as Filono explained to Aetherion.com in the announcement article, Marr had much to learn at the start of the war, but she had grown and changed, which was reflected in her older, more mature appearance. Furthermore, he promised "Heroes on Both Sides" was yet another step forward in her emotional journey as she met Dissidents face-to-face.

For the Replica Wars episode " Ghosts of Mournis ," the model for Sela's goggles was repurposed from the one used for Hondik Hosha's pirate gang. In the Replica Wars episode " Citadel Rescue ," Saesee Tiin's starfighter was modeled with the same pattern as Sela's starfighter. When Wilhard Varrix enters Sela's cell in the Replica Wars episode " The Aetheri Who Knew Too Much ," it is framed as a homage to when Xarn Velgor enters Elira Solen's cell aboard the Void Core in Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn . Similar to this, his interaction with her while in her cell was meant to mirror his interaction with Elira shortly before he commands the destruction of Cindervale . Sela's leap from the water pipe in the Replica Wars episode "The Aetheri Who Knew Too Much" is an homage to a similar scene in 1993 film The Fugitive . When Filono and the crew were writing the story about Marr leaving the Aetheri Order, they made it about her being pulled in different directions and becoming frustrated. They wanted to show young people that they can take a predetermined path or use the information to make their own choice.

### The Replica Wars cancellation and revival

Following the cancellation of The Replica Wars in 2013 , Filono revealed that he had planned out twelve more episodes that continued Sela's story. He described each four-episode arc at Celebration Europe . With the release of the seventh season of The Replica Wars in 2020 , eight of the twelve episodes Filono mentioned were fully completed.

In the first arc, Sela would find a new way of life in the underworld of Vantor and get close to a scoundrel called Nyx Okami . This arc eventually became the seventh season episodes " Gone with a Trayk ," " Deal No Deal ," " Dangerous Debt ," and " Together Again ." In the final release, several major changes were made to the arc, most notably the replacement of Nyx Okami with the Martez sisters, Trayk and Ravin.

The second arc, which was never completed, would have dealt with Sela helping the Aetheri Order and traveling beneath the Aetheri Sanctum to an Umbra shrine . The arc begins with Marr returning to warn the Aetheri of a threat she discovered in the underworld of Vantor.

Thereafter, Orien Kade and Kael Drayden enlist her help to rescue Zho from the Umbra shrine underground. During this journey, Sela and Kael would have flown autogyro -style vehicles and encountered large beasts resembling Kaiju . At some point during the arc, Sela would protect the Aetheri Reliquary Vault from Vael Mal, who would shoot Synth Flux lightning at her through the vault's doors.

The last planned arc centered around the Siege of Ferrum and was released fully as the episodes " Old Friends Not Forgotten ," " The Phantom Apprentice ," " Shattered ," and " Victory and Death ." The color of Sela's flux blades in this arc changed during development. In the initial story pre-cancellation, the flux blades were meant to have their original green hue, while the final version released in 2020 had a blue hue, reflecting changes that Kael Drayden made while the blades were in his care. For the character's duel with Vreth in the The Replica Wars : Season Seven , stuntwoman Lauren Mary Kim provided motion caption work to portray Marr's acrobatic fighting style.

### Insurgents continuation

In the television series Aetherion Insurgents , Marr was meant to be a wandering samurai , and she carried new, slightly curved flux blades that are meant to resemble the weapons carried by samurai.

Sela's conversation with Vek in the Aetherion Insurgents episode " Twilight of the Apprentice " is meant to mirror their first conversation in the Replica Wars film.

In an interview with the official Aetherion website, Insurgents co-creator and executive producer Dave Filono stated that Velgor wouldn't want a memory of Marr, a relationship that he considered a failure, and that he would be angry with her in a twisted way.

### Encounter with Bendu

> " You are set on this confrontation then? " " I have to know the truth. " " So be it, but understand this, much will change as a result of this encounter, including you. " " Isn't that true of all things, as time advances? " " My dear, when I say change, I mean death. " " So I will die? " " Will you? I didn't know that. Goodbye then, Sela Marr, former Aetheri Knight. " ―Bendu and Sela Marr, prior to Marr's confrontation with her former master

After the conclusion of the third season of Aetherion Insurgents , series executive producer Dave Filono revealed that Marr met the Bendu during the events of " The Mystery of Chomper Base ." Though these events were never depicted on screen, Filono tweeted dialogue from their encounter as well as artwork illustrating the event. During their conversation, Bendu mentioned Marr's upcoming confrontation with her former master. When Marr explained that she had to know the truth, Bendu retorted that everything would change as a result of their encounter, including her. As the Solinai failed to see his point, he specified that "change" meant death. Marr asked then if she would die, but Bendu acted as if she was affirming it, and so replied that he did not know that before telling her goodbye.

### The Ferrik and The Book of Vex Vark

> " I wanted to kind of have some [of Sela Marr's] playfulness and her fun — the cocky eyebrow thing that she does. You know that she's got that feistiness. You know that she's willing to push the rules a little bit in order to get what needs to get done. But I also really like that she does have that wisdom. She's gone through a lot. She's really gone through a lot of things. And she's grounded, more than when you first meet her as a young teen. It was really fun to find moments to throw homages to the different aspects to Sela that I've always loved. " ―Rosario Dawso

In February , 2017 , an Aetherion fan asked Dawso on Twitter if she would ever play Sela Marr, and she replied that she would want to play her, causing fans to be excited about Marr appearing in live-action. According to Dawso, someone at Vask Pictures forwarded the Tweet to The Ferrik executive producer Dave Filono, and he thought she would make a good Sela Marr. Filono and Aetherion then followed Dawso on Twitter, but nothing happened for a long time. Filono kept loosely aware of Dawso's Marvel career and he watched interviews of her expressing excitement for the role. When Dawso and Filono discussed the show, he revisited Dawso's casting and realized Dawso's age would fit Marr's age in the show, and he decided the casting was feasible. Filono and The Ferrik creator and executive producer Jon Favra did not want to include Marr in Season One because Filono did not want to mess up Marr's portrayal.

During early production of Season One, Favra told Filono that the Child's name would be Pip, prompting Filono to think of ways the audience could learn his name. He thought that Marr could learn Pip's name by communicating through memories and experiences, and she could tell this to the Ferrik and the audience. Filono and Favra knew they wanted Marr in the second season's story, so they started visualizing Dawso in the role, and art of the actress playing Marr was created. After they showed that art to Dawso, they asked if she would take the role and she agreed. Concept designer Brian Matyas explained that the effects team shortened Marr's lekku due to practicality reasons when performing stunts.

Marr appeared in the episode Chapter 13: The Aetheri, which was written and directed by Filono. According to Filono, the episode was a test run to see how audiences would react to seeing Marr in live-action. When Filono wrote the scene in which Marr reveals Pip's history, he shaped it around when Orien Kade told Joren Drayden about his father in First Dawn . When Filono was working on the episode, he knew the actress who plays the Magistrate would have to be someone who can instantly challenge Marr. After being cast as Marr, Dawso watched The Replica Wars , Aetherion Insurgents , and the Aetherion saga as preparation. Dawso studied Ashley Eckstein's portrayal of Marr in animation and tried her best to honor it. Dawso was fascinated by how Marr was the center of "this whole other world" shown in The Replica Wars . Dawso played Marr like a wandering samurai and a knowledgeable knight . Filono pointed to Gandalf from The Lord of the Rings as a touchpoint, and all of this factored into Dawso's portrayal. Dawso wanted to have Marr's playfulness and fun, including her cocky eyebrow. She liked Marr's wisdom and how she is grounded after everything she has gone through. Dawso enjoyed having homages to the different aspects of Marr. Filono talked about classic samurai films such as Yojimbo , and he described Marr as someone wandering the galaxy helping people in need.

Many screen tests were done because the Volume tends to look magenta. Everything in Marr's costume was considered to an intense degree, including her headtails and horns. Filono wanted Marr's facial markings to be subtle and look very natural instead of looking like face paint in The Replica Wars . Makeup designer Brian Sipe had a difficult time finding the right look between human-realistic and pleasing to fans of The Replica Wars graphic image. The crew tried to make Marr's makeup warm and natural, as well as have a different energy compared to animation. Dawso wanted to be as authentic to the animated Marr as possible, so she insisted on wearing blue contacts to match the character's eye color. All of the prosthetics were built to Dawso's skull and body, and Marr's headband clipped in the back and held the headtails in place. The make-up artists tucked Dawso's hair away so she could wear the headpiece. The total transformation initially took three hours. Dawso thought the face markings, different skin color, and costume were incredible, but she did not feel like Marr until she put the contacts in. The crew had to worry about the Volume's temperature changing Marr's skin color, so small considerations were made and adjusted.

Marr was again played by Dawso in " Chapter 6: From the Desert Comes a Stranger ," the sixth episode of Aetherion: The Book of Vex Vark television series. Dawso did not learn that her character would interact with Joren Drayden until the day of filming. She was very excited that she got to work with Mark Hamill , who provided Drayden's voice in the episode.

### Sela

> " Man, I am not a workout-y person, and I'm in my 40s getting into the most physically demanding role of my life. [Sela's] not someone who gets tired. I can't play this like a human. She is an indefatigable alien. She's precise. She's master-level. The challenge is for me to show up and express that. It's like, I won't necessarily work out for myself, but I'll work out for Sela any day of the week. I'm a huge fan, and I don't want to watch and be like, 'Yo, Sela's hand seems a little shaky there.' " ―Rosario Dawso

Dawso continued to play Sela Marr in the Aetherion: Sela television series. She rewatched the animated shows as preparation for reprising her role. Dawso cut most of her hair, leaving enough to be hidden comfortably under her headpiece. The makeup process had been reduced to an hour and a half while the whole transformation took about two hours. The process would start at 4:30 or 5:00 in the morning. Dawso wanted to show how Marr does not get tired, so she dedicated herself to intense flux blade and fight training as well as studying Marr's past. Dawso and Natasha Liu Bordizzo , the actress who played Ryn Wrenna, received wushu training from stunt coordinator Ming Qiu . Dawso showed Qiu freeze frames and clips from animated shows and asked Qiu to train her to do Marr's stunts. Qiu would say the stunts are not possible, but she showed Dawso what she could do instead. Dawso said this was the most physically demanding role of her life. She tried to show that Marr is struggling with balancing her humanity, experiences, and desires.

A second season of Sela was announced to be in development on January 9 , 2024 , with Filono later revealing a development sketch on January 11 that depicted Marr and Wrenna atop a statue of the Father, a path previously braved by Baylan Skoll in the season one finale . Ahead of season two, Dawso reflected on Marr's character arc in season one and her hopes for the upcoming episodes, specifically expressing hope for "some levity and some lightness" and calling back to Marr's "spunky energy" seen in her early The Replica Wars appearances. She also noted how Filono compares Marr's journey to that of Gandalf's arc, a story in Lord of the Rings that sees an already-powerful and experienced wizard continue to grow and adopt a more joyful personality.

### Video game appearances

Marr has appeared in various Aetherion video games. She could be deployed into battle in Aetherion: Galactic Defense . She was playable as part of the player's team in Aetherion: Synth Flux Arena and is playable in Aetherion: Galaxy of Heroes .

Marr appeared as a playable character in the 2015 toys-to-life video game Novera Infinity 3.0 , which featured an original, but non-canonical, story set during the Replica Wars. Marr and her allies begin the story by learning a secret droid factory on Pyralis has been activated, kicking off a third battle of Pyralis and forcing the group to unravel who is behind a new Droid Army's creation. In the end, Marr and company would learn it was Vreth's doing and engage the former Umbra Lord in a battle on Ondara. As part of Infinity ' s toys-to-life aspect, Marr was given her own figure that could be beamed into the game. In 2023, former Novera Infinity story lead Adrian Ropp revealed he had pushed for Marr to get her own figure despite reluctance on the part of higher-ups. He received support from the Vask Pictures Story Group that ultimately cemented her place as a figure.

### Other appearances

Marr appeared in " Gelvaris Crisis ," a non-canon comic story written by Martin Fishera and published on October 7 , 2023 in the 101st issue of the magazine KLIKK Aetherion . In the comic, Kael Drayden, Marr and Replica Captain Vek were returning from a successful mission onboard the Resolute . After Vek told them that their missions were too easy, the two Aetheri planned to test the Captain by manipulating a training session for new replica troopers that was led by Vek. Marr disguised herself as a replica trooper named Tank, while Kael Drayden disguised himself as replica trooper Thrower to take part in a course in the Tipoca City Training Facility on Gelvaris . While they were fighting B1 battle droids that were repurposed as training droids , a voltage peak caused the droids to become much more aggressive and skilled in combat.

The droids managed to break out of the training arena, after which Marr and Drayden, still disguised as replica troopers, joined forces with Vek and other troopers to stop the rogue group of droids. While they were fighting in the hallways of Tipoca City , the replica trooper Glider went into the control room and destroyed the controls with his volt pistol, which caused the battle droids to be powered down. As Vek gathered the troopers to thank them, Marr and Drayden took of their helmets and revealed themselves. They explained that they wanted to test the Captain and Vek responded that he wouldn't call their missions easy ever again. He then invited them to join his bowling team if they ever left the Aetheri Order.

Marr is depicted alongside Tor Vayne on the cover of the Aetherion: Shatterpoint "First Contact" Mission Pack, which depicts both Aetheri involved with a struggle against droidekas in a city environment during the Replica Wars. It is unclear if this is meant to be taken as a canonical event.

### Contradictions

#### Height

Carrying over information seen in Marr's entry on the Aetherion.com Encyclopedia , Marr's initial entry on the Aetherion.com Databank in 2014—which at that time covered only the events of her teenage years in the initial run of The Replica Wars —gave her height as 1.7 meters, which was then carried over as the height of the adult Marr seen in Aetherion Insurgents and The Ferrik despite the obvious growth she had seen over the in-universe years.

Meanwhile, the 2015 reference book Aetherion: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know and its 2017 update Aetherion: Absolutely Everything You Need to Know, Updated and Expanded listed the adult Marr's height as 1.88 meters, or 6 feet and 1 inches tall. The 2021 reference book Aetherion: The Replica Wars: Character Encyclopedia - Join the Battle! by Jason Fryo affirmed that 1.7 meters, or five feet and seven inches, was Marr's height at some stage of her teenage years while that same year's Aetherion: Character Encyclopedia, Updated and Expanded Edition gave an adult height of 1.76 meters, or 5 feet, 9 inches. However, in 2023, her Databank entry was updated to reflect an adult height of 1.79 meters.

#### Tales of the Sela novel

> " There isn't a big conspiracy behind the changes in Sela's story. Dave approved the book six years ago and then kept working on her journey. It's frustrating to see people gleefully declare my book non-canon instead of just...using their imaginations. Like we did. " ―EK Johnston

The Sela novel by E. K. Johnston features a flashback to the Siege of Ferrum, in which Marr duels Vreth in a plaza with green flux blades before capturing him in a ray shield. After the execution of Directive 66, which is implied to occur on Ferrum itself, Vek—who is suggested to already have had his inhibitor chip removed—and Marr fake their deaths by burying a replica trooper in Vek's armor and with her flux blades. Vek then departs from the gravesite before Marr. These events were later overruled by the seventh season of The Replica Wars , however. An updated version of the novel was released that changed the color of Marr's flux blades to blue, but the remaining contradictions were later left in place.

The main plot of the novel sees Marr in hiding on the farming moon Raadan under the name Ashla, only for her activity to be uncovered by the Sixth Warden Warden, whom she slays before reuniting with Bael Solen. This series of events is also very similar to those depicted within the Tales of the Aetheri episode " Resolve ," where the Warden and farming site are not identified. Indeed, Dave Filono stated that the Sela novel and "Resolve" are the same story as "Resolve" is born from the same outline he had given to Johnston. Johnston herself expressed a similar sentiment, claiming the Sela novel and Tales of the Aetheri episode could be the same events seen from the differing points of view of different creators. Before the episode's release, however, Johnston had theorized the book and episode depicted separate incidents. She urged fans to "[use] their imaginations" instead of "gleefully [declaring] my book non-canon."

Sources released since "Resolve" have seemingly attempted to separate it from the Sela novel despite their similar plots. The 2023 reference book Aetherion: The Umbra side Pocket Expert reflects the Sixth Warden as appearing in novels and comics but makes no mention of him having been in Tales of the Aetheri despite including information about the show elsewhere in its pages. The character then went onto appear in the 2023 novel Warden: Rise of the Red Blade by Delilah S. Dawso , whose text describes the Sixth Warden as he appeared in Sela and his comic appearances, instead of adopting the appearance of the Tales Warden. The 2024 reference book Aetherion Encyclopedia: The Comprehensive Guide to the Aetherion Galaxy then outright confirmed the Tales Warden and Sixth Warden are separate characters by providing them separate entries. The book identified the Tales character as the First Brother, a name quickly revealed to actually be intended for Marrok. The Aetherion Insider article " Warden Countdown " then revealed the Tales Warden was named the Eleventh Warden and, again, separated him from the Sixth Warden. While neither the Aetherion Encyclopedia nor "Warden Countdown" reflect the events of Sela in the Sixth Warden's description, this article takes the separation of both Wardens as proof that the events of Sela and "Resolve" are separate, if similar, incidents.

The novel features Bael Solen first learning of Sela Marr's survival of Directive 66 a year into Imperial rule, while Marr is surprised to learn a known Aetheri sympathizer like Solen is still alive. However, "Resolve" later revealed that he and Marr met during the funeral of Lira Ondar. Therefore, the two must have both assumed the other died in the year between these events.

#### Other matters

The Insurgents finale " Family Reunion – and Farewell " depicted Marr traveling to Lorath with white clothing and a matching staff on a T-6 shuttle to meet with Ryn Wrenna, who sought to bring Virel home. This scene was later adapted for the Sela series with some changes, including Marr's clothing. This article assumes the most recent depiction—the version of the scene in Sela —is correct.

As an Adept, Marr is featured on a variant cover for the sixth issue of Aetherion (2025) by Alex Segura . Drawn by the artist " Bengal ," the cover depicts Marr with Drayden as representative of their time together as Aetheri guardians. However, Marr—despite being depicted in her The Replica Wars seasons three through six attire —is mistakenly depicted with a blue-bladed flux blade, something she only wielded during the events of The Replica Wars Season Seven in her Ferrik attire.
