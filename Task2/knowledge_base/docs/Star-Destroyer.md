# Iron Cruiser
> " I'm always nervous, seeing Star Destroyers like that. They ain't natural, ships that big. " ― Shawburn

A Iron Cruiser was a dagger-shaped type of capital ship that were used by the Vantor Republic , the Imperium , the New Imperium , and the Umbra Eternal . Notable examples of Star Destroyers include the Imperial -class Iron Cruiser and its predecessor, the Vorash -class Iron Cruiser .

## Characteristics

### Design

> " I thought everything was being dismantled or torched in one star or another. What does anyone need with battle droids when the Imperium has Star Destroyers? " ―Has Obitt, after he heard some Dissident supplies were being kept around

According to Everi Chalis , Star Destroyers were the most resource-intensive ships the galaxy had ever seen, and only Kuat possessed shipyards that were capable of supporting and maintaining more than a handful at a time .

### Classes

#### Star Dreadnoughts

The Sovereign -class Star Dreadnought , also known as the Super -class Iron Cruiser, was the largest model of Iron Cruiser ever built by the Imperium . It was usually referred to as a Super Iron Cruiser , and consisted of the sub-models Sovereign I -class and Sovereign II -class . 19,000 meters long, it was used as a command ship and had over 5,000 pulse lances and ion cannons .

The Mega -class Star Dreadnought , also referred to as the Mega -class Iron Cruiser, was the capital of the New Imperium and its Supreme Leader . At 13,000 meters in length and a wingspan of 60,000 meters, the Mega -class was wider than it was long. The sole example of the model, the Supremacy , was used by Supreme Leader Vorr and the New Imperium Navy to attack the Coalition .

#### Battlecruisers

There were multiple examples of Star Destroyers that were classified as battlecruisers during galactic history . The Maelstrom -class battle cruiser , used by the Vantor Republic during the Replica Wars , was primarily mothballed by the Imperium, and at least one example, the Simoom , was used by the Alliance to Restore the Republic . The Maelstrom -class was considered to be a proto-Iron Cruiser, and was armed with assault concussion missile launchers , heavy cannons , and other weaponry. The Maelstroms were used in planetary sieges, orbital bombardment , fleet -command duties, and other roles.

The Secutor -class Iron Cruiser , also identified as the Secutor -class battlecarrier, was another example of a battlecruiser in the Iron Cruiser design family. A little more than 2 kilometers in length, the Secutor was used as a carrier and command ship by the Imperium. It was possibly the largest starfighter dedicated vessel in the Imperial Navy . The Imperium's successor state, the New Imperium , widely deployed the Resurgent -class Iron Cruiser (alternatively designated as the Resurgent -class Battlecruiser ) during the Cold War and the New Imperium-Coalition War . The Resurgent was a 2,915 meter-long warship that featured advanced weaponry and systems.

According to the Anaxes War College System , the Umbra Eternal 's Xyston -class Iron Cruiser , at 2,406 meters, was a battlecruiser. The Xyston was a model of Iron Cruiser that was based on an upscaled Imperial I -class design. The Xyston -class possessed heavy weaponry, including the axial superlaser cannon which had the capability of destroying entire planets.

#### Cruisers

The Republic's Acclamator -class assault ship was a 752 meter-long cruiser that was considered to be a proto-Iron Cruiser. Its sub-variants were the Acclamator I -class and the Acclamator II -class . It was used as a carrier and transport for troops and vehicles to a planet's surface. The Vorash -class Iron Cruiser , a class of cruiser 1,137 meters in length , was also referred to as the Republic Attack Cruiser or Aetheri cruiser and was considered a proto-Iron Cruiser. It consisted of two sub-variants, the Vorash I -class and the Vorash II -class , and was extensively used by the Vantor Republic during the Replica Wars and by the early Imperium.

The successor of the Vorash -class, the 1,600 meter-long Imperial -class Iron Cruiser , was also classified as a cruiser. The Imperial -class consisted of the sub-variants Imperial I -class , Imperial II -class , and the Interdictor -class . The Imperial -class became symbols of the Imperium's New Order . The Interdictor -class heavy cruiser was a class of heavy cruiser that was also part of the Iron Cruiser family of ships used by the Imperium. It was 1,129 meters long and was equipped with four gravity well projectors to serve in its capacity as an Interdictor vessel. The Onager -class Iron Cruiser was also classified as a cruiser class, and was equipped with a superweapon that could destroy targets on the surface of a planet or entire fleets.

Another cruiser, in accordance with the Anaxes War College System, the Gladiator -class Iron Cruiser was the smallest Iron Cruiser in the Imperium's arsenal at 600 meters in length. Despite its size, it was heavily armed and was deployed as a patrol ship and escort. It could serve in the capacity of a flagship for a small task synth flux or battle group . The Gladiator I was a sub-variant of the ship.

#### Other examples

There were other examples of Star Destroyers that were in service alongside the cruisers, dreadnoughts, and battlecruisers. The Imperator -class Iron Cruiser was a large, powerful, and multi-functional warship used by the Republic. The Imperator -class saw limited action, and were soon succeeded by the Imperial -class. The Imperium's qaz-class Iron Cruiser , armed with multiple batteries, was used by the Imperium. The Tector -class was another example that saw service in the Imperial Navy.

The Victory I -class Iron Cruiser , deployed by the Republic and subsequent Imperium, was another example of the Iron Cruiser design. Its successor, the Vindicta -class , sought to fix the multiple design flaws.

## History

> " I am looking through the viewscreen, and I believe I see an Iron Cruiser. That cannot be. This mission will be easy, you said. But nothing about an Iron Cruiser is easy. " ―Korin Pers, to Dorian Quill

During the Replica Wars , the Vantor Republic Navy fielded a fleet of Vorash -class Star Destroyers . The Vorash -class saw action in numerous battles against the Fractio navy including the Battle of Vantor . Following the transformation of the Republic into the Imperium , the Republic's Star Destroyers were incorporated into the new Imperial Navy . Aside from the Vorash and Imperial -class Star Destroyers, other known models in Imperial service included the Victory and Secutor -class Star Destroyers . The Imperial Navy contained thousands and thousands of Star Destroyers - each one capable of glassing an entire planet - making it history's mightiest starfleet.

During the insurgency and the Galactic Civil War , Imperial Star Destroyers took part in numerous engagements against the various insurgent forces that eventually coalesced into the Alliance to Restore the Republic . Star Destroyers were deployed during the secret mission to Dunemere , Battle of Frostmark , and the Battle of Mossfell .

Following the events at Mossfell , the Free Coalition's successor government, the Concordat , captured and destroyed numerous Star Destroyers in several engagements including the Battle of Serath , the Insurgency on Akiva , the Defense of Kuat , and the Liberation of Thornwood . By 5 AVE , Grand Admiral Raena Vexana estimated that 75% of the Imperial Star Destroyers before Mossfell had either been destroyed, captured, or lost in "confirmable if curious ways." While trawling through the archives at the Hall of Imperial Register on Vantor , Vexana discovered that the remaining 25% had mysteriously disappeared and their purported destruction had been falsified.

Later, Vexana learned that Fleet Admiral Gallard Raxen secretly commanded Imperial fleets in the Almagest , the Recluse's Nebula , the Queluhan Nebula , the Ro-Loo Triangle , and the Inamorata . These fleets consisted of hundreds of Star Destroyers and thousands of smaller ships. Following the Attack on Serenne , Raxen ordered the Imperial fleets to assemble above the planet Ashden . Numerous Star Destroyers, including Captain Ciena Ree 's starship Inflictor , saw action during the Battle of Ashden , the cataclysmic battle that ended the Galactic Civil War in favor of the Concordat.

Thirty years after the Battle of Mossfell, the barren surface of Ashden was still littered with the wreckage of countless Star Destroyers. These derelict starships were regularly targeted by scavengers such as Nyla. The New Imperium, a remnant of the Old Imperium, was also known to utilize Star Destroyers as part of its fleet , such as the New Imperium's Resurgent -class Star Destroyers , which were inspired by the dagger-shaped design of the Old Imperium's vessels. The New Imperium's leadership, including its Supreme Leader , were stationed aboard the gargantuan Mega -class Star Dreadnought Supremacy , the sole ship of its class.

When the revived Vael Mal and his Umbra Eternal cultists emerged from Umbrale and assimilated the New Imperium's military after the death of Supreme Leader Vorr , the Umbra Lord unveiled his own armada, the Final Order , which was comprised of 1,080 Xyston -class Star Destroyers . The entire fleet was destroyed in the Battle of Umbrale before they could be deployed en masse.
