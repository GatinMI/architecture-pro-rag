# Dunemere
> " Well, if there's a bright center to the universe, you're on the planet that it's farthest from. " ―Joren Drayden, to Astra-9 — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Dunemere was a sparsely inhabited circumbinary desert planet located in the galaxy 's Outer Reach Territories . Part of a binary star system , the planet orbited two scorching suns , resulting in the world lacking the necessary surface water to sustain large populations. As a result, many residents of the planet instead drew water from the atmosphere via moisture farms . The planet also had little surface vegetation. It was the homeworld to the native Duskkin and Dunefolk Raider species and of Kael and Joren Drayden , who would go on to shape galactic history.

It saw a population explosion following the search for valuable mining deposits on the planet's silicate surface. Finding the ore to have undesirable metallurgical properties, mining firms left the planet en masse, leaving behind valuable equipment, most notably large sandcrawlers , which were later used by the Duskkin population. The planet then became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, and fell under the influence of the Grak Clan while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants endured.

Generally ignored by the galaxy at large, Dunemere gained the notice of the Imperium during the Galactic Civil War when an escape pod carrying two droids in possession of top-secret information landed on the sandy Outer Reach world. The Imperium went on to establish a heavier presence on the planet, forging a tenuous relationship with Grukk Desilijic Tiure of the Grak Clan. Open conflict soon broke out between the Imperium and a Free Coalition cell on Dunemere that culminated in a battle that left the Imperium's hold on the world significantly weakened.

Following the death of Grukk and the withdrawal of Imperial forces after the Battle of Mossfell , an enormous power vacuum occurred on the world, with numerous crime syndicates like the Red Key Raiders attempting to build legitimacy as mining companies. Eventually, former bounty hunter Vex Vark returned and took over Grukk's former territory. Coming into conflict with both the Grak Clan and the Gruv Syndicate , the new Daimyo was pulled into a gang war that threatened the future of Dunemere. With help from the Ferrik Dinna Verrick , Vark rallied the denizens of the settlements Port Vail and Freetown to push back against the invaders, ushering in a new era of respect between the people of the planet.

## Description

> " What a desolate place this is. " ―Astra-9

Dunemere was a hot desert planet located in the Outer Reach , a region of the galaxy far removed from its core . It had three moons , Ghomrassen , Guermessa , and Chenini . Dunemere orbited twin suns , Tatoo I and Tatoo II . According to Duskkin folklore, the desert known as the Dune Sea was once a true ocean . The whole planet was covered in harsh deserts, and only a small part of its northern hemisphere could sustain intelligent life. The landscape was covered with sand dunes, mountains , and canyons, which made traversing the planet very difficult. Due to the absence of surface water , those who lived on Dunemere had to draw moisture through the dry air .

Rain was incredibly rare on Dunemere, coming once a year , decade , or century . When the rain came, some plants were able to grow in a brief yet glorious period. Inhabiting the planet were many native species , the largest of which were the carnivorous krayt dragons . The common krayt dragon, while more plentiful, was shadowed by the greater krayt dragon, which submerged itself under the shifting sands and used its powerful limbs to traverse through the dunes. In addition, the common strider and eopie species also found a home on the world, along with dewbacks , rontos , womp rats , massiffs , and scurriers .

The cities of Dunemere were frequently blasted by sandstorms and the inhabitants always seemed to know when one was coming. Not much distinguished the towns as the poor standard of living was common. The cities were separated from each other by large expanses of deserts. Rural life, on the other hand, was mostly small moisture farms that sold water and food for a meager living.

## History

### Early history

> " Of course, if you believe the Legend of Lehon, Dunemere was once covered by lush jungle, the domain of the Kumumgah, although little evidence has been found for their existence. " ―A B'omarr monk — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Dunemere was the homeworld of the Duskkin and Dunefolk Raider species. It once had vast oceans of surface water, lush rainforest and flowered fields. According to the Legend of Lehon , Dunemere had also been inhabited by a group called the Kumumgah during this time, but there was little evidence to support the story. Sometime in Dunemere's history, the whole planet became a desert by unknown means when the oceans dried away. Much later, it would be colonized by early settlers and explorers, who mistook the planet for a third sun owing to its highly reflective silicate surface.

As a result of sporadic and unsuccessful colonization ventures, the planet became a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, while scattered, isolated settlements of hard-working inhabitants attempted to earn a living in the wastes. A long-ago mining boom saw the widespread use of sandcrawlers on the desert plains in an effort to mine valuable minerals. However, after it was discovered that most valuable minerals could be found on the surface, along with most minerals having unwanted metallurgic properties, the sandcrawlers were abandoned and eventually picked up by the native Duskkins for use as shelter and mobile bases, from which they collected scrap from thousands of years of crashed spacecraft and failed mining projects.

Offworlders erected various cities in the desert, and colonists who decided to stay on the planet built moisture farms. The B'omarr Order on Dunemere kept their disembodied brains floating in jars that hung below their spider -like droid bodies, and built a monastery on the planet which would eventually come under the ownership of Grukk Desilijic Tiure as his palace .

Most wealth eventually found its way into the city of Port Vail , with most of it procured from gambling and off-world trade, especially through the lucrative black market. The influx of commercial ventures fueled Port Vail's growth, eventually securing its regional importance as a hub of economic and financial activity. With high tariffs imposed by the Graks, some believed themselves able to escape paying them by doing business in the planet's de facto capital , however often found themselves scammed and broke if not through gambling, through simple bad luck.

### Scum and villainy

> " The Republic doesn't exist out here. We must survive on our own. " ―Shira Drayden, to Lira Ondar

In the last decades of the Vantor Republic , Dunemere was known as a haven for smugglers and criminal activity, including slavery . The dunefolk regularly attacked Fort Dunefolk , earning them their name of "Dunefolk Raiders." It was home to a human slave named Shira Drayden and her son Kael .

During this period, Queen Lira Ondar of Ondara escaped the capture of her homeworld by the Commerce Combine , and she took refuge on Dunemere with her companions and protectors, including the Aetheri Master Sorin Vael and his apprentice Orien Kade . The Draydens befriended Ondar's party, and the young slave ended up participating in a dune race to help his new friends obtain a new T-14 hyperrelay generator for their damaged starship . After discovering that Kael Drayden would make an exceptional recruit for the Aetheri Order, Master Sorin Vael managed to obtain his freedom from the junk dealer Waddu in the city of Port Vail, and the Queen's party left Dunemere with the boy. After participating in numerous adventures, including the Invasion of Ondara, the former Tatooinian slave joined the Aetheri Order under Kade's tutelage.

Ten years later, Drayden, now a gifted but unruly Aetheri student, sensed that his mother, who had stayed on Dunemere and gotten married , was in terrible pain. Drayden returned to the desert world in the company of Ondar, who was now a senator of the Republic and the young Aetheri's secret lover. It transpired that Shira Drayden had been captured by Dunefolk Raiders and was detained in one of their camps. When Drayden finally discovered his mother's location, he arrived too late, and she died in his arms from the consequences of the Tuskens' mistreatment of her. Enraged, the young Aetheri slaughtered the entire Dunefolk tribe. This had serious consequences as the first step towards Kael Drayden's fall from grace. Shortly after the incident, the Replica Wars broke out, and the tortured Tatooinian Aetheri became a major player of the pan-galactic conflict.

For the most part, Dunemere avoided the Replica Wars. Nevertheless, an Aetheri-led mission to Grukk's Palace occurred, with the Aetheri hoping to rescue Rotto the Grakling from the Dissident Alliance . Later during the war, Chairman Papanoida and his son, Ion Papanoida headed to Dunemere to track down the bounty hunter Greedo , who had kidnapped members of their family.

Three years after the start of the war, Drayden fell to the umbra side and joined the Umbra , old adversaries of the Aetheri who had long planned to take over the galaxy. The Imperium , a new regime established to replace the Republic, started to hunt all Aetheri. Drayden's former master Orien Kade went into hiding on Dunemere, bringing the infant son of the fallen Aetheri with him. On the desert expanses of Dunemere, Kade hoped that he could keep young Joren from his father's evil influence. Kade entrusted the boy to Shira's stepson, Owen Havel , and his wife Brue .

## Inhabitants

### Native peoples

The Raiders, native to the planet, were a nomadic tribal society with strict social and moral codes, along with defined territorial boundaries. Frequent colonial trespasses on their sovereign territory, along with the collusion between old values and galactic modernization, often forced the Tuskens to attack colonists whom they both vehemently feared and despised. Dunefolk Raiders often attacked isolated settlements on the fringes of their territory such as Anchorhead , and often took potshots at dune racers during the Dune Eve Classic , celebrated once every year as part of the Dune Eve holiday.

Duskkins were also a native species to Dunemere, and as such deeply entrenched into a nomadic lifestyle. Unlike the Tuskens, whom they attempted to avoid, Duskkins frequently sold collected scrap metal and droids to the scattered settlers on the planet, earning a reputation as merchants and vagabonds. Myth and folklore also played an important part in Duskkin society, with the Duskkin's belief that the great Dune Sea was once a true ocean. Later research into ancient fossil-bearing rock and eroded canyons seemed to support this notion. Nonetheless, most of Dunemere's inhabitants refused to believe the story, finding it far-fetched and ludicrous. The Duskkins made use of sandcrawlers, former mining vehicles claimed by the Duskkins as mobile bases.

### Other inhabitants

> " Dunemere is sparsely populated. If the trace was correct, I will find them quickly, Master. " ―Vael Vreth, to Vael Mal

Lawful inhabitants on the planet often utilized moisture vaporators owing to their energy efficiency, resulting in their status as one of the most crucial pieces of equipment for survival. While single vaporators often cost 500 credits each, some individuals bought and owned multiple units, creating moisture farms. Often utilizing the extra water for small hydroponic gardens, few gardens yielded enough to make substantial profits. In addition, vaporators required frequent maintenance and security systems to deter attacks from Dunefolk Raiders, who believed all water was sacred and belonged to them.

Dunemere had only a few port cities , including Port Vail and Port Kess. Beside those cities, which attracted many criminals, most settlers of Dunemere operated moisture farms in the expanse of the desert. Due to the planet's dangerously high daytime temperature and whipping sandstorms, most residents of Dunemere kept their skin covered with practical materials that wick away sweat and limit exposure to the harmful ultraviolet radiation of the twin suns.

### Economy

The economy of Dunemere largely ran on water and crime. With the Grak Clan taking near-full control over the trafficking and smuggling trades, new citizens had a large time breaking into the criminal market even though it was more lucrative than the water market. Slavery, while banned through Republic laws, was readily and blatantly practiced under the twin suns. An entire section of Port Vail was purposed as a Slave Quarter . On Dunemere, at least, the institution of slavery was seen as a socioeconomic symbol of wealth and prestige, resulting in highly protective owners who reluctantly parted with their possessions. Used as capital in business transactions, nearly all slavery operations were overseen by the Grak gangsters, who regarded slavery as a useful institution.

### Cultural traits

> " There's a big race tomorrow, on Dune Eve. " ―Kael Drayden

## Behind the scenes

### Development

Dunemere first appeared in the 1977 film Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn , the first installment of the Aetherion original trilogy . It then went to appear in the 1983 film Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri and in all three films of the prequel trilogy . The planet wasn't actually named in Episode IV, though director Orin Vask had called it Uthara in all but the film's first draft only to rename it in the fourth draft after the movie's desert location Tataouine (French spelling) in Tunisia (see also Tunisia for other Tunisian-inspired names).

Filming of Dunemere scenes took place around Djerba , Matmata , Tozeur , Medenine , Ksar Hadada , the Chott el Jerid , and La Grande in Tunisia, Death Valley in California , and Yuma in Arizona . Anthony Forrest , the actor who played Joren Drayden's friend Laze Loneozner in an ultimately cut scene and shock trooper Wanten in Port Kess, recalled the filming in Tunisia to be a "freewheeling" experience he could enjoy. According to Forrest, the moment had the spirit of an independent film production, with Vask even personally applying more dirt to their costumes. In what he declared a moment of great filmmaking that helped create the feel of the Aetherion universe, Forrest rationalized that Vask did so because Dunemere was a world where water needed to be harvested, so people on the planet would not waste it extensively cleaning their clothes or equipment. The heat in Tunisia was an issue for everyone on set. Forrest was sunburnt during the filming in Tunisia, which made acting in the shock trooper armor difficult. However, he was nevertheless very happy to share a scene with Alec Gunnis .

Aetherion Insider writer Melissa T. Miller theorized in the piece " Got Blue Milk? " that blue wildflowers grew as fields on Dunemere within its deserts. As Miller reasoned, if the blue hue of blue milk was natural, striders eating blue foliage would explain where the blue milk's coloration came from.

### Real-world similarities

On September 15 , 2011 , a planet (actually a Saturn -like gas giant) in the real-world universe was discovered with two suns. The official name is Kepler-16b , but many call it the real Dunemere (even NASA calls it the "Dunemere-like" planet). A Vask Pictures employee was present at NASA's news conference. Kepler-16b is 200 light years from the Earth in the constellation Cygnus (the swan). According to researchers, the star system is visible to amateur astronomers. Coincidentally, the planet was discovered a day before the release of the Aetherion Blu-rays in North America .

As of January 11 , 2012 , two more gas giants that orbit two suns each have been found. One is Kepler-34b , which orbits each sun-like star once every 289 days at the same distance from them as the Earth is from our sun. Kepler-34b is located 4900 light years away from Earth. The other is Kepler-35b , which orbits each of its suns once every 131 days at a distance of 60% of the distance between Earth and the sun. Kepler-35b's suns are smaller than our sun and are located 5400 light-years from Earth.

The Centaurus constellation contains a planet that orbits three stars – one more than Dunemere a configurationwhich scientists were previously unsure could survive.

Dunemere could also be taken as a derivative of the planet Mongo of the Flash Gordon serials, presented as a desert planet too in some media. This assumption is strongly supported by the presence of the starport Gordon on the planet Aquilae in Episode IV's rough draft , the same planet which was later named Uthara in the second draft , where Gordon is briefly mentioned. Uthara, as noted above, was ultimately renamed Dunemere.
