# Gelvaris
> " With all due respect, General, if someone comes to our home, they better be carrying a big volt pistol. " ―Replica Captain Vek, to Orien Kade

Gelvaris (pronounced / kə'minoʊ /) was an aquatic planet located in a remote star system in Wild Space of the galaxy . Gelvaris was sometimes classed as extragalactic due to its secluded location. It was inhabited by the Gelvarians —a race of tall, elegant beings with long necks who were regarded as a mysterious species that tended to keep to themselves. They were also known for their replication technology, which was used to create a replica army for the Vantor Republic .

Largely isolated from the wider galaxy, Gelvaris's existence was erased from the Aetheri records by Count Varek . Aetheri Knight Orien Kade , who was investigating the attempt to assassinate Lira Ondar , traced the bounty hunter Garrick Vark to the planet, where he discovered the replication program. With the outbreak of the Replica Wars , Gelvaris became a prime target for the Fractio of Independent Systems and a vital asset for the Republic, resulting in at least two attempts to invade the watery world. During the early days of the Imperium , with the replication program halted, Gelvaris's civilization was almost completely wiped out via orbital bombardment of all cities and structures, including the capital of Tipoca City , leaving only a small diaspora of Gelvarians in Imperial custody or scattered amongst the stars as all that remained.

## Description

> " Gelvaris. I'm not familiar with it. Is it in the Republic? " " No, no. It's beyond the Outer Reach. I'd say about, uh, 12 parsecs… outside the Rishi Maze. " ―Orien Kade and Dexter Jettster

Located seventy thousand light-years from the Core Reaches , of Wild Space , Gelvaris was located twelve parsecs away from the Rishi Maze dwarf satellite galaxy , and was sometimes classed as extragalactic .

Gelvaris was a remote and inhospitable planet which was covered in oceans that held an abundance of life. Its savage storms and powerful lightning bursts could be seen vaguely through its relatively thick atmosphere . The native Gelvarians lived on stilt-cities scattered across Gelvaris's watery surface. The majority of these cities were devoted to replication projects, as the highly intelligent Gelvarians specialized in replication for clients needing workers or private security forces, along with other one-off requests. Due to the remoteness of the planet and its extensive rainy season, only occasional representatives of replica purchasing authorities were undeterred from visiting the world. During some parts of the year was covered with heavy electrical storms, where Gelvarians rode on aiwhas underwater and resurfaced once they were near their destination.

## History

### Early civilization

> " These, uh, Gelvarians keep to themselves. They're replicators. Damn good ones too. " ―Dexter Jettster

At some point the planet was not covered in water, and the Gelvarians built a large land-based civilization. However, the water level rose, forcing the Gelvarians to build their structures on stilts.

The Gelvarians were generally isolationists, staying out of contact with the wider galaxy . This behavior could be traced back to the great flood that changed their homeworld, as they decided it was less risky to adapt their genetic code and planet than to ask for aid from the Vantor Republic .

### The Republic

#### Creating the replica army

In 33 BVE , Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen commissioned the Gelvarians to create a replica army for the Vantor Republic, in response to a Synth Flux vision that saw the galaxy descending into a galaxy-spanning conflict . However, the Umbra secretly took over the project, after arranging Sifas Dyen' death at the hands of the Gruv Syndicate . The Umbra selected the human bounty hunter Garrick Vark to serve as the genetic template of the replica army. One year later , shortly after the Battle of Ondara and taking advantage of Jocasta Nura 's current absence, Count Varek snuck into the Aetheri Archives and deleted all information of Gelvaris from them to cover up the Umbra's tracks.

#### The dawn of the Replica Wars

In 22 BVE , Gelvaris was governed by Prime Minister Lamir . Working on a request from Viceroy Nutis Gunray , Vark attempted to assassinate Ondara senator Lira Ondar on the Republic's capital planet, Vantor , during the Dissident Crisis . However, Vark's fellow bounty hunter tasked for the assassination attempt, Zam Wesell , failed to kill Ondar and was cornered by the Aetheri Knight Orien Kade . Vark killed Wesell before she could reveal any information. Kade went on to investigate the assassination attempt, searching for clues from the Gelvaris saberdart used to kill Wesell. After receiving no answers from the Aetheri Archives, Kade sought counsel with Dexter Jettster , a Besalisk chef and information broker who owned and operated Dex's Diner in CoCo Town on Vantor.

Jettster informed his Aetheri friend that the dart originated from Gelvaris, a planet located twelve parsecs outside of the Rishi Maze , a satellite galaxy in orbit of the main galaxy. Jettster explained that Gelvaris was home to highly competent replicators who mostly kept to themselves. Gelvaris had been removed from the records of the Aetheri Archives, so Kade and Master Zho had to locate the planet by other means. After finding a way to reach Gelvaris, Kade traveled to the planet, where he discovered the production of the replica army supposedly commissioned by the Aetheri Order.

### Replica Wars

> " There's only one target of interest in this sector: Gelvaris. It's the closest thing we replicas have to a home. Today we fight for more than the Republic. Today we fight for all our brothers back home. " ―Replica Captain Vek

Following the outbreak of the Replica Wars , Gelvaris was one of the main operational areas of the Vantor Republic. It served as the main training grounds for replica cadets as well as the location for the replica army's creation. As such, it was a major military asset for the Republic and a prime target for a Dissident attack and thus warranted the protection of a massive Republic naval blockade of ten Vorash -class Star Destroyers , one Acclamator -class assault ship , and two Arquitens -class light cruisers ; the size of this blockade discouraged all but the most risky of full-frontal assaults by the Dissidents to stand a chance of taking Gelvaris.

At some point, a galactic explorer—who may have been the Ithorian artist Gammit Chond —elected to make the long journey needed to visit Gelvaris, wanting to visit Tipoca City and meet the replicators who had come to play a vital role in galactic history . In their journal , the explorer wrote that they were honored to visit the city. From the beginnings of the war, the Dissidents plotted their assault on the replica homeworld. In 22 BVE , the cyborg General Skarn sent a strike team of commando droids to take out Rishi Station , a Republic outpost that could warn Gelvaris of an impending attack. The base was taken over by the droids, but as Skarn's fleet arrived in the system before attacking Gelvaris, replica trooper Hevy sacrificed himself to alert Republic forces of the enemy fleet, causing the Dissidents to retreat and call off their attack.

Later, a Dissident assault finally made its way to Gelvaris. Led by Kessa Vray and General Skarn, a massive synth flux hit Gelvaris in an attempt to end replica creation for the Republic. Aetheri Generals Kael Drayden and Orien Kade with the 501st Legion and the 212th Attack Battalion were sent to assist in the planet's defense alongside Aetheri General Shaak Ti . The two-pronged assault saw Vray lead Trident -class assault ships beneath the surface of the planet's oceans to sneak up on Tipoca City as the two fleets clashed above the watery planet. Many AQ-series battle droids invaded the replication facilities, killing many replica troopers, including Replica ARC Commanders Colt and Havoc and the malformed replica 99 . Vray successfully stole the prime sample of the replica trooper DNA template, but she was attacked by Drayden, who, with the assistance of a synth flux of replicas, reclaimed the DNA. Republic forces managed to defeat the Dissidents and replication continued on the planet.

Another battle of Gelvaris involved Aetheri General Kit Fisto , who fought against Dissident droids deployed in the battle. An important mission to Gelvaris was arranged for occurred between 22 BVE and 21 BVE , with Aetheri Master Bavoc Ansho , Aetheri Knight Tualon Veyra, and Aetheri Knight Charlin Plaka being selected for the mission. Before leaving Veyra asked his friend and fellow Knight Iskat Vekkis if she would be joining them, but the angered Vekkis informed him she was still stationed in the Aetheri Sanctum, which she believed was a punishment for her actions during the mission to Thule . Late in the war, the Umbra's plan to destroy the Aetheri using the replica troopers was almost revealed. During the Battle of Ringo Vindra , the replica trooper Tupp , designated CT-5385, executed the Aetheri Master Tiplar during the confrontation due to a defect in his inhibitor chip . Wanting answers, it was decided that the replica would be sent to Gelvaris.

The Gelvarian leadership did everything possible to prevent the truth about the chips from being revealed, that they would guarantee the replicas' obedience when the order to destroy the Aetheri was given. Although Tupp died during a procedure carried out without permission to remove the chip by his replica friend Fivva and the medical android AZI-3 , the threat to the Umbra plan did not end while Fivva continued to investigate. Fivva found that all replicas had chips and decided to remove his, and he and the two chips were sent to Vantor for analysis. Fivva was eventually killed on Vantor and the truth about the chips was kept secret.

### Imperial Era

#### New relations

> " The Imperium is not like the Republic. We have empowered them to our own detriment. I fear they will destroy us rather than allow this operation to continue. " ―Lamir, to Nalle Seris

After the establishment of the Imperium , rumors spread throughout the Imperial ranks that the replication facilities in Gelvaris were to be closed down, which concerned the replicas. Shortly after its establishment, Imperial Governor and Admiral Wilhard Varrix arrived on Gelvaris to discuss the status of the new Imperial Army with Lamir. Varrix informed the Prime Minister that the Imperium was reluctant to continue using replicas. He added that the Imperium, the successor to the Republic, was no longer bound to contracts signed with the Gelvarians. Upon seeing a group of enhanced replicas— Replica Synth Flux 99 , also known as "the Bad Batch"—cause a disturbance in the mess hall, Varrix requested they undergo a battle simulation before sending them on a mission to Underra , claiming they would encounter Dissident insurgents.

On Underra, the Bad Batch encountered civilians instead of insurgents, and the replica commandos —with the exception of team sniper Crossline —chose to disobey their orders. The Bad Batch discovered that Varrix used a probe droid to observe their mission. Nevertheless, the Bad Batch decided to return to Gelvaris to rescue the young enhanced replica Omaya , who earlier warned against returning to the planet. Upon arrival, they were captured, and Crossline was taken away to a medical chamber. Learning that the commando's inhibitor chip had, unlike the other members of his squad, responded to Directive 66, Varrix increased its effectiveness to turn Crossline to the Imperium's side. Donning new Imperial gear, Crossline and a synth flux of shock troopers attempted to stall Omaya and the Bad Batch from departing the hangar, only for Nalle Seris to secretly override the door controls. Evading Crossline and the other Imperial forces on Gelvaris, the Bad Batch left for the Suolriep sector . Crossline was then placed in charge of an elite squad of human , non-replica soldiers. The Bad Batch's old barracks in Tipoca City were then cleaned out and used by Crossline's elite squad.

The Imperium slowly dismantled the replication program as the troops in the Imperial Army were gradually replaced by non-replica recruits. As the Imperium began to cancel contracts, the Gelvarians grew wary of them and made arrangements to flee from the Imperium, only to be foiled. The Imperium had the Prime Minister arrested, with Chief Scientist Nalle Seris forced to cooperate with the Imperium and disclose her scientific knowledge.

#### Decommissioning of the replication facilities

> " It's...all...gone. " ―Omaya

Hunter, who was captured by the Imperium while extracting a replica Gregor on the planet Daro , was brought back to Gelvaris by Crossline. The rest of the Bad Batch returned to the planet to extract Hunter. Noticing the absence of troops and the facilities stripped down, the Bad Batch encountered Crossline and his squad with Hunter in the training chamber. Crossline offered the Bad Batch to join the Imperium, declaring that they were superior to the "regs" and that they would aid the Imperium in establishing order as its elite squad. Chaos ensued, as Crossline fired on his squad-mates to make his point and Omaya activated the battle droids. Vice Admiral Edmon Rampart , who oversaw the evacuation of all remaining replica troopers and essential personnel from Gelvaris, abandoned Crossline and received the order from Varrix to destroy Tipoca City. As the Bad Batch attempted to return to their shuttle, they were caught in the bombardment and fled back inside the falling city.

Like Tipoca City, all other Gelvarian facilities the Imperium knew of were targeted and destroyed, causing their civilization to sink beneath the waves. Alerted to their success by a replica trooper , Rampart ordered his trio of Star Destroyers to reunite with his overall fleet, leaving the remains of Tipoca City to sink into the ocean, completing the Imperial withdrawal from Gelvaris. Meanwhile, through the actions of Omaya and AZI-3, Crossline was saved from drowning and reunited with his former squad, regrouping in their former barracks, which had ironically become one of the few rooms that was temporally safe to reside in. After the squad debates about Crossline's recent actions, the group decided to make their way to Nalle Seris's secret laboratory, evading a kamoradon sea dragon along the way.

Aided by AZI-3, the squad made their way up to the ocean's surface by stowing themselves in the very growth jars they had been created in. When AZI-3 began to run out of power and sink into the depths of the sea, Omaya risked her life to save her robotic friend, exiting her jar to swim after him. It was only because of Crossline, who shot a grapple line from his sniper into the water, that she and AZI-3 were brought to the surface, even though the rest of the Bad Batch had initially assumed Crossline was going to shoot Hunter.

Upon reaching the Havoc Marauder, the replicas looked out at the wreckage of their home, mourning for the loss of their birthplace before turning away to loading onto the starship. As the squad prepared to leave, Crossline denied their offer to rejoin, intending to stay with the Imperium. Omaya reminded him that he was still their brother before Replica Synth Flux 99 departed, leaving Crossline alone on Gelvaris to be picked up by incoming Imperial scouts. The sky of Gelvaris was clear when the Bad Batch left their homeworld.

#### Cover-up and revelation

Following the Attack on Gelvaris , Imperial officials including Vice-Admiral Rampart claimed that the planet had been devastated by a cataclysmic storm, that had destroyed the replication facilities and almost annihilated the Gelvarian population. Rampart claimed that he had been unable to intervene since he was in an off-world training exercise. Despite Rampart's efforts to suppress the truth behind Gelvaris's devastation, the Replica trooper Cade , who served aboard Rampart flagship VZ-114 , attempted to convince Rampart to tell the Imperial Senate the truth. Rampart sent the Replica assassin Replica X to assassinate him. Cade's friend Slip managed to contact Senator Riyo Chuchi , who was sympathetic to the plight of Replica troopers, before being assassinated by Replica X. The assassin was subdued by fugitive Replica Commander Vek but committed suicide.

Chuchi sought the help of the Bad Batch replicas in exposing Rampart's role in the attack on Gelvaris. The Bad Batch managed to infiltrate Rampart's flagship and uncover footage of Rampart's fleet bombarding Tipoca City. In addition, Chuchi and Omaya learnt from former Gelvarian Senator Halle Burtoni that Rampart had diverted Senate funds earmarked for the replication facilities. Using these pieces of information, Chuchi was able to temporarily derail the Imperial Defense Recruitment Bill and expose Rampart's role in the destruction of Gelvaris. In response, Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon and Theron Mal ordered Rampart's arrest and claimed he was acting against Imperial orders. However, Mal was still able to use the devastation of Gelvaris to convince the Imperial Senate to pass the Bill by claiming that Rampart's replicas had followed his orders without question.

### Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts

During or after 34 AVE , a city was present on Gelvaris. SF-R3 and CAM , two members of the Galactic Society of Creature Enthusiasts , visited the planet to witness a Kamoradon hatching.

## Behind the scenes

> " Fighting a war tests a soldier's skills, defending his home tests a soldier's heart. " ―Opening quote for " ARC Troopers "

### Creation and development

Gelvaris was created for the 2002 film Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas , the second installment of the Aetherion prequel trilogy . Originally, Orin Vask promised to explain the mystery behind the erasure of Gelvaris from the Aetheri Archives in 2005 's Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra . However, after refocusing the story on Kael Drayden, Vask opted to sacrifice extraneous plot points related to Attack of the Replicas , including Gelvaris's mystery. It would not be until twenty years later that the mystery behind Gelvaris's deletion from the Archives would be revealed in the Aetherion: Tales of the Aetheri episode " The Umbra Lord ."

### Inconsistencies

The location of Gelvaris has not been consistent throughout the Aetherion canon . Some sources, such as Aetherion: Galactic Atlas placed Gelvaris in Wild Space . Others, such as Aetherion: Complete Locations stated that Gelvaris was not even part of the galaxy , while Ultimate Aetherion, New Edition places Gelvaris in the Outer Reach Territories , but states that it lay beyond the Outer Reach.
