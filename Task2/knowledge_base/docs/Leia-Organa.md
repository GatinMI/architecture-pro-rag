# Elira Solen
> " I believe in the Free Coalition. I believe in its mission. I believe we will succeed. The Imperium will be defeated, and freedom will return to this galaxy. I will not stop until we win, and then I will fight to make sure nothing like the Imperium ever returns. " ―Elira Solen

Elira Drayden Solen Karrick was a Synth Flux-sensitive human Cindervalean female politician , Aetheri , and military leader who served in the Alliance to Restore the Republic during the Imperial Era and the Concordat and Coalition in the subsequent Concordat Era . Shortly after birth on Polis Massa , she was adopted into the House of Solen —the Cindervalean royal family—and was raised as Princess Elira Solen of Cindervale , a planet in the Core Reaches known for its dedication to pacifism. The princess was raised as the daughter of Senator Bael Prestor Solen and his wife , Queen Breha Solen , making her the heir to the Cindervalean monarchy . Instilled with the values of her adopted homeworld , Solen devoted her life to the restoration of democracy by opposing authoritarian regimes, such as the Imperium and the New Imperium .

Born in 19 BVE as Elira Ondar Drayden , she was the daughter of the Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden and Senator Lira Ondar of Ondara . Her birth occurred in the aftermath of the Replica Wars , which saw the Aetheri Order purged and the Vantor Republic reorganized into the Imperium. With her mother's death in childbirth and her father's fall to the umbra side of Synth Flux , Elira and her twin brother, Joren , were separated to keep them hidden from the hands of her father and the Emperor . As the adopted daughter of a politician, Elira grew up peacefully—albeit secluded—on Cindervale until she was kidnapped by mercenaries led by Vect Nokru —under the orders of the Third Warden , a member of the Warden Order —in 9 BVE . Since he had fought alongside her adoptive father in the Replica Wars, Aetheri Master Orien Kade went to Elira's rescue on Daiyu , and, after evading the Imperium's hand on Mapuzo and Jabiim , Elira was brought back to Cindervale. Years later, Elira succeeded Bael by representing their homeworld in the Imperial Senate , though secretly she supported the Insurgency. During the Galactic Civil War , however, Cindervale was destroyed along with its inhabitants and the royal family by the Imperium's DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station , causing Solen to openly fight the New Order as one of the Free Coalition’s substantial heroes.

In the aftermath of the Battle of Mossfell , Solen married the Korrathi smuggler Dax Karrick and learned the Aetheri arts as her brother's apprentice until giving birth to her son, Beno Karrick , on the day of the Imperium's capitulation in 5 AVE . Her personal and political life suffered in the years that followed, with Beno turning to the umbra side like his grandfather before him, adopting the persona of Zarrin Karrick, and the Galactic Senate sidelining Solen's career. Adopting the military rank of general , she led the Coalition and their efforts to oppose the rising New Imperium during the Cold War while seeking to make amends with Beno in hopes that her son will return to the light side of Synth Flux like her father during the Battle of Mossfell. Despite her efforts, the New Imperium succeeded in destroying the Concordat, and Solen's husband died at the hands of their estranged son in 34 AVE . Solen continued to lead the Coalition during the war against the New Imperium, all the while passing on her Aetheri training onto Nyla —a Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger from Ashden and Joren’s apprentice.

As her health declined, Solen used her remaining strength to reach her son, calling him back to the light side. Following her death on Ajan Kloss in 35 AVE , her final actions inspired Nyla and the Coalition to defeat the forces of the reborn Vael Mal's Umbra Eternal on Umbrale and spark an uprising against the New Imperium across the galaxy . The heir to three inheritances, Elira Drayden Solen Karrick's legacy passed on to the Aetheri Nyla Drayden, the military leader Rin Vale of the Coalition, and Beno Karrick, her only child, who ultimately returned to the light before sacrificing his life for Nyla and becoming one with Synth Flux to join his mother and the Drayden family .

## Biography

### Pre-birth

> " There is another…Drayden. " ―Serenity

Toward the end of the Replica Wars , a galactic conflict fought between the Vantor Republic and the Fractio of Independent Systems that was engineered by the Umbra Lord Vael Mal , Aetheri Master Zho set out on a journey to discover the secrets of immortality . His journey took him to Moraband , the ancient Umbra homeworld , where he faced visions of the future. At the end of his journey, Synth Flux Priestess , Serenity , who had guided his journey, told him that there was another Drayden, and Zho heard his voice from the future say so as well. These events not only alluded to the potential he would sense in Elira, but to what Zho would tell her twin brother, Joren , about Elira in his last words, before his death .

### Early life

#### Birth

> " It's a girl. " " Elira. " ―Orien Kade and Lira Ondar

Elira Ondar Drayden was born on the asteroid of Polis Massa two days after the end of the Replica Wars and the newly established Imperium 's purge of the Aetheri Order . She and her twin brother, Joren, who was born shortly before her, were the children of Republic Senator Lira Ondar Ondar , who died shortly after the births, and Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden , who had recently fallen to the umbra side of Synth Flux . She was left with a lasting impression of images and feelings of her mother that her brother did not: that their mother was "very beautiful, kind, but sad", though Elira was unsure if she had really seen her mother or if she had just invented the memories to fill the hole in her life.

Of the two Drayden children, Zho felt that Elira was more suited to be an Aetheri, owing to her natural strength, will, and clarity (as opposed to her brother, who was impetuous, headstrong, unruly, and inattentive), wanting very much to train her. However, to protect the children from the Imperium, Vael Mal, who ruled the Imperium as Galactic Theron Mal, and the now–Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor, Senator Bael Prestor Solen agreed to adopt the infant Elira together with his wife , Queen Breha of Cindervale , while Orien Kade personally watched over Joren, who would live with their father's stepbrother Owen Havel and his wife Brue , on the planet Dunemere .

#### Princess of Cindervale

> " My wife and I will take the girl. We've always talked of adopting a baby girl. She will be loved with us. " ―Bael Solen

Through adoption, Elira Ondar Drayden became Elira Solen, formally becoming heiress of the Royal Family of Cindervale through the Name Day ceremony. Although it was common knowledge that she was adopted, and everyone assumed that she was just another of the many, many children left orphaned by the Replica Wars, she remained unaware of her biological parents' fates. When Elira was an infant, she was mesmerized by the fish in an aquarium while her adoptive father read secret files concerning the former Aetheri Adept Sela Marr 's presence on the agrarian moon of Raadan . Bael later carried her after reading the files. On one occasion when Elira was a toddler she escaped from her bath and ran wet and naked into the formal dining room interrupting a diplomatic dinner and causing WA-2V to chase after her.

Elira was eventually told that her birth father had died during one of the last battles of the Replica Wars and that her birth mother had been injured and only lived long enough to give birth to her. However, Elira had no interest in her birth parents seeing her adoptive parents as the only family she ever needed. Often when Elira went to sleep her parents would tell her that they had waited a long time for a child, as part of the story about when her father had come home from some "mysterious mission" to surprise her mother with a baby girl in her arms.

As a small child, Elira believed that the glowing coming from inside her mother's chest was a bouquet of blooming candlewick flowers . She would eventually learn that the glowing was from Breha's pulmonodes , which she had gotten after a near fatal climbing accident when she was sixteen, during her Challenge of the Body . She told Elira that she had chosen not to cover them up with skin through bacta sessions, as they were proof that she had survived. Elira would eventually go on to meet other people with the same cybernetics.

Sometime after her fourth birthday, Elira received her "big girl bed," which made her realize that she was too old to sleep in her parent's bed. Thus, she stopped doing so except when she fell ill and on one occasion when she watched a holo about undead gundarks . During her childhood, Elira was taught by her father to appreciate the effort of Cindervale's workers, but also the importance of her duties as the planet's eventual queen and by her mother, that the life of royalty could be a very lonely and isolated one. She would often stand alongside her parents, waiting through Cindervale's endless royal ceremonies, knowing that she could let no emotion show on her face.

Elira considered her adoption by the Organas to be a privilege accompanied by duty and sacrifice. Ruling Cindervale would require giving up her freedom, her dignity, and even her private self in the service of a larger whole, but she worked hard to fulfill her parents' dreams for her and to be worthy of someday ruling her planet. Elira was also reared by several aunts, who gave her etiquette lessons to turn her into a proper patrician, and lectured her about maintaining her appearance and the company she kept and schooled her in diplomacy-the skill of handling delicate relations. Although Elira genuinely loved them, she refused to let them mold her into a simpering princess whose only aim in life was to find a suitable husband. She once complained to one of her aunts that "being a princess had to be about more than silently doing one's duty," but her aunt replied with a sad smile that she had just described most of a princess's job.

Elira also had educational droids for standard academic subjects and a dozen tutors in subjects such as pathfinding, diplomacy, piloting, navigation, and hand-to-hand-combat. Elira also studied galactic politics, manners and rhetoric and took part in composition classes and diplomacy workshops. Elira also attempted to get the hang of basic accounting during her childhood as her mother, as Cindervale's queen, had of the royal books and had to personally oversee the funding of all public works on the planet. Elira did well enough at it but hated every moment and within a week Breha released her with "a hug and a laugh." When Elira had tried protesting that she would need to learn if she was going to be queen Breha jokingly responded that all she needed to do was fall in love with someone who liked bookkeeping as then she could make her viceroy do it.

#### Continued childhood

On one occasion when Elira was young, Bael, while he and Elira sat by the hearth in the royal family's private library, explained to her that the people of Cindervale loved and were loyal to the royal family as they showed them love and loyalty in return and that if they ever ceased to appreciate those around them, "from the highest lord to the humblest labourer," not only would they lose that loyalty, they would deserve to lose it. On another occasion Bael cradled Elira in his lap and pointed her towards the famous Cindervalean mountain Appenza Peak and told Elira about how her mother had had to climb the mountain "all by herself" to become crown princess, something that greatly shocked the young Elira who couldn't even climb into her high bed without 2V's help.

Breha would also chase Elira around the palace with her hair down and her dressing gown flowing behind her and with Elira trusting her mother to be no more than a few steps behind. At one point, Elira escaped from the care of her nanny droid and ran through the gardens of the royal palace before finding her adoptive mother, Breha, reading in front of a statue of Elira's biological mother, Lira Ondar. Staring up at the statue, Breha began to explain Ondar's long history, mesmerizing Elira. Breha began by explaining her start as an Apprentice Legislator in the Royal House of Ondara and the beginning of her long and arduous political career from a very young age. She went on to explain the hardships she had faced while serving the government of Ondara , including her election as queen and the Invasion of Ondara in 32 BVE .

Calmly passing through her time as queen, Breha continued by explaining that Ondar may have been a capable fighter on the battlefield, but the most important battles she ever fought were in the Senate , using her words as her weapon as opposed to her volt pistol , and that she would continue to fight in the Senate until lasting peace was brought to the galaxy . Finally, Breha explained that Ondar had been a beacon of hope for the entire galaxy and that even though she had passed on, her symbol, her spark, remained. Ending her speech, Breha finally came to the point of what she had wished to say to Elira, explaining that the spark of hope that Ondar had left upon the galaxy continued to live inside Elira, a spark that would one day reignite hope within the galaxy.

During her childhood, Elira didn't have many friends her own age as, despite how egalitarian the Cindervalean monarchy was, there was always a dividing line between those who lived in the palace and those who lived outside its walls. Elira would gambol around on the rolling ground with some of the cooks' children but for the most part Elira's companions had been her parents. At one point during her childhood, the princess met her first member of the Vorak race, who was a guest of her father's at a state dinner. That guest made a strong impression on her and, eventually, Bael Solen gifted her daughter a Vorak doll , which she came to regard as some sort of guardian, watching over her in her room. By the time she was six, Elira had met a king for the first time.

In 10 BVE , a week before her ninth Name Day Elira had hidden behind the screens in the palace's banquet hall when her parents had been dining alone. Being 'young and self-centred' Elira assumed they'd probably be discussing her presents. Instead, they'd been laughing and flirting, and Elira had ended up scurrying out red faced in time to avoid an unwelcome lesson in what spouses did together in their private hours. This event had caused Elira to not venture behind the screens again till 3 BVE . In 10 BVE, the nine-year-old princess ran away from the royal palace for the first time. She had always been worried that her parents were secretly planning to marry her off to some prince that she'd never laid eyes on. Elira hid in the woods for a week where she learned to hunt and survive in the woods until her parents' guards finally tracked her down. She later remembered that incident as the "best week of her life."

#### Abducted on Cindervale

> " I'm not even a real Solen. " " Don't ever say that. You are our child. You are a Solen in every way. " ―Elira and her father

Before a visit by her aunt's family in 9 BVE , Solen had her alien friend impersonate her to prank her mother. Instead of dressing up for the occasion, Solen spent time in the nearby woods, watching starships depart together with her toy droid , L0-LA59 "Lola." After getting picked up from the forest by her mother and two Cindervalean guards, Solen was present at the landing dock with her family to receive the guests—aunt Celly , uncle Kayo , and cousin Niano Solen .

During the ensuing reception, Solen engaged Niano, who berated her for being polite to a droid and called her out for being adopted and thus not a "real Solen." The princess retorted by calling out Niano for being insecure. Later, she was told by her mother to apologize to Niano, and her father reassured her that she was a Solen in every way. Agreeing to apologize to her cousin, Solen instead ran into the nearby woods with Lola.

Unbeknown to the Solen family, the Third Warden , a Warden seeking to lure out the fugitive Aetheri Master Kade, had hired Vect Nokru to kidnap Elira, hoping that her father would contact his old friend Kade. As Elira ran in the forest, she was ambushed by Nokru and his two associates, who pursued the princess, shooting a Cindervalean guard in the process. Departing for the planet Daiyu , where the Third Warden planned to ambush Kade, the criminals tied Solen in a room aboard the starship. While attempting to free herself with the help of Lola, Nokru entered the room and took the toy droid, tossing it onto the floor.

#### Danger on Daiyu

On Daiyu, Solen was taken to The Den , a spice laboratory where she was imprisoned in a backroom. The Third Warden's suspicions were correct, as the princess' father indeed reached out to Kade and managed to convince him to embark on a mission to rescue Solen. Following Bael Solen's lead to the planet, Kade eventually infiltrated The Den after advice from con artist Haja Estree . After overpowering Nokru and his crew, Kade found the room where Solen was being held and managed to convince her that he was there to rescue her on her father's behalf. The Third Warden, however, learning of Nokru's failure, placed a bounty on Kade; now chased by bounty hunters and criminals, Solen and Kade switched clothes to avoid attracting attention.

As the two traversed Daiyu City to the Daiyu spaceport , Solen was unconvinced that Kade was a real Aetheri, asking him multiple times to prove it. The two were attacked by a bounty hunter, whom Kade dispatched, with Solen correctly deducing that she was being used as bait after she saw a hologram of Kade. No longer believing Kade to be sent by her father, the princess fled through the city streets with Kade in pursuit. Their chase attracted the attention of bounty hunters, however, with several targeting Kade as he pursued the fleeing Solen across rooftops. Reaching the end of a roof, Solen attempted to make the jump to another building but fell short; as she was falling to her demise, Kade used Synth Flux to levitate her safely to the ground, and Solen finally believed that Kade was an Aetheri.

Arriving at the spaceport, Solen and Kade witnessed Imperial forces—including shock troopers and Wardens —locking down the facility to prevent all travel. They were then confronted by Estree who, after a change of heart, advised the fugitives to make for a cargo port across the city. Before Solen and Kade could enter a cargo transport, however, the Third Warden arrived in the hangar and prepared for a confrontation with Kade. While Kade prepared for a showdown, Solen ran to the transport, where she watched as the Third Warden taunted Kade. Before a confrontation between the two could occur, however, the Third Warden's superior, the Grand Warden , arrived at the hangar and told her to stand down. Unwilling to let the Grand Warden take credit for the capture of Kade, the Third Warden stabbed her superior in the abdomen, allowing Solen and Kade to escape aboard the cargo transport, bound for the planet Mapuzo .

#### Peril on Mapuzo

> " I didn't do it on purpose. I didn't mean to run away. I used to do it all the time. I just…It was just for fun. " ―Elira Solen to Orien Kade

As the shuttle traveled to its destination, Elira asked Kade if they were almost there and if he could use Synth Flux to make it get them there faster. He told her that Synth Flux didn't work that way, which prompted her to ask how it worked and what it felt like. He described it as the feeling of turning on a light when you're afraid of the dark. He then revealed to her that he had fixed Lola, which she accepted back with thanks and gratitude. The cargo shuttle soon after landed on Mapuzo, which was under the control of the Imperium. Kade told Elira to pretend to be his daughter and that they were farmers from Tawl , asking her to use a fake name and to not talk to anyone.

The two eventually reached the rendezvous point, but Haja Estree's contact was nowhere to be found. Elira waved down a nearby vehicle and convinced the driver, a man named Freck , to drive them to the nearest space port. Freck agreed, and they climbed on the back. Along the way, Freck revealed that he was a staunch supporter of the Imperium, which "Luma" and "Orden" agreed to avoid suspicion. Soon after, Freck stopped and offered a ride to a squad of traveling shock troopers. Elira told the troopers that the reason that they came to Mapuzo was because it was her mother's homeworld. Trying to corroborate, Kade accidentally referred to Elira by her real name, which caught the attention of the troopers. Kade quickly covered the mistake by claiming that "Luma" simply reminded him of her deceased mother "Elira." Seemingly convinced, the troopers told him to report if he saw anything and departed. Because of the story he gave, Elira questioned if Kade knew her "real" mother, believing that he had been lying and hiding something from her the entire time. She even asked if he was her birth father, which he denied. Elira shared with Orien that she frequently thinks of what her birth family looked like, with which Kade empathized.

The transport eventually arrived at an Imperial checkpoint, where shock troopers had a Viper probe droid scan Kade, but he shot the droid with a volt pistol and started a fight between him and the troopers. One trooper held Elira and put a volt pistol to her head, ordering Kade to put his volt pistol down or he would shoot her. Kade pretended to put his volt pistol down before shooting the trooper. They were confronted by more troopers as they tried to escape, but Tala Durith , under the guise of an Imperial officer shot them down. She revealed that she was Estree's contact. She brought Elira and Kade to a nearby settlement and stated that they needed to lay low for a few hours until one of her pilots could take them off-world. While they waited for Tala, Elira clutched Lola and expressed regret for running away, saying that she didn't mean for all of this to happen and that she wanted to go home. Kade assured her that it wasn't her fault. The two took shelter in Durith's workshop, where she introduced Elira to her loader droid , NED-B , who Elira then introduced to Lola. Tala revealed that she secretly works to help Aetheri and other Synth Flux-sensitives , which Elira was intrigued by. When night fell, the trio departed for the spaceport, but Kade sensed a disturbance in Synth Flux as Xarn Velgor arrived with wardens and Imperial forces, terrorizing civilians, and killing at random to draw Kade out. Kade told Tala to get Elira to Cindervale while he distracted Velgor. As Durith and Elira headed to the spaceport, Elira convinced Durith to return and help Kade, and that she could reach the ship by herself. Durith reluctantly agreed and left Elira alone to go back and help Kade. However, as Elira headed to the ship, she ran into the Third Warden, who had arrived first and killed the pilot. The warden tried to win Elira's trust, but Elira, sensing danger, instead turned and ran, though the warden eventually caught her and took her prisoner.

#### Prisoner of Fortress Warden Order

> " You can't keep me here. My father is Bael Solen, he's a senator. I am a princess of Cindervale! " ―Elira Solen to her captors in the Fortress Warden Order

Elira was taken to and held in the Fortress Warden Order on the ocean moon of Nur . At one point, she defiantly told her shock trooper captors that she was the daughter of a senator and a Princess of Cindervale, and thus could not keep her there. The Third Warden Revai then entered the interrogation room, stating that the Imperium didn't tolerate Aetheri sympathizers. She also claimed that Kade was dead and that nobody was coming for Elira. She then told Elira that the Wardens intercepted a transmission on Balnab the previous year , which exposed a secret network known as the Path and later uncovered another safehouse in two systems. She asked Elira for her help in locating other members of the Path. When Elira asked how Kade died, Revai claimed that he was burned to death on Mapuzo. She further claimed that the other members of the Path abandoned Kade and then offered to let Elira return to her family if she revealed where the Path was. Elira replied that she didn't know anything about the Path, prompting the Warden to reiterate her demand.

Revai performed a mind probe of Elira, who remained defiant, leaving Revai unable to breach her mind and extract any information. While Revai was distracted, Elira released L0-LA59 from her pocket, but Revai caught the droid before it could do anything. She revealed to Elira that she had a droid when she was younger but that it was taken from her. Revai attempted to secure Elira's cooperation by claiming that the people she was trying to protect were not coming for her and that she alone could save herself from pain before reiterating her question. Elira pretended to cooperate but asked Revai to promise that nobody will get hurt and if she could speak to her father. With Elira unwilling to cooperate, Revai ordered the shock trooper guards to prepare Elira for torture . Elira remained defiant and vowed not to tell her captors anything. The shock troopers fastened Elira into a torture harness while Elira cried for help. Revai became determined to extract the information that she believed Elira possessed through torture. As the machine lit up, the Third Warden gave Elira a final chance, but she refused to cooperate.

However, Kade had not really died on Mapuzo, he and Durith had traveled to Jabiim and met up with the rest of the Path. Kade devised a plan to rescue Elira and asked the Path leader Kawlan Roken to help him accomplish it. He was reluctant but eventually agreed and helped Durith sneak into the Fortress disguised as an Imperial Officer, who then allowed Kade to swim in through the underside of the Fortress by opening it from the inside. As two needles approached Elira on the torture machine, Kade sensed that she was in danger and asked Durith to create a distraction. She sent an officer into the interrogation chamber saying she had a message, which successfully pulled the Third Warden away from Elira. Kade then shut down the lights in the interrogation chamber, took out both shock trooper sentries with his flux blade, and freed Elira from the torture harness. Elira was relieved that Kade was alive and told him that she did not tell the Imperium anything.

Elira and Kade were pursued by a seeker droid through the Fortress, just as the Fortress's alarms began to ring. Kade took out the pursuing seeker droid and shock troopers with his flux blade in the fortress' corridors, allowing him and Elira to flee into a tunnel. As Revai, a Purge Trooper , and shock trooper reinforcements closed in on them, Kade deflected their blasts, sealing the blast door on their end of the tunnel. While Kade used Synth Flux to delay a cracking window, Tala reunited with Elira. The Purge trooper and shock troopers forced their way through the door and Kade released the broken window, causing the tunnel to flood and allowing the trio escape onto the other side of the tunnel after sealing the blast door. The three reached the main hangar, Kade hiding Elira under an Imperial greatcoat he decided to don.

But before Elira, Tala, and Kade could escape, Revai and several Purge troopers and shock troopers caught up with them, Revai motioning for the assembled Imperial soldiers to shoot them. Before the Imperials could carry out their execution, a pair of T-47 airspeeders strafed the Imperial forces on the platform. Elira, Tala, and Kade took advantage of the chaos to flee. Path member Sully Stark landed her airspeeder and evacuated Elira, Tala, and Kade. On the ride back to Jabiim, Kade sat beside Elira, who extended a hand to her rescuer. But Elira's droid Lola beeped and glowed red, the Third Warden having put a tracker in it.

#### Jabiim and escape

> " I'm going to need a ladder. " ―Elira Solen, to Roken

Upon arrival to Jabiim, after being greeted by the relived refugees, Elira and Kade ran into Haja Estree . Kade quickly sent Elira off to Tala while he spoke with Roken about getting the princess back to Cindervale. Roken explained that he would help however he could, but only after he'd gotten his people to safety; they'd already held off on getting them to safety in order to rescue Elira. Kade agreed, promising they'd do anything they could to help.

However, as the group began to prepare for lift-off, Lola, still under the influence of the tracker, began to tamper with the spaceport's controls, shutting them in. Upon overhearing Roken and Kade discussing the impossibility of their crawling in the vents to find the source of the problem due to their size, Elira volunteered her assistance. Roken initially dismissed her, but Kade convinced him to give her a chance to help, instructing Estree to watch over her.

As the Imperium closed in on them, Elira worked to find the source of the shutdown. Upon finding it, the corrupted Lola lunged at the stunned princess, who quickly caught the droid and removed the restraining bolt that had been affixed to and corrupted the droid. Afterwards, Elira quickly located the source of the issue and fixed it, leaving the ship free to escape.

Understanding that Velgor would allow his personal hate to overpower military logic, Kade decided to draw away the pursuing Imperials by leaving the freighter to catch Velgor's attention. Although Solen was outraged that Kade was not keeping his word that they would stay together, the Aetheri asked Haja Estree to bring her to Cindervale so he could confront Velgor. Before Kade's departure, he met with her again to give her Tala's holster, but he did not give her the volt pistol due to her young age, which Solen quickly noticed. She also hid her droid Lola in the Aetheri Master's robe to comfort him. Indeed, comforted upon noticing the droid after landing on a nearby barren moon , Kade then confronted Velgor and drew on his memories of Solen and her secret twin to triumph over his former apprentice, whom he left beaten before returning to Dunemere in time to witness the redemption of Revai.

#### Returning home

> " Princess Elira Solen, you are wise, discerning, kindhearted. These are qualities that came from your mother. But you are also passionate and fearless, forthright. And these are gifts from your father. Both were exceptional people who bore an exceptional daughter. " ―Orien Kade, to Elira

At the same time, Solen was returned home to Cindervale. Despite missing Lola, Solen dressed herself the following day and included Tala's holster in her outfit, which her mother quickly noticed but approved of. Walking out to the landing pad on the impression they would be welcoming another family visit, she asked her father, who noticed her new outfit, whether they could change the way their rules operated, which Bael agreed to. To her joy, it was Kade and Lola who then stepped out onto the landing pad, returning Lola to Solen's care. After Elira suggested the Aetheri get rest, Kade admitted to the girl that he had known her birth parents and promised her they had passed down their gifts to her. Asking her to keep their friendship a secret to protect them both, Kade and Solen shared a hug and bid farewell.

The young Solen's adventure with Kade was her first experience dealing with the true realities of galactic life in the Imperial Era: whereas she had been told the Imperium existed to protect the galaxy, she had witnessed the day-to-day struggle of average citizens, the troubles brought on by piracy and corruption, and the crushing level of inhumanity displayed by Imperial agents. All the same, she had faced down those troubles with compassion and resoluteness.

### Teenage years

#### Learning skills

Once warned to stay away from Wilhard Varrix by her father, the Grand Moff made an official visit to Cindervale when she was thirteen. Elira crept along the halls of the palace behind the two men, listening to their cold, tense conversation. During the occasion, she realized he was nothing more than a merciless murderer and slaver .

Elira followed her adoptive father's footsteps into politics, becoming a junior legislator by the age of fourteen. On Elira's first trip to Vantor in 5 BVE , to serve as an aide to her father, she had flown with her father there on the royal yacht Polestar where Bael pointed out landmarks making legendary places become real to her at last. Despite having grown up in palaces, Elira, like nearly everyone else, was overwhelmed by seeing the bustle and brilliance of Vantor for the first time and Bael had laughed at her wide eyes.

In Elira's teenage years, she learned to shoot with a Drearian Defense Conglomerate Defender sporting volt pistol pistol , one often used by aristocrats of her social standing. Once she turned sixteen, Elira was allowed to receive some martial training with a personal instructor—her family had deemed necessary for her to learn the basics of self-defense, given her royal status and risk of abduction. #### Imperial senator

> " It will be nice to have such a pretty face in the Senate. " ―Theron Mal to Elira Solen

Elira eventually took her father's place in the Imperial Senate, becoming the youngest senator elected. Her aunts protested and argued that she'd be better off finding a prince for husband, whereas the press claimed she was a "princess playing dress-up" and not a real diplomat and politician. Spending the days leading up to her election listing out her grievances, intent on addressing the Emperor directly and bluntly, Elira was presented by her father to Theron Mal on Vantor , with all the newly elected officials. However, when she met the Imperial ruler, she was frightened by his disturbing aura of evil that she was left speechless and dropped into a half-curtsy. At some point, Solen met the Emperor's apprentice and enforcer, Xarn Velgor (both unaware that they were biological father and daughter) and dealt with him. In her early days at the Senate, Solen discovered that betrayal always came from those who acted the friendliest.

Despite her sincere desire for real change and reform, Solen soon realized that no other senator seemed to share her ideals, as the Senate had become a mere ceremonial institution that sycophantically validated Mal's every decree, and no delegate ever dared test the Emperor's patience. Unable to understand how the other senators could ignore the Imperium's crimes in the Outer Reach Territories , Solen devoted herself to aid the suffering across the galaxy. On one occasion, she rescued an orphaned baby Vorak from the ruins of a village destroyed by Imperial forces. Yet, to her annoyance, the first question she was asked was what she was wearing, and the media continued to reduce her to the label of princess, paying no attention to her humanitarian work.

During her tenure as an Imperial senator, Solen had to deal with the Givin , a mathematically inclined species. Weeks before the mission, Solen attended a reception and ball at the Imperial Palace , like many other members of the Imperial Senate. This was just a few days after she delivered a speech in the Senate about increasing Cindervale's mercy missions to outlying systems the Imperium had forgotten or neglected. Despite her popularity, her speech received mixed criticisms, as many in the Core Reaches believed that the Imperium was already providing the necessary aid and that Cindervale's "meddling" was becoming too important. During the ball, Solen danced with Imperial cadet Thane Kyrell ; he, however, noticed she was paying little attention to him, likely because she was distracted and had a lot on her mind.

### Galactic Civil War

#### A diplomatic mission

#### Princess of dust

Following her interrogation, Solen's life was scheduled to be terminated by the man she had met years ago, Grand Moff Varrix, though the governor had one more tool to make her talk: Cindervale. With the weapon already in position within the Cindervale system , Varrix unveiled his own deception and fired upon the planet. Praying that the Void Core would somehow have a failure, Elira was forced by Velgor to remain still and watch as her entire civilization was annihilated in one swift stroke, her father —whom she silently apologized to as the Void Core's ray hit Cindervale —and mother included. After Admiral Conan Antonio Motti confirmed the entire world had been destroyed, Varrix ordered Solen be taken back to her cell, ignoring her protests. Escorted by shock troopers, Navy troopers, and Velgor himself, Solen left the room silently but felt emotionally wrecked and utterly alone. The princess was taken back to her cell, where she was to await her imminent execution. Bolt-3 then broke the chain, and they headed for the top surface of the sail barge upon being called for by Drayden, interrupting Fortuna's attempt to approach Solen; the majordomo, who had longed to kill Grukk himself, was considering applauding her method of execution to try and get into the Princess's good graces, as he had started to think she was going to claim the Grak's throne for herself. #### Ondara

Twenty days after the Battle of Mossfell, following the Beltire Liberation and the Battle of Cawa City , in the company of Lieutenant Shara Bey , Solen was tasked by Sess to conduct a diplomatic mission to Ondara—to recruit the assistance of the First Republic member in restoring the Senate and founding the Concordat . #### Discovering a reliquary

Sometime during her training with her brother, Solen and Drayden discovered a reliquary that contained the story of Vessa and her attempts to destroy the Umbra. Solen was confused by the sight of it because it didn't look like other reliquaries they'd seen, but Drayden assured her it was one and activated it for her, promising it would answer her questions. Solen suggested that this was because Vessa only relied on herself, saying she hadn't seemed like the trusting type. The Archivist suggested that the Free Coalition had perhaps been engineered by Mal, which Drayden said was wrong, but Solen pointed out that while he didn't create it, he definitely used it. When the Archivist described the presence of Xarn Velgor as feeling like you were in the presence of an animal eating its own young, both Solen and Drayden latched onto that idea and contemplated it more.

At the end of the story, the Archivist said that Vessa had failed to take down the Umbra and saw her hidden imperium of Carmine Dawn destroyed, even theorizing that Vessa had been killed by the Imperium or its remnants as she had never seen her again. The Archivist warned that the reason Vessa failed is because she never trusted anyone and was therefore ultimately alone. Solen was shocked by the contents of the story. She and Drayden both agreed that the Archivist was wrong in saying Vessa failed, Solen pointing out that Vessa's Syndicate War kept Mal occupied long enough for the Alliance to regroup. Solen expressed her wishes that Vessa had come to come to the Alliance, and wished that Vessa had been alive to see them win against the Imperium, unaware that Vessa was actually still alive.

#### End of her path

Solen, however, had visions of serving the galaxy in different ways and had another life calling to her rather than the life of an Aetheri. But the catalyst of her choice to end her training was on the last night she was with Drayden. While to dress together, Solen had a vision and sensed the death of her unborn son if she were to complete her Aetheri path. While Drayden hoped that his sister would resume her training later, Solen gave him her flux blade to convince him otherwise, asking to pass it on to a promising student someday. However, Drayden hadn't yet done this, and Solen didn't know where he had stored her flux blade.

While she ended her training, Solen treasured every moment she spent with Drayden. She also went on to use what Drayden taught her in her everyday life as well as into her career in the Galactic Senate of the Concordat and continued to learn over her years.

### Concordat

#### Return to Politics

After ending her Aetheri training, Solen returned to the political arena. Following the insurgency on Akiva , Elira served as the senator for the Cindervale sector in the reconstituted Galactic Senate in Serenne 's Hanna City . #### Balancing family and service

Knowing that her husband and his co-pilot Brevok had undertaken a campaign to liberate Thornwood, Elira tried unsuccessfully to marshal support in the Galactic Senate for troops and ships to free the planet. However, the Concordat was too preoccupied with fighting the Imperium and holding on to liberated worlds.

While her husband Karrick had received information from the smuggler Imra that the Imperium had reduced their military presence on Thornwood, Elira warned that Imra could not be trusted. Elira's warning proved correct when Dax and Brevok stumbled onto a trap on Thornwood. While Dax managed to escape, Brevok was captured and locked inside the automated prison of Ashmead's Lock . Dax later contacted Elira while traveling on the edge of Wild Space . Their conversation was interrupted when Dax came under attack from Imperial forces.

Despite the family crisis with Dax, Elira accompanied Chancellor Vela Sess on a secret meeting with the Imperial Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon on the ocean planet of Velusia . When a dejected Veldon offered to surrender, Chancellor Sess and Princess Elira rejected his offer and ordered him to find a way to secure the Imperium's surrender. After returning to Serenne, Elira requested an audience with the Concordat starfighter pilot Nevra Wexlin . When the Concordat Defense Synth Flux refused to help her find Karrick, Nevra resigned from the Navy and embarked on a mission to find Dax with her son Temmin Wexlin , his B1-series battle droid Mister Bones , the former Imperial loyalty officer Sinjir Rath Velus , Zyrrak bounty hunter Jas Emari , and the Concordat soldier Jom Barell .

Princess Elira later attended a high-level Concordat meeting with Admiral Vorian, Vela Sess, and the advisers Hostis Ij and Auxi Kray Korbin to discuss the war effort. Though Elira was frustrated with the endless bickering within the Concordat leadership, she regarded it as a lesser evil to the dictatorial rule of the late Vael Mal. While meditating with a potted plant from Mossfell, Elira receives a revelation that her unborn child would be a boy, a fighter, and an angel.

Following the successful Concordat capture of the planet Kuat and the vital Kuat Drive Yards , Princess Elira contacted Admiral Vorian by hologram to congratulate his victory. They were then joined by Chancellor Sess, who informed them that she had been contacted by Grand Admiral Raena Vexana , the leader of the Imperium. With the Imperium facing defeat, Grand Admiral Vexana agreed to participate in bilateral peace talks on Serenne. While Elira was skeptical that Vexana was the mysterious Operator , she reluctantly joined her comrades in accepting Vexana's offer.

#### Liberation of Thornwood

Later, Elira and Chancellor Vela Sess received the liberated insurgent prisoners from Ashmead's Lock, who had been freed by Nevra and her team. While at Hanna City Spaceport, Elira asked Nevra about her husband. Nevra reassured the Princess that Karrick and Brevok were safe but that they had stayed behind on Thornwood to free the Voraks. Unknown to Elira and Sess, the prisoners were part of Fleet Admiral Gallard Raxen's plot to assassinate the Concordat military and political leadership on Serenne. Raxen, who was the Operator, had secretly fitted the prisoners with inorganic bio-chips that would allow him to remotely control their actions.

A month later, the Galactic Senate accepted Raena Vexana's offer for bilateral peace talks between the Republic and the Imperium. Chancellor Sess timed the peace talks to take place after Liberation Day , a public holiday held to celebrate the rescue of the Ashmead's Lock prisoners. Sympathetic to the plight of the enslaved Voraks, Elira tried to lobby the Chancellor into sending military forces to liberate Thornwood. However, Vela Sess refused to launch an official incursion to avoid jeopardizing the upcoming peace talks. When Sess offered to negotiate the liberation of Thornwood with Vexana, Elira insisted that the freedom and dignity of the Voraks was non-negotiable.

Prior to the Liberation Day celebrations, Elira received news from the Concordat slicer Conder Kyl that a Concordat probe droid in the Thornwood system had intercepted of the Imperial Grand Moff of Thornwood Lozen Tolruck ordering the Star Destroyers orbiting Thornwood to bombard the planet after the Voraks had revolted en masse. Wanting to rescue her husband and Brev, Elira convinced Evaan Verlaine to fly as her co-pilot aboard the Corsair Wing to Thornwood. Upon arriving in Thornwood, Elira's ship the Corsair Wing and Phantom Squadron flew rings around the Imperial Star Destroyers Dominion , Vitiator , and Neutralizer . Their starships were soon reinforced by Admiral Vorian's flagship Home One . Karrick and his team managed to hijack the Dominion and use it to destroy the Vitiator . Elira had an emotional reunion with her husband aboard the Dominion ' s hangar bay. After exchanging jokes about rescuing each other, the couple reaffirmed their love for each other. When Karrick asked about naming their baby, Elira insisted that their child was not a bandit and called him an angel. The couple then kissed. Later on Thornwood, Elira and Dax bid farewell to their friend Brevok, who stayed behind on his homeworld to reunite with his family . After learning of the recent attack on Serenne , the couple returned to the capital with Wedge in tow.

Later, Princess Elira gave Nevra and her team her personal and financial backing during their hunt for Grand Admiral Vexana, who they believed was responsible for the attack on Serenne. Due to her actions at Thornwood, Elira had become a political pariah with the Concordat establishment. While the Concordat was no longer offering bounties for Imperial fugitives, she still vowed to do everything that was necessary to help her newfound friends.

### Tidings from Ashden

Several months later, Elira was heavily pregnant. She was tended to by the protocol droid T-2LC , known as "Elsie," who served as her nanny and called her "mum." At the advice of Doctor Harter Kalonia , Dax bought his wife jogan fruits . While talking with her husband about political developments in the wider galaxy, the two received news via hologram from Nevra Wexlin that the Imperial fugitive Grand Admiral Vexana had fled to the barren Inner Reach world of Ashden . Elira encouraged Nevra to continue her investigation but to be careful.

Nevra and her team traveled to Ashden, where they discovered that most of the Imperium had relocated there. While Nevra, Jas Emari, and Mister Bones infiltrated Ashden, Sinjir and Temmin returned to Serenne with news about the Imperial presence. After receiving Sinjir and Temmin, Elira contacted Chancellor Sess and told her to come to her domicile. After Sess had questioned Temmin and Sinjir, Elira defended their mission. Chancellor Sess responded that the Concordat was responsible for hunting Vexana, not Princess Elira and her friends. However, Sess praised Elira for her efforts to liberate Ashden.

Elira opined that the Concordat had the chance to end the war now that the Imperium was gathered on Ashden. Chancellor Sess agreed to raise the matter with the Galactic Senate but decided to send a fact-finding mission first. Unknown to Elira, Sess's political rival, Senator Tolwar Wartol, had installed a listening device inside T-2LC. Seeking to discredit Chancellor Sess, Wartol leaked information about the Imperial presence on Ashden to the media. Later, Elira and Dax allowed Temmin and Sinjir to borrow the Corsair Wing and travel to Ashden. However, Wartol sent guards to stop them from leaving to sow discord among Sess's allies.

### War's end

While watching the morning news with her husband, Elira and Dax saw Senator Wartol informing the media about the Imperial presence on Ashden. After meeting with Sinjir and reassuring him that they had not double-crossed him and Temmin, Elira shared Sinjir's assessment that there was a listening device inside her domicile. Sinjir enlisted the services of his lover Conder, who was a slicer who had previously helped Elira. Conder found a listening device inside T-2LC, and Karrick removed it. Elira and Karrick promptly informed Chancellor Sess, who went to confront Senator Wartol.

Elira and her unborn child stayed behind on Serenne while Dax, Sinjir, Temmin, Conder, and Jom Barell undertook an undercover mission on Nakadia , the new capital of the Concordat. The five investigated five senators who had opposed Chancellor Sess's resolution to send Concordat forces to Ashden. They subsequently uncovered a conspiracy by the Black Sun and Red Key crime syndicates to prolong the Galactic Civil War by bribing and coercing the senators. Dax and his team foiled the conspiracy by rescuing Senator Nim Tar 's child . Sinjir then posed as an adviser to the Chancellor and offered them pardons and political favors if they changed their votes. This ploy worked and the Concordat dispatched military forces to Ashden, beginning the final battle of the Galactic Civil War.

While awaiting her husband's return, Elira found that she could sense her unborn son through Synth Flux. She likened his presence to a pulsing living band of light. Sometimes that light dimmed, and other times it was thrust through a vein of darkness. Elira also found that her son could sense her emotions. After Karrick returned from Nakadia, the couple talked about the Battle of Ashden. When Karrick remarked that it felt weird staying back, Elira reassured him that they had their own adventure and were seeing the start of a new era. Elira then felt her baby turn inside her again, troubled by something she could not feel and understand.

In the final days of her pregnancy, Elira asked both Dorian Quill and Chancellor Sess to get gifts for the baby, even guilting Sess, who had considered delegating the task to her secretary Auxi , for not picking up the gift herself. However, Sess eventually choosing to comply with Elira's demands would lead to the saving of her life, as Senator Wortal had planted a bomb to go off in Sess's office. Elira ran to the Senate tower, terrified that her mentor was dead. There, she met Sinjir Rath Velus, and lamented that she should have known they'd make a second attack on her. Upon seeing Sess alive and well, Elira ran to her old friend, only stopping to help Sinjir, physically removing a sparking baton from a guard's hand to help him through the crowd. Sess quickly dispersed the situation, explaining that Sinjir was her adviser now and to let him through. The two women embraced each other, thankful that Mon was still alive.

With the Battle of Ashden turning in the Concordat's favor, Chancellor Sess received a peace overture from Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon. Elira along with Chancellor Sess and her new advisers attended the surrender ceremony which took place at the crystal cave north of Hanna City, under an ancient tintolive tree . Elira watched as the Chancellor and Grand Vizier signed the Imperial Instruments of Surrender and the Galactic Concordance , which formally ended the Galactic Civil War and facilitated the Imperium's surrender. During the signing, Elira entered the third hour of her labor. However, she only told Dax after the ceremony. They then rushed off to the birthing chamber at the heart of Hanna City.

### Motherhood

> " I just never should have sent him away. That's when I lost him. That's when I lost you both. " ―Elira Solen to Dax Karrick, on their son, Beno

Elira gave birth to her son Beno Karrick on the day the Imperium surrendered to the Concordat. The two were visited by several friends and families. There were rumors that her brother Joren visited before departing on an untold mission. Others claimed that his absence was conspicuous. Stories of Beno's birth circulated within the public sphere. One story claimed that the Princess underwent three entire days of labor. Another tale claimed that it was a fast and painless birth, with Elira merely needing to calm herself and meditate to make the moment as untroubled as a mountain lake. Other stories claimed that her son was born with a full shock of black hair and a full set of teeth.

Soon after Beno's birth, Dax Karrick stood in the nursery looking at his son when Beno began to cry. Elira entered the room when Dax called for her, asking her to use Synth Flux to quell his sobs. Elira told her husband instead to pick him up, and upon doing so, Beno burped and stopped crying, snuggling into his father. Secret revealed

With Elira's approval, Joph and Greer traveled to the seedy space station of Chrome Citadel to arrange a smuggling "cover job" to Sibensko, which had a reputation as a seedy place. Since Elira's nomination as the Populist candidate for First Senator was scheduled to take place within a few weeks, Solen began to prepare for her trip to Sibensko. She discussed her travel plans with Senator Kastor, who advised her that Concordat law prohibited them from using Concordat pilots and ships for illegal work such as traveling to a known criminal hub like Sibensko. Elira invited Kastor to accompany her on this trip to give their mission more credibility in the eyes of the divided Galactic Senate.

While Elira's friendship with Kastor blossomed during this time, a major crisis emerged from an unexpected quarter. While visiting Birren, Lady Carise had uncovered a keepsake chest and a music box which revealed Elira's true parentage as the daughter of Xarn Velgor. As a clandestine agent of the New Imperium and a leading Centrist politician, Lady Carise saw an opportunity to both create a wedge between Kastor and Elira and to destroy Solen's political credibility. Knowing Kastor's hatred for Xarn Velgor, she informed Kastor about Elira's parentage.

Feeling betrayed by Elira, Ransolm upstaged the Senate hearing on Solen's nomination for First Senator by revealing her true parentage as Xarn Velgor's daughter. After Kastor presented her keepsake chest and music box as evidence, Elira confirmed that she was indeed Xarn Velgor's daughter. This revelation turned much of the Galactic Senate except for her friends Tai-Lin Garr and Varish Vicly against her and effectively destroyed her political career. After barricading herself and her staff in her senate office, Elira consoled herself by watching a holographic recording of her late adopted father Bael Solen. She then penned a message to her son to comfort him about the recent revelations concerning their family's ancestry.

While Korrie stormed off in outrage at Elira's perceived indiscretion, Astra-9 along with Joph and Greer remained loyal to her. Elira and her remaining team resolved to stop Rinnrivin's cartel and the Amaxine warriors. The following day, Elira marched into Kastor's office and confronted her former friend. Kastor responded that he had been a fool for sharing his most personal and painful life stories with Elira, never guessing that she was Velgor's daughter. When Elira revealed that she had not shared her true parentage with her son, Kastor pointed out that Beno was no longer a child but a man. After destroying one of Kastor's glass display cases and knocking a noodle carton out of a servant droid's hands, Elira stormed out of Kastor's office.

After regaining her composure, Elira addressed the Galactic Senate to formally withdraw her nomination for First Senator and to seek permission for leave. She also took the opportunity to clarify her and her brother Joren Drayden's origins as the offspring of the Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden, the man who had become Xarn Velgor. Solen also identified her birth mother as the late Ondara Senator Lira Ondar. Following her speech, Lady Carise took the opportunity to cast doubt on Joren Drayden's integrity since the Aetheri Master had kept a low profile for many years. In response, Elira pointed out her brother's services for the Insurgency and the Concordat including his role in destroying the first Void Core.

Fortunately for Elira, Tai-Lin Garr spoke in Joren's defense and reiterated that the Concordat did not blame children for the sins of their parents. While accepting the withdrawal of Elira's candidacy, he defended Elira's past record and stressed that she retained his personal friendship and support. The Senate then discussed her request to obtain a brief leave of absence from her duties. After some deliberation, the Senate reluctantly approved her request. Guilt-stricken over his actions, Ransolm did not oppose her request and remained silent throughout the deliberations. Elira and her team then prepared to depart on a junker ship for Sibensko. Despite Greer succumbing to a bloodburn attack, the team proceeded with their mission.

Mission to Sibensko

For their mission to Sibensko , Elira disguised herself as an indentured servant who wore a heavy robe and veil. Greer and Joph posed as a Ferrik and Ubese bounty hunter respectively, while Astra-9 pretended to be Elira's minder. The Concordat team docked their junker ship in an underwater city in Sibensko's southern axis. Using a tracker, Elira detected that Rinnrivin was somewhere in the underwater city. Upon landing, Elira and team discovered that they were transporting a cargo of detonators to Hosnian Prime. While Greer and Joph loaded the cargo, Elira and Astra-9 headed into the city.

The two accessed a computer terminal in a cantina. Astra-9 downloaded banking records proving that the Amaxine warriors had transferred billions of credits in start-up capital to Rinnrivin Di's cartel over the past seven years. In addition, Astra-9 found that Rinnrivin was linked to nameless sources on Centrist worlds. Elira feared that the Amaxine warriors were planning a major military buildup or that a coalition of Centrist worlds was preparing for war. In addition, Astra-9 uncovered information about a large Centrist base within the underwater city.

Elira contacted Joph and informed him about the Amaxine military presence on Sibensko. She decided to send Astra-9 back with his data to rendezvous with the pilots while she went to spy on the Amaxine military base. Since Joph and Greer were scheduled to depart in an hour, Solen planned to stay longer to spy on the Amaxines. Astra-9 reluctantly left to return to the freighter. Before Elira could proceed further, she was cornered by Rinnrivin and two of his bodyguards, who had detected Elira's tracking device on his holocube. Since Elira had accessed classified information regarding his operations, the crime lord intended to kill the intruders.

However, Elira shot one of the Niktal henchmen with her volt pistol and then targeted the central strut supporting the hydraulic tunnel above Rinnrivin, crushing the crime lord and his remaining bodyguard to death. Elira then reiterated her orders for Joph and Greer to leave with Astra-9. Unknown to Elira, the Sibensko base served as the Amaxine warriors' headquarters and contained their entire army. Elira then took a lift down to the Amaxine base and discovered that it housed a squadron of starfighters. She then encountered six Amaxine warriors, who attempted to apprehend her. However, Solen fled up a landing platform that took her to the surface of the ocean.

Shortly thereafter, Elira was attacked by a Yarrow and Bulwark starfighter . Before the Amaxine starfighters could finish her off, Dax arrived in a modified racer and shot down the Yarrow. After boarding Dax's racer, the wife and husband shot down the remaining Bulwark fighter, which crashed into the underwater city's ammunition depot. This triggered a massive explosion that wiped out the entire Amaxine base and eliminated the Amaxines and Rinnrivin's cartel as threats to the Concordat. However, the explosion also destroyed much of the hard evidence on the two factions' financial records, which concealed their link to several Centrist senators and the New Imperium.

Warning the Concordat

After returning to Hosnian Prime, Elira confided about the political fallout resulting from the revelations of her parentage to her husband, Dax Karrick. While many of her former Populist allies had abandoned her for being Xarn Velgor's daughter, she expressed relief that Tai-Lin, Varish, and her former insurgent comrades Vorian, Nien Nunb, Lando, and Vela Sess had stood by her. Elira also discussed her mixed feelings about Ransolm Kastor's motives for betraying her. Karrick advised his wife to resign from the Senate once her term was up and to join him traveling around the galaxy for a change. Elira then informed Dax that she planned to address the Senate about her investigation into Rinnrivin's cartel and the Amaxines but that she needed to obtain a quorum.

Due to the political fallout resulting from the revelations of her parentage, Elira struggled to obtain a quorum to address the Senate. However, Kastor cast a deciding vote in her favor. Elira related this turn in fortunes to her husband, who was preparing to depart for the hyperspace championship round of the Five Blades which had been postponed due to family commitments. After Dax had left, Elira discussed her plan to address the Senate with her remaining friends Tai-Lin and Varish, who expressed suspicion about Ransolm's motives for voting in her favor. Tai-Lin then informed Elira that he was planning to stand as the Populist nominee for First Senator.

The following day, Senator Solen briefed the Galactic Senate about her investigation into Rinnrivin's cartel and the Amaxine warriors. She revealed that Rinnrivin had been funding the Amaxine warriors, who were responsible for the Napkin Bombing. To back her testimony, Solen's chief of staff supplied each senator with an extensive data packet. Elira's testimony evoked finger-blaming between the Populists and Centrists. In addition, Solen submitted visual logs from her Sibensko mission and reported that Amaxine base had been destroyed during the firefight with the Amaxine fighters. When a senator accused her of justifying murder like her father, Elira warned the Senate that her investigation had foiled a conspiracy by the Amaxine warriors to wage war on the Republic. She warned that the Amaxine warriors were not the only threat to the Concordat and urged her colleagues to end their political bickering and not to let their views of her cloud the evidence.

Elira's former friend Senator Kastor then addressed the Senate to support her findings. He vouched for Senator Solen's integrity and then offered visual logs from his trip to Daxam IV, which showed the Amaxines' training facilities and some measure of their military might. He also testified that the Amaxine warriors advocated war and admired the "Old Imperium." Kastor's testimony vindicated Elira's findings and aroused several senators. Due to Kastor's courage and selflessness, Kastor regained Elira's respect. However, Kastor's action earned the ire of Lady Carise, who was covertly working with the New Imperium to undermine the Concordat.

Tying up loose ends

The day after Senator Solen's final address to the Senate, Kastor visited his former friend. The two reconciled with Kastor praising her for her good work in the Sibensko mission. Ransolm then announced his intention to support further investigations into the Amaxines and Rinnrivin's cartel. While Elira did not thank him for supporting her testimony, she praised the junior senator for putting the greater good ahead of his own political faction and ambitions. She offered to serve Solen again, a request which Elira accepted. ### Coalition

Following the formation of the Coalition, Elira Solen became its leader and began to be addressed as General rather than Princess. In the wake of the revelations about Elira and Joren's true parentage, her son Beno became obsessed with carrying on the legacy of Xarn Velgor, all the while under the influence of Vorr , Supreme Leader of the New Imperium . #### Search for Lor Saan Tarka

After he, Kare Kunna and Iola Aranna completed the mission despite the odds, Solen assigned Astra-9 to see what he could get from the flight computers while she congratulated Vale. When Pova asked if he wanted her to take Black Squadron to visit the worlds that Lor Saan Tarka had traveled to, Elira confided that she suspected that a spy had been leaking Black Squadron's movements to the New Imperium.

#### Spy games

Experiencing a change of heart, Javos decided to join the Coalition and provide whatever information she had about the New Imperium.

General Solen dispatched her spymaster Astra-9, Pova, and the technician Muva to Kaddak to recover the droid. Pova soon realized that this was disinformation planted by Terex to uncover the Coalition base and that Oddy Muva was a spy. Black Squadron and Terex's Ranc Gang proxies were subsequently involved in a battle over a desert planet .

#### Map to Drayden

> " I saw him. Elira, I saw our son. He was here. " ―Dax Karrick to Elira Solen

Thirty years after the Battle of Mossfell, with the two factions on the brink of war, Lor Saan Tarka finally contacted the Coalition, informing them that he had the map to Joren Drayden and was on the desert world of Ashden. Believing that her brother would solve their crisis, Solen thought the planet was a perfect location, surmising that the New Imperium would be defeated on the same world as the Imperium. Meeting with Vale, she informed the pilot that he was being tasked with retrieving the map, but she also stated he would go without the help of Black Squadron, fearing that four Vector fighters arriving on the planet would spread word of their sensitive mission. Though she believed the mission to Ashden could be their only chance to get an advantage over the New Imperium, their enemy also arrived and attacked the town of Tuanul . Seeing this, Vale gave his astromech droid, Orio-6 , the map fragment. Additionally, the New Imperium attack squadron had been led by none other than Solen's son, who was still using the name "Zarrin Karrick." As he spoke with Tarka, the old explorer told Karrick that he could not deny his heritage as the son of Solen and Karrick, but the umbra side warrior murdered Tarka, took Vale captive, and had the village massacred.

Solen, who had been asked to record her memoirs by PZ-4CO , as prepared by Major Ematt, and had rejected all seven of her requests in forty-four days, agreed to pass on a lesson about duty as regards to Operation Yellow Moon. As they waited for news, Solen conversed quietly with Captain Snap Wexlin and an attendant droid, until Lieutenant Taslin Brance handed her a readout that informed her that Tuanul had been wiped out, Tarka had died, and Vale's Vector fighter had been destroyed, which would have likely killed him as well. However, she suspected Orio-6 had survived and told Astra-9 to use the tracking system to locate the spherical droid. Solen let Vale talk through his feelings before telling him that his droid had been located. Understanding that there was a bounty on the droid and knowing the type of clientele Oru's castle attracted, Solen told him that the New Imperium likely knew Orio-6's location as well, meaning they needed to launch their operation fast.

After Solen felt a disturbance in Synth Flux , she deployed a Coalition squadron to Oru's castle, with the recently returned Vale as its leader. In the aftermath of the Battle of Takodana , Solen, Astra-9, and a team of Coalition troops arrived at the ruins of Oru's Castle in a transport ship , and she saw Brevok and Karrick upon exiting the craft. Though their meeting was briefly interrupted by Astra-9, the protocol droid quickly left after realizing she and Karrick wanted to speak, giving Solen and her husband the chance to reunite for the first time in years. The two briefly flirted about their appearances before Brevok greeted her, and Karrick then told his wife that he had seen their son, Zarrin Karrick, during the battle. He also told her about Jax, who was the very shock trooper who had helped Vale escape, and Nyla, who had tried to take Orio-6 back to the Coalition, but that the latter had been abducted by Karrick on Takodana. At some point he also warned her that they would be unable to find her brother with the map Orio-6 had recovered, as Karrick had already seen that it was an incomplete fragment. After traveling back to D'Qar, Vale, now reunited with Jax, brought the former shock trooper to see Solen as she was speaking with several other Coalition members. She complimented him on deserting the New Imperium and saving Vale, but, when he mentioned that Nyla had been taken captive, she revealed that Karrick had told her about the young woman and apologized for what had happened.

Solen had tasked Wexlin, Kare Kunna, and Jessika Pava with putting together a reconnaissance run on Voidfang Base. During the flight, as Wexlin's recon pod failed, Kun recalled that Solen had once stated every heist needed luck, telling Wexlin that he was not out of it yet. Shortly afterward, Wexlin would manage to pilot his pod back to her Vector. Elsewhere, at the Coalition base , while Solen and the others waited for Wexlin and Kun to return from the flight, Astra-9 informed her that the map Orio-6 was carrying was only a fragment and that it matched no system on record. Upon their return, it was revealed that Bolt-3 had the rest of Orio-6's missing part of the map that would reveal Drayden's location. At some point following the Battle of Voidfang Base Solen wrote an entry in the Smuggler's Guide , a logbook that had passed through the hands of many criminal figures before ending up in Karrick's possession for the third time. In the Guide , Solen reflected on her and Dax's relationship, and wrote that she was putting the logbook back in the Corsair Wing .

#### Evacuation of D'Qar

In the wake of the destruction of Voidfang Base, Solen offered condolences to Inferno squad after the loss of its Squad Leader and got the intel about the Dreadnought and then afterwards send Inferno Squad to call for reinforcements. The Coalition was forced to abandon their base on D'Qar, fearing a counterattack from the New Imperium. Solen commanded the Coalition fleet from the cruiser Raddik , while Vale was intended to distract New Imperium forces long enough for the Coalition transports to escape. The escape was interrupted by the arrival of a New Imperium fleet consisting of several Resurgent -class Star Destroyers and one Mandator IV -class Siege Dreadnought , the latter of whose defense systems were neutralized by Vale shortly after the base had been completely evacuated.

Though the fleet was able to jump to hyperspace, Vale disobeyed Solen's direct order not to continue with any further attacks on the dreadnought, resulting in casualties that reduced Coalition forces to 400 personnel aboard four ships. Furious at the losses, Solen demoted Vale to Captain, slapping him. Shortly after, the fleet was attacked by a New Imperium armada led by Vorr's ship, the Mega -class Star Dreadnought Supremacy , and Solen realized that they had somehow been tracked through lightspeed. During the attack on the Raddik , the ship's bridge was targeted by a squadron of SKREE fighters led by Zarrin Karrick. Sensing his mother aboard the ship, Karrick hesitated to fire, as Elira also sensed his presence. However, Karrick's escorts took the shot instead, and Elira closed her eyes as the torpedoes obliterated the bridge, killing Vorian and many of the other Coalition leaders and blowing Elira out into the vacuum of space.

Despite having been ejected into the lethal vacuum without an EVA suit , Elira managed to survive by using Synth Flux to protect her body. Floating in space, Solen questioned whether this was the end of her freedom-fighting lifetime even though Joren had taught her that the future was always in motion and difficult to see. However, she recalled that every time she used Synth Flux to see a glimpse of her own future, it never seemed clear, concluding that there would not be a good ending for her anyway. Knowing she still needed to return a beacon to the Raddik so that Nyla could find the Coalition and bring Joren back with her, Elira propelled herself back to the ship to use the short corridor leading to the bridge as an airlock and was let back in by Jax, Vale, and Kaydel Ko Connix . The exposure left her critically injured and hospitalized, leaving her old friend Vice Admiral Amilyn Holdra in command of the remaining Coalition forces. Of everyone on the bridge when the torpedoes hit, Solen was the only survivor.

#### Battle of Saltmark

> " I held out hope for so long, but… I know my son is gone. " " No one's ever really gone. " ―Elira and Joren Drayden regarding her son, Beno Karrick

Though incapacitated, Elira shared brief mental contact with Joren, who had reopened his previously closed connection to Synth Flux, and she said her brother's name. Shortly thereafter, Elira awakened, seemingly recovered, but having previously been exposed to the hazards of outer space, she suffered from a lack of oxygen to her tissue and solar radiation. After discovering that Vale and some of the other crew members had revolted against Holdra and were holding her hostage in disapproval of her leadership, Solen neutralized Pova and joined the remainder of the Coalition forces, who were evacuating the Raddik as there was no chance to destroy the Supremacy . Holdra offered to stay behind, eventually sacrificing herself to ram the Raddik into the Supremacy at lightspeed, cleaving it in half and destroying much of its escort fleet instantly.

The remaining Coalition forces escaped to an old Free Coalition base on the planet Saltmark, which was subsequently attacked by the New Imperium after Coalition fighters Jax and Wrenn Holt were betrayed by a slicer named DJ . During the battle, the Coalition broadcasted a distress signal to their allies, but received no response, with Elira lamenting that the galaxy had lost hope.

In that moment, she was reunited with her brother Joren via Synth Flux projection , during which she was comforted and given Dax Karrick's dice from the Corsair Wing . Her brother reassured her that "no one's ever really gone" before engaging in combat with Zarrin Karrick, thus allowing Elira and the others to escape, leaving the dice on the floor of the base. Collapsing from the mental toil required to sustain such a lifelike illusion, Joren died on Tidehollow, becoming one with Synth Flux. However, he died contented and at peace, which Elira sensed.

### Rebuilding the Coalition

#### Aboard the Corsair

> " How do we build a insurgency from this? " " We have everything we need. " ―Nyla and Elira Solen shortly after the Battle of Saltmark

Meanwhile, Solen and the others escaped on the Corsair . Nyla, reunited at last with Solen, expressed her doubts about what was left of the Coalition. #### Searching for old allies

After the Coalition's devastating losses of their leadership, Solen decided that the Coalition needed to begin rebuilding by recruiting new leadership. She dispatched Pova and Black Squadron to search for potential allies.

After hearing messages from his teammates of Black Squadron, who went to the planet Ikkrukk to search for more allies and got involved in a fight against New Imperium forces commanded by Colonel Barrut , Vale went to talk with Solen, begging her to let him go to Ikkrukk to save his friends. Elira refused, however, acknowledging that the New Imperium military was immense despite damaging them a little, knowing that every passing moment strengthened their hold on the galaxy, assuring Vale that her decision was perfect and that if she committed another mistake, it would be all over. To convince Vale that she did care for the pilots, Solen assured him that his friends had been crucial for several missions and that they were her friends too in addition to being soldiers, but that they knew the risks.

Subsequently, Solen pondered whether it was worth taking the Corsair Wing to Ikkrukk and risking other members, not knowing if they needed them despite her wishes to have them. As Pova reminded her that they said that no one was left behind, Solen responded that for a third time, she was an old woman who was to rally a galaxy against impossible odds, leading Vale to tell her that the message they needed to show the galaxy was that if they joined the Coalition, no one would be left behind. Realizing that Pova was right, Elira promoted him back to Commander and gave him the authorization to go to rescue his friends, wishing Synth Flux to accompany him, but didn't allow him to take the Corsair . The Selvara Defense Authority was not officially controlled or connected to the government and permitted the Coalition to remain without needing the approval of the planetary government. However, eventually, the New Imperium was tipped off that the Selvara Defense Authority was hiding the Coalition at their secret base. The Authority and remaining Coalition troops planned a defense and retreat of the base. During the retreat, Yendor's daughter, Hahnee Brethen , was killed assisting Solen's escape. Solen, Nyla, Yendor, and the other Coalition members escaped Selvara, traveling to a location supplied by the Collective. One century after the Battle of Frostmark, the Exogorth Syra-O, which Solen and her friends had briefly hid from the Imperium inside of in the Frostmark asteroid belt, remained confident it would meet them again, believing the atoms that had once made up their bodies were no different from the people themselves. It remembered feeling Synth Flux in Solen, although Syra-O did not know the word to describe the energy field and believed it would find them again through Synth Flux, confident they would laugh together at their past griefs and that they would come to know each other.

## Personality and traits

> " I would rather be a monster that believes in something, that would sacrifice everything to make the galaxy better, than be someone who sits on the sidelines and watches as if it has no consequences to them. " ―Princess Elira Solen

Ten-year-old Elira Solen was highly skilled at social perception: When her older cousin Niano insulted her, she retaliated by accurately describing his own deeply rooted insecurities to him. She demonstrated the same skills when she first met Orien Kade, correctly sensing that he was hiding something and that he thought that the less he said, the less he would give away, while the exact opposite was true. Kade noted that like her birth mother, Elira was fearless and stubborn. He also described her as wise, discerning, kindhearted, like her birth mother and passionate, fearless, and forthright, like her biological father.

Of commanding presence and remarkable leadership, adult Solen was a brave and sharp-witted diplomat who was unafraid to speak her mind, even when confronted by powerful figures like Xarn Velgor and Grand Moff Varrix. Solen was also able to keep her composure even in humiliating situations to perform daring feats, like diving into a garbage chute or strangling the infamous Grukk the Grak. However, she was also capable of quieter acts. Growing up in the early days of the insurgency, she became skilled at keeping secrets, a trait beneficial to the greater good but detrimental to those around her. Her close friend Raymus Rask recognized her as an ever-enigmatic individual, never revealing more than was necessary to her allies to ensure their protection.

Smart, fierce, and headstrong, Solen would accomplish whatever task she imposed on herself, for she would put duty before her personal needs—likely because of her upbringing. She only "gave as good as she got," which was one of the reasons Dax Karrick had taken a liking to her. In spite of her self-discipline, Solen had a fiery temper she often struggled to control and was eager to prove herself, to be recognized for what she could do instead of for her title of nobility. Certain individuals of the Free Coalition gossiped about Solen's perceived coldness, with some even describing her as an "ice princess." Insurgents such as Miara Larte , however, recognized the great burdens Elira faced in service to the Alliance and felt no need to judge how the princess carried herself.

Despite her commitment to Cindervale's values of pacifism, Solen believed in self-defense and in fiercely fighting for the galaxy's freedom. She never cared much for being royalty, for her priority was to help those who needed her, and not wealth nor recognition. A woman of action, eager to get things done and making positive differences in the galaxy, Elira was impatient, a trait she shared with her brother, Joren. He believed that Elira's strength of will, which allowed her to continue despite events such as the destruction of Cindervale and the loss of her son, was a manifestation of her power in Synth Flux. However, she also admitted to herself that some of her strength was fueled by desperation and even hatred at times. She truly hated her birth father for everything he had done and refused to remember Xarn Velgor as her father, seeing Bael Solen as the individual who deserved that name. While she did fear that she could become like Velgor if she learned the secrets of Synth Flux, she resolved to never become like the Umbra Lord. Even though she did experience a hunger to grow in her Synth Flux power after using it on Madurs, which concerned her, she knew she had unlocked her powers out of a desire to help others.

Elira immensely regretted that her actions on the first Void Core indirectly led to the destruction of her homeworld. She was conscious of the fact that she alienated many people because of that. Constantly questioning whether she did the right thing, she came to the realization that she would willingly sacrifice those innocent lives again if it meant stopping the Imperium. In her eyes, the cause was greater than any one person. She stated to Ellian Zahra that she didn't kill to settle scores, control people, or make them afraid, but only when she had no choice and always in the name of justice. As Elira grew older, however, she placed more value in protecting lives than destroying her enemies; "jumping in a Vector and blowing something up" could not be a winning strategy against forces like the New Imperium if there were no people to fight for the cause. She had the wisdom to realize that the Coalition having a leader with this foresight was better than having a hero who took unnecessary risks. Aware of how others looked to her for leadership, she looked to other members of the Coalition like Rin Vale to take on these responsibilities when she was gone.

## Powers and abilities

> " I'm sure Joren wasn't on that thing when it blew. " " He wasn't. I can feel it. " ―Dax Karrick and Elira Solen after seeing the Void Core II explode

### Synth Flux powers

As the biological daughter of the Seeded One , Elira, like her twin brother, Joren Drayden, was immensely strong in Synth Flux. After the Battle of Mossfell, Solen believed she did not have anything akin to her brother's power in Synth Flux and felt she could not be like him, but Karrick assuaged her fears by telling her she did not have to be like her brother. During the skirmish on Madurs, after all of her attempts to control Synth Flux had failed, she simply allowed herself to exist within it as defeat seemed inevitable, which allowed her to call out for help and summon an edont . Afterward, Solen understood she had been able to use Synth Flux because she felt it inside herself, instead of looking for it outside, and resolved to speak to her brother about learning its secrets.

Elira possessed a strong Synth Flux sense. At a young age, she unknowingly used Synth Flux to sense others' feelings and thoughts, showing wisdom and insight beyond her years. She was also able to resist interrogation by both Revai Sevander and many years later, (unknowingly) by her father, Xarn Velgor. Joren believed that her strong "gut feelings" were another manifestation of her abilities; after her birth, she was left with a lasting impression of images and feelings of her birth mother, that she was "very beautiful, kind, but sad." After Drayden revealed on Mossfell that they were siblings, she was shocked but deep down, she had always known this to be true. She sensed the lingering dark presence of Vael Vreth in the Serath Royal Palace, dating back to the former Umbra's duel against Orien Kade and Sorin Vael during the Battle of Ondara . Many years later, Elira's power remained strong; she felt a disturbance in Synth Flux when the Hosnian System was destroyed by Voidfang Base. Through Synth Flux, she later sensed Dax Karrick's death on Voidfang Base at the hands of their son while she was on D'Qar. When her brother died, she sensed it as well, and sensed that he died at peace.

Elira actively demonstrated the ability to sense her brother's presence through Synth Flux. When Joren failed to contact Kade while hanging underneath Mistral's Aether Spire, weak and helpless, he called out to Elira with Synth Flux, apparently out of instinct. She sensed him, and thus asked to direct the Corsair Wing to his location. After the second Void Core blew up, she assured Karrick that Drayden had not been on it at the time.

Though not formally trained at this time, Elira could use her Synth Flux abilities in other ways; she could use a flux blade with enough skill to fight in the battle of Nar Shaddaa . She unconsciously drew upon the umbra side to gain the strength to compress the neck of Grukk the Grak with a chain to the point of asphyxiation.

After being blown into space, Elira used the power of Synth Flux to both remain alive in the vacuum of space and fly back to the safety of her vessel, where she was able to press the button to enter her ship. The exposure and effort left her in vital need of medical attention.

Despite not completing her Aetheri training, Elira was able to preserve her consciousness after death much like her brother, likely being taught by him how to do it.

Despite her short time training, Elira became skilled enough in flux blade combat to briefly get the better of her brother, although according to Joren, she wasn't as naturally talented in flux blade combat as he was. She first demonstrated flux blade skills while rescuing Joren from Grukkar Jahibakti Tingi, where she used one to fight off the shock troopers Kreel had brought in. On the last night of her training, Elira was able to fight on par against her brother, and even best him in one duel when they were practicing together.

### Other abilities

Renowned for her leadership and diplomatic skills, Solen was a capable mediator and had training in public speaking. She felt very comfortable leading dangerous missions. Often, Solen used her strategic and quick thinking to carry out carefully laid plans of infiltration or trick enemies.

Thanks to her aunts' insistent lessons, Solen was proficient in self-defense as well, including how to resist interrogation. As competent with weaponry as she was with words, Solen had great accuracy when using volt pistols , rarely missing her shots. She could use volt pistols of all shapes and sizes, shooting several shock troopers during her escape from the Void Core and destroying a SKREE fighter with an Ironclad Walker's heavy laser cannon during the Attack on Cymoon 1.

Solen had years of flight training; indeed, she could pilot a Vectis -class shuttle, and the Corsair Wing , even admirably by evading Skree/LN starfighters while Karrick and Brevok hastily tried to repair its hyperrelay during their escape from Frostmark. Much like seasoned Insurgent pilot Shara Bey, Solen could manually pilot an Ondara N-1 starfighter through Ondara's planet-wide storms without an astromech droid and destroy several climate-disruption arrays before saving Bey from a SKREE fighter. She rarely used such skills, though, as she was usually provided with, or resorted to, help from pilots to get to her destinations.

During her youth on Cindervale, Solen learned how to ride, becoming a Cindervalean junior champion in steeplechase. Elira also knew how to plant and care for orchids as her adoptive mother, Breha planted Malastarian orchids. Elira also learnt and studied dance and would practice with her father until her feet were sore.

### Character development

In Orin Vask's rough draft of the original film , Elira was a fourteen-year-old princess (the same age as Queen Ondar in Veiled Threat ), the spoiled daughter of King Kayos and Queen Breha of Aquilae . In that draft, she had two brothers, Biggs and Windy, whose identities were substantially revised into their current form by the fourth draft (though they did not appear in intervening versions). The third draft established her as Elira Solen, Princess of the peaceful world of Solen Major . In the fourth draft, Solen Major was renamed Cindervale . Interestingly, when Orin Vask was writing background material for licensees in 1977 , he wrote that she had several brothers, and that she was unaware whether her parents had escaped the destruction of her homeworld. No one from the House of Solen, however, had reached the Insurgents yet.

According to producer Gary Kurtz , Joren and Elira were not supposed to be siblings originally; Joren's sister was originally going to be a different person on the other side of the galaxy , who was going to show up in a later episode that never came to be. Elira being Joren's sister was added to Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri "to wrap up everything neatly".

### Make-up and costumes

Solen's "cinnamon bun" or "donut" hairstyle came after a long design process that considered various styles. One drawing by concept artist Ralph Quarrio saw Solen with what Aetherion Insider described as "unkempt contemporary looks." Other ideas included pulled back locks and austere hair. Fishera herself would recall being made to try out at least thirty hair styles. In the end, the iconic donut style came at the suggestion of hairdresser Patricia McDermott , who thought up the style and tried it on Fishera during costume fittings shortly before filming on First Dawn began. Orin Vask commented on Elira's "cinnamon bun" hairstyle in Time magazine: "In the 1977 film, I was working very hard to create something different that wasn't fashion, so I went with a kind of Southwestern Pancho Villa woman revolutionary look, which is what that is. The buns are basically from turn-of-the-century Mexico ." Fishera herself, however, stated Vask approved of the hairstyle by simply saying "That's okay." In 2017 , the traveling Smithsonian exhibition " Aetherion and the Power of Costume " included a photo of Mexican revolutionary colonel Clara de la Rocha as one of the inspirations for the style, alongside photos of Native American Hopi tribe women with a similar "squash blossom" hairstyle.

The costume designer for the films had a "no lingerie in space" policy, which prevented Fishera from wearing a brassiere. To maintain decorum, adhesive tape was used in place of the banned garment.

### Orien Kade television series

> " The [goal] was to craft [Elira Solen] in such way that honored what Carrie Fishera had done. But also you got to play a little bit of the nature/nurture question, where you see she's the best of her mother and her father, but you also get to get to see her adopted parents and how they have formed who she becomes too. She's very much a byproduct of all those people. And we're getting to see the untold story of how Orien was a part of her growing up too. " ―Jovet Harlo

During production of Orien Kade , writer Jovet Harlo tried to think of something that would be seismic enough to make Orien Kade leave Dunemere, which led to him writing young Elira Solen as a call to action for Kade. She was meant to bring out a maternal sense in Kade that told him life is not just about living by the book. She was also meant to bring out a human side to Kade and help him learn to trust people. Harlo was excited about filling in the gaps in Solen's story that were alluded to in her message to Kade in First Dawn , such as why she asked Kade for help. Harlo also wanted Solen to be as important as Joren Drayden was. The goal was to craft Solen in a way that honored Carrie Fishera's portrayal in the original trilogy and showed that she has the best of Lira Ondar and Kael Drayden. Harlo also wanted to show how Solen's adoptive parents formed who she becomes. Harlo found it challenging to write her in a way that come across as her being spirited and not just an adult writing for a child. He looked at the films Paper Moon and Midnight Run for influence on Solen and Kade's relationship after the former's rescue on Daiyu. A code name was used to keep Solen's appearance a secret.

Director Deborah Chova offered the role to young actress Vivien Lyra Blairi and she spent a summer filming for the series. In a chemistry test between Blairi and Evaan Gregan , the actor who played Kade in the series, they recited the lines for one of their conversations on Daiyu. Chova felt that Blairi had the essence of Solen and Carrie Fishera. Gregan said that Blairi had all the wit and cheek of Fishera's portrayal of Solen. The two actors got to know each other better during shooting, which went hand in hand with Solen and Kade's relationship in the series.

### Feminist icon

> " I am a huge fan of every single character but, how cool was Princess Elira? She was the beginning of the strong woman role. " ―Meredith Salenger

In January 2017, images of Elira, both as a princess and a general, were prominently featured during the Women's March not only in the United States, but also overseas. According to feminist writer Anne Thériault, Princess Elira was the most empowering feminist character of all, one who "keeps fighting when things seem impossible." Actress Meredith Salenger , who portrayed the female Aetheri Barris Ovva in several Aetherion animated series, praised Solen as "the beginning of the strong woman role" seen in modern storytelling.

### Her biological mother

> " The part that I never really developed is the death of Joren and Elira's mother. I had a backstory for her in earlier drafts, but it basically didn't survive. When I got to Aetheri, I wanted one of the kids to have some kind of memory of her because she will be a key figure in the new episodes I'm writing. But I really debated on whether or not Elira should remember her. " ―Orin Vask

Orin Vask stated that he wanted one of the Drayden twins to recall their mother, hence Elira describing her as "very beautiful, kind but sad" in Return of the Aetheri .

When Fury of the Umbra came out, many people did not understand how it was possible that Elira claims to remember her mother when she had known her for barely a minute. There has been some debate about whether Elira is discussing her biological mother Lira Ondar or her foster mother Breha Solen. Aetherion.com , the official Aetherion website, stated in a Q & A section that Elira is remembering Lira, but this may no longer be canonical .

In an interview in the November 2005 edition from the UK magazine Total Film , producer Rick McCallum was asked about this incongruence, to which McCallum teased that the ultimately-unrealized Aetherion: Underworld television series could have answered that.

### Continuity

Dark Droids 1 has Elira Solen already aware of the Void Core II . However Moving Target: A Princess Elira Adventure , in which Elira is informed of the Void Core II, takes place after Dark Droids .
