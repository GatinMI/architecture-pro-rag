# Pip
> " Let it be written in song that Dinna Verrick is accepting this foundling as his son. You are now Dinna Pip. Ferrik apprentice. " ―The Armorer

Dinna Pip , known only as Pip until being formally adopted by Dinna Verrick after the Liberation of Ferrum , was a male Synth Flux-sensitive Ferrik belonging to the same mysterious species as the legendary Grand Master Zho . Pip was born around the year 41 BVE , and was raised at the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor as an Aetheri Initiate of the Aetheri Order . Pip had survived the Great Aetheri Purge and was hidden away. Years later, he was rediscovered by a remnant of the Imperium after it fell and led by Moff Giedan . At this point, Pip became known to many simply as the Child , during the Concordat Era . However, Pip was rescued by Dinna Verrick , a Ferrik bounty hunter where he became a foundling . Sharing many adventures together, Verrick was eventually able to find an Aetheri to train Pip and he rejoined the Aetheri Order under Aetheri Master Joren Drayden . Later on though, Drayden sensed that Pip's heart was not in learning the Aetheri path, thus he subsequently gave in to his attachment to the Ferrik and was returned to him upon which he would start to train to become a Ferrik himself.

Pip was born around 41 BVE and was raised at the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor as an Aetheri Initiate of the Aetheri Order . At one point, Pip encountered a group of dust bunnies that gathered around him and offered him a flower , which he accepted. With the beginning of the Aetheri Purge and dawn of the Imperial Era , he was taken into hiding by Aetheri Master Kelleran Beq . Eventually, he was held at a hideout run by Niktal mercenaries on Arvala-7 during the era of the Concordat , having survived the siege of the Aetheri Sanctum decades prior. While still an infant at the age of fifty years , he was sought by the " the Client ," on behalf of Moff Giedan and Doctor Penn Pershing , by an Imperial client who hired Verrick to retrieve him.

However, Verrick chose not to leave Pip with the remnant and rescued him from the Imperial Remnant safe house . After attempting to hide with Pip along the fringes of the galaxy , Verrick returned to Nevarro to deal with the Imperials chasing Pip in hopes of keeping him safe. In the process, Pip became a Ferrik foundling and the single other member of Verrick's newly created clan . As a result, Verrick took up a quest to deliver Pip back to the Aetheri. Verrick, alongside Pip, would then travel across the galaxy , searching for the few remaining Aetheri who were left. As advised by former Aetheri Sela Marr , Verrick would eventually travel with Pip to Tython , where he could reach out through Synth Flux to find another member of his kind. However, Pip was captured by Imperial forces, and Verrick was forced to lead a mission to rescue him. After being reunited with Pip, Verrick delivered him to Joren Drayden , an Aetheri who agreed to take the child into his care and train him in the ways of Synth Flux.

Pip renewed his training under Drayden and spent a relatively short period training with Drayden on Ossus , where Drayden was building an Aetheri Academy . However, Drayden sensed that the child's heart was not in learning the Aetheri path. After Verrick visited the planet bearing a gift of chain mail armor for Pip, Drayden presented the child with a choice: accepting the gift and returning to the Ferrik, or taking Zho's flux blade and continue with his Aetheri training. Pip chose to return to Verrick and was reunited with him on Dunemere in the middle of a gang war between Vex Vark 's forces , whom Verrick was aiding, and the Gruv Syndicate .

Afterward, he and Verrick would travel the galaxy in hopes of restoring their home planet Ferrum , and the people as well. With the aid of Bo-Katin Velara and the Children of the Watch working together, they were able to establish an alliance with the with other Ferriks in retaking their home planet, only to discover in horror that it was being occupied by Imperial remnants lead by non other than Moff Giedan . Working together, Pip and the Ferriks defeated Moff Giedan. With this, the Ferriks reclaimed their homeworld and Pip would go on to be adopted by Dinna Verrick. The two continued their adventures together, and worked with the Anzellan Droidsmiths to recover warheads under the surface of Nevarro.

Years later, Pip assisted Verrick in hunting Imperial warlord Barro on Phostera , using Synth Flux to help Verrick before accompanying him to Shakari to rescue Rotto the Grak , on behalf of the Concordat . Pip befriended Rotto and helped Verrick uncover that crime lord Janu was actually Imperial fugitive Commander Coin. After Embo captured Verrick and the Anzellan Droidsmith Minch , Pip escaped with the Anzellans and helped rescue the two from the Grak Twins . When Verrick was poisoned by a dragonsnake , Pip cared for him and obtained an antidote from the fisherman Gatori , saving his life. Pip later accompanied Verrick in rescuing Rotto from the Grak Palace , using Synth Flux to calm Keibu and save Rotto during the palace's destruction . After Rotto joined the Concordat, Pip departed with Verrick aboard the Sable Crest 2.0 and was allowed to initiate the jump to hyperspace.

## Biography

### Aetheri Order

#### Training

> " He was raised at the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor. Many Masters trained him over the years. At the end of the Replica Wars when the Imperium rose to power, he was hidden. Someone took him from the Temple. Then his memory becomes dark. He seemed lost. Alone. " ―Sela Marr feeling Pip's thoughts

Born in approximately 41 BVE , during the era of the Vantor Republic , the mysterious alien known as Pip was raised in the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor and trained by several masters until the Aetheri Order fell in the Great Aetheri Purge at the end of the Replica Wars .

At some point during or after 41 BVE , Pip was meditating over a puddle of water with Synth Flux . While this happened, a group of dust bunnies noticed him and begin to bounce and swing around him. One dust bunny caused Pip to sneeze, making him fall into the puddle. As Pip lifted himself from the puddle with Synth Flux and slowly descended to his feet, he noticed the dust bunnies and began to back away. Pip continued to walk away from the creatures as they followed, and when he noticed them still behind him, he began to run. When he turned to see if they were still pursuing, one of the dust bunnies collided with his face, knocking the youngling over. Pip picked himself back up once again and observed the dust bunnies, which began to dance in front of him before presenting him with a flower . Pip accepted the gift, bowing in appreciation. He and the dust bunnies then piled together.

#### Great Aetheri Purge

> " We've got one fleeing on sub-level platform 1215. " ―A replica trooper witnessing Kelleran Beq and Pip

As part of the Great Aetheri Purge in 19 BVE , during the siege of the Aetheri Sanctum , Pip was defended by four Aetheri who attempted to evacuate him from the Temple and take him to an extraction point where a shuttle was waiting for them; the three eventually perished at the hands of the 501st Legion but not before managing to load Pip's pram onto a turbolift away from danger. Pip successfully escaped from the Aetheri Sanctum with the help of Aetheri Master Kelleran Beq who evacuated him from the burning building aboard a BARC speeder . Despite being hunted by Republic LAATs , Beq was able to lose them in the tunnels of the station by Monument Plaza and reach a repulsor platform where sympathetic Ondara troopers were waiting with the H-type Nubian yacht , Heyblibber Duck to evacuate him and his companions. Ambushed by replicas led by shock troopers , Beq took Pip onto the ship under cover from the Ondara troopers and they successfully escaped the planet. After escaping Vantor, Beq found a way to keep Pip safe. After the Imperium rose to power , Pip, along with all other surviving Aetheri, went into hiding. During the Imperial Era , he remained undetected by concealing his Synth Flux powers , which allowed him to be unnoticed by the Warden Order .

### Hunted by Giedan

#### Liberation

> " The commission was quite specific. The asset was to be terminated. " ―VX-11

By 9 AVE , Pip had fallen into the hands of a Niktal encampment on the planet Arvala-7 . The Ferrik bounty hunter Dinna Verrick was dispatched by a member of Giedan's Imperial remnant known as " the Client " and Imperial Doctor Penn Pershing to capture Pip alive, though the Client gave Verrick the option to return Pip dead for a lower price. The assassin droid VX-11 was also dispatched to terminate him. After working together to storm the encampment the infant was being held in, Verrick and VX-11 found Pip. Upon seeing the infant, Verrick was confused as he was told that the asset was 50 years old, but VX-11 explained that Pip must have had a unique aging process. VX-11 then attempted to terminate the infant, but Verrick shot the droid before he was able to do so.

Shortly after, Verrick took Pip back to his ship , the Sable Crest . On the return journey, they were attacked by a trio of Trandoshan bounty hunters , who attempted to kill Pip. After Verrick defeated them, he and Pip camped out in the desert for the night . While Verrick sat by the fire , Pip got out of his pram and then approached the bounty hunter, raising his hand up to the Ferrik's wounds. Verrick stopped him and placed him back into his pram twice. The next day, the pair made it to the Sable Crest only to find it being scavenged by Duskkins . Verrick attacked their dune hauler for the scavenged parts and attempted to climb it while Pip followed in his pod. However, Verrick was knocked to the ground.

The duo then sought help from the only other known living local. While Verrick was explaining the situation, Pip chased some frogs meant for the local Blurrgs and eventually ate one of the creatures. Eventually, Verrick got the assistance of the local Ugnaught Moisture farmer Kuiil to try to convince the Duskkins to give back the parts peacefully. The Duskkins wanted Pip and Verrick's armor , but eventually Verrick agreed to retrieve a mudhorn 's egg in return for the parts. Verrick fought the mudhorn, but was quickly sprawled across the mud while the infant just watched. Just as the mudhorn was about to charge at Verrick, who only had a vibro-knife to defend himself, Pip then used Synth Flux to lift the beast into the air long enough for Verrick to get up and kill it. After using Synth Flux, Pip passed out from Synth Flux exhaustion. When Pip eventually awoke, the Sable Crest had been repaired, and they were enroute to Nevarro .

#### Into hiding

> " We want you to stay. The community's grateful. You can pack all this away in case there's ever trouble. You and your boy can have a good life. He could be a child for a while. " ― Omera , to Dinna Verrick

Nearing their destination, Pip left his pod to take a knob off one of the most important controls for the Sable Crest . Just when he was about to put it in his mouth , Verrick took it from him stating "That's not a toy", and then put him back in his pod. Upon returning to Nevarro, Verrick took Pip to the Client's facility . The Client and Dr. Pershing were excited at their arrival and immediately began examining Pip. Pershing then took him away, and the bounty hunter left wondering what the Client wanted with him, especially when Pip let out a cry for help. However, shortly before leaving Nevarro to carry out a new bounty, Verrick, having a change of heart , returned to rescue Pip. Shortly after Verrick stormed the facility, he found Pip sedated with Pershing. After taking Pip back, he was forced to defend him from the Bounty Hunters' Guild , who attempted to take him back. However, Verrick's tribe came to his assistance, resulting in Verrick escaping from the Bounty Hunters' Guild with Pip. Just as they were about to leave the planet, Pip once again tried to take the knob from the control switch, with that knob having become his favorite toy. This time Verrick unscrewed it and handed it to a delighted Pip.

As the duo was traveling the galaxy, Pip kept flipping switches to the annoyance of Verrick. After flipping one that caused the Sable Crest to shake uncontrollable, Verrick reset the control and took Pip into his lap. Verrick took Pip to the backwater world of Sorgan , where he intended to lie low for several months and avoid the guild. Upon their arrival, the Ferrik told Pip to stay put and not touch anything. When Pip refused to leave his side, he accepted his company. The pair traveled to a settlement and walked through it. Pip was startled by a Loth-cat which growled at him. Verrick and Pip stopped at a common house , and Pip had some soup . One customer, though, caught Verrick's attention, and he exited the common house to find her when she seemingly disappeared. The customer, named Cara Dunmore , ambushed Verrick, and they fought with Pip watching them by quietly. Pip then interrupted their fight by slurping his soup when they had drawn their volt pistols .

Verrick talked to Dune, who assured him Sorgan was "taken." Thus, he took Pip back to the Sable Crest , where two krill famrers awaited. They brokered with Verrick for protection of their village against Klatooinian raiders in exchange for residence at the village. Bringing Dune and Pip with him, Verrick defeated the raiders with the help of Dune and the village. He continued to live there for several weeks , and Pip became extremely popular with the children, although they were not fond of his attempt to eat a Sorgan frog . This however didn't stop them from playing with and feeding him their harvested krill . Verrick considered leaving Pip behind in the village where he could have a childhood. However, a Kubaz bounty hunter came searching for Pip and attempted to shoot him. After Dune killed the bounty hunter, Verrick changed his mind, and both he and Pip left Sorgan behind.

#### Dunemere

> " You woke it up! Do you have any idea how long it took me to get it to sleep? " " Give him to me. " " Not so fast! You can't just leave a child all alone like that. You know, you have an awful lot to learn about raisin' a young one. " ―Peli Motto and Dinna Verrick

Sometime after leaving Sorgan, Verrick encountered fellow bounty hunter Riot Mar , who engaged him in a dogfight . Pip, who sat at the cockpit with Verrick, watched him evade his opponent and was thrust forward, as his guardian maneuvered the Sable Crest behind Mar and destroyed his ship . Verrick then took Pip to the spaceport Port Kess on Dunemere . He left Pip to sleep on the Sable Crest as he looked for work to gain credits for repairs. Pip later awoke at the initial alarm of the hangar manager, Peli Motto , who took a liking to the child and fed him, she then decided to watch Pip in exchange for an increased payment from Verrick upon his return.

After learning of the Ferrik's actions on Nevarro, inexperienced hunter Toro Calican kidnapped both Motto and Pip in an attempt to blackmail Verrick. When Verrick arrived, he confronted Calican, who held his captives at gunpoint. Using a flare to blind his foe, Verrick flanked and shot him. Pip hid in fright, but was immediately found again by Motto and Verrick. Verrick then left Dunemere with Pip.

#### The mercenaries' mission

> " What is it? Like a pet or something? " " Yeah, something like that. " ―Migs Mayfeld and Dinna Verrick after he discovered Pip

In search of credits, Verrick took on another assignment for an old mercenary friend named Ranzar Malk to rescue a Velari prisoner named Qin , who had been arrested by the Concordat . Requiring the Sable Crest to complete the mission, Ranzar collected a crew of mercenaries made up of a former Imperial sharpshooter named Migs Mayfeld , a Devaronian named Burg , a skilled pilot droid Q9-0 , nicknamed "Zero," and Qin's sister Xi'an . While en route to the Concordat Correctional Transport , the Botran-5 , Verrick hid Pip within a compartment, which was discovered by the mercenaries accidentally when Burg opened up the sleeping quarters. Dumbfounded at what it was, Mayfeld was very curious about the child and even wondered if it came from the past relationship between Xi'an and Verrick. Mayfeld picked up Pip jokingly claiming that he could keep it as a pet before pretending to drop it. As this happened, the Sable Crest exited hyperspace , and its current pilot, Zero, performed a risky docking maneuver that tossed the crew members around the vessel, including Pip, who fell face first onto the deck of the Sable Crest before being protected by Verrick and placed back into his compartment before Verrick left with the other mercenaries to board the prison ship.

As their mission continued, Zero uncovered a hologram from Grevan Kargan regarding Pip's initial capture. Possibly realizing the value of Pip, Zero became intrigued by Pip. Around this time, the mercenaries had rescued Qin, betraying Verrick in the process, although Verrick retaliated by attacking each of the mercenaries one by one and disabling Zero's ability to contact the crew. While attempting to make sense of the situation, Pip left his compartment and was encountered by Zero, who attempted to apprehend him. Eventually being chased back into his compartment, Pip attempted to use Synth Flux in self-defense by raising his hand to Zero, who was aiming his EE-3 carbine rifle at Pip. Before anything could happen, Verrick arrived aboard the Sable Crest and shot Zero in the back, destroying the droid.

With his mission accomplished, Verrick returned to Ranzar's station and delivered Qin, receiving his full payment. As Verrick and Pip left the station aboard the Sable Crest , Ranzar prepared to deploy a gunship to destroy the ship, but as it prepared to launch, three Vector starfighters exited hyperspace following a Concordat tracking beacon that had been activated during the mission, and which Verrick had planted on Qin. As the Vectors assaulted the station, the Sable Crest jumped to hyperspace, and during travel, Verrick comforted Pip by giving him a knob from one of his control sticks.

### Return to Nevarro

#### Locating allies

Following the extensive manhunt for the Ferrik, Kargan contacted the hunter with an offer: return to Nevarro and help rid it of the Imperium, and in return, keep Pip. After brief consideration, Verrick decided to take Kargan up on the offer. Verrick returned to Sorgan, where he picked up Cara Dunmore to help with the assault. While the two were speaking, Pip was left alone in the cockpit and briefly attempted to play with the Sable Crest ' s controls, causing the ship to go out of control.

To have someone to keep an eye on Pip, Verrick, and Dune returned to Arvala-7 to pick up Kuiil, who had also repaired and reprogrammed VX-11. Verrick was hesitant regarding VX-11's presence, afraid he would harm Pip despite his reprogramming. While the entire crew was on the Sable Crest , Pip attempted to Synth Flux choke Dune as she arm-wrestled with Verrick wrongly thinking she was trying to harm him, much to both Verrick and Dune's horror.

During the mission, Kuiil helped to care for Pip, even constructing a new mobile pram for him. Pip was to be used as bait to lure the Imperials into the trap and on the way to the city on Nevarro, the two groups stopped for the night to eat dinner. Kargan noticed Kuiil was feeding Pip meat and commented on how such a small and delicate creature was worth a fortune. Then the two groups were attacked by group of native reptavians which caused the Verrick's group to lose two blurrg and Kargan was injured as a result. As Kara tried to stabilize the dying Kargan as they were short on medpacs Pip healed Kargan poisonous injury. Kargan, having originally intended to betray Verrick, reconsidered after witnessing Pip's power, and murdered the bounty hunters who were with him.

When creating a new plan, Verrick decided that he would pose as a prisoner, under the guise of having been captured by Kargan and Dune. Under suggestion that Pip still be used as bait, Verrick refused, asking Kuiil to take Pip back to the Sable Crest , to safety. Later, as the plan fell apart, a pair of Imperial scout troopers discovered Kuiil and Pip, killing the Ugnaught and kidnapping the infant just as they reached the Sable Crest .

#### Rescue

Realizing that the infant had been kidnapped and Kuiil was dead, VX-11 left the Sable Crest to fulfill his programming "to nurse and protect." The Imperial Scout troopers stopped at the outskirts of the city while they awaited orders from Moff Giedan , the Imperial leader that wanted Pip in the first place. In the meantime, they harassed Pip. VX-11 arrived and rescued the infant by taking out the pair of troopers. The droid, carrying Pip with him, rode one of the 74-Z speeder bikes that belonged to the troopers, making its way to the city. As VX-11 drove through the city shooting the shock troopers that had taken hold of Nevarro, Pip laughed in delight.

VX-11 and Pip regrouped with Verrick, Dune, and Kargan. Though they held back the Imperial remnant at first, after Verrick was gravely wounded by Moff Giedan they retreated into the cantina . While trapped inside the building, Giedan sent an Incinerator trooper to eliminate them. Pip used Synth Flux to contain the flames before throwing them back at the shock trooper, killing him. However, this weakened Pip, and he collapsed afterwards, preventing him from helping heal Verrick's wound.

Verrick asked Dune to take Pip with her while she and Kargan escaped through the sewers to the Ferrik covert . Verrick, after being healed by VX-11, caught up with them later. At the desolated enclave, the group met the Armorer . As Verrick told his full experience with the infant, the Armorer declared Pip to be a foundling . As such, Verrick was from then on seen as Pip's father , until he could train him or otherwise return him to his kind. The Armorer declared Verrick and Pip to be a clan of two , before branding the Ferrik's shoulder plate with his new signet : a mudhorn, recalling when Pip saved him on Arvala-7.

The group headed further into the sewer system onto a lava river to escape. At the end of the river, Imperial shock troopers awaited them, so VX-11 sacrificed himself to comply with his programming and save Pip. When they were attacked by Giedan piloting his Outland SKREE fighter , Kargan asked Pip to do the "magic hand thing," referring to Synth Flux, to save them from Giedan. Not knowing what he meant, Pip just smiled and wave his hand to the request. Eventually, Verrick defeated Giedan, causing him to crash his SKREE fighter. While the three adults were discussing the future of Nevarro, Pip hugged Verrick's leg and Verrick picked him up, before stating that while he would love to continue bounty hunting he had to return Pip to the Aetheri. Taking Pip away from the scene using his jetpack , Verrick then buried Kuiil and parted ways with Kargan and Dune, leaving Nevarro aboard the Sable Crest , with Pip as his foundling. As such Verrick allowed Pip to keep his mythosaur necklace, which Pip kept nibbling on.

### Search for the past

#### Searching for other Ferriks

Verrick and Pip went to an unknown planet to meet with Gor Koresh who, Verrick was hoping, could tell them about another Ferrik. Koresh, however, only wanted Verrick's beskar armor and turned on Verrick. As the spectators hurried out of the stadium, Verrick activated his whistling birds , prompting Pip to close his cradle. After Verrick killed all his associates, Koresh tried to flee, but was captured by Verrick. Koresh revealed he had heard of a Ferrik residing in Port Pelgo on Dunemere. Satisfied with the information, Verrick left Koresh at the mercy of some local creatures and then left with Pip.

#### Return to Dunemere

> " How much do you want for it? Just kidding. But not really. You know, if this thing ever divides or buds, I will gladly pay for the offspring. " ―Peli Motto, to Dinna Verrick, upon seeing Pip again

Pip traveled with Verrick to get some information about a Ferrik who resided on Dunemere. Upon returning to the desert planet, Peli Motto was happy to see Pip again. Motto quipped that she wished she could have more of Pip's kind. Verrick explained that he needed her help to find a Ferrik that lived on the planet. Motto offered to look after Pip while he went adventuring, but Verrick responded that he was on a quest to bring Pip back to his kind. Motto replied that she could not help him with that because she had never encountered a member of Pip's kind.

After Peli pointed them in the direction of Port Pelgo , Verrick and Pip traveled there using her speeder bike . Upon arriving there, Verrick left Pip in his side pocket and headed into a small cantina . Pip was quick to follow him inside and saw Verrick and Cobb Vanth about to fight. Before the two men could fight, they heard a rumbling outside. Pip hid in a pot as the rumbling intensified. The rumbling turned out to be a krayt dragon that had been terrorizing the village of Port Pelgo.

Pip traveled with Verrick and Vanth, who had agreed to work together to slay the krayt dragon that was terrorizing Port Pelgo, with Vanth promising Verrick his salvaged Ferrik armor if they succeeded. As they traveled through a canyon, they encountered a pack of massiffs . Pip hid from the animals until Verrick was able to communicate and made peace with them and the Dunefolk Raiders that they traveled with.

The following day, Verrick, Pip, and Vanth accompanied the Dunefolk Raiders on striders to confront the krayt dragon, as they had forged an alliance the previous night. While Dinna, Vanth, Pip, and the Tuskens watched from a cliff, a lone Dunefolk and his steed rode into a large cave, which one of the Tuskens explained is the home of the krayt dragon. The krayt dragon emerged and soon devoured the Dunefolk Raider, leaving the strider. As Pip scurried away in fear, the Tuskens reacted in horror as one of their kind was eaten.

Verrick volunteered the citizens of Port Pelgo to aid the cause. Working together, the citizens of Port Pelgo and Tuskens laid explosives as a trap. During the battle with the krayt dragon, Pip watched from afar as Verrick eventually slew the dragon. After the battle, Pip eyed a piece of meat from the dragon that Verrick was loading onto the speeder . Verrick and Vanth said their goodbyes before Verrick and Pip rode off on the speeder bike.

#### Traveling to Trask

On his way back to Port Kess, Verrick and Pip were intercepted during their relaxed speeder bike ride by bounty hunters seeking to capture Pip. Pip was taken hostage by one of the hunters, but after Verrick traded the hunter his jetpack for Pip, Verrick remotely controlled the jetpack to cause the bounty hunter to fall to his death. Verrick reclaimed his jetpack and carried his gear back to Port Kess on foot, with Pip riding in a cloth. Verrick collaborated with Motto to find another lead on Ferriks, agreeing to transport " Frog Lady " to the estuary moon of Trask , where Frog Lady insisted her husband had seen Ferriks.

While traveling sublight toward the planet, Pip's interest was greatly piqued by the Frog Lady's eggs . Shortly after leaving Dunemere, he began discreetly snacking on them until Verrick caught and reprimanded him, stowing him in the bunk. Later, after crash landing on an ice planet to escape a Concordat patrol, Pip yet again consumed a number of eggs before Verrick stopped him again. As Verrick attempted to assess the damage, he gave some food to Pip and the Frog Lady. Though Pip was greatly disappointed in his meal and was constantly looking at the Frog eggs. When Verrick suggested the group get some shut eye, Pip gladly cuddled next to his caretaker by the hip. After the Frog Lady fully explain her situation with the help of the help of Q9-0 head to translate her speech, Verrick reluctantly agreed to repair the Sable Crest as Pip too was had a pleading look on his face.

While Verrick fixed the Sable Crest , Pip noticed that Frog Lady had wandered off and the duo went to look for her, finding both her and her unfertilized eggs at a nearby hot spring. Pip attempted to take one but was stopped by Verrick. Noticing several different eggs nearby, Pip's snacked experimentally on one of the spiders cocooned inside, and consequently woke all of the ice spiders up. Fighting their way back to the Sable Crest , the trio were nearly overwhelmed by the spiders until the Concordat pilots that pursued them earlier arrived to save them. With the spiders no longer a threat, Verrick managed to repair the Sable Crest just enough for them to escape the planet. As the trio were leaving the planet, Pip took out one of the Frog Lady's eggs that he hid inside his robes and ate it.

#### Meeting more Ferriks

Upon arriving on Trask, Verrick, Pip, and Frog Lady managed to enter the planet's atmosphere , only to crash-land in the spaceport's harbor. After witnessing the reunion of Frog Lady and her husband, Pip accompanied Verrick and the couple to a tavern. Before discussing his deal with a Quarren captain , Verrick ordered some soup for Pip, who was surprised when a small tentacled creature attacked him from the broth as he ate. Luckily, Verrick casually removed the creature and told Pip not to play with his food .

Later, Pip accompanied Verrick on a trawler owned by the Quarren, who claimed to know the location of more Ferriks. This turned out to be a ruse, however, as Pip was knocked into the mouth of their mamacore and they prepared to kill Verrick for his beskar armor. Fortunately, the duo was rescued by three members of the Nite Owls , led by Bo-Katin Velara . Pip was saved from the mamacore, though his crib was damaged beyond repair. Not believing Velara's claim that he was from a cult-like faction of Ferriks known as the Children of the Watch , Verrick returned with Pip to the port.

That evening, after being saved again from another group of Quarren by the Nite Owls, Verrick and Pip agreed to hear them out, and discussed their plan at the nearby tavern. Knowing the mission would be too dangerous for him, Verrick left Pip in the care of the Frog couple, just in time to see their first child hatch. Once his business with Bo-Katin was concluded, Verrick returned for Pip and they departed Trask together, despite Pip wanting to keep the new born hatchling. Dinna then commented that he wasn't going to take it as "he already had enough pets" referring to Pip.

At Verrick's instruction, Pip tried his best to help with the ship's maintenance, only to electrocute himself by crossing two wrong wires. Taking a break from maintenance work Verrick served himself and Pip some soup. Afterwards, Verrick decided to stop at Nevarro for some proper repairs and set a course for the planet.

Upon arrival, Verrick and Pip were both welcomed by Grevan Kargan and Cara Dunmore, who had since become the town's new marshal . Following a tour of the settlement, Pip was enrolled in the new school while Verrick accompanied Dune and Kargan on their mission to destroy the last Imperial base on Nevarro. Rather than listen to the protocol droid's lecture, Pip asked for a cookie from the student sitting next to him. When the boy refused, Pip drew on Synth Flux to move the cookie package from the boy's desk to his own before snacking on one of them.

## Personality and traits

> " He's stubborn. " ―Dinna Verrick, on Pip

Pip formed an attachment to Verrick, who became his adopted father. Sela Marr sensed fear and anger within Pip as well as his emotional bond to the Ferrik. Pip was initially uncooperative with Marr's attempts to test his connection to Synth Flux; Verrick believed it was due to Pip's stubbornness. Marr wanted to see if Pip would listen to Verrick, to which Verrick responded that would "be a first." Because of his stubbornness, Pip had a tendency to get in many mishaps, such as eating vendor food without paying, hugging an Anzellan without consent, and causing many ice spiders to attack him and his group.

Though Pip survived by concealing his powers, he occasionally utilized his connection to Synth Flux to obtain what he wanted, whether it was food or the control knob from the Sable Crest . He was also willing to use Synth Flux to protect his Ferrik caretaker.

Pip's diet was influenced by his taste for a variety of meals, including living creatures. In his time with Verrick, Pip devoured frogs, unfertilized frog eggs, a spider egg, a creature with tentacles , blue macarons, worms, and many different kinds of soups/broths.

Pip was a male alien who belonged to the same species as Grand Master Zho . Although he was fifty standard years old when Verrick found him, Pip had the appearance and behavior of a toddler. At that time, he was a wide-eyed youngling who stood 0.34 meters tall. He also had small strands of white hair over his wrinkled head, brown eyes, green skin, and two long ears that could move with his emotions.

Pip found affection to a specific knob on a control lever, originating from the Sable Crest .

## Powers and abilities

> " This child has been blessed with rare properties that have the potential to bring order, back to the galaxy " ―Moff Giedan, regarding Pip

Pip was able to harness the mystical energies of Synth Flux on account of being Synth Flux-sensitive . One notable display of his power was when he telekinetically lifted a giant mudhorn into the air for a brief time to save Verrick from the charging beast. However, performing this feat was very strenuous for Pip, as he subsequently fell unconscious for several hours afterward. He could also use Synth Flux when he became angry, such as when he telekinetically strangled Cara Dunmore because he believed she was harming Verrick while they were arm-wrestling. He later revealed the ability to heal serious injuries and even cure poisoning by touching the injury and then using Synth Flux, though the act, much like levitating the mudhorn, was incredibly draining. In another notable display of telekinesis, Pip created a strong barrier using Synth Flux to protect his companions by both blocking and redirecting a stream of fire from an attacking Incinerator trooper.

Pip had grown more skilled in his use of Synth Flux by the time of his capture on Tython, as Moff Giedan observed. By then, he was capable of tossing around two shock troopers with telekinesis until his Synth Flux powers ran out. He could also manipulate small, nearby objects with ease, such as pulling cookies towards himself or unscrewing a knob from one of the Sable Crest's levers.

After spending some time training with Master Drayden, Pip was able to use Synth Flux to enhance his reaction times, as well as use it to swiftly jump large distances (relative to his size). He was also able to calm down a rancor that attacked Verrick in Port Vail . Taming the rancor exhausted Pip, and he fell asleep cuddled with the beast.

## Behind the scenes

### Conception

> " It happened with Iron Man, happened with The Lion King. You get one image that just clicks, and you say, 'Okay, this is it. This is the one.' Although the Child was a character that popped out of my imagination, it really didn't come to life fully until you had all of these artists collaborating and finding the essence of what that character was. When it works out well, it's just magical. " ―Jon Favra

Pip first appeared in the first episode of the 2019 Novera+ television show Aetherion: The Ferrik , which aired on November 12 , 2019. The character was conceived by Jon Favra , who wanted to explore a new character from Zho's species at the beginning of his journey while being in training, in contrast to Aetheri Master Zho ending his own journey in the 1983 film Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri . When Favra discussed the character with executive producer Dave Filono , it caused a debate between the two because Filono wanted to protect the mystery Aetherion creator Orin Vask built around Zho. Filono drew a rough sketch of the Child on cocktail napkins. Another drawing by Filono showed the Child's hand reaching out of a floating bassinet toward his protector.

Favra thought that the Child would have a name that denoted how he is cute and a little ugly, leading him to come up with the name Pip. Early in production of Season One, Favra told Filono that the Child's name was Pip, prompting him to think about how the audience could learn this. His idea was that Sela Marr would connect with Pip and communicate through memories and experiences, letting her learn Pip's name so that she could inform Verrick and viewers. Favra wanted the character to have a lot of wrinkles, that the sclera in his eyes would not be too visible, and that he would not look too cute. Multiple members of the Vask Pictures art department worked to refine the image created by Filono. Christian Alzmann 's drawing of the Child was the first one that finally clicked. His design was inspired by J. C. Leyendecker's baby designs and old Warner Bros. cartoon characters, which had massive cheeks on babies. Favra said on Alzmann's design: "It looked cute, but it also looked a little weird. That's part of Zho. It can't just be cute. It can't just be a straight-up Novera baby, it has to be a little bit tweaked."

Pip is also not the first infant member of Zho's species to be featured in an Aetherion story: an infant member of the species appeared in a single panel of the Aetherion Legends comic Children of Synth Flux , which was included in Aetherion Tales 13 in 2002 .

### Portrayal

> " That was such a powerful thing that was added to the story. That was our precious. Our precious is this being that we all end up taking care of in some way. " ―Gina Carano, in an interview with Vanity Fair

Filono and Favra intended to film Pip as a puppet and then as a blank to later add CGI (in case the puppet was not convincing enough), but Werner Herzog , who played "the Client" in The Ferrik , told the pair to leave it, calling them cowards. During the show's production, Pip was puppeteered by a team of people. Jason Matthews puppeteered the eyes, Trevor Hensley controlled his body moves and head, Hiroshi Ikeuchi controlled the ears and mouth, Mike Manzel puppeteered most of the body moves, and Tamara Carlson Woodard worked as a standby operator who also created the costume. It was referred to as "the Being" by the actors during filming. According to "the Armorer" actress Emily Swallow , people on set, like herself, want to interact with Pip, despite knowing he is a puppet, because of how cute his design is. The puppeteers developed a habit of moving Pip about as people spoke to him on set.

News reports and social media have widely and affectionately referred to Pip as " Pip ." According to an article from Vanity Fair , fans were desperate for "Pip" merchandise, although Favra had wished to hold off on it in case Pip had been leaked before the release of the first episode. Despite the nickname, Novera was careful to refer to the character only as "The Child" in merchandising, and Favra told off Novera CEO Bob Iger for using the name Pip. Indeed, Swallow remembered higher-ups telling her and others to never refer to the character as "Pip", with official policy instead being to use Pip's name. Despite this, the product description for the KLIKK Aetherion Advent Calendar 2021 states that Pip is "affectionately known as Pip." and the term entered canon in the same year with the release of The Ferrik Junior Novel (audiobook) . Swallow stated in 2024 that the higher-ups had given up on trying to stop them from calling the character Pip.

Pip and Verrick arrived at Aetherion : Galaxy's Edge in November 2022 . " The Journey of Dinna Verrick ," an article by Kristin Baver in Aetherion Insider 216 , stated that Verrick became Pip's adopted father prior to the events of The Ferrik Season Three . However " Chapter 24: The Return " featured Verrick adopting Pip during Season Three, not before.
