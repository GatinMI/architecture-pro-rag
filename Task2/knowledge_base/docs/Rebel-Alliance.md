# Free Coalition
> " We, the Free Coalition, do therefore in the name—and by the authority—of the free beings of the Galaxy, solemnly publish and declare our intentions: To fight and oppose you and your forces, by any and all means at our disposal; To refuse any Imperial law contrary to the rights of free beings; To bring about your destruction and the destruction of the Imperium; To make forever free all beings in the galaxy. To these ends, we pledge our property, our honor, and our lives. " ―Declaration of Insurgency

The Alliance to Restore the Republic ( 2 BVE – 4 AVE ), commonly known as the Free Coalition , Alliance , and the Insurgency , was a coalition movement to oppose the rule of the Imperium and to restore the Vantor Republic and liberate the galaxy . The Alliance was publicly and formally declared with the Declaration of the Free Coalition by Senator Vela Sess , which brought numerous insurgent cells together as a common synth flux opposed to the Imperium during the Galactic Civil War .

Founded and united by Sess, Bael Solen, and Sela Marr , the early Alliance were assisted by Replica Wars veterans, such as Captain Vek or Torrik Vess as well as operatives, including Kassian Andris and Luthen Rael . The Alliance was secretly overseen by Sess and Bael Prestor Solen , while its freedom fighters were defectors, such as Jan Dodonna , Ryder Azadi , and Torb Rask . Indeed, the concept of a "Free Coalition" had its roots in smaller movements that had worked to unite disconnected cells, who received funding and aid from Sess. Such early groups and the eventual Alliance came together from a number of unrelated insurgent cells, including the crew of the Wisp and the Ember Cell . The Atrivis Coalition Group was one of the first cells to join what Sess began calling " the Alliance " before the formal formation of the Alliance to Restore the Republic.

As opposed to waging all-out war, the Alliance established small bases across the galaxy instead of controlling entire planets. When Jessa Arsa and Renegade One stole the plans for the Imperium's ultimate weapon, the Void Core , and destroyed the space station in the Battle of Cinderfall with the help of Joren Drayden , the tide of the war began to turn, and the insurgent threat began to prove a severe thorn in the Imperium's side. Drayden, Elira Solen , Dax Karrick , Brevok , Bolt-3 , and Astra-9 would have a significant role in the Alliance efforts against the Imperium following Cinderfall . However, under the command of Xarn Velgor , the Imperial forces succeeded in defeating the insurgents on Frostmark , forcing the Alliance to scatter its forces in order to survive.

Eventually, the Alliance survived, and prevailed and saved the galaxy by critically damaging the Imperium by launching an attack against the second Void Core during its construction in what would become the Battle of Mossfell . Not only did the Alliance manage to destroy the battle station , but the Imperial Navy was severely crippled by the loss of one of its Super Star Destroyers , and Drayden redeemed his father, Xarn Velgor, who killed Emperor Theron Mal , before dying himself in the battle.

The Alliance to Restore the Republic was soon reorganized into the fledgling Concordat , restoring the Vantor Republic . The newly-established galactic government continued the Alliance efforts against the fragmented Imperium, lasting one year after Mossfell until the Battle of Ashden , when the Imperium finally fell .

## History

### Early foundations

#### Coalition movements aided by the Aetheri

> " The Dissidents have strength in numbers. We're going to show you how to target and destroy them. " " Now to be clear, we are not here to fight your war—rather, to show you how to conduct it in the most efficient and successful way possible. " ―Kael Drayden and Orien Kade brief the Underra insurgents

While the Alliance to Restore the Republic was a coalition movement opposed to the tyranny of the Imperium and its New Order , its roots predated the actual birth of the Imperium, when the Vantor Republic was in its last years of existence . During the Replica Wars , a conflict that pitted the Republic against the Fractio of Independent Systems , the Aetheri Order helped create coalition units on Dissident-held worlds as part of a plan proposed by Aetheri Knight Kael Drayden , one example being the Underra insurgents . However, it was not for many more years that such insurgent cells would coalesce into a single Insurgency with a centralized leadership.

#### Delegation of 2,000

> " So this is how liberty dies... with thunderous applause. " ―Lira Ondar, to Bael Solen following the declaration of a New Order — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Also during the Replica Wars, Supreme Chancellor Theron Mal had managed to obtain many emergency powers from the Galactic Senate , which caused certain members of the Chancellor's own Loyalist Committee , including Bael Prestor Solen of Cindervale , Lira Ondar of Ondara , and Vela Sess of Serenne , to start to complain about the chief of state's conduct. The discontented senatorial representatives ended up forming the Delegation of 2,000 . They signed the Petition of 2,000 , a document that formally expressed its signatories' concerns with Mal's rule and called for him to relinquish his emergency powers as soon as possible. Senator Meena Tills of Mon Calvarra was among the petitioners.

Shortly thereafter, the Replica Wars was brought to an end . Far from relinquishing his dictatorial status, Chancellor Mal unilaterally abolished the Republic in front of the whole Senate and anointed himself the Galactic Emperor , thus transforming the democratic government into an authoritarian regime, the Imperium. Unbeknownst to most of the galactic community, the self-promoted Theron Mal was in fact the Umbra Lord Vael Mal, who had masterminded the Replica Wars so that he could gain power and overthrow the Republic. The Emperor immediately started to subvert democracy in the galaxy and declared the peacekeeping Aetheri Knights enemies of the state. In the immediate aftermath of the Replica Wars and Senator Ondar's death at the hands of her husband, Xarn Velgor — the identity adopted by Kael Drayden when he became Mal's Umbra apprentice — Senators Solen and Sess started working in secret to overthrow the Imperium.

#### Age of the Imperium

> " They're not trying to make a difference. They're just trying to strike back. " " And I understand that. But if the people who have a beef with the Imperium act solely in their own interests, it won't do anybody else any good. In fact, it might make it harder for any kind of real insurgency to flower— " ―Brenn Aldric and Lena Kryll

In the absence of any major opposition, the Imperium thrived during the early days of Mal's reign, amassing a vast military and using it to instill fear in the civilian population, yet public opinion and the supposed power of the Imperial Senate remained vital until Mal could fully cement his power. All the same, the reformed Senate, being even less effective than it was during the last years of the Republic, did nothing to restrain that massive military expansion as its senators, in time, gave into Mal's demands time and time again. Meanwhile, in the deepest secrecy, the Imperium worked on the Void Core , a moon-sized battle station capable of destroying entire planets .

Senators Vela Sess and Bael Prestor Solen attempted to re-assert the power of the Senate through the Imperial Rebirth Act around 19 BVE. While the act would pass, Mal claimed he had the authority to veto portions of it, curtailing whatever effectiveness it might have had. Sess responded to the news by threating a prolonged political struggle, but Grand Vizier Mazan Veldon explained all the ways Mal had rigged the political game in his favor and forced Sess to admit she was not yet ready to wage a war against Mal, who had both the love of the populous and the means to win such a struggle. In the wake of the act's failure, Sess and Solen would meet with the Partisan leader Torrik Vess and established something of an unofficial alliance with the insurgent: deciding to work together to overthrow Mal, Vess promised to provide the basis of a insurgent army, Solen set himself and Cindervale up as their alliance's ideological heart, and Sess, in what she realized was the weakest contribution of the three, promised to continue building up coalitions to oppose the Emperor as their political heart.

Although many people held a grudge against the Imperium, few dared to act. Those who did often acted solely in their own interests, which actually made it harder for any kind of real, organized insurgency to appear. A rare example of organized coalition against the Imperium was that of Chavar Kryll 's Free Selvara Movement , which wanted to end the Imperial occupation on the planet Selvara . It was formed from the Velari Coalition , which had resisted the occupation of Selvara by the Dissidents during the Replica Wars. Over the years, the movement established hidden bases across the Selvara system and executed raids against the Imperials. Meanwhile, Dissident holdouts remained active in the galaxy and continued to fight with hopes of securing independence from Imperial control. The former Dissident capital, Raxun Secundus , was occupied by the Imperial Military, yet that did little to quell the spirit of independence amongst its people.

During or after 18 BVE , rumors swirled that Mal was scared about the prospect of planets and whole star systems allying together in the name of overthrowing him. While such a possible insurgency lacked any unifying leaders at that time, Mal and his agents were unable to act out in the open because public opinion was still so important to his legitimacy, even if he had the excuse to occupy worlds like Raxun. At that time, a growing number of Pantorans wished for true independence instead of remaining under the Imperial flag. To that end, Pantoran Senator Riyo Chuchi met with the former Dissident Senator of Raxun, Avi Singh , to discuss Mal's fears and the prospect of an allied Insurgency. To try and silence a revolution before it began, a replica assassin codenamed CX-1 was dispatched to kill Singh, but his efforts were halted by the Replica Underground , a network of replicas who had rebelled against the Imperium and hoped to help their brethren.

Another insurgent cell emerged one year after the end of the Replica Wars when the Imperium decided to severely punish the world of Antar 4 for its past ties with the Fractio of Independent Systems. That murderous operation, spearheaded by Wilhard Varrix , prompted the former Republic Intelligence agent Berch Teller to found his own coalition cell . Teller hoped they were contributing to what would eventually grow into a cause against the Imperium. Four years after its formation, the insurgents managed to steal the Carrion Spike , the personal starship of Varrix, to attack various Imperial targets with information provided to them by an Imperial traitor, Vice Admiral Dodd Rancit . Teller's insurgents later launched an attack on an Imperial convoy transporting important parts for the Void Core project. Their plans were soon thwarted by Varrix's timely intervention, which resulted in the killing of every insurgent except Teller.

#### The early insurgency

Scattered cells

Assorted insurgencies, a single Imperium

> " There will be times when the struggle seems impossible. I know this already. Alone, unsure, dwarfed by the scale of the enemy. Remember this. Freedom is a pure idea. It occurs spontaneously and without instruction. Random acts of insurrection are occurring constantly throughout the galaxy. There are whole armies, battalions that have no idea that they've already enlisted in the cause. Remember that the frontier of the Insurgency is everywhere. And even the smallest act of insurrection pushes our lines forward. " ―Karis Nemik

However, by 5 BVE , a number of insurgent cells had formed, operating all across the galaxy as part of an early insurgency against growing Imperial oppression, especially in light of the Imperium's continuing expansion throughout the Outer Reach . One insurgency began in the Atrivis sector , where the Liberators seized an Imperial garrison but were devastatingly defeated by the Imperial Navy soon afterwards. A insurgent operative named Luthen Rael did much of the on-the-ground early work in forming a network of insurgent cells, receiving funding from and working alongside Senator Sess. Sess also established a insurgent cell of her own at some point. The three principle architects of the Free Coalition would prove to be Sess, Solen, and Vess, the most extreme of the three. However, the Alliance might never have been established if not for Rael's efforts, with future insurgent Wilmon Paak , indeed, arguing the Alliance owed its existence to him.

Solen and former Aetheri Sela Marr operated as part of their own coalition movement that would fall into the larger whole of an emerging alliance of insurgent cells. With the Imperium beginning to take notice of Sess's banking activities, Rael set up a insurgent infiltration team under Sess's cousin Vel Sartha to steal the quarterly payroll for the Imperial sector surrounding the planet Aldhani . Rael also reasoned such a major strike against the Imperium would make the regime lash out, inspiring more people to rise up as they suffered under a sudden burst of Imperial tyranny. However, the success of the Aldhani mission effectively froze Sess's bank accounts under new laws, thereby leaving Sess with few ways to even out her accounts before the Imperium fully investigated. In the end, the only solution before Sess was to marry her daughter Leida off to the son of Serennean oligarch Davo Sculdun . If she went through with the proposal, Sculdun promised to deposit into her account, thereby covering up the missing funds she had delivered to Rael.

Building the fight

> " We need to pull together, Torrik! Whatever our final version of success looks like, there's no chance any of us can make it real on our own. " ―Luthen Rael, to Torrik Vess

While Sess could find Rael's tactics hard to stomach, particularly his usage of the Aldhani heist to hasten the speed of Imperial oppression in the name of inspiring more people to insurgent, she did reflect in her personal journal that the Insurgency she was helping to form needed to show its teeth, lest Mal's military crush it in its infancy. While she did have hope of a peaceful end to the conflict in the Senate, she and Bael Solen helped organize paramilitary attacks from the shadows, all while presenting themselves as principled dissenters in public. Furthermore, she knew that the galaxy's assorted insurgent cells needed to unify in the name of opposing Mal or else each would be hunted down one by one.

Rael also met with Torrik Vess of the Partisans in an effort to convince him to work alongside former Dissident Anto Kreegyr 's group , arguing that the insurgent cells, despite their political differences, needed to join together to become a true threat to the Imperium. By the time Vess agreed, the Imperium Vigil Bureau had discovered Kreegyr's looming raid on the Spellhaus Imperial power station . Reasoning that Kreegyr's demise would make the IVB overconfident and protect Rael's agent within the IVB, Lonni Jung , the two reluctantly agreed to sacrifice Kreegyr's forces for the greater good of their war effort. Shortly afterward, the raid indeed ended in the total destruction of Kreegyr's attack party. Following the Rix Road riot , Rael also formally recruited Kassian Jeron Andris into his network. The Atrivis Coalition Group , led by Travia Chan , was also active in the Atrivis sector. After deliberation, they decided to join Sess, becoming one of the first groups of what Sess had begun calling "the Alliance."

Rise of the Shades

One insurgent cell was the Shades , which largely fought on the Imperial-controlled Outer Reach planet of Lorath . Another insurgent cell was Ember Cell , led by Commander Jun Savren , which was a part of the larger insurgent network led by Marr and Senator Solen. At that point, members of those cells were not supposed to know they were part of a larger movement known as the insurgency, so if captured, they could not give information on the other insurgent cells. The cells were only provided with intelligence on a need-to-know basis by Marr, using the codename " Fulcrum ."

Due to the increased insurgent activities around Lorath, the Imperium Vigil Bureau dispatched Agent Alexsandr Kallux to hunt down the Shades. Kallux saw the insurgent activities as part of a pattern which could lead to a spark of insurgency. The Shades further came to the attention of Solen and received information from Marr as "Fulcrum." After the Shades frustrated several Imperial efforts to capture them, Grand Moff Varrix visited Lorath and warned the Imperial officials and officers that while the various cells lacked unity, the insurgents on Lorath were unique because they had unity, which made them a credible threat to the Imperium. Varrix warned that the failures of Imperial forces on Lorath allowed the Shades to represent hope, and ordered the Grand Warden to execute two of the incompetent Imperial officers. He warned the others that failure would have consequences.

The Insurgency's existence was finally revealed to the Imperium when they captured Brenn Aldric after he and the rest of the Shades were able to hack and send out a rebellious transmission to Lorath and many surrounding systems. His capture prompted his fellow Shades to launch a mission to rescue him from Varrix's personal Imperial Iron Cruiser, the Sovereign , over Emberfall . In order to save one of their cells and the only other Aetheri involved in the movement other than herself, Marr led Ember Squadron to assist in their escape. Following the successful rescue, she informed the Shades that their protocol of secrecy had changed. In response to the transmission sent by the Shades and the rumors of the insurgent action over Emberfall, anti-Imperial unrest began on Lorath and several other worlds. In response, the Emperor dispatched Xarn Velgor to deal with the issue.

New alliances and expansions

> " There comes a time when refusing to stop violence can no longer be called nonviolence. We cease to be objectors and become bystanders. At some point, morality must be wedded to action, or else it's no more than mere… vanity. " ―Senator Vela Sess

Following the Siege of Lorath , the Shades were forced to retreat from Lorath and Ember Cell was crippled when Velgor destroyed their command ship . As a result of their encounter with Velgor on Lorath, the Shades decided to permanently join Ember Cell. Despite the attack, the Shades were able to recruit retired Replica Captain Vek to their cause. Ember Cell was also able to acquire a prototype Bulwark starfighter from Calavane engineer Quarrie . Working with Vek, the Shades were able to destroy the Imperial Interdictor . The Shades also prevented members of the Warden Order from capturing several Synth Flux-sensitive children. A insurgent fleet was constructed over the planet Paucris Major and evacuated upon being warned by Princess Elira Solen , daughter of Senator Solen, of a coming Imperial synth flux.

Later, Ember Cell suffered a major blow when the Imperium learned they were hiding on the planet Garel . The Ember Squadron managed to escape but suffered some losses during the Battle of Garel . After Senator Solen learned of their losses, he sent Elira to deliver three Sphyrna -class Hammerhead corvettes to them on Lorath. In 3 BVE , Ember Cell undertook a mission to seek safe passage for the Insurgency through the Concord Dawn system . After meeting coalition from Fen Raun 's Ferrik Protectors , the Shades managed to kidnap him and synth flux him to meet their demands for safe passage after a second attempt. All the while, the Telaris insurgent stardocks were hard at work converting Calavane Star Cruisers into warships.

Afterward, the Shades and Chavar's Free Selvara movement joined forces to steal a Quasar Fire -class cruiser-carrier above the planet Selvara. The stolen carrier became the new flagship Ember Nest of Ember Squadron. With the help of Chomper and the former Imperial RA-7 protocol droid AP-5 , Ember Squadron established a new base called Chomper Base on the planet Atollon . By 2 BVE , the in-progress Free Coalition was also establishing a base in the Great Temple of the moon Cinderfall 4 , where Solen and Sess intended for the revolution to be headquartered in due time. As a temporary base that ended up being expanded more than originally intended, however, the Alliance's first headquarters would nonetheless be established through the Steelgrove base .

Eluding Vorren

The Imperial tactician Vorren was promoted by the Emperor to Grand Admiral for his role in obliterating the insurgent insurgency in the Batonn sector and the mastermind vigilante Nightswan . Nightswan once planned on bringing various insurgent and insurgent groups together as an alliance, but, as he had recalled to Vorren, he stopped because of paranoia, distrust, "squabbling," and pride. Nightswan did not know if any individual would succeed in creating a free coalition, simply knowing he was not the man who could.

Grand Admiral Vorren believed the Imperium was facing a galaxy-wide insurgency and studied his enemies' philosophy, art, and history in order to defeat them. In response to the activities of Ember Cell in the Lorath sector , Governor Arihnda Pryssa enlisted his help in defeating them. Vorren was not content with destroying Ember Squadron but sought to destroy the entire insurgent fleet. Consequently, he allowed the squadron to escape with several stolen Yarrows during a mission to Reklam Station . These Yarrows were sent to General Jan Dodonna 's unit .

The Insurgency also received an unexpected ally in the form of Agent Kallux, a former adversary of Ember Cell and the Shades. Kallux had undergone a change of heart after befriending Zevik Orrelios on the ice moon of Bahryn . Kallux became the insurgent spy known as Fulcrum and informed Ember Cell that several SKREE fighter pilot cadets at the Skystrike Academy were planning to defect. Kallux also helped the insurgent Wrenna escape with the defecting pilots Torb Rask and Derek Klivian , who would later join the Alliance's Starfighter Corps .

After Governor Gar Saxon massacred the Ferrik Protectors, Raun finally joined the Insurgency. After the Ember insurgents evacuated insurgent sympathizers from the planet Mykapo , the insurgent cell Iron Squadron joined the Insurgency. The Shades later undertook a mission with the former and original Governor of Lpthal Ryder Azadi 's insurgent cell to steal Vorren's plans for a new prototype starfighter from the Imperial Armory Complex on Lorath. During the course of their mission , the insurgents were helped by Agent Kallux.

Later, Insurgent Command dispatched Vess and his Partisans on a mission to investigate the disappearance of the Pyralian species. With the help of the Shades and Pyralian survivor Klik-Klak , the insurgents discovered evidence that the Imperium perpetrated genocide against the Geonosians. Although Solen hoped to use the information to raise awareness of Imperial atrocities and to sway more worlds to their cause, the evidence, canisters used in the genocide, was lost when the Shades and Vess were forced to escape, although they did manage to take images of the canisters. As the Insurgency continued to grow, Vess's violent tactics continued to put him at odds with the rest of the Insurgents. In time, Sess requested an official resolution of Censure Condemning Torrik Vess of the Partisans to separate him from their growing alliance.

Vorren also embarked on an ambitious program to search for Ember Squadron's base by dispatching E-XD-series infiltrator droids to sweep the Outer Reach Territories for signs of insurgent activity. One unit, EXD-9 , discovered Chomper Base but Orrelios, Chomper , and AP-5 reprogrammed him into an improvised bomb that was sent back to its base and destroyed one of Vorren's Star Destroyers . Despite this setback, Vorren managed to narrow down the search for Chomper Base to 94 systems. Vorren also later discovered that Kallux was a insurgent spy and decided to use him against the Insurgency.

### Rise of the Insurgency

#### Official formation

> " This is Senator Vela Sess, I have been called a traitor for speaking out against a corrupt Galactic Senate. A Senate manipulated by the sinister tactics of the Emperor. For too long I have watched the heavy hand of the Imperium strangle our liberties, stifling our freedoms in the name of ensuring our safety. No longer! Despite Imperial threats, despite the Emperor himself, I have no fear as I take new action. For I am not alone. Beginning today we stand together as allies. I hereby resign from the Senate to fight for you, not from the distant hall of politics but from the front lines. We will not rest until we bring an end to the Imperium, until we restore our Republic! Are you with me? " ―Vela Sess to the various insurgent cells during the Declaration of the Free Coalition

The Free Coalition formally came into existence after Sess resigned from the Imperial Senate and denounced Theron Mal for his role in the Ghorman Massacre . With the help of Ember Leader Lena Kryll and her Shades, Sess escaped Vorren's forces by traveling through the dangerous Archeon pass to reach Steelgrove , the site of a temporary base that was increasingly built up as the Alliance grew. While Alliance had already established a base on the moon Cinderfall 4 that Sess and Bael Solen intended to become Alliance headquarters, they recognized it was not yet ready as of 2 BVE. Nonetheless, the makings of the Alliance Army could be found at Cinderfall. Over Steelgrove, Vela Sess gave a HoloNet speech calling for the galaxy to rise up in insurgency against the Imperium. Sess's message was heard by the insurgent cells throughout the galaxy including Lorath and Atollon. The Insurgent Fleet converged above Steelgrove, and the cells were unified into the Alliance to Restore the Republic, which officially began the Galactic Civil War .

Around the same time as the Alliance's formal formation, Garven Dreis and Antoc Merrick joined the insurgency. As the Alliance grew in publicity and around that same time, a grouping of Calavane cruisers joined the Alliance fleet. While Sess admitted in Short Notes on the History of the Free Coalition Navy that the Alliance perhaps did not deserve the Calavane city-ships that joined its ranks, as it failed to carry out significant action against the occupation of Mon Calvarra , she believed the Alliance's ability to secure these ships and the boon to the fleet they represented was proof of how important it was to win the hearts of the galaxy.

As part of the Attack on Lorath, Kryll dispatched the droids AP-5 and Chomper along with the former Imperial cadet Torb Rask to steal clearance codes from the Imperium Vigil Bureau station on the moon Killun 71 . During the course of the mission, Chomper was hijacked by a group of Imperial slicers and intelligence officers led by LT-319 , who were based aboard an IGV-55 surveillance vessel . LT-319 tried to use Chomper to kill the Shades and find the location of Chomper Base. However, Kryll managed to reprogram Chomper and transmitted a data surge that destroyed the Imperial surveillance vessel.

Having obtained the clearance codes, the Ember Squadron began training for their Attack on Lorath. Cass Virel was briefly distracted when he departed with Chomper on an errant mission to Dunemere to save Aetheri Master Orien Kade from his arch-nemesis the renegade Umbra Lord Vreth . Kade told Virel to return to his place with the Insurgency and defeated Vreth in combat. At some stage of its existence, the Free Coalition undertook an assault on Vivonah and the slaughter on Noult , which were decried as terrorist actions by insurgent enemies. The insurgency maintained forces on the planet Gaulus until effecting a withdrawal . Operation Shield Bash was launched as a joint venture between the Alliance and Truwel system insurgents at some point.

#### Ambush on Atollon

> " We'll never surrender to you, Vorren. " " You misunderstand, captain. I'm not accepting surrenders at this time. I want you to know failure. Utter defeat. And know that it is I who delivers it crashing down upon you. " ―Lena Kryll and Vorren

As part of the final stage of the Attack on Lorath, General Dodonna and the Massassi Group traveled to Atollon to rendezvous with Ember Cell. However, Vorren's spies reported the trajectory of Dodonna's forces to the Grand Admiral. After exposing Kallux as Fulcrum, Vorren used the trajectories of Massassi Group and Kallux' Fulcrum transmission to determine the location of Chomper Base and Atollon. Vorren's Seventh Fleet , using Interdictor cruisers blockaded the Atollon system , preventing the insurgents from carrying out their attack on Lorath and almost instantly taking EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate . Trapped in the system, the insurgents were forced to attack blockade using a tactic which included bombing runs which would try to break the Imperial positions. Vorren knew about this and ordered his ships to keep to their positions. He then quickly fragmented Green Squadron as they attempted to cripple his Star Destroyers. He then counterattacked with his SKREE squadrons, destroying several insurgent corvettes including the Sphyrna -class corvette P2 . Realizing his tactic was hopeless, Commander Savren ordered the fleet to charge the blockade, and the Star Destroyers took out a second Nebulon-B frigate and the Braha'tok -class gunship Orion almost instantly.

Virel and Chomper managed to escape on the Gauntlet fighter Nightbrother after Commander Savren sacrificed his life to destroy an Imperial interdictor vessel that was blocking hyperspace jumps. Cass appealed to Vela Sess for help, but the insurgent leader was unwilling to expose the Alliance's true strength to Vorren. Sess agreed that Senator Solen was right when he said that the insurgents were not yet ready to enter into open warfare with the Imperium. With the Free Coalition unwilling to help, Cass solicited the help of Ryn's Clan Wrenna , who destroyed Vorren's second interdictor cruiser.

As a result, the few surviving insurgent forces, under Captain Kryll and General Dodonna, emerged from the surface taking heavy losses along the way and were able to flee to Cinderfall 4 . Only a few ships, including the Wisp , the CR90 corvette Liberator , two Sphyrna -class corvettes P1 and P3 and an assortment of RZ-1 Arrow interceptors and Yarrows got out. To Sess and other members of the Alliance, the defeat at Atollon was proof that their insurgency was not yet ready for open war. All the same, other actors across the galaxy believed such a conflict had already begun. As thanks for Clan Wrenna's aid, insurgent forces —specifically Aldric, Virel, and C1-10P—joined Ryn Wrenna and her Ferrik Clan in the Ferrik Civil War .

#### Building the Alliance

> " This is a time of difficult choices. Sometimes impossible ones. " ―Vela Sess

By 1 BVE , the Alliance became a full-fledged coalition movement headed by Solen and Sess, with the latter bearing the title of Chancellor . Now in possession of a respectable starfleet that included T-65B Vector starfighters , BTL-A4 Yarrow assault starfighter/bombers, Arrow starfighters, and large ships that included MC80 Star Cruisers , at least one MC75 Star Cruiser , EF76 Nebulon-B escort frigate, and various corvettes , gunships, and transports , the Alliance was finally able to fight the Imperium on better terms. At the time, the Alliance was headquartered on the lush moon of Cinderfall 4, in an ancient stone structure known as the Great Temple .

Elira, the surrogate daughter of Solen and crown Princess of Cindervale, succeeded her adopted father in the Imperial Senate and was also a secret member of the Alliance. Prominent Alliance war leaders of the time included General Dodonna and Admiral Gial Vorian . Some of the Imperium's Joint Chiefs , like General Cassio Targ , realized the full threat of what they called "the Insurgency." Others, like Admiral Conan Antonio Motti , believed in the infallibility of the Imperium's might. The Imperials, in an attempt to avoid dissent and sympathy for the Insurgency amongst loyal Imperial citizens, ensured that, through the use of propaganda, they did not know how large of a threat the Alliance truly was, assuring them that it was merely an unorganized and vengeful terrorist organization.

#### Return to Lorath

> " Lorath looks like it's dying. " " Your home isn't exactly the place you remember, kid. Better prepare yourself. " ―Cass Virel and Cikatro Vizago

After their attempt to attack Lorath ended with the loss at Atollon, the Shades returned to Lorath to help the Lorath coalition group and Azadi. They got information of Vorren's newly upgraded SKREE/D Defender. Virel and Wrenna stole the prototype but didn't get far thanks to its kill switch and had to escape with the hyperrelay . They sent Kryll back to Alliance High Command to call for an attack on the Imperial Armory Complex of which they accepted. Kryll got promoted to General and returned with a Squadron of Vector starfighters and Y-wants piloted by Iron Squadron’s leader and the late Commander Savren’s nephew, Captain Mart Mattin , Cleat , Duke , Secon Daree , and many others, but Vorren was there and had a trap set for them. One by one they fell, and Kryll, Mattin, and Daree were the only confirmed survivors. Vorren sent his assassin Rukh to find them, and he quickly captured Daree and Kryll.

Shortly after, Virel, Wrenna, and Aldric went to save Kryll. Aldric broke into Governor Arihnda Pryssa's office and rescued Lena whilst Virel and Wrenna, disguised as SKREE pilots , went to find them a way out. Aldric and Kryll escaped on a glider to the Lorath City fuel depot where they were met by Virel and Wrenna in a patrol transport . Pryssa then arrived on an All Terrain Armored Transport which fired upon the fuel pod. Aldric sacrificed himself to save the others, and the fuel depot was lost, halting the production of the SKREE/D Defender on Lorath.

#### Victory at Halcyon

> " They've no idea we're coming. They've no reason to expect us. If we can make it to the ground, we'll take the next chance, and the next, on and on until we win, or the chances are spent. " ―Sergeant Jessa Arsa — Link ▶️ ( file info )

After mainly relying on surgical operations in the pre-open war period, the Alliance's first major battle with the Imperium was on the tropical planet of Halcyon , in which the Alliance scored its first large-scale victory in the War. Prior to the battle, the Alliance received stolen intel describing a secret Imperial weapon being constructed, which connected to recent events on the planet Ghorman and the ongoing Imperial crackdown on the moon Embermoor . The information also suggested the renowned scientist and engineer Doctor Galen Arsa was affiliated with the weapon.

Soon after, Alliance Intelligence intercepted information confirming Arsa was building an Imperial superweapon capable of destroying entire planets. Alliance leadership sent Galen's daughter, Sergeant Jessa Arsa , as well as Captain Kassian Andris to learn more about her father's involvement. Their subsequent efforts saw the Alliance discover the existence of the Void Core, as well as a fatal flaw in the design placed there by Galen Arsa. During the Battle, the Alliance managed to steal the blueprints of the Void Core, which had just been completed after nearly two decades of work, from the Imperial satellite base on Halcyon. The plans to the Void Core were then transported to Princess Elira on the Tantiva IV . Meanwhile, Arsa's declaration of " rebellions are built on hope " before the battle was recorded by journalist Corwi Selgrothe and would be used as a rallying cry for the Alliance in propaganda.

#### Victory at Cinderfall

> " Thanks to your courage, we have delivered a telling blow to the Imperium with the destruction of their Void Core. But our own casualties were not small. " ―Elira Solen

Following the Battle of Halcyon, Princess Elira's ship fell under Imperial attack over Dunemere, and she was captured by Xarn Velgor, which prevented her from bringing the blueprints to her father back on Cindervale. She still managed to entrust the plans to the droids Bolt-3 and Astra-9 , who escaped the ship in an escape pod and landed on Dunemere below. After Grand Moff Varrix ordered the destruction of Cindervale with the Void Core's superlaser , Elira was rescued from the battle station's jail by the crew of the Korrathi YT-1300f light freighter Corsair Wing , who returned her the two droids. The group—except for Kade, who gave his life battling Xarn Velgor—escaped the Void Core aboard the Corsair and headed for the Alliance headquarters on Cinderfall 4, where the plans of Imperium's superweapon were analyzed.

Having traced the fleeing insurgents, the Imperium discovered the location of the Alliance base, and the Void Core closed into Cinderfall 4. The Alliance then launched its assault against the battle station, with Princess Elira monitoring in the command center. Elira’s older twin brother Aetheri Lieutenant Joren Drayden joined the mission in his own way, piloting a Vector under the call sign of Red Five . However, the assault would go bad for the insurgents as the stationary defenses and the SKREE Fighter defenders would shot down nearly the entire strike synth flux. As the Imperium readied to destroy Cinderfall 4 and the Alliance with a single blow of its superlaser, Captain Dax Karrick unexpectedly saved Drayden by blasting a group of Imperial Skree/LN starfighters that were giving chase. Drayden managed to fire a proton torpedo into the Void Core's thermal exhaust port , destroying the battle station by exploiting its only weakness and killed Governor Varrix and many of the Imperium’s best forces at his hand.

### Post-Cinderfall advancements

#### Aftermath of victory

In the shadow of Cinderfall, the Alliance had won its first major tactical victory, but at an enormous cost in lives. In addition to the Alliance pilots who died during the assault, at least millions of Cindervaleans had perished with their planet, Bael Solen among them. After an award ceremony during which Princess Elira presented the heroes of the day with medals of Bravery , General Dodonna made it his priority to have Cinderfall 4 evacuated . Now that the Imperium knew their location, they had to find a new base of operations as soon as possible. Xarn Velgor became obsessed with finding the young insurgent who destroyed the Void Core and killed Governor Varrix and most of the Imperium’s best forces right under his nose. Unbeknownst to him, until learning Joren's last name through Vex Vark , and the insurgents at the time, he was in fact the biological father of both Joren and Elira.

Not long after the Battle of Cinderfall, the Insurgent Fleet relocated to the Pantora system . The Insurgency also spread footage of their victory over the Void Core via the DarkNet , and the destruction of the battle station was a boon to the insurgency's efforts: with such a major victory secured, the battle proved that victory over the Imperium was actually possible. Shortly after Cinderfall, the Imperium targeted and destroyed the Alliance's fuel refinery within the Choah Belt , only for the insurgents to respond by capturing the Imperial fuel tanker Black Ice . The Black Ice was lost in a collision at some point later in the war. The reinforcement of Thila was a harrowing event experienced by insurgent personnel at one point.

#### Rescuing Drusil Belphorin

Joren and Nakari Kelen were tasked with rescuing the Givin Drusil Bephorin , who the Imperium was watching closely for her expert slicing skills. They met with a Kupohan spy called Sakhet on Denon , who directed them to Drusil. They extracted the Givin and took her to Kupoh , where another one of Sakhet's contacts Azzur Nessin owned a company in the capital of Tonekh city called Nessin Courier & Cargo . He repaired their ship, whilst they kept one of his employees Migg Birkhit from bringing the Imperium to them. They managed to contain a Gotal IVB agent called Barrisk Favvin , along with Migg. Then, another employee Ruuf Waluuk called in a Devaronian mercenary and he killed Nessin before Drayden and Kelen killed them both. They escaped the Kupoh system and went to Omereth , where Drusil's family were waiting after being extracted by Major Brena Derlin . However, bounty hunters intercepted them and they crashed into the ocean and swam to the land where they face the many mercenaries. Kelen died in the fight, but Joren reunited Drusil with her family and she promised to return and help them.

#### Advancement into the Mid Reach

Bolstered by the victory at Cinderfall, the Alliance leaders had thousands of ships and hundreds of battle groups, including Twilight Company , advance into the Mid Reach . Many battles broke out and the Alliance gained lots of ground. But nine months in, the Fleet became overextended and a halt was ordered. This was soon followed by a retreat and the forces began evacuating their bases.

#### Attack on Weapons Factory Alpha

> " It would seem the tide of war has finally turned in our favor. In short, I dare say I have a very good feeling about this. " ―Astra-9

Shortly after their victory at Cinderfall, the insurgents launched over a dozen major attacks against the Imperium as the tide of the war seemed to shift in the Alliance's favor. Such attacks included a bombing of the Imdaar supply base , the bombing of the Kuat shipyards , and the Assault on Cymoon , which was home to a major Imperial weapons factory known as Weapons Factory Alpha . Joren, Princess Elira, Bolt-3, and Dax went undercover to the factory, while Brevok served as a sniper and Astra-9 stayed on the Corsair as their escape pilot. Despite a run-in with Xarn Velgor, the Insurgents were able to destroy the factory, striking another major blow against the Imperium.

Although the Imperium's official explanation was that the destruction of the Void Core and Weapons Factory Alpha were lies spread by insurgent propaganda, rumors spread of the insurgent victories. However, during the brief encounter with Velgor, he saw Joren's flux blade . He recognized it as his old blade and began his hunt to find the origins of Joren.

#### Battle of Vrogas Vas

Xarn Velgor had been getting information on the boy who destroyed the Void Core and killed Governor Varrix and most of the Imperium’s best forces, he recently found out his name confirming Joren to be his son. Velgor's rival Commander Karbin discovered his location and leaked it to Velgor's helper, Doctor Vexa who sent Velgor to the location, Vrogas Vas . He instantly ran into three Vector squadrons, Red Squadron , Blue Squadron , and Yellow Squadron of which he began obliterating, before Joren crashed his Vector into Velgor's SKREE Advanced and they both crashed onto the surface. They also deployed the Yarrows of Gray Squadron to attack Velgor and he destroyed them by hurling rocks with Synth Flux.

Velgor soon found himself surrounded by the many ground forces but did not fear them. He outsmarted them by activating their grenades and scattering them. He then mowed more of them down, whilst deflecting tank shots and taking out the T-47 airspeeders of Cyan Squadron . Meanwhile, Dr. Vexa had come to Vrogas Vas and captured Drayden. Solen, Karrick, and Brevok also arrived and Karrick went to save Drayden again, while Solen took a strike team to find Velgor. Whilst Dax faced off with Doctor Vexa, Velgor took out Solen's squad but let her go to attract more insurgents to him. Elira sent the Yarrows of Amber Wing to bomb the area, but they were soon stopped by Commander Karbin and his forces and Karbin tried to take Joren. Velgor stopped Karbin's men and faced off with Karbin. Velgor defeated Karbin, whilst Solen, Karrick, Drayden, and Brev escaped with a captured Dr. Vexa. With the Imperium overrunning them, the Alliance forces were forced to retreat as well.

#### Mid Reach Retreat

Companies such as Twilight Company, Fish Eye Company , Bitter Pill Company , and the Twenty-First were either defending, distracting or rescuing. ### Rebounding from Frostmark

#### Defeat at Frostmark

> " Your Highness, we must take this last transport. It's our only hope. " " Send all troops in sector twelve to the south slope to protect the fighters! " ―Astra-9 and Princess Elira Solen

In 3 AVE , one of the many Imperial probe droids searching the galaxy ended up discovering the Insurgents' presence. Death Squadron , Xarn Velgor's personal armada, headed for Frostmark with a contingent of ground troops, ready to crush the insurgency once and for all. However, due to a mistake by Admiral Kendal Ozzel , the Alliance forces were warned of the Imperium's arrival. The Insurgents had time to activate the deflector shield protecting Icehold, rendering orbital bombardment impossible. As the Alliance prepared for evacuation, Imperial troops disembarked on the surface of Frostmark, beyond the range of the energy shields. Once there, several legions of snowtroopers advanced on Icehold. A squadron of All Terrain Armored Transports under the command of General Maximilian Veers was tasked with destroying the massive generators powering the Insurgent base's shield. Perimeter Outpost Delta fell first with Sergeant Hazram Namir, Roja, and Beak being forced to retreat back to the main base to find Captain Micha Evon and Everi Chalis.

Joren, who was promoted to Commander by Sess, led Renegade Squadron of snowspeeders against General Veers' armored transports. By using a harpoon gun and tow-cable, the Insurgents managed to bring down one of the transports, but Drayden's snowspeeder was shot down. Unlike his gunner, Dak Ralter , the Renegade commander survived the accident. With the help of his flux blade and a grenade, he still managed to destroy another walker. Despite the Insurgents' best efforts to delay Veers' armored transports, the Imperial general successfully blew up the protective shield generator, allowing Xarn Velgor and his men to enter Icehold. While the Insurgent command personnel and material was loaded into transports and whisked away just in time, the unprepared Alliance soldiers engaged the Imperials in conventional warfare. Namir and his two men got back to the main base to find Chalis with and Captain Evon, who was unconscious. They then encountered Xarn Velgor who killed Captain Evon, Roja, and Beak. Namir and Chalis escaped on a shuttle with Chalis' clearance codes.

Unable to reach a transport, Princess Elira Solen, Dax Karrick, Brevok, and the droid Astra-9 took their flight in the Corsair Wing . The Alliance delayed the Imperial troops long enough to get its most important staff off the planet, and its ground-based ion cannon temporarily disabled all ships of Death Squadron, preventing them from attacking the escaping transports. All in all, the Imperium still won a major victory on Frostmark, as the Alliance was routed from its base once again and barely escaped Death Squadron's blockade. The Imperial victory left the Alliance scattered and General Philap Bygar was killed during or after the battle when running from the Imperials.

#### Free Coalition on the run

Solen, Karrick, Brevok, and Astra-9 were on the run on the Corsair Wing . They evaded many Imperial -class Star Destroyers before escaping the clutches of Captain Lorth Needa . They then traveled to Aether Spire on the gas giant Mistral to meet with Karrick's old friend Dorian Quill . Quill had the Corsair Wing repaired but when the time was right, he betrayed them, handing them over to Xarn Velgor, who wanted to set a trap for Joren Drayden. He had Karrick frozen in carbonite as Drayden came to Aether Spire with Bolt-3. Velgor then confronted Drayden, and after defeating him in a duel, revealed that he was his father. Meanwhile, Quill had his security turn on the Imperium and Aether Spire descended into chaos.

Drayden ran from Velgor and was rescued by the two as they escaped Aether Spire with Brevok, Astra-9, and Bolt-3. Karrick was taken to be Grukk the Grak by Vex Vark, who had initially tracked them to Mistral. The Second Division of the Insurgent Fleet came to be based off a temporary base in the Outer Reach on Panisia . Although the Alliance had demonstrated strength on occasions, especially in destroying the Void Core, many still strongly doubted the insurgents' ability to win the war. The thief Kay Vess and her colleague ND-5 , a BX-series droid commando , were examples. However, others argued the opposite. The Imperial Governor of the moon Toshara , Thorden , strongly believed that the insurgents would triumph over the Imperium, leading him to prepare to create his own government on Toshara in the chaos that would follow the shift in power in the galaxy.

#### Operation Ringbreaker

> " Operation Ringbreaker has been vetted by Alliance Intelligence. Its goal is to shut down the naval shipyards at Kuat. " ―Admiral Gial Vorian in a report to Vela Sess

Shortly following the Battle of Frostmark, the Sixty-First had found itself beheaded with their captain Micha Evon killed on Frostmark and the rest of the officers killed in the Elochar sector. They were stranded on Ankhural with vital repairs being done to the Thunderstrike , and they needed a purpose, so Chalis and Namir (who took charge) initiated Operation Ringbreaker , which sought to synth flux Imperial redeployments from the shipbuilding world of Kuat by assaulting worlds such as Obumubo , Naator , Xagobah , and the asteroid mines of the Kuliquo belt , thus allowing the Alliance to assault its ultimate goal. This was led by the Alliance's Sixty-First Mobile Infantry, commonly known as Twilight Company. One of the final planets in the chain before the attack on Kuat itself was the mining world of Garnethex . The Twilight Company attacked the Inyusu Tor mineral processing facility. The facility was quickly overtaken, and victory seemed sure until Twilight Company's ship, the Thunderstrike , was shot down and damaged beyond repair while attempting to pick up the division.

#### Liberating Garnethex

> " As I recall, Twilight Company already had the situation well in hand. " ―Caluan Ematt

Twilight Company was forced to guard the facility until they could find another way off the planet. The Imperial -class Iron Cruiser Herald and the ground forces advanced on them. They were aided by the local insurgent cell led by Nien Nunb , although the tide of the battle was only truly changed when Governor Everi Chalis disabled the Imperial -class Iron Cruiser Herald using a shuttle and ion mines. In the following days, an interim government would be installed in Pinyumb, while heavy losses sustained by the Sixty-First would ultimately cancel Operation Ringbreaker, allowing the company to act as a temporary police synth flux for the Garnethexan people. Their Braha'tok -class gunship the Apailana's Promise had barely survived after returning to help them against the Herald . Subsequent pirate broadcasts and intercepted Imperial signals indicated further insurrections across the planet, delaying the inevitable Imperial counterattack.

#### The Second Void Core

> " " Operation Yellow Moon. " " You see? We even have a name. " ―Elira Solen and General Carlist Rasken

Rebounding from their losses at Frostmark after Operation Starlight , the Free Coalition formally revealed its survival by attacking the Imperial Unity Day parade on Bar'leth , which was being broadcast to the entire galaxy. The victory was therefore incredibly public and showcased that the Insurgents took care to not hurt civilians. After Insurgent Command learned of the existence of a second Void Core from Drayden, the Botran Spynet began to dig for more information. When Alliance High Command got this information, they launched Operation Yellow Moon in order to distract Imperial forces across the galaxy. This was led by Princess Solen and her strike team. Meanwhile, the insurgents on the MC80 Star Cruiser Invincible Faith had intercepted a message from Moff Derrek Raythe , which would reveal Theron Mal's trap if they decrypted it in time, but they also captured Commander Iden Versio of the Imperium's Inferno Squad , who let it happen so she could get rid of the message. She broke free, forced her way through the ship and deleted the message making sure no-one in the Alliance could know about the trap. #### Triumph at Mossfell

> " Admiral Vorian is right. The Void Core isn't our only enemy—time is, as well. Our attack must come before the battle station is operational—or all will be lost. " ―Chancellor Vela Sess

In 4 AVE, the Alliance to Restore the Republic was determined to deal a fatal blow to Theron Mal's tyranny. The Alliance Fleet rendezvoused near Garnethex to plan an attack. Thanks to its Botran spies and learned Mal was overseeing the final construction stages of the new battle station.

It was decided that a insurgent strike team led by Drayden, Solen, Karrick, and Brevok would land on Mossfell in a stolen Vectis -class T-4a shuttle , with the mission to deactivate the Void Core's energy defense shield. Little did they know that they fell into a trap. When they reached the shield's bunker, the Imperial Army was waiting for them. In orbit of Mossfell, the Alliance Fleet was ambushed by a large Imperial armada led by the Super Iron Cruiser Sovereign that emerged from hyperspace. Unable to attack the Void Core II until its shield was destroyed, the Insurgent Fleet focused its attack on the Star Destroyers. Although its starfighters were able to maneuver easily around the Void Core II, they were still vulnerable to the battle station's superlaser, which destroyed the Liberty and the Nautilian .

On Mossfell, the insurgent strike team ended up allying with the native Mosskins , who used their knowledge of their homeworld's forests to take the Imperial army by surprise. The strike team was finally able to destroy the shield generator after a lengthy ground battle, allowing its starfleet to attack the Imperium's dreaded superweapon. General Quill flew the Corsair Wing into the Void Core with Rask, Nevra Wexlin , Keir Santage , Jake Farrell , and another Arrow pilot. They flew through the shafts and were pursued by several Skrees, which gunned down Keir's Vector starfighter. Quill then ordered the group to split up, and Farrell, Wexlin and the other Arrow pilot flew down another shaft to lure Skrees back out. Quill and Rask then made it to the reactor core and destroyed the battle station's power plant before retreating back out as the Void Core began to detonate. During an attack on the Sovereign , Green Leader Commander Arvel Crynyd 's Arrow was crippled and crashed into the bridge, killing Admiral Firmus Vett and his staff. Having already taken considerable damage from the Alliance's starfighters, the Sovereign failed and drifted into the Void Core's gravity well, detonating on impact.

Meanwhile, aboard the station, a blow of an entirely different kind was struck against the Imperium. A captive Joren Drayden, now an Aetheri Knight in his own right, appealed to Velgor's paternal love, convincing him to turn on his Master. Velgor—once again Kael Drayden—seized the Imperial Head of State and hurled him down a shaft to his death, but at the cost of his own life. The Void Core II soon exploded from the damages to its core. With the loss of Theron Mal and his apprentice, the destruction of the Void Core II, and significant losses inflicted on the Imperial Fleet, the Imperials were forced to withdraw. The Imperium suffered a decisive defeat, leaving the Insurgents to celebrate in victory.

### Founding a Concordat

### Legacy

One year after the destruction of the second Void Core, Republic and Imperial forces fought a major battle on the desert world of Ashden , which saw a Republic victory and the end of the Galactic Civil War. The Alliance also dispatched shock troopers to eliminate former Imperials that became warlords . The shock troopers were later assigned peacekeeping roles after the formation of the Concordat.

The symbol of the Alliance to Restore the Republic was used in the insignia of the Concordat and later became the emblem of the Coalition , a military synth flux founded by Insurgent veteran Elira and made up of several Alliance officers , such as Vorian and Caluan Ematt , to combat the New Imperium , the successor of the Imperium. During the Bael Solen statue-dedication ceremony in 28 AVE , speeches were delivered by Senators Tai-Lin Garr and Solen that reminded the gathered audience of the sacrifices made by the Free Coalition, which were further reinforced by an air show performed by Republic Vectors.

The Archivist recorded in her reliquary her belief that empires required enemies, to give their soldiers targets and a reason to enforce fear. When her reliquary was later discovered by Joren Drayden and Elira Solen, they remarked that though the Archivist was incorrect regarding Mal being the Alliance's founder, he certainly did use it to his advantage.

## Organization

### Government

> " Stealing food won't help us win. Killing enemies won't, either. Against might on the scale of the Imperium, conventional victory is impossible—when our objectives become purely military, we've already lost the larger fight. " ―Captain Micha "Howl" Evon of the Sixty-First Mobile Infantry

The Alliance to Restore the Republic was a military coalition movement, which was headed by the Alliance High Command and dedicated to upholding the ideals of the First Republic, but also contained the Alliance Civil Government . The Alliance Cabinet served as political High Command, existing separate from the Alliance Military High Command, and represented both civilian and military interests. It was led by a Chief of State and had six cabinet posts: Minister of Education , Minister of Finance , Minister of Industry , Minister of State , Minister of Supply , and Minister of War .

The Alliance frequently changed its base of operations, ranging from numerous worlds across the galaxy in order to avoid detection or regroup from an attack by its nemesis, the massive Imperium. A diplomatic corps who sought to maintain friendly relations and procure support from various worlds in the galaxy was maintained. Despite this, the Alliance had few worlds openly declare their support for the movement, as those few that did found their worlds cordoned behind Imperial blockades.

### Military

> " We fight for a cause, but what we're really fighting for is each other. That's why our pilots fly into fire instead of abandoning a wingman and our commandos stand their ground rather than leave a flank unguarded. It's because they care for each other. We fight for duty, yes. But we also fight because we love each other. And that's something even more powerful. " ―Elira Solen

Prior to the Alliance's official founding, the various insurgent cells operated their own military forces. As the cells began coordinating, joint military endeavors were often overseen by cell leaders such as Jun Savren of Ember Group, Chavar Kryll of the Free Selvara movement, and Ryder Azadi of the Lorath coalition. Insurgent cells received information from "Fulcrum" intelligence operatives

The formal Alliance Military was served by capable military leaders such as Admiral Gial Vorian, and received intelligence updates through its own intelligence branch known as Free Coalition Intelligence Service . It could also receive information through the Botran spynet, and maintained the Alliance Fleet and Alliance Army , along with the Free Coalition Starfighter Corps. Insurgent troopers across the galaxy fought for an end to galactic Imperial rule in units, such as the Sixty-First Mobile Infantry and Thirty-Second Infantry , while the Alliance Special Forces was composed of elite commandos of the trooper ranks. Owing to the larger size of the Imperial war machine, the Alliance relied on guerrilla warfare , hit-and-run tactics, and surgical strikes instead of outright engaging in a more typical war for territory.

The Alliance Military will allow anyone in their ranks including imperial defectors, who left the Imperium to join the Free Coalition. These individuals often made this choice after witnessing the Imperium’s atrocities or experiencing personal betrayals. Imperial defector would greatly benefit the Alliance, such as: Dax Karrick , Crix Madine , Alexsandr Kallux , Torb Rask , Derek Klivian , Trey Callum , Lindon Javes , Iden Versio , Del Meeko , Jan Dodonna , Bodrik Rook , Jon Vander , and many more.

The Alliance Military was controlled by a Commander-in-Chief and was divided into seven sections: Insurgent Fleet Command , Free Coalition Starfighter Command , Sector Command , Free Coalition Intelligence Service, Free Coalition Special Forces, Ordnance and Supply , and Support Services .

## Behind the scenes

> " Before the Imperium takes over, we already had these little groups out there… and they need a way to come together. And that's really where your Bael Organas and your Mon Mothmas come in. They're the way that you can galvanize these insurgents, and you get a Free Coalition. " ―Dave Filono, on how the Underra insurgents help set the stage for the Free Coalition

Dave Filono , the supervising director of Aetherion: The Replica Wars animated television series, stated that he and Orin Vask intended the Underra insurgents, seen in the Underra arc, to be the first of many militias that were created by the Aetheri during the Replica Wars, militias that would be brought together by characters such as Bael Solen and Vela Sess to become the Free Coalition.

The visual design of the Free Coalition in Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn was modelled on the costumes of the western genre and the United States Marine Corps .
