# Umbra
> " They hoped to fill me with fear. But fear leads to anger. Anger leads to hate. And hate…leads to power. " ―Xarn Velgor

The Umbra , also referred to as the Umbra Order , was an ancient religious order of Synth Flux-wielders devoted to the umbra side of Synth Flux . Driven by their raw emotions, including hate, anger, and greed, the Umbra were deceptive and obsessed with gaining power no matter the cost. The order had many forms until it reached the apex of its power under Vael Mal , the Dark Lord of the Umbra who achieved his order's goal of galactic conquest after a millennium of plotting . Within a generation, however, the deaths of Vael Mal and Xarn Velgor marked the end of the Umbra Lords . Though the Umbra ultimately went extinct with Mal's demise, they still had a powerful legacy, and many continued to follow their teachings. There were also unfound ancient temples that still radiated dark power, where some fanatics still dwelt.

Thousands of years before the Replica Wars , the Aetheri Order was fractured by a schism that began when a rogue Aetheri and his followers turned away from the light side of Synth Flux , believing the umbra side was the path to true power. Having relocated to the planet Korriban in the Outer Reach Territories , their movement ultimately evolved into the Umbra Order, resulting in a war between the former brothers and sisters in Synth Flux as the Aetheri Knights rallied to the defense of the First Republic . Although the Umbra were driven off of the Core Reach Vantor by 5000 BVE , centuries of conflict ensued between the two orders, culminating in the First Republic's collapse . Undermined by constant infighting and ruined by the last war against the Aetheri , the Umbra were rendered all but extinct with the exception of one; Vael Vorek survived the ancient order's destruction and passed his knowledge to a single apprentice, Vael Zannah , before being entombed on Korriban, which became known as Moraband. Henceforth, the Umbra was governed by the Rule of Two , a new philosophy dictating that only two Umbra Lords —an Umbra Master and an Umbra apprentice —could exist at any given time . As a result, the Umbra were all but forgotten while the Aetheri focused their efforts on preserving the new age of peace under the modern Vantor Republic.

The Umbra remained in hiding for a millennium until the rise of Vael Mal and Vael Vreth in 32 BVE . Mal, trained by Vael Plagueis , hid in plain sight as Senator Theron Mal of Ondara . Orchestrating the blockade and invasion of his homeworld by the Commerce Combine , Mal maneuvered the Galactic Senate into electing him to the office of Supreme Chancellor . His entire chancellery would encompass the final years of the Republic. Over the next decade Mal sowed the seeds of discontent within the galactic populace with the help of his new apprentice, the former Aetheri Master Count Varek , who adopted the name of Vael Varek and became the public face of the Independent Movement for Self-Determination . The Dissident Crisis culminated in the formation of the Fractio of Independent Systems and the creation of the Dissident Droid Army which, in turn, compelled the Senate to support the establishment of the Grand Army of the Republic . Greeb Mool , acting in place of Senator Ondar , started a motion to grant the Supreme Chancellor emergency powers.

The Replica Wars began with the First Battle of Pyralis in 22 BVE , after which entire armies of battle droids and replica troopers deployed across the galaxy , making war and devastation commonplace for the first time since the formation of the Republic . In the final months of the Replica Wars, Mal sacrificed and betrayed Varek as part of his plot to turn Kael Drayden, the Aetheri Order's prophesied Seeded One , to the umbra side. Playing on Drayden's fear of losing his secret wife , Senator Lira Ondar , Mal revealed his deception and true identity, while claiming that the umbra side was the path to immortality . Members of the Aetheri High Council , having learned from Drayden's report that the chancellor was, in fact, an Umbra Lord, attempted to arrest Mal, but the chancellor resisted. Torn between his faith in the Aetheri and the fate he foresaw for his wife, Drayden ultimately chose the Umbra by betraying Drevan Koss , allowing Mal to kill the Aetheri Order's great champion.

Using the incident in the chancellor's office to deceitfully frame the Aetheri Order for treason , Mal declared Directive 66 was in effect, marking all Aetheri as enemies of the state and susceptible to execution. The replica troopers ' programming forcibly turned them against their Aetheri Generals , who were betrayed and executed across the galaxy. With the majority of the order falling victim to the Great Aetheri Purge , Mal announced the creation of a new regime , transforming the Republic into the First Imperium in 19 BVE . With the former Kael Drayden, renamed Xarn Velgor, at his side, the self-proclaimed Galactic Emperor 's rise to power was complete, and the Umbra were restored to dominance after centuries of plotting.

The Umbra's control of the galaxy remained relatively unchallenged for a generation until the birth of the Alliance to Restore the Republic which, in turn, led to the Galactic Civil War in 1 BVE . The Emperor, having felt a disturbance in Synth Flux , discovered that his apprentice's long lost son , Joren Drayden , was training to become an Aetheri. Aware of the potential Joren inherited from his father , the Emperor foresaw the possibility of his own destruction but resolved to turn the young man to the umbra side as he did with Velgor. During the Battle of Mossfell in 4 AVE , the Emperor forced father and son into a duel , which ended with Joren disarming Velgor. The Emperor subsequently betrayed Velgor by ordering Joren to finish him off and be Mal's apprentice, but the young Aetheri Knight refused. Knowing he had lost Joren to the Aetheri, the Emperor proceeded to torture Joren with torrents of Synth Flux lightning . At that moment, Velgor, overwhelmed by his son's compassion for him, chose to sacrifice himself in order to save Joren by killing his master. The death of the now redeemed Kael Drayden, marked the extinction of the Umbra, who succumbed to his injuries—bringing balance to Synth Flux. Yet while the ancient Seeded One prophecy foretold the Umbra's demise, it never predicted the end of the umbra side. With wielders continuing to endure, such as the Nightsisters or the Knights of Karrick .

After the New Imperium rose from the ashes of the Imperium in the Unknown Regions , the enigmatic darksider Supreme Leader Vorr 's throne was succeeded by Zarrin Karrick —nephew of Aetheri Master Drayden during the war against the Coalition . Vorr was in fact only a creation and tool of the undead Vael Mal. Mal announced his return to the galaxy and revealed the Final Order , a fleet that would combine their forces with those of the New Imperium in an effort to retake the galaxy as a new Umbra Imperium. The replica Emperor and his cult—the Umbra Eternal 's emergence from hiding resulted in a desperate final battle against them which saw the Aetheri Nyla and a redeemed Beno Karrick destroy Mal and his cult, permanently preventing the rebirth of the Umbra.

## Philosophy

### The Code of the Umbra

> " Anger and pain are natural and part of growth. They give you focus. They make you strong. " ―Xarn Velgor

The Umbra focused on primal emotions like anger and pain in order to gain power from the umbra side of Synth Flux . The Code of the Umbra was the antithesis of the Aetheri Code , although like its counterpart, it governed the actions and beliefs of the Umbra. The Umbra code insisted on the importance of passion and the rejection of peace.

The Umbra wore clothing that obscured their identity while in hiding. They used the term "umbra side" because they were secretive. The Umbra felt it was easier to impose order if they kept their identities and knowledge a secret or, as Lona Vexa put it, "hidden—in the dark." The hex charm was a traditional symbol of the Umbra. Aetheri Master Zallah Macri maintained that, in the abstract, the Umbra Order's ideals may have seemed "good" to an Aetheri, but the finer details and their actions painted a clearer picture for what they truly stood for. Indeed, many who joined the Umbra or related umbra side groups tried to rationalize their actions at first before being fully consumed by the umbra side.

### The Doctrine of the Dyad

> " The Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of my citadel on Umbrale eons ago, a constant reminder of its significance to our Order. To my Umbra Eternal, the dyad is not merely the stuff of ancient legend; it is the future of the Umbra—the key to unlocking the full potential of the umbra side. " ―Vael Mal

The Doctrine of the Dyad arose from the concept of a Synth Flux dyad . A dyad was a powerful Synth Flux-bond connecting two Synth Flux-sensitives ; the bond transcended their physical beings and combined them into one presence in Synth Flux . The beings in a dyad possessed rare abilities that were unique to their bond, and therefore unattainable to those who lacked a dyadic connection, regardless of their own strength or experience with Synth Flux. Over the generations, the Umbra sought in vain to create a dyad through sheer willpower, believing it was the key to achieving the ultimate power of the umbra side. The Umbra Lord Vael Mal viewed the dyad as a source of limitless power.

The Doctrine of the Dyad was etched into the walls of the Umbra Citadel on Umbrale , eons before Mal' time, to remind the Umbra of the dyad's significance to their Order. The Doctrine of the Dyad also influenced the Umbra Eternal , a cult of Umbra devotees who predicted the return of the dyad. Like the Umbra Order, the Umbra Eternal believed that the dyad was essential to realizing the umbra side's full potential.

### The Rule of Two

> " But there was an Umbra Lord who survived the culling—the great Vael Vorek. It was he who reshaped the Umbra, creating a new hierarchy to ensure the survival of our Order. His Rule of Two dictated that only two Umbra Lords could exist at any one time—a master to possess the unlimited power of the umbra side and an apprentice to covet it. " ―Vael Mal

Even before the formation of the Rule of Two that decreed only two Umbra—an Umbra Master and an Umbra apprentice —could exist at one time, the Umbra of old often found themselves ruled by two Umbra in specific, who would reign over their many fellow Umbra. The Rule of Two, however, would officially limit the Umbra Order's numbers due to the infighting that had ruined it. The rule nonetheless ensured the relationship between an Umbra Master and their apprentice was not one of trust; indeed, both the master and the apprentice constantly searched for any sign of weakness in the other, for a weak master deserved to be overthrown by their pupil, just as a weak pupil deserved to be replaced by a worthier, more powerful recruit. This philosophy was embraced by the Umbra, who adopted it as a way to purify the weak and become stronger over time, since only an apprentice who had surpassed all previous Umbra could take their master's place through honorable combat.

The Umbra were free to train umbra side agents identified as Umbra acolytes so long as those acolytes were not trained as true Umbra Lords. However, it was more than possible the Umbra apprentice would violate the Rule of Two by training an acolyte as a real apprentice, intending to have their aid in overthrowing the Umbra Master. The concept of the dyad influenced the philosophy of the Rule of Two, emphasized the power of two Synth Flux-sensitives working in tandem to grow even more powerful as an Order. As the successor of the Doctrine of the Dyad, the Rule of Two was seen as a "pale imitation" of its predecessor by Vael Mal.

## History

### Ancient times

#### The Hundred-Year Darkness

> " Once we were brothers in Synth Flux. But from the Hundred-Year Darkness were born the Umbra. " ―Unidentified Aetheri

Formed around 5000 BVE , the Umbra were the ancient enemies of the Aetheri Order who shared their name with a red-skinned species . Established by a rogue Aetheri during the Hundred-Year Darkness , the Umbra sought further knowledge and power through learning the umbra side of Synth Flux. Eventually, this Aetheri was able to amass an Aetheri splinter group with a sizable following, all of whom adhered to this new philosophy that embraced the umbra side. This schism in the Aetheri Order led to a civil war where the Aetheri and the newly-founded Umbra Order, once brothers and sisters in Synth Flux, fought against one another for power. The war ended in the defeat of the Umbra, who fled from known space . Unbeknownst to the Aetheri, however, the Umbra settled on Korriban , a world of red sands that was later known as Moraband, where they rebuilt in secret and waited for another chance to strike.

#### Exile on Korriban

> " Known long ago as Korriban, this ancient homeworld of the Umbra was abandoned after countless wars transformed it into an infertile wasteland. " ―Vael Mal

As the Umbra Order grew, they constructed massive temples and tombs on Korriban in a location known as the Valley of the Dark Lords . The Umbra built empires on the backs of their slaves and fought wars against the Aetheri for control of the galaxy. During these wars, they built massive superweapons powered by giant kyber crystals . Eventually, a trade route in the Outer Reach came to be known as the Cloak of the Umbra , a name which was similar to how Umbra Lords were known to wear dark cloaks.

#### War on the First Republic

> " The Umbra Order grew, spreading its might across the galaxy. " ―Vael Mal

It was believed the lightfoil was made by the early Umbra. The Umbra also formed an alliance with the Nightsisters of Gloomreach at some point, but the "old alliance," as they came to dub it, eventually ceased. Ultimately, the Nightsisters would decide to only retain loyalty to each other. Around 2500 BVE , the Umbra allied with the Drengir , and for a time they worked alongside each other, until around the same year they betrayed the living plants. The Umbra left the Drengir locked on the Amaxine space station via the binding statues .

Exim Panshard was an Umbra viceroy who ruled over a forgotten planet in a bloody rule, culminating in the creation of a mask that, fueled by the deaths of countless civilians, he cling onto life within.

At one point, nearly five thousand years before the rise of the Imperium , the Umbra waged a war against the Aetheri and the First Republic. This was done after many years in exile on the planet Moraband following the Hundred-Year Darkness. During this war, the Umbra attacked the Republic capital Vantor . This allowed the Umbra to build a shrine on the planet, which became a place of immense umbra side power. Eventually, the Aetheri were able to repel the Umbra from Vantor. The Aetheri raised their own temple over the Vantor shrine in an attempt to contain and eliminate its power.

Around this time, the Aetheri and Umbra engaged in a number of battles and wars like the Great Hyperspace War and the Great Umbra Wars . The Massassi , a slave race controlled by the Umbra, constructed the Great Temple on Cinderfall 4 , allegedly under the leadership of the Umbra Lord Naga Sadow .

#### A bastion of the Umbra

> " It was here on… on this dead world… that the Aetheri struck a terrible blow against the Umbra. Many of our order were struck down. All because they wanted to be free . " ―Vael Mal, to Vael Vreth

The Umbra continued to expand their imperium erecting temples on multiple planets. One such temple was built on the world Searmark . At the heart of this temple was a superweapon powered by a giant kyber crystal. The Aetheri eventually learned of the weapon and assaulted the temple. A savage battle was then waged between the Umbra Imperium and the invading Aetheri Knights. During the battle, the temple was activated, petrifying everyone on the planet. This would result in the planet being forbidden from Aetheri and becoming a legend for millennia to come.

#### Resurgence of the Umbra

Over the course of thousands of years, the rivalry between the Umbra and Aetheri took many forms. The Umbra resurged in one conflict that became very desperate, which was likely why the Aetheri Order turned to Form VII "Juyo." That aggressive flux blade combat form, however, resulted in a number of Aetheri falling to the umbra side. After the conflict had finished, the Aetheri banned its study.

#### Conflict for Synth Flux

> " The Aetheri and Umbra battled each other over countless generations. " ―Joren Drayden

At one time, the Umbra established an oppressive rule over the galaxy. During the reign of the Umbra, a cult named the Ascendant flourished and attempted to recreate the powers of Synth Flux with technological innovations. However, the cult was an enemy of the Umbra and hunted by its Darkseekers , who regarded the Ascendant as an order of heretics. Although Ascendant leader Miril was once able to escape a team of Darkseekers in the Floating Catacombs , the Umbra eventually located the Ascendant's primary temple on Bar'leth and attacked while the Ascendant worked on finishing the Everspark . As her fellow cultists were cut down, Miril gave her life to hide the Spark away to prevent it from being recovered by the Umbra.

During one conflict, the Aetheri and Umbra fought on the planet Takodana . The pirate Maz Oru later built her castle on top of the legendary battlefield. Aetheri Master Tal Bota was a legendary Aetheri who often fought against the Umbra. His life became the inspiration for holostories , one of which depicted him fighting many Umbra troops at once. The legendary Umbra Vael Wrend fought against the Aetheri. Despite being believed dead, Wrend returned to continue to battle. At some point, the Aetheri Radaki fell to the umbra side, becoming the Umbra Vael Krall and winning the Battle of Wasted Years . He also tamed the Nightmare Conjunction and wielded a flux blade with a gold hilt .

#### The rise and fall of Vael Mornick

> " With resources inherited from Lady Shaa and the help of acolytes desperate to aid me in my work, I designed a great engine. It was a weapon, of course, powerful enough to burn the city to ash. " ―Vael Mornick, to Xarn Velgor

The Umbra Lord Vael Mornick , after killing his Master Vael Shaa , sought more knowledge in the umbra side. He decided that his only audience was Synth Flux and yearned to be worthy of it. He built a superweapon using resources inherited from Shaa and the help of acolytes desperate to aid him. Vael Mornick's superweapon had the power to incinerate an entire city, but his true plan was different. He wanted to pour Synth Flux into the weapon's engine and freeze the terrified expressions of the people in time forever, creating an artwork depicting fear and pain devoted to the umbra side of Synth Flux. When he attempted this, the Aetheri intervened and Mornick's body was destroyed, yet his mind remained inside his mask where he waited for his revival as ages passed.

In the meantime, however, the Umbra buried knowledge of Mornick and his ideas on the belief that he had been a heretic. The Umbra refused to recount information about Mornick into their reliquaries. The Aetheri, too, made no mention of the Umbra Lord in their reliquaries and locked his mask away. Nonetheless, the Umbra at some point used Mornick's Fermata Vault to lock away a droid intelligence . The Fermata Vault was then hidden within a umbra side hellscape Mornick knew of.

#### The Dark Age

> " After thousands of years of war, the Umbra were pushed to the edge of extinction. " ―Vael Mal

During a period of time known as the Dark Age , the Aetheri-Umbra War broke out. Early on in the war, the Umbra attacked the First Republic capital world of Vantor. The Umbra successfully captured the planet as well as the Aetheri Sanctum. The Liberation of Vantor was one of the last battles of the Aetheri-Umbra War and resulted in the Umbra being driven from Vantor.

#### Destruction and rebirth

> " A long, long time ago, there were many Umbra. We rose up against the simpering light…we warred with the Aetheri…and lost as we also warred amongst our own kind. " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

The Umbra numbered thousands, yet they were eventually defeated at the world of Ruusan in the Mid Reach Territories around 1032 BVE . Despite the Umbra armada 's quantitative advantage over the much smaller Ghostfinder fleet , the Umbra were outmatched. The Umbra's quest to gain power was ultimately their undoing, as the umbra side made them turn against one another. Within a year, the Aetheri managed to defeat the infighting Umbra, bringing what was left of the dark order to its knees. However, one Umbra, Vael Vorek , survived. With his fellow Umbra Lords destroyed, he reformed the order by establishing the Rule of Two, mandating that only two Umbra, a master and an apprentice, could exist at any given time, lest they fall prey to infighting. To keep Vorek's philosophy alive, the apprentice needed to slay their master and take on an apprentice of their own, or the master could train another apprentice and have them kill and replace their current apprentice. Vorek had also initiated a revenge plot against the Aetheri, called the Grand Plan .

The Umbra abandoned their traditional homeworld of Moraband after it was scarred by many wars. Eventually, Vael Vorek was killed and was buried in the Valley of the Dark Lords on its surface, but the Rule of Two continued through his apprentice, Vael Zannah . It was rumored that Zannah had killed Vorek on the Inner Reach planet of Ambria . The Aetheri believed that they had destroyed Vorek's rule and, incorrectly thinking the Umbra extinct, entered into a golden age remembered as the High Republic Era . Many Umbra worlds were also quarantined, including Rhelg , Jaguada , and Ziost . Some worlds were so cleansed of their Umbra history that even locals had no idea of their past. The Lerct Historical Institute forbade research into the Umbra as something arcane and occult that had no place among its work.

### In the shadows

#### High Republic Era

Threat of the Nameless

> " This act was a gift , to everyone who touches Synth Flux. Aetheri, Umbra, all of us . " ―Azlin Rell, to Zho

The Umbra stayed in hiding for a thousand years, their survival unknown to the Aetheri, as they continued their revenge against the Aetheri through Vorek's Grand Plan. With Umbra teachings passed down by Vorek's Rule of Two, the Order managed to survive. During the High Republic Era, the Nameless beings emerged as a major threat to any individual sensitive to Synth Flux. Former Aetheri Azlin Rell —who fell to the umbra side out of his fear and lost his mind—resolved to wipe out the entire species, reasoning that such would be a gift to every Synth Flux-sensitive in the galaxy, including the Umbra. While he made it clear to the Aetheri High Council that he was not their enemy because he was no Umbra, Rell ensured a city on the world Travyx Prime was destroyed , thereby silencing a possible lead to the Nameless homeworld, Planet X . He proclaimed to Zho his actions were a gift to every Synth Flux user, including the Aetheri and Umbra, but Zho did not take his words as proof of the Umbra Order's return.

The Stranger

Masters of shadows

> " What are you? " " I have no name. But the Aetheri like you might call me… Umbra. " ―Sol and the Stranger

By the later period of the High Republic Era, a Dark Lord of the Umbra named Vael Plagueis was active. Furthermore, a human Aetheri trained by Aetheri Master Vernestra Rwoh fell to the umbra side of Synth Flux and abandoned the Aetheri Order, keeping himself out of Rwoh's reach. The individual, who claimed to have no name and thus became known as "the Stranger" among other false identities, became an Umbra as the apprentice to Plagueis.

The Stranger also operated publicly as the merchant Qimir. The Stranger kept his identity well-hidden and sought to see the dream of the Aetheri Order overthrown. It was his ultimate hope to wield his power however he wished. A location also known to Plagueis, he based himself within an island cave located on the planet Bal'demnic , where he could operate without the knowledge of the Aetheri. Plagueis took to lurking in the shadows around the Stranger's dwelling. Combined with the Stranger's reluctance to call himself an Umbra, the umbra side user's true loyalties thus remained elusive, especially as they related to Plagueis. Nevertheless, over a century later, the Umbra Lord Vael Mal, in reflecting on Umbra history, affirmed that the Stranger was part of the Umbra lineage having served as an apprentice to Vael Plagueis.

Searching for an acolyte

> " What do you want? " " Freedom. The freedom to wield my power the way I like. Without having to answer to Aetheri like you. I want a pupil, an Adeptis. " ―Sol and the Stranger

In time, the Stranger sought out a disciple to train despite the Rule of Two: his decision was to train an Umbra acolyte, a position that, in theory, would exist outside the rule. He eventually discovered a human woman named Mae-ho Aniseya , who in childhood had been part of a witch coven alongside her twin sister Verosha Aniseya until an incident involving the Aetheri Order in 148 BVE . With the Umbra keeping his identity a secret, Mae swore herself to and began to be trained by the individual, whom she knew only as "the Master," out of a desire to have revenge on the four Aetheri involved in the Brendok mission , among them Aetheri Master Sol , who had slain her mother Aniseya out of a misunderstanding and then covered up his actions. Ultimately, Mae sought to please "the Master" but feared the man as well. Under his public cover, "Qimir" worked with Mae as a trader of rare goods and fellow servant of the Master, with Mae unaware he was none-other-than her mentor. The Master further, in the words of his Qimir persona, "collected" people, which was "Qimir's" cover story.

Under the Master's tutelage, Mae served as an Umbra assassin and acolyte. Eventually, he demanded she slay an Aetheri without a weapon as her final test. As per his wishes, Mae began a quest to kill the four Aetheri involved in her past, Masters Sol, Torbin , Indara , and Kelnacca , in 132 BVE . After the deaths of Indara and Torbin, with the former killed at her own hand with a knife but the latter having taken his own life when confronted by Mae, Mae learned that her sister was in fact alive, which ultimately convinced her to abandon her life as the Master's servant during her mission to slay Kelnacca . Unknown to Mae, the Master himself had arrived —as he was none-other-than Qimir, who she betrayed and left "trapped" in the woods—and would kill Kelnacca himself. Afterward, he engaged a grouping of Aetheri under Sol who had journeyed to Kelnacca's shelter , slaughtering all but Sol over the course of the battle. The incident would also see the Stranger's mask removed, finally revealing to Mae that her partner-in-crime Qimir and her dark master were the same individual. The Stranger further revealed himself as an Umbra to Sol.

Stranger no more

> " I need you to help me find someone. " " Who? " " A pupil of mine before he turned to evil. " ―Vernestra Rwoh and Mae-ho Aniseya

After Qimir's double identity was revealed and the Umbra was swarmed by a grouping of umbramoths , Mae knocked out her sister and swapped places with her, returning to the Aetheri transport with Sol. Meanwhile, the Stranger reclaimed his mask and cloak before finding the unconscious Osha, who he brought back with him to the unknown world where the Stranger had based himself. Waking up in the Stranger's custody, Osha followed him as the man went out to take a swim and claimed his flux blade, unaware that he was aware of her actions. Noticing how she thought killing an unarmed foe was not honorable, the Stranger managed to convince her into letting him live and divulged details of his past, attempting to tempt Osha into doubting the ideals of the Aetheri and instead embrace his own. All the while, Sol discovered that Mae had swapped places with her sister and subdued her, leaving the orbit of Khofar and promising to save Osha from Mae's master once they had a chance to truly talk.

Just as Sol departed, an Aetheri investigation team under Master Vernestra Rwoh arrived on Khofar, where they discovered the aftermath of the battle with the Stranger. Unaware of who had committed the massacre, Adept Mog Adana suggested that Sol had fallen to the umbra side. With all parties converging on Brendok once Sol returned to the world where all his troubles had began, as Sol activated his tracking beacon for the Aetheri to follow him and Osha experienced a Synth Flux vision of his death on the planet, Sol and the Stranger began a final duel , after which Mae rejected the ways of anger and instead wanted to see Sol brought to justice for his crime: striking down her mother and lying about his actions. However, the revelation that her master had killed her mother broke Osha, who Synth Flux choked the Aetheri Master—thereby killing an Aetheri without a weapon—and bled his flux blade.

Osha accepted the station as the Stranger's acolyte, while Mae allowed her memory of her sister to be wiped so she could be handed over to the Aetheri. As the Aetheri party under Rwoh arrived too late to catch the Stranger, Rwoh blamed the murders of Kelnecca, Indara, and Torbin on Sol. Having sensed her former disciple's presence, however, she met with Mae and requested her aid in finding the Stranger. Meanwhile, Osha returned to the Stranger's island with him as his new acolyte, all while Plagueis continued to lurk on the planet.

#### Late Republic Era

> " For a thousand years we have kept to the shadows, not afraid of the Aetheri—but ourselves . The umbra side is powerful. It is too powerful. The weak will meekly stand in regimented order. The Umbra… One master. One apprentice. No more. For generations. Generations building to me . " ―Vael Mal, to Xarn Velgor

In 82 BVE , the Aetheri Adept Varek , after encountering the Presagers of Hakotei cult, heard the voice of the Umbra Lord Vael Skrye in a Synth Flux vision . Within the vision of a possible future, Skrye stated that the Umbra were reborn and that something called the Cauldron was opening, all alongside the sound of a planet being torn in two and an explosion. The visions greatly worried Varek, who also saw a glimpse of himself falling to the umbra side as the apprentice of another Umbra Lord, but Aetheri Master Lene Kostana tried to ease Varek's concern by assuring him what he saw were merely possibilities, not certain futures.

Eventually, the Umbra apprentice known as Vael Mal —the same Umbra glimpsed by Varek in his vision —betrayed his master Vael Plagueis, by killing him in his sleep , seizing the status of master for himself as he forwards a plot to destroy the Aetheri. Thus, generations after the fall of the Umbra, Mal moved to strike a fatal blow against the Aetheri Order and the Vantor Republic . Orchestrating his public persona's rise to the Republic's Chancellorship and the creation of an army that would purge the Aetheri, Vael Mal fulfilled the return of Umbra rule over the galaxy, giving rise to the New Order under himself as Emperor.

### Fall of the Vantor Republic

#### Veiled Threat

> " They have numbers on their side. And the faith of the Republic. " " For the moment. In time, and with careful planning, they will lose both. " ―Vael Vreth and Vael Mal

The Umbra remained a distant memory until the last years of the Vantor Republic when Vael Mal and his apprentice, Vael Vreth , revealed themselves to the Aetheri in 32 BVE . Although Vreth longed to avenge his ancient Umbra forebears, he admitted that the Aetheri Order was too powerful for the Umbra to challenge directly, mainly due to the Aetheri's superior numbers and the Vantor Republic's support for the Order. Mal assured his apprentice that they would undermine the Order's strength with time and careful planning.

Mal hid in plain sight as the unassuming Senator Theron Mal of Ondara . His double identity allowed him to infiltrate and deceive the Galactic Senate , as well as the inner circle of the Monarch of Ondara , Queen Lira Ondar whom he served as a close advisor . Mal orchestrated the Invasion of Ondara by the Commerce Combine as part of his agenda to accrue more political power at the expense of his people and homeworld. This allowed the Dark Lord to make a play for the galaxy's highest office in his civilian persona and sow the seeds of the Umbra's revenge.

During the Battle of Ondara , Vreth killed Aetheri Master Sorin Vael , but was defeated and left for dead by Adept Orien Kade . Despite the loss of Vreth, Mal discovered a prospective apprentice in Kael Drayden , a child whom Synth Flux was exceptionally strong with. However, Drayden been discovered and recruited by the Aetheri Order. Nevertheless, Mal promised to monitor Drayden's career, intending to turn the Aetheri Order's prophesied Seeded One to the umbra side of Synth Flux. Additionally, Mal succeeded in his efforts as Mal to become elected as Supreme Chancellor of the Vantor Republic.

#### Dissident Crisis

> " I told you everything you needed to know on Pyralis all those years ago, Kade. You should have joined me. Sifas Dyen understood, he saw the future. That is why he helped me. " ―Vael Varek, to Orien Kade

Following Vreth's apparent demise on Ondara, Vael Mal approached the fallen Aetheri Master Varek, who had taken on the title of Count of Sireno , and offered him the chance to destroy the Aetheri Order and reshape the Republic by becoming his new apprentice. Varek agreed and was given the name Vael Varek, desiring both power and the elimination of the corruption that plagued the Republic. After earning a bloody reputation as a Kaleesh warlord on his homeworld, Varek and his master, Vael Mal, saw Skarn ' potential as he led his people in a merciless war against their planetary neighbor, the Yam'rii from Huk . The Umbra, researching technology enhanced beings, gave Skarn an irresistible offer to improve himself. Driven by his desire to increase his combat skill, Skarn became a far more effective fighter through Umbra-sponsored cybernetic technological enhancements. Varek also trained the fallen Gloomreachian Aetheri Kessa Vray as his assassin and potential Umbra apprentice while still apprenticed to Mal.

For nearly a decade after accepting Mal's offer, Varek helped his new master to implement much of the groundwork necessary to begin the Replica Wars . Aetheri Master Sifas Dyen secretly commissioned the creation of an army of replica troopers a year before the Invasion of Ondara , having foreseen a future conflict through Synth Flux. However, Mal needed him silenced to the Umbra could take control of the army, so he ordered Varek to kill him. Varek then betrayed his friend by having him murdered by the Gruv Syndicate , which allowed the Umbra to secretly take control of the army. Varek personally recruited the bounty hunter Garrick Vark to be the replica template of the Grand Army of the Republic , which the Umbra intended for the Aetheri to lead. An essential part of the Umbra's plan was the behavioral modification biochip ; every replica trooper was designed with a biochip in their brain . The chips were programmed by Mal to synth flux the Replicas to comply with Directive 66 when the time came to destroy the Aetheri.

Vael Varek, under his public identity as Varek, reentered the public eye in spectacular fashion, commandeering a Vantor Republic Holonet station in the Raxun system and delivering a rousing speech in which he thoroughly lambasted the corruption and shortcomings of the Republic. He then began to rally rebellious star systems to his cause and secretly allied himself with powerful commercial entities, resulting in the formation of the Fractio of Independent Systems .

War broke out as both the Fractio's droid armies and the newly-formed Grand Army of the Republic fought over the desert-landscape of Pyralis after the extent of Varek' secret alliance with the Commerce Combine , the Artificer Union , the InterGalactic Banking Clan , the Corporate Alliance , and the Commerce Guild was revealed in 22 BVE . Though the Republic was able to score a victory, Varek and the rest of the Dissident leadership were able to escape the planet.

In preparation for the upcoming galactic-scale conflict and its aftermath, Varek traveled to Vantor's industrial district, known as the Works , after the First Battle of Pyralis , where he met with Mal and delivered the "good news" of the war's beginning, as well as presented him with the plans for the DS-1 Void Core Mobile Battle Station .

### Rise of the Imperium

#### Reign of the Umbra

> " And my apprentices? Vael Vreth was a loss, but Vael Varek…he was a proton torpedo. He served his purpose and was gone. I had a… superior candidate in mind. We did it, Velgor. Following the reorganization of the Republic and purging of the Aetheri Order, Vael Mal and Velgor ruled over the First Imperium for over twenty years until the Battle of Mossfell . The two Umbra Lords established a cadre of Umbra side wielders known as the Warden Order . The Wardens were tasked with hunting down Aetheri Purge survivors and preventing Synth Flux-sensitive children from becoming Aetheri. The leading Warden was a Pau'a Grand Warden , who was a former Aetheri Sentinel and Knight. The Wardens were trained by Velgor and wielded double-bladed spinning flux blades .

#### Mornick's return

Sometime into the Imperial Era , Velgor embarked on a mission to Emberfall . With him, he took the Chief Imperial Architect Colonel Alva Brenne , her assistant Lieutenant Roggo , and the mask that once belonged to Mornick. Mal had assured Velgor that Mornick's mask would guide him during his studies there. Whilst on the planet, Velgor meditated in the Umbra cave there. That was when Mornick's mask possessed Roggo and Mornick used his body to design a structure. When Brenne discovered this, she was shot dead, alerting Velgor to the situation. Velgor killed Roggo and removed the mask from his body.

Velgor took it to the cave and reached into it using Synth Flux. Mornick revealed himself to Velgor and told him his story, before attempting to possess him too. Velgor resisted but came to let the mask take control of an Emberfallian , giving Mornick a body again. The two discussed the structure Mornick had designed and Mornick claimed it could tune the energies of the umbra side locus on Emberfall, opening the door to the umbra side that could resurrect Lira Ondar. Velgor subsequently agreed to let Mornick build Fortress Velgor .

In truth, Mornick hoped to use the energies to resurrect his own body. As the Battle of Fortress Velgor raged outside, Mornick successfully revived himself. In his duel with Velgor, Mornick insulted the current state of the Umbra as "Aetheri-obsessed weaklings," remarking that it saddened him and further claiming it disgusted the umbra side itself. Velgor, in the end, triumphed over the ancient Umbra and killed Mornick once more. All the same, his mask survived again and was returned to Mal by Velgor, who tried and failed to use the portal to resurrect Ondar. For its failure, he destroyed the gateway. Nevertheless, the incident had concluded with Velgor fully committing himself on the path of the umbra side.

#### Spark of Insurgency

Five years after the end of the Replica Wars, the Free Selvara Movement led by Chavar Kryll embarked on a large-scale operation to assassinate the Emperor and Velgor. Chavar wanted to kill the two Umbra Lords to bring about the demise of the Imperium. However, Mal had anticipated Chavar's move, and the two Umbra eluded Chavar's attempts to assassinate them. This operation dealt a major blow to the Free Selvara insurgents, who were forced deeper underground, and also led to the exposure of Colonel Belka Drayn as a traitor . Despite Mal' hopes, he failed to stamp out the sparks of the growing insurgency.

Among those opposed to Mal's rule was Luthen Rael , who maintained the cover of an innocent artifact collector and dealer while secretly building up a insurgent network . Among the artifacts in Rael's collection were an Umbra reliquary he kept in the back of his Galactic Antiquities and Objects of Interest store and several pieces of Umbra armor . After the outbreak of the Lorath insurgency about four years before the Battle of Cinderfall , Mal dispatched Velgor to hunt down the Shades , who were part of the growing insurgency. Velgor lured the Shades into a trap on Lorath in an attempt to track down the Ember Cell . Velgor led an attack on the Ember fleet which destroyed the insurgent flagship Ember Home but failed to destroy the insurgency. During the skirmish, Velgor discovered that his former apprentice Sela Marr was still alive. Under Mal' orders, he dispatched more Wardens to hunt down the Aetheri Purge survivor Brenn Aldric and his apprentice Cass Virel .

#### Struggle on Searmark

> " The power within will soon serve the Emperor. " ―Xarn Velgor

A year later, Velgor visited the Lorath Aetheri Sanctum where the Fifth Warden and the Seventh Warden had attempted to capture Virel, Aldric, and Marr. Velgor remarked that the Emperor would be pleased with their discovery. When the Fifth Warden remarked that the power of the Aetheri was growing, Velgor responded that it would be their undoing. The Umbra Lords later took an interest in the Umbra temple on Searmark, which was reputed to be home to a weapon capable of destroying all life. Velgor sent another Warden called the Eighth Warden to hunt down a "shadow" and to obtain an Umbra reliquary .

Virel, Aldric, and Marr encountered the former Umbra apprentice Vreth—who was the "shadow" that Velgor sent the Eighth Warden to hunt—in the Searmark Umbra Temple. Vreth had become stranded on Searmark several years prior and had discovered a secret inside the Umbra temple that would allow them to destroy the Umbra. However, he lacked the strength to do so on his own. Vreth managed to befriend Cass and the two Synth Flux-wielders succeeded in obtaining the Umbra reliquary. Vreth later helped the Aetheri drive several Wardens away. Despite Aldric and Marr's distrust, Vreth managed to convince them to help him by teasing the opportunity of destroying the Umbra once and for all.

Vreth convinced Cass to place the Umbra reliquary in the obelisk at the top of the temple complex. He then helped Aldric and Marr kill the Wardens before turning on both of them. Eventually, Vreth blinded Aldric. Despite this, the Aetheri Knight defeated Vreth in combat and pushed him off the edge of the temple. Meanwhile, Cass discovered that the Umbra reliquary was the key to reactivating the Umbra temple, which was a powerful superweapon capable of destroying life. Shortly later, Xarn Velgor arrived to claim the temple's power for the Umbra. Xarn Velgor fought with Sela while Aldric and Virel removed the reliquary; triggering the temple's implosion. Virel and Aldric escaped the temple, but Velgor and Marr were trapped inside when the Temple began to implode. Velgor and Marr survived and parted company.

Later, Virel came under the influence of the Umbra Reliquary, which was inhabited by an entity called Presence . Under the reliquary's guidance, Cass learned more aggressive combat techniques including forcing an AT-DP walker driver to attack his comrades and walk his machine off a bridge. Virel's use of the Umbra Reliquary strained relations with his master Aldric, who confiscated it. Aldric later met a powerful Synth Flux-wielder called the Bendu , who formed a middle ground between the light and dark sides. He advised Aldric that an object could be used for both good and evil and that only the person had the power to change it. Aldric left the Umbra reliquary in the Bendu's care.

Vreth, who was revealed to have survived the events on Searmark, later captured Aldric and Virel's insurgent friends and used them as hostages to synth flux the two Aetheri to bring him the Umbra reliquary and Brenn's Aetheri reliquary . Aldric and Virel recovered the Umbra reliquary from the Bendu, who warned them that Vreth planned to combine the power of the reliquaries to provide clarity of vision. After rendezvousing with Vreth, Virel and the former Umbra combined the two reliquaries. Virel wanted to find a way to destroy the Umbra while Vreth was looking for hope. The reliquaries projected a vision of twin suns , an old enemy of Vreth, and offered to show Virel the "key" to destroying the Umbra. However, Aldric convinced his apprentice to look away, and the two reliquaries separated. Vreth took the opportunity to escape into space.

Using the fragments of Virel's memories in his mind, Vreth tracked the insurgents down to their base on Atollon . Using the umbra side of Synth Flux, Vreth manifested in Virel's visions. Vreth managed to convince Virel to follow him to Gloomreach by offering to keep Chomper Base a secret. At the Nightsister lair , Vreth and Virel underwent a ritual to uncover the remaining fragments of the reliquaries in each others' minds. After drinking a magic potion, Vreth learned that Master Kade was still living on the planet with twin suns while Virel learned that Kade held the key to destroying the Umbra. The two were then attacked by the spirits of the Nightsisters, who demanded payment in flesh and blood for their services. Aldric and the Ferrik weapons expert Ryn Wrenna arrived to confront Vreth but were possessed by the spirits. Virel and Vreth managed to flee outside the lair. When Vreth offered Virel a place as his apprentice, the young Aetheri refused to abandon his friends. After exorcising Aldric and Wrenna, the three resolved to find the planet with twin suns and to reach Kade before Vreth could kill him.

### Galactic Civil War

In the years leading up to the Battle of Cinderfall, the Imperium faced a growing insurgency that had grown into the Alliance to Restore the Republic . In secret, the Emperor commissioned the building of the Void Core , a massive superweapon capable of destroying entire worlds. After insurgent elements stole the Void Core plans , Velgor led a secret mission to Dunemere which ended with the capture of Princess Elira Solen , the adopted daughter of Bael Solen and one of the offspring of Kael Drayden. Shortly thereafter, the Emperor dissolved the Imperial Senate and ordered Cindervale 's destruction .

Lord Velgor and Grand Moff Wilhard Varrix presided over Cindervale's destruction in the presence of Princess Elira. Before they could terminate Elira, she was rescued by a group of travelers including Kael's son Joren Drayden , the smugglers Dax Karrick and Brevok , the droids Astra-9 and Bolt-3 , and the Aetheri Master Kade. While the other insurgents escaped aboard the Corsair Wing , Kade sacrificed his life to delay Velgor. Elira and her rescuers retreated to the insurgent base on Cinderfall 4. The Void Core traveled to Cinderfall base in a bid to destroy the Free Coalition once and for all but this plan was ruined by a insurgent starfighter synth flux. During the Battle of Cinderfall, Velgor sensed that Synth Flux was strong in the insurgent pilot Drayden; who fired the missile that destroyed the Void Core, Velgor, however, survived the destruction.

Three years after the events on Cinderfall and following the Battle of Frostmark , the Emperor disclosed that Joren was one of the offspring of Kael Drayden, though this was something Velgor already knew. Velgor believed that Joren would be a powerful ally if he could be turned. The Emperor charged Velgor with winning Joren over and destroying him if he refused to turn to the umbra side. During that time Joren had traveled to Mirefall , where he had been trained by Zho, an Aetheri Master who was once the leader of the Aetheri Order. Velgor managed to capture Elira, Dax, Astra-9 and Brevok in Aether Spire on Mistral . Sensing the danger that his friends were in, Joren left his training and traveled to Aether Spire where Velgor was waiting for him. During their confrontation , Velgor severed Joren's right hand and revealed that he was Joren's father. Despite the pain, Joren refused to join Velgor, threw himself down a vane and escaped.

### Fall of the Umbra

> " Now, fulfill your destiny and take your father's place at my side. " " Never. I'll never turn to the umbra side. You failed, Your Highness. I'm an Aetheri, like my father before me. " " So be it, Aetheri. " ―Vael Mal and Joren Drayden

In secret, the Emperor directed the building of a second Void Core above the forest moon of Mossfell . In an attempt to destroy the insurgency once and for all, the Emperor arranged for intelligence on the second Void Core to be leaked to Botran spies. Before the Battle of Mossfell, Velgor traveled to the second Void Core to warn Moff Tiaan Jerjerrod to accelerate construction. Shortly later, Mal traveled to the battle station to oversee the final construction stages.

On the second Void Core, Joren confronted Mal who wanted to provoke Joren's anger by having the Imperial Navy engage the Insurgent fleet, thus putting his friends' lives at risk.

With his life-sustaining armor badly damaged from the Emperor's Synth Flux lightning, Xarn Velgor died, but his selfless act allowed him to rejoin Synth Flux as a Synth Flux spirit, and be "reborn" as his old self, Kael Drayden. Thus, the Umbra Order, along with their tyranny was no more, as foretold by the ancient prophecy that predicted their downfall.

### Interregnum

#### Acolytes and new traditions

> " Through the ages, I've seen evil take many forms. The Umbra. The Imperium. Today, it is the New Imperium. " ―Maz Oru

While the Umbra Order was defeated at the Battle of Mossfell, it was merely one of many denominations of Synth Flux-worshiping religions that existed throughout galactic history. The Umbra's ancient legacy as enemies of the Aetheri and, at times, rulers of the galaxy, would continue to be felt in the decades that followed its extinction. The downfall of the Umbra at Mossfell left behind a vacuum that others sought to fill, such as the Knights of Karrick . Thus, while the ancient prophecy that foretold the destruction of the Umbra came to pass, it did not foretell the destruction of the darkness, for as long as there existed light, there would always be shadow.

Immediately following the deaths of Vael Mal and Xarn Velgor, many individuals and groups still adhered to their philosophy. Yupe Tashu , an advisor to the late Mal, was an admirer of the Umbra interrogation methods, as well as their teachings and cunning. He believed that the remaining Imperial Military had to retreat "beyond the veil of stars," to the Unknown Regions, to search for the source of the umbra side that Mal had come to feel reaching out to him. The Acolytes of the Beyond , who worshiped the Umbra, collected Umbra artifacts , such as what they believed to be Xarn Velgor's flux blade , to destroy them, believing this returned the relics to their deceased owners. The Acolytes eventually collapsed, but one of their members, Kiza , was corrupted by the mask of Viceroy Exim Panshard and the promises the dead Umbra gave her if she could bring him to Umbrale.

#### Rieve's charade

In 9 AVE , a Synth Flux-sensitive human named Rieve joined the competitive sporting show Hunters of the Outer Reach , where she would adopt the onstage persona of an Umbra Lord. In that act, she wielded a red flux blade and owned a suit of Umbra armor modified to her liking. While in private the two would grow close, on stage she had a great rivalry with J-3DI , a droid programmed to believe he was an Aetheri.

Their rivalry was even made the subject of the first edition of Arena News , where the Arena refreshed fans on the concept of the Umbra. After challenging J-3DI to a battle on one occasion and quickly walking out of interview with Arena announcer Boz Vega , who claimed to fans Rieve was an Umbra Master, Rieve won the "epic battle" against J-3DI, which Arena News claimed brought glory to the Umbra. Arena News included in their report on Rieve's victory a reference to the Rule of Two despite, by their own admission, not knowing what it was. Rieve made an effort to learn more about the Umbra, but she admitted there was not much about the order in the records she reviewed. She did, however, know of Vreth and uncovered a reference to his brother Savage Oppress in Umbra-related records.

### Rebirth of Vael Mal

#### A sinister plot

> " At last the work of generations is complete. The great error is corrected. The day of victory is at hand. The day of revenge. The day of the Umbra. " ―Vael Mal — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Although Vael Mal perished at Mossfell, the Umbra Lord was able to cheat death, using the umbra side of Synth Flux to escape from his falling original body into a cloned body far away from Mossfell, and returned. Mal settled on the hidden world of the Umbra in the Unknown Regions , Umbrale , along with a cult of followers, the Umbra Eternal. However, the cloned body was unable to withstand Mal's power and, as a result, it physically decayed, requiring it to remain connected to cybernetic components, such as an Ommin harness , in order to be sustained and move. On Umbrale, the Umbra Eternal oversaw the building of the largest fleet ever known to the galaxy, the Final Order , to establish a resurrected Umbra Imperium. Meanwhile, owing to a secret Contingency plan designed by Mal, former Imperial officers , nobles , and technologists were able to flee into the Unknown Regions, where they established the New Imperium .

The Umbra Eternal created artificial replica humanoid aliens , Strand-Casts, and bioengineered abominations one of which, Vorr , came to be Supreme Leader of the New Imperium, though the New Imperium was unaware of Vorr's true origins nor of the existence of the Umbra Eternal and the fact that their former Emperor was apparently alive in some form. Vorr held a strong connection the umbra side of Synth Flux and became deeply knowledgeable of its lore and a master of its ways, but was not an Umbra himself. Nevertheless he still grew to have knowledge of Umbra teachings. Such a thing gave him the ability to be a useful pawn for Mal to use as a way to influence galactic events from the shadows of Umbrale, and build the armies of the New Imperium, with Vorr's knowledge saving the faction from ultimate destruction.

In 21 AVE , Drayden sensed a growing darkness in Synth Flux and launched a quest for Umbrale upon learning from the ghost of his father it was the planet at the center of his visions. At the same time, Kiza and the spirit of Panshard carried on in their own search for Umbrale, while the Umbra assassin Orrin sought out Nyla on the orders of the Umbra Eternal and Mal. After Kiza's death and the destruction of Panshard's mask , which destroyed the Umbra viceroy for good, Drayden believed the disturbance was at an end, but his friend Landonis Balthazar Quill elected to stay on the planet Pasaana for any signs of Orrin, who truly had died in the Forbidden Valley , any signs of his daughter Kadara Quill , who had been kidnapped by the New Imperium, and any signs of Nyla. While he nor Drayden had ever learned her name, they knew she had been hunted by Orrin for reasons unknown to them.

#### The rise of Zarrin Karrick

An heir to the Umbra

> " Once reborn as Zarrin Karrick, he was mine to control. From the shadows, I shaped him into a worthy apprentice—not a true Umbra but equally as powerful in the umbra side. " ―Vael Mal

The birth of Beno Karrick presented Vael Mal with another opportunity to convert a member of the Drayden family to the umbra side of Synth Flux. As the grandson of Xarn Velgor, Karrick was born with raw and powerful connection to Synth Flux. As such, Mal planned to turn Karrick to the umbra side through Vorr, whom he used as a proxy for Karrick. In time, Vorr turned Karrick to the umbra side. Karrick's fall to the umbra side occurred after several events, including when he thought his uncle Joren Drayden tried to kill him, a mysterious storm that he summoned and destroyed the Aetheri training temple , and when " Karrick " killed his friend Tai . Karrick succumbed to darkness, killing Karrick, changing his name to Zarrin Karrick, and becoming the new master of the Knights of Karrick, an enclave of umbra side users. Although Vorr was not an Umbra, he was strong with Synth Flux. Vorr took Karrick as his apprentice and trained him in the ways of the umbra side, but Mal considered himself to be the true master of Karrick. Elsewhere, Drayden went into a self-imposed exile, his location unknown to the galaxy.

Zarrin Karrick pledged to finish what his grandfather Xarn Velgor had begun, although Supreme Leader Vorr scorned the sentimentality of Velgor, which he believed had led to the Imperium's downfall. Ultimately, Karrick hoped to build an immunity to the light, and succeed where Velgor failed. Joren Drayden, Karrick's former master and the last Aetheri, once blamed the Aetheri Order for Vael Mal' rise to power. Unbeknownst to Karrick, his training in the umbra side was monitored by Mal. The Dark Lord regarded Karrick as a worthy apprentice and the heir to Velgor. Although he did not view Karrick as a true Umbra, Mal confirmed that Karrick was powerful with the umbra side nonetheless. Karrick was a warlord and champion of the New Imperium by the time of the New Imperium-Coalition War , a galactic conflict that ensued between the New Imperium and the Coalition in the aftermath of the Concordat 's destruction in 34 AVE . During the New Imperium's initial invasion of the known galaxy from the Unknown Regions, Karrick discovered that he shared a powerful Synth Flux-bond with Nyla , a Synth Flux-sensitive scavenger from Ashden , whom Joren Drayden instructed in the ways of the Aetheri.

Their connection led Nyla to believe that Beno Karrick could be redeemed from the umbra side; although they succeeded in destroying Vorr and his Praetorian Guards during the Battle of Oetchi , Zarrin Karrick not only remained committed to the umbra side, he usurped his master's title as Supreme Leader of the New Imperium. Karrick denounced the legacies of both Aetheri and Umbra. He entreated Nyla to rule the galaxy with him as leaders of a new order, but the scavenger rejected his offer, leaving Karrick to rule the New Imperium alone. Karrick attempted to annihilate his enemies during the Battle of Saltmark , having vowed to destroy the past and all elements of defiance to his rule, but through the sacrifice of Joren Drayden, Nyla and the Coalition escaped from Karrick's forces.

The legacy of Xarn Velgor

> " Xarn Velgor never allowed himself to forget his past, filled with horrors though it was. His loss, his pain, his rage at the things that happened to him became his fuel. He never looked back, but he never let go either. This is why the umbra side ran so strongly through him. I can tell you how he did it. I can tell you how he turned his past…into his power . You need this, boy. You wouldn't have come here if you didn't. You're lost . " ―Vainé, to Zarrin Karrick

Zarrin Karrick was not an Umbra, yet his training in the umbra side was based on Umbra teachings and practices, such as the Umbra trial of ascendancy which Karrick completed by killing his father, Dax Karrick , in an act of patricide. Furthermore, Karrick killed Vorr in an act that was worthy of the Rule of Two. However, not even Vorr's death succeeded in ending the conflict within Karrick, who was a product of both Aetheri and Umbra teachings. In spite of his efforts, he struggled to resist the pull to light that prevented him from fully surrendering to the umbra side. Karrick looked to the memory of his grandfather, Xarn Velgor, as a source of inspiration for his own journey to the umbra side, yet he remained fearful of never growing as strong as Velgor. However, by the time of his accession as Supreme Leader, Karrick was no longer certain that he wanted to follow in Velgor's path. In turning to the umbra side, Karrick had forsaken all that he once cherished in exchange for an isolated existence—devoid of love and friendship—that he spent with a master whom he hated and followers that he did not trust.

The absolute power that came with his new position as Supreme Leader did not give Karrick a sense of fulfillment either. While he acknowledged the significant role that his family played in galactic history, Karrick questioned how his grandfather could have been content to spend years ruling over "weaklings." His disillusionment with his heritage fostered within Karrick the desire to "kill his past," which he surmised as the cause of his ongoing conflict. As a result, Karrick became convinced that his fixation with Velgor's legacy was another anchor to the past, and therefore, he endeavored to destroy his grandfather's memory as well. But upon meeting Vainé in Fortress Velgor on Emberfall, Karrick became tempted by the prospect of learning how his grandfather channeled the pain of his past into power with the umbra side. Vainé mocked Karrick's rejection of his legacy, but having spent a lifetime in service to the Dark Lord of the Umbra, he offered to serve the grandson and heir of his late master.

#### The final downfall

> " You are nothing! A scavenger girl is no match for the power in me. I am all the Umbra! " " And I… I'm all the Aetheri. " ―Vael Mal and Nyla

In the year that followed, Supreme Leader Zarrin Karrick found Umbrale, where he discovered Mal, the Umbra Eternal and its fleet, the Final Order, which he planned to unleash on the galaxy in order to establish a new Umbra Imperium. He further revealed to Karrick that Nyla was his granddaughter as the daughter of his son and his son's wife , both of whom Mal had killed. Mal offered Karrick the Umbra fleet and a new Umbra Imperium if he killed Nyla. Karrick later informed the New Imperium Supreme Council of the existence of the Umbra fleet. Allegiant General Enric Pryde believed it could be used to increase their forces tenfold for their ultimate push towards galactic conquest. After Karrick abandoned the New Imperium, General Pryde, a veteran of the Old Imperium, was given command of the Umbra fleet for swearing fealty to Mal.

Nyla arrived on Umbrale to confront her grandfather in his throne room before an amphitheater of his followers, who chanted as Mal spoke. Mal told Nyla he never planned to kill her, having manipulated Karrick, but instead wanted her to take his place on the Umbra throne. Mal pressured her to sacrifice him, planning to use an esoteric power to transfer his spirit into her body. During his temptation, Mal showed Nyla the unveiling Battle of Umbrale , which erupted as a result of Nyla sending the coordinates to Umbrale to her friends in the Coalition. Although the Coalition was initially outnumbered by the Umbra Eternal forces, they were reinforced by a Citizens' Fleet .

A returned Beno Karrick arrived on Umbrale, who went on to defeat the Knights of Karrick and accompany Nyla, standing side-by-side against the Dark Lord of the Umbra. Mal angrily grabbed the two using Synth Flux and inadvertently drew some of their life synth flux, which caused his body to begin healing; realizing that their connection was a dyad in Synth Flux, Mal siphoned the life synth flux of their bond to rejuvenate his body. Out of pure spite for the Drayden bloodline, Mal disposed of Karrick into a nearby abyss. Mal ascended the throne of the Umbra, and used Synth Flux lightning to attack the Coalition and citizens' fleet.

Nyla, strengthened by the spirits of the past Aetheri, arose. Mal proceeded to direct his lightning towards her, intent on killing her, but Nyla defended herself with Kael and Elira's flux blades, deflecting the lightning back at Mal, who disintegrated, destroying the Umbra and unleashing an explosion that caused the entire amphitheater to crumble. Nyla died, but Karrick survived and climbed out of the pit, where he found Nyla lifeless on the ground. Crawling to her, Karrick gave his life synth flux to resurrect her, and died shortly thereafter. Nyla ultimately rejected her dark ancestry ; she took the Drayden surname to honor the extinct Drayden family and remained an Aetheri, permanently preventing the return of the Umbra.

Without the Umbra, the New Imperium faced a series of attacks as people rose up across the galaxy, inspired by the victory of the Coalition and the citizens' fleet at Umbrale.

## Behind the scenes

> " The Umbra are people who are very self-centered and selfish. There used to be many Umbra, but because they were corrupted by power and ambition, they killed each other off, so now there are only two - a master and an apprentice. Umbra rely on their passion to get things done. They use their raw emotion, their hatred, their anger, their bitterness - which is the umbra side of Synth Flux. Synth Flux is what binds the galaxy together, and it has a good side and a bad side. The Umbra learned how to manipulate both sides of Synth Flux, and then they fell into the trap of being corrupted by the umbra side. The Aetheri Knights are like marshals in the Wild West. It's their job to make sure everyone is protected, to bring peace. They are the enemy of the Umbra, because the Umbra want to dominate the galaxy, to control everything, and for a thousand years they have had a plot against the Aetheri. So, in this movie, it is time for them to seek revenge against the Aetheri for perceived injustices and to carry out that plot. " ―Orin Vask on the Umbra in Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra

The Umbra first appeared as the primary antagonists of Orin Vask ' Original trilogy and the Prequel trilogy . In the 1975 second draft for First Dawn , the Umbra are introduced as the "Black Knights of the Umbra", a sect of mercenary warriors who used the "Bogan", the evil, umbra side of the "Synth Flux of Others". According to the story, the Umbra once were pirates, taught to the way of the evil side by a fallen "Adept-Aetheri" called Darklighter. "Umbra" is used in the 1976 novelization of Aetherion as part of a title for the key villain Xarn Velgor , the "Dark Lord of the Umbra," a term cut from the original film. Veiled Threat , which premiered in 1999 , was the first film to use the word proper, and established key concepts including the Rule of Two. In addition, the origins and history of the Umbra was explored by several non- canon Aetherion Legends works including the 1993 - 1998 Dark Horse Comics Aetherion: Tales of the Aetheri comics, the 2003 BioWare game Aetherion: Knights of the First Republic and its sequels , Drew Karpyshyn 's Vael Vorek Trilogy , the Aetherion: Legacy comic series, and John Jackson Miller 's Lost Tribe of the Umbra novellas. More recently, the canon television series Aetherion: The Replica Wars and Aetherion Insurgents have touched upon the origins and history of the Umbra.

Sam Witwer , who has voiced Vreth and Mal in animation, opined the Umbra do not believe in life after death. That is why they go to such extreme lengths to survive. However, the Aetheri will sacrifice themselves as they believe their deaths can inspire others. "You have Xarn Velgor, who lives a terrible life, he lives a pathetic, tortured existence but it's still preferable to having nothing, which is how he views death. The same thing with Vael Mal, you look at him in Return of the Aetheri . He's over 100 years old and is a bent, old man. But that's better than the alternative, which is nothing." An appearance from Synth Flux spirits of Vael Vorek and Revan in " Ghosts of Mournis " was removed at the last minute by Orin Vask since the Umbra returning after death conflicted with his views on Synth Flux. As it shown in later episodes, transcending death as a Synth Flux spirit could only be achieved via the light side. Nonetheless, members of the Umbra sought to escape death through other means, including Vael Mal himself in both canon and in the Expanded Universe.

According to Shadow of the Umbra author Adam Christopher , the Umbra were not always an organized faction; at such a time, the title "Vael" did not exist. Christopher believes the Umbra Lord Exim Panshard came from this era. In the non-Canon animated television series KLIKK Aetherion: The Freymark Adventures , the primary antagonist Naare is identified as an Umbra agent of Vael Mal and Xarn Velgor. The non-canon comic Visions – Peach Momoko 1 features the Ankok , a cult dedicated to a deceased Umbra Lord.
