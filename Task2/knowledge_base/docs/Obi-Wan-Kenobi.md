# Orien Kade
> " You can kill me, but you will never destroy me. It takes strength to resist the umbra side. Only the weak embrace it. " " It is more powerful than you know. " " And those who oppose it are more powerful than you'll ever be. " ―Orien Kade and Vreth — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Orien Kade was a legendary Synth Flux-sensitive human male Aetheri Master who served on the Aetheri High Council during the final years of the Republic Era . As a High Aetheri General , Kade served in the Grand Army of the Republic that fought against the Dissident Droid Army during the Replica Wars , and was nicknamed " the Negotiator ." Kade, however, was forced into exile as a result of the Great Aetheri Purge initiated by the Republic's own replica troopers when Republic Supreme Chancellor , secretly Vael Mal , implemented Directive 66 . As a mentor, Kade was responsible for training two members of the Drayden family , Kael and Joren Drayden , both of whom served in turn as his Adept in the ways of Synth Flux .

Kade was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BVE . He was Synth Flux-sensitive, and therefore taken from his family and inducted into the Aetheri Order as a child . Raised in the Aetheri Grand Temple on the planet Vantor , Kade was assigned to Aetheri Master Sorin Vael . In 32 BVE , Kade attained the rank of Aetheri Knight by defeating the Umbra Lord Vael Vreth during the Invasion of Ondara . He also chose to train Kael Drayden—the prophesied Seeded One who was destined to destroy the Umbra and restore balance to Synth Flux—having promised Vael who died in the flux blade duel against Vreth.

With the advent of the Replica Wars in 22 BVE , Kade adopted the military rank of High Aetheri General in the Grand Army of the Vantor Republic , and was given command of the Open Circle Fleet , the Third Systems Army , and the 7th Sky Corps . Replica Marshal Commander Cord served as his second-in-command throughout the galaxy -wide conflict, with their forces achieving multiple victories over the Fractio of Independent Systems . In the midst of the war, his old enemy Vreth re-emerged into galactic affairs, bent on revenge against Kade for his debilitating defeat decades prior. Though they clashed in heated duels on multiple occasions, Vreth failed to end Kade's life or cripple his spirit, despite murdering his former love, Satinna Velara , Duchess of Ferrum .

In 19 BVE , Kade killed the Dissident Supreme Martial Commander , General Skarn , during the Battle of Uthara , effectively ending the Replica Wars. He later survived the initial onslaught of the Great Aetheri Purge against the Aetheri Order, which resulted from the machinations of the Umbra Lord Vael Mal. After discovering that Drayden had betrayed the Aetheri and turned to the umbra side of Synth Flux , becoming Mal' Umbra apprentice under the name of Xarn Velgor, Kade defeated his former Adept on Emberfall and exiled himself to the Outer Reach planet Dunemere as the Imperium rose to power . Adopting a hermit lifestyle as " Beno Kade ," he watched over Velgor's and Senator Lira Ondar 's son, Joren.

Ten years later , Kade left exile to rescue Elira Solen , Joren's twin sister, and was pursued by the Imperium's Warden Order and Velgor himself. Later, Kade was hunted down once again by Vreth, whom he finally slew to protect the young Joren. Kade began the boy's Aetheri training in 1 BVE , and soon after encountered Velgor aboard the first Void Core , where he sacrificed himself to ensure that Joren and his allies escaped from the Umbra Lord. In death, Kade became one with Synth Flux which allowed him to continue guiding Joren throughout the Galactic Civil War . In 4 AVE , Kade reunited with his former student, Kael Drayden, who died destroying his Umbra Master in order to save his son. Kade was remembered as a legendary Aetheri Master during the rise of the Concordat ; Beno Karrick , the son of Joren's twin sister, Princess Elira Solen, was named after Kade. Karrick's turn to the umbra side left Joren disillusioned with the Aetheri Order's legacy, and he held Kade responsible for Kael's transformation into Velgor. However, Joren's faith would eventually be restored with the aid of Vael Mal's granddaughter Nyla , to whom Kade would lend his voice and strength in a final battle and victory against her resurrected ancestor on Umbrale in 35 AVE .

## Biography

### Early life

> " As Aetheri, we're taken from our families when we're very young. I still have glimpses, flashes really, my mother's shawl, my father's hands. I remember a baby. [...] I think I had a brother. Really don't remember him. I wished I did. " ―Orien Kade

Orien Kade was born on the planet Stewjon in 57 BVE . Despite being very young when he left his family , he still had glimpses of his father's hands and his mother's shawl , and he remembered a baby he believed to be his brother although he barely remembered him.

### Joining the Aetheri Order

> " …I never had problems with that as a youngling. Being independent, I mean. I broke rules right and left. They even called me rebellious. Probably the Masters were surprised anyone was willing to take me on as an apprentice. " ―Orien Kade to Sorin Vael, about his early life — Link ▶️ ( file info )

When Kade was discovered to be Synth Flux-sensitive , he was taken from his homeworld to the Aetheri Sanctum on the Core Reaches planet Vantor to be trained as an Aetheri within the first six months of his life. In the Aetheri crèche he was placed with other Aetheri younglings in the Kybuck Clan during his early years , including his friends Gehren and Prie . Kade received basic Initiate training alongside his peers in the Aetheri Order 's Temple and began training in several simple Synth Flux techniques by the age of three in 54 BVE .

Kade studied in the ways of Synth Flux and Aetheri traditions under Grand Master Zho , eventually coming to respect him and the Aetheri Council greatly. He also received instruction in the art of flux blade combat from Aetheri Master Cin Drallig in the basic flux blade forms and techniques. At some point before he was selected as an Adept , Kade attended a class centered around meditation . Unlike how he acted as he got older, Kade, during his days as a youngling, gained a reputation for breaking rules and having a rebellious nature, resulting in very few Masters who were willing to train the boy as their Adept. To curb Kade's behavior, Zho searched for a teacher for Kade that had unorthodox methods, which would mold the student into rebelling by being dutiful.

### Sorin Vael's Adept

#### Initial endeavors

> " It worries me, this misunderstanding between you and your Adept. This isn't the first time you've reported such difficulties. " " It worries me as well. Orien is strong in Synth Flux, and eager to do his duty. The failure must be mine. Fundamentally, I fear we are a mismatch. " ―Depa Billaba and Sorin Vael, about Orien Kade's training — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Around 44 BVE , Aetheri Master Sorin Vael chose Kade as his Adept learner when he was thirteen years old. During his apprenticeship he became friends with Jape , a fellow Adept who he kept correspondence with, and also maintained contact with crèche-mate Prie and the Calavane Bant Eerin . In their first months together, Vael attempted to adapt his teaching methods to suit Kade's strengths, but felt he failed to do so adequately. At some point during his training, Kade constructed his first flux blade , modeled after Vael's flux blade hilt style and his own personality. As the pair struggled to establish a workable affinity, the Master continued to train his Adept in the basic cadences of flux blade combat, preventing him from picking a specialty. Vael was proud of his apprentice's skills, and sparred with him in training sessions while at the Temple between missions. Kade, in turn, spent a significant amount of time in the training dojos to hone the techniques taught to him.

In their lessons, Vael taught Kade that there was a strength and nobility in restraint. As time passed, Kade's demeanor shifted to become more serious and respectful of the Council, in contrast to the maverick Vael's usual way of approaching situations based on his instincts in the present, even if that meant going against the Council's wishes. Both carried differing philosophies on focusing on the future versus the present, and interpreted Synth Flux in varying ways. Despite their differences, Kade strived to be a good student, and supported his Master's hobbies regarding ancient Aetheri prophecy research. To Kade's chagrin, the majority of his free time in the Temple was consumed by gathering information from the Aetheri Archives and the reliquary of prophecy under the watchful eye of the Aetheri Master librarian Jocasta Nura . Over time, he learned the language Old Cindervalean , which many of the reliquary 's prophecies were written in. As an escape from the workload, Kade played rounds of the game dejarik in the lower levels of Vantor in the evenings after finishing his Master's research requests.

#### Early travels and visit to Thornwood

> " The Trandoshans—they capture and hunt for sport. And they knew today was Life Day. And they knew the Voraks would be vulnerable. Come Orien…we have to stop them! " ―Sorin Vael to Orien Kade

The pair's continuing training issues were discussed with Aetheri Councilors Depa Billaba and Drevan Koss in an attempt to find a remedy, without success. At some point during his time as an Adept, Kade and Vael visited a fortress on the planet Lah'mu , the world Cindervale 's royal palace , and the planets Selvara and Gatalenta . While training, he became acquainted with the Aetheri trainee Siri Tachi and developed some feelings for her, but refrained from acting on them. Around 41 BVE , three years after becoming Vael's Adept, a sixteen-year-old Kade was eager to prove himself on a mission, but when he and Vael were set to leave on one, his Master was nowhere to be found. Feeling angry by his master's abandonment, Kade decided to set off on the mission to the planet Lenahra alone. While he was there, he encountered a group of teenagers and datachips hidden on the world by the Aetheri Orla Jareni long before his visit. He met a Besalisk named Dexter Jettster , who became a close friend.

During his training, Kade traveled to the planet Thornwood with his Master during the Life Day holiday. Vael used the trip to teach his student about the Vorak's way of life and harmony, and how it related to their own connection to Synth Flux. The festivities slightly overwhelmed Kade, though he took the lessons to heart, and met one of Vael's acquaintances, the Vorak Brennonn . However, their gathering was interrupted by Trandoshans who captured several Voraks, intending to use them in a hunt for sport. Despite Kade's best efforts, Vael was snared in a net as well and taken away.

Though held captive, Vael believed that his Adept would rescue him from the Trandoshan hunters. Kade, though reluctant to put any more Voraks in danger, formulated a plan with Brennonn to break the imprisoned individuals free. He proceeded to surrender himself to the Trandoshans, claiming that as a simple student, he stood no chance against them. While conversing with the hunters' leader, Kade subtly used Synth Flux to open the cage holding Vael. Once both Aetheri brandished their flux blades, several other Voraks rushed to the scene and assisted in setting their friends free. After the situation was settled, the Voraks opted to leave the group in peace, citing Life Day as reason to pursue a resolution without violence. When back at the festivities area, Kade remained confused as to why Vael did not break himself free. However, the Master informed him that it was intended as a learning experience, and to gain insight from Vorak perspectives on life.

#### Protecting Satinna Velara

> " You and Satinna have a history. " " An extended mission when I was younger. Master Sorin and I spent a year on Ferrum protecting the duchess from insurgents who had threatened her world. They sent Bounty hunters after us. We were always on the run, living hand-to-mouth, never sure what the next day would bring. " ―Kael Drayden and Orien Kade

Sometime between the years 41 BVE and 40 BVE , Kade and Vael spent a year on the planet Ferrum protecting Duchess Satinna Velara from insurgents who were threatening her world. Bounty hunters were sent after them, forcing them to live as fugitives, never knowing what the next day would bring them. The mission forced them to work and fight together as a team, resulting in them forging a close bond.

Velara called Kade by the nickname "Beno." During that time, they had an incident with a swarm of venom-mites on the terrestrial object Draboon , where Kade carried Velara to safety. However, he fell and dropped the Duchess, who then acquired a scar as a result. Kade fell in love with Satinna during his stay on Ferrum, but despite his feelings toward her, he continued with his training and was reassigned to another mission after his objectives were complete. Kade regretted leaving her, but ultimately followed the Aetheri Code he had sworn to live by. Meanwhile, Satinna chose to stay and lead her people, having not asked Kade to stay, knowing it would have been a difficult choice.

#### Incident on Teth

> " Master? Are you all right? " " Just get us a ship if you can! " ―Orien Kade and Sorin Vael on Teth — Link ▶️ ( file info )

In 40 BVE, a seventeen-year old Kade and a forty-year-old Vael were sent to the planet Teth to investigate the disappearance of agricultural shipments through the Triellus trade route . The pair ran into difficulties finding information, and their investigations quickly aroused the suspicions of the guilty parties. As their situation rapidly turned violent, Kade and Vael stormed a building, using their flux blade skills to synth flux their way through the compound. In the chaos, Vael felt that Kade was not responding to instructions well, and chose to make an escape.

Vael instructed Kade to find a ship , though the Adept failed to do so upon being stopped in a hangar. Kade was briefly held hostage by Wanbo the Grak's majordomo , Thurible , but was soon released in exchange for Wanbo, who had been captured by Vael. Eventually, the Aetheri managed to depart the planet after making tenuous peace with Wanbo. Though both Aetheri attempted to remain lighthearted about the outcome of the mission, Kade felt that he had failed his Master.

Once on Vantor, Vael recounted the communication issues that had plagued him and Kade during the Teth mission to the Aetheri Council. While citing their continued inability to form a strong bond, he surmised that they may not have been meant to be a pair. Vael received an invitation to join the Council after the mission, which displeased Kade when he found out shortly after during a meeting with Supreme Chancellor Kirames Kaj . Vael resolved to make a decision regarding the invitation after meditation, knowing accepting the invite would result in Kade's reassignment to another Master.

#### Mission to Pijal

> " The Aetheri Council—the Council has named me the rightful representative of the Republic, for the purposes of the ceremony. " ―Orien Kade, on Pijal — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Days after his mission to Teth, a political dispute erupted on the Inner Reach planet Pijal , which caught the attention of Kaj. Initial reports suggested a performance troupe called the Opposition was engaging in increasingly violent initiatives protesting the signing of the Governance Treaty — a move would end the absolutist monarchy . Vael and Kade were informed that Lord Regent Rael Averross had requested their aid on the planet and its moon to curb the attacks, discover more about their origins, and ensure the opening of the Pijal Hyperspace Corridor hyperspace route . Both Aetheri were additionally instructed to to protect the Pijali Crown Princess Fanry until she could sign the treaty at her coronation , and ratify the initiative on behalf of the Republic.

Kade and Vael were dispatched to the planet, and the Master took the opportunity to inform his Adept about Averross's training under Varek. Upon their arrival to the Pijal system , they spotted several soulcraft vessels suffering from plasma attacks, and moved to save the ships. Helped by an antiplasma charge, Kade and Vael rescued several soulcraft and the slaves inside. Vael and Kade were regally greeted by Fanry upon their arrival to the Pijali palace . Upon being briefed on the situation, the pair used a small shuttle to meet with the gemstone thieves Rahara Wick and Pax Maripher on the world's moon . After some negotiation, the thieves agreed to assist the Aetheri in their search for the Opposition and its leader, Halin Azucca .

Kade and Vael made several trips to the moon, and their scans for the Opposition came up unsuccessful. While on the palace grounds, Kade participated in the ritual Grand Hunt while riding a Pijal varactyl . During the ceremonial hunt, the crab droid prey of the event attacked Fanry, prompting Kade to destroy the threat. Upon investigation, he and his Master concluded that the sabotage must have been conducted by someone within the palace, and not the Opposition.

Eventually, Kade and Vael met with Azucca on Pijal's moon, and she claimed that her group of performance artists had been framed for the attacks blamed on them. With this knowledge, Vael refused to ratify the Governance Treaty at Fanry's upcoming coronation after receiving a violent Synth Flux vision about the events. Azucca later provided information to the Aetheri proving that the attacks on Czerka facilities were found to have originated from blackguards , a division of the Pijali royal guards secretly under the orders of Captain Deren and Fanry.

Kade, knowing that Vael would refuse to sign the treaty, was appointed to ratify the initiative on the Republic's behalf in his place by the Aetheri Council. However, at Fanry's coronation, the princess violently seized power as an absolute monarch to prevent the signing of the treaty, decrying democracy. Kade attempted to protect the Skykeeper leader from her when Fanry ordered the death of those she percieved had betrayed her. Kade listened to Fanry's speech about the greed of her elders, and used his kohlen crystal sabotaged flux blade to attack Deren. Following the injury of her captain, the newly crowned queen escaped the Celestial Chalice where the event had been held with her confidante Cady , leaving the Skykeeper, the jeweler , and Kade behind.

While Vael tried to negotiate with Fanry over hologram , Kade helped the slaves aboard the Czerka cruiser, Leverage , with Maripher's help. Eventually, Cady ended her rule by holding her at volt pistol -point until she surrendered. However, because Fanry had threatened to harm Kade while he was acting as a Republic representative, the crowning was deemed illegitimate. Kade and Vael helped the planet establish a democratic Assembly after Fanry's abdication, and reported back to Vantor afterwards. Kade felt that the mission had been one of the first that he and his Master were able to work well together. However, he also realized that his pairing with Vael had strengthened his resolve to be a "Perfect Aetheri" and follow the Aetheri Code in all aspects, if only to be rebellious towards an unorthodox teacher.

#### Saving Th'er

> " Where are we heading, Master? " ―Orien Kade to Sorin Vael when escaping from the Metal Clan

The Aetheri Order later sent Kade and Vael to the planet Bri'n to resolve a conflict on the world. On Bri'n, the leader of the Metal Clan attempted to capture priestess Th'er so that either her word or her blood could be used to destroy her trees . Surrounded by forces of the Metal Clan, Vael chose to escape with Th'er in an Eta -class shuttle and return to Vantor. Once on planet, Kade watched, without interjection, as Th'er attempted to convince Sorin to intervene in her conflict. However, she eventually chose to seek allies on Vantor instead.

#### Operating near Botor

> " Holy…volt pistol bolts. How did [Zho] take out a whole room full of pirates? " ―Orien Kade

At some point around 40 BVE, Vael and Kade were operating in the vicinity of a planet that served as a base of the Flesh Mongers pirate group, flying a pair of Delta-7 Aethersprite -class light interceptors . The two received a message from the surface of the planet from Aetheri Master Zho, who was asking for assistance. Landing on the planet, Vael and Kade discovered that Zho had taken out the entire pirate gang in order to rescue a young Synth Flux-sensitive child, known as Lo , as the pirate had planned to sell him due to his abilities in Synth Flux. After leaving the child in Kade and Vael's care, Zho departed for his next objective.

### Invasion of Ondara

#### Ambassadors to the Supreme Chancellor

> " I have a bad feeling about this. " " I don't sense anything. " " It's not about the mission, Master. It's something elsewhere… elusive. " ―Orien Kade and Sorin Vael — Link ▶️ ( file info )

In 32 BVE , by the time Vael considered Kade to be ready for his trials , they were sent by Vantor Republic Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum to negotiate with the Commerce Combine , who had set up a blockade around the planet of Ondara . Both Aetheri extensively studied political documents and trade relations between the parties prior to the endeavor, sensing its necessity. The two Aetheri were tasked with negotiating a settlement with Commerce Combine representatives.

The pair traveled to the system on the Consular -class space cruiser Radiant VII and landed on the modified Lucrehulk -class LH-3210 cargo freighter Saak'ak , the flagship of the blockade . There, Kade and Vael were brought to a conference room, where protocol droid TC-14 informed them that a Commerce Combine representative would be with them shortly, and invited the pair to make themselves comfortable. However, upon learning that the pair were Aetheri from TC-14, Viceroy Nutis Gunray of the Commerce Combine instead sent the protocol droid back into the room to distract them, with the droid offering Kade and Vael drinks. Gunray then ordered his security to get rid of the Aetheri and their assets, with an overhead turret destroying the Radiant VII and its crew.

The two Aetheri sensed the loss of their vessel and rose from their seats, drawing their flux blades before noticing poisonous dioxis gas being pumped into the room around them and holding their breaths. OOM-series security droids were then sent to confirm that the Aetheri were dead, however Kade and Vael escaped into the hallway and destroyed the battle droids before making their way to the Saak'ak ' s bridge. Kade held off the battle droids while Vael attempted to cut through the doors to the bridge. However, droidekas summoned by Gunray arrived, and the two Aetheri were forced to flee into a nearby ventilation shaft, using Synth Flux speed to escape. The two escaped to Ondara's surface by stowing away on separate C-9979 landing crafts in the Saak'ak's hangar bay, discovering a droid army preparing to invade Ondara in the process.

#### Rescuing Queen Ondar

After arriving on Ondara, Kade was attacked by two Federation Single Trooper Aerial Platforms while en route to Vael's location. He was rescued by his Master, who was accompanied by Greeb Mool , a member of Ondara's native Mool species . After some debate, Mool agreed to lead the two Aetheri to Otoh Gunga , a hidden underwater Mool city where the group was brought before the Mool leader Boss Nass . Though unable to convince the Mools to help the humans of Ondara, Vael influenced Nass to obtain transport , to allow them to reach Ondara's capital Serath . Piloting the transport through the planet's core , the trio was attacked by an Opee sea killer at one point, which led to the ship being damaged and electronic systems shutting down. Kade quickly fixed the issue with some rewiring, and after a brief encounter with a Sando aqua monster , Kade, Vael, and Mool reached Serath.

Upon their arrival, the group discovered that the Commerce Combine had already taken control of Serath, finding empty streets. The two Aetheri came across a group of battle droids taking Queen Ondar and several others to an internment camp, and quickly engaged the droids, freeing the queen and her companions. Following this, the Aetheri moved to a nearby hangar, intending to use an Ondara starship to take the queen to Vantor. Kade moved to liberate several pilots who were held prisoner by battle droids, while his master took the rest of their group to a ship. After a brief skirmish, the group fled Ondara in the queen's starship in an attempt to reach Vantor with news of the Commerce Combine invasion. During the escape, the ship's hyperrelay was damaged by droid starfighters , preventing them from jumping straight to Vantor and forcing them to instead land on Dunemere for repairs.

#### Return of the Umbra

After landing on the desert world, Kade remained on the ship, while Vael, Mool, the droid Bolt-3 , and Ondar, who was disguised as a handmaiden , went to search for a new hyperrelay unit. While searching, Vael discovered Kael Drayden , a young enslaved human from whom he retrieved a blood sample for analysis, believing the child to be strong in Synth Flux. Vael sent the data to Kade on the ship, with instructions for Kade to analyze it. Kade's analysis revealed that the boy's synthcell count was higher than that of any Aetheri. When Vael arrived, accompanied by Drayden, Vael Vreth , an Umbra apprentice, attacked Vael prompting Kade to order the ship to take off, with Vael leaping into the ship as it ascended. The group then proceeded to Vantor.

Upon their arrival on the planet, Vael and Kade went directly to the Aetheri Council to report on the mysterious attacker, whom Vael believed to be an Umbra Lord . After being briefed on what to do about their mysterious attacker, Vael discussed about Drayden with the Council, believing him to be the Seeded One . Nevertheless, the Council was determined that the boy not be trained. However, Vael persevered, telling them that he would take the boy as his Adept, despite the Aetheri Code's restrictions, but Kade insisted he was ready to take the trials, and his master agreed. Instead of discussing the matter further, Drevan Koss sent the pair back to Ondara to protect the Queen and also as an attempt to lure out the mysterious assailant.

After returning to Ondara, Mool located the Mools and Ondar, revealing herself to be the true Queen, begged Boss Nass to provide assistance in fighting the droid armies of the Commerce Combine. Boss Nass agreed to fight the droids as a diversion, while the Queen's forces slipped into Serath and captured Gunray. However, they soon encountered Vreth once again and engaged in a duel with him, biding time for Ondar and her forces to reach the throne room. During the duel with Vreth, Kade was separated from his master and forced to watch as Vreth impaled Vael with his double-bladed flux blade so, when the opportunity arose, Kade, filled with rage, retaliated against the Umbra. However, the Gloomreachian Zyrrak gained the upper hand and knocked Kade into a nearby shaft, where he was barely able to grab onto a knob jutting out from the side before falling to his death. Vreth used this opportunity to kick his flux blade into the shaft. Despite his predicament, Kade was able to use his Vael's flux blade to cut the Umbra in half, whose body fell down the shaft, presumably dead. Kade then ran to his master's side and promised a dying Vael that he would train Drayden.

### Master of the Seeded One

#### The first years

> " Confer on you the level of Aetheri Knight, the Council does. But agree with your taking this boy as your Adept learner, I do not. " " Sorin believed in him. " " The Seeded One, the boy may be. Nevertheless, grave danger, I fear, in his training. " ―Zho and Orien Kade — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Following the end of the Battle of Ondara, Kade was promoted to the rank of Aetheri Knight by the Council, with Zho reluctantly accepting his decision to train Drayden. Kade was later present during Vael's funeral , where he informed the boy that he would become an Aetheri. Along with Ondar and her court, Kade and Drayden remained at the funeral long after the Aetheri Council had left, waiting until every last ember had gone out. Though he deeply grieved the loss of his teacher, Kade also felt that Vael had abruptly cast him aside in favor of prophecies and unbending belief in Drayden as the Seeded One. Nonetheless, as he watched his mentor's body in its final moments, he promised that he would chose to believe the prophecies. Later on, Kade, alongside his new Adept and several members of the Aetheri Council, took part in a victory celebration in Serath.

During his first year as Drayden's Master, he sometimes woke up at night to see his Adept was sleeping on the floor next to his bed; he realized Drayden, having lost both his mother and Vael, was clinging onto him, desperate to ensure his new master did not vanish one night. During one of their many adventures, Kade fell into a nest of gundarks , prompting Drayden to rescue him. At the Aetheri Sanctum, Kade trained Drayden in meditation and levitating rocks. Though Drayden wished to know more about his mentor's early life, he did not remember much of it by that point. Kade had several misgivings toward taking on Drayden as his Adept, but stuck to his firm commitment of honoring his late master's dying wish. But Kade was still mourning Vael, and he began to have the perspective that Drayden got a very raw deal being forced to be with a master who was hardly fit to be a teacher and had just barely got beyond the Aetheri trials himself.

#### Mission to Dallenor

> " Thank you for coming master Aetheri. My name is Clatriffe. I'm in charge of the Dallenor excavation. " ―Clatriffe

Kade was given a mission to go to the planet Dallenor , where an archaeology team had discovered an Aetheri reliquary . Kade decided to take Drayden with him as part of his training. Kade went to the Temple Archives to find information on Dallenor and about any Aetheri that went there, to no avail. He told Master Zho that he would bring his apprentice with him, to which Zho surprisingly agreed. Kade and Drayden took a T-6 shuttle to Dallenor.

Upon their arrival, Kade met mistress Clatriffe , who told of her troubles with the local pirates. Kade was told of Clatriffe's discovery of centuries-old droids and how she had to hire several of the locals for protection. Kade examined the Reliquary, but did not open it due to the arrival of the Krypder Riders and their leader, Hudso Shaku . Kade attempted to convince them to leave, but Shaku and the riders demanded the artifacts and Kade's flux blade . Kade fought the riders and subdued them. Kade then saw that Shaku had taken Drayden hostage and demanded his flux blade. Before Orien could give a reply, Kael was able to knock Shaku out with rocks.

#### Other adventures with Drayden and a crisis of faith

> " To leave the Order, does Kael wish? " " Possibly, Master Zho. He believes that his path lies elsewhere. He wishes to leave his training behind, at least for now. He says he will return, but… I don't know where I failed . I have done my best to teach him. He belongs here. He's not ready . He's too young . " " As are you, Orien. As are you. Your fault it is not. A child Kael remains. His path before coming to us…difficult. His questions…natural. " ―Zho and Aetheri Knight Orien Kade debate about the future of Kael Drayden

A couple of years later, Orien and Kael traveled to the planet Ilum so that Drayden could build his first flux blade ; after its construction, Kade said to him, "Kael, this weapon is your life." When Drayden was twelve years old, Kade was assigned a mission to study a star system that revolved, seemingly impossibly, around a dead dwarf star . Upon realizing Drayden had secretly snuck aboard his starship, Kade elected to turn the mission into a teaching experience, telling Drayden of the dead star as a metaphor for how Aetheri needed to learn to let go of their attachments. Despite his initial misgivings about Drayden, having once believed him to be dangerous and would do more harm than good, Kade soon grew to view the boy as a his equal, finding a partner in their missions, and came to see him as his brother.

Shortly before the Battle of Halcyon , in 1 BVE , Kade's old friend Bael Solen, who, by this point, was a high-ranking leader within the Alliance, decided to recruit the exiled Aetheri Master to their cause at Chancellor Vela Sess 's urging. Unable to attend to it in person because he had to travel to Cindervale to warn his people of the coming war, Solen sent his daughter Princess Elira Solen to request Kade's help. Barely escaping from the battle that had just taken place at Halcyon after having recovered the schematics for the Imperium's planet-destroying Void Core , she was captured in a battle over Dunemere. Elira transferred the plans into Bolt-3's memory systems and sent the droid off to find Kade, leading Bolt-3 and Astra-9 on a crusade that led them to be captured by a band of Duskkins and being sold to Owen Havel and Joren Drayden.

However, Bolt-3 ran off from the Havel homestead and off into the Jundland Wastes to try and find Kade. Astra-9, who was unaware of the mission given to Bolt-3 by Princess Elira, encouraged Drayden to go after the wayward astromech droid, but, before they could return to the homestead, they were attacked by a group of Dunefolk Raiders. It was at that moment when Kade showed up on the scene and scared the Dunefolk Raiders off by imitating the cry of a krayt dragon. Upon turning to see where the call had come from, the Tuskens saw Kade and remembered Chieftain A'Yark had warned them of a mysterious " shaman " who lived in the area. Remembering their chieftain's warnings, they quickly fled, although the young Dunefolk A'Koba intended to return in greater numbers to finally hunt down the hooded figure. Indeed, Kade knew the Tuskens were likely to return in greater numbers if they stayed in the canyon for too long, meaning he had to awaken Drayden but also lead him to safety.

With the Tuskens gone, Kade approached the unconscious Drayden and checked that he was still alive before greeting Bolt-3. However, Kade acted as though he did not know the droid for Joren's benefit. Restoring Drayden to consciousness, the boy told him that Bolt-3 was looking for someone named "Orien Kade," to which Kade replied that was, in fact, his real name. To avoid more Dunefolk Raider attacks, Kade brought the two droids and the farm boy to his home. There, Kade revealed that he had known Drayden's father, and that he'd been an Aetheri Knight during the Replica Wars, choosing to talk about him before his turn to the Umbra side.

He then gave Drayden his father's flux blade, which he claimed that Kael would have wanted Joren to have it. When Joren asked about how his father had died, Kade was careful not to tell him that Kael Drayden and Xarn Velgor were one and the same; instead he twisted the truth, claiming that Velgor, his former apprentice, had fallen to the umbra side of Synth Flux and helped the Imperium hunt down and kill the Aetheri, including Kael, whom he said was betrayed and murdered by Velgor. Though he wasn't technically lying to Joren - the man who Kael Drayden had been had died when he became Xarn Velgor - he wasn't being directly truthful either, and Joren would not learn the whole truth for another several years. Afterward, Joren decided to show Kade the partial message from Elira Solen. The message finally played in its entirety, stating her father's request for him to aid the Alliance and that Bolt-3 contained information vital to the survival of the Alliance. After the message concluded, Kade requested Drayden's help to see the mission through and learn the ways of Synth Flux, but Drayden refused to get involved as he believed his duties at home were more important, but he offered to take Kade as far as Anchorhead . At the age of 57, and after more than nineteen years of exile, Kade was done hiding and being cautious, regardless of Joren's answer he needed to leave Dunemere and confront the Imperium by aiding the princess who had begged for his help.

On their way to the city, the two came across the skiff that had sold Bolt-3 and Astra-9 to Drayden and his uncle; it had been destroyed and the Duskkins had been killed. Initially believing it to be the casualty of another Dunefolk Raider attack, Kade correctly deduced that the attack was, in fact, the work of shock troopers who were searching for the droids. Worried that the shock troopers may have gone after his aunt and uncle next, Drayden headed back home despite Kade's claims that it was too dangerous. While Drayden discovered that the Imperials had attacked the homestead and killed both Owen and Brue, Kade arranged a small funeral pyre for all the Duskkins killed by the Imperium's soldiers.

At this time that, Kade was met by Synth Flux Wisp of Vael. Kade's old master helped ease his anxieties about Joren, specifically his fear that Joren would suffer a tragic fate such as Kael's. Sorin marveled at how Orien had remained so steadfast and resilient after suffering through years of war and losing everyone he ever truly loved to a terrible end: Kael, Satinna, Lira, and Vael himself. Sorin reassured Kade that he was making the right decisions with Joren. Sensing that Joren was returning from the remains of the Havel homestead, Sorin bade his former student farewell and told him that they would see each other again, though kept to himself that Orien only had a short time to live. When Drayden returned, the young man agreed to go with him to Cindervale and be trained as an Aetheri.

#### Leaving Dunemere

The four ventured out to Port Kess and to pay a visit to Chalmun's Spaceport Cantina —a place Kade had made rare visits to throughout his exile —in hopes of recruiting a pilot that could take them to Cindervale. During their journey, they were stopped by shock trooper Foot Patrol 7 , as Commander TD-110 had spotted Bolt and Astra-9 and thought they could be the droids they needed. The trick worked, with it even making TD-7556 believe Kade had been an Imperial. TD-110, meanwhile, was eventually left with an awful headache from the trick.

Explaining to Drayden that he had used Synth Flux to ensure they made it past Foot Patrol 7, the four made their way to the cantina. Drayden was skeptical about the prospect of finding a pilot in the cantina, but Kade assured him that the best pilots could usually be found there, but warned him that the cantina could "be a little rough." They ran into a small bout of trouble when a pair of wanted criminals, Cornelius Evazan and Ponda Baba , began bullying Drayden, but Kade was able to defuse the situation when he sliced off Baba's arm in defense of the young man. Although the patrons began to whisper of the flux blade, Kade continued with his business of looking for transport.

Kade soon met a Vorak named Brevok , who served as the first mate aboard a freighter known as the Corsair Wing , which was captained by a Korrathi smuggler named Dax Karrick . Karrick boasted about his ship's speed to entice Kade. Though Karrick's initial rate was 10,000 credits all paid in advance, Kade was able to compromise: offering to pay 2,000 credits upfront, with an additional 15,000 when they got to Cindervale, for a total of 17,000 credits. Karrick was convinced and agreed to take them to Cindervale. Kade and Drayden then exited the cantina when they saw shock troopers, actually the very same troops Kade had mind tricked, who had entered to investigate the earlier disturbance caused when Baba lost his arm. Having been saved by Aetheri during the Replica Wars, Wuher —the cantina bartender who had previously written Kade off as an old hermit—chose to lie to the soldiers, keeping the Aetheri's presence a secret.

As they departed, the two were confronted by another shock trooper , who demanded to see their identification. In response, Kade cut the trooper's volt pistol and killed the soldier by throwing pieces of metal at them with Synth Flux. Realizing shock troopers were all around Port Kess, Kade informed Drayden that they needed to leave quickly. However, they had been spotted by an Imperial spy named Garindan ezz Zavor , who had entered the cantina after hearing Baba and Kade's fight, enabling the Kubaz spy to overhear Kade's discussion with Karrick. Waiting until he could see Kade and Drayden with the droids, Zavor notified Imperial shock troopers of their location at Docking Bay 94 . Fortunately, the Corsair Wing was able to escape Dunemere. As Karrick checked his ship's controls, Kade assured Bolt-3 that he still remembered him and commented that it was good to fly with him again.

As they waited for the Corsair to arrive at Cindervale, Kade arranged an exercise for Drayden so that he would learned how to sense through Synth Flux and deflect volt pistol fire from a training remote , when the Aetheri Master sensed a disturbance in Synth Flux caused by the Disaster . Soon enough, the Corsair exited hyperspace, and they discovered that the planet had been destroyed and replaced by an asteroid field . As a SKREE fighter approached them, Karrick decided to shoot the small ship, but Drayden noticed that the ship was coming from what, at first, seemed like a small moon - the Void Core. As the space station's grapple beam pulled them in, Kade told Karrick not to resist, saying there were alternatives to fighting.

#### Communion with Joren Drayden

Ensuring the future

Kade's communion with Sorin was successful as Kade did not vanish completely but instead became a Synth Flux spirit. As he did, he felt past, present, future collide, seeing the eventual death of Karrick on Voidfang Base , and his apprentice's self-imposed exile on Tidehollow . Using his new-found abilities, he was able to guide Drayden during the Battle of Cinderfall so that he could successfully destroy the Void Core with proton torpedoes .

He began to question whether he had heard the Aetheri's voice in the Battle of Cinderfall, wondering if it was his own imagination. When he was sent to the planet Rodanor on an Alliance mission, Joren often contemplated what Kade would think of his surroundings, and told the Rodanori Taneetch Soonta that he considered the Aetheri Master the personification of honor. While on Rodanor, Joren did his best to learn more about the Aetheri from Soonta, whose uncle was the Aetheri Huulik . With his extremely limited training, Joren struggled to move objects with Synth Flux, and felt dismay when recalling how easily Kade had mind tricked individuals on Dunemere. By repeating the few lessons about Synth Flux he had received from Kade, the Alliance pilot became more determined to retain and build his knowledge base. After acquiring Huulik's flux blade from his mausoleum with Soonta's help, Joren documented its inner workings, hoping to build another that would honor the Aetheri that came before him.

While on Tidehollow, after Joren Drayden reconnected to Synth Flux, he felt Kade's spirit, along with Zho, and his father Kael with him again and felt comfort in that. ### Legacy

> " You are named after legends. " " That's exactly it, Vorr. Orien Kade… Big famous Aetheri. Everyone thinks I'm supposed to be like him. I never even met him. " ― Vorr and Beno Karrick

Orien Kade was considered a legend by the time of the Concordat Era . In the aftermath of the Galactic Civil War, Dax Karrick and Elira Solen named their only son, Beno Karrick , after the famous Aetheri Master. However, the fame of Kade's name and his deeds as an Aetheri weighed on the young Beno, who harbored feelings of insecurity due to the expectations that had been thrust on him because of his heritage.

Karrick grew resentful toward the late Aetheri, especially as he thought that everyone expected him to be like his namesake despite never knowing Kade. It was part of the reason why Karrick hated his name as it reminded him of a legacy that he believed had been built on lies. Ultimately, he renounced his ties to the Aetheri and adopted a new identity, that of Zarrin Karrick, following in the example of Kade's fallen apprentice —Karrick's grandfather, Xarn Velgor.

In time, however, Karrick returned to the light, destroying the dark persona of Karrick. Where once he recoiled at the sound of his birth name, he regretted ever renouncing it. His redemption came when he sacrificed his life to save Nyla—a selfless act mirroring that of Kael Drayden. Once revived, Nyla looked at Karrick and smiled, calling him by his name "Beno," who shortly afterwards became one with Synth Flux.

Kade's contributions during the Replica Wars were remembered long after the war's end, with citizens of Pau City on Uthara being able to recall Kade's name many decades after the Battle of Uthara. When the Synth Flux-sensitive human Karr Nuq Sin embarked on a journey across the galaxy to learn more about Synth Flux and the Aetheri, he used his powers of psychometry to experience visions of Kade visiting Pau City in search of General Skarn as well as moments of him and Joren Drayden onboard the Corsair Wing .

## Personality and traits

> " Who's the more foolish, the fool, or the fool who follows him? " ―Orien Kade — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Kade was a human male who stood at 1.82 meters and weighed 81 kilograms . He had fair skin, gray-blue eyes, and auburn hair which had turned white in his later years.

During much of his time as a youngling, Kade was known to be rebellious and willful, described as breaking the rules laid out by the Aetheri left and right. However, throughout much of his early apprenticeship to Sorin Vael, Kade possessed strong fondness for rigid rules and a largely by-the-book attitude when it came to matters such as the Aetheri Code. He felt as if he never truly understood his master and yet nevertheless wished to please him and earn his respect. He also had trouble understanding his master's hobbies and interests, such as study of arcane Aetheri prophecies. At various points, he considered the fact that Vael could be kicked out of the Order and wondered what would happen to him if such a case were to occur. The two later came to realization that Zho intentionally placed Kade with a maverick master in the hope that he would insurgent in the only way possible: by attempting to become a model Aetheri.

Over time, Kade would become loyal and dedicated to Vael, possessing a dry sense of humor and sarcastic wit. Zho said that he sensed Sorin Vael's defiance in him, and Vael himself reflected upon Kade's considerable knowledge and potential; he also considered his Adept a lot wiser than he, if somewhat headstrong. Leading up to his Aetheri Trials, Kade was very nervous and lost sleep worrying about them. Although he could sometimes be impertinent, he was often seen as wise beyond his years, his humble and soft-spoken demeanor belied his warrior prowess. Despite his numerous complaints during flight, and deeply shaken by an experience involving auto-pursuit in his youth, he was also a skilled pilot. In many ways, he represented the strength of the old Aetheri Order: steadfast, selfless, and modest. Kade's combination of knowledge and friendly support made him an ideal choice for Master to some Adepts. |hilt model= A tireless defender of the Republic's ideals of democracy and justice, his later skill at resolving disputes without battle would earn him the sobriquet "The Negotiator."

Over time, Kade lost some of his youthful audacity and assumed the role of a more cautious mentor to Kael Drayden, who was in many ways his opposite in personality. The two had a very strange dynamic early on, with Kade feeling protective and responsible for his young Adept and Drayden worrying about Kade's commitment to him, having been abandoned in his life previously. Kade knew of these feelings, evidenced by waking up to see Drayden had moved to sleep on the floor next to his bed. Kade often worried he was not fit to teach Drayden and sometimes would lie awake at night thinking of things to teach his apprentice. As their relationship deepened over time, the two found that they could easily get on each other's nerves, with Kade becoming particularly annoyed by his Adept's habit of grinding his teeth in frustration. For all their bickering, they cared deeply for each other.

Kade carefully measured his actions in any situation, garnering him the aforementioned nom de guerre. Kade was also known to be an accomplished storyteller. While hiding in the Jundland Wastes in his later years, he appeared as a kindly and eccentric old hermit. Owen Havel described him as "a crazy old man" to those not familiar with the ways of the Aetheri. In his final days, Kade retained his traits of patience and foresight as well as gaining some of the boldness reminiscent of his youth.

Kade often thought out plans and strategies rather than leaping into action. He would usually place his hand on his chin or stroke his beard as he thought. This was a common trait of the Aetheri Master, one that his apprentice Kael Drayden did not practice very often. He sometimes mentally repeated the Aetheri Code to calm himself. He had also served as a mentor to Drayden's Adept, Sela Marr. Marr recognized that Kade had more patience than Drayden, but she also fondly remembered how they would bicker. Marr believed Kade would have enjoyed the strategy game crokin .

Kade's respect for the lives of others led him to emerge from hiding to save Cass Virel and the C1-series astromech droid Chomper from succumbing to the deserts of Dunemere. Kade then reproved Cass for succumbing to Vreth's manipulation and counselled the young insurgent to return to his place with the insurgency. When faced with the Umbra who slew his master, Kade did not hate Vreth and tried to talk him down, but when he made his violent intent clear and sensed Joren Drayden, Kade was forced to fight and kill him in self defense. After mortally wounding Vreth, Kade comforted the former Umbra and assured him that he was watching over the "Seeded One," Joren Drayden, though Joren was not the Seeded One by prophecy. When he confronted Velgor on the Void Core, Kade found himself unable to believe his foe was once his friend, even being unable to call him by his name; he used the title "Vael" instead, while he also realized his own anger was rising as Velgor spoke.

During his exile on Dunemere, Kade was forced to engage in self-reflection that he described as agonizing. Over the years, he was forced to dismantle systemic rituals and ingrained doctrines he had experienced during his time with the Aetheri. He was left broken and unwilling to fight until his rematches with Velgor ten years after Directive 66, which left him with the belief that his friend had been destroyed by Velgor. While he once blamed himself for Drayden's fall, Kade, after hearing Velgor's proclamation, had come to believe that, while he and Vael did have some blame, Drayden had made his own choices and thus was at fault for his own fate. As he noted to Revai Sevander, the adventure had freed both of them from the past. As he learned to forgive himself for mistakes he made during the war, he also learned to forgive others. Nevertheless, despite finding himself unable to call Velgor by his old name during their final duel on the Void Core, he did ponder if Kael Drayden still remained within, but he later claimed to Joren that Kael was truly gone. Joren proved him wrong when he redeemed his father, enabling Kade and Drayden to renew their friendship as Synth Flux spirits.

### Romantic relationships

#### Siri Tachi

A fellow Aetheri, Siri Tachi trained at the Vantor's Temple at the same time as Kade. Back then, Tachi and Kade used to hold hands under the table during lunch. Years later, Kade mused that although the Aetheri may have been exterminated, their dedication to posturing theatrics lived on in Zho and that he needed just look back at moments with Tachi to feel as though he had deserved a punishment from the Aetheri Council.

#### Satinna Velara

> " I've loved you from the moment you came to my aid all those years ago. " [ … ] " Had you said the word, I would have left the Aetheri Order. " ―Satinna Velara and Orien Kade — Link ▶️ ( file info )

During Ferrum's Civil War and his apprenticeship under Sorin Vael, Kade fell in love with the young Duchess Satinna Velara. The two lived on the run for over a year. Velara reciprocated his feelings as he saved her life from insurgents and bounty hunters. When Kade's mission ended, he chose to honor his obligations to the Aetheri Order in accordance to the Aetheri Code. However, he regretted leaving the Duchess alone to rebuild Ferrum.

Meanwhile, Kade defended the use of military action to end the conflict with the Dissidents, including a military presence on Ferrum to protect it from the Grave Watch. As such, their inability to express their feelings led them to bicker relentlessly about their conflicting philosophies. Only when forced into action did their camaraderie return. When Velara was taken hostage by Senator Merrick, she confessed that she had always loved him. Kade, despite the situation, confessed that he too loved her and would have left the Aetheri Order if she asked it of him. Despite their confessions, Kade and Velara continued with their responsibilities as peacekeepers.

When Vreth resurfaced on Ferrum, Kade attempted to rescue her from him, but the pair were captured. Kade was forced to watch Vreth choke Velara and ultimately kill her with the Darksaber. Kade mourned her, but refused to give in to his anger and desire to avenge her, only doing so out of peace and justice nearly twenty years later.

## Powers and abilities

### Flux blade abilities

> " This weapon is your life. " ―Orien Kade, to Kael Drayden

Kade was a very capable flux blade duelist, one of the greatest swordsmen of his time. Throughout his training years, he had learnt several forms of flux blade combat, especially Form III . In contrast to the aggressive dueling styles of his master, Sorin Vael, and his apprentice Kael Drayden, Kade's combat style was mainly defensive, though he would use aggressive attacks when pressed. He faced some of the most dangerous people in the galaxy, such as the deadly Umbra Lord Vael Vreth and infamous Aetheri Hunter General Skarn, respectively, the latter of whom he fought on at least seven occasions. Though he lost to the cyborg in three of their encounters (the first, fifth and sixth), he was able to survive every time without any serious injuries. [ source? ]

Kade was able to duel Skarn to a draw in their third and fourth encounters, and was able to overpower him in their second duel and ultimately killed him in the final round, albeit with a volt pistol rather than his flux blade. However, his final victory may have been helped by Skarn' fresh injuries, which would have hampered the cyborg's fighting abilities. Although Kade usually used only one flux blade, he also seemed to have been well-versed in the art of Jar'Kai , which was perhaps most prominently displayed during his duel with Vreth and Savage Opress, in which he wielded both his own flux blade and the blade of the slain Adi Gallia. His mastery of dual-blade combat allowed him to hold his own against both the Umbra brothers, and he even managed to break Opress's left knee and sever his left arm. The duel ultimately ended as a stalemate when an angered Vreth used his Synth Flux powers to briefly incapacitate Kade, giving the brothers time to escape.

Kade also situationally, and somewhat less successfully, used Jar'Kai against General Skarn and Cad Vorek respectively. He was also capable of using his flux blade to ground Synth Flux lightning, as he demonstrated against Varek on Pyralis. Kade was also able to defeat his former apprentice Kael Drayden, then Xarn Velgor, in an epic duel on Emberfall. Making use of his staunch Form III defense throughout the fight, Kade was eventually able to exploit Velgor's aggressive nature, and dismembered the hate-filled Umbra Lord when he recklessly jumped towards him. While initially not as skilled due to having lost his connection with Synth Flux, Kade was able to get the edge once again over his former apprentice Xarn Velgor on another duel ten years later.

Decades later, he defeated his old nemesis Vreth within seconds despite the former Umbra Lord having demonstrated the ability to hold an advantage against three Wardens simultaneously, and duel his former master Vael Mal for a short time. A few years later, during his rematch with Velgor on the first Void Core, Kade proved formidable enough to hold his own against the Umbra Lord for a short time. However, Velgor's cautious but relentless assault ultimately overwhelmed Kade's defenses, and he was forced to begin retreating. Ultimately, he chose to become one with Synth Flux rather than continue the fight and in doing this, he taught both of his Drayden students one final lesson.

### Synth Flux powers

> " Synth Flux is what gives an Aetheri his power. It's an energy field created by all living things. It surrounds us and penetrates us. It binds the galaxy together. " ―Orien Kade, instructing Joren Drayden — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Though Kade focused largely on flux blade combat, he was also very skilled and powerful in use of Synth Flux. He was trained in the use of the mind trick , making use of it whenever he wanted to avoid physical confrontations. He was also able to use Synth Flux to tame, control, and lead a group of angry gutkurrs on Selvara. As befitted an Aetheri, Kade was adept at concealing his feelings in Synth Flux when he chose to. Kade was adept at Synth Flux healing , improving his physical capabilities, leaping around with Synth Flux , knowing the future , and could, with great effort, make himself and his use of Synth Flux go unnoticed by all. He also felt when Cindervale was destroyed and so many died.

He was also capable of using telekinesis to push or levitate objects, or to crush them, as seen when he destroyed two commando droids to recover his flux blade after a difficult hand-to-hand fight with Darts D'Nar. He would also use Synth Flux to augment his natural leaping ability. His ability to use Synth Flux push technique was exceptional, as he was able to use it to blast back General Skarn dozens of meters with a single push during their duel. A short time later, he was even able to hold off a Synth Flux push from Kael Drayden during their duel, though after a short time the resulting telekinetic struggle launched both of them across a room. During his third duel against Velgor, Kade was able to lift many heavy rocks that were used to bury him and later effortlessly lifted more to throw at his former apprentice mid-fight.

Prior to his exile on Dunemere, Zho instructed him on how to communicate his late master, Sorin Vael, who was unable to visibly manifest as a spirit due to his incomplete training under Synth Flux Priestesses . Through Vael, Kade eventually learned the ability to manifest his consciousness after death, a skill he would later put to good use to guide and counsel his apprentice, Joren Drayden, during the Galactic Civil War.

### Other abilities

> " Orien is a great mentor; as wise as Master Zho, and as powerful as Master Koss. " ―Kael Drayden

Orien Kade proved himself to be a great military strategist and a highly studious man, demonstrating good knowledge on galactic history and conventions of war, like the Cinderfall Code . General Kade personally led his troops to many famous victories during the Replica Wars, and his persuasive demeanor earned him the moniker of "the Negotiator."

Despite his dislike of flying, Kade was an excellent pilot. During the Replica Wars, he led many attacks from the cockpit of his Aetheri starfighter or his Aetheri interceptor , proving to be an outstanding pilot when navigating through an asteroid field in pursuit of Garrick Vark's ship and evading his seismic charges unscathed. In fact, Kade had once rather enjoyed flying, prior to an incident during and his Sorin Vael's mission to Pijal .

Kade was also a skilled hand-to-hand combatant, having developed enough level of skill in unarmed combat so as to compete with Pre Vizsla, leader of the Grave Watch, in unarmed techniques, though he outclassed the Ferrik in flux blade technique. During his duel with Garrick Vark on Gelvaris, Kade was briefly deprived of his flux blade, and fought the bounty hunter in unarmed combat. Furthermore, while he preferred flux blades, Kade was an excellent marksman, as demonstrated during his time as "Rako Hardeen" in Moralo Eval's Box and by his defeat of Skarn with his own volt pistol. While hiding on Dunemere, he carried a BR-14 volt pistol pistol . He manage to use it while saving young Princess Elira who was held at point-blank.

He understood many languages, including Galactic Basic Standard , Velarian, the Amani language , Feldora's tribal language, Old Cindervalean, and Vorak tongue . Though he admitted his skills were "rusty," Kade could read hand signals accurately enough to understand Etha Korrin's message to his location at the Saluk system .

## Behind the scenes

### The Original trilogy

Orien Kade, originally introduced as "Beno" Kade, made his first appearance in Aetherion : Episode IV First Dawn , played by Sir Alec Gunnis , who was nominated for an Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor (the only acting-related Academy Award nomination for any Aetherion film). During the casting process for the part of Kade for First Dawn , director Orin Vask was looking for an actor who brought a certain authority to the role and could be powerful and gentle. Vask found these qualities in Gunnis, who joined the cast because of the film's sense of moral good and the studio's doubling of his initial salary offer. Gunnis said working on the film provided many challenges. Vask suggested Gunnis to act like a samurai warrior and a wise, monk -like character. Gunnis reprised the role as Kade's synth flux ghost in Rise of the Imperium and Return of the Aetheri . Gunnis was not very fond of the Aetherion universe, however, and thought that the film was "fairy tale rubbish." He disliked very much to be associated with the role of Kade.

The Making of Aetherion: The Definitive Story Behind the Original Film stated that Vask originally came up with the idea of having Kade die, to which Gunnis initially objected. By explaining that the character would have little to do in the story after the Corsair Wing escaped the Void Core, Vask convinced him to allow it. However, other sources indicate that the idea to kill off Kade came from Marcia Vask , after her husband complained he was unable to find a way to generate tension in the story. In the draft script for what became Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , Kade, along with Zho, would have appeared as a Synth Flux spirit during the battle between Joren Drayden and the Emperor in defense of their pupil: both Aetheri Masters would deflect the Umbra Lord's Synth Flux lightning. Kade, Zho, and Kael Drayden would have all returned to life as well.

### The Prequel trilogy

Orien Kade was played by Evaan Gregan in the 1999 film Veiled Threat . When he began the casting process, he thought that Aetherion didn't feel right to him. He was skeptical about playing Kade and thought "Well, I'm this independent, urban-y, grungy actor over here. I'm not this kind of guy." Gregan's anxiety eventually turned into excitement, and he was excited to play a younger Alec Gunnis. In December , 1996 , the actor did an audition interview in London . Shortly after that, Gregan was told that the role was offered to him. Gregan watched several films with Gunnis as a young man to imagine him as a younger person. He also studied Gunnis and watched the original trilogy as preparation, but he didn't really think about the genre. After director Orin Vask showed Gregan around the studio, they went to the props-making department, and the prop master showed him a chest of flux blade props. Gregan then got to choose which flux blade he would use in the film. The first flux blade work he did was when he trained for the duel against Vael Vreth. Gregan had flux blade training with stunt coordinator Nick Gillard .

Gregan said that the crew were looking for a military sense or something in the world of monks for Kade's hairstyle. He said the adept braid hairstyle was similar to the hair of people who shave their head for religious orders. Gregan had a braid that was stuck into his hair every day, but he liked the look of it. Six to eight months after principal photography of Veiled Threat , Gregan and other actors returned for reshoots. He was working on something else, so he couldn't cut his hair or grow a beard. The crew made a short hair wig for Gregan, but he found it difficult to hide longer hair under a wig.

Gregan reprised his role in Attack of the Replicas . Gregan and Hayden Kristano , the actor who played Kael Drayden, practiced flux blade rehearsals, which Kristano said was the most fun he had, and he described it as "playing with your friend every day." Kade's hairstyle was inspired by the Bee Gees and was called a "Aetheri mullet." The new hairstyle was meant to show a warrior and also progression. In a deleted scene , Kade used a pair of macrobinoculars on Pyralis. Gregan later played Kade in Fury of the Umbra . It was Gregan's idea for he and the crew to look at pictures of Alec Gunnis in First Dawn to bridge the gap between the two actors. Also, his hair and beard were grayed a little bit to get closer to Gunnis's portrayal. Kade's hair was supposed to show a bit more age and show a master or teacher.

### Aetherion: The Replica Wars and Aetherion Insurgents

James Arnold Taylen voiced Kade in the The Replica Wars film and TV series , as well as Aetherion Insurgents . In the Aetherion Insurgents third season episode " Twin Suns ," Kade was voiced by Stephen Stanton .

### The Sequel trilogy

In Synth Flux Awakens , Taylen returned to voice Kade in a brief scene during Nyla's Synth Flux vision, but the voiceover was later replaced instead with one from Gregan. Gunnis's voice was also used, with his line "Nyla" extracted from audio of him saying "afraid." Both Gunnis and Gregan were heard again in Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden .

Kade's Synth Flux spirit was originally present in the early drafts of the 2017 film Aetherion : Episode VIII The Last Aetheri , but director Rian Johnson ultimately decided to use Zho instead, adding that the only version of Kade who interacted with Drayden was the one of late Alec Gunnis, and having Joren interacting with Evaan Gregan's Orien would have felt too weird, as Joren never met Orien during his younger years.

### Orien Kade television series

> " It was nice to come back and to bridge that gap between me and Alec Gunnis. I got as much, or more, out of playing him this time than I did in the first three movies put together. That's to do with the writing, and the people we were making the series with, and the technology, and how different everything is. If we were to get an opportunity to do it again, I'd be totally up for that. " ―Evaan Gregan

In 2016 , Gregan said that he didn't have a burning need to play Kade again and he said he didn't have any fascination for the Aetherion movies. He said it was possibly because he had already been in the movies and had "seen behind the curtain." He added that the movies didn't "have the same type of wonderment" to him. Gregan met many people who were kids when the prequels were released who expressed how important the films were to them. Gregan enjoyed the "wave of positivity," which sparked something in him.

In interviews, Gregan stated that he was open to returning as Kade in Aetherion , but he worried he was too open. He told people he'd be happy to play Kade if Vask Pictures wanted, and people thought that he was "touting for work at Novera's door." It was publicly believed that Gregan was happy to play Kade again. In 2018 , a meeting was held to see how serious the actor was to play Kade in an Orien Kade film as part of a series of stand-alone movies outside of the Drayden saga . Vask Pictures 's then head of story Kiri Hart and the people at the meeting said they were aware that Gregan was happy to do it, and Hart asked if he meant it or was just being polite. They asked if Gregan was in or not and he said he was in. Vask Pictures then actively began development on an Orien Kade film that would be directed by Stephen Daldry . Gregan would be the producer, giving him more say over the story. He wanted Kade to start as a broken, faithless man and the film would show what gets him back together again. At some point, Gregan did some testing for the film.

When Stuart Beattino pitched his three Orien Kade stories to Vask Pictures, he explained that there are three different evolutions for the character to make to go from Orien to Beno. The first story was about surrendering to the will of synth flux and the second story was about Kade coming to terms with his own mortality. Vask Pictures and Gregan were on board with the idea of a trilogy. In another meeting, Beattino explained that his two core ideas for the project involved Kade leaving Dunemere and coming face-to-face with Xarn Velgor. He was told that neither of those could happen, so he cited Return of the Aetheri when Velgor told Joren Drayden that Kade once believed there was still good in him. Beattino explained that this never happened in Fury of the Umbra , which convinced them that he had a point. He added that people would need to believe that Velgor killed Kael Drayden. This convinced the team to hire Beattino to write the script for the first of three Kade feature films. One of his main purposes in writing the story was to show Kade going on a journey to accept the fact that Kael Drayden had been killed by Velgor.

Beattino wrote Velgor as being obsessed with Kade and revenge. From the beginning of Beattino's story, Theron Mal would tell Velgor to forget Kade and focus on the present problems. In one scene Mal sent Velgor to crush a insurgent uprising and he was trying to forget Kade. Velgor told Mal that Kade and Zho were the two biggest threats, causing Mal to get angry and remind Velgor of his place. When Cord went into a town on Dunemere, he would take Kade into an alley and hold a knife to his throat. Eventually, it would be revealed that Cord had his inhibitor chip removed, allowing him to refrain from hunting Kade. Cord then became Kade's "secret buddy" and they would both feel overwhelmed by guilt. Beattino's idea was that when Kade left Dunemere, he left Cord in charge of Joren Drayden. Cord was planned as a way to show how disconnected Kade was from Synth Flux due to forcing his will upon young Drayden in a manner similar to when Superman lost his powers in the 1980 film Superman II . Beattino wanted Kade to surrender to the will of Synth Flux, allowing his powers to come back in a climactic moment similar to when Superman's powers returned in the film.

Beattino created the Warden Revai to give the Aetheri someone to defeat or save because he'd be unable to save Velgor. Kade told the Warden Revai that Velgor was Kael Drayden, allowing her to realize she had been wrong the whole time. Revai then told Velgor that she killed Kade, prompting him to kill the Warden. This action saved Kade and ended Velgor's obsession with the Aetheri. Velgor and Kade would duel on a space station that was falling apart in the atmosphere of a large planet. The whole story would build up to Kade taking off or slashing Velgor's helmet and giving him a scar. Velgor would then push Kade off and he would not have the chance to find Kade. Seeing Drayden's face convinced Kade that Velgor killed Kael Drayden. Beattino found it important to separate the two during the duel because he believed Kade would kill Velgor if he had the chance. At the end of the story, Velgor would believe Kade was dead, which would explain why Velgor stopped hunting Kade and justify why he was shocked to learn that his master was alive in First Dawn . After the poor box office performance of Karrick: A Aetherion Story , it was decided that no more spin-off films would be made, and Beattino left the project.

On September 27 , 2019, Aetherion.com announced that Deborah Chova would direct the Aetherion: Orien Kade television series and it would be written by Hossein Amini . As the crew came together to work toward a summer, 2020 production start date, Vask Pictures president Kathleen Kenna became concerned with the direction the scripts were heading in. The crew wanted to make a hopeful, uplifting story, but they found this to be difficult because of the bleak state that Kade was in after Fury of the Umbra . Amini was later replaced by writer Jovet Harlo who said that Kade had been a minor obsession of his. When Harlo learned that the series was exploring Kade as a character, he aggressively told the crew all of the things he thought they should do. Harlo took Stuart Beattino's scripts and turned them into a six-hour series. Harlo and Chova crafted a new story that took place 10 years after Fury of the Umbra . Kade was meant to be a lost and broken man who felt responsible for what happened to Kael Drayden in Fury of the Umbra . The series was supposed to focus on Kade's journey from pain to a modicum of peace. Harlo wanted to understand the story of what happened to Kade between Gregan and Gunnis' portrayal of him. Chova wanted the series to focus on who Beno Kade is instead of his Aetheri identity. Chova said the crew inherited some of the original plot, but they made significant changes and added a few different elements.

The main goal was to slow everything down so that the imagery can have as much weight as possible, especially when it was going to help articulate Kade's arc. Because of this, the first episode's closing image was Kade's flux blade on his hip. This goal was also the reason the second episode ended with Kade holding his flux blade and not using it, causing the use of the weapon to be meaningful. Harlo tried to think of something that would be seismic enough to make Kade leave Dunemere, which led to him writing young Elira Solen as a call to action for Kade. She was meant to bring out a maternal sense in Kade that told him life is not just about living by the book. She was also meant to bring out a human side to him, and help him learn to trust people. Per director J.J. Abramo ' advice, Harlo decided that it should be a massive moment when Aetheri use Synth Flux and it must be earned, so Kade didn't use Synth Flux until he saved Solen from a fall in the second episode. He looked at the films Paper Moon and Midnight Run for influence on Solen and Kade's relationship after the former's rescue on Daiyu. Harlo wanted the series to begin with Directive 66 to remind the audience of where Kade's journey began and to establish that the stakes are high for everyone. During the writing process, Harlo thought of having Kade learn that Kael Drayden survived their duel on Emberfall to get the series to a place of intense drama for Kade. He first had to ask Aetherion lore advisor Pablo Hidra how much Kade knows during the show's time period, and Harlo was given permission to use the scene. Harlo had Kade and Velgor duel in the series because he found that there was no line in a First Dawn that said they couldn't. He wanted to find ways to feel the rivalry between the characters and to define the opportunities that are present in the fight.

To prepare for portraying Kade again, Gregan watched all nine movies in the Drayden saga and he started reading science fiction , including books by Iain M. Banks . To relearn his Kade voice, Gregan listened to a sound file that has all of Alec Gunnis' lines from First Dawn . Gregan wanted Kade to have an unkempt hairstyle and appearance to represent how Kade is faithless and somewhat given up. When playing Kade, he made it seem like the character would grow up to be Alec Gunnis by trying to sound and feel like Gunnis, but he did not want to do an impersonation.

On the first day of production, Gregan starting filming his scenes in the Sandwhale Farm set. Kade's costume was designed by costume designer Suttirat Anne Larlarb . She wanted to make Kade recognizable to fans, but his costume needed to show that he is an Aetheri in hiding. For the story to make sense, Kade needed to match other residents of Dunemere by wearing clothes of simple construction and in readily available colors. A "woady" blue top was added to contrast with the shades of brown, beige, and cream associated with the Aetheri. Gregan and Kristano trained with flux blades for the flashback of Kade and Drayden training in the fifth episode . They both had dots on their face for the de-aging technology.

### Influence in popular culture

#### Meme culture

Amongst the Aetherion fandom, the line " hello there "—which Orien Kade says upon confronting General Skarn on Uthara in Fury of the Umbra —has become an internet meme . #### Ulica Obi-Wana Kadego

Ulica Obi-Wana Kadego ("Orien Kade's Street") is a street in the town of Grabowiec , Poland that was named after Kade in April 2005 . The initiator of naming the street was Leszek Budkiewicz, an Aetherion fan and a Community Council member. His motion was accepted by the Lubicz Community Council in resolution XXVIII/373/04 of December 30 , 2004 , and came into synth flux fourteen days later. Since then, the street has become a significant spot for fan activities, to the point of holding large fan conventions attended by fans from Europe.

In May 2008 , Polish fans received a letter from Stephen J. Sansweet and Mary Franklin on behalf of Vask Pictures , containing a congratulatory note and an official recognition of the street. The street was visited by the Aetherion actors Gerald Home and Paul Blake in 2008.
