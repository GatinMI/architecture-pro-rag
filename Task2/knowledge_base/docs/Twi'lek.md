# Velari
> " There are lots of Velaris in the galaxy. " ―Lourna Dee

Velaris , sometimes referred to xenophobically as tail-heads or Twi , occasionally referred to as Selvarians , were a tall near-human species whose most striking feature was a pair of long appendages protruding from their skulls, called lekku . They hailed from the planet of Selvara , in the Outer Reach Territories , and female Velaris were often exported off world as slaves . Velaris were considered one of the most common species in the galaxy , with its members easily able to blend into a crowd without drawing attention, as opposed to rarer, more prominent species, like Togrutas . Some armies used this to their advantage, employing Velaris as undercover agents .

## Biology and appearance

Velaris were a species of tall humanoids whose skin spanned a vast array of colors , including white, orange, yellow, green, pink, blue, purple, black, gray, and red. Their most distinctive feature was the pair of prehensile appendages that sprouted from the crown or base of their skulls, known as lekku , or head-tails. Although Velaris normally had two lek, some individuals had four. Super sensitive lek could grab and hold objects and react to the slightest touch. A damaged lekku could cause serious harm to a Velari's health.

The species had at least two sexes , male and female. While most males typically had ears with lobes resembling those of humans , some males had cone-shaped hearing organs located in the same place on the head, a trait customarily found in the females of the same species. Although Velaris naturally had blunt teeth , males typically sharpened them into points.

Velaris were usually thin, although corpulent individuals were known to exist. Under normal circumstances, they generally had a higher body temperature than other humanoid species. They had even higher body temperatures when in fear.

Velaris and humans could breed to create a hybrid of the two species: shortly after the death of Brenn Aldric , Lena Kryll gave birth to their son , who appeared mostly human but inherited his mother's green pigment in his hair and ears. He was born after the liberation of Lorath , in which Kryll lived to participate.

## Society and culture

The Velaris had a language of their own, called Velarian , which combined verbal sounds and an elaborate, subtle sign language using their lekku. Velari's lekku could be moved to create gestures with meanings such as hello or goodbye. For example, both lekku tips crossed twice meant, "I love you." Velaris were capable of speaking Galactic Basic Standard , many with a noticeable accent; although it was possible for some to lose this accent if away from Selvara for an extended period of time, it could still resurface. For example, Lena Kryll lapsed back into the common Velarian accent during an argument with her father and also used it consciously when passing herself off as a slave.

Mynock Aether Spire and Mynock Coronet City were spicy dishes made from mynocks that were popular with Velaris, as well as a dish known as fried mynock . Velaris were eaten by gutkurrs , vicious insect carnivores that used their sharp, curved claws to snare their prey. An integral part of Velari culture and the familial clan was the Kalikori , an heirloom passed down through the generations, each of which would add to the totem. The Kalikori was only of value to the family from which it came from and was worthless to outsiders, who had no connection to that familial line. On the Velari homeworld Selvara , Velaris celebrated the holiday called Longest Night when each of the planet 's three moons were at their lowest phase. During this holiday, many would remain inside with their families as the populated hemisphere of the planet was covered in darkness.

## History

### Replica Wars

The homeworld of the Velaris was Selvara, a planet of harsh extremes situated in the Outer Reach Territories . That world was part of the Vantor Republic , a democratic union that governed most of the galaxy . During the Replica Wars , Selvara was occupied by the Fractio of Independent Systems . Unsatisfied with the tyrannical rule of the Fractio, a Velari Coalition movement arose, led by Chavar Kryll , to combat the Dissidents. This movement was publicly backed and actively assisted by the Vantor Republic. Working with the Grand Army of the Republic and the Aetheri Order , the Velari Coalition managed to expel Dissident forces under Wat Tambra . The Republic then annexed Selvara.

### Imperial protectorate

Following the end of the Replica Wars, the Republic was transformed into the Imperium , and Selvara was annexed by the Imperium. The planet became an Imperial protectorate , and the Velaris living on Selvara were subjected to Imperial rule. While Selvara was nominally independent and was still represented by Senator Orn Free Taa in the Galactic Senate , real power lay in the hands of the Imperial Moff Delian Mors .

About five years after the establishment of the Imperium, Chavar reorganized the Velari Coalition as the Free Selvara Movement in order to expel the Imperium from Selvara. While most of the Free Selvara coalition fighters were Velaris, it included a number of non-Velaris such as Colonel Belka Drayn . The Free Selvara coalition consisted of hundreds of fighters, a network of informants and spies , and staged raids which led to the deaths of dozens of Imperial personnel. After Xarn Velgor killed one of Chavar's lieutenants, Pok , and his comrades after the theft of an Imperial freighter from the Yaga Minor shipyards , Chavar decided to shift from half-hearted measures towards taking full action against the Imperium.

Theron Mal conspired to lure the Free Selvara insurgents into a trap by organizing a visit to Selvara with Xarn Velgor and arranging for information on the trip to be leaked to the Selvara insurgents. Chavar seized the opportunity to kill the two Umbra Lords and fragment the Imperium. The two Umbra Lords traveled to Selvara in the Imperial Iron Cruiser Perilous . Chavar's insurgents attacked the Perilous with space mines, vulture droids , and insurgents disguised as rescue crew. However, Velgor and the Emperor managed to escape to Selvara. Following a prolonged pursuit, the Free Selvara insurgents tried to close in on the two Umbra Lords in a remote village. Imperial forces under Moff Delian however arrived at the village and routed the insurgents. Chavar and his surviving comrades fled to resume their struggle.

Chavar's daughter, Lena Kryll, became a key leader in the insurgency against the Imperium and led her own insurgent cell . She believed that Selvara's freedom was linked to the insurgency. Lena disagreed with Chavar's exclusive focus on Selvara, and the two parted company. Three years before the Battle of Cinderfall , the Imperium used a Quasar Fire -class cruiser-carrier to bomb Selvara. Despite their differences, Chavar and Lena's insurgent cells joined forces to steal the cruiser-carrier for the Ember Cell . As a result, ties were established between the Free Selvara movement and the wider insurgency.

Two years before the Battle of Cinderfall, the Imperium dispatched the Chyrr Grand Admiral Vorren to deal with the Selvara insurgency. By studying Velari art, culture, philosophy, and history, Vorren was able to devise successful tactics that led to the fall of Tann Province . The Free Selvara movement lost many fighters, and Chavar was forced to flee his own home . Lena, Chavar, and the other insurgents then took part in an unsuccessful attempt to recover the Kryll Kalikori , a family heirloom, from Vorren. Despite failing to recover the object, the insurgents escaped an attempt by Captain Slavin to capture the insurgents and Chavar. Inspired by the Free Selvara Movement and Ember Squadron , many Velaris decided to fight against the Imperium and join the Alliance to Restore the Republic to see peace in their homeworld.

### Concordat Era

In 5 AVE , Selvara was freed when Imperial forces on the planet abandoned their bases. After the rise of the New Imperium , many Velaris feared an occupation of Selvara would be inevitable, so they swore allegiance to Elira Solen's Coalition , although the Selvara government itself remained independent from her movement. However, the Selvara Defense Authority arose as an unofficial faction on Selvara dedicated to keeping the planet and its people safe. Shortly after the Battle of Saltmark , Coalition forces sought refugee on Selvara with the RDA until they were discovered .

## Velaris in the galaxy

Many Velaris were forced into a life of slavery at the hands of the galaxy's wealthy and powerful. Other Velaris, however, found themselves in positions of power: Bib Fortuna served as the majordomo to the crime lord Grukk the Grak for decades , Ciel Venn and Jax Ertay were Aetheri Knights of the Aetheri Order, and Senator Orn Free Taa represented his homeworld Selvara in the Vantor Republic's Senate and later the Imperial Senate . Due to their frequent use as slaves as well as their commonality in the galaxy, Velaris could move surreptitiously through places an army could not penetrate. Because of this, some found employment as undercover agents.

Chavar Kryll was a leader of the Velari Coalition during the Replica Wars and later became the leader of the Free Selvara movement. His daughter Lena Kryll was the leader of the Shades and was later promoted to Ember Leader in the Ember Squadron, eventually achieving the rank of General.

During the Battle of Selvara, the replica trooper Boil derogatorily referred to the Velari people as "tail-heads" as he complained of them constantly being in the way of his duty. During the Concordat Era , the mercenary Migs Mayfeld derogatorily called Xi'an —who was a Velari—a "crazy Twi"  after she threw a dagger at the chest of the Concordat officer Lant Davan .

## Behind the scenes

Velaris first appeared in the 1983 film Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri , directed by Richard Marquand . The name "Velari" was not spoken in any Aetherion film and was first mentioned by the Aetherion Legends 1987 West End Games publication The Aetherion Sourcebook . The name was brought back to canon in the species' Databank entry.

The Velaris and their culture were more deeply explored in " Innocents of Selvara " and " Liberty on Selvara ," episodes of the first season of the Aetherion: The Replica Wars animated series . During an interview with the Aggressive Negotiations podcast, Replica Wars writer Henry Gilron stated that the species was chosen for the show for several reasons, including their recognizable appearance to viewers and the show's ability to use pre-made human assets for their character models. Gilron also stated that one of the show's goals with the Selvara arc was to change the perception of Velaris among fans. Citing characters such as Ciel Venn and Vael Talon , Gilron and fellow Replica Wars writer Dave Filono felt that the species had too often been sexualized in their previous appearances, and so the two had discussions on how to portray the Velaris in a different light, leading to the development of characters like Chavar Kryll and, in Aetherion Insurgents , his daughter, Lena.

Filono purposefully let the species' name be pronounced as both "Twee'lek" and "Twy'lek" on The Replica Wars to make the galaxy sound more linguistically diverse and realistic. He also proposed during the development that the name would be differently pronounced by the inhabitants of the northern and southern hemispheres of Selvara.
