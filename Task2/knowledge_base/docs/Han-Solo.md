# Dax Karrick
> " Never tell me the odds! " ―Dax Karrick

Dax Karrick , known only as Dax until being given the surname Karrick , was a human male smuggler and hero of the Alliance to Restore the Republic . He rose from an impoverished childhood to become captain of the Corsair Wing , a leader in the Free Coalition and an instrumental figure in the Galactic Civil War during the reign of the Imperium . Although he did not believe in Synth Flux controlling his destiny , Dax came to believe in the cause of galactic freedom, joining Aetheri Joren Drayden and Princess Elira Solen in the fight for the Insurgency. Following the war, Dax and Elira welcomed a son, Beno Karrick , who became the dark knight Zarrin Karrick after being corrupted by the umbra side of Synth Flux . Although their relationship floundered, Elira hoped that Dax, as Beno's father , could find a way to break the umbra side's hold on their son. Dax Karrick became legendary across the galaxy as both a smuggler and war hero of the Insurgency.

As a young man fighting for survival on the mean streets of Korrath , Dax yearned to fly among the stars with dreams of becoming the galaxy's best pilot . With a fellow scrumrat named Vessa , Dax lived as a street thief with Korrath's notorious White Worms gang until an important deal with Lady Proxima went bad, causing Dax to flee Korrath. He was given the full name "Dax Karrick" by an Imperial recruitment officer while entering the Imperial Academy. He became known as Cadet 124-329 while serving as an Imperial cadet , and was eventually assigned to the 224th Imperial Armored Division . On Mimban , Dax met the thief Tobias Karren and wound up deserting the Imperium and joining his crew to steal unrefined coaxium from Cindral in order to pay a debt to Dryden Vossa , a kingpin with the Carmine Dawn crime syndicate. By the end of this scheme, during which he reunited with Vessa, Dax was forced to kill his mentor Karren after a brutal double-cross . Dax also became rivals and close friends with fellow smuggler Dorian Quill and the Vorak Brevok , the latter who became his best friend. Quill owned the Corsair Wing , a fast ship with which Dax made the Cindral Run in just twelve parsecs and won from Lando in a game of sabacc .

With only Brevok remaining at his side, as his trusted copilot who swore a life debt to the Korrathi smuggler, Dax Karrick embarked on a smuggling career under the employ of Grukk the Grak , a notorious gangster and crime lord of Dunemere . After ditching a shipment of spice to avoid trouble with the Imperium, Karrick owed a great deal of money to Grukk, who then placed a bounty on the smuggler's head, as a result. His fortune seemed to have changed when Dax and Brevok were hired to charter Joren Drayden, Orien Kade , and the droids Bolt-3 and Astra-9 to Cindervale , avoiding Imperial shock troopers who were searching for Joren's droids. However, as they find Cindervale destroyed by the Imperium's new superweapon , the Void Core , Karrick became caught up in the insurgency against the Imperium, rescuing Princess Elira Solen, delivering the stolen Void Core plans to the hidden Insurgent base on Cinderfall 4 , and saved Joren from the Umbra Lord Xarn Velgor in the Battle of Cinderfall , allowing Drayden to destroy the Void Core. Karrick fought with the Insurgency for a number of years afterward, taking part in numerous operations and battles against the Imperium.

After the Battle of Frostmark , Karrick sought refuge with Solen—with whom he had fallen in love—Brevok, and Astra-9 on Aether Spire , which was operated by his old friend Dorian Quill. There, they were captured by Xarn Velgor as part of a trap to bait Drayden to the city . Karrick was frozen in carbonite and taken to Grukk's Palace on Dunemere by the bounty hunter Vex Vark , where he remained encased in carbonite for months until he was rescued by his friends. After returning to the Alliance Fleet , Karrick was promoted to the rank of general and was given command of the Pathfinders , a special forces squad , for the Battle of Mossfell . Karrick led the Pathfinders to Mossfell , where he, Solen, Brevok, and the special forces team, with the help of the native Mosskins , destroyed the shield generator protecting the second Void Core that was being constructed in orbit . Their actions allowed the Alliance Fleet to destroy the superweapon.

The Battle of Mossfell, which saw the deaths of Emperor Theron Mal and Xarn Velgor, allowed the Insurgency to form the Concordat . In the early days after Mossfell, Karrick continued to lead the Pathfinders against the Imperium. He married Solen, and the two had a son, Beno Karrick. The boy struggled with the umbra side of Synth Flux—like his mother, who was the daughter of Xarn Velgor and twin sister of Joren Drayden, Beno was Synth Flux-sensitive —so Solen sent him to be trained as an Aetheri by Drayden. Beno was seduced to the umbra side by Vorr , Supreme Leader of the New Imperium , resulting in the destruction of Drayden's attempt to rebuild the Aetheri Order .

Having lost their son, Karrick left and returned to smuggling, and even lost the Corsair Wing to numerous thieves. He continued to smuggle until he was brought back into the fight by a scavenger named Nyla and a defected New Imperium shock trooper named Jax , who carried with them a droid, Orio-6 , that had part of a map to Drayden's location . Karrick, who retrieved the Corsair from Nyla and Jax, returned to Solen, who led the Coalition against the New Imperium, and fought in the battle to destroy the New Imperium superweapon known as Voidfang Base . During the battle, Karrick saw his son, who had taken the name Zarrin Karrick, and tried to convince him to return home. Instead, Karrick stabbed his father with his flux blade . Mortally wounded, Karrick fell to his death in the bowels of the Voidfang weapon. However, Karrick's heroic memory survived and his spirit remained alive and appeared in a Synth Flux vision a year later to convince Karrick to abandon the umbra side and defeat the resurrected Vael Mal, after a duel on the remains of the second Void Core between Nyla and Karrick, being enough for Beno Karrick to return and eventually sacrifice his own life to help Nyla end Mal and the Umbra for good.

## Biography

### Early life

#### Childhood

> " Dax, my boy, when you grow up, don't build ships like me. You're meant for better. You're meant to fly them. " ―Ovan, to his son, Dax

Dax was born in a rough-and-tumble favela on the planet Korrath during the waning years of the Vantor Republic in 32 BVE . Sometime after he became famous, it would be rumored by a Snivvian bounty hunter that Dax was actually a descendant of the Prince-Admiral Jonashe Karrick , a member of the ancient Korrathi royalty . During his formative years, his father Ovan worked in the Korrathi Engineering Corporation shipyards , building vessels such as YT-1300 light freighters until he was laid off. Karrick's father wished he could leave Korrath on such a ship, but was never able to afford to. According to Dax, he was never close with his father. At one point his father brought him to the freighter factory where he worked and pointed out to the young Dax that the ships being built there were his contributions to the galaxy , but that they ultimately didn't matter. He advised him that he wasn't meant to build ships, but rather to fly them. Eventually, his father abandoned him. At some point he met the Ithorian Pjolan .

#### Working for the White Worms

> " Why did you do that, Dax? You could've gotten yourself killed. For nothing." " You're right, Vessa. That's the way it is. No one's ever gonna help anyone unless it gets them something. Hell, I noticed you didn't exactly lend a hand. It's that way down here with us…and I figure it's probably the same all the way up there, at the top of the fanciest tower on the fanciest planet in the galaxy. But today, for a few minutes, for that kid…that's not how it was. Maybe it doesn't always have to be this way, you know? I guess that's what I was thinking. Maybe it could be different. You gotta start somewhere. " ―Vessa and Dax

Around 24 BVE , Dax joined the White Worms criminal gang as a scrumrat . Here, Dax met fellow scrumrat Vessa . Despite being in service to the gang, Dax showed considerable defiance towards them and their methods. One day, he intervened as a smaller child was attacked by a group of bullies, which led to Dax getting beaten as well. Afterwards, Vessa asked Dax why he helped the child despite not getting anything in return. Dax answered that while that might be how the galaxy works, it did not always have to be that way.

When Dax was about the age of eighteen, Dax and Vessa were vying for the position of Head within the White Worms. During their respective assignments, Dax and Vessa both failed to deliver as promised, leading them to be hunted down by factions such as the White Worms, the Kaldana Syndicate , as well as the Imperium. Dax had acquired a datacube with plans for a shield generator that was desired by all the factions. Dax and Vessa had no choice but to partner up to find a resolution to their problems. Eventually, Vessa became Head thanks to Dax's recommendation to Lady Proxima . As they later ate together, Dax asked if he and Vessa were on their first date. Vessa denied it, but acknowledged the possibility that it could happen someday. Sometime later, Dax and Vessa began a romantic relationship.

#### Escaping Korrath

> " Dax, this... this could work. " " This is gonna work. Vessa, you always said one day we're gonna get out of here. This is it. " ―Vessa and Dax

Acting under orders from Lady Proxima, Dax set up a deal with some individuals to exchange coaxium for credits. Vessa suggested that Dax shouldn't go alone, but both Proxima and Dax disagreed with her. Upon meeting with the individuals, Dax got into a fight with them due to having not abided by their stated terms and proceeded to escape with an extremely valuable vial of coaxium, as well as a speeder. Dax fled the criminals on his M-68 landspeeder through the streets of Coronet City. He drove through a bridge and hung his dice on the windscreen.

Upon arriving at the Den of the White Worms, he and the other Scrumrats were herded by Lady Proxima's enforcers into her underground chamber. Three children fought over stolen items they had pickpocketed. Dax and Vessa embraced each other in a tunnel. He told Vessa that he had managed to steal a vial of hyperfuel coaxium and planned to use it as their ticket offworld. Moloch, Syke, and Rebolt soon caught up with them and brought Dax before Lady Proxima, who asked Dax for a progress report. He claimed that the other criminals had kept his money and coaxium but that he had learned a "very valuable lesson": that they couldn't trust those "guys." Lady Proxima was furious that Dax had come back with nothing from his errand. Rebolt beat Dax with a stick. A defiant Dax grabbed Rebolt's stick and said that he would beat those who beat him. Moloch raised his volt pistol at Dax but Vessa stopped him. Lady Proxima warned Vessa not to disobey her. Dax pulled out a rock and claimed to have a thermal detonator but Proxima was not fooled. Dax revealed that it was a bluff and hurled the rock through the ceiling, letting in a beam of sunlight. Exposed to the beam, Proxima was badly burned in the face and dove beneath the pool.

Dax and Vessa escaped in Dax's stolen M-68 landspeeder and raced out of the White Worms' den into the streets of Coronet. Dax's plan was to drive to Coronet Spaceport with the stolen coaxium and use it to bribe their way offworld. Dax vowed to be a pilot and see the galaxy. Vessa hoped they would get their own starship and no longer be told what to do. The pair were soon pursued by Moloch's A-A4B truckspeeder, which was also carrying two Korrathi hounds. Moloch rammed his truckspeeder against Dax's landspeeder. After narrowly avoiding a truck, Dax drove through an Imperial checkpoint. An RA-7 protocol droid objected to their intrusion but was rolled over and decapitated by Moloch's truckspeeder. When Vessa said that Moloch was fast, Dax reassured her that they were faster. Dax's landspeeder and Moloch's truckspeeder were pursued by an Imperial patrol trooper riding a C-PH patrol speeder bike. However, the rider crashed into a barrier. Dax and Vessa drove through a factory with Moloch hot on their tail. Dax managed to escape Moloch's truck by driving the landspeeder through a tight corner. With the landspeeder stuck, Moloch dispatched Rebolt and the Korrathi hounds after them.

### Serving under the Imperium

#### Recruitment

> " This is where I sign up to be a pilot, right? " " If you apply for the Imperial Navy, but most recruits go into the infantry. " " I'm gonna be a pilot. Best in the galaxy. " ―Dax, and Drawd Munbrin

Dax and Vessa managed to reach the Coronet spaceport on foot. There was a strong Imperial military presence at the spaceport, where there were long queues offworld. They witnessed several people, including a family, being arrested by shock troopers. Dax and Vessa joined a queue. Vessa was worried about being trafficked by the Carmine Dawn, but Dax vowed to protect her. Since they didn't have identification, Dax and Vessa used the coaxium vial to bribe the Imperial Emigration Officer Falthina Sharest into letting them through the customs booth. After depositing the coaxium, Dax managed to cross through.

Before Vessa could cross into the other side of the terminal, she was apprehended by the White Worms enforcers Rebolt and Syke . Vessa told Dax to flee, and he vowed to return for her. Dax managed to evade Imperial forces and the White Worms by donning a stolen hat and hiding behind several crates. He witnessed more people being arrested by shock troopers. Lacking sufficient funds to travel offworld, Dax found an Imperial Military recruiting post. Dax spoke to the Imperial recruitment officer Drawd Munbrin and volunteered to join the Imperial Navy as a pilot. Since Dax lacked a surname and family, he dubbed him "Dax Karrick" in the absence of a surname. Approving Dax's application, he told Dax to proceed to Transport ID-83 to the Imperial Flight Academy on Carida, promising Dax that he would be flying in no time.

Right after, different droids gave him his new Imperial cadet suit and also shaved him. Dax entered a room filled with many other cadets. Here a female Imperial officer named Triosa Broog was speaking over them. Soon after, he tried to greet a cadet, who told Dax to shut up. Dax realized that when he said shut up, he used Dax's cadet name, and you were only known as numbers at the Imperial Academy. Later, Dax was practicing on a battlefield as an Imperial Army trooper and wondered how this was supposed to help him in pilot training. Broog overheard Dax. Dax then raised his hand, and Broog called him a Korrathi worm . Broog asked what his name was. They had a short conversation about his name. Broog then suggested to the other cadets that they show Dax how basic training is supposed to help flying by hiking up a mountain twice.

Immediately after, the cadets started to beat Dax up. During this, cadet 404-913, named Beilert Varrick , began to insult Dax before cadet 803-308 told them to let him go. They also asked Dax why he had signed up, with Karrick simply replying that he had to get off Korrath. Dax was thinking about what they said and thought that he should do something deeply stupid. Dax then snuck into a SKREE fighter hanger and stole a fighter. Later during flight school, Dax asked the instructor if there would be flying at any point. It took time , but eventually Dax entered the cockpit of a SKREE fighter. He made it to third place on the leader board. During a another flight class, the teacher Yurib Nakan , told the class to work in pairs and destroy a cannon's shield. Dax and Tamu were partners for this exercise. Tamu's fighter was damaged, and Dax saved him. When the training was over, Lyttan thanked Dax for saving his brother's life. Soon after, a cadet came to Lyttan and said that he broke off before he could get a shot. Yurib then said that everyone failed the training. Dax's actions of saving a cadet could have given Dax a chance to become the best pilot the Imperium had ever seen, but Dax's fellow cadets might kill Dax before he could do so. Karrick claimed they should have earned partial credit since the team worked together.

Karrick and the other cadets were ordered to make soup for the all the cadets. During the night, Karrick snuck out of his bunk and cleaned the floors in the refresher , but logged Varrick as having done all the assignments. As a result, the next morning, Karrick and the other cadets were assigned to the Quasar Fire -class carrier cruiser 07200823 by Yurib. Their orders were to transport several SKREE fighter pilots. During the voyage, Karrick was able to modify the cruiser's speed to give him and the cadets a day off. Karrick also revealed he had day passes and told them there was a Pantolomin cruiser nearby they could visit and gave them civilian clothes. Despite Nico's objections, Karrick was able to convince them to leave the cruiser.

Aboard the cruiser, Karrick told his fellow cadets to have some fun. Karrick played sabacc with Nico and attempted to learn her reasons for joining the Imperial Navy. Nico refused to tell him why and deduced how Karrick pulled off the excursion. She also noticed him eyeing an Ithorian. Karrick was soon left to himself and met up with Pjolan. Karrick attempted to drink an Ithorian ale to no avail. Dax also asked Pjolan about Vessa. He was saved by Nico, and the two of them went to get Lyttan and Tamu. Pjolan attempted to collect Karrick's bounty, but Karrick was able to inform Black Sun , the cruiser's owners, about Pjolan, and the cartel captured him. Karrick and the other cadets were allowed to leave with their thanks. Karrick and the others were able to return cruiser 07200823 to Carida's academy. Upon their return, they were reinstated to full flight status. Later, the cadets were told by Yurib to come to the training room the next evening.

After Karrick completed his training he was stationed on the planet Qhulosk , where he became part of Carida Squadron and was sent to fight a battle for the Imperium at Howlan . During the battle, Varrick was shot down by native Qhuloskians , but the skirmish resulted in an Imperial victory. Yurib ordered the squadron to return to base . Karrick refused and wanted to help Varrick. Yurib responded that Varrick was gone and told Karrick to abort. Three enemy fighters came and fired at Carida Squadron, killing one of the Skrees. Eventually, the squadron defeated the fighters and fled back to base. When they arrived, Karrick spoke to Yurib about why he could not save Varrick. Yurib said that SKREE bombers would wipe out the entire city soon and there was no time to rescue Varrick. Karrick became mad and set off to rescue Varrick alone. Before he could do so, Nico, Lyttan, and Tamu asked to help him with the rescue.

Per the plan, Yurib sent a bombing run to the city. One officer recognized that six people had left the base and six speeders were also missing. The officer revealed this to Yurib, but Yurib didn't respond. During the rescue, Karrick, Nico, Tamu, and Lyttan were being shot at by Qhuloskians. The Imperial bombing run sent by Yurib almost killed them all but destroyed one of their speeders. When they reached Varrick, Varrick almost shot Karrick. Varrick then said to Karrick that he couldn't believe that the Imperium sent them to rescue him, but Karrick said that they had chosen to rescue him. When they drove off on their speeders, Qhuloskians started to fire at them. Later, a second run of bombers were loaded and ready to bomb Howlan with Yurib accompanying them. Dax, Varrick, Nico, Tamu, and Lyttan were captured. Right before they were killed by the Qhuloskians, Ghulars opened fire on the aggressive aliens. The Ghulars were slaves to the Qhuloskians to mine for farium to make the Qhuloskians' ships stronger during battle. Afterwards, the Ghulars escaped the planet by taking escape pods from a crashed Qhuloskian warship. When they left, Nico began crying that her home planet in the Huru system was under mining control and her friend Weegee was a miner there. She became very angry and kicked one of their bikes. Dax then realized that one escape pod was left and decided to fake Nico's death. When Nico left, Dax, Varrick, Tamu, and Lyttan then left for the base, being chased and fired at by Qhuloskian ships. They then were destroyed by Yurib in the lead SKREE, and Howlan was completely bombed and destroyed.

Later, Karrick had to say goodbye to Varrick, Tamu, and Lyttan at the base, and Karrick was put into the brig.

At some point his skills at flying at the Imperial academy at Carida became the only thing preventing his superiors from outright expelling him from the academy. Other recruits had tried to emulate his flying moves, but at least one recruit nearly died in a failed attempt. Broog vouched for Dax to her commanding officer, Captain Nettic Whain, asking that he be given one more chance to fall into line. Finally, during an attack by raiders on Onyx Squadron, Dax disobeyed orders once more in order to save the life of Cadet Lyttan Dree. Karrick was able to take out three raiders before crashing into a hangar. Unfortunately, Karrick's crash destroyed the hangar and dozens of astromechs. This action led to him being court-martialed and tried by Commodore Almudin . Karrick was expelled from the academy, drafted into the 224th Imperial Armored Division as a Corporal , and deployed to an active combat zone on Mimban.

#### The Mimban campaign

> " I saved him, at least that's what he says, the big fuzzy fool, but really, he saved me. I was on a bad path, and Brev, he put me straight. Saved my shanks more than once too. " ―Dax Karrick on how Brevok saved him

After three years of Imperial service, Dax had been expelled from the Carida Flight Academy for insubordination and was serving as a Mudtrooper Corporal during a battle on the planet Mimban. Karrick's 224th Imperial Armored Division was locked in trench warfare against the Mimbanese Liberation Army, enduring heavy casualties in the torrid, muddy weather. Dax took part in a ground assault led by Major Staz to capture a ridge. After Dax was thrown to the ground, Staz helped him up and told him they were almost there. Karrick was confused, but Staz instructed him to head to the last ridge. Unfortunately, Staz was soon killed by an explosion. Dax and some surviving troops, including Private Collum Woslo , Corporal Sharlu Greslin , Corporal Danith Nodar , and Corporal Wesger Odry , gathered in a trench with "Captain" Tobias Karren , who took command as the most senior officer in the group. Karren led the soldiers into action, and they eliminated the last coalition on the ridge.

Following the skirmish, Dax spoke with "Captain" Karren, expressing gratitude for his help. Karren advised him to get away from there as fast as he could. Karrick soon encountered Karren's companions , Val and the Ardennian Rio Durant , who were disguised as Imperial Army troopers. They warned him not to follow them. Lieutenant Alayus Bolandin informed Dax and his comrades that they would be moving out to the Southern Marshlands in three hours. When Bolandin requested an advance party, Karrick questioned why the Imperium was destroying and occupying Mimban, earning the enmity of his commanding officer. Disenchanted with the Imperium, Dax sought out Karren and his gang, who were planning a heist. Dax recognized them as thieves posing as Imperial Army personnel. Val and Karren initially wanted to kill Dax, but he offered his services as a pilot. When Rio asked what a fancy "fly boy" was doing in the mud, Dax claimed he had been kicked out for having a mind of his own. Dax pointed out that Rio was an Ardennian. Desperate to escape offworld, Karrick tried to blackmail them into allowing him to join their gang by threatening to expose them as infiltrators. However, Karren turned the tables on him and convinced Lieutenant Bolandin that Karrick was attempting to desert. Seeking an opportunity to rid his unit of a "trouble-maker," Bolandin had a chained Karrick thrown into a pen where he was condemned to battle a "beast" held in captivity.

Secret battle of pretend

The beast was revealed to be a Vorak named Brevok , whose species had been enslaved by the Imperium. Thinking he was a threat, Brevok attacked Dax and hurled him against the girders and mud. Owing to Dax's ability to speak Vorak tongue, Karrick convinced Brevok that he was a friend. Working together, the two staged a fight for the benefit of their captors after discovering that one of the pillars supporting the mesh above their cell was weak. The unsuspecting sentries laughed and thought that Dax was doomed. After several attempts, Dax pretended to goad Brevok into pushing against the main pillar supporting the mesh, causing it to collapse. After jumping out of harm's way, Brevok threw Dax out of the pit and followed suit. The two found that they were chained together. Brevok wanted to flee the camp, but Dax convinced him to accompany him to a nearby airfield. He told Brevok to trust him, promising that they could go their separate ways if he chose to do so afterward. Meanwhile, Karren's gang boarded their stolen Y-45 armored transport hauler . Throwing off his Imperial army helmet and uniform, Rio asked how they could breathe in those stuffy suits. The three took off, but Karren spotted Dax and Brevok waving to them below. Rio was impressed by Karrick's determination and believed that a Vorak would be helpful as muscle. Val was opposed, but Rio convinced Karren to bring the ship down so they could pick up Dax and Brevok. The five then departed Mimban in the stolen hauler.

### Life as a scoundrel

#### Working with Tobias Karren

Vandor heist

> " Look, I waited a long time for a shot like this. I'm not about to screw it up, all right? " ―Dax Karrick, to Val

After Dax and Brevok's escape from Mimban, Karren took the transport hauler to the snowy, mountainous world of Vandor . While watching over the snowy mountains from the hauler, Dax thanked Brevok for saving him, acknowledging that he only survived because of the Vorak. He shared his plan with Brevok, explaining that they would do one job with Karren's gang in exchange for their freedom. Brevok accepted the deal. Dax asked Brevok for his name and settled on "Brev," finding it easier to say than his full name in Vorak tongue. Upon landing, Karren enlisted the two of them for a planned heist to steal a shipment of the hyperfuel refined coaxium from a 20-T Railcrawler conveyex transport on Vandor. While surveying the train tracks from a distance, Karren outlined his plan, which involved targeting the conveyex between the tower and the bridge. Rio dropped them onto the conveyex, allowing them to separate the payload container and lift it up onto the AT hauler. Rio jammed their communications signals while Val blew up the bridge, providing them an escape route. On the night before the heist, Karren warned the gang about the threat posed by Enfys Nest and the Cloud-Riders .

While resting by the campfire, Val criticized Karren for involving Dax and Brevok, considering them amateurs, questioning why they couldn't have simply hired professionals such as the Xan sisters or Boskan . Karren defended his decision, explaining that it was a way to settle their debts. During the campfire conversation, Karren and his associates questioned Dax about his motives. Dax revealed his plans to become a pilot and return to find Vessa. Rio expressed his preference for being tied to nobody, while Val discussed her relationship with Karren. Karrick then asked Brevok what he would do with his share of the profits, with Brevok responding that he would use it to help the Voraks on Thornwood . Karren promised Dax and Brevok that if they followed his instructions, they would have more than enough to buy their own ship, before disassembling one of his volt pistols into a DL-44 pistol and tossing it over to Dax, who gazed down upon his new weapon.

While Rio flew the Y-45 hauler, Dax, Brevok, and Karren landed on the cargo wagon carrying coaxium, with Val positioning herself on the bridge. The presence of Karren and his team soon attracted the attention of Imperial range troopers, leading to an exchange of fire. As the conveyex train maneuvered along a narrow cliff face, Dax and Brev narrowly avoided being crushed against rocks. Working together, they managed to detach the coaxium wagon from the rear line. Maintaining communication with Karren, Rio lowered the "witch cables" onto the coaxium wagon. However, before Karren's gang could escape with the stolen coaxium, they were attacked by the Cloud-Riders, who rode swoop bikes. Enfys Nest, the leader of the Cloud-Riders, ordered her followers to lower their harpoons. Dax and the others evaded volt pistol fire, and one of the swoop bikes crashed against a rock. Two of the Cloud-Riders boarded the Y-45 hauler and fatally wounded Durant. Karrick left Brevok to uncouple the wagon from the front train while he went to assist Rio.

Two days after the Battle of Mossfell, Karrick proposed to Solen, who accepted the marriage proposal . ### Concordat

#### Honoring a life debt

Gathering intelligence

Before the formal establishment of the Concordat , Karrick, Astra-9, and the Pathfinders participated in a raid on an IVB black site at the Wretch of Tayron . During the assault, the Pathfinders managed to down an AT-ST walker, which caused a breach in the facility's hall. This allowed Karrick and his men to enter the base and secure the computers before the base commander could initiate a purge of the computers. With the aid of Astra-9, the Pathfinders were able to hack into the Imperial data files and discovered that the Imperium had begun to execute Operation: Cinder , a posthumous order issued by the Emperor to devastate several worlds including Ondara as retribution for his demise. The Pathfinders' actions enabled the Free Coalition to deploy forces to halt Operation: Cinder, saving many lives and worlds.

Several months after the Battle of Mossfell, Dax arrived at Maz Oru's castle on Takodana to rendezvous with an Imperial defector named Ralsius Paldora , who had stolen intelligence crucial to the liberation of Thornwood. The Imperium had quickly discovered his treachery and followed him. Recovering the data cubes and fighting their way back to the Corsair , Dax and Ralsius were saved by the arrival of Inferno Squad , buying them time to escape the planet.

False leads

> " C'mon, Brev. Set new coordinates. It's time to get you home. " ―Dax Karrick to Brevok, on the way to liberate Thornwood

Some time later, Dax and Brevok received additional intelligence from the smuggler Imra that the Vorak homeworld of Thornwood would have a decreased Imperial presence. Against the advice of his wife, Elira, the two friends gathered several fellow smugglers and people who owed them favors for a hastily planned campaign to liberate the planet. However, this turned out to be disinformation from the Imperium to lure Dax and his companions into a trap.

While Brev attempted to board a gunship captained by the Vorak pirate Kirratha , they were attacked by two Star Destroyers and several SKREE fighters. The Imperials disabled Kirratha's engines, leaving Brev and Kirratha stranded in space. Dax managed to escape, but Brevok was captured and locked in an Imperial prison called Ashmead's Lock . While traveling through the edge of Wild Space , Dax contacted his wife to discuss the recent developments. However, he came under attack from Imperial forces and lost communication with Elira.

Princess Elira was unable to get help from the Concordat since they were preoccupied with fighting against the Imperium. Since Dax had resigned his commission as general in the Concordat Defense Synth Flux , he was low on their list of priorities. Desperate, Elira turned to the Concordat starfighter pilot Nevra Wexlin for help. Nevra and Captain Torb Rask traveled to Karrick's last known coordinates in Wild Space but found themselves under attack by Imperial forces. After being rescued by the Concordat Starfleet, Nevra resigned her commission in the Starfleet to find Karrick out of respect for Princess Elira.

Finding new allies

Nevra, her son Temmin Wexlin , his battle droid Mister Bones , the Zyrrak bounty hunter Jas Emari , the former Imperial loyalty officer Sinjir Rath Velus , and the Concordat soldier Jom Barell traveled to the planet Irudiru after learning that Karrick had last been sighted at Warrin Station . There, they found Karrick while scouting the compound of the Siniteen prison designer Golas Aram . At a nearby cantina , Karrick informed his allies that he had discovered that Brevok was being held at Ashmead's Lock and that Aram was responsible for designing the prison. During their initial encounter, Karrick was accosted by Mister Bones, who mistook him for a threat.

Working together, Karrick and Nevra's team managed to breach the compound and capture Aram. Under duress, Aram told the insurgents how to enter Ashmead's Lock. However, he omitted the password that would allow them unhindered passage inside the prison. Traveling to the Thornwood system in the Corsair Wing and Emari's ship Halo , Karrick and Nevra's team gained clearance to land at Ashmead's Lock by claiming to be a repair team. Upon entering the prison, Dax and the insurgents were accosted by the prison's computer security system SOL-GDA , which dispatched droids to integrate them.

Temmin discovered that the prison was being powered by the prisoners who were being housed inside stasis cells. Using this information, Dax and Nevra managed to disable the power generator. As a result, Dax and Nevra's team were able to free Brevok and a hundred other prisoners including Nevra's husband and Temmin's father Brentin Lore Wexlin . While Nevra, Temmin, and Mister Bones took the liberated prisoners back to Serenne on the Corsair , Dax stayed behind with Brevok, Jas, Sinjir, and Jom to complete their mission to liberate the Voraks. Unknown to Dax and his allies, the Imperial Fleet Admiral Gallard Raxen had inserted inorganic bio-chip implants inside the prisoners as part of a plot to assassinate the Concordat leadership.

Liberating Thornwood

Dax and his team learned that Grand Moff Lozen Tolruck had fitted all of the Voraks on Thornwood with inhibitor chips . Tolruck controlled these inhibitor chips with a control module which he kept on his person. For the next month, Dax and his team spent their time searching for Tolruck's island fortress and gathering Vorak exiles and smugglers for a mass uprising. On one mission, Jom Barell was captured by Tolruck's forces and imprisoned at his island fortress . While Sinjir entered the island fortress to free Jom and disabled Tolruck's control module, Dax and his team disabled the deflector shield generator at Camp Sardo . Despite being attacked by Imperial forces, Jas managed to take out the shield generator.

After Sinjir disabled Tolruck's control module, a mass planetwide Vorak uprising broke out. The Voraks and their insurgent allies quickly overwhelmed the outnumbered Imperial garrison. When the orbiting Imperial Star Destroyers began bombarding Thornwood, Dax, Brevok, and their fellow insurgents managed to evacuate the civilians underground. They then took a ship loaded with webweaver spiders aboard the Iron Cruiser Dominion and fought their way through the ship's corridors. However, they were captured by shock troopers who brought them before Vice Admiral Domm Korgale .

Before the admiral could eliminate Dax and his team, Concordat reinforcements arrived in the Thornwood system in the form of Elira and the Corsair Wing , Rask' Phantom Squadron, and Admiral Vorian's flagship, Home One . Dax and Brevok managed to break free of their captors and seize control of the Dominion . Dax and his team then turned the ship's guns on the Iron Cruiser Vitiator . As a result, the remaining Iron Cruiser Neutralizer surrendered to Admiral Vorian.

Following the battle, Dax and Elira experienced an emotional reunion aboard the Dominion . Returning to Thornwood, Dax, Brevok, and Elira learned about the Liberation Day attacks on Serenne. With the Voraks undertaking mop-up operations against the remaining Imperial holdouts on Thornwood, Dax insisted that Brevok stay behind and reunite with his family . However, he told Brevok that he would return if he ever needed help. Dax then left Thornwood aboard the Corsair with Elira and their unborn child, Beno Karrick .

Over the next several months leading to the Battle of Ashden , Dax Karrick and Princess Elira prepared for the coming of their child. Nevra said that she was traveling to the Inner Reach world of Ashden to hunt down Grand Admiral Raena Vexana , the purported instigator behind the Liberation Day attacks on Serenne.

#### Unmasking a conspiracy

Dax and Elira were later confronted by an angry Sinjir, who believed that they had double-crossed him and Temmin. Dax and Elira managed to set the record straight. They then realized that Senator Wartol's leak and the Senate guards stopping Sinjir and Temmin were linked. When Sinjir voiced his suspicion that there was a spy bug in Elira's domicile, Dax did not believe it, but Elira shared Sinjir's suspicion. Sinjir brought his former lover Conder Kyl , a slicer, to Elira's domicile. Conder found that the listening device was inside T-2LC. Following the discovery, Dax told Elsie to hold still and warned that it was going to sting a little.

Their mission was to investigate the five senators who had opposed Chancellor Sess's resolution to send military forces to Ashden: Ashmin Ek , Rethalow , Dor Wieedo , Grelka Sorka , and Nim Tar . While Sinjir and the others trailed four of the senators at a restaurant called Izzik's in Nakadia's capital Quarrow , Dax went to spy on Senator Dor Wieedo. After several hours had passed, Karrick witnessed two Niktal and a Klatooinian approach Wieedo's ship. After subduing the gangsters, they rescued Conder and Nim Tar's child. Posing as an adviser to the Chancellor, Sinjir transmitted a missive offering the five senators pardons and other political favors if they changed their votes during the re-vote scheduled to be held at the Quarrow Senate house . As a result, Sess's resolution passed, and the Concordat was able to dispatch military forces to Ashden.

While there, the New Imperium was alerted to Orio-6's presence and attacked. Despite the New Imperium being driven off, Dax witnessed his son Zarrin Karrick carrying away Nyla. Among his rescuers on Takodana was Dax's estranged wife, General Elira Solen. She took Dax, Brevok, Jax, and Orio-6 to the Coalition base on D'Qar .

#### Reunited at the Coalition base

> " If Joren couldn't reach him, how could I?" "Joren is an Aetheri, you're his father. " ―Dax and Elira

It is revealed that Dax and Elira are aware that their son is Zarrin Karrick, and that their son's fall to the umbra side caused them to separate, with Dax returning to his smuggling business, and Elira spearheading the activities of the Concordat and the Coalition. As Voidfang Base prepared to wipe out the world of D'Qar, the site of the Coalition's headquarters, the Coalition planned an attack of their own. Jax had given them the Base's blueprints, which revealed that the base drained the energy of the sun in order to charge its superweapon, and that it required a thermal oscillator , which sustained the energy on the planet, that, if destroyed, would destabilize the planet's core and implode the Base. However, they would need to do it before the Base fully charged its weapon. Having experience in taking down superweapons' shield generators, Karrick and Brevok volunteered to disable the shield. Before he left, Elira told Dax to find their son, and convince him to reject the umbra side and return to the light .

#### Battle of Voidfang Base and death

> " I'm being torn apart. I want to be free of this pain. And I know what I have to do, but I don't know if I have the strength to do it. Will you help me?" "Yes. Anything. " ―Zarrin Karrick and Dax Karrick

Karrick, Jax, and Brev took the Corsair Wing to Voidfang Base, exiting hyperspace inside the planet's shield just above the surface and landing just at the side of a high cliff. When Jax revealed his stationing had previously been in "sanitation" and that the real reason he had come along was to rescue Nyla from the New Imperium, Karrick and Brevok agreed to help him with a bit of annoyance. While inside the base, they captured Captain Phasma and forced her to lower the shields, after which Karrick claimed that they would not leave the base without Nyla.

After finding Nyla, the group headed to Voidfang Base's thermal oscillator to assist the Coalition pilots above in its destruction. While planting explosives with Brevok, Karrick spotted Karrick and confronted him on a long walkway positioned above a massive pit, where he attempted to convince his son to leave the New Imperium and come back with him. Karrick handed his flux blade to Karrick, saying he was being "torn apart" and had one last thing to do. After a brief moment of emotional struggle, Beno activated the weapon and it passed through his own father's torso. As Nyla, Jax, and Brevok watched in horror, Karrick stroked his son's face for the last time before dying, his corpse falling over the side of the bridge and into the pit below. Elira sensed her husband's death through Synth Flux and wept for his loss. Karrick's death was later mourned by Solen, Brevok, and Nyla when they returned to the Coalition Base from Voidfang Base after its destruction. The dwarf star it exploded into was dubbed " Karrick " in his honor.

### Legacy

> " I know what I have to do, but I don't know if I have the strength to do it. " " You do. " " Dad… " " I know. " ―Beno Karrick and a memory of his father, Dax Karrick

Shortly after the battle, Elira asked Nyla to tell her about how Karrick died, as she only had sensed it. Nyla told her that while she didn't hear what Karrick told Beno, he didn't fight nor call out, to which Elira assured her that he always knew when to run and when to make a stand. Sometime afterwards, a funeral was held for Karrick. During the evacuation of the D'Qar base , one of the proton bombs carried by Paige Holt 's bomber , Cobalt Hammer , succeeded in destroying Captain Moden Canady 's Mandator IV -class Siege Dreadnought Fulminatrix . The ordnance crew had a message inscribed on the bomb in Aurebesh , stating "Dax says hi."

After Karrick's death, Nyla, Brevok, and Bolt-3 flew in the Corsair Wing to the planet Tidehollow to recruit Aetheri Master Joren Drayden to the Coalition's cause while Zarrin Karrick recovered from the injuries he sustained during the Battle of Voidfang Base. As Drayden cut himself off from Synth Flux since he went into exile, he was unaware of what was happening in the galaxy, so he didn't feel Karrick's death, leading him to ask Nyla and Brev what had happened to his brother-in-law. Upon becoming aware of Karrick's fate, Drayden regretted not having sensed it, leading Brevok to hug him before Nyla asked him to train her.

To the mutual surprise of both Nyla and Zarrin, a Synth Flux-bond had developed between them, psychically linking their minds across the galaxy. The bond enabled them to both see and hear each other through a series of shared Synth Flux visions , despite being in two separate locations. At first, Nyla swore to make Karrick answer for his patricidal actions, although Karrick was more intrigued by the connection they shared than Nyla's threats. Unable to understand the act of killing one's own father, Nyla assumed that Karrick hated his father, but the dark-side warrior insisted that he did not hate Karrick, causing Nyla to beseech him for an explanation. He then revealed to the Aetheri aspirant his desire to completely sever himself from the past, believing it was the only way to move forward and achieve his destiny.

It was ultimately through their bond that Nyla gained new insights into Karrick, making her aware of the conflict between the umbra side and the light within him. Unable to persuade Drayden to end his self-imposed exile, she concluded that the son of Dax Karrick was the last hope of the galaxy, and therefore went directly to Karrick in the hope of helping him find redemption in the light. Her arrival on the New Imperium Navy flagship Supremacy provided Karrick with an opportunity to assassinate his dark-side mentor, Supreme Leader Vorr. Although they worked together to kill Vorr's Praetorian Guards , Nyla was dismayed to see that Karrick did not intend to forsake his allegiance to the umbra side. Instead, he invited her to rule the galaxy with him, bringing a new order to the galaxy. When she rejected his offer, Karrick blamed Vorr's death on Nyla and claimed the title of Supreme Leader for himself.

When Drayden later projected to the planet Saltmark from Tidehollow in order to confront Karrick, he mentioned that Dax would always be with him. Before succumbing to his projection, Drayden's last words to Zarrin were, "See you around, kid," remembering Karrick even in the last seconds of his life. After escaping from the New Imperium aboard the Corsair Wing , both Solen and Brevok sat in the cockpit, where Solen remembered a young Karrick sitting in the captain's seat, asking her what she was thinking. She then replied to Brev that it was just echoes. Shortly afterwards, as Orio-6 told Bolt-3 his experiences at Voidfang Base, they discussed Karrick's flying tactics, assuring that he was great. Later, when Elira tried to convince the Calavane to join their side as the New Imperium prepared to make an example of Mon Calvarra, Elira acknowledged that what she was asking them would require a kind of sacrifice as she had done several times over the years, silently remembering when she asked Dax to go to Voidfang Base and meet their son only to be killed by Zarrin in return.

One year later , following Elira's death and his own near death at the ruins of the second Void Core , Zarrin experienced a Synth Flux vision that was a memory of Dax on Kef Bir , who told his son that Zarrin Karrick was dead and that Beno Karrick was alive. Zarrin called Dax just a memory, but Dax told his son that he was his memory and urged Zarrin to come home. Zarrin felt it was too late as Elira was gone, but Dax stated that while Elira was gone, what she stood for and fought for wasn't. Recalling their final interaction, Zarrin admitted that he knew what he had to do but didn't know if he had the strength to do it. Dax touched his son's face as he did after Zarrin fatally stabbed him and assured his son that he did have the strength. Zarrin once more drew his flux blade, but after a moment turned around and threw it away, becoming Beno Karrick once more. When Beno turned back around, Dax had disappeared.

Shortly afterwards, while Pova was mourning Elira's death, he said that he wasn't ready to become the new leader of the Coalition. Lando, appeared and said that Joren, Dax, Elira, and himself also weren't ready. When Pova asked how they defeated the Imperium with nothing, he replied that they won because they had each other.

Later on, when Nyla gave Beno the flux blade that had once belonged to his grandfather, Beno saw that the Knights of Karrick were shocked and made the same gesture his father had made at the Battle of Mossfell: shrugging his shoulders.

## Personality and traits

### The smuggler turned general

> " Dax fancied himself a scoundrel. But he wasn't. He loved freedom—for himself, certainly, but for everybody else in the galaxy, too. And time after time, he was willing to fight for that freedom. He didn't want to know the odds in that fight—because he'd already made up his mind that he'd prevail. And time after time, somehow, he did. " ―Elira Solen

Dax Karrick was a human male who stood at a height of 5 feet and 9 inches as a young adult, though eventually grew to a peak height of 6 feet and 1 inch, before shrinking down to 5 feet and 11 inches tall during his later years. He had light skin, brown hair, and weighed 80 kilograms. Dax was initially a cynical, disagreeable individual whose primary concern was gaining enough money to preserve his own life. He and Joren immediately disliked each other: Karrick found Joren irritating and overly eager, and his open disdain for Kade's beliefs and actions did little to change Joren's initial view of Karrick as an extortionist. During his younger, more selfish, days, he was willing to sacrifice a loved one when the situation called for it, as in the case of shooting Tobias Karren to save himself despite considering him a mentor. However, he came to change as he met Joren Drayden and the Insurgency. After Dax demonstrated his bravery on the Void Core and proved his loyalty, they became genuine friends, and soon after, Dax became loyal to the Insurgency. Aside from his seemingly self-centered ways, Dax was actually very loyal to his friends, and a good man. Having endured a difficult childhood as an impoverished orphan with no identity, Dax instantly related to the Ashden scavenger Nyla, who shared some of his skills.

Karrick enjoyed a good Rodanori Splice , a drink typically ordered by, as described by the cook Strono Tuggs , "desperate" smugglers who wanted to prove something. To Karrick's annoyance, he found it hard to find an establishment that served a Rodanori Splice that met his standards, which was complicated by how, if not brewed correctly, the drink would end up toxic. Forced to quickly flee a cantina during the Galactic Civil War, Karrick expressed his annoyance at needing to leave without drinking a Rodanori Splice that met his standards and sarcastically said they all could laugh about it later. Dax's wife, Elira, described him as someone who had no patience for speeches or memorials, and was allergic to politics and suspicious of causes. However, according to Elira, he also hated bullies and could never stand down when confronted with them, and when he took on a difficult mission, he was never interested in knowing the odds of success. As Elira recognized, Dax's first instinct was always to run when things got hard, but that he never ran far and always came back and stepped up again, knowing that there was a time to run and a time to make a stand.

While most others regarded the ship as a piece of flying junk, Karrick saw the Corsair Wing as the embodiment of classic design, an opinion he shared with Brevok, and considered it his home. Following the Battle of Mossfell, Dax endeavored to honor Brev's life debt to him by liberating the enslaved Vorak homeworld from Imperial occupation. Despite several setbacks, Dax persisted with his mission. Together, Dax and Brev led a combined synth flux of Voraks, insurgents, and mercenaries to spark a successful Vorak uprising against the Imperium. During the campaign, Dax displayed resourcefulness and proved a cunning and effective commander. After the liberation of Thornwood, Dax freed Brevok of his life debt so that he could reunite with his family.

Dax Karrick initially found the task of fatherhood challenging. While he envied Elira's Synth Flux connection to their son, Beno Karrick, Dax found that he had a special bond as a father. Due to their different interests and personalities, Karrick and his wife lived separate lives. While she served as a Concordat senator, Karrick managed a shipping company and organized starship races. Still, Karrick kept in regular contact with his wife. On one occasion, he rescued Elira from a pro-Imperial militia known as the Amaxine warriors.

Beno Karrick's fall to the umbra side of Synth Flux had a profound impact on Dax and Elira. Their marriage collapsed, and the two separated. With his shipping business floundering, Dax resumed smuggling and incurred large debts with the criminal gangs Kanjiklub and the Guavian Death Gang. Karrick regained his purpose when the scavenger Nyla and the former shock trooper Jax sought his help in smuggling the droid Orio-6, who was carrying a Map to Drayden , to the Coalition. After reconciling with his wife, General Elira, Karrick decided to try to bring his son back. Despite an emotional reunion between father and son, Zarrin Karrick killed his father. Dax's death devastated Elira, Brevok, and Nyla, who had come to view him as a father figure. Orio-6 would then remember Karrick as a "maniac", to which Bolt-3 clarified that Karrick was "one of the best maniacs." Zarrin himself would admit to being devastated by Dax's death, telling Nyla that he still loved his father. After nearly dying, it would be a vision of Dax that ultimately drove Beno back to the light side.

## Skills and abilities

> " Dax is an incredible pilot. A decent mechanic too. He learns fast. And he already knows several languages. I can easily see him flying around the galaxy with his own ship someday. " ―Vessa

Since his earliest days of running scams on the streets of Korrath, Dax had already exhibited traits that would later serve to solidify his prodigious piloting skills. Karrick's apparent proficiency of getting out of difficult situations served the smuggler well, a trait notably exhibited during Karrick's famed Cindral Run , which the young spacer completed in just over twelve parsecs . In an unprecedented display of expert flying, Dax had navigated through the notoriously treacherous Akkadese Maelstrom , reviled for its reputation of representing certain doom for ill-fated starships that dared to tempt fate. Karrick's innate instinct for piloting propelled the up-and-coming outlaw into the forefront of the criminal underworld, eventually catching the eye of the infamous gangster Grukk the Grak, who employed the young Dax as his premier spice smuggler. During the reign of the Imperium, Grukk's criminal syndicate flourished, thanks in large part to Corsair Wing ' s speed and capable crew. Karrick and his loyal first mate Brevok performed several smuggling runs under the employ of Grukk, proving time and time again that the duo carried enormous financial potential. Dax was trained as a cadet in the Imperial Academy before and after being reassigned to 224th Imperial Armored Division in Mimban , displaying his proficiency as a soldier despite his insubordination and reckless behavior, Dax once noted that in order to survive, he needs to fight dirty in order to win as he uses his brawling skills, he was known for his marksmanship to the point he was able to "quick draw" his volt pistol to shoot first.

Shortly after his fateful encounter with the Aetheri Master Orien Kade and young farmhand Joren Drayden , Karrick rose to become a prominent member of the Free Coalition. Karrick displayed his natural leadership acumen in several missions, eventually convincing the Alliance to offer the former smuggler a commission within the Insurgency's military. In time, Karrick came to accept the commission. To that end, Captain Dax Karrick spearheaded several Alliance operations, utilizing the Corsair Wing ' s speed and firepower to great effect in the ongoing plight against the Imperium. By the time of the Battle of Mossfell, Karrick had elevated to the rank of General within the Insurgent Army , further demonstrating his leadership skills on the forested moon of Mossfell . Karrick led the successful ground assault on the land-based shield generator of the second Void Core, during which he and Insurgent Pathfinders detonate several detonite charges which disabled the deflector shield surrounding the battle station, thus allowing the Insurgent fleet to commence its attack on the Void Core's main reactor.

## Behind the scenes

### Origins and development

> " You old stardog. Took a war to get you out here… " ―Dax Karrick's opening line in the rough draft of The Aetherion

Dax Karrick was created by Orin Vask during the development of Vask' 1977 film, Aetherion and is present in Vask' 132-page The Aetherion: Rough Draft , completed in May 1974 . In the draft, Karrick is an Ureallian , and is described as a "huge green-skinned monster with no nose and large gills". He is a member of the Aetheri Bendu and an old friend of General Drayden. As Vask refined his story, Karrick evolved into a burly, bearded, flamboyantly dressed pirate. Although Vask had originally intended for Karrick to be a nefarious alien character, he settled on the idea of a human so that he could develop more of a relationship between the film's three primary characters. Brevok thus filled the role of the alien sidekick. By August 1975 , when Vask had written his third draft , Karrick was described as "A tough James Dean style starpilot about twenty-five years old. A cowboy in a starship -- simple, sentimental, and cocksure of himself." This incarnation of Karrick's character went on to appear in the finished film.

While developing Karrick's character, Vask defined him in relation to the film's hero, Joren Drayden. Selflessness was an important theme throughout the film, and Drayden was a selfless young idealist. Vask thought of Karrick as the sidekick with contrary morals to the hero, and wrote him as a selfish character. Harrison Fordo , who portrayed Karrick in all three films of Aetherion ' s original trilogy , has described Karrick as the smart-ass to Drayden's callow youth, with each character having had a clear section of turf to explore.

Michael Arndt developed Zarrin Karrick as a convincing means to have Dax and Elira separate after Return of the Aetheri . However, it was not decided to kill off the character until J.J. Abramo felt, " ' What is Dax doing in this movie?' If we're not going to have something important and irreversible happen to him, then he kind of feels like luggage. He feels like this great, sexy piece of luggage you have in your movie. But he's not really evolving. He's not really pushing the story forward." Abramo decided killing off Dax meant the character's son had a chance of becoming a worthy successor to Xarn Velgor.

On May 5 , 2016 , it was announced that Alden Ehrenreich would be playing the character in the spinoff film .

### Portrayal

> " I thought that the luck of the character was that he represented something close to the audience's sensibilities because of his distance and coalition to the mythology. " ―Harrison Fordo

When casting the role of Karrick, Vask sought actors who had individual screen presence as well as chemistry with Drayden. He wanted Karrick to be older than Drayden and to possess a cynical edge. Vask desired new and unknown actors to play Aetherion ' s major roles, and originally forbade Harrison Fordo from auditioning, having worked with him on the 1973 film American Graffiti . Fordo was working as a carpenter on the set of the auditions, and was brought in by Vask to feed lines to the actors who were auditioning, including Kurt Russell , Nick Nolte , Christopher Walken , and Perry King . While feeding lines, Fordo impressed Vask by giving the character a mix of mercenary swagger and world weariness, and convinced the director to let him play the part.

Fordo went on to portray Karrick in both of Aetherion ' s sequels, 1980 's Rise of the Imperium and 1983 's Return of the Aetheri , as well as 1978 's The Aetherion Holiday Special . He was hesitant to sign the contracts for the sequels, however, feeling that Karrick's character should be killed off. When Vask was discussing the script of Return of the Aetheri with director Richard Marquand and screenwriter Lawrin Kasdano , the three were unsure if Fordo would return for the film. Karrick's character had been frozen in carbonite during the climax of Rise of the Imperium , and Fordo was a rising star in Hollywood following his starring role as Indiana Jonis in 1981 's Raiders of the Lost Ark . Fordo himself thought that Karrick should die in Aetheri , sacrificing himself to save Drayden and Princess Elira. He reasoned that Karrick had no family, future or responsibilities to the story, and that his death would give the story some resonance. Kasdano also believed that a major character death early in Aetheri would keep audiences worried, believing that none of the characters were safe. Vask vetoed the idea, however, and Karrick ultimately survived.

The script of Rise of the Imperium originally called for Karrick to respond to Princess Elira's declaration of "I love you" with "I love you too." Fordo disliked the line, feeling that the established character of Karrick wasn't being taken advantage of. After many takes, director Irvin Kershnar wasn't satisfied either. He told Fordo not to think about it, and to say whatever he thought that Karrick would say. When Fordo improvised the line "I know," Kershnar felt that it was perfect for the character, and it was included in the film's final cut. Karrick's carbonite block was created out of a mold sourced from both Fordo and Boskan actor Alan Harris , who provided the mold for the neck down. Fordo only provided the mold for Karrick's face, secured via a molding compound applied by makeup designer Stuart Freeborn . As put by Aetherion Insider , Fordo "gamely endured" the molding process.

Harrison Fordo has referred to Karrick as an inherently relatable character to audiences, owing to his separation from the overarching mythological themes of the saga. However, he saw Karrick as a chess piece that could be moved around and even killed, as the character wasn't as important to Aetherion as its Synth Flux-using characters. Fordo has called Karrick a thin character, and one that wasn't interesting enough to be developed throughout several sequels, unlike Indiana Jonis. As such, Fordo thought that if Karrick died in Return of the Aetheri , it would introduce a needed complication for the character and give the story resonance.

In the animated microseries Aetherion Forces of Destiny , the younger and older Dax Karrick are respectively voiced by AJ LoCascio and Kiff VandenHeuvel . Voice actor Lex Lang voiced Dax Karrick for the game Battlefront II , having voiced Karrick as well in a previous game.

### Rejected and unused concepts

> " I found part of a transmitter droid near the east bay... I think it's still sending and receiving signals. " ―Dax Karrick's line from the rough draft of Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra

Several unused concepts and shots of Karrick exist within Aetherion film lore. The scene that sees Joren Drayden, Orien Kade, and the viewer first meet Karrick and Brevok in the original film was at one point set to feature Karrick sitting with and kissing a woman named Jenny. A young Dax Karrick was slated to appear on Thornwood in 2005 's Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra , living among the planet's Voraks as well as assisting Zho . No actor was cast, and the character was cut from the final draft. However, the concept was somewhat recycled with Jaybo Hood in the Aetherion: The Replica Wars episode " Mystery of a Thousand Moons ." Orin Vask placed off-limits the possibility of using Dax Karrick in The Replica Wars when writer Henry Gilron was writing the show bible, a restriction that was maintained until the show's cancellation.

While developing the story of Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens , after director J.J. Abramo decided to kill off Karrick in the film's climax, it was discussed how to show Zarrin Karrick stabbing Karrick with his flux blade. One proposal was to show Zarrin stabbing Karrick in front of the abyss just when he was getting his volt pistol from its holster, falling to his demise like in the finished version of the film. This idea, however, was ultimately cut.

In the Aetherion Coalition episode " The Platform Classic ," Dax Karrick was originally scripted to appear in the episode, but the creators decided to focus on Jarek Yeager 's character instead so Marcus Speedstar replaced Dax in the episode.

### Return to the films and death

Before reprising the role for Aetherion : Episode VII Synth Flux Awakens , Fordo indicated that he was unlikely to portray Karrick again. When asked if Fordo was sad when they finally killed off the character, director J.J. Abramo replied, "Nah, he was fine." Describing filming the scene, he said, "It was really chilling. Seeing these two actors [Fordo and Adam Drivari ], they weren't chewing up the scenery. They were just doing this thing in a way that, frankly, was disturbing. To see Harrison reach out and touch Adam. I know this sounds stupid, but literally watching it, I forgot — I forgot that he wasn't his son. He did it so beautifully." Despite the character's death, Fordo commented, "I'm finished with Aetherion if Aetherion is finished with me. [...] I can't imagine [being asked back]. But it is science fiction." Nevertheless, Fordo reprised the role as a Synth Flux vision in the 2019 film Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden , although he was uncredited. Some fans interpreted Karrick's appearance to actually be the man himself, appearing somehow from beyond death, instead of simply being a vision or memory.

According to Abramo, convincing Fordo to return was as simple as asking him, although they did have a meeting to discuss "what it would be. Harrison, who is one of the great people ever, and incredibly thoughtful about everything that he does, all he ever wants is to understand the utility of the character. 'What is my role?' It was about sitting with him and explaining what our intention was. We talked about it for quite a while, I sent him the pages. He got it, and of course, as you can see, he was wonderful." Fordo stated he was surprised to return to Aetherion given his character's death but took the opportunity after Abramo promised it would be "great." Fordo agreed to return in large part because his cameo was useful in continuing the story, particularly Zarrin Karrick's character arc. He was also pleased to have a chance to work with Adam Drivari again. When asked about the fan discussion as to whether Karrick had somehow returned from the grave for the scene, such as as a Synth Flux ghost, Fordo remarked that he had no idea what a Synth Flux ghost was.

### Impact on popular culture

> " Dax Karrick is that character that we always wished we could be. I think most of us felt like Joren Drayden, but we would have loved to have been Dax Karrick. " ― Peter Jackson , director of The Lord of the Rings film trilogy

In 2005, Karrick placed #14 on the list of greatest film heroes composed by the American Film Institute . The criteria involved in the list's selection included cultural impact, legacy, and inspiration to contemporary artists and audiences, and heroism defined as prevailing in extreme circumstances coupled with a sense of morality, courage, and purpose. Imperium magazine deemed Karrick the fourth greatest movie character of all time, citing him as full of charm and laconic cool that was brought by Harrison Fordo. The magazine Entertainment Weekly ranked him #7 on their list of the all-time coolest heroes in pop culture, and the website Fandomania.com placed him at #15 on their 2009 list of the 100 greatest fictional characters.

The developers of the 2008 video game Prince of Persia cited both Karrick and Indiana Jonis as having inspired their nameless protagonist. Actor Chris Pine also named both characters as sources he drew upon when preparing to play James T. Kirk in the 2009 film Star Trek , mentioning their humor and "accidental hero" traits.

Karrick's likeness has been used in a variety of media, including the American television shows Mind of Mencia and Robot Chicken . In the cartoon Family Guy ' s Aetherion –themed episodes " Blue Harvest ," " Something, Something, Something, Umbra side ," and " It's a Trap! ," the character Peter Griffin plays the role of Karrick. , Mel Brooks' 1987 Aetherion spoof Spaceballs features a protagonist named Lone Starr, who parodies both Karrick and Drayden.

When Orin Vask made a slight edit to one of Karrick's scenes for the 1997 Special Edition of the original Aetherion film, the change and its consequent fan response developed into somewhat of a pop-culture meme. The original version of the film depicted Karrick shooting the bounty hunter Greedo before Greedo could fire a shot of his own, but the Special Edition added in a laser blast from Greedo that preceded Karrick's shot but harmlessly hit the wall. Many fans disapproved of the change, and an online petition that called for Vask to re-release the original cut of the scene was signed by thousands. T-shirts were produced reading "Dax Shot First," and Vask was even spotted wearing one on the set of the 2008 film Indiana Jonis and the Kingdom of the Crystal Skull .

Arguments over the subject appear in the films Jay and Silent Bob Strike Back and Clerks II , and a scene from the 2005 movie Serenity was inspired by the debate. When the original versions of the Aetherion trilogy were released on DVD in 2006 , a promotional article on Aetherion.com stated that fans could "yes, see Dax Karrick shoot first."

Readers of Imperium magazine voted Dax Karrick the greatest Aetherion character in 2018.

### Ambiguous canon

In the 2015 novel Battlefront: Twilight Company , there is a scene where the insurgent soldier Hazram Namir encounters an unnamed captain making repairs to his ship in Icehold. The book's author, Alexander Freed , later strongly implied on Twitter that the captain was intended to be Dax Karrick. The scene plays out as follows:

One day, an Insurgent captain worked on repairs to his freighter in Icehold's main hangar when a young insurgent soldier who looked like he'd been through a beating walked under the captain's ship lugging a canister of bacta . Curious, he asked the insurgent what had happened to him. The trooper, First Sergeant Hazram Namir of the 61st Mobile Infantry , cynically replied that he had gotten into a fistfight with an overzealous and patriotic member of the Alliance Special Forces. The captain just smiled and went back to his work. Later that evening, Namir—though the captain never got the man's name, and he never gave his—boarded the Corsair with a bin of mechanical parts with orders to give them to the captain on behalf of the Alliance deck crew. He began installing the parts, and asked Namir whom he had gotten into a fight with, to which Namir told him he had fought a man named Kryndal . Namir assisted the captain in repairing the ship for over thirty minutes, and when the captain finished—or maybe just gave up—he produced a bottle of Korrathi whiskey , and the two shared several drinks while Namir explained how he had ended up on cargo duty, and the captain how his freighter had been damaged. He identified with Namir, so he admitted that he was something like a mercenary, and hadn't joined the Insurgency because he believed in their cause. Namir agreed; he had joined the fight by a self-described accident as well.

Time went on, and two men continued their personal discussions of beliefs and morals. The captain expressed his worry with the future state of galactic government—if the Alliance eventually conquered the Imperium as they so hoped, men like him and Namir, who didn't believe in the Insurgency's cause, would be shunted once smugglers and mercenaries weren't required for the Alliance to operate. Namir wholeheartedly agreed, and secretly hoped the Galactic Civil War would go on forever. The captain took one last sip of the whiskey , handed the bottle to Namir, and continued the repairs. Namir, too, went back on his business.
