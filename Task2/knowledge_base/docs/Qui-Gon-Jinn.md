# Sorin Vael
> " It matters which side we choose. Even if there will never be more light than darkness. Even if there can be no more joy in the galaxy than there is pain. For every action we undertake, for every word we speak, for every life we touch, it matters. I don't turn toward the light because it means someday I'll win some sort of cosmic game. I turn toward it because it is the light. " ―Sorin Vael

Sorin Vael , a Synth Flux-sensitive human male , was a venerable if maverick Aetheri Master who lived during the last years of the Republic Era . He was a wise and well-respected member of the Aetheri Order , and was offered a seat on the Aetheri Council , but chose to reject and follow his own path. Adhering to a philosophy centered around the Living Synth Flux , Vael strove to follow the will of Synth Flux even when his actions conflicted with the wishes of the High Council . After encountering Kael Drayden , Vael brought him to the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor , convinced he had found the Seeded One . His dying wish was for Drayden to become an Aetheri and ultimately restore balance to Synth Flux.

Born on the Core Reach Vantor, the capital of the Vantor Republic around 80 BVE , Vael learned the ways of Synth Flux as the Adept of Aetheri Master Varek before ultimately attaining the rank of Aetheri Knight in around 58 BVE . In 44 BVE he would go on to take an apprentice of his own, Orien Kade , who adhered to the principles of the Aetheri Code more than his own master. Although their relationship was sometimes tough, the two Aetheri overcame early difficulties during a mission to Pijal , which concluded in Vael turning down a request to join the Aetheri Council to continue teaching his apprentice. When the Commerce Combine initiated a blockade of the Mid Reach planet Ondara in 32 BVE , Vael and Kade were dispatched to resolve the crisis at the request of Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum . Influenced by the mysterious Umbra Lord Vael Mal , the Federation launched a full-scale invasion of Ondara, forcing the Aetheri to rescue Queen Lira Ondar after the fall of Serath .

During their journey to the galactic capital, the group was forced to land on the Outer Reach planet Dunemere where Vael encountered a young slave named Kael Drayden. Using a scientific method to gauge the boy's potential, he learned that Drayden had more synthcells in his blood than any Synth Flux-sensitive in galactic history , including Grand Master Zho . The revelation that he was the result of a miracle birth—a child born of a mother , but had no father —confirmed Vael's suspicion that Drayden was the Seeded One destined to restore balance to Synth Flux. Before departing the desert world with Drayden, Vael was confronted by Mal' Umbra apprentice Vael Vreth . Although their duel was brief, it convinced Vael that the Umbra had returned after a millennium of silence.

At the Temple , Vael was adamant that Drayden was the Seeded One and should, therefore, be inducted into the Aetheri Order. However, having inspected Drayden for themselves, the High Council deemed him too old, emotional, and dangerously full of fear and anger. Vael saw their decision as a temporary setback, but the need to resolve the situation on Queen Ondar's homeworld forced him to set aside his plans for Drayden. During the Battle of Ondara , Vael and Kade confronted Vael Vreth together. While they initially managed to fend off the Umbra Lord 's attacks, master and apprentice were eventually separated during the flux blade duel , resulting in Vael sustaining a mortal injury. Rushing to his master's aid, Kade fought and ultimately defeated the Gloomreachian Umbra, but could not save Vael. With his last breath, Vael made Kade promise that he would take Drayden as his apprentice. Having secured Kade's word, Vael became one with Synth Flux , dying with the certainty that he had found the prophesied Seeded One.

## Biography

### Early life

> " Sorin Vael would never join you. " " Don't be so sure, my young Aetheri. You forget that he was once my apprentice, just as you were once his. " ―Orien Kade and Count Varek

Sorin Vael was a male human born on the galactic capital of Vantor circa 80 BVE . He was taken to the Aetheri Sanctum as a boy for training by the Aetheri Order and was assigned to the Heliost Clan where he learned flux blade combat from Aetheri Master Tera Sinube .

When Vael was twelve years old, he was assigned as an Adept to Aetheri Master Varek , from whom he learned the ways of Synth Flux . He'd spent his last night in the younglings' crèche laughing with his friends, imagining all the adventures he would have as an Adept, and practicing with his flux blade in the sparring room until Master Yaddle ordered him to bed.

When the future Aetheri Master Eno Cordova first joined the Aetheri Order, he became close friends with Vael, who helped Cordova learn about and get used to Vantor.

### Apprentice to Varek

#### Becoming an Aetheri

Around 68 BVE , when a twelve-year-old Vael presented himself to his new Master in Varek's quarters, Varek observed that Vael was frightened. After a moment of silence, and with pressing from Varek, Vael confessed that he was. When asked why, after looking into himself through the fear, Vael answered that he was not afraid of Varek, but of not becoming an Aetheri, that he was afraid of failing and of not being worthy. Varek then summarized it as Vael being afraid of himself, of a future not being the one he wanted. Vael confirmed it, fearing that Varek would reject him for his cowardice. Varek instead said that it was very wise, surprising Vael. Smiling, Varek explained that most Adepts would deny their fear, and even if they admitted it, they would not have Vael's self-knowledge. Varek further praised Vael for his honesty, insight, and intelligence.

To help Vael understand the Aetheri Sanctum more completely, Varek then took him throughout most of the Temple, including the Adepts' sparring dojo, a dojo where Aetheri Knights trained, and the Great Assembly Room . Vael also saw the various meditation chambers, which he didn't find as exciting, but still interesting. On the other hand, Vael spent long minutes in the arboretum, wandering through the trees, ferns, and flowers of a thousand different worlds. Their last stop was the Aetheri Archives , where they examined reliquaries of various eras, not to study them, but simply for Vael to learn his way around. While doing so, an ancient reliquary, more ancient and of a different shape than the rest, caught Vael's eye. When he questioned his Master about it, he was shocked by the expression on Varek's face, almost as if he considered it an enemy. Varek answered that it was a reliquary of Aetheri prophecies. Vael asked if there are Aetheri prophets, to which Varek replied not any longer. He explained to Vael that the mystics sought undue knowledge of the future, which led them down dangerous paths. The umbra side often tempted those drawn too deeply into them. Varek then finished by saying that is why the Aetheri study prophecy no longer, then turned to leave, which meant Vael had to follow. Vael then asked Varek if just wanting to know the future can lead to the umbra side. Varek replied that it takes more than that. This was Sorin's first encounter with the reliquary of prophecies, and it would not be the last.

After four months as an Adept, the Council sent him with his master to Shurrupak to participate in a battle on its surface, which was Sorin's very first. While the shuttle they were in approached the planet under heavy fire, a nervous Vael reassured himself that he was ready for battle, looking to his Master Varek as an example to aspire to as a fearless Aetheri. As they arrived at Primus Base , Sorin had his first meeting with Rael Averross , Varek's first Adept and a famous flux blade duelist. After greeting each other heartily, Varek introduced Rael to his new Adept. While Varek then conferred with the generals, Rael put one hand on the still-nervous Vael's shoulder. Rather than comforting Vael about the battle, Rael instead commented that Varek is hard to get to know and offered to help Sorin out. Since he was Varek's previous Adept, Rael told Sorin that he could ask him any questions that he might not feel comfortable asking Varek yet and be his sparring partner if he wanted. With his first battle looming ahead, Sorin accepted his offer. As they, too, went to talk to the generals, Sorin felt as though Rael Averross was the link between the Adept he was and the Aetheri Knight he desperately wished to be. He viewed his journey to be easier now that he had a friend to show him the path.

Sometime after that battle, Sorin was doing homework in Varek's quarters while his Master was in conference with an official from Badtibira . He then had a surprise visit from Rael Averross, who greeted Vael warmly. Sorin greeted him with a similar warmth, then asked what he was doing there. Rael explained that he had just returned from Shurrupak, then asked what Vael was doing in Varek's quarters since he wouldn't be returning for another few hours. Vael explained that Varek lets him do his homework in his quarters if he doesn't disturb him. In this case, Vael was reporting on the different schools of theosophy a century ago. Upon Rael's grimace and questioning, if he had angered Varek somehow, Vael admitted that he chose the topic despite knowing that it wouldn't be interesting because he thought it would be easy. It wasn't.

Upon receiving a negative from Vael if it was too late to change his topic, Rael grinned, and motioning for Sorin to follow him, offered to show him some history worth studying. They went to the Aetheri Archives, and together they studied the one and only reliquary that had ever interested Vael: the one that held the ancient prophecies. They were all fascinating to him, and Sorin kept reading, unable to stop. He asked Rael if the ancient mystics had these visions in trances, to which he confirmed. After contemplating these prophecies, Vael remarked that Varek said he should ignore this reliquary and that he doesn't believe in the prophecies. This confused Rael since Varek, he said, was the one who introduced Rael to it and that it used to be that one could hardly pry the reliquary out of his quarters. Sorin said that he didn't know when or why that changed and that Varek didn't explain. A surprised Rael said he would ask Varek about it and remarked that Varek had changed a lot. Sorin protested, surprising Rael, then said not until he's finished his report. Rael then laughed so loud he received a stern look from Jocasta Nura .

#### Tracking Shenda Mol and love life on Feldora

> " I have a little game I play. I need more kills to win—though, of course, I'm only competing with myself. That's the only competition that really matters, you know. More people ought to un­derstand that. " ―Shenda Mol, to Sorin Vael

Vael and his Aetheri Master Varek became part of a Numidian Prime strike team . They were assembled to track and find the notorious Falleen bounty hunter Shenda Mol . They eventually tracked her at her hideout on the planet Numidian Prime. While searching for her hideout, Mol captured Vael. Before Mol could kill Vael, Varek struck her down with Synth Flux lightning to save his apprentice, causing Vael a degree of concern.

Vael later confided what Varek did to Rael Averross, though at the time, Averross expressed that he was not all too concerned regarding his former master's conduct. In 60 BVE , Vael went through trials on the Outer Reach planet Feldora when he fell in love with someone , beginning a romantic and physical relationship. As the Aetheri Code forbade romantic relationships, Vael found his commitment to the Aetheri ways tested, but he ultimately stayed loyal to the Aetheri Order. He would, however, keep an Emberfall fire diamond that was given to him on Feldora as a memento.

At some point during his apprenticeship, Vael met the Vorak Brennonn and befriended her. They shared many adventures together, with Brennonn saving Vael on two occasions. During the second of those adventures, events transpired that resulted in Vael feeling as though they saved each other. He also came to know the Vorak Balfus .

#### Rescuing Senator Dagonet's son

> " Your actions saved many lives today. " " Just thinking in the moment, Master. " " Well, then. You're a much wiser man than I, Sorin Vael. " " Thanks to your teachings. " ―Varek and Sorin Vael on his actions during the rescue mission

Between 64 BVE and 60 BVE , Vael and Varek had been sent on a mission to rescue the son of Republic Senator Dagonet , who was being held captive by the residents of the Senator's home planet. After their investigation, they discovered that the Dagonet was allowing his people to starve for his own personal greed and corruption. The kidnappers abducted Dagonet's son to protest his inhumane policies and discovered that Dagonet's son was yet unharmed. Dagonet arrived to rescue his son, resulting in a brief shootout between the Senator's forces and the Aetheri-backed kidnappers. During a lull in the firefight, Dagonet threatened to burn the entire village down and many others to make an example of them. An enraged Varek began Synth Flux-choking Dagonet with the intent to kill him, but Sorin released Dagonet's son and managed to defuse the situation without any further violence. In the aftermath, Varek commended Sorin's actions and praised him as a wiser man than him.

#### Investigating at the Dragonfire Air Rally

Shortly before his trials, both he and Varek were invited by council member Jor Aerith to join her in representing the Aetheri at the annual Vantor Dragonfire Air Rally in hopes of making a good impression on the Candovant ambassador who had invited her. Varek agreed to attend and took the opportunity to teach Sorin some Candovant customs, but while they were there, they ran into Ramil , whom Sorin learned was Varek's brother, much to his shock. Sorin and Varek learned that Ramill was was participating in the race . While Ramil did very well in the race, it quickly became apparent his Airspeeder was sabotaged and it crashed. Sorin and Varek discreetly went to see what had happened, and while there they discovered the Investigator was trying to cover up the incident. Following the man, they found he was working for a Jenet crime boss named Cenevax . Ignoring a message from Aerith asking them to return to the races, they chose to confront and captured the criminal, and while questioning her they realized that she was privy to Council communications, as Cenevax made clear she knew they that had been recalled and were disobeying order by being there. With this information, Varek deduced that she had a spy on the Council, much to the shock of Sorin.

Varek and Sorin escorted Cenevax to the Council Chamber, where Varek made his accusation, and discovered that Aetheri Master Yula Braylon was the spy. Further investigation revealed that Cenevax had been blackmailing her with the fact that Arath Tarrex, the latter being Varek's old classmate and was also shockingly revealed to be in fact her son. Sorin and Varek confronted and exposed Tarrex, who was then taken into custody for his actions and also for having sold out his mother's influence to criminals. As the Temple guards were escorting Tarrex, Varek encouraged Sorin to go with them and ensure that the guards treated him well, both of which Sorin agreed to do. Around 58 BVE , Varek joined the Aetheri Council in the aftermath of Braylon's removal from the council as a result of her actions. Vael would eventually be promoted to become an Aetheri Knight around the same time.

### Master to Orien Kade

#### Becoming a mentor

Vael became an Aetheri Master in his own right, a master who developed an independent attitude towards the Aetheri Order. Others saw him as a maverick Aetheri, one who would disobey the Aetheri Code if he felt it was the right decision. In 44 BVE , a thirty-six-year-old Vael took on an Adept named Orien Kade , who believed that Vael could've been a member of the Aetheri High Council if he followed the Code, though as Kade knew, Vael had rejected an offer to join. On one occasion, Kade watched in silence as his master told the council he disagreed with them. At another point, the master and apprentice sparred, with Vael complimenting Kade's skill. On another occasion, the two visited a plain and overlooked its native creatures, giving Vael the chance to teach Kade about how Synth Flux existed in all the life that surrounded them.

Vael was able to sense whenever Kade was discontent, which proved useful on some missions, yet also annoyed Kade. At one point, Vael and Kade traveled to the planet Lah'mu to visit a fortress . Vael also maintained contact with his old Master after taking on Kade and spoke highly of his Adept to Varek. While Varek left the Aetheri Order after the invasion of Sireno in 42 BVE , secretly becoming an Umbra apprentice to the elusive Umbra Lord Vael Mal sometime later, he was still allowed to visit the Aetheri Sanctum. Varek's decision to leave the Aetheri came as a shock to Vael, who felt lost for a time as he grappled with feelings of doubt and fear. However, Vael trusted in the Aetheri teachings and himself, which allowed him to recover and replace his shaken feelings with resolve. At some point, Vael met the Togorian Aetheri Jak'zin , who had also looked up to Varek.

Vael taught Kade as best he could, although Kade did not always agree with his master's teaching strategies. Despite his Adept's clear proficiencies in flux blade techniques, Vael held Kade to practicing only basic methods, which impacted his apprentice's confidence and their relationship. Although Vael did so to not rush his apprentice and allow Kade to carve out his own distinct style out of mastery of the fundamental cadences , his lack of clarity on his teaching philosophy hurt Kade.

At some point, Orien came up with the idea of a new weapon powered by kyber crystals which consisted of two short-bladed blades each attached to a thin chain. After hearing this idea from Kade, Sorin told his adept that flux blades were about how the Aetheri wished to be seen, and that was not intended to be a display of power. That lesson resonated with his young Adept, who would carry it for years to come.

#### Protecting Duchess Satinna

> " Master Sorin and I spent a year on Ferrum, protecting the duchess from insurgents who had threatened her world. They sent bounty hunters after us. We were always on the run, living hand to mouth, never sure what the next day would bring. " ―Orien Kade

Between 41 BVE and 40 BVE , a civil war began to rage on the planet Ferrum , with the world's martial traditionalists fighting against a reformist faction known as the New Ferriks . The leader of the New Ferrik movement, Duchess Satinna Velara , became a target for the traditionalists, who hired bounty hunters to eliminate her. However, Vael and Kade were sent on an extended mission to Ferrum, where they were assigned to protect Velara.

Vael and Kade spent a year on Ferrum with Velara, remaining on the run from the bounty hunters. The three remained unsure of what the future would bring, but they continued their effort to survive, living hand to mouth everyday. During the mission, Kade developed romantic feelings for Velara. Vael became aware of the unlikely romance between his apprentice and the noble. Eventually, the civil war came to an end, with the New Ferriks coming to power and Velara becoming the ruler of Ferrum. Although Kade would have left the Aetheri and stayed behind to be with her had she asked him to, Velara never did, so he and Vael left the planet.

#### Life Day adventure

Around 41 BVE , Vael told Kade he would be taking him on a training exercise, keeping it secret that they would be going to observe the Voraks' celebration of Life Day on the planet Thornwood in the village of his old friend Brennonn, who had accepted his offer to visit. After entering the village, Vael advised his Adept to remain patient after the young man expressed confusion, reminding him to focus on what was instead of what he had expected. Though Kade found it hard to focus amid a celebration, Vael continued their visit, educating his Adept about Vorak culture before asking what he made of it. Pleased with Kade's answer that it was as if the Voraks were celebrating Synth Flux, he explained the unconventional training he wanted Kade to partake in; he was asking him to focus on the energy of Thornwood, given that it was Synth Flux. He then informed his Adept they would speak with the Voraks when Kade revealed he felt something different.

Suddenly, the two Aetheri were interrupted when Brennonn spotted Vael, resulting in the Vorak hugging him from how such she had missed him. Vael shared her sentiment and began to introduce her to Kade, only for her to hug him as well. After Vael explained his history with the Vorak to Kade and thanked her for letting them come, she told him that Balfus was missing. As Vael inquired about what actions she had taken concerning the missing Vorak, both he and Kade felt a disturbance in Synth Flux ; several Trandoshan hunters suddenly attacked the village, launching nets from the trees to kidnap more Voraks. Vael and Kade fought against the Trandoshans, defeating many of them. However, Vael himself was eventually captured when he demanded the hunters to surrender. Along with the captured Voraks, Vael was taken away by a MSP80 Pteropter hover pod .

Although Vael, as he was taken away, had told Kade it was up to him to rescue the Voraks, the Aetheri Master tried to negotiate the Voraks' release with the Trandoshans' leader, Korgan . However, Korgan wanted to capture Kade too, and Vael warned him that his greed would have been his downfall. Vael waited in his cage until Kade arrived . Kade pretended to surrender but, when Korgan was distracted, he opened Vael's cage with Synth Flux. Vael Synth Flux-pulled his flux blade and ignited it. As Vael was freed, many Voraks arrived to support the two Aetheri: the Trandoshans, being outnumbered, surrendered and freed the Vorak prisoners. After returning to the village, the Life Day celebration began.

#### Mission to Pijal

> " I've never been to Pijal. " ―Sorin Vael

In 40 BVE, a forty-year-old Vael and seventeen-year-old Kade undertook a mission to Teth to thwart Wanbo the Grak . The two Aetheri escaped Teth, but not without complications including a key miscommunication between master and apprentice. Upon returning to Vantor, the Aetheri High Council extended to Vael an invitation to join their ranks, as with Master Poli Dapatian was stepping down by that month's end. Vael was shocked to be asked due to his disagreements with the Council on various occasions, and while he was initially pleased to hear the offer, he thought of his Adept, who he would have to pass on to a new master if he were to accept. While it would solve the growing problems between them, Vael felt guilt at the thought of it and thus told the Council that he would meditate on their proposition.

Meanwhile, a political dispute erupted on the Inner Reach planet Pijal . A performance troupe known as the Opposition that began playing political pranks to protest a treaty signing that would end the absolute monarchy on Pijal, allow the world to join the Republic, and give more power to Czerka Corporation was thought to be making increasingly violent attacks nearing the treaty's signing. Vael's old friend Rael Averross requested Vael and his Adept's assistance to end the dispute and ensure the signing of the treaty. The Council sent Vael and his Adept to Pijal to help. However, as the mission began, Kade became aware of the offer presented to Vael regarding the Aetheri Council when the two met with Supreme Chancellor Kirames Kaj . Vael felt great shame at not being the first to tell his Adept learner. Before they left, Vael sought out Kade and the Adept's dojo. He attempted to apologize for not being the first to tell him the news and said that he had several reservations that he had to dwell on before committing to the position, but Kade made a sarcastic reply. Vael told his Adept that he would continue to discuss the matter when Kade was in a more rational mood, and when Kade quipped again, Vael sadly said that he supposed that Synth Flux always allowed things to turn out for the best. As they traveled to Pijal, the tension between them grew.

Vael and Kade both were briefed on the situation, learning that Averross served Crown Princess Fanry , who was only fourteen years old, as an advisor and that he needed to protect her from the Opposition as she waited to take Pijal's throne. Vael and Averross became reacquainted quickly, although Vael was skeptical of several of Averross's choices during his service, as Averross also loosely interpreted the Aetheri Code. As he and Kade began to do more investigating on Pijal's moon , they encountered two jewel thieves, Pax Maripher and Rahara Wick . Although Maripher was very abrasive towards Vael, Wick was more cooperative. Onboard their ship, the Meryx , Vael recruited the two to help them find the terrorists that posed a threat to the treaty. Shortly after returning, Vael received a vision of the event, which turned into a bloody battle. He brought it to Averross's attention, but the older Aetheri did not share his level of concern.

As their investigation continued, Vael eventually met with the Opposition's leader, Halin Azucca , and learned that her group was being framed. Although they had peacefully made demonstrations against the treaty, the violent terrorist acts that had alerted the Republic were not caused by Azucca's people. As Sorin learned of the complications surrounding the treaty due to the lack of Pijali representation and the heavy influence gained by the Czerka Corporation , he decided to oppose the treaty himself and refused to act as a Republic representative.

However, eventually, the Aetheri discovered that Fanry was deceiving them and had been building a military group called the blackguards, framing the Opposition to restore the full monarchy. At the coronation, she rejected the treaty as well as the Republic's influence, proving herself to having been underestimated by the Aetheri. Ready to remove Czerka influence from the system by synth flux, she prepared to attack their ships. Vael attempted to convince her to stop and prevent the innocent casualties that were the Czerka slaves onboard the ships, but when she refused, Vael took matters into his own hands, and with the help of Kade as well as their two new friends Maripher and Wick, they succeeded in saving the slaves. After the mission, Vael decided to stick with training Kade and declined the offer to join the Aetheri Council.

#### Helping Master Zho

Around 40 BVE, Vael and Kade were operating near a planet that served as a base of the Flesh Mongers pirate group, flying a pair of Delta-7 Aethersprite -class light interceptors . The two received a message from the planet's surface from Aetheri Master Zho , who asked for assistance. Landing on the planet, Vael and Kade discovered that Zho had taken out the entire pirate gang to rescue a young Synth Flux-sensitive child , known as Lo , as the pirate had planned to sell him due to his Synth Flux abilities.

Zho asked Vael and Kade to escort the infant to the Aetheri Sanctum , with Zho assuring Lo that he was in safe hands with Vael. Vael expressed that he was happy to help out as they had been so close. While the two conversed, Kade took particular interest in that Zho took out an entire room of pirates and was totally in awe. Vael then proceeded to ask Zho if everything was all right, to which the revered master replied that he could sense a calling disturbance in Synth Flux that he felt he must follow. Vael noted that he could not feel the presence himself, but offered to join him on his quest if he so wanted. As Zho climbed into his starship , he responded that he felt it was simply a calling for him alone and embarked on a mission to a planet in the Vagadarr system , where he became involved in a tribal war between the planet's native inhabitants.

### Truths of Synth Flux

#### Discovering the secrets of life after death

Sometime during his life, Vael began discovering the secrets to manifesting his consciousness after death . He traveled to a planet strong with Synth Flux, a world that legend said was the birthplace of life and the origin of synthcells . He learned from five Synth Flux Priestesses who had retained their consciousness after death. He learned that, when a living thing died, its life passed through the Living Synth Flux and into the Cosmic Synth Flux to become one with Synth Flux.

As the Living Synth Flux and Cosmic Synth Flux existed in tandem, it became possible for one to retain consciousness and physically manifest themselves after death. Vael was considered worthy of the knowledge of eternal life and secretly began his training to unlock its mysteries fully. He also learned from a Shaman of the Whills that achieving eternal consciousness required absolute selflessness.

#### Questioning his purpose

> " Being called a coward isn't what bothered me, Master Zho. It was being called a great warrior. " ―Sorin Vael

Vael began experiencing an inner turmoil over how the Aetheri should interact with Synth Flux. Although Zho sensed that internal debate in the man, Vael continued on with his Aetheri duties for sometime. In 37 BVE , when Vael and Kade were sent to the planet Bri'n to establish negotiations between the the Priestess Th'er of Wooda and the Metal Clan , Kade stayed within their Eta -class shuttle while Vael met with the Priestess's rivals. When the Metal Clan instead tried to kill Vael and the Priestess as a justification to begin cutting down the world's trees, he moved to defend Th'er and escape with her. Deflecting many volt pistol bolts with his flux blade, the Aetheri contacted Kade to start their shuttle and inform him about the failed negotiations. After being surrounded by the Metal Clan, Th'er proclaimed that Vael was a warrior who could take down all who opposed her, yet Vael instead used Synth Flux jump to escape with her aboard their shuttle, ordering Kade to take them to Vantor so as to protect the Priestess.

Th'er was nevertheless disgusted by Vantor—viewing it as a city of metal—and demanded Vael to act as a warrior, ordering him to cut down every member of the Metal Clan. Although Vael informed her that was not the path of the Aetheri Order, Th'er felt Vael was instead a coward who did not live up to the legends that proclaimed the Aetheri to be great soldiers. Greatly distressed at being called a warrior, he returned to the Aetheri Sanctum, where he did not report to the Aetheri High Council. Deciding to confront Vael over his internal turmoil, Zho located the Aetheri Master on one of the Temple's balconies under the pretense of asking for him to report to the Council. Aware that Kade had filed his own report, Vael quickly saw through Zho's claim, leading to a discussion about how the Aetheri should act between the two masters.

Even though Zho and him had different opinions, they both agreed that Vael should seek answers. Needing a world full of life to find a deep communion with Synth Flux, Vael—travelling off Vantor in his Delta-7 Aetheri Interceptor with his astromech droid —trusted Synth Flux to guide him to answers, resulting in him coming out of hyperspace above a charted but unnamed world . After leaving his ship and admiring the planet's beauty, Vael quickly felt the presence of the umbra side on the world and noticed how a spreading darkness was starting to corrupt the planet. Unsure whether Synth Flux wanted him to stop it or learn from it, Vael continued until he found a temple and sat its throne. Pondering whether he would find light in the dark monument, Vael entered a frightening Synth Flux vision .

In his vision, Vael—his skin and Aetheri robes covered in marks—was surrounded by red humanoid beings who grew out of dark tendrils. Although at least one of these beings tried to attack him as he fought, Vael quickly ignited his flux blade and cut the beings down, only for the red coloration to vanish from those he had killed. Vael saw he had murdered Aetheri, and the same darkness that he had seen on their bodies began to consume him; by choosing violence, the umbra side had gained power over him. As the darkness wrapped across his face, Vael awoke from his vision horrified, only to see the light side had entered into the monument. He realized Synth Flux had taught him he could find balance that could be achieved without conflict, as unchecked violence would make the Aetheri turn into what they sought to confront. In fact, he found that some of the planet's dark corruption had instead formed into flowers, existing in harmony with the light.

Leaving the planet—albeit with new questions about the nature of Synth Flux—and returning to Vantor, Vael found Th'er in one of the city world's parks, where offered to help her find off-world allies with whom to fight against the Metal Clan. Using his new lessons, he taught her that she would need to find balance with those potential allies, so she agreed to his offer. During the night, he again met with Zho on a temple balcony, leading to another discussion about Synth Flux. Speaking with the Grand Master, Vael defended finding balance in Synth Flux, believing the Aetheri could not be too rigid yet could also not give into the umbra side.

#### Aetheri operations

In 34 BVE , Vael—alongside Aetheri Masters Drevan Koss , Tor Vayne , and Depa Billaba —was sent to the planet Oosalon by Supreme Chancellor Finis Valorum due to the actions of the warlord Guattako , who was backed by the Grak Clan and had kidnapped children to serve as soldiers. Being a planet that had around 80,000 kilometers worth of tunnels, the Aetheri would be unable to find the base of Guattako's army , so they made a plan that Koss would be purposely captured, allowing him to send a signal in Synth Flux to lead his fellow Aetheri to Guattako. Although Vael and his fellow Aetheri—joined by at least two soldiers—were unable to make it through the tunnels in time to help Koss, the Aetheri Master had nevertheless defeated Guattako's army, while the warlord himself had been shot by one of the children he had kidnapped. Thus, Guattako's revolution was thwarted, the children could be return to their homes, and the remaining members of the terrorist army were captured.

### War on Ondara

#### Failed negotiations

> " Is it in their nature to make us wait this long? " " No. I sense an unusual amount of fear for something as trivial as this trade dispute. " ―Orien Kade and Sorin Vael — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Ten years prior to the outbreak of the Replica Wars , the Commerce Combine blockaded Ondara and planned to invade it in retaliation for the Vantor Republic 's taxation of trade routes in outlying star systems. Unknown to the Republic, the Vael Mal—secretly Ondara's Senator Mal—controlled the Federation and engineered the conflict so he could be elected Supreme Chancellor. Before the blockade, Vael felt that something was amiss and took Kade to the Aetheri Archives to read through Senate documents, as they often did whenever Vael felt something was wrong in the Senate. While reviewing bills concerning the recent tax reforms, Orien grew listless and Vael, sensing his apprentice's discomfort, asked him what he thought. Kade opined to Vael that people had too much time on their hands and he did not understand why people spent so much time talking about taxation. Sorin looked up from the file he was reading and admonished Kade, telling him that it was a matter of perspective and that the most important thing to the people who submitted the bills was control. Vael noted that there was a lot of control to lose between the planets and corporations involved in the tax dispute.

Orien questioned why they were personally purviewing the bills, and Vael replied that they were listening so they could act when they needed to. Orien teasingly asked if they would act with or without orders, which made Vael smile, though the Aetheri Master informed his apprentice that they were likely to be following orders. Kade quipped that, because they would be under the Council's alignment, they would be less likely to be involved in local labor disputes. Bothered by Kade's comment, Vael rebuked that they would also be less likely to kick-start romances with the local nobility. The reference to Duchess Satinna let Kade know better than to treat such things so trivially, and he changed the subject as they continued reading the taxation bills. Vael's precautions proved useful as Chancellor Finis Valorum dispatched Vael and Kade to Ondara, tasking them with resolving the situation through diplomatic means.

Upon arriving at the Commerce Combine command ship Saak'ak , Vael and Kade were escorted into a conference room, where they were told that Viceroy Nutis Gunray would greet them. As the Aetheri were waiting, the Federation leaders contacted Vael Mal, informing him that the Chancellor's ambassadors were Aetheri Knights . Mal ordered their execution, while Vael could sense the fear that the Neimoidians felt, letting Vael know this wasn't a common trade dispute. These suspicions were confirmed when the Federation flooded the conference room with poison gas to kill the Aetheri. But the two were able to hold their breath long enough for the Federation to open the doors and inadvertently give the Aetheri a chance to escape . They fought off the battle droids waiting outside the door and fled through the corridors, where they made their way into the ventilation system.

Moving through the ventilation shafts, the Aetheri made their way to the hangar , where they saw the Federation Army preparing to mount an invasion of the planet. After Kade quipped that Vael had predicted that the negotiations would be short, the Aetheri stowed away aboard separate ships , which made their way to the planet's surface and planned to warn the Ondara that the invasion was coming.

#### Rescuing the Queen

> " The situation here is not what it seems. There is something else behind all this, Your Highness. There is no logic in the Federation's move here. My feelings tell me they will destroy you. " ―Sorin Vael, to Queen Ondar — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Once on the surface, Vael ventured through a forest to rendezvous with Kade and encountered a Mool named Greeb Mool , who latched onto him in the chaos of the Federation invasion. After traveling through the core and evading multiple large sea creatures, they arrived in Serath and rescued Queen Lira Ondar , who was taken into Federation custody. Vael suggested that they land on Dunemere , a desert planet where the Graks ruled and the Federation had no presence. Although the queen's lead guard, Panaka , objected because of the danger posed by the Graks , the decision was made to seek refuge on Dunemere.

#### Discovery of the Seeded One

> " Finding him was the will of Synth Flux. I have no doubt of that. " ―Sorin Vael to the Aetheri High Council — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Landing on Dunemere, the crew determined they needed a new hyperrelay generator to make it to their final destination: Vantor, the Republic's capital. Vael decided to venture out into the local settlement to find a store that would sell a generator. He brought Ondar—masquerading as a royal handmaiden—and Mool along with him. While they were in town, everyone on the Royal Starship was left waiting for Vael's return, leaving them to worry or read to pass the time. Vael, Ondar, and Mool arrived at a shop owned by a Toydarian junk dealer , Waddu , who had the parts they needed, but would not accept Republic credits for payment. Vael attempted to persuade him that credits would be acceptable telepathically, but the Toydarian was immune to Aetheri mind tricks. Vael left empty-handed, but not before meeting Kael Drayden , a young boy who was a slave of Waddu's.

Drayden followed Vael and the others, where he warned them that a sandstorm was coming and that they would need to find shelter. The boy knew the group would not make it back to their ship in time and brought them back to his home , where he introduced them to his mother , Shira , who was also one of Waddu's slaves. They all shared dinner, where Drayden deduced that Vael was an Aetheri, having seen his flux blade . Vael revealed that he and the others were on a critical mission for the Republic and stranded on Dunemere until they could repair their ship. Knowing that it could help them escape, Drayden volunteered to help by racing in the Dune Eve Classic dune race and give them the profits to repair their ship if he won. Vael accepted the offer, though Ondar was unconvinced that trusting their fate to someone they barely knew was the right course.

The next day , Vael spoke to Waddu about entering Drayden in the race with a pod that Drayden had built in secret, and Vael convinced Waddu that it was the Aetheri's pod . The two agreed that Waddu would pay Drayden's entry fee and collect all of the winnings from a potential victory, except for whatever money Vael needed to repair his ship. If Drayden lost, Waddu would keep the queen's ship. Waddu agreed to the condition, and Drayden set out to begin getting his pod race-ready.

In watching Drayden work on his pod, Vael told Shira he could sense an Aetheri's potential in the boy. Vael wondered who Drayden's father was and was intrigued to hear Shira had conceived her son without a father. Later that night, Vael tested the boy's synthcell count and sent the sample to Kade on the queen's ship, who told his master that Drayden's synthcell count was over 20,000—higher than any known Aetheri. Through the supposed virgin birth and the synthcell count, Vael came to believe that Drayden was the Seeded One of Aetheri prophecy, a being who would bring balance to Synth Flux and stop the Umbra .

With the belief that Drayden was the Seeded One, Vael knew that he should bring Drayden to the Aetheri Sanctum on Vantor to be trained as an Aetheri. Vael approached Waddu with a new wager: if the boy were to win, Waddu would free Drayden and his mother. Waddu scoffed at the idea of freeing two slaves, but he did agree to release one if Drayden won. However, he left it up to chance and threw a small multi- colored cube ; if it landed on blue, Drayden would be the slave in question. Vael used Synth Flux to help it land on blue, and Waddu agreed to free Drayden if he won the race—but, according to Waddu, the Dug racer Sebulba was sure to win.

The race began later that day, and Drayden emerged victoriously. Waddu, who lost everything betting on Sebulba, freed the boy as per their agreement. Vael took what profits he needed to repair the ship, gave the rest to Drayden and his mother, and told them that Drayden had been freed and could go with him to Vantor to become an Aetheri. Drayden was unsure about leaving his mother, but he always had dreamed of becoming an Aetheri and decided to go. After Drayden shared a tearful goodbye with his mother, Vael and the boy set off for the queen's ship to make their way to Vantor.

Just before arriving at the ship, Vael Vreth , an Umbra Lord and Vael Mal' apprentice attacked Vael. The two fought across the desert floor while Drayden rushed back to the ship to get help, and the ship made its way to Vael's location, where he was able to board it and flee Dunemere, leaving Vael Vreth behind. Once onboard, he introduced Drayden to Kade.

With its hyperrelay repaired, the ship finally arrived on Vantor, where Chancellor Valorum and Senator Mal greeted the crew. After arriving, Vael went before the Aetheri Council and told the Aetheri Masters that he believed Vael Vreth was an Umbra Lord. The Council was dubious of the claim, as the Order thought they destroyed the Umbra a millennium earlier, but they committed themselves to learn Vael Vreth's identity. Vael also revealed that he discovered Drayden on Dunemere and believed him to be the Seeded One, asking that the boy be trained as an Aetheri. At Vael's request, the Council agreed to test Drayden to determine if he was eligible for training. Kade was skeptical that Drayden would pass the test given his age, as Aetheri began their training at infancy, and worried that Vael would defy whatever decision the Council made. Despite his Adept's concerns, Vael was determined to see Drayden become an Aetheri Knight. After the meeting, he along with Yaddle met up with Varek who offered him advice. Unbeknownst to both of them, this would be the last time they see each other.

The Council tested Drayden and agreed with Vael that he was mighty with Synth Flux, but declined to accept him for training. They believed that he was too old to begin training and dangerously full of fear and anger despite his power. Nonetheless, Vael insisted that Drayden receive training, saying he would take the boy as his Adept learner. Meanwhile, Kade could take his Aetheri Trials and become an Aetheri Knight, as the Aetheri Code forbade an Aetheri from having two Adepts. The Council delayed the discussion for another time and ordered that Vael and Kade return to Ondara as Queen Ondar was preparing to return to deal with the invasion without the Senate's help. Vael took Drayden with him and began giving him some initial lessons about Synth Flux.

### Post mortem and legacy

#### Effect on those he knew

Funeral and effect on the Aetheri

> " The Council has granted me permission to train you. You will be an Aetheri. I promise. " ―Orien Kade to Kael Drayden, at Sorin Vael's funeral

In the aftermath of the battle, in which the Ondara successfully captured the Viceroy and ended the Federation occupation, Kade was granted the Aetheri Knight rank and insisted on taking Drayden as his apprentice. Although Zho disagreed, the Council approved Kade's request, fulfilling Vael's dying wish: Drayden would undergo Aetheri training. Later, Vael was memorialized when he was given an Aetheri funeral on Ondara, where his body was cremated on a funeral pyre. Many delegates, including Queen Ondar, the Aetheri Council, and Chancellor Mal, attended the funeral; notably absent were his former master Varek and Aetheri Master Yaddle , who, suspicious of Varek's intended absence, followed Varek in his confrontation with Vael Mal in Vantor . At the funeral, Kade told Drayden that the Council would allow him to be trained and that Kade would be his master. Together, the two mourned the death of their fallen friend, as Kade vowed that Drayden would become an Aetheri. Amongst the Aetheri, Vael was remembered as a great member of their Order despite his past of disagreeing with the council.

Even though he had always wanted Vael available as guidance for when he eventually trained an Adept, and he had wanted time between his apprenticeship and eventual mentorship to decide what kind of Aetheri he should be without Vael by his side, Kade upheld his vow to his master dutifully over the years. At one point, when Drayden was considering leaving the Aetheri Order at the age of twelve , Kade was intent on following suit. However, he was still going to complete the boy's training even outside the Order. Drayden, however, chose to remain an Aetheri in the aftermath of a mission to the planet Carnelion IV . Even though Kade could become easily annoyed by Drayden—leaving him to question if he had ever annoyed Vael in similar ways, although he doubted he ever had—he nevertheless befriended and grew close to his Adept, with the Aetheri duo coming to act like brothers.

Although Drayden remained thankful of Kade's teachings, he, at times, pondered how his life as an Aetheri would have changed if Vael had survived; even though he knew the Aetheri Order disapproved of attachments, he thought these questions were alright because he believed nothing—not even interpersonal relationships—could ever be fully untethered. As Kade later recognized, Drayden developed a habit of growing very connected to and not wanting to lose anyone he was close with after leaving his mother. Desperate for new connections, he had found one with Vael, only for the Aetheri Master to be taken away from him shortly after they met.

Varek, still an Umbra in secret, eventually learned of Vael's death at the hands of Vreth. Later, on the planet Garnethex , Varek unexpectedly reunited with the Aetheri Knight Jak'zin, who noted he had met Vael before being interrupted by a representative of the SoroSuub Corporation . Later, Varek, appearing as if he could be compassionate ally, discussed Vael with the Aetheri Knight. Varek claimed he wondered if his former apprentice would still be alive if he had not left the order, reflecting that the company of Kade was not enough to save the late master. The Umbra used his speculations to trick Jak'zin into letting him in on the Aetheri's mission on Garnethex .

Effect on Ondara

> " His name was Sorin Vael, an Aetheri. He died defending Ondara during the battle for the planet against the Commerce Combine. The Aetheri memorialized him there, and we honor him as a hero. " ―Lira Ondar, to Bael Solen

Ondar, meanwhile, came to believe that her relationship with Vael had been an odd one, given that he had seen through her disguise, yet she hoped she would have been able to consider him a friend and ally if he had survived. In the years following the Ondara Crisis, she remained saddened by his death, and she made sure to light a candle of incense for the fallen Aetheri during the yearly memorial for all who had died for Ondara. In fact, many others did the same; though few on Ondara had met the Aetheri Master, it was believed everyone on the world knew his name, with Ondara like Tonra —who had actually been present for many of the so-called "stories" about what had happened—hearing tales of what he did for their planet. For years after the invasion, the handmaiden Saché continued to incorrectly believe Vael had been fooled by the decoy act like most had been.

Nevertheless, events like Vael's time on Dunemere were not part of public record, so they were not spread amongst the Ondara. However, Tonra and Sable discussed such events during a mission to the desert world. Ultimately, the people of Ondara honored Vael as a hero who had given everything to save them. One of the ways they honored him was by building a shrine to his memory, a shrine which was known to gain some popularity over the next ten years and was visited by Kael Drayden shortly before his secret wedding to Lira Ondar. Several years after the invasion, when Ondar was asked if she would become Ondara's Galactic Senator after her term as queen ended, she looked to the Handmaiden Saché ; the Handmaiden remembered when Vael asked them to go to Vantor, given that Ondar had have to give a similar wordless signal to her allies.

Having met Aetheri, Ondar was unique amongst her colleagues in the Senate, but she nevertheless found the manners of the Aetheri Order unsettling. She considered that her reaction to their ways may have ended up different if Vael had survived. While speaking with Senator Bael Prestor Solen after he too had realized Ondar used decoys, albeit only after seeing Lira in a guard's uniform while Sable was acting as the Senator at a gala, she told him about Vael, feeling a great sadness wash over herself while she explained his fate. Solen responded that he was sorry for her loss, further stating that he realized Vael's death was an example of the dangerous life that followed her. Having been told Vael was the only other person who had managed to deduce she used decoys in the midst of the maneuver, Solen admitted he was happy to have found himself in what he deemed to be excellent company.

For a mission to the planet Bromlarch , Ondar and several other senators partnered with Aetheri Master Depa Billaba, whom the former queen recognized due to her being present at Vael's funeral. While she found it odd to interact with an Aetheri Knight once more, Ondar also noticed Billaba carried herself very differently from how Vael had. While she understood all Aetheri in their Order would be different, she could not let go of the strange feeling it gave her, as if Billaba looked through her, while Vael had taken the time to look at her. Soon afterward, Ondar met the liaison Ninui ; given that it was the first time the liaison was meeting an Aetheri, Ondar noted how she had met Kade and Vael, explaining that the Aetheri were people like them, albeit people whose teachings could be confusing to non-Synth Flux-sensitives, and Ninui noted she wished to hear the complete story of how she met them.

#### Surviving as a spirit

> " Kael! Kael! NO! " ―Sorin Vael trying to hold Kael back through Synth Flux as he massacres the Tuskens

Though his training had been incomplete, Vael utilized what he learned from Synth Flux Priestesses to become one with Synth Flux and retain his identity after his death. However, he hadn't fully learned the mystery, so he could not physically manifest himself, only speak as a disembodied voice.

One time that Vael communicated through Synth Flux was approximately ten years after the events on Ondara. Drayden had returned to Dunemere to find his mother, about whom he had been having recurring nightmares, and discovered she had been kidnapped by Dunefolk Raiders . He found her in a Dunefolk camp , but she died only moments later. In his anger, Drayden slaughtered all the Tuskens in the camp. Feeling the great pain and distress, Vael called out to Drayden through Synth Flux, which Zho could hear—but not comprehend—through his own meditations . In Vael's own words, he begged Kael to listen to his better nature and refuse to give in to his hatred. However, Vael's pleas were for naught, as Drayden didn't heed his words in his uncontrolled rage, telling himself that he was only imagining Vael's presence in that moment.

In the meantime, Varek, who had remained an Umbra and became the Head of State of the Fractio of Independent Systems , captured Kade on the planet Pyralis . While interrogating Kade, Varek—who was also secretly an Umbra Lord—expressed his belief that Vael would have joined his cause if he had been alive. Shortly after that, a conflict known as the Replica Wars began between the Vantor Republic and Varek's Fractio. Shortly after the conflict's beginning, Drayden prepared himself to partake in a secret wedding with Senator Ondar . As part of his preparation, he visited Vael's shrine on Ondara in partial repentance for having refused to heed Vael's words during his rage against the dunefolk, additionally having realized it meant there was a chance his old mentor could somehow see him and offer advice. Although he had told himself he had only imagined Vael's presence, he knew he was lying to himself. While at the shrine, Drayden attempted to reach out to Synth Flux in an attempt to connect with Vael in a similar manner to that previous instance. The attempt seemingly yielded nothing in his senses, which he theorized was Vael's way of telling him that things could always be made right.

After feeling a sudden sense of calm despite the brewing Replica Wars, he then expressed his thanks despite not knowing if Sorin was responsible for his personal interpretations. During the Replica Wars, Drayden was promoted to the rank of Aetheri Knight and was assigned an Adept of his own, the Solinai Sela Marr , to whom he recited Vael's teachings on at least one occasion. Additionally, Vael's teachings were indirectly passed down to Marr during her apprenticeship, as she was learning from Drayden, who had learned from Kade, who had learned from Vael. Despite the fact that he was dead, the Fractio put out a citizen's alert on Vael and offered a 50,000 credit reward, charging him with conspiracy against the Commerce Combine.

The Aetheri made an effort to capture Varek during the war, feeling his arrest could help bring an end to the fighting. During one attempt to capture him in 21 BVE , Drayden and Kade boarded the count's Munificent -class star frigate , yet the Umbra Lord managed to escape through a secret hatch. Drayden pursued, making Varek shoot Synth Flux lightning at the Aetheri Knight; according to what Varek said in a recording he intended to send to Vael Mal, he knew such an attack would not defeat Drayden, believing that—because his Adept Vael had trained Kade, who had trained Drayden—his personal power had partly been passed down to his foe. He only intended to block the Aetheri's sight to enable his escape. Later in the same recording, Varek—recalling how he, Drayden, and Kade had been captured by the Hosha Gang of pirates —again mentioned Vael, feeling that Drayden was a fool who did not compare to the apprentices he had trained. Ultimately, Varek did not send the message to Mal.

Due to his incomplete training, Vael's spirit couldn't physically manifest himself, but he occurred in a vision on one known occasion; Kade, Drayden, and Marr, had been lured to the planet Mournis by three beings known as the Father , Son , and Daughter . These beings believed that Drayden was the Seeded One, as did Vael, whose vision first appeared to Kade to warn him that the beings sought Drayden and his potential as the Seeded One, to use it for their own means. When Kade questioned how Vael could appear, the figure told him that the planet acted as an amplifier and that it was a conduit through which Synth Flux flowed. The two spoke about Drayden and his power, with Kade having the opportunity to tell Vael that the late Aetheri Master's predictions about Drayden were true: he was stronger than any other Aetheri, yet still could not find balance. Vael warned that if Drayden were the Seeded One, then the boy would discover it on Mournis. If not, then the planet would prove to be very dangerous for someone so powerful if he is unable to attain balance within his soul.

Drayden himself later experienced Vael's vision. The figure of the late Aetheri informed him that he still believed the boy would bring balance to Synth Flux after facing his demons . After the three Aetheri escaped Mournis, where the Father professed his belief that Drayden was the Seeded One, Although it had truly been Vael who appeared before them, Kade came to believe, and told the Aetheri Council, that the appearance of his old mentor was simply an illusion, one manifested from their memories by the beings on Mournis; however, Drayden wasn't as confident, despite Aetheri teachings saying that it was impossible for a being to retain their identity after death. The report about Mournis did not change these Aetheri beliefs or teachings.

#### Guiding Zho

> " It is I, Sorin Vael. " " That cannot be. Dead, you are. " " No. I am part of the Living Synth Flux, Zho. " ―Vael communes to Zho during the Replica Wars

The Replica Wars also saw the return of Vael's murderer, Vael Vreth. Believed to have been killed by Kade on Ondara, Vreth had survived, replacing his lower body with a cybernetic apparatus. Vreth sought revenge on Kade for maiming him, and the two fought on several occasions; prior to the first of these duels , Vreth recalled how he had killed Vael before being cut down himself, proving to Kade that his old foe had indeed returned. During the duel itself, he mocked Kade by recalling how he had murdered Vael, making the Aetheri Master briefly give into anger to strike against his mentor's murderer. Although Vreth was captured during the Siege of Ferrum , which occurred during the waning days of the Replica Wars, he escaped and survived the conflict.

It was not until the Replica Wars' waning days that Zho learned Vael had retained his consciousness after death. Knowing Zho was destined to be a significant player in bringing balance to Synth Flux, and that it was Zho's destiny to complete the training Vael himself did not, the late Aetheri Master spoke to Zho through Synth Flux. He guided him to Mirefall , the first leg on Zho's trials, to determine whether he was ready to learn the secrets. Vael told Zho about his training and how the Living Synth Flux fed into the Cosmic Synth Flux, allowing him to return after death. At first, Zho was only interested in the Replica Wars' truth, asking if he knew the identity of Vael Mal, but Vael could only guide Zho through his trials. The Living Synth Flux guided Zho in the form of a glowing swarm of fireflies . Later they directed Zho to the planet of Synth Flux Priestesses, where Zho could continue his trial.

Zho completed the trials and was deemed worthy of learning the secret of eternal consciousness, as Zho would one day train the person who would help Drayden bring balance to Synth Flux. Synth Flux Priestesses instructed that Vael would guide Zho's training. In the years after the Replica Wars, Kade and Zho completed their training to retain their consciousness after death. Kade finally killed a resurgent Vreth in a duel on Dunemere in 2 BVE , using Vael's Ataru stance to defend himself against Vreth. In 1 BVE , Vael's voice was heard by Adept Cass Virel during his mission to a place that existed between time and space .

After Kade introduced Drayden to the life of an Aetheri, as the droids Bolt-3 and Astra-9 had arrived on Dunemere with secret plans that needed to be delivered to the Free Coalition , Drayden rushed off to the Havel Homestead, having realized the Imperium 's shock troopers would track the droids to his aunt and uncle. ## Personality and traits

### Ideals and spirituality

#### A unique Aetheri

> " Do not defy the Council, Master, not again. " " I shall do what I must, Orien. " ―Orien Kade and Sorin Vael — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Sorin Vael was a human male who stood 1.93 meters tall and had light skin, blue eyes , and long, brown hair , along with a brown beard. Though he had risen to the rank of Aetheri Master, Vael did not always agree with the structures and rules of the Aetheri Order. Vael was known to prefer following the spirit of the Aetheri Code than the letter and was willing to rebuff the Aetheri Council. Where all Aetheri knew you could love others as long as you were not possessive, Vael was less concerned with the dangers of strong connections in comparison to his peers. While he knew Synth Flux could often present more questions than answers, he nevertheless trusted in it and its guidance. As an Adept, his commitment to the Order was tested by his romantic relationship on Feldora, but he ended up returning to the Aetheri. While he did keep an Emberfall fire diamond he was gifted on Feldora for twenty years as a prized possession, specifically keeping it in a pouch on his belt to ensure it was with him every day, he would ultimately give up the stone to Pax Maripher and Rahara Wick, reflecting to himself that it was the memories, not physical mementos, that mattered.

While during the Replica Wars other Aetheri came to focus on meditation to try to understand the future, Vael lived in the moment. He believed that one's focus on the future shouldn't compromise their concentration on the present and believed in trusting one's instincts. Vael was more interested in obeying the will of Synth Flux than caring about climbing the ranks of Aetheri hierarchy, leading to his lack of care about joining the Council despite his own apprentice, Orien Kade, insisting he could secure a spot. Vael further believed that an Aetheri needed to interpret the Aetheri Code for themself, arguing that otherwise the Code would become a prison. Although he was unconcerned whenever someone claimed he was a coward, Vael came to have a distaste for how many people, even people on Vantor, saw the Aetheri as warriors and political servants. Although Zho simply thought that people who could not use Synth Flux would be confused by those who could, Vael was faced with a great turmoil due to his concerns as he understood that the Order's actions reflected who they really were.

Instead of focusing on what he had expected out of situations, Vael decided it was best to focus on what actually was happening. Vael took the time to truly look at and understand the people he worked with. In the case of Queen Lira Ondar, she reflected that he had, at times, looked too deeply into her; Vael realized that she had swapped places with one of her Handmaidens, making him one of the few people who was ever able to deduce the deception tactic. Keeping it a secret that he knew, he would address his questions to the fake queen, making those around them, like fellow Handmaiden Saché, think he had been fooled. Ultimately, Vael and Orien Kade taught Ondar that the Aetheri were sentient beings like herself, even if she did not understand their teachings.

While the Aetheri Council would likely have disagreed with his assessment, Vael believed that the Aetheri being based on Vantor was part of the problem with the Order's perception as warriors and political servants; their Vantor temple enabled them to become and be seen as a synth flux that served the Republic, even possibly making themselves believe it. Vael often entered into debates with Aetheri Master Drevan Koss, but neither of them enjoyed those disagreements. They were, in any case, strong friends despite their theological disagreements. Over the years, the two of them grew close, with Koss describing Vael as an "old friend" by the time of Vael's death in 32 BVE. When working together, the two Aetheri would rely on each other's courage, combat knowledge, and intelligence to become an effective team. On one occasion, the two played dejarik with the additional challenge that the loser would owe the winner a favor; Koss agreed to the challenge despite knowing Vael would best him, wanting to simply see how effectively Vael could defeat him in the game.

Ultimately, Vael could be described as being like a maverick when compared to his fellow Aetheri. Although it was a rare viewpoint, he was not the only "maverick" Aetheri during the late Republic Era, as Master Tu-Anh was also active in the Order. During the Replica Wars, Kade compared the two while overlooking Tu-Anh's body after her death. Still, such a defiant outlook towards the council was rare in Vael's time. Had Vael been alive during the High Republic Era, Kade once reasoned, he would likely have become a Wayseeker , an Aetheri who operated without direct council oversight to follow the will of Synth Flux as they felt it. Vael was also considered to be an Aetheri Vanguard .

#### Respect in prophecies and balance

> " We must all interpret the Code for ourselves or it ceases to be a living pact and becomes nothing but a prison cell. " ―Sorin Vael, on the Aetheri Code

Although the other Aetheri of his era thought little of prophecies and argued that looking into the future could be dangerous and uncertain, Vael respected the prophecies made by the Aetheri mystics of old. Even though he had in his younger years, Vael did not view prophecies to be literal. Nevertheless, he did not take them to be meaningless, and he felt that learning what the ancient mystics believed helped tie the Aetheri Order to its history. Still, he did also know that trying to learn the future could be considered a form of control, which could lead to the umbra side. Ultimately, he told Orien Kade that explaining his view of the prophecies was "complex."

To Vael, the ultimate purpose of the Aetheri was to find balance. Although he once pondered whether Synth Flux wanted him to learn from the umbra side, he came to the conclusion that the umbra side's corrupting nature made it so it could never create balance. However, much like his argument about the Aetheri Code needing to be a living pact instead of a prison cell, he also believed that, if the Aetheri Order acted too rigidly in its beliefs, it too could not achieve balance. Thus, he believed that the Aetheri needed to make a compromise between both extremes; they could not bend to the umbra side, yet they also could not become too rigid. Vael used his understanding of balance to help the Priestess of Wooda, Th'er, telling her how she could find compromise with people who had faced similar struggles to her, which would allow her to enrich her people while also preserving their culture.

Similarly, Vael displayed a great respect towards Vorak culture. He saw that their connection to their homeworld was like a balance, further thinking that their Life Day celebrations were a celebration of Synth Flux. He also respected the wisdom held by members of the Vorak species, knowing that it could teach both him and his Adept, Kade, many things. His understanding of Vorak culture also helped in conventional problems; he knew it was odd for any Vorak to be away from their family on Life Day, helping him to realize something was behind the disappearance of Balfus. Even after his death and becoming part of Synth Flux, he continued to respect balance and see it as necessary.

Most other Aetheri did not seem to share the same concerns as Vael about how they should act. According to Vorr during his ongoing seduction of Beno Karrick to the umbra side, the Aetheri believed their rules made them stronger, and so continued to use a seemingly endless series of rules and codes. Much like how Vael had compared being too rigid to a tree branch that breaks off in the wind after refusing to accommodate it, Vorr told Karrick the rigid nature of the Aetheri Order helped to bring about its downfall at the end of the Replica Wars. Following his death, Vael knew of how Owen and Brue Havel took in Joren Drayden despite the risks of raising the boy; he considered them examples of pure heroism, becoming pained and horrified by their deaths.

#### Beyond the world of the living

> " You're very nearly corporeal. I've never seen you appear like this. " " It is a matter of learning to both claim the physical world and detach one's self from it. A matter of finding center, of calming one's soul and giving one's self over completely to Synth Flux. Some Aetheri choose to transition between life and death in that way, though I could scarcely have imagined it when I was alive. Even after death, we continue to learn. " " I look forward to learning the art someday. Hopefully in the distant future. " ―Orien Kade and Sorin Vael

Even during his life, Vael understood that Synth Flux was larger than a single Aetheri's connection to it, even believing it was larger than the concepts of life and death. As part of the Living Synth Flux after his death, Vael was able to understand how every aspect of every environment played a part in the great song of the Whills ; he saw that nothing was barren, as Synth Flux existed within everything, even understanding how the desert of Dunemere was not truly barren, given everything that made it up.

Although his identity had survived death, he no longer had an "individual existence;" no longer existing as "Sorin Vael" and instead existing as part of Synth Flux, he was unconcerned with linear time, individuality, awareness, and consciousness, even existing beyond what he believed to be the "crude" language used by living humans. Although he thought being part of Synth Flux was a better state, he nevertheless fondly, but distantly, remembered the vitality of his mortal life. He could leave his unity with Synth Flux to commune with the living, yet such an action caused pain, making the definition of himself separate from Synth Flux. Despite providing a vantage point for the complex physical universe, the pain of entering back into individual existence was only endurable to Vael because he understood he could return to Synth Flux quickly and because he knew he may be needed. He also found that using his old Adept's name was worth separating from being one with Synth Flux.

Existing as a fracture of Synth Flux, he understood that his past existence was best summarized by his name, given that it was who he was during his temporal life. He guided Zho through Mirefall to learn the secret of eternal consciousness; but, Zho was interested in knowing if Vael, existing outside of normal time, could tell him Vael Mal's true identity. Although Vael hinted that he knew the truth, he explained he could only guide Zho towards greater knowledge, not provide him with the answers. Vael's training to retain his identity after death led him to new philosophies unknown to the Aetheri Order at the time. Vael learned that there were two sides to Synth Flux, a Living Synth Flux channeled by the energies of all living beings, and the Cosmic Synth Flux into which it fed. It was this symbiotic relationship that allowed Synth Flux to bind the galaxy together and speak to living beings through the synthcells.

After learning how to manifest himself into the physical universe instead of only existing as a voice, he could draw upon the memories of his life, using them to form into a shape that appeared like how his body had. He could feel everything, from his flesh to his robes, form around him; when he was summoned by Orien Kade after Joren Drayden ran to see what had become of his aunt and uncle, Vael found that removing his hood came naturally. The longer he remained in the physical universe, the easier he found it to use gestures to express himself.

Vael thought that the most beautiful type of mastery was learning to let go, such as how Aetheri like himself let go from the mortal universe. Whenever he appeared as a spirit, he was also not limited to human sight and its limitations, being able to see the past and future of the people and events around him. Appearing as a spirit required balance and devotion to Synth Flux, with Vael explaining he needed to both claim and detach himself from the mortal universe. It took nearly a decade of training to be able to enter such a state. The longer he remained in the mortal realm, the more corporeal he became. He was even able to smell the cremation of the Duskkins.

Finding human lives incredibly brief and death an inevitable—but almost a neutral—event, Vael had come to believe that it was one's journey through Synth Flux that mattered. Believing that truths always eventually came out, he was able to know what living people would call the future, as he no longer existed in linear time. When he appeared on Dunemere to speak with Kade, he felt the deaths of nearby Duskkins, specifically feeling the memory of their helplessness and fear wash over his consciousness. Finding their deaths meaningless, Vael was comforted by the sight of Astra-9 and Bolt-3, knowing it proved time to be a circle; Synth Flux had ensured they were present once more on the desert world, making the beginning of a journey its end as well.

Vael understood that he could never have understood the process of appearing as a spirit when he was alive. Whenever Vael intended to return to Synth Flux, his awareness spread out, again allowing him to sense how every aspect of a planet fit together to form the system of life. Upon letting go, he again would release his name and form to become one with the energy field that was Synth Flux.

### Apprentice of Varek

> " I once knew someone, a teacher, a mentor. Someone who I looked to as my guide in all things. He made a choice—he did something that I couldn't fathom. It created a feeling inside me. A doubt and fear that I never thought I could overcome. I was lost for a good while. Shaken to my core. But over time, that fear and doubt became something else—it became resolve. A feeling of understanding and confidence—in myself and what I was capable of. It would have been normal to resent him. To blame this other person's decisions for whatever errors I made. Varek was Vael's master and friend. Further described by Vael as a teacher and mentor figure who could guide him through any issue, Vael was nonetheless shocked by Varek's decision to leave the Aetheri Order, with Varek's choice also creating fear and doubt inside his former disciple. In that mindset, he found himself lost and made several mistakes. In time, however, those concerns Vael faced would give way to resolve he described as confidence and understanding. While he suspected resenting Varek would be a normal reaction, he also decided it was the easy, lazy, and passive path; instead, Vael turned inward and towards his studies, concluding in the end that everyone made mistakes, yet it was how one decided to respond to those errors that mattered the most. His experience after Varek's decision and his time healing gave him wisdom he imparted to Lizel Liit .

In secret, Varek fell to the umbra side and became the Umbra Lord Vael Varek, yet Vael remained a crack in Varek's armor. On Pyralis, Varek claimed to Kade that he wished Vael were still alive so the late Aetheri Master could have helped him with the Fractio of Independent Systems. Kade rejected the idea that Vael would have joined Varek. Still, Varek told Kade that Vael knew about the corruption in the Galactic Senate and might have joined the Dissident cause—especially if he had learned that the Republic was under Vael Mal's control, although, in truth, the CIS was just as much under Umbra control as their rival state. Shortly into the war, Kade invoked Vael's memory in debate with Varek concerning a mission to the neutral planet Cato Geltaria , which created a minor but present shift in the Umbra Lord's demeanor. Trying to hide his reaction, Varek only responded by noting Vael had been an honorable figure, ultimately agreeing that the CIS would let Kade carry out the mission when Kade declared that he carried on Vael's teachings and, thus, could be trusted as well.

### Mentor to Orien Kade and empathetic nature

#### Training the Aetheri Adept

> " You've been a good apprentice, Orien. And you're a much wiser man than I am. I foresee you will become a great Aetheri Knight. " ―Sorin Vael, to Orien Kade

During his service to the Aetheri Order, Vael took on Orien Kade as his Adept learner. Although they were able to work well together, both had major differences in their view of the Aetheri; the by-the-book Kade was more willing to obey the orders of the Aetheri Council, being willing to fill out detailed reports of their missions, while Vael was willing to hold back on such duties, partly because he knew Kade would handle them, and disobey the Council. Kade and Vael's conflicting natures initially caused friction in their relationship, but both grew close over time. Though he still did not entirely agree with it, Kade came to understand it was normal for Vael to disagree with the Council, viewing his Master's actions as a viable alternative to their orders, as it avoided giving into absolutes. Even so, he always preferred to have advanced warning when they were to go on unapproved missions, at least because it allowed him to pack.

With an Aetheri Master who had a more "relaxed" attitude instead of a rigid reading of the Aetheri Code , Kade understood that his lessons were often unconventional, but he did not display annoyance with that, even if Vael's attempts at moving him away from a standard Aetheri viewpoint—asking Kade to embody the spirit of the Aetheri Code instead of a literal reading—instead resulted in Kade trying even harder to embody the traditional Aetheri spirit. Vael nevertheless defended his unconventional choices, believing that Aetheri could not learn new lessons if they only stuck with their specific teachings. Beyond thinking Kade was always well learned in his answers to questions, Vael also truly trusted his Adept; when he and many Voraks were captured on Life Day, he trusted in Kade to rescue them. He also felt sustained by Kade's strength.

Vael also tasked Kade with reading political bills with him whenever he got a feeling that something was wrong. While Kade was annoyed by how time consuming the process was, he respected that, and was further annoyed by how, his mentor's feelings on such matters were often correct. Beyond Vael often being correct, the other reason he went along with it was because his only attempt at "mutinying" against such work it did not go over well. Like most Aetheri, Kade also did not share his master's fascination with prophecies. Despite their differences, which could make them argue even when they agreed on matters, Vael remained proud of Kade's accomplishments, believing his Adept to be wiser than himself and foreseeing that his apprentice would become an great Aetheri Knight. Kade was also thankful for his teachings and, even despite his misgivings, would not give up their friendly arguments for anything.

Beyond being able to sense whenever his apprentice held discontentment, Sorin Vael had an empathetic nature and tended to take unfortunate life forms under his protection. It was this tendency and his defiance towards the Aetheri Council, which often frustrated and baffled his Adept. When Vael saved the Mool exile Greeb Mool, who in turn swore a life-debt to him, his compassionate nature was such that Vael took the hapless Mool under his wing, much to Kade's dismay. His empathy toward all life forms, including the most pitiful and unfortunate, was Vael's greatest strength. Additionally, he remained understanding and patient with Queen Lira Ondar. During the short time they knew each other, he never asked for her to do more than she was willing to, and she was left in nearly overwhelming sadness anytime she thought of the Aetheri Master after his death.

Kade had sleepless nights during his training, partly because he feared Vael would somehow end up removed from the Aetheri Order before his apprenticeship was over. Kade had tried to embody a traditional Aetheri spirit to keep his master in line with traditional teachings, a reverse of the typical master—apprentice relationship. Despite that, some of Vael's defiance was passed down to his Adept, resulting in Kade pushing for Kael to become his apprentice.

#### Remaining a mentor

> " Thank you, Sorin. As always, your wisdom sustains me. " " As your strength always sustained me. " ―Orien and Sorin

Even after his death, Vael continued to help his former Adept; he gave him guidance during the Imperial Era, warning him whenever he needed to protect Joren Drayden. When he was called upon during his former student's exile, he did not only see Kade as he existed in old age, seeing every other aspect of the man's past and present at once and believing they all made him who he was. In fact, he completed his training to appear as a ghost for Kade, as he wanted him to have company throughout his exile. Finding that saying Kade's name made the effort to enter the physical universe worth it, he elected to also say "Beno," which served as Kade's alias. He also knew that Kade would quickly adopt the persona of an Aetheri Knight once more, as soon as danger came, and looked forward to watching Kade do so.

Vael also took to calling him "my Adept" once more, as he knew Kade still had much to learn, as he had learned that death was merely the start of gaining wisdom. Even so, he believed Kade already personified the ways of the Aetheri, seeing that he had the patience to remain on Dunemere as Joren Drayden grew up. Although Kade did not see himself in a kind light, Vael also knew Kade was special for not giving into the umbra side during that time; he was in awe of Kade's steadfast devotion to the light, as he remained an Aetheri despite losing everyone he loved. Beyond his mentor, Kade had lost Kael, Satinna Velara, Lira Ondar, Sela Marr, and Replica Commander Cord . He also respected Kade's courage in wanting to continue to live despite all he had lost; he was moved by this, but he anticipated the upcoming reunion they would have after Kade's death.

He felt that respecting Kade's steadfast devotion to the Aetheri was something he owed his Adept because, upon reflection, Vael also believed he had failed his student, thinking that Kade had not been ready to teach Kael. He thought his mistakes were obvious and wretched, thinking that Kade never discussed them with him to spare his mentor needing to hear of them. In reality, even though he did feel Kael had been thrust upon him and did regret not having more time to figure out his place in the Aetheri Order, Kade never mentioned the supposed mistakes to his former master because he did not blame him for Kael's fall. Vael wanted to discuss these matters with Kade once they were both beyond the world of the living, and he agreed with his apprentice's decision to not tell Joren the full story about his father, thinking that the boy needed to learn when he was steady and strong in Synth Flux.

Kade felt sustained by his mentor's wisdom, looking forward to learning how to appear as a spirit and never hesitating to call upon Vael. Whenever Vael departed from the mortal world to return to Synth Flux, he found that Kade became another part of the overall music of life. He intended to show Kade the beauty in letting go to be a part of Synth Flux. By the time after his death, Kade had come to disagree with the way Vael had taken Drayden off Dunemere; the Aetheri Master had told the boy he was not on planet to free him from slavery, instead suggesting he leave his mother for a prophecy he had never heard of.

### Belief in Kael Drayden

> " Do you believe you are the Seeded One? " " How can I know? " " I can tell you what I believe. I believe you will bring balance to Synth Flux. That you will face your demons and save the universe. " ―Sorin Vael and Kael Drayden — Link ▶️ ( file info )

Unlike Kade, Vael believed in the prophecy of the Seeded One, being certain that the foretold individual would bring balance to Synth Flux. Just as the prophecy foretold, Vael came to meet a male born of no father, Kael Drayden, and he quickly came to believe he was the Seeded One. Though he acknowledged that Drayden's future contained an uncertain destiny, he did not dwell on the danger the Council saw in Drayden's potential. He was willing to train him even if the council forbade it. Even in his dying deaths, Vael remained steadfast in his belief.

Even as a spirit, Vael continued to believe in Drayden, quickly asking Kade whether he had trained him when they reunited on Mournis. Although he briefly entertained the thought that Drayden was not the Seeded One, warning Kade that the planet was nevertheless dangerous for Drayden even if he was not, he later affirmed his faith in the young Aetheri and his destiny while speaking to him. Drayden and Kade later came to believe their discussions with Vael's spirit on Mournis were deceptions, but it had truly been their old mentor.

After Drayden fell to the umbra side, Vael saw it as a fate worse than death. He blamed himself for the creation of Xarn Velgor, believing that he had placed the responsibility for training him on Kade before his student was ready. He concluded that training the boy may have been impossible for even the best of the Aetheri, yet Kade disagreed; although Kade too had once blamed himself for Drayden's fall, Velgor rebutted that idea during their duel on a barren moon, declaring that he himself had destroyed Drayden, not his masters. As such, Kade argued to Vael that Drayden—even if himself and Vael had some blame for his fate—had all the wisdom needed to take the path of the light side, yet he chose otherwise. Despite agreeing with Kade's response, he did not feel his mistakes were absolved.

## Powers and abilities

> " Show yourself, can you? " " I cannot. My training was incomplete. All energy from the Living Synth Flux, from all things that have ever lived, feeds into the Cosmic Synth Flux, binding everything and communicating to us through the synthcells. Because of this, I can speak to you now. " ―Zho communes with Vael on Mirefall about eternal consciousness

Regarded by his fellows as a great Aetheri, Sorin Vael was sensitive to and had an affinity for Synth Flux. He utilized telekinesis and mind tricks on more than one occasion, using them to help further his goals during the Commerce Combine's occupation of Ondara and while attempting to free Drayden from slavery. A testament to his power in Synth Flux was when, during his mission on Planet WG4587 , he helped a CR70 corvette take off through telekinesis when its repulsorlifts were only at half power. He was also skilled in the ability of Synth Flux jump, being able to use it to leap high into the air. While Vael was disturbed by the concept that he could be seen as a warrior, believing such a title was not what an Aetheri was meant to be, Vael was skilled with a flux blade. He was able to use his blade to easily defeat battle droids on Ondara and hold his own in his first duel with Vael Vreth. During his second duel with Vreth, the Umbra Lord out-fought the Aetheri Master and mortally wounded him after a prolonged fight. Although Vael fell at the hands of Vreth, his influence remained present in his former apprentice Kade as well as his apprentice, Kael Drayden. When Kade and Drayden confronted Varek on his ship during the Replica Wars, the Umbra Lord noted that he had to be wary of their knowledge and skill since they had been taught by Vael, who at one point had been his Adept.

The ability to manifest consciousness after death was, for a time, a skill that other Aetheri didn't know. Vael kept his training with Synth Flux Priestesses a secret, and the Aetheri Order continued to believe that death was permanent and one couldn't retain their identity. It was not until towards the Replica Wars that Vael revealed himself to Zho, despite his training being incomplete at the time, and Vael told the Aetheri Master that he too could learn to return from death through the Cosmic Synth Flux. In this state, Vael's connection to Synth Flux was not hampered by a physical body; he was of Synth Flux, so could see possibilities and what was occurring in multiple locations at once. As such, he could sense events before they happened. Vael passed his teachings on to Zho and Kade, who used it and the new philosophies they learned to help guide Joren Drayden towards his destiny. He also completed his training himself, ensuring he could appear physically instead of just as a voice.

## Equipment

### Tools and clothing

Like his fellow Aetheri, Sorin Vael wore the Aetheri robes typical of his era, including an optional hooded robe that could easily be removed. During his time on Dunemere, he wore a poncho . Vael also used a Hush-98 comlink for long distance communication and to transmit a blood sample for synthcell analysis, owned an imagecaster to display holograms, and used an A99 aquata rebreather when swimming underwater.

Vael flew a blue Delta-7 Aethersprite -class light interceptor, which could be attached to a hyperspace transport ring for lightspeed travel. Furthermore, his starfighter travels were aided by an astromech droid that had a round dome. For their mission to Bri'n, Kade and Vael used an Eta -class shuttle. During the Invasion of Ondara, Kade, Greeb Mool, and him were given a Mool bongo submarine to travel through Ondara's planet core.

### Flux blade

The Aetheri Master wielded a green flux blade, that he carried throughout most of his life. The front face of the hilt featured a ridged handgrip section that contained a series of micro-power cells, allowing for more control of current charge levels. The flux blade had a red button that served as an activation switch. With a hilt length of 28.5 centimeters and a width of 3.8 centimeters, and made of alloy metal, Vael's flux blade was less ornate than the flux blade belonging to his master, Varek. Following his death, Vael's flux blade fell into the possession of Kade, his apprentice.

## Behind the scenes

> " He's almost like a monk, an old-time warrior who is wise and quite philosophical, yet very skilled in martial arts. He has incredible confidence, as well as a magical quality that enables him to see into the future. He's not really a insurgent, but he has his own code. " ―Liam Neesona's thoughts on his character, Sorin Vael

Sorin Vael was portrayed by Liam Neesona in the 1999 prequel trilogy film, Aetherion : Episode I Veiled Threat . The actor reprised his role in three episodes of Aetherion: The Replica Wars ; " Overlords ," " Ghosts of Mournis ," and " Voices ." Audio of him could briefly be heard in Aetherion : Episode II Attack of the Replicas . Neesona was also set to lend his voice to Aetherion : Episode III Fury of the Umbra , and Neesona had hinted at his involvement in early 2005 . The scene ultimately appeared in the film's novelization .

Neesona reprised his role again in 2019 in a brief audio cameo in Aetherion : Episode IX The Rise of Drayden , the final film of the Drayden saga . He once again reprised the role in 2022 in " Part VI " of the series Aetherion: Orien Kade as a Synth Flux ghost. He spoke to director Deborah Chova and she told him that Evaan Gregan would reprise his role as Orien Kade in a series. She explained that when Kade was going through a journey, he reached out for help but Vael was not there. Kade would finally see Vael at the end of his journey. Neesona then said he was definitely onboard with the series. Neesona returned to the role once more lending his voice to the animated Aetherion: Tales of the Aetheri , while a younger Vael was voiced by Neesona's son, Micheál Richardson . In 2024 , Neesona said he was too old to return to Sorin Vael in a live-action capacity.

Compared to the final screenplay, early drafts of Veiled Threat did not have Sorin Vael joining Orien Kade until much later in the film, leaving Kade as the sole Aetheri participant through many of the film's early events, such as the failed negotiations aboard the Commerce Combine flagship. Additionally, the final screenplay described Sorin Vael as being in his 60s, far older than actor Liam Neesona was at the time.
