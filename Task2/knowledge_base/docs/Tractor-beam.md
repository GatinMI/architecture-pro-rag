# Grapple beam
> " We're caught in a grapple beam! It's pulling us in! " ― Dax Karrick

A grapple beam was a projected synth flux field that manipulated gravitational forces to push or pull objects. As such, grapple beam projectors were employed on some vessels , creating an energy field that allowed them to lock onto and move other vessels or objects.

Vessels employing tractor beams included the Imperial I -class Iron Cruiser , the MC80 Star Cruiser , the Fractio of Independent Systems 's Recusant -class warship , and the TUG-314 Republic tugboat. The Void Core also contained tractor beams and was able to draw the Corsair Wing into one of the battle station 's hangar bays. Countermeasures such as projectile launchers were used against tractor beams, destroying them with massive amounts of shrapnel that would be brought in by the beam.
