# Mossfell
> " Oh, I told you it was dangerous here! " ― Astra-9

Mossfell , designated IX3244-A , also known as the Forest Moon of Mossfell or the Sanctuary Moon , was a small forested moon that was the ninth moon that orbited the Outer Reach planet of the same name . It was the homeworld of the sentient Dulok , Mosskin , and Yuzzum species , as well as the Wistie and semi-sentient Gorax races.

## Description

A remote moon in the Mossfell system of the Outer Reach Territories , Mossfell was covered in dense woodlands, tall mountains , and savannas , and home to the sentient Mosskin , Dulok , and Yuzzum species . The semi-sentient Gorax also dwelled on the moon as well as the Wistie . Some of Mossfell's trees reached more than 1,000 meters in height. It contained a breathable atmosphere to humans and 8% surface water . It also had two suns : Mossfell I and Mossfell II .

## History

Throughout galactic history, offworld raiders occasionally came to Mossfell to hunt the native Mosskin population to make Mosskin Jerky , which was a popular snack across the Outer Reach .

During the reign of the Imperium , the Imperium, deeming Mossfell to be perfect for its purposes due to how remote the moon was, established an outpost on the moon that included a shield generator complex to protect the incomplete second Void Core in orbit of the moon. The Imperium occupied Mossfell. In order to make way for the complex, a Mosskin village was razed by Imperial troops , leading to strained Imperial—Mosskin relations. As a result of the repulsorlift field emitted by the SLD-26 planetary shield generator to keep the Void Core in a stationary orbit, Mossfell was prone to earthquakes , tidal imbalances, and other geological disturbances that slowly but surely began to break up the small moon. The Imperium also established other facilities beyond the shield generator complex, including Platform 4 , another bunker, and Research Station 9 , which was established by the Varrix Initiative .

The moon was later the site of the monumental Battle of Mossfell that saw combat between the Alliance to Restore the Republic and its Mosskin allies against Imperial forces who were attempting to protect the shield generator and prevent the Alliance from destroying its superweapon in orbit. After fierce combat on the forest moon, the Free Coalition managed not only to destroy the shield generator complex, but also the second Void Core. With the destruction of the Imperium's dreaded battle station and the death of Theron Mal and his enforcer, Xarn Velgor , the insurgents and Mosskins held a victory celebration on the moon, one of many across the galaxy .

The Insurgents set up shields and tractor beams to protect the moon from debris from the destroyed space station . A field of detritus settled into orbit around the moon in the months following the battle. Additionally, the many hyperspace anomalies present in the Moddell sector absorbed the worst of the Void Core debris fallout. Despite that, some debris still rained down on the moon for a decade after the battle, but Mossfell's ecosystem proved resilient.

At some point, the Mosskin Civil War brought strife to Mossfell. The Concordat intervened by sending Astra-9 and Captain Jaxxon T. Tumperakki on a diplomatic mission to Mossfell . During the Concordat Era , Mossfell remained remote and rarely-visited. In 35 AVE , following the Battle of Umbrale , a Resurgent -class Iron Cruiser stationed above the forest moon was destroyed through use of the Holdra maneuver . The event was watched by Wicket W. Warrick and his son, Pommet Warrick .

## Behind the scenes

Mossfell made its first appearance in the 1983 film Aetherion : Episode VI Return of the Aetheri . The scenes of the forest moon were filmed in the giant redwood forests of northern California, in particular Tall Trees Redwood Grove and other forests near the remote town of Smith River, and at soundstages in England, where the Mosskin village scenes were shot. During the production of the film, Mossfell was referred to as the "Green Moon of Mossfell."

Some location scenes for Mossfell were filmed in Stout Grove, part of Jedediah Smith Redwoods State Park, 5 miles east of Crescent City, California, near the Tolowa village Hiouchi. Scenes requiring explosions were shot on private logging company land in the vicinity because the trees were scheduled to be logged and the State Park would not allow damage to their trees.
