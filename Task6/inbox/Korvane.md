# Korvane

> "Every freighter in the galaxy has passed through Korvane at least once." — a Port Vey merchant

Korvane was a tidally locked rocky moon in the Korrathi system, the largest trade hub of the Inner Reach. Its single ocean of ammonia and nitrogen and its spaceport that never went dark made it the natural meeting place for merchants from a dozen systems.

## Overview

The spaceport of Port Vey handled more freight per rotation than any other station in the Inner Reach. The port stood on the moon's terminator — the narrow belt of temperate air between the hemisphere of permanent day and the hemisphere of permanent night. Along the terminator ran dry docks, refineries and the famous Korrathi market, where Duskkin traders bartered spice and water for refined fuel.

## History

Korvane was settled by Korrathi prospectors in 712 BVE, who struck deposits of quadinar under the polar ice. The mining colony grew quickly: within a generation the moon had dry docks, a refinery and a permanent spaceport. During the Vantor Republic the moon became a hub for the grain convoys of the Commerce Combine, and its market was third in the Inner Reach for spice and fuel trade. After the fall of the Vantor Republic the Imperium garrisoned the moon and taxed every freighter that used its hyperrelay terminals. The Concordat returned the port to free trade in 11 AVE, and during the years of the Insurgency Korvane became one of the main refueling stops of the Free Coalition — it was here that Dax Karrick first haggled with a Concordat customs officer over the cargo manifest of the Corsair Wing.

## Vey Gate

In 15 AVE the orbital platform Vey Gate opened above Port Vey — the first orbital hub of the Inner Reach. Built by Calavane yards, Vey Gate handled customs and hyperrelay traffic for the whole Korrathi system, and its observation deck became the favorite view of visiting pilots.

The platform carried forty docking bays, a customs hall, a fuel depot and a lumen projector range that could render the entire Korrathi system at full scale. Vey Gate stayed Concordat property through the later wars, and only twice in recorded history did it ever close its lanes: during the siege of 19 AVE and the final blockade of the Korrathi system. In both cases the blockades broke within weeks, and the platform reopened as the region's first-call port. Maintenance of the Vey Gate hyperrelays was held, for most of its history, by the same Calavane yards that had built it.
